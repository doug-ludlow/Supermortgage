### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Servicing Guide E-4.1-01, Notifying Fannie Mae of an Acquired Property (05/10/2023; Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026).** When a foreclosure sale occurs or a Mortgage Release is executed the servicer provides two notifications: the REOgram confirmation in Property 360 and the removal transaction through investor reporting (5.3). The servicer "must review and confirm the REOgram notification in Property 360 **within one business day upon receipt of the daily REOgram notification** from Property 360" and "review and resolve any exceptions from the daily notification in Property 360 **no later than three business days**." In jurisdictions where title vests by court order without a foreclosure sale, confirmation is due "within one business day of when the court's order is entered on the docket." The rule applies to all conventional loans and to special servicing option RD loans "even before clear title is obtained post-redemption." "Fannie Mae may charge the servicer a compensatory fee for each day it is late in confirmation" absent a reasonable explanation (A1-4.2-02); CRS receipt code **313** "Delayed REOgram Notification Fees" is the billing vehicle (CRS Remittance Codes, Oct. 15, 2025). By confirming, the servicer represents that all required resale-restriction notices were provided, the foreclosure/Mortgage Release complied with any restriction agreement, and actions terminating resale restrictions were completed. After confirmation the servicer must monitor the property's status and file timely expense reimbursement requests (E-5-01 → 15.2).

**Property 360 REOgram Notification User Guide (06/24/2026).** Cases are auto-created from SIR loan action codes 70/72, DRA foreclosure-sale events entered by the law firm, and eBoutique (reverse); a daily e-mail lists loans in the Potential, Exception or Confirmed queues. Statuses: **Potential** → **Confirmed** (manual or auto) → **Accepted** (onboarded to REO inventory at 7:00 p.m. ET) or **Exceptions** (validation failures; overridable exceptions may be overridden up to two attempts, non-overridable ones require correction in the source system) or **Eliminated**. Editable fields for **5 business days** after confirmation: servicer loan number, borrower/co-borrower names, property address/unit/city/county/ZIP, property type, MI indicator/company/certificate/coverage %, foreclosure or DIL attorney. Roles `PROP360-PROD-REOGRAM-DECISION` (confirm/reset) and `PROP360-PROD-REOGRAM-READONLY`; escalation mailbox `npdc_reogram@fanniemae.com`. **No bulk upload or API exists for REOgram confirmation** → portal-only.

**E-4.1-02, Eliminations and Rescissions of Foreclosure Sales (07/14/2021).** "Elimination" removes the property from Fannie Mae's REO inventory; "rescission" reverses the sale and removes Fannie Mae as titleholder. The servicer must submit the **Elimination/Rescission Request Template** (Excel) "within five days" of identifying the issue; on approval, re-add the file to its systems "within 24 hours of notification," resume Guide-standard management, coordinate with counsel, and initiate title-restoration steps "within two days." Approvals and Fannie Mae-initiated actions appear on the **Eliminations/Rescissions Daily Report** (Fannie Mae Connect). Fannie Mae may assess compensatory fees for servicer-caused rescissions and does not reimburse foreclosure fees for the re-foreclosure.

**E-4.2-01, Completing Conveyance Documents (04/10/2019).** Use the deed "customarily used to convey a property in the jurisdiction," with street address, unit and legal description; when the foreclosure was in the servicer's name and title vested in the servicer, the deed to Fannie Mae must be "submitted for recordation … the day following the foreclosure sale" (or court-order entry); counsel must vest title "in a manner that will not result in the imposition of a transfer tax"; the servicer is responsible for "failure to obtain good and marketable title in a timely manner" and must retain the foreclosure deed/Mortgage Release documents and any marketing-relevant material in the loan file. Address for instruments of record (E-3.1-03 → F-4-02): "Fannie Mae, 5600 Granite Parkway VII, Plano, TX 75024." E-3.5-03: no owner's title policy post-sale except Hawaii non-judicial or when Fannie Mae/the MI directs (not reimbursed otherwise). **E-4.2-02 (08/17/2016)** covers reconveyance to an insurer/guarantor that takes title (rare for conventional MI — acquisition option); failure exposes the servicer to A1-3-02 remedies.

**E-4.3-01…04 (03/08/2023; 11/13/2019; 11/12/2014; 11/12/2014).** The servicer must "cease property preservation activities following the foreclosure sale" or court-order vesting "regardless of whether the title transfer has completed (such as during redemption periods)"; Fannie Mae assumes ground rents, taxes, HOA/condo/co-op fees and designates "a broker, agent, vendor, or property management company"; "Fannie Mae must approve all repair and marketing costs"; the servicer ensures the deed is recorded and reports post-sale management/marketing problems to SF CPM (E-4.3-03). Post-sale inspections are performed by Fannie Mae's designee except when Fannie Mae or FHA/VA require the servicer to inspect (reimbursed only then, E-4.3-02). Evictions: Fannie Mae initiates and manages evictions for properties it will dispose of (conventional portfolio and special servicing option MBS); the servicer must provide documentation for the notice to vacate and "return requested documents as soon as possible, or request that the law firm … provide the documents … within three business days," and schedule the lock-out with Fannie Mae's eviction attorney. The servicer retains counsel only for government loans to be conveyed and for regular servicing option (shared-risk) MBS loans where it has disposition responsibility — then it obtains state maximum eviction fees from SF CPM, pays counsel promptly, files 1099-MISC, files supplemental MI claims for claimable eviction costs and "aggressively monitor[s]" counsel; reimbursement via F-1-05. The Guide is silent on cash-for-keys amounts and relocation assistance (Fannie Mae's vendor handles them) and on the Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act — the PTFA (Pub. L. 111-22 title VII, restored by Pub. L. 115-174 §304, May 2018) binds the "immediate successor in interest" to a 90-day notice to vacate and to bona fide leases, so it governs the servicer-retained-counsel path and the information we supply Fannie Mae.

**E-4.4-01…04 (11/13/2019; 04/10/2019).** "Within 14 days after the foreclosure sale or acceptance of an executed Mortgage Release" the servicer must ask the carrier to cancel the property policy and refund unearned premium, and if the carrier refuses (Fannie Mae not the named insured) ask it to remove Fannie Mae's mortgagee interest; the same 14-day rule cancels flood coverage "unless prohibited by the policy or applicable law" (E-4.4-03); Fannie Mae's property recovery firms file post-sale loss claims and the servicer must "provide policy and inspection information to recovery firms within three business days upon request." Loss proceeds received after the sale are remitted through CRS; unearned premium refunds are either remitted (code **318** "Hazard Insurance Premium Refund Proceeds") or offset on the next expense request; if a carrier refuses a refund the servicer must say so on its final expense request, otherwise Fannie Mae computes the expected refund and treats it as an unremitted amount due (E-4.4-02).

**Third-party sales — E-3.5-02 (07/12/2017), F-1-08 (05/10/2023), F-1-20 (03/11/2026), Property 360 Liquidation Reconciliation User Guide (04/03/2025).** The servicer must "collect the gross sale proceeds and remit the amount Fannie Mae is due **within five business days after it receives the final payment** from the third-party bidder" (CRS **311**, curtailment portion **351**); the amount due is "the lesser of" total indebtedness — "the UPB of the mortgage loan and interest (based on the applicable PTR) for the period from the due date of the LPI to the latter of the liquidation or settlement date" — or the restricted resale price; surplus is "distributed in accordance with applicable law"; if the sale does not finalize, "remit the third-party bidder's initial deposit to Fannie Mae within five business days after discovery"; cancel insurance within 14 days of completion/court confirmation; continue inspections and Matrix preservation until the sale completion date; forward the closing statement to SF CPM the same day funds are remitted with a breakdown of principal, interest, servicing fees, advances and other items through the sale date; report action code 71. Property 360 auto-creates **TPS cases** from SIR action code 71 and DRA sale events; the servicer (role `PROP360-PROD-TPS-DECISION`) updates the Foreclosure Sale Information screen (attorney-reported sale date, bid type, foreclosure bid amount, successful bid amount, judgment amount for judicial states, attorney, "Servicer TPS Proceeds Deposit Date"), uploads bidding instructions, the proceeds check/wire copy and, for judicial sales, the final judgment; statuses Intake → Recon Ready → Recon in Progress → On Hold → Reconciled / Servicer Billed; exceptions 217/302/305 (missing or partial proceeds) and 219 (missing documents) are cleared by remitting code 311 or uploading documents/requesting overrides. The guide states no servicer deadline for the P360 steps; the operative clocks are the 5-BD remittance and the 30/60-day expense-claim rules (15.2).

**LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026).** Liquidation events — Government Conveyance, REO, Third-Party Sale — will be reported directly to Property 360, "eliminating REOgram duplication"; CIT 11/02/2026–02/12/2027, go-live March 2027 **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Implementation Timeline v3.0 row alignment; research/00a §3.1, 00b F4]**. Until then LAR 70/71/72 + REOgram/TPS remain the rails.

**Other law.** State redemption periods (e.g., MN/MI six months, AL one year) and court-confirmation regimes (OH, IL, NJ sheriff's deed) shift when title vests and when MI claims are due (15.3) but not the 1-BD REOgram clock for conventional loans; state transfer-tax exemptions for FNMA deeds vary; SCRA §3953 (50 U.S.C. §3953) makes a sale during protected service voidable (Section 13 gate; a voided sale becomes an E-4.1-02 rescission); bankruptcy stays (11 U.S.C. §362) likewise; FDCPA/Reg F apply to any post-sale communication with the former borrower about the debt (deficiency), not to key/possession logistics, which Fannie Mae's vendor runs.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** the governing topics are E-4.1-01/02, E-4.2-01, E-4.3-01…04, E-4.4-01…04, E-3.5-02/03, F-1-08 and F-1-20 — not "E-4.3" alone; the deadlines are concrete (1 BD / 3 BD / 5 BD / 14 days / 5 days / 24 hours / 2 days / next-day deed recording), not "per Fannie Mae"; "conveyance to Fannie Mae" is mostly automatic (Fannie Mae is the grantee at sale) with the servicer's duties being deed recording, title marketability and the REO handoff; the third-party-sale reconciliation (TPS) and eliminations/rescissions are missing from the row; automation is (b) only because REOgram/TPS confirmation is portal-bound — everything else is (a).

#### State machine
`reo_cases.status`: `sale_reported` (fact received) → `awaiting_p360_case` (expect notification ≤1 BD after LAR acceptance/DRA entry) → `pending_confirmation` (notification received; 1-BD clock) → `confirmed` → `accepted` (7 p.m. ET onboarding) → `handoff_in_progress` (deed/insurance/eviction-doc/recovery-firm tasks) → `handoff_complete` → `claims_open` (15.2/15.3 running) → `closed` (final claim paid, MI settled, advances recovered). Side states: `exception` (3-BD clock; overrides ≤2 attempts, else source-system correction via `investor-reporting`), `elimination_requested`/`rescinded` (E-4.1-02; returns the loan to active servicing — `sda_status`, delinquency and foreclosure cases reopen), `reconveyance_pending` (E-4.2-02, MI acquisition option). `tps_cases.status`: `sale_reported` → `awaiting_final_payment` → `proceeds_received` → `remitted` (311/351 instructed and matched) → `documented` (P360 uploads complete) → `reconciled` (P360 Reconciled) → `closed` (final claim + MI). Side: `sale_failed` (deposit remitted ≤5 BD; foreclosure re-set), `servicer_billed` (P360 says we owe — reconcile or dispute via IRT). Transitions by the `claims-reo` agent except `confirmed`/uploads (`fnma_portal_operator`) and `accepted`/`reconciled` (Fannie Mae).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Case creation and code linkage.** Purchaser `fnma` (credit bid) → `reo_cases` + LAR **70** (uninsured) or **72** (MI/FHA/VA insured) per 5.3; purchaser `third_party` → `tps_cases` + LAR **71**. A Mortgage Release opens `reo_cases` with `acquisition_type = mortgage_release` on `dil.deed.recorded`.
2. **Confirmation clock.** `confirm_due_at = nextBusinessDayEnd(notification_received_at, 'fannie_et')` — i.e., a notification received Wed Oct 6, 2027 (any time) must be confirmed by close of business Thu Oct 7, 2027; a notification received Wed Nov 24, 2027 is due Fri Nov 26 (Nov 25 Thanksgiving). Court-order jurisdictions anchor at docket entry. The agent pre-builds the package on `foreclosure.sale.held` so the operator's task is a review-and-click.
3. **REOgram package (portal task).** `{fnma_loan_number, servicer_loan_number, borrower names, property address/unit/county, property_type, mi_indicator/company/certificate/coverage_pct (from mi_policies), fcl/DIL attorney, sale/deed date, purchaser, successful bid, occupancy per last inspection, keys/lockbox code and preservation vendor contact, HOA/utility notes, resale-restriction attestation (checkbox evidence: notices provided, compliance with restriction agreement, termination actions completed), known exceptions and their proposed overrides}` — the operator confirms field-by-field against the P360 case; discrepancies (e.g., wrong MI company) are edited within the 5-BD window or escalated to `npdc_reogram@fanniemae.com`.
4. **Resale-restriction representation.** If `properties.resale_restriction` ≠ none (NSP, Community Land Trust, inclusionary/shared-equity), confirmation is gated on evidence that the restriction holder received the required notices (Section 13 pre-sale) — `assertGateOpen(loanId, 'SM_RESALE_RESTRICTION_NOTICES')`; missing evidence → escalate to `attorney` before confirming (the representation is made under the partner's servicer number).
5. **Deed and title.** If `foreclosed_in_name_of = servicer` (or MERS assignee), `deed_record_due = legal_date + 1 day` (next recording day); grantee = "Federal National Mortgage Association" at the F-4-02 address; the firm's deed package must confirm the transfer-tax exemption approach for the county (`jurisdiction_rules.transfer_tax_exempt_fnma`). If foreclosed directly in Fannie Mae's name (trustee's deed to the successful bidder, e.g., Texas), the task is to obtain the recorded deed copy within `SM_TITLE_MARKETABLE_30` and retain it (E-4.2-01).
6. **Preservation stop and expense cut-offs.** On `reo_cases` creation the `FNMA_E4301_PRESERVATION_STOP` gate closes 9.9 work orders (emergency orders after the sale require SF CPM direction and are flagged non-reimbursable by default); escrow/insurance/HOA advances remain reimbursable only "up to 14 days after" the sale/Mortgage Release (F-1-05) — the `advances` rows after that date are tagged `post_sale_nonreimbursable` (15.2 rule 4).
7. **Insurance.** Two-step per E-4.4-01: cancellation + refund request (9.5 flow) by day 14; if refused, mortgagee-interest removal request; the refund, when received, is credited on the final 571 claim or remitted with code 318 if the claim was already paid (15.2 rule 7); LPI policies are cancelled as of the sale date (9.5). Flood: cancel unless the policy/law prohibits.
8. **Third-party sale arithmetic (F-1-20).** `fnma_total_indebtedness = UPB + PTR interest from LPI due date to max(liquidation_date, settlement_date)`; `amount_due_fnma = min(gross_proceeds, fnma_total_indebtedness, restricted_resale_price?)`; `servicer_recovery = min(gross_proceeds − amount_due_fnma, unrecovered advances + fees per E-3.3-05 total-debt order)`; `surplus = gross_proceeds − amount_due_fnma − servicer_recovery` → distributed per applicable law (never remitted to Fannie Mae). Interest uses PTR, 30/360 months plus actual days ÷ 365 for the stub **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — F-1-20 states the period, not the day-count; align with 5.3's removal-interest convention and confirm with the Investor Reporting Representative]**. Rounding: half-up to cents at each component.
   *Worked example (fixture L15, TPS variant):* UPB $249,088.61, PTR 6.00%, LPI due date Nov 1, 2026, sale and settlement Tue Oct 5, 2027, successful third-party bid $270,000.00 paid in full the same day. Monthly PTR interest = 249,088.61 × 0.06 ÷ 12 = $1,245.44; 11 full months (Nov 1, 2026 → Oct 1, 2027) = $13,699.87; stub Oct 1–5 = 4 days × (249,088.61 × 0.06 ÷ 365 = $40.946) = $163.78; `fnma_total_indebtedness` = **$262,952.26**; `amount_due_fnma` = $262,952.26 (< $270,000); CRS **311** for $262,952.26 due within 5 `fannie_et` BD of Oct 5 → Oct 6, 7, 8, 12, 13 (Oct 11 Columbus Day) → settle by **Wed Oct 13, 2027** (instruction entered by Tue Oct 12, 16:00 ET); closing statement to SF CPM on the remittance date. Servicer's unrecovered items: advances $11,219.94 (taxes $4,820.00, hazard $1,450.00, MI premiums 11 × $124.54 = $1,369.94, inspections 9 × $30 = $270.00, preservation $445.00, attorney fee $2,300.00, costs $565.00) plus note-rate/PTR spread interest (0.25% × 11 months + stub ≈ $577.66); `servicer_recovery` = 270,000.00 − 262,952.26 = **$7,047.74** applied FIFO to reimbursable advances first; surplus $0; remaining advances $4,172.20 claimed on the 571 (15.2), spread interest written off (not reimbursable). If instead the bid were $280,000: 311 = $262,952.26, servicer recovery = $11,219.94, surplus $5,827.80 per state law (junior liens → borrower), 571 claim $0 for advances.
9. **Failed third-party sale.** Deposit remitted with code 311 within 5 BD of discovery; the foreclosure case reopens for re-sale (Section 13); a refund of over-remitted amounts is requested through a 571 claim/IRT ("regardless of whether or not the sale is finalized or falls through," 5.3 edge case).
10. **Eliminations/rescissions.** Triggers: sale set aside (court), bankruptcy-stay or SCRA violation discovered, reinstatement funds accepted pre-sale but sale held, wrong loan, title defect. Within 5 days the agent completes the Excel template (loan, property, reason, requested action, supporting documents) for `fnma_portal_operator` e-mail submission; on approval the loan is re-activated within 24 h (`loan.reactivated` → 5.x re-add via `readd_requests@fanniemae.com` if the LAR removal was accepted; `sda_status`, escrow, statements resume) and counsel is instructed within 2 days on title restoration. Re-foreclosure attorney fees are **not reimbursable** when the rescission was servicer-caused (tag `fees.nonreimbursable_reason = 'e4102_rescission'`).
11. **Compensatory-fee exposure.** `late_days = businessDaysBetween(confirm_due_at, confirmed_at)`; the daily fee formula is not published in E-4.1-01/A1-4.2-02 (**[UNVERIFIED]**); the platform records the exposure, the reasonable-explanation evidence (outage, notification not received) and any code-313 draft for reconciliation and rebuttal.
12. **LL-2026-05 rail.** When `investor_reporting.liquidation.mode = event`, the same facts project a P360 liquidation event (REO / TPS / Government Conveyance) instead of LAR 70/71/72 (5.3 rule 9); REOgram confirmation tasks remain until Fannie Mae confirms the round-trip is subsumed.

#### Integrations
- **Property 360 REOgram / TPS (portal-only).** `human_portal_task` types `p360.reogram.confirm`, `p360.reogram.exception`, `p360.tps.update_upload`, each with the package above, the P360 case id (parsed from the notification e-mail), field-by-field expected values, and a required evidence capture (case export/screenshot hashed into `documents`). Notification e-mails are parsed by `integrations/email-in` (subject/loan list) into `reogram_confirmations`. No API; no scraping.
- **`fnma-lsdu` / `fnma-servicing-events`** — removal LARs and future liquidation events (5.3). **`fnma-crs`** — codes 311/351 (TPS), 310 (REO sale proceeds if ever received by the servicer), 314 (redemption proceeds), 317 (escrow balance of a liquidated loan **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — code list only]**), 318 (hazard refund), 313 (delayed REOgram fee draft, inbound). **`fnma-connect`** — Eliminations/Rescissions Daily Report; REO status/disposition reports feeding the E-5-01 60-day clock.
- **`attorney-network`** — sale results, deed status, DRA event copies, eviction-document requests; **`insurance-tracking/lpi`** — cancellation/refund workflow; **`preservation`** — stop-work and vendor keys/lockbox handoff; **`erecording`** — deed submission where the servicer records.
- Failure modes: notification e-mail missing → `SM_REOGRAM_CASE_EXPECTED_1BD` prompts the operator to check the Potential queue; P360 outage → document the outage (timestamped attempts), confirm the next BD, keep the evidence for the compensatory-fee rebuttal; CRS cut-off missed → settle next BD and log the late-remittance exposure.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Records: `reo_cases`, `reogram_confirmations`, `tps_cases`, `reo_handoff_tasks`, `elimination_rescission_requests`, `documents` (recorded deed, closing statement, bidding instructions, proceeds evidence, P360 evidence), `remittances` (311/351/318), `investor_events` (5.3), `agent_decisions`.
- Ledger: proceeds Dr `custodial_pi_cash` Cr `fnma_remittance_payable` (amount due Fannie Mae) and Dr corporate cash Cr `corporate_advances`/`escrow_advances` for `servicer_recovery`; surplus Dr custodial Cr `surplus_payable` (paid per state law); loan sub-ledgers closed to `deficiency_receivable` (memo) or written off per 15.2.
- No borrower notices (post-sale occupant communications are Fannie Mae's vendor's; servicer-counsel evictions use counsel's PTFA/state notices, logged as `notices` with `template = external_counsel`).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`claims-reo` agent owns the case end-to-end. Tools: `openReoCase`, `computeTpsSplit`, `buildReogramPackage`, `openPortalTask`, `parseP360Notification`, `scheduleHandoffTasks`, `requestInsuranceCancellation` (9.5), `buildCrsBatch` (5.2), `draftClosingStatement`, `draftEliminationTemplate`, `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{loan_id, case_id, acquisition_type, legal_date, title_vests_at, purchaser, action_code, amounts{indebtedness, due_fnma, recovery, surplus}, package_fields[], gates_checked[], confidence, escalations[]}`. Guardrails: never confirm a REOgram without the resale-restriction gate and MI data check; never remit more than `amount_due_fnma`; never order preservation after the sale for Fannie Mae-acquired property; confidence < 0.9 on purchaser type, bid amount or vesting date → hold and escalate before deadline − 4 h. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (P360/CRS/e-mail template steps); `attorney` (deed/title/rescission/eviction documents, PTFA); `officer` (compensatory-fee rebuttals, A1-3-02 make-whole responses). Toggle-off path: the package generator runs deterministically and a human reviews/approves each case in `ops-console`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Sale held but DRA not entered** → P360 case may not exist; the 5.3 DRA reconciliation fires a sev-1 and the operator confirms via the Potential queue once created; document the firm's delay for compensatory-fee defense.
- **Redemption-period states**: REOgram still due within 1 BD of the sale; `title_vests_at` = redemption expiry; MI claim clock uses redemption expiry (15.3); redemption by the borrower → proceeds code 314 and elimination request.
- **Bankruptcy filed before the sale** (stay) or **SCRA** discovered after → rescission path; fees for re-foreclosure non-reimbursable if servicer-caused.
- **Transfer-out after sale**: Form 629 lists acquired properties separately (A2-7-03); open claims and P360 cases transfer with the servicing — export the package (MISMO projection) and hand off the pending timers.
- **Successor-in-interest / tenant occupancy**: PTFA/state tenant rights information passed to Fannie Mae's eviction counsel; the servicer never initiates lock-outs for Fannie Mae-managed properties.
- **Disaster after sale**: Fannie Mae's recovery firm files claims; we supply policy data within 3 BD.
- **Partial data** (bid amount unknown at sale): confirm with known fields, edit within 5 BD, or raise an exception override with evidence.
- **Sale to servicer for Fannie Mae's benefit** (some states) → treat as `fnma` purchaser with `foreclosed_in_name_of = servicer` and next-day deed.
- **Duplicate cases** (SIR and DRA both create) → confirm the surviving case; elimination for the duplicate via `npdc_reogram@fanniemae.com`.
