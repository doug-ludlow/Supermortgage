# Section 23 — Desktop Underwriter and the credit decision

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 23 turns the verified application (§22) into a lender credit decision and the compliance determinations that make the loan saleable: 23.1 runs the DU casefile end to end (creation under the partner's seller number through the Direct Integration XML rail, credit association by reissue, submission types, the B3-2-10 resubmission tolerances, the unconditional "data must reflect the loan as it was closed" rule, the 270/540-day archival clocks, DU 12.1's policy generations of June 27 and Sept 26, 2026, the Sept 9, 2026 VantageScore 4.0 update and the JSON v2 findings format); 23.2 interprets every findings report (B3-2-05 to -08 recommendations, verification/eligibility/red-flag/observation/CU/validation message families) into `conditions`, investigations, MI and value-acceptance signals, HomeReady/AMI evaluation, homeownership-education gating and the restructuring loop; 23.3 records the lender's comprehensive risk assessment (B3-1-01), issues the conditional approval as the Reg B approval notification, runs the PTD/PTF condition lifecycle, the clear-to-close checklist and the rep-and-warrant relief ledger, and routes every adverse outcome through `underwriting_reviewer`; 23.4 makes the ATR/QM, HPML, HOEPA and state high-cost determinations at LE, lock, CD and consummation with the 2026 dollar tiers, the Reg Z "total loan amount" points-and-fees base, and Fannie Mae's B2-1.5-02 eligibility overlay.

**Agents.** `underwriter` (23.1–23.3: DU orchestration, findings interpretation, conditions, decision, clearing, CTC) and `compliance-tester` (23.4: deterministic determinations consuming 25.1's APR). Shared: `compliance-sentinel` (rule-set drift, annual threshold updates, DU release readiness), `qc-audit` (prefunding holds, red-flag sampling), `security-records` (Fannie Mae-confidential findings). Human touchpoints are limited to `underwriting_reviewer` (denials, counteroffers, NOIAs, waivers, decisions reopened inside three business days of closing, second reviews), `fnma_portal_operator` (DU UI fallback and Technology Manager administration), `officer` (state-law risk acceptance, points-and-fees cure payments, TSP credential changes), `qc_officer` (prefunding QC independence) and `licensed_specialist` where a state licenses underwriting activity.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the creditor (Reg B/Reg Z), the Fannie Mae seller under whose 9-digit number every casefile is created and whose representations (A2-2-01/-04/-06/-07) the decision supports, and the party that adopts the credit policy "DU Approve/Eligible only; no manual underwriting; no variances" (open decision 23.2-Q1). SM performs every step as Technology Service Provider under partner-org System IDs assigned to SM's TSP Product in Technology Manager, keeps the evidence, and never appears as creditor: the conditional-approval letter (`NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL`) and every notice carry the partner's name; DU findings never reach the borrower. DU fees are the partner's under its Software Subscription Agreement and are booked to SM-borne `third_party_costs` under the Grander economics.

**Dependencies on other origination processes.** Inbound: 21.1 (ULAD data, automated-underwriting consent), 21.4 (locks — rate-set date for APOR; decision validity), 21.5 (`changed_circumstances` for every restructure; `tolerance_tests`/`runToleranceTest` for TRID tolerances — distinct from DU tolerances), 21.6 (`REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30`, counteroffer/adverse-action notices, `adverse_actions`), 22.1 (`FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_DOCS_4M`, document freshness), 22.2 (`applications.score_model`, credit reports and refresh/expiry), 22.3 (`FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD`, `FNMA_B3_2_02_DU_CLOSE_BY_GATE`, validation reports), 22.4/22.5 (assets, reserves, liabilities, DTI inputs), 22.6 (identity/occupancy investigations), 24.1/24.2 (value-acceptance offer age, appraisal/CU, HPML appraisal mechanics), 24.3–24.6 (project, title, insurance/flood, MI certificate), 25.1 (`apr_calculations`), 25.2 (CD data for the final DU match; `issueCD` gate), 20.4 (LLPAs, undiscounted rate for bona fide discount points). Outbound: 25.2/26.1/26.3 (`ptd_cleared`, `clear_to_close`, `ptf_cleared`), 28.1 (prefunding selection and holds), 28.3 (action taken; rate-spread inputs), 29.3/29.4 (casefile ID, SFC set, relief components, `fnma_eligible`), 30.3 and servicing 3.x (`hpml_determinations` → escrow cancellation restriction), 31.2 (decision records for LL-2026-04 monitoring).

**Dependencies on the servicing spec.** Servicing 3.1/3.x enforce `REGZ_1026_35_HPML_ESCROW_5Y` on `escrow_accounts`; 5.1/6.x consume the purchase date that starts the 36-payment relief clock (A2-3.2-02); 9.x share `insurance_policies` evidence used by CTC; the shared `agent_decisions`, `escalations`, `timers`, `documents` and `loan_events` tables carry every artifact defined here.

### Section-level test plan and sequencing

**Build order.** (1) 23.4's rule sets and tables first (`regz.qm.general.2021` with 2026 tiers, `regz.hpml`, `regz.hoepa`, `jurisdiction_rules` state definitions, `apor_tables` ingestion) because they are pure computation with no external dependency other than 25.1's APR engine and public FFIEC data — they belong to Stage O-1 (foundation) alongside the fee/tolerance engine. (2) 23.1's `integrations/fnma-du` adapter and the DU Specification Test Case Suite in the DU Integration environment (Stage O-2 rails; TSP onboarding 2–6 months is the critical path; JSON v2 findings and the Sept 25, 2026 message catalog from day one; Return File Types 16/17 never used). (3) 23.2's `du_message_rules` catalog, condition templates, HomeReady/AMI API adapter and education intake (Stage O-2/O-3). (4) 23.3's decision engine, clearing standards, CTC checklist, relief ledger and `underwriting_reviewer` console (Stage O-3 judgment), integrated with 21.6 notices and 28.1 holds last.

**Cross-process integration tests on the fixture calendar.**
- **O4-IT1 (refinance happy path).** Application Mon Oct 5, 2026 → credit received (22.2) → 23.1 casefile Tue Oct 6 (`policy_generation = 2026_09_26`), Approve/Eligible with a value-acceptance offer → 23.2 opens 14 PTD conditions and emits `sfc_required = {127, 007, 801}` → 23.3 conditional approval Wed Oct 7 (`NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL`; `valid_until` Nov 21) → 23.4 lock-stage determination Oct 7 (APOR 6.020%) → Oct 20 undisclosed liability (22.2) → 23.1 resubmission same day (DTI 38.00% → 41.75%, ≥ 3-point rule) → 23.2 supersedes/opens conditions → CTC Fri Oct 30 → 23.4 CD-stage determination Mon Nov 2 (APR 6.310%, spread 0.290, `general_safe_harbor`) → `SM_UW_PTD_CLEARED_GATE` open → CD delivered Nov 2 (25.2) → 23.1 final-match submission Tue Nov 3 → PTF (VVOE/credit refresh Thu Nov 5; 22.3/22.2) → consummation Fri Nov 6 → 23.4 consummation-stage re-run identical → rescission expiry Tue Nov 10 → funding Thu Nov 12 → delivery Mon Nov 16 with casefile ID and SFCs → purchase Thu Nov 19 → relief ledger `payment_history_36` opens (36th payment due Dec 1, 2029). ### Section open questions

1. **23.2-Q1 — DU Approve/Eligible only; no manual underwriting; no variances.** Default: adopt. Refer with Caution/Out of Scope/Approve/Ineligible loans are restructured or declined; no A2-2 "negotiated terms."
2. **23.1-Q1 — Always perform a final closed-loan-match DU submission even when B3-2-10 waives resubmission.** Default: yes.
3. **23.1-Q2 — Resubmission cap before human review.** Default: rationale after 10, `underwriting_reviewer` after 15 (DU's "excessive resubmissions" threshold is undocumented).
4. **23.1-Q3 — Findings formats.** Default: JSON v2 for automation plus the new standard PDF as the loan-file copy; never types 16/17.
5. **23.1-Q4 — Credential attribution of casefiles.** Default: partner-org System ID assigned to SM's TSP Product; confirm with the Fannie Mae Integration Team.
6. **23.1-Q5 — Reissue vs order-new credit through DU.** Default: reissue.
7. **23.1-Q6 — DU casefile fees in the SM cost model.** Default: `third_party_costs` at the partner's subscription rate (**[UNVERIFIED]**).
8. **23.2-Q2 — Minimum-coverage MI option.** Default: allowed when the borrower's all-in cost is lower.
9. **23.2-Q3 — Counteroffer trigger.** Default: any SM-initiated change of terms is a §1002.9 counteroffer; borrower-initiated changes are not.
10. **23.2-Q4 — Education provider acceptance.** Default: HomeView, HUD-approved agencies, NIS-aligned list; others to `underwriting_reviewer`.
11. **23.2-Q5 — AMI values.** Never hard-coded; DU/AMI API dataset is the source.
12. **23.2-Q6 — HomeReady financed-property cap (2).** **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** — confirm before enforcing.
13. **23.3-Q1 — Overlays.** Default: none beyond Fannie Mae eligibility; layering recorded for monitoring.
14. **23.3-Q2 — Identity of the `underwriting_reviewer`.** Default: partner employees; SM licensed staff only under written delegation and 31.1 licensing; appeals to a different person.
15. **23.3-Q3 — Conditional approval letter as the Reg B approval notification (`NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL`).** Default: yes; state commitment-letter content rules **[UNVERIFIED]**.
16. **23.3-Q4 — Gate for `issueCD`.** Default: `ptd_cleared` (not full CTC); CTC gates the closing appointment.
17. **23.3-Q5 — Decision validity cap.** Default: 90 days or the earliest expiring component.
18. **23.3-Q6 — Prefunding hold scope.** Default: selected loans held from CTC until the review closes; selection by day −7.
19. **23.4-Q1 — SM compensation under §1026.32(b)(1)(ii).** Default: legal position before launch; compute points and fees both ways and block on the inclusive figure meanwhile.
20. **23.4-Q2 — Originate rebuttable-presumption (HPML) QMs.** Default: yes with escrow enforced; partner flag available.
21. **23.4-Q3 — Fannie Mae's spread limit for loans below $137,958.** Default: apply the QM tier; confirm with Fannie Mae.
22. **23.4-Q4 — State coverage at launch.** Default: only states with quoted statute text in `jurisdiction_rules`.
23. **23.4-Q5 — Who funds a points-and-fees cure.** Default: creditor pays; SM reimburses for SM-retained fees; `officer` approves.
24. **23.4-Q6 — Treasury-yield/PMMS reference-date rules (NY, MA).** **[UNVERIFIED]** — states disabled until quoted.
