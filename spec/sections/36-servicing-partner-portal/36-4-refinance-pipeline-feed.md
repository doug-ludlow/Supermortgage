### Blueprint row
The partner asked for one thing beside the board: which of its MSR portfolio members is actively going through a refinance right now. The platform already knows, in rows it writes for its own reasons — 33.2 delivers the offer and 20.1 marks the opportunity `offered`, the homeowner's Yes marks it `engaged`, 33.3 opens the refinance application with `prior_loan_id` and writes the readiness checklist, 23.x runs DU, 21.2 and 25.2 deliver the LE and the CD, 23.3 clears to close, 26.x consummates and funds, 30.2 boards the new loan `active`, 35.10 retires the old one and links the two — and 34.3 shows most of it to staff on a loan's page. This process is the partner's feed over those rows and nothing more: one item per member in motion, its current stage, when it entered it and how many days ago, newest first; a detail with the member's stage history and, while the checklist is the stage, what is still missing in 33.3's words. Members who never left the board are not on the feed; a stage with no row is not shown; nothing is emitted, ordered, nudged or decided from here.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-16)
**Every stage is an owning section's row, projected (20.1; 33.2 rules 5–6; 33.3; 21.1, 21.6; 23.x; 21.2, 25.2; 23.3, 26.2, 26.3; 30.2; 35.10).** `refi_opportunities.status` moves `offer_ready → offered` when 33.2's pass delivers the offer (`refi.opportunity.offered`), `→ engaged` on the homeowner's Yes (`refi.opportunity.engaged`), `→ converted` when 33.3's `refi.open` creates the refinance application with `prior_loan_id` = the monitored loan (`refi.opportunity.converted`, `partner_book.refinance.opened`), `→ declined` on the homeowner's No (`refi.opportunity.declined`) and `→ expired` when `expireOffers` runs 20.1's date rule (`refi.opportunity.expired`); from there the application's own rows carry the file — `readiness_checks` (33.3), the DU casefile and its findings (23.x, `du.findings.received`), the delivered disclosures (21.2 `disclosure.le.delivered`, 25.2 `disclosure.cd.delivered`), clear to close (23.3 `clear_to_close.issued`), the `closings` row through `consummated` (26.2 `closing.consummated`), `loan.funded` (26.3), `loan.boarded` and `loan.active` on the new loan (30.2), `loans.refinanced_by_loan_id` and `applications.loan_id` on the link (35.10 `loan.staged`), and `applications.disposition` at the end (21.6: `originated`, `denied`, `withdrawn`, `closed_incomplete`, `approved_not_accepted`). The feed reads those rows and their events' timestamps and writes none of them. **[VERIFIED against src/domain/leads-pricing/ops-20-1.ts OpportunityStatus and its `refi.opportunity.*` events, src/runtime/partner-book-offers.ts deliverOffers / expireOffers, 33.3 rule 3, 21.6's disposition machine, 30.2's boarding machine and 35.10's closeout state machine.]**

**A member in motion stays on the board (33.2 rule 5; 36.3 rule 1).** 33.2's review pass continues an open offer: an opportunity `offer_ready`, `offered`, `engaged` or `converted` reads verdict `candidate` on the day's review, so the loan is listed on Eligible now with `pipeline_stage` set while its item is on the feed; when the offer expires the next review is free to write `not_now` with the cooldown, and when the new loan boards the prior loan pays off through 35.10 and leaves the board. The feed is therefore not a fourth bucket: it is the same member, seen in motion. **[VERIFIED against src/runtime/partner-book-review.ts CANDIDATE_STATUSES (`offer_ready | offered | engaged | converted`) and 33.2-T2; the board's rule is 36.3's.]**

**Nothing is emitted, ordered or decided here (20.1 rules 7–8; FCRA §604(a); 33.2 `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES`; 33.3 `NO_PULL_BEFORE_YES`, `YES_REQUIRED`).** The feed reads no consumer report and orders nothing; it emits no `offer.deliver`, `offer.expire`, `refi.open`, application, DU, disclosure, closing or boarding event; the partner cannot nudge a homeowner, cancel or re-send an offer, withdraw or decline an application, or change a stage — every transition is the homeowner's own act or an owning section's pass. **[VERIFIED — the owners' guardrails, unchanged; the brief's rules 2, 3 and 11.]**

**The partner sees its own members, masked partner-grade (36.1; 16 CFR §314.4(c)(1); GLBA §1016.13).** Every item is `partner_party_id = session.partner_party_id`; another partner's items are absent from the feed and its detail is `404 NOT_FOUND`, never `403`; an item carries the servicer loan number's last four and the homeowner's first name and last initial, and no e-mail, phone, SSN, DOB, ZIP, street, score, DTI or investor column — the stage and its dates are the only facts added to 36.3's row. **[VERIFIED — the rule text; the mask is 36.1's.]**

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) The brief names the terminal stages `declined` / `withdrawn` and says to use the names that already exist: those are 20.1's `declined` (the homeowner's No on the offer, `refi_opportunities.status = declined`) and 21.6's terminal dispositions `withdrawn`, `denied`, `closed_incomplete` and `approved_not_accepted`; the feed uses each as it is stored and mints no umbrella word. (2) The LE is due within three business days of the TRID application (21.2) and can land before the DU run; the current stage is the furthest reached in the declared order, so a member whose LE landed first reads `disclosures` and the later `du` row is recorded in the detail's `stages[]` in time order without stepping the current stage back. (3) `boarded` is the end of the feed, not a refusal: the item stays on the feed at `boarded` and is excluded from Home's `in_flight` (36.5) with the terminal names. (4) The brief's three banner sentences have none for the retired prior loan 36.4-T4 opens ("paid off / refinanced"): DELTA-03 in docs/partner-portal/BACKEND-DELTAS.md — until accepted the old loan's `banner` is null and its `status` and `refinanced_by_loan_id` say what happened (rule 7).

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `GET /v1/partner/pipeline` → `{ items: PipelineItem[] }`, newest `entered_at` first. `PipelineItem` = `{ loan_id, servicer_loan_last4, homeowner: { legal_name } (first name and last initial), stage, entered_at, days_in_stage, opportunity_id?, application_id? }`. No query key in V1; any key is dropped unread.
- `GET /v1/partner/pipeline/:loan_id` → `{ loan: PartnerLoanRow, stages: PipelineStage[], current: stage | null }` for the member's loan (the monitored loan, or the same row once 35.10 has retired it). `PipelineStage` = `{ stage, entered_at, opportunity_id?, application_id?, missing? }`, in time order, one entry per stage reached; `missing` on the `readiness` entry is the latest `readiness_checks.missing` for the application (33.3 rule 1's item names, in the order they are asked). A tenant loan with no motion answers `stages: []`, `current: null` (it exists; it is simply on the board); another tenant's loan or an unknown id is `404 NOT_FOUND` (36.1 rule 4).
- The rows: 34.3's `book.loan` with the tenant filter for the member's row, masked partner-grade (36.1) with `bucket` as 36.5 rule 5 sets it; the pure projection pipelineOf over the loan's `refi_opportunities` entity rows, its refinance `applications` row (`prior_loan_id` = the loan), its `readiness_checks`, the application's `du_casefiles` / `du_submissions`, `disclosures` and `closings` rows, the new `loans` row it links to, and the `loan_events` that carry each row's timestamp (rule 2).
- Events: none of its own; 36.1's `partner_actions` row per request with `partner_portal.viewed`, `view = pipeline` (the list) or `view = pipeline.loan` (the detail, `subject_id` = the loan id), no homeowner field.
- Nothing starts here: the feed changes when 20.1 writes (`refi.opportunity.offered`, `.engaged`, `.converted`, `.declined`, `.expired`), 33.3 writes (`partner_book.refinance.opened`, `partner_book.readiness.checked`), 23.x writes (`du.submitted`, `du.findings.received`), 21.2 and 25.2 write (`disclosure.le.delivered`, `disclosure.cd.delivered`), 23.3 and 26.x write (`clear_to_close.issued`, `closing.scheduled`, `closing.consummated`, `loan.funded`), 30.2 writes (`loan.staged`, `loan.boarded`, `loan.active`), 35.10 writes (`refinance.prior_loan.retired`, `partner_book.loan.paid_off`) or 21.6 writes (`application.withdrawn`, `decision.issued{kind=denial}`, `application.closed_incomplete`).

#### Data model
New tables: none — the stage is computed on every read and stored nowhere (no `pipeline_stage` column on `loans`; brief §4.6 and §10); every row the feed reads is an owning section's, unchanged.
- Baseline tables read: the `refi_opportunities` entity (20.1: `opportunity_id`, `loan_id`, `status`, `offer_valid_until`, `as_of_date`), `applications` (`prior_loan_id`, `disposition`, `loan_id` — 33.3, 21.6, 35.10), `readiness_checks` (`application_id`, `ready`, `missing` — 33.3), `du_casefiles` and `du_submissions` (23.x), `disclosures` (21.2 the LE, 25.2 the CD), `closings` (`status`, `consummation_at` — 26.2), `loans` (`status`, `partner_party_id`, `servicer_loan_number`, `refinanced_by_loan_id`, `origination_application_id` — 33.1, 30.2, 35.10), `loan_events` (the timestamps of `refi.opportunity.offered`, `refi.opportunity.engaged`, `refi.opportunity.converted`, `refi.opportunity.declined`, `refi.opportunity.expired`, `partner_book.refinance.opened`, `application.received`, `partner_book.readiness.checked`, `du.submitted`, `du.findings.received`, `disclosure.le.delivered`, `disclosure.cd.delivered`, `clear_to_close.issued`, `closing.scheduled`, `closing.consummated`, `loan.funded`, `loan.staged`, `loan.boarded`, `loan.active`, `refinance.prior_loan.retired`, `partner_book.loan.paid_off`, `application.withdrawn`, `decision.issued{kind=denial}`, `application.closed_incomplete`), `timers` (20.1's `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30` and 35.10's `SM_REFI_PARTNER_CONFIRM_21`, read for the detail's clocks), `partner_book_facts`, `partner_book_reviews`, `properties`, `parties`, `borrowers` and `loan_borrowers` (through 34.3's row), `partner_sessions` and `partner_users` (36.1).
- Baseline tables written: `partner_actions` (36.1; one row per request, `view = pipeline` or `pipeline.loan`, no PII). Nothing else.

#### State machine
None of its own — a projection. Per member, computed and never stored: `offered —(refi.opportunity.engaged: the homeowner's Yes)→ engaged —(refi.opportunity.converted / partner_book.refinance.opened: 33.3's refi.open)→ readiness —(du.findings.received)→ du —(disclosure.le.delivered | disclosure.cd.delivered)→ disclosures —(clear_to_close.issued | closing.scheduled | closing.consummated | loan.funded)→ closing —(loan.boarded on the new loan; loans.status = active; the link through applications.loan_id / loans.refinanced_by_loan_id)→ boarded`. Terminal: `expired` (`refi.opportunity.expired` — `expireOffers` on 20.1's date rule or its `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30`; from `offered`), `declined` (`refi.opportunity.declined` — the homeowner's No; from `offered` or `engaged`), `withdrawn`, `denied`, `closed_incomplete`, `approved_not_accepted` (21.6's dispositions, as stored; from any stage after the application opens). The current stage is the furthest reached in that order (rule 2); a member with no row of any stage is not on the feed. Every transition is the homeowner's act or the owning section's pass; none is performed here.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **A member enters the feed on `offered`, never before.** The feed begins where the static board ends: an opportunity `detected`, `suppressed` or `offer_ready` (delivery pending under 20.1's `SM_REFI_OFFER_SLA_2BD`) is the board's business (36.3 lists the candidate); the item appears when 33.2's pass has delivered the offer and 20.1 marked it `offered` (33.2 rule 5; e-mail or the portal channel alike). The feed is not a fourth bucket and adds no member the engine did not select.
2. **The stage is the furthest row in the declared order.** pipelineOf is a pure function of the member's rows (no I/O, no figure): it reads the stages below, takes the furthest one for which a row exists as `current`, and records every stage reached, in time order, on the detail's `stages[]`. A stage with no row yet is omitted, never assumed from the calendar or the stage before it.

| Stage | Source of truth, read as stored | `entered_at` |
|---|---|---|
| `offered` | `refi_opportunities.status = offered` (33.2 rule 5 delivered the offer) | `refi.opportunity.offered` |
| `engaged` | `refi_opportunities.status = engaged` (the homeowner's Yes on the OfferCard, 32.11) | `refi.opportunity.engaged` |
| `readiness` | the refinance `applications` row with `prior_loan_id` = the loan, open (33.3 `refi.open`; the opportunity `converted`); `missing` = the latest `readiness_checks.missing` for it | `partner_book.refinance.opened` (else `application.received`) |
| `du` | a `du_casefiles` row on the application with findings received (23.x; 32.18's DU moment) | `du.findings.received` |
| `disclosures` | a delivered `disclosures` row for the application — the LE (21.2) or the CD (25.2) | `disclosure.le.delivered` or `disclosure.cd.delivered`, whichever first |
| `closing` | 23.3's clear to close, or a `closings` row (`scheduled` through `consummated`, 26.2), or `loan.funded` (26.3) | the earliest of `clear_to_close.issued`, `closing.scheduled`, `closing.consummated`, `loan.funded` |
| `boarded` | `loan.boarded` on the new loan (30.2) whose `origination_application_id` is the application, `loans.status = active`, linked to the member by `applications.loan_id` / `loans.refinanced_by_loan_id` (35.10 `loan.staged`) | `loan.boarded` |
| `expired` (terminal) | `refi_opportunities.status = expired` (33.2 rule 6's `expireOffers`; 20.1's date rule and `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30`) | `refi.opportunity.expired` |
| `declined` (terminal) | `refi_opportunities.status = declined` (20.1 — the homeowner's No, with 20.1's cooldown) | `refi.opportunity.declined` |
| `withdrawn`, `denied`, `closed_incomplete`, `approved_not_accepted` (terminal) | `applications.disposition` (21.6's terminal states, each by its stored name) | `application.withdrawn`, `decision.issued{kind=denial}`, `application.closed_incomplete`, the disposition's own event (21.6) |

3. **One item per member, the newest motion.** A loan with an expired or declined opportunity and a later delivered one has one item — the later opportunity's — on the list; the detail's `stages[]` keeps the earlier motion's entries, terminal included, so the history is whole. A terminal item stays on the list, newest first, until a later motion on the same loan supersedes it (open question 1). `opportunity_id` and `application_id` on the item are the rows the stage came from.
4. **`days_in_stage` is the only arithmetic, and it is on dates.** `days_in_stage` = the count of `calendar_days` from the date of `entered_at` in America/New_York to the day of the read (0 on the day it was entered). No money, no rate, no score and no likelihood is computed or shown; every other value on an item or a stage is a stored row's field or its event's timestamp (34.3 rule 7).
5. **The member stays on the board.** While its opportunity is `offered`, `engaged` or `converted` the loan's review reads `candidate` (33.2 rule 5) and the board lists it on Eligible now with `pipeline_stage` = the item's current stage (36.3 rule 1; 36.4-T1); on `expired` the next 33.2 review is free to write `not_now` with the cooldown and the loan moves to Not near with that code while the item reads `expired` (36.4-T3); on `boarded` the prior loan is retired `paid_off` by 35.10 and leaves the board and its counts, and the new `active` loan is never on the board (36.3 rule 4).
6. **`missing` is 33.3's list, in 33.3's words.** While the current stage is `readiness`, the detail carries `missing` = the latest `readiness_checks.missing` for the application — the item names of 33.3 rule 1 (`contact`, `account`, `esign`, `credit_authorization`, `verification_authorization`, `identity`, `ssn`, `credit`, `income`, `assets`, `value`), in the order they are asked — and nothing else about them: no figure, no document, no ask. The asks are 33.3's, through the homeowner's own cards; the partner reads what is missing and cannot request it (36.4-T2).
7. **Boarded, and the two rows it leaves.** On `loan.boarded` for the new loan whose application carries `prior_loan_id` = the member, the item reads `boarded`; the old row (35.10 `monitored_partner`: `refinance.prior_loan.retired`, `partner_book.loan.paid_off`) reads `loans.status = paid_off` with `loans.refinanced_by_loan_id` = the new loan and, until DELTA-03 is accepted, `banner` null — "paid off / refinanced" is read from those two fields (36.4-T4); the new row reads `loans.status = active`, `bucket = serviced`, banner `Active — Supermortgage subservicing` (36.5 rule 4) and a serviced pane that answers 36.6's `409 SERVICED_PANE_NOT_BUILT`. 35.10's `SM_REFI_PARTNER_CONFIRM_21` runs on the partner's own confirmation by tape or acknowledgement; the feed shows it on the detail's clocks and never arms or satisfies it.
8. **Nothing is emitted from the feed.** No origination event, no offer, no expiry, no review, no application, no notification to the homeowner or the partner leaves this process; there is no control on the feed but navigation to the loan page (36.5). A homeowner's No, a withdrawal or a denial reaches the feed from 20.1 or 21.6 only.
9. **Tenant and mask.** The list is the tenant's members only; another tenant's items are absent, never refused (36.4-T5); the detail for another tenant's loan is `404 NOT_FOUND` and logged `result = refused` (36.1 rule 4). An item carries the loan's last four and the homeowner's first name and last initial; the detail's `loan` is 36.3 rule 5's PartnerLoanRow (the full servicer loan number for `partner_admin` and `partner_ops` only) with `bucket` as 36.5 rule 5 sets it; no e-mail, phone, SSN, DOB, ZIP, street, score, DTI or investor column anywhere on the feed.

No money figure is computed here.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`portfolio` agent owns the projection and declares nothing of its own on the bus. End-to-end: on every read the runtime takes the tenant's members from 34.3's `book.loan` rows with the tenant filter, applies the pure stage mapping (rule 2) over the owners' rows and events, orders the items newest first (rule 3), counts the days (rule 4), masks partner-grade (36.1) and logs the look (36.1); the motion it shows is 20.1's, 33.3's, 21–26's, 30.2's and 35.10's, written by their own passes and the homeowner's own acts; nothing runs unprompted, nothing is proposed for a human, nothing is written but the log row. Decision record: none of its own — the offer's decision is 20.1's, the application's 33.3's, the disposition's 21.6's. Guardrails: `READ_ONLY` (34.3), `NO_COMPUTED_FIGURE` (34.3), `NO_PULL_BEFORE_YES` and `YES_REQUIRED` (33.3 — the feed orders nothing and opens nothing), `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES` (33.2 — no partner user changes a verdict or a stage), `NO_CREDIT_PULL`, `NO_INVESTOR_FIELDS` and `NO_PROHIBITED_BASIS` (20.1 and 33.2, inherited by the item), `ROLE_MASK` (34.2's principle at 36.1's partner grade), `NO_PII_IN_LOG` (34.1, reused by 36.1). Escalations: none of its own — a breached expiry is 20.1's, a stalled closeout or an unconfirmed payoff is 35.10's (`ops_analyst`), and the feed shows the owners' clocks meanwhile.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- A candidate whose offer is `offer_ready` and not yet delivered (the SLA running, or no officer to approve the campaign in a non-FAKE stage) → not on the feed; on Eligible now (36.3) with no `pipeline_stage`.
- A portal-only delivery (no e-mail on file, 33.2 rule 5) → `offered` from the portal touch's `refi.opportunity.offered` like any channel.
- The offer expires with no Yes → `expired`; the next review's `not_now` with the cooldown moves the loan to Not near; the item stays on the feed until a later offer supersedes it.
- The homeowner says No (`refi.opportunity.declined`) → terminal `declined` with 20.1's cooldown; nothing more from the feed.
- Two Yes taps → one application (33.3 `refi.open` returns the open one) → one item.
- The LE lands before the DU run → `disclosures` current, `du` recorded when it lands (discrepancy 2).
- The homeowner withdraws after the CD → terminal `withdrawn` (21.6); readiness rows stop (33.3 rule 5); the loan returns to the daily review under the cooldown and to the board.
- A denial → terminal `denied` (21.6); the adverse-action notice is 21.6's and never on the feed.
- `loan.funded` lands and 35.10 has not yet retired the prior loan (`SM_REFI_PRIOR_SETTLE_1BD` running) → `closing`; the old row still `monitored`; `boarded` only on `loan.boarded`.
- `loan.staged` never lands (35.6's hand-off breaches) → `closing` stays current; 35.10's board shows the stall; the feed shows the clock.
- The partner's next tape still carries the retired loan as active → 35.10's `partner_book.retirement.disputed` and its `ops_analyst` escalation; the feed still reads `boarded`; the hold and the resolution are 33.1's.
- A held loan (33.1 rule 8) with an open offer → the item stays; the board shows it on Holds (36.3 rule 1's precedence).
- The new `active` loan's id on the detail path → `404 NOT_FOUND` (the item is the member's; the new loan's page links back, 36.5).
- `partner_auditor` → the same feed, the same mask (the last four is already the list mask; the detail's servicer loan number stays the last four for the auditor).
- Partner B's session → partner A's items absent from the list, `404 NOT_FOUND` on A's detail path (36.4-T5).

#### Audit and evidence
What an examiner is shown: for any day, the feed as it was — reproducible from the owners' rows and events as of then, because nothing is stored here; the projection's code (pure, unit-tested) and its stage table; 36.1's action-log rows for every look with no PII; behind any item, the owners' own evidence — 20.1's opportunity row and decision, 33.2's touch and notice, 33.3's readiness rows and the application's creation event, 23.x's DU records, 21.2's and 25.2's disclosure rows with their hashes, 26.x's closing and funding records, 30.2's boarding validations and 35.10's closeout journal with the partner notification. Exported through 34.4's evidence pack (staff); the partner reads the feed and the daily report (36.5).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should terminal items age off the feed? **Default: no window in V1 — one item per member, the newest motion, terminal items included newest first; a later motion on the same loan supersedes the earlier one on the list and the detail keeps both.**
2. Should an `offer_ready` opportunity (delivery pending) be on the feed? **Default: no — the board's Eligible now shows the candidate; the feed begins at delivery, so "in a refinance" always means the homeowner has been offered.**
3. What is the retired prior loan's banner? **Default: DELTA-03 — `banner` null with `status = paid_off` and `refinanced_by_loan_id` shown until the sentence is accepted; never a sentence minted here.**
4. Should the detail accept the new loan's id? **Default: no — the item is the member's; the new loan's page (36.5) links back through `origination_application_id` → `applications.prior_loan_id`.**
5. Should the feed offer a `stage=` filter? **Default: no key in V1 — the list is short and the page groups by stage; a filter is a later extension that cannot change membership.**
