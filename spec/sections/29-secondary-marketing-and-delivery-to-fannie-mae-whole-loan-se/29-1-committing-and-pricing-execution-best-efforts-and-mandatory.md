### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Execution options — Selling Guide C1-1-01 (last updated 02/05/2025; Guide edition Sept 2, 2026) — VERIFIED.** Whole-loan commitments come in two forms. Mandatory: "The lender agrees to deliver a specified dollar amount of loans, within certain tolerances, to Fannie Mae by a specified future date." Best efforts: "The lender agrees to deliver a specific loan to Fannie Mae by a specified date if that loan closes. If the loan does not close, the lender does not have to pay a fee." Pricing: "When lenders commit to sell whole loans for cash, Fannie Mae provides a 'live' price, so named because prices move throughout the day, generally in tandem with the MBS market." LLPAs: "Fannie Mae may apply one or more loan-level price adjustments (LLPAs) based on certain loan-level credit risk characteristics"; "Whole loans: LLPAs are calculated on the 'Purchase Ready' date based on the unpaid principal balance of the loan"; "All applicable LLPAs will be deducted from the purchase proceeds" (20.4). Loan age: "a loan must be no more than six months old measured from the first payment date to the 'Purchase Ready' date (whole loans)". Premium recapture: "With respect to any loan that pays off within 120 days from the whole loan purchase date … Fannie Mae … may require reimbursement" (20.1 owns the solicitation consequence). Volume: "Fannie Mae may establish, amend, or cancel any mortgage loan volume limitations specific to a lender … Fannie Mae may impose volume limitations at any time, in its sole discretion." Assignment of commitments is governed by the Lender Contract provisions referenced to A2-1-01 — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — A2-1-01 not read; the design assumes a commitment cannot be assigned from the partner to SM]**.

**Mandatory commitment process — C2-1.1-01 (10/30/2009) — VERIFIED.** Commitments are obtained through "Fannie Mae's whole loan committing application" ("a free Web-based application available to subscribers") or by contacting the Capital Markets Pricing and Sales Desk; "Lenders can obtain commitments on any weekday, with the exception of bond market observed holidays as defined by the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association (SIFMA)."; "Fannie Mae confirms its acceptance of the lender's request for a mandatory commitment by electronically transmitting a commitment confirmation to the lender."; for Desk commitments "the legally binding terms of the commitment are captured in a recorded telephone conversation between the lender and the Desk."

**Mandatory pricing and fees — C2-1.1-02 (06/07/2023) — VERIFIED.** "Fannie Mae purchases regularly amortizing whole mortgages either at par (100% of the unpaid principal balance), at a discount, or at a premium."; "the rate at which the lender must remit interest on the mortgages it sells to Fannie Mae via a mandatory commitment is the pass-through rate, defined as the difference between the gross note rate and the servicing fee."; "Lenders can choose a range of five consecutive pass-through rates in increments of 0.125% under the same mandatory commitment." Extension fees "are based on" the amount to extend, the "Actual number of days by which the commitment expiration date is being extended" and a "Daily interest charge, which is based on the minimum pass-through rate posted on the original commitment"; "Whole loan prices captured at commitment and again at the time of the overdelivery or pair-off are used to determine if a fee will be charged or if cash back is due." LLPAs are referenced to the LLPA Matrix.

**Mandatory terms, amounts, periods — C2-1.1-03 (07/05/2023) — VERIFIED.** "Commitments to deliver most loan products can be taken for 1 to 90 days."; "There is no minimum commitment amount for mandatory whole loan commitments."; "commitments are issued in multiples of $1."; "Lenders may enter into multiple commitments but may not exceed $200 million in aggregate commitment volume per day."; "Lenders exceeding the limit without Fannie Mae approval may be required to pair off their commitment(s) at the lender's expense."; "Commitments are based on calendar days but must expire on a business day." The topic contains no delivery-tolerance percentage; the band is in C2-2-01 ("Whole Loan Good Delivery Amounts": "the minimum required delivery amount is an amount that will not fall below the original commitment amount by more than the greater of $10,000 or 2.5%"; "maximum delivery amount is an amount that will not exceed the original commitment amount by more than the greater of $10,000 or 2.5%"; "The maximum overdelivery amount is 25% of the original commitment amount."; "After an overdelivery occurs, the maximum delivery tolerance level decreases to $50 above the new commitment amount.") and restated in the PE–WL guide below.

**Mandatory extensions and pair-offs — C2-1.1-04 (05/30/2017) — VERIFIED.** "Lenders may extend commitments on or before the original commitment expiration date for up to a maximum of 30 days from that date."; "They may request multiple extensions as long as the total extension period is no longer than 30 days."; "Each request must be made before the contract expiration date in effect following the previous extension."; "Commitments expiring with outstanding unpaid principal balances will be automatically extended a minimum of five calendar days."; "A lender may purchase (pair off) all or part of a mandatory delivery commitment … if it is unable to satisfy Fannie Mae's minimum delivery requirement."; cash back: "In conjunction with a lender-requested pair-off, if the whole loan price at commitment is greater than the price at the time of the pair-off solely as a result of market fluctuations … then Fannie Mae will not charge a lender a pair-off fee but will provide cash back to the lender."; "Fannie Mae automatically pairs off the entire remaining commitment balance for any expired, unfulfilled mandatory commitment that has been previously extended."; extension and pair-off fees are "assessed and drafted from the lender's designated account."

**Delivery tolerance and mandatory mechanics — Fannie Mae "Pricing & Execution – Whole Loan: Mandatory Committing" and "PE – Whole Loan Mandatory Process Overview" (both "© 2025 Fannie Mae February 2025"), PE–WL Glossary and job aids — VERIFIED (document text).** "Good delivery for mandatory commitments as well as negotiated commitments includes the amount that falls within the greater of $10,000 or +/- 2.5% of the original commitment amount." (worked in the guide: a "$500,000" commitment yields "$487,500" to "$512,500"); "The maximum over-delivery amount is 25 percent of the original commitment amount."; "over-delivery amounts are based on the original commitment amount and not the high tolerance level."; "When committing, the lender must choose a 50-basis point range of pass-through rates (PTR)."; "The minimum PTR on live priced commitments may start on any 1/8 percent"; extension: "Calculate an extension by taking the remaining balance of a commitment and multiplying it by the lowest pass-through rate (PTR) and dividing that product by 360 days to determine the per diem extension cost."; "All pair-offs and extensions should be requested no later than Fannie Mae's close of business (5:00 p.m. ET) on the commitment expiration date."; "Extensions can be exercised via PE – Whole Loan seven days a week from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. ET or by contacting the Sales Desk"; automatic one-day extension "When you deliver loans against a commitment and they are in 'Purchase Requested' status without errors, but they were not yet moved to 'Purchase Ready' status, and The commitment has neither extended past 25 days nor previously extended automatically"; otherwise "it will be automatically extended for five calendar days so long as the commitment has not extended past 25 days and has not previously extended for five-days."; "If a commitment goes past its expiration date unpaid, and it is ineligible for an automatic extension, then a pair-off will be automatically performed and the appropriate fee assessed."; pair-off fee "based upon the amount being paired-off and the difference between the commitment price and the current price in the market"; the UI gives "60 seconds in which to accept or reject the pair-off" and "automatically generates an email confirmation". The mandatory commitment details page carries "execution type, product name, remittance type, commitment amount, and expiration date", "commitment delivery tolerances", an "HBL Cap (%)" and "Max Allowable HBL Delivery Amount" (a cap on high-balance loans delivered against a commitment — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the percentage is not published]**), and a Pricing tab with "range of pass-through rates (PTRs)", "price associated with each PTR", "servicing fee", "lender-paid mortgage insurance (LPMI) percentage" and "loan-level price adjustments". PE–WL UI hours, committing windows (Mon–Fri 8:15 a.m.–5 p.m. ET standard; 5–11:30 p.m. ET extended best efforts), "close-of-business prices … may not be used to execute any commitments", and the "$200 million" combined daily limit are in 00b-orig F7 (VERIFIED); the best-efforts committing job aid says prices are "live from 8:15 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ET" with the ability to commit "after business hours until 10:00 p.m. ET" — the two Fannie Mae pages disagree on the extended-window close (10:00 vs 11:30 p.m. ET) — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the adapter reads the window from configuration]**.

**Servicing fee for whole loans — C2-1.1-05 Servicing Fees (05/01/2019); Servicing Guide A2-3-02 (07/13/2022) and F-2-09 (09/11/2019) — VERIFIED.** C2-1.1-05: "A lender's servicing compensation when obtaining mandatory commitments is described in its applicable servicing contract. Lenders must also specify a maximum allowable servicing fee, which may differ, depending on the mortgage product. For example, the maximum servicing fee for all fixed-rate loans is 50 basis points (.50%)."; "Unless otherwise provided in the related servicing contract, every month, lenders deduct their servicing fee from the borrower's interest payment before remitting the remainder to Fannie Mae."; it refers to "Servicing Guide F-2-09, Servicing Fees for Portfolio Mortgage Loans." A2-3-02: "The total servicing fee (after deduction of the applicable guaranty fee for an MBS mortgage and/or the applicable renewal premium accrual for a mortgage with lender-purchased MI) must at least equal Fannie Mae's required minimum servicing fee for the particular type of mortgage loan." F-2-09 table (rows as served): "Conventional fixed-rate, fully amortizing first-lien scheduled/scheduled remittance — 0.25% minimum fee; 0.5% maximum (delivered on/after June 1, 2019)"; "Fixed-rate monthly payment first-lien actual/actual or scheduled/actual remittance — 0.25% or 0.375% minimum (lender's option); 0.5% maximum (delivered on/after June 1, 2019)"; "All ARM loans — 0.25% minimum fee". PE–WL FAQ: "A servicing strip of 25 bps is required on all commitments for ARM products."; "When committing a loan on PE – Whole Loan that has LPMI that is paid for out of the gross note rate, the LPMI percentage will need to be factored in to the total servicing fee."; the PE–WL mandatory guide's example: "gross note rate of 5.000% – servicing fee of 0.250% = pass-through rate of 4.750%".

**Buy-up/buy-down — C3-3-02 (12/04/2019); Developer Portal catalog (00b-orig F7) — VERIFIED as MBS-only.** "Loan level guaranty fee buyups and buydowns allow lenders to pool a wider range of note rates under one MBS coupon."; the mechanism is a trade of guaranty fee for "a one-time payment" and does not touch the servicing fee. The "Buy Up Buy Down Daily" API returns "loan-level details from the previous day's closed MBS pools" (00b-orig F7: "MBS only; not applicable to whole-loan cash execution"). For whole loans the servicing strip is simply `note_rate − pass_through_rate`, chosen at commitment inside the F-2-09 band (fixed: 0.25%–0.50%; ARM: 0.25%).

**Remittance types for whole loans — C2-1.1-06 (06/28/2011; 00a-fnma §5.5), C2-1.2-03, PE–WL committing job aid — VERIFIED / PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** C2-1.1-06 describes interest at purchase for A/A ("from the last paid installment date … up to, but not including, the purchase date"), S/S and S/A ("from the first day of the purchase month up to, but not including, the purchase date"), so all three exist for whole loans; C2-1.2-03: "Those retaining or releasing servicing may do so using the actual/actual or scheduled/scheduled remittance type. **Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. Citations: "C1-2 (whole loan commitments…)" is wrong — C1-2-02 is loan data/documentation delivery and C1-2-04 is eMortgage delivery; commitments are C2-1.1 (mandatory) and C2-1.2 (best efforts); execution options are C1-1-01 (00a-fnma §5.3; 20.4/21.4 discrepancy 1).
2. "C2-1.1 (servicing fee; buy-up/buy-down; remittance types)": the servicing-fee topic is C2-1.1-05 with the figures in Servicing Guide F-2-09; buy-up/buy-down is an MBS guaranty-fee mechanism (C3-3-02) and the Buy Up Buy Down Daily API is MBS-only — nothing to build for whole-loan cash; the whole-loan analogue is the choice of pass-through rate (fixed 25–50 bps strip; ARM exactly 25 bps).
3. The over/under-delivery tolerance (greater of $10,000 or ±2.5% of the original amount; over-delivery capped at 25%; $50 above the new amount after an over-delivery) is in Selling Guide C2-2-01 ("Whole Loan Good Delivery Amounts", 08/07/2018) — not in the commitment topics C2-1.1-03/C2-1.2-02 the row cites — and is restated in the PE–WL Mandatory Committing guide and Glossary (Feb 2025).
4. The row's trigger "per lock (best efforts)" omits the clocks that actually bind: the one-business-day key-data notice, the DU Approve/Eligible not older than 60 days at commitment, the expiration-on-a-business-day rule, the 30-day duplicate window, "closed status within one business day of funding", the 60-second acceptance window, the $200 million daily limit, and the conversion of a closed best-efforts loan into a mandatory obligation.
5. "PE–Whole Loan (UI/API)": the API covers best-efforts pricing and committing; mandatory commitments, pair-offs, extensions and over-deliveries are UI (or Sales Desk by recorded call) → `fnma_portal_operator` (00b-orig Part (b) 3).
6. "Delivery deadline" is not the commitment expiration: the custodian must hold the documents by the first morning delivery the day prior to expiration (C2-2-01), and LLPAs are struck on the Purchase Ready date — the matrix version can change between lock and purchase (`SM_LLPA_TABLE_VERSION_GATE`, 20.4).
7. Remittance types: best efforts offers A/A and S/S (S/A exists for whole loans but is not offered in the best-efforts UI), and the type cannot be switched after commitment.

#### State machine

Best efforts: `requested` (on `lock.executed`) → `priced` (Loan Pricing API quote captured; guardrails passed) → `executed` (Loan Committing API confirmation; `commitment_id_fnma`; emits `commitment.executed`) → `committed` (PE–WL status Committed; loan not yet closed) → {`modified` (child row; status stays `committed`), `extended` (manual, ≤30 days total; status stays), `fallout` (terminal; `pair_off_expected=false` for borrower withdrawal / lender declination / ineligible key-data change; `dpa_exposure_until` set), `expired` (auto-expiry without closing → treated as fallout; `dpa_exposure_until` set), `closed` (closed status set after `loan.funded` — now a mandatory obligation)} ; `closed` → {`auto_extended` (5-day increments up to 60 days; status stays `closed`), `delivered` (Loan Delivery Purchase Requested, 29.4), `purchased` (terminal; `commitment.fulfilled`), `paired_off` (terminal; lender-requested after non-delivery is certain, or automatic at the 60-day cap; fee)}. `requested`/`priced` → `rejected` (duplicate casefile, TBD address, outside window, quote expired, daily limit, adapter refusal) → retried or queued. `executed` without PE–WL confirmation (timeout) → `unconfirmed` → reconciled from the confirmation e-mail / UI within 15 minutes, else operator task.

Mandatory (flag-gated): `authorized` (29.2 request + `officer` authorization) → `executed` (UI by `fnma_portal_operator` or Sales Desk; confirmation captured) → `open` (remaining balance > 0) → {`extended` (≤30 days manual), `over_delivered` (≤25% of original), `partially_paired_off`, `auto_extended_1d`/`auto_extended_5d`, `auto_paired_off` (terminal), `paired_off` (terminal), `fulfilled` (terminal; purchased amount within [tolerance_low, tolerance_high])}.

Guards: `executed` requires `lock.status ∈ {executed, confirmed}`, `mlo approval` recorded on the lock (21.4), no other open commitment on `lineage_id`, complete property address, `underwriting_method='du'` only with `du_recommendation='approve_eligible'` and `du_recommendation_at ≥ executed_at − 60 calendar_days`, a live (not close-of-business) price, `quote_expires_at > now`, aggregate day volume + amount ≤ $200,000,000, and `expires_on` a `fannie_sifma` business day ≤ `effective_on + 90`. `closed` requires `loan.funded`. `paired_off (lender_requested)` requires `officer` approval when `fee_cents > policy threshold` (default $2,500). Mandatory `executed` requires `execution.mandatory_enabled=true` and an `officer_mandatory_authorization` escalation resolved `approved`.

#### Business rules and calculations

Money in cents; prices as percent of par to 3 decimals as quoted by PE–WL (stored 5 dp); rates 4 dp; fee arithmetic in exact decimal with half-up rounding to cents at the fee total (Fannie Mae's rounding is not published — **[UNVERIFIED]** — the fee draft is the truth and any variance is reconciled, not argued); Fannie Mae day-count for fees is 360 (per the published formulas).
1. **Execution selection.** `execution.mandatory_enabled=false` → one best-efforts commitment per lock lineage, requested on `lock.executed`. When the flag is true, 29.2 decides per lock whether the loan is covered by the pipeline hedge (`commitments.type='mandatory'` at delivery) or committed best efforts (e.g., low pull-through products); 29.1 executes what 29.2 requests. A best-efforts commitment can never be converted to mandatory or vice versa (C2-1.2-03) — the choice is final at execution.
2. **Commitment expiration.** `expires_on = nextBusinessDay(locks.expires_on + commitment_buffer_days, calendar='fannie_sifma')`, default `commitment_buffer_days = 14` (funding, custodian shipping and certification after a closing on the last lock day); constraint `expires_on − effective_on ≤ 90 calendar_days`, else `expires_on := effective_on + 90` rolled *backward* to a business day and the agent flags that the lock outruns the commitment (relock/extension planning in 21.4). The commitment period is `expires_on − effective_on` in calendar days. Never later than 29.4's `FNMA_B2_1_5_02_LOAN_AGE_6M_PURCHASE_READY` due date.
3. **Pass-through rate and servicing strip.** `pass_through_rate = note_rate − servicing_fee − lpmi_pct` with `servicing_fee = 0.2500%` for every product (fixed: F-2-09 minimum 0.25% under S/S, or 0.25% at the lender's option under A/A, maximum 0.50%; ARM: exactly the 25 bps strip). Excess servicing (strip > 25 bps) is a policy option that raises the price and the partner's servicing income; v1 does not use it (open question 2). The remittance type is chosen at commitment (`actual_actual` default) and cannot change afterwards; 30.1 boards the loan with the same type.
4. **Price capture.** The Loan Pricing API response for (product, note rate, PTR, remittance type, expiration date, loan amount, LTV, score, units, occupancy, property type, state) is stored raw in `pewl_price_captures` with `quote_id_fnma` and `quote_expires_at`; the commit call must reference an unexpired quote. Close-of-business prices (5–8 p.m. ET) are never executable. After-hours locks queue to the next window (21.4 open question 5) unless `secondary.after_hours_commit` permits the extended window.
5. **LLPA forecast and net price.** LLPAs are not in the commitment price. `llpa_forecast_pct = Σ grid cells` per 20.4 rule 2 using the `llpa_tables` version whose `effective_from ≤ expected_purchase_ready_date` (`SM_LLPA_TABLE_VERSION_GATE`); `llpa_forecast_cents = round_half_up(llpa_forecast_pct/100 × upb_at_purchase_cents)`, where `upb_at_purchase_cents = loan_amount_cents` unless a scheduled payment falls before the expected Purchase Ready date (then the amortized UPB). `net_price_forecast = commitment_price − llpa_forecast_pct`; `proceeds_forecast_cents = round_half_up(upb_at_purchase_cents × net_price_forecast / 100) + credits_forecast_cents` (HomeReady −$2,500 credit is a positive cash item to the lender) — before the interest adjustment of C2-1.1-06, which 29.4/27.2 compute from the purchase advice. `expected_purchase_ready_date = disbursement_date_planned + 5 business_days_fannie_et` by default and is refreshed on `loan.funded`, `delivery.submitted` and `custody.certified`.
6. **Execution variance.** `execution_variance_cents = round_half_up((commitment_price − pricing_quotes.base_price)/100 × loan_amount_cents)` measures the live commitment price against the price the borrower was quoted from the rate sheet (20.4); positive variance is program surplus (open question 7), negative variance is SM's cost under the Grander economics unless caused by a lock-desk policy breach.
7. **Key-data changes.** On any change to loan amount, product, note rate, units, seller loan number, borrower or address on a committed loan, the agent files `commitment_modifications` and reports through PE–WL within the timer; product or note-rate changes re-price at worse case: `new_commitment_price = min(commitment_price, live_price_for_new_terms)` when the loan remains eligible (C2-1.2-03 "worse case pricing when applicable"); a loan-amount increase raises `amount_cents` and `max_amount_cents`; a decrease lowers `amount_cents` only (`max_amount_cents` stays — it is the fee base "throughout the life of the commitment"). A complete address change or DU→Other underwriting change requires fallout + recommitment; because the property differs, the new commitment is not a duplicate; a change that makes the loan ineligible is fallout without fee.
8. **Fallout classification.** `lock.cancelled{borrower_withdrawal|lender_declination}` and ineligibility changes → `fallout`, `pair_off_expected=false`, `dpa_exposure_until = fallout_date + 30 calendar_days`. Expiry before closing → the same. A recommit for the same borrower and property inside the window carries the DPA (worse-case pricing); the agent's economics rule compares `expected DPA cost` with the uncommitted interest-rate exposure over the remaining window (29.2 marks) and chooses the cheaper path under the policy cap.
9. **Closed status.** On `loan.funded` (disbursement, not consummation — the PE–WL glossary: "funds have been disbursed to the borrower(s)"; for the refinance fixture that is Nov 12, not Nov 6), set closed status the same day where possible and never later than 5:00 p.m. ET the next Fannie Mae business day. From that moment the loan is a mandatory obligation and the loan is visible in Loan Delivery within 15–20 minutes.
10. **Best-efforts extension fee.** `per_diem_cents = max_amount_cents × max_ptr / 360`; `fee_cents = round_half_up(per_diem_cents × days)`; `max_ptr` is the highest PTR carried during the commitment's life; manual extensions ≤ 30 days cumulative before closing; closed loans auto-extend in 5-day steps to 60 days from the original expiration. Payer: SM when the delay is a fulfillment delay (missing documents, late shipping, late certification caused by SM's process), partner when caused by its custodian or its own act (open question 3). A borrower-caused closing delay is handled in 21.4 (lock extension) — the commitment extension that follows is still the lender's cost vis-à-vis Fannie Mae.
11. **Best-efforts pair-off.** Only for closed loans. `fee_cents = round_half_up(max_amount_cents × max(0, market_price − commitment_price)/100)`; `market_price` is the live price captured at the moment of pair-off for the same product/PTR/remaining period; no cash back is assumed when `market_price < commitment_price` **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. Decision rule: as soon as non-delivery is *certain* (loan ineligible and not curable within the auto-extension cap, or the partner elects to retain the loan), pair off immediately rather than carry auto-extension fees; while cure is possible, compare `cure_probability × 0 + (1 − cure_probability) × (fee_now + remaining carry)` against `fee_now` daily and pair off when the expected carry exceeds the expected saving; escalate to `officer` above the fee threshold.
12. **Mandatory tolerance, extension, pair-off, over-delivery.** `tolerance_cents = max($10,000, round_half_up(0.025 × original_amount_cents))`; good delivery ⇔ `purchased ∈ [original − tolerance, original + tolerance]`; `over_delivery_cap_cents = 0.25 × original_amount_cents`. Extension per diem `= remaining_balance_cents × min_ptr / 360`. Pair-off: `amount × (market_price − commitment_price)/100` — positive = fee, negative = cash back (C2-1.1-04; lender-requested). Over-delivery priced the same way on the over-delivered amount. Requests before 5:00 p.m. ET on the expiration date; otherwise PE–WL applies the automatic 1-day/5-day extension or auto pair-off rules. Five consecutive PTRs (a 50 bp range) per commitment; loans delivered against it must carry a PTR inside the range; high-balance deliveries capped by `hbl_cap_pct`.
13. **Duplicate prevention.** The adapter refuses a second `commit` for an open `lineage_id`, for a DU casefile already committed, or for a TBD/incomplete address (PE–WL auto-DPA on fallout).
14. **Daily limit.** Before each execution, `Σ amount_cents executed today (ET) + amount_cents ≤ $200,000,000`; above it, queue and let the `officer` seek Sales Desk approval.
15. **Fee drafts.** Every `committing_fee_drafts` row must match a child row within ±$1.00 (rounding); otherwise `committing_fee.exception` → `secondary` re-derives the fee from the confirmation; unresolved variances escalate to `officer` for a Fannie Mae inquiry within 30 days (C2-2-05 purchase-advice adjustment window as the analogue — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — no published window for committing-fee disputes]**).
16. **Time and calendars.** Fannie Mae times are ET; `business_days_fannie_et` uses `holiday_calendars.fannie_sifma`; early-close days count as business days with the committing window ending at the early close; the borrower's lock runs on the creditor calendar (21.4) — the two calendars are reconciled in rule 2.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`secondary` agent (tools: `priceForCommitment`, `commitBestEfforts`, `modifyCommitment`, `setClosedStatus`, `requestExtension`, `moveToFallout`, `preparePairOffPackage`, `prepareMandatoryPackage`, `captureMarketPrice`, `reconcileFeeDrafts`, `reconcilePewlStatus`, `computeNetPriceForecast`, `openEscalation`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: on `lock.executed` it validates the lineage, DU window, address, day limit and window, prices through the API, checks the quote against the borrower's quote (execution variance), commits inside the quote window, stores the confirmation, emits `commitment.executed`; it listens for key-data events and files modifications within the one-business-day clock; on `loan.funded` it sets closed status; it runs the 4:30 p.m. ET expiring-commitments sweep (extension quotes, closing-date checks with 26.1, pair-off economics), the DPA economics on recommit requests, the fee-draft reconciliation each morning, and the nightly PE–WL/Loan Delivery status reconciliation. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{commitment_id, lock_id, lineage_id, quote_id_fnma, price, ptr, remittance_type, expires_on, guardrail_results[], du_window_check, day_volume_check, execution_variance_cents, llpa_forecast{version, cells}, net_price_forecast, alternatives_considered[] (e.g., pair-off now vs carry), escalation_ids[], rationale, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails (never alone): never commit without an executed, MLO-approved lock; never place a mandatory commitment, pair-off, over-delivery or Sales Desk order without the `officer` authorization the policy requires; never commit on a close-of-business or expired price; never create a second open commitment on a lineage or a commitment on a TBD address; never change a commitment's remittance type or execution type (impossible in PE–WL — refused in code); never let a closed loan pass its auto-extension cap without a pair-off decision escalated at least 5 business days earlier; never allocate a fee to the partner without the documented cause. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (every UI-only step; 4-hour SLA, 1 hour for expiring-today), partner `officer` (mandatory authorization; pair-off/over-delivery fees above $2,500; DPA acceptance above $1,000; uncommitted position beyond 5 business days; Sales Desk calls; fee-draft disputes), `compliance-sentinel` (key-data timer breach), `qc-audit` (sampling). No borrower interaction occurs in this process; `human_agent` is not a touchpoint here. Human path: the secondary console shows the same queue with manual price/commit/modify/close/fallout actions (still recorded as `commitments` rows with `recorded_by=user`) and the operator runbooks for PE–WL.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Lock executed after 5:00 p.m. ET or on a SIFMA holiday (e.g., Mon Oct 12, 2026 Columbus Day — a creditor business day for the partner if it is open, but no committing): commitment queued to the next window (Tue Oct 13 8:15 a.m. ET); 29.2 carries "locked, uncommitted"; SM bears the overnight move within the policy cap (21.4 open question 5).
- Quote ID expires between pricing and commit (API latency, MLO approval already given): re-price; if the new price is worse than the borrower's quote by more than the cap, commit anyway (the borrower's lock is honored) and record the negative variance.
- Loan Committing API returns a confirmation but the e-mail confirmation never arrives: the API response is the record; UI reconciliation confirms the commitment exists before the day ends.
- API accepts a commit but PE–WL shows a different expiration (rolled to a business day by Fannie Mae): store Fannie Mae's date; recompute timers.
- DU casefile older than 60 days at lock (long pre-approval): commit under `other` or trigger an 23.1 resubmission first (open question 5); a later change from DU to Other is a fallout + recommit event — avoid by deciding at commitment.
- Address correction after commitment (unit number, ZIP): a "complete property address change" requires fallout + recommit — a typo fix is reported as key data first; if PE–WL treats it as a new address, recommit without DPA (different address).
- Borrower changes product from fixed to ARM after commitment: key-data/product change → re-price at worse case; ARM strip is exactly 25 bps.
- Loan closes but funding is delayed past the commitment expiration (dry state recording delay): the loan is not "closed" until disbursement; extend manually before expiry (≤ 30 days cumulative) rather than let the commitment expire (expiry before closing = fallout + DPA exposure).
- Closed loan cannot be certified before the 60-day cap (trailing document): pair off when non-delivery is certain; otherwise extension carry; 27.1 warehouse aging runs in parallel.
- Fee draft with no matching child row (e.g., an automatic extension the platform did not observe): create the child row from the draft with `source='draft'` and reconcile PE–WL history; escalate if the fee contradicts the confirmation.
- Fannie Mae imposes a volume limitation or the daily $200M limit is reached: commitments queue; 29.2 hedges the uncommitted position; `officer` engages the account team.
- Duplicate DU casefile across two applications (same borrower, two properties): distinct addresses are not duplicates; the casefile check is per casefile — a second casefile must exist for the second property.
- Mandatory commitment expiring on an early-close day (e.g., Fri Nov 27, 2026): requests by 2:00 p.m. ET are the safe assumption (**[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**); the sweep runs at 12:30 p.m. ET on early-close days.
- PE–WL maintenance window (6:00–6:30 a.m. ET; Sunday windows): no impact on commitments; fee-draft ingestion retried after 6:30 a.m.
- Partner's seller approval suspended or volume limitation imposed mid-pipeline: no new commitments; open commitments still bind; `officer` and 31.1 engaged; 29.2 reports the unhedged pipeline.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Commitment buffer and calendar.** Default: `expires_on = nextBusinessDay(lock.expires_on + 14, fannie_sifma)`, capped at 90 days and at the six-month loan-age gate; 21.4's "+15 from lock" is superseded by this rule (both yield Dec 7, 2026 for the fixture).
2. **Remittance type and servicing strip.** Default: A/A, 25 bps for every product (F-2-09 minimum; ARM strip fixed at 25 bps); no excess servicing in v1; revisit with the partner's MSR economics (a 37.5 or 50 bps strip on fixed-rate loans lowers the price and raises the partner's servicing income — a term-sheet question).
3. **Fee allocation between SM and the partner.** Default: SM bears extension, pair-off and DPA fees caused by fulfillment (documents, shipping, certification, eligibility defects found post-closing, after-hours basis); the partner bears fees caused by its own acts (custodian it selected, volume limitations imposed on it, its retention election); borrower-caused delays are recovered from the borrower only through 21.4's lock-extension policy, never as a Fannie Mae fee pass-through.
4. **Mandatory execution gating.** Default: `execution.mandatory_enabled=false`; when enabled, every mandatory commitment, over-delivery and pair-off requires an `officer` authorization recorded in `escalations`, executed by a `fnma_portal_operator` (UI) — no Sales Desk orders by SM personnel unless the partner authorizes named individuals in writing.
5. **Underwriting method at commitment.** Default: `du` with the casefile id when an Approve/Eligible not older than 60 days exists or is expected within one business day (the agent waits ≤ 1 business day, carrying the uncommitted position); otherwise `other`, never switched later (a DU→Other switch is a fallout + recommit in PE–WL; an Other→DU switch is **[UNVERIFIED]**).
6. **After-hours committing.** Default: use the extended best-efforts window (5–11:30 p.m. ET per the FAQ; the job aid says 10:00 p.m.) when the API accepts it at a live price; otherwise queue to 8:15 a.m. ET; the partner's policy sets the overnight basis cap (default 25 bps × uncommitted volume).
7. **Execution variance treatment.** Default: positive variance (commitment price above the borrower's quoted base price) is program surplus retained to fund the 20.4 lender-credit cap for future borrowers and reported monthly to the partner; negative variance is SM's cost unless caused by a partner-directed pricing exception.
8. **Pair-off threshold and DPA acceptance.** Default: `officer` approval for pair-off/over-delivery fees > $2,500 and DPA acceptances > $1,000; the agent may execute below those amounts with a decision record.
9. **Best-efforts cash back and auto-extension fees.** Default assumptions (no cash back; auto-extensions are charged the per-diem) stand until the first fee drafts prove otherwise; both are reconciled, not hard-coded.
