### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Servicing Guide B-2-01, Property Insurance Requirements Applicable to All Property Types (08/12/2026)** — the servicer "must maintain policies and procedures" ensuring continuous required property insurance for first-lien loans and must: confirm each policy meets the requirements of B-2-02/B-2-03/B-2-04 for the property type; ensure premiums are paid (B-1-01 — Section 3.7); "obtain lender-placed insurance in response to notification that coverage is being cancelled, non-renewed, or has lapsed, in accordance with B-6-01"; contact its Fannie Mae Servicing Representative when additional coverage may be needed (Selling Guide B7-3-05); adjust coverage when it is inadequate to protect Fannie Mae's interest **or when lender-placed insurance causes over-insurance** (examples given: vacant properties, renovation loans); and — new, **mandatory Jan. 1, 2027** (LL-2026-03; SVC-2026-05) — "at least annually" remind the borrower of the responsibility to maintain insurance and recommend reviewing coverage with the insurance provider (the reminder may ride with other communications and may point to Fannie Mae's or the servicer's website). The topic now delegates coverage, deductible, perils, rating and evidence rules to Selling Guide B7-3-01, B7-3-02, B7-3-07 and B7-3-08. Source: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/b-2-01/property-insurance-requirements-applicable-all-property-types (verified 2026-09-09).

**B-2-02, Property Insurance Requirements for One- to Four-Unit Properties (08/12/2026)** — minimum monitoring for first liens: for a **new policy**, verify insurer rating (B7-3-01), required perils, coverage sufficiency and maximum deductible (B7-3-02); for a **renewal policy**, confirm the same "at a minimum annually." If the servicer cannot initially determine whether a renewal meets the replacement-cost requirement it may compare the current coverage amount to the **last known coverage amount**: if coverage decreased or the prior amount is unknown, the servicer "must take additional steps it deems appropriate to confirm that the property insurance policy meets the replacement cost coverage requirements" and document the effort in the loan file; if unchanged, further steps are discretionary. On any non-compliance "the servicer must notify the borrower of the insufficiency." The servicer must maintain **documented procedures** describing its monitoring methods, how it detects coverage decreases, and the steps used to resolve a non-compliant policy "which may include lender-placed insurance." Source: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/b-2-02/property-insurance-requirements-one-four-unit-properties (verified 2026-09-09).

**Selling Guide B7-3-02 (08/05/2026)** — policy must be written on a "Special" coverage form or equivalent; minimum perils: fire or lightning, explosion, windstorm (including named storms designated by NWS/NOAA), hail, smoke, aircraft, vehicles, riot or civil commotion; an excluded or limited required peril must be covered by an acceptable stand-alone policy (e.g., wind). **Coverage sufficiency:** "The property insurance policy must provide coverage on a replacement cost basis, with the exception of roofs; property insurance policies that provide such terms of coverage will be deemed to provide sufficient coverage"; "Roofs must be insured, but do not have to be insured on a replacement cost basis"; ACV terms for personal property and non-building structures are acceptable. **Maximum deductible:** "5% of the property insurance coverage amount," and each separate peril deductible (windstorm, wildfire) must individually be ≤ 5%. Source: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-02/property-insurance-requirements-one-four-unit-properties (verified 2026-09-09). **LL-2026-03** confirms the prior "lesser of 100% RCV / UPB / 80% insurable value" documentation regime is retired (verified 2026-09-09 at https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/lender-letter-ll-2026-03-updates-project-standards-property-insurance-requirements).

**Selling Guide B7-3-01 (12/14/2022)** — insurer must meet **one** of: AM Best Financial Strength Rating "B" or better; Demotech Financial Stability Rating "A" or better; S&P Insurer Financial Strength "BBB" or better; Kroll IFSR "BBB" or better. Non-rated insurers acceptable only with a qualifying reinsurance cut-through arrangement (Form 858 Assumption of Liability Endorsement, 100% reinsurance, 90-day termination notice to Fannie Mae) or, for state plans (FAIR plans, state windstorm/beach pools), "if they are the only coverage that can be obtained at the time of … policy renewal." If the servicer carries mortgage-impairment insurance from an AM Best/S&P-rated issuer, Fannie Mae does not require confirmation of the borrower's insurer rating. "The lender or servicer must ensure that the insurer, policy, and coverage meet Fannie Mae's requirements." Source: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-01/general-property-insurance-requirements-all-property-types (verified 2026-09-09).

**Selling Guide B7-3-08 (12/14/2022)** — 1–4 unit and unit policies need a "standard" or "union" mortgagee clause (without contribution); when Fannie Mae must be named: "Fannie Mae, in care of (servicer's name and address)"; otherwise "the lender's name, followed by the phrase 'its successors and/or assigns,' and mailing address," substituting the servicer when the lender is not the servicer; **"MERS must not be named as mortgagee or loss payee"**; a loss-payable clause is not a substitute; all title-holders must be named insureds; the policy must provide written notice to the mortgagee before cancellation (no day count in the Guide — carrier/state-law driven). Master/NFIP policies are exempt from the mortgagee-clause requirement. Source: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-08/mortgagee-clause-named-insured-and-notice-cancellation-requirements (verified 2026-09-09).

**Selling Guide B7-3-07, Evidence of Property Insurance (08/05/2026)** — acceptable evidence: the complete policy; an insurer-signed certificate containing all information needed to verify compliance; **electronic data files** if they carry sufficient policy/property/borrower data to verify terms, coverage, deductible, premium status and loss-draft handling, the servicer's E&O policy acknowledges electronic transfers, the insurer gives written assurance the file equals a printed policy, risk procedures exist, and legible hard copies can be produced on Fannie Mae request; for projects, a copy of the master policy or a certificate showing the unit is covered. Source: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-07/evidence-property-insurance (verified 2026-09-09).

**B-2-03 (02/14/2024) / Selling Guide B7-3-03 (08/05/2026)** — servicer must confirm **annually** that the master policy for a PUD/condo/co-op unit meets requirements; evidence = current master policy + endorsements + a certificate showing the unit is covered, or a servicer-paid blanket policy covering every project it services, or a servicer-maintained "walls-in" plus loss-assessment policy — with an uncapped indemnity: "In no event shall a servicer's obligation to Fannie Mae thereunder be limited to the amount of coverage in force." Master policy: 100% of estimated replacement cost of project improvements (five documentation methods); special form with the extended peril list (vandalism, sprinkler leakage, sinkhole collapse, volcanic action, falling objects, weight of snow/ice/sleet, water damage); per-occurrence deductible ≤ 5%; **per-unit deductible ≤ $50,000** (mandatory July 1, 2026) and any per-unit deductible triggers a unit-owner policy; building-ordinance coverage; boiler & machinery (lesser of $2M or RCV) for central plants. **B-2-04 (12/21/2022) / B7-3-04 (08/05/2026)** — unit-owner (HO-6-type) policy required when any interior/improvements are not covered by the master or the master has a per-unit deductible; amount = greater of the cost to restore the interior to pre-loss condition or the master per-unit deductible; replacement-cost basis; deductible ≤ greater of 5% or $2,500. Sources: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/b-2-03/master-property-insurance-requirements-project-developments ; https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-03/master-property-insurance-requirements-project-developments ; https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/b-2-04/individual-property-insurance-requirements-units-project-developments ; https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b7-3-04/individual-property-insurance-requirements-unit-project-development (all verified 2026-09-09).

**Regulation X** — §1024.31 defines hazard insurance as insurance "that protects the property against loss caused by fire, wind, flood, earthquake, theft, falling objects, freezing, and other similar hazards" (eCFR current as of Sept. 8, 2026). §1024.37(b) permits force-placed charges only on "a reasonable basis to believe that the borrower has failed to comply with the mortgage loan contract's requirement to maintain hazard insurance"; comment 37(b)-1: information from the borrower, the insurance provider or the agent may establish the basis; without such information the servicer must act with reasonable diligence, and a servicer that follows the (c)(1)(i)–(ii) notice process "is deemed to have acted with reasonable diligence." §1024.17(k)(1)–(2) and (k)(5) (Section 3.7): an escrowed borrower's hazard premium must be paid/advanced while the borrower is ≤ 30 days overdue, and for a borrower > 30 days overdue LPI may be purchased only on a documented inability to disburse (cancellation for reasons other than non-payment, or vacancy — comment 17(k)(5)(ii)(A)-1). Evidence standard, comment 37(c)(1)(iii)-2: the servicer may require the declarations page, certificate, policy "or similar written confirmation" and may reject evidence "if neither the insurance provider nor the agent confirms" it or the terms do not comply with the loan contract. Sources: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1024/subpart-C/section-1024.37 ; https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1024/appendix-Supplement%20I%20to%20Part%201024 (verified 2026-09-09).

**Other federal law**: GLBA §502(e)(1) service-provider exception governs data sent to tracking vendors (contract + confidentiality); E-SIGN/§1024.32(a)(1) for electronic delivery of Reg X notices; FCRA is not implicated; TCPA consent gating for any outbound AI-voice call about insurance (00a §1.4). **State variation**: some states regulate the amount of hazard insurance a lender may require (e.g., Cal. Civ. Code §2955.5 — not more than the replacement value of improvements **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — cite from general knowledge]**) and force-placed practices (e.g., N.Y. 11 NYCRR Part 227, Insurance Regulation 202 **[UNVERIFIED specifics]**) — both are `jurisdiction_rules` parameters consumed by 9.2.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) B-2 is reorganized; LPI is B-6-01 and flood is B-3-01. (2) "Continuous" understates the hard obligations: annual renewal verification (B-2-02/B-2-03), annual borrower reminder (2027-01-01), borrower notification on insufficiency, documented procedures, 30-day production of LPI documentation to Fannie Mae (B-6-01), 10-business-day flood evidence (B-3-01). (3) The blueprint's row omits project policies entirely. (4) Coverage-amount arithmetic (100% RCV/80% test) that older trackers still enforce is **retired**; replacement-cost basis is the test.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `loan.boarded` (Section 1.1) → create `insurance_policies` rows from the boarding file and open a `pending_verification` review; `property.updated` (occupancy, project type, renovation) → re-evaluate requirements.
- Vendor feed messages (`insurance-tracking/lpi` inbox): `policy_snapshot` (daily full/delta), `eoi_received` (image + extracted data), `cancellation_notice`, `nonrenewal_notice`, `reinstatement_notice`, `expiration_alert`, `carrier_rating_update`.
- Documents from any channel (mail scan, borrower portal upload, e-mail, carrier ACORD/EDI, agent call) → `insurance_evidence` intake.
- `escrow.bill.received` (hazard/flood/wind line — Section 3.7) → treated as renewal evidence once the disbursement is `confirmed`; `escrow.disbursement.confirmed` for `disbursement_kind ∈ {hazard, flood, wind}` → policy `verified` for the paid term.
- Schedules: daily `insurance_sweep` (expirations at −60/−30/−15/0/+1 days; verification due; annual reminders; project annual checks); `carrier_rating_sweep` weekly.
- `property.vacancy_confirmed` (9.8/9.9), `insurance.claim.opened` (9.7), `lossmit.*`/`foreclosure.*` states (coverage adjustment, over-insurance rule of B-2-01).

#### State machine
Policy: `pending_verification` → `verified` (adequacy pass + evidence confirmed) | `deficient` (adequacy fail → `insurance_deficiencies` row → notice) → `verified` (cured) ; `verified` → `expiring` (expiration − 60 days) → `verified` (renewal evidence/paid renewal) | `expired` (expiration + 1 day, no evidence) → 9.2 ; `verified`/`expiring` → `cancelled` (carrier cancellation effective) | `nonrenewed` → 9.2 ; any → `replaced` (new policy supersedes) | `superseded` (LPI cancelled on evidence). Deficiency: `open` → `notified` → `cured` | `escalated_to_fpi` | `waived` (policy rule, e.g., state plan is the only coverage) — terminal `cured/waived/closed_loan`. Actors: `insurance-property` agent (all transitions), vendor adapter (feed-driven), `officer` (waiver of a rating failure under mortgage-impairment cover), no external ack required.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Requirement derivation** per loan: hazard always required (uniform instrument §5); wind separately required when the base policy excludes windstorm/named storm; unit-owner policy required when the master has a per-unit deductible or leaves interior/improvements uncovered (B7-3-04); master policy required for condo/co-op and attached-PUD projects (B7-3-03); flood per 9.6.
2. **Adequacy test** (rule set `fnma.insurance.2026-08`, LL-2026-03 semantics): PASS iff `coverage_basis ∈ {replacement_cost, extended_rc, guaranteed_rc}` (roof may be ACV) AND `coverage_form = special` (or all eight required perils present) AND `deductible_pct ≤ 5.0000%` of `coverage_dwelling_cents` for the policy and for every per-peril deductible AND carrier rating meets one of AM Best ≥ B / Demotech ≥ A / S&P ≥ BBB / Kroll ≥ BBB (or state plan / cut-through exception / mortgage-impairment relief) AND mortgagee clause valid (partner ISAOA/ATIMA c/o Supermortgage; MERS absent) AND named insureds = title holders (parties feed). `deductible_pct = deductible_cents ÷ coverage_dwelling_cents`, decimal.js, compared without rounding (a $12,500 deductible on $250,000 = exactly 5.0000% → PASS; $12,501 → FAIL). Unit-owner policies: deductible ≤ max(5% × coverage, $2,500.00).
3. **Renewal shortcut (B-2-02)**: if the renewal evidence does not state the loss-settlement basis, compare `coverage_dwelling_cents` to `last_known_coverage_cents`: decrease or unknown → `coverage_decrease_unconfirmed` deficiency → agent takes "additional steps" (carrier/agent confirmation call, request of the policy jacket) and records them; unchanged → verified with a note.
4. **Evidence acceptance**: declarations page, certificate, policy or "similar written confirmation" (comment 37(c)(1)(iii)-2); vendor or agent confirmation required when extraction confidence < 0.90 on any of {policy number, effective/expiration dates, coverage amount, deductible, mortgagee clause, property address}; rejection only for the two comment reasons, recorded with the reason.
5. **Escrow interplay**: for escrowed loans the paid renewal (Section 3.7 `confirmed` disbursement) is verification of the term; the tracker never asks an escrowed current borrower for money; premium increases flow to 3.2 (analysis) — no action here.
6. **Over-insurance/coverage adjustment (B-2-01)**: when a loan enters `vacant` or `renovation` status, the agent reviews coverage (vacancy endorsement, LPI amount) and, for LPI, requests an amount adjustment to the RCV estimate (9.2 rule 4).
7. **Annual reminder**: one per loan per 12 months; may be appended to the annual escrow statement transmittal (3.3) or a periodic statement insert (Reg Z permits inserts) — separate page, plain language, links to Fannie Mae's consumer insurance page **[URL to be selected — UNVERIFIED]**.
8. Worked example: renewal EOI shows Coverage A $312,000, "Special Form," roof "ACV endorsement," all-peril deductible $10,000 and wind deductible 2% → deductible_pct = 10000/312000 = 3.2051% ≤ 5% ✓; wind 2% ✓; roof ACV ✓ (permitted); basis "replacement cost" ✓; AM Best A- ✓; mortgagee clause "Partner ISAOA/ATIMA c/o Supermortgage PO Box …" ✓ → `verified`; `last_known_coverage_cents` was 30,000,000 (prior year $300,000) → no decrease. If instead Coverage A were $280,000 with no basis stated → `coverage_decrease_unconfirmed` → agent calls the agent of record (AI voice, disclosed) and obtains the RC endorsement page → cured.

#### Integrations
- **`insurance-tracking/lpi` adapter** (00b N6; vendor layouts contract-gated **[UNVERIFIED]**): outbound daily loan/property/mortgagee file (boarding adds/removals, address/escrow-flag changes, occupancy, claim flags); inbound policy snapshot, EOI images + data, cancellation/non-renewal/reinstatement notices, rating updates; idempotency = (vendor loan id, policy id, message type, vendor sequence); acks per file; reject file → agent triage; outage > 1 business day → the daily sweep continues on stored data and the deficiency SLA still runs; sandbox stub with synthetic feeds; contract tests per message type.
- **Carriers/agents**: ACORD 25/27 certificates and EDI where offered; otherwise e-mail-in and AI voice confirmation calls (Twilio) logged as `contacts`.
- **`print-mail` / `e-delivery`**: deficiency notices and annual reminders per Notice Registry channel rule (electronic only with valid `esign` consent for the "insurance" notice class; mail otherwise).
- **Escrow (Section 3.7)**: bill/renewal events; `escrow_lines` updates for premium changes.
- **Fannie Mae**: no system interface for tracking itself; document requests answered by e-mail/upload via `fnma_portal_operator` when required.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Notices: `INS_DEFICIENCY_NOTICE` (B-2-02 "notify the borrower of the insufficiency"; checklist: property, policy, the specific deficiency, the requirement, cure options, that LPI may follow with its cost warning, contact; mail or e-delivery), `INS_ANNUAL_REMINDER` (B-2-01; checklist: obligation to maintain insurance, recommendation to review coverage with provider, website pointer), `INS_EOI_REQUEST` (policy courtesy request at −30 days; not a §1024.37 notice and must not contain FPI language).
- Records: `insurance_policies`, `insurance_evidence`, `insurance_deficiencies`, `insurance_requirements`; `loan_events`: `insurance.policy.received/verified/deficient/expiring/expired/cancelled/nonrenewed/replaced`, `insurance.evidence.received/confirmed/rejected`, `insurance.deficiency.detected/notified/cured`, `insurance.lapse_detected` (→ 9.2), `insurance.reminder.sent`. No ledger postings in 9.1 (premium payments are 3.7). Investor events: none.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- Agent `insurance-property`, tools: `extractEvidence(document)` (vision extraction → schema with per-field confidence), `verifyWithCarrier(policy)` (vendor API / voice call), `evaluateAdequacy(loan, policy)`, `recordDeficiency`, `sendNotice(template)`, `requestEscrowLineUpdate`, `openCase`, `escalate`.
- End-to-end: ingest → extract → confirm → adequacy → verify or deficiency → borrower notice → follow-up outreach (SMS/e-mail/AI voice with disclosure and TCPA consent check) → cure or hand to 9.2. Decision record: {loan, policy, evidence hashes, extraction confidences, adequacy rule results, last-known comparison, notices, rationale, rule-set/model/prompt versions, outcome}.
- Guardrails: the agent cannot mark a policy `verified` without a confirmed evidence record; cannot waive a rating failure (only `officer` under documented mortgage-impairment cover); cannot ask an escrowed current borrower to pay a premium; all borrower calls disclose automation and offer a human. Escalations: `officer` (waivers; Fannie Mae documentation requests; E&O/electronic-file election), `human_agent` on request, `attorney` on title/named-insured disputes (trusts, divorces) beyond templated handling.
- AI-off path: ops-console queues (extraction review, adequacy exceptions) worked by staff; timers and vendor feeds run regardless.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Transfer-in with expired policy (W-005): open deficiency immediately; the prior servicer's FPI notices do not carry over — 9.2 restarts the cycle (open decision 9.2-Q3).
- Transfer-out: export open deficiencies, evidence and FPI state in the MISMO projection; stop notices at the transfer date (17.x).
- Bankruptcy: notices carry the bankruptcy disclaimer overlay (Section 14); coverage tracking continues; no "you must pay" language beyond the model form when a discharge is recorded — counsel-approved variant `INS_DEFICIENCY_NOTICE_BK`.
- Successor in interest (4.4): confirmed successors receive the same notices; policies must be re-issued in the successor's name (named-insured rule) — agent guides the successor.
- Disaster: carriers issue moratoria/non-renewals; state plans as last resort (B7-3-01); coverage verification tolerance during declared events (open decision 9.1-Q4).
- Vendor outage/feed gaps: stale-feed detector (no snapshot in 2 business days) → sev-2; EOI intake continues from direct channels.
- Duplicate/overlapping policies from two carriers → mark one `replaced`; refund questions belong to the borrower's carrier, not the servicer.
- Partial data (no deductible on a certificate): `needs_carrier_confirmation`, never assumed.
- Retro-corrections: a mis-keyed expiration that opened a 9.2 cycle → cancel the cycle with reason `servicer_error`, reverse any charges (9.5 path), apology notice.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Vendor: single full-service tracker/LPI provider vs. tracking-only plus separate LPI carrier — **default: single provider under a partner-named LPI program**, notices generated by the platform (see 9.2-Q1).
2. Mortgagee clause wording under subservicing — **default: "[Partner] ISAOA/ATIMA c/o Supermortgage"**; partner to confirm with Fannie Mae Servicing Representative whether "Fannie Mae c/o" is ever needed.
3. Mortgage-impairment insurance to relieve rating checks — **default: do not rely on it; enforce ratings**.
4. Disaster-period verification tolerance — **default: 30-day extension of the deficiency SLA in FEMA-declared counties, no extension of the lapse rule**.
5. Reminder vehicle — **default: separate page in the annual escrow statement mailing for escrowed loans; stand-alone mailing/e-delivery for non-escrowed**.
