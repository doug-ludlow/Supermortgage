The application package must include "A copy of the Supplemental Consumer Information Form (Form 1103)". **SAFE Act — 12 CFR 1008.103 and Appendix A (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026) — VERIFIED.** §1008.103(b): "An individual engages in the business of a loan originator if the individual, in a commercial context and habitually or repeatedly: (1)(i) Takes a residential mortgage loan application; and (ii) Offers or negotiates terms of a residential mortgage loan for compensation or gain; or (2) Represents to the public... that such individual can or will perform the activities described in paragraph (b)(1)". (c)(1): "An individual 'takes a residential mortgage loan application' if the individual receives a residential mortgage loan application for the purpose of facilitating a decision whether to extend an offer of residential mortgage loan terms to a borrower or prospective borrower (or to accept the terms offered by a borrower or prospective borrower in response to a solicitation), whether the application is received directly or indirectly from the borrower or prospective borrower." (c)(2): offers or negotiates = "(A) Presents for consideration by a borrower or prospective borrower particular residential mortgage loan terms; (B) Communicates directly or indirectly with a borrower, or prospective borrower for the purpose of reaching a mutual understanding about prospective residential mortgage loan terms; or (C) Recommends, refers, or steers..." plus "(ii) Receives or expects to receive payment of money or anything of value". Clerical exemption (e)(3): "An individual who performs only clerical or support duties and: (i) Who does so at the direction of and subject to the supervision and instruction of an individual who: (A) Is licensed and registered in accordance with paragraph (a) of this section..."; (e)(4): "An individual who performs only purely administrative or clerical tasks on behalf of a loan originator." Appendix A (a): taking an application means "Receipt by an individual, for the purpose of facilitating a decision whether to extend an offer of loan terms to a borrower or prospective borrower, of an application", received "directly or indirectly"; an individual takes an application even if the individual "Only inputs the information into an online application or other automated system" and "Is not responsible for verifying information". Not taking: "Receives a loan application through the mail and forwards it, without review, to loan approval personnel"; "Assists a borrower or prospective borrower who is filling out an application by explaining the contents"; "Generally describes for a borrower or prospective borrower the loan application process without a discussion" of particular loan products; "In response to an inquiry regarding a prequalified offer that a borrower or prospective borrower has received", collecting only basic identifying information. Appendix A (b)(1): offering or negotiating includes "Presenting for consideration by a borrower or prospective borrower particular loan terms, whether verbally, in writing, or otherwise", "Communicating directly or indirectly with a borrower or prospective borrower for the purpose of reaching a mutual understanding about prospective residential mortgage loan terms", and "Responding to a borrower or prospective borrower's request for a different rate or different fees on a pending loan application". Not offering/negotiating (b)(2): "Providing general explanations or descriptions in response to consumer queries, such as explaining loan terminology"; "Arranging the loan closing or other aspects of the loan process, including by communicating with a borrower" about agreed terms; "Providing a borrower or prospective borrower with information unrelated to loan terms, such as the best days" to close; "Making an underwriting decision about whether the borrower or prospective borrower qualifies for a loan"; "Explaining or describing the steps that a borrower or prospective borrower would need to take in order to obtain" a loan offer; "Communicating on behalf of a mortgage loan originator that a written offer has been sent to a borrower" without details; "Offering or negotiating loan terms solely through a third-party licensed loan originator". **Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Colorado AI Act (SB24-205 as amended)" no longer exists in that form: SB 26-189 repealed and re-enacted it (00a-fed §13.1, §17 item 1); the duties for this process are pre-use notice at the point of interaction and, downstream, the 30-day adverse-outcome explanation and human review (21.6/31.2).
2. "B2-2 (borrower eligibility incl. non-permanent residents)": the current text of B2-2-02 is dated 07/28/2015 and delegates documentation to the lender behind a rep and warrant of legal presence; the DACA/EAD guidance lives in a 2019 fact sheet whose current status is unverified. The build cannot hard-code an eligible-status list from the Selling Guide; it carries a `jurisdiction_rules`-style `citizenship_evidence_policy` owned by the partner (open question 4).
3. "Form 1103 SCIF (language preference, homeownership education)" is understated: the SCIF is mandatory to present for every application dated on or after March 1, 2023, must be retained in the loan file, and its data are delivered through DU; the language section is optional to the borrower.
4. The row's "Reg C §1003.4 & App. B (demographic collection incl. by phone/electronically)" is correct, but the row omits the rule that visual-observation/surname collection is permitted only for in-person applications — a voice/chat/web intake may never infer demographics, and the "Demographic Information Addendum" is not a component of the 1/2021 form.
5. "SAFE Act... Reg G/H": Reg G applies to depository-employed registered MLOs; the partner as a non-bank is governed by state law implemented per Reg H. The clerical exemption in §1008.103(e)(3)–(4) is written for "an individual"; it does not by its terms cover a software system, so the AI-intake question is not answered by the exemption (open question 1).
6. §1026.36(g) placement: the row's "state MLO rules" hides the federal placement rule — the MLO of record's name and NMLSR ID go on the application, the LE and CD, the note and the security instrument (00a-fed §17 item 12).
7. "B1-1-03 (age of documents)" is 22.1's obligation (four months on the note date); it has no application-day deadline.
8. The row omits Reg B §1002.4(c)/comments 4(c)-2 and -3 — the rule that makes a computerized voice/chat record a "written application" — and §1002.2(f)'s "completed application" definition that anchors 21.6's 30-day clock.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `lead.qualified` / 20.3 hand-off: `lead_id`, verified identity, `consents` (esign, tcpa_ai_voice, credit_soft_pull), channel, and any pre-qualification data (never treated as submitted application data).
- Borrower actions: starts an application in the borrower app, by voice (inbound or consented outbound AI call), or by chat; uploads documents (22.1 consumes); requests a human.
- Refinance-trigger prefill (20.1): servicing data offered as *suggestions* that the borrower must confirm item by item — a prefilled field is not a "submission" until confirmed (open question 2).
- `mlo_of_record` queue decisions (escalation outcomes).
- Schedules: `SM_O21_INTAKE_ABANDON_30` sweeps; nightly NMLS status re-check of assigned MLOs (31.1 feed).

#### State machine

`started` (interview open; nothing submitted) → `received` (Reg B application: the borrower has requested credit for a stated transaction type and property/occupancy through the partner's procedure; emitted by the `intake` agent the moment the request is recorded — this is also the HMDA application date, open question 6) → `trid_received` (sixth of the six items submitted; 21.2 starts `REGZ_1026_19E1_LE_3BD`) → `intake_complete` (initial 1003 e-signed by every borrower, SCIF presented, demographics asked, joint intent evidenced, MLO of record assigned and application-stage review approved) → hand-off to 21.2 (LE), 22.x (verification), 23.1 (DU). Side states: `human_agent_active` (interview continued by a human; data model unchanged), `mlo_returned` (MLO of record returned the package with questions; agent re-opens the interview). Terminal here: `abandoned` (no borrower activity for 30 calendar days before `trid_received`; after `trid_received` an inactive file is 21.6's `withdrawn`/`incomplete` path, never silently abandoned). Guards: `received` requires `consents.esign` (if e-delivery) and identity verification; `trid_received` requires every item `source ∈ {borrower_stated, borrower_confirmed_prefill}`; `intake_complete` requires `applicant_demographics` rows for every borrower (answered or declined) and `scif_forms.presented_at` for every borrower; `visual_observation_used` must be false unless `collection_method='in_person'`.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Three application dates.** `application_received_at` (Reg B) = first timestamp at which the borrower requests credit for a specific transaction through the interview (rule: transaction_type, occupancy and either a property or a "property to be determined" purchase request recorded). `trid_received_at` = max over the six items of `submitted_at`. `hmda_application_date` = date of `application_received_at` (28.3 may override to the date on the signed 1003 per its own rule; the two are recorded separately). A prefilled item counts as submitted only at the borrower's explicit confirmation.
2. **Six-item detection.** `income` counts when any income amount is stated for any borrower; `property_address` for a purchase without a contract is not satisfied by "TBD" (the TRID application cannot arise until a property address exists — 20.3 keeps the file in pre-qualification); `property_value_estimate` is the borrower's estimate (an AVM shown by SM is not the borrower's submission); `loan_amount_sought` counts when the borrower states or confirms an amount.
3. **Demographic collection.** For `collection_method ∈ {telephone, internet, video}` the agent reads/shows the App. B instruction-2 statement, offers multi-select ethnicity/race with disaggregated sub-categories, and records declines; it never populates from name, voice, accent, or image. `video` is treated as not in person (open question 3).
4. **Joint intent.** A second borrower is added only after each borrower separately affirms joint intent through the `joint_intent_method`; the affirmation artifact is separate from the accuracy attestation in Section 6 and is captured before any credit report is ordered for that borrower. A spouse who is only needed to sign the security instrument under §1002.7(d)(4) is recorded as `non_borrowing_spouse` and is never asked for credit information.
5. **Prohibited inquiries (§1002.5).** The interview never asks about religion, race, color, sex or national origin outside the monitoring questions, never asks about childbearing intentions (§1002.5(d)(3)), and asks about public-assistance income only as an income source to be evaluated like any other. The URLA 1a citizenship-status question is asked as written on the form; Reg B §1002.5(e) permits inquiries about permanent residency and immigration status **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — §1002.5(e) text not re-read today; 00a-fed §3.4 verified (a)–(d)]**. Alimony, child support and separate maintenance are introduced with the §1002.5(d)(2) disclosure that they need not be revealed unless the borrower wants them considered.
6. **SCIF.** Presented to every borrower after Section 1 is complete; the language question is preceded by the form's own statement; a blank answer is stored as `not_answered` and is never followed up as a condition. Education/counseling answers seed 22.1 needs-list items when B2-2-06 requires education (HomeReady FTHB, LTV > 95% all-FTHB, no-tradeline DU purchase) and seed 20.4's SFC 184 counseling-credit eligibility test (counseling completed within the 12 months before closing).
7. **MLO of record.** Assignment at `application.received`: pick the partner's licensed MLO for the property state with the lowest open queue; the MLO's name and NMLSR ID are written to Section 9 of the 1003 and to `applications.mlo_nmlsr_id`, and flow to the LE/CD (21.2/25.2), note and security instrument (26.1). Stage `application` review package: the rendered initial 1003, the `safe_activity_log`, the pricing scenario the borrower saw, and open questions.
8. **AI-intake mode.** `assisted` (default): the agent may perform every App. A (b)(2) activity and data capture; it presents indicative pricing from 20.4 only as "estimates prepared for [MLO name], NMLS #n, who will review your Loan Estimate" and logs each such screen as `particular_terms_presented` under the MLO's id; the MLO approves LE terms (21.2 gate) and every lock (21.4). `supervised_present`: no particular terms are shown until the MLO approves the pricing scenario for that borrower. `autonomous`: prohibited unless 31.1 records a written legal position for the state.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`intake` agent (tools: `startInterview`, `discloseAI`, `captureField`, `confirmPrefill`, `validateULAD`, `detectSixItems`, `askDemographics`, `presentSCIF`, `affirmJointIntent`, `assignMLO`, `renderForm1003`, `requestESign`, `openEscalation`, `logSafeActivity`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end behavior: opens the session with the AI disclosure, runs the 1003 as a conversation in the borrower's chosen language (English binding; other languages assistive, open question 5), validates every field against ULAD enumerations as it goes, detects the six items and emits `trid_received`, presents the SCIF and demographic questions with the required statements, obtains joint-intent affirmations, renders and e-signs the initial 1003, assigns the MLO of record and files the application-stage escalation, then hands off. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{application_id, session_ids, six_items, application_received_at, trid_received_at, demographics_collection_method, declines, joint_intent_evidence_ids, scif_presented, citizenship_evidence, mlo_of_record_id, ai_intake_mode, safe_activity_summary, model_version, prompt_version, rationale}`. Guardrails (never alone): never infer or record demographic data from anything but the borrower's answers; never ask a §1002.5-prohibited question; never present particular loan terms outside the flag mode; never tell a borrower they do or do not qualify (App. A (b)(2)(iv) underwriting communications are 23.x's, and eligibility statements before DU are prohibited); never treat a prefilled field as submitted; never discourage an applicant (§1002.4(b)); never skip the SCIF or the demographic request; never continue if the borrower asks for a human. Escalations: `mlo_of_record` (application stage; LE and lock stages in 21.2/21.4), `human_agent` on request or on three consecutive failed captures of the same field, `licensed_specialist` where `jurisdiction_rules` require, `compliance-sentinel` on any attempted prohibited inquiry detected in the transcript. Borrower-facing disclosure/consent: AI disclosure at start; E-SIGN scope confirmation before the first electronic document; SCIF statements verbatim; App. B statement verbatim. Human path: the ops console shows the same interview with a human agent continuing the script; all gates and records are identical.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Borrower asks "am I approved?" — the agent explains the process (App. A (b)(2)(v)) and never states a qualification conclusion.
- Borrower supplies a property address only later (purchase without contract): the file stays `received` (Reg B) but not `trid_received`; 21.2's clock does not start; the agent explains that a Loan Estimate follows once a property is identified.
- Co-borrower unavailable at the time of the first interview: the application proceeds for the first borrower; the second borrower's own interview, joint-intent affirmation and demographics are captured on her own timestamp; `trid_received` is not re-anchored (six items are per application, not per borrower); the LE lists both borrowers once the second is added, otherwise a revised LE follows under 21.5 (borrower-requested change).
- Borrower states a language preference other than English: recorded; the interview may continue with AI translation labeled as a convenience; all binding documents remain English; in California the 21.3/26.1 state matrix tests Civ. Code §1632 obligations.
- Non-permanent resident with an EAD expiring before the note date: 22.6 condition; if the status cannot be documented as current at closing, 21.6 handles the decision with `underwriting_reviewer`.
- ITIN-only applicant: flagged for 22.6/23.1 eligibility determination (open question 4); the agent does not state eligibility.
- Borrower declines the AI: `human_agent` takes over within the same session; data captured so far is preserved.
- Voice recognition ambiguity on SSN or income: the agent reads back and requires confirmation; three failures → human.
- MLO of record loses license status between assignment and LE: gate closes automatically; reassignment; the LE cannot issue under the stale NMLSR ID.
- Borrower is a trust (inter vivos): trustee/settlor capture per B2-2-05 (26.1 owns documentation); the individual borrower still completes the 1003.
- Same borrower starts a second application: duplicate detection by identity; the older `started` file is merged or abandoned with a record.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **21.1-T1** Given a voice interview on Mon Oct 5, 2026 where the sixth item (loan amount $560,000) is stated at 10:41 MST, when the agent processes the utterance, then `trid_received_at` = 2026-10-05T10:41 MST, `application.trid_received` is emitted once, and 21.2's `REGZ_1026_19E1_LE_3BD` shows due date Thu Oct 8, 2026.
- **21.1-T2** Given a prefilled property address from servicing data, when the borrower has not yet confirmed it, then `six_items.property_address.submitted_at` is null and `trid_received` is not emitted even if the other five items are submitted.
- **21.1-T3** Given a telephone application where the applicant declines ethnicity and race, when the record is saved, then `declined_*` are true, `visual_observation_used=false`, and any attempt to write ethnicity/race values is rejected by the constraint.
- **21.1-T4** Given a web application where the applicant selects "Asian" and "White", when saved, then both values persist and the FIG "more than one race" handling is available to 28.3.
- **21.1-T5** Given two applicants on Mon Oct 19, 2026 where B has not affirmed joint intent, when 22.2 requests a credit pull for B, then `SM_O21_JOINT_INTENT_GATE` blocks it; after B's affirmation at 20:31 EDT the pull proceeds; the affirmation artifact id differs from the accuracy-attestation artifact id.
- **21.1-T6** Given no SCIF presented to borrower B, when 23.1 attempts `du.submit`, then `SM_O21_SCIF_PRESENT_GATE` blocks; after `scif_forms.presented_at` is set (even with all answers blank), the submission proceeds.
- **21.1-T7** Given `ai_intake_mode='assisted'` and no MLO of record assigned, when the agent attempts to display a specific rate/fee scenario, then the `SAFE_1008_103_MLO_OF_RECORD_GATE` prevents the `particular_terms_presented` utterance and the agent falls back to general explanations; once the MLO is assigned and approves at 14:02 on Oct 5, the scenario displays with the MLO attribution.
- **21.1-T8** Given an application-stage escalation opened Fri Oct 9, 2026 at 16:00, when no MLO decision exists by end of creditor day Tue Oct 13, 2026 (Mon Oct 12 is a federal holiday and the calendar `creditor` marks it closed), then `SM_O21_MLO_REVIEW_SLA_1BD` breaches, the package is reassigned, and an `escalations` sev-2 row exists.
- **21.1-T9** Given a `started` application with last activity Mon Oct 5, 2026 and no `received` transition, when the sweep runs on Wed Nov 4, 2026, then the application is `abandoned` and purged per the pre-application retention rule; the same file with `received` set is retained 25 months.
- **21.1-T10** Given the interview transcript contains the agent asking about childbearing plans, when `compliance-sentinel` scans, then a sev-1 escalation is opened and the prompt version is quarantined.
- **21.1-T11** Given an Arizona married applicant applying individually, when the 1003 is rendered, then the spouse appears only as `non_borrowing_spouse` for the security instrument and no credit data are requested from the spouse.
- **21.1-T12** Given the MLO of record's NMLS status changes to "inactive" on Wed Oct 7, 2026, when the nightly feed loads, then the gate closes, the LE issuance in 21.2 is blocked until reassignment, and the reassignment is logged with the new NMLSR ID before any LE renders.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Can the AI "take an application" and "present particular terms" without an individual MLO doing so?** The SAFE Act tests apply to "an individual"; no regulator has spoken. Default: `origination.ai_mlo_intake='assisted'` — the AI is framed as a tool operating "at the direction of and subject to the supervision and instruction of" the MLO of record; the MLO's name/NMLSR ID is attributed on every particular-terms screen, and the MLO approves LE terms and locks. Re-open per state in 31.1.
2. **Does a servicing-data prefill confirmed by the borrower count as a "submission" of a TRID item?** Default: yes only on explicit item-level confirmation; the CFPB guide's statement that information "is not deemed submitted simply because it exists in a creditor's system" is the basis. Counsel to confirm.
3. **Is a video interview an "in person" application for App. B visual observation?** Default: no — treat video as telephone/internet; never use visual observation. Confirm against Reg C commentary (not read today).
4. **ITIN-only and DACA applicants.** Default: DU/Fannie Mae eligibility determined in 23.1 from current Selling Guide text; the partner sets `citizenship_evidence_policy`; the 2019 fact sheet is not relied on until re-verified.
5. **Non-English interviews.** Default: English is the binding language; AI translation is an assistive convenience with an on-screen/oral caveat mirroring the SCIF statement; state translation duties are tested in 21.3/26.1.
