### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Application (six items) — §1026.2(a)(3) (eCFR current as of 9/04/2026; 00a-fed §1.1) — VERIFIED.** "The submission of a consumer's financial information for the purposes of obtaining an extension of credit"; for §1026.19(e)/(f) transactions the application consists of "the consumer's name, the consumer's income, the consumer's social security number to obtain a credit report, the property address, an estimate of the value of the property, and the mortgage loan amount sought" (§1026.2(a)(3)(ii)). The CFPB's Small Entity Compliance Guide (v5, May 2018, p. 44–45) restates the six items and adds that "the information is not deemed submitted simply because it exists in a creditor's system or in its file". Reg X's own definition (§1024.2(b), 00a-fed §6.7) lists the same six items "and any other information deemed necessary by the loan originator" — the Reg X 3-business-day disclosures in 21.3 key off "receives an application, or information sufficient to complete an application".

**Business days — §1026.2(a)(6) (eCFR 9/04/2026; 00a-fed §1.2) — VERIFIED.** General: "A day on which the creditor's offices are open to the public for carrying on substantially all of its business functions." Specific: "For purposes of rescission under §§1026.15 and 1026.23, and for purposes of §§1026.19(a)(1)(ii), 1026.19(a)(2), 1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B), 1026.19(e)(1)(iv), 1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A), 1026.19(e)(4)(ii), 1026.19(f)(1)(ii), 1026.19(f)(1)(iii), 1026.20(e)(5), 1026.31, and 1026.46(d)(4), the term means all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays." Mapping used here: the 3-day delivery clock in (e)(1)(iii)(A) runs on the general definition (`business_days_creditor`); the 7-day pre-consummation rule in (e)(1)(iii)(B), the mailbox presumption in (e)(1)(iv) and the fee/intent rule in (e)(2)(i)(A) run on the specific definition (`business_days_regz_specific`).

**Timing — §1026.19(e)(1)(iii) and comments 19(e)(1)(iii)-1 and -2 (eCFR 9/08/2026; CFPB eRegs) — VERIFIED.** "(A) The creditor shall deliver or place in the mail the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section not later than the third business day after the creditor receives the consumer's application"; "(B) ... not later than the seventh business day before consummation of the transaction." Comment 19(e)(1)(iii)-1: "If an application is received on Monday, the creditor satisfies this requirement by either hand delivering the disclosures on or before Thursday, or placing them in the mail on or before Thursday, assuming each weekday is a business day." Comment 19(e)(1)(iii)-2 (re-read 2026-09-11 — the June example sits in -2, not -1): "The seven-business-day waiting period begins when the creditor delivers the disclosures or places them in the mail, not when the consumer receives or is considered to have received the disclosures. For example, if a creditor delivers the early disclosures to the consumer in person or places them in the mail on Monday, June 1, consummation may occur on or after Tuesday, June 9, the seventh business day following delivery or mailing of the early disclosures, because, for the purposes of § 1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B), Saturday is a business day, pursuant to § 1026.2(a)(6)." The anchor of the seven-day rule is therefore the day the creditor delivers or mails, and the day of consummation may be the seventh business day after it.

**Receipt — §1026.19(e)(1)(iv) (eCFR 9/08/2026) — VERIFIED; comment 19(e)(1)(iv)-2 — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (read on a secondary compliance site dated Nov 5, 2019; eCFR/CFPB interpretation pages truncated before it today).** Rule: "If any disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section are not provided to the consumer in person, the consumer is considered to have received the disclosures three business days after they are delivered or placed in the mail." Comment (as quoted on the secondary site): "if a creditor sends the disclosures required under § 1026.19(e) via email on Monday, pursuant to § 1026.19(e)(1)(iv) the consumer is considered to have received the disclosures on Thursday, three business days later."; "The creditor may, alternatively, rely on evidence that the consumer received the emailed disclosures earlier."; "if the creditor emails the disclosures at 1 p.m. on Tuesday, the consumer emails the creditor with an acknowledgement of receipt of the disclosures at 5 p.m. on the same day, the creditor could demonstrate that the disclosures were received on the same day."; "Creditors using electronic delivery methods, such as email, must also comply with § 1026.37(o)(3)(iii), which provides that the disclosures in § 1026.37 may be provided to the consumer in electronic form, subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the E-Sign Act."; "if a creditor delivers the disclosures required under § 1026.19(e)(1)(i) to a consumer via email, but the creditor did not obtain the consumer's consent to receive disclosures via email prior to delivering the disclosures, then the creditor does not comply with § 1026.37(o)(3)(iii)".

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. The row's deadline "LE received ≥ 7 specific business days before consummation" misstates the anchor: §1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B) requires the creditor to "deliver or place in the mail" the LE not later than the seventh business day before consummation (comment 19(e)(1)(iii)-2: mailed Monday June 1 → consummation on or after Tuesday June 9). Receipt (actual or deemed) governs the fee/intent gate in 21.4, not the 7-day gate.
2. The row lists §1026.19(e)(2) only as "no fees except credit report before intent"; it omits (e)(2)(ii) (mandatory statement on any pre-LE written estimate — binds 20.4's quotes and 21.1's screens) and (e)(2)(iii) (no verifying documents before the LE — binds 22.1's needs-list timing).
3. "E-SIGN": the LE-specific hook is §1026.37(o)(3)(iii) (consent obtained before electronic delivery); the general rule is §1026.17(a)(1). An LE e-mailed without prior consent is non-compliant even if the consumer later opens it.
4. The brief's illustration "application Thu Oct 8 → LE due Tue Oct 13 given Columbus Day ... if the creditor is closed that day" does not hold under the baseline addendum's `business_days_creditor` decision (Mon–Fri, federal holidays closed): the third creditor business day after Thu Oct 8 is Wed Oct 14 (Fri 9, Tue 13, Wed 14). Tue Oct 13 results only if Sat Oct 10 counts (creditor open Saturdays) or if the creditor is open on Columbus Day. The build keeps the calendar `creditor` explicit and the partner declares its open days (open question 1).
5. The fixture P&I of $3,402.63 is off by one cent: 560,000 × 0.0051041667 ÷ (1 − 1.0051041667⁻³⁶⁰) = 3,402.619… → $3,402.62 (rounded half-up at the cent).
6. The 21.3 inventory row (and the Notice Registry seed) carries the RESPA Servicing Disclosure Statement; for forward loans it is replaced by the §1026.37(m)(6) statement issued here.
7. "Fannie Mae A3-2-01" is a general compliance covenant (12/10/2025), not an LE rule; the only Fannie Mae-specific LE consequence is that the servicing statement must match the executed operating model (partner services; SM subservices — "to service your loan" is checked, with SM disclosed at closing as the payment address, 25.4/30.2).

#### State machine

LE lifecycle (per `disclosures` row, kind `le`, version 1): `assembling` (fees and pricing gathered) → `rendered` (H-24 produced, data hash) → `pending_mlo` (escalation, stage `le_terms`) → `approved` → `delivered` (esign_portal/in_person) or `mailed` (print) → `received` (evidence) or `deemed_received` (mailbox) → `superseded` (a revised LE from 21.5 or the CD) — terminal `superseded`, `withdrawn_application` (21.6). Application-level: `trid_received` → `le_issued` (on delivered/mailed) → `le_received` (actual or deemed) → hand-off to 21.4 (intent to proceed/fees/lock). Guards: `delivered` via electronic channel requires a valid `esign_consent_id`; `approved` requires `SAFE_1008_103_MLO_OF_RECORD_GATE` open (21.1) and an `mlo_reviews` row with `decision='approved'` for this exact `data_hash`; a change of any term after approval returns the LE to `rendered`.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Calendars.** `creditor`: Monday–Friday excluding the federal legal public holidays and any partner-published closures (baseline addendum §4 decision; open question 1 covers Saturdays and Columbus Day). `regz_specific`: every calendar day except Sundays and the federal legal public holidays (Columbus Day Mon Oct 12, Veterans Day Wed Nov 11, Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26, Christmas Fri Dec 25, 2026; New Year's Day observed Fri Jan 1, 2027). Counting: day 0 = the anchor day; the deadline is the Nth qualifying day after it.
2. **LE due date.** `le_due_at = end_of_day(nth_business_day(trid_application_date, 3, creditor))`. The agent targets same-day issuance; the timer is the outer bound.
3. **Delivery and receipt.** In-person or e-sign-portal delivery with a logged acknowledgement is receipt on that date; e-mail or portal delivery without acknowledgement and print-mail delivery are "not in person": `deemed_receipt_date = nth_business_day(delivery_or_mailing_date, 3, regz_specific)`; any earlier evidence (portal view event tied to the authenticated borrower, acknowledgement reply, e-signature) sets `received_at` and `effective_receipt_date` earlier.
4. **Tolerance class derivation** (written to `fee_items.tolerance_class` at `fee.baseline.set`): `le_section ∈ {A_origination}` → zero; `B_cannot_shop` (creditor-required, consumer may not shop, any provider) → zero; `C_can_shop` with `provider_source ∈ {list_provider, creditor_selected_third_party}` and provider not an affiliate → ten_percent (aggregated with recording fees); `C_can_shop` with `provider_source = affiliate` or paid to the creditor → zero; `C_can_shop` with `consumer_selected_off_list` → unlimited; `E_taxes_gov`: transfer taxes → zero, recording fees → ten_percent; `F_prepaids` (prepaid interest, property insurance premiums, property taxes) → unlimited; `G_initial_escrow` → unlimited; `H_other` required by the creditor → zero unless a third-party service the consumer may shop for; not required by the creditor (e.g., owner's title policy, optional) → unlimited; `J_lender_credit` → zero (a lender credit may not decrease). 21.5 tests against these classes.
5. **Estimation standard.** Every `fee_items` row records `estimate_source` and `estimated_at`; "best information reasonably available" (unlimited items) means the most recent vendor quote/table available on the estimate date; a stale table (older than `rule_sets.fee_table_max_age_days`, default 30) blocks rendering.
6. **Lender credits under the SM economics.** Third-party costs that SM bears are disclosed as consumer charges in sections B/C with an equal "Lender Credits" line in section J (general credit) unless the partner elects fee-specific credits; the credit is zero-tolerance, so 21.5 may never reduce it below the LE amount. Premium pricing that funds the credit appears in the rate, never as negative points.
7. **Servicing statement.** `servicing_intent='service'` for every loan in the operating model (partner is the servicer; SM subservices from day one); the CD's servicing block and the first-payment letter name SM as the payment address (25.4/30.2).
8. **Contact block.** Lender = partner legal name + NMLSR ID; Loan Officer = `mlo_of_record` name as it appears in NMLS + NMLSR ID; e-mail/phone = the partner-branded queue routed to SM's platform.
9. **Calculations.** P&I = L·r/(1−(1+r)⁻ⁿ) with r = note rate/12 at full precision, rounded half-up to the cent once. TIP = Σ scheduled interest ÷ loan amount, three decimals then displayed to three. In 5 years = 60 × P&I (+ MI) + loan costs (sections A+B+C totals); principal in 5 years = loan amount − scheduled balance after payment 60. APR per Appendix J on the amount financed (loan amount − prepaid finance charges), displayed to three decimals; an `apr_calculations` row stores inputs and method (25.1 re-tests it against the §1026.22 tolerance at the CD).

#### Integrations
- Pricing engine (20.4, in-house): `GET pricingScenario(quote_id)` → rate, price, points/credits, lock status; idempotent by `quote_id`; a changed scenario after MLO approval re-renders.
- Fee sources: title/settlement rate engine or provider quote API (00b-orig N7, vendor-specific; fallback: rate-card table with `estimate_source='fee_schedule'`), AMC fee schedule (N5), credit vendor price list (N2), flood vendor (N6), MI rate quote (N9, MISMO Estimated Rate Quote API), county recording/transfer-tax tables (`jurisdiction_rules`), tax/insurance estimates (22.1/30.3). Each call is logged with the quote reference on `fee_items`.
- APR/TIP/In-5-Years engine (`origination/disclosures`): pure function of `fee_items` + terms; produces `apr_calculations`.
- Document engine and e-delivery portal (00b-orig N15 / 00b-svc N9): render H-24 (PDF + MISMO 3.4 data), deliver with the provider list; receipt events (authenticated view, acknowledgement, e-signature) → `disclosure.le.received`; print-mail vendor returns a mailing-date proof → `disclosure.le.mailed`; outage: print fallback within the same creditor day; if both fail before `le_due_at`, sev 1 and hand delivery/courier (`delivery_channel='courier'` is not in person; mailbox rule applies).
- Consent store (20.3): `assertConsent(borrower_id, scope='disclosures', channel)` before any electronic delivery.
- MLO queue (21.1): escalation stage `le_terms` with the rendered LE and the pricing rationale.
- Timer Engine: `assertGateOpen` for consummation/CD scheduling; 21.4 reads `effective_receipt_date`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`disclosure` agent (tools: `assembleFees`, `getPricingScenario`, `deriveTolerance`, `computeAPR`, `renderH24`, `buildProviderList`, `openEscalation(mlo_of_record, le_terms)`, `assertConsent`, `deliver`, `recordReceipt`, `setTimers`, `writeDecision`). Behavior: on `application.trid_received` it assembles every fee with a source reference, computes the disclosures, renders the H-24 and the provider list, files the MLO escalation, and on approval delivers through the consented channel the same day; it records receipt evidence as it arrives, sets `deemed_receipt_date`, opens the 7-day gate date and the closing-cost expiration, and emits `fee.baseline.set`. Decision record: `{application_id, disclosure_id, le_version, trid_application_date, le_due_at, fees[{fee, section, source, ref, amount, tolerance_class}], pricing_scenario_id, calculations{apr, tip, in5y}, mlo_review_id, data_hash, delivery{channel, consent_id, delivered_at/mailed_at}, receipt{evidence, effective_receipt_date}, earliest_consummation_date, closing_costs_expire_at, model_version, prompt_version, rationale}`. Guardrails: never deliver electronically without a prior valid consent; never release an LE whose data hash was not approved by the MLO of record in `assisted` mode; never impose or collect any fee (only 21.4 may, after receipt and intent); never condition the LE on verification documents; never disclose an estimate older than the freshness rule; never alter the servicing statement from `service`; never issue an LE without the MLO's NMLSR ID. Escalations: `mlo_of_record` (LE terms), `compliance-sentinel` on any timer breach or calendar anomaly, `human_agent` when the borrower asks for an explanation of the LE (the agent may explain the form's contents — App. A (b)(2)(i) — but routes rate/fee negotiation requests to the MLO). Human path: ops console shows the assembled LE with the same approval and delivery controls.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Six items received at 23:50 on a creditor business day: `trid_application_date` is that day in the creditor's time zone; the clock does not slip to the next day.
- Borrower revokes E-SIGN consent between rendering and delivery: deliver by print the same day; mailbox rule applies.
- Portal shows "delivered" but the borrower never opens: deemed receipt on the third specific business day; 21.4's fee gate waits.
- Borrower adds a co-borrower after the LE: revised LE under 21.5 (borrower-requested change) — the initial LE's 7-day anchor stands.
- Property state has a transfer tax paid by the borrower (e.g., a Florida purchase with documentary stamps on the note — `jurisdiction_rules`): section E with zero tolerance.
- Vendor fee API down: use the rate-card table, flag `estimate_source='fee_schedule'`, and schedule a refresh; the LE still issues on time.
- MLO returns the package (e.g., wants a different lock strategy): re-render; the 3-day clock keeps running; if the deadline is within 4 hours, sev 1 and the MLO's supervisor (partner `officer`) may approve.
- Loan amount changes before the LE issues (e.g., payoff updated): the LE reflects the latest borrower-confirmed amount; no revised LE is needed before the initial one exists.
- Application withdrawn before the LE: 21.6 records the withdrawal; no LE; the 3-day timer is cancelled with reason.
- Consumer requests a paper copy after electronic delivery: provided free (E-SIGN (c)(1)(B)(iv) statement); receipt date unchanged.
- Bona fide personal financial emergency: written, dated, signed statement from every consumer; the 7-day gate opens on receipt of the statement; printed forms prohibited.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Creditor calendar.** Is the partner open on Saturdays or on Columbus Day/Juneteenth? Default: Mon–Fri, all federal holidays closed (baseline addendum §4); the partner may publish additional open days, which only shorten deadlines.
2. **Lender-credit presentation of SM-borne costs.** General lender credit vs fee-specific credits. Default: general credit in section J equal to the SM-borne third-party charges; zero tolerance accepted; 21.5 must never reduce it.
3. **Delivery channel default.** Default: e-sign portal with authenticated view as receipt evidence; e-mail attachment is not used (no receipt evidence); print-mail only on consent revocation or portal failure.
4. **7-day waiver policy.** Default: never solicited; accepted only on the consumer's initiative with the written statement; `underwriting_reviewer` not required, `compliance-sentinel` review after the fact.
5. **Revised-LE effect on the 7-day rule.** Default: the initial LE's delivery/mailing date is the anchor; revised LEs (21.5) do not restart the seven-day period (rule text keys the requirement to "the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i)"); counsel to confirm.
6. **Prepaid-interest day basis on the LE.** Default: 365-day basis for the estimate (uniform note accrual conventions govern the CD figure, 25.2/26.3).
