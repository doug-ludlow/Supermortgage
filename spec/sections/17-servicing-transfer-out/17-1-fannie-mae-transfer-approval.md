### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**A2-7-03, Post-Delivery Servicing Transfers (05/13/2026; Guide edition Aug 12, 2026).** "The servicer must obtain Fannie Mae's prior written consent for any transfer of servicing"; "the servicer or subservicer must not transfer its responsibility for servicing or subservicing any mortgage loans" without it. "Any unauthorized transfer of servicing or subservicing will not be recognized by Fannie Mae"; an unauthorized transfer "may occur when the servicer or subservicer fails to give Fannie Mae adequate notice," "may be the basis for terminating the contractual relationships," and Fannie Mae "may choose to impose sanctions; impose compensatory fees" and "hold any transferor or transferee servicer or subservicer that enters into an unauthorized transfer of servicing jointly and severally liable" for losses. The transferor "must submit a fully completed Form 629 at least 60 days prior to the earlier of proposed sale or transfer date" for servicing transfers and "30 days prior to the earlier of proposed sale or transfer date for subservicing transfers"; "the proposed transfer date must be the first business day of the month"; "the servicer must submit Form 629 for all active mortgage loans" and "a separate Form 629 for acquired properties, REMIC, and reverse mortgage loans." Fannie Mae "will also evaluate the transferee and transferor servicers' and the subservicer's performance prior to approval." From the earlier of sale or transfer date "the transferor servicer and transferee servicer will be jointly and severally liable to Fannie Mae for all warranties and for repurchase, all special obligations," and "all reporting, compliance, and audit oversight related duties"; both "must ensure that all existing special servicing obligations associated with the transferred mortgage loan are disclosed." The transferor must "provide special notification to the transferee servicer" for eMortgages and loans "subject to a resale restriction agreement or shared equity transaction agreement." **Servicing transfer as a result of the servicer's termination:** "the transfer of the servicer's entire servicing portfolio must include all mortgage loans that are being serviced even if they no longer generate any servicing fee income," and acquired properties removed from active accounting "must be transferred, unless Fannie Mae has notified the servicer that Fannie Mae's records have been closed or the servicer is only waiting for a request of reimbursement claims payment." Assignments: "if the transferee servicer is a master servicer utilizing a subservicer and the subservicer will be the mortgagee of record, the required assignment must be either from the transferor to the subservicer if the mortgage loan is not registered with the MERS or from the MERS to the subservicer"; Fannie Mae "will hold both the transferor servicer and the transferee servicer accountable for ensuring all assignments are appropriately prepared and recorded."

**A2-1-07, Subservicing (05/13/2026).** Fannie Mae's consent is required for master-to-subservicer, subservicer-to-subservicer and subservicer-to-master moves; "the transferor servicer must indicate on the Request for Approval of Servicing Transfer (Form 629) if the transferee servicer will use a subservicer"; the master servicer and the subservicer "must execute and submit the Data Access Authorization Form (Form 101) at the inception and termination" of the arrangement; "the master servicer must confirm its existing subservicing arrangements when it submits the Lender Record Information (Form 582) each year"; the subservicing agreement must acknowledge "Fannie Mae's right to rescind its recognition of the subservicing arrangement if Fannie Mae decides to transfer the master servicer's portfolio for any reason," and "the master servicer's and the subservicer's rights to receive the servicing fee and subservicing fee will be terminated if Fannie Mae transfers the servicing portfolio for any reason."

**Fannie Mae-initiated and voluntary terminations.** A1-2-02 (11/12/2014): Fannie Mae may terminate the Lender Contract without cause; the servicer "may attempt to arrange for a sale of the servicing … to another Fannie Mae-approved servicer" within 90 days (approval "will not be unreasonably withheld"), with the transfer occurring 60 days after approval; if the servicer does not arrange a sale Fannie Mae transfers the servicing itself **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the "15th day following the 90-day period" mechanic was summarized, not quoted]**; delinquency-based terminations carry at least 90 days' notice, are effective "as of the last day of the transfer month," and pay no termination fee; otherwise the fee is two times annualized servicing compensation (pre-2013 deliveries) or the lesser of two times the Net Servicing Rate × non-delinquent UPB and market value (2013+ deliveries); in all cases the servicer "must comply with instructions provided by Fannie Mae regarding requirements reasonably necessary to effectuate the transfer of servicing" and bears "all reasonable and customary costs and expenses related to the transfer." A1-4.1-02 (09/09/2020): remedies are "distinct, cumulative, and non-exclusive," including termination for cause "effective immediately" with no termination fee, "requiring the servicer to take steps to sell and transfer all of its Fannie Mae servicing … to an unrelated entity," and denial of servicing transfer requests; after termination "the servicer will have no further rights in the servicing." A1-2-01 (11/12/2014): a servicer's voluntary termination is effective "on the last business day of the third month following the month in which the notice is given," may not be for less than all loans, does not release prior liabilities, and the servicer bears transfer costs.

**A2-1-01 (12/17/2025; research/00b Part B(b)).** For portfolios ≥20,000 loans, 180 days' prior written notice to Fannie Mae of a change of third-party technology provider, a transition plan on request, notice within 5 business days of any termination/breach of the provider contract, and provider cooperation "with any transfer of such mortgage loans to a new servicer, technology service provider or servicing platform … upon payment of reasonable and customary transition fees."

**Quick Exchange mechanics.** Servicing Transfers Transition Guide (Aug 26, 2025): eTransfers closed Oct 31, 2025; from Nov 1, 2025 "all servicing and internal transfers must be submitted via a 629 form in Quick Exchange"; loan additions by the 10th calendar day of the month prior to the effective transfer date, deletions by the 25th (or the prior business day), "attestation confirmation is required for all submissions by CD25," and "all servicing and internal transfers will be processed in Fannie Mae's Investor Reporting system on Business Day 3." Servicing Transfers User Guide: "This form must be completed by the Transferor"; template columns = transferor servicer number (9 digits), transferee servicer number, Fannie Mae loan number (10 digits), UPB (dollars and cents), transferor custodian and transferee custodian "exactly as shown in the Custodian Matrix" (29 custodians incl. "FNMAeNote"); subservicing is captured by the question "Will a subservicer be used by the transferee?" with additional fields; statuses New → Pending Servicing Transfer Review → Pending Servicing Transfer Analysis → Pending Internal Sign Off → Pending Final Approval → Approval Letters Sent / Denied / Cancelled; automated reconciliation emails "between the 20th calendar day and 25th calendar day"; attestation by "Click Agree," after which "no changes can be made"; approval letters are emailed. Document Transfers Job Aid v5 (Jan 23, 2026): Fannie Mae issues the D-Code; the transferor gives both custodians "the electronic Form 629 (Servicing Transfer Approval Letter)," "the transfer D-Code" and "a final trial balance." IRM 3-01 (10/13/2021, in the Apr 8, 2026 manual) still describes Transaction Type 32 due "no later than 15 days before the effective date" and finalization "by the 25th calendar day of the month prior" **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — retained in the manual; Quick Exchange now carries the loan list]**.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint:** (1) "Per Fannie Mae" is concrete: 30 days (subservicing) / 60 days (servicing), first-business-day transfer date, CD10/CD25/BD3 cadence; (2) the blueprint's system name is the retired eTransfers/"Servicing Transfer Approvals" — the live system is Quick Exchange; (3) the blueprint omits Form 101 at termination, Form 582 confirmation, the termination-portfolio scope rule, Fannie Mae-initiated transfers (A1-2-02/A1-4.1-02) and the A2-1-01 180-day technology-provider notice; (4) "SoR" is right for submission, but the loan-level list, attestation reconciliation and every deliverable are Supermortgage's.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `transfer.batch.proposed{direction=out}` — created by the `transfer` agent from (a) the partner's written instruction (`partner.instruction.received{kind=transfer_out}`), (b) a Fannie Mae termination or transfer notice (`fnma.termination_notice.received`, A1-2-02/A1-4.1-02), or (c) Supermortgage's own exit notice under the subservicing agreement.
- Loan universe: all active loans under the partner's servicer number subserviced by Supermortgage (or the subset in the instruction); separate lists for acquired properties (A2-7-03) and any REMIC/reverse (n/a for this platform).
- Quick Exchange e-mails (validation results, CD20–25 reconciliation reminders, approval letter with D-Code) → `email-in` → `transfer.fnma_query.received`, `transfer.fnma_consent.received`.
- Fannie Mae Connect "Servicing Transfers Origination and Modification Data Report" pulls after approval.
- Timer sweeps.

#### Data model
- `transfer_batches` (1.1) + `direction` enum {`in`,`out`} (new); `transfer_type` extended with `sub_to_master`, `servicing_sale`, `fnma_directed`, `master_change_sub_retained` (the §1024.33(b)(2)(i)(C) case — not a transfer-out, see rules); `transferee_party_id`, `transferee_servicer_number char(9)`, `transferee_subservicer_party_id null`, `termination_basis` enum {`partner_instruction`,`fnma_without_cause`,`fnma_for_cause`,`partner_voluntary_termination`,`supermortgage_exit`,`sale`} , `fnma_notice_document_id`, `sale_agreement_document_id`, `fee_settlement jsonb` (A1-2-02 termination fee facts, transition fees), `qx_request_id text`, `qx_status` enum mirroring the Quick Exchange statuses, `approval_letter_document_id`, `d_code`, `attested_at`, `attested_by`, `form101_termination_document_id`, `form582_reflected_at`, plus the 1.2 columns (`form629_document_id`, `custodian_matrix_document_id`, `fnma_status`, `fnma_conditions`, `liability_start_date`).
- `transfer_batch_loans` (1.1) + `offboarding_status` enum {`listed`,`frozen`,`packaged`,`cutover`,`support_window`,`retained`,`withdrawn`} and `transferee_loan_number text null`.
- `transfer_batch_loan_list_versions` (1.2) reused; `escalations{kind=human_portal_task}` reused.
- Retention: `life_of_loan_plus_4y` for the package, approval letter and D-Code (Fannie Mae records; A2-7-03 "maintain adequate records … in their corporate records"); `respa_5y` for the transfer plan.

#### State machine
`transfer_batches.status` (`case_type='transfer_out'`): `proposed` → `plan_approved` (Bulletin 2020-02 plan; partner `officer`) → `package_ready` (Form 629 Excel + Custodian Matrix + loan list + subservicer answer + special notifications list + Form 101 termination draft) → `submitted` (`fnma_portal_operator`/partner user completion evidence; `qx_request_id`) → `info_requested` ⇄ `submitted` → `approved` (approval letter + D-Code hashed; `officer` confirms conditions) → `loan_list_frozen` (CD25 attestation "Agree" evidence) → `notice_window` (17.2) → `pre_cutover` (17.3 preliminary tape, counterparties, MERS/eNote/custodian arrangements) → `cutover` (T: final tape, trial balance as of T-1, funds wire, `payment_holds{transfer_out_cutover}` set on every loan) → `post_transfer` (60-day payment window, 30-day final accounting, custodian shipment, transferee requests) → `retention` (one-year NoE/RFI window; archive) → `closed`. Exceptions: `denied` (terminal; partner decision), `withdrawn`, `on_hold` (Fannie Mae requires a longer timeframe; transfer date re-based to a later first business day with all timers re-issued). Guards: `proposed → package_ready` requires `FNMA_A2_7_03_TRANSFER_DATE_GATE`; `submitted` requires a portal completion record; `approved` requires the approval-letter hash; `loan_list_frozen` requires the attestation evidence; `cutover` requires 17.2 goodbye run `complete` and 17.3 preliminary tape acknowledged.

#### Business rules and calculations
- Transfer-date rule: `transfer_date = firstBusinessDay(month, 'fannie_et')`. Worked example (used throughout Section 17): transfer date **Tue Dec 1, 2026**. Subservicing Form 629 deadline = Dec 1 − 30 = **Sun Nov 1, 2026** → internal buffer **Oct 25**; the portal task is scheduled for Fri Oct 30 (last business day before the deadline). Servicing-sale example: sale date Nov 16, 2026, transfer date Dec 1 → 60-day clock from Nov 16 → **Sept 17, 2026**. Quick Exchange cadence: additions by **Nov 10**, reconciliation download by **Nov 20**, deletions and attestation by **Wed Nov 25** (Thanksgiving is Nov 26), processing **Thu Dec 3, 2026** (BD3; Dec 1 = BD1, Dec 2 = BD2).
- Batch-type mapping: `sub_to_sub`/`sub_to_master` → 30-day rule, partner is transferor of record, Supermortgage attests the loan list; `servicing_sale` → 60-day rule from the earlier of sale/transfer date, purchase price recorded (Quick Exchange purchase-price tolerance not present in the user guide; research/00b's ±5 bps note is **[UNVERIFIED]**); `fnma_directed` → Fannie Mae instructions govern timing; the subservicing recognition is rescinded and the servicing-fee/subservicing-fee rights end on Fannie Mae's transfer (A2-1-07); `master_change_sub_retained` → Form 629 by the selling master (60 days), Supermortgage re-executes Form 101 with the new master and re-evidences Forms 1013/1014 under the new master's authorization (6.1/6.2), MERS TOS by the sellers (1.5), and **no RESPA notice** if payee, address, account number and payment amount are unchanged (§1024.33(b)(2)(i)(C)) — `officer` sign-off records the exclusion.
- Portfolio scope on termination (`fnma_directed`, `partner_voluntary_termination`, `supermortgage_exit`): every serviced loan, including zero-fee loans, plus acquired properties not yet closed by Fannie Mae on a separate Form 629 (A2-7-03); loans awaiting only claim reimbursement are excluded with evidence.
- Loan-list integrity: the platform's system of record is the universe; the Form 629 list = active loans as of package date; payoffs/repurchases/foreclosure completions between submission and CD25 are deletions (new list version); nothing may be added after CD10; the CD20 reconciliation compares the Quick Exchange download to `transfer_batch_loans` and must show zero differences before attestation.
- Joint-and-several liability start = `min(sale_date, transfer_date)` recorded as `liability_start_date` (partner risk register); Supermortgage's own liability for its servicing period survives (A2-7-03; A1-2-01).
- Termination-fee facts (A1-2-02) are recorded, never computed by the platform (partner/counsel matter); the platform supplies UPB, delinquency status and servicing-fee data as of the termination date.

#### Integrations
- **Quick Exchange (portal-only)** — `human_portal_task` package: Form 629 Excel (template version), Custodian Matrix selections, transferor/transferee servicer numbers, subservicer answer and fields, sale/transfer dates, special notifications (eMortgages, resale-restricted loans), acquired-property list, contact e-mails, expected artifacts (request ID, validation e-mail, approval letter). Attestation task at CD25: the operator downloads the Fannie Mae list, the agent reconciles it, the partner `officer` authorizes "Agree." Operator: partner user (default; Consolidated Technology Guide credentials) or Supermortgage `fnma_portal_operator` under Related-Party authorization (decision 1).
- **E-mail intake** (`servicing_transfers@fanniemae.com`, Quick Exchange notifications, approval letter) — `email-in` into `integration_messages`; the approval letter is parsed for D-Code, conditions and loan count; `officer` confirms before `approved`.
- **Fannie Mae Connect** — "Servicing Transfers Origination and Modification Data Report" (API where available, else UI download by `fnma_portal_operator`) to confirm loan-level status and, on BD3, that the loans now sit under the transferee servicer number.
- **Technology Manager / Form 101** — termination form e-mailed by the partner `officer`; partner CA removes Related-Party users and System IDs; Supermortgage disables `fnma-*` adapters for the partner scope (`SM_XFER_OUT_ACCESS_REVOCATION_5BD`).
- Failure handling: Quick Exchange validation errors → corrected template within the SLA; denial → `denied` with a partner decision record; Fannie Mae "longer timeframe" → `on_hold`.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Documents: transfer plan (Bulletin 2020-02), Form 629 package and versions, Custodian Matrix, approval letter, D-Code, attestation evidence, Form 101 termination, Fannie Mae termination correspondence — all `documents` with SHA-256.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`transfer` agent (tools: `buildTransferPlan`, `buildForm629`, `buildCustodianMatrix`, `validateLoanList`, `reconcileQxDownload`, `createPortalTask`, `parseApprovalLetter`, `computeDeadlines`, `draftFnmaResponse`, `draftForm101Termination`, `writeDecision`, `notifyPartner`). It builds the plan and package from the system of record, computes every deadline, files portal tasks, reconciles the CD20 download, drafts responses to Fannie Mae queries and the Form 101 termination, and tracks the approval. Decision record: `{batch_id, direction:'out', transfer_type, package_version, checks_run, deadline_table, portal_task_ids, rationale, model_version, rule_set_version}`. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (Quick Exchange, Connect downloads); `officer` (partner) for plan approval, attestation, acceptance of conditions, any Fannie Mae correspondence, Form 101 termination and termination-fee matters; `attorney` only when Fannie Mae terminates for cause and counsel review is requested by the partner. No borrower contact in 17.1. Human path: the ops-console package builder with manual entry.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Fannie Mae requires a longer timeframe or denies: `on_hold`/`denied`; goodbye notices already mailed → corrective run (17.2); nothing moves.
- Transferee not in good standing or lacking Form 2017 (A2-7-03 conditions): package blocked; partner selects another transferee.
- Loans in foreclosure/bankruptcy/workout: allowed; identified on the list (A2-7-03) and routed to 17.4; loans with a foreclosure sale in the transfer month are flagged so the transferee can take the REOgram (15.1) — DRA firm reassignment noted **[UNVERIFIED mechanics]**.
- Payoff/repurchase after CD25 attestation: the loan still moves in Fannie Mae's system on BD3 unless Fannie Mae removes it; Supermortgage reports the removal (5.3) before BD2 and informs `servicing_transfers@fanniemae.com`; the transferee is told in the final tape.
- Partial portfolio moves (partner splits the book): multiple batches with separate D-Codes; the platform prevents a loan from being on two open batches.
- `fnma_directed` for cause with immediate effect: Fannie Mae's instructions override the cadence; the platform switches all 17.x timers to `fnma_instruction` anchors recorded on the batch; the RESPA 30-day exception (17.2) may apply to the goodbye timing only where §1024.33(b)(3)(ii) is met.
- Supermortgage's own exit while the partner keeps the loans: `sub_to_master`; Supermortgage may not stop servicing before the approved transfer date (unauthorized-transfer sanctions).

- 17.1-T3 Given a `servicing_sale` with sale date Nov 16, 2026, then the 60-day deadline is Sept 17, 2026.
- 17.1-T4 Given the Quick Exchange CD20 download differs from `transfer_batch_loans` by one loan, then attestation is blocked until a new list version resolves it.
- 17.1-T5 Given a `master_change_sub_retained` batch with identical payee/address/account/amount, then no goodbye run is created and an `officer` exclusion record exists.
- 17.1-T6 Given the last batch for a partner cuts over Dec 1, 2026, then Form 101 termination is due Dec 8, 2026 and partner-scoped credentials are revoked by Dec 8.
- 17.1-T7 Given a Fannie Mae without-cause termination notice dated Oct 1, 2026, then `FNMA_A1_2_02_SALE_ARRANGEMENT_90` is due Dec 30, 2026 and an `officer` task exists.
- 17.1-T8 Given a loan pays off Nov 27 after attestation, then it is flagged `withdrawn_after_attestation`, the removal is reported by BD2 and the transferee tape marks it.

#### Audit and evidence
Package versions and hashes, portal-task completion evidence, Quick Exchange e-mails, approval letter and D-Code, CD20 reconciliation and CD25 attestation evidence, Form 101 termination, credential revocation log, all timers with satisfying events, and `agent_decisions` — the record that the transfer was authorized (A2-7-03 non-recognition defense) and that Supermortgage cooperated (A1-2-02/A1-4.1-02, A2-1-01).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Quick Exchange operator for transfer-out: partner employee (default) vs Supermortgage `fnma_portal_operator` under Related-Party authorization.
2. Whether Transaction Type 32 is still required in addition to the Quick Exchange list — default: not filed; confirm with `servicing_transfers@fanniemae.com` per batch (mirrors 1.2-Q3).
3. Post-transfer support window length in the subservicing agreement — default 90 days (see 17.3), with transition fees per A2-1-01.
4. `master_change_sub_retained` treatment of custodial accounts and Form 101 — the accounts remain **Supermortgage's** throughout (A2-1-07; 6.1), so what changes is the servicer number on the title and the master servicer named on the forms, not the account holder — default: re-execute Form 101 and re-evidence Forms 1013/1014 under the new master before the transfer date, keeping the same depository accounts open (no closure under `SM_XFER_OUT_CUSTODIAL_CLOSE_60`, because no population leaves Supermortgage).
