### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**50 U.S.C. §3953 (uscode.house.gov, current through Sept. 8, 2026 — verified today).** (a) Applies to an obligation on real or personal property owned by a servicemember that "(1) originated before the period of the servicemember's military service and for which the servicemember is still obligated; and (2) is secured by a mortgage, trust deed, or other security in the nature of a mortgage." (b) In an action filed during, or within one year after, the period of military service, the court "may" (and "shall" on application, if "the servicemember's ability to comply with the obligation is materially affected by military service") stay the proceedings or adjust the obligation. (c) "A sale, foreclosure, or seizure of property for a breach of an obligation described in subsection (a) shall not be valid if made during, or within one year after, the period of the servicemember's military service except—(1) upon a court order granted before such sale, foreclosure, or seizure with a return made and approved by the court; or (2) if made pursuant to an agreement as provided in section 3918 of this title." (d) Knowing violations: fine under title 18 and/or imprisonment up to one year. The one-year tail became permanent after the 2018 amendment (statute notes).

**§3931 (verified today).** In "any civil action or proceeding ... in which the defendant does not make an appearance," before entering judgment the court "shall require the plaintiff to file with the court an affidavit—(A) stating whether or not the defendant is in military service and showing necessary facts to support the affidavit; or (B) if the plaintiff is unable to determine whether or not the defendant is in military service, stating that the plaintiff is unable to determine"; if the defendant is in military service the court "may not enter a judgment until after the court appoints an attorney"; a stay of at least 90 days on the conditions in (d); the affidavit "may be satisfied by a statement, declaration, verification, or certificate, in writing, subscribed and certified to be true under penalty of perjury"; false affidavits are punishable (title 18; up to one year); default judgments may be reopened on application "not later than 90 days after the date of the termination of or release from military service" (g); bona fide purchasers protected (h).

**§3911 definitions (verified today).** "Servicemember" = member of the uniformed services (10 U.S.C. §101(a)(5)); "military service" = active duty (10 U.S.C. §101(d)(1)) for the Armed Forces incl. Space Force and Coast Guard, National Guard service under a federal call of more than 30 consecutive days (32 U.S.C. §502(f)) for a national emergency, PHS/NOAA commissioned officers' active service, and "any period during which a servicemember is absent from duty on account of sickness, wounds, leave, or other lawful cause"; "period of military service" = entry to release or death; "judgment" = "any judgment, decree, order, or ruling, final or temporary"; "dependent" (spouse, child, ≥50%-supported individual). §3918 waivers must be in a written agreement executed during or after service **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — text not retrieved today; 12-point type/separate-instrument detail from general knowledge]**; §3932 (stay when the servicemember has notice) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; §3919 (13.9/8.x): a stay/postponement request "shall not itself" support an adverse credit report or a National Guard/reserve annotation.

**Fannie Mae D2-3.4-01, Military Indulgence (06/13/2018 — verified today).** Covers civilians who "incur a mortgage debt and subsequently enter military service," National Guard members called by federal authorities and "members of the National Guard or other state-supported military unit who are called to active duty by a state governor" (broader than the statute). Documentation: "Fannie Mae's Request for Military Indulgence (Form 180)" or equivalent, a DMDC certificate, Form 710 for hardship where relief beyond the rate cap is sought; "The servicer is authorized to identify servicemembers ... by using the Defense Manpower Data Center's SCRA website database" and "to proactively inform active duty servicemembers." Foreclosure: "The servicer must stay any foreclosure proceedings that were already in process or postpone the initiation of foreclosure proceedings against a servicemember eligible for military indulgence"; "The servicer must attempt to ascertain the military status of the mortgagor(s) before initiating foreclosure proceedings"; "must comply with specific state or local laws that address the effect of the SCRA"; "must also provide a 12–month extended stay of foreclosure and other legal proceedings from the date on which military service ends"; "must not attempt to obtain the eligible servicemember's written consent or petition the court to continue or commence foreclosure proceedings"; "should contact the eligible servicemember or their family, at a minimum, every three months to determine when their active duty status will end." Waive late charges after the call to active duty; a military-indulgence forbearance keeps the loan "current" if the agreed amount is paid. Reporting: status code 32 ("The servicer has granted a delinquent servicemember a stay of foreclosure proceedings under a military indulgence or any similar applicable law") and reason code 014 (F-1-21); delay credit 455 days first occurrence (13.5 exhibit). E-3.2-02 conditions expedited foreclosure on the borrower not being eligible for military indulgence. E-1.1-03: "borrower's current military status (if any)" in the referral data. **Form 180** exists at singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/form-180 (PDF did not render today — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED contents]**).

**DMDC (research/00b N4; SCRA User Guide v5.29, May 27, 2026).** Free account (password expiry 60 days); single-record request (SSN optional, DOB, names, Active Duty Status Date) → PDF certificate in seconds; multiple-record batch: 120-character fixed-width .txt (SSN 1–9, DOB 10–17, last name 18–42, first name 43–62, customer record id 63–92, status date 93–100, middle name 101–120), ≤250,000 records, results ≤24 h with On-Active-Duty (Y/X/N/Z), Left-Active-Duty-≤367-days, Future-Call-Up, begin/end dates, service component, certificate id; files purged after 15/30 days; **no API** — browser upload by a human (`dmdc` adapter prepares/parses files). Certificates evidence the "necessary facts" for §3931 affidavits.

**State/federal overlays.** Texas §51.002(i) requires the servicemember statement in notices ("Assert and protect your rights as a member of the armed forces of the United States"); Maryland §7-105.1 order-to-docket affidavit of SCRA compliance; NY/NJ/FL judicial practice requires non-military affidavits before default judgment; several states have SCRA-analog statutes for state-active-duty Guard members (e.g., CA Mil. & Vet. Code §800 et seq.) **[UNVERIFIED cites]** — encoded in `jurisdiction_rules.foreclosure.scra`. HUD's SCRA notice (HUD-92070) is an FHA requirement (11.4) — not required on conventional loans; Fannie Mae's authorization to "proactively inform" supports sending a plain-language SCRA rights letter to identified servicemembers (policy).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) The row's trigger "active-duty pre-service mortgage" is right for §3953 but Fannie Mae extends relief to state-activated Guard members and requires an affirmative DMDC check before *initiating* foreclosure regardless of borrower assertion. (2) The row omits the §3931 affidavit (judicial default judgments) and the perjury exposure, the §3953(c)(2) waiver path (which Fannie Mae forbids the servicer to seek), the quarterly-contact duty, the 12-month tail and the status-code/delay-credit mechanics. (3) "Court order required" is one of two statutory exits, but Fannie Mae policy is "must not ... petition the court" — the platform never seeks the order.

#### State machine
`scra_cases.status`: `open_active_duty` (verified active duty on a pre-service obligation) → `open_tail_12m` (service ended; protection through `protection_ends_on` = `addYears(service_end_on, 1)`, inclusive) → `closed` (tail expired, loan paid/liquidated with court order, or verification shows never on active duty). Foreclosure overlay: `SCRA_3953C_FC_PROTECTION_GATE` closed for steps `refer`, `first_notice`, `judgment_motion`, `sale_schedule`, `sale_conduct`, `eviction` while `open_*`; re-verification cadence timers run while a foreclosure is open on any loan (not only SCRA cases). Actors: agent (verification requests, case open/close on evidence, timers), `dmdc_operator` (portal), `signing_officer` (affidavits), `attorney` (filing, court stays), `officer` (none required; informed of any violation risk).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Who is checked**: every obligor on the note, every confirmed successor in interest (4.4), and — where state law protects them or the court requires — spouses/dependents; names from `borrowers` with aliases; DOB/SSN required for reliable matches (missing DOB ⇒ name-only search flagged `low_confidence` ⇒ `unable_to_determine` affidavit form (B) only with `attorney` concurrence).
2. **Pre-service test**: `pre_service_obligation = origination_date < service_begin_on` (statute); Fannie Mae's indulgence is not limited to pre-service obligations for foreclosure stays in its text ("eligible for military indulgence") — policy: apply the stay whenever the borrower is on active duty (decision 13.8-1).
3. **Protection end — a calendar year, not 365 days.** §3953(c) invalidates a sale, foreclosure or seizure made "during, or within one year after, the period of the servicemember's military service," so the tail is a **calendar-year addition**, not a fixed day count: `protection_ends_on = addYears(service_end_on, 1)`, and the gate stays closed **through and including** that date (it opens the next day). A `+365 calendar_days` offset is wrong and unsafe: any tail window that contains a Feb 29 is 366 days long, so 365 days opens the gate one day early — and a sale one day inside the window is invalid under §3953(c) and a knowing violation is a §3953(d) misdemeanor. **Feb 29 handling:** when `service_end_on` is Feb 29, the target date does not exist in the following year (the year after a leap year is never a leap year), so the addition clamps to the last day of the month — protection runs through **Feb 28** of the following year; the clamp is expressed generally (use Feb 29 if the target year is a leap year, otherwise Feb 28) so the rule stays correct if the anchor is ever re-based. Implement with a calendar library (`Temporal.PlainDate.add({years: 1})` / `date-fns addYears`, both of which clamp), never with day arithmetic; recompute `protection_ends_on` whenever `service_end_on` changes. DMDC "Left Active Duty within 367 days" flag ⇒ compute from the reported end date; if the end date is unknown, keep the gate closed and contact per the 90-day cadence.
4. **Worked timeline**: origination Mar. 1, 2024; active duty Mar. 15, 2026 – Feb. 28, 2027 (orders); loan delinquent from Apr. 1, 2026; day 121 = July 31, 2026 → 13.1 gate open but `SCRA_3953C_FC_PROTECTION_GATE` closed; service ends Feb. 28, 2027 ⇒ `addYears(2027-02-28, 1)` = protection through **Feb. 28, 2028** inclusive (this window happens to be 365 days because Feb. 29, 2028 falls *after* it — see 13.8-T11 for a window where the day count differs); referral permitted Mar. 1, 2028 at the earliest (subject to all other gates); status code 32 reported Apr. 2026 → Feb. 2028 (455-day cap on credit — 13.5: credit = min(actual 699, 455) = 455).
5. **Affidavit content** (judicial): party names, DMDC certificate ids and dates, statement (A) "is not in military service" with the facts (certificate results, borrower statements), or (B) unable to determine; executed under penalty of perjury by a `signing_officer` with records review; notarized where the court requires; attached certificates. Never executed on a certificate older than 30 days (policy) or with an unresolved "Future Call-Up" flag.
6. **Non-judicial sales**: firm/trustee declarations rely on the same certificates (≤7 days before sale); TX notices carry the statutory statement.
7. **Violations**: a sale held during protection is voidable/invalid (§3953(c)) — immediate rescission via `attorney` and 15.1; §3953(d) criminal exposure ⇒ `officer` notification the same day.
8. **Late charges/fees**: waive late charges accruing after the call to active duty (2.7 `FNMA_C1102_MILITARY_INDULGENCE_LC_WAIVER_GATE`); foreclosure fees incurred before the stay remain but are not pursued during the stay (policy; 13.9 fee-in-cap rule).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- DMDC "X" (active duty status date reported but not on active duty on that date) or "Z" (name/DOB mismatch): treat as unknown ⇒ single-record retry with alternates; unresolved ⇒ no affidavit (A); `attorney` decides (B).
- Future Call-Up flag: no protection yet, but re-verify weekly until the sale; postpone if the call-up date precedes the sale (policy).
- Co-borrower on duty, property occupied by a civilian borrower: protection applies to the obligation (the servicemember is "still obligated"); gate closed.
- Post-service obligation (loan originated during service): §3953 does not apply; Fannie Mae indulgence stay still applied by policy? — decision 13.8-1 (default: apply stay only if §3953/state law applies; offer 12.x workouts otherwise; document).
- Service ended more than 12 months ago but borrower asserts SCRA: verify; explain the tail; no gate.
- Sale held during protection: rescind; §3953(d) exposure; `officer` same day.
- Bankruptcy plus SCRA: both gates; DMDC before any relief-from-stay motion (14.x).
- Transfer-in: transferor certificates accepted if ≤30 days; else re-verify before any step.
- DMDC file purge (15/30 days): results imported within 5 days; certificates archived immediately.

- 13.8-T10 Given a borrower asks to waive SCRA protection so the sale can proceed, Then the agent declines to solicit/accept and routes to `attorney` (Fannie Mae forbids seeking consent).
- 13.8-T11 **(leap-crossing fixture — regression guard on the tail arithmetic)** Given `service_end_on` = **2027-06-01**, so the tail window contains **Feb 29, 2028** (366 actual days), Then `protection_ends_on` = **2028-06-01** (`SCRA_3953_TAIL_1Y`, a calendar-year addition); `SCRA_3953C_FC_PROTECTION_GATE` **must still be closed on 2028-05-31** — the date a `+365 calendar_days` offset would have opened — and on 2028-06-01, and opens only on **2028-06-02**. A build that permits `foreclosure.refer`, `first_notice`, `judgment_motion`, `sale_schedule`, `sale_conduct` or `eviction` on 2028-05-31 or 2028-06-01 fails this test (invalid sale under §3953(c); §3953(d) exposure).
- 13.8-T12 **(Feb 29 clamp)** Given `service_end_on` = **2028-02-29**, Then `protection_ends_on` = **2029-02-28** (the target date does not exist in the following year; the addition clamps to the last day of the month) and the gate opens **2029-03-01**; no exception is thrown and no null date is written.

#### Audit and evidence
Every verification (purpose, certificate id, result, operator), the SCRA case timeline, instructions to counsel with acknowledgments, executed affidavits with the records-review checklist and notary evidence, quarterly contact records, tail computations and gate refusals are stored in the foreclosure file — the record a court (or DOJ under §3953(d)/§3931(c)) would examine. Compliance Sentinel reports stale certificates on active foreclosures weekly.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Apply Fannie Mae's stay to borrowers on active duty whose loans originated *during* service (outside §3953) — default: no automatic stay; evaluate for workouts; document; partner may choose a broader policy.
2. DMDC via the portal (human batch) vs. a vendor reseller — default: portal batch daily by `dmdc_operator`; vendor as resilience option.
3. Certificate freshness thresholds (30 days pre-referral/first-notice/judgment; 7 days pre-sale) — default as stated.
4. Include spouses/dependents in checks — default: obligors and confirmed successors; dependents only where `jurisdiction_rules` requires.
5. Send the SCRA rights letter to all identified servicemembers — default: yes.
