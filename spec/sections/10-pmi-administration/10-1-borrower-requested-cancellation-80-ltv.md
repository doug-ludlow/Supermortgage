### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**HPA, 12 U.S.C. 4902(a) (law.cornell.edu and uscode.house.gov, verified 2026-09-09).** A BPMI requirement "shall be canceled on the cancellation date or any later date that the mortgagor fulfills all of the requirements under paragraphs (1) through (4)": (1) the mortgagor "submits a request in writing to the servicer that cancellation be initiated"; (2) "has a good payment history with respect to the residential mortgage"; (3) "is current on the payments required by the terms of the residential mortgage transaction"; and (4) "has satisfied any requirement of the holder of the mortgage (as of the date of a request under paragraph (1)) for—(A) evidence (of a type established in advance and made known to the mortgagor by the servicer promptly upon receipt of a request under paragraph (1)) that the value of the property securing the mortgage has not declined below the original value of the property; and (B) certification that the equity of the mortgagor in the residence securing the mortgage is unencumbered by a subordinate lien."

**Definitions, 12 U.S.C. 4901.** *Cancellation date* (fixed rate): "at the option of the mortgagor, the date on which the principal balance of the mortgage—(i) based solely on the initial amortization schedule for that mortgage, and irrespective of the outstanding balance for that mortgage on that date, is first scheduled to reach 80 percent of the original value of the property securing the loan; or (ii) based solely on actual payments, reaches 80 percent of the original value"; for ARMs the schedule is the "amortization schedule then in effect." *Original value*: "the lesser of the sales price of the property securing the mortgage, as reflected in the contract, or the appraised value at the time at which the subject residential mortgage transaction was consummated. In the case of a residential mortgage transaction for refinancing the principal residence of the mortgagor, such term means only the appraised value relied upon by the mortgagee to approve the refinance transaction." *Good payment history*: the mortgagor has not "(A) made a mortgage payment that was 60 days or longer past due during the 12-month period beginning 24 months before the later of (i) the date on which the mortgage reaches the cancellation date, or (ii) the date that the mortgagor submits a request for cancellation…; or (B) made a mortgage payment that was 30 days or longer past due during the 12-month period preceding the later of" those two dates. *Residential mortgage*: a security interest on "a single-family dwelling that is the principal residence of the mortgagor"; *single-family dwelling*: "a residence consisting of 1 family dwelling unit"; *residential mortgage transaction*: consummated on or after July 29, 1999. **Scope consequence:** the HPA does not reach second homes, investment properties, 2–4 unit properties, or loans consummated before July 29, 1999 (4903(b) annual notice only); for those, Fannie Mae's B-8.1-04 rules and any state law govern. 4902(e)(1): no PMI payments may be required "more than 30 days after the later of" the request receipt date or the date the mortgagor satisfies the evidence/certification requirements. 4904(b) (denial grounds, 30 days) is process 10.6; 4904(a) (termination notice, 30 days) and 4902(f) (refund, 45 days) are shared with 10.2/10.5. 4910(b): nothing precludes earlier cancellation "by agreement between a mortgagor and the holder."

**Fannie Mae Servicing Guide B-8.1-04 (05/15/2019), "Borrower-Initiated Termination Based on Original Value of the Property" (verified 2026-09-09, Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026).** The servicer must evaluate "the borrower's written or verbal request for MI termination due to reduction in the UPB through the payment of scheduled monthly payments or an unscheduled principal curtailment." Thresholds: one-unit principal residence or second home — "on the date the mortgage loan balance is first scheduled to reach 80% (or actually reaches 80%) of the original value of the property"; one- to four-unit investment property or two- to four-unit principal residence — "on the date the outstanding principal balance of the mortgage loan reaches 70% of the original value of the property." Acceptable payment record: the loan is "current when the termination is requested, which means the mortgage loan payment for the month preceding the date of the termination request was paid"; no payment 30 or more days past due in the last 12 months; no payment 60 or more days past due in the last 24 months; for loans outstanding fewer than 24 months or newly assumed, "apply the acceptable payment record criterion to the length of time the mortgage loan has been outstanding." Value verification: the servicer obtains a valuation from "Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system to verify that the current value of the property is at least equal to the original value" (the SMDU AVM); if no value is returned or the value has declined, the borrower may verify current value "by following the procedure in *Ordering Property Values for Mortgage Insurance Termination* in F-1-02." Original value: "The servicer must determine the original value of the property in accordance with applicable law." **No subordinate-lien certification appears in B-8.1-04** — the blueprint's "no subordinate lien" condition is an HPA *holder option* (4902(a)(4)(B)) that the holder (Fannie Mae) has not exercised in its Guide; default: do not require it (open question 10.1-Q2). No seasoning requirement applies to the original-value path. Denial: "notify the borrower and provide the grounds for denial, including the results of the AVM value, BPO, or appraisal used to make the determination" within 30 days of receiving the valuation "if applicable and in accordance with applicable law."

**B-8.1-04, "Borrower-Initiated Termination Based on Current Value of the Property."** "The servicer must not solicit a borrower for MI termination based on current value of the property." Eligibility (one-unit principal residence or second home): seasoning of at least two years but less than five years → LTV **75% or less**; five years or more → **80% or less**; two- to four-unit principal residence or one- to four-unit investment → at least two years and **70% or less**; the two-year seasoning may be waived where borrower-made improvements "increased the property value" (renovations that "substantially improve marketability and extend the useful life of the property (e.g. kitchen and bathroom renovations and/or the addition of square footage)"), in which case the LTV must be 80% or less. Value must be "a property valuation based on an inspection of both the interior and exterior of the property from Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system." Assumed loans: "the servicer must not agree to the termination unless the new borrower has a 24-month payment history." Denial notice within 30 days after the later of the request date or the valuation receipt date.

**F-1-02 (05/13/2026), "Ordering Property Values for Mortgage Insurance Termination."** One-unit: "order an interior and exterior BPO, or appraisal if the servicer determines it is required by law, using Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system"; two- to four-unit: "an interior and exterior appraisal." "The BPO or appraisal to determine the current value of the property is at the expense of the borrower and the servicer must only order the valuation after the servicer receives the applicable fee." Fees: BPO **$190**; restricted appraisal (one-unit) **$450**; appraisal (two- to four-unit) **$750**.

**Fannie Mae FAQ, "Borrower-Initiated MI Termination Requests Using SMDU" (Apr. 17, 2025).** "Servicers must use SMDU to evaluate borrower-initiated requests to terminate MI based on Original Value and/or Current Value as well as ordering the valuation," via the SMDU UI or the "Mortgage Insurance (MI) Termination Evaluation and Loan Servicing Property Valuation Order Management" APIs. SMDU's "Evaluation UPB" is "based solely on the borrower's actual payments, not on the amortization schedule"; for payment deferrals, non-principal deferred amounts ("Deferred Interest, Deferred Escrow Advances, Deferred Other Advances, Deferred Attorney Fees and Costs") are excluded, and modification principal forbearance is included. Data elements the servicer supplies/confirms: Total UPB, Property Usage type, Property Number of Units, Is Loan Current?, Number of 30 Days or More Past Due in the Last 12 Months, Number of 60 Days or More Past Due in the Last 24 Months, Disaster Attestation, Loan Assumption Date, substantial-improvement documentation (no NPI). Limited liability relief attaches to original-value decisions made on SMDU's AVM and data; the servicer "warrants the accuracy of the data" it overrides and must report data corrections to Fannie Mae systems "no later than 30 days after the SMDU MI termination evaluation." Borrowers "may not appeal the AVM values utilized for Original Value" but "may choose to pay for a BPO or appraisal," which replaces the AVM and is "valid for 120 days from the date the initial valuation product is delivered to SMDU"; appeals are encouraged within 60 days; turnaround "typically, 14 calendar days or less." Valuations are "invoiced the following month" through Fannie Mae invoicing (role `INV_CSRS_MI_TERMINATION_RECOVERY_USER`) and remitted through CRS "special remittance code 360 - MI Termination Recovery Proceeds." AVM values shared with the borrower must carry the FAQ Q6 disclaimer that the estimate "was developed by an automated valuation model" and is not an appraisal. SMDU decision messages use codes of the form `MI.R010` (release notes v2024.5). The SMDU MI Termination Servicer User Guide (May 15, 2025) is login-gated **[PORTAL-GATED SPEC]**.

**CFPB Compliance Bulletin 2015-03 (Aug. 4, 2015; PDF verified; not withdrawn).** "Investor guidelines cannot restrict the PMI cancellation and termination rights that the HPA provides to borrowers"; examiners cited a servicer that "incorrectly applied an investor's 75% PMI cancellation LTV threshold to the original value" and one with "no policies in place to ensure that PMI was properly canceled in accordance with HPA requirements." Design consequence: the HPA original-value path is always evaluated first and never subordinated to the Fannie Mae current-value table.

**State overlays (protected state laws, 12 U.S.C. 4908(a)(2)).** The Federal Reserve Consumer Compliance Handbook (Nov. 2007) lists eight states with PMI laws predating Jan. 2, 1998: California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Missouri, New York. Verified today: **Minnesota Stat. §47.207** — cancellation on written request when "the current unpaid principal balance of the mortgage is 80 percent or less of the current fair market value" (appraisal within 90 days at the mortgagor's cost), the same 30/60-day payment-history test, loan made ≥24 months earlier, owner-occupied; the servicer must, "within 30 days of receipt," approve, request information, or deny with written reasons; no cancellation fee other than an appraisal the lender arranges; annual notice in ≥12-point type. **New York Ins. Law §6503(d)** — "a mortgagor shall not be required to pay, directly or indirectly, the cost of continuing mortgage guaranty insurance… when the unpaid principal amount of the real estate loan represents seventy-five percent or less of the real estate's appraised value at the time the loan was made" (no request, no payment-history condition). **California Civ. Code §2954.6** — PMI cancellation notice (10-point bold) within 30 days after close of escrow and with each written statement required by §2954.2, stating the cancellation ratio, whether an appraisal may be necessary, and the procedure. **Connecticut §36a-725/726** — origination-stage disclosures only. Colorado, Maryland, Massachusetts and Missouri citations **[UNVERIFIED — not retrieved; carry as `jurisdiction_rules` placeholders]**.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) trigger is "written **or verbal**" under B-8.1-04, whereas 4902(a)(1) requires a written request for the HPA right — the platform accepts verbal requests as Fannie Mae cases and obtains the written confirmation (portal e-sign) to perfect the HPA path; (2) the "no subordinate lien" condition is not a Fannie Mae requirement; (3) the row omits the current-value path, the 70% rule for 2–4 unit/investment, the AVM verification, the disaster exception, the mandatory SMDU evaluation, the borrower-paid valuation fees, and state overlays (MN 30-day response; NY 75% rule); (4) the HPA payment-history windows are anchored to the *later* of the cancellation date or the request date, not simply "in 12 mo/24 mo".

#### State machine
`pmi_cancel` case: `received` → (`awaiting_written_confirmation` if verbal and `hpa_path` sought) → `evaluating_original_value` → {`eligible` → `cancellation_issued` → `closed`} | {`value_check_needed` → `awaiting_fee` → `valuation_ordered` → `valuation_received` → `evaluating` → …} | {`ineligible` → `denial_issued` → `closed`}; side branches `withdrawn` (borrower withdraws; fee refunded if no order placed), `expired` (fee not received within 60 days; valuation older than 120 days without decision), `escalated` (see AI design). Transitions are executed by the `pmi` agent through commands; `cancellation_issued` also triggers 10.5 (refund) and the shared finalization steps (notice, insurer cancel, LAR 89, escrow) defined in 10.2's business rules R-F1…R-F6. Terminal: `closed`.

#### Business rules and calculations
- **R1 — Paths and order of evaluation.** Evaluate (a) HPA original-value path (if `hpa_covered`), (b) Fannie Mae original-value path (all loans; 80% or 70% by property type), (c) state paths (MN current-value 80%; NY 75% automatic), and (d) Fannie Mae current-value path only if the borrower asked for it or original-value fails and the borrower elects a paid valuation. A grant under any path cancels the MI; a denial must cite every path evaluated.
- **R2 — Original value.** `original_value = is_refinance ? appraised_value : min(sales_price, appraised_value)` (4901(12)); NY §6503(d) uses `appraised_value` alone. Missing components → decision cannot be made → escalation `boarding` (document retrieval) with the HPA clock still running (R9).
- **R3 — Balances.** Scheduled path: `scheduled_upb(date)` from the active `mi_schedules` version (initial schedule for fixed-rate; "then in effect" for ARMs; modified schedule after 4902(d)). Actual path: `evaluation_upb = interest_bearing_upb + deferred_principal + forborne_principal` as of the evaluation date (SMDU FAQ Q10); deferred interest, escrow advances, other advances and attorney fees are excluded. LTV in basis points: `ltv_bps = floor(evaluation_upb_cents × 10000 / original_value_cents)`; the threshold test is `ltv_bps ≤ 8000` (or 7000). Rounding: floor at basis points so a borrower is never denied by a rounding artifact.
- **R4 — Current.** Fannie Mae test: the payment due in the month preceding `received_at` was paid by the end of that month and no earlier installment is unpaid — evaluate with `fnma_delinquency_status = current` as of the last day of the month preceding `received_at`; HPA "current on the payments" is satisfied by the same test plus `regx_days_delinquent = 0` on the decision date or any later date the borrower cures (the cancellation date is "any later date that the mortgagor fulfills all of the requirements"). This spec uses **both** counters as stated.
- **R5 — Payment history.** Let `D = max(cancellation_date_reached, received_at)`. Window A: installments due in [D − 24 months, D − 12 months): none paid ≥ 60 days after its due date. Window B: installments due in [D − 12 months, D): none paid ≥ 30 days after its due date. "Days past due" = `paid_date − due_date` in calendar days, FIFO allocation (consistent with `regx_days_delinquent`). Fannie Mae's "last 12 / last 24 months" counts are computed the same way over [received_at − 12m, received_at) and [received_at − 24m, received_at) and passed to SMDU as `late30_12m`/`late60_24m`. Disaster exception: installments flagged `disaster_attributable=true` where the borrower complied with the forbearance/repayment/trial/deferral plan (Section 12 flags) are excluded and `Disaster Attestation = Y` is sent to SMDU; the HPA has no such exception but 4910(b) permits the holder's more favorable rule. Loans outstanding < 24 months: shorten the windows to the loan's age (Fannie Mae); assumed loans: history since assumption, and current-value requests require 24 months since `loan_assumption_date`.
- **R6 — Value not declined (original-value path).** Call SMDU MI Termination Evaluation with the data set; SMDU returns the AVM and the decision. `value_not_declined = avm_value ≥ original_value`. If no AVM or AVM < original value → `value_check_needed`: the acknowledgment/evidence letter tells the borrower the evidence types (BPO $190 / restricted appraisal $450 / 2–4 unit appraisal $750) and that the AVM cannot be appealed; on fee receipt, order through the Valuation Order Management API; the delivered value replaces the AVM for 120 days.
- **R7 — Current-value path thresholds.** `seasoning_months = months(consummation_date → received_at)`; 1-unit principal/second home: 24 ≤ seasoning < 60 → threshold 7500; seasoning ≥ 60 → 8000; improvements documented and accepted → 8000 at any seasoning; 2–4 unit principal or 1–4 unit investment: seasoning ≥ 24 → 7000. LTV = `evaluation_upb / valuation_value`.
- **R8 — Effective date.** Original-value grant: `cancellation_effective_date = max(date actual/scheduled 80% reached, received_at, evidence_satisfied_at)`; current-value grant: `max(received_at, valuation_delivered_at)`; MN: date of approval notice at the latest 30 days after receipt. Premium stop, notice, refund, LAR 89, escrow: shared rules R-F1…R-F6 in 10.2.
- **R9 — Deadlines are HPA-anchored.** The 30-day denial/decision clock runs from `received_at` (re-anchored only when the borrower supplies evidence/fee); SMDU or vendor delay never extends it (see failure modes).
- **Worked example (original value, curtailment).** Loan: sales price $400,000, appraisal $410,000 → original value **$400,000.00**; loan $380,000, 30-year fixed 6.50%, first payment 2024-05-01, P&I $2,401.86. Initial schedule reaches 80% ($320,000) with payment 124 due **2034-08-01** (scheduled UPB $319,500.09) and 78% ($312,000) with payment 135 due **2035-07-01** ($311,913.25). Borrower pays a $30,000 curtailment on 2027-06-15 (UPB $335,548.68 after the June cycle) and requests cancellation on 2027-07-10: `ltv_bps = floor(33,554,868 × 10000 / 40,000,000) = 8388` → 83.88% > 80% → **ineligible on LTV**; denial notice due by 2027-08-09 stating the UPB, original value, LTV and that the request may be renewed (10.6). Actual UPB reaches ≤ $320,000 with the payment due **2029-07-01** ($319,950.95); borrower requests in writing on 2029-07-10: windows — no 60-day late in [2027-07-10, 2028-07-10), no 30-day late in [2028-07-10, 2029-07-10); June 2029 payment paid 2029-06-14 → current; SMDU AVM $455,000 ≥ $400,000 → `value_not_declined`; `ltv_bps = 7998` → **eligible**; effective date 2029-07-10; premium stop gate by 2029-08-09; termination notice by 2029-08-09; refund by 2029-08-24; LAR 89 action code 51, action date 071029, due by the close of the July 2029 reporting period.
- **Worked example (current value).** Same loan, request 2026-08-20 (seasoning 29 months, 1-unit principal residence): UPB $370,522.40, fee $190 received 2026-08-24, BPO ordered via API 2026-08-24, delivered 2026-09-03 at $500,000 → LTV 74.10% ≤ 75% → eligible (action code 52); had the BPO come in at $490,000 → 75.62% → denial by 2026-10-03 (30 days after the later of request and valuation) with the BPO result attached.

#### Integrations
- **Fannie Mae SMDU — MI Termination Evaluation API** (`fnma-smdu`): HTTPS/JSON, OAuth 2.0 ROPC via `fnma-auth`, headers `x-fnma-api-key`/`x-fnma-access-token`; request = servicer number, Fannie Mae loan number, request type (original/current/current-with-improvements), request date, Total UPB (evaluation UPB), property usage, units, `is_current`, `late30_12m`, `late60_24m`, disaster attestation, assumption date; response = decision, AVM value/date, messages (`MI.Rxxx`), evaluation ID. Idempotency: `case_id + evaluation attempt` as the client reference; store raw request/response hashes. Sandbox: `api-clve.fanniemae.com` CIT with the static loan snapshot. Field names and the bulk "preliminarily eligible on original value as of a date" list call are **[UNVERIFIED — Swagger behind login]**. On failure (5xx/timeouts) retry with backoff for 4 hours, then `human_portal_task` (SMDU UI evaluation) if the HPA clock is at day 15 or later.
- **Fannie Mae SMDU — Loan Servicing Property Valuation Order Management API**: order BPO (interior/exterior) or appraisal (restricted 1-unit; Form 1025 for 2–4 units), status polling, report retrieval; borrower contact fields for the interior inspection (phone/email — TCPA-consented contact info only); cancel order; appeal. Turnaround typically ≤ 14 days. Invoice arrives the following month in Fannie Mae invoicing (portal role) → `human_portal_task` to download the invoice; remittance via CRS special remittance code 360 prepared by `investor-reporting` (Section 5.2 CRS batch) and executed by `fnma_portal_operator`; reconcile invoice ↔ collected borrower fees (ledger below).
- **Mortgage insurers** (`integrations/mi/<insurer>`): cancellation notice with certificate number, servicer loan number, borrower name, property address, reason (`HPA_borrower_cancellation` / `FNMA_current_value`), effective date, refund payee; channels per insurer (MGIC: MGIC/Link, SFT "Cancellations," EDI batch, or secure email `cancel_requests@mgic.com`); ack = insurer confirmation or refund advice; unacked after 5 BD → re-send and escalate; refund receipt handled in 10.5.
- **Fannie Mae investor reporting** (`fnma-lsdu` today; `fnma-servicing-events` later): LAR 89 (Section 5.1 layout; action codes 51/52) — emitted by 10.2's finalization rules.
- **Escrow** (Section 3): `mi.cancelled` → interim analysis (3.2) with the MI line closed as of the effective date.
- **Print/mail and e-delivery** (`print-mail`, `e-delivery`): notices below.
- **Borrower portal**: request form (basis, e-signature, optional subordinate-lien certification, improvement uploads, fee payment), status tracker.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
The `pmi` agent runs the case end-to-end: classifies the request (basis, channel, requester authority), opens the `pmi_cancel` case, calls `pmi.evaluate(case_id)` (deterministic decision service; returns paths, values, reasons), calls `smdu.mi_termination.evaluate`, drafts and sends the acknowledgment with the exact evidence list, orders the valuation when the fee posts, re-evaluates on delivery, issues the grant (`mi.cancel` command → finalization pipeline) or the denial (10.6), and answers borrower questions through `borrower-comms` with a scripted, non-solicitation stance ("we can evaluate a current-value request if you ask; we do not solicit it"). Tools: `pmi.*`, `smdu.*`, `mi_insurer.cancel`, `notices.render/send`, `escrow.interim_analysis.request`, `ledger.post`, `investor_events.emit`, `case.*`, `contacts.log`, `documents.store`. Decision record: `{case_id, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, inputs_hash, paths_evaluated[], smdu_evaluation_id, result, reasons[], notices[], confidence, escalation?}`. Guardrails: the agent cannot alter `original_value`, payment-history counts or SMDU inputs without an evidence-backed `mi.data.corrected` event (which voids liability relief and starts `FNMA_SMDU_DATA_CORRECTION_30`); cannot charge any fee other than the tabulated valuation fee; cannot deny for reasons outside the rule set; must attach the valuation result to any denial; must add the AVM disclaimer; never solicits current-value cancellation. Escalations: missing original-value documents → `boarding`/`security-records` retrieval task; disputed original value or borrower-supplied appraisal outside SMDU → `officer` (policy: only SMDU-ordered valuations count, borrower told in the ack); SMDU unavailable at day 15 → `fnma_portal_operator` (package: loan data set, request, payment-history table, screenshots not required); borrower asks for a human → `human_agent`; MN/NY/CA overlays are automatic; suspected fraud in improvement documentation → `qc-audit`. Disclosures: AI disclosure at the start of voice/chat (baseline §8); TCPA consent for outbound AI calls about the case. AI-off path: the same commands run from the ops console by a human with the checklist.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Verbal request without written confirmation: Fannie Mae evaluation proceeds; if eligible, the platform grants (holder permits) — HPA 4902(a)(1) is not needed to cancel early (4910(b)); the written form is requested for the record.
- Request before the 80% scheduled date and above 80% actual → denial with the projected scheduled date and the actual-payment alternative.
- Curtailment posted after the request but before decision → re-evaluate at decision time (4902(a) "any later date").
- Payment reversed (NSF) after the grant but before the effective date → hold the grant; re-evaluate; if no longer current, deny with reasons.
- Transfer-in mid-case (Section 1.7-style): the transferor's `received_at` binds; timers are rebuilt from the boarded case data; if the transferor already ordered a valuation, the report is accepted if SMDU-ordered and within 120 days.
- Transfer-out mid-case: case status, timers and valuation exported in the transfer file; the goodbye letter is unaffected.
- Bankruptcy: a Chapter 13 debtor's request is processed; "current" is the contractual test (pre-petition arrears in the plan → not current) — open question 10.1-Q4; no stay issue for a borrower-favorable action.
- SCRA 6% cap: not a "modification" agreed by the parties — the initial schedule stands; actual-UPB path benefits.
- Successor in interest (confirmed): treated as requester; payment history is the loan's.
- Multi-unit/second home/investment: HPA path off; Fannie Mae thresholds (70% or 80%) and midpoint-only automatic termination.
- SMDU returns `MI.R010`/`MI.R010.1` (no payment history / no servicer loan number in Fannie Mae data): supply data, re-submit; if unresolved by day 15, portal fallback; the HPA clock does not pause.
- Original value disputes (e.g., borrower says appraisal was higher): the rule is statutory; explain; offer the current-value path.
- Valuation vendor cannot access the property: order cancelled after 30 days; fee refunded per Fannie Mae's invoice treatment **[UNVERIFIED — cancellation fee policy]**.
- Retro-correction: an erroneous denial discovered later (QC or NoE) → grant effective as of the date the borrower originally qualified, refund premiums collected since (10.5), and correct LAR 89 action date through the Investor Reporting representative.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Written vs verbal (10.1-Q1):** default — accept verbal requests and evaluate immediately (B-8.1-04); request e-signed confirmation; grant without it if eligible.
2. **Subordinate-lien certification (10.1-Q2):** default — not required for Fannie Mae loans (`mi_holder_config.require_sub_lien_cert=false`); confirm with the partner and Fannie Mae whether any requirement exists outside B-8.1-04.
3. **Borrower-supplied appraisals (10.1-Q3):** default — not accepted; only SMDU-ordered valuations (FAQ Q9); disclose in the ack.
4. **Chapter 13 "current" (10.1-Q4):** default — contractual test (not current while pre-petition arrears exist); obtain counsel opinion; allow `officer` override with rationale.
5. **Fee intake channel (10.1-Q5):** default — card/ACH in the borrower portal, refundable if the order is cancelled before assignment.
6. **State placeholders (10.1-Q6):** verify CO/MD/MA/MO PMI statutes before go-live in those states.
