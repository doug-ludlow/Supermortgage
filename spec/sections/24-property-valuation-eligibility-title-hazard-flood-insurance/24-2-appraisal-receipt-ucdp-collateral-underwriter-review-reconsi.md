### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**1. UCDP submission and the Doc File ID.** Selling Guide B4-1.1-06, *UAD and UCDP* (06/04/2025): Forms 1004, 1004 Desktop, 1004 Hybrid, 1004C, 1025, 1073, 1073 Hybrid and 2090 "must be submitted through the UCDP and receive a 'Successful' status from the UCDP prior to sale of the loan to Fannie Mae"; "lenders must ensure it is the unaltered report submitted by the identified appraiser" and "the final version of the report that was utilized in making the underwriting decision must be submitted through the UCDP". UCDP Overview (Sept 2025; 00b-orig F2): "Appraisal reports for all conventional mortgages delivered to the GSEs must be submitted to UCDP before the delivery date of the mortgage"; every successful upload receives an SSR per GSE and "a Document File Identifier (Doc File ID)"; "One Doc File ID is assigned per loan and is the same for delivery to either GSE"; UCDP "does not allow a lender to reuse a Document File ID created from another lender's UCDP submission". Channels: UCDP UI; Direct Integration; Developer Portal **Appraisal File Retrieval** (XML), **Appraisal Findings Summary** ("appraisal summary status, findings, messages, and Collateral Underwriter (CU) risk score"), **UAD Compliance API** (pre-submission validation of a UAD 3.6 file); SSR JSON Schema v1.0. UCDP Fannie Mae Messaging Guide for UAD 2.6 (June 2026): a submission is "Successful" when all hard stops are resolved "either automatically or through manual override approval" and "Not Successful" if "at least one message that has either a manually overridable or a non-overridable hard stop" remains; hard stops arise from UCDP basic edits, UAD compliance checks and Fannie Mae proprietary edits (examples: "The appraiser's license state does not match the subject property state"; "fewer than the three closed sales required per Selling Guide B4-1.3-08"; C6 condition); overrides are requested in the UCDP UI by selecting a reason code ("Manually Overridable" stops need "a reason code to change the submission status to a 'Successful' status"); SSR findings are "sorted by severity in the following order: Fatal, Overridable, Warning"; FNM0391 makes UAD 2.6 fatal for new submissions on/after Nov 2, 2026.

**2. Collateral Underwriter.** CU Introduction (job aid): "Each appraisal receives a score on a scale of 1.0 to 5.0, and 999 if we are unable to score, with 1 indicating the lowest risk and 5 indicating the highest risk."; "There are four flags: Overvaluation, Undervaluation, Property Eligibility and Policy Compliance, and Appraisal Quality."; "CU does not provide 'approvals' or 'denials' of appraisals and/or the corresponding loan. It does not replace or alter lenders' obligations."; "Fannie Mae expects lenders to use the CU feedback in combination with human due diligence."; "Only appraisals submitted to UCDP on Fannie Mae form 1004 (Uniform Residential Appraisal Report) and form 1073 (Individual Condominium Unit Appraisal Report) are analyzed by CU." (UAD 3.6 URAR scoring coverage: **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — job aid pre-dates UAD 3.6; CU V.8.5 release notes of March 2, 2026 not read]**). A2-2-06, *Representations and Warranties on Property Value* (06/03/2026): relief requires that "the appraisal must receive a CU risk score of 2.5 or below"; with relief the lender is not responsible for "underwriting the appraisal report to determine whether the subject property presents adequate collateral", "ensuring the appraisal accurately reflects the market value of the property" or "analyzing the comparable sales used in the appraisal report", but remains responsible for "the description of the property, adherence to fair lending, local, state, and federal laws", "the accuracy and completeness of all data on the appraisal that pertains to the property", the "property's condition and quality ratings" and that "the property meets the property eligibility requirements" (24.3).

**3. Appraisal review standards.** B4-1.3-01, *Review of the Appraisal Report* (06/04/2025): the lender must validate that "the appraiser has provided an accurate and reliable opinion of value that reflects the market value, condition, and marketability of the subject property" and that "the property meets Fannie Mae's eligibility criteria". B4-1.1-02 (06/04/2025): appraisals must "not contain subjective and prohibited language relating to discriminatory practices"; the lender must ensure "the appraiser described the property and the market area in factual, unbiased, and specific terms". B4-1.1-04, *Unacceptable Appraisal Practices* (06/04/2025): "development of a valuation conclusion based either partially or completely on the sex, race, color, religion, disability, national origin, familial status" of owners or occupants; unsupported terms "pride of ownership", "no pride of ownership", "poor neighborhood", "good neighborhood", "crime" and variants, "desirable neighborhood or location", "undesirable neighborhood or location"; "misrepresentation of the physical characteristics of the subject property, improvements, or comparable sales"; "use of adjustments to comparable sales that do not reflect market reaction to differences"; "failure to use comparable sales that are the most locationally and physically similar"; "failure to comment on negative factors". Condition/quality rating policy (C1–C6/Q1–Q6) is 24.3's; 24.2 reads the ratings from the UAD file and routes.

**4. Reconsideration of value.** B4-1.3-12, *Appraisal Quality Matters* (09/03/2025; SEL-2025-07): lenders must have ROV policies that "meet Fannie Mae requirements and adhere to all applicable local, state, and federal laws"; "The lender must provide a disclosure to the borrower outlining the ROV process when the appraisal report is provided to the borrower."; "Only one borrower-initiated ROV is permitted per appraisal."; "After a loan has closed, an ROV request is no longer allowed to be submitted by the borrower."; the request must contain "Borrower(s) name, Property address, Effective date of the appraisal, Appraiser name, and Date of the ROV request", "Identification and description of unsupported, inaccurate, or deficient areas", "Additional data, information, or comparable properties (not to exceed five)" with sources; lenders must "designate an underwriter or other appraisal subject matter expert to review the ROV request", "standardize communication to the appraiser" including "a definition of turn-time expectations", require "a revised appraisal report that includes commentary on conclusions regardless of the outcome", "align its ROV policies and procedures with Appraiser Independence Requirements (AIR)", "Ensure documentation related to the outcome of the ROV is retained in the loan file", and "report suspected overt violations of anti-discrimination laws to the proper local, state, or federal agency". History: SEL-2024-03 (May 1, 2024) required a "borrower disclosure that must be provided at the time of loan application and again upon delivery of the appraisal report", mandatory for applications on/after Aug 29, 2024, moved to Oct 31, 2024 by Selling Notice; SEL-2025-07 (Sept 3, 2025) "Removed the requirement to provide an initial ROV disclosure at the time of loan application" and simplified retention to the outcome documentation **[VERIFIED — Guide topic; announcement wording via TENA summary, PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. Interagency Guidance on Reconsiderations of Value of Residential Real Estate Valuations, 89 FR 60549 (July 26, 2024; FRB, CFPB, FDIC, NCUA, OCC): ROV = "A request from the financial institution to the appraiser or other preparer of the valuation report to reassess the report based upon potential deficiencies or other information that may affect the value conclusion."; institutions may "Establish a process to inform consumers how to raise concerns about the valuation early enough in the underwriting process", "Establish timelines in the complaint or ROV processes", and train staff "to identify deficiencies (including practices that may result in discrimination)"; "The guidance does not have the force and effect of law or regulation and does not impose any new requirements" **[status after the CFPB's May 12, 2025 guidance withdrawal not re-checked — PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**5. Reg B §1002.14 (CFPB eRegs; eCFR Part 1002 unreachable — 00a-fed §3.3).** (a)(1): "A creditor shall provide an applicant a copy of all appraisals and other written valuations developed in connection with an application for credit that is to be secured by a first lien on a dwelling" — "promptly upon completion, or three business days prior to consummation" (whichever is earlier); waiver only if the applicant, "no later than three business days prior to consummation or account opening", provides "an affirmative oral or written statement waiving the timing requirement", after which copies are due "at or prior to consummation"; comment 14(a)(1)-4: "'Completion' occurs when the last version is received by the creditor, or when the creditor has reviewed and accepted the appraisal or other written valuation to include any changes or corrections required, whichever is later."; examples of prompt delivery: "Sending a copy of an appraisal within a week of completion with sufficient time before consummation"; comment 14(a)(1)-7: "If a creditor already has provided a copy of one version… and the creditor later receives a revision… then the creditor also must provide the applicant with a copy of the revision"; where several applicants, copies "need only be given to one applicant" but "must be given to the primary applicant where one is readily apparent"; (a)(3) "shall not charge an applicant for providing a copy" (comment: no "photocopy, postage, or other costs"); (a)(4) applies "whether credit is extended or denied or if the application is incomplete or withdrawn", copies within 30 days after the creditor determines consummation will not occur; (a)(5) electronic copies subject to E-SIGN; (b)(3) "valuation" = "any estimate of the value of a dwelling developed in connection with an application for credit"; comment 14(b)(3)-1 examples: an appraiser's report, "A report generated by use of an automated valuation model to estimate the property's value", a broker price opinion, "A document prepared by the creditor's staff that assigns value to the property"; comment 14(b)(3)-3 excludes "Internal documents that merely restate the estimated value" and public "Governmental agency statements of appraised value". Reg B defines no "business day" **[VERIFIED absence in §1002.14 and its commentary; decision below]**.

**6. HPML appraisal rules, §1026.35(c) (eCFR 9/03/2026; 00a-fed §5.7).** (c)(3)(i): no HPML "without obtaining… a written appraisal… performed by a certified or licensed appraiser who conducts a physical visit of the interior"; safe harbor (c)(3)(ii) via appendix N; flips (c)(4)(i): second appraisal when the seller acquired the property "90 or fewer days prior… and the price… exceeds the seller's acquisition price by more than 10 percent" or "91 to 180 days prior… by more than 20 percent"; "The two appraisals… may not be performed by the same certified or licensed appraiser"; one must analyze "The difference between the price…Changes in market conditions…Any improvements made to the property"; "the creditor may charge the consumer for only one of the appraisals"; (c)(5): disclosure "We may order an appraisal to determine the property's value and charge you for this appraisal. We will give you a copy of any appraisal, even if your loan does not close." delivered "no later than the third business day after the creditor receives the consumer's application" (21.3); (c)(6): "a copy of any written appraisal performed in connection with a higher-priced mortgage loan" "No later than three business days prior to consummation of the loan; or…In the case of a loan that is not consummated, no later than 30 days after the creditor determines that the loan will not be consummated"; "A creditor shall not charge the consumer for a copy". Exemptions (c)(2): QMs; loans "at or below the threshold" — **$34,200 for 2026** (comment 35(c)(2)(ii)-3.xiii; 90 FR 58141); the (c)(4)(vii) flip exemptions. Business day for (c)(5)/(c)(6) = Reg Z general definition (not in the §1026.2(a)(6) specific list — 00a-fed §1.2). No waiver provision appears in (c)(6) as read **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**7. AVM quality control.** 12 CFR 1026.42(i) (89 FR 64538, Aug 7, 2024; effective Oct 1, 2025): standards for AVMs used "in determining the value of collateral in connection with making a credit decision or covered securitization determination"; the preamble states the originator "would not be making a covered securitization determination… because the GSE would be using its AVM to make the appraisal waiver decision"; policies must cover AVMs used "through or in cooperation with a third party or affiliate". Fair-lending posture: 00a-fed §3.8/§13.6 (Reg B effects test removed effective July 21, 2026; EO 14281) — the statutes, state law and Fannie Mae contractual expectations (A3-2-01; LL-2026-04) are unchanged.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. The ROV topic is B4-1.3-12 (not "B4-1.1-?"); the application-stage ROV disclosure required by SEL-2024-03 was removed by SEL-2025-07 — the disclosure is now due once, with the appraisal copy.
2. The Reg B duty attaches to "completion", a defined moment that includes lender review and corrections; each revision (including an ROV response) restarts it, and the 3-business-day gate is measured from the latest version — the blueprint's single "copy" deadline understates this.
3. The waiver is not a closing-table device: it must be obtained ≥ 3 business days before consummation, and copies are still due at or before consummation.
4. AVM outputs and staff-prepared value documents created after application are "valuations" to be copied — the blueprint omits them; PDCs and DU value-acceptance messages carry no value estimate and are not copied by default (open question 4).
5. "UCDP SSR with Doc File ID before delivery" is right but incomplete: the *final* version used in the decision must be the one with "Successful" status, and a Doc File ID from another lender cannot be reused (transfers, 24.1).
6. Overrides of UCDP hard stops are UI-only (`fnma_portal_operator`); CU rep-and-warrant relief (≤ 2.5) does not relieve condition/quality ratings, property eligibility, data accuracy or fair-lending duties.
7. HPML rules apply only to non-exempt HPMLs (QMs and loans ≤ $34,200 are exempt); the second-appraisal flip test needs the seller's acquisition date/price from `purchase_contracts`/title (24.4), which the blueprint does not list as an input.
8. "FHFA/agency appraisal-bias guidance" is soft law (89 FR 60549 is non-binding); the binding hooks are B4-1.1-04/B4-1.3-12 (contractual), AIR, and ECOA/FHA statutes.

#### State machine

`appraisals.review_status`: `pending` → (UAD pre-check + UCDP submit) → `ucdp_pending` → `ucdp_successful` | `ucdp_not_successful` (→ `correction_requested` back to the appraiser, or `override_requested` via `fnma_portal_operator`, → resubmit) → `in_review` (CU + rules + bias scan) → `accepted` (sets `value_used_cents`, `completion_at`, `is_final_version`) | `correction_requested` (→ new version) | `field_review_ordered` / `second_appraisal_ordered` (24.1 child order) | `rejected` (terminal; new order). Copy sub-state per version: `copy_pending` → `copy_delivered` → `copy_received` (evidence) ; `copy_waived` (only if waiver obtained ≥ 3 BD before consummation). `rov_requests.status`: `received` → `screened` (`complete`/`incomplete`/rejected) → `sme_review` → `forwarded` | `declined` → `awaiting_appraiser` → `response_received` → `closed`. Guards: `rov.requested` rejected when `closing.consummated` exists or a prior borrower-initiated ROV exists for the same `appraisal_id`; `consummate` requires `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD_GATE` (and `REGZ_1026_35C_HPML_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD` when HPML) open for the latest version; `submitDelivery` (29.4) requires `FNMA_B4_1_1_06_UCDP_SUCCESSFUL_GATE`.

| `REGZ_1026_35C_HPML_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD` | not_before_gate on `consummate` | `hpml_determinations{is_hpml, not exempt}` | consummation_at | every written appraisal (incl. second) provided ≥ 3 `business_days_creditor` before consummation; no waiver | `valuation.copy.delivered` for all HPML appraisals | block `consummate` |
| `REGZ_1026_35C_HPML_COPY_NOT_CONSUMMATED_30` | deadline | as above, not consummated | determination_at | +30 `calendar_days` | delivery | sev 1 |
| `FNMA_B4_1_1_06_UCDP_SUCCESSFUL_GATE` | not_before_gate on `submitDelivery` | `valuation.review.completed` | — | final version `ucdp_status = successful` for Fannie Mae, Doc File ID stored | `ucdp_submissions.status = successful` | block delivery (29.4) |
| `SM_ROV_SCREEN_SLA_2BD` | deadline | `rov.requested` | requested_at | +2 `business_days_creditor` | `rov.screened` + SME decision | sev 2 |
| `FNMA_B4_1_3_12_ROV_TURNTIME_5BD` | deadline | `rov.forwarded` | sent_to_appraiser_at | +5 `business_days_creditor` (the policy's "turn-time expectation") | `rov.response.received` | AMC escalation; borrower status update |
| `FNMA_B4_1_3_12_ROV_CLOSING_GATE` | not_before_gate (window closes) | `closing.consummated` | — | no borrower ROV after consummation | — | request rejected with explanation |
| `SM_CU_HIGH_RISK_REVIEW_1BD` | deadline | `valuation.cu.scored{score ≥ 4.0 or overvaluation flag}` | scored_at | +1 `business_days_creditor` | enhanced review record | sev 2 |

Unit decision: Reg B has no business-day definition; the platform uses `business_days_creditor` (Reg Z general definition — days the partner/SM origination office is open) for §1002.14 and for §1026.35(c)(5)–(6) (which are outside the §1026.2(a)(6) specific list). Counting convention for "three business days prior to consummation": copy day D → earliest consummation = D + 3 business days (copy Tue Nov 3 → Fri Nov 6). Mailed copies: "provided" is treated as received 3 business days after mailing — this is comment 14(a)(1)-4's own rule, not merely policy: "Delivery occurs three business days after mailing or delivering the copies to the last-known address of the applicant, or when evidence indicates actual receipt by the applicant, whichever is earlier" (CFPB Official Interpretation, verified 2026-09-11) — see open question 2.

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — Receipt and pre-check.** On `valuation.received`: verify the package (UAD 3.6 ZIP with XML, PDF, images; or UAD 2.6 before Nov 2, 2026), run the UAD Compliance API, confirm the lender/client is the partner, the appraiser matches the order and license record (24.1), the effective date and form match, and create `appraisals` version 1 and a `valuations` row. Submit to both GSEs within 1 business day.

**R2 — UCDP/CU outcome routing.** `Not Successful` with correctable stops → `correction_requested` to the appraiser (content restricted to the stop text; AIR-safe); non-correctable or policy stops needing an override → `fnma_portal_operator` escalation with the AI-prepared reason code and justification (UI-only); `Successful` → CU: score ≤ 2.5 and no flags → standard review; 2.6–3.9 or any flag → targeted review of flagged items; ≥ 4.0 or overvaluation flag → enhanced review (comparable re-analysis, optional field review/second appraisal under §1026.42(c)(3) "obtaining multiple valuations"); 999 → manual-equivalent review. `rw_relief_property_value = (cu_score ≤ 2.5 ∧ ucdp_status = successful ∧ form ∈ {1004,1073 or UAD 3.6 URAR equivalent})`.

**R3 — Review checklist (executable).** (a) Value support: three closed comparable sales, adjustments explained, market-conditions addendum consistent; (b) property data vs `application_properties` and PDC (units, GLA, year built, ADU, condition/quality ratings → 24.3 routing when C5/C6/Q6 or "subject to"); (c) prohibited-language scan against the B4-1.1-04 lexicon and any reference to protected characteristics of occupants or neighborhood composition → `bias_scan_result`; any hit of severity ≥ medium → `correction_requested` (factual re-description) and a fair-lending record; suspected overt discrimination → `officer` referral (`valuation.discrimination.referred`) and AIR §7 / §1026.42(g) referral (24.1 timer); (d) HPML checks (R6); (e) acceptance sets `value_used_cents` = appraised value for refinances; for purchases `lower_of_two(appraised, purchase_price)` for LTV (§23/§25 consume), and `completion_at = now`.

**R4 — Reg B copy engine.** For each `valuations` row: `copy_due_prompt = completion_at + 7 calendar days` (target next business day); `copy_due_gate = consummation_at − 3 business_days_creditor`; `copy_required_by = min(copy_due_prompt, copy_due_gate)`. Package = cover `NTC_REGB_1002_14_VALUATION_COPY` (+ HPML variant text when applicable) + every undelivered valuation + `NTC_FNMA_B4_1_3_12_ROV_DISCLOSURE` (first version) + ROV form link. Delivery electronic when `consents.esign` covers disclosures, else print/mail with the 3-business-day receipt assumption. All applicants receive copies (`valuation.copy_all_applicants=true`), satisfying the "primary applicant" rule. Re-run on every new version. Waiver: accepted only via `consents{regb_1002_14_timing_waiver}` with `obtained_at ≤ consummation_at − 3 business_days_creditor`; even then copies go out before consummation.

**R5 — Worked example 1 (refinance fixture).** Report received Thu Oct 15, 2026 14:20 MST (v1). UAD Compliance API pass 14:31; UCDP DI submission 15:05 (`SM_UCDP_SUBMIT_SLA_1BD` due Fri Oct 16 → satisfied); SSRs 15:07: Fannie Mae Successful, Freddie Mac Successful; Doc File ID `1200000123456`; CU 1.9, no flags (`rw_relief_property_value = true`). Review completes Fri Oct 16 10:30 (`SM_APPRAISAL_REVIEW_SLA_2BD` due Tue Oct 20 → satisfied): value $800,000 (80,000,000 cents), C3/Q3, no bias hits, "as is" → `accepted`; `completion_at = 2026-10-16 10:30`; `value_used_cents = 80,000,000`; LTV = 56,000,000 / 80,000,000 = 0.7000 → 70.00% (4-decimal ratio, displayed to 2 places). Copy engine: `copy_due_prompt = Fri Oct 23`; consummation Fri Nov 6 → `copy_due_gate`: 3 `business_days_creditor` before = Tue Nov 3 (Thu Nov 5, Wed Nov 4, Tue Nov 3); `copy_required_by = Oct 23`. Package e-delivered Fri Oct 16 10:45 MST; portal view logged 11:02 → `valuation.copy.delivered{v1}` with `esign_confirmed`; both gates satisfied. 23.1 resubmits DU with the appraised value Oct 16.

**R6 — Worked example 2 (ROV on the fixture, two branches).** Borrower submits an ROV Tue Oct 20 (three comparables with MLS sources, disputes the GLA adjustment). Screen Wed Oct 21 (`SM_ROV_SCREEN_SLA_2BD` due Thu Oct 22): complete, first ROV, pre-closing → SME (`underwriting_reviewer`, SLA timer) forwards Wed Oct 21 15:00; `turn_time_due_at` = Wed Oct 28 (5 `business_days_creditor`: Oct 22, 23, 26, 27, 28). Branch A: revised report (v2, commentary, value unchanged) received Mon Oct 26 → UCDP resubmission same day (new SSR, same Doc File ID), review Tue Oct 27 → `completion_at = Oct 27` → copy of v2 e-delivered Tue Oct 27 → gate for Nov 6 still satisfied (Oct 27 ≤ Nov 3); `rov.closed{no_change}`; outcome documentation retained. Branch B: the appraiser's revised report (value raised to $815,000) arrives Wed Nov 4 08:00 → `completion_at = Nov 4` → the Nov 6 consummation fails `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD_GATE` (copy Nov 4 → earliest consummation Mon Nov 9: Thu Nov 5, Fri Nov 6, Mon Nov 9); a waiver is impossible because it had to be obtained by Tue Nov 3; the platform reschedules consummation to Mon Nov 9, 25.2 re-tests the CD (no new 3-day wait unless an APR/product/prepayment change), 25.3 recomputes rescission (`business_days_regz_specific`: Tue Nov 10 = day 1, Wed Nov 11 Veterans Day excluded, Thu Nov 12 = day 2, Fri Nov 13 = day 3 → expires midnight Fri Nov 13), 26.3 moves disbursement to Mon Nov 16, and the lock (expires Sat Nov 21) still covers.

**R7 — HPML tests.** `hpml_appraisal_rules_apply = is_hpml ∧ ¬qm ∧ loan_amount > 3,420,000 cents ∧ ¬(c)(2) other exemption`. When true: require `assignment_type = traditional` with interior visit; flip test with `days = contract_date − seller_acquisition_date`, `pct = (price − acquisition_price) / acquisition_price`; second appraisal if `(days ≤ 90 ∧ pct > 0.10) ∨ (91 ≤ days ≤ 180 ∧ pct > 0.20)` unless a (c)(4)(vii) exemption is documented; the second appraisal is a distinct 24.1 order to a different appraiser; the consumer may be charged for one only (`fee_items` guard); both copies ≥ 3 business days before consummation, no waiver. Worked: purchase fixture Columbus OH, contract Thu Oct 15, 2026 at $412,000; title chain shows the seller acquired Sat Aug 1, 2026 for $340,000 → days = 75 (≤ 90); pct = 72,000 / 340,000 = 0.2118 > 0.10 → second appraisal required if the loan is a non-QM HPML; consummation Wed Nov 18 → both copies by Fri Nov 13 (Tue Nov 17, Mon Nov 16, Fri Nov 13). If 23.4 classifies the loan as a General QM, `hpml_appraisal_rules_apply = false` and only Reg B governs.

**R8 — Not consummated.** On withdrawal/denial (21.6) or cancelled closing: `copy_required_by = determination_at + 30 calendar days` for every undelivered valuation (Reg B and, if HPML, §1026.35(c)(6)(ii)(B)). Example: denial issued Fri Oct 30, 2026 → copies by Sun Nov 29, 2026 (scheduled Fri Nov 27).

#### Integrations
- `fnma-ucdp` (Direct Integration; partner lender-agent credentials): submit ZIP/XML per GSE; receive SSR JSON + PDF; idempotency by (`appraisal_id`, `version_no`, `gse`); resubmission of revisions keeps the Doc File ID; overrides: UI-only → `fnma_portal_operator` queue with prepared reason code; outage → UCDP UI upload by the operator from the frozen package hash.
- `fnma-api`: Appraisal Findings Summary (poll until both GSE results; CU score/flags/messages), Appraisal File Retrieval (XML for QC), UAD Compliance API (pre-check; six published test cases).
- `amc` adapter (24.1): correction requests, ROV standardized communication (fields fixed by template; no value language), revised deliveries.
- Notice Registry / `e-delivery` / `print-mail`: `NTC_REGB_1002_14_VALUATION_COPY` etc.; receipt evidence back into `disclosures`.
- `fnma-du` (23.1): appraised value/condition data resubmission; 29.3 ULDD: Doc File ID, appraisal form/UAD version, CU-independent data points.
- `cases`/complaints: bias allegations create a case and an ROV candidate; referrals via `officer`.

#### Outputs and artifacts

`appraisals` versions with UCDP SSRs (PDF + JSON; retention `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`), `ucdp_submissions`, CU results, review findings and bias-scan records, `valuations` register, copy packages (`disclosures.kind = valuation_copy`; retention `regb_25m` and loan-file), waiver consents, `rov_requests` with outcome documents, HPML second-appraisal records and the single-fee evidence, `value_used_cents` for LTV (23.1, 25.2), Doc File ID for ULDD (29.3/29.4), fair-lending monitoring feed to 31.2 (value vs contract/AVM gaps by geography), QC evidence for 28.1/28.2.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`valuation` agent (same package as 24.1; distinct tools): `ingestReport`, `runUadCompliance`, `submitUcdp`, `pollFindings`, `applyReviewChecklist`, `scanLanguage`, `requestCorrection` (template-locked), `prepareOverride` (for the operator), `setValueUsed`, `buildCopyPackage`, `deliverNotice`, `recordWaiver`, `screenRov`, `prepareRovAnalysis`, `forwardRov` (template-locked), `closeRov`, `runHpmlTests`, `writeDecision`, `fileEscalation`. Behavior: ingest → pre-check → UCDP → review → copy within one business day → gates maintained until consummation; ROV intake screened within two business days with an analysis (comparable relevance, data conflicts, error checks) for the SME; every borrower contact discloses automation; all appraiser-facing text comes from templates that cannot carry values, targets or comparables outside the ROV's ≤ 5. Decision record: `{appraisal_id, version_no, ucdp_results, cu_score, cu_flags, checklist_results[], bias_scan, value_used_cents, value_basis, copy_plan{due_prompt, due_gate, channel}, hpml_tests, rov{screen, analysis, sme_decision}, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: never alter a report (only the appraiser may revise); never decline an ROV, refer discrimination, or approve an override alone; never set `value_used_cents` above the appraised value; never waive the 3-day timing without a dated borrower statement; never deliver copies without a valid channel consent. Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` (designated ROV SME; SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`), `fnma_portal_operator` (UCDP overrides; SLA 1 BD), `officer` (discrimination/misconduct referrals; SLA 5 BD), `human_agent` on request. Human path: review console with the same checklist and the copy engine; overrides of agent conclusions are themselves logged decisions.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Revision after copy delivered: re-deliver; recompute gate; consummation may move (R6-B).
- Two versions where v2 corrects only clerical items: v2 is still the "latest version" — deliver it; `completion_at` moves to v2 acceptance.
- Borrower opts out of e-delivery after the first copy: switch to mail with the 3-business-day receipt assumption; recompute gates.
- Freddie Mac SSR "Not Successful" while Fannie Mae is Successful: delivery to Fannie Mae proceeds; record both; no override needed for Freddie for this execution.
- UCDP hard stop "appraiser license state ≠ property state": correction impossible → new appraisal (24.1) — license gate failure recorded.
- CU 999 (unscored, e.g., 1025/1004C): full manual-equivalent review; no property-value relief.
- ROV requested after consummation: reject with explanation and complaint path; ROV requested a second time on the same appraisal: reject as duplicate (lender-initiated ROV remains possible).
- ROV comparables exceed five: ask the borrower to choose; do not forward more than five.
- Appraiser refuses to respond to the ROV: AMC escalation; if unresolved by turn-time + 5 BD, lender-initiated field review/second appraisal; AIR misconduct assessment.
- Bias lexicon hit that is factual (e.g., "crime" in a quoted police report): human-equivalent review by the agent with rationale; if uncertain → `underwriting_reviewer`.
- HPML with seller exemption (e.g., REO sale by a GSE) → document exemption code; no second appraisal.
- Appraised value below purchase price: `value_basis = lower_of_two`; 23.1 resubmission; 21.5 changed-circumstance check for MI/LTV pricing (24.6).
- Withdrawal after report received but before review: copies still due within 30 days.
- E-SIGN consent missing: mail; timer arithmetic uses the mailbox assumption.
- Appraisal for a transferred loan (24.1): resubmit under the partner; original lender's Doc File ID ignored.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **24.2-T1** Given v1 received Thu Oct 15, 2026 14:20 and UCDP Successful both GSEs 15:07 with CU 1.9, when review completes Fri Oct 16 10:30, then `completion_at = 2026-10-16`, `rw_relief_property_value = true`, and the copy package is due by Fri Oct 23 and by Tue Nov 3 (`copy_required_by = Oct 23`).
- **24.2-T2** Given consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 and the latest version copy e-delivered Tue Nov 3 at 17:59 MST, then `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD_GATE` is open; delivered Wed Nov 4 08:00 → gate closed and the proposed earliest consummation is Mon Nov 9.
- **24.2-T3** Given a borrower waiver statement recorded Wed Nov 4 for a Nov 6 consummation, then `REGB_1002_14_WAIVER_3BD_GATE` rejects it; recorded Tue Nov 3 → accepted, and copies must still be delivered at or before consummation.
- **24.2-T4** Given an ROV received Tue Oct 20 with three sourced comparables, then screening completes by Thu Oct 22, the SME escalation is created, and after forwarding on Oct 21 the appraiser response is due Wed Oct 28.
- **24.2-T5** Given a second borrower ROV on the same appraisal, or an ROV submitted after `closing.consummated`, then the request is rejected with the stated reason and no appraiser contact occurs.
- **24.2-T6** Given a report narrative containing "undesirable neighborhood" without factual support, then `bias_scan_result.severity = high`, `correction_requested` is issued using the template, and a fair-lending record is written; the value is not used until the corrected version is accepted.
- **24.2-T7** Given a non-QM HPML purchase with seller acquisition Aug 1, 2026 at $340,000 and a contract Oct 15, 2026 at $412,000, then the flip test yields days = 75, pct = 21.18%, a second appraisal by a different appraiser is required, only one appraisal fee may be borrower-paid, and both copies are due by Fri Nov 13 for a Nov 18 consummation.
- **24.2-T8** Given a General QM classification, then `hpml_appraisal_rules_apply = false` and only the Reg B timers exist.
- **24.2-T9** Given UCDP "Not Successful" with a manually overridable stop, then a `fnma_portal_operator` escalation with reason code and justification is created and delivery remains blocked by `FNMA_B4_1_1_06_UCDP_SUCCESSFUL_GATE` until Successful.
- **24.2-T10** Given denial issued Fri Oct 30, 2026 with an undelivered AVM report developed Oct 12, then `REGB_1002_14_COPY_NOT_CONSUMMATED_30` is due Sun Nov 29 and the package includes the AVM report.
- **24.2-T11** Given a revised report (v2) received Mon Oct 26 after v1 was delivered Oct 16, then v2 is resubmitted to UCDP under the same Doc File ID, delivered to the applicants, and `is_final_version` moves to v2.
- **24.2-T12** Given CU score 4.3 with an Overvaluation flag, then `SM_CU_HIGH_RISK_REVIEW_1BD` is opened, the enhanced review record includes a comparable re-analysis, and `rw_relief_property_value = false`.

Per appraisal version: SSRs (both GSEs), Doc File ID, CU score/flags/messages, checklist results, bias-scan output with reviewer rationale, `completion_at` derivation, copy package with channel consent and receipt evidence, waiver statements, ROV file (request, screening, SME decision, standardized communication, response, outcome), HPML determinations and flip arithmetic, override justifications with operator identity. ### Open questions / decisions
1. **Designated ROV SME.** Whether the `valuation` agent may itself be the "underwriter or other appraisal subject matter expert". Default: `underwriting_reviewer` (human) approves forward/decline on AI-prepared analysis; revisit under LL-2026-04 governance evidence.
2. **Mailbox assumption under Reg B.** Resolved 2026-09-11: comment 14(a)(1)-4 supplies the rule — delivery occurs three business days after mailing/delivering to the last-known address, or on evidenced actual receipt, whichever is earlier. Default stands: mailed copies count as provided on mailing for the "prompt" test and as received 3 business days later for the 3-day gate unless earlier receipt is evidenced. Remaining question: which business-day calendar (Reg B defines none; creditor calendar used).
3. **Business-day unit.** Default `business_days_creditor` for §1002.14 and §1026.35(c); alternative: `business_days_regz_specific` (more conservative on Saturdays). Decision recorded in `rule_sets.regb.2013`.
4. **Are DU value-acceptance messages "valuations"?** Default: no (no value estimate is developed); AVM outputs and staff value memos are; legal sign-off requested.
5. **Second-appraisal/field-review triggers on CU.** Default: CU ≥ 4.0 or Overvaluation flag → enhanced review; field review only where the review finds unsupported value; second appraisal only for HPML flips or documented unreliability.
6. **Interagency ROV guidance status** after the CFPB's May 12, 2025 withdrawals: treat as best practice regardless (Fannie Mae's B4-1.3-12 is contractual).
