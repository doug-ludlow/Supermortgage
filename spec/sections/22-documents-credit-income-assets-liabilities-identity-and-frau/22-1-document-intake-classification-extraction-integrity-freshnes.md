### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Age of credit documents — Selling Guide B1-1-03, Allowable Age of Credit Documents and Federal Income Tax Returns (last updated 04/02/2025; Guide edition published Sept. 2, 2026). VERIFIED.** "Credit documents must be no more than four months old on the note date." Credit documents "include credit reports and employment, income, and asset documentation." "When consecutive credit documents are in the loan file, the most recent document is used to determine whether it meets the age requirement." Example in the topic: "When two consecutive monthly bank statements are used to verify a depository asset, the date of the most recent statement must be no more than four months old on the note date." "If the credit documents are older than allowed, the lender must update them." Exceptions are cross-referenced to B2-3-05 for loans impacted by a natural disaster, and "For loans with income validated by DU, lenders may rely on the age of tax transcript methodology provided by the service." Note beneath the table: "Any documents provided by the borrower must clearly identify the source of information including identifying information in the Internet banner."

The same topic governs **federal income tax return age**. "The 'most recent year's' tax return is defined as the last return scheduled to have been filed with the IRS." Table (application date → disbursement date → requirement): (1) application Oct 15 of the prior year–Apr 14 of the current year, disbursement in the same window → "The most recent year's tax return is required. The use of a Tax Extension (IRS Form 4868) is not permitted."; (2) same application window, disbursement Apr 15–Jun 30 → "The most recent year's tax return is recommended; however, the previous year(s) is also acceptable. In the event the most recent year's tax return is not obtained, the loan file must include a completed and signed IRS Form 4506-C for transcripts."; (3) same application window, disbursement Jul 1–Oct 14 → most recent year recommended, previous year(s) acceptable; if the most recent year is not obtained the lender must "Obtain one of the following documents from the borrower: copy of IRS Form 4868 ... filed with the IRS" (or confirmation of electronic payment), "Review the total tax liability ... compare it" to the prior year, and "Obtain IRS response from the filing of IRS Form 4506-C" confirming that no transcripts are available; (4) application Apr 15–Oct 14, disbursement Apr 15–Dec 31 → rendered as a cell merged with row (3), i.e., the same "recommended / previous year acceptable / Form 4868 path" **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — merged-cell rendering; the extraction tool returned the cell as empty]**; (5) application Apr 15–Oct 14, disbursement Jan 1–Apr 14 of the following year → "The most recent year's tax return is required. The use of a Tax Extension (IRS Form 4868) is not permitted."

**Employment and income documentation standards — B3-3.2-01, Standards for Employment and Income Documentation (03/04/2026). VERIFIED.** The most recent paystub must be "dated no earlier than 30 days prior to the initial loan application date" and must "include all year-to-date earnings" and "include sufficient information to appropriately calculate income." "IRS W-2 forms must cover the most recent one- or two-year period, based on the documentation requirements for the particular income type, and must clearly identify the borrower as the employee." A "year end paystub reflecting earnings from the entire year is acceptable in lieu of the W-2." Documents "must be computer-generated or typed by the borrower's employer(s), although paystubs that the borrower downloads from the Internet are also acceptable"; they must "clearly identify the employer's name and source of information" and be "complete and legible." The lender "may use the Request for Verification of Employment (Form 1005) to document employment and income"; certain fields are optional but "the remaining fields on the form must be completed as applicable to the borrower." "When employment is validated through the DU validation service using an asset verification report, the lender is not required to obtain the employment verification documentation" otherwise required. (Income-type documentation and calculation rules are 22.3's; this process enforces only the document-level standards.)

**Asset documentation standards — B3-4.2-01, Verification of Deposits and Assets (05/04/2022). VERIFIED.** Form 1006: "The information must be requested directly from the depository institution, and the complete, signed, and dated document must be sent directly from the depository institution." Statements must "clearly identify the financial institution," "clearly identify the borrower as the account holder," "include at least the last four digits of the account number," "include the time period covered by the statement," "include all deposits and withdrawal transactions," and "include the ending account balance." Period: purchases — "the most recent full two-month period of account activity (60 days, or, if account information is reported on a quarterly basis, the most recent quarter)"; refinances — "the most recent full one-month period of account activity (30 days, or, if account information is reported on a quarterly basis, the most recent quarter)." "Documents that are faxed to the lender or downloaded from the Internet must clearly identify the name of the depository or investment institution and the source of information." A lender holding the account "may produce a printout or other alternative verification of the asset(s) directly from its system." (Sufficiency, large deposits, gifts and reserves are 22.4's.)

**Electronic records and imaging — A2-4.1-03, Electronic Records, Signatures, and Transactions (05/06/2026). VERIFIED.** "All documents used to originate, service or modify a loan, may be generated, signed, processed, stored or transmitted electronically," except promissory notes and unrecorded ink-signed originals modifying security instruments (unless eNote approval is held — 26.2). "All electronic records must be valid and enforceable and accurately reflect all information and formatting"; "When documents are converted to an alternative format, the legibility and integrity of the information and formatting ... must be preserved"; records must be "securely stored and remain accessible by all persons entitled to access them for as long as access is required" and systems must "be sufficiently secure to preserve the integrity and authenticity of the records." Electronic signatures "must comply with, and be enforceable under ESIGN and UETA adopted by the state"; the lender must retain evidence of the "authenticated identity of the signer" and "date of the signature." **Loan file — A2-4.1-01, Establishing Loan Files (09/04/2024). VERIFIED.** "The loan file consists of the loan origination file, the loan custodial file, and the loan servicing file"; "The loan file includes all records needed to service the loan and support the validity of the loan"; the origination file must contain "all documents, records and reports used to support the underwriting decision." **Ownership/retention — A2-4.1-02 (12/19/2017). VERIFIED.** "All records related to loans ... sold to or serviced for Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property"; "After a loan is liquidated, the servicer must keep the individual loan records for at least four years" unless law or Fannie Mae requires longer; records must "continue to be obtainable and readable in the future"; on request "the seller/servicer must deliver all records to Fannie Mae or to whomever Fannie Mae designates within the time frame specified by Fannie Mae."

**Borrower authorization — B1-1-02, Blanket Authorization Form (04/01/2009). VERIFIED.** The form authorizes the lender to "verify my past and present employment earnings records, bank accounts, stock holdings" and to obtain consumer credit reports and mortgage/landlord references; "a photocopy of this form also will serve as authorization"; verification forms must be "signed and dated, and must be sent directly from the verifying institution"; use of the information is limited to "the processing of my application for a mortgage loan." B3-2-02 (02/05/2025) adds: "For loans assessed by the DU validation service, the lender must obtain borrower authorization to receive the information from the vendor."

**Data integrity and fraud expectations — A3-4-02, Data Quality and Integrity (08/07/2018) and A3-4-03, Preventing, Detecting, and Reporting Mortgage Fraud (12/10/2025). VERIFIED.** A3-4-02: "Lenders must supply Fannie Mae with complete and accurate data."; "All data entered into DU must be verifiable."; "The lender must have adequate procedures in place to validate the integrity of specific data for each underwriting recommendation."; "The lender remains responsible for representations and warranties for the life of the loan related to misstatements, misrepresentations, omissions, and data inaccuracies." A3-4-03: written policies covering hiring, vetting of employees/contractors against exclusion lists and suspected-fraud reporting; lenders should "aggressively sample loans that have a high risk for fraud as part of the quality control process"; "A seller/servicer must notify Fannie Mae within 30 days using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect" when there is a reasonable basis to conclude misrepresentation or fraud occurred (28.4 owns the timer `FNMA_A3_4_03_FRAUD_SELF_REPORT_30`). Fannie Mae's Mortgage Fraud Prevention resources (singlefamily.fanniemae.com, fetched 2026-09-10) list document red flags the platform operationalizes: "Documentation that includes deletions, correction fluid, or other alterations"; "Numbers on the documentation that appear to be 'squeezed' due to alteration"; "Year-to-date or past-year earnings are even dollar amounts"; "Withholding not calculated correctly (check FICA tables)"; "Recently deposited funds without a plausible paper-trail or explanation"; "Balances verified as even dollar amounts." The "Best practices for income and employment verifications" resource (© 2024): "Annual, year-to-date, or past-year earnings are rounded dollar amounts."; "Recent large pay increases."; "Documentation (paystub or bank account data) reflects different employment source"; "When discrepancies are identified, lenders must conduct additional due diligence to determine the validity of the information."

**Appraisal age (referenced, owned by 24.1) — B4-1.2-04, Appraisal Age and Use Requirements (06/04/2025). VERIFIED.** "When an appraisal is obtained, the property must be appraised within the 12 months prior to the date of the note and mortgage." "When the effective date of the original appraisal report is more than four months but less than 12 months from the date of the note and mortgage, the appraiser must perform an appraisal update" (Form 1004D); "If the appraiser indicates on the Form 1004D that the property value has declined, then the lender must obtain a new appraisal"; more than 12 months "a new appraisal report is required"; desktop appraisals: more than four months → new appraisal. Value-acceptance offers: "not more than four months old on the date of the note and the mortgage" (B4-1.4-10, 00a-fnma §4.12).

**E-SIGN — 15 U.S.C. 7001(c)(1) (00a-fed §8). VERIFIED.** Consumer consent must be affirmative and preceded by the (B)(i)–(iv) and (C) statements (paper option, scope, withdrawal procedure, paper-copy procedure, hardware/software requirements), given "in a manner that reasonably demonstrates that the consumer can access information in the electronic form"; 7001(e): records must be "capable of being retained and accurately reproduced for later reference." 21.1 owns `NTC_SM_ESIGN_CONSENT`; this process consumes `consents`.

**Retention (00a-fed §15). VERIFIED.** Reg B §1002.12(b)(1): application, information on applicant characteristics, notices and reasons "For 25 months" after notification of action taken or incompleteness; Reg Z §1026.25(c)(3): ATR/QM evidence "for three years after consummation"; §1026.25(c)(1)(i)/(ii): LE 3 years / CD 5 years; Fannie Mae A2-4.1-02: at least four years after liquidation.

**No rule sets a needs-list SLA.** The only external clocks on document requests are Reg B §1002.9(a)(1)(i)/(c) — 30 days from a completed application to notify of action, and the NOIA, which must "designate a reasonable period of time" for the applicant to respond (00a-fed §3.1; 21.6 owns `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` and `NTC_REGB_1002_9_NOIA`) — and the TRID rule that "creditors cannot require a consumer to provide verifying documents in order to receive a Loan Estimate" (CFPB TRID FAQ, 00a-fed §1.10; 21.2).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. **B3-3.1-02 is no longer the employment-documentation topic.** After the 2026 restructuring (SEL-2026-02, 00a-fnma §4.6/§10), B3-3.1-02 is "Tax Return and Transcript Documentation Requirements" (06/03/2026); the document standards the row means are **B3-3.2-01, Standards for Employment and Income Documentation (03/04/2026)**.
2. **A3-4-02 is "Data Quality and Integrity"**, not fraud prevention; the fraud prevention/detection/reporting duties are **A3-4-03 (12/10/2025)** with the 30-day Loan Quality Connect self-report, supplemented by the non-Guide Mortgage Fraud Prevention resources.
3. There is no Selling Guide topic titled "acceptable electronic documents"; the operative text is **A2-4.1-03 (05/06/2026)** plus the document-source sentences inside B3-3.2-01 and B3-4.2-01.
4. **B1-1-02 is the Blanket Authorization Form**, not "application documentation" (the application package is B1-1-01, 21.1); it matters here as the borrower's authorization for Forms 1005/1006 and vendor pulls.
5. "Appraisal 12 months" is right but incomplete: an update (Form 1004D) is required once the effective date is more than four months before the note date; desktop appraisals and value-acceptance offers expire at four months (24.1 owns the timers).
6. "Paystub within 30 days of application" is precisely "dated no earlier than 30 days prior to the **initial loan application date**" — a not-before floor keyed to the first application date, not a rolling 30-day window, and it is not refreshed by a later re-application on the same file.
7. The row omits the **tax-return age table** (two-dimensional: application date and disbursement date) and the Form 4868/4506-C fallback paths — a second freshness engine that 22.3 consumes.
8. For refinances the asset statement period is **one month**, not two (B3-4.2-01) — the 22.4 row's "2 months statements" is the purchase figure.
9. "Needs-list SLAs" are internal policy; the only legal clocks are Reg B's 30-day decision/NOIA rules (21.6).
10. DU-validated components are not "documents" with a paper age: the DU validation report is the evidence and DU applies the vendor data's age (B3-3.1-04: "DU includes in its assessment the age of the information in the vendor's database"); B3-2-02's "Close by Date" message is the binding date for validated employment (22.3).

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- Borrower actions: upload in `borrower-app` (file or camera capture), reply to a needs-list e-mail with attachments, paper mailed to the partner's lockbox (scanned by the print/mail vendor), fax to the partner's number (rare; treated as scan).
- Vendor deliveries through `verification/*` adapters: VOE/VOI reports, asset verification reports (30/60-day and 12-month), IRS transcripts via IVES A2A or a DU tax-transcript supplier (22.3), SSA CBSV results (22.6), credit reports (22.2), appraisal/PDC deliverables (24.1), title commitments (24.4), MI certificates (24.6), flood determinations (24.5), HOA/condo questionnaires (24.3), insurance evidence (24.5).
- Platform events: `application.received` (opens the initial needs list from the intake interview), `du.findings.received` and `condition.opened{source=du|underwriter|qc|compliance|closing}` (23.2/23.3 conditions that require a document), `closing.scheduled` and every reschedule (recomputes freshness against the new note date), `lock.executed` / `disclosure.cd.delivered` (freshness re-check before CD issuance), `du.submitted` (final-submission document-set snapshot).
- Schedules: nightly freshness sweep (every open application), needs-list reminder cadence, retention-class transitions at `loan.funded`, `decision.issued{denial|withdrawal}`, and `loan.purchased`.

#### State machine

Document: `received` → `classifying` → (`classified` | `unclassified`) → `extracting` → `extracted` → `integrity_checking` → (`accepted` | `flagged` | `failed`) → `linked` (matched to one or more `document_requests`/`conditions`) → (`superseded` | `expired` | `retained`). Transitions are performed by the `verification` agent; `flagged` → `accepted` requires a written agent decision with the resolving evidence; `flagged` → `failed` on a confirmed alteration; `failed` opens a `cases{case_type='fraud'}` candidate and an `escalation{role=underwriting_reviewer}` (22.6 takes over). `unclassified` after two classifier passes (second pass with OCR at 400 dpi) → agent prompts the borrower for the document type in the portal; still unclassified after the borrower's answer → `human_agent` queue. Terminal: `retained` (after funding, class-driven retention), `superseded`, `expired` (never used for a decision), `failed`.

Document request: `open` → `reminded` (n times) → `received` (a candidate document is linked) → `under_review` → (`satisfied` | `open` with a new reason when the document does not meet the standard) | `waived` (only by the owning process's rule, e.g., DU validation replaced the paper) | `expired` (application withdrawn/denied) | `cancelled` (condition withdrawn). Guards: a request cannot be `satisfied` by a document whose `integrity_status ≠ passed`, whose `freshness_status = expired` against the scheduled note date, or whose extracted subject does not match the borrower.

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — Document date.** `document_date` is the date the document bears, by class: paystub → pay date (fallback: period end); bank/brokerage statement → statement closing date (`period_end`); W-2/1099 → tax year end (Dec 31 of the tax year) with `freshness_basis = b1_1_03_tax_year_table`; Form 1005/1006 → the verifier's signature date; VOE/VOA vendor report → report generation date (`du_validation_vendor_age` basis; DU applies its own age logic); explanation letters → signature date; ID → n/a (validity governed by 22.6 expiry rules); purchase contract → n/a. Default for open question Q1: calendar-month arithmetic — `expires_at = add_months(document_date, 4)`; if the resulting month has no such day, the last day of that month.

**R2 — Four-month freshness (B1-1-03).** For every relied-upon credit document: `fresh iff expires_at ≥ scheduled_note_date`. "Consecutive documents" rule: when a request needs n consecutive statements, only the most recent statement's date is tested. Worked example (refinance fixture): scheduled note date Fri Nov 6, 2026 → a document dated on or after Mon Jul 6, 2026 is fresh (`add_months(Jul 6, 4) = Nov 6 ≥ Nov 6`). Bank statement for the period Jun 1–Jun 30, 2026 (statement date Jun 30) → `expires_at = Oct 30, 2026 < Nov 6` → expired; the July statement (Jul 31 → Nov 30) is fresh. Refinance asset requirement is one month (B3-4.2-01), so the July statement alone satisfies the period rule, provided the August or September statement is not also in the file as the "most recent" (if it is, the most recent is the one tested — Aug 31 → expires Dec 31, 2026). If closing slips to Mon Dec 7, 2026, the threshold becomes Fri Aug 7, 2026: the July statement expires (`Nov 30 < Dec 7`) and the agent opens a request for the August statement 14 days before Nov 30 (`SM_DOC_EXPIRY_WARN_14` fires Nov 16).

**R3 — Paystub floor (B3-3.2-01).** `paystub_ok iff document_date ≥ application_date − 30 calendar_days AND gross_ytd_cents present AND employer_name present`. Refinance fixture: application Mon Oct 5, 2026 − 30 days = **Sat Sept 5, 2026**; a paystub with pay date Fri Oct 2, 2026 (period Sept 16–30) qualifies; one dated Fri Sept 4 does not. Purchase fixture: application Mon Oct 19, 2026 − 30 days = **Sat Sept 19, 2026**. Both floors are computed from the initial application date and are never re-based when the application is amended.

**R4 — Tax-return year (B1-1-03 table).** Inputs `application_date`, `scheduled_disbursement_date`. Refinance fixture: application Oct 5, 2026 falls in "April 15–October 14, current year"; disbursement Thu Nov 12, 2026 falls in "April 15–December 31, current year" → row (4): 2025 return recommended, 2024 acceptable only with the Form 4868 path (extension evidence or electronic-payment confirmation, tax-liability comparison, and an IRS "no transcript available" response to Form 4506-C) [row (4) PARTIALLY VERIFIED — merged cell]. If the same application disbursed on Fri Jan 15, 2027 → row (5): "The most recent year's tax return is required" (2025) and "The use of a Tax Extension (IRS Form 4868) is not permitted." An application dated Mon Jan 11, 2027 with disbursement Fri Feb 26, 2027 → row (1): the 2025 return is required (the 2026 return is not yet "scheduled to have been filed"), no extension path.

**R5 — Integrity checks (A3-4-02/A3-4-03; Fannie Mae red flags).** Every document passes the check battery; results are `pass/warn/fail` with details:
- `pdf_metadata`: producer/creator strings on a known-editor list (image editors, PDF editors, "template" generators), `ModDate` later than `CreationDate` by more than 24 hours on a bank/payroll document, missing or inconsistent XMP history, flattened layers → `warn`; detected text-layer edits over an original raster → `fail` candidate.
- `font_layout`: mixed font families or sizes within a numeric column, baseline misalignment, kerning anomalies ("squeezed" numbers) → `warn`.
- `arithmetic` (paystub): `gross_current = Σ earnings lines`; `net_current = gross_current − Σ deductions`; YTD monotonic vs the prior paystub; Medicare withholding `≈ 1.45% × gross_ytd` (26 U.S.C. 3101(b)) and Social Security withholding `≈ 6.2% × min(gross_ytd, ssa_wage_base[year])` (26 U.S.C. 3101(a); `ssa_wage_base` is a loaded parameter, not asserted here); tolerance `|expected − actual| ≤ max(500 cents, 0.5% × expected)`, otherwise `warn` ("Withholding not calculated correctly"). Worked example: semi-monthly gross $6,250.00 (625,000 cents), 18 pay periods through Sept 30, 2026 → expected `gross_ytd = 18 × 625,000 = 11,250,000` cents ($112,500.00); expected Medicare YTD `= round(11,250,000 × 0.0145) = 163,125` cents ($1,631.25); tolerance `max(500, 816) = 816` cents; a paystub showing $1,631.25 passes; one showing $1,500.00 (150,000 cents; deviation 13,125 cents) fails the tolerance → `warn` + request the employer-sourced VOE or DU validation report before the income is used.
- `even_dollar`: YTD gross or ending balance divisible by 10,000 cents ($100) with zero cents → `warn` (Fannie Mae red flag), never `fail` alone.
- `running_balance` (statements): every transaction reconciles `prev + amount = running` and `opening + Σ = ending` → mismatch is `fail` candidate (fabricated statements rarely reconcile); deposits ≥ 50% of monthly qualifying income are tagged for 22.4's large-deposit rule.
- `template_match`: page geometry and static text compared with the issuer's known template library (vendor-supplied) → mismatch `warn`.
- `cross_document`: employer name/address on paystub vs W-2 vs 1003 vs VOE; borrower name/SSN last-4 across documents; account last-4 across statements; address on ID vs statements vs 1003 → mismatch `warn` and a targeted request ("Documentation ... reflects different employment source").
- `sender_authentication`: e-mail intake must pass SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment with the borrower's verified address or a contracted vendor domain; otherwise the attachment is quarantined and the borrower is asked to upload through the portal.
- `vendor_fraud_score`: the contracted document-forensics vendor's score (contract-gated API; PARTIALLY VERIFIED) mapped to `pass/warn/fail` at thresholds set in `rule_sets` (default: vendor's own "high risk" band = `fail` candidate).
- `source_verification`: a document is `passed` without vendor scan when the same facts arrive from the source (DU validation report, Form 1006 sent directly by the institution, IVES transcript, CBSV, vendor VOE). Source-obtained evidence always outranks borrower-supplied paper for the same fact.
Aggregation: any `fail` → `integrity_status = failed`; ≥ 2 `warn` on one document or a `warn` on an income/asset document that is the sole evidence for a qualifying fact → `flagged`; otherwise `passed`. A `failed` document is never silently dropped: 22.6 opens the fraud review and, if fraud is confirmed, 28.4's 30-day self-report clock starts at confirmation.

**R6 — Needs-list generation.** `document_requests` are derived, never free-typed: (a) intake rules by income/asset type (22.3/22.4 documentation matrices) at `application.received`; (b) DU verification messages and approval conditions (`condition.opened{source=du}`), mapped by DU message id to `doc_class` + qualifier; (c) underwriter/QC/compliance conditions; (d) freshness replacements (R2). De-duplication: one open request per (`borrower_id`, `doc_class`, `qualifier`); a DU validation outcome of `validated` for a component waives the paper requests for that component (`waived_by = du_validation`) and the waiver is recorded with the DU submission number. The consolidated `NTC_SM_NEEDS_LIST` is sent once per batch (never one e-mail per item), and never before the LE has been delivered when the item is a "verifying document" (TRID FAQ; 21.2 gate).

**R7 — SLA and Reg B interaction.** Worked example (refinance fixture): DU findings on Wed Oct 7, 2026 open three requests at 14:05 MST → `due_at` Mon Oct 12, 2026 (Columbus Day is irrelevant: `calendar_days`); reminders Fri Oct 9 and Sun Oct 11; borrower uploads Sat Oct 10 → `SM_NEEDS_LIST_REVIEW_1BD` due Mon Oct 12 (next `business_days_creditor` day after a Saturday receipt is Monday; Columbus Day Oct 12 is on the federal list and therefore closed in calendar `creditor`, so the review is due **Tue Oct 13, 2026**). If nothing arrives by Sat Oct 17 (+10), the agent evaluates whether the application is "incomplete" under Reg B and hands 21.6 a NOIA recommendation with the missing items and a response period; 21.6's `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` runs from 21.6's conservative anchor (`application_date`; never later than the completed-application date) and is never extended by this process.

**R8 — Superseding and versioning.** A new document of the same class for the same subject and period supersedes the prior (`supersedes_document_id`); the superseded document stays in the file (A2-4.1-01: "all documents, records and reports used to support the underwriting decision"), but only the newest can satisfy a request. Documents that were relied on for a decision are frozen (hash recorded in `du_submissions.findings_document_id` set and `decisions`).

**R9 — Retention class assignment.** At intake: `regb_25m` (Reg B minimum for every application document). At `loan.funded`: `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` (A2-4.1-02) applied to the whole file; ATR evidence additionally tagged `regz_atr_3y` (§1026.25(c)(3), 23.4); E-SIGN consent evidence `esign_consent_life`; IVES-related records `irs_ives_2y` (22.3); SSA-89 `ssa_89_5y` (22.6). Denied/withdrawn: `regb_25m` from the notification date (21.6 event) — then purge unless `legal_hold`. Credit-report data use is limited to the transaction (FCRA; 22.2).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`verification` agent (tools: `ingestDocument`, `classifyDocument`, `extractFields`, `runIntegrityBattery`, `orderForensicScan`, `computeFreshness`, `matchToRequests`, `deriveNeedsList`, `renderNeedsListNotice`, `sendNotice`, `openRequest`, `waiveRequest`, `proposeConditionClear`, `writeDecision`, `escalate`). End-to-end behavior: on `document.received` it classifies, extracts, runs the battery, computes freshness against the current scheduled note date, matches the document to open requests, and either satisfies them (proposing condition clearance to 23.3) or opens a replacement request with a plain-language reason; on `condition.opened` it derives requests and sends one consolidated notice; nightly it re-evaluates freshness for every open application against the scheduled note date and opens replacements 14 days ahead. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{document_id | request_id, inputs (hashes, model versions, rule_set_version), classification {class, confidence}, integrity {checks, aggregate}, freshness {basis, document_date, expires_at, note_date, status}, action, rationale, confidence, escalation_id?}`. Guardrails — the agent may never: alter, redact or "enhance" a document (only derived data is created); accept a `failed` document or waive a Fannie Mae documentation requirement other than through a DU validation outcome or an owning-process rule; use a phone number or e-mail from a borrower-supplied document to verify a source (B3-3.1-04 independence; 22.3); request a verifying document before the LE is delivered (TRID FAQ); send a needs-list item that is not linked to a condition, an intake rule or a freshness rule; contact a borrower by SMS without TCPA consent; process a document for a person who is not an applicant on the file (wrong-borrower upload is quarantined). Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` (sev 2, SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`) for integrity `failed` and for any `flagged` document that is the sole evidence of a qualifying fact; `human_agent` when the borrower asks or after the third unanswered reminder; `licensed_specialist` where `jurisdiction_rules` require it; `officer` only through 28.4 (fraud self-report/SAR). Borrower-facing rules: every needs-list message discloses that it was generated by an automated system on behalf of the partner, names the MLO of record, and offers a human contact; the borrower may withdraw E-SIGN consent at any time, after which the paper path is used. Human path: the ops console shows the same queue with manual classify/extract override, manual request creation (reason code mandatory) and manual notice release, all logged as `agent_decisions{decided_by=human}`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Password-protected or encrypted PDF → request the password through the portal (never by e-mail); store decrypted copy plus original.
- Bundle uploads (one PDF with paystubs + statements) → page-level classification and splitting; each child document carries `parent_document_id` in `sender_identity`.
- Duplicate upload (same hash) → linked, not re-processed; a different rendering of the same statement (print-to-PDF vs bank PDF) → both retained, the bank-sourced one preferred.
- Screenshot or photo of an online statement without banner/URL → B1-1-03 note and B3-4.2-01 require the source identification; `warn` + request the institution's PDF or a VOA report.
- Foreign-language document → B3-3.2-02 requires English or a complete translation "attached to each document"; agent requests a translation and marks the pair.
- Document for the wrong person (name/SSN mismatch with all applicants) → quarantine, delete after 30 days unless the borrower explains (privacy incident check under 31.3).
- Employer or bank letter received by e-mail from an unverified sender → not acceptable as source-obtained; treated as borrower-supplied and verified independently (22.3).
- Statement period straddling the 4-month threshold → tested on `period_end` (R1); if the closing moves later, the nightly sweep re-tests.
- Closing rescheduled earlier than planned → freshness can only improve; no action.
- DU validation report present but the borrower also uploads paper → the paper is retained, the request is `waived_by = du_validation`; conflicting facts between the two trigger B3-2-02's "investigate and resolve any conflicting or contradictory information" duty (22.3/22.4).
- Borrower withdraws E-SIGN consent mid-loop → open requests re-issued on paper; `due_at` extended by the mail-time policy (default +5 calendar days).
- Natural-disaster area (B2-3-05) → freshness exceptions are decided by 24.1/23.3; this process only flags.
- Vendor forensic tool outage → in-house battery only; `vendor_fraud_score = n_a` prevents a `passed` result on income/asset documents that lack source verification (they stay `flagged` for review).
- Application denied/withdrawn with open requests → requests `expired`; retention class `regb_25m` starts at the notification date.
- Fannie Mae requests the file (A2-4.1-02) → export bundle with hashes and lineage within Fannie Mae's stated timeframe (`fnma_portal_operator` uploads to Loan Quality Connect, 28.2).

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **22.1-T1 (four-month gate, refinance fixture).** Given scheduled note date Fri Nov 6, 2026 and a bank statement with `period_end` Jun 30, 2026, when freshness is computed, then `expires_at = Oct 30, 2026`, `freshness_status = expired`, and `assertGateOpen('FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_DOCS_4M')` fails for any decision relying on it; a statement with `period_end` Jul 31, 2026 yields `expires_at = Nov 30, 2026` and `fresh`.
- **22.1-T2 (consecutive statements rule).** Given July and August 2026 statements in the file and note date Dec 7, 2026, when the gate is evaluated, then only the August statement (`expires_at = Dec 31, 2026`) is tested and the gate is open even though the July statement expired Nov 30.
- **22.1-T3 (paystub floor).** Given initial application date Mon Oct 5, 2026, when a paystub with pay date Fri Sept 4, 2026 is classified, then `FNMA_B3_3_2_01_PAYSTUB_30D_GATE` is not satisfied and a request for "most recent paystub (dated on/after Sept 5, 2026) with year-to-date earnings" is opened; a paystub dated Fri Oct 2, 2026 with YTD satisfies it.
- **22.1-T4 (paystub floor not re-based).** Given the same application amended on Mon Oct 26, 2026, when the floor is recomputed, then it remains Sept 5, 2026.
- **22.1-T5 (tax-year table).** Given application Oct 5, 2026 and disbursement Fri Jan 15, 2027, when `FNMA_B1_1_03_TAX_YEAR_GATE` is evaluated, then the 2025 return is required and a Form 4868 extension path is rejected; given disbursement Nov 12, 2026 instead, then the 2024 return is acceptable only with extension evidence, the tax-liability comparison and an IRS no-transcript response recorded.
- **22.1-T6 (arithmetic integrity).** Given a paystub with `gross_ytd = 11,250,000` cents and Medicare YTD 150,000 cents, when the battery runs, then `arithmetic = warn` (deviation 13,125 cents > tolerance 816 cents), the document is `flagged`, and a source-obtained VOE/validation report is requested before 22.3 may use the income; with Medicare YTD 163,125 cents the check passes.
- **22.1-T7 (running balance).** Given a bank statement whose transactions do not reconcile to the ending balance, when the battery runs, then `running_balance = fail`, `integrity_status = failed`, an `escalation{role=underwriting_reviewer}` with a 1 `business_days_creditor` SLA is created, and 22.6 opens a fraud case candidate.
- **22.1-T8 (needs-list SLA with holiday).** Given three requests opened Wed Oct 7, 2026, when the borrower uploads Sat Oct 10, 2026, then reminders were sent Oct 9 only, `document.received{request_id}` is logged Oct 10 (satisfying `SM_NEEDS_LIST_BORROWER_RESPONSE_5`), and `SM_NEEDS_LIST_REVIEW_1BD` is due Tue Oct 13, 2026 (Columbus Day Oct 12 closed in calendar `creditor`).
- **22.1-T9 (DU validation waiver).** Given a DU employment validation outcome `validated` for borrower 1 with employer Acme, when the finding is received, then the open `form_1005_voe`/`paystub` requests for that borrower and employer are `waived_by = du_validation` with the submission number, and no needs-list notice mentions them.
- **22.1-T10 (TRID ordering).** Given an application whose LE has not been delivered, when DU opens verification conditions, then the needs-list notice is queued and released only after `disclosure.le.delivered`.
- **22.1-T11 (e-mail authentication).** Given an e-mail attachment from a sender failing DMARC alignment, when ingested, then the document is quarantined (`integrity_status = failed`, `sender_authentication = fail`), no request is satisfied, and the borrower receives a portal-upload prompt.
- **22.1-T12 (retention transitions).** Given a loan funded Thu Nov 12, 2026, when `loan.funded` is processed, then every file document carries `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`, the ATR evidence set also carries `regz_atr_3y`, and a withdrawn application notified Mon Nov 2, 2026 carries `regb_25m` with purge eligibility on Dec 2, 2028.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **How is "four months" counted?** Default: calendar-month arithmetic (`add_months`, end-of-month clamp); a document dated on the threshold day (Jul 6 for a Nov 6 note) is fresh. Confirm with the partner's QC counsel; alternative is 120 days.
2. **Which date is the statement's "document date"?** Default: statement closing date (`period_end`), consistent with B1-1-03's example; alternative is the print/issue date.
3. **Are W-2s/1099s and tax returns subject to the four-month rule?** Default: no — they are governed by the "most recent one- or two-year period" and the tax-year table; only dated verification documents (paystubs, statements, VOEs, letters) carry `b1_1_03_4m`.
4. **E-mail as a borrower intake channel.** Default: allowed only from the borrower's verified address with DMARC alignment; everything else is redirected to the portal.
5. **Forensic vendor and thresholds.** Default: one contracted document-forensics vendor with its "high risk" band mapped to `fail`; in-house battery as floor; vendor `n_a` caps at `flagged`.
6. **Needs-list SLA values.** Default: borrower 5 calendar days with reminders at +2/+4; agent review 1 `business_days_creditor`; NOIA evaluation at +10 days (21.6 decides).
7. **Row (4) of the tax-return table.** Default: treat as identical to row (3) (merged cell); re-read the printed Guide PDF (Sept 2, 2026 edition) before release.
8. **Whether the partner requires human sign-off on integrity `flagged` documents that the agent clears.** Default: no — agent clears with a written decision; `failed` always goes to `underwriting_reviewer`.
