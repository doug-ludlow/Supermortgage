### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**C-3-01, Responsibilities Related to Remitting P&I Funds to Fannie Mae (03/11/2026).** Remittance types: *actual/actual* — remit what is collected (biweekly A/A reported daily as received); *scheduled/actual* — scheduled interest whether or not collected, actual principal as collected; portfolio S/A loans advance "scheduled interest only through the third month of delinquency" (concurrent-sales participation pool loans through the foreclosure sale date); *scheduled/scheduled* — scheduled P&I whether or not collected: for MBS special servicing option and portfolio S/S special servicing loans "until the mortgage loan becomes four consecutive months delinquent," for regular servicing option loans until the loan is "removed from Fannie Mae's active accounting records"; at four consecutive months "Fannie Mae will suspend drafting scheduled P&I amounts from the servicer's custodial account until the mortgage loan becomes current or a full contractual payment is made" (5.4). Buydown funds held by the servicer are remitted with the borrower's payment as a full contractual payment and returned when the buydown ends or the loan liquidates (SVC-2026-02 removed the obsolete "Fannie Mae holds buydown funds" language). Over-remittances: exercise due diligence, then submit a documented refund claim. Funds must be remitted "on or before the remittance due date" or Fannie Mae "may impose a compensatory fee."

**F-1-20, Remitting and Accounting to Fannie Mae (03/11/2026)** — operative remittance schedule:
- *Summary-reporting A/A:* report a remittance "whenever the total amount collected is greater than $2,500 after deducting servicing fees," "at least once a month" if less, and "on the first work day of each month if any collections on the last work day in the preceding month were not remitted"; Fannie Mae drafts the following business day. *Detailed-reporting A/A:* report daily as received; reporting "automatically initiates a draft of the remittance amount from the servicer's custodial account within 48 hours."
- *S/A (portfolio and MBS):* funds "available for drafting on the 20th calendar day of each month (or the preceding business day if the 20th is not a business day)," based on activity reported for the prior month; electronic draft notice "by the third business day of each month."
- *S/S portfolio:* funds available "by the 18th calendar day of the month (or the preceding business day …)"; draft notice by BD3. *S/S MBS standard cycle:* the 18th (preceding BD); pools with a 6th-day designated remittance date: the 5th; other designated dates: that date (preceding BD). *RPM cycle:* the designated remittance date (calendar shows RPM dates of the 6th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 12th, 15th, 17th **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — graphic calendar]**). *MBS Express:* unscheduled principal (payoffs, curtailments, repurchases, removals) drafted "on the fourth business day of the month after they were collected"; scheduled P&I on the 18th.
- *Guaranty fees:* available on the **7th calendar day** (preceding BD) — see 5.5. *Upfront commitment/pair-off fees:* drafted the business day after the request.
- *CRS:* bank instructions "by 8 p.m. ET, one day prior to the effective date"; remit "at any time up until Fannie Mae's cut-off time at 4 p.m. ET"; each remittance type code linked to a single drafting account (the P&I custodial account for that remittance type or a consolidated account); special remittances are reported via CRS with the Fannie Mae loan number.
- *Payoff proceeds:* A/A > $2,500 "immediately," ≤ $2,500 on the regular schedule, amount = UPB + interest "up to but not including, the payoff date" + FHA charges + prepayment premium; S/A "by the 20th day of the month following the month in which they were received" = Fannie Mae's share of UPB + one-half month's interest at the net certificate yield/PTR + prepayment premium; S/S on the 18th of the following month (or RPM early date, or BD4 for MBS Express) = scheduled UPB + a full month's interest at PTR (+ prepayment premium for MBS), and "the servicer must use its own funds to cover the difference between the interest Fannie Mae is due and the interest collected from the borrower when a mortgage loan is paid off before the end of the month" — the S/S payoff month exception: no full-month interest if the liquidation is "processed on the first business day and reported to Fannie Mae by the second business day."
- *Short sale proceeds (portfolio, PFP, special servicing MBS):* CRS special remittance **code 357** (never 001); borrower cash contribution **code 324**; "within two business days of the servicer's receipt of the net sale proceeds, but no later than three business days after the short sale." *Third-party sale proceeds:* all proceeds up to total indebtedness (UPB + interest at PTR from LPI due date to the later of liquidation or settlement date, or the restricted resale price); never surplus proceeds. *Settlements:* deposit immediately, report in the current month, remit on the next remittance date.
- *Payoff date for S/S loans:* settlement/closing date when a closing agent handles the payoff; receipt date when the borrower pays directly.

**Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026) formulas** (Ch. 2, pp. 15–16, 21–22; Ch. 5): monthly interest to Fannie Mae = (prior month's actual UPB [A/A, S/A] or scheduled UPB [S/S]) × pass-through rate ÷ 12 × Fannie Mae's percentage interest; A/A prepaid installments × months prepaid; A/A biweekly = actual UPB × PTR ÷ 365 × 14 days; scheduled UPB for a loan due on the 1st = ending actual UPB − (installment − ending actual UPB × note rate ÷ 12), repeated per delinquent month plus one; prepaid two or more months uses reverse amortization (interest factor to 9 decimals); S/A delinquent interest advanced for the LPI month plus each successive month through the **third** month, recovered in the fourth month by reporting **negative three months' interest** on the LAR 96, with the fourth month reimbursed after liquidation (p. 26–27 example); payoff interest A/A = full months at ÷12 plus days at ÷365 from the LPI date up to but not including the payoff date, S/A = ÷24 (half month), S/S = ÷12 (full month); shortage/surplus = cumulative (cash remitted − P&I reported) for portfolio A/A loans, reconciled monthly on Schedule 3 (Form 472) with Forms 473/473A (portfolio) or Form 512 (S/S MBS/MRS); unreconciled shortages are remitted "immediately"; a surplus unreconciled after **90 days** may be zeroed by Fannie Mae. DSI loans accrue daily on a 365-day year.

**A1-4.2-01 (12/21/2022):** late remittance fee = late amount × days late × (WSJ prime + 3%), minimum $250 first instance, $500 second, $1,000 subsequent within a year.

**CRS User Guide (Mar. 22, 2025):** drafting instructions entered before 8:00 p.m. ET default to next-BD effective, after 8:00 p.m. to two BD, up to six months ahead; draft requests before 4:00 p.m. ET settle next BD, after 4:00 p.m. in two BD; CRS processes requests at 4:00 p.m. ET one BD before settlement (deletable until then); settlement dates up to 20 BD ahead; maximum draft $99,999,999.99, positive only; batch import `.txt`, ≤100 KB or ≤1,000 transactions, one record per line: pos 1–9 LenderID, 10–13 Remittance Code (left-justified, space-padded), 14–28 Draft Amount (`$` and commas allowed), 29–38 Fannie Mae loan number (required for code 028/029 and 3xx codes), 39–48 settlement date mm/dd/yyyy (blank = next BD); failed lines reported individually. **CRS Remittance Codes (Oct. 15, 2025):** 001 A/A P&I cash remittance; 002 Fannie Mae-initiated S/A P&I draft; 003 S/S MRS; 004 S/S RPM; 005 S/S MBS Express; 106 excess servicing fee strip; 110 MBS P&I DSI & A/A biweekly (consolidated); 208 S/S cash DelMod/PD P&I advance reimbursement; 309 make-whole/settlement proceeds; 310 HUD-1 REO sales proceeds; 311 third-party foreclosure sale proceeds; 314 borrower redemption proceeds; 315 REO repurchase proceeds; 317 liquidated-loan escrow balance proceeds; 318 hazard premium refund; 323 additional interest curtailment; 324 loss-mitigation mortgagor contribution; 336 servicer MI premium refund; 351 third-party foreclosure sale curtailment; 352 payoff/repurchase advance recoverable; 353 reinstated advance recoverable; 357 short-sale workout proceeds; 571 REO-related servicer disbursements (codes 309–337, 350–359, 370 require the loan number).

**LL-2026-05 / Reference Guide v1.0 / FAQ v1.6 (future A/A auto-draft):** Fannie Mae "will automatically initiate a draft of the P&I remittance amount from the servicer's custodial account two business days after successfully processing payment events"; pre-draft notification "no later than the next business day after the event is successfully processed" (always one business day to review); Federal Reserve holidays shift the draft; eliminates Schedule 3/Form 472 and settles outstanding shortage/surplus at conversion; "no remitting or draft date changes for any other remittance types." Draft data via the Loan-Level Draft Notifications API (Fannie Mae Connect Insights & Reporting).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) "A/A auto-draft 2 business days after event processing (LL-2025-02)" is **not in force** — it is the last LL-2026-05 phase (no published date); today A/A is servicer-initiated through CRS under the $2,500/monthly/BD1 rule (48-hour auto-draft only for detailed-reporting loans); (2) "S/S per type" = 18th for portfolio and MBS standard cycle, BD4 for MBS Express unscheduled principal, RPM designated dates, 5th for 6th-day pools; (3) guaranty fees have their own 7th-day draft; (4) the row omits special remittances (CRS 3xx codes) and the shortage/surplus regime; (5) LL-2025-02 is superseded by LL-2026-05.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `investor_events` accepted (5.1) — each accepted payment/curtailment/reversal/removal event creates or adjusts `remittance_calculations` rows.
- Schedules on `fannie_et`: BD3 draft-notice reconciliation; CD18/CD20/BD4/RPM/CD7 draft-date funding checks (T−1 16:00 ET); daily 15:00 ET A/A `$2,500` sweep; BD1 09:00 ET prior-month catch-up; period close BD2.
- Inbound: Loan-Level Draft Notifications API pulls (daily), Fannie Mae Connect Remittance Detail – P&I (BD3), Remittance Detail – Cash Adjustments, custodial bank statements (daily), CRS Draft Request Report (human pull when needed), LSDU Cash Position (daily).
- Loan events: `payoff.funds.received`, `shortsale.proceeds.received`, `tps.proceeds.received`, `settlement.received`, `repurchase.approved`, `reclass.purchase_advice.received`, `sda.entered/exited` (5.4).

#### Data model
- `remittance_calculations`: `loan_id`, `activity_period`, `remittance_type` ∈ {AA, SA, SS}, `cycle` ∈ {standard, rpm_<dd>, mbs_express, mrs}, `prior_actual_upb_cents`, `prior_scheduled_upb_cents`, `ptr` (decimal 9,6), `note_rate`, `participation_pct`, `interest_due_cents` (Fannie Mae's), `principal_due_cents`, `servicing_fee_cents`, `excess_servicing_cents`, `gfee_cents`, `collected_interest_cents`, `collected_principal_cents`, `advance_cents` (servicer delinquency advance component), `sda_flag`, `basis` ∈ {contractual, curtailment, payoff, repurchase, liquidation, none}, `computed_at`, `rule_set_version`.
- `remittances` (baseline): `id`, `servicer_number`, `remittance_type`, `remittance_code` (CRS code), `custodial_account_id`, `kind` ∈ {pi_scheduled, pi_actual, payoff, curtailment, repurchase, gfee, special, tps_proceeds, short_sale, reo_proceeds, settlement}, `initiator` ∈ {fnma, servicer}, `amount_expected_cents`, `amount_notified_cents`, `amount_drafted_cents`, `draft_date`, `settlement_date`, `crs_batch_id`, `status` ∈ {computed, funded, instructed, notified, drafted, matched, variance, failed, cancelled}, `loan_id?`, `variance_cents`, `variance_reason`.
- `crs_batches`: `id`, `document_id` (the .txt), `line_count`, `total_cents`, `prepared_at`, `portal_task_id`, `uploaded_at`, `crs_result` (jsonb: per-line accept/reject), `status`.
- `draft_notifications`: `source` ∈ {api, connect_report}, `filing_date`, `draft_type`, `servicer_number`, `remittance_code`, `draft_date`, `amount_cents`, `loan_level` (jsonb rows), `document_id`.
- `cash_positions` (LSDU snapshot): `servicer_number`, `remittance_type`, `period`, `pi_applied_cents`, `cash_received_cents`, `adjustments_cents`, `draft_cents`, `outstanding_fm_pi_receivable_cents`, `as_of`.
- `shortage_surplus` (portfolio A/A until auto-draft): `servicer_number`, `period`, `opening_cents`, `remitted_cents`, `reported_pi_cents`, `closing_cents`, `explained_items` (jsonb), `form_472_document_id`, `surplus_first_seen_at`.
- Ledger accounts added (per custodial account): `custodial_pi_cash`, `fnma_remittance_payable`, `servicer_advance_receivable` (baseline) plus `servicing_fee_income` and `gfee_payable` (corporate books), `fnma_pi_receivable_sda` (memo of Fannie Mae's outstanding P&I receivable during SDA).

#### State machine
`remittances.status`: `computed` → `funded` (custodial balance covers it by T−1 16:00 ET; for S/S and S/A delinquent loans this may include a corporate advance transfer) → `instructed` (servicer-initiated: CRS batch uploaded and accepted) or `notified` (Fannie Mae-initiated: draft notification received and matched to expected within tolerance) → `drafted` (bank statement shows the ACH debit on settlement date) → `matched` (amount = expected, ledger posted) | `variance` (difference ≠ 0 → triage; resolves to `matched` after adjustment or explanation) ; `failed` (NSF/return, rejected line) → re-instruct; `cancelled`. Transitions to `drafted` come only from the bank feed; to `notified` only from Fannie Mae data.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Rate stack per loan** (`loan_terms`, effective-dated): `note_rate`; `servicing_fee_bps` (retained); `gfee_bps` (MBS; drafted separately, 5.5); `excess_servicing_bps` (strip sold to Fannie Mae; remitted, code 106) ; `ptr = note_rate − servicing_fee − gfee − excess_servicing` for MBS S/S; for portfolio loans `ptr` = the loan's stated pass-through/net yield from the purchase advice. `participation_pct` (Fannie Mae's percentage interest) multiplies every amount. All rate math in `decimal.js` (precision 20); money rounded half-up to cents at each named line item; drafts summed from rounded loan-level items.
2. **Remittance calculator by type** (all per `activity_period`):
   - **A/A:** interest_due = collected interest at note rate − servicing fee retained = prior actual UPB × PTR ÷ 12 × participation, only when a contractual payment posts (× months prepaid); principal_due = principal collected × participation (curtailments included); payoff = UPB + interest from LPI up to but not including the payoff date (full months ÷12, days ÷365) + prepayment premium. Nothing is advanced.
   - **S/A:** interest_due = prior actual UPB × PTR ÷ 12 × participation every month whether or not collected, through month 3 of delinquency (portfolio); month 4 → report **−3 months** of advanced interest (recovery) and stop advancing; the 4th month's advance is reimbursed at liquidation; principal_due only when collected; payoff = share of UPB + half month's interest (÷24) + premium; drafted on CD20 from prior-month activity.
   - **S/S:** interest_due = prior **scheduled** UPB × PTR ÷ 12 × participation; principal_due = scheduled principal (installment − scheduled UPB × note ÷ 12) × participation, plus curtailments as reported; whether or not collected until the 4th consecutive delinquent month (special servicing/portfolio S/S) → SDA (5.4); payoff = scheduled UPB + full month's interest at PTR (servicer funds the shortfall vs. interest actually collected) unless processed on BD1 and reported by BD2; drafted CD18 (standard) / BD4 unscheduled (MBS Express) / RPM date.
3. **Worked example — S/S with delinquency advances (loan from 5.1: $250,000, note 6.500%, PTR 6.000%, P&I $1,580.17, 100%).** Missed installments due Nov 1, Dec 1, Jan 1, Feb 1. Fannie Mae's scheduled amounts (prior scheduled UPB × PTR ÷ 12; scheduled principal): Nov-activity draft (Dec 18) interest $1,250.00 + principal $226.00 = $1,476.00; Dec (Jan 18, using scheduled UPB $249,774.00) $1,248.87 + $227.23 = $1,476.10; Jan (Feb 18, $249,546.77) $1,247.73 + $228.46 = $1,476.19; Feb (Mar 18, $249,318.31) $1,246.59 + $229.70 = $1,476.29. Because nothing was collected, each draft is funded by a corporate advance: Dr `servicer_advance_receivable` / Cr `custodial_pi_cash` (transfer in), then Dr `fnma_remittance_payable` / Cr `custodial_pi_cash` on the draft. Cumulative servicer advances after four drafts = **$5,904.58**; the servicer has also forgone four months of servicing fee (≈$52.08 each) which is not advanced. On BD2 of the month in which the loan is four consecutive months delinquent Fannie Mae sets Stop Advance; the March-activity draft (Apr 18) shows expected P&I $1,476.39 offset by a Stop Advance credit of the same amount → net $0 (5.4). Guaranty fees ($52.08 → $51.94/month on the 7th) are drafted through month 4 and stop under Guaranty Fee Relief/SVC-2026-02 thereafter (5.5).
4. **Worked example — S/A ($199,500.00 UPB, PTR 6.000%, 100%).** Months 1–3 delinquent: LAR interest +$997.50 each (advanced from corporate funds, drafted CD20 of the following month); month 4 LAR interest = **−$2,992.50** (three months recovered through a reduced draft); no principal reported until collected. If the loan then reinstates in month 6 with six installments, interest reported = 199,500 × 0.06 ÷ 12 × 6 = $5,985.00 less nothing already recovered — the engine reports prior UPB × PTR ÷ 12 × months from prior LPI through period end (IRM 4-07) so Fannie Mae ends the period whole; principal reported = actual principal collected.
5. **Worked example — A/A with future auto-draft (Reference Guide numbers):** UPB $400,000.00, note 6.875%, PTR 6.625%, P&I $2,627.72, contractual payment processed Monday Nov 23, 2026 → interest at note rate $2,291.67, principal $336.05, Fannie Mae interest at PTR **$2,208.33**, servicing fee retained $83.34, P&I remittance **$2,544.38**; event reported by Tue Nov 24 03:00 ET; pre-draft notification Tue Nov 24; draft Wed Nov 25; custodial funding gate Tue Nov 24 16:00 ET. Under today's rules the same $2,544.38 is instead accumulated with other A/A collections and a CRS code-001 request is created the day the net total exceeds $2,500 (here, that same day), settling next BD. Processed Wed Nov 25 → report Mon Nov 30 03:00 (Nov 26–27 Fannie Mae holidays), draft Tue Dec 1 **[Fannie Mae-only holiday treatment PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.
6. **Payoff interest by type ($199,500.00, PTR 6.000%, LPI Sept 1, payoff funds received Oct 16):** A/A = one month ($997.50) + 15 days × $32.7945 = **$1,489.42**; S/A = **$498.75**; S/S = **$997.50** — the borrower paid interest through Oct 15 at the note rate (Section 16.1), so for S/S the servicer funds the gap between a full month at PTR and the collected PTR-equivalent interest.
7. **Funding rule.** For every Fannie Mae-initiated draft, at T−1 16:00 ET the `custodial-recon` agent compares expected draft (Σ `remittance_calculations` for the code, adjusted by draft notification) with the custodial account's available balance from the bank feed; a shortfall triggers a corporate-to-custodial transfer command (advance) with ledger entries and a `remittances.funded` transition; never a draft against escrow funds.
8. **Draft notification reconciliation (BD3 and daily pre-draft).** Match Fannie Mae's loan-level draft rows to `remittance_calculations` by Fannie Mae loan number; tolerance $0.00 for S/S and S/A (differences mean a reporting error or an SDA credit/recovery adjustment); classify each variance (SDA credit, SDA recovery, reclass reimbursement code 208, PTR mismatch, LPI mismatch, Fannie Mae projection) and either correct reporting before the draft (if still inside the correction window) or accept and book the adjustment with an explanation. Post-draft, the bank debit is matched to `remittances` by amount/date/originator; the ledger posts Dr `fnma_remittance_payable` Cr `custodial_pi_cash`; any unmatched debit is a sev-1 alert.
9. **Shortage/surplus (portfolio A/A until auto-draft).** Monthly: closing = opening + cash remitted − P&I reported (from accepted LARs); explain to zero using known timing items (BD1 catch-up, payoff-in-transit, reversals); prepare Schedule 3 (Form 472) + Forms 473/473A (portfolio) or Form 512 (S/S MBS/MRS) as Excel artifacts for Section 6.3; unexplained shortage → immediate CRS 001 remittance; surplus → resolve within 90 days.
10. **Servicing-fee retention and excess strip.** Servicing fee = collected interest − PTR interest − g-fee (MBS) is swept from custodial to corporate only after the related P&I is drafted/remitted (never pre-draft); excess servicing strip (code 106) is remitted on Fannie Mae's draft **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — IRM Exhibit 5 formula not extracted; confirm at onboarding]**.
11. **Compensatory-fee exposure calculator:** on any late draft, compute amount × days × (prime + 3%)/365 vs. the $250/$500/$1,000 minimum ladder and record the instance count in `compfee_instances` for the officer's dashboard.

#### Integrations
- **`fnma-crs` (servicer-initiated remittances) — portal/file only.** The adapter produces the batch `.txt` (positions 1–9/10–13/14–28/29–38/39–48; ≤1,000 lines, ≤100 KB; split into multiple files by servicer number and settlement date) and a manifest; upload is a `human_portal_task` (`fnma_portal_operator`) with a 16:00 ET cut-off SLA; the operator attaches the CRS confirmation (Draft Request Report detail export) which the adapter parses to mark lines `instructed`; failed lines are re-batched. Drafting-instruction changes (bank account per code) are also portal tasks (before 20:00 ET the BD prior). No API exists.
- **Fannie Mae-initiated drafts (S/S, S/A, MBS Express, RPM, g-fee, future A/A):** no outbound action; inbound **Loan-Level Draft Notifications API** (`fnma-connect`; OAuth ROPC; returns loan/commitment/seller-level details by filing date and draft type) and, as fallback, Fannie Mae Connect "MBS P&I Draft Notifications," "Cash Remittance System Draft Notifications," "Remittance Detail – P&I," "Remittance Detail – Cash Adjustments" (human pull if no API coverage).
- **`fnma-lsdu` Cash Position** (P&I applied, cash received, adjustments, drafts; A/A shows current and prior period; downloads CSV/XLSX) — via the Master Servicing Loan Position API where exposed **[UNVERIFIED]**, else UI export by `fnma_portal_operator` on BD3 and pre-draft.
- **`custodial-bank`** BAI2/camt.053 prior-day and intra-day balances; ACH debit detail with Fannie Mae originator ID — Section 6.
- **Sandbox/test:** CRS has no test region; contract tests validate the batch codec against the user-guide layout and against a captured CRS rejection sample; draft-notification parsing tested with fixture reports.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `remittances`, `crs_batches` (file + manifest + confirmation), `draft_notifications`, `cash_positions`, `shortage_surplus` and the monthly Forms 472/473/473A/512 Excel artifacts (inputs to Section 6.3 Form 496).
- Ledger postings per remittance: A/A payment applied → Dr `custodial_pi_cash` Cr `fnma_remittance_payable` (Fannie Mae share) / Cr `servicing_fee_income` (fee); draft → Dr `fnma_remittance_payable` Cr `custodial_pi_cash`; advance → Dr `servicer_advance_receivable` Cr corporate cash / Dr `custodial_pi_cash` Cr transfer clearing; advance recovery when borrower pays or Fannie Mae reimburses → reverse.
- Investor events: none (reporting is 5.1); memo `remittance.drafted`/`remittance.variance` loan events.
- No borrower notices.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`custodial-recon` agent owns funding and reconciliation; `investor-reporting` agent owns the calculations and CRS batch preparation. Tools: `computeRemittance(loan, period)`, `buildCrsBatch`, `openPortalTask`, `pullDraftNotifications`, `matchBankDebits`, `explainVariance`, `postLedger` (through commands only), `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{remittance_id, expected, notified, drafted, variance, classification ∈ {sda_credit, sda_recovery, reclass_reimb, ptr_mismatch, lpi_mismatch, fnma_projection, our_error, unknown}, action, confidence}`. Guardrails: cannot move money between custodial and corporate accounts except through the `advance`/`fee_sweep` commands with dual-control thresholds (single transfer > $250,000 or daily > $1,000,000 requires `officer` approval — internal control, not Fannie Mae rule); cannot cancel a CRS request after 16:00 ET T−1; never uses escrow (T&I) funds for P&I drafts; variance > $500 per loan or > $5,000 per draft code with `unknown` classification → `officer`. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` for every CRS upload/instruction and for Connect pulls; `officer` for funding shortfalls the corporate facility cannot cover and for any compensatory-fee instance. Toggle-off: same calculations run; humans approve batches in `ops-console`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Draft returned NSF** — immediate sev-1, re-fund and request Fannie Mae re-draft (Investor Reporting Representative), compensatory-fee exposure logged.
- **Reversal after remittance (A/A):** next remittance nets the negative; if the period closed, report backward LPI movement (5.1) and let the draft notification carry the credit.
- **Reclassification mid-period:** remittance type flips to A/A on the 1st of the reclass month; scheduled draft on the 18th no longer includes the loan; Fannie Mae reimburses outstanding delinquency advances (code 208 / purchase advice) — match the credit.
- **Payoff on BD1 reported by BD2 (S/S):** no full-month interest — the calculator must honour the exception.
- **Transfer-in/out:** transferor remits through the transfer date's period; transferee's first draft is the following cycle; custodial accounts and CRS instructions must exist before the first draft date after transfer.
- **Bankruptcy cramdown (A/A):** unsecured portion remitted per the Bankruptcy Cramdown FAQ via code 001 with Fannie Mae guidance.
- **SCRA:** MBS interest continues at PTR; Fannie Mae disburses the shortfall two days before month-end into the designated custodial account (F-1-19); the engine expects and matches that credit.
- **Disaster/forbearance:** S/S advances continue until four consecutive months delinquent (LL-2026-01 forbearance does not stop advances).
- **Federal Reserve holiday on draft date:** settlement moves to the next BD; funding gate uses the earlier of the two calendars.
- **Fannie Mae draft notification late (after BD3):** fund from `remittance_calculations` alone and reconcile post-draft.
- **Duplicate CRS request:** batch builder blocks a second 001 request for the same servicer number/settlement date unless the first is `failed`; operator confirmation screenshot required.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Who funds delinquency advances** (partner vs Supermortgage corporate facility) — default: partner funds via a committed advance line; Supermortgage executes transfers under dual control; receivable sits on the partner's books.
2. **CRS operator cadence** — default: one daily 15:00 ET batch per servicer number plus an on-demand batch for payoffs > $2,500 and special remittances; consider Fannie Mae's consolidated draft codes to reduce uploads.
3. **A/A summary vs detailed reporting election** — default: summary reporting (fewer LARs, CRS-initiated remits) until the payment-event phase; detailed only where required (DSI, A/A biweekly).
4. **Servicing-fee sweep timing** — default: weekly, post-draft, only for periods with matched drafts.
5. **Draft-notification API coverage** — confirm at onboarding which Connect reports the Loan-Level Draft Notifications API returns; default plan assumes UI pull fallback on BD3.
