### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-11)

**Boarding standard — MISMO Loan Boarding Data Segment (LBDS) of the Industry Transfer of Servicing Dataset (ITSD) [VERIFIED].** "MISMO published the Loan Boarding Data Segment of the Industry Transfer of Servicing Dataset (ITSD) as Candidate Recommendation status on October 2, 2025." "The Loan Boarding Data Segment (LBDS) of the ITSD is a comprehensive list of MISMO data points and enumerations to assist the industry in establishing a baseline standard for the boarding of newly originated residential mortgage loans." "The purpose of the dataset is to improve transparency and efficiency in data exchanges for borrowers, lenders, and servicers." The specification is based on the MISMO Version 3.6 Reference Model. The data-point spreadsheet itself is a MISMO member resource **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — path names below use the 3.4/3.6 container names already used by 1.1's `boarding_staging.canonical_path`; confirm against the LBDS workbook at build]**.

**No RESPA transfer notice at boarding — Reg X §1024.2(b) (eCFR current as of 9/09/2026) and §1024.33(b) (current as of 9/08/2026) [VERIFIED].** "Servicing means receiving any scheduled periodic payments from a borrower pursuant to the terms of any federally related mortgage loan, including amounts for escrow accounts under section 10 of RESPA, and making payments to the owner of the loan or other third parties." "Servicer means a person responsible for the servicing of a federally related mortgage loan (including the person who makes or holds such loan if such person also services the loan)." §1024.33(b)(1): "Each transferor servicer and transferee servicer of any mortgage loan shall provide to the borrower a notice of transfer for any assignment, sale, or transfer of the servicing of the mortgage loan." (b)(2)(i) excludes, "if there is no change in the payee, address to which payment must be delivered, account number, or amount of payment due": "A transfer between affiliates; A transfer that results from mergers or acquisitions of servicers or subservicers; A transfer that occurs between master servicers without changing the subservicer." (b)(3)(iii): "Notices of transfer provided at settlement by the transferor servicer and transferee servicer, whether as separate notices or as a combined notice, satisfy the timing requirements." (00a-fed §6.3.) "Effective date of transfer" = "the date on which the mortgage payment of a borrower is first due to the transferee servicer" (12 U.S.C. 2605(i)(1); 1.3). Reading: because SM receives the first and every scheduled periodic payment, nobody "transfers" servicing to SM — the partner never services; the §1024.33(b) notice attaches only if the payee/payment address later changes (then 1.3 applies). The at-settlement safe harbor (b)(3)(iii) is available as a belt-and-braces option (open question 1). §1024.5(b)(7): the whole-loan sale to Fannie Mae is a secondary-market transaction outside RESPA except §6/Subpart C (00a-fed §6.7).

**Servicing file and records — Reg X §1024.38(c) (current as of 9/08/2026) [VERIFIED].** (c)(1): "A servicer shall retain records that document actions taken with respect to a borrower's mortgage loan account until one year after the date a mortgage loan is discharged or servicing of a mortgage loan is transferred by the servicer to a transferee servicer." (c)(2): "A servicer shall maintain the following documents and data on each mortgage loan account serviced by the servicer in a manner that facilitates compiling such documents and data into a servicing file within five days: (i) A schedule of all transactions credited or debited to the mortgage loan account, including any escrow account as defined in § 1024.17(b) and any suspense account; (ii) A copy of the security instrument that establishes the lien securing the mortgage loan; (iii) Any notes created by servicer personnel reflecting communications with the borrower about the mortgage loan account; (iv) To the extent applicable, a report of the data fields relating to the borrower's mortgage loan account created by the servicer's electronic systems in connection with servicing practices; (v) Copies of any information or documents provided by the borrower to the servicer in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 1024.35 or § 1024.41." Selling Guide A2-4.1-02 (12/19/2017) [VERIFIED 2026-09-11]: "All records related to loans (including all data and materials representing, based on, or compiled from such records) sold to or serviced for Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property"; "If the seller does not service the loan, it must transfer the loan file to the servicer"; the servicer and responsible party "must keep all of the individual loan records and all servicing records for the time it serviced the loan"; "After a loan is liquidated, the servicer must keep the individual loan records for at least four years, unless the local jurisdiction requires longer retention"; the liquidation clock "is measured from the date of the loan payoff or the date that any applicable claim proceeds are received, whichever is later"; records must "continue to be obtainable and readable in the future." Servicing Guide A2-5-01 (02/14/2024) incorporates it (1.1). Retention classes per 00a-fed §15: LE `regz_le_3y` (§1026.25(c)(1)(i)), CD `regz_cd_5y` ((c)(1)(ii)), ATR `regz_atr_3y` ((c)(3)), Reg B `regb_25m` (§1002.12(b)(1)), HMDA `hmda_3y` (§1003.5), AfBA `respa_afba_5y` (§1024.15(d)), escrow (no §1024.17 period — §1024.38(c)(1)), SFHDF `fdpa_life_of_loan`, E-SIGN `esign_consent_life` (7001(e) "capable of being retained and accurately reproduced").

**Fair-lending data elements — Servicing Guide F-1-11 (05/13/2026) [VERIFIED].** The transferor must deliver: "For mortgage loans originated on or after March 1, 2023, the following fair lending data elements in a format such that they are queryable for each mortgage loan if obtained during the origination process: race of borrower(s), ethnicity of borrower(s), age of borrower(s), gender of borrower(s), and preferred language of borrower(s)." Boarding therefore copies these from `applicant_demographics` (21.1; restricted, access-logged) into the servicing `borrower_fair_lending` restricted table (1.1) so they are queryable from day one. HMDA Appendix B forbids inferring demographics for telephone/internet applications (00a-fed §4.5) — boarding never derives them.

**First payment date and note terms — Selling Guide C2-2-01 (08/07/2018) [VERIFIED via 00a-fnma §5.4].** "The first payment date must be no later than two months from the final disbursement date of the loan proceeds." Reg Z §1026.36(g) [VERIFIED via 00a-fed §12]: the organization's "name and NMLSR ID" and the individual loan originator's name/NMLSR ID must appear on "the credit application; the disclosures required by §1026.19(e) and (f); the note or loan contract; and the security instrument" — boarding captures both IDs from the signed note (26.1) for later document generation and complaint handling.

**First periodic statement — Reg Z §1026.41 (current as of 9/03/2026) [VERIFIED via 7.1].** (a): "A servicer ... shall provide the consumer, for each billing cycle, a periodic statement"; (b): "delivered or placed in the mail within a reasonably prompt time after the payment due date or the end of any courtesy period provided for the previous billing cycle"; comment 41(b)-1: "within four days of the close of the courtesy period of the previous billing cycle generally would be considered reasonably prompt." There is no "previous billing cycle" for the first payment; the rule attaches from the first cycle's close, so the *first* statement's timing is a design matter (rule 9). The small-servicer exemption can never apply (Fannie Mae is the assignee — 7.1). Servicing Guide D2-2-03 payment reminder by the 20th applies from the first cycle (7.1).

**E-SIGN scope — 15 U.S.C. 7001(c)(1) [VERIFIED via 00a-fed §8 and 7.4].** Consent is valid only if the consumer "has affirmatively consented to such use and has not withdrawn such consent" ((A)) after a "clear and conspicuous statement" informing the consumer, among other things, "whether the consent applies (I) only to the particular transaction which gave rise to the obligation to provide the record, or (II) to identified categories of records that may be provided or made available during the course of the parties' relationship" ((B)(ii)), and after consenting "in a manner that reasonably demonstrates that the consumer can access information in the electronic form that will be used to provide the information that is the subject of the consent" ((C)(ii)); (c)(6): "an oral communication or a recording of an oral communication shall not qualify as an electronic record." Reg X §1024.32(a)(1) and Reg Z §1026.17(a)(1)/§1026.41(c) allow electronic servicing disclosures "subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the E-Sign Act" (7.4). Consequence: an origination consent scoped only to `origination_disclosures` (20.3) does **not** cover statements, escrow statements or Reg X correspondence; boarding may treat a consent as covering servicing only when the origination disclosure named the servicing categories (7.4 classes) and both the partner and SM as providers, and the demonstration test used the same portal/PDF channel servicing will use.

**Fannie Mae boarding/QC expectations.** 1.1's DQ gate (`HF-001`…`HF-020`, `W-001`…`W-016`) encodes A2-7-03/F-1-11/Bulletin 2020-02 for transfers; for originations the same semantics apply with the *origination record* as the source of truth instead of a transferor tape (rules below). LL-2026-05 requires event reporting only once the loan is a Fannie Mae loan (30.1). Selling Guide A3-3-01 (02/07/2024): the partner "remain[s] fully responsible to Fannie Mae for functions that are outsourced to third parties" (00a-fnma §8) — SM's boarding data is the partner's delivery data (29.3 uses the same record).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. No §1024.33(b) notice is required at boarding because no *servicing* is transferred (§1024.2(b) "servicing" = receiving the scheduled payments — SM does from payment 1); the blueprint correctly frames "when a transfer notice is/is not required" but the (b)(2) exclusions are irrelevant here — the operative point is the definition, with (b)(3)(iii) available as a safe harbor.
2. The blueprint's "MISMO ITSD/LBDS" is right and stronger than it suggests: LBDS is explicitly "for the boarding of newly originated residential mortgage loans" (Candidate Recommendation Oct 2, 2025; MISMO 3.6).
3. "Board before the first statement cycle" is a policy deadline; the binding constraints are Reg Z's statement clock from the first cycle, D2-2-03's reminder by the 20th, C2-2-01's first-payment-date rule (already a funding gate, 26.3), and the LBDS/F-1-11 completeness a future transfer would need.
4. "Consents verified before e-delivery/AI voice" understates it: E-SIGN consent must be *category-scoped* to servicing records and cannot be oral (7001(c)(6)); TCPA voice consent (20.2) is a separate ledger.
5. §1024.38 is not a boarding deadline but a five-day compile standard; boarding must create the file skeleton (rule 8).
6. A2-4.1-02's origination-record retention is "for the time it serviced the loan" plus "at least four years" after liquidation (measured from payoff or claim proceeds) — the addendum's `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` is right; the citation is A2-4.1-02 via A2-5-01.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `loan.funded` (baseline; 26.3 — funding date, disbursement date, wire id, per-diem interest, interest credit, `rescission_expires_at`, funded amount) → boarding starts. In dry-funding states this is the disbursement date (fixture: Thu Nov 12, 2026); in wet states the closing date (purchase fixture: Wed Nov 18, 2026).
- Origination aggregate snapshot at funding: `applications` (+ `application_borrowers`, `applicant_demographics`, `application_properties`), `locks`, `loan_terms` (final note terms from 26.1's signed note data), `closing_documents`, `fundings`, `fee_items`/final CD (25.2), `escrow_analyses{type=initial, source=origination}` (30.3), `insurance_policies`/`flood_determinations` (24.5), `mi_certificates`/`hpa_disclosures` (24.6), `qm_determinations`/`hpml_determinations` (23.4), `enotes`/`recordings`/`custody_records` (26.2/26.4), `consents` (20.3/21.1), `disclosures` (all delivered origination notices), `documents`.
- `loan.purchased` (30.1) → investor update (not a re-boarding).
- Post-funding corrections: `closing.corrected_cd.issued` (25.2), `loan_terms.corrected` (26.1 re-sign/modification agreement) → new `loan_terms` version + `boarding.correction.applied`.
- Schedules: `SM_ORIG_BOARD_T1BD` sweep; nightly re-validation of `boarded_with_warnings` loans until warnings clear.

#### State machine

`loans.boarding_status`: `staged` (snapshot mapped at `loan.funded`) —(gate: zero open `OB-*` failures)→ `validated` —(opening ledger posted; consents/documents indexed; timers seeded)→ `boarded` (emits `loan.boarded`) —(any open `OW-*`)→ `boarded_with_warnings` —(warnings cleared or waived with reason; first-payment letter sent; vendor activations acked (30.4))→ `active`. `exception` (any `OB-*` failure): the `boarding` agent proposes corrections from the origination record (non-money fields) or routes money-field defects to the owning origination process (26.1 note terms, 25.2 CD, 30.3 escrow) — the loan cannot board until the source record is corrected; `SM_ORIG_BOARD_T1BD` keeps running. Terminal: `active` (servicing lifecycle) or `unwound` (funding reversed/rescinded — the loan never boards; 26.3). Transitions are performed by the `boarding` agent; a waiver of a hard rule requires `officer` (money) or the `boarding` lead (non-money, documented).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`boarding` agent (1.1's package in origination mode; tools: `snapshotOrigination`, `mapField`, `runValidation`, `proposeCorrection` (non-money fields only), `routeToOwner` (money fields → 26.1/25.2/30.3 agents), `allocateServicingLoanNumber`, `boardLoan`, `postOpeningEntries`, `indexDocuments`, `boardConsents`, `seedTimers`, `sendNotice`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: at `loan.funded` it snapshots the origination aggregate, maps it to LBDS paths, runs `OB-*`/`OW-*`, boards, posts the opening ledger, indexes documents with retention classes, evaluates consent scopes, seeds every timer in 30.4's table, sends the first-payment letter, opens the first statement cycle, and hands vendor activation to 30.4. Decision record: `{loan_id, application_id, funding_date, validations{rule_code, result, evidence_document_ids}, corrections[], consent_scope_decisions[{party_id, classes_granted, basis_disclosure_version}], ledger_entry_ids, timers_seeded[], letter_notice_id, rule_set_version, mapping_version, model_version, prompt_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: money fields (amount, rate, P&I, escrow deposit, prepaid interest, buydown funds) come only from the signed note/CD data hashes — the agent may never edit them; a hard-rule waiver needs `officer` (money) or the boarding lead (non-money) with a written reason; the agent may not infer demographics, consents or TCPA authority; it may not board a loan whose `rescission_expires_at` is in the future; it never contacts the borrower except through the first-payment letter and the 7.4 invitation. Escalations: `officer` (money waivers; boarding past `SM_ORIG_ACTIVE_BEFORE_FIRST_DUE_GATE`); `human_agent` if a borrower calls before boarding completes (`borrower-comms` reads `boarding_status`); origination agents (`title-closing`, `disclosure`, `escrow`) for source-record fixes. Borrower-facing: the letter and any AI voice/chat interaction start with the automation disclosure; outbound AI voice only with `tcpa_voice` consent. AI-off path: the ops console runs the same pipeline with identical rule codes and a manual release for the letter.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Funding reversed / rescission exercised after disbursement** (rare; 26.3): `unwound` — reverse opening entries, cancel timers, recall the letter if unsent; no servicing record survives except the audit trail.
- **Corrected CD after boarding** (25.2 30/60-day paths): escrow figures or prepaid interest change → new `loan_terms`/`escrow_analyses` version and reversing entries; the initial escrow statement is re-issued by 30.3 if the escrow payment changed.
- **First payment received before boarding completes** (early ACH or a borrower paying at closing): 2.2 suspense keyed by application id; applied as of receipt when boarded.
- **Wet-funding loan boarded on the closing date with an unrecorded, unregistered security instrument**: `OW-004`/`OW-008` — allowed; the 7-day MERS clock (26.4) and trailing-document timers run.
- **MIN registration rejected** (data mismatch): `OW-004` escalates to `post-closing`; boarding is not undone.
- **Borrower declines all electronic delivery**: every notice by mail; no `OW-002` (declining is not a defect); the invitation is omitted.
- **Co-borrower with a different mailing address / ACP participant**: separate addresses per party (`OW-011` review; 1.3 ACP rule).
- **Trust vesting / non-borrower title holder**: `parties` rows; statements to borrowers only; successor rules (4.4) apply later.
- **Temporary buydown**: `buydown_funds` liability boarded; 2.x applies the subsidy monthly; the 90-day buydown-step notice (7.2 `FNMA_C2_1_02_BUYDOWN_STEP_NOTICE_90`) is seeded (30.4).
- **Holdback escrow (24.3)**: `holdback_escrow` boarded with the completion timer; not an escrow line.
- **HPML with escrow waiver requested**: refused at origination (23.4/30.3); `OB-021` guarantees an active account.
- **Property state license missing**: `OB-020` — the loan should never have funded (31.1 gate); escalate `officer`; interim servicing by a licensed sub-servicer is a partner decision.
- **Origination consent captured through a different portal/PDF form than servicing will use**: scope limited to origination classes; re-enroll via 7.4.
- **Purchase never happens (loan repurchased/retained on warehouse)**: the loan stays `investor='partner_warehouse'`; servicing continues under the partner's rules; 27.1 aging timers escalate.
- **Duplicate `loan.funded` event**: idempotent (application id) — second event is ignored with a receipt.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **At-settlement §1024.33 notice as a belt-and-braces measure** — include a combined MS-2-style notice in the closing package (b)(3)(iii) even though no transfer occurs? Default: **no** — the CD contact table, LE servicing statement and the first-payment letter name SM as payee from payment 1; a "notice of transfer" where nothing transfers risks confusion. Revisit if a partner insists on being the initial payee (then 1.3 applies with an at-settlement combined notice).
2. **E-SIGN scope at origination** — single disclosure covering origination + servicing categories (default, 7001(c)(1)(B)(ii)(II)) vs separate servicing enrollment after boarding (7.4). Default: single disclosure with a separate servicing checkbox group and both providers named; counsel to confirm the joint-provider framing.
3. **Boarding SLA** — 1 servicer business day after funding (default) vs same-day; same-day is the target, the SLA is the breach line.
4. **First-statement lead time** — 15 calendar days before the first due date (default) vs 20; coordinate with the print vendor's SLA.
5. **First-payment letter branding and sender** — SM name with "servicing on behalf of [Partner]" (default; 1.3-Q1) vs partner-branded "serviced by Supermortgage".
6. **Origination interaction logs as servicing contact notes** — link only (default) vs copy into `contacts` (privacy minimization; §1024.38(c)(2)(iii) covers servicing communications).
