### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Sources verified:** Fannie Mae D2-3.3-01 Fannie Mae Short Sale (08/13/2025), D2-3.3-02 Fannie Mae Mortgage Release (08/13/2025), F-1-14 (05/10/2023), F-1-13 (title exists; content not re-verified), F-1-24 (09/11/2024), F-2-02, F-2-06, F-2-10, E-3.4-01 (07/14/2021), D2-3.4-01 Military Indulgence (06/13/2018), D2-3.4-04 Qualifying Mortgage Assumption (11/08/2017), F-1-21 status codes 15/17/44; 12 CFR 1024.41(c)(1) comment -3, (g)(3) comment 41(g)(3)-1; Cal. Civ. Code §2924.11 (foreclosure prevention alternatives incl. short sale with proof of funds).

**Short sale — eligibility (D2-3.3-01).** Evaluate by delinquency at evaluation: **current or <90 days delinquent** → complete BRP required, and if current/<60 days the payment must be in imminent default (D2-1-01) with a qualifying hardship: death, divorce, disability, or employment relocation **>50 miles one way** (principal residence), or PCS orders >50 miles (property is/was the principal residence); **90 days to 18 months delinquent** → complete BRP unless an exception applies (failed Flex Mod trial within 12 months; Flex Mod that went 60+ days delinquent within 12 months without reinstating; three or more prior modifications; forbearance with special credit reporting then 90+ days delinquent; non-investment property with classic FICO **≤620**, score ≤90 days old — lower of two/middle of three; lowest across borrowers); **>18 months delinquent** → no BRP; **Chapter 7 discharge** → no BRP (proof of discharge). Property must be **listed with a licensed agent** (not the borrower) with **active MLS status for at least 5 consecutive calendar days including a Saturday and Sunday** (or customary advertising where no MLS); the servicer refers agents to **HomePath for Short Sales** to register offers; contracts must be contingent on the mortgage holder's (and MI's) agreement and carry the required cancellation clause. New-mortgage check (BRP evaluations): review the credit report for a new mortgage in the 6 months before delinquency/evaluation — eligible only with the >50-mile relocation/new-employment/PCS hardships; otherwise submit to Fannie Mae. Valuation: order through Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system once eligible and interested; results typically within **10 calendar days**; valuation dated/refreshed **within 90 calendar days of approval** (F-1-14). Non-delegated cases (per F-1-24: borrower non-retirement reserves **>$50,000**, contribution refusals, new-mortgage non-qualifying hardship, fraud indicators, lien exceptions, closing-extension and deed-restriction waiver requests, relocation despite non-contribution, PCS without government assistance) require Fannie Mae's decision via the system.

**Short sale — timelines and terms.** Acknowledge an initial offer **within 5 business days**; provide the written response (approve/counter/decline) **within 30 calendar days** of a complete BRP and initial offer; decisions on revised offers **within 10 business days**; close **within 60 calendar days** of approval (extension only with Fannie Mae's written approval). **Cash contribution:** not evaluated if prohibited by law, if evaluated without a complete BRP, or for PCS servicemembers (>50 miles; principal residence); required if non-retirement cash reserves **>$10,000** or the housing expense-to-income ratio **≤40%** (F-1-14 ratio: housing expense incl. P&I, taxes, insurance, HOA, assessments, ground rent ÷ gross income excluding unemployment benefits/severance); initial request = **greater of 20% of non-retirement reserves or 4× the contractual PITI**, rounded to the nearest $100, not exceeding the deficiency; waived if **<$500**; current/<60-day borrowers refusing the minimum 20% → Fannie Mae approval; the servicer may request/negotiate less. Promissory notes as a contribution form are **not surfaced in the current D2-3.3-01 text retrieved [UNVERIFIED — design supports a note instrument behind flag `shortsale.promissory_note`]**. **Relocation assistance $7,500** (principal residence at the time of the Evaluation Notice; not if a cash contribution is required — unless Fannie Mae approves — or the borrower receives DLA/government relocation assistance; reduce by other third-party assistance; disbursed by the closing agent from proceeds; not to be renegotiated downward or redirected to lien releases). **Subordinate liens:** aggregate **≤$6,000** from proceeds for recorded mortgage liens only (no HOA/judgment/mechanic's liens), against written commitments to release the lien and the borrower and to waive deficiency without demanding more cash. **Allowable costs:** customary commission up to **6%** (negotiation fees come out of it), prorated taxes, transfer taxes/stamps, seller title/settlement charges, seller's attorney, pest inspection where customary, past-due HOA, customary buyer costs; prohibited: outsourcing fees, commissions to borrower/buyer, buyer points/origination. **Anti-fraud:** Short Sale Affidavit (**Form 191**) signed by all parties at closing; deed restriction — no conveyance within **30 days**, none above **120% of the price** until **90 days** after closing (waiver only for DoD HAP/employer relocation via Fannie Mae); no funds to borrower/buyer except the relocation incentive shown on the settlement statement. **Deficiency waiver** (Form 189 optional minimum) required for loans without MI or with delegated MI; non-delegated MI must agree in writing to waive acquisition rights and settle at the lesser of the percentage option or make-whole. **Closing:** review the settlement statement within **48 hours** for buyer/price/instructions/deed; remit proceeds and return escrow/buydown funds (C-3-01); report via SMDU (D2-4-02) and the Investor Reporting Manual; file the MI claim (E-4.5-01). Incentive: **$2,500 / $1,500 / $750** by delinquency ≤210 / 211–300 / >300 days (F-2-02).

**Mortgage Release — eligibility and terms (D2-3.3-02).** Same delinquency/BRP tiers and new-mortgage hardship rules as short sale; no prior listing required. **Clear and marketable title** must be conveyable (F-1-13 procedures; title policy optional, reimbursable per E-5-07); subordinate liens releasable within the **$6,000** aggregate (recorded mortgage liens only; other liens impeding closing need Fannie Mae's prior written approval); property **vacant and broom-swept** unless a transition option applies; interior inspection: a prior interior BPO dated **within 90 days** of approval may be used, otherwise order an interior inspection **within 60 days** of the borrower's acceptance confirming no environmental hazards, vacancy/security and habitability for transition options; personal property left behind with cash value ≥$500 needs Fannie Mae approval. **Exit options:** (1) immediate move-out; (2) **three-month transition option** — no-rent use-and-occupancy agreement; principal residence only; income verification not required; active bankruptcy permitted; (3) **twelve-month lease option** — market-rent lease; principal residence or investment property leased to a tenant using it as a principal residence; at least three payments made since origination/last modification; not 12+ payments past due when referred to Fannie Mae; verifiable income; no active bankruptcy or property/loan litigation; both transition options require a first-lien single-family property, not FHA/HUD/VA/RD, clear title and releasable subordinate liens. **Relocation assistance $7,500** with the same disqualifiers, reduced by other assistance and by remediation costs for borrower-caused damage/non-broom-swept condition; paid within **30 days** after the servicer accepts the executed deed (non-transition) or, for transition options, by Fannie Mae's property manager within 30 days after vacancy. **Cash contribution** rules identical to short sale. **Timelines:** obtain all required items (deed and personal property release — **Form 192** optional minimum; subordinate-lien agreements; MI approval if non-delegated; title verification; cash contribution; interior inspection report) **within 60 days** of the borrower's acceptance (extendable to **90 days** with weekly status updates); executed deed received **no later than 30 days before the foreclosure sale date** (or the estimated court-order docket date) — otherwise prior approval; submit the deed for recordation **within 5 business days** of accepting it; deficiency waiver (Form 189 optional minimum) after acceptance where required (no MI or delegated MI); lien release **within 30 business days** after acceptance and inspection confirmation of vacancy/security (unless a transition option applies). Reporting: final acceptance through the servicing solutions system per E-4.1-01 timing; REOgram review/confirmation within 1 business day (15.1/00b F5); HOA contact information. Incentive tiers as short sale. E-3.4-01: after a Mortgage Release acceptance, delay the next legal action **60 days** (+30 with extension); no Mortgage Release offers when a BRP arrives ≤37 days before a sale.

**Other D2-3.4 options.** **Military Indulgence (D2-3.4-01, F-1-19):** servicemembers who incurred the debt before active duty (federal or state-ordered; SCRA or state relief); 6% rate cap (13.9); late charges accrued after the call to active duty waived; forbearance/repayment/modification from D2-3 as relief; contact at least every three months; no foreclosure initiation/continuation during the relief period and the 12-month post-service stay (13.8); Form 180 Request for Military Indulgence, DMDC verification, Form 710 or equivalent; status code 32. **Qualifying Mortgage Assumption (D2-3.4-04, F-1-28, F-1-17):** delinquent loan with an enforceable due-on-sale clause; complete BRP from the existing borrower; transferee qualified under Fannie Mae underwriting; appraisal or interior BPO; MI written approval if applicable; Fannie Mae approval via the servicing solutions system (F-1-24) to forgo due-on-sale enforcement; MBS loans repurchased before completion; assumption (and release) agreement recorded per state law; assumption fee/expenses may be charged. D2-3.4-02 (second-lien Mortgage Release) and D2-3.4-03 (assignment to insurer/guarantor) are out of scope (first-lien conventional only).

**Reg X interplay.** A short-sale/DIL offer conditional on later third-party information (valuation/title) satisfies (c)(1) by offering the listing/marketing opportunity (comment 41(c)(1)-3); a borrower in the listing period is performing (comment 41(g)(3)-1) — non-performance can be found only after the listing period ends without an approved transaction (all parties approved, proof of funds/financing received); short-sale/DIL denials are not modification denials (no Reg X appeal; courtesy reconsideration 12.3). CA §2924.11: an approved short sale with proof of funds bars NOD/NOS/sale and requires rescission/cancellation; NY 419.7 grant-notice content applies to liquidation approvals.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** "Per Fannie Mae" conceals hard clocks: 5-business-day offer acknowledgment, 30-day decision, 10-business-day revised-offer decision, 60-day closing, 48-hour settlement review, 60/90-day DIL documentation window, 30-days-before-sale deed cut-off, 5-business-day deed recordation, 30-business-day lien release, 30-day relocation payment. "HomePath for Short Sales" remains the agent offer-registration reference. D2-3.4's Military Indulgence and QMA are separate options folded into this process for inventory purposes.

#### State machine
Short sale: `eligible` → `listing` (agent referral; MLS ≥5 days; valuation ordered) → `offer_received` → `offer_acknowledged` → `under_review` (SMDU delegated decision / non-delegated referral) → `approved` (approval letter; 60-day closing) | `countered` | `declined` → `closing_scheduled` → `closed` (settlement reviewed; proceeds remitted; MI claim; reporting) → `liquidated`; `approved` → `expired` (60 days without extension) → re-evaluate. Mortgage Release: `eligible` → `offered` → `accepted` (exit option chosen) → `documenting` (60/90-day window; inspection; title; liens; MI; contribution) → `deed_received` → `deed_recorded` (≤5 BD submission) → `released` (lien release ≤30 BD; relocation paid ≤30 days) → `reo_conveyed` (REOgram; 15.1). Denials → reviewer + notice; Fannie Mae declines → `NTC_FNMA_D2330x_FNMA_DECLINED` and foreclosure continuation per F-1-24.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Tiering and BRP:** compute `delinquency_tier` from `fnma_delinquency_status` at evaluation; apply the exception list; Chapter 7 discharge from `bankruptcy` cases (proof retained).
2. **Housing ratio and reserves** (F-1-14): ratio = (P&I + taxes + insurance + HOA + assessments + ground rent + escrow shortage) ÷ gross income (excluding unemployment/severance), 4-place decimal; reserves from Form 710 (verbally verified where the Guide allows).
3. **Contribution:** if required → initial request = max(20% × reserves, 4 × PITI) rounded to the nearest $100 (half-up), capped at the deficiency (UPB + arrears − net proceeds/value); waive if <$500; negotiate down within partner limits; refusal → Fannie Mae referral. **Worked example:** reserves $18,400 (>$10,000 ✓), PITI $2,100 → max($3,680, $8,400) = **$8,400** (already a multiple of $100); deficiency $41,000 → request $8,400; borrower offers $5,000 → within partner limit (≥50% of request) → accepted and recorded; relocation assistance then **not** payable (contribution required) unless Fannie Mae approves.
4. **Net proceeds:** price − allowable costs (commission ≤6%, prorations, transfer taxes, seller title/settlement, seller attorney, pest, past-due HOA, customary buyer costs, subordinate-lien payoffs ≤$6,000, relocation $7,500 if payable) → compared with SMDU's decision parameters (delegated approval within the system's acceptable range; otherwise referral) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — SMDU minimum-net mechanics gated]**.
5. **Relocation:** $7,500 − third-party assistance (not servicer-provided); DIL: − remediation cost estimate; disbursed by the closing agent (short sale) or servicer/Fannie Mae property manager (DIL).
6. **Deed restriction and affidavit:** approval letter and closing instructions carry the 30-day/120%-90-day restriction and Form 191 requirement; settlement statement review confirms no ineligible payments.
7. **Deficiency waiver:** required (no MI or delegated MI) at closing/after deed acceptance; language per Form 189 minimums; state law may require more (e.g., CA CCP §580e anti-deficiency for short sales — `jurisdiction_rules`) **[UNVERIFIED cite]**.
8. **DIL exit-option selection:** borrower choice subject to eligibility tests (rule list above); lease option requires income verification and the payment-history tests (≥3 payments since origination/mod; <12 payments past due at referral).
9. **Foreclosure interplay:** holds per the timer table; deed must be received ≥30 days before the sale; otherwise Fannie Mae prior approval; E-3.4-01 delays.
10. **Ledger at liquidation:** proceeds applied per 16.x liquidation posting (principal, interest to the paid-through date per investor rules, escrow reconciliation, advances recovery); deficiency written off per Fannie Mae; MI claim receivable (10.x); incentive receivable (15.2).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `lossmit-underwriter` sub-role `liquidation`, with `claims-reo` taking over at conveyance. Tools: `liquidation.case.*`, `ratio.compute`, `contribution.compute`, `valuation.order/get`, `title.order/get`, `inspection.order/get`, `smdu.case.submit/poll`, `offer.evaluate`, `notice.render_send`, `closing.instructions.send`, `settlement.review` (document AI on the CD/HUD-1), `erecording.submit`, `lien_release.request` (16.x), `mi.claim.prepare`, `reogram.watch`, `timers.*`, `attorney.instruct`.
- **Decision record:** `{case_id, tier, brp_required, exceptions, hardship, new_mortgage_check, ratio, reserves, contribution computation, relocation eligibility, liens, mi_path, valuation, offer analysis{price, costs, net, smdu parameters}, decision, fnma_referral?, holds, notices}`.
- **Guardrails:** contribution and net-proceeds math is code; no approval outside SMDU's decision/Fannie Mae approval; no deed acceptance without title verification and inspection; no relocation payment when a contribution is required (absent Fannie Mae approval); denials via `lossmit_reviewer`; settlement-statement anomalies (related-party buyer, undisclosed payments) → `qc-audit`/`officer` fraud review before funding.
- **Escalations:** `signing_officer` (deed acceptance/estoppel/lien release execution), `attorney` (deed preparation where required; sale postponements; litigation flags on lease-option eligibility), `fnma_portal_operator` (SMDU UI; REOgram confirmation), `officer` (fraud reports; deed-restriction waiver requests), `licensed_specialist` (state negotiation licensing), `human_agent` on request.
- **AI-off:** human liquidation specialists use the same calculators, SMDU adapter and templates.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Offer below SMDU parameters:** counter with the acceptable net; revised-offer clock 10 BD; escalate to Fannie Mae for exceptions.
- **Multiple offers / bidding:** evaluate the highest net; HomePath registration reference; anti-flip checks on buyer identity.
- **Subordinate lienholder demands >$6,000 or borrower cash:** refuse; Fannie Mae approval path; borrower may pay from own funds only if permitted and disclosed.
- **HOA super-lien states / HOA delinquencies:** not payable from the $6,000; past-due HOA as an allowable cost at closing (short sale); DIL requires resolution.
- **Sale scheduled inside 30 days for DIL:** Fannie Mae prior approval; counsel postponement.
- **Bankruptcy:** stay relief/trustee consent for sale/deed; Chapter 7 discharge tier; lease option barred in active bankruptcy.
- **Successor in interest / probate:** confirmed successor may pursue; estate authority documents for deeds.
- **SCRA:** PCS >50 miles hardship; no contribution; relocation payable absent government assistance.
- **Disaster/insured loss:** claim proceeds handling before conveyance (9.x).
- **Transfer-out mid-case:** all artifacts and holds shipped; transferee honors approvals (CA §2924.11(e)).
- **Closing fails after approval:** re-market; extension request; holds re-evaluated.
- **Property not broom-swept/damaged at DIL inspection:** relocation reduced by remediation estimate; Fannie Mae approval for personal property ≥$500.

- **12.9-T1:** Given a complete BRP and an initial offer received 2026-10-05 (loan 8 months delinquent), when processed, then the acknowledgment is sent by 2026-10-12 (5 BD), the valuation is ordered on eligibility, and the approval/counter/decline is sent by 2026-11-04 (30 days).

- **12.9-T4 (listing rule):** MLS active 4 days → offer held; 5 days incl. weekend → review proceeds.
- **12.9-T5 (closing clock):** approval 2026-11-04 → close by 2027-01-03; funds 2027-01-10 without extension → approval expired, case re-evaluated.
- **12.9-T6 (settlement review):** CD shows a $2,000 payment to the borrower beyond the $7,500 incentive → funding blocked; fraud review.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Promissory-note contributions.** Default: off (`shortsale.promissory_note=false`) until confirmed in the current Guide/SMDU case fields.
2. **Contribution negotiation floor.** Default: accept ≥50% of the initial request without Fannie Mae referral; below that, refer.
3. **DIL transition options.** Default: offer both transition options where eligible (Fannie Mae property-manager coordination via 15.1).
4. **Title policy on DIL.** Default: order a title search always; a policy only when the search shows risk (reimbursable per E-5-07).
5. **State anti-deficiency overlays** (e.g., CA CCP §580e). Default: counsel-provided matrix in `jurisdiction_rules.deficiency`.
