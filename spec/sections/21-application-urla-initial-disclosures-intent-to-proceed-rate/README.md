## Overview

**Scope.** §21 turns a qualified lead into a dated, disclosed, priced and dispositioned application. It owns the three application dates (Reg B receipt, TRID six-item receipt, HMDA application date), the conversational Form 1003 (1/2021)/ULAD MISMO 3.4 intake with the SCIF, demographic and joint-intent captures and the MLO-of-record touchpoint (21.1); the Loan Estimate — fee assembly, tolerance classes, APR/TIP/In-5-Years, the H-24 render, delivery/receipt evidence, the 3-general-business-day delivery clock and the 7-specific-business-day pre-consummation gate (21.2); the companion early disclosures — homeownership counseling list, Your Home Loan Toolkit, AfBA, Reg B appraisal notice (and the HPML statement it satisfies), FCRA §609(g)/Reg V H-3 score notices, ARM program disclosure and CHARM booklet, GLBA initial privacy notice, the retired RESPA Servicing Disclosure Statement, and the state early-disclosure matrix (21.3); intent to proceed, the fee gate, rate locks, extensions, relocks and float-downs with the lock-triggered revised LE and the PE–Whole Loan linkage (21.4); changed circumstances, revised LEs, the 4-specific-business-day limit, tolerance tests, cures and the 60-day refund (21.5); and every application-status outcome — NOIA, withdrawal, counteroffer, denial with ECOA/FCRA/Colorado content and HMDA action-taken recording (21.6). §21 ends when the file is either dispositioned (21.6) or handed to 25.2 for the Closing Disclosure with every §21 gate open.

**Processes and their anchors.** 21.1 emits `application.received` and `application.trid_received` and opens `SAFE_1008_103_MLO_OF_RECORD_GATE`. 21.2 starts `REGZ_1026_19E1_LE_3BD` at `trid_application_date`, sets `fee_items` baselines and `REGZ_1026_19E1III_LE_7SBD_GATE`. 21.3 runs its clocks from the Reg B application (`REGX_1024_20_HCL_3BD`, `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_NOTICE_3BD`), ARM interest (`REGZ_1026_19B_ARM_DISCLOSURE_GATE`), credit receipt (`FCRA_609G_SCORE_NOTICE_1BD`, `REGV_1022_74D_SCORE_NOTICE_CONSUMMATION_GATE`), referrals (`REGX_1024_15_AFBA_REFERRAL_GATE`) and the state matrix (NY/NJ pre-application gates, AZ fee-agreement gate, TX/MA/CA at-application notices). 21.4 opens `REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE` and `REGZ_1026_19E3IVD_LOCK_REVISED_LE_3BD`; 21.5 owns the revised-LE clocks, the 4-day gate, `REGZ_1026_19F2V_TOLERANCE_REFUND_60` and the `runToleranceTest` contract 25.1/25.2 build against; 21.6 owns `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30`, the NOIA and counteroffer clocks and the Colorado SB 26-189 timers.

**Agents.** `intake` (21.1 interview, consents, SCIF/demographics, MLO assignment), `disclosure` (21.2 LE, 21.3 companion notices, 21.5 revised LEs and tolerance — one package, `origination/disclosures`), `pricing` (21.4 intent, fee gate, locks; ARM illustration refresh for 21.3), `underwriter` (21.6 dispositions, reasons and notices), with `compliance-sentinel` (timer breaches, calendar anomalies, discouragement and prohibited-inquiry scanning, state-matrix confidence), `hmda` (28.3 store writes from 21.6) and `security-records` (retention classes) shared. Deterministic code owns every clock, gate, tolerance test, APR and fee derivation; the language model conducts the interview, explains documents within App. A (b)(2) limits, and classifies free text (intent, withdrawal, changed-circumstance narratives, reasons) — never rates, fees, eligibility or decline language.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the creditor on every LE, the ECOA creditor that takes every action, the lender for Reg X purposes, the HMDA reporter, the FCRA user of every report and score, the financial institution whose privacy notice is delivered, the employer/sponsor of the MLO of record whose name and NMLSR ID appear on the 1003, LE, CD, note and security instrument, and the party bound by every lock and Fannie Mae commitment. SM performs every step as the partner's processor and Fannie Mae TSP, is itself "any other person" bound by the fee-before-intent rule, bears third-party origination costs (disclosed as consumer charges offset by a zero-tolerance lender credit), funds cures and refunds where the estimation error is SM's, and supplies the `underwriting_reviewer` under the partner's delegated authority and state licensing. Human touchpoints in §21 are exactly: `mlo_of_record` (application package, LE terms, every lock/relock — `origination.ai_mlo_intake='assisted'`), `underwriting_reviewer` (every denial, counteroffer and NOIA), `human_agent` on request, `licensed_specialist` where a state requires one, and partner `officer` on breaches and template/edition changes.

**Dependencies.** Upstream: 20.3 (identity, E-SIGN/TCPA/credit consents, six-item detection, pre-qualification data never treated as submitted), 20.4 (pricing scenarios, LLPA 09.09.2026, fee schedule, the pre-LE written-estimate wrapper). Downstream and lateral: 22.1/22.2 (needs list only after the LE; credit-report fee handling; score payloads for 21.3; refresh/re-pull effects on notices), 22.6 (identity/legal-presence conditions), 23.1–23.4 (DU findings and decision factors; HPML/QM determinations at the rate-set date feed 21.3's HPML statement and 21.6's reasons), 24.1/24.2/24.4/24.5/24.6 (fee events through the fee gate; provider referrals through the AfBA gate; appraisal copies on every disposition), 25.1 (consumes `tolerance_tests` and `apr_calculations`), 25.2 (the CD as the revised disclosure; corrected CDs; refunds), 25.4 (`GLBA_1016_4_INITIAL_PRIVACY_GATE`), 26.x (`assertGateOpen` on the 7-day, 4-day, score-notice and appraisal-copy gates), 28.3 (HMDA fields, `rate_set_date`), 29.1/29.2 (commitments, key-data changes, fallout), 31.1 (MLO/reviewer licensing; `jurisdiction_rules.early_disclosures` ownership), 31.2 (reason accuracy, discouragement classifier, state AI overlays), 31.3 (retention classes `regz_le_3y`, `regb_25m`, `respa_afba_5y`, `regz_general_2y`, `co_admt_3y`). Servicing: the Notice Registry, `consents`, `holiday_calendars`, `timers` and `documents` (7.x/baseline) are reused; nothing in sections 1–19 is redefined; 30.2 boards the file at funding, and the LE's servicing statement ("to service your loan", partner as servicer, SM as subservicer) is the promise servicing must honor.

**Changes in flight that shape the build.** No TRID rulemaking in 2025–2026 and the TRID FAQs still published; the Reg B final rule effective July 21, 2026 (effects test removed, discouragement narrowed) with §1002.9(b)(2) untouched and Circulars 2022-03/2023-03 withdrawn May 12, 2025; Colorado SB 26-189 replacing the 2024 AI Act with point-of-interaction notice, 30-day adverse-outcome explanation and human review from Jan 1, 2027; the RESPA Servicing Disclosure Statement confirmed reverse-only (LE §1026.37(m)(6) carries servicing intent); the CFPB's August 2026 re-issue of Your Home Loan Toolkit (earlier editions usable while supplies last) against a June 2020 CHARM booklet that predates SOFR; Texas SML's rule consolidation (7 TAC §57.200 effective Nov 23, 2024) and Ohio's 2018 RMLA that removed the old origination disclosure statement; VantageScore 4.0 live with its own LLPA grids (LL-2026-06, Sept 9, 2026) and DU 12.1's tightened credit-risk standards (June 26, 2026) that change how often 21.6 sees Refer with Caution; the unresolved SAFE Act question of an AI "taking an application" and "presenting particular terms" (no regulator statement located), which keeps the MLO of record in every §21 loop; and the baseline `creditor` calendar decision (Mon–Fri, federal holidays closed) that fixes every 3-business-day date in this section — Columbus Day Mon Oct 12, 2026 moves a Thu Oct 8 application's LE and companion notices to Wed Oct 14 unless the partner declares Saturday or Columbus Day open.

**What §21 does not do.** No verification documents are required before the LE; no fee other than a bona fide credit-report fee is imposed before receipt plus intent; no demographic inference in any channel; no AI negotiation of rate or fees; no eligibility statements before DU; no risk-based pricing notice (the H-3 exception notice replaces it); no Servicing Disclosure Statement on forward loans; no state notice goes live without counsel sign-off in `jurisdiction_rules`; and no revised LE is ever issued on or after the CD date.
