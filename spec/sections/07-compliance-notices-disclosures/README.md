# Section 7 — Compliance Notices & Disclosures

## Overview

Section 7 is the platform's outbound-disclosure factory: the documents federal law requires a servicer to put in front of every borrower whether or not anything is wrong with the loan. Six processes: the **Reg Z periodic statement** (12 CFR 1026.41, with its delinquency, trial-period, bankruptcy, charged-off and coupon-book variants — plus the IRS Form 1098 and Fannie Mae's payment reminder notice, which the blueprint omitted and which 7.1 absorbs as sub-items); the **ARM rate-adjustment** (1026.20(c)) and **initial-adjustment** (1026.20(d)) notices with the ARM adjustment engine behind them; the **E-SIGN consent lifecycle** (15 U.S.C. 7001(c)) that decides whether any notice may leave electronically instead of by first-class mail; the **GLBA/Reg P privacy notice** program (12 CFR 1016) run on the partner's behalf; and the **payoff statement** as a compliance notice (1026.36(c)(3); the computation is built in 16.1). The Fannie Mae layer: Servicing Guide C-2.1-01/C-2.1-02/C-2.2-01/F-1-01 (ARM administration, error correction), D2-2-03 (payment reminder by the 20th, SVC-2025-05), C-4.2-01 (Form 1098 under the servicer's TIN), A2-3-05 (payoff fees) and A4-2.1-04 (outreach channels "as permitted by law"). Guide citations are to the **August 12, 2026** edition; eCFR text verified current as of **September 3–4, 2026** (no 2025–2026 CFPB changes to §§1026.41, 1026.20 or 1026.36 — research/00a §1.3).

**Architecture fit.** This section builds the **Notice Registry** of baseline §6 — versioned Handlebars→HTML→PDF templates, a machine-checkable required-content checklist per template version tied to its citation, plain-language QA, channel selection, proof-of-delivery capture and immutable `notices` rows — and is its first consumer (Stage 1, baseline §11). Because no baseline agent owns loan-terms disclosures, the section defines one new agent, **`disclosures`** (`agents/disclosures`, defined in 7.1's data model), which runs the statement cycle, the ARM engine, the privacy-notice calendar and the E-SIGN lifecycle end-to-end, reading from `cashiering`, `escrow`, `default-collections`/`lossmit-underwriter`, `bankruptcy-ops` and `payoff-release`, and handing the rate-change event to `investor-reporting` (5.1). `borrower-comms` captures/revokes consents and answers notice questions; `compliance-sentinel` owns timers; `security-records` owns retention. Core services: Timer Engine, ledger (every statement number derives from `ledger_entries`), `consents`, `jurisdiction_rules`, `rule_sets`, and the `print-mail`, `e-delivery` and new `index-feed` adapters (00b N9; the NY Fed SOFR API was verified live today).

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** Supermortgage prepares and sends every document here in its own name as the borrower-facing servicer (1.3). Two nuances are load-bearing: (a) under Reg P the institution with the customer relationship is the *holder of the servicing rights* — the partner — while Supermortgage, an entity that "act[s] as agent for, or provide[s] processing or other services to" it, has no Reg P consumer of its own (1016.3(e)(2)(iv)–(v)); the privacy notice is therefore the partner's (or a joint notice, 1016.9(f)) produced by Supermortgage under a 1016.13 service-provider contract; (b) Form 1098 is filed under Supermortgage's own TIN (C-4.2-01), whereas 1099-A/C use Fannie Mae's TIN (F-1-23; Sections 12.9/15). Everything else is Supermortgage's operational liability, with the partner liable to Fannie Mae (A2-1-07).

**Dependencies.** 1.1/1.3 (boarding terms, ARM data, hello-notice inserts); 2.1/2.2/2.7 (payments, suspense, late charges); 3.1–3.3 (escrow portion; 1098 co-mailing); 4.1/4.2/4.4 (payoff errors, RFIs, successors); 5.1 (rate-change event); 10.4 (PMI disclosure); 11.2/11.4 (early intervention, FDCPA, HUD-92070 SCRA notice); 12.x (trial-period, forbearance, charge-off facts); 13.x (first-notice flag; state pre-foreclosure notices such as NY RPAPL §1304 and CA HBOR); 14.2/14.3 (Rule 3002.1; bankruptcy statements); 16.1 (payoff engine); 17.x (transfer-out cut-offs); 19.1/19.2 (retention; Safeguards Rule).

**Changes in flight.** No governing CFPB rule has changed since 2018; the unfinalized 2024 Reg X NPRM touches only Sections 11–12 notices. SVC-2025-05 moved the payment reminder to the 20th (mandatory Dec. 1, 2025) and added a temporary-buydown 90-day notice (C-2.1-02). LL-2026-05 retires LAR 83 in favor of a rate/payment-change servicing event (5.1 dual-rail, 2027). LL-2026-04 (Aug. 6, 2026) makes the `disclosures` agent's decision records and vendor flow-down contractual. The FCC revocation rule (April 2025) and state AI-disclosure laws shape 7.4. Current Fannie Mae ARM plans (4926–4929) use 30-day Average SOFR; legacy LIBOR loans carry a replacement-index history the engine must honor.
