### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Note terms.** Form 3200 Multistate Fixed Rate Note (07/2021), §6(A): "If the Note Holder has not received the full amount of any Monthly Payment by the end of ___ calendar days after the date it is due, I will pay a late charge … The amount of the charge will be ___% of my overdue Monthly Payment. I will pay this late charge promptly but only once on each late payment." §6(B): "If I do not pay the full amount of each Monthly Payment on the date it is due, I will be in default." "Monthly Payment" in the note is the principal-and-interest payment. URL: https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/legal-documents/form-3200 (verified 2026-09-09). **Selling Guide B8-3-02, Special Note Provisions and Language Requirements (06/03/2020):** "The note for a conventional first mortgage must include information about the late charge to be assessed for any payment that is not received by the 15th day after it becomes due." "The late charge must be a minimum of 0% and up to 5% of the principal and interest (P&I) payment." "The amount must be permissible under applicable law." URL: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b8-3-02/special-note-provisions-and-language-requirements (verified 2026-09-09). So the conforming default is **5% of P&I after a 15-day grace**, with state law able to lower the percentage, lengthen the grace or change the basis; the boarded note controls (1.1) and `jurisdiction_rules` validate. The Form 3200 instructions (state-variation guidance) could not be parsed **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**Fannie Mae Servicing Guide.** *A2-3-04, Late Charges as Compensation (12/11/2019):* "The servicer may collect late charges, and if it does, it must do so in compliance with the requirements set forth in Selling Guide B8-3-02"; "The servicer must report the amount of late charges it collects each month for a given mortgage loan as part of the monthly activity information it provides through Fannie Mae's investor reporting system" (LAR 96 "other fees" / `fees.collected` — 5.1). URL: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/a2-3-04/late-charges-compensation (verified 2026-09-09). F-1-03 (6.1): late charges are deducted before P&I is transferred to the custodial account — they are servicer compensation. *C-1.1-02 (08/13/2025):* PITI payments that exclude late charges "must" be accepted and applied; military indulgence — "the servicer must waive the collection of late charges during the period for which the reduced interest rate remains in effect." *D2-3.2-01, Forbearance Plan (04/08/2026):* "The servicer must not accrue or collect late charges from the borrower during the forbearance plan"; on default of the plan "the servicer is authorized to accrue late charges from the date the borrower defaulted on the plan." *D2-3.2-02, Repayment Plan (08/13/2025):* the servicer must "waive late charges accrued during the repayment plan period as long as the terms of the repayment plan are maintained by the borrower"; late charges due when the plan is established may be included in it. *D2-3.2-06 / F-1-27:* assessable during a trial, waived on conversion, never capitalized (2.6). *D2-2-03, Sending a Payment Reminder Notice (08/13/2025; SVC-2025-05):* "The servicer must send a payment reminder notice to the borrower no later than the 20th day of the month if the payment has not been received," and the notice must "state the amount of late charges that are due, if applicable" (owned by 11.x; the late-charge engine must have assessed by then). *A2-3-05:* no fees for "sending notices relating to nonpayment" or "facilitating routine borrower collections." URLs in Sources (all verified 2026-09-09).

**Reg Z 12 CFR 1026.36(c)(2) (eCFR current 2026-09-04):** "a servicer shall not impose any late fee or delinquency charge for a payment if: (i) Such a fee or charge is attributable solely to failure of the consumer to pay a late fee or delinquency charge on an earlier payment; and (ii) The payment is otherwise a periodic payment received on the due date, or within any applicable courtesy period." Comment 36(c)(2)-1: construe consistently with the FTC credit practices rule, 16 CFR 444.4. Reg Z 1026.41(d)(2)(ii) requires the periodic statement to show "the amount of any late payment fee, and the date that fee will be imposed if payment has not been received" (7.1 reads this engine's parameters). **Reg X 1024.33(c)(1) (eCFR current 2026-09-03):** "During the 60-day period beginning on the effective date of transfer of the servicing of any mortgage loan, if the transferor servicer (rather than the transferee servicer that should properly receive payment on the loan) receives payment on or before the applicable due date (including any grace period allowed under the mortgage loan instruments), a payment may not be treated as late for any purpose." Reg X 1024.35(b)(5): a fee "the servicer lacks a reasonable basis to impose" is a covered error (4.1). **SCRA, 50 U.S.C. 3937:** the 6% cap applies "during the period of military service and one year thereafter" for mortgages; (d)(1): "The term 'interest' includes service charges, renewal charges, fees, or any other charges (except bona fide insurance)" — late charges therefore count within the cap and, per C-1.1-02/F-1-19, are waived during the reduced-rate period (13.9). **Bankruptcy:** Rule 3002.1(c) requires post-petition "fees, expenses, or charges" to be noticed "within 180 days after the fees, expenses, or charges are incurred," with preclusion for failure (Rule 3002.1(h)); 14.2 owns the notice; this engine must expose every post-petition charge to it. **FDCPA §808(1)** for default-boarded loans (11.4): only charges "expressly authorized by the agreement creating the debt or permitted by law" — the note authorizes late charges; returned-payment fees need separate authority.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (a) "Per note grace" is right but the row omits the amount basis (P&I, up to 5%), state caps, the "only once" rule, the no-pyramiding rule, and the five Fannie Mae/federal suppression regimes (forbearance, repayment plan, trial, SCRA/military indulgence, bankruptcy) plus the transfer shield it does cite. (b) There is no dedicated Servicing Guide late-charge assessment topic — the rule set is A2-3-04 + B8-3-02 + the note + workout topics. (c) "Fee ledger" is the `fees` table plus `late_charges` ledger account; late charges collected must also be reported to Fannie Mae monthly (5.1). (d) Returned-payment (NSF) fees are not mentioned anywhere in the blueprint; they are included here with a conservative default.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Daily `late_charge.assessment.run` at 00:30 servicer local for every installment whose grace end was the previous day (gated by `SM_CASHIERING_POSTING_BACKLOG_GATE`); re-run on `payment.reversed`, `payment.reapplied`, `loan_terms.activated` (retroactive corrections), overlay changes (`case.*.opened/closed`).
- `fee.collect` (funds allocated to `late_charges` by 2.1), `late_charge.waive` command (4.1, 12.x, 2.6, courtesy), `case.forbearance.defaulted` (accrual resumes from the default date), `case.repayment.completed` (waive accrued), `lossmit.modification.effective` (waive all), `scra.period.started/ended`, `bankruptcy.filed/dismissed/discharged`, `foreclosure.referred`, `transfer_in.cutover` (60-day window).
- `ach.return.received` / lockbox returned-item file (NSF fee evaluation).

#### Data model
`fees` (baseline, fixed): `id`, `loan_id`, `fee_type` ∈ {late_charge, nsf_fee, other_fee}, `installment_due_date?`, `basis_cents`, `pct` (decimal 6,4), `amount_cents`, `assessed_on`, `grace_end_on`, `state` ∈ {assessed, accrued_suspended, collected, partially_collected, waived, reversed, written_off}, `suppression_reason?` ∈ {forbearance_active, repayment_plan_pending_waiver, trial_pending_waiver, scra_reduced_rate, bankruptcy_active, transfer_window_60, noe_dispute, foreclosure_referred, posting_backlog}, `collected_cents`, `waived_cents`, `waiver_reason?` ∈ {workout_completion, trial_conversion, scra, transfer_misdirected, error_correction, courtesy, disaster_policy, fnma_request, bk_plan}, `waived_by` (agent/human), `decision_id`, `investor_reported_period?`, `bk_3002_1_notice_id?`.
New: `late_charge_suppressions` (`loan_id`, `reason`, `source_case_id`, `starts_on`, `ends_on?`, `mode` ∈ {no_accrual, accrue_suspended, no_collection}); `late_charge_waiver_counters` (`loan_id`, `courtesy_waivers_12m`, `nsf_waivers_12m`); `loan_terms` late-charge fields above. Ledger: `late_charges` (loan receivable), `nsf_fees`, corporate `late_charge_income`/`nsf_fee_income`. Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y`; no PII beyond loan linkage.

#### State machine
Per installment `late_charge_state`: `not_due` (before grace end) → `evaluating` (grace end passed; gate checks) → `assessed` | `accrued_suspended` (overlay with `mode=accrue_suspended`) | `not_assessed` (paid on time / overlay `no_accrual` / shield). `assessed` → `collected` (funds allocated) | `waived` | `reversed` (payment re-dated to on-time) ; `accrued_suspended` → `waived` (workout completion/conversion) | `assessed` (plan/trial failed) ; `assessed` → `written_off` (payoff/liquidation without collection). Actors: engine (assessment), `cashiering` agent (waivers within policy), case agents via commands, `officer` only for policy exceptions beyond limits.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Assessment test.** For installment due `d` with grace `g`: at the grace gate, sum the funds credited (`credited_as_of ≤ d + g`, including funds held in suspense that are attributable to the installment — decision 2.7-Q1) toward the installment's basis amount (P&I when `late_charge_basis=pi`). If `received_toward_basis ≥ basis_cents` → `not_assessed`. Otherwise `amount = round_half_up(late_charge_pct × basis_cents)` capped by `per_charge_cap_cents`, subject to the gates: only once per installment; no pyramiding (an installment whose periodic payment was received on time is never charged because of an unpaid earlier fee — and because the Allocation Engine never diverts a periodic payment to fees, this holds by construction); overlays (rule 3); transfer shield.
2. **Basis.** `pi` (default: note "overdue Monthly Payment" / B8-3-02 "P&I payment"); `piti` only where the boarded note says so and state law permits; SCRA-capped installments use the capped P&I; modified loans use the modified P&I from the effective date; interest-only or step-rate loans use the scheduled payment for that installment.
3. **Overlays (in precedence order).** (i) `transfer_window_60` shield (1024.33(c)(1)) — not late for any purpose; (ii) `forbearance_active` — `no_accrual` from plan start; on plan default, accrual resumes "from the date the borrower defaulted on the plan" (D2-3.2-01) — installments due after the default date are assessable, earlier ones are not; (iii) `scra_reduced_rate` — assess nothing; waive collection of any existing charges for the period; (iv) `bankruptcy_active` — `accrue_suspended` into `bk_postpetition_fees` memo state with no collection and no statement billing (14.3 statement rules), exposed to 14.2 for any 3002.1(c) notice decision; on dismissal the case agent decides whether suspended charges become `assessed` (default: waived — decision 2.7-Q3); (v) `repayment_plan_pending_waiver` — accrue suspended; waive on completion; assess on plan failure; (vi) `trial_pending_waiver` (2.6); (vii) `foreclosure_referred` — stop assessing new late charges after referral/acceleration (decision 2.7-Q4; default stop), existing ones remain and appear in reinstatement figures where permitted (13.x); (viii) `noe_dispute` — no new assessment on the disputed installment until the NoE closes (4.1); (ix) `posting_backlog` — assessment deferred until receipts dated ≤ grace end are posted, then evaluated with the true `credited_as_of` dates.
4. **Collection.** Only from funds beyond the periodic payment(s) due (instrument order, 2.1 rule 5) or funds the borrower designates for fees; never netted from P&I or escrow; included in payoff (16.1) and reinstatement (13.x) figures where the note/state permit; written off at payoff/liquidation if unpaid (`written_off`, no borrower liability post-payoff).
5. **Waivers.** Commands with reason codes; automatic: workout completion, trial conversion, SCRA, error correction (a re-dated payment reverses the charge), transfer misdirected (1.3 decision 3), Fannie Mae request, bankruptcy plan; discretionary: one courtesy waiver per loan per 12 months by the agent (policy), more only with `officer` approval; waived charges are `fee.waived` events and reduce `late_charges` (Dr `late_charge_income`-contra / Cr `late_charges`); waivers are never conditioned on enrollment in any product (Reg E compulsory-use analog; UDAAP).
6. **Reversal/re-dating.** When a payment is re-applied with an earlier `credited_as_of` (4.1 correction, lockbox date error, transferor forwarding), the engine re-evaluates the installment: an assessed charge becomes `reversed` (Dr `late_charge_income` / Cr `late_charges`, or refund if collected — `fee.reversed`), and 8.1 receives a correction if the delinquency was reported.
7. **Returned-payment (NSF) fee.** Assessed only if `jurisdiction_rules.nsf_fee.allowed` and the loan documents or state law provide authority, at `min(policy 2,500¢, cap_cents)`, once per returned item, never for returns caused by our error (wrong amount/date — R11) or bank error, never during bankruptcy/SCRA/forbearance; waivable once per 12 months on request; disclosed on the statement as a fee (1026.41(d)(4)).
8. **Reporting and income.** `fee.collected{late_charge}` in the period → 5.1 `fees.collected` (LAR "other fees"; A2-3-04 monthly reporting); late charges are Supermortgage income (F-1-03 deduction before custodial transfer), subject to the subservicing agreement's fee-sharing terms with the partner (contract).
9. **Statement/reminder data.** For every unpaid installment the engine exposes `late_fee_amount_if_unpaid` and `late_fee_date` (grace end + 1) for 1026.41(d)(2)(ii) (7.1), and `late_charges_due_cents` for the D2-2-03 reminder (11.x).
10. **Worked example K — standard assessment.** Fixture L-1: installment due 2026-09-01, P&I 158,017¢, grace 15 → last timely day 2026-09-16 (Wednesday). No P&I credited by then → run 2026-09-17 00:30: `amount = round_half_up(0.05 × 158,017) = round_half_up(7,900.85)` = **7,901¢ ($79.01)**; `fees{late_charge, assessed_on 2026-09-17, grace_end 2026-09-16}`; Dr `late_charges` 7,901 / Cr `late_charge_income` 7,901 (accrual; recognized when collected under the servicer's revenue policy — accounting choice, decision 2.7-Q5). Statement dated 2026-09-12 had shown "late fee of $79.01 will be charged if payment is not received by 09/16/2026." The reminder due by 2026-09-20 (11.x) states "late charges due: $79.01."
11. **Worked example L — transfer shield.** Loan transferred in with RESPA effective date 2026-09-01; the borrower mails 219,257¢ to the transferor, received there 2026-09-14; forwarded and received by Supermortgage 2026-09-22. `received_on` 2026-09-22 but `credited_as_of` 2026-09-14 (1024.33(c)); the run on 2026-09-17 sees `misdirected_payments.protected=true` for the loan (window through 2026-10-30) — no assessment; when the funds post on 2026-09-22 the installment is `satisfied` with no late charge and no negative reporting.
12. **Worked example M — no pyramiding.** After example K, the borrower pays exactly 219,257¢ on 2026-10-01 for the October installment: the Allocation Engine applies it fully to the installment (nothing to the $79.01); the 2026-10-17 run finds the October installment received on time → `not_assessed`; the $79.01 remains outstanding and is disclosed but never generates another charge.
13. **Worked example N — forbearance default.** Forbearance plan 2026-10-01 → 2026-12-31 (payments suspended); the borrower defaults on the plan's terms (fails to contact/pay the agreed amount) on 2026-11-20 → installments due 2026-10-01 and 2026-11-01: `not_assessed`; installment due 2026-12-01: assessable from grace end 2026-12-16 (accrual resumes from the default date) → 7,901¢ on 2026-12-17.
14. **Worked example O — SCRA.** Borrower on active duty from 2026-10-15 (13.9 sets the 6% cap effective for installments from 2026-11-01): installments during service + one year are computed at the capped rate; late charges are not assessed and any charge assessed after 2026-10-15 is waived (`fee.waived{scra}`); the $79.01 from example K (pre-service) is not collected during the period (`no_collection`) — decision on post-period collection: 2.7-Q6 (default: waive).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
The engine is deterministic; the `cashiering` agent handles waiver requests (any channel), interprets overlay edge cases (e.g., plan default date disputes), and prepares the bankruptcy fee exposure list for 14.2. Tools: `fees.read`, `fees.assess` (engine command with gates), `fees.waive` (policy-limited), `fees.reverse`, `cases.get_overlays`, `payments.history`, `notice.send`, `escalation.create`. Decision record: `{loan_id, installment_due_date, test{basis_cents, received_toward_basis, credited_dates[]}, overlays[], outcome, amount_cents, waiver{reason, counter_state}?, rationale}`. Guardrails: cannot assess without the grace gate and posting-backlog gate; cannot exceed note/state caps; cannot assess twice; cannot collect from P&I/escrow; cannot waive beyond the courtesy limit without `officer`; cannot assess post-petition or during forbearance/SCRA. Escalations: `officer` (policy exceptions, systemic mis-assessment found by QC), `human_agent` (borrower request), `attorney` only through 13.x/14.x case owners (e.g., contested reinstatement figures). Human path when AI off: Fees Queue with identical gates; humans cannot override the legal gates either.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in**: prior-servicer late charges boarded as `fees{state=assessed, source=transferor}` only with supporting history (1.6); disputed ones held under NoE rules; the 60-day shield applies to misdirected payments.
- **Transfer-out**: unpaid late charges transfer with the loan; those collected post-cutover are forwarded (17.x).
- **Bankruptcy**: no assessment/collection; 3002.1(c) exposure list; discharge → charges written off unless secured and permitted (14.x).
- **SCRA**: as rule 3(iii); the one-year tail after service.
- **Disaster**: forbearance overlay (D2-3.2-01); Fannie Mae has no separate disaster late-charge topic (D1-3-01 verified silent).
- **Grace end on a weekend/holiday**: business-day extension policy (rule table) — configurable off for states that compute strictly on calendar days **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Payment received timely but posted late (our backlog)**: `credited_as_of` governs → no charge; QC monitors backlog-caused near-misses.
- **Partial P&I received (e.g., $1,500 of $1,580.17)**: charge applies (full Monthly Payment not received) unless the $50 rule made it a full P&I payment.
- **ARM/escrow payment change mis-communicated**: if the borrower paid the prior correct amount because our notice was late (7.2/3.3 breach), waive as `error_correction`.
- **Modified loan with deferred balance**: basis = modified P&I; no late charge on the NIB.
- **Loan paid ahead**: installments prepaid are `satisfied` — no charges.
- **Duplicate assessment attempt after reversal/re-post**: the once-only gate keys on installment, not on payment id.

#### Audit and evidence
Per installment: the assessment decision with the credited-funds test (amounts and `credited_as_of` dates), overlay state at the gate, note terms and jurisdiction rule versions, ledger entries, waiver decisions with reason and approver, reversal linkage to the correcting payment, statement/reminder snapshots showing the disclosed fee and date, investor reporting of collected charges, and bankruptcy exposure lists — supporting 1024.35(b)(5) defenses, state-exam fee reviews and Fannie Mae QC.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Late-charge test basis and held funds** — default: P&I basis; funds held in suspense that cover the P&I count as received (borrower-favorable reading of the note); alternative: applied funds only.
2. **Grace end on non-business days** — default: extend to the next servicer business day.
3. **Post-petition charges on dismissal** — default: waived (not retroactively assessed).
4. **Assessment after foreclosure referral/acceleration** — default: stop assessing new charges at referral; keep accrued ones in reinstatement figures where permitted.
5. **Revenue recognition** — default: recognize late-charge income on collection (accrual booked as receivable with allowance) — finance decision.
6. **SCRA pre-service charges after the protected period** — default: waive.
7. **NSF fee** — default: $25 or state cap (lower), only where document/state authority exists; consider $0 for simplicity (UDAAP-safest).
