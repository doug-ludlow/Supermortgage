### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Title insurance is mandatory unless an attorney opinion letter qualifies.** Selling Guide B7-2-01, *Provision of Title Insurance* (last updated 04/06/2022): "Each mortgage loan purchased by Fannie Mae must have a title insurance policy in place or an attorney title opinion letter that meets Fannie Mae's requirements." The lender must ensure coverage by "a title policy issued by an acceptable insurer, including any required endorsements" or "a title opinion letter issued by an attorney."

**Title insurer requirements.** B7-2-02, *Title Insurer Requirements* (06/05/2019): a title insurer must be "duly authorized and licensed, as required, to issue title insurance in the state where the property is located." Lenders evaluate the insurer on factors including the "financial strength of the title insurer, adequate reserves, record related to satisfactory title claim resolution, or strength of a reinsurance arrangement," and "an acceptable rating from a rating agency." "Iowa Title Guaranty is an acceptable title guarantor for properties located in the state of Iowa." Where reinsurance is relied on, the primary and reinsuring companies must execute "an *Assumption of Liability Endorsement* (Form 858) or an equivalent endorsement that provides for 100% reinsurance of the primary insurer's policy and a 90-day written notice of termination." **The topic names no rating agency and no minimum rating** — unlike property insurance (B7-3-01). The platform therefore treats a Demotech/AM Best/S&P/Fitch/KBRA rating as *one* acceptable evidence of financial strength and records which factor was relied on.

**Policy form, amount, effective date, insured, mandatory endorsement, prohibited language.** B7-2-03, *General Title Insurance Coverage* (07/06/2022): for loans originated on or after January 1, 2024 the policy must be "the 2021 American Land Title Association (ALTA) Loan Policy" (the ALTA Loan Policy adopted 07-01-2021 per ALTA's policy-forms library); loans before that date may use the 2006 or 2021 form; ALTA short forms or state-promulgated forms are acceptable if they provide equivalent coverage without materially impairing protection. "The amount of title insurance coverage must at least equal the original principal amount of the loan." Policies on the 2006/2021 ALTA forms "may be effective as of loan closing"; other forms' effective date may be "no earlier than the later of the date of the final disbursement of loan proceeds or the date the mortgage was recorded." "Under no circumstances may MERS be named as the insured of a title policy" (the mortgage may name MERS as nominee if properly identified). "The title insurance coverage must include an environmental protection lien endorsement (ALTA 8.1 ... or equivalent state form)." "Title policies may not include the creditors' rights exclusion language that ALTA adopted in 1990."

**Endorsements by property/loan type.** B7-2-04, *Special Title Insurance Coverage Considerations* (05/03/2023): "For condo units, an ALTA 4 or 4.1 endorsement or its equivalent is required." "For PUD units, an ALTA 5 or 5.1 endorsement or its equivalent is required." ARMs: "The policy must include ALTA Endorsement 6." Manufactured homes: "The policy must include ALTA Endorsement 7, 7.1, or 7.2." Leaseholds: "A mortgage that is subject to a leasehold estate must have an ALTA Endorsement 13.1." Texas Section 50(a)(6): "Mortgagee Policy of Title Insurance (Form T-2), supplemented by an Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42) ... and a Supplemental Coverage Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42.1)." Condo policies must ensure "the mortgage is superior to any lien for unpaid common expense assessments," insure "against any impairment or loss of title ... caused by any past, present, or future violations of any covenants, conditions, or restrictions," "that the unit does not encroach on another unit or on any of the common elements," "that the loan is secured by a unit in a condo project that has been created in compliance with the applicable enabling statutes," and "that real estate taxes are assessable and lienable only against the individual condo unit"; PUD policies must insure "that the owner of a PUD unit is a member of the homeowners' association."

**Exceptions and impediments.** B7-2-05, *Title Exceptions and Impediments* (07/06/2022): Fannie Mae will not purchase loans with an "unacceptable title impediment[s], particularly unpaid real estate taxes and survey exceptions." Minor impediments acceptable for conventional loans include customary public-utility subsurface easements not under buildings and above-surface utility easements along property lines not exceeding 12 feet; restrictive covenants and cost/size/setback restrictions where no forfeiture, reversion or lien results; encroachments of one foot or less by eaves/projections and hedges or removable fences; outstanding oil, water or mineral rights customarily waived; property-line variations up to 2% (front) and 5% (other) with a survey; lawful possession rights without a right of first refusal and tenant leases of at most two years; minor area-description discrepancies with survey and affirmative insurance; an exception to Indian claims with loss insurance. "The lender must indemnify Fannie Mae ... for any Fannie Mae losses that can be directly attributed to the impediment(s)." A requirements table for unexpired redemption periods exists in the topic (not extracted — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**).

**Attorney title opinion letter (AOL) alternative.** B7-2-06, *Attorney Title Opinion Letter Requirements* (12/10/2025): "Lenders are authorized to use an attorney title opinion letter in lieu of a title insurance policy if all of the conditions in the following table are satisfied." The letter must "be addressed to the lender and all successors in interest of the lender"; contain the indemnity "We [I] agree to indemnify you and your successors in interest in the [mortgage] [deed of trust] opined hereto, to the full extent of all losses attributable to a breach of our [my] duty to exercise reasonable care and skill in the examination of the title and giving of this opinion"; "provide the gap coverage for the period of time between the loan closing and the recordation of the mortgage"; "state that the title condition of the property is acceptable and the mortgage constitutes a lien of the required priority on a fee simple estate in the property"; "list other liens and state that they are subordinate"; include ALTA 8.1-equivalent environmental language, ARM lien-validity language and PUD representations (no restrictive-covenant violations, dues current, no exercised rights of first refusal) where applicable; and "not take exception to survey matters." The attorney must be "licensed to practice law in the jurisdiction where the subject property is located," insured against malpractice "in an amount commonly prevailing in the jurisdiction," and the letter must "be commonly acceptable in lieu of title insurance by private institutional mortgage investors in the area where the subject property is located." Ineligible: co-op share loans, "leasehold dwellings (including community land trust properties)," manufactured homes, HomeStyle Refresh and HomeStyle Renovation loans, Texas Section 50(a)(6) loans, and "loans using power of attorney." "The lender must report Special Feature Code 155 when delivering a loan that uses an attorney title opinion letter in lieu of a title insurance policy." Condominiums are **not** on the current ineligible list.

**Inter vivos revocable trusts.** B2-2-05 (10/31/2017): an inter vivos revocable trust is one that "an individual creates during their lifetime; becomes effective during its creator's lifetime; and can be changed or canceled by its creator at any time, for any reason, during that individual's lifetime." "The primary beneficiary of the trust must be the individual(s) establishing the trust." "The trustee(s) must include either: the individual establishing the trust (or at least one of the individuals, if there are two or more); or an institutional trustee ..." For a principal residence "at least one individual establishing the trust must occupy the security property and sign the loan documents"; "the income or assets of at least one of the individuals establishing the trust will be used to qualify for the mortgage"; "The trustee(s) must have the power to mortgage the security property for the purpose of securing a loan to the individual (or individuals) who are the borrower(s) under the mortgage or deed of trust note." The title policy "ensures full title protection to Fannie Mae" with "no exceptions with respect to the trustee(s)." Delivery: "Special Feature Code 168." B8-5-02 (10/31/2017): "The note must be executed in accordance with E-2-04, Signature Requirements for Mortgages to Inter Vivos Revocable Trusts"; "The trustee(s) of the inter vivos revocable trust also must execute the security instrument and any applicable rider"; "Each individual establishing the trust whose credit is used to qualify for the mortgage must acknowledge all of the terms and covenants in the security instrument and any necessary rider, and must agree to be bound thereby, by placing their signature after a statement of acknowledgment"; a revocable trust rider is optional ("If the lender chooses to require a revocable trust rider ..."), with Fannie Mae's sample rider for California and a state-modified rider elsewhere. Trust certification versus full trust review is not prescribed by these topics — SM's default (below) is a state-law trust certification plus attorney review where the certification statute is absent **[policy]**.

**Power of attorney.** B8-5-05, *Requirements for Use of a Power of Attorney* (10/05/2022; last related announcement SEL-2022-09): "Loans with documentation executed by an agent on behalf of the borrower under a POA are eligible for delivery to Fannie Mae if all requirements referenced in this Guide are met." Eligible transactions are purchase and limited cash-out refinance only — cash-out refinances are not eligible. Persons who may not act as agent unless "a relative of the borrower": the lender or its affiliate, the loan originator, employees or affiliates of the title company, the property seller, and a real estate agent with a financial interest in the transaction. Exception path: "In a recorded, interactive session conducted via the internet, and to the satisfaction of the agent, the borrower must" confirm identity, review the loan documents and "reaffirm their agreement to the execution of the loan documents by the agent"; "At Fannie Mae's request the lender must produce a recording and other documentary media memorializing the entirety of the interactive session"; where the agent is an employee of the title insurer or of its policy-issuing agent, "such title insurer must issue a closing protection letter (or similar contractual protection)" unless unavailable under applicable law. The POA must be notarized, dated so as to be valid when the documents are executed, "must reference the address of the subject property," be copied to the loan file, and match the names on the loan documents. "In jurisdictions where a POA used for a signature on a security instrument must be recorded with the security instrument, the lender must ensure that recordation has been effected"; "If applicable law requires an original POA for enforcement or foreclosure purposes, an original must be forwarded to the document custodian." Override: "If a lender determines that it is required by applicable law to accept a POA presented by a borrower, none of the following requirements for, or limitations on, the use of a POA apply," and "the lender must include a written statement in the loan file that explains that determination." "An agent under a POA may sign the note and/or security instrument on behalf of a borrower."

**Subordinate financing and resubordination.** B2-1.2-04, *Subordinate Financing* (08/06/2025): "If subordinate financing is left in place in connection with a first mortgage refinance transaction, Fannie Mae requires execution and recordation of a resubordination agreement." "If state law permits subordinate financing to remain in the same subordinate lien position established with the prior first mortgage that is being refinanced, Fannie Mae does not require resubordination." Acceptable terms include "Mortgages with regular payments that cover at least the interest due so that negative amortization does not occur" and "Mortgage terms that require interest at a market rate"; unacceptable: "Mortgages with negative amortization" (employer deferred-payment exception) and subordinate financing whose "maturity or balloon payment date is less than five years after the note date." "For HELOCs, the monthly payment does not have to remain constant." Subordinate liens must be "clearly subordinate to Fannie Mae's first mortgage loan or co-op share loan." Shared-appreciation arrangements are not permitted "Unless it qualifies as a Community Seconds loan."

**Payoff statements from the existing servicer.** 12 CFR 1026.36(c)(3) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026): "a creditor, assignee or servicer, as applicable, must provide an accurate statement of the total outstanding balance that would be required to pay the consumer's obligation in full as of a specified date. The statement shall be sent within a reasonable time, but in no case more than seven business days, after receiving a written request from the consumer or any person acting on behalf of the consumer," with a "reasonable time" exception for bankruptcy, foreclosure, reverse or shared-appreciation mortgages, and "natural disasters or other similar circumstances." Comment 36(c)(3)-1 (as verified in servicing 7.6) includes "a creditor with which the consumer is refinancing and which requires the payoff statement to complete the refinancing" among persons acting on the consumer's behalf; "business day" here is the §1026.2(a)(6) general definition (the servicer's own calendar). **This duty binds the existing servicer, not the partner as new creditor.** When SM subservices the existing loan, the duty is SM's own and is executed by servicing 16.1 under `REGZ_1026_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT_7BD` (unit `business_days_servicer`; defined in 7.6/16.1 — referenced, never redefined).

**Escrow balance on the existing loan.** 12 CFR 1024.34(b) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026): "(1) ... Within 20 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays) of a borrower's payment of a mortgage loan in full, a servicer shall return to the borrower any amounts remaining in an escrow account that is within the servicer's control." (b)(2): with the borrower's consent the servicer may instead credit the remaining funds to the escrow account of a new mortgage loan where the new lender was the original lender, owns or was assigned the prior loan, or "uses the same servicer for both loans." Servicing 3.5 owns `REGX_1024_34B_PAYOFF_ESCROW_REFUND_20BD` (unit `business_days_federal`) and the `escrow_credit_to_new_loan` consent; 24.4 only decides, before closing, which path the same-servicer refinance takes.

**RESPA §8 / AfBA** (00a-fed §6.6): §1024.14(b) "No person shall give and no person shall accept any fee, kickback or other thing of value" for referrals; §1024.15 requires the affiliated-business disclosure at referral and permits required use only of an attorney, credit reporting agency or appraiser — SM may recommend a settlement agent from its vetted panel but must let the borrower shop (the §1026.19(e)(1)(vi) written list is 21.2's) and must never condition anything on the choice.

**Closing location and title forms for Texas 50(a)(6).** B5-4.1-03 (03/04/2026): "a title insurance policy written on Texas Land Title Association forms (standard or short form), supplemented by an Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42) and a Supplemental Coverage Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42.1), is required"; "There may be no exceptions or deletions to the coverage provided by Paragraphs 2(a) through (e)" of the T-42; "The maximum allowable LTV and combined LTV for any Texas Section 50(a)(6) loan is 80%"; Forms 3044.1 and 3185 are required (26.1). 7 Tex. Admin. Code §153.15 (effective Nov 26, 2020; read on Cornell LII — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — TAC portal is JavaScript-only]**): "An equity loan may be closed only at an office of the lender, an attorney at law, or a title company"; "The closing office must be a permanent physical address so that the closing occurs at an authorized physical location other than the homestead"; the consent under §50(a)(6)(A) must be signed by the owner and spouse (or attorney-in-fact) "at the permanent physical address of an office of the lender, an attorney at law, or a title company." The constitutional text of Art. XVI §50(a)(6)(N) itself could not be read today (00a-fed §16.1) — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**New York CEMA** (00a-fed §16.2, VERIFIED): Tax Law §255(1)(a) exempts a consolidating/supplemental instrument from mortgage recording tax except to the extent it "creates or secures a new or further indebtedness or obligation," and §255(2)(a) requires a "statement under oath of the facts" filed with the recording officer; Selling Guide B8-2-02 (12/10/2025) requires Form 3172 and, for new money, Fannie Mae's standard security instrument. The title-side consequences are: the existing note and mortgage must be *assigned* by the existing lender to the partner (not satisfied), the title insurer insures the consolidated lien, and the §255 affidavit is a recording deliverable.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Fannie Mae title insurer rating requirements" — B7-2-02 sets **no** rating agency or minimum; it lists financial strength, reserves, claims record, reinsurance or "an acceptable rating" as evaluation factors. The platform stores the factor relied on rather than enforcing a fixed grid.
2. "2024–2025 changes" to B8-5-05 — none exist; the topic was last updated 10/05/2022 (SEL-2022-09). The current operative novelties are the recorded interactive-session exception and the applicable-law override, both from 2022.
3. The blueprint lists POA "limits for cash-out" — the Guide has no limits: cash-out refinances are simply **ineligible** for POA execution (absent the applicable-law override).
4. The AOL alternative now permits condominiums (B7-2-06, 12/10/2025); the blueprint's "condo/PUD" framing of endorsements applies to policies, not to the AOL eligibility list, and AOLs are barred for **POA** loans — a cross-dependency the row omits.
5. §1026.36(c)(3) is cited as if it bound the new lender; it binds the *existing* creditor/assignee/servicer and its clock runs on that servicer's business days. In the same-servicer case the obligation is SM's under servicing 16.1.
6. The row omits §1024.34(b) (20-day escrow refund; same-servicer credit with consent), B7-2-03's MERS-not-insured rule, the mandatory ALTA 8.1, the creditors'-rights exclusion prohibition, the coverage-amount floor, the 2021-form effective-date advantage, and the AOL's own gap-coverage and indemnity content.
7. "CPL dated before funding" is not a Fannie Mae rule — the Guide mentions CPLs only in the POA topic; the CPL gate is a warehouse/partner risk control (SM's facility terms) and state CPL forms govern content.
8. B2-1.2-04's state-law exception (no resubordination where the lien keeps its position by statute) is missing from the row, as is the five-year balloon floor for subordinate liens.
9. The Texas closing-location rule is regulatory (7 TAC §153.15) and constitutional; it excludes RON/remote closings for 50(a)(6) loans, which conflicts with the platform's eNote+RON default (26.2) — the row does not flag it.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `intent.to_proceed.received` (21.4) → title order; `application_properties` (address, APN, legal description, property type, project), `application_borrowers` (vesting parties, trust flag, POA flag), `application_liabilities` and `application_reo` (existing liens to be paid or resubordinated), `purchase_contracts` (seller, purchase price, closing date), `locks` (target consummation).
- Inbound from the `title` adapter: commitment (PDF + data), tax certificate, HOA/condo status letter, CPL, pro forma policy, final policy (post-recording), settlement statement drafts.
- `valuation.received` / `project.review.completed` (24.1–24.3): legal description reconciliation, condo/PUD/MH/leasehold flags that drive endorsements; `flood.determination.received` (24.5) shares the parsed legal description.
- Payoff channel: `payoff.statement.received` (external servicer document) or servicing 16.1 `payoff.statement.sent` (same-servicer).
- `disclosure.cd.delivered` (25.2) → date-down and final figure reconciliation; `closing.scheduled` (26.2) → CPL, wire verification and good-through checks; `funding.authorized` request (26.3) → gate evaluation.

#### State machine

`title_orders.status`: `ordered` → `commitment_received` → `reviewed` (every Schedule B item classified; endorsements computed) → `curative_open` (any `title_curative_items` open) ⇄ `reviewed` → `cleared` (no blocking items; CPL received; agent vetted; wire verified; payoffs current; subordinations executed) → `dated_down` (bring-down within the window before consummation) → `closed` (`closing.consummated`) → `policy_received` (final policy or AOL delivered; endorsements match `required_endorsements`) [terminal] | `cancelled` (application withdrawn/denied) [terminal]. Transition `reviewed → cleared` is performed by the `title-closing` agent; `officer` approval is required when any item resolves with `indemnity_accepted_by_officer`. `payoff_demands.status`: `requested` → `received` → (`good_through_date < planned disbursement`) `stale` → `refreshed` → `funded` | `rejected` (short payoff). `subordinations.status`: `requested` → `received` (terms checked) → `executed` → `recorded` | `waived_statutory` | `rejected` (terms fail B2-1.2-04 → the lien must be paid off or the loan restructured).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Required endorsements** are computed from property facts: base = {ALTA 8.1 (always)}; + ALTA 4/4.1 if condo; + ALTA 5/5.1 if PUD; + ALTA 6 if ARM (any SOFR plan); + ALTA 7/7.1/7.2 if manufactured home; + ALTA 13.1 if leasehold; TX 50(a)(6) → T-2 + T-42 + T-42.1 with no deletion of ¶2(a)–(e); state-promulgated equivalents substitute where `jurisdiction_rules.title.state_promulgated_forms` applies. Missing endorsement → `title.curative.opened(kind='other')` addressed to the agent; the gate refuses funding until issued or committed on the pro forma.
2. **Form and effective date.** Policy form must be the 2021 ALTA Loan Policy (or short form/state equivalent) for every loan in scope (all originated after Jan 1, 2024); effective date may be the closing date. If the agent proposes a non-ALTA state form, the effective date must be ≥ later of final disbursement and recording, which in a dry-funding state pushes the gap onto the CPL/AOL — the agent must confirm gap coverage in writing.
3. **Coverage amount** `policy_amount_cents ≥ note_amount_cents` (original principal, including any financed MI premium — 24.6). Fixture: $560,000.00 → policy amount ≥ 56,000,000 cents.
4. **Exception classification** (B7-2-05): each Schedule B-II item is classified by rule: utility easements along property lines ≤ 12 ft and subsurface not under improvements → `minor_b7_2_05`; CC&Rs without forfeiture/reversion/lien → minor; encroachments ≤ 1 ft (eaves/projections) or removable fences → minor; mineral rights customarily waived → minor; unpaid taxes → `to_be_paid` (blocking); survey exception → `curative` (blocking unless deleted or affirmatively insured); unreleased prior mortgage → `to_be_paid`/`released`; HOA assessment lien → `to_be_paid`; PACE → `to_be_paid` (24.3 eligibility); anything else → `curative` for the underwriter to delete, insure over, or for the partner `officer` to accept with the B7-2-05 indemnity recorded in `agent_decisions`.
5. **Taxes and HOA dues.** Tax certificate parsed: any installment delinquent → pay at closing; installment due within 60 calendar days after consummation → pay at closing (policy; escrow analysis in 30.3 then starts clean); HOA status letter: delinquent assessments and transfer fees paid at closing; for condos the policy must insure superiority over unpaid common-expense assessments (rule 1's ALTA 4).
6. **Payoff arithmetic (external servicer).** `total_at(date) = total_cents + per_diem_cents × max(0, days(date − good_through_date))` is used only for planning; funding uses a statement whose `good_through_date ≥ disbursement_date`. Refresh when the planned disbursement moves past good-through, when a payment posts on the old loan, or when the statement is > 30 days old. Worked example (refinance fixture, existing first lien serviced by an external servicer): written payoff request sent Mon Oct 26, 2026 (creditor business days: Oct 27, 28, 29, 30, Nov 2, 3, 4) → expected by Wed Nov 4, 2026 (`SM_PAYOFF_DEMAND_FOLLOWUP_7BD`); statement dated Wed Nov 4, principal $531,240.00, interest paid through Oct 31, per diem $105.52 (= 531,240.00 × 0.0725 ÷ 365 = 105.5186…, rounded to cents by the servicer), good through Thu Nov 12: interest Nov 1–12 = 12 × $105.52 = $1,266.24; recording fee for the release $30.00; total $532,536.24 (53,253,624 cents). Disbursement Thu Nov 12, 2026 → gate satisfied. If funding slipped to Fri Nov 13, the statement would be stale and the planning figure would be $532,641.76; the agent refreshes rather than "adding a day," because the servicer's statement is the only accurate figure (comment 36(c)(3)-3).
7. **Same-servicer payoff (SM subservices the existing loan).** SM issues the statement through servicing 16.1 (the request from 24.4 is a "person acting on behalf of the consumer," comment 36(c)(3)-1) and posts the payoff on the disbursement date; escrow treatment is decided pre-closing: (a) refund by 3.5 under `REGX_1024_34B_PAYOFF_ESCROW_REFUND_20BD`; (b) with the borrower's `escrow_credit_to_new_loan` consent, credit the balance to the new loan's initial escrow deposit (allowed because "the new lender ... uses the same servicer for both loans," §1024.34(b)(2)(iii)) — reducing the cash-to-close on the CD (25.2) and the initial deposit computed by 30.3; (c) netting against a payoff shortfall only under feature flag `escrow.payoff.net_against_shortfall` (3.5) with disclosure on the statement. Worked example: payoff posted Thu Nov 12, 2026; escrow balance after posting $2,347.18; path (a): 20 days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal public holidays (Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26 excluded): Nov 13, 16–20, 23–25, 27, 30, Dec 1–4, 7–11 → due Fri Dec 11, 2026; path (b): the initial escrow deposit on the CD of $2,912.50 (30.3's figure) is reduced by $2,347.18 to $565.32, with the consent evidenced in the closing package.
8. **Short payoff / rejection.** If the existing lienholder issues a short-payoff statement (conditional on a release for less than the balance), the loan is not a rate/term refinance of a lien "paid in full"; route to `underwriter` (DU resubmission) and treat the existing lien as unreleasable until a written release commitment is received — `status='rejected'` blocks funding.
9. **Resubordination.** Required whenever a subordinate lien remains after a refinance unless `jurisdiction_rules.subordination.statutory_position_preserved`; the agreement must be executed by the lienholder before consummation and recorded with (or immediately after) the new security instrument. Terms check: interest-covering payments (no negative amortization, except employer deferred plans), market rate, maturity/balloon ≥ 5 years after the new note date, no shared appreciation unless Community Seconds. HELOC ratios: `cltv_bps = floor((first + drawn) × 10000 ÷ value)`, `hcltv_bps = floor((first + line) × 10000 ÷ value)`. Worked example (fixture): HELOC line $50,000.00, drawn $18,000.00, value $800,000.00, first $560,000.00 → CLTV 72.25% (7225 bps), HCLTV 76.25% (7625 bps); DU is run with both; request sent Thu Oct 8, 2026 (findings Wed Oct 7 + 1 business day; due by Mon Oct 12 is Columbus Day, so the 3-business-day deadline is Tue Oct 13); agreement executed Wed Oct 28; gate satisfied for consummation Fri Nov 6.
10. **Trust review.** Eligible iff `revocable ∧ primary_beneficiary_is_settlor ∧ (settlor_is_trustee ∨ institutional_trustee) ∧ power_to_mortgage ∧ occupancy_ok (primary residence) ∧ qualifying_party_ok`; title must insure without trustee exceptions; SFC 168 flagged for 29.3; note/security instrument signature blocks per E-2-04 (26.1 renders: trustee capacity signature plus settlor acknowledgment).
11. **POA review.** Eligible iff `transaction_type ∈ {purchase, limited_cash_out}` (cash-out → ineligible unless `applicable_law_override` with the written file statement), agent not in an ineligible class or is a relative, or the recorded interactive session is completed (and a CPL exists where the agent is a title-insurer/agent employee), and the document tests pass (notarized, dated valid at execution, references the property address, names match); recording and original-to-custodian per `jurisdiction_rules`; an AOL cannot be used on a POA loan (rule 12).
12. **AOL path** (feature flag `title.aol_enabled`, default off): allowed only when the transaction is not co-op, leasehold/CLT, MH, HomeStyle, TX 50(a)(6) or POA, the attorney is licensed in the state with prevailing-amount malpractice cover, the letter contains every B7-2-06 element (addressee, indemnity text, gap, priority on fee simple, subordinate liens listed, ALTA 8.1 language, ARM/PUD language as applicable, no survey exception), and the jurisdiction test ("commonly acceptable ... by private institutional mortgage investors in the area") is documented; SFC 155 at delivery.
13. **Wire controls.** Closing-funds and payoff-wire beneficiaries are verified by the vendor against the agent's/servicer's registered account, plus an SM-initiated callback to a number obtained from the ALTA Registry/underwriter or a prior verified record — never from the e-mail carrying the instructions; a change to instructions within 48 hours of funding blocks the wire unless the `funding_approver` (26.3) releases after a second callback; all payoff wires go to the servicer's account of record, never to an individual.
14. **Settlement-agent vetting**: license active in the property state (attorney bar status in attorney states), E&O ≥ $1,000,000 per claim (policy default; open question 24.4-Q3), fidelity/crime cover, ALTA Registry presence or underwriter confirmation, Best Practices attestation, wire instructions on letterhead re-verified annually; RESPA §8: no thing of value flows to or from the agent for referrals; the borrower may choose any agent meeting the vetting standard (the provider list in 21.2 is informational).
15. **NY CEMA title mechanics**: confirm the existing lender will assign (not satisfy) the mortgage(s), obtain the assignment(s), have the title insurer insure the consolidated lien for the full new amount, compute mortgage recording tax on new money only (`new_money = new_note − unpaid principal of consolidated notes`), prepare the §255 affidavit for recording; 26.1 renders Form 3172. Worked figure: NY refinance $560,000 consolidating an existing $531,240 note → taxable new money $28,760 (mortgage-tax rate per county — 26.1/`jurisdiction_rules`).
16. **TX 50(a)(6)**: closing scheduled only at an office of the lender, an attorney or a title company (physical address); RON/hybrid disabled for the transaction (`closings.closing_type ∈ {ipen, wet}` at the office); T-2/T-42/T-42.1 with no deletions; AOL barred; POA only if signed at an authorized office (7 TAC §153.15(2)).

- **`wire-verification` adapter** (FundingShield/CertifID): request `{beneficiary, routing, account, purpose, transaction_id}`; response `{match, confidence, registered_on, last_verified}`; idempotent by `(application_id, purpose, instructions_hash)`; outage → manual callback path with dual control recorded in `wire_verifications.vendor='manual_callback'`.
- **Existing servicers** (external): written payoff request via the servicer's designated channel (comment 36(c)(3)-2 permits reasonable channel requirements) with the borrower's authorization; inbound statement parsed (principal, interest-paid-through, per diem, good-through, fees, wire instructions); callback verification of wire instructions against the servicer's published payoff instructions.

- **26.1/26.2/26.4**: closing instructions, recording of resubordination agreements and POA where required, final policy trailing-document timer.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`title-closing` agent (package `origination/closing`; tools: `orderTitle`, `parseCommitment`, `classifyException`, `computeRequiredEndorsements`, `openCurative`, `requestUnderwriterDeletion`, `requestDateDown`, `vetSettlementAgent`, `lookupAltaRegistry`, `verifyWireInstructions`, `placeCallback`, `requestPayoff`, `parsePayoffStatement`, `computePayoffAtDate`, `decideEscrowTreatment`, `requestSubordination`, `checkSubordinateTerms`, `reviewTrust`, `reviewPOA`, `requestCPL`, `evaluateGates`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: orders title within two business days of intent to proceed; parses the commitment into Schedule A/B data and reconciles vesting against the application and the appraisal's legal description; classifies every exception; opens and drives curative items with the agent, borrower or underwriter; computes endorsements; vets the settlement agent and verifies wires; requests payoffs (or issues them via 16.1) and refreshes them against the funding date; obtains resubordinations; reviews trusts and POAs; obtains the CPL and date-down; evaluates the gates for 25.2/26.1/26.3. Decision record: `{application_id, title_order_id, commitment_hash, exceptions[{text, classification, rule}], endorsements_required, endorsements_issued, curative[{kind, resolution, evidence}], agent_vetting, wire_verifications, payoffs[{servicer, good_through, total, refreshed}], subordinations, trust/poa results, gates, rationale, model_version, rule_set_version, confidence}`. Guardrails — the agent may never: accept an impediment outside B7-2-05's minor list without the partner `officer`'s indemnity acknowledgment; release or alter wire instructions; treat a non-ALTA-2021 policy as acceptable for an in-scope loan; proceed with a POA cash-out; use an AOL where barred; skip the callback; compute a funding payoff from a stale statement; disable a gate. Escalations: `officer` (impediment acceptance, AOL program, agent exception) SLA 2 `business_days_creditor`; `funding_approver` (late wire change) same day; `licensed_specialist`/closing attorney where `closing.attorney_required`; `settlement_agent` external tasks with 2-business-day follow-ups; `human_agent` on borrower request. Borrower-facing: every message discloses automation; payoff authorizations and escrow-credit consents are e-signed under `NTC_SM_ESIGN_CONSENT` scope. Human path: ops console with the same commitment view, classification overrides (logged as `agent_decisions.override`), and manual gate evaluation.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Legal description on the commitment differs from the appraisal/deed → `legal_description_mismatch` curative; 24.1/24.3 notified; closing blocked until the underwriter corrects Schedule A.
- Vesting includes a deceased owner or a divorced spouse → curative (probate/affidavit of heirship/deed) owned by the agent; the loan cannot close in the borrower's name alone until cured.
- Unrecorded prior mortgage release → agent obtains release or the underwriter insures over with an indemnity from the prior lender; if neither, treat as `to_be_paid` (funds to the release).
- Title insurer licensed but strength basis unavailable → `underwriter_strength_basis` unknown → hold; the `officer` may accept on reinsurance (Form 858) evidence.
- Agent not on ALTA Registry and no underwriter confirmation → `approved_with_conditions` only with a CPL and a verified callback number from the underwriter; otherwise reject and offer the borrower alternatives.
- Payoff statement with a per-diem that does not reconcile to the note rate (±$0.02 tolerance on the computed per diem) → query the servicer; never "correct" the servicer's figure.
- Existing loan in forbearance/loss-mit or foreclosure → the servicer may take "reasonable time"; the agent extends the follow-up timer with the documented reason and alerts 21.4 on lock exposure.
- Same-servicer case where SM subservices the *existing* loan for a different owner/master servicer: the §1024.34(b)(2)(iii) path still applies ("uses the same servicer"); the payoff funds flow through the existing loan's custodial account (6.x) and Fannie Mae/other-investor payoff reporting is 16.2's.
- HELOC lender refuses to resubordinate → options: pay off the HELOC (DU resubmission; cash-out classification check in 23.1/B2-1.3), or the borrower withdraws; no funding with an unsubordinated lien.
- Trust amended after the certification date → re-review; trustee change invalidates the signature plan.
- POA executed abroad → notarization/apostille requirements per `jurisdiction_rules`; military POAs under 10 U.S.C. 1044b are acceptable where state law recognizes them **[UNVERIFIED — legal review]**.
- Wire instructions arriving by e-mail with a domain that differs from the agent's registered domain → automatic block + callback; any "urgent change" pattern triggers the fraud-risk review (22.6).
- TX 50(a)(6) with a RON request → refused with an explanation; closing rescheduled at an authorized office.
- NY CEMA where the existing lender declines to assign → standard refinance with full mortgage tax; the LE/CD fee change is an 21.5 changed circumstance if the CEMA was assumed.
- Final policy not received after recording → 26.4 trailing-document timer; endorsements missing on the final policy → curative reopened post-closing with the underwriter (Fannie Mae QC exposure logged).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Attorney opinion letter program** — enable the AOL path (B7-2-06) as a borrower cost saver? Recommended default: `title.aol_enabled=false` at launch; revisit after the partner's counsel confirms the "commonly acceptable ... by private institutional mortgage investors in the area" test per state and the warehouse facility accepts AOL collateral.
2. **Commitment age / date-down window** — Fannie Mae sets none. Recommended default: commitment effective date within 30 calendar days of consummation, date-down (bring-down) within 5 business days before funding in dry states; underwriter gap coverage confirmed in writing.
3. **Settlement-agent E&O/fidelity minimums and annual re-vetting** — Recommended default: E&O ≥ $1,000,000 per claim, fidelity/crime ≥ $500,000 (or underwriter CPL covering the loss), annual re-vetting, ALTA Best Practices attestation required for agents handling > 10 SM closings/year.
4. **Same-servicer escrow credit as the default** — Recommended default: offer credit-to-new-loan (§1024.34(b)(2)(iii)) as the default option in the closing package with an explicit consent; refund path if no consent by the CD date.
5. **Wire-change freeze window** — Recommended default: 48 hours before funding; changes inside the window require dual callback and `funding_approver` release.
6. **Payoff per-diem cover in dry states** — Recommended default: request good-through = planned disbursement + 5 calendar days; over-collected interest is refunded by the existing servicer (their obligation), and the CD shows the statement's figure.
