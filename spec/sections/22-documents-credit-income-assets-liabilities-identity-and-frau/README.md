# Section 22 — Documents, credit, income, assets, liabilities, identity and fraud (the verification engine)

<!-- imported from the Origination build specification v1.0 (2026-09-11), section O3; process O3.M is 22.M here. tools/import_origination.py -->

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 22 is the verification engine that turns the conversational application (21.1) into evidence Fannie Mae, the CFPB and a court would accept: 22.1 takes in, classifies, extracts, integrity-checks and age-checks every document and runs the borrower needs-list loop; 22.2 orders and analyzes the credit report, sets the loan's score model and representative score, and manages freezes, disputes, inquiries and undisclosed-debt monitoring through closing; 22.3 documents, calculates and verifies income and employment (DU validation service, Income Calculator, IRS transcripts, self-employment, rental and other income, the verbal VOE windows); 22.4 verifies assets, reserves, cash to close, gifts, EMD, interested-party contributions and subordinate financing; 22.5 builds the liability set, the qualifying payments and the DTI, including debts paid at closing and post-decision liability changes; 22.6 screens identity, fraud, OFAC/BSA, Red Flags, occupancy and non-arm's-length risk and prepares SARs and Fannie Mae self-reports. The section produces the data DU consumes (23.1), the conditions the credit decision clears (23.2/23.3), the ATR/QM verification manifest (23.4), the pricing inputs (representative score and model, 20.4), the delivery codes (SFC 067, 707, 29.3) and the QC evidence bundle (28.1/28.2).

**Agents.** One shared `verification` agent (package `origination/verification`) performs 22.1–22.5 end-to-end with per-process tool sets and versioned formula/rule sets; the `fraud-risk` agent performs 22.6 and receives hand-offs from every other process when a document fails integrity, a credit file mismatches the applicant, or a fraud alert appears. Human touchpoints are the enumerated ones only: `underwriting_reviewer` (integrity failures, dispute investigations that conclude the borrower is responsible, and any verification conclusion that would drive a denial/counteroffer/NOIA under 21.6), `bsa_officer`/`officer` (SAR sign-off and Fannie Mae fraud self-report, 28.4), `licensed_specialist` where a state licenses processing/underwriting (31.1), and `human_agent` on the borrower's request. Every escalation is an `escalation` row with an SLA timer; every decision persists an `agent_decisions` record with inputs, rule-set and model versions and rationale (LL-2026-04).

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the Fannie Mae lender whose representations and warranties cover every verified fact (A3-4-02: responsibility "for the life of the loan"), the FCRA user certifying permissible purpose (§604(f)), the creditor under Reg B §1002.6 and Reg Z §1026.43(c), the Red Flags "creditor", and — for 22.6 — a "loan or finance company" whose BSA/AML program (31 CFR 1029) and OFAC obligations are engaged (28.4's default: SM, as the "residential mortgage originator" that accepts the application in the partner's name, runs its own AML program with its own `bsa_officer` and the parties file joint SARs where both are involved; OFAC binds both); the partner's name is on the Blanket Authorization, Form 1005/1006, Form 4506-C/8821, the vendor authorizations and every borrower notice. SM performs the work under the partner's vendor subscriber codes, DU credit-provider linkage, Technology Manager System IDs and (by decision) the partner's IVES participation; SM holds the loan file as the partner's processor and, from funding, as subservicer; loan-file records become Fannie Mae's property on sale (A2-4.1-02). Verification-report data never transits SM to DU — suppliers transmit to Fannie Mae directly and SM cites identifiers in the casefile (00b-orig Part (c)(5)). No SM signature appears on any loan-file artifact in this section; the only signatures are the borrower's (authorizations, 4506-C/8821), the verifying institution's (Forms 1005/1006), the filing `bsa_officer`'s on SARs (28.4 — SM's own officer under the joint-filing default, or the partner's where the partner files) and the partner officer's authorization of Fannie Mae self-reports (28.4).

**Dependencies.** Upstream: 20.3 (identity/consent capture, soft pull with a consumer-initiated permissible purpose), 21.1 (URLA data, E-SIGN consent `NTC_SM_ESIGN_CONSENT`, joint-intent evidence, Blanket Authorization), 21.2 (LE delivered before verifying documents may be required; credit-report fee is the only pre-intent fee), 21.4 (lock pricing keyed to the representative score). Downstream: 23.1 (DU submissions carry credit reference numbers, validation-report and Income Calculator identifiers; resubmission tolerances under B3-2-10), 23.2/23.3 (conditions and their clearance; rep-and-warrant relief records), 23.4 (ATR "consider and verify" manifest; QM determination), 21.6 (decision clock `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30`, NOIA when the needs list stalls, adverse-action content including the §615(a) score), 21.3 (§609(g)/Reg V score disclosures), 24.1/24.2 (appraisal-age timers referenced, never computed here), 25.2/25.3/26.1 (gates `FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_DOCS_4M`, `FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_REPORT_EXPIRY_4M`, `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD`, `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_SE_VERIFY_120`, `FNMA_B3_2_02_DU_CLOSE_BY_GATE`, `SM_CREDIT_REFRESH_PRECLOSE_GATE` block CD issuance, closing-package release and consummation), 28.1/28.2 (prefunding and post-closing QC reverification, transcript reuse under D1-3-03), 28.3 (HMDA score/model data points), 28.4 (`FNMA_A3_4_03_FRAUD_SELF_REPORT_30`, `BSA_1029_320_SAR_30`, `OFAC_501_603_BLOCKED_REPORT_10BD`), 29.3/29.4 (SFC 067/707 assertions), 31.2 (fair-lending monitoring of needs-list burden, Reg B no-discount checks and model mix), 31.3 (retention classes `regb_25m`, `regz_atr_3y`, `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`, `irs_ives_2y`, `ssa_89_5y`). Servicing: the `documents` table and retention framework are shared with servicing 19.x; the loan file seeded at boarding (30.4) is this section's output; credit-report data may not be reused for the refinance trigger in 20.1 (FCRA transaction-only use).

**Changes in flight that shape the build.** (1) VantageScore 4.0 is broadly available as of Sept. 9, 2026 (LL-2026-06): optional, tri-merge, one model per loan, DU-only, SFC 067, separate LLPA grids; FICO 10T is approved but not deliverable; bi-merge has not happened — `applications.score_model` and the two grid families are day-one requirements. (2) No minimum credit score for DU casefiles (SEL-2025-09) and DU 12.1's tightened "minimum credit risk standards" (June 26–27, 2026) move the gate from a score floor to DU's own assessment; a further DU 12.1 update is scheduled for Sept. 25, 2026. (3) Chapter B3-3 was renumbered in 2026 and the variable-income arithmetic was rewritten (SEL-2026-02); rental income moved to B3-3.8 with new property-management-experience, lease-term, ADU and departing-residence rules mandatory for applications on/after Nov 1, 2026 (SEL-2026-08). (4) Day 1 Certainty now includes rep-and-warrant relief for undisclosed non-mortgage liabilities (DU message 3941, from Nov. 15, 2025) conditioned on closing by the credit report expiration date; SEL-2026-06 accepts Form 8821 in lieu of 4506-C and removes lender review of authorized-user tradelines on DU loans; SEL-2026-05 counts IRS installment agreements as debts. (5) Reg B's effects test was removed effective July 21, 2026 while §1002.6(b) (no discounting of part-time, retirement, public-assistance or alimony income; no age or childbearing assumptions) is unchanged; Colorado SB 26-189 (operative Jan 1, 2027) keeps lending decisions in scope for ADMT notice and explanation. (6) FinCEN's April 10, 2026 AML/CFT NPRM for RMLOs is pending (no final rule; CIP still reserved for non-bank RMLOs), OFAC recordkeeping is now 10 years, and the SFHDF/OFAC/SSA form versions rotate — 22.6 must not hard-code them. (7) LL-2026-04 (effective Aug. 6, 2026) makes AI/ML governance a Lender Contract obligation with vendor flow-down: every classifier, extractor, income formula and fraud model in this section ships with an evaluation suite, version pinning and an override log. (8) Fannie Mae's fraud alert on "Income and Valuation Misrepresentation in New York" (Sept. 2026) and the 2024 best-practices guidance make document forensics and source-obtained verification the default rather than an exception.

## Closing

### Section-level test plan and sequencing

**Build order.** (1) **22.1 first** — the shared `documents` extensions, `document_classes`, extractors, integrity battery and the two freshness engines (`FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_DOCS_4M`, the paystub floor, the tax-year table) are consumed by every other process and by §23/§24/§25; they belong to Stage O-1 with the needs-list notices (`NTC_SM_NEEDS_LIST`, `NTC_SM_NEEDS_LIST_REMINDER`). (2) **22.2** — the `credit` adapter (tri-merge, reissue, UDM, refresh), `applications.score_model`, representative-score logic and `FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_REPORT_EXPIRY_4M`/`SM_CREDIT_REFRESH_PRECLOSE_GATE`; it is the first Stage O-2 rail because DU (23.1), pricing (20.4) and the FCRA/Reg V notices (21.3) cannot run without it. (3) **22.6 identity/OFAC/Red Flags core** in parallel with 22.2 — `SM_IDENTITY_IAL2_GATE`, `OFAC_SDN_SCREEN_GATE`, `FCRA_605A_H_ALERT_CONTACT_GATE`, `FNMA_B2_2_01_SSN_VALIDATION_GATE`, the ITPP log and the two AML programs must exist before the first credit pull and the first DU casefile; the investigation/SAR-package tooling and the occupancy/REO/non-arm's-length models follow in Stage O-3 with 28.4. (4) **22.3** — DU validation-service and Income Calculator adapters, income formulas, VVOE windows (`FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD`, `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_SE_VERIFY_120`, `FNMA_B3_2_02_DU_CLOSE_BY_GATE`), transcripts (`FNMA_B3_3_1_02_4506C_VALID_120`); the B3-3.8 rental rule set ships before Nov 1, 2026. (5) **22.4** — asset adapters, `application_assets`/`asset_deposits`/`gift_records`/`ipc_items`, the 45-day statement gate (`FNMA_B3_4_4_02_ASSET_STMT_45D_GATE`), reserves, IPC and LCOR cash-back tests, and the funds-to-close worksheet with `SM_CASH_TO_CLOSE_RECONCILED_GATE` wired to 25.2's CD figures and 23.3's `CTC_ASSETS_CASH_TO_CLOSE`. (6) **22.5** — `application_liabilities`, `qualifying_payments`, `dti_calculations`, `debt_payoff_plans`, the `FNMA_B3_6_02_DU_DTI_50_GATE`, `FNMA_B3_6_01_LIABILITY_RECALC_GATE` and `FNMA_B3_6_07_PAYOFF_FUNDS_GATE`, wired to 23.1's `du_resubmission_checks` and 23.4's ATR record. (7) Cross-cutting from day one: LL-2026-04 evaluation suites for every classifier/extractor/formula/fraud model (31.2), retention classes (`irs_ives_2y`, `ssa_89_5y`, `ofac_records_10y`, `bsa_sar_5y`, `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`) in 31.3, and the 28.4 hand-off contracts (`sar.candidate.prepared`, `fnma.self_report.candidate.prepared`, `ofac.match.confirmed`).

### Section open questions

1. **22.1-Q1 — Counting "four months."** Default: calendar-month arithmetic with end-of-month clamp; alternative 120 days.
2. **22.1-Q2 — Statement "document date."** Default: statement closing date (`period_end`).
3. **22.1-Q3 — W-2/1099/tax returns and the four-month rule.** Default: not subject; governed by the one-/two-year and tax-year rules.
4. **22.1-Q4 — E-mail intake channel.** Default: verified borrower address with DMARC alignment only.
5. **22.1-Q5 — Forensic vendor thresholds.** Default: vendor "high risk" → `fail`; in-house battery as floor.
6. **22.1-Q6 — Needs-list SLA values.** Default: 5 calendar days (+2/+4 reminders); review 1 `business_days_creditor`; NOIA evaluation at +10.
7. **22.1-Q7 — Row (4) of the tax-return table.** Default: same as row (3); re-read the Sept 2, 2026 PDF.
8. **22.1-Q8 — Human sign-off on agent-cleared `flagged` documents.** Default: no; `failed` always to `underwriting_reviewer`.
9. **22.2-Q1 — Default score model.** Default: Classic FICO with VantageScore 4.0 by partner flag; per-loan selection only after 31.2 review.
10. **22.2-Q2 — Refresh cadence.** Default: UDM through closing plus one soft refresh ≤ 3 `business_days_creditor` before consummation.
11. **22.2-Q3 — Credit-report fee.** Default: borrower-paid on the LE only if the partner's fee schedule says so; otherwise SM-borne.
12. **22.2-Q4 — Two-repository acceptance for a no-hit.** Default: only with reseller no-hit evidence.
13. **22.2-Q5 — Freeze-lift reminders.** Default: +2 days, then every 3 days; NOIA evaluation at +10.
14. **22.2-Q6 — Retention of soft pre-qualification reports.** Default: 25 months if an application follows; else 12 months.
15. **22.3-Q1 — Calendar for B3-3.1-04 business days.** Default: `business_days_creditor`.
16. **22.3-Q2 — VVOE by AI voice.** Default: permitted with automation disclosure; recording only in one-party-consent states.
17. **22.3-Q3 — Transcript policy and channel.** Default: Return Transcripts when returns are relied upon; DU transcript supplier first, IVES A2A second; partner is the IVES participant.
18. **22.3-Q4 — Stable-trend band.** Default: ±5%.
19. **22.3-Q5 — Rental rule-set switch before Nov 1, 2026.** Default: apply B3-3.8 immediately unless it removes previously permitted income.
20. **22.3-Q6 — Self-employment YTD triggers and departing-residence rules.** Default: as 22.3 R4/R5 pending B3-3.6/B3-3.7/B3-3.8-05 re-fetch.
21. **22.3-Q7 — ATR safe-harbor verification standard (June 3, 2020 Guide vs current).** 23.4 decides; default: record both, operate on the current Guide.
22. **22.4-Q1 — Reserve haircuts for securities/retirement.** Default: 0% (Guide text); partner-configurable `haircut_bps`.
23. **22.4-Q2 — Joint accounts with non-borrower co-holders.** Default: full balance usable; access statement only when indicated.
24. **22.4-Q3 — DU asset report as default evidence.** Default: yes; statements only when not aggregatable.
25. **22.4-Q4 — HomeReady cash-on-hand.** Default: disabled until B5-6-02 conditions are verified.
26. **22.4-Q5 — Cryptocurrency without conversion.** Default: conversion required (B3-4.1-04); feature flag `assets.crypto_reserves_unconverted=false`; re-check each Guide edition.
27. **22.4-Q6 — LCOR overage cure.** Default: principal curtailment at closing; loan-amount reduction only pre-CD.
28. **22.4-Q7 — Common-and-customary seller fees by state.** Default: `jurisdiction_rules.customary_seller_fees` maintained with 24.4; anything unlisted counts as an IPC.
29. **22.5-Q1 — Payoff-to-qualify review threshold.** Default: `underwriting_reviewer` when excluded payments > 10% of qualifying income or the payoff consumes > 50% of post-closing liquid assets.
30. **22.5-Q2 — Alimony/equalization/separate-maintenance election.** Default: the lower-DTI treatment, both computations recorded, DU submitted consistently.
31. **22.5-Q3 — Student-loan fallback.** Default: the lower of 1% and the documented amortizing payment; record both.
32. **22.5-Q4 — "Significantly affects" test.** Default: payment > 15% of income or revolving utilization > 80%.
33. **22.5-Q5 — Purchase property-tax estimate.** Default: greater of the current bill and the reassessment estimate (`jurisdiction_rules.reassess_on_sale`), aligned with 30.3.
34. **22.5-Q6 — Non-occupant co-borrower housing expense.** Default: include (conservative) until B3-6-02's exception text is re-read.
35. **22.6-Q1 — Two AML programs (SM as "residential mortgage originator").** Default: SM maintains its own §1029.210 program and prepares as the partner's agent; partner's `bsa_officer` files; legal position before launch.
36. **22.6-Q2 — SAR "initial detection" anchor.** Default: the investigation's conclusion date, bounded by `SM_FRAUD_INVESTIGATION_10BD`.
37. **22.6-Q3 — CBSV enrollee.** Default: SM enrolls and holds SSA-89s; eCBSV eligibility unverified.
38. **22.6-Q4 — ITIN borrowers.** Default: `borrower.itin_enabled=false` until DU/Loan Delivery handling and documentation are confirmed.
39. **22.6-Q5 — Expiring non-permanent-resident documentation.** Default: unexpired at the note date; extension receipts only with the partner flag.
40. **22.6-Q6 — Self-report scope for undelivered loans.** Default: self-report when a SAR is filed or a delivered loan with the same parties exists; 28.4 confirms with Fannie Mae.
41. **22.6-Q7 — Occupancy-model thresholds and biometric-consent states.** Default: R7 thresholds with quarterly 31.2 validation; consent gating per `jurisdiction_rules.biometric_consent_required`.
