### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Loan Delivery is a web application with XML import and UI actions only (Loan Delivery User Guide, Updated May 2026; Loan Delivery FAQs; 00b-orig F6, Part (b)/(c)).** The User Guide describes XML import ("MISMO V3.0 Reference Model, established under the Uniform Mortgage Data Program® (UMDP®)"; Home → Import → Whole Loan Delivery tab → optional Commitment Number override → "Import File") and manual entry; neither the User Guide nor the FAQs documents an API or direct integration for Loan Delivery (00b-orig: "no API/DI documented… the `secondary` agent builds ULDD XML and a human/RPA-free path does not exist for the submit click"). The Consolidated Technology Guide forbids scraping/RPA against Fannie Mae UIs (baseline §0). Status definitions (User Guide pp. 42–43): **Draft** "Loan record created in Loan Delivery, but not submitted"; **Purchase Requested** "Loan submitted, awaiting certification"; **Purchase Error** "Loan submitted and certified, fatal edit. Once error is resolved, 2nd submit required"; **Purchase Ready** "Loan certified, awaiting purchase and funding"; **Purchased and Funded** "Loan is purchased and funding disbursed". Certification status: blank "Loan has not been submitted for certification"; **Awaiting Certification** "Loan has yet to be certified or Data revision identified awaiting seller response"; **Certified** "Loan is certified with no changes"; **Qualified Cert** "Loan is certified with a data change". Submission: "the eligible loan is submitted successfully and is assigned a Fannie Mae Loan Number"; certification status becomes "Awaiting Certification" and loan status moves from "Draft" to "Purchase Requested" (pp. 59–60). Users may "update non-certifiable fields directly in Loan Delivery up until a loan is in Purchase Ready status" (p. 61); "Users should reference the Cancel Certification section…to move a submitted loan back to Draft status" (pp. 61–62); "*LLPAs calculated based on the Purchase Ready date indicated in Loan Delivery" (p. 44). Edits: Fatal "must be corrected to successfully submit"; Warning "may be bypassed for successful submission"; prefixes "A" (UCDP appraisal data), "C" (UCD closing data), "D" (DU), 3000-series commitment/contract (FAQ Q9; 00b-orig F6); Edit History report in CSV. Purchase timing (User Guide p. 64): "If Fannie Mae's document custodian receives the complete loan documentation package by 7:30 a.m. ET on the day following an error-free transmission of loan data, Fannie Mae will purchase the loan and wire proceeds by the next business day"; FAQ Q36: "Proceeds are typically wired to the lender 24 hours after the purchase of the loan." Commitments: "All mandatory commitments automatically appear in Loan Delivery, and all Best Efforts commitments are available in Loan Delivery once moved to a closed status" (FAQ Q26); expiration dates display in red when approaching (p. 40). Custodians: the seller must have "Executed a custodial agreement Form 2017 with an approved full-service certification custodian (FCC)"; custodians are added in Seller Profile with FIN, Seller Number and Delivery Type; "When you submit a loan to Fannie Mae, the loan data is also transmitted to the specified document custodian for certification (based on the Financial Institution Number (FIN))"; "Fannie Mae acts as the custodian for all eNotes and provides in-house automated document certification (auto-certification) for eligible eNotes" (p. 14). Wires: "Cash wire instructions use payee codes to instruct Fannie Mae where to deliver your organization's whole loan purchase proceeds. You can assign payee codes at the loan level"; "New payee codes cannot be added directly in Loan Delivery. Complete and submit Form 482 to add a new payee code" (pp. 22–23); for loans delivered with a bailee letter, Wire Details → Receiver Type "Warehouse Lender" → Letter Type "Bailee" or "2004A" → status Pending until the warehouse lender approves (Active) (pp. 23–26). **VERIFIED.**

**Warehouse lender side (Loan Delivery User Guide for Warehouse Lenders, September 2025; Form 2004A; ULDD FAQ Q5/Q6).** The warehouse lender reviews the wire instructions, must enter the text from the physical bailee letter header in the "Letter Name" field, and clicks "Approve" (Pending → Active); "The name entered in Loan Delivery must exactly match the Bailee letterhead text in order to pass the Bailee validation" (p. 5); "A notification is sent to a warehouse lender each time a lender lists such warehouse lender in their wire instructions" (p. 9); registration requires "at least two active Corporate Administrators" and "takes 1 to 2 weeks" (p. 7). **Form 2004A, Release of Interest in Mortgages (January 2018):** the warehouse lender agrees to "relinquish any and all right, title or interest it may have in the Mortgages to be acquired by Fannie Mae" upon transfer of the proceeds according to the delivery instructions; "The warehouse lender agrees to waiving their wire instructions listed on their Bailee letter by signing Form 2004A"; "The warehouse lender's security interest will only be released if the proceeds…are delivered to the disbursement agent"; the form carries a warehouse-lender signature block only, consents to electronic records and signatures, and the warehouse lender must mail the original to "the Fannie Mae document custodian," retain one copy and send one to the mortgage seller. ULDD: "A Payee Identifier (Payee Code) is required for: Cash loans (Whole Loans)…if the loan is under Bailee Letter at time of delivery" (Sort ID 642); Warehouse Lender Identifier (Sort ID 650.1). **VERIFIED.**

**Whole-loan good delivery and timing (Selling Guide C2-2-01, 08/07/2018; C2-2-02, 05/01/2019; C2-2-03, 05/04/2022; C2-2-04, 05/04/2022).** C2-2-01: "the loans delivered must meet all of Fannie Mae's underwriting and legal criteria and satisfy the terms of the original commitment"; "the applicable error-free mortgage documents and data reach the Fannie Mae-approved document custodian no later than the **first morning delivery**, the day prior to the expiration date of the commitment"; the data must pass all purchasing edits; "the first payment date must be no later than two months from the final disbursement date of the loan proceeds" (26.3 `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M` — referenced); over-delivery "25% of the original commitment amount", thereafter "$50 above the new commitment amount" (29.1). C2-2-02: "See E-2-01, Required Custodial Documents, for a list of the mortgage documents that must be included in the document submission package for all loans"; "Mortgage document submission packages must be submitted to the document custodian via overnight delivery (with instructions for first morning delivery)"; the seller must "ensure the loan document submission package is submitted to its overnight carrier (with instructions for first morning delivery) the same day that the seller submits the loan data to Fannie Mae for purchase"; "Loan data delivered by the seller to Fannie Mae will be certified the next business day provided the documents have been delivered to the document custodian via first morning delivery." C2-2-03: "Fannie Mae will send the proceeds from the whole loan sale via a wire transfer into the lender's designated account on the 'Purchased and Funded' date (as reflected in Loan Delivery) if: the designated payee code is valid; the loan is eligible for purchase; and the document custodian received a complete, accurate mortgage document submission package and loan delivery data by the applicable cutoff times"; "To avoid delays in purchasing and funding, Fannie Mae accepts delivery of any mortgage up to 45 days from the due date of the reported last paid installment"; "When Fannie Mae purchases a mortgage, the mortgage seller represents and warrants that title to the mortgage note is free and clear of any security interest, lien, pledge, or other encumbrance, which means that any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note"; procedures apply when the custodian "receives with the mortgage delivery documents a bailee letter notifying it of a warehouse lender's security interest, or is otherwise aware that a warehouse lender is claiming an interest of any kind in the mortgage notes being delivered"; "A lender is solely liable for transfers that are initiated (either directly or indirectly) as the result of a breach in its security arrangements or as the result of its failure to give Fannie Mae timely notice of an error, omission, or irregularity in establishing payee arrangements." C2-2-04: "Fannie Mae will fund the purchase proceeds for whole loan deliveries of regularly amortizing mortgages provided the following procedure has already occurred": the "lender has electronically submitted clean, error-free loan delivery data through Loan Delivery by 9:00 p.m. (Eastern time)"; "the document custodian has received a complete and accurate mortgage document submission package from a lender by first morning delivery the following day"; "the document custodian has certified the mortgage document submission package"; "Following the funding of the purchase proceeds the loan status in Loan Delivery will be 'Purchased and Funded'"; "If the required delivery data and documentation are not received by the applicable cutoff time, Fannie Mae will disburse the purchase proceeds on the business day following the date it receives these materials, assuming they are in order and the mortgage is eligible for purchase." **VERIFIED.**

**Loan age and seasoning (Selling Guide B2-1.5-02 Loan Eligibility, 06/05/2024).** "To be eligible for purchase by Fannie Mae on a flow basis, a loan must be no more than six months old measured from the first payment date to the 'Purchase Ready' date." "Seasoned loans are loans that are more than one year old from the first payment date to" "the loan purchase date for whole loans, or the pool issue date for MBS loans"; seasoned-loan conditions include "The borrower has not had a 30-day delinquency in the 12-month period that precedes" delivery and "The current value of the property cannot be less than the original value". HOEPA/state high-cost loans are "not eligible for delivery to Fannie Mae" (23.4). **VERIFIED.**

**Custodial documents and certification (E-2-01, 05/04/2022; A3-3-04 and A3-3-05, 05/04/2022; Requirements for Document Custodians (RDC) Version 15.0, August 2025; Document Custodian Certification Job Aids Version 4.0, August 2025; Document Certification page).** E-2-01 (all mortgages): a cover letter (lender name, contact, loan number, property address, document list); "The original note endorsed 'in blank' and without recourse and there is no break in the chain of endorsements"; "Originals of any instruments that modify the terms and conditions of the mortgage note"; a repair rider where a set-aside for repairs applies; power of attorney (copy, or original "if applicable law mandates it"); name affidavit "if the borrower signed under an 'also known as (AKA) name' or used a signature that significantly differs from the typed name"; New York CEMA consolidated note and recorded CEMA; "Notes signed by trustee" (borrower as individual and trustee); "For MERS registered loans, the MIN should be added to the upper right-hand corner of the note." A3-3-05: the custodian holds "Original note and note addenda" and "Other documents needed for certification of whole loans…such as powers of attorney, and interest rate buydown agreements"; all other documents stay in the seller/servicer's file; releases require a request "containing substantially the same information as required by *Request for Release/Return of Documents* (Form 2009)". A3-3-04 (00b-orig F8): "Each custodian arrangement must be evidenced by the execution of a Master Custodial Agreement (Form 2017)"; "For whole loans or MBS loans delivered as eNotes, Fannie Mae is the document custodian but has designated certain third parties to certify eNotes". Document Certification page: "Document Certification is an online tool for document custodians to certify MBS pools and whole loans"; "Loan data submitted by lenders to Fannie Mae via Loan Delivery is available to the document custodian in Document Certification"; "On the same day the lender submits the loan data in Loan Delivery, they must also ship related loan documents to the document custodian for certification and retention"; "By certifying the loan data, the document custodian is confirming that it has the required documents in its possession; has reviewed the data, and has found no errors or inconsistencies"; "It transmits the certification status to Fannie Mae and the lender." Job Aids v4.0: certification for cash loans "must be completed within the daily agreed upon SLA" — "CASH, ASAP, MBS: 4:00 PM EST"; custodian-editable data fields include property address/city/state/zip, loan amount, interest rate, payment dates and P&I; non-editable fields (Bailee Letter Name, MERS MIN, Special Feature Code, Cooperative Indicator, Participation Percentage, rounding) must be corrected by the seller in Loan Delivery; when the custodian submits a data revision "Data is locked, and the document custodian no longer has the capability to make changes"; the lender "accepts" (status "Qualified-Cert") or "declines" (status returns to "Pending"); document exceptions must be flagged "in every instance"; bailee packages: "the Document Custodian must immediately perform a loan inventory to validate that Documents for the loans listed on Loan List are in the loan package", the letterhead name is matched against the certification data with "a secondary quality control check…prior to certification", a mismatch "must [be flagged] as a document exception(s) that cannot be certified", and bailee documentation "must be retained for at least two (2) years following certification". RDC v15.0 §5.1: Document Certification is a "Web-based application that Document Custodians must use to certify Mortgage Loans and transmit certification status to Fannie Mae and the Seller"; §1.3 (Definitions) "Certification: Notice by the Document Custodian to Fannie Mae that…it has examined all the related documents required for certification" and that the loan data "matches the terms of those Documents, without exception" (the certification procedure itself is §8 Document Review and Certification / §9 Data Review and Certification); §8.1 (per the RDC's Version 12.1, April 2019 revision note) the borrower's name on the note "does not need to be compared to the loan data"; §2.1 the servicer "is responsible for working with the Document Custodian to resolve any document or data errors that are subsequently discovered". The RDC's own service-level table (§9.4.1) and full data-element comparison table were not readable **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Job Aids v4.0 used for the SLA and fields]**. **VERIFIED otherwise.**

**eNote delivery (C1-2-04, 12/13/2023; B8-8-01, 11/05/2025; eClosing/eMortgage FAQs; MERS eRegistry Procedures; 00b-orig F9).** "The eNote and any documents required for certification…must be transmitted to Fannie Mae's eVault via MERS eDelivery"; "A 'Transfer of Control and Location' request must be transmitted to the MERS eRegistry to initiate the transfer of ownership"; "The transfer request must include an 'Effective Date' of the same day as the request"; warehouse providers with a security interest must have an "eNote Control Transfer and Custodial Agreement" with Fannie Mae (FAQ Q9: "In lieu of a Bailee Letter, we utilize a bilateral funding and custodial agreement between Fannie Mae and the warehouse funding provider" that serves as "a master bailee agreement for eNotes transferred to us by the warehouse provider"); "Fannie Mae provides in-house automated document certification (auto-certification) for eligible eNotes"; the seller must "Add the eNote Indicator flag to the corresponding loan(s) in Fannie Mae's Loan Delivery application"; "Fannie Mae requires that the MERS eRegistry records…contain the Servicer's MERS Org ID…in the 'Master Servicer' field"; after purchase Fannie Mae is Controller and Location of the Authoritative Copy. FAQ Q3 sequence: sign and tamper-seal; "The eNote is registered on the MERS® eRegistry within one business day"; "The lender transmits the eNote and other investor documents using MERS® eDelivery to Fannie Mae's eVault"; "The lender initiates a transfer of Control & Location to Fannie Mae via the MERS® eRegistry"; "The lender submits delivery data to Fannie Mae, including the eNote Indicator in the loan delivery file". FAQ Q4: eDelivery may carry "other needed documents for certification (like a certified POA, if used)". FAQ Q10: "eNotes are machine-readable XML documents which permit key data to be extracted and compared directly to loan data submitted to Fannie Mae"; "the eNote Indicator in the Uniform Loan Delivery Dataset (ULDD) file reflects the presence of an eNote which triggers that auto-certification process"; for non-auto-certified products "the seller's custodian will complete a manual review by accessing documents in Fannie Mae's eVault". MERS eRegistry (Rel. 16.25): Secured Party role, Transfer of Control / Location / Control and Location transactions, digitally signed XML, HTTPS notifications. **VERIFIED.**

**Purchase advice and payee codes (C2-2-05, 10/04/2023; C2-2-06 and C2-2-07, 05/01/2024; C2-1.1-06, 06/28/2011; Purchase Advice APIs).** C2-2-05: the Purchase Advice lists "each loan, identified by a Fannie Mae loan number (which the servicer should enter into its records immediately because it must be used in reporting all subsequent activity related to the mortgage to Fannie Mae)" with "loan information, price adjustments, and purchase proceeds" and "a report with the applicable payee code for each wire transfer request"; it "will be available on the business day Fannie Mae funds the purchase proceeds" in Fannie Mae Connect; "The lender must submit a post-purchase adjustment request to provide notice of any error, omission, or irregularity in the transfer of funds or in processing the request for the repetitive transfer within 30 days of the date of the Purchase Advice." C2-2-06: the lender authorizes Fannie Mae to "transfer purchase proceeds for whole loan deliveries" to the designated accounts after providing an executed Form 360; "A repetitive transfer is one in which all aspects of the transfer, other than the date of the transfer and the amount of funds transferred, remain constant over time"; the lender is "fully responsible for any and all losses incurred by Fannie Mae that result from Fannie Mae's reliance on any instruction given to Fannie Mae by the lender's authorized representatives." C2-2-07: lenders must "specify a valid and appropriate payee code at delivery"; "The lender's authorized representative submits a *Seller's Designation of Wire Transfer Instructions* (Form 482) to Fannie Mae"; Fannie Mae "will call the authorized representative who signed the Form 482 request to confirm validity prior to processing"; changes/deletions require a new Form 482 and Fannie Mae will "make the requested change within three business days"; emergency verbal deletions are possible but "Fannie Mae cannot guarantee that it will be able to act quickly enough to prevent the execution of transfers." C2-1.1-06 (00a-fnma §5.5): A/A — "Fannie Mae purchases accrued interest from the last paid installment date for the mortgage up to, but not including, the purchase date" on the UPB "at the time it is submitted for purchase" at "the designated pass-through rate"; S/S and S/A — "from the first day of the purchase month up to, but not including, the purchase date"; "If interest is prepaid, Fannie Mae deducts accrued interest from the purchase proceeds"; no day-count convention is stated **[UNVERIFIED — 27.2 resolves from the Purchase Advice field definitions]**. Purchase Advice APIs (00b-orig F7): "Whole Loan Purchase Advice Sellers" and "Servicers" APIs return "daily-updated purchase advice details currently available through Fannie Mae Connect" in "comprehensive JSON file format"; field-level layout not public **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. **VERIFIED otherwise.**

**Post-purchase data corrections (C1-2-02; Seller/Servicer-Initiated PPA page, FAQs and Job Aid dated 4.29.26).** C1-2-02: lenders "must notify Fannie Mae when they determine there are discrepancies in the data attributes submitted to Fannie Mae at loan delivery and their loan records", "must follow the post-purchase adjustment process", and "must submit data corrections within 18 months of the loan's acquisition date to be considered for repricing of loan-level price adjustments (LLPAs)." PPA page: "A post-purchase adjustment (PPA) may be required if a data discrepancy is discovered between a lender's loan records and the data attributes submitted at the time of delivery to Fannie Mae"; lenders and servicers "submit data changes via LSDU when a discrepancy exists…" through "the PPA web portal via LSDU" ("Loan Data Change tab") with a PPA Request Form; Fannie Mae-initiated: "Fannie Mae's Loan Quality Connect system (LQCS) manages the data validation process." FAQs: "There is an 18-month lookback period (from the date of acquisition) for all post-purchase adjustments of loan-level price adjustments" (Q9); data corrections have "no lookback period or threshold" (Q9); "There is a $100 minimum (credit or debit) threshold for PPA loan-level price adjustments" (Q9); credit score changes use the "Borrower Credit Score" attribute (Q13) and "Borrower Credit Score Source Type" (Q14); "The 'P&I Amt' adjustment code should be used only to correct the P&I Amt as of the acquisition date" (Q4); supporting-document naming "FM Loan No._Document Name.pdf", 3MB max (Q6). Job Aid (4.29.26): "Log into the PPA web portal via Loan Servicing Data Utility and proceed to the Loan Data Change tab"; a .csv "Sample PPA File"; CAPTCHA submit; standard processing "10 business days from initial submission through data correction"; "Whole loan LLPA drafts or refunds typically occur within five business days"; "Review the completed Post-Purchase Adjustment Notification report available in Fannie Mae Connect"; "The 18-month lookback period does not apply to the drafting or collection of repurchase alternative fees"; VantageScore attributes "Vantage4 Borrower Credit Score" and "Vantage4 Borrower Credit Score Source Type" exist alongside "FICO4". **VERIFIED.**

**Rep and warrant relief (A2-2-04, 04/01/2026; A2-2-06, 06/03/2026; A2-3.2-02, 08/06/2025; 23.3).** A2-2-04: the limited waiver for DU Approve/Eligible loans requires that "All data pertaining to the mortgage loan is complete, accurate, and not fraudulent", that "All data on which DU's recommendation is based complies with Fannie Mae's verification requirements", SFC 127 and delivery "with DU-assigned unique casefile ID"; it excludes product eligibility, property-related reps, appraisals and seasoned loans; DU validation relief requires that "The DU message must indicate that the amount of income entered into DU was validated", the loan closes by the "Close by Date", and all verification messages/approval conditions are resolved; Income Calculator relief: "the amount of income used for qualifying cannot be more than the amount calculated by the tool"; undisclosed non-mortgage liabilities: "Fannie Mae will not enforce representations and warranties related to such debt obtained by the borrower(s) prior to or concurrent with the day of closing" when DU issues the relief message on the final submission — "Undisclosed mortgage-related debt (including HELOCs and second liens) are excluded". A2-2-06: CU relief for one-unit properties when the appraisal "receive[s] a CU risk score of 2.5 or below" (the lender is not responsible for "ensuring the appraisal accurately reflects the market value of the property" but "remains responsible for the description of the property, adherence to fair lending…and the accuracy and completeness of all data on the appraisal"); value acceptance: "Fannie Mae accepts the value estimate submitted by the lender as the value for the subject property"; the topic's table assigns "Value and marketability" = not warranted for value acceptance and VA+PD, with VA+PD retaining "Physical property characteristics" and "Property eligibility including condition" (including "warranty that the property is safe, sound, and structurally secure") **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — table as extracted]**. A2-3.2-02: relief on "payment by the borrower of the first 36 monthly payments" with no more than two 30-day delinquencies and no 60-day delinquency; on Fannie Mae's "full-file QC review" with an acceptable conclusion (or cured deficiencies, or expiry of a repurchase alternative); life-of-loan exclusions (Charter Act matters, misstatements/misrepresentations/omissions, data inaccuracies, clear title/first lien, compliance with laws, acceptable mortgage products — A2-2-07); "Fannie Mae will provide lenders with reports listing those loans that met the eligibility requirements for relief." **VERIFIED.**

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Loan Delivery (UI/API)" — there is **no** Loan Delivery API or direct integration (00b-orig F6); the process is file + UI with a human operator; "Loan Delivery user guide/API" in the sources column is wrong.
2. "Certification before commitment expiry" understates the rule: **documents and data must reach the custodian by the first morning delivery on the day before the commitment expiration date** (C2-2-01), and the custodian package must ship the same day as the data submission (C2-2-02; Document Certification page).
3. The blueprint omits the loan-age rule ("no more than six months old measured from the first payment date to the 'Purchase Ready' date", B2-1.5-02) and the seasoned-loan definition (> 1 year from first payment to purchase date), and states the 45-day rule vaguely: it is "45 days from the due date of the reported last paid installment" (C2-2-03).
4. "Funding by Fannie Mae per settlement schedule" — whole-loan funding is event-driven: 9:00 p.m. ET data cutoff, first-morning documents (7:30 a.m. ET in the User Guide), custodian certification (custodian SLA 4:00 p.m. EST for cash), then proceeds "on the business day following" (C2-2-04) — no settlement calendar.
5. "Post-delivery corrections" are two different mechanisms: pre-purchase custodian **data revisions** (Qualified Cert) and non-certifiable field edits in Loan Delivery; post-purchase **PPAs in LSDU** (18-month LLPA lookback; $100 minimum; 10-business-day processing) and Fannie Mae-initiated data validation through Loan Quality Connect.
6. The "Purchase Advice API" is two Developer Portal products (Sellers and Servicers) delivering daily JSON; the purchase advice is also "available on the business day Fannie Mae funds" in Fannie Mae Connect (C2-2-05) — the blueprint's "Fannie Mae Connect Purchase Advice" is the report, not the interface.
7. Wire designation for whole loans is by **payee code** (Form 482, C2-2-07; ULDD Sort ID 642), not by wire nickname (see 29.3 discrepancy 3); Form 482 changes take Fannie Mae "three business days", so payee setup is a prerequisite, not a per-loan step.
8. eNotes have no paper custodian step: Fannie Mae is the custodian, certification is automated from the eNote XML, and the warehouse interest is handled by the eNote Control Transfer and Custodial Agreement, not a bailee letter.
9. Rep & warrant relief is not "tracked at delivery" by any Fannie Mae acknowledgement; eligibility is asserted from delivery data (SFC 127/801/774, casefile, DU messages) and **confirmed only by Fannie Mae's relief reports** (A2-3.2-02) — the platform records `eligible` at delivery and `confirmed_by_fnma` on the report.
10. Repurchase/make-whole "execution" from the delivery side is limited to funding the demand and returning collateral; the book removal is servicing 5.6 (LAR 65/67), the appeal ladder is 28.2.

#### State machine

`deliveries.loan_delivery_status` mirrors Loan Delivery and is only ever set from evidence (`status_source`), never from an intent to act: `not_started` → `draft` (import evidence captured; Fannie Mae loan record exists without a loan number) → `purchase_requested` (submit evidence with Fannie Mae loan number; `certification_status = awaiting_certification`) → `purchase_ready` (custodian certification observed: `certified` / `qualified_cert` / `auto_certified`; `purchase_ready_at` set — the LLPA date) → `purchased_and_funded` (purchase advice or Connect report; `purchase_date`, `acquisition_date`) [terminal]. Branches: `purchase_requested` → `purchase_error` (fatal edit after certification; operator resolves, "2nd submit required") → `purchase_requested`; `purchase_requested`/`purchase_ready` → `cancelled_to_draft` (Cancel Certification — e.g., stale package superseded by a corrected CD, wrong commitment, custodian document exception that cannot be cured before expiry) → `draft` with `delivery_attempt + 1`; any pre-purchase state → `withdrawn` (loan not deliverable: rescinded within the extended right, repurchased by SM/partner from the warehouse, commitment paired off — 29.1) [terminal]. `certification_status`: `none` → `awaiting_certification` → {`certified`, `qualified_cert` (seller accepted the custodian's data revision), `auto_certified` (eNote)}; a declined revision returns to `awaiting_certification` ("Pending" on the custodian side) until resolved or cancelled. Post-purchase sub-machine (`post_purchase_adjustments.status`): `open` → `submitted` → `processed` (notification report; LLPA draft/refund) → `closed` | `declined`. Guards: `import_and_submit` task cannot open unless `SM_O103_PACKAGE_FREEZE_GATE` is open and `wire_instructions.status = active` for the payee code (or `pending` with the SM warehouse-approval task opened in parallel and completed before submit); `submit` evidence is accepted only when the operator confirmed the package hash (`hash_confirmed`) and the captured commitment number equals `commitments.commitment_id_fnma`; `purchase_ready` is not set from an operator screenshot alone when a custodian notice is expected — both sources are reconciled within one business day.

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — LPI and the 45-day window.** `lpi_due_date` is the due date of the last installment actually paid; for a newly disbursed loan with no payment posted, the platform reports the conventional LPI = `first_payment_date − 1 month` (the loan is interest-paid through the day before, because prepaid interest at closing covers disbursement through month-end — 26.3) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED convention; matches the investor-reporting LPI definition in servicing 5.1]**. `delivery_lpi_due = lpi_due_date + 45 calendar days`. Fixture: first payment Fri Jan 1, 2027 → LPI Tue Dec 1, 2026 → **Fri Jan 15, 2027** (Dec 1 + 30 = Dec 31; + 15 = Jan 15). If the Jan 1 installment posts before purchase, LPI → Jan 1, 2027 → **Mon Feb 15, 2027**. Loan age: first payment Jan 1, 2027 → Purchase Ready no later than **Wed Jun 30, 2027** (the sixth month counting January as month one; Jul 1, 2027 — the same-day anniversary — would already be "more than six months old" under B2-1.5-02's example); seasoned on **Sat Jan 1, 2028**. Fixture purchase Thu Nov 19, 2026 is 57 days before the LPI deadline and 223 days before the loan-age deadline.

**R2 — Custodian and purchase timing (paper note).** Expected dates are computed, never assumed: `expected_receipt = next business_days_fannie_et after submitted_at (ET) at 07:30`; `expected_certification = expected_receipt date` (custodian cash SLA 4:00 p.m. EST) when received by the custodian's cutoff, else the following business day; `expected_purchase = expected_certification + 1 business_days_fannie_et` (User Guide p. 64: "purchase the loan and wire proceeds by the next business day"; C2-2-04: proceeds "on the business day following the date it receives these materials"). Commitment guard: `receipt_deadline = commitments.expires_on − 1 day` at first-morning delivery; the operator task is scheduled so that `expected_receipt ≤ receipt_deadline` with one business day of slack.

**Worked example A — refinance fixture, paper note under SM's bailee letter (dates per the fixture).** Disbursed Thu Nov 12, 2026; ULDD frozen Fri Nov 13 10:05 MST (29.3); best-efforts commitment from the Wed Oct 7 lock expires Mon Dec 7, 2026 (29.1: lock expiry Sat Nov 21 + 14-day buffer = Sat Dec 5, rolled to the next `fannie_sifma` business day) → `FNMA_C2_2_01_CUSTODIAN_RECEIPT_BEFORE_EXPIRY` is anchored on Sun Dec 6 ("the day prior to the expiration date") and, because no first-morning delivery exists on a Sunday, the platform's due-at rolls backward to Fri Dec 4 07:30 ET. Operator task due Mon Nov 16 15:00 MT (`SM_LOAN_DELIVERY_OPERATOR_SLA_1BD`: one `business_days_creditor` after Fri Nov 13; Sat/Sun excluded). Mon Nov 16 11:20 MT (13:20 ET): operator imports the frozen XML (hash confirmed), Loan Delivery reports 0 fatal / 0 warning edits, commitment number matches, payee code (SM warehouse account; Form 482 on file since onboarding) assigned at loan level with Receiver Type "Warehouse Lender", Letter Type "Bailee"; SM's warehouse-lender org had already approved the instruction (`status = active`, `bailee_letter_name` = the letterhead text "SUPERMORTGAGE WAREHOUSE LENDING, LLC" byte-for-byte); operator submits 13:31 ET → Fannie Mae loan number captured; `loan_delivery_status = purchase_requested`, `certification_status = awaiting_certification`; `submit_before_2100_et = true`. Same day: the `post-closing` agent's package (cover letter, bailee letter listing the loan, note already endorsed in blank by the partner's `signing_officer` on Fri Nov 13, no POA, no name affidavit, deed of trust copy not required for certification) is tendered to the carrier at 15:40 MT for first-morning delivery Tue Nov 17 (`FNMA_C2_2_02_SHIP_SAME_DAY_AS_SUBMIT` satisfied). Actual: carrier weather delay; package delivered Tue Nov 17 17:05 ET (`received_by_0730_et = false`; `FNMA_LD_UG_CUSTODIAN_0730_ET_EXPECTATION` missed — carrier claim logged, no Fannie Mae obligation breached); the custodian inventories the bailee package Tue evening and certifies **Wed Nov 18 07:15 ET** (`certification_kind = certified`, bailee validation passed, no data revisions) → `purchase_ready`, `purchase_ready_at = 2026-11-18` (the LLPA date); Fannie Mae purchases and wires **Thu Nov 19** (`purchased_and_funded`; C2-2-04's "business day following"); the Purchase Advice is available Thu Nov 19 in Fannie Mae Connect and in the Sellers API on Fri Nov 20's daily pull (the platform polls the API each morning and reconciles). `FNMA_C2_2_04_CERTIFICATION_EXPECTED_1BD` (received Nov 17 after cutoff → expected Nov 18) and `FNMA_C2_2_04_PURCHASE_EXPECTED_1BD` (Nov 18 + 1 → Nov 19) are satisfied on the recomputed expectations. Cost of the one-day slip: one extra day of SM warehouse interest on the $548,800.00 advance (98% advance rate — 27.1 rule 2 and worked example A) — at 27.1's worked all-in rate 6.80% (SOFR 4.30% + 2.50%, act/360), 548,800 × 0.0680 ÷ 360 = **$103.66** (27.1 owns the rate, day count and cumulative rounding) — borne inside SM's economics, not by the partner or borrower.

**R3 — Purchase advice reconciliation (formula owned by 27.2; 29.4 checks tie-out).** `principal_proceeds = round(upb_cents × price ÷ 100)`; `interest_adjustment` per C2-1.1-06 for the loan's remittance type; `llpa_total = Σ LLPA lines (from the 09.09.2026 matrix as applied at the Purchase Ready date)`; `net_proceeds = principal_proceeds + interest_adjustment − llpa_total − fees`. Illustrative tie-out for Example A (A/A remittance; all inputs marked illustrative except the UPB, rate and dates): UPB 56,000,000 cents; note rate 6.125%; servicing fee 0.250% → pass-through 5.875%; commitment price 101.125000 → principal proceeds 56,000,000 × 1.01125 = **56,630,000 cents ($566,300.00)**. Interest: A/A purchases interest "from the last paid installment date…up to, but not including, the purchase date"; LPI Dec 1, 2026 is after the purchase date Nov 19, so interest is prepaid and "Fannie Mae deducts accrued interest from the purchase proceeds" for Nov 19–Nov 30 = 12 days: on a 30/360 basis 56,000,000 × 0.05875 ÷ 360 × 12 = **−109,667 cents (−$1,096.67)**; on an actual/365 basis −108,164 cents (−$1,081.64) **[UNVERIFIED day count — 27.2 resolves from the Purchase Advice field definitions; the platform's pre-computation is stored as a range until then]**. LLPA (illustrative 0.125% = 70,000 cents; 20.4/29.1 own the matrix lookup for LCOR/762/70%). Net proceeds (30/360 case) = 56,630,000 − 109,667 − 70,000 = **56,450,333 cents ($564,503.33)**. Tie-out rule: `variance_cents = advice.net_proceeds − expected_net_proceeds`; |variance| ≤ 100 cents → auto-reconciled; otherwise the `secondary` agent decomposes the variance by component (price, interest days, LLPA line, fee) and, if a Fannie Mae error is indicated, files the adjustment request within 27.2's `FNMA_C2_2_05_PPA_REQUEST_30` (due **Sat Dec 19, 2026** from an advice dated Nov 19 → the platform's due-at is the preceding business day, Fri Dec 18). Proceeds are received in SM's warehouse account (payee code under the bailee letter); 27.2 repays the advance and settles the remainder with the partner the same day (`warehouse.advance.repaid{repaid_from=purchase_proceeds}`), and `FNMA_C2_2_03_WAREHOUSE_RELEASE_BY_ACQUISITION_GATE` is satisfied by the bailee terms (release effective on receipt of proceeds; `warehouse.bailee_letter.released{effective=2026-11-19}` — 27.2; eNote path: `warehouse.secured_party.released`).

**R4 — Custodian data revisions (Qualified Cert).** If the custodian proposes a revision to a custodian-editable field (address, loan amount, rate, payment dates, P&I), the `secondary` agent compares the custodian value to the note (`closing_documents` data hash) and the ULDD: (a) note = custodian value ≠ ULDD → the ULDD was wrong → **accept** (Qualified Cert) and open a `delivery.edit.observed` so 29.3 corrects the source record (and 25.2/26.1 check whether the CD/note themselves need a post-consummation correction); (b) note = ULDD ≠ custodian value → **decline** with the note page reference; the custodian re-examines; (c) non-editable field (MIN, SFC, bailee letter name) → the operator corrects in Loan Delivery (non-certifiable fields may be updated "up until a loan is in Purchase Ready status"), or Cancel Certification → Draft → re-import if the ULDD must change. Acceptance is an operator UI action prepared by the agent with the evidence page; response within `SM_LD_DATA_REVISION_RESPONSE_1BD`.

**R5 — Bailee letter and Form 2004A mechanics (SM as warehouse lender).** Every warehoused paper loan is delivered "under Bailee Letter at time of delivery" (SID 642 payee code + SID 650.1 warehouse identifier). SM's bailee letter (27.1 template) lists the loan(s), the letterhead text equals `wire_instructions.bailee_letter_name`, and the wire instructions direct proceeds to SM's payee code; the custodian validates letterhead ↔ Letter Name and flags a mismatch as a document exception "that cannot be certified". Form 2004A is used only when SM waives its bailee wire instructions in favor of a disbursement agent (multi-lender arrangements) — not the default; if used, SM's officer signs, the original goes to the custodian with the package, copies to SM's file and the partner. Release of interest is effective when proceeds reach SM's account (bailee terms) — same day as purchase, satisfying C2-2-03.

**R6 — Pre-purchase corrections and re-planning.** A superseded package after submission → Cancel Certification (operator) → Draft → re-import of the new frozen file (new hash) → second submit; documents already at the custodian stay in custody (a new cover letter is sent only if the document set changes). Commitment expiry approaching with an uncured exception → 29.1 extension (best efforts up to 30 days total; closed-loan auto-extension five days at a time to 60) before pair-off.

**R7 — Rep & warrant relief bookkeeping at submission.** On `delivery.submitted`, for each `rep_warrant_relief` row: `limited_waiver_du` → conditions: final DU Approve/Eligible, SFC 127 delivered, SID 322 delivered, data hash matches; `income_validated`/`employment_validated`/`assets_validated` → DU validation message on the final findings, closed on/before the close-by date, no unresolved verification messages; `undisclosed_debt` → DU relief message on the final submission; `income_calculator` → qualifying income ≤ calculator amount; `value_acceptance` → SFC 801/774 delivered with `InvestorCollateralProgramIdentifier = ValueAcceptance`; `cu_score_2_5` → CU score ≤ 2.5 on the delivered Doc File ID, one-unit; `payment_history_36` → target = 36th payment due date (fixture: Dec 1, 2029). **R8 — Post-purchase adjustments (seller-initiated).** Discovery → `post_purchase_adjustments.open` with evidence → `repricing_eligible = (today ≤ acquisition_date + 18 months)` → PPA Request Form (.csv per the job aid; attributes such as Borrower Credit Score / Source Type, P&I amount as of acquisition, property address, SFC add/remove) uploaded in LSDU with documents named `<FM Loan No.>_<Document Name>.pdf` ≤ 3 MB → `submitted` → notification report in Fannie Mae Connect → LLPA draft/refund (≥ $100 threshold; typically within five business days) → 27.2 posts the draft/refund against `gain_on_sale` (partner economics per the term sheet) → `closed`. Example: purchase Nov 19, 2026; on Mon Mar 8, 2027 the `qc-audit` agent finds the delivered representative score was 762 while the loan-level score per 22.2's rule should have been 754 (one borrower's middle score was mis-selected): a correction is required regardless of price effect (C1-2-02 notification duty); 754 is still in the 740–759 band, so the LLPA does not change (illustrative) and no draft results; PPA submitted Mar 9, processed by Mar 23 (10 BD); `FNMA_C1_2_02_PPA_LLPA_REPRICING_18M` (due May 19, 2028) is irrelevant to a no-price-change correction.

**R9 — Remedy execution from the delivery side.** A Fannie Mae demand (LQC; 28.2 case) that ends in repurchase: the partner's `officer` authorizes payment within A2-3.2-01's 60 days (or appeals — 28.2); the price is "with accrued interest and other adjustments…through the date of repurchase", LLPAs excluded; SM as subservicer reports the removal (5.6: LAR action code 65/67, removal clock) and prices the removal on the partner's behalf; collateral returns — paper note released by the custodian on a Form 2009-equivalent request, eNote by a Transfer of Control (and Location, if the partner's eVault will hold it) from Fannie Mae to the partner; MERS investor field updated (30.1's reverse); the partner funds the price from corporate funds (SM's warehouse does not re-advance unless 27.1's facility permits a repurchase advance). A make-whole (liquidated property) has no collateral return.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`secondary` agent (29.4 tools: `openOperatorTask`, `renderInstructionSheet`, `parseOperatorEvidence`, `observeStatus`, `scheduleShipment`, `trackShipment`, `prepareCustodianPackage` (with the `post-closing` agent), `requestEnoteTransfer` (eVault adapter), `approveWarehouseWire` (prepares SM-org task), `computeExpectedDates`, `ingestPurchaseAdvice`, `reconcileProceeds`, `evaluateRelief`, `preparePpa`, `prepareRemedyPackage` (hands to 28.2), `writeDecision`). End-to-end: on `delivery.package.frozen` the agent checks the payee/wire state, computes the expected timeline against the commitment expiry, LPI window and loan-age rule, renders the instruction sheet, opens the operator task (and, if needed, the SM-org wire-approval task), schedules the custodian shipment for the submission day (or triggers eDelivery + Transfer of Control for eNotes), parses the operator's evidence into `deliveries`, monitors custodian and Fannie Mae signals, ingests and reconciles the purchase advice, notifies 27.2/30.1, and manages PPAs and relief confirmations for the life of the loan. Decision record: `{delivery_id, package_id, sha256, planned_dates{submit, ship, receipt, certification, purchase}, deadlines{commitment_expiry, lpi_45, loan_age_6m}, wire{payee_code, receiver_type, letter_type, bailee_letter_name, status}, evidence_ids[], observed_statuses[], variances[], relief_evaluation[], rationale, confidence, rule_set_versions}`. Guardrails: the agent never operates a Fannie Mae UI, never scripts a browser, never accepts a status without evidence, never approves a wire instruction (SM warehouse org) without a human `fnma_portal_operator` in that org, never files a PPA or remedy payment without the partner `officer`'s approval where money moves, never changes the eNote Controller without the partner's authorization on file (Delegatee for Transfers), never releases SM's warehouse interest before proceeds are confirmed received, and never marks relief `confirmed_by_fnma` without a Fannie Mae report. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (every UI action; SLA `SM_LOAN_DELIVERY_OPERATOR_SLA_1BD`), `signing_officer` (note endorsement/allonge before shipment; MERS signing where required), `officer` (Form 482/360, Form 2004A, remedy payments, commitment extensions with fees, any variance > $1,000 on a purchase advice, seasoned/aged loans), `qc_officer` (relief lost; repeated custodian exceptions), `human_agent` n/a (no borrower contact). Human path (`origination.ai_delivery = off`): the ops console shows the same plan, tasks and evidence checklist; all gates and timers apply unchanged.

**`fnma_portal_operator` task design (the un-automatable step).** Each task carries: (1) the frozen file (name, size, SHA-256) and the instruction sheet; (2) a step list with expected screen values (commitment number, loan count = 1, expected edits = none, payee code, Receiver Type/Letter Type, Bailee Letter Name, eNote Indicator = true/false); (3) mandatory evidence: import-result screenshot, Edit History CSV export, loan-record print (PDF) after submit showing the Fannie Mae loan number and statuses, Wire Details screenshot; (4) an attestation checkbox "file imported = file named on this sheet"; (5) a "blocked" path with a reason code (unexpected edit, commitment missing, wire pending, UI outage) that returns the task to the agent with the captured screen. The evidence parser compares every captured value to the package; any mismatch reopens the task. Two operators are never assigned the same loan in the partner org and the SM warehouse org (segregation: the person who submits the delivery does not approve the warehouse wire).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Best-efforts commitment not yet "closed" in PE-WL** → the loan is not in Loan Delivery ("available…once moved to a closed status"; 15–20 minutes later): 29.1 must move it to closed within one business day of funding (PE-WL FAQ) — the operator task waits on `commitment.status = closed`.
- **Commitment expiry within 2 business days of freeze** → the agent requests an 29.1 extension before opening the operator task if `expected_receipt > receipt_deadline`; pair-off is 29.1's economics.
- **Fatal edit on import that 29.3 could not foresee** (3000-series contract edits, product eligibility, MI coverage) → operator captures the edit → `delivery.edit.observed{fatal}` → 29.3 supersedes and rebuilds only if data must change; if the fix is a commitment/pricing matter, 29.1 acts; the Loan Delivery record stays in Draft.
- **Warning edits at import** → the operator may bypass only those listed as pre-justified in the instruction sheet (from 29.3's `delivery_edits.resolution = bypassed_with_justification`); an unlisted warning blocks the task.
- **Purchase Error after certification** → second submit within `SM_LD_PURCHASE_ERROR_RESUBMIT_1BD`.
- **Custodian document exception** (missing endorsement, signature mismatch without a name affidavit, trust signature block) → `post-closing` agent obtains the corrected document (partner `signing_officer` for endorsements/allonges) and re-ships; if the commitment cannot be extended, Cancel Certification and withdraw from the commitment (29.1).
- **Bailee letter name mismatch** → cannot be certified; SM's warehouse-org operator corrects the Letter Name to the letterhead text (or SM reissues the letter); the custodian re-validates.
- **Payee code inactive/unknown at submit** → do not submit; Form 482 path (three business days) — `officer`.
- **Wire fraud control:** Form 482 changes are confirmed by Fannie Mae's call-back to the signer; the platform additionally requires dual approval (`officer` + `funding_approver`) for any Form 482 that changes a beneficiary account, and never transmits account details by e-mail.
- **Proceeds arrive but net differs from expectation** → variance decomposition; adjustment request within 30 days of the advice date; 27.2 holds the difference in suspense.
- **Proceeds arrive at the wrong payee** (e.g., partner's account instead of SM's warehouse account) → C2-2-03/C2-2-06 make the lender liable for its own instruction errors; the partner remits to SM under the warehouse agreement the same day; a Form 482 review follows.
- **eNote transfer request rejected by the eRegistry** (Secured Party not released; Location mismatch) → eVault exception; SM as Secured Party approves/releases; re-request with a new same-day effective date; submission held until the request is accepted.
- **eNote auto-certification fails** (XML data ≠ delivery data) → treat like a data revision: compare the eNote XML to the ULDD; correct the ULDD (Cancel Certification → Draft) or, if the eNote is wrong, 26.2's eNote correction path (amendment vs Registration Reversal per B8-8-02).
- **Loan paid or curtailed before purchase** → UPB and LPI change; the ULDD "At Current" UPB must be corrected (non-certifiable field or rebuild); interest computation changes (27.2).
- **Borrower's first payment received by SM before purchase** (early payer) → LPI re-anchored; delivery window extends; A/A interest adjustment recomputed.
- **Loan becomes delinquent before purchase** → the loan may not be "current"-eligible under the commitment terms; 29.1 pair-off/hold; not delivered.
- **Partner's Fannie Mae approval suspended** → nothing can be submitted; 31.1 escalation; warehouse aging rules (27.1).
- **Fannie Mae-initiated data validation (LQC) after purchase** → 28.2 case; 29.4 supplies the delivered-data package and files the PPA if the correction is accepted; LLPA repricing only within 18 months.
- **Repurchase demand on a delivered loan** → 28.2 ladder; execution per R9; SM's warehouse does not re-fund by default.

#### Audit and evidence
- For Fannie Mae (QC, MORA, LQC): the complete delivery trail per loan — frozen file and hash, operator evidence (import result, edit history, submit confirmation with Fannie Mae loan number, wire details), shipment tracking and custodian certification notices (or eVault/eRegistry transaction records for eNotes), purchase advice payload and reconciliation, PPA forms and notification reports, relief evaluation and confirmations, remedy correspondence (28.2).
- For the partner: proof that every submission used the partner-approved package, that wires were designated only to Form 482 payees, that SM's warehouse interest was released no later than the purchase date, that proceeds were applied per the warehouse agreement (27.2), and the expected-vs-actual timeline per loan with the cost of slips.
- For CFPB/state exams: the purchase (acquisition) date that anchors the §1026.39 notice (25.4/30.4) and the servicing-file record of ownership (§1024.38(c)).
- For litigation: the chain of custody of the note (endorsement in blank by the partner's signing officer, carrier records, custodian certification, Fannie Mae ownership from the purchase date) or the eNote's eRegistry transfer history (Controller/Location/Secured Party) — the evidence needed to prove holder status.
- LL-2026-04: every plan, observation and reconciliation is an `agent_decisions` row; every UI action is a human task with captured evidence; segregation of duties between the seller-org and warehouse-org operators is logged.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Loan Delivery DI for TSPs.** Not documented publicly. *Default:* build the human operator path with evidence capture; ask Fannie Mae's Integration Team in writing during TSP onboarding; if a channel exists, replace the import/submit task with a file transfer and keep the evidence model.
2. **Custodian model (26.4-Q1) and pre-positioned notes.** If the partner's FCC also holds the note as SM's bailee from post-closing, the "ship the same day" rule is met by documents already in the vault. *Default:* single FCC acting as bailee; the cover letter and bailee loan list are transmitted electronically the same day; confirm with the custodian that "first morning delivery" is deemed satisfied for pre-positioned notes.
3. **Purchase-interest day count.** C2-1.1-06 does not state it. *Default:* store both 30/360 and actual/365 expectations until 27.2 confirms from the Purchase Advice field definitions; reconcile against the first live advice and freeze the convention as a rule-set constant.
4. **eNote wire/letter-type options in Loan Delivery when the warehouse interest is under the eNote funding agreement.** *Default:* Receiver Type "Warehouse Lender" with the funding-agreement designation as instructed by Fannie Mae's eMortgage team; capture the exact UI options in the onboarding runbook.
5. **Fannie Mae Connect reports for delivery status.** Which report(s) expose loan-level Loan Delivery status daily. *Default:* rely on operator captures and custodian notices; add the report ingester when identified.
6. **LQC data-validation response window.** *Default:* 30 calendar days from the request unless the request states otherwise; 28.2 aligns with its file-request timer.
7. **Master Servicer field for a subservicer.** C1-2-04 requires the "Servicer's MERS Org ID (or sub-servicer, if applicable)" in the "Master Servicer" field; 30.1-Q4 owns the decision. *Default (aligned with 30.1-Q4, this process's `FNMA_C1_2_04_ENOTE_TRANSFER_SAME_DAY_GATE` and servicing 1.4's Servicing Agent default):* **SM's Org ID** in the Master Servicer field (SM is the subservicer that performs eRegistry transactions), with SM also as Servicing Agent/Delegatee; the partner's Org ID is the alternative only if Fannie Mae's account team or the MERS eRegistry Procedures (member-only — [PARTIALLY VERIFIED]) require the master servicer's own ID. The earlier default here (partner's Org ID) is withdrawn so that 29.4's gate, 30.1's verification timer and the Transfer of Control request all carry the same value.
8. **Operator staffing and cutoffs.** *Default:* SM staff under the partner's Technology Manager org (Form 101 authorization), tasks due 15:00 MT so that carrier pickup (16:30 MT) and the 9:00 p.m. ET data cutoff are met; a second daily window (08:00 MT) for eNote-only loans.
