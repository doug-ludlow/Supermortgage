### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Two dispute channels with different legal bases.** (1) **Indirect (CRA-forwarded) disputes — 15 U.S.C. 1681s-2(b)** (verified at law.cornell.edu): after receiving notice from a CRA under §1681i(a)(2) the furnisher shall "(A) conduct an investigation with respect to the disputed information; (B) review all relevant information provided by the consumer reporting agency ...; (C) report the results of the investigation to the consumer reporting agency; (D) if the investigation finds that the information is incomplete or inaccurate, report those results to all other consumer reporting agencies to which the person furnished the information and that compile and maintain files on consumers on a nationwide basis; and (E) if an item ... is found to be inaccurate or incomplete or cannot be verified after any reinvestigation ... promptly modify that item, delete that item, or permanently block the reporting of that item." (b)(2): complete "before the expiration of the period under section 1681i(a)(1)" — i.e., the CRA's **30-day** reinvestigation period from the CRA's receipt of the consumer's dispute, which "may be extended for not more than 15 additional days if the consumer reporting agency receives information from the consumer during that 30-day period that is relevant to the reinvestigation" (§1681i(a)(1)(A)–(B)). The CRA must forward the dispute to the furnisher "before the expiration of the 5-business-day period" after it receives the dispute, "including all relevant information ... provided by the consumer" (§1681i(a)(2)(A)–(B)). Information found "inaccurate or incomplete or cannot be verified" is deleted or modified by the CRA (§1681i(a)(5)(A)); reinsertion of deleted information requires the furnisher to certify it is "complete and accurate" (§1681i(a)(5)(B)). **(b) duties carry private liability** (§1681s-2(c) carve-out; §1681n/§1681o). The CFPB's Circular 2022-07 (furnishers must conduct a reasonable investigation; CRA-forwarded disputes cannot be deemed frivolous) and Bulletins 2013-09/2014-01 were **withdrawn May 12, 2025**; the statutory "reasonable investigation" standard developed by the courts (e.g., *Johnson v. MBNA* (4th Cir. 2004), *Hinkle v. Midland Credit Mgmt.* (11th Cir. 2016) — an investigation that merely re-checks the furnisher's own summary data is not reasonable where underlying records exist **[case law from general knowledge — not fetched]**) is unaffected. (2) **Direct disputes — 15 U.S.C. 1681s-2(a)(8) and Regulation V 12 CFR 1022.43** (eCFR current as of Sept. 1, 2026): the furnisher must investigate a dispute submitted directly by the consumer concerning "(1) The consumer's liability for a credit account or other debt with the furnisher; (2) The terms of a credit account or other debt ...; (3) The consumer's performance or other conduct concerning an account or other relationship with the furnisher; or (4) Any other information contained in a consumer report regarding an account or other relationship with the furnisher that bears on the consumer's creditworthiness, credit standing, credit capacity, character, general reputation, personal characteristics, or mode of living." **Exceptions (b):** identifying information (other than liability), employers, inquiries, public-record information, "Information provided to a consumer reporting agency by another furnisher," and disputes the furnisher "has a reasonable belief" were submitted by or prepared for a credit repair organization. **Address (c):** the duty applies to disputes sent to "(1) The address of a furnisher provided by a furnisher and set forth on a consumer report relating to the consumer; (2) An address clearly and conspicuously specified by the furnisher for submitting direct disputes that is provided to the consumer in writing or electronically; or (3) Any business address of the furnisher if the furnisher has not so specified and provided an address." **Contents (d):** "Sufficient information to identify the account," "The specific information that the consumer is disputing and an explanation of the basis for the dispute," and "All supporting documentation or other information reasonably required by the furnisher to substantiate the basis of the dispute." **Duty (e):** "(1) Conduct a reasonable investigation with respect to the disputed information; (2) Review all relevant information provided by the consumer with the dispute notice; (3) Complete its investigation of the dispute and report the results of the investigation to the consumer before the expiration of the period under section 611(a)(1) of the FCRA (15 U.S.C. 1681i(a)(1)) within which a consumer reporting agency would be required to complete its action if the consumer had elected to dispute the information under that section; and (4) If the investigation finds that the information reported was inaccurate, promptly notify each consumer reporting agency to which the furnisher provided inaccurate information of that determination and provide to the consumer reporting agency any correction to that information that is necessary to make the information provided by the furnisher accurate." **Frivolous or irrelevant (f):** permitted only where "(i) The consumer did not provide sufficient information to investigate," "(ii) The direct dispute is substantially the same as a dispute previously submitted by or on behalf of the consumer, either directly to the furnisher or through a consumer reporting agency, with respect to which the furnisher has already satisfied the applicable requirements ... provided, however, that a direct dispute is not substantially the same ... if the dispute includes information ... not previously provided," or "(iii) ... one or more of the exceptions listed in paragraph (b)" applies; notice "not later than five business days after making the determination, by mail or, if authorized by the consumer for that purpose, by any other means available to the furnisher," including "the reasons for such determination" and "any information required to investigate the disputed information, which notice may consist of a standardized form describing the general nature of such information." Statute (a)(8)(G): the direct-dispute duty does not apply to disputes "submitted by, ... prepared on behalf of the consumer by, or ... submitted on a form supplied to the consumer by, a credit repair organization." **(a)(3) applies to every dispute, however received:** the furnisher "may not furnish the information to any consumer reporting agency without notice that such information is disputed by the consumer" (Metro 2 XB). **Reg X:** an NoE that is also an accuracy dispute triggers both 4.1 and this process; the §1024.35(i) 60-day bar applies (8.3). **FDCPA (FDCPA-covered loans only, 11.4):** 15 U.S.C. 1692e(8) prohibits communicating credit information "which is known or which should be known to be false, including the failure to communicate that a disputed debt is disputed" **[text from general knowledge — not fetched]**.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint.** (1) "Investigate within 30 days" is the *CRA's* clock; the furnisher's ACDV deadline is the CRA-assigned Response Due Date inside that window (and 45 days when the consumer supplements) — missing it forfeits the tradeline data. (2) The row omits Reg V direct disputes entirely (30/45-day results notice to the consumer, 5-business-day frivolous notice, designated address). (3) The row omits the (a)(3) dispute-flag duty and the post-investigation Metro 2 CCC transitions. (4) Automation class "b" becomes AI-first with policy-configured reviewer conditions (below); no human touchpoint is legally required, but LL-2026-04 and the private-liability exposure under §1681s-2(b) justify reviewer gates on adverse outcomes.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- **ACDV intake:** `eoscar` adapter polls `/acdvreq/vX/find` every 15 minutes (status PENDING-SENDREQUEST, date range = last 7 days) → `credit.dispute.acdv.received` per control number → `/view` (images downloaded to `documents`) → `credit_disputes` row (`source='acdv'`).
- **Direct-dispute intake:** the 4.1 Intake Router classifies any written communication (mail/OCR, web form, secure message, email to the designated address, CFPB/state complaint portals via 4.5, attorney letters) with `kind='credit_dispute'` (alongside `noe` when applicable) → `credit.dispute.direct.received`. Oral disputes (AI voice/chat/phone via `borrower-comms`) are logged as `contacts` with `credit_dispute_asserted=true` and open a direct-dispute case anyway (policy: over-recognize; the (a)(3) XB duty applies to oral disputes).
- **Notifications:** `/notification/vX/find` (Block/DR) and `/audnotif/vX/find` polled hourly → `credit.block.notice.received`, `credit.aud.notice.received`.
- **Internal triggers:** `case.noe.opened` with reporting-related assertions (4.1) → linked direct dispute; `credit.correction.created` (8.1) → AUD; `case.fraud.opened` → identity-theft path (8.3).

#### Data model
- `credit_disputes` — `id`, `loan_id`, `borrower_id`, `source` ∈ {acdv, direct_written, direct_oral, noe_linked, complaint, attorney, cfpb_portal}, `cra` ∈ {equifax, experian, transunion, innovis, null}, `acdv_control_number`, `subscriber_code`, `dispute_codes text[]`, `fcra_relevant_information text`, `consumer_statement text`, `image_document_ids uuid[]`, `cra_received_at date` (from ACDV, if present), `received_at timestamptz` (our receipt), `response_due_at` (ACDV payload) / `results_due_at` (direct: +30 calendar days), `extended_to` (direct: +15 when relevant info arrives within 30 days), `category` ∈ {not_mine, identity_theft, mixed_file, liability, terms, status_or_rating, payment_history, balance, amount_past_due, dates (opened/dofd/closed/last_payment), special_comment_or_ccc, bankruptcy_cii, deceased, scra, transfer_duplicate, other}, `frivolous_basis` (direct only), `status`, `determination` ∈ {verified_as_reported, modified, deleted_consumer, deleted_account, unverifiable, frivolous_irrelevant, out_of_scope}, `response_code`, `response_payload jsonb` (full Metro 2 field set returned), `submitted_at`, `eoscar_status`, `investigation jsonb` (records examined, snapshots, findings), `agent_decision_id`, `reviewer_id`, `reviewer_action`, `correction_ids uuid[]`, `results_notice_id`, `frivolous_notice_id`, `ccc_transition` ∈ {XB, XC, XH, XR}.
- `credit_dispute_events` — append-only timeline (received, viewed, investigated, escalated, reviewed, responded, corrected, closed, reopened).
- `eoscar_messages` — request/response log per call (`endpoint`, `version`, `request_hash`, `http_status`, `validation_errors jsonb`, `control_numbers text[]`), idempotency key = (endpoint, control number, request hash).
- `credit_dispute_evidence` — `dispute_id`, `document_id | system_snapshot_id`, `evidence_type` ∈ {ledger_history, payment_image, allocation_trace, notice_copy, contact_log, lossmit_agreement, bankruptcy_docket, scra_certificate, prior_servicer_record, boarding_reconciliation, prior_dispute, borrower_submission}, `relied_upon bool`.
- Reuses 4.1 `system_snapshots`; 8.1 `credit_reporting_corrections`; `credit_reporting_suppressions` (8.3) for the XB flag (`reason='fcra_dispute_open'`, `mechanism='flag_only'`).
- Retention `fcra_furnishing_5y`; images and consumer statements are PII (encrypted; access logged).

#### State machine
`received → (viewed: ACDV only) → classified → (frivolous_pending: direct only) → investigating → (review_pending)? → responded → (correcting)? → closed`; terminal `closed`, `closed_frivolous` (direct), `closed_out_of_scope` (direct (b) exceptions — still answered with a letter), `expired_no_response` (must never occur; breach state). Guards: `investigating → responded` requires `determination` set, ≥1 `credit_dispute_evidence.relied_upon` row for any `verified_as_reported`, the response payload validated by `/acdvresp/vX/validate` (ACDV) or the results letter checklist (direct), and all reviewer conditions satisfied; `responded → closed` requires e-OSCAR RESOLVED-RETURNEDTOAGENCY (ACDV) or mailing evidence (direct) and, when the determination changed data, AUDs to every other bureau that received the data plus a `credit_reporting_corrections` row; `frivolous_pending → closed_frivolous` requires `officer`/`human_agent` approval and the (f)(2) notice within 5 business days. Actors: `credit-reporting` agent; reviewers per conditions; e-OSCAR/CRA acks; the borrower (supplementing a direct dispute extends the clock).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Receipt and clocks.** ACDV `received_at` = poll timestamp; the ACDV's `responseDueDate` governs (never later than the CRA's 30/45-day outer bound). Direct: `received_at` = mail vendor receipt date / electronic receipt (America/New_York); day 0 = receipt; results are due "before the expiration" of 30 calendar days → the results notice must be *sent* no later than day 30 (policy: dispatch by day 25). Worked example: direct dispute received Friday **2027-09-03** → results due by **2027-10-03** (a Sunday; policy dispatch Friday 2027-10-01); the borrower emails bank statements on 2027-09-20 (within the 30 days) → `extended_to` = **2027-10-18**. Frivolous determination made Tuesday 2027-09-07 → notice due 5 federal business days later = **2027-09-14**. ACDV example: received 2027-09-15 09:12 ET with Response Due Date 2027-10-01 → internal target 2027-09-22; outer bound (CRA received 2027-09-12) 2027-10-12.
2. **Classification** maps dispute codes/text to `category` and selects the evidence plan: e.g., "disputes payment history / account status" → ledger installment ledger for the disputed months, payment images/ACH records, allocation traces, protected-payment and effective-date rules (1.x/2.x), late-charge assessments, any workout agreements in force, and the furnished `metro2_snapshots` for those months; "not mine / identity theft" → origination documents in `documents` (note, closing package), boarding identity data, SSN/DOB match, fraud indicators, 8.3 identity-theft path; "balance" → ledger balances incl. deferred/forborne, capitalization events, escrow advances; "dates" → note date, DOFD derivation history, payoff/transfer dates; "bankruptcy/CII" → 14.x docket (PACER monitor), discharge/dismissal orders, reaffirmation; "deceased" → 4.4 death evidence; "SCRA" → DMDC certificates, relief case; "duplicate/transfer" → transferor's 05 record and our Date Opened/DOFD.
3. **Reasonable investigation standard (as code).** A determination of `verified_as_reported` requires: (i) the disputed field values re-derived from primary records (not from the prior Metro 2 snapshot alone); (ii) every consumer-supplied image reviewed and each factual claim in it answered in `investigation.findings` (with a "why it does/does not change the outcome" note); (iii) for periods before boarding, the prior-servicer record or the boarding reconciliation consulted, and if neither substantiates the disputed item, the determination is `unverifiable` → delete/modify the item for that period (a furnisher may not verify what it cannot substantiate); (iv) the agent compares the ACDV's consumer identifiers against ours (name/SSN/DOB/address) and treats mismatches as potential mixed-file/identity issues.
4. **Response payload.** ACDV responses return the full current Metro 2 field set (the CRA overlays it on the tradeline): Account Status, Payment Rating, PHP, Special Comment, CCC, CII, ECOA, balances, dates, consumer identifiers — always generated from the 8.1 snapshot logic as of the response date (not typed by hand), with the dispute outcome applied. Response Code selection: `verified_as_reported` → "accurate as reported" code; `modified` → "modify as indicated" with the changed fields; `deleted_account` (DA/DF) or `deleted_consumer` (ECOA Z) → the delete codes — these require `officer` approval; codes 12/13 per the eo2026.07 definitions **[UNVERIFIED]**.
5. **Corrections fan-out.** Any `modified`/`deleted` outcome creates a `credit_reporting_corrections` row, AUDs to every other bureau that received the data (≤2 BD), the corrected value in the next 8.1 cycle, and — if the correction adds negative information not previously noticed — a B-2 notice (8.1). Reinsertion of previously deleted data is never automatic: it requires a `credit_reporting_corrections` row with evidence, `officer` approval and a BRR/certification (§1681i(a)(5)(B)).
6. **Compliance Condition Code lifecycle.** On receipt (any channel): XB for the affected consumer's segment(s) from the next furnishing (AUD if the next cycle is >10 days away; policy). On close: `modified`/`deleted` → **XR** (dispute concluded, data corrected — the "disputed" flag is no longer accurate); `verified_as_reported` and the consumer has expressly stated continuing disagreement (statement of dispute, repeat dispute without new information) → **XC**; `verified_as_reported` otherwise → **XH** (previously in dispute, investigation completed) †. Codes † are per the CRRG as recalled and must be confirmed; the CCC field is sticky, so only XR clears it (8.1 rule 8).
7. **Direct-dispute exceptions and frivolous determinations** are made only by a human reviewer on the agent's recommendation (default policy), never for ACDVs. A dispute missing information under (d) is handled by asking for it in the (f) notice (standardized list) rather than by silence; a repeat dispute that includes *any* new document is investigated. Credit-repair-organization indicators (form templates, identical mass letters, third-party return addresses) are logged; the determination still requires human approval and the notice still issues.
8. **Results notice (direct).** `NTC_FCRA_1022_43E_RESULTS` states each disputed item, the determination and reason in plain language, what was corrected and when it will appear (next cycle/AUD), that the consumer may dispute with the CRAs and add a statement, and the direct-dispute address; where the outcome is adverse to the consumer and the determination was AI-generated, the letter includes the human-review path (Colorado AI Act posture, baseline §8 item 5 analog; **[UNVERIFIED that a furnisher dispute determination is a Colorado "consequential decision" — conservative default]**).
9. **NoE linkage.** A direct dispute that asserts a servicing error (misapplied payment, wrongful late fee) is also an NoE: the 4.1 case runs its 30-business-day clock and corrections; the credit dispute's results letter may be combined with the NoE response if both clocks are met by the earlier date.
10. **Worked example — ACDV.** Loan SM-1001; ACDV control 2027091500123 received 2027-09-15 from Experian, dispute code "disputes present/previous account status / payment history," consumer statement "I was never late — my February 2027 payment was mailed on Feb 3," image: a check copy dated 2027-02-03 for $2,459.55. Investigation: lockbox/BAI2 records (2.x) show no deposit of that check in Feb–Apr 2027; the borrower's bank clearing evidence is absent (the image is a check copy, not a cleared-check image); the Mar-31 snapshot derivation shows Feb-1 and Mar-1 installments unpaid → status 71 correct; the Feb-28 snapshot (11) and the PHP `1` for March are correct. Determination: `verified_as_reported`, confidence 0.93 → no reviewer condition → response "accurate as reported" with the full field set as of 2027-09-22 (status 11 post-deferral, K4 balloon, PHP `222210…`, CCC **XC**? No — the consumer has not indicated continuing disagreement → **XH** from the next cycle; XB carried in the response). Submitted 2027-09-22; RESOLVED-RETURNEDTOAGENCY 2027-09-23. Had the lockbox shown the check deposited 2027-02-05 and misposted to suspense, the determination would be `modified`: Feb/Mar statuses corrected to 11, PHP position for March `0`, DOFD moved to 03012027 (earlier delinquency start eliminated — this is a permitted correction because the original DOFD was wrong), AUDs to Equifax/TransUnion/Innovis within 2 BD, a `payment.reapplied` correction in 2.x, a late-charge reversal, and an NoE-style correction letter.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- **e-OSCAR transactions:** ACDV responses, AUDs, BRRs (rare), notification reviews — each logged in `eoscar_messages` with control numbers and statuses.
- **Notices:** `NTC_FCRA_1022_43E_RESULTS` (direct-dispute results; checklist: each item, determination, reasons, corrections + effective cycle, CRA dispute rights, address); `NTC_FCRA_1022_43F_FRIVOLOUS` (reasons + standardized list of information required; sent by mail unless the consumer authorized another means); `NTC_FCRA_1022_43_ACK` (optional acknowledgment — policy, not required); `NTC_FCRA_1681S2A7_B2` when applicable; the 4.1 NoE letters when linked. Channel: mail by default; e-delivery only with `esign` consent class `fcra_notices` (or the consumer's authorization for the (f) notice).
- **Records:** `credit_disputes`, `credit_dispute_events`, `credit_dispute_evidence`, `system_snapshots`, `credit_reporting_corrections`, `agent_decisions`, XB/XC/XH/XR flags in `credit_reporting_suppressions`.
- **Ledger:** only when a linked servicing correction posts (4.1 commands). **Investor events:** none unless a correction changes UPB/LPI (5.x).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `credit-reporting` (dispute mode), with the 4.1 Intake Router for direct intake. End-to-end: retrieve/classify → evidence plan → investigation with snapshots → determination → response generation (from the 8.1 snapshot logic) → reviewer gate check → submit/mail → corrections fan-out → CCC transition → close → metrics.
- **Tools:** `eoscar.acdv.find/view/validate/submit`, `eoscar.aud.validate/submit`, `eoscar.notification.*`, `metro2.snapshot.build(loan_id, as_of)`, read tools (ledger, payments incl. images, allocations, loan_terms, cases, notices, contacts, documents, bankruptcy docket, DMDC certificates), `credit.correction.create`, 4.1 correction commands, `notice.render_and_send`, `snapshot.capture`, `escalation.file`.
- **Decision record:** `{dispute_id, source, cra, dispute_codes, category, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, evidence_ids[], claims[] (each consumer claim → finding → effect), determination, response_code, fields_changed, ccc_transition, rationale_plain_language, confidence, reviewer_conditions_triggered[], reviewer_id?, outcome, timers_met bool}`.
- **Reviewer conditions (policy defaults, all on):** (1) `verified_as_reported` with confidence < 0.85 → `human_agent` review; (2) category ∈ {identity_theft, mixed_file, not_mine, deceased, bankruptcy_cii, scra} → `officer`; (3) any deletion (DA/DF, ECOA Z) or reinsertion/BRR → `officer`; (4) direct-dispute frivolous/irrelevant or (b) out-of-scope determination → `human_agent` (with `officer` for repeat-dispute basis); (5) litigation/attorney/CFPB/state-regulator complaint context → `officer`; (6) third dispute on the same item within 12 months → `officer`; (7) pre-boarding period with incomplete prior-servicer records → `human_agent`; (8) discrimination or fair-lending allegation → `officer` and 19.4 log. Escalation package: the draft response payload, decision record, evidence list with snapshots, the consumer's images, timer status and a one-paragraph summary. Reviewer SLA 1 BD; the timers keep running, so at 90% of the Response Due Date an unreviewed case auto-escalates to `officer`.
- **Guardrails:** never respond "verified" without evidence rows; never deem an ACDV frivolous; never let a due date lapse (a "best-available" modify/delete response with a follow-up correction is preferred to silence); never reinsert deleted data without certification; never contact the consumer's employer or third parties; never use the dispute to collect; disclose automation in any oral follow-up (baseline §8 item 6).
- **AI-off path:** the ops console exposes the same worklist (ACDV find/view/respond, direct-dispute cases) to `human_agent` with the agent producing evidence packs and draft responses only.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-out mid-dispute:** if the Response Due Date falls before the transfer date, respond; otherwise respond anyway with data as of the transfer (the furnisher of the disputed data remains responsible) and flag the transferee; direct disputes received after transfer about our period are still ours.
- **Transfer-in with open disputes:** the transferor must respond to disputes about its own furnishing; disputes forwarded to us about pre-boarding periods are investigated from the hand-off records; `unverifiable` if none.
- **Bankruptcy:** disputes are answered (no stay issue); CII outcomes coordinate with 14.x; a dispute that the debt was discharged and should show a zero balance is investigated against the discharge order (8.3 matrix).
- **SCRA:** a dispute alleging adverse reporting during relief → immediate suppression check; correction if 8.3 was not applied.
- **Deceased borrower disputes from an estate/successor:** authority verified (4.4); ECOA X applied; the estate representative receives the results letter.
- **Identity theft claims without a §1681c-2 report:** investigate as a dispute; if credible, open a `fraud` case and suppress pending outcome (8.3).
- **Mixed file (wrong consumer):** delete the consumer from our tradeline (ECOA Z) only if we never had liability from that person; if our data is right and the CRA mis-merged, respond verified with full identifiers so the CRA can fix its match.
- **Duplicate ACDVs across bureaus for the same item:** one investigation, four responses; AUD fan-out only where a bureau did not send an ACDV.
- **e-OSCAR API outage / auth failure:** switch to the web app (human) for anything due within 3 days; log the outage; tokens are cached and reused (no per-call auth).
- **Validation rejects on submit:** fix Metro 2 field errors and resubmit the same day; never let a reject stand at the due date.
- **Consumer supplements a direct dispute after day 30:** treat as a new dispute (new clock) if it contains new information; otherwise answer by letter referencing the prior results.
- **Credit-repair-organization disputes:** (a)(8)(G)/§1022.43(b)(2) exception applies only with a "reasonable belief"; default still investigates if the dispute has substance (over-investigate), with the exception reserved for template floods (officer decision).
- **Retro-corrections of prior responses:** a later-discovered error in a "verified" response creates a correction + AUD and a courtesy results letter.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **8.2-T1 (ACDV happy path):** Given an ACDV with Response Due Date 2027-10-01 received 2027-09-15, when investigated, then a response with the full field set is submitted by 2027-09-22, status reaches RESOLVED-RETURNEDTOAGENCY, and the case closes with XH scheduled for the next cycle.
- **8.2-T2 (ACDV modify + fan-out):** Given lockbox evidence of a misposted Feb-5 deposit, then response "modify" with corrected Feb/Mar statuses, AUDs to the other three bureaus within 2 BD, `credit_reporting_corrections` and `payment.reapplied` posted, late charge reversed.
- **8.2-T3 (due-date breach prevention):** Given no reviewer action by 90% of the Response Due Date, then the case auto-escalates to `officer` and, absent action by the due date, the agent submits a best-available response (never RESOLVED-NORESPONSEPROVIDED).
- **8.2-T4 (view-before-submit):** Given an ACDV never viewed, then `submit` is refused by the adapter until `/view` has been called.
- **8.2-T5 (direct dispute clocks):** Given receipt 2027-09-03, then results are mailed by 2027-10-03; given supplementation on 2027-09-20, the due date moves to 2027-10-18; supplementation on 2027-10-05 opens a new case.
- **8.2-T6 (frivolous notice):** Given a repeat direct dispute with no new information determined frivolous on 2027-09-07 and approved by `human_agent`, then the (f) notice with reasons and the standardized information list is mailed by 2027-09-14; given the repeat includes a new bank statement, the case is investigated instead.
- **8.2-T7 (XB flag):** Given any open dispute, then the next Metro 2 file (or an AUD if >10 days away) carries XB on the consumer's segment; on close with correction, XR; on verified with stated disagreement, XC.
- **8.2-T8 (unverifiable pre-boarding):** Given a dispute about a 2026 late mark reported by the transferor and no hand-off evidence, then determination `unverifiable`, the item is deleted/modified, and the transferor is notified.
- **8.2-T9 (identity theft):** Given an ACDV with an identity-theft dispute code and a §1681c-2 block notification, then furnishing for that consumer stops immediately, a `fraud` case opens, and `officer` approves any DF/ECOA Z.
- **8.2-T10 (oral dispute):** Given a borrower says on an AI voice call that "my credit report shows me late in March and I wasn't," then a direct-dispute case opens, XB applies, and the results letter goes out within 30 days.
- **8.2-T11 (API outage):** Given e-OSCAR API 5xx for 4 hours with a response due tomorrow, then a `human_agent` task is created with the response payload for web-app entry.
- **8.2-T12 (reviewer conditions):** Given `verified_as_reported` at confidence 0.7, then the case waits for `human_agent` review with the evidence pack; given confidence 0.9 and no other condition, it submits without review.
- **8.2-T13 (NoE linkage):** Given a letter alleging a misapplied payment and a wrong credit report, then both an NoE case (4.1) and a direct dispute exist, the corrections are shared, and both letters meet their clocks.
- **8.2-T14 (AUD is not in-cycle):** Given an AUD sent on 2027-09-25, then the 2027-09-30 cycle still carries the corrected values (no reliance on the AUD alone).

#### Audit and evidence
Per dispute: the ACDV payload and images (or the inbound document), `credit_dispute_events`, evidence rows with snapshot hashes, the decision record with model/prompt versions, reviewer identity and action, the exact response payload and e-OSCAR status history, AUD control numbers, notices with mailing proof, CCC transitions, timer history. Portfolio: monthly dispute volume by category/bureau, verification vs. modification rates by field (Appendix E III(l) — a high modification rate on a field is an accuracy defect signal that feeds 8.1's validators), response timeliness vs. Response Due Date, frivolous-notice counts, repeat-dispute rates, fair-lending monitoring of outcomes by protected-class proxies (19.4), and QC re-review of ≥10% of closed disputes by `qc-audit`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **8.2-Q1 Reviewer thresholds.** Default: as listed (confidence < 0.85 for "verified"; categories; deletions). Alternative: pure AI with post-hoc QC. Recommend keeping gates for 12 months of production evidence (LL-2026-04).
2. **8.2-Q2 Oral disputes as Reg V direct disputes.** Default: yes (over-recognize). Alternative: XB flag only, with an invitation to submit in writing.
3. **8.2-Q3 Post-investigation CCC.** Default: XR after corrections; XH after verified without stated disagreement; XC with stated disagreement. Confirm against the CRRG 2026.
4. **8.2-Q4 Acknowledgment letters for direct disputes.** Default: send a short acknowledgment with the direct-dispute address and expected date (not required by Reg V).
5. **8.2-Q5 Business-day calendar for the 5-business-day frivolous notice.** Default: `business_days_federal` (earlier deadline); alternative servicer calendar.
6. **8.2-Q6 Response Code table and 12/13 semantics** (eo2026.07) — load from e-OSCAR docs at build; blocked until the API License Agreement is signed.
