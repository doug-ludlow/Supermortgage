### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Servicing Guide C-3-02, Remitting Payoff Proceeds (11/12/2014; edition Aug. 12, 2026):** "The servicer must determine the appropriate schedule and the correct amount of funds due to Fannie Mae based on the mortgage loan's remittance type and remittance cycle," with the procedures in F-1-20. **F-1-20 (03/11/2026)** as verified in 5.2: A/A — payoff proceeds > $2,500 (after servicing fees) are remitted "immediately," ≤ $2,500 on the regular schedule; amount = Fannie Mae's share of UPB "+ interest up to but not including the payoff date"; S/A — "by the 20th day of the month following the month in which they were received," = share of UPB + one-half month's interest at the pass-through rate; S/S — on the 18th of the following month (RPM designated date; MBS Express unscheduled principal on BD4) = scheduled UPB + a full month's interest at PTR, and "the servicer must use its own funds to cover the difference between the interest Fannie Mae is due and the interest collected from the borrower when a mortgage loan is paid off before the end of the month," except that no full-month interest is due when the liquidation is "processed on the first business day and reported to Fannie Mae by the second business day"; S/S payoff date = settlement/closing date when a closing agent pays, receipt date when the borrower pays directly; CRS instructions by 8 p.m. ET the day before the effective date, remittances accepted until 4 p.m. ET. **Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026) §2-04** as verified in 5.3: "the servicer must submit a LAR with an Action Code 60 on the first business day after the servicer processes the transaction on its system," removal transactions by 8 p.m. ET the next business day (5 p.m. ET if that day is BD2), corrections by 5 p.m. ET BD2; principal reported includes any non-interest-bearing forbearance before applying the participation percentage; interest by remittance type (A/A: months ÷ 12 and days ÷ 365 from the LPI date up to but not including the payoff date; S/A ÷ 24; S/S ÷ 12). **IRM §4-08 / SVC-2026-03 (eff. July 1, 2026):** no update to an action code 60 after the period closes — the loan "is not reactivated" and the servicer must remit/advance to liquidate.

**F-1-09 (10/19/2016), satisfaction task table:** after "verifying the amount required to pay the mortgage loan in full has been received," the servicer must "Remove Fannie Mae's interest (or that of the mortgagee of record) from all applicable property insurance policies," "Notify the taxing authorities that future tax bills should be sent to the borrower," "Notify the mortgage insurer or guarantor of the payoff," "Remit Fannie Mae's share of the payoff proceeds in accordance with the remittance schedule established for the remittance type," and "Code the payoff as Action Code 60 when it next reports a LAR." Advances: "Collect any funds advanced on behalf of the borrower. Remit the repayment as a special remittance to Fannie Mae, and within 30 days of the payoff date, if Fannie Mae advanced the funds"; "The repayment of advances must not be included as part of the payoff proceeds." **C-1.2-03:** remaining buydown funds held by the servicer "must [be] include[d] with its remittance of the payoff proceeds." **F-1-05 (06/11/2025):** the servicer "must not net costs for recording the satisfaction of a mortgage loan out of the proceeds"; government-imposed recording costs for satisfactions are reimbursable on portfolio and special-servicing-option MBS loans when law/documents bar charging the borrower (claim via the 60-day expense-claim window, 00b F6). **C-4.2-01 (06/09/2021):** "The servicer must file IRS Form 1098 ... using its own name and taxpayer identification number." **C-4.1-01 / 8.1:** paid-in-full tradeline (Account Status 13). **Reg X §1024.34(b)(1):** escrow refund within 20 business days (3.5); **§1024.17(i)(4)(ii):** short-year statement within 60 days of payoff (3.3). **HPA / 10.5:** MI ends at payoff; unearned premiums refunded. **LL-2026-05:** liquidation and loan-data events move to event-based reporting in Feb–Mar 2027 and A/A P&I auto-draft is the final phase — until then CRS code 001 (servicer-initiated) is the A/A payoff rail and S/A / S/S drafts are Fannie Mae-initiated from the reported LAR.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) "Systems: CRS" is only right for A/A payoffs (code 001) and advance repayments (special remittance); S/A and S/S payoff proceeds are drafted by Fannie Mae from the LAR on the 20th/18th (BD4 MBS Express) — the LAR, not CRS, is the trigger. (2) "Per Fannie Mae (action code 60)" hides three clocks: the removal LAR (next BD 8 p.m. ET / BD2 5 p.m. ET), the A/A "immediately" remittance, and the BD2 5 p.m. ET finality after which the payoff cannot be undone. (3) The S/S servicer-funded interest gap and the BD1/BD2 exception are not in the row; 5.2 books the gap to `servicer_advance_receivable` — it is not recoverable from Fannie Mae and 16.2 expenses it (open question 1). (4) Advances repaid by special remittance within 30 days and buydown funds remitted with proceeds are separate flows. (5) Post-payoff housekeeping (six regulatory/Guide duties with their own timers) is absent from the row.

#### State machine
`funds_received` → `held` (good-funds hold) | `cleared` → `matched` (to a quote; or `unmatched` → research 1 BD) → `applied_full` (exact figure ≤ amount) → `paid_in_full` → `remitted` (A/A draft settled; S/A–S/S LAR accepted and draft matched on the 20th/18th) → `housekeeping_complete` → `closed`. Branches: `applied_short` (amount < exact − tolerance) → `shortage_demand` → `cured` → `paid_in_full` | `absorbed` → `paid_in_full` | `uncured_30d` → funds applied per note (installment(s) then curtailment) and the loan stays active; `applied_over` → `paid_in_full` + `overage_refund_pending` → refunded. `reversed_pre_close` (returned item before BD2 17:00 ET) → correcting removal event, ledger reopened; `reversed_post_close` → `fnma_liquidated_in_error` (5.3 open question 2). Actors: `payoff-release` (all), `cashiering` (posting), `investor-reporting` (LAR/CRS), `fnma_portal_operator` (CRS upload), `officer` (post-close funding decision).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Payoff date.** `payoff_date = credited_as_of` = date funds are received (F-1-09), or the closing agent's settlement date for S/S loans when a closing agent pays (F-1-20); the F-1-09 non-business-day rule applies; a check that later returns unpaid reverses everything as of the original date.
2. **Exact figure at receipt.** Recompute the 16.1 quote as of `payoff_date` (`exact_total`). `variance = amount − exact_total`. If `|variance| ≤ tolerance` (`payoff.short_tolerance_cents`, default $50; over-tolerance $1) → apply as paid in full and write the difference to `payoff_tolerance_expense`/refund; if `variance > $1` → paid in full + overage refund within 10 BD; if `variance < −$50` → short-payoff path (rule 5).
3. **Application to zero** (Allocation Engine, payoff mode): order = accrued interest at the note rate → principal (interest-bearing UPB) → NIB deferred → NIB forborne → escrow advance → late charges → NSF/other fees → corporate advances → third-party recording/release fee → buydown/suspense credits applied first as offsets. Every loan account reads zero afterwards except `escrow` (refund pending) and `suspense/unapplied` (overage pending).
4. **Fannie Mae's share and interest by remittance type.** `principal_due = (UPB + NIB) × participation_pct`; interest: A/A = `UPB × PTR ÷ 12 × months_full + UPB × PTR ÷ 365 × days_partial` from the LPI date up to but not including the payoff date; S/A = `UPB × PTR ÷ 24`; S/S = `UPB × PTR ÷ 12` (zero for the payoff month when processed BD1 and reported by BD2). Servicing fee = interest collected at the note rate − interest due at PTR (A/A); for S/A the servicer keeps any collected interest beyond the half month and funds any deficit; for S/S the servicer funds `ss_interest_gap = full month at PTR − collected PTR-equivalent` from corporate funds (expense).
5. **Short payoff.** Demand within 1 BD to the party that remitted (closing agent/borrower) with the exact shortfall and per diem; funds held in `suspense_items{short_payoff}` (never applied as a curtailment during the cure window); disposition: cured → paid in full as of the original payoff date with additional interest only if the state and statement allow (reliance states: no additional interest); statement error or reliance state → `servicer_absorbed`/`reliance_absorbed` (corporate expense; a §1024.35(b)(6) NoE is opened by 4.1 if the borrower disputes); not cured in 30 days and no reliance → funds applied per the note, loan continues, borrower notified; Fannie Mae is always remitted the full amount due — the servicer funds any gap.
6. **Reversal.** Before BD2 17:00 ET of the reporting period: correcting removal event, ledger reopened by the Reversal Engine (2.x), housekeeping tasks cancelled/reversed (refund stop-pay, MI reinstatement request, tracker re-add). After close: `fnma_liquidated_in_error` — the amount due to Fannie Mae has been (or will be) remitted; `officer` decides funding per 5.3 open question 2; borrower servicing continues.
7. **Advances and buydown.** Fannie Mae-advanced funds recovered from the borrower are remitted by special remittance within 30 days (never inside the payoff draft); servicer advances are corporate recoveries; remaining buydown funds reduce the borrower's payoff and are remitted with the proceeds (A/A: added to the CRS 001 amount; S/S–S/A: reported on the LAR and drafted **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED mechanics]**).
8. **Recording costs.** Collected from the borrower only under C-1.2-05; otherwise paid from corporate funds and, for portfolio/special-servicing MBS loans, claimed under F-1-05 within the 60-day claim window — never netted from proceeds.
9. **Housekeeping fan-out** (tasks table): escrow refund (3.5; after the 5-BD in-flight-disbursement hold, never beyond 20 BD), short-year statement (3.3), MI notify/refund (10.5), insurance mortgagee-interest removal and LPI cancellation (9.1/9.5), tax authority/tax-service delete (3.7), credit status 13 (8.1), 1098 tagging (interest at the note rate collected in the payoff year), autodraft stop (2.3), paid-in-full letter, records retention start, eNote paper copy where required (16.4), custody-document request (16.3).
10. **Post-payoff receipts** (autodraft that pulled after payoff, duplicate wire, borrower's extra check): `suspense_items{post_payoff_receipt}` → refund within 10 BD (6.5); never applied to fees.

**Worked example (A/A portfolio, 100% participation).** UPB $199,500.00, note rate 6.250%, PTR 6.000% (25 bps servicing fee), LPI 09/01/2026 (`accrual_start` 09/01), payoff wire received and final Fri 10/16/2026 11:40 ET citing quote `Q-1187` (good-through 10/16). Exact figure: September full month at the note rate = 199,500 × 0.0625 ÷ 12 = **$1,039.06**; October 10/01–10/15 = 15 days = 199,500 × 0.0625 ÷ 365 × 15 = **$512.41**; interest collected **$1,551.47**; total with $0 fees = **$201,051.47** = amount wired → variance $0 → posted to zero 10/16 (`loan.paid_in_full`, payoff date 10/16). Fannie Mae's interest at PTR: $997.50 + $491.92 = **$1,489.42** (5.3-T1); servicing fee retained **$62.05**. Remittance: proceeds > $2,500 → CRS code 001 for **$200,989.42** instructed in the 15:00 ET batch Fri 10/16 (before 16:00 ET) → settles Mon 10/19; `FNMA_F120_PAYOFF_AA_IMMEDIATE` satisfied. LAR 96 action code 60 (principal `0019950000{`, interest `0000148942{`, LPI advanced to the payoff month) due Mon 10/19 20:00 ET (processed 10/16; next `fannie_et` BD), accepted 10/19 09:10 ET; period close (finality) Tue 11/03/2026 17:00 ET (BD2 of November). Housekeeping: autodraft stopped 10/16; MI notified by Tue 10/20; insurance interest removed and tax-service delete by Fri 10/23; escrow $2,412.90 refunded by **Mon 11/16/2026** (20 federal business days; Veterans Day 11/11 excluded) — issued 10/26 after the 5-BD in-flight hold; short-year statement by **Tue 12/15/2026** — sent with the refund; status 13 in the November Metro 2 cycle; paid-in-full letter 10/19; Form 1098 for 2026 includes $1,551.47 plus January–September interest. *S/S variant:* Fannie Mae is due a full October month at PTR, **$997.50**, drafted 11/18/2026 with the LAR-reported amounts; the borrower's October PTR-equivalent was $491.92, so the servicer funds **$505.58** (`payoff_interest_shortfall_expense`); had the payoff been processed Mon 11/02 (BD1) and reported by Tue 11/03 (BD2), no November interest would be due. *S/A variant:* Fannie Mae is due half a month, **$498.75**, drafted 11/20/2026; the servicer funds the $6.83 deficit against the $491.92 collected.

Custodial: Dr `custodial_pi_cash` / Cr `clearing_cash` (UPB + NIB + interest collected + buydown); Dr `corporate_cash` / Cr `clearing_cash` (fees, advance recoveries, recording fee); Cr `fnma_remittance_payable` (Fannie share) and Cr `servicing_fee_withdrawable` (fee) against `custodial_pi_cash` recognition; on draft Dr `fnma_remittance_payable` / Cr `custodial_pi_cash`; S/S gap Dr `payoff_interest_shortfall_expense` (corporate) / Cr `corporate_cash` and Dr `custodial_pi_cash` / Cr `fnma_remittance_payable`; overage Dr `custodial_ti_cash` / Cr `clearing_cash` then refund Dr `suspense/unapplied` / Cr `custodial_ti_cash`; escrow refund per 3.5; shortage absorbed Dr `payoff_shortfall_expense` / Cr `custodial_pi_cash` funding.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`payoff-release` agent runs receipt-to-close: matches funds to quotes (`matchPayoffFunds`), recomputes the exact figure (`computePayoffQuote{as_of}`), decides variance disposition (`disposeVariance` with the tolerance table), posts (`postPayoff` via the Allocation Engine), computes Fannie Mae's share (`computeFnmaPayoffShare`), builds the CRS batch (`buildCrsBatch`) and the removal event (`projectRemovalPayoff` via `investor-reporting`), fans out housekeeping (`createHousekeepingTasks`) and drafts borrower/closing-agent communications. Decision record: `{settlement_id, payoff_date, method, exact_total, amount, variance, disposition, remittance{type, amount, code, due}, removal{due_at, submitted_at}, tasks[], evidence[], confidence}`. Guardrails: no `removal.payoff` without `payoff.funds.cleared`; no demand for more than a reliance-protected figure; absorbed shortages > $500 need `officer` approval (package: quote, statement, funds evidence, state reliance rule, variance analysis); no reversal of a closed-period payoff; CRS amounts must equal `payoff_settlements` values; the LLM never computes money. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (CRS upload; LSDU UI fallback), `officer` (post-close error funding; absorbed shortages > $500; wire recall response), `human_agent` on request, `security-records` on any wire recall/fraud signal, `attorney` when a closing agent disputes a shortage under state reliance law. Disclosure: outbound calls to closing agents are business calls (TCPA consent rules for cell phones still enforced by `borrower-comms`). AI-off path: ops-console payoff workbench with the same tools and timers.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-out with funds in transit:** payoff received by the transferor after the transfer date is forwarded within 1 BD (17.x) with the original receipt date; if received by us before cut-over, we complete the payoff and the loan is excluded from the transfer tape (1.6 reconciliation).
- **Bankruptcy:** Chapter 13 payoff through the trustee — funds may arrive in pieces; apply on the final receipt; 3002.1(f)–(h) notice of final cure interplay (14.x); automatic stay does not bar accepting a payoff.
- **SCRA:** interest at the capped rate; any over-collection refunded (13.9).
- **Foreclosure/reinstatement mix-ups:** funds that only reinstate are not a payoff (13.x); a payoff during foreclosure triggers dismissal instructions to the firm (13.x) and firm fees are collected only if included in the statement.
- **Disaster/forbearance:** deferred/forborne balances always collected; forbearance status does not delay posting.
- **Successor in interest / estate payoff:** payer may be an estate or buyer; refunds go to the borrower of record or confirmed successor per 4.4 payee rules.
- **Partial data:** unmatched wire (no reference) → research 1 BD (6.5); if it equals an open quote ± tolerance, apply; else hold.
- **Wire recall/fraud claim** after posting: funds are not returned without the sending bank's indemnity and `officer` approval; if returned before close → correcting event; after close → `fnma_liquidated_in_error`.
- **Duplicate payoffs** (borrower and closing agent both pay): second receipt is `post_payoff_receipt` → refund within 10 BD to the remitter.
- **Fannie Mae ack reject on LAR 60** (e.g., NIB omitted): fix and resubmit before 20:00 ET; hard reject after BD2 → coordinate with the Investor Reporting Representative (5.3).
- **Escrow disbursement in flight at payoff:** 3.5 hold ≤ 5 BD; refund never later than 20 BD.
- **MBS Express:** unscheduled principal drafted BD4 of the following month — funding gate in 6.3.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Account for the S/S payoff-interest gap as expense (16.2) rather than `servicer_advance_receivable` (5.2)? **Default: expense; 5.2 naming to be aligned.**
2. Short-payoff tolerance and absorption authority? **Default: $50 auto-absorb; ≤ $500 agent; > $500 `officer`.**
3. Uncured shortage after 30 days: apply per the note (loan stays open) or hold indefinitely? **Default: apply per the note with notice; escalate to `officer` if > $1,000.**
4. Payoff by ACH debit (borrower-initiated pull): permit? **Default: ≤ $25,000 with a 5-BD good-funds hold; above that wire only.**
5. Who funds `fnma_liquidated_in_error` gaps — partner or Supermortgage? **Default: per 5.1/5.3 (partner funds; Supermortgage bears cost only if caused by its error).**
