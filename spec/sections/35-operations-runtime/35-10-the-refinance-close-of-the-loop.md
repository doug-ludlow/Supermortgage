### Blueprint row
A refinance on this platform ends where it began: the loan that 20.1 evaluated and 33.3 readied is paid off by the loan that 35.6 funds. Today the end is one SQL statement. When the refinance application's `loan.funded` / `loan.boarded` arrives, the 33.3 flow runs `UPDATE loans SET status = 'paid_off' WHERE id = $1 AND status = 'monitored'` and appends `partner_book.loan.paid_off` (`src/runtime/borrower/flows/16-readiness.ts:40-41` states the rule; `:289-290` is the write, inside `paidOff`). No 16.1 quote is computed, no 16.2 funds are matched or posted, no ledger account is zeroed, no 16.3 release task opens, no 3.5 escrow refund or credit is issued, no partner is told anything, and a prior loan Supermortgage itself services — `status = 'active'`, the lifecycle journey's own loan — is not retired by that path at all, because `PgLoanRepository.projectStatus` flips a row to `paid_off` only on `loan.paid_in_full` and only from `staged`/`active` (`src/infra/db/loans.ts:49-50`), and nothing unattended emits `loan.paid_in_full`: the only non-test callers of 16.1 `computePayoffQuote`, 16.2 `matchPayoffFunds` and `postPayoff` are the journey fixture's hand-fed `payoff()` phase (`src/runtime/borrower/fixtures/journey.ts:476-484`, which types the UPB, the rate and the escrow balance into the tool inputs) and `src/runtime/lifecycle.test.ts` phase e. The gap assessment lists this as "Refinance payoff of the prior loan" (severity blocking; evidence `16-readiness.ts:40-41, 289-290`, `loans.ts:49-50`, `journey.ts:476-484`). On the origination side 24.4 already models the demand (`payoff_demands.same_servicer`, `servicing_loan_id`, `db/migrations/0091_title_settlement.sql:146`) and 26.3 already gates funding on the demand's good-through date (`FC_PAYOFF_GOOD_THROUGH`, `src/domain/closing/ops-26-3.ts:374`), but nothing joins the demand to 16.1's `payoff_requests` or carries the disbursement back to the prior loan's ledger. This process is that join: one closeout per refinance application with a prior loan on the platform, in one of two modes — `serviced_same_servicer` (the prior loan is `active`: 16.1 quotes, 16.2 settles and zeroes, 3.5 refunds or credits the escrow, 16.3 releases the lien, 16.2's housekeeping fans out) or `monitored_partner` (the prior loan is `monitored`: 24.4 demands the partner's statement, the settlement agent wires the partner, the retirement is evidenced by the final settlement statement, the partner is notified and its next tape confirms) — driven by the owning sections' events, run by the sweep, ending with the prior `loans` row retired by projection of an event that names its evidence and the new `loans` row linked to it.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-15)
**Payoff statement to the refinancing lender (12 CFR §1026.36(c)(3); comment 36(c)(3)-1; eCFR current as of 2026-09-04 per 16.1).** "A creditor, assignee or servicer, as applicable, must provide an accurate statement of the total outstanding balance that would be required to pay the consumer's obligation in full as of a specified date … sent within a reasonable time, but in no case more than seven business days, after receiving a written request from the consumer or any person acting on behalf of the consumer." Comment 36(c)(3)-1 names the refinancing lender's request as one made on the consumer's behalf. 24.4 rule 7 adopts this for the same-servicer case ("SM issues the statement through servicing 16.1"); 16.1 owns the clock (`REGZ_1026_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT_7BD`), the accrual (F-1-09: interest to but not including the day funds are received, the partial month on a 365-day year) and the accuracy gate. Nothing here recomputes a figure: the quote is 16.1's row, the exact figure at receipt is 16.2's.

**Escrow on payoff (12 CFR §1024.34(b)(1)–(2)).** "Within 20 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays) of a borrower's payment of a mortgage loan in full, a servicer shall return to the borrower any amounts remaining in an escrow account" (b)(1); (b)(2) (24.4 cites it as (b)(2)(iii); the paragraph is one exception — [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — sub-paragraph numbering not re-checked against the eCFR here]): the servicer may instead credit the balance to the new loan's escrow account where "the new lender … uses the same servicer for both loans" and the borrower agrees in writing before the new loan's settlement. 3.5 owns `REGX_1024_34B_PAYOFF_ESCROW_REFUND_20BD` (satisfied by `disbursement.issued{payoff_refund}` or `escrow.credit_to_new_loan.posted`); 30.3 owns the consent gate `REGX_1024_34B_SAME_SERVICER_CREDIT_AGREEMENT_GATE` (`consents{kind=escrow_credit_to_new_loan}` captured at or before the new settlement date). A `monitored` loan's escrow is the partner's obligation under the same rule; Supermortgage holds none of it and issues nothing.

**Payoff processing and release (Fannie Mae Servicing Guide F-1-09, C-3-02 as 16.2 cites it, edition per 16.x's sources).** Interest is collected to the payoff date, proceeds are remitted under the loan's remittance type (A/A: CRS code 001 the day funds are final; the LAR action code 60 by the next Fannie Mae business day 20:00 ET), the lien is released within the state's deadline and the MIN deactivated after recording; statutory penalties are never passed to the borrower or Fannie Mae. 16.2, 16.3 and 16.4 own every one of those clocks; this process emits their trigger events by running their tools in order and never arms one itself.

**Arizona release (A.R.S. §33-707; §33-712 — 16.3's matrix row, [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the matrix is 16.3's, current through its 2026-09-09 verification; not re-verified against the statute here]).** Deed of release and reconveyance within 30 days after satisfaction; actual damages, and $1,000 plus actual damages if not released within 30 days after written request. Both worked examples are Arizona loans (the lifecycle fixture at 100 N Central Ave, Phoenix; partner-book demo loan 1 at 1200 W Maple Ave, Phoenix), so `jurisdiction_rules.release.deadline_days = 30`, `deadline_anchor = payoff`, `instrument_type = deed_of_release_and_reconveyance`.

**The partner's payoff notification [UNVERIFIED — a contractual term of the partner-book agreement, not a rule of law].** 33.1 states the partner's tape as the record of a monitored loan and its later tape as the confirmation of a payoff ("The partner marks a loan paid … on a later tape → `paid_off`"). That a partner receives a same-day notification of a payoff routed through the platform's refinance program, and what it carries, is a clause of the partner agreement the build assumes (servicer loan number, payoff date, amount and wire reference; never the new loan's terms). Counsel confirms before the first non-FAKE partner.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) The flow writes `loans.status` directly and only for `monitored` (`16-readiness.ts:40-41`, `:289-290`); the build removes `paidOff` from the flow and makes the flip a projection (35.1's contract) of `loan.paid_in_full` (serviced) or `refinance.prior_loan.retired{mode=monitored_partner}` (monitored) in `PgLoanRepository.projectStatus` (`loans.ts:49-50` gains the second event and the `monitored` source state). The literal `partner_book.loan.paid_off` and its payload are kept — emitted by `closeout.retire` in the monitored mode — so 33.3-T6 and 34.3-T3 hold unchanged. (2) The only drivers of 16.1/16.2 are hand-fed (`journey.ts:476-484` types `upb_cents`, `rate_pct`, `lpi_due`, the escrow balance and the custodial ids into the inputs; `lifecycle.test.ts` phase e likewise); the build derives every input from the record inside the pass (the UPB and LPI from the 35.5 installment schedule and the ledger, the rate from `loan_terms`, the custodial ids from `custodial_accounts`) and refuses a client-supplied figure on the hosted API (`NO_CLIENT_STATE`, 35.1-T10's rule). The journey's `payoff()` phase becomes an assertion over what the sweep did (T14). (3) 24.4 records the demand on the application (`payoff_demands.same_servicer`, `servicing_loan_id`) while 16.1 records the request on the loan (`payoff_requests`); nothing links them. The build has `closeout.quote` run 24.4 `requestPayoff` and, in the serviced mode, 16.1 `computePayoffQuote` + `renderStatement` in the same unit of work scoped to both ids (`PgUnitOfWork.run({ loanId: prior, applicationId })`), writing `payoff_demands.servicing_loan_id` and `refinance_closeouts.quote_id` / `payoff_demand_id` from the same commit. (4) 32.11-T7 says the same-servicer payoff completes "without a third-party wire"; 26.3's worksheet and the settlement agent's statement carry the payoff as a disbursement line. The build reads them as one: the payoff line is disbursed as an `internal_transfer` (16.2's `payoff_funds.method` already lists it) from the funding disbursement to the prior loan's custodial clearing account, booked by 26.3 `postLedger` and 2.1's receipt set in the same sweep as `loan.funded`, so the funds are `cleared` on receipt (no good-funds hold for a transfer between the platform's own accounts) and 16.2 posts the same day. (5) 35.6's orchestration folds `loan.funded` into the new loan's hand-off in the same sweep; the closeout runs after `orchestration.pass` in every sweep (rule 10) so `loan.staged` — which names the new loan — is on the log before `closeout.retire` links it; when 30.2 stages later (a snapshot gap), the link waits and the retirement does not. (6) The earlier §35 plan (`/root/gate-logs/s35/plan35.html`, written at d1238cc) had no refinance process; its table, journal, receipt and global-clock patterns (35.6's `closing_orchestrations` / `_steps`, 33.2's global override) are reused here rather than re-invented.

#### State machine
Per closeout (`refinance_closeouts.step`, in order; `→` is the event that completes the step):
`opened` —(`application.received{prior_loan_id}`; `mode` from `loans.status`: `active` → `serviced_same_servicer`, `monitored` → `monitored_partner`; `paid_off`/`transferred_out`/`staged` → `cancelled{prior_not_retirable}` and an `ops_analyst` escalation)→ `refinance.closeout.opened` → `awaiting_schedule` (`clocked = false`; the application's own pipeline) —(`closing.scheduled`: `closeout.quote` runs 24.4 and, serviced, 16.1 as `payoff-release`; good-through = the projected disbursement date + 3 calendar days of per-diem cover, 24.4 rule 6; #### Business rules and calculations
1. **The mode is the prior loan's status, read once.** `closeout.open` reads `loans.status` for `applications.prior_loan_id`: `active` → `serviced_same_servicer` (Supermortgage services the loan — originated on the platform through 30.2, or transfer-boarded through 1.1; the ledger, the schedule and the custodial accounts exist), `monitored` → `monitored_partner` (33.1's row — no ledger, no schedule; the partner's tape facts are the record). A prior loan not on the platform has no `prior_loan_id` and no closeout: 24.4's external path runs alone, as today.
2. **Nothing here computes a payoff figure.** The quote is 16.1's `payoff_quotes` row (serviced) or 24.4's `payoff_demands` row from the partner's statement (monitored); the exact figure at receipt is 16.2's; the planning figure between statements is 24.4 rule 6's `total_cents + per_diem_cents × days past good-through`. The closeout copies `quoted_total_cents`, `per_diem_cents` and `good_through` from those rows for the board and refuses any tool input carrying a figure (`NO_CLIENT_STATE`).
3. **Inputs come from the record.** For the serviced mode the pass derives 16.1's inputs itself: `upb_cents` = the loan's `principal` ledger balance at the quote's `ledger_snapshot_id`, `rate_pct` from the current `loan_terms`, `lpi_due` = the first `loan_installments` row with `status = due` (35.5) — so `accrual_start` is the day after the paid-through date — `state` from `properties`, late charges and fees from the ledger, the escrow balance from the `escrow` account; the custodial ids for 16.2 from `custodial_accounts` of the loan's partner. A hosted call that supplies any of these is refused.
4. **Same-servicer funds move by internal transfer, once.** On `loan.funded`, the settlement statement's payoff line for the prior loan (matched to the demand by `payoff_demands.id` on the line) is booked by 26.3 `postLedger{op: payoff_transfer}` as a balanced set — funding disbursement account (−) / the prior loan's custodial clearing (+), `rule_ref = 35.10:r4:transfer` — and by 2.1's receipt set (clearing / `suspense_unapplied`, `rule_ref = 2.1:r8:receipt`); 16.2 `matchPayoffFunds{method: internal_transfer, bank_reference: <funding wire IMAD>}` clears it on receipt (no good-funds hold between the platform's own accounts — `SM_PAYOFF_GOODFUNDS_GATE` is satisfied by the same commit), `postPayoff` applies it in 16.2 rule 3's order and emits `loan.paid_in_full{payoff_date = disbursement_date}`. Idempotent on `(closeout_id, funded_event_id)`: a second sweep folding the same `loan.funded` finds `funds_id` set and runs nothing.
5. **The line must equal the figure.** The statement's payoff line is compared to 16.2's exact figure at the disbursement date; `|variance| ≤ payoff.short_tolerance_cents` (16.2 rule 2, $50 short / $1 over) applies as paid in full through 16.2's own tolerance accounting; a larger variance is 16.2's short or over path — `waiting_human{officer}` for `disposeVariance` (a money field; the pass never disposes) — and the closeout is `held{money_mismatch}` with `SM_REFI_PRIOR_SETTLE_1BD` still running, because the new loan disbursed.
6. **Escrow is credited or refunded, never netted, and never by this process's arithmetic.** `escrow_treatment` is 24.4 `decideEscrowTreatment`'s pre-closing decision: `credit_to_new_loan` requires a `consents{kind=escrow_credit_to_new_loan}` row captured at or before the new loan's settlement date (30.3's gate) and posts 3.5's credit — the prior loan's `escrow` balance to the new loan's `escrow` account, `rule_ref = 3.5:credit_to_new_loan` — in the settlement transaction so 30.2's opening escrow set reads it and the CD's initial deposit is reduced by exactly that amount; `refund` issues 3.5's `payoff_refund` disbursement after the 5-BD in-flight hold and never beyond 20 federal business days; `partner_obligation` (monitored) records that the partner refunds under §1024.34(b) and the borrower's Thread says so (32.11). The `escrow.payoff.net_against_shortfall` flag is 3.5's and is off.
7. **The retirement is an event with evidence; the status is its projection.** `closeout.retire` refuses (`NO_SETTLEMENT`) unless the serviced prior loan has `loan.paid_in_full` on its log with a `payoff_settlements.status = paid_in_full` row, or the monitored prior loan's demand reads `paid` with the settlement statement's payoff line (amount, payee = the partner's account of record, wire reference) and `evidence_document_id`. It writes the `prior_loan_retirements` row and emits `refinance.prior_loan.retired`; `PgLoanRepository.projectStatus` flips `loans.status` from `active` (on `loan.paid_in_full`, as today) or from `monitored` (on the retirement event) — no service, flow or screen writes `loans.status` (`NO_DIRECT_STATUS_WRITE`; a contract test greps the tree). `loans.refinanced_by_loan_id` is written when `loan.staged` names the new loan, in whichever sweep that lands.
8. **The partner is told the same day and confirms by tape.** In the monitored mode `closeout.notify_partner` writes one outbox row (`integration_messages`, adapter `partner-book.notify`, idempotency key `retirement:<prior_loan_id>`) carrying the partner's servicer loan number, the payoff date, the amount wired, the wire reference and `refinance_program: true` — never the new loan's number, rate or amount — and a `partner_retirement_notifications{kind: notified}` row; `SM_REFI_PARTNER_CONFIRM_21` runs from `notified_on`. The next 33.1 tape whose row carries the loan as paid satisfies it (`partner_book.retirement.confirmed{source: tape}`); an acknowledgement on the channel does too (`source: ack`); a tape that still carries the loan as active after the notification writes `disputed` with the tape's status, and the analyst's `book.resolve{paid_off}` writes `resolved` (`source: ops_resolve`). A loan the partner reports paid before we notify (the settlement agent's wire beat the sweep) confirms on notification.
9. **The lien release is 16.3's; the closeout only opens and watches it.** Serviced mode: `closeout.lien_release` runs 16.3's tools through `routeForExecution` on `loan.paid_in_full` (16.3's `SM_RELEASE_PREPARE_5BD` from the payoff date) and then reads `lien_release.*` events; the `signing_officer`'s execution, the eRecording and `NTC_LIEN_RELEASE_RECORDED` are 16.3's acts and notices. Monitored mode: the partner releases; no task opens; the closeout reads nothing about it (34.3's report shows the partner's tape status).
10. **One pass per sweep, after the orchestration, under the lease.** `closeout.pass{as_of}` runs after `orchestration.pass` (so `loan.staged` precedes the link) and before the breach pass (so what it satisfies is never breached by the same sweep), claims closeouts with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` under 35.1's `record.lease`, folds events newer than `last_event_sequence` over both aggregates, and journals every command with its trigger event and decision id. Three failures on one step → `held{attempts}`.
11. **The board is a receipt.** `closeout.board{as_of}` writes `refinance_closeout_daily_receipts` and `refinance.closeout.daily.run_completed`; the 35.8 Refinance board reads the receipt and the open rows; the receipt is stored as a document (35.2).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`payoff-release` agent (tools: `closeout.open`, `closeout.pass`, `closeout.quote`, `closeout.settle`, `closeout.escrow`, `closeout.lien_release`, `closeout.retire`, `closeout.notify_partner`, `closeout.confirm_partner`, `closeout.hold`, `closeout.resume`, `closeout.board`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: unprompted, every sweep, the agent opens a closeout for every new refinance application with a prior loan on the platform, orders the quote when the disbursement date is known and re-orders it when the date moves past good-through, books the internal transfer and runs 16.2 to zero on the day the refinance disburses, disposes the escrow as the borrower elected, retires the prior loan on the owner's settlement event, opens the release or notifies the partner, links the new loan when 30.2 stages it, produces the daily receipt, and journals every act with the owner's decision id. It proposes for a person: the disposition of a variance beyond 16.2's tolerance (`officer`), a reversal after settlement (`officer`), the release's execution (`signing_officer`, 16.3's act), a partner discrepancy after 21 days (`ops_analyst`). It never computes a payoff figure, never writes `loans.status`, never nets an escrow balance, never sends the partner the new loan's terms. Decision record schema `{closeout_id, application_id, prior_loan_id, mode, step, command: {process, name, op}, trigger_event_id, owner_decision_id, figures: {quoted_total_cents, per_diem_cents, exact_total_cents, variance_cents} (cents-strings, copied from the owner's row), rule_set_version: closeout.v1, model_version: deterministic, prompt_version: 35.10-v1, confidence: 1, rationale}`. Guardrails: `ONE_CLOSEOUT_PER_APPLICATION`, `NO_CLIENT_STATE`, `NO_MONEY_FIELD` (every figure is 16.1's, 16.2's or 24.4's; `disposeVariance` and a reversal are `officer` commands the agent may only propose), `NO_SETTLEMENT` (no retirement without `loan.paid_in_full` or a paid demand with evidence), `NO_DIRECT_STATUS_WRITE`, `ESCROW_CREDIT_NEEDS_CONSENT` (30.3's gate; absent → `refund`), `NO_NETTING`, `PARTNER_PAYLOAD_MINIMAL`, `OWNER_AGENT_ACTS` (each owning tool runs as its owning agent), `IDEMPOTENT_ON_FUNDED_EVENT`. Escalations: `officer` for `SM_REFI_PRIOR_SETTLE_1BD`, `SM_REFI_ESCROW_CREDIT_0`, a variance beyond tolerance and any post-settlement reversal; `ops_analyst` for `SM_REFI_CLOSEOUT_STALLED_2BD`, `SM_REFI_PARTNER_CONFIRM_21`, `SM_REFI_CLOSEOUT_BOARD_DAILY` and a prior loan that cannot be retired; `signing_officer` and `attorney` as 16.3 names them.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- The prior loan is `active` but not yet purchased (`fnma_loan_number` null, `warehouse_advance_id` set) → the payoff repays 27.1's advance through 27.2's waterfall (`remitted_to = warehouse_paydown`), 16.2's CRS and LAR steps are `skipped` in the journal with the reason; `loan.paid_in_full` still retires it.
- Two Yes taps, one application (33.3) → one closeout; a second application with the same `prior_loan_id` while a closeout is open → `cancelled{prior_loan_in_closeout}` on the second and an `ops_analyst` escalation.
- `loan.funded` lands and the settlement statement carries no payoff line for the prior loan → `held{payoff_line_missing}`, `waiting_vendor{settlement_agent}`, `SM_REFI_PRIOR_SETTLE_1BD` running.
- The demo clock advances past good-through with no disbursement (a stalled orchestration) → `closeout.quote` re-runs on `funding.date.resynced` only; the stale quote stays `superseded_by` null until then; the board shows the closeout `quoted` with days past good-through.
- Rescission exercised after the quote → `unwound`; the quote superseded, the demand cancelled, nothing posted, the prior loan untouched; 16.1's statement retained.
- A settlement reversed inside the finality window (16.2 rule 6, `officer`) → `payoff.reversed` reopens the prior loan (`loans.ts:49-50`'s existing path), the retirement row is followed by a `prior_loan_retirements` row with `retired_on` null and `retirement_event_id` = the reversal (append-only: the second row supersedes), `refinanced_by_loan_id` cleared, the closeout back to `settling`.
- The partner's tape lists the loan as paid before the notification is sent → `confirmed` on notification; the tape lists it as active 21 days after → `disputed`, escalation, `book.resolve`.
- A monitored prior loan whose demand the partner answers `short_payoff` (24.4 rule 8) → the closeout `held{short_payoff}`; 24.4's rejection blocks funding; no retirement.
- The prior loan's `escrow` balance is negative (an advance) → 16.1 includes it as `escrow_advance_cents` in the quote; `escrow_treatment = none`; nothing to credit or refund.
- `loan.staged` never lands (35.6's `SM_ORCH_HANDOFF_AFTER_FUNDED_4H` breaches) → the closeout completes every step but `linked`, stays `open{waiting_on: 35.6}` and appears on both boards.
- The pass finds a closeout whose prior loan is already `paid_off` with a settlement from an unrelated 16.x payoff (the borrower paid it off outside the refinance) → `completed{already_retired}` with the existing settlement id; no second retirement row (unique `prior_loan_id`).

What an examiner is shown, per refinance: the closeout row and its journal (every step, the owning command it ran, the trigger event, the actor and the owner's decision id), 16.1's quote and statement with its hash and token, 24.4's demand and statement, the transfer, receipt and application ledger sets with `rule_ref`, 16.2's settlement and CRS instruction, the escrow consent or the refund disbursement, the retirement row with its evidence document, the release task and the recorded image, the partner notification's outbox row and the tape row that confirmed it, the timers' histories (the owners' clocks and this process's five), the escalations and who completed them — each as the stored row, exported through 34.4's evidence pack for the prior loan, the application or the new loan (the pack follows `prior_loan_id`, `refinanced_by_loan_id` and `applications.loan_id` so one request returns the whole loop).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should the same-servicer payoff move as an internal transfer or as the settlement agent's wire to the servicer's account of record (24.4 rule 13)? **Default: internal transfer booked by 26.3 `postLedger{op: payoff_transfer}` in the sweep that folds `loan.funded` (32.11-T7's "without a third-party wire"); the settlement statement still lists the line, and a partner that requires a wire is a jurisdiction-free configuration on the partner row, not a rule.**
2. Does the closeout wait for `lien_release.recorded` before `completed`, or complete at `linked` and leave the release to 16.3's clocks? **Default: it waits (`released_or_confirmed`), unclocked, so the board shows the loop open until the county records; 16.3's clocks alone escalate.**
3. What does the partner notification carry, and by which channel? **Default: servicer loan number, payoff date, amount, wire reference, `refinance_program: true`, over the FAKE `partner_api`; nothing about the new loan — counsel confirms the clause before the first non-FAKE partner.**
4. Should `PgLoanRepository.projectStatus` learn the retirement event, or should the monitored mode emit `loan.paid_in_full`? **Default: the projector learns `refinance.prior_loan.retired{mode=monitored_partner}`; `loan.paid_in_full` stays 16.2's and means a settlement on this platform's ledger.**
5. A prior loan still in the warehouse (not purchased) when the refinance funds — is 16.2's remittance replaced by 27.2's waterfall? **Default: yes (`remitted_to = warehouse_paydown`), the CRS and LAR steps journaled `skipped`; 27.2 owns the money.**
