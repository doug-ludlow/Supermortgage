### Blueprint row
docs/ux/03 ends the application at R8 "Underwriting runs — `du.submitted → du.findings.received`" and times it at "1–3 min, system", but nothing on the platform starts it: 23.1's casefile, credit association, request, submission and findings are tools only fixtures and tests call, the request's ULAD snapshot is a hand-built object, the 32.x flows only react to `du.*` events, the credit report is ordered by tests "as the platform", and the P7 assets connection (`ConnectCard{plaid_assets}`) is a journey step no flow sends. The product goal is a DU-ready application at the end of the conversation with an immediate answer. This process closes the loop: the assets card rides with the other connectors, the credit pull is the platform's on the borrower's authorization, and when the last prerequisite lands the platform assembles the request from the application tables, puts the 12-month asset report on it, submits, receives the findings and interprets them in one settlement — the FAKE vendors answering at once in every build stage, the real ones on their own clocks.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-14)
**The DU validation service (Selling Guide B3-2-02, 02/05/2025).** DU validates income, employment and assets from a verification report obtained from an approved report supplier, referenced in the casefile; a 12-month asset verification report may validate assets, and — for salaried and hourly borrowers with regular payroll deposits that match the employer stated on the application — employment and income. The lender does not pass bank data to Fannie Mae: the report is ordered with the borrower's authorization, and the DU submission carries the supplier's report identifier; DU retrieves the report from the supplier. Validated components earn representation-and-warranty relief on the validated data; a component DU could not validate is documented the standard way (B3-3.1). The report must be recent relative to the note date. 22.4 already holds the report as `verification.received{kind=assets}` with `supplier_code`, `report_reference_id` and `report_days` (23.1 reads them into `validation_report_refs`); 23.1's findings already carry `validation_results` and 23.2 already gives relief per validated component. **[UNVERIFIED tolerance]**: the FAKE's 10% income tolerance stands in for DU's own matching, which the real findings replace.

**Findings are confidential (B3-2-11; 23.1/23.2 guardrails).** No DU message text, recommendation word or DU figure reaches the borrower (32.3 T-03-19); the borrower sees the StatusCard while DU runs and the ChecklistCard of conditions with owners afterwards, and the assistant speaks about it in the copy library's plain words.

**The credit pull (FCRA §604(a)(3)(A); 22.2).** A consumer report may be furnished in connection with a credit transaction involving the consumer; 22.2 refuses an order without a recorded permissible purpose and the borrower's authorization reference. 32.17 rule 20 wrote the hard-pull authorization on the goal's tap (`credit.authorization.captured{kind=hard_pull, authorization_id}`); 32.3 E5 captured the SSN as the one typed field. Nothing then ordered the report.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) docs/ux/03 P7 sends a ConfirmCard of accounts after the assets report and reserves the assets connection for purchases ("Assets appear only if DU asks" — R8); this process sends the assets card with the other connectors on every application and treats the connected report as the borrower's statement of assets (no accounts ConfirmCard: the connection is the tap, rule 1), because the report is what validates employment and income through DU. (2) 32.3's timeline places R8 after R7 (the six items); the platform's persona test ran DU after the LE, the lock and the appraisal order because 24.1 read the FAKE findings' value-acceptance offer as a string — rule 7 fixes 24.1 and the persona test now lets the platform run DU at its moment.

### Operational prerequisites
- Fannie Mae seller number, DU system ID and TSP product reference for the partner (23.1's `CreateCasefileInput`) — partner configuration; the nonprod build carries FAKE values (`123456789`, `SYS-PARTNER-01`, `SM-TSP`).
- A DU validation service asset report supplier agreement covering Plaid (22.4's `DU_ASSET_REPORT_SUPPLIERS`); the FAKE stands in for Plaid in every build stage (32 principle: every vendor is an in-repo FAKE).
- The partner's credit reseller subscriber code and certification reference (22.2's `subscriber_code`, `certification_ref`) — partner configuration; FAKE values in nonprod.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `application.received` (32.3 E6: the goal's tap) — the assets `ConnectCard{plaid_assets}` is sent with the identity and payroll cards (rule 1).
- `application.trid_received` with the hard-pull authorization and the SSN on file (`application.six_item.captured{item=ssn}`) — the last of the three orders the credit report (rule 2).
- `application.trid_received`, `credit.report.received`, `verification.received{kind ∈ {income, assets}}`, `application.six_item.captured{item=income}`, the assets card declined — each re-evaluates the DU moment (rule 3); the last prerequisite to land runs it.
- `POST /v1/borrower/connect/plaid_assets/session {card_instance_id, fake_complete?}` — the page's tap on the assets card (32.17 rule 19: the FAKE finishes on the tap).
- `du.findings.received` / `du.findings.interpreted` — 32.3's StatusCard and ChecklistCard, unchanged; the situation's `underwriting` view for the turn (rule 6).

#### Data model
No new tables. Baseline tables written: `application_assets` (+ rows from the report: `asset_kind`, `institution`, `account_last4`, `balance_cents`, `verified = true`), `verifications` (22.4's `kind = assets` row), `credit_reports` (22.2), `du_casefiles`, `du_submissions`, `du_resubmission_checks` (23.1), `du_findings_interpretations`, `conditions` (23.2), `card_instances` (the assets ConnectCard; the StatusCard and ChecklistCard), `loan_events` (`asset_report.ordered`, `verification.received`, `credit.report.ordered`, `credit.report.received`, `du.casefile.created`, `du.credit.associated`, `du.submitted`, `du.findings.received`, `du.findings.interpreted`), `agent_decisions` (`underwriting.run`).

#### State machine
Per application: `assets: not_started → in_progress → connected | declined | failed` on the ConnectCard (32.3 §1 card states; `declined` is "Not now"); `credit: authorized → ordered → received` (22.2 owns the report's own states); `du: waiting → running → findings_received → interpreted` — `waiting → running` only when every prerequisite of rule 3 holds and no `du_casefiles` row exists for the application (the platform performs it; no human); `running → findings_received → interpreted` inside the same settlement on the FAKE, on the vendor's clock otherwise. Terminal here: `interpreted` (23.3 takes the decision).

#### Timers and gates
None owned by this process. 23.2's conditions clock (`SM_DU_CONDITIONS_SLA_4H`) is armed by `du.findings.received` as before; 22.2's report expiry and 23.1's archival clocks are theirs.

Jurisdiction overrides: none.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **The assets card rides with the connectors.** On `application.received` the flow sends `ConnectCard{vendor=plaid_assets, copy_key=assets.connect.purpose, command_ref=verification.connect}` beside the identity and payroll cards ("Connect with Plaid"; what we get: the accounts your down payment and reserves come from, twelve months of balances and deposits). `POST /v1/borrower/connect/plaid_assets/session` opens the vendor session on the card (a pending card is resolved first: the tap is the order); on the FAKE with `fake_complete: true` the deterministic report lands at once the way the vendor's callback would: the report onto the card, 22.4 `orderAssetReport{op=receive}` (`supplier_code = plaid`, `report_days = 365`, the accounts, the borrower's authorization = the goal card's authorization), one `application_assets` row per account (`verified = true`), the card `connected` with the `verification_id`. Nothing is posted by the page. "Not now" declines the card; DU then runs without a report (rule 3) and the standard asset documentation applies. The P7 accounts ConfirmCard is not sent (discrepancy 1).
2. **The credit pull is the platform's.** When the hard-pull authorization (the goal's tap, 32.17 rule 20 — on the lead record's `credit_authorizations[]`), the SSN (`application.six_item.captured{item=ssn}`) and the six items (`application.trid_received` — 22.2 R1 / §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(B): no hard pull before them, `SIX_ITEMS_AND_FEE_FIRST`) are on the application and no `credit.report.ordered` is, the flow orders the report once: 22.2 `orderCreditReport` (tri-merge; `permissible_purpose = credit_transaction_604a3A`; `borrower_authorization_ref` = the authorization id; the partner's `certification_ref` and `subscriber_code`; `fee_sm_borne = true` — the pull is free to the borrower before the LE, 32.3 R2) for every borrower on the intake record, then `parseCreditReport`. The FAKE bureau answers at once: `credit.report.ordered`, `credit.report.received`, and 32.3 R2's liabilities and current-loan cards follow as before. A second capture of either event orders nothing more; a refusal is logged (`borrower.flow.32-18.credit.refused`) and the record's next need stands.
3. **The DU moment.** When the last of these lands — `application.trid_received`; a usable `credit.report.received`; income on file (an `application_income` row or `verification.received{kind=income}`); the assets card settled (`connected`, or `declined`, or no assets card on the application) — and no `du_casefiles` row exists for the application, the flow runs `underwriting.run` (this process's one tool, on the bus as `borrower-app`) in that settlement: the snapshot from the application tables (rule 4); 23.1's casefile (the partner's seller number, system id and TSP reference; `score_model` = the report's); `associateCredit` for every borrower with the report; `buildDuRequest{credit_and_underwriting, initial}` with `validation_report_refs` = every `verifications{kind=assets}` row as `{supplier_type: supplier_code, identifier: report_reference_id, report_type: asset_verification_<report_days>d}`; `submitCasefile` (`scif_facts.borrowers[].scif_presented_at` = the profile card's resolution, else the application's receipt; `projected_note_date` null before a lock); `fetchFindings`; 23.2 `parseFindings{op=interpret}` with the findings' messages, `validation_results`, offer and MI requirement. On the FAKE this is one settlement: `du.casefile.created`, `du.credit.associated`, `du.submitted`, `du.findings.received`, `du.findings.interpreted`; 32.3's StatusCard `du.running` rises and the ChecklistCard of conditions follows. A second trigger with a casefile on file runs nothing. A refusal from any owning tool is logged (`borrower.flow.32-18.du.refused`) with its code and never shown as DU text; the record's next need stands.
4. **The projection (the ULAD snapshot from the tables).** `loan_purpose` from `applications.transaction_type` (`limited_cash_out → limited_cash_out_refinance`, `cash_out → cash_out_refinance`, `purchase → purchase`); `occupancy` (`primary → principal_residence`, `second_home`, `investment`); `product`, `amortization`, `loan_term` from the product on file (`FRM30 → fixed_30, fixed, 360`; `FRM15 → fixed_15, fixed, 180`; default `fixed_30`); `property_type` from `application_properties.property_type` (default `sfr_detached`); `sales_price_cents` = the purchase price on a purchase else null; `appraised_value_cents` = `property_value_estimate_cents`; `loan_amount_cents` = `loan_amount_sought_cents`; `note_rate_pct` = the newest pricing quote for the application, else the newest published rate sheet's par rate for the product, else `6.500`; `qualifying_income_cents` = Σ `application_income.monthly_amount_cents` where `qualifying` (else the six-item income); `total_obligations_cents` = Σ `application_liabilities.monthly_payment_cents` not `paid_at_closing` and not the refinanced mortgage, plus the proposed principal and interest at the note rate (22.4's `monthlyPI`); `borrowers[]` = every `application_borrowers` row as `{borrower_id, last_name (the last word of the legal name), suffix null, ssn_last4 = tin_last4}`; `max_ltv_pct` `95.00` for a refinance, `97.00` for a purchase. A snapshot missing a value, a loan amount, an income or a borrower's last four is refused (`DU_SNAPSHOT_INCOMPLETE`) and logged; nothing is submitted.

**Worked example A (the proposed payment in the snapshot).** $560,000.00 at 6.125% over 360 months: r = 0.06125 ÷ 12 = 0.005104167; (1 + r)^360 = 6.252620; P&I = 560,000 × 0.005104167 × 6.252620 ÷ 5.252620 = 3,402.6205 → **$3,402.62**. The fixture bureau's report carries no tradelines (the refinanced mortgage would be excluded in any case), so `total_obligations_cents` = **$3,402.62**; with `qualifying_income_cents` = **$8,200.00** the FAKE's DTI is 340,262 ÷ 820,000 = 41.4954% → 4,149.54 basis points, half-up to 4,150, displayed `41.50`.

5. **Validation through the report (the FAKE DU).** The FAKE DU validates from the request alone: an asset verification reference present → `assets: validated` for every borrower; a 365-day reference whose FAKE report shows payroll deposits for at least two months → `employment: validated`; `income: validated` when the snapshot's qualifying income is within 10% of the report's monthly payroll deposits, else `income: not_validated` (employment stays validated). A 30- or 60-day reference validates assets only. No reference → no validation results. 23.2's interpretation then gives relief on the validated components (no income documentation condition for the borrower when income is validated — the FAKE, like DU, issues no documentation message for a validated component); a component not validated keeps its documentation condition (the paystub and the W-2 as its evidence; 23.3's decision opens the requests the UploadCards answer). The real DU replaces the FAKE's arithmetic with its own matching; the shapes are 23.1's already.
6. **The read-back.** The situation the turn reads carries `underwriting` once findings exist: `{ran_at, validated: [components], conditions_for_you: n, checklist_card_instance_id}`, and the journey names the checklist as the current ask when conditions wait on the borrower. The turn speaks about it in the copy library's plain words — underwriting has run, what is on the checklist — never a DU message text, a recommendation word (Approve, Eligible, Refer, Ineligible) or a DU figure (the guard's provenance rule already refuses figures outside tokens; 32.3 T-03-19 keeps every `/v1/borrower/*` payload clean).
7. **24.1 reads the findings' offer as an object.** 23.1's findings carry `value_acceptance_offer = {offered, property_value_cents}`; 24.1's `readDuOffer` read it as a string and threw on the object. It now reads `{offered: true}` as `value_acceptance`, `{offered: false}` and null as `none`, and the string forms as before.

#### Integrations
- **`plaid-assets`** (outbound; Plaid Link + Assets API, `POST /asset_report/create` with `days_requested = 365`, the report retrieved on the `PRODUCT_READY` webhook; the DU validation service report identifier is the Plaid asset report token registered with Fannie Mae; idempotency: one report per card; retry: the webhook re-delivers; outage: the card shows the fallback UploadCard for bank statements — 32.13 T-X-12) — **[UNVERIFIED endpoint/fields]**; the FAKE (`FakePlaid`, `vendor: "FAKE"`) returns a deterministic report (two accounts: checking `····4821` $18,400.00, savings `····7730` $42,250.00; payroll deposits of $8,200.00 a month for twelve months from "Acme Manufacturing (FAKE payroll)") and completes on the tap.
- **`fnma-du`** (23.1's port, unchanged): the FAKE port computes `validation_results` from the request's `validation_report_refs` and the FAKE asset report registry (rule 5).
- **`credit_bureau`** (22.2's port, unchanged): the fixture bureau answers the order at once.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Rows: `application_assets` (from the report), `verifications{kind=assets}`, `credit_reports`, `du_casefiles`, `du_submissions`, `du_findings_interpretations`, `conditions`, `card_instances` (`assets.connect.purpose`; `du.running`; the ChecklistCard), `agent_decisions{underwriting.run}`.
- Events: `asset_report.ordered`, `verification.received{kind=assets}`, `credit.report.ordered`, `credit.report.received`, `du.casefile.created`, `du.credit.associated`, `du.submitted`, `du.findings.received`, `du.findings.interpreted`.
- Documents: 23.1's request document and findings (Fannie Mae-confidential; never a borrower document). Notices: none of its own (23.3's decision notices follow).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`borrower-app` agent (tools: `underwriting.run`) — `underwriting.run` is the DU moment as one bus command: the projection from the tables, then 23.1's `associateCredit`, `buildDuRequest`, `submitCasefile`, `fetchFindings` and 23.2's `parseFindings` through the same delegation 32.2 uses, each under its owner's guardrails (never a ULAD edit without a source, never a submission without every borrower's report, never findings to the borrower). It runs unprompted on the last prerequisite (rule 3), proposes nothing to a human (23.3's decision and any counteroffer or denial stay the reviewer's), and raises `DU_SNAPSHOT_INCOMPLETE` or the owning tool's refusal as a logged exception with the record's next need unchanged. The 32.16 turn never calls it: the model reads the outcome from the situation (rule 6).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- The assets card declined ("Not now") → DU runs without a report; no validation; the standard documentation conditions.
- The credit report unusable (frozen repositories, no score, mixed models) → 22.2's own states; the DU moment waits for a usable report; the neutral re-run line (32.3 T9) stands.
- A co-borrower joins after the run → 23.1's resubmission rules (a new borrower's report associated, `data_change`); out of scope here beyond the first run.
- The FAKE DU returns `approve_ineligible` (DTI over 50%) → 23.2 interprets it; the journey's R8 shows underwriting started; 23.3's restructure path (32.3 R8) follows.
- A refusal from 23.1 (e.g. `SCORE_MODEL_MIXED`, `SM_O21_SCIF_PRESENT_GATE`) → logged with the code; nothing is shown as DU text; the next trigger re-evaluates.

#### Audit and evidence
What an examiner is shown: the `agent_decisions{underwriting.run}` record with the snapshot hash and the report references; 23.1's request and findings documents (Fannie Mae-confidential, exported to the examiner through 23.1's evidence pack, never to the borrower); the `verifications` row and the `application_assets` rows it produced; the credit order's permissible purpose and authorization reference (22.2); the `card_instances` trail (the assets card's tap, the StatusCard, the ChecklistCard) and the `ui_events` beside it; the timer history of `SM_DU_CONDITIONS_SLA_4H`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should the assets card be optional for a refinance with no cash to close? **Default: sent on every application; "Not now" is one tap and DU runs without it.**
2. Re-run DU on pricing (the note rate changes at the quote)? **Default: not here — 23.1's `data_change` resubmission rules own it; the first run uses the quote on file, else the sheet's par rate.**
