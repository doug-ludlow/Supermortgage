# Section 1 — Boarding / Servicing Transfer-In

## Overview

Section 1 covers everything that happens between the moment the partner (master servicer of record, "SoR") decides to move a Fannie Mae portfolio onto Supermortgage and the moment every loan in that portfolio is a fully reconciled, fully instrumented record in the platform's Postgres system of record. Three kinds of inbound moves are in scope: (a) the partner moves loans it was servicing itself to Supermortgage as subservicer (master-to-subservicer); (b) the partner replaces a prior subservicer with Supermortgage (subservicer-to-subservicer); (c) the partner buys servicing from a third party and names Supermortgage as subservicer at the same time (servicing transfer with a subservicer, Form 629 with the subservicer indicated). All three need Fannie Mae's prior written consent (A2-7-03, A2-1-07), all three produce a RESPA §6 "transferor/transferee" relationship for the borrower-facing entity that changes, and all three end with Supermortgage as the "acting servicer" that must report events to Fannie Mae from the transfer date (LL-2026-05 FAQ definition, research/00a §3.1).

The section is executed by the `transfer` agent (portfolio-level orchestration, Form 629 package, counterparties, notices) and the `boarding` agent (loan-level intake, data-quality gate, exception resolution), with `custodial-recon` (funds and balance reconciliation), `escrow` (escrow-line setup, initial statements), `lossmit-underwriter` (in-flight applications), `investor-reporting` (first-cycle LAR/events, Escrow Setup events), `security-records` (document ingestion, retention classes) and `compliance-sentinel` (timers, breach reporting). It relies on the Timer Engine (every deadline below is a `timer_definition`), the ledger (opening balances are posted as balanced `ledger_entries` linked to `loan.boarded`), the Notice Registry (hello notice, initial escrow statement, acknowledgment notices) and the integration framework (`mers`, `custodian`, `fnma-lsdu`, `fnma-servicing-events`, `print-mail`, `tax-service`, `flood`, `insurance-tracking/lpi`, `mi/*`).

Partner/Supermortgage boundary for this section: the partner owns the Fannie Mae relationship (Form 629 submission in Quick Exchange, the servicer number the loans move under, MERS Servicer-of-record status on each MIN, Form 101 execution at inception) and, as master servicer of record, remains fully liable to Fannie Mae for every servicing obligation (A2-1-07) — including the custodial accounts — and executes wherever a form requires the servicer of record. **Custodial accounts are Supermortgage's, not the partner's:** A2-1-07 provides that "each subservicer must establish custodial accounts for all Fannie Mae mortgage loans that it subservices for a master servicer," so Supermortgage establishes and owns the Fannie Mae-only P&I and T&I custodial accounts for the subserviced loans, executes Forms 1013/1014 in CBAM under the partner's 9-digit servicer numbers (Form 101 grants that access), and keeps them separate from any other investor's funds and from loans it services directly for Fannie Mae. Section 6.1 owns this boundary and resolves the architecture-baseline §1 open decision in favor of Supermortgage-established accounts; Sections 1, 2 and 6 all state it that way. Supermortgage also prepares every package, performs every operational step, is named Subservicer on each MIN, sends the RESPA "hello" notice as the entity that will receive payments, and becomes the acting servicer for reporting. Every Fannie Mae portal step (Quick Exchange, CBAM) is a `human_portal_task` escalation with an AI-prepared package — no browser automation (Consolidated Technology Guide, Apr 21, 2026).

Dependencies: 1.6 hands reconciled opening balances to 2.1/2.2 (cashiering) and 6.3/6.4/6.5 (custodial reconciliation and unapplied funds); 1.6 and 3.1/3.2 (initial statement / first analysis); 1.7 hands in-flight cases to 12.1–12.3 and seeds 13.1/13.2 gates; 1.1 seeds 11.1/11.2 early-intervention timers for loans boarded delinquent and the FDCPA flag used by 11.4; 1.4/1.5 feed 16.3/16.4 (lien release, MERS deactivation) and 13.3 (assignment out of MERS before foreclosure); 1.2 is the inbound mirror of 17.1–17.4 (transfer-out); 1.1 populates the 19.4 fair-lending data elements; investor reporting responsibilities in the transfer month are split with 5.1/5.7.

Changes in flight that shape the build: the Servicing Guide edition is August 12, 2026 (A2-7-03, F-1-11 and A2-1-07 last changed 05/13/2026 by SVC-2026-04); eTransfers were retired 10/31/2025 and Form 629 now goes through Quick Exchange only; Forms 1013/1014 must be executed in the Custodial Bank Account Management app (mandatory Aug 1, 2026) and Form 101 is required at inception of the subservicing arrangement; LL-2026-05 makes Escrow Setup events mandatory for "newly acquired loans" from December 1, 2026 and requires the acting servicer to report within 3:00 a.m. ET next business day; the CFPB's 2024 Reg X proposal is not final, so 1.7 is built to current §1024.41(k) with the NPRM behind the `regx.lossmit.2024nprm` rule-set flag; CFPB Bulletin 2020-02 survived the May 2025 guidance withdrawal and remains the supervisory yardstick for transfer planning.
