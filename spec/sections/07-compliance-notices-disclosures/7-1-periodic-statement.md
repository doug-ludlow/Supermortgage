### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**12 CFR 1026.41 (eCFR current as of Sept. 3, 2026; CFPB interactive regulation and Official Interpretations verified today).**
- **(a) Scope.** Closed-end consumer credit transactions secured by a dwelling. "A servicer ... shall provide the consumer, for each billing cycle, a periodic statement meeting the requirements of paragraphs (b), (c), and (d)." For a billing cycle shorter than 31 days (bi-weekly loans, Section 2.5) "a periodic statement covering an entire month may be used" (comment 41(a)(2)-1). "Servicer includes the creditor, assignee, or servicer, as applicable"; a creditor/assignee that no longer owns the loan or the servicing rights is not subject to the section. The obligation sits with the acting servicer; Fannie Mae relies on it.
- **(b) Timing.** "The periodic statement must be delivered or placed in the mail within a reasonably prompt time after the payment due date or the end of any courtesy period provided for the previous billing cycle." Comment 41(b)-1: "Delivering, emailing or placing the periodic statement in the mail within four days of the close of the courtesy period of the previous billing cycle generally would be considered reasonably prompt." Comment 41(b)-2 points to comment 7(b)(11)-1 for "courtesy period" (an informal or contractual period after the due date before a late fee is assessed; the due date disclosed is still the contractual due date).
- **(c) Form.** "Clearly and conspicuously in writing, or electronically if the consumer agrees, and in a form that the consumer may keep." Sample forms H-30(A)–(F) in appendix H; "proper use of these forms complies with" (c) and the layout requirements of (d). Comment 41(c)-3: statements "may be provided electronically if the consumer agrees. The consumer must give affirmative consent to receive statements electronically"; a notification that the statement is available "with a link to where the statement can be accessed" may be sent "in place of the statement itself." Comment 41(c)-4: a consumer "currently receiving disclosures for any account ... electronically from their servicer shall be deemed to have consented" — a presumption this platform does **not** rely on (see 7.4; policy: explicit E-SIGN-grade consent for every class).
- **(d) Content and layout.** (1) *Amount due* — grouped at the top of the first page: payment due date; the amount of any late payment fee and the date it will be imposed if payment is not received; the amount due, "shown more prominently than other disclosures on the page." (2) *Explanation of amount due* — on the first page, grouped: monthly payment amount with a breakdown of principal, interest and escrow (if applicable); total of fees or charges imposed since the last statement; any payment amount past due. (3) *Past payment breakdown* — on the first page, grouped: total payments received since the last statement with allocation to principal, interest, escrow, fees and charges, and any amount sent to a suspense or unapplied funds account; and the same totals since the beginning of the calendar year, including the amount "currently held in any suspense or unapplied funds account" (comment 41(d)(3)-1 and its three examples govern how suspense funds roll across statements). (4) *Transaction activity* — every credit/debit to the amount currently due since the last statement, with date, description and amount; a late fee description "includes the date of the late fee, the amount of the late fee, and the fact that a late fee was imposed" (comment 41(d)(4)-2); a partial payment sent to suspense "must be in the transaction description" (comment 41(d)(4)-3). (5) *Partial payment information* — if a partial payment was placed in suspense, "information explaining what must be done for the funds to be applied," on the front page or a separate enclosure. (6) *Contact information* — toll-free number and, if applicable, an email address, on the front page. (7) *Account information* — outstanding principal balance; current interest rate; the date the rate may next change (ARMs); existence of any prepayment penalty; the CFPB/HUD homeownership-counselor website and HUD toll-free number (H-30(D) clause). (8) *Delinquency information* — "if the consumer is more than 45 days delinquent," on the front page or a separate enclosure: the date delinquency began; a notification of possible risks such as foreclosure and expenses; an account history for the previous six months or since the account was last current, showing the amount remaining past due from each billing cycle or the date a payment was credited as fully paid; any loss-mitigation program the consumer has agreed to; whether the servicer has made the first notice or filing for foreclosure; the total amount needed to bring the account current; and a reference to the counselor information. Comment 41(d)(8)-1: delinquency "is measured as of the date of the periodic statement," begins "on the date an amount sufficient to cover a periodic payment ... becomes due and unpaid, even if the consumer is afforded a period after the due date to pay before the servicer assesses a late fee"; comment 41(d)(8)-2: FIFO application advances the start date (its example: due Feb. 1, unpaid; on Feb. 4 the consumer is "three days delinquent, ... using February 2 as the first day of delinquency" — i.e., day 1 is the day after the due date, which is exactly the baseline's `regx_days_delinquent` counter).
- **Temporary loss-mitigation programs (trial period plans).** Comment 41(d)-4: payment application must be shown "according to the loan contract, regardless of the temporary loss mitigation program"; comment 41(d)(1)-2: the amount due "may identify either the payment due under the temporary loss mitigation program or the amount due according to the loan contract"; comment 41(d)(2)-2: the explanation "must include both." Acceleration: comments 41(d)(1)-1 and 41(d)(2)-1 (show the lesser reinstatement amount as the amount due; the explanation lists both the reinstatement and the accelerated amount, not the monthly payment).
- **(e) Exemptions.** (1) reverse mortgages, (2) timeshares, (7) PACE — not in portfolio. **(3) Coupon books** for fixed-rate loans only, if each coupon carries the (d)(1) items, the book contains the (d)(6)/(d)(7) items and instructions for obtaining the (d)(2)–(5) information on request, and the servicer "provides the consumer the information listed in paragraph (d)(8) ... in writing, for any billing cycle during which the consumer is more than 45 days delinquent." **(4) Small servicer** — 5,000 or fewer loans "for all of which the servicer (or an affiliate) is the creditor or assignee" (comment 41(e)(4)-2); comment 41(e)(4)-3 lets a master servicer keep the status when it retains a subservicer only if both qualify. **Not available here:** Fannie Mae is the assignee, so neither the partner nor Supermortgage can ever qualify — statements are required on every loan. **(5) Bankruptcy** — exempt while a consumer is a debtor (or has a discharge) *and* one of four conditions holds (written request to cease, plan surrender/lien avoidance/non-payment, court order, or Chapter 7 statement of intention to surrender with no post-petition payment); the exemption ends on reaffirmation or a written request for statements (comment 41(e)(5)(ii)-1); a request is effective on receipt (comment 41(e)(5)-3), the most recent request controls (-2), an agent's request counts (-1), a revived case re-triggers the exemption (-4), any one joint obligor's status triggers it (-5) and either spouse's request for statements must be honored (-6); **(e)(5)(iv)** one "single-statement" exemption after each triggering event, then full compliance. **(6) Charged-off loans** — exempt if no further fees or interest will be charged and, within 30 days of charge-off, a statement titled "Suspension of Statements & Notice of Charge Off — Retain This Copy for Your Records" is sent with the seven listed disclosures; fees/interest charged during the exemption may not be retroactively assessed and statements must resume. **(f) Modified statements in bankruptcy** (H-30(E) Chapter 7/11, H-30(F) Chapter 12/13): omit late-fee, delinquency-length and risk language; add the debtor/discharge and "for informational purposes only" statements; Chapter 12/13 statements limit amount due and explanation to post-petition amounts, show pre-petition arrearage figures, and add the trustee-payment statements; any obligor may receive it ((f)(4)); comment 41(f)-4 allows further modification "to facilitate compliance with title 11, the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, court orders." CFPB FAQs (Mar. 20, 2018) confirm trustee payments actually received are transaction activity, that H-30(E)/(F) use is "unlikely to violate the automatic stay," that statements may go to counsel where bankruptcy law bars direct contact, and that confirmed successors in bankruptcy get (f)-modified statements. **(g) Successor in interest** — no statements to a confirmed successor who has not assumed the debt until the 1024.32(c)(1)(iv) acknowledgment is executed (Section 4.4).
- **Reg Z 1026.36(c)(1)(ii)** (verified): a servicer holding a partial payment in suspense "shall (A) disclose ... the total amount of funds held in such suspense or unapplied funds account on the periodic statement as required by § 1026.41(d)(3)... and (B) on accumulation of sufficient funds to cover a periodic payment ... treat such funds as a periodic payment" (2.2). Comment 36(c)(1)(i)-4: a "periodic payment" during a temporary loss-mitigation program is still the contractual amount.

**Fannie Mae (Aug. 12, 2026 Guide).** No statement content rule beyond "applicable law." **D2-2-03, Sending a Payment Reminder Notice (08/13/2025, SVC-2025-05):** the servicer must send a payment reminder "no later than the 20th day of the month if the payment has not been received," unless the loan is a second lien or "the borrower is subject to an active forbearance plan"; the notice must "address the borrower by name," "state a desire to work with the borrower to preserve homeownership," "state the amount of late charges that are due, if applicable," point to HUD-approved housing counseling on HUD.gov and to "additional educational resources on Fannie Mae's consumer website." Delivery method is not prescribed (A4-2.1-04 permits "mail, email, texting, and voice response unit technology" "as permitted by applicable law"). **C-4.2-01, Filing IRS Forms (06/09/2021):** the servicer files Form 1098 "when applicable for each mortgage loan held in whole or in part by Fannie Mae" using its own name and TIN (1099-A/C are filed on Fannie Mae's behalf with TIN 52-0883107 per F-1-23 — Sections 12.9/15).

**IRS Form 1098 (Instructions for Form 1098 and General Instructions for Certain Information Returns, irs.gov, verified today; revision year as displayed **[UNVERIFIED — page showed a "12/2026" revision label that is likely a rendering artifact; confirm the tax-year 2026 revision]**).** File for each payer of record from whom "$600 or more of mortgage interest" was received in the calendar year in the course of trade or business; the first recipient of the interest (the collecting servicer) files; **furnish to payers by January 31**, file with the IRS by **February 28 (paper) / March 31 (electronic)**; electronic filing is mandatory at **10 or more** information returns in aggregate. Boxes: 1 interest received (excluding points), 2 outstanding principal as of January 1 (or the origination/acquisition date in the year), 3 origination date, 4 refunds of overpaid interest, 5 mortgage insurance premiums (reportable again for premiums paid after Dec. 31, 2025 under the July 4, 2025 reconciliation act's permanent MIP-deduction restoration **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**), 6 points, 7/8 property address, 9 number of properties, 10 other (e.g., real-estate taxes paid), 11 acquisition date. Electronic furnishing requires affirmative recipient consent demonstrating access, specified disclosures, posting by January 31 and continued access through **October 15** of the following year; withdrawal restores paper (Treas. Reg. §1.6050H-2; Pub. 1179).

**FDCPA overlay.** A borrower who has invoked cease-communication under FDCPA §805(c) still receives periodic statements; the CFPB's 2016 FDCPA interpretive rule provided a safe harbor for statements sent in compliance with §1026.41 **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — confirm the interpretive rule was not among the 67 guidance documents withdrawn May 12, 2025; research/00a §1.3 lists only Reg F advisory opinions]**; §1026.20(c) (7.2) has its own FDCPA exemption, §1026.41 does not.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) "reasonably prompt" is operationalized by commentary as **four days after the close of the courtesy period**, which the row does not state; (2) the row omits every exemption and variant — the 45-day delinquency box, the trial-period dual disclosure, the (e)(5)/(f) bankruptcy regime (cross-owned with 14.3), the (e)(6) charge-off notice, the coupon-book alternative and the successor rule; (3) the small-servicer exemption can never apply to Fannie Mae-owned loans; (4) research/00a attributed the "payment reminder by the 20th" to D2-2-02 — the topic is **D2-2-03**; (5) the IRS Form 1098 statement (January 31) is absent from the blueprint entirely and is built here as sub-process 7.1-A.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `statement.cycle.opened` — emitted by the cycle scheduler for each loan at 00:05 (property-state timezone) on the day after `courtesy_period_end` for the previous cycle (`courtesy_period_end = prior_due_date + loan_terms.late_charge_grace_days`, default 15 → due Oct 1 → Oct 16); also on `loan.boarded` for the first cycle (1.1: first statement due per the transfer-month rules in 1.3/17.2) and on `loan.reinstated`/`bankruptcy.*` transitions that change the variant.
- Data events consumed as of the snapshot cut-off (23:59:59 the day before generation): `payment.received/applied/reversed`, `suspense.item.created/applied`, `late_charge.assessed/waived`, `fee.assessed`, `escrow.payment.changed` (3.2), `loan_terms.version.activated` (7.2 rate/payment change), `lossmit.trial_period.started/ended`, `forbearance.*`, `foreclosure.first_notice.filed`, `bankruptcy.case.opened/closed/discharged/dismissed/reaffirmed`, `bankruptcy.statement_request.received` (cease/resume), `loan.charged_off`, `sii.acknowledgment.executed` (4.4), `address.changed`, `consent.esign.granted/withdrawn` (7.4).
- Mid-cycle: `payment.cycle.unpaid_day16` (fired at 00:05 on the 17th of the month for loans whose payment due that month is unpaid) → payment reminder path.
- Annual: `tax_year.closed` (Jan 2, 00:05 ET) → Form 1098 run; `tax_form.1098.correction_requested` (4.2 RFI or NoE) → corrected 1098.
- Borrower requests: `rfi.statement_copy.requested` (4.2), `statement.paper_requested` (7.4 withdrawal), `coupon_book.requested` (if enabled).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Cycle anchor.** `prior_due_date` = the due date of the previous billing cycle; `courtesy_period_end = prior_due_date + late_charge_grace_days` (Fannie Mae uniform note: late charge if not received "by the end of fifteen calendar days after the date it is due" → due Oct 1, 2026 → courtesy ends Fri Oct 16); `statement_due_by = courtesy_period_end + 4` → Tue Oct 20, 2026. The snapshot cut-off is `courtesy_period_end + 1 day, 01:00 local`, i.e., after the 2.7 late-charge job (00:05) has assessed the fee for the payment that missed the courtesy period, so the fee appears on the statement as "imposed since last statement"; the scheduler renders on Oct 17 and the vendor mails by Oct 19–20. No business-day roll: if day 4 is a Sunday the file goes to the vendor on Friday.
2. **Amount due** = current contractual payment(s) for the cycle (P&I from the active `loan_terms` version effective for the due date + escrow portion from `escrow_accounts.current_monthly_escrow`) + `past_due_cents` (sum of unpaid prior periodic payments, FIFO) + unpaid late charges + unpaid borrower-payable fees. Suspense balances are **not netted** against amount due; they are disclosed under (d)(3)/(d)(5) with the exact shortfall needed to complete a periodic payment (2.2 accumulation rule). Under acceleration (13.x) amount due = reinstatement amount.
3. **Late-fee line** ((d)(1)(ii)): `late_fee_cents = round_half_up(pi_cents × late_charge_pct)` capped by `jurisdiction_rules.late_charge_cap` (2.7); displayed as "If payment is received after {due_date + grace_days}, a late fee of {late_fee} will be charged."
4. **Delinquency box** ((d)(8)): include iff `regx_days_delinquent(statement_date) > 45`; `began_on = earliest_unpaid_due_date + 1` (comment 41(d)(8)-2 convention); history = last six cycles or since last current; `reinstatement_cents` = past due + late charges + fees + (if accelerated) allowable costs from 13.x; `first_notice_filed` from `cases(foreclosure).first_legal_filed`; `lossmit_program` from active `cases(lossmit|forbearance|repayment)` with status agreed.
5. **Variant selection order:** charged_off (e)(6) → bk exemption (e)(5)(i) → bk modified (f) by chapter → successor without acknowledgment (g) → tpp (active trial period, 12.8 / 2.6) → accelerated → delinquent (>45) → standard. Coupon book only for fixed-rate loans when the feature flag is on (default off).
6. **Trial period plan:** amount due shows the TPP payment (comment 41(d)(1)-2, policy choice for clarity); explanation shows both the contractual amount and the TPP amount (41(d)(2)-2); application shown per contract (41(d)-4); the delinquency box stays because the contract is still delinquent.
7. **Bankruptcy (f):** rendered from `bankruptcy` case facts (chapter, petition date, plan treatment, post-petition due, pre-petition arrearage from `ledger_accounts.prepetition_arrearage` maintained by 14.1/14.2); Chapter 12/13 shows only post-petition amount due and the (f)(3)(v) arrearage figures; "for informational purposes only" legend; no late-fee or foreclosure language; addressing per 14.3 (counsel when required, any obligor per (f)(4)).
8. **Single-statement exemption** ((e)(5)(iv)(B)): the first cycle after a triggering event may be skipped or sent unmodified; the second must comply — recorded in `single_statement_exemption_used`.
9. **YTD roll-forward:** YTD figures reset on Jan 1 from `payment_allocations`; suspense "currently held" is the live `suspense_items` balance at cut-off (comment 41(d)(3)-1).
10. **Payment reminder panel:** for statements dated on/after the 17th where the payment due that month is unpaid and no active forbearance exists, the front page carries a D2-2-03 panel (borrower name, "we want to work with you to preserve homeownership," late charges due, HUD.gov counseling, Fannie Mae's consumer website `knowyouroptions.com` **[UNVERIFIED URL — confirm the "consumer website" the Guide means]**). If the loan's statement cycle cannot carry it by the 20th (non-1st due dates, held statements), a standalone reminder is sent by the 20th (mail, or email/SMS under 7.4 consent) — feature `reminder.literal_20th` (default on; alternative "20 days after due date" as open question 3).
11. **Form 1098 (7.1-A):** `box1 = Σ interest applied (ledger `interest_due` credits from borrower funds) in the calendar year` excluding points and any interest paid by governmental assistance; `box2 = UPB as of Jan 1` (or the boarding-year acquisition date balance with box 11 set); `box5 = borrower-paid MI premiums` (10.x lines; ≥ $600 aggregate rule per instructions); `box10 = escrow real-estate taxes disbursed` (3.7) as an informational item; threshold: furnish/file when box1 ≥ $600 (policy: furnish to all payers regardless; file only ≥ $600). Co-borrowers: one 1098 to the primary payer of record with the account number; multiple payers → primary borrower (instructions). Corrections: new form with "CORRECTED" box; prior-year interest refunds go to box 4 of the year refunded.
12. **Successor addressing:** confirmed successor with executed acknowledgment (4.4) is added as recipient; a successor who has assumed the loan replaces the deceased borrower; otherwise the estate/borrower of record.

**Worked example (standard → delinquent).** Loan: $400,000 5/6 SOFR ARM at 5.750%, P&I **$2,334.29**, escrow **$612.50**, total payment **$2,946.79**; payments due the 1st, 15-day courtesy; late charge 5% of P&I = **$116.71**. September 1, 2026 payment not received. Cycle for the Oct 1 due date: courtesy for Sept 1 ends Sept 16; late charge assessed Sept 17 00:05; statement rendered Sept 17 (cut-off 01:00), mailed Sept 18: amount due **$6,010.29** ($2,946.79 current + $2,946.79 past due + $116.71 late charge); late fee line "if the Oct 1 payment is received after Oct 16, $116.71 will be charged"; fees since last statement **$116.71** (transaction activity: "09/17/2026 Late fee — $116.71"); delinquency on Sept 17 = 16 days → no (d)(8) box. Cycle for the Nov 1 due date: courtesy for Oct 1 ends Fri Oct 16; Oct late charge assessed Oct 17; rendered Sat Oct 17; mailed Mon Oct 19 (≤ Oct 20 ✓). `regx_days_delinquent` on Oct 17 = 46 (Sept 1 due; day 1 = Sept 2) → **(d)(8) box required**: "As of Oct. 17, 2026 you are 46 days delinquent; your first unpaid payment was due Sept. 1, 2026," six-month history (Sept: $2,946.79 remaining; Oct: $2,946.79 remaining; May–Aug: dates credited as paid), no loss-mit program, no foreclosure filing, amount to bring current **$6,127.00** ($5,893.58 + $233.42 late charges); amount due for Nov 1 **$9,073.79** = $2,946.79 current + $5,893.58 past due + $233.42 unpaid late charges, with "fees imposed since last statement $116.71." We need $1,446.79 more to apply a full payment."

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `disclosures`. Per cycle it (1) takes the snapshot via deterministic services (no LLM arithmetic), (2) selects the variant by the rule order above, (3) renders and runs the checklist, (4) resolves holds where a deterministic remedy exists (e.g., stale escrow payment → pull 3.2 output; address CASS failure → use last deliverable address and open research), (5) decides channel from `consents`, (6) sends, (7) logs a decision record `{loan_id, cycle_due_date, variant, exemption_inputs, days_delinquent, consent_id, channel, checklist_result_id, hold_reasons[], model_version, prompt_version, rule_set_versions}`. The LLM's role is limited to classifying hold reasons, drafting internal annotations, and answering borrower questions through `borrower-comms` with the snapshot as ground truth; it may not alter any number or template text.
- **Guardrails:** statements with any `block` rule failure cannot be sent; a cycle cannot be marked exempt without a linked evidence document (court order, written request image, charge-off approval); variant `bk_*` requires an open `bankruptcy` case with a PACER/vendor docket reference (14.1); the agent cannot suppress a statement for returned mail or FDCPA cease requests.
- **Escalations:** none legally required. `attorney` (via `bankruptcy-ops`) when a court order or trustee instruction conflicts with (f) content or addressing; `officer` for the annual IRS transmittal sign-off (policy) and for any decision to stop statements portfolio-wide (vendor outage > 2 days); `human_agent` on borrower request; `case` agent (4.1) when a borrower disputes a statement (NoE §1024.35(b)(1)–(3)).
- **Disclosure/consent:** e-delivery only with class consent (7.4); SMS availability alerts only with `tcpa_sms` consent; AI chat/voice explanations of a statement start with the automation disclosure (baseline §8.6).
- **AI-off path:** ops-console "Statement cycle run" executes the same deterministic pipeline; holds are worked from a queue by ops staff with the same checklist gate.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in month:** the transferor's statement for the first post-transfer due date may already have been sent; 1.3 decides which servicer statements the first cycle (default: Supermortgage sends from the first due date after the transfer date, with the hello notice); no duplicate statements.
- **Transfer-out:** statements stop for cycles with due dates after the transfer date (17.2); the last statement carries the goodbye reference; 1098 for the partial year is Supermortgage's (interest it received).
- **Bankruptcy filed mid-cycle:** rendered statement not yet mailed → re-render as (f) variant or apply the single-statement exemption; mailed → next cycle complies; cease request received (e)(5)(i)(A) → exempt from receipt date; reaffirmation/written request → resume next cycle.
- **SCRA 6% cap (13.9):** statements show the reduced rate/payment from the effective `loan_terms` version; retroactive credits appear as transaction activity.
- **Disaster/forbearance (12.4):** amount due shows the forbearance-plan amount? No — forbearance is not a temporary loss-mitigation "payment" unless the plan sets reduced payments; statements continue with contractual amounts, the (d)(8) box shows the forbearance as the agreed program; reminder notices suppressed (D2-2-03).
- **Successor in interest without acknowledgment:** no statement to the successor; continue to the borrower of record/estate (4.4).
- **Partial data at boarding** (missing escrow line or late-charge terms): statement held and `boarding` exception opened; if `statement_due_by` would breach, send with the best-evidence amount and a corrected statement when resolved (both retained, superseded link).
- **Vendor outage:** in-house print/mail path (certificate of mailing not required); e-delivery outage → mail.
- **Returned mail/undeliverable:** continue statements to the address of record while researching; NCOA-confirmed moves create address changes after 4.x verification.
- **Retro corrections/reversals:** a payment reversal after the statement is shown on the next statement's transaction activity; a statement with a material error (wrong amount due) is superseded by a corrected statement within 2 business days and the NoE process (4.1) is invoked if the borrower raised it.
- **Charged-off loan later collected on:** if any fee/interest is charged, the exemption lapses and statements resume ((e)(6)(ii)).
- **Coupon books (if enabled):** ARM loans excluded; when a coupon-book borrower crosses 45 days delinquent, the written (d)(8) notice issues each cycle.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Coupon books for fixed-rate loans? **Default: off** (one statement engine; no ARM/fixed split; delinquency notices still required).
2. Amount due on trial-period statements — TPP amount or contractual? **Default: TPP amount** with dual explanation (comment 41(d)(1)-2), matching Fannie Mae's TPP expectations.
3. Payment reminder timing for non-1st due dates — literal "20th of the month" or "day 20 after due date"? **Default: literal 20th** (Guide text), flag `reminder.literal_20th`.
4. Bankruptcy statements — send to debtor or counsel by default? **Default: debtor unless counsel/trustee instruction or court order says otherwise** (CFPB FAQ), decided per case in 14.3.
5. Furnish 1098s below $600? **Default: yes** (uniform borrower experience; IRS filing only ≥ $600).
6. Rely on comment 41(c)-4 presumed consent for borrowers already e-receiving other disclosures? **Default: no** — explicit 7.4 consent only.
