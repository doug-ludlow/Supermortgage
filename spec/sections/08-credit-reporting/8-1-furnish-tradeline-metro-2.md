### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Fannie Mae.** Servicing Guide **C-4.1-01, Notifying Credit Repositories (07/15/2020)** — verified at servicing-guide.fanniemae.com, Aug. 12, 2026 edition. The servicer must "Provide the status of each mortgage loan to each of the four major credit repositories as of the last day of each month." The statuses to report are: "new originations; current mortgage loans; delinquent mortgage loans, including 30–, 60–, 90+ day delinquencies, forbearances, repayment plans, Trial Period Plans, modifications; and liquidated mortgage loans, including short sales, Mortgage Releases, foreclosures, law enforcement property seizures and forfeitures under applicable state or federal law, and charge offs." The servicer must "Verify the appropriate reporting instructions with each of the four major credit repositories" and "Comply with all applicable laws when reporting to the four major credit repositories," and must "follow all procedures in Reporting to Credit Repositories in F-1-23." **F-1-23, Reporting to Third Parties (06/09/2021)** — the "Reporting to Credit Repositories" subsection is two paragraphs: when initially establishing the relationship "the servicer must indicate the relationship is being established in compliance with Fannie Mae's requirements," and the four repositories are listed as **Equifax Information Services, Experian, Innovis Data Solutions, TransUnion Corporation**. F-1-23 contains no Metro 2, frequency, disaster, bankruptcy, SCRA or workout language; everything operational is delegated to "the appropriate reporting instructions" of the repositories — i.e., the CDIA Metro 2 Format and CRRG. (F-1-23's other subsection, IRS Forms 1099-A/1099-C filed on Fannie Mae's behalf with TIN 52-0883107 and Form 1100, belongs to 15.x/16.x.)

**Discrepancies vs. the blueprint row.** (1) The blueprint's "credit bureaus" is four named repositories including **Innovis**, which most furnishers omit; Fannie Mae requires all four. (2) The "as of the last day of each month" snapshot rule is a hard Fannie Mae requirement (the blueprint says only "monthly"). (3) Fannie Mae's status list explicitly includes forbearances, repayment plans, Trial Period Plans and modifications — so the Metro 2 file must carry those states, not merely delinquency buckets. (4) SVC-2020-03 renumbered "Reporting to Third Parties" from F-1-24 to F-1-23 and retired C-4.1-02 (see 8.3). (5) "Automation class a" holds, but the FCRA accuracy program (Reg V §1022.42) requires documented human oversight of the automated pipeline (Appendix E III(d) sample verification, III(l) periodic evaluation).

**FCRA furnisher duties (15 U.S.C. 1681s-2(a), verified at law.cornell.edu).** (a)(1)(A): a person "shall not furnish any information relating to a consumer to any consumer reporting agency if the person knows or has reasonable cause to believe that the information is inaccurate"; "reasonable cause to believe" means "specific knowledge, other than solely allegations by the consumer, that would cause a reasonable person to have substantial doubts about the accuracy of the information." (a)(1)(B): no furnishing after the consumer notifies the furnisher, "at the address specified by the person for such notices," that specific information is inaccurate, "and the information is, in fact, inaccurate." (a)(1)(C): a furnisher that "clearly and conspicuously specifies" an address for such notices is not subject to (A) as to notices sent elsewhere — the platform designates one. (a)(2): a person who "regularly and in the ordinary course of business furnishes information" and "determines that any such information is not complete or accurate, shall promptly notify the consumer reporting agency of that determination and provide ... any corrections ... and shall thereafter not furnish" the incomplete/inaccurate information. (a)(3): if a consumer disputes the completeness or accuracy of furnished information to the furnisher, the furnisher "may not furnish the information to any consumer reporting agency without notice that such information is disputed by the consumer" (Metro 2 Compliance Condition Code XB). (a)(4): notify the CRA of voluntary account closures. (a)(5): for an account "placed for collection ..., charged to profit or loss, or subjected to any similar action," furnish "not later than 90 days after furnishing the information" the "date of delinquency," meaning "the month and year of the commencement of the delinquency on the account that immediately preceded the action." (a)(6): reasonable procedures to respond to identity-theft block notices and no re-furnishing of blocked information (see 8.3). (a)(7): a **financial institution** that furnishes "negative information" (late payments, missed payments, insolvency, "any form of default") must give the customer a written notice "prior to, or no later than 30 days after, furnishing the negative information"; one notice per account suffices for subsequent negative information; it may be delivered on a billing statement or other mailing but must be "clear and conspicuous"; the CFPB's model notices (Reg V Appendix B: **B-1** "We may report information about your account to credit bureaus. Late payments, missed payments, or other defaults on your account may be reflected in your credit report."; **B-2** "We have told a credit bureau about a late payment, missed payment or other default on your account. This information may be reflected in your credit report.") are a safe harbor. A nonbank mortgage servicer is a GLBA "financial institution" (15 U.S.C. 6809), so (a)(7) applies. **CARES Act §4021 — 1681s-2(a)(1)(F)**: "accommodation" (deferral, partial payment, forbearance, modification or other relief for a consumer affected by COVID-19) made during the "covered period" (Jan. 31, 2020 to the later of 120 days after Mar. 27, 2020 or 120 days after termination of the national emergency) must be reported "as current" (or, if delinquent before the accommodation, at the frozen delinquent status, then current if cured); charged-off accounts excepted. Pub. L. 118-3 (approved **April 10, 2023**) terminated the emergency, so the covered period ended **August 8, 2023**; only accommodations made before that date (and still in effect) remain subject to (F). **Obsolescence (15 U.S.C. 1681c, verified):** CRAs may not report bankruptcies older than 10 years, or accounts "placed for collection or charged to profit and loss" and "any other adverse item of information" older than 7 years (§1681c(a)(1), (4), (6)), and "The 7-year period referred to in paragraphs (4) and (6) of subsection (a) shall begin ... upon the expiration of the 180-day period beginning on the date of the commencement of the delinquency which immediately preceded the collection activity, charge to profit and loss, or similar action" (§1681c(c)(1)); a CRA notified under §1681s-2(a)(3) "shall indicate that fact in each consumer report that includes the disputed information" (§1681c(f)). The CRAs purge from the furnished Date of First Delinquency, which is why DOFD accuracy and non-re-aging (rule 3) are the most litigated furnisher issue; the platform additionally stops furnishing any 93/97 item whose DOFD is more than 7 years + 180 days old (policy). **Enforcement**: (a) duties are enforced by the CFPB/FTC/states only (1681s-2(c)–(d)); (b) duties (8.2) carry private liability under 1681n (willful: actual or $100–$1,000 statutory damages, punitive damages, fees) and 1681o.

**Regulation V Subpart E (12 CFR 1022.40–.43, eCFR current as of Sept. 1, 2026).** §1022.41: "accuracy" means information that "correctly reflects the terms of and liability for the account or other relationship; reflects the consumer's performance and other conduct with respect to the account; and identifies the appropriate consumer"; "integrity" means information that "is substantiated by the furnisher's records at the time it is furnished; is furnished in a form and manner that is designed to minimize the likelihood that the information may be incorrectly reflected in a consumer report; and includes the information in the furnisher's possession ... that the Bureau has determined ... the absence of which would likely be materially misleading" — Appendix E I(b)(2)(iii) lists only **the credit limit, if applicable** (not applicable to closed-end mortgages) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the eCFR extract confirmed the structure of I(b)(2), not the item text]**. §1022.42(a): "establish and implement reasonable written policies and procedures regarding the accuracy and integrity of the information" furnished, "appropriate to the nature, size, complexity, and scope" of the furnisher's activities; (b) consider the Appendix E guidelines; (c) review and update periodically. **Appendix E** objectives: accurate information identifying the right consumer, reflecting terms/liability and performance; substantiated by records; minimizing incorrect reflection; "conduct reasonable investigations of consumer disputes"; "update the information it furnishes as necessary to reflect the current status of the consumer's account," including transfers and cures. Components III(a)–(m): appropriate systems; "standard data reporting formats and standard procedures" (Metro 2); record retention "for a reasonable period of time" to substantiate disputed information; internal controls including "verifying random samples"; training; oversight of service providers; controls for "mergers, portfolio acquisitions or sales" to prevent "re-aging of information, duplicative reporting, or other problems"; deleting/updating/correcting; reasonable investigations; systems that prevent "duplicative reporting of accounts, erroneous association of information with the wrong consumer(s)"; sufficient identifying information; periodic evaluation of practices, CRA practices, disputes and corrections; FCRA compliance.

**Reg X / RESPA overlay.** §1024.35(i)(1): for 60 days after receipt of a notice of error, a servicer "may not furnish adverse information to any consumer reporting agency regarding any payment that is the subject of the notice of error" (verified in 4.1); RESPA §6(e)(3): during the 60 days after receipt of a QWR "relating to a dispute regarding the borrower's payments, a servicer may not provide information regarding any overdue payment, owed by such borrower and relating to such period or qualified written request, to any consumer reporting agency." §1024.36(h): an RFI does not bar reporting. ### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- **Schedule:** `credit.cycle.snapshot_due` fires at 00:05 America/New_York on the 1st of each month for the prior month (`as_of_date` = last calendar day of the prior month, per C-4.1-01). A `credit.cycle.opened` event creates `metro2_cycles` row `{cycle_id, as_of_date, status='building'}`.
- **Loan-state inputs (read at snapshot):** `loans` (status, boarded_at, transfer_out_at, paid_off_at), `loan_terms` (note date, original amount, rate type, term, P&I, escrow payment, maturity, modification effective dates, deferred balances), ledger balances (`principal`, `deferred_principal`, `forborne_principal`, past-due installments), `payments` (last payment date/amount in the period), `regx_days_delinquent` and the earliest unpaid installment due date (FIFO, from 2.x), `cases` of type forbearance/deferral/modification (trial vs permanent)/shortsale/dil/foreclosure/bankruptcy/scra/noe/fraud/sii, `borrowers`/`loan_borrowers` (ECOA role, deceased flag, SSN/DOB/address), `parties` (confirmed successors — excluded), `credit_reporting_suppressions` (8.3), `credit_disputes` open flags (8.2), `jurisdiction_rules.credit_reporting`.

#### State machine
`metro2_cycles`: `building → validated → (held)? → transmitted(per bureau) → acknowledged(per bureau) → closed`; `rejected` (bureau file-level reject) → `resubmitted` → `acknowledged`. Guards: `building → validated` requires zero record-level hard validation errors and anomaly score under threshold (business rule 9); `validated → transmitted` requires all four `metro2_files` built, PGP-encrypted and hash-recorded, and no open `hold` escalation; `transmitted → acknowledged` on bureau acknowledgment ingestion; `closed` when every reject item is resolved or explicitly accepted with reason. Per-loan `loans.credit_reporting_status`: `active → hold` (8.3 suppression with `omit_account`) `→ active`; `active → final_reported` on transfer-out/payoff/foreclosure/charge-off final cycle (after which the loan is reported only if a correction requires it); `never_report` for loans that must not be furnished (e.g., confirmed identity theft with DF sent). Actors: `credit-reporting` agent (all transitions), `officer` (release of a held cycle), bureau acks (external).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Snapshot date and delinquency counting.** `as_of_date` = last calendar day of the month (C-4.1-01). Days past due = `as_of_date − due_date_of_earliest_unpaid_installment` (FIFO; the same anchor as `regx_days_delinquent`, which this section uses; Fannie Mae's month-bucket `fnma_delinquency_status` from 5.7 is *not* used for Metro 2 because the CRRG buckets are day counts). Account Status: 0–29 → **11**; 30–59 → **71**; 60–89 → **78**; 90–119 → **80**; 120–149 → **82**; 150–179 → **83**; 180+ → **84**. An installment is "unpaid" when the ledger's `installment_due` for that due date is not fully satisfied as of `as_of_date` using effective-date posting (a payment posted after `as_of_date` but with an effective date on/before it — e.g., a protected transfer payment, 1.x — counts as received; the snapshot is rebuilt if such a posting arrives before transmission).
2. **Metro 2 monetary fields** are whole dollars in 9-digit right-justified zero-filled fields; the generator **truncates cents** (floor) **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG states "whole dollars"; confirm truncate vs. round]**. `current_balance` = interest-bearing UPB + non-interest-bearing `deferred_principal` + `forborne_principal` (principal-type balances only; accrued interest, escrow arrears and fees are excluded — default; the alternative "total amount owed" reading is **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG definition of Current Balance for mortgages]**, 8.1-Q9); `amount_past_due` = sum of unpaid installments' P&I + escrow portions due on or before `as_of_date`, **excluding late charges, NSF and other fees** **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG definition of Amount Past Due]**; `scheduled_monthly_payment` = current PITI installment from `loan_terms` (P&I + escrow) **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG mortgage guidance on including escrow]**; `actual_payment_amount` = total borrower payments posted with effective dates in the month; `original_loan_amount` = note amount (`highest_credit_or_original_loan_amount`); `original_charge_off_amount` only with status 97.
3. **Dates.** `date_opened` = note date; `date_of_first_delinquency` (DOFD) = due date of the earliest unpaid installment in the *current* continuous delinquency (the "commencement of the delinquency," 15 U.S.C. 1681c(c)(1)); required for statuses 61–65, 71–84, 88, 89, 93–97; **zero-filled when the account is current**; **never moved later** while the same delinquency continues (re-aging is an Appendix E failure), and reset only when a *new* delinquency begins after a cure; `date_closed` for 13, 61–65, 88, 89, 94–97 and 05; `date_of_last_payment` = effective date of the last borrower payment; `date_of_account_information` = `as_of_date`.
4. **Payment History Profile (24 characters, position 1 = the month before `as_of_date`).** Character = the Account Status reported for that month mapped 11→`0`, 71→`1`, 78→`2`, 80→`3`, 82→`4`, 83→`5`, 84→`6`, 93→`G`, 94→`H`, 95→`J`, 96→`K`, 97→`L`; `B` = no history before this month (new loan/boarded without history), `D` = no history available this month (omitted cycle, e.g., an FDCPA gate month), `E` = zero balance and current. The profile is *carried* from `metro2_snapshots` history plus the boarding hand-off; it is never recomputed from today's ledger (that would silently re-write history — a correction goes through rule 12).
5. **Consumer segments and ECOA.** Base segment = the first obligor on the note (`loan_borrowers.position=1`); each additional obligor → J1 (same address) or J2 (different address); ECOA `1` individual, `2` joint contractual liability, `5` co-maker/guarantor (rare), `X` deceased (8.3), `Z` delete consumer (never liable/identity theft), `T` association terminated (e.g., released co-borrower after an assumption); authorized users (`3`) do not occur on Fannie Mae mortgages. Confirmed successors in interest (4.4) who have not assumed the note are **not** furnished (Reg V "accuracy" = reflects "liability for the account" and "identifies the appropriate consumer"); after a qualifying assumption (D2-3.4 / 12.x), the assuming party is added as an obligor from the assumption date and the released borrower gets ECOA `T`, with Special Comment `H` (loan assumed by another party) on the released consumer **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG code H]**.
6. **Portfolio/Account Type and K3.** Portfolio Type **M**; Account Type **26** (conventional real estate mortgage, incl. purchase-money first) for every loan in scope **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — 26 appears in public secondary sources; 2026 CRRG changed Account Type codes within Portfolio Type; confirm]**; Terms Duration = remaining/original term in months (e.g., `360`) **[UNVERIFIED which]**; Terms Frequency `M` (biweekly-draft loans remain contractually monthly); Interest Type Indicator `F` fixed / `V` variable **[UNVERIFIED code values]**. K3 segment on every loan: Agency Identifier = the CRRG value for Fannie Mae (**01 [UNVERIFIED — one secondary source lists 02]**), Secondary Marketing Agency Account Number = the 10-digit Fannie Mae loan number, Mortgage Identification Number = the 18-digit MERS MIN.

8. **Special Comment vs. Compliance Condition persistence.** Special Comment codes are re-stated every cycle while the condition exists and are removed by reporting blank; Compliance Condition Codes are sticky at the bureaus and are cleared only by `XR` (8.3) †.
9. **Pre-transmission validation and anomaly gate.** Hard errors (block the record): invalid code combinations (e.g., 11 with DOFD populated; 13 without Payment Rating; 71–84 without DOFD; PHP length ≠ 24; Current Balance > original amount + capitalized + deferred + accrued arrears tolerance; Amount Past Due > 0 with status 13/94/89; ECOA missing; SSN/DOB format; MIN not 18 digits; K3 missing). Soft anomalies (hold the *cycle* for human release when the portfolio rate exceeds thresholds): status improvements without a payment or workout event; status jumps of more than one bucket in a month; DOFD moved later; >0.5% of records deleted (DA/DF); consumer count changes; >2% of records with status changes not explained by `loan_events`. Every hard error routes the record to the `credit-reporting` agent for same-day resolution; unresolved records are **omitted** from the file (with PHP `D` next month) rather than furnished wrong — §1681s-2(a)(1)(A).
10. **Per-bureau rendering.** One canonical `metro2_snapshots` set → four files: Header with that bureau's Program Identifier (Innovis/Equifax/Experian/TransUnion fields), `Identification Number` = that bureau's subscriber code, identical Base/J/K/L segments, Trailer totals recomputed. Files are PGP-encrypted to the bureau key and named per `furnisher_config.file_naming`.
11. **Idempotency and replay.** `metro2_files.hash` + `cycle_id` + `bureau` form the idempotency key; a resubmission is a new `metro2_files` row with `resubmission_of`; the bureaus treat the latest full file as authoritative, so a corrected *full* resubmission is preferred to partial files where the bureau accepts it **[UNVERIFIED per bureau]**.
12. **Corrections.** Any determination that previously furnished data was inaccurate creates `credit_reporting_corrections` (before/after), an **AUD** through e-OSCAR to every bureau that received the data (within 2 BD; §1681s-2(a)(2) "promptly"), and a corrected value in the next cycle (the AUD is not a substitute for in-cycle reporting — e-OSCAR: "may not be used to add or create a record ... or as substitute for 'in-cycle' reporting"). Corrections that *add* negative information not previously noticed trigger `FCRA_1681S2A7_NEG_INFO_NOTICE_30` (B-2) unless a B-1 notice is on file.

UPB after the 2027-01-01 payment (#23): **$293,063.94**.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `credit-reporting`. End-to-end each cycle: (1) build snapshots from loan state with full derivations; (2) run validators; (3) resolve every hard error by investigating the underlying record (e.g., DOFD missing because the boarding hand-off lacked it → query prior-servicer file (1.6) → if unavailable, DOFD = earliest unpaid installment per our ledger and a `derivation.note`); (4) compute anomaly metrics and, if under thresholds, transmit to all four bureaus; (5) ingest acknowledgments, classify rejects, fix data or configuration, resubmit/AUD; (6) produce the partner certificate and the Appendix E monthly evaluation (dispute rate by field, reject rate by bureau, correction root causes).
- **Decision record** (`agent_decisions`) per resolved exception and per cycle release: `{cycle_id, loan_id?, rule_set_version (crrg.2026 + fcra.regv.2026-09), model_version, prompt_version, inputs_hash, exception_type, records_examined[], determination, fields_changed, rationale, confidence, human_review_reason[], reviewer_id?, outcome}`.
- **Guardrails:** the agent may not alter `payment_history_profile` history, `date_opened` or `date_of_first_delinquency` except through a `credit_reporting_corrections` row with evidence; may not send DA/DF (delete account), ECOA `Z`, or a full-file resubmission without `officer` approval; may not release a cycle whose anomaly score exceeds thresholds; may not furnish any record with an unresolved hard error; must apply 8.3 overlays before rendering; cannot bypass the FDCPA/NoE gates.
- **Human touchpoints:** none legally required for furnishing. Policy-configured (defaults on): `officer` releases held cycles and approves deletions/resubmissions; `human_agent` handles bureau portal tickets and credentialing; `officer` signs the annual Reg V policy review and regulator/exam responses. AI-off path: the same tools in the ops console with the human running each step and the agent producing drafts only.
- **Disclosure/consent:** none (no consumer interaction in 8.1). LL-2026-04: the agent is inventoried with evaluation suites (fixture portfolios with known correct Metro 2 output) and monthly monitoring dashboards.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in mid-month / boarding without Metro 2 history:** PHP `B`-filled; DOFD taken from the transferor's data or, failing that, the earliest unpaid installment in our ledger (never later than the transferor's DOFD if known); no reporting until 1.6 reconciliation is complete (a boarded-but-unreconciled loan is omitted with reason `boarding_unreconciled`, max one cycle).
- **Payment reversed after transmission (NSF):** the next cycle reports the true status; if the reversal makes a previously furnished 11 wrong for that month, it is *not* an inaccuracy (the payment was received as of the snapshot); no AUD.
- **Misapplied payment corrected (4.1):** AUD + next-cycle correction; PHP corrected for the affected months via `credit_reporting_corrections`.
- **Forbearance ends without a resolution:** CP dropped; status per default policy resumes contractual aging from the frozen position (e.g., 78 → 80 the next month) — 8.1-Q4.
- **Trial period failed:** AC dropped; contractual status continues.
- **Modification rescinded/unwound:** revert terms; report CO removed; correction record.
- **Short sale with deficiency pursued:** status 97 with charge-off amount only if Fannie Mae authorizes deficiency pursuit; otherwise 13/65 + AU.
- **Bureau outage / transport failure:** retry with exponential backoff for 24 hours, then `human_agent` ticket; the cycle stays `transmitted` for the other bureaus; the timer `SM_METRO2_TRANSMIT_HARD_CD10` still governs.
- **File-level reject (header/trailer):** fix configuration, resubmit the whole file (`resubmission_of`).
- **Record-level rejects:** classify (data vs. configuration), correct, and either resubmit (if the bureau accepts partial files) or AUD + next cycle.
- **Duplicate consumers / wrong-consumer association:** SSN/DOB/name validation at boarding; a bureau "mixed file" complaint opens an 8.2 case and an ECOA `Z` correction where the consumer was never liable (officer approval).
- **Deceased sole borrower with estate paying:** ECOA X on the deceased; continue reporting the account (8.3).
- **Loan in litigation (contested foreclosure):** no special Metro 2 treatment; disputes handled in 8.2; legal hold flag on records.
- **CARES-era accommodation still in effect** (made before 2023-08-08): apply §1681s-2(a)(1)(F) "report as current / frozen" rule; flag `cares_accommodation=true` on the case.
- **Retro-corrections of DOFD:** only earlier, never later, unless the earlier date was itself an error with evidence; every change is a correction record with AUD to all four bureaus.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **8.1-Q1 Furnisher of record.** Default: Supermortgage furnishes under its own subscriber codes and name; the hello notice tells borrowers the tradeline will appear as Supermortgage. Alternative: private-label under the partner's codes (requires partner e-OSCAR affiliation, third-party addendum, and bureau consent).
2. **8.1-Q2 Rounding of monetary fields.** Default: truncate cents. Confirm CRRG rule.
3. **8.1-Q3 Bureau minimum-volume waivers** for the pilot (TransUnion 100; Equifax 200–500). Default: begin furnishing only when the portfolio clears every bureau's minimum, or obtain written waivers; Fannie Mae's four-repository rule means no bureau may be skipped for long — escalate to the partner if a waiver is refused.
4. **8.1-Q4 Forbearance status policy.** Default: freeze the status at plan entry with CP (Reg V accuracy: the plan suspends the payment obligation; consistent with the CARES-era and CDIA disaster guidance as recalled). Alternative: contractual aging with CP. Requires CRRG 2026 FAQ confirmation and counsel sign-off.
5. **8.1-Q5 Trial Period Plan aging.** Default: contractual aging + AC (CRRG HAMP-era guidance as recalled). Alternative: freeze.
6. **8.1-Q6 Modification comment code.** Default: CO (loan modified) for Flex Mod; CN only for federal-government plans. Confirm.
7. **8.1-Q7 Payment Deferral representation.** Default: status 11 + K4 balloon for the deferred balance, no Special Comment. Alternative: CO. Confirm with CRRG/CDIA.
8. **8.1-Q8 Boarding-month reporter.** Default: transfer date ≤ 15th → Supermortgage reports the boarding month; otherwise the transferor's 05 covers it. Write into the transfer agreement.
9. **8.1-Q9 Amount Past Due composition and Scheduled Monthly Payment (PITI vs P&I).** Default: installments incl. escrow, excluding fees; PITI. Confirm CRRG.
10. **8.1-Q10 Optional AZ (amortized mortgage) and AS/H codes.** Default: not used until confirmed.
