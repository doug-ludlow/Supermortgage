# Section 28 — Quality control, HMDA, and fraud/AML reporting

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 28 is the origination platform's second line: the controls that look at what production did and report on it to the partner, Fannie Mae, the CFPB/FFIEC and FinCEN/OFAC. 28.1 runs the prefunding QC program required by Selling Guide D1-1-01/D1-1-02/D1-1-03 and D1-2-01 (04/01/2026): a daily risk-based-plus-random selection with no Guide-imposed minimum since SEL-2026-03, full-file and component reviews covering the eight D1-2-01 areas, findings that reopen conditions or reverse decisions through 23.3 before closing, a closing gate (`FNMA_D1_2_01_PREFUNDING_PRIOR_TO_CLOSING_GATE`, asserted by 23.3 before clear-to-close as its checklist item `SM_QC_PREFUNDING_HOLD`), and monthly management reporting within 30 days of completion with three-month trending. 28.2 runs the post-closing program under the current three-topic chapter D1-3 — a monthly 10% (or statistical) random sample plus discretionary targeting, the whole cycle "within 90 days from the month of the disbursement date," the narrowed reverification standard (approved-vendor and DU-validated components exempt; a new tri-merge credit report and an occupancy assessment always required; desk/field reviews only when the collateral assessment cannot be completed), re-underwriting on variances, the QC-defect self-report within 30 days via Loan Quality Connect, the partner's own 10% re-review of SM's vendor work, Fannie Mae's post-purchase reviews (file within 30 days of notification; NOPD/Resolution Request handling), the remedies framework and its 60/60/15/60/15/30/15/30/15-day ladder (A2-3.2-03: appeal 1 within 60 days of the demand; Fannie Mae response 60; appeal 2 within 15; Fannie Mae response 60; impasse initiated within 15 with 30 days to resolve; management escalation within 15 with Fannie Mae's officer involved within 30; IDR initiated within 15), and the 36-payment/satisfactory-QC relief tracking that 23.3's `rep_warrant_relief` ledger records. 28.3 captures every Reg C data point from `application.received`, assigns the ULI with the Appendix C MOD 97-10 check digit, derives the rate spread from 23.4's APOR snapshot at the final rate-set date, finalizes 21.6's action-taken codes on funding, runs the daily LAR-completeness job against §1003.4(f)'s quarter-plus-30-days rule, builds and files the pipe-delimited TS+LAR file through the FFIEC HMDA Platform API by March 1 (quarterly only if the partner ever reports ≥ 60,000), and keeps the §1003.5 public notices. 28.4 is the fraud/AML/OFAC function of two "loan or finance companies" (the partner as residential mortgage lender; SM as an originator by default): §1029.210 programs, SAR decisions and filings on the 30/60-day clocks from initial detection, continuing-activity filings at 120 days, OFAC blocked/rejected reports within 10 federal business days with 10-year records, Fannie Mae's A3-4-03 self-report within 30 days of a reasonable basis, the FTC Red Flags program and annual board report, and FCRA §609(e) victim records within 30 days.

**Agents.** `qc-audit` (28.1/28.2 — deployed separately from every production agent, read-only on production tables, writing only `qc_*` records, `escalations`, `agent_decisions` and `loan_events`), `hmda` (28.3 — the only production-side reader of `applicant_demographics` besides 31.2 monitoring, under access logging), `fraud-risk` (28.4 — investigation, SAR/OFAC/Fannie Mae drafting, confidentiality-compartmented). Shared: `compliance-sentinel` (every timer breach), `security-records` (retention classes `fnma_qc_3y`, `hmda_3y`, `bsa_sar_5y`, `ofac_10y`). Human touchpoints: `qc_officer` (independent of production, underwriting and closing per D1-1-02; approves every severity-1/2 finding, samples the AI's no-defect conclusions, signs monthly reports), `fnma_portal_operator` (every Loan Quality Connect action — file uploads, NOPD responses, self-reports, appeals; relief-report downloads), partner `officer` (HMDA certification/sign; QC plan and report acknowledgement; self-report authorization; remedy payments, repurchase alternatives and appeals; program approvals), `bsa_officer` (SAR and OFAC decisions and filings; triage confirmation), `underwriting_reviewer` (production-side decision reversals triggered by QC or fraud findings, through 21.6/23.3), `human_agent` (borrower request during a verification interview).

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the Fannie Mae seller bound by D1, D2 and A2-3.2 and the counterparty to every demand; the HMDA "financial institution" whose LEI heads every ULI and whose officer certifies; a loan or finance company with its own AML program and SAR duty; the Red Flags creditor; and the A3-4-03 seller/servicer. SM performs everything as the partner's outsourced QC vendor (the partner remains "fully accountable"), as the HMDA preparer under partner-authorized platform access, as a service provider under the ITPP, and — by default — as an RMLO in its own right that files jointly with the partner. Loan Quality Connect, the HMDA sign step, FinCEN filings and OFAC reports are human acts under the officer's own credentials; the AI prepares packages and never files. SAR confidentiality (§1029.320(d)) draws a hard line between the two organizations that the data model enforces with `sar_confidentiality_acl`.

**Dependencies on other origination processes.** Inbound: 21.1 (`application.received`; `applicant_demographics`; NMLSR ID; 21.1-Q6 application date), 21.4 (`rate_set_date`), 21.6 (`decisions`, `adverse_actions`, action-taken codes/dates, denial reasons, the reason taxonomy used for fraud-driven denials), 22.1 (`document_integrity_checks`), 22.2 (`credit_reports`, `score_model`, `credit_alerts`), 22.3 (`tax_transcript_requests`, VVOE, validation reports), 22.6 (identity/OFAC screening execution), 23.1/23.2 (DU casefiles, findings, red-flag messages), 23.3 (`ctc_checklists` incl. the `SM_QC_PREFUNDING_HOLD` item, `rep_warrant_relief`, decision reopening), 23.4 (`apor_tables`, APR/HOEPA/HPML), 24.1/24.2 (value acceptance, appraisals, CU scores), 24.4/26.3 (settlement/wire evidence; funding and disbursement dates), 25.1/25.2 (APR; CD money fields; corrected CDs), 25.3 (rescission), 26.1 (closing schedule), 29.3/29.4 (delivery gates; purchase date; data revisions; PAL LLPA tables). Outbound: 23.3 (condition reopening; decision reversal), 26.3/29.3 (holds), 27.2 (repurchase settlement), 31.2 (model-monitoring strata; fair-lending datasets), 31.3 (retention/breach). Servicing spec: 5.1/6.x (delinquency for EPD and the 36-payment relief), 9.x (insurance/occupancy signals), 16.x (payoffs end relief tracking) — referenced, never redefined.

**Changes in flight that shape the build.** SEL-2026-03 (April 1, 2026; mandatory for reviews on/after July 1, 2026): no prefunding minimum sample, streamlined reverification, simplified management reporting, chapter D1-3 collapsed to three topics; D1-3-01's 90-day cycle from the month of disbursement (no 30/60 split); D2-1-02's 30-day file delivery and the LQC portal-only reality (no API; bulk upload ≤ 400 MB/3,000 pages); A3-4-03's hard 30-day fraud self-report (Dec 10, 2025); A2-3.2-02's 36-payment relief (Aug 6, 2025) and A2-2-04's April 1, 2026 update; LL-2026-04 AI/ML governance (Aug 6, 2026) applied to the QC and fraud agents themselves; Reg C thresholds 25/200 with no 2025–2026 rulemaking, the 2026 FIG with VantageScore 4.0 (15) and FICO 10T (14) model codes, Login.gov for every HMDA Platform user, and VantageScore 4.0 broad availability (LL-2026-06, Sept 9, 2026); FinCEN's April 10, 2026 AML/CFT program NPRM (2024 NPRM withdrawn; no final rule) and the reserved §1029.220 CIP; OFAC's 10-year recordkeeping; 12 CFR 1022 Subpart J reserved (16 CFR 681 governs). Flagged as unverified on a primary source: Reg C comments 4(a)(15)/4(a)(35) in full, the LQC NOPD/Resolution Request day counts, Fannie Mae's EPD definition, the blueprint's "12 payments" relief, HMDA Platform unattended API access, SM's own RMLO status, and state mortgage-fraud reporting statutes.

## Closing

### Section-level test plan and sequencing

**Build order.** (1) 28.3's `hmda_records`/`hmda_ulis` and the derivation engine first — Stage O-1 cross-cutting ("HMDA capture from day one" in the baseline's build stages): the ULI is assigned at `application.received`, so 21.1 cannot go live without it; the rule set `regc.hmda.2026`, the local S/V/Q edit engine and the daily completeness job follow, with the FFIEC Check Digit and Rate Spread adapters (public, no auth) and the geocoder; the platform filing client and the officer sign-off console are needed only before the first filing (March 1, 2027 for a covered partner). (2) 28.4's screening/report side next (Stage O-2/O-3): the OFAC list ingestion and `SM_OFAC_CLEAR_BEFORE_FUNDING_GATE` are funding prerequisites (26.3), the `fraud_cases` model and production hold are prerequisites for 22.6 and 23.3, and the AML programs, `bsa_officer` designations, BSA E-Filing enrollments and Red Flags ITPP must exist before the first application. (3) 28.1 (Stage O-3, with 23.3): the separately deployed `qc-audit` service, the `qc_*` tables, the selection job and the hold integration; it must be live before the first loan reaches `ptd_cleared`. (4) 28.2 last (Stage O-3, after 26.3/29.4): the monthly cycle engine, reverification adapters with QC purpose codes, the LQC package builder and notification parser, the remedies ladder and the relief tracker; the first cycle is due 90 days after the end of the first disbursement month (November 2026 → Feb 28, 2027), and the partner's LQC roles must be assigned before the first purchase (Nov 19, 2026).

### Section open questions
