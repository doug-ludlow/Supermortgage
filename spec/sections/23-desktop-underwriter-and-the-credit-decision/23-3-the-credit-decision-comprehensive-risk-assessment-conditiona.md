### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**B3-1-01, Comprehensive Risk Assessment (11/05/2025).** "Fannie Mae's underwriting policies include an evaluation of the borrower's (or spouse's to the extent required by applicable law) equity investment, credit history, liquid reserves, reliable and recurring income, and the cumulative effect that these and other risk factors have on mortgage loan performance." "Lenders must evaluate the overall level of serious delinquency risk that is present in each mortgage application by taking into consideration any layering of risk factors, the significance of risk factors, and the overall risks present in the mortgage application." "Fannie Mae offers lenders two options for conducting a comprehensive risk assessment–automated underwriting through DU or manual underwriting." "Lenders are fully responsible for: evaluating the delinquency risk of each loan; reviewing the credit report, as well as all other credit information, to determine that [the credit report meets Fannie Mae's requirements]; assessing the adequacy of the property as collateral for the mortgage requested; determining whether or not the loan meets Fannie Mae's eligibility requirements [as described in the Guide]; determining whether or not it is appropriate to deliver the mortgage loan to Fannie Mae; fully documenting the assessment and the documentation on which the assessment was based." (Bracketed text completes clauses cut by the extraction — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — bracketed words]**.) B3-2-01 (04/01/2026) adds the seven lender requirements quoted in 23.1 ("employ prudent underwriting judgment…", "apply due diligence when reviewing the documentation in the loan file", "take action when erroneous data… would justify additional investigation").

**Form 1008.** B3-2-04 (03/31/2015): "Lenders are not required to prepare a Uniform Underwriting and Transmittal Summary (Form 1008) for loans underwritten with DU and subsequently delivered to Fannie Mae"; "The final DU Underwriting Findings report" must be "maintained in the permanent loan file."

**Representations, warranties and relief.** A2-2-01 (02/05/2020): "the lender makes representations and warranties as to certain facts and circumstances concerning the lender and the mortgage loans it is selling or delivering"; they "are made to Fannie Mae as of the date a lender transfers mortgage loans to Fannie Mae and continue and survive: the sale of mortgage loans to Fannie Mae…, any subsequent resale of the mortgage loans by Fannie Mae, and termination of the MSSC." A2-2-04 (04/01/2026): the limited waiver covers "the mortgage loan's eligibility for delivery to Fannie Mae and the borrower's creditworthiness," conditioned on "All data pertaining to the mortgage loan is complete, accurate, and not fraudulent," the casefile ID at delivery, SFC 127, all "Verification Messages/Approval Conditions… satisfactorily resolved" and payment of LLPAs; it excludes Out of Scope, Approve/Ineligible and Refer with Caution recommendations, "Product eligibility and ability-to-repay representations," "Property condition, value, or marketability," government loans and seasoned loans. Day 1 Certainty enforcement relief: income — "accuracy of the lender's income calculations related to the validated income" when the "DU message must indicate that the amount of income entered into DU was validated"; employment — relief for "borrower's employment… with the employer attested to on the loan application" when validated; assets — "sufficiency of the borrower's assets to satisfy Total Funds to be Verified as required by DU" when validated; "The loan must close by the 'Close by Date' stated in the DU message." Undisclosed non-mortgage debt relief (SEL-2025-09) requires the DU relief message, resolution of all verification messages, excludes "Undisclosed mortgage-related debt (including HELOCs and second liens)," and "The loan must close by the credit report expiration date." Income Calculator relief "for the accuracy of the income calculation, on an income-source basis" if "All information submitted to Income Calculator must be accurate and complete," "A copy of the Findings Report must be kept in the loan file," and "The amount of income used for qualifying cannot be more than the amount calculated by the tool." Relief is lost "if the lender's investigation of conflicting or contradictory information contained in the loan file or within the verification report would have impacted the information entered by the lender in DU." Property value: A2-2-06 (06/03/2026) — with value acceptance "Fannie Mae accepts the value estimate submitted by the lender as the value for the subject property"; the "Lender remains responsible for accuracy and completeness of all data pertaining to property and project (if applicable) submitted to DU (other than the property value)"; appraised loans with "a CU risk score of 2.5 or below" on a one-unit property get relief on value/appraiser methodology; the lender remains responsible for "Property eligibility requirements" and for condition/quality-rating data. **Payment-history relief — A2-3.2-02, Enforcement Relief for Breaches of Certain Representations and Warranties Related to Underwriting and Eligibility (08/06/2025)**: relief "upon payment by the borrower of the first 36 monthly payments due following the loan acquisition date" with "no more than two 30-day delinquencies" and "no 60-day or greater delinquencies"; alternatively when "Fannie Mae completes a full-file QC review of the loan file… and determines that the loan is acceptable"; eligible loans are "whole loans purchased on or after July 1, 2014"; "Fannie Mae will provide lenders with reports listing those loans that met the eligibility requirements for relief." **Life-of-loan exclusions — A2-2-07 (08/02/2023)**: "A lender is not relieved from the enforcement of breaches of its representations and warranties on any mortgage loan, including eligible mortgage loans, with respect to the following matters": Fannie Mae Charter Act Matters; Misstatements, Misrepresentations, and Omissions; Data Inaccuracies; Clear Title/First-Lien Enforceability; Compliance with Laws and Responsible Lending Practices ("A mortgage loan must be originated in compliance with applicable laws and regulations as set forth in A3-2-01… Fannie Mae's responsible lending policies as set forth in A3-2-02…"); Unacceptable Mortgage Products. For misrepresentation on a relieved loan Fannie Mae asserts a remedy only if it involves "three or more mortgage loans delivered to Fannie Mae by the same lender," is "made pursuant to a common pattern of activity," and is "significant" ("the loan would not have been eligible for sale to Fannie Mae under the terms of the lender's contract… or… would have been eligible for purchase, but under different terms").

**Prefunding QC — D1-2-01 (04/01/2026).** Reviews "must be conducted early enough in the origination process to allow adequate time to make loan selections, complete the reviews, and properly inform the loan production staff so that corrections can be made prior to loan closing or acquisition"; "The lender must establish and document a monthly process for selecting loans"; "The monthly sample must include selections from each of the lender's production channels"; full-file reviews assess "data entered into an automated underwriting system," "borrower Social Security number(s)," "income calculations and supporting documentation," "employment documentation, including verbal verification of employment," "assets needed to close or meet reserve requirements," "appraisal or other eligible collateral data or documentation," "adequate mortgage insurance coverage," and "occupancy"; "prefunding QC must be conducted by individuals who have no involvement in the origination, processing or underwriting functions of the loan being reviewed." 28.1 owns the program; this process exposes the hooks.

**Reg B.** §1002.9(a)(1)(i): notify within "30 days after receiving a completed application concerning the creditor's approval of, counteroffer to, or adverse action on the application"; comment 9(a)(1)-1: "Once a creditor has obtained all the information it normally considers in making a credit decision, the application is complete and the creditor has 30 days in which to notify the applicant of the credit decision"; comment 9(a)(1)-2: "Notification of approval may be express or by implication"; §1002.9(a)(1)(iv): "90 days after notifying the applicant of a counteroffer if the applicant does not expressly accept or use the credit offered"; comment 9(a)(1)-6: a combined counteroffer/adverse-action notice "need not send a second adverse action notice if the applicant does not accept the counteroffer"; §1002.9(b)(2): reasons "must be specific and indicate the principal reason(s)." Timer `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30`, NOIA and counteroffer timers/notices are 21.6's. Circulars 2022-03/2023-03 were withdrawn May 12, 2025; §1002.9(b)(2) and its commentary still govern (00a-fed §3.2). The Reg B final rule effective July 21, 2026 removed the effects test (00a-fed §3.8) — state fair-lending law and Fannie Mae contractual expectations remain.

**AI governance.** LL-2026-04 (April 8, 2026; effective Aug 6, 2026): policies must "be transparent and communicated to appropriate personnel," "incorporate the characteristics of trustworthy and ethical AI/ML," "reflect an understanding of legal and regulatory requirements," "reflect risk management activities based on the lender's risk tolerance," "have an owner(s) that implements, maintains and reviews the policies and procedures at least annually"; sellers must "Manage risks and appropriate governance of subcontractor and vendor use of AI/ML that is no less protective of these requirements" and "Upon request by Fannie Mae… promptly disclose the types of AI/ML used, the purpose and manner for such use, the safeguards…" (00a-fnma §1). Colorado SB 26-189 (signed May 14, 2026; operative Jan 1, 2027 per secondary sources): "financial or lending services" is a consequential-decision domain; consumers may "request meaningful human review and reconsideration following a covered ADMT making a consequential decision resulting in an adverse outcome"; records "for at least 3 years" (00a-fed §13.1). California CPPA ADMT regulations (effective Jan 1, 2026) treat "financial or lending services" as a significant decision (00a-fed §13.3).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) The 36-payment relief is in A2-3.2-02, not A2-2-04/-07 (the section notes' citation is wrong); A2-2-04 covers the DU limited waiver and Day 1 Certainty relief, A2-2-06 property value, A2-2-07 the life-of-loan exclusions. (2) Form 1008 is not required for DU loans — the "Form 1008 policy" is a decision to *not* produce one (an internal underwriting summary replaces it). (3) "Decision ≤ 30 days from completed application" is 21.6's clock; a conditional approval letter is an express approval notification that satisfies §1002.9(a)(1)(i) even before the application is complete, but the clock for a later adverse action still runs from completion. (4) "Clear-to-close before CD issuance" is policy (see nuances); the law-driven gates are TRID's CD timing (25.2) and the DU final-match gate (23.1). (5) "D1-2 (prefunding QC linkage)" is D1-2-01, monthly selection with a review scope that includes AUS data integrity; the hook is "corrections… prior to loan closing," so a selected loan cannot be CTC'd with an open prefunding finding. (6) Colorado's regime is SB 26-189 (repeal-and-reenact), not "the Colorado AI Act (human review)" of SB24-205; impact assessments are gone, human review and 30-day explanations remain (21.6/31.2).

Retention `regb_25m` for the notice/reasons and `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` for the file; PII restricted.

Notice code defined here: `NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL` — the conditional approval / commitment letter (express notification of approval under §1002.9(a)(1)(i)); channels: e-delivery with E-SIGN consent (21.1) or mail; content: creditor (partner) name/NMLSR, terms approved, conditions list (borrower-facing wording), validity date, statement that the approval is conditioned on the listed items and on no adverse change; no ECOA notice content is required for an approval (the ECOA notice is required on adverse action/counteroffer under §1002.9(a)(2)).

#### State machine

Application decision sub-status: `underwriting_pending` → `conditionally_approved` (`decision.issued{conditional_approval}`, letter sent) → `ptd_cleared` (all PTD conditions cleared, no open investigations, no QC hold) → `clear_to_close` (`ctc_checklists.passed=true`) → `ptf_cleared` (all PTF conditions cleared) → hand-off to 26.3 (`funding.authorized`). Branches: `suspended` (NOIA — 21.6), `counteroffered` (21.6 notice; returns to `underwriting_pending` on acceptance with the restructured terms), `denied` (21.6 adverse action; terminal), `withdrawn` (terminal), `reopened` (from `conditionally_approved`/`ptd_cleared`/`clear_to_close` on contradictory information, a worse DU recommendation, an expired `valid_until`, or a failed compliance test; returns to `underwriting_pending`). Transition actors: the `underwriter` agent for every forward transition on Approve/Eligible loans; `underwriting_reviewer` for `counteroffered`, `denied`, `suspended`, waivers and any transition out of `reopened` when consummation is < 3 `business_days_creditor` away. Guards: `conditionally_approved` requires `policy_outcome = proceed` and an 23.4 preliminary QM/HPML/HOEPA result of `qm` and `not_high_cost`; `clear_to_close` requires the 23.1 final-match gate open, `ptd_cleared`, the checklist passed, and no open 28.1 prefunding hold (`qc.hold.applied{kind=prefunding}` without a matching `qc.hold.released`).

Condition lifecycle (`conditions.status`, columns owned by 23.2): `open` → `waiting_borrower` (needs-list item sent) or `waiting_third_party` (vendor order) → `satisfied_pending_review` (evidence attached; `auto_clear_rule` did not fully pass or `requires_role` set) → `cleared` (`condition_clearances` row); `open`/`satisfied_pending_review` → `waived` (only `underwriting_reviewer`; never for a DU verification message or an eligibility item — A2-2-04 requires resolution; waivers allowed only for SM-added conditions); `cleared` → `reopened` (contradictory information; document expired under B1-1-03 before the note date; DU resubmission changed the requirement) ; any → `superseded` (23.2).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **The decision is the lender's, informed by DU.** The agent performs B3-1-01's evaluation on every Approve/Eligible loan: it reads the credit report (not just DU's summary) for derogatory or contradictory information DU did not evaluate (B3-2-01), reconciles income/assets/liabilities to the verified figures (22.3–22.5), confirms collateral adequacy inputs (24.x), confirms Fannie Mae eligibility outside DU's scope (project, title, insurance, legal — B2-1.5-02/-03), and confirms legal compliance (23.4/25.1) — "DU does not evaluate a loan's compliance with federal and state laws." The `risk_assessment` records layering (e.g., DTI > 45% with reserves < 2 months and a 97% LTV) even when DU approves; layering never overrides an Approve/Eligible under the partner's policy (no overlays by default — open decision 23.3-Q1), but it is recorded and monitored (31.2).
2. **Conditional approval.** Issued when `policy_outcome = proceed`, 23.4's preliminary determination is `qm` and not high-cost, and no open red-flag investigation exists. `valid_until = min(credit_reports.expires_at (22.2), locks.expires_at (21.4; null if floating), valuation expiry (24.1), DU close-by date (22.3), 90 days from issuance)`. The letter lists the borrower-facing conditions and states the partner as creditor and the MLO of record with NMLSR ID (§1026.36(g) does not require it on this letter, but the MLO's name appears for consistency with the LE/CD).
3. **Clearing standards.** A condition is cleared only when (a) the evidence type is in the template's `evidence_kinds`; (b) the document passes 22.1 integrity/freshness (B1-1-03 age at the projected note date, four months for credit documents); (c) the amount/fact matches or exceeds what DU used (income ≥ qualifying income used; assets ≥ Total Funds to be Verified; reserves per the 90% rule; liabilities as disclosed); (d) for validated components, the DU message says validated and the close-by date is not passed; (e) `requires_role` is satisfied where set. The `standard_ref` records the Selling Guide topic and the DU documentation level applied; "a more comprehensive level of documentation is always acceptable" (B3-2-04). Who may clear: the agent for (a)–(d) auto-clear rules; `underwriting_reviewer` for conditions marked `requires_role`, for any waiver, and for reopened-then-cleared conditions inside 3 BD of closing; `qc_officer` never clears production conditions (independence) but can reopen; `funding_approver` clears only funding-stage items (26.3).
4. **PTD vs PTF.** PTD: everything needed to build an accurate CD and closing package (income/employment/assets/liabilities, valuation, title commitment, insurance binder, MI certificate, flood, project, education, compliance tests, final DU match, identity/OFAC/fraud). PTF: verbal VOE within 10 business days before the note date (22.3), pre-closing credit refresh/undisclosed-debt check (22.2), evidence of insurance effective on/before disbursement (24.5), executed closing documents review, VA+PD Property Data API submission before the note date (24.1), rescission expiry (25.3), funding conditions (26.3). Post-closing: trailing documents and SFC delivery items (26.4/29.3).
5. **Clear-to-close criteria (checklist codes).** `CTC_DU_FINAL_MATCH` (23.1 gate open; recommendation approve_eligible); `CTC_PTD_ALL_CLEARED`; `CTC_NO_OPEN_INVESTIGATION` (22.6/23.2 red flags closed); `CTC_CREDIT_VALID` (credit report `expires_at` ≥ projected note date; 22.2); `CTC_DU_CLOSE_BY` (closing date ≤ close-by date; 22.3); `CTC_ASSETS_CASH_TO_CLOSE` (verified funds ≥ CD cash to close + reserves required; 22.4/25.2); `CTC_VALUATION` (appraisal with UCDP SSR/Doc File ID and CU review complete, or value acceptance exercised with offer age ≤ 4 months at note date, or VA+PD with property data ID; ROV closed; 24.1/24.2); `CTC_PROPERTY_PROJECT` (24.3 eligibility; project review valid); `CTC_TITLE` (commitment, payoffs, subordinations, CPL; 24.4); `CTC_INSURANCE_FLOOD` (hazard/flood evidence; flood notice delivered a reasonable time (≥ 10 days practice) before closing; 24.5); `CTC_MI` (certificate at required coverage; 24.6); `CTC_COMPLIANCE` (ATR/QM, HPML, HOEPA, state high-cost, TRID tolerances, APR, fee reasonableness, licensing — 23.4/25.1 all pass); `CTC_EDUCATION` (23.2 gate where applicable); `CTC_LOCK` (lock covers the closing date or is extended; 21.4); `CTC_IDENTITY_OFAC` (22.6 clear); `CTC_QC_PREFUNDING` (no open hold; 28.1); `CTC_MLO_APPROVALS` (LE and lock approvals recorded; 21.1/21.4); `CTC_REGB_TIMING` (decision issued within 21.6's window — informational, never blocks); `CTC_DECISION_VALID` (`valid_until` ≥ closing date). CTC is issued when every item is `pass` or `n/a`; `waived` items require `underwriting_reviewer`.
6. **Denials and counteroffers.** Any `decline_candidate` or `counteroffer` decision record is prepared by the agent with §1002.9(b)(2)-grade specific reasons drawn from the underlying facts (e.g., "debt-to-income ratio of 53% exceeds the maximum of 50% for this program," "insufficient verified funds: $X verified vs $Y required," "value of the collateral insufficient: appraised $X vs required $Y for the requested loan amount") — never "DU returned Refer with Caution" and never "internal standards." The `underwriting_reviewer` approves, modifies or rejects; only then does 21.6 render `NTC_REGB_1002_9_ADVERSE_ACTION`/`NTC_REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER`, record `adverse_actions` (21.6) and HMDA action taken (28.3). Second reviews/appeals (Colorado "meaningful human review", partner policy) go to a different reviewer.
7. **Rep-and-warrant relief ledger.** For each component the agent evaluates eligibility at CTC and again at funding: `limited_waiver_du` (Approve/Eligible, all messages resolved, SFC 127, data accurate); `income/employment/assets_validated` (message present; close by date); `undisclosed_debt` (message; close by credit expiration); `income_calculator` (findings report in file; qualifying income ≤ tool amount); `value_acceptance` (offer on final submission; SFC 801/774; offer age) or `cu_score_2_5` (one-unit; CU ≤ 2.5); `payment_history_36` (opened at purchase; confirmed by Fannie Mae reports — 28.2/30.4). Status flips to `at_risk` when a closing date moves past a close-by/credit-expiration date and to `lost` when it passes.
8. **Worked example 1 — refinance fixture timeline.** Mon Oct 5, 2026 application (six TRID items; credit pulled; 22.1 needs list). Tue Oct 6 first DU submission → Approve/Eligible with a value-acceptance offer (23.1/23.2); interpretation opens 14 PTD conditions. Wed Oct 7 conditional approval issued (`SM_UW_CONDITIONAL_APPROVAL_SLA_1BD` satisfied; letter `NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL` e-delivered under the E-SIGN consent; `valid_until` = min(credit expiry Fri Feb 5, 2027; lock expiry Sat Nov 21, 2026 (45 days from Oct 7); offer expiry Sat Feb 6, 2027; DU close-by date (employment validated) — say Mon Dec 7, 2026; 90 days) = Nov 21, 2026). Completed application (comment 9(a)(1)-1) reached Wed Oct 14 when the title commitment arrived (the creditor "normally considers" credit, income/assets, valuation and title) — the legal clock would run to Fri Nov 13, 2026, but 21.6 anchors `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` conservatively on the Oct 5 application date → due Wed Nov 4, 2026 (21.6-T1) — already satisfied by the Oct 7 express approval; `SM_UW_REGB_HANDOFF_25` (Fri Oct 30) moot. Tue Oct 20 undisclosed auto loan → 23.1 resubmission; new liability condition (payoff not required; DTI 41.75%) — decision not reopened because the recommendation is unchanged and DTI < 45%. Fri Oct 30 `ptd_cleared` (last PTD: HOI declaration page Oct 29) → `ctc_checklists` evaluated 09:30 → CTC issued Fri Oct 30, 2026 (`clear_to_close.issued`). Mon Nov 2 CD delivered (25.2; gate `SM_UW_PTD_CLEARED_GATE` was open). Tue Nov 3 23.1 final-match submission (closing docs). Thu Nov 5 VVOE and credit refresh (PTF; 22.2/22.3). Fri Nov 6 consummation; Tue Nov 10 rescission expires at midnight (25.3); Wed Nov 11 Veterans Day (federal holiday; not inside the rescission period); Thu Nov 12 funding after `ptf_cleared` (evidence of insurance effective Nov 12; executed docs reviewed) → 26.3. Relief ledger at funding: `limited_waiver_du` eligible; `income_validated`, `employment_validated`, `assets_validated` eligible (closed Nov 6 ≤ close-by Dec 7); `value_acceptance` eligible (offer Oct 6, note Nov 6); `payment_history_36` opens at purchase Thu Nov 19, 2026 — first payment due Jan 1, 2027, so the 36th monthly payment due date is Dec 1, 2029.
9. **Worked example 2 — reopened decision near closing.** Same fixture; on Wed Nov 4 the 22.2 refresh shows a new $1,200/month HELOC draw on the departing property's second lien (mortgage-related, so no undisclosed-debt relief) — DTI recalculated 51.75% > 50% → `ineligible_change` (23.1) → `decision.reopened` two `business_days_creditor` before the Fri Nov 6 consummation → `underwriting_reviewer` escalation (SLA: same business day). Options: borrower pays down the HELOC before closing (PTF condition with payoff evidence; DTI recomputed), or the closing moves. If the loan cannot be restructured, the reviewer approves a denial and 21.6 issues the adverse action within 30 days of the completed application (Oct 14 → Nov 13); the CD already delivered does not create an obligation to close.
10. **Worked example 3 — purchase fixture, counteroffer arithmetic.** From 23.2 rule 9: proposed $360,000.00 at 6.375% (P&I $2,245.93), no MI, taxes $520.00, insurance $95.00, other debts $410.00 → DTI 46.73% at $7,000.00 income. The counteroffer decision record carries reasons ("requested loan amount produces a debt-to-income ratio of 53.32%, above the 50% maximum") and the offered terms; `underwriting_reviewer` approves Thu Oct 29; 21.6 sends `NTC_REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER` (combined with adverse-action content per comment 9(a)(1)-6) and starts `REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER_90` (expires Wed Jan 27, 2027); borrower accepts Oct 29 → 21.5 changed circumstance → 23.1 resubmission → Approve/Eligible → conditional approval re-issued Fri Oct 30 with the new terms.

#### Integrations
- Internal only: 23.1/23.2 (findings, conditions), 22.x (evidence), 24.x (collateral/title/insurance/MI), 23.4/25.1 (compliance results), 21.4 (locks), 21.5/21.6 (changed circumstances; notices), 28.1 (prefunding QC holds via `qc.review.*` events; QC has read access to `condition_clearances` and `decisions`), 29.3/29.4 (relief ledger, SFCs), 31.2 (monitoring feeds: decision outcomes by protected-class proxies are *not* computed here; 31.2 consumes `decisions` with demographic data joined under access logging).
- External: none directly. Notice delivery through the shared print/e-delivery adapters (00b-svc N9) for `NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL`.
- Portal fallback: none needed; the ops console exposes the same checklist and clearance screens for `underwriting_reviewer`.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `decisions` rows and the AI decision record (`agent_decisions`), the conditional-approval letter (`documents`, retention `regb_25m` and `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`), `condition_clearances`, `ctc_checklists`, `rep_warrant_relief`.
- Internal underwriting summary (replaces Form 1008): a rendered document `DOC_SM_UNDERWRITING_SUMMARY` with the risk assessment, DU recommendation/casefile ID, income/asset/liability figures, ratios, collateral, conditions and clearances — kept in the permanent loan file alongside the final findings.
- Hand-offs: to 21.6 (approval/counteroffer/denial/NOIA inputs with reasons), 25.2 (`ptd_cleared`), 26.1/26.3 (`clear_to_close`, `ptf_cleared`), 28.1 (selection feed), 28.3 (action taken), 29.3 (relief components/SFCs).
- Reports: decision cycle times (findings → conditional approval → CTC), conditions per loan and aging, waiver log, reopened decisions, relief-eligibility coverage, fair-lending monitoring extracts (31.2).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`underwriter` agent (tools: `assessRisk`, `issueConditionalApproval`, `renderApprovalLetter`, `evaluateClearance`, `clearCondition`, `reopenCondition`, `runCtcChecklist`, `issueClearToClose`, `evaluateReliefLedger`, `prepareAdverseDecision`, `writeDecision`, `openEscalation`). End-to-end: it turns 23.2's `proceed` into a conditional approval within one business day, evaluates every piece of evidence against the clearing standard within one business day, runs the CTC checklist continuously and issues CTC the moment it passes, keeps the relief ledger current, and prepares adverse/counteroffer decisions for the reviewer. Decision-record schema (LL-2026-04, Colorado SB 26-189 records, §1002.12 retention): `{decision_id, application_id, kind, inputs_hash, du:{casefile_id, submission_number, recommendation, policy_generation, findings_hash}, evidence:[{document_id, kind, age_check}], risk_assessment, reasons:[{code, text, principal:boolean, source_fact}], rule_set_versions:{fnma.selling, fnma.du, regb, regz.qm, jurisdiction}, model_version, prompt_version, confidence, reviewer:{id, action, at, notes}, outcome, notice_id}`. Guardrails: never issue CTC with a failing checklist item; never clear a DU verification condition without evidence meeting DU's level; never waive a Fannie Mae eligibility item; never issue a denial, counteroffer or NOIA without `underwriting_reviewer`; never use protected-class data or proxies in the assessment (demographics live in `applicant_demographics` with access logging and are not readable by the agent); never state DU, a score cutoff, or "internal standards" as a reason; never alter the reasons after reviewer approval. Escalations (`escalations` with SLA timers): `underwriting_reviewer` (adverse/counteroffer/NOIA — SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`; reopened decision < 3 BD before closing — same business day; waiver requests; unmapped critical conditions), `qc_officer` (prefunding hold disputes), `licensed_specialist` (states requiring a licensed individual to underwrite), `human_agent` (borrower request). Borrower-facing disclosure: the approval letter identifies the partner as lender; SB 26-189/CCPA ADMT notice content rides on 21.1 (pre-use) and 21.6 (adverse outcome). Human path: the ops console lets a reviewer issue the same decisions with the same record schema.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Conditional approval before the application is complete** (e.g., before the title commitment): permitted; Reg B approval notification is satisfied early; 21.6's clock still measures adverse action from completion.
- **Lock expires after CTC**: `valid_until` passes → `decision.reopened`; relock (21.4) may change the rate → 23.1 tolerance check; CTC re-issued after the checklist passes again.
- **Credit report expires before a rescheduled note date**: `CTC_CREDIT_VALID` fails; 22.2 pulls a new report; 23.1 resubmits (new report is always a resubmission trigger); relief ledger for undisclosed debt resets to the new expiration.
- **Prefunding QC finds a defect after CTC**: hold applied; CTC revoked (`decision.reopened`); defect must be corrected "prior to loan closing"; if uncorrectable → adverse action path.
- **Contradictory information after clearance** (e.g., a paystub shows a garnishment not in the credit report): `condition.reopened`; A2-2-04 relief is lost if the investigation would have changed DU inputs — resubmit with corrected data.
- **Reviewer unavailable within SLA before a scheduled closing**: the escalation auto-escalates to the partner `officer`; closing is not held on an unapproved adverse decision — the loan simply does not close until a decision exists.
- **Borrower requests human review (Colorado/California)**: route to `underwriting_reviewer` (second reviewer for appeals); record the request and outcome in the decision record; 21.6 handles notice timing.
- **Value-acceptance offer exercised but the property is later found damaged (disaster)**: A2-2-06 keeps the lender responsible for condition after a disaster — 24.1/24.5 add an inspection condition; CTC revoked until cleared.
- **Form 1008 requested by an investor or examiner**: provide the internal underwriting summary; state that B3-2-04 does not require Form 1008 for DU loans.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **23.3-T1** Given `du.findings.interpreted{proceed}` at 09:12 Tue Oct 6, 2026 and 23.4 preliminary `qm/not_high_cost`, when the agent runs, then `decision.issued{conditional_approval}` and `notice.sent{NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL}` occur by Wed Oct 7, 2026 (`SM_UW_CONDITIONAL_APPROVAL_SLA_1BD`), with `valid_until = 2026-11-21` and 14 borrower-facing conditions listed.

- **23.3-T3** Given all PTD conditions cleared but an open `investigation{kind=du_red_flag}` (Occupancy Modified), then `ptd_cleared` is not emitted and `SM_UW_PTD_CLEARED_GATE` blocks `issueCD` on Mon Nov 2, 2026 with `blocking_codes = {CTC_NO_OPEN_INVESTIGATION}`.
- **23.3-T4** Given the CTC checklist on Fri Oct 30, 2026 with every item `pass` except `CTC_INSURANCE_FLOOD` (declaration page missing), then no CTC; when the declaration page is classified at 09:20, then `clear_to_close.issued` at ≤ 10:20 the same day.
- **23.3-T5** Given a DTI recalculated at 51.75% on Wed Nov 4, 2026 for a Fri Nov 6 consummation, then `decision.reopened` and an `escalation{underwriting_reviewer}` with same-business-day SLA; `SM_UW_CTC_GATE` blocks `consummate` until re-issued.
- **23.3-T6** Given a `decline_candidate` outcome, when the agent prepares the record with reasons `["debt-to-income ratio of 53.32% exceeds the 50% maximum"]`, then 21.6 is not invoked until `reviewer_action = approved`; a reason text containing "DU" or "Refer with Caution" fails validation.
- **23.3-T7** Given a counteroffer notice sent Thu Oct 29, 2026 that the borrower neither accepts nor uses, then 21.6's `REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER_90` expires Wed Jan 27, 2027 and `restructure_proposals.status = expired` (23.2); no second adverse-action notice is required when the combined notice was used (comment 9(a)(1)-6).

- **23.3-T10** Given an `underwriting_reviewer` attempts to waive `COND_DU_VERIFY_ASSETS`, then the waiver is refused (`WAIVER_NOT_PERMITTED_DU_MESSAGE`); waiving an SM-added `COND_SM_LETTER_OF_EXPLANATION` succeeds with a recorded reason.
- **23.3-T11** Given the loan is purchased Thu Nov 19, 2026 with first payment due Fri Jan 1, 2027, then `rep_warrant_relief{payment_history_36}` opens with a target of the 36th payment due Dec 1, 2029 and status `eligible` pending Fannie Mae's relief report (28.2/30.4).
- **23.3-T12** Given any decision record, then it contains `rule_set_versions`, `model_version`, `prompt_version`, `inputs_hash` and `reasons[].source_fact`, and no field derived from `applicant_demographics` (access log shows zero reads by the `underwriter` agent).

#### Audit and evidence

For Fannie Mae QC/MORA: the underwriting summary, final findings, every clearance with its standard and evidence, the CTC checklist, the relief ledger and the reviewer approvals reproduce B3-1-01's "fully documenting the assessment and the documentation on which the assessment was based" and A2-2-04's conditions; prefunding QC holds evidence D1-2-01 timing. For CFPB/state exams: the Reg B notification dates versus the completed-application date, specific reasons with source facts, reviewer identities for every adverse decision, second-review records for human-review requests, and the absence of demographic reads in decisioning. For LL-2026-04: model/prompt/rule-set versions on every decision, evaluation-suite results, monitoring outputs (31.2). For litigation: immutable `loan_events` timeline from findings to funding.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **23.3-Q1 — Overlays.** Default: none beyond Fannie Mae eligibility and the DU Approve/Eligible policy; the risk assessment records layering for monitoring only. Any partner overlay must be written into `rule_sets` and applied uniformly.
2. **23.3-Q2 — Who is the `underwriting_reviewer`.** Default: partner employees (the creditor decides); SM licensed staff may act under a written delegation where state law allows (31.1 licensing check); appeals go to a different individual.
3. **23.3-Q3 — Approval letter as the Reg B approval notice.** Default: yes; the letter is coded `NTC_REGB_1002_9_APPROVAL` and its e-delivery/mailing date is the notification date; state commitment-letter content rules to be confirmed (**[UNVERIFIED]**).
4. **23.3-Q4 — CTC before CD.** Default: `ptd_cleared` (not full CTC) gates `issueCD`; CTC gates the closing appointment. This lets the CD go out on day −3 while flood/insurance evidence arrives, without sacrificing CD accuracy.
5. **23.3-Q5 — Decision validity cap.** Default: 90 days or the earliest expiring component.
6. **23.3-Q6 — Prefunding hold scope.** Default: any selected loan is held from CTC until the review closes; 28.1 selection runs by day −7 to avoid closing delays.
