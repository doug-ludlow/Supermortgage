# Section 29 — Secondary marketing and delivery to Fannie Mae (whole loan, servicing retained)

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 29 takes a locked loan from the moment the partner's rate lock exists to the moment Fannie Mae's purchase proceeds repay the warehouse line: committing and pricing execution in PE–Whole Loan (29.1 — best-efforts commitments per lock as the default, mandatory commitments behind a feature flag, key-data modifications, extensions, pair-offs, over/under-delivery tolerance, the servicing strip and the LLPA/net-price forecast); pipeline and interest-rate-risk management (29.2 — daily position, pull-through and fallout, the hedge program for mandatory execution with Fannie Mae commitments or TBA forwards, mark-to-market and the ASC 815/948/825 outputs, rate-shock and liquidity reporting to the partner); delivery data preparation and pre-delivery validation (29.3 — ULDD Phase 5 (5.2.0) build, Special Feature Codes, identifier consistency across DU/UCD/UCDP/Property Data/CPM/MI/MERS, EarlyCheck on the DU Spec and ULDD files, the frozen delivery package); and Loan Delivery submission, custodian certification, purchase, purchase-advice reconciliation and post-delivery corrections and remedies (29.4). Product scope is the inventory's: Fannie Mae conventional conforming 1–4 unit first liens sold whole loan for cash with servicing retained by the partner and subserviced by SM; MBS swap and servicing-released executions are out of scope. Everything is written to the Selling Guide edition of Sept 2, 2026, the LLPA Matrix and SFC list of 09.09.2026, ULDD 5.2.0 (May 26, 2026), the Loan Delivery User Guide of May 2026 and the PE–WL documentation of Feb 2025.

**Agents.** One agent — `secondary` — owns the section end-to-end (commitments, hedging, ULDD/EarlyCheck, Loan Delivery packages, purchase advice, remedies), with the `pricing` agent (20.4/21.4) supplying quotes and locks, the `warehouse` agent (§27) consuming proceeds forecasts and purchase advices, `post-closing` (26.4) shipping collateral to the custodian, `compliance-sentinel` for timer breaches and governance, and `qc-audit` for independent sampling. Humans appear only where Fannie Mae offers no machine interface or the partner's policy requires a person: `fnma_portal_operator` for every UI-only step (Loan Delivery import/submit/revise, PE–WL mandatory commitments, pair-offs, extensions and over-deliveries, Loan Quality Connect), the partner's `officer` (or its authorized trader) for mandatory-execution authorizations, hedge trades, pair-off fees above threshold, fee disputes and the hedging policy, `funding_approver` for margin wires, and `signing_officer` (partner) for allonges/assignments (26.4) and for a bailee-letter counter-signature where the custodian requires one — the bailee letter and Form 2004A themselves are SM's documents signed by SM's `officer` (27.1 rule 5); Forms 482/360 are signed by the partner's `officer` (29.4).

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the Fannie Mae seller: every commitment, delivery, certification, purchase, fee draft, rep and warrant, and every derivative is in its name and on its books; the servicing strip (25 bps by default) is its MSR; Loan Delivery runs under its seller number with its custodian (Form 2017), its payee codes (Form 482) and its draft account. SM performs every step through partner-scoped Technology Manager System IDs assigned to its TSP product (Form 101), builds and hashes every file before a human imports it, marks and reports the position, and bears the committing fees its own fulfillment failures cause; SM is also the warehouse lender whose bailee letter and wire instructions Loan Delivery validates, and whose line is repaid from the proceeds (§27). No consumer artifact is produced in this section — borrower-facing lock terms are 21.4's and the ownership-transfer notice is 25.4/30.4's.

**Dependencies.** Inbound: 20.4 (`pricing_quotes`, `rate_sheets`, `llpa_tables`, `FNMA_PEWL_QUOTE_ID_WINDOW`, `SM_LLPA_TABLE_VERSION_GATE`), 21.4 (`locks`, `lock_extensions`, `lock.executed`, `lock.commitment.linked`, the key-data events), 23.1 (DU casefile, final submission hash, `FNMA_B3_2_10_DU_FINAL_MATCH_GATE`), 24.2/24.3/24.5/24.6 (UCDP Doc File ID and SSR, CPM IDs, flood, MI activation gates), 25.2 (UCD casefile and `FNMA_UCD_ACCEPTED_GATE`), 26.2/26.4 (eNote registration and transfer of control, MERS MIN, note shipping), 26.3 (`loan.funded`, first-payment gate), 27.1 (bailee letters, wire instructions, Form 2004A), 28.x (post-closing QC and self-reports). Outbound: 27.2 (purchase advice, gain-on-sale baseline), 30.1/30.2 (purchase date, Fannie Mae loan number, remittance type, servicer setup), 20.1 (120-day premium-recapture window), 31.2 (pull-through model governance), servicing 5.1/6.x (first reporting cycle, custodial deposits) and 1.4 (eVault/custody records).

**Changes in flight that shape the build.** (1) VantageScore 4.0 is live for all sellers since Sept 9, 2026 (LL-2026-06) with its own LLPA grids and SFC 067 — pricing and ULDD carry the score model. (2) The LLPA Matrix 09.09.2026 strikes LLPAs on the Purchase Ready date, so the matrix version is a function of the expected purchase date, not the lock date; the HomeReady very-low-income credit runs through Feb 28, 2027. (3) Loan Delivery has no API: import, submit and revisions are human steps on AI-frozen, hashed packages; EarlyCheck and UCD run on the legacy Direct Integration rail while pricing, committing, purchase advice and fee drafts are Developer Portal APIs (00b-orig F6/F7). (4) PE–WL: best-efforts pricing/committing by API; mandatory commitments, pair-offs, extensions and over-deliveries UI-only; the Buy Up Buy Down Daily API is MBS-only — whole-loan cash has no buy-up/buy-down. (5) ULDD Phase 5 (5.2.0, May 26, 2026); UCD v2.0 mandate dates deferred to Q4 2026; UAD 3.6 required Nov 2, 2026. (6) FINRA Rule 4210 covered-agency margin (effective May 22, 2024) makes any TBA hedge a liquidity exposure; SAB 109 puts servicing value inside IRLC fair value. (7) SIFMA 2026 closes (Columbus Day Oct 12, Veterans Day Nov 11, Thanksgiving Nov 26, Christmas Dec 25; early closes Nov 27, Dec 24, Dec 31) drive the Fannie Mae committing calendar and the fixture arithmetic. (8) Fannie Mae's TLS cipher cutover (Oct 23, 2026) and the Consolidated Technology Guide's ban on UI automation fix the portal islands as human-operated steps. (9) LL-2026-04 AI/ML governance (Aug 6, 2026) covers the pull-through model and every `agent_decisions` record in the section.

## Closing

### Section-level test plan and sequencing

**Build order.** (1) 29.1 first — `commitments` and the PE–Whole Loan adapters (`fnma-pe-whole-loan` Loan Pricing/Committing APIs for best efforts; UI tasks for mandatory, pair-off, extension, over-delivery) plus the `fannie_sifma` calendar behind `business_days_fannie_et`, because every downstream clock (delivery expiry, LLPA date, loan age) is anchored on a commitment. (2) 29.3 second — the ULDD Phase 5 (5.2.0) builder with the Appendix D/E rule set (`fnma.uldd.5.2.0`), the 09.09.2026 SFC list, the Business Rules Dictionary snapshot, the EarlyCheck Direct Integration adapter (`fnma-earlycheck`, certified in the integration environment against the ULDD Phase 5 Integration Test Cases) and the freeze-and-hash package model; it consumes the 23.1/24.x/25.2/26.x gates, so those sections' gate events must exist in the test harness before 29.3 is exercised. (3) 29.4 third — the `fnma_portal_operator` task framework with evidence capture, the custodian shipping/tracking and Document Certification notice ingestion (with the `post-closing` agent), the eVault/eRegistry Transfer of Control path (shared `evault` adapter, 1.4), the SM warehouse-lender Loan Delivery org workflow, the Purchase Advice Sellers API ingester and reconciliation hand-off to 27.2, and the PPA/LSDU and relief-confirmation bookkeeping. (4) 29.2 last and in two stages — best-efforts operations (position, pull-through, fallout, monthly review) at launch; the mandatory hedge program (`execution.mandatory_enabled=true`, dealer margin agreements, TBA roll and FINRA 4210 margin timers, MTM) only after the partner's policy, the calibrated pull-through model and the 31.2 review exist. Cross-cutting from day one: Technology Manager TSP Product assignment, Form 101, Form 482 payee codes (including SM's warehouse account), Form 360, the SM warehouse-lender org (1–2 weeks), Form 2017/FCC linkage or the 26.4-Q1 alternative, and the eMortgage/eNote agreements (partner eSeller approval; SM on the eNote sub-servicer and warehouse-bank lists; the eNote Control Transfer and Custodial Agreement).

### Section open questions

1. **Commitment buffer and calendar (29.1-Q1).** Default `expires_on = nextBusinessDay(lock.expires_on + 14, fannie_sifma)`, capped at 90 days and at the six-month loan-age date; supersedes 21.4's "+15".
2. **Remittance type and servicing strip (29.1-Q2).** Default A/A and 25 bps for every product; excess servicing deferred to the partner's MSR decision.
3. **Fee allocation SM vs partner (29.1-Q3).** Default: SM bears fulfillment-caused extension/pair-off/DPA fees; the partner bears fees caused by its own acts; no Fannie Mae fee pass-through to borrowers.
4. **Mandatory execution gating (29.1-Q4, 29.2-Q2/Q3).** Default `execution.mandatory_enabled=false` for at least six months; when enabled, `officer` authorization per commitment/pair-off, Fannie Mae mandatory commitments preferred over TBA, no options.
5. **Underwriting method at commitment (29.1-Q5).** Default `du` with the casefile id when an Approve/Eligible ≤ 60 days exists; Other→DU switch [UNVERIFIED].
6. **After-hours committing (29.1-Q6).** Default: extended best-efforts window when the API accepts a live price; overnight basis cap 25 bps × uncommitted volume.
7. **Execution variance treatment (29.1-Q7).** Default: positive variance funds the 20.4 lender-credit cap; negative variance is SM's cost unless partner-directed.
8. **Pair-off/DPA approval thresholds (29.1-Q8).** Default `officer` approval above $2,500 (fees) / $1,000 (DPA).
9. **Best-efforts cash back and auto-extension fees (29.1-Q9).** Default assumptions reconciled against the first fee drafts, never hard-coded.
10. **Whose P&L is the hedge and accounting elections (29.2-Q1/Q6).** Default: the partner's; FVO for held-for-sale loans, no hedge-accounting designation; the partner's accountants decide.
11. **Coverage band, limits, pull-through model, dealer margin, roll lead (29.2-Q4/Q5/Q7/Q8).** Defaults 85–110% coverage, 5-business-day uncommitted cap, lookup pull-through v1 then logistic under 31.2, $25,000/$250,000 margin terms, 3-day roll lead.
12. **Property Data ID, Income Calculator and DU validation identifiers in ULDD (29.3-Q1).** Default: persisted and asserted, not written to the ULDD unless Appendix D shows a Sort ID.
13. **SID 398.3 for whole loans (29.3-Q2).** Fannie Mae's guide is MBS-only; default blank; payee code (Form 482) + SIDs 642/650.1; confirm any whole-loan wire-nickname import with the Integration Team.
14. **TPO codes 211/212 for SM's fulfillment role (29.3-Q3).** Default none; partner counsel confirms with its Fannie Mae account team; feature flag `delivery.sfc_tpo_code`.
15. **SFC 873 / FTHB ≤ 100% AMI LLPA waiver (29.3-Q4).** Not found in the 09.09.2026 extraction; default treated as data-derived; re-read the PDF.
16. **EarlyCheck run reuse and warning-bypass policy (29.3-Q5/Q6).** Default: reuse the DU-file run on an identical hash; warnings need a rule-referenced justification; warning-to-fatal edits are treated as fatal from the announced effective date.
17. **Loan Delivery DI for TSPs (29.4-Q1).** Default: human operator with evidence capture; ask the Integration Team in writing; collapse the task to exception handling if a channel exists.
18. **Custodian model and pre-positioned notes (29.4-Q2; 26.4-Q1).** Default: one FCC acting as SM's bailee; electronic cover letter and loan list the same day; confirm "first morning delivery" treatment for notes already in the vault.
19. **Purchase-interest day count (29.4-Q3).** C2-1.1-06 is silent; default: keep 30/360 and actual/365 expectations until 27.2 confirms from the Purchase Advice field definitions.
20. **eNote wire/letter-type options in Loan Delivery under the eNote funding agreement (29.4-Q4).** Default per Fannie Mae's eMortgage team instruction captured in the onboarding runbook.
21. **Fannie Mae Connect reports exposing Loan Delivery status (29.4-Q5).** Default: operator captures and custodian notices; add the report ingester when identified.
22. **LQC data-validation response window (29.4-Q6).** Default 30 calendar days unless the request states otherwise; aligned with 28.2.
23. **Master Servicer vs Servicing Agent field for the subservicer (29.4-Q7).** Default: partner Org ID as Master Servicer, SM as Servicing Agent, pending 30.1.
24. **Operator staffing and daily cutoffs (29.4-Q8).** Default: SM staff under the partner's Technology Manager org; tasks due 15:00 MT (carrier 16:30 MT; data 9:00 p.m. ET); an 08:00 MT window for eNote-only loans; segregation between seller-org and warehouse-org operators.
25. **Loan-age gate naming.** `FNMA_B2_1_5_02_LOAN_AGE_6M_PURCHASE_READY` (29.4) governs; 29.1's `FNMA_C1_1_01_LOAN_AGE_6M_GATE` is retired as an alias.
