# Section 15 — REO / Claims / Expense Reimbursement

## Overview

Section 15 is the **Liquidation & Claims Engine**: everything that happens to a Fannie Mae loan between the moment a foreclosure sale (or Mortgage Release) legally ends the borrower's ownership and the moment every dollar the platform advanced has been recovered from Fannie Mae, the mortgage insurer, the sale proceeds or the borrower. Four processes make it up: **15.1** notifying Fannie Mae of an acquired property (the Property 360 REOgram), conveying title, handing the property to Fannie Mae's REO vendor and reconciling third-party foreclosure sales (TPS); **15.2** the expense-reimbursement claim engine (advance ledger → validated claim lines → Property 360 "571" claims → payment reconciliation → IRT disputes); **15.3** the mortgage-insurance claim engine (claim computation, MICP document package, direct filing for non-participating insurers, curtailment monitoring, proceeds reconciliation); and **15.4** recovery of delinquency (P&I) advances at liquidation and Stop Delinquency Advance exits — re-based from the blueprint's "Form 4828" (a Multifamily form; see 15.4) to the single-family mechanics of Servicing Guide F-1-20 and the Investor Reporting Manual.

Architecturally this section is owned by the `claims-reo` agent (architecture baseline §8) with `investor-reporting` (removal LARs/liquidation events, 5.3), `custodial-recon` (draft-adjustment matching, 5.2/5.4), `insurance-property` (policy cancellation and refunds, 9.x), `pmi` (MI policy data, Section 10) and `foreclosure-ops` (sale results, DRA, attorney invoices, Section 13). It consumes the baseline `advances`, `fees`, `ledger_entries`, `liquidation_facts` (defined in 5.3) and `dra_milestones` projections and adds the tables defined in each process's *Data model*. Every deadline is a Timer Engine definition on the `fannie_et` calendar unless a master policy or statute counts calendar days. The Fannie Mae rails are the `fnma-p360` adapter (Expense Claims API/B2B where credentialed, otherwise the bulk-upload package), `fnma-crs` (special remittance codes 311/351/310/314/317/318/336/571), `fnma-lsdu`/`fnma-servicing-events` (codes 70/71/72 today; Property 360 liquidation events from Q1 2027 under LL-2026-05), `fnma-connect` (Expense Reimbursement Reconciliation, Eliminations/Rescissions Daily, Remittance Detail reports) and `mi/*` (EDI/portal claim filing for non-MICP insurers). Portal-only islands — REOgram confirmation, TPS document upload, MICP document upload, IRT inquiries, HomeTracker bids, the Elimination/Rescission Request Template — are `human_portal_task` escalations to `fnma_portal_operator` with an AI-prepared package (Consolidated Technology Guide, Apr. 21, 2026: no robots/scrapers; credentials only to employees).

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: every REOgram confirmation, claim, MI document upload and special remittance is made under the **partner's 9-digit servicer number** and Technology Manager roles provisioned to Supermortgage employees (Form 101 data-access authorization). The partner is the insured under each MI master policy and the party Fannie Mae holds liable for late REOgrams (compensatory fees, A1-4.2-02), late or defective claims (denials, curtailments, and the E-4.5-01 obligation to reimburse Fannie Mae for MI losses caused by servicer failures) and unremitted proceeds. Supermortgage prepares, validates, submits, reconciles and disputes; the partner's `officer` signs any make-whole/indemnification response (A1-3-02).

Dependencies: 5.3 (removal LAR 70/71/72 and the CRS proceeds codes — this section owns the downstream REOgram/TPS/claims work and reuses 5.3's `FNMA_E4101_REOGRAM_CONFIRM_1BD` timer), 5.2/5.4 (draft-adjustment matching and Stop Delinquency Advance receivables), 9.1/9.5/9.7 (policy cancellation, LPI refunds, loss drafts), 9.8/9.9 (inspection and preservation costs, HomeTracker over-allowable bids, `allowable_matrix`), 10.x (`mi_policies`, insurer default reporting, MI premium advances), 12.x (short sale/Mortgage Release facts and workout expense claims), 13.x (bidding instructions, sale results, attorney milestone invoices, foreclosure time-frame/compensatory-fee tracking, Form 200 firms), 14.x (bankruptcy fee invoices), 16.x (reinstatement/payoff recovery of advances and the 60-day repayment of recovered advances to Fannie Mae).

Changes in flight (verified 2026-09-09): Servicing Guide edition **Aug. 12, 2026** (E-4.5-01 updated 07/09/2025 for MICP; F-1-06 updated by SVC-2026-04, 05/13/2026; F-1-05 updated 06/11/2025); Property 360 release of **May 1, 2026** retired the "Third Party Sales Reimbursement" line item (itemize instead); the Servicer Expense Reimbursement Job Aid (05/01/2026); **LL-2026-05** moves REO/TPS/government-conveyance liquidation events into Property 360 (CIT Nov. 2026–Feb. 2027, go-live March 2027 — 00b F4/F5), which will eventually subsume the REOgram round-trip; the MI Claims Portal Participants Exhibit (June 1, 2025) lists Arch, Essent, MGIC, UGI (10/01/2021), Radian (10/18/2021), Enact and PMI (11/15/2021) and NMI (10/01/2022) — RMIC merged into UGI June 1, 2025 — so **Fannie Mae files virtually every conventional MI claim** and the servicer's job is documents, data accuracy and monitoring.
