### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**12 CFR Part 1016 (Regulation P; CFPB interactive regulation verified today).**
- **Who has the obligation.** §1016.3(e)(1): a "consumer" is "an individual who obtains or has obtained a financial product or service from you." §1016.3(e)(2)(iv): "If you hold ownership or servicing rights to an individual's loan ..., the individual is your consumer, even if you hold those rights in conjunction with one or more other institutions," and remains so "even if you, or another institution with those rights, hire an agent to collect on the loan." §1016.3(e)(2)(v): "An individual who is a consumer of another financial institution is not your consumer solely because you act as agent for, or provide processing or other services to, that financial institution." §1016.3(j)(1): a "customer relationship" is "a continuing relationship"; (j)(2)(i) examples include holding a loan or its servicing rights; (j)(2)(ii): no continuing relationship when "you sell the consumer's loan and do not retain the rights to service that loan." §1016.4(c)(2): "You establish a customer relationship with a consumer when you originate or acquire the servicing rights to a loan to the consumer for personal, family, or household purposes"; (c)(3)(iv): a customer relationship arises when you "(A) Originate the loan to the consumer; or (B) Purchase the servicing rights to the consumer's loan." **Consequence:** the partner (MSR holder) is the financial institution with the customer; Supermortgage, as its agent/processor, has no Reg P consumer of its own for these loans and receives NPI under the §1016.13 service-provider exception (contract "that prohibits the third party from disclosing or using the information other than to carry out the purposes for which you disclosed the information") and the §1016.14 processing/servicing exception ("servicing or processing a financial product or service that a consumer requests or authorizes"; "maintaining or servicing the consumer's account"). Supermortgage is separately a "financial institution" for the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR 314; Section 19.2) and, if it ever holds MSR of its own, becomes a Reg P institution for those loans.
- **Initial notice §1016.4(a)(1):** "not later than when you establish a customer relationship." §1016.4(e)(1): a later notice "within a reasonable time" is allowed if "establishing the customer relationship is not at the customer's election" or immediate notice "would substantially delay the customer's transaction and the customer agrees"; (e)(2)(i) example — acquiring "the servicing rights to a customer's loan from another financial institution" where "the customer does not have a choice about your acquisition" **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — example paraphrased by the fetch; the deposit-liability example was quoted]**. §1016.4(d): an existing customer receiving a new product needs no new notice if the prior notice is accurate. §1016.4(b): no initial notice to a mere consumer if NPI is shared only under §§1016.14/.15.
- **Annual notice §1016.5(a)(1):** "not less than annually during the continuation of the customer relationship"; "annually means at least once in any period of 12 consecutive months"; (a)(2) example: calendar-year basis is acceptable. **(b):** no annual notice after the relationship ends (payoff/transfer). **(e) Exception:** no annual notice while the institution "provide[s] nonpublic personal information to nonaffiliated third parties only in accordance with the provisions of § 1016.13, § 1016.14, or § 1016.15" and has "not changed [its] policies and practices with regard to disclosing nonpublic personal information from the policies and practices that were disclosed to the customer under § 1016.6(a)(2) through (5) and (9) in the most recent privacy notice"; **(e)(2):** when the exception lapses — (i) if a revised notice is required under §1016.8, treat it as the initial notice for annual-timing purposes; (ii) otherwise "provide an annual privacy notice within 100 days of the change."
- **Content §1016.6 / model form:** categories of NPI collected and disclosed; categories of affiliates and nonaffiliated third parties (service providers and joint marketers under §1016.13 described by category; §§1016.14/.15 sharing may be described as "as permitted by law"); former-customer practices; opt-out explanation only if sharing outside the exceptions; FCRA affiliate-sharing notices where applicable; security-policy statement. Use of the **model privacy form in Appendix A**, consistent with its instructions, "constitutes compliance with the notice content requirements" (§1016.2(a)) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — cite from general knowledge of §1016.2(a)]**.
- **Delivery §1016.9:** "each consumer can reasonably be expected to receive actual notice in writing or, if the consumer agrees, electronically"; reasonable methods include hand delivery, mailing to the last known address, and "posting the notice on the electronic site and requiring the consumer to acknowledge receipt" for consumers who conduct transactions electronically; unreasonable: signage, or emailing a customer who does not obtain services electronically; **annual notices** may be delivered by continuously posting on the website for customers who use it as their primary means and agreed to electronic notices, or where the customer requested no mailings and the notice is available on request ((c)); oral notice insufficient ((d)); the notice must be retainable ((e)); **joint notices** from more than one institution are permitted "as long as the notice is accurate for all institutions" ((f)); one notice per joint account ((g)).
- **Related:** §1016.8 revised notice and new opt-out before sharing in a new way; §1016.11 limits on reuse/redisclosure by recipients (binds Supermortgage and its vendors); §1016.12 account-number sharing prohibition for marketing; §1016.15 other exceptions (consent, fraud, legal process, CRAs, sale of business, regulators).
- **GLBA statute:** 15 U.S.C. 6803(a) (initial and annual notices) and (f) (annual-notice exception added by the FAST Act, 2015) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED citation]**.

**State overlays.** California: CCPA/CPRA does not apply to "personal information collected, processed, sold, or disclosed subject to the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act ... or the California Financial Information Privacy Act," except the §1798.150 breach private right of action (Civ. Code §1798.145(e), 2025 code — verified); CalFIPA (Fin. Code §4050 et seq.) requires opt-in for nonaffiliated sharing outside exceptions and its own notice form when such sharing occurs — not triggered if sharing stays within the exceptions **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; Vermont opt-in regime similar **[UNVERIFIED]**. Fannie Mae: no privacy-notice rule; Consolidated Technology Guide/A2-1-01 data-use restrictions and the Information Security Supplement govern Supermortgage's use of Fannie Mae data (19.3).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) "SoR/Sub: Sub" is only half right — the *obligation* is the partner's (holder of servicing rights); Supermortgage performs delivery in the partner's name or by joint notice; (2) with sharing limited to §§1016.13–.15 and stable policies, the annual notice is **not required at all** (1016.5(e)); (3) "Print/mail" ignores the electronic posting/acknowledgment and website-only annual-notice options; (4) the initial-notice trigger is the partner's acquisition of servicing rights (transfer-in with MSR sale), not Supermortgage's boarding; for master-to-subservicer moves no new initial notice is due if the partner's prior notice remains accurate (which the template guarantees by disclosing service-provider sharing generically).

### Operational prerequisites

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `transfer_in.msr_acquired_by_partner` (1.2/1.3: partner acquires servicing rights concurrently with subservicing) → initial notice with the hello notice.
- `loan.boarded` (master-to-subservicer or subservicer-to-subservicer) → no initial notice; verify `partner_privacy_notice_version` on file (transfer file/partner attestation); optional courtesy copy (decision 2).
- `sii.confirmed` with assumption (4.4) → initial notice to the new customer.
- `privacy_policy.changed` (partner change-control) → §1016.8 assessment → revised notice and/or annual notice within 100 days.
- Annual: `privacy.annual_cycle.opened` (Jan 2) → exception determination; if not met, annual notices by Dec 31 of that year.
- `loan.paid_in_full`/`transfer_out.effective` → relationship terminated (no further notices).
- Borrower requests: `privacy_notice.copy_requested` (any channel) → send within 5 business days (policy).

#### Data model
- `privacy_notice_programs`: `partner_id`, `notice_version`, `model_form_variant`, `sharing_profile` ∈ {exceptions_only, optout_required, optin_state}, `annual_exception_eligible bool`, `last_partner_attestation_at`, `effective_from/to`.
- `privacy_notice_deliveries` (view over `notices` with template `NTC_REGP_*`): `party_id`, `loan_id`, `kind` ∈ {initial, annual, revised, courtesy, on_request}, `basis` ∈ {mail, hello_insert, portal_ack, website_only}, `acknowledged_at`.
- `parties.privacy_optout jsonb` (dormant unless the sharing profile requires opt-outs).
- Retention: `respa_5y`-equivalent policy class `privacy_notice_5y` plus life-of-loan for the version served.

#### State machine
Per party: `no_notice_due` → `initial_due` (MSR acquisition or assumption) → `initial_sent` → `annual_exempt` (while 1016.5(e) holds) | `annual_due` → `annual_sent` → … ; `revised_due` → `revised_sent` (+ opt-out window if any) → back to exemption assessment; `terminated` (payoff/transfer-out). Actors: `disclosures` agent; partner (attestation, policy changes); `officer` (partner-side approval of notice versions).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Institution of record:** the notice is issued in the partner's name (or jointly, decision 1) and describes the partner's practices; Supermortgage's role is disclosed generically as a service provider ("companies that service your loan on our behalf") so subservicer changes never make the notice inaccurate (avoiding §1016.8 revised notices).
2. **Initial notice trigger:** only when the partner *acquires* servicing rights (transfer-in with MSR purchase) or a party becomes a new customer by assumption; for master-to-subservicer or subservicer-to-subservicer moves the partner's existing notice stands (§1016.4(d)); Supermortgage records the partner's notice version and date from the transfer file.
3. **Timing:** because the borrower "does not have a choice" about the acquisition, the initial notice may follow within a reasonable time — delivered as an insert with the RESPA hello notice (1.3; 15 days after transfer) and never later than 30 days (policy).
4. **Annual exception test (Jan 2 each year):** `annual_exception_eligible = (sharing_profile == exceptions_only) AND (no change in practices disclosed under §1016.6(a)(2)–(5),(9) since the last notice, per partner attestation)`; if true → no annual notice (log `privacy.annual_exception.applied` with the attestation id); else annual notices by Dec 31.
5. **Loss of exception:** if the partner starts sharing outside the exceptions → §1016.8 revised notice + opt-out (30-day reasonable opportunity, policy) before sharing, and the revised notice restarts the annual clock; if a change does not require a revised notice but ends the exception → annual notice within 100 days.
6. **Delivery:** mail (with hello notice or standalone); electronic for parties with active `privacy_notices` E-SIGN consent — portal posting with acknowledgment required for the initial notice (§1016.9(b)(1)(iii)); website-only continuous posting acceptable for annual notices of e-consented customers (§1016.9(c)(1)); joint accounts → one notice addressed to all obligors at the mailing address of record (§1016.9(g)).
7. **Content assembly:** model form fields populated from `privacy_notice_programs` (reasons for sharing grid: everyday business purposes = Yes / cannot limit; marketing = as configured (default No); joint marketing = No; affiliates' everyday purposes = per partner; affiliates marketing = No; nonaffiliates marketing = No); "Who we are" = partner (and Supermortgage if joint); "How does [partner] protect" = security statement; "Other important information" = state notices (CalFIPA/VT lines when applicable).
8. **Successor/other parties:** confirmed successors who have not assumed are not customers; they receive notices only if they assume (then rule 2) — policy: courtesy copy on confirmation (decision 2).
9. **Termination:** no notices after payoff or transfer-out; former-customer sharing per the notice's former-customer statement.

**Worked example.** Transfer-in effective Nov 2, 2026 in which the partner buys MSR from a third party and names Supermortgage subservicer (scenario (c) in Section 1): hello notice mailed Nov 10 with the partner's Appendix A privacy notice v3 as an insert → `REGP_1016_4A1_INITIAL_NOTICE_REASONABLE_30` satisfied Nov 10 (due Dec 2). Jan 2, 2027: partner attestation (received Jan 20) confirms no change and `sharing_profile = exceptions_only` → `privacy.annual_exception.applied` for 2027; no annual mailing. June 15, 2027: the partner decides to share with a nonaffiliated insurance marketer → gate blocks sharing; revised notice with opt-out mailed July 1 (opt-out through July 31); sharing may begin Aug 1 for non-opt-outs; annual clock restarts from July 1, 2027 (next annual by Dec 31, 2028 on the calendar-year basis, or within 12 months by policy).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `disclosures` — computes the initial-notice trigger from transfer metadata, assembles inserts, evaluates the annual exception from structured attestation data, runs the revised-notice gate, and answers borrower privacy questions through `borrower-comms` from the current notice text only. Decision record: `{party_id, loan_id, kind, basis, notice_version, attestation_id?, trigger_event_id, sent_at}`.
- **Guardrails:** the agent cannot alter notice text or the sharing grid (partner-controlled); a `privacy_policy.changed` event with `new_sharing=true` hard-blocks any data export tagged `marketing_share` until the gate clears; opt-out preferences (if ever enabled) are enforced in the data-export layer, not by prompt.
- **Escalations:** `officer` (partner) approves notice versions and attestations; `attorney` on state opt-in regimes before enabling any non-exception sharing; `human_agent` on request. AI-off: calendar jobs + ops console.
- **Consent interplay:** electronic privacy notices require `privacy_notices` class consent; posting-with-acknowledgment is captured in the portal; no TCPA touchpoints.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in without an MSR sale** (partner already the servicer): no initial notice; if the transfer file lacks the partner's notice evidence, send a courtesy copy (decision 2) and record it — it is not an "initial" notice.
- **Transfer-out:** no goodbye privacy notice required; the transferee's obligations begin when it acquires the servicing rights.
- **Payoff:** relationship ends; no annual notice for that year unless already sent.
- **Bankruptcy/foreclosure:** notices continue to be due (not collection communications); addressing per 14.3.
- **Successor in interest confirmed without assumption:** courtesy copy only.
- **Returned mail:** no re-mail obligation beyond "reasonably expected to receive" at the last known address; re-send after an address update.
- **Partner attestation late:** default to sending annual notices (conservative) if not received by Mar 31.
- **Multiple partners (future):** `privacy_notice_programs` keyed by partner; loans map to their SoR.
- **Vendor breach/reuse:** §1016.11 violations by a vendor trigger the 19.2 incident runbook and Fannie Mae's 36-hour notice where Fannie Mae data is involved.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Partner-only or joint (partner + Supermortgage) notice? **Default: joint notice** naming both, because borrowers see the Supermortgage brand on statements and calls (1016.9(f) permits it if accurate for both).
2. Courtesy copy of the partner's notice on every boarding? **Default: yes, as a hello-notice insert** (cheap; removes ambiguity), labeled as the partner's current notice, not an "initial notice."
3. Calendar-year vs rolling 12-month annual cycle if annual notices become required? **Default: calendar-year (by Dec 31)** with a rolling-12-month check as a guard.
4. Offer Spanish notices? **Default: yes** (voluntary; §1016 permits other languages if English available on request — parallels §1024.32(a)(2)).
