### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**General liabilities — B3-6-01, General Information on Liabilities (05/04/2022). VERIFIED.** The risk analysis must include "all liabilities affecting income or assets that will affect the borrower's ability to fulfill the mortgage payment obligation": housing payments, revolving charge accounts, installment loans with more than 10 months remaining, lease payments, real estate loans, HELOCs, alimony, child support and "all other debts of a recurring nature." For each liability the lender must "determine the unpaid balance, the terms of repayment, and the borrower's payment history." "If the credit report does not contain a reference for each significant open debt shown on the loan application ... the lender must provide separate credit verification." "If a current liability appears on the credit report that is not shown on the loan application, the borrower should provide a reasonable explanation for the undisclosed debt." "If the borrower discloses, or the lender discovers, additional liabilities after the underwriting decision has been made, up to and concurrent with closing, the lender must recalculate the borrower's debt-to-income ratio." (Refresh/undisclosed-debt mechanics and the A2-2-04 relief — 22.2.)

**DTI — B3-6-02, Debt-to-Income Ratios (04/02/2025). VERIFIED.** DTI = "total monthly obligations" ÷ "total monthly income of all borrowers, to the extent the income is used to qualify for the mortgage." Obligations include the principal-residence housing payment (PITIA at the qualifying payment), mortgage payments on other properties, installment debts beyond ten months, revolving debts, lease payments, "Monthly alimony, child support, or maintenance payments that extend beyond ten months", other recurring obligations and net rental losses. Manual underwriting: "36% of the borrower's stable monthly income. The maximum can be exceeded up to 45%" with credit-score and reserve conditions (manual is out of scope, 23.2-Q1). DU: "For loan casefiles underwritten through DU, the maximum allowable DTI ratio is 50%." No rounding rule is stated in the topic (rule R1 fixes it). Exceptions listed for cash-out refinances, high-LTV refinances, nontraditional credit and non-occupant borrowers.

**Monthly housing expense — B3-6-03, Monthly Housing Expense for the Subject Property (12/16/2020). VERIFIED.** PITIA comprises "principal and interest (P&I); property, flood, and mortgage insurance premiums (as applicable); real estate taxes; ground rent; special assessments; any owners' association dues; any monthly co-op corporation fee; any subordinate financing payments on mortgages secured by the subject property." When the subject is the principal residence the housing expense uses the qualifying payment per B3-6-04; for a second home or investment property "the qualifying payment amount constitutes one monthly debt obligation within DTI" (as extracted) and the borrower's current housing expense is carried in obligations. Taxes: based on no less than the current assessed value, and for purchase/construction "reasonable estimates reflecting land and improvement values" (as extracted); abatements of five or more years from the note date may be used at the abated amount **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — paraphrased extraction]**.

**Qualifying payment — B3-6-04, Qualifying Payment Requirements (02/07/2024). VERIFIED.** "For fixed-rate mortgages, the qualifying interest rate is the note rate." ARMs with an initial fixed period of three years or less: "the maximum interest rate that could apply during the first five years after the first payment is due"; five-year ARMs: the "greater of the maximum rate that could apply during the first five years after the first payment date (note rate plus first rate change cap), or the fully indexed rate"; 7- and 10-year ARMs: "No less than note rate. Exception: Greater of the note rate or the fully indexed rate for loans that are higher-priced mortgage loans or higher-priced covered transactions under Regulation Z." Fully indexed rate: "The index plus the margin as entered in the online loan application." "Loans subject to temporary interest rate buydowns must be qualified without consideration of the bought-down rate." DU casefiles with unrecognized ARM plans use "the qualifying rate entered in the ARM Qualifying Rate field in DU"; DU 12.1 (June 26, 2026) honors that field "when that rate is higher" (00a-fnma §3.1). **SEL-2025-10 (Dec 10, 2025). VERIFIED.** "We updated our Guide to remove the 3% acceptability requirement for 7- and 10-year ARMs" (the test that the fully indexed rate minus the initial note rate "must not exceed 3%") — "Lenders may take advantage of this policy change immediately"; the change is in B2-1.4-02 (12/10/2025), not in B3-6-04, whose qualifying-rate rules are unchanged.

**Monthly debt obligations — B3-6-05, Monthly Debt Obligations (08/05/2026). VERIFIED.** *Alimony etc.:* "When the borrower is required to pay alimony, child support, equalization payments, or separate maintenance payments under a divorce decree, separation agreement, or any other written legal agreement—and those payments must continue to be made for more than ten months—the payments must be considered as part of the borrower's recurring monthly debt obligations. However, voluntary payments do not need to be taken into consideration and an exception is allowed for alimony." "For alimony, equalization payments, and separate maintenance obligations, the lender has the option to reduce the qualifying income by the amount of the obligation in lieu of including it as a monthly payment in the calculation of the DTI ratio." (Child support has no income-reduction option.) "A copy of the divorce decree, separation agreement, court order, or equivalent documentation confirming the amount of the obligation must be obtained and retained in the loan file." *Installment debt:* "All installment debt ... must be considered part of the borrower's recurring monthly debt obligations if there are more than ten monthly payments remaining. However, an installment debt with fewer monthly payments remaining also should be considered as a recurring monthly debt obligation if it significantly affects the borrower's ability to meet their credit obligations." *Revolving:* "If the credit report does not show a required minimum payment amount and there is no supplemental documentation to support a payment of less than 5%, the lender must use 5% of the outstanding balance as the borrower's recurring monthly debt obligation." *HELOC:* "When the mortgage that will be delivered to Fannie Mae also has a home equity line of credit (HELOC) that provides for a monthly payment of principal and interest or interest only, the payment on the HELOC must be considered as part of the borrower's recurring monthly debt obligations."; "If the HELOC does not require a payment, there is no recurring monthly debt obligation so the lender does not need to develop an equivalent payment amount." *30-day accounts:* "Fannie Mae does not require open 30–day charge accounts to be included in the debt-to-income ratio." *Deferred installment (non-student):* "if the borrower's credit report does not indicate the monthly amount that will be payable at the end of the deferment period, the lender must obtain copies of the borrower's payment letters or forbearance agreements so that a monthly payment amount can be determined." *Garnishments:* "All garnishments with more than ten months remaining must be included." *Student loans:* "If a monthly student loan payment is provided on the credit report, the lender may use that amount for qualifying purposes."; "If the credit report does not provide a monthly payment for the student loan, or if the credit report shows $0 as the monthly payment, the lender must determine the qualifying monthly payment using one of the options below." — income-driven plan: "the lender may obtain student loan documentation to verify the actual monthly payment is $0"; deferred/forbearance: "the lender may calculate a payment equal to 1% of the outstanding student loan balance (even if this amount is lower than the actual fully amortizing payment), or a fully amortizing payment using the documented loan repayment terms." *Debts paid by others:* "When a borrower is obligated on a non-mortgage debt - but is not the party who is actually repaying the debt - the lender may exclude the monthly payment" with "the most recent 12 months' canceled checks (or bank statements)" showing "a 12-month payment history with no delinquent payments." *Business debt in the borrower's name:* excluded only when the account has no delinquency, the business provides "12 months of canceled company checks" and "the lender's cash flow analysis of the business took payment of the obligation into consideration." *Loans secured by financial assets:* not counted "provided the lender obtains a copy of the applicable loan instrument that shows the borrower's financial asset as collateral." *Court-ordered assignment:* when the creditor "does not release the borrower from liability, the borrower has a contingent liability. The lender is not required to count this contingent liability." *Non-applicant accounts:* "If the debts do not belong to the borrower, the lender may provide supporting documentation to validate this, and may exclude the non-applicant debts for the borrower's DTI ratio. If the debts do belong to the borrower, they must be included." *Lease payments:* "Lease payments must be considered as recurring monthly debt obligations regardless of the number of months remaining on the lease." *Federal income tax installment agreements (SEL-2026-05, May 6, 2026):* "When a borrower has entered into or applied for an installment agreement with the IRS to repay delinquent federal income taxes, and there is no indication that a Notice of Federal Tax Lien has been filed against the borrower in the county in which the subject property is located, the lender must obtain the following documentation." Approved: "a copy of the approved IRS installment agreement with the terms of repayment, including the monthly payment amount and total amount due; and evidence the borrower is current on the payments associated with the tax installment plan." Pending: "a copy of the application for the installment agreement with the terms of repayment, including the monthly payment amount and total amount due." "In both cases, if the borrower is not paying off the amount owed in full, the lender must include the monthly payment amount as part of the borrower's monthly debt obligations."

**Other real estate owned — B3-6-06, Qualifying Impact of Other Real Estate Owned (09/02/2026). VERIFIED (as extracted).** Current principal residence when buying a new one: sold before closing → PITIA not counted; pending sale → both PITIAs count unless the file has "the executed sales contract for the current residence, and confirmation that any financing contingencies have been cleared"; otherwise the full PITIA of the current residence is a recurring debt. Conversion to investment → qualify under the B3-3.8 rental rules (22.3; SEL-2026-08 structure mandatory for applications on/after Nov 1, 2026) with net rental loss added to obligations and positive rental income credited.

**Debts paid off at or prior to closing — B3-6-07 (04/05/2023). VERIFIED.** "Payoff or paydown of debt solely to qualify must be carefully evaluated and considered in the overall loan analysis. The borrower's history of credit use should be a factor in determining whether the appropriate approach is to include or exclude debt for qualification." "Installment loans that are being paid off or paid down to 10 or fewer remaining monthly payments do not need to be included in the borrower's long-term debt." "If a revolving account balance is to be paid off at or prior to closing, a monthly payment on the current outstanding balance does not need to be included in the borrower's long-term debt, i.e., not included in the debt-to-income (DTI) ratio. Such accounts do not need to be closed as a condition of excluding the payment from the DTI ratio." Open 30-day charge accounts without a payment (or whose payment equals the balance): "lenders must verify borrower funds to cover the account balance" — "in addition to any funds required for closing costs and reserves"; "If the borrower paid off the account balance prior to closing, the lender may provide proof of payoff in lieu of verifying funds"; DU adds 30-day balances to "Reserves Required to be Verified", reduced by any cash-out proceeds (as extracted); debts paid from refinance proceeds are documented on the settlement statement.

**Community Seconds payment — B5-5.1-02 (05/03/2023). VERIFIED (22.4).** "When repayment is deferred for five years or more, a lender is not required to include" the subordinate payment in the DTI.

**DU tolerances — B3-2-10 (12/04/2019), owned by 23.1. VERIFIED.** Resubmit when the recalculated DTI "exceed[s] 45%, or increase[s] by 3 percentage points or more"; non-applicant accounts flagged on the application are included in DU's assessment.

**Reg Z ATR — §1026.43(c)(2)(vi)–(vii), (c)(7) (eCFR current as of 9/04/2026; 00a-fed §5.4; 23.4 owns). VERIFIED (rule) / referenced.** The creditor must consider "the consumer's current debt obligations, alimony, and child support" and "the consumer's monthly debt-to-income ratio or residual income," verified with reasonably reliable third-party records; the Fannie Mae Selling Guide chapters B3-3 through B3-6 are a QM verification safe-harbor manual (00a-fed §5.4 — PARTIALLY VERIFIED there). **HMDA — Reg C §1003.4(a)(23) (00a-fed §4.2). VERIFIED (data point exists).** The DTI relied on is a LAR data point (28.3 consumes `dti_calculations.dti_bps` of the decision-of-record).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. **Topic numbering:** B3-6-05 (08/05/2026) is *Monthly Debt Obligations* (alimony, student loans, business debt, contingent liabilities, leases, HELOCs, IRS installment agreements); B3-6-06 (09/02/2026) is *Qualifying Impact of Other Real Estate Owned*; B3-6-07 (04/05/2023) is *Debts Paid Off At or Prior to Closing*; undisclosed liabilities are B3-6-01 (22.2). The row's "-06 debts paid off / -07 alimony etc. / -05 undisclosed" mapping is wrong.
2. **SEL-2025-10 did not change ARM qualifying rates.** It removed the 3% acceptability test (B2-1.4-02); B3-6-04 (02/07/2024) still qualifies ≤ 5-year ARMs at the greater of note + first cap or the fully indexed rate, and 7/10-year ARMs at no less than the note rate (fully indexed if HPML/HPCT).
3. **Buydowns:** "qualified without consideration of the bought-down rate" — the qualifying rate is the note rate, never the bought-down rate.
4. **Student loans:** the "1%" is one of two fallbacks used only when the report shows no payment or $0; a documented income-driven $0 payment is acceptable; the reported payment is used when present.
5. **Alimony income-reduction option covers alimony, equalization payments and separate maintenance — not child support.**
6. **Revolving accounts paid off need not be closed**; installment debts paid down to ≤ 10 payments drop out, subject to the "carefully evaluated" standard.
7. **IRS installment agreements count (SEL-2026-05)** unless paid in full, and only when no Notice of Federal Tax Lien is indicated in the subject county (a lien is an 22.2/24.4 payoff matter).
8. **HELOC:** payment counts only if the line requires a P&I or interest-only payment; no imputed payment for a zero-payment line.
9. **DU 50% is an eligibility ceiling, B3-2-10 45%/3-point is a resubmission trigger** — two different gates.
10. **Reg Z §1026.43(c)(2)(vi)–(vii) and Reg C §1003.4(a)(23)** are missing federal sources: the liability set is ATR evidence and the DTI is a HMDA data point.

#### State machine

Per liability: `declared`/`discovered` → `matched` (tradeline ↔ application) | `unmatched_significant` (needs separate verification, B3-6-01) → `basis_selected` (payment basis and evidence) → `included` | `excluded{reason}` (evidence attached) → `payoff_planned` → `payoff_evidenced` → `finalized` | `reopened` (refresh/UDM alert/closing move). Guards: `excluded` requires the reason's evidence (e.g., `student_idr_zero_documented` needs the servicer's IDR statement; `paid_by_other_12m` needs 12 months' canceled checks/statements with no delinquency; `paid_off_at_closing` needs a payoff figure, verified funds "in addition" and — at closing — the settlement statement line); `le_10_payments` cannot be applied when `significantly_affects = true`. DTI: `computed{version}` → `checked_against_du` (23.1 tolerance result attached) → `decision_of_record` (the version 23.3 approves) → `final` (matches the final DU submission and CD-final data) | `superseded`. Terminal for the process: `liabilities.finalized` + `dti.final` with `du_cap_ok = true`; reopened only by 22.2 alerts, 22.3 income changes, 22.4 asset-source liabilities, 21.4/21.5/24.x term changes or a closing move.

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — DTI arithmetic.** All amounts in integer cents. `obligations_cents = pitia_cents(subject) + Σ other_reo_pitia_or_net_loss + Σ included liability qualifying payments`; `income_cents = qualifying income (22.3) − income_reductions_cents`; `dti_bps = round_half_up(obligations_cents × 10000 / income_cents)`; display two decimals (`dti_bps / 100`). Comparison to thresholds uses `dti_bps` (5000 = 50.00%; 4500 = 45.00%). Rounding is applied once, at the ratio; component payments are rounded half-up to cents at the step the source defines (P&I from the amortization formula; taxes/insurance monthly = annual ÷ 12 rounded half-up; MI per 24.6; 5% revolving = `ceil(balance × 5 / 100)`? — no: `round_half_up(balance × 5 / 100)`; 1% student = `round_half_up(balance / 100)`).

**R2 — Qualifying payment.** `qualifying_rate` by product: fixed → note rate; ARM ≤ 3-year fixed → max rate in the first five years after the first payment date (note + caps as the plan allows); 5-year ARM → `max(note + first_cap, index + margin)`; 7/10-year ARM → `note` unless `hpml_or_hpct` → `max(note, index + margin)`; Generic ARM plan → the DU "ARM Qualifying Rate" field, and DU uses it when higher (June 2026). Temporary buydown → note rate, `buydown_ignored = true`. `pi_cents = round_half_up(L × r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n))` with `r = qualifying_rate / 12` at full precision. `pitia = pi + mi + taxes + hazard + flood + hoa + coop + ground_rent + special_assessments + subordinate_payment`.

**R3 — Payment basis per liability (B3-6-05).** installment → reported payment (creditor statement overrides); revolving → reported minimum, else supplemental statement, else `round_half_up(balance × 5 / 100)`; student loan → reported payment if > 0; if none/$0 → IDR documentation showing $0 → 0; else `max option chosen by the agent` between `round_half_up(balance / 100)` and the documented amortizing payment (the Guide permits either; policy: use the lower documented figure — Q3); HELOC → the required payment (P&I or interest-only) per statement; none if no payment is required; lease → full payment regardless of months remaining; alimony/equalization/separate maintenance → per decree, with the income-reduction election; child support → per order, always a liability when > 10 months remain; garnishment → per order if > 10 months; IRS installment → agreement payment unless paid in full; deferred non-student installment → payment letter; bridge loan → documented terms; Community Seconds → 0 when deferred ≥ 5 years, else the scheduled payment; secured-by-financial-asset → 0 with the loan instrument; co-signed/paid-by-other → 0 with 12 months' evidence and no delinquency; business debt in personal name → 0 with 12 months' company checks and 22.3's cash-flow treatment; court-assigned → 0 (contingent) with the order; non-applicant → 0 with documentation that the debt is not the borrower's; open 30-day → 0 but the balance is added to funds to verify (22.4) unless paid off with proof.

**R4 — Remaining months.** `remaining_months = creditor-stated remaining payments` if available, else `ceil((balance_cents) / payment_cents)` as of the scheduled note date; the ≤ 10 exclusion requires `significantly_affects = false` (policy default: false unless the payment exceeds 15% of qualifying income or the borrower's revolving utilization exceeds 80% — Q1).

**R5 — Paydown arithmetic.** To exclude an installment loan by paydown: `target_balance = 10 × payment_cents` (per the creditor's schedule; interest accrual per the creditor's payoff letter governs), `paydown = balance − target_balance`; funds verified in addition (22.4 worksheet adds `Σ payoff_amount` to `funds_to_verify`). Revolving payoff: `payoff = current balance` (statement date); no closure required.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`verification` agent, 22.5 tool set: `buildLiabilitySet`, `matchTradelines`, `selectPaymentBasis`, `computeQualifyingPayment`, `computeDti`, `evaluateExclusion`, `planPayoff`, `evidencePayoff`, `checkDuCap`, `notifyTolerance` (→ 23.1), `finalizeLiabilities`, `writeDecision`. End-to-end behavior: on `credit.report.received` it matches every tradeline to a declared liability, asks the borrower one structured question per unmatched significant debt (Guide-required "reasonable explanation"), selects the payment basis with the cheapest *permitted* evidence path, computes the qualifying payment and DTI in cents, and thereafter recomputes on every upstream change; it proposes payoffs only when the borrower has verified funds beyond closing needs and records the B3-6-07 "carefully evaluated" rationale (credit-use history from trended data). Decision record `{application_id, dti_version, liabilities[] with basis/evidence/exclusion, qualifying_payment inputs, dti_bps, du_cap_ok, tolerance_result (23.1), rule_set_version, formula_version, model_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: never exclude a debt without the Guide-named evidence; never use a bought-down rate, a HELOC imputed payment, or a non-applicant exclusion without documentation; never apply the alimony income-reduction to child support; never "optimize" the payment basis beyond what the Guide permits (the student-loan choice is recorded with both figures); never change income or assets — those belong to 22.3/22.4. Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` for any DTI outcome that drives a counteroffer/denial/NOIA (21.6) and for payoff-to-qualify plans above the Q1 threshold; `fraud-risk` (22.6) when liabilities suggest undisclosed real estate (mortgage tradelines with no REO entry), straw arrangements or altered payoff letters (22.1 integrity); `human_agent` on request. Borrower-facing rules: explanations are requested in plain language with the reason; the partner is named; AI use disclosed. Human path: ops console with the same liability grid, overrides logged as `agent_decisions.override`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Tradeline duplicated across bureaus → deduplicated by creditor/account/opening date; the highest reported payment is used when the bureaus disagree and no statement exists.
- Authorized-user tradelines → not the borrower's debt; excluded as `non_applicant_documented` per DU's treatment (B3-5.3-09; no further investigation on DU casefiles).
- Disputed tradelines → 22.2's DU dispute workflow governs; the payment is included unless DU's re-assessment excludes it and the borrower is not responsible.
- Student loan in forbearance with a documented amortizing payment lower than 1% → the documented amount is permitted; the agent records both.
- HELOC on the subject property being paid off and closed by the refinance → excluded, payoff on the settlement statement (24.4); HELOC remaining with a zero balance and no required payment → 0.
- Credit report shows a mortgage with no matching REO → 22.6 undisclosed-REO review; PITIA of the property is included until resolved.
- Departing residence pending sale where the contract is contingent on financing → both PITIAs count until the contingency is cleared in writing.
- Co-signed debt with 12 months' third-party payments but one 30-day late → not excludable.
- Balance-transfer or new revolving account after the decision → 22.2 alert → recompute; relief for undisclosed non-mortgage debt (A2-2-04) requires closing by the credit expiration date.
- IRS installment agreement plus a Notice of Federal Tax Lien in the subject county → 24.4 lien payoff/subordination; the payment stays in DTI if not paid in full.
- ARM plan not in the DU catalog (Generic ARM) → the agent enters the qualifying rate per B3-6-04 into the ARM Qualifying Rate field; a plan switch after lock → 21.4/21.5 and recompute.
- HPML status changes at CD (APR moves across APOR + 1.5) → 7/10-year ARM qualifying rate flips to the fully indexed rate → recompute → possible 23.1 resubmission.
- Rate lock extension with a higher rate → new P&I → DTI recompute; increase ≥ 3 points or crossing 45% → 23.1.
- Community Seconds payment deferred four years → included (only ≥ 5 years is excludable).
- Closing slips and an excluded ≤ 10-payment debt now has 11 payments left as of the new note date → re-included.
- Non-borrowing spouse's debts in a community-property state → not counted for conventional loans; the platform never pulls the spouse's credit without an application (21.1).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Payoff-to-qualify review threshold.** B3-6-07 requires careful evaluation but no test. Default: agent decides; `underwriting_reviewer` review when the excluded payments exceed 10% of qualifying income or the payoff consumes more than 50% of verified liquid assets after closing.
2. **Alimony/equalization/separate-maintenance income-reduction election.** Default: elect whichever yields the lower DTI, recorded with both computations; DU is submitted consistently with the election.
3. **Student-loan fallback choice.** Default: the lower of 1% of balance and the documented amortizing payment (both permitted); record both.
4. **"Significantly affects" test for ≤ 10-payment debts.** Default: payment > 15% of qualifying income or revolving utilization > 80%.
5. **Property-tax estimate for purchases.** Default: greater of the current tax bill and the estimated post-sale assessment (county millage × price where the county reassesses on sale — `jurisdiction_rules.reassess_on_sale`), aligned with 30.3's escrow analysis.
6. **Non-occupant co-borrower housing expense.** B3-6-02 lists an exception not extracted. Default: include the non-occupant borrower's own housing expense in obligations (conservative) until the topic text is re-read.
