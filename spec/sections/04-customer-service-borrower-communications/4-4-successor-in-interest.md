### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Sources verified:** 12 CFR 1024.30(d), 1024.31 (definitions), 1024.32(c), 1024.36(d)(3) and (i), 1024.38(b)(1)(vi) and comments 38(b)(1)(vi)-1 to -5 (eCFR/CFPB interactive regs, current as of Sept. 3–8, 2026; all from the 2016 amendments, 81 FR 72371); 12 CFR 1026.2(a)(11), (a)(27), 1026.20(f), 1026.41(g) (Reg Z, current as of Sept. 1, 2026); 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d) (Garn-St Germain); Fannie Mae Servicing Guide D1-4.1-02 (08/13/2025) with procedures F-1-17 and F-1-28.

**Definitions (§1024.31).** "Successor in interest" = a person to whom an ownership interest in the property securing the loan is transferred from a borrower, provided the transfer is (1) by devise, descent or operation of law on the death of a joint tenant or tenant by the entirety; (2) to a relative resulting from the death of a borrower; (3) one where the spouse or children of the borrower become an owner; (4) one resulting from a decree of dissolution of marriage, legal separation agreement or incidental property settlement agreement by which the spouse becomes an owner; or (5) into an inter vivos trust in which the borrower is and remains a beneficiary and which does not relate to a transfer of rights of occupancy. "Confirmed successor in interest" = a successor "once a servicer has confirmed the successor in interest's identity and ownership interest in a property." Reg Z §1026.2(a)(27) mirrors both, and §1026.2(a)(11) makes a confirmed successor a "consumer" for §§1026.20(c)–(e) (ARM notices), 1026.36(c) (payment crediting and payoff statements), 1026.39 (mortgage-transfer disclosure) and 1026.41 (periodic statements). The five transfer types track the Garn-St Germain exemptions in 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)(3), (5), (6), (7), (8), under which a lender "may not exercise its option pursuant to a due-on-sale clause."

**Effect of confirmation (§1024.30(d)).** "A confirmed successor in interest shall be considered a borrower for purposes of §1024.17 and this subpart" — i.e., escrow statements, transfer notices (§1024.33), payment/force-placed rules (§§1024.34, .37), NoE/RFI (§§1024.35, .36), early intervention (§1024.39), continuity of contact (§1024.40) and loss mitigation (§1024.41) — regardless of whether the successor has assumed the debt.

**Policies and procedures (§1024.38(b)(1)(vi)).** Upon notification of the death of a borrower or of any transfer of the property, "promptly facilitate communication with any potential or confirmed successors in interest"; upon notification of a potential successor, "promptly determine the documents the servicer reasonably requires to confirm that person's identity and ownership interest in the property and promptly provide to the potential successor in interest a description of those documents and how the person may submit a written request under §1024.36(i)"; upon receipt of such documents, "promptly make a confirmation determination and promptly notify the person" that the servicer has confirmed status, has determined that additional documents are required (and which), or has determined the person is not a successor in interest. Comment 38(b)(1)(vi)-1: notice may arrive "in a variety of ways" and the servicer need not search for successors absent notice. Comment -2: the documents "reasonably require[d]" depend on the laws of the relevant jurisdiction, the person's specific situation and the documents the servicer already has; they may include a death certificate, an executed will, a court order, or documents needed to prevent fraud. Comment -3 examples: joint tenancy/tenancy by the entirety — a recorded deed plus a death certificate is reasonable, probate documentation generally is not; where state law permits affidavits of heirship — the affidavit plus the death certificate; divorce/legal separation — the final decree and executed separation agreement, not a deed; a living spouse or parent transferring by quitclaim deed or act of donation — that deed, not more. Comment -4: if a determination cannot be made on what was provided, the servicer must specify what more is required (e.g., a court determination where ownership is in litigation). Comment -5: notification "is not prompt … if it unreasonably interferes with a successor in interest's ability to apply for loss mitigation options."

**RFI path (§1024.36(i)) and privacy (§1024.36(d)(3))** — as set out in 4.2: the potential successor's written request is an RFI answered with the document description within the §1024.36(d)(2) limits (comment 36(i)-2); supplemental information restarts a new request; the servicer may omit other borrowers'/successors' personal data.

**Optional notice with acknowledgment (§1024.32(c)).** Upon confirmation, the servicer *may* give a confirmed successor who is not liable on the loan a written notice explaining that the servicer has confirmed identity and ownership, that the successor "is not liable for the mortgage debt" (except that the security interest and foreclosure rights remain), that the successor may elect to receive notices if no other confirmed successor or borrower is receiving them, with an acknowledgment form that (i) asks whether they want the notices, (ii) states that receiving them does not create liability (liability arises only by assuming under state law), (iii) says there is no deadline but notices begin only when the form is returned, and (iv) explains the successor's §§1024.35/.36 and 12 CFR 1026.36(c)(3) rights. §1024.32(c)(2): until the acknowledgment is returned (or the successor assumes), the servicer need not send §§1024.17, .33, .34, .37 disclosures or the §1024.39 notices/live contact; Reg Z §§1026.20(f), 1026.39(f), 1026.41(g) do the same for ARM notices, transfer disclosures and periodic statements. §1024.32(c)(3): additional copies on request; (c)(4): no duplicate disclosures if another borrower/successor already receives them.

**Fannie Mae (D1-4.1-02, 08/13/2025; F-1-17 "Processing a Transfer of Ownership"; F-1-28 release-of-liability review).** Exempt transfers (death of a joint tenant/tenant by the entirety; transfer to a relative on the borrower's death; to a spouse/children; divorce/legal separation; inter vivos trust with the borrower as beneficiary; plus junior-lienholder foreclosure, ≤3-year leases, subordinate liens, LLCs controlled by the borrower for loans purchased/securitized on/after June 1, 2016) must be processed "without reviewing or approving the terms of the transfer" and without enforcing due-on-sale; creditworthiness review only if the prior borrower requests a release of liability; an assumption agreement is obtained per F-1-17 for most family/divorce transfers; occupancy conditions were dropped for loans purchased/securitized after June 1, 2016 **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — from the topic summary; confirm exact table text before coding]**; the servicer notifies property insurers, tax authorities, the MI company and other interested parties; MI approval per F-1-17 where MI exists; a delinquent loan transferred to an exempt transferee who cannot reinstate must be evaluated for all workout options, with the assumption agreement executed with the modification (D2-3.x; Section 12); if a due-on-transfer clause is believed unenforceable, notify Fannie Mae Legal (F-4-02) and wait for non-objection or 60 days.

**Other overlays.** CFPB Bulletin 2014-01 (ATR for successors on assumption) was withdrawn May 12, 2025 — the origination-side assumption analysis is left to Reg Z §1026.43 commentary and Fannie Mae's F-1-28 review **[design note; origination out of scope]**. Reg F §1006.6(a) (verified 2026-09-09) treats "the executor or administrator of the consumer's estate, if the consumer is deceased" and "a confirmed successor in interest, as defined in Regulation X, 12 CFR 1024.31, or Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.2(a)(27)(ii)" as the "consumer" for FDCPA communication rules on loans in default at transfer (11.4) — so a confirmed successor may be communicated with as the consumer, and a mere potential successor may not receive debt information beyond §1024.36(i). ECOA/fair lending: successor determinations must not turn on marital status, familial status, age or national origin. The 2024 NPRM would let a servicer honor foreclosure safeguards for a potential successor pre-confirmation and require it upon confirmation (00a §1.2 item 10) — wired behind `rule_sets.regx.lossmit.2024nprm`.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** "Prompt confirmation" is correct but incomplete: there are three separate "promptly" duties (facilitate communication; describe documents; determine and notify) plus the §1024.36(i) 30-day cap and comment -5's loss-mitigation test. The row's source omits §1024.30(d), §1024.31, §1024.32(c) and the Reg Z mirrors, which drive everything the platform must do *after* confirmation.

#### State machine
`opened → identifying_successor → documents_described → awaiting_documents → evaluating → (additional_documents_required → awaiting_documents)* → (confirmed | not_successor | withdrawn)`; post-confirmation sub-process: `confirmed → ack_sent → (ack_returned | ack_declined) → (assumption_in_progress → assumed)?`. Guards: `documents_described` requires a matrix row (or a counsel-reviewed generic list plus the (i)(2) "more information" response); `evaluating → confirmed` requires every required doc `accepted` and identity verification passed; `evaluating → not_successor` requires a written reason mapped to the definition (transfer type not within §1024.31, or no ownership interest) and, by policy, `officer` sign-off; `confirmed` immediately updates `parties.role` and re-routes borrower-facing processes; the `lossmit_pending` flag shortens every policy timer.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Worked timeline.** Daughter calls Thu **2026-10-01** to report her mother's death and asks about the loan (oral → `sii_inquiry`; she is a potential successor under type (2)). Facilitate-communication outreach by 2026-10-05; document-description letter by **2026-10-08** (5 federal BD); if her follow-up letter arrives 2026-10-02 it is an RFI: ack by 2026-10-09 (or the docs letter serves as early response), response no later than **2026-11-13** (30 federal BD from 10-02; Columbus Day and Veterans Day excluded). Documents (death certificate, recorded deed showing mother as sole owner, letters testamentary naming the daughter as executor and the will devising the property to her) received Tue **2026-10-20** → determination and confirmation letter by **2026-11-03** (10 federal BD). Confirmation letter + §1024.32(c) notice/acknowledgment mailed 2026-11-03. Acknowledgment returned 2026-11-20 → periodic statements (7.1), escrow statements (3.3), EI notices (11.2) begin with the next cycle; NoE/RFI/payoff rights applied from 2026-11-03 regardless.
2. **Document determination.** Look up `sii_document_matrix[state][transfer_type][scenario]`; subtract documents already held (comment -2: e.g., the recorded deed from boarding/title records, a death certificate already received from another source); never require probate documents for joint-tenancy/entirety survivorship or a deed for divorce transfers (comment -3); if the scenario is unknown, send the (i)(2) examples response and ask the specific questions needed to pick a scenario (how the deceased held title; whether there is a will/probate; relationship).
3. **Verification.** OCR + structured checks: names match across documents (fuzzy match with human review below 0.9), property legal description/APN matches `properties`, recording stamp present on deeds, death certificate issuing authority/seal detected, dates coherent (death before deed/letters), trust certification lists the borrower as beneficiary and trustee powers. Identity: government ID + matching mailing/contact details; fraud signals (fresh quitclaim to an unrelated party, mismatched notary) → `officer`.
4. **Determination mapping.** Confirmed if (a) the transfer is within one of the five types and (b) ownership interest is evidenced. Not a successor if the transfer is outside the types (e.g., sale to an unrelated buyer — a Garn-St Germain/Fannie Mae assumption question, not an SII) or the person has no ownership interest (e.g., heir before any vesting where state law requires probate — then "additional documents" rather than denial, unless probate has concluded against them).
5. **Post-confirmation setup.** `parties.role=confirmed_successor`; `loan_borrowers` gains a `successor_non_obligor` row (never an obligor unless assumed); credit reporting unaffected (no tradeline for a non-obligor, 8.1); statements/notices route per acknowledgment state; NoE/RFI/payoff (§1026.36(c)(3)) available immediately; loss-mit evaluation available (12.x; Fannie Mae requires the assumption agreement to be executed with any modification, per D1-4.1-02); continuity of contact if delinquent (4.3); escrow analysis statements (§1024.17) after acknowledgment; TCPA/E-SIGN consents collected fresh from the successor.
6. **Garn-St Germain / Fannie Mae assumption.** No due-on-sale enforcement for the five types; assumption agreement offered (required by Fannie Mae for most family/divorce transfers per D1-4.1-02/F-1-17) — the successor may decline; declining does not affect confirmed-successor rights. Release of the original borrower's liability only via the F-1-28 evaluation.
7. **Fees.** No fee for confirmation; any assumption fee only if permitted by the note/state law and Fannie Mae (**[UNVERIFIED — check F-1-17 for allowable assumption fees]**); default none.
8. **Multiple potential successors** (several heirs): each is a separate party; confirm each who evidences an ownership interest; §1024.32(c)(4) avoids duplicate disclosures where one already receives them; `omissions_applied` when answering one about another.
9. **Language:** the successor's preferred language captured at first contact (`parties.preferred_language`); document-description letters exist in English and Spanish (voluntary; §1024.32(a)(3)).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- `case` agent runs identification → matrix lookup → letters → document verification → determination → post-confirmation setup; `borrower-comms` handles the empathetic first contact (scripted: condolences, what happens next, no collection pressure on a non-obligor, disclosure of automation). Decision record: `{case_id, transfer_type, scenario_code, matrix_version, documents_required[], documents_received[], verification_results, determination, reasons, fraud_signals[], omissions_applied[], model_version}`.
- Guardrails: cannot demand documents outside the matrix row; cannot deny for "no assumption" or "not on the note"; cannot disclose account details to a potential successor before confirmation beyond §1024.36(i); cannot treat a confirmed successor as an obligor; must expedite when a loss-mit application is pending (comment -5).
- Human touchpoints: **legally required** — `signing_officer` executes assumption/modification agreements and any recordable instrument; `attorney` for contested title/probate questions or litigation. **Policy** — `officer` approves every `not_successor` determination and any fraud-flagged file; `human_agent` on request. AI-off path: the same checklists in the ops console for `human_agent`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Death of a sole borrower with no successor identified:** facilitate communication with the estate (letters to "Estate of," known contacts); continue statements to the estate address; EI/foreclosure processes proceed per 11.x/13.x with FDCPA/Reg F estate rules (11.4); no successor rights attach until someone is confirmed.
- **Successor who already is a co-borrower/co-owner** (spouse on title and note): no SII case needed — they are a borrower; just update the deceased borrower's status (8.1 "deceased" reporting for the decedent's tradeline; 7.1 statement addressing).
- **Divorce where the transferee spouse is on the note but not on title / vice versa:** on title & not on note → successor (type 4); on note & not on title → already a borrower.
- **Trust transfer:** confirm the trust instrument/certification shows the borrower as beneficiary; the trustee is the successor party of record; occupancy rules per security instrument.
- **Transfer-in/out mid-case:** open SII cases board with documents and timers (1.7/17.3); confirmed-successor status transfers with the loan (§1024.38(b)(4) — the transferor must pass it).
- **Bankruptcy of the decedent's estate or the successor:** counsel involvement; automatic-stay rules (14.x).
- **Foreclosure in progress:** a potential successor's loss-mit application triggers the expedited path; under the current rule, §1024.41 protections attach on confirmation (design: 12.x treats the application as received on the confirmation date, matching NPRM comment 41(f)(2)-7 and the conservative reading); foreclosure-ops is alerted to a pending SII case (13.2).
- **Fraud/elder abuse indicators:** quitclaims from an incapacitated borrower, notarization anomalies → `officer` + attorney; possible SAR-type reporting per 18.5 policies.
- **Conflicting claimants:** confirm each with evidence; if litigation is pending, comment -4 permits requiring a court determination.
- **Address confidentiality (domestic violence):** honor `address_confidential`; omission rules.
- **Vendor feed false positives (death scrub):** never change account status on a scrub alone; open a case to verify.

- **4.4-T3:** Given a divorce decree and separation agreement awarding the home to the non-borrower spouse, then confirmation without requiring a deed (comment -3).
- **4.4-T4:** Given documents that do not establish vesting (will, no letters, probate pending), then `additional_documents_required` with specific items within 5 federal BD, not a denial.
- **4.4-T5:** Given a buyer under an arm's-length sale claiming successor status, then `not_successor` with `officer` approval, the reason letter, and an NoE pathway statement; the D1-4.1-02 assumption path is offered separately if applicable.
- **4.4-T6:** Given confirmation and no returned acknowledgment, then periodic/escrow statements and EI notices to the successor are held while an RFI from the successor is answered on the 4.2 clock and a payoff request is answered within the Reg Z window (16.1).
- **4.4-T7:** Given the acknowledgment is returned, then statements start on the next cycle and the escrow annual statement includes the successor as addressee.
- **4.4-T8:** Given a potential successor submits a loss-mit application, then all SII policy timers halve and `lossmit_reviewer` sees the pending-confirmation flag; upon confirmation the 12.1 application is treated as received on the confirmation date.
- **4.4-T9:** Given a confirmed successor requests the payment history, then the response omits the deceased borrower's SSN and contact data (4.2-T6 linkage).
- **4.4-T10:** Given an assumption is executed, then the `signing_officer` signature record exists, interested parties are notified within 10 servicer BD, MI approval is on file where MI exists, and the loan-data change (if any) is reported per 5.x.
- **4.4-T11:** Given a state with a counsel-unreviewed matrix row, then the (i)(2) examples letter is used and the case is flagged `matrix_gap` for counsel.
- **4.4-T12 (fraud):** Given a quitclaim from a 90-year-old borrower to an unrelated party filed last week, then `officer`/`attorney` escalation and no confirmation.

#### Audit and evidence
Full SII file with document hashes, verification results, matrix version, decision records, notices with delivery evidence, timer history (rule and policy timers labeled), acknowledgment status changes, assumption instruments and signing-officer records, interested-party notification logs, and the audit view `v_sii_log` (notice source, dates of each "promptly" step, determination, elapsed days) for exams and MORA.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **4.4-Q1 Policy timers for "promptly"** (2/5/10 federal BD; halved when loss mit is pending). Default as stated.
2. **4.4-Q2 Send the §1024.32(c) notice/acknowledgment by default?** Default: yes — it gives the successor a clear election and lets the platform hold duplicate disclosures lawfully; alternative: treat every confirmed successor as fully noticed from day one (simpler, more mail).
3. **4.4-Q3 Loss-mit application from a potential successor:** default = hold the §1024.41 clock until confirmation and treat as received on the confirmation date (NPRM-consistent; conservative); alternative: evaluate pre-confirmation informally.
4. **4.4-Q4 Assumption fee** — default none pending F-1-17 verification.
5. **4.4-Q5 Death-scrub vendor and cadence** (monthly SSA DMF vs. bureau indicator only).
