### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Source verified:** 12 CFR 1024.39 on eCFR, "current as of September 4, 2026," amended 90 FR 20792 (May 16, 2025 — COVID rescission), 86 FR 34899 (2021), 82 FR 47957 (2017), 81 FR 72160 (2016), 78 FR 10876 (2013); Supplement I comments 39(a)-1 to -6 (eCFR) and the CFPB eRegs mirror (which still displays pre-rescission COVID clauses in comments 39(a)-3, -4 and -6 — treat eCFR as authoritative); §1024.31 "Delinquency" and comments 31(Delinquency)-1 to -4 (eCFR current Sept. 8, 2026); §1024.30(b) (exemptions) and (c)(2) (principal-residence scope, verified in 4.3); Fannie Mae Servicing Guide (Aug. 12, 2026 edition) D2-2-02 (11/14/2018), D2-2-01 (11/14/2018), A4-2.1-04 (12/16/2015), D2-2-06 (11/14/2018), D2-2-10 (05/10/2023); 47 CFR 64.1200 (eCFR current Sept. 8, 2026); 15 U.S.C. 1692a; 12 CFR 1006.6 and 1006.14 (eCFR current Sept. 4, 2026).

**§1024.39(a) — the duty.** "A servicer shall establish or make good faith efforts to establish live contact with a delinquent borrower no later than the 36th day of a borrower's delinquency and again no later than 36 days after each payment due date so long as the borrower remains delinquent. Promptly after establishing live contact with a borrower, the servicer shall inform the borrower about the availability of loss mitigation options, if appropriate." Scope: closed-end consumer mortgage loans secured by the borrower's principal residence (§1024.30(c)(2)); not applicable to small servicers, reverse mortgages or qualified lenders (§1024.30(b)) — Supermortgage is never a "small servicer" (a subservicer services loans it neither owns nor originated, so it fails the definition regardless of size), and reverse mortgages are out of scope.

**Delinquency and day counting (§1024.31; comments 31(Delinquency)-1 to -4; 39(a)-1).** "Delinquency means a period of time during which a borrower and a borrower's mortgage loan obligation are delinquent," beginning on the date a periodic payment sufficient to cover principal, interest and (if applicable) escrow "becomes due and unpaid," until no periodic payment is due and unpaid. Comment 31(Delinquency)-1: a grace period before a late charge is assessed does not change when delinquency begins. Comment -2 (FIFO): with a $2,000 payment due January 1 unpaid through the 30-day period after January 1, the borrower is "30 days delinquent as of January 31"; if the borrower pays $2,000 on February 3 and the servicer applies it to the January 1 payment, the borrower is "3 days delinquent" the next day — i.e., delinquency is measured from the **earliest unpaid periodic payment's due date** and a payment credited to the oldest installment shortens it. Comment -3 (payment tolerance): if the servicer's own policy treats a borrower who pays less than the full periodic payment as not delinquent, the borrower is not delinquent. Comment -4: contractual acceleration is not restricted and failure to pay the accelerated amount continues delinquency. Comment 39(a)-1: the day-36 deadline for a January 1 due date is **February 6** (due date + 36 days); one good-faith effort can satisfy several missed payments whose 36-day windows overlap (efforts on February 5 satisfy both January 1 and February 1); a borrower performing under a loss-mitigation option that cures a prior delinquency "is not delinquent for purposes of §1024.39"; a timely payment misdirected to the transferor during the §1024.33(c) 60-day window and documented by the transferee does not make the borrower delinquent; and if the missed January 1 payment is made on February 1, no live contact is due by February 6. **Platform day counter:** `regx_days_delinquent = today − earliest_unpaid_due_date` (0 when none), earliest unpaid computed from the `loan_installments` FIFO projection (2.1/2.2, including Reg Z §1026.36(c)(1)(ii) suspense accumulation and the C-1.1-02 "$50 rule" applications, which are the platform's comment-31-3 tolerance); day 36 of a due date D is calendar date D + 36; day 45 is D + 45. No business-day adjustment (the rule says "36th day").

**Live contact (comment 39(a)-2).** "Live contact with a borrower includes speaking on the telephone or conducting an in-person meeting with the borrower but not leaving a recorded phone message." The servicer may rely on borrower-initiated contact and may combine the contact with collection calls or loss-mitigation discussions. Comment 39(a)-5: contact with the borrower's authorized agent counts; reasonable verification of authority is permitted. Comment 39(a)-6: a servicer that "has established and is maintaining ongoing contact with the borrower under the loss mitigation procedures under §1024.41" (including during application completion/evaluation), or that has sent a §1024.41(c)(1)(ii) ineligibility notice, complies with (a) without a separate live-contact effort — but must resume after the borrower cures and becomes delinquent again.

**Good-faith efforts (comment 39(a)-3).** "Good faith efforts to establish live contact consist of reasonable steps, under the circumstances, to reach a borrower and may include telephoning the borrower on more than one occasion or sending written or electronic communication encouraging the borrower to establish live contact." Delinquency length and responsiveness matter: for a borrower two payments behind, "a single telephone call" may be reasonable; for a borrower six or more consecutive payments behind who has not responded, a sentence in the periodic statement requesting contact may suffice (the COVID carve-out formerly in this comment was removed May 2025).

**Inform of options "if appropriate" (comment 39(a)-4).** Servicer discretion: appropriate where the borrower reports a material adverse change likely to cause long-term delinquency; not appropriate where the borrower missed one payment and says it will be made by a date. Oral, written or electronic; "promptly after" live contact; generic ("loss mitigation options may be available") suffices; the (b)(1) notice satisfies it if timely.

**Exemptions (verified in 11.2/11.4 detail).** §1024.39(c)(1)(i): while any borrower on the loan is a debtor in a title 11 case, no live contact is required (comment 39(c)(1)(iii)-1 — joint obligors: bankruptcy of any borrower); §1024.39(c)(2)(ii): after a discharge under 11 U.S.C. 727, 1141, 1228 or 1328 the servicer is *never* required to resume (a) (it must resume (b) if the borrower makes any partial or periodic payment after the case began); §1024.39(d)(1): a servicer "subject to the FDCPA" with respect to the loan is exempt from (a) once the borrower sends an FDCPA §805(c) cease-communication notice (Reg F §1006.6(c)); (a) also ends when delinquency ends (payoff, current, title transfer).

**Fannie Mae overlay (all loans, not only principal residences).** D2-2-02 (11/14/2018): outbound contact attempts to achieve QRPC "must begin … no later than the 36th day of delinquency" and "continue every 7 days until one of the following outcomes is attained": QRPC achieved and/or the borrower is adhering to a workout, the delinquency is resolved, a complete BRP is received, the borrower promises to pay the delinquent amount "by a specified date" (no more than 30 days out), or QRPC is achieved and the borrower states no interest in a workout; "unless required by applicable law to continue," attempts must stop **60 days before a judicial foreclosure sale date or 30 days before a non-judicial sale date**; beyond day 210 the servicer "is authorized to continue" under the same rules; attempts before day 36 using the servicer's own behavioral models are authorized; every conversation must "emphasize the importance of making payments on or prior to their due dates." A4-2.1-04 (12/16/2015): outreach "by mail, email, texting, and voice response unit technology" is authorized "as permitted by applicable law"; the servicer must vary days and times "and include some evening and weekend calls," document "all contact attempts in the mortgage loan servicing file," meet the minimum attempt counts in the Guide, move attempts to "the next day the servicer is open" when the scheduled day is a closed day, keep foreclosure-prevention staff available during collection activity, and hit call-center metrics (ASA ≤60 s, blockage ≤1 %, abandonment ≤5 %, chat ≤5 min, email ≤48 h — owned by 4.3). D2-2-06: breach letter by day 75 (13.x). D2-2-10: monthly inspections from day 90 may be suspended when "QRPC has been established within the last 30 days" (9.x/13.x). D2-1-01: no workout *solicitation* of borrowers current or <30 days delinquent (11.5). No Fannie Mae requirement to record calls was found in A4-2.1-04, D2-2-01 or D2-2-02; recording is a policy choice governed by state consent law.

**TCPA (47 U.S.C. 227(b)(1)(A)(iii); 47 CFR 64.1200).** (a)(1)(iii): no call to a cellular number "using any automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice" without "the prior express consent of the called party" (except emergencies and the (a)(9) exemptions — package delivery, financial-institution fraud/breach alerts, healthcare — none of which covers servicing/collection calls). Non-telemarketing informational calls need "prior express consent" (any form); telemarketing needs "prior express written consent" ((a)(2)). FCC Declaratory Ruling FCC 24-17 (Feb. 8, 2024; CG Docket No. 23-362 **[docket number PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**) confirms AI-generated voices are "artificial or prerecorded voice," so **every outbound AI-voice call is a (a)(1)(iii)/(a)(3) call**; a human-dialed call from a system that lacks random/sequential number generation (Facebook v. Duguid, 141 S. Ct. 1163 (2021)) and uses no artificial voice is outside 227(b). (a)(3): artificial/prerecorded calls to **residential landlines** require prior express *written* consent unless exempt; the (a)(3)(iii) exemption for commercial non-telemarketing calls is capped at "no more than three calls within any consecutive 30-day period" and requires the (b) opt-out mechanics. (a)(10)–(12) (2024 revocation order, effective April 11, 2025, with the "applies to all robocalls/texts" scope element effective April 11, 2026 **[effective-date history PARTIALLY VERIFIED — eCFR text verified]**): consumers may revoke "using any reasonable method"; "stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel, or unsubscribe" by text are per-se revocations; revocation must be honored "within ten business days"; one confirmation text within five minutes is allowed. (b): artificial-voice messages must state the caller's identity "at the beginning of the message" and give a callback number. (c): the 8 a.m.–9 p.m. rule applies only to "telephone solicitations" — servicing calls take their time limits from Reg F (debt-collector loans), state law and Fannie Mae policy. Providing a cell number to the creditor in connection with the loan is prior express consent for debt-related calls (FCC ACA Declaratory Ruling, 23 FCC Rcd 559 (2008)) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — ruling not re-fetched]**; numbers obtained by skip-tracing carry no consent. State mini-TCPAs (Florida FTSA, Oklahoma TSA, Washington CEMA — "telephonic sales calls"; servicing calls are outside their core scope but text-consent provisions are broader) and two-party call-recording states (CA Penal Code §§632/632.7, FL, IL, MD, MA, MT, NH, PA, WA and others) **[UNVERIFIED cites — load via `jurisdiction_rules.call_recording` after counsel review]**. State AI-disclosure regimes: 00a §5.6 (Colorado, Utah, California baseline; Oregon/Washington 2027; Nebraska/Idaho 2027).

**Reg F frequency/time overlay for debt-collector loans (11.4).** §1006.14(b): presumption of violation if more than seven calls in seven consecutive days per person per debt, or any call within seven days after a telephone conversation (day of conversation = day 1); excluded: calls with the person's prior consent given directly and within seven days, calls "not connected to the dialed number," calls to the professionals in §1006.6(d)(1)(ii)–(vi). §1006.6(b)(1): communications (any medium, including the send time of texts/emails) before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. "local time at the consumer's location" are presumptively inconvenient absent knowledge to the contrary.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) The row omits Fannie Mae's cadence (start by day 36, every 7 days, cessation triggers, 60/30-day pre-sale stop), the principal-residence scope of §1024.39, the bankruptcy/FDCPA/discharge exemptions and the §1024.41 ongoing-contact safe harbor — all hard rules below. (2) "Again ≤36 days after each due date" is correct but the row does not say the counter is §1024.31 FIFO delinquency — the rolling one-month-behind borrower never trips it. (3) "Contact log" = `contacts` + `good_faith_effort_records` (Fannie Mae requires evidence "upon request"). (4) Automation class "c (live contact)" is re-rated: AI-first with the `live_contact.ai_voice_counts` flag and a human fallback (analysis in the AI-agent subsection). (5) TCPA consent gating and Reg F counters are prerequisites the row does not mention.

#### State machine
Per `regx_ei_windows` row (live-contact leg): `open → satisfied_live | satisfied_good_faith | satisfied_ongoing_lossmit | cancelled_paid | exempt_* → (terminal)`. Guards: `satisfied_live` requires a `contacts` row with `live_contact=true` dated within (due_date, live_due_at]; `satisfied_good_faith` requires a `good_faith_effort_records` row whose attempts fall inside the window and whose rationale passes the policy minimum (rule 6); `satisfied_ongoing_lossmit` requires an active `lossmit.ongoing_contact` state on the loan (12.x) at any time in the window; `cancelled_paid` when `loan_installments[due_date].paid_in_full_at ≤ live_due_at`; exemptions set by overlay events. A window past `live_due_at` with status `open` becomes `breached` (timer breach) — it is never deleted.

Per loan (Fannie Mae cadence, `contact_attempt_plans`): `not_started → active (cycles every 7 days) → ceased{reason} → active (resume trigger: broken promise, workout failure, BRP incomplete, new delinquency, sale postponed)`; `suspended` for bankruptcy/cease/attorney/pre-sale; terminal `closed` on cure, payoff, title transfer, transfer-out.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Window generation.** For each periodic due date D with `loan_installments[D].status ≠ paid` at 00:05 on D+1 (loan-local), create `regx_ei_windows(D)` with `live_due_at = D+36`, `notice_due_at = D+45`, `principal_residence = properties.occupancy ∈ {principal_residence, unknown}`. When `payment.applied` marks installment D paid with `credited_as_of ≤ live_due_at`, set `live_status = cancelled_paid` (comment 39(a)-1: paying the missed payment before day 36 removes the duty); if paid after `live_due_at`, the window's obligation stood and its outcome is preserved. Overlapping windows: any qualifying contact/effort dated inside (D, D+36] satisfies every open window containing that date (comment 39(a)-1 multi-payment example).
2. **Counter.** `regx_days_delinquent(t) = t − min{D : installment D unpaid}`; installments are marked paid FIFO by `payment.applied` (2.1), including suspense accumulation (`REGZ_1026_36C_APPLY_ON_ACCUMULATION_1BD`, 2.2) and `applied_with_50_rule` shortfall applications (comment 31(Delinquency)-3 tolerance). Late charges and fees never make a borrower delinquent (definition covers P&I and escrow only). Transfer-in: seeded from the transferor's payment history (1.1); a timely payment misdirected to the transferor within 60 days of transfer, once documented (`payment.received{received_by=transferor}`), is credited as of the transferor's receipt date (comment 39(a)-1).
3. **Worked example (Nov 1 due, FIFO partials).** Loan-local TZ America/Chicago; P = $2,000.00 ($1,650.00 P&I + $350.00 escrow); due 2026-11-01 (Sunday) unpaid. Day 1 = 2026-11-02; **day 36 = 2026-12-07 (Mon)** → `REGX_1024_39A_LIVE_CONTACT_36` due 2026-12-07 23:59 CT; **day 45 = 2026-12-16 (Wed)** (11.2). 2026-11-20: $1,200.00 received → suspense (partial; `a + U = $1,200 < P`). 2026-12-01: second installment due unpaid → second window: live by **2027-01-06**, notice by **2027-01-15**. 2026-12-10: $1,200.00 received → suspense $2,400.00 ≥ P → $2,000.00 applied FIFO to the 2026-11-01 installment, `credited_as_of 2026-12-10`, $400.00 remains unapplied. Effects: window(Nov 1).live — the duty stood (payment came after Dec 7): satisfied only if a live contact/good-faith record exists by Dec 7; window(Nov 1).notice (11.2) — `cancelled_paid` because the installment was paid before Dec 16; `regx_days_delinquent` on 2026-12-11 = 2026-12-11 − 2026-12-01 = **10**; `fnma_delinquency_status` at 2026-12-31 = 30 days (LPI 11/01, Dec 1 unpaid) — 5.7 reports 30, not 60. If instead the $1,200 had arrived on 2026-12-05, application on Dec 5 would have cancelled both legs of the Nov 1 window (paid ≤ Dec 7) and only the Dec 1 window would remain.
4. **Rolling delinquency.** A borrower who pays each installment exactly on the next due date (Jan 1 paid Feb 1, Feb 1 paid Mar 1 …) has every window cancelled on day 28–31, so no §1024.39 live-contact or notice duty ever matures — but the Fannie Mae cadence still runs (D2-2-02 counts "days of delinquency" the same way, and the payment reminder issues by the 20th). Design keeps both engines independent so this outcome is explicit in the audit trail (`cancel_reason = paid_before_36`).
5. **Cadence table (policy layered on the rules; all loans; `regx_days_delinquent` scale).** Day 1–15 (grace): no collection calls; courtesy SMS/email reminder on day 8 if consented (policy); statement/coupon unaffected. Day 16–20: late charge assessed (2.x); D2-2-03 payment reminder by the 20th (7.1). **Day 17: first outbound attempt** (AI voice if consent; else human manual dial; plus SMS/email if consented) — well ahead of both day-36 deadlines; further attempts on a 3–4-day rhythm with rotating dayparts (morning/afternoon/evening/weekend). Day 28 (warning): channel escalation — second number, co-borrower, authorized agent, "please contact us" letter (`NTC_SM_CONTACT_REQUEST_LETTER`, counts toward good-faith efforts). Day 36: live contact or good-faith record must exist; Fannie Mae attempts continue every ≤7 days. Day 45: EI notice + Form 745/BSP if no QRPC (11.2); §1024.40 assignment (4.3). Day 46–75: every ≤7 days; breach letter by day 75 (13.x). Day 76–120: every ≤7 days; day 90 PFPIP submission and inspections (9.x/13.x); pre-referral review (13.4); referral eligible after day 120 (13.1/13.3). Post-referral to sale: every ≤7 days until 60/30 days pre-sale, unless a cessation trigger. Cadence resumes on `borrower.promise_to_pay.broken`, workout failure, incomplete BRP after cure-of-window, or sale postponement.
6. **Good-faith determination (comment 39(a)-3).** At `live_due_at − 1 day` with no live contact, `default-collections` must record a `good_faith_effort_records` row. Policy minimum: ≥2 telephone attempts on different days/dayparts to every known good number **and** ≥1 written or electronic "please contact us" communication inside the window; for loans ≥6 consecutive payments behind and non-responsive for ≥90 days, the periodic-statement contact sentence (7.1 template flag `ei_contact_sentence=true`) plus one call attempt is the minimum. The record cites each attempt id and the reasonableness rationale. Fewer attempts → the window breaches (never self-certified).
7. **Channel selection matrix (per number/address; evaluated at dial time).** Mobile/VoIP/unknown: AI voice only with unrevoked `tcpa_voice`; SMS only with `tcpa_sms` (and for DC loans the Reg F §1006.6(d)(5) conditions — consumer texted from that number within 60 days or consent + reassigned-number check ≤60 days old); otherwise human manual dial (no ATDS, no artificial voice). Landline: AI voice ≤3 per 30 days without written consent (`tcpa_voice_written`), unlimited with it (subject to Reg F/state caps); human dial unrestricted. Email: only with an email address the borrower gave (Reg F (d)(4) conditions for DC loans) and with the opt-out line. Mail: always available. Numbers from skip-tracing: human dial only until the borrower confirms the number (which creates consent for informational calls, logged). `contact_preferences.human_only=true` → no AI voice.
8. **Quiet hours / location.** Candidate time zones = {property address, mailing address, phone area code}; permitted window = intersection of 08:00–21:00 in each candidate (Reg F comment-style "when in doubt satisfy both"); policy narrows voice to 08:00–20:30 and SMS/email to 08:00–20:00; borrower-stated inconvenient windows override; workplace numbers blocked if `employer_prohibits`; Sunday calls allowed after 09:00 local (policy). Fannie Mae's evening/weekend requirement is met inside these bounds.
9. **Frequency counters.** For DC loans: `calls_7d(person, loan)` counts every outbound voice attempt that rang (answered or not, including voicemail) to any number of that person about this loan, by AI or human; excludes `busy_or_failed` (not connected), consent-based callbacks within 7 days of the consent, and calls to counsel; the 8th is refused. After any `conversation`/`qrpc`, `POST_CONVERSATION_7` blocks calls through day 7 unless the borrower asked for a callback (logged consent with date). Non-DC loans: policy cap 5 counted calls per 7 days per person (headroom), same cooling-off (UDAAP hygiene), and the strictest `state_call_frequency` cap. All counters are per person: co-borrowers are separate counts; the same call to a shared line counts for the person reached/intended.
10. **Reg F 7-in-7 worked scenario (DC loan, borrower B, co-borrower C).** Mon 2026-12-07 09:05 AI call to B's mobile — no answer (count B=1). Tue 12-08 18:40 AI call to B's mobile — voicemail, limited-content message (B=2). Wed 12-09 12:10 call to B's landline — busy/failed (excluded, B=2). Wed 12-09 12:15 AI call to C's mobile — no answer (C=1). Thu 12-10 08:30 human call to B's landline — no answer (B=3). Fri 12-11 18:05 AI call to B's mobile — **conversation**, QRPC achieved, promise to pay $4,000.00 by 2026-12-28 (B=4; conversation day 1 = 12-11 → no calls to B through 12-17; Fannie Mae cadence ceases on the ≤30-day promise). B says "call me Tuesday the 15th at 6 pm to confirm" → consent-based call on 12-15 is permitted and excluded from the count. 2026-12-29 00:05: promise not covered by `payment.applied` → `promise_to_pay.broken` → cadence resumes 12-29 (post-conversation window long expired; 7-day count restarts from the trailing window: B=0).
11. **Pre-sale cessation.** `foreclosure.sale.scheduled` sets `stop_outbound_at = sale_date − 60 (judicial) / − 30 (non-judicial)`; plan → `suspended{pre_sale_stop}`; inbound contact is always answered; if the sale is postponed (`foreclosure.sale.continued`) the plan resumes until the new stop date. If `jurisdiction_rules.contact_required_through_sale`, the gate is bypassed and the reason is logged.
12. **Holiday shift.** `FNMA_D2202_*` due dates falling on a `servicer` calendar closed day move to the next open day; the Reg X day-36 deadline never moves (a closed day-36 means the effort must be made earlier — the planner front-loads).
13. **Money in conversation.** Amounts quoted to the borrower (past-due total, late charges, reinstatement) are read from the ledger at call time in `bigint` cents and rendered to dollars with round-half-up; the AI never computes balances itself. Example: past due 2 × $2,000.00 + late charges 2 × $82.50 ($1,650.00 × 5 %, 2.x) = **$4,165.00** stated as of the call date with the "as of" caveat.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

**Legal analysis — can an AI voice agent establish "live contact"?** *For:* (1) comment 39(a)-2 defines live contact by the *mode* of interaction — "speaking on the telephone or conducting an in-person meeting … but not leaving a recorded phone message" — the contrast drawn is synchronous two-way conversation versus a one-way message, not human versus machine; a real-time conversational agent that listens, answers, verifies identity and captures the borrower's statements is "speaking on the telephone" in the ordinary sense; (2) the regulation's purpose (2013 preamble: ensure delinquent borrowers are told that help exists and reached early) is served by any conversation in which the borrower is informed and engaged; (3) §1024.39 is channel-neutral and the 2024 NPRM did not propose to change that; (4) comment 39(a)-3 lets *written* and *electronic* communications count as good-faith efforts, so the rule tolerates non-human means at the effort level; (5) Fannie Mae A4-2.1-04 expressly authorizes "voice response unit technology" for contact attempts "as permitted by applicable law." *Against:* (1) "speaking … with the borrower" in 2013 presupposed a person; a regulator or court could read "live" as "a live person," and no CFPB guidance addresses it (00a §1.4); (2) the CFPB's June 2023 chatbot spotlight criticizes systems that recognize disputes only on "specific words or syntax," create "doom loops," give inaccurate information and hinder access to humans — an AI live contact that failed on those dimensions would be an attractive enforcement or RESPA §6(f) target; (3) TCPA: outbound AI voice is an artificial voice — no consent, no call; (4) state AI-disclosure laws and the Colorado AI Act raise the bar on transparency and human review; (5) Fannie Mae QRPC (D2-2-01) requires a substantive two-way discussion whose *quality* the master servicer must evidence "upon request." *Assessment:* a synchronous, disclosed, two-way AI conversation with human handoff is defensible under the text of comment 39(a)-2 and clearly superior to the "recorded message" the comment excludes; the residual risk is interpretive, not textual, and is mitigated by (a) design features that make the AI conversation at least as good as a human one on the dimensions regulators care about, (b) a human-verified fallback, and (c) the ability to flip a flag and route every live-contact obligation to humans without re-architecture.

**Design that maximizes defensibility (the `live_contact.ai_voice_counts` design).**
1. **Disclosure at the start** of every AI voice/chat interaction: "I'm an automated assistant for Supermortgage…" plus recording disclosure, plus (DC loans) the §1006.18(e) statement; state overlays from `jurisdiction_rules.ai_disclosure_script`. Logged with timestamps (`ai_disclosure_at`, `recording_disclosure_at`, `fdcpa_disclosure_at`).
2. **Synchronous two-way conversation**: barge-in enabled; the agent must obtain at least one verified borrower utterance responsive to a question before `live_contact=true` is set; a one-way script with no borrower response is logged `answered_unverified` (not live contact).
3. **Identity verification before any account detail** (two factors from name/property address/loan number/last-4 SSN/DOB; no voiceprints — Illinois BIPA); for DC loans, no debt disclosure to an unverified answerer (§1006.6(d)(1)) — the agent uses limited-content-message wording.
4. **Human handoff on request or trigger**: "representative," "person," "human," "supervisor," distress/abuse cues, a stated dispute, bankruptcy or attorney mention, language not supported, two consecutive comprehension failures → warm transfer to `human_agent` (queue ASA target ≤60 s; if unavailable, scheduled callback with `REGX_1024_40A3_LIVE_RESPONSE_1BD`); the transfer preserves `live_contact` status if the human completes the conversation.
5. **QRPC element capture** (11.3 schema) and the "if appropriate" loss-mitigation information (comment 39(a)-4) delivered from `lossmit_facts` (4.3), never from model memory.
6. **Logging**: full recording, transcript, structured decision record, disclosures with timestamps, `live_contact_basis=ai_voice_flag`, counters and consent ids at dial time.
7. **Flag semantics**: `live_contact.ai_voice_counts` (global default **true** after the sign-off gate; `jurisdiction_overrides` per state; `partner_override`). When **true**, an AI conversation meeting items 1–6 is recorded `live_contact=true` and satisfies `REGX_1024_39A_LIVE_CONTACT_36` and D2-2-01 QRPC (when the elements are captured). When **false** for the loan, the AI still places calls and conducts the dialog (it is at minimum a good-faith effort and a Fannie Mae contact attempt), but `live_contact=false`; the engine opens `SM_LIVE_CONTACT_HUMAN_FALLBACK_5CD` and either (a) a `human_agent` joins the same call for a **human-verified QRPC** (30–60 seconds: confirms identity, restates the hardship summary and commitment, asks if anything is inaccurate; logged `mode=human_voice`, `live_contact=true`), or (b) a human callback is completed before the window closes. In either mode a human reviews a sample (≥10 %, 100 % for the first 90 days) of AI QRPC transcripts within 1 BD (`qrpc_verified_by`).
8. **Go-live gate**: the flag may be set to `true` in production only after a written legal opinion and the partner's sign-off are attached (`documents`) to the flag-change record; the Compliance Sentinel reports the flag state per state daily.
9. **Metrics** (dashboards; LL-2026-04 monitoring): live-contact-by-day-36 rate (target ≥98 % of windows with `satisfied_live` or `satisfied_good_faith`, 0 breaches); QRPC rate by day 36/60/90; AI-conversation-to-QRPC conversion; human-request rate and transfer completion time; disclosure compliance (100 % by automated transcript check); prohibited-statement rate (0); quiet-hour and counter refusals (0 violations); revocation-to-honor latency (≤1 minute system, ≤10 BD legal); complaint rate per 1,000 contacts vs human baseline; promise-kept rate; borrower satisfaction sample.

**Agents and tools.** `default-collections`: `windows.list`, `plan.build`, `channel.select` (consent/line-type/quiet-hours/counters), `dial.request`, `sms.send`, `email.send`, `letter.request`, `good_faith.determine`, `timer.satisfy`, `decision.record`, `escalate`. `borrower-comms` (collections mode): `identity.verify`, `disclosure.play`, `lossmit_facts.get`, `ledger.balances`, `payments.history`, `qrpc.capture`, `promise.record`, `payment.take` (2.x), `callback.schedule`, `human.transfer`, `preference.set`, `consent.record/revoke`, `dispute.intake` (4.1), `contact.log`. Decision record (per call): `{contact_id, loan_id, plan_id, rule_set_versions{regx_ei, regf, tcpa, fnma_d2, contact_policy}, model_version, prompt_version, pre_dial_checks{consent_id, line_type, tz_window, regf_count_before, landline_ai_30d, cease_flags, bk_flag, attorney_flag, scra_flag, pre_sale_gate}, disclosures{ai_at, recording_at, fdcpa_at}, identity{method, verified_at}, live_contact{value, basis}, qrpc{...11.3}, options_informed{text, source_version}, commitments[], statements_made[], escalations[], confidence, outcome}`. Guardrails: no dial without every pre-dial check passing; no balance not read from the ledger; no option, deadline or foreclosure statement not present in `lossmit_facts`; no threat of action not actually intended/lawful (§1006.18(c)); no negotiation of modification terms — only intake and referral (licensing rule); no more than one voicemail per number per 7 days (policy); terminate and mark `human_only` on borrower request. Escalations: `human_agent` (request/trigger), `licensed_specialist` (state-keyed), `officer` (timer breach), `attorney` (borrower represented by counsel in litigation), `fnma_portal_operator` (none in 11.1). Human path when AI is off: the dialer assigns the plan's attempts to the human queue with the same scripts and disposition codes; timers and counters are unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in with delinquency**: windows seeded from the transferor's unpaid due dates; if a window's `live_due_at` is already past at boarding, the window is created `breached_at_boarding` (evidence: transferor contact history, 1.7) and a human call task is opened for day 1–3; new windows run normally.
- **Transfer-out mid-window**: window closed `transfer_out`; the transfer file carries `contacts`, promises and consents (17.x).
- **Bankruptcy**: all §1024.39(a) windows `exempt_bk` from petition date; cadence `suspended{bankruptcy}`; communications through counsel per 14.x; after dismissal/closure/reaffirmation, windows resume from the next due date (`REGX_1024_39A_RESUME_AFTER_BK_NEXT_DUE`); after discharge, live contact never resumes (notice may — 11.2).
- **FDCPA cease (DC loans)**: (a) exempt; cadence suspended; inbound still answered; 11.2 modified notice.
- **Oral "stop calling"** on any loan: TCPA revocation (voice/SMS) honored at once; policy treats it as a call cease for human calls too (UDAAP), mail continues; the Reg X duty is satisfied by good-faith written efforts thereafter (comment 39(a)-3) and logged as such.
- **Attorney representation**: DC loans — no direct contact if counsel is known (§1006.6(b)(2)); all loans — policy routes to counsel; live contact through the borrower's representative counts (comment 39(a)-5).
- **Deceased borrower / successor in interest**: contact the estate/potential successor (4.4); Reg F treats the executor/administrator and confirmed successor as "consumer"; no debt discussion with unverified relatives (DC loans).
- **SCRA active duty**: cadence continues but scripts add the military-indulgence path (D2-3.4-01); no threats of foreclosure inconsistent with SCRA.
- **Disaster declaration** (FEMA IA area): forbearance offers per LL-2026-01 via QRPC; outbound cadence continues; reason "Disaster Impact."
- **Wrong number / reassigned number**: mark `bad_number_count`, stop AI voice to it, human verification; SMS to a number reassigned = TCPA exposure → RND check before reuse.
- **Telephony outage**: human-dialer mode on the failover trunk; inbound line failover to the staffed number (4.3); timers unchanged.
- **Voice-model failure mid-call** (latency > 3 s, comprehension loop): auto-transfer to human queue; disposition `human_transferred`; counts as a call attempt.
- **Borrower performing under loss mitigation**: not delinquent for §1024.39 (comment 39(a)-1); windows `not_applicable` while `lossmit.plan.active` and payments are timely; new delinquency after a failed plan reopens windows.
- **Non-principal-residence**: Reg X windows `not_applicable`; Fannie Mae cadence and Reg F counters still apply.
- **Partial data** (no phone at all): written good-faith efforts only (letters, statement sentence, email if consented); skip-trace after returned mail; recorded in the good-faith record.
- **Retro-corrections**: a `payment.reversed` (NSF) re-opens the affected installment and its window from the original due date; a window cancelled `paid` is reinstated with a note; no duplicate notices if one was sent (11.2).

#### Audit and evidence
`regx_ei_windows` full history (created, satisfied/cancelled/exempted with event ids); `contacts` with recordings/transcripts hashes, disclosure timestamps, counters and consent ids at dial time, local-time checks; `good_faith_effort_records` with rationale; `contact_attempt_plans` cycles and cessation reasons; `consents` ledger with evidence documents and revocation latency; `agent_decisions` per call with rule-set/model/prompt versions; flag-change records for `live_contact.ai_voice_counts` with the legal opinion and partner sign-off documents; daily Compliance Sentinel report (windows due in 7 days, breaches, counter refusals, revocations honored); monthly Fannie Mae contact evidence pack per loan (A4-2.1-04 "upon request"): attempts by day of delinquency, channels, outcomes, QRPC date. Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y`; TCPA consent evidence `tcpa_consent_4y` minimum (extend to life of loan + 4y by policy).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **11.1-Q1 AI voice as live contact.** Default: `live_contact.ai_voice_counts=true` in production only after the legal opinion + partner sign-off; until then `false` with the human-verified join (item 7(a)) as the standard path so AI conversations still capture QRPC and human time is ~1 minute per call.
2. **11.1-Q2 First-attempt day.** Default day 17 (post-grace, post-late-charge); partner may set 16–25.
3. **11.1-Q3 Voicemail policy.** Default: DC loans — Reg F limited-content message once per number per 7 days; non-DC — identified message with callback number (§64.1200(b)) once per 7 days; never on a number flagged shared/third-party.
4. **11.1-Q4 Call recording.** Default: record all calls with an announced disclosure in every state (simplest two-party compliance); Illinois BIPA — no voice biometrics.
5. **11.1-Q5 Non-DC call caps.** Default policy 5 counted calls per 7 days per person and a 7-day post-conversation cooling-off — stricter than law, protects against UDAAP/harassment claims.
6. **11.1-Q6 Skip-tracing.** Default on after two bad numbers and one returned mail piece; human-dial only for traced numbers.
7. **11.1-Q7 Sunday/holiday calling.** Default: Sundays 09:00–20:30 local; no calls on Christmas/Thanksgiving/New Year's Day; state-specific restrictions loaded by counsel.
