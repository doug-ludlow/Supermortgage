### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Federal.** 12 CFR 1026.36(c)(3) (eCFR current as of 9/04/2026): "a creditor, assignee or servicer, as applicable, must provide an accurate statement of the total outstanding balance that would be required to pay the consumer's obligation in full as of a specified date. The statement shall be sent within a reasonable time, but in no case more than seven business days, after receiving a written request from the consumer or any person acting on behalf of the consumer," with the reasonable-time exception for bankruptcy, foreclosure, reverse/shared-appreciation loans, "natural disasters or other similar circumstances." Statutory basis: 15 U.S.C. §1639g (Pub. L. 111-203 §1464(b)): "A creditor or servicer of a home loan shall send an accurate payoff balance within a reasonable time, but in no case more than 7 business days, after the receipt of a written request for such balance from or on behalf of the borrower." Commentary 36(c)(3)-1..3 (agents; reasonable request requirements; "accurate when issued"), the §1026.2(a)(6) business-day definition (calendar `servicer`) and the §1024.35(b)(6) error linkage are as verified in 7.6 and are not restated. §1024.34(b)(1) (eCFR current 9/03/2026): "Within 20 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays) of a borrower's payment of a mortgage loan in full, a servicer shall return to the borrower any amounts remaining in an escrow account" (3.5 owns the refund; the statement must describe the treatment).

**Fannie Mae (Guide Aug. 12, 2026).** *C-1.2-03, Processing Payments in Full (11/12/2014):* "The servicer must provide payoff statements to the borrower or their agent, when requested"; "must also collect and remit sufficient funds to Fannie Mae to satisfy the debt"; "must apply any funds remaining in an interest rate buydown plan account after payoff in accordance with the applicable mortgage loan agreement" and "if the servicer is holding the remaining buydown funds, it must include them with its remittance of the payoff proceeds"; a first-lien payoff statement must reference any HomeSaver Advance (HSA) note and, on sale/transfer, "remind the borrower that the HSA note is due and payable in full," on refinance that it "must continue to be paid" and that its payoff "is not required to release the first lien." *F-1-09 (10/19/2016), Calculating Interest on a Payoff:* interest is "based on the UPB of the mortgage loan, as of the LPI date, and using the current interest accrual rate"; "A full month's interest should be calculated on the basis of a 360-day year, while a partial month's interest should be based on a 365-day year"; for conventional first and second liens interest runs "up to, but not including, the day the payoff funds were received" (the through-end-of-month rule applies only to certain FHA and HUD §184 loans); "When the installment due date falls on a non-business day, the receipt of the payoff funds shall be considered received on the installment due date provided they are received on the next business day." *Prepayment premiums:* "the servicer cannot impose or collect a prepayment premium on most mortgage loans" — only under a negotiated contract meeting Selling Guide B8-3-02 and disclosed on the LAR (never for Supermortgage's conforming book). *Buydown funds:* "Reduce the payoff amount by the amount of any remaining buydown funds" and "not subtract the buydown funds from the mortgage loan balance because an incorrect interest calculation would result." *Advances:* "Collect any funds advanced on behalf of the borrower"; Fannie Mae-advanced funds are repaid by special remittance within 30 days (16.2). *A2-3-05:* payoff-statement fees only where law expressly permits, reasonable, never to Fannie Mae (7.6). *C-1.2-05 (05/15/2019):* a release fee may be added only if paid to a third party (recorder, notary, trustee), not prohibited by law/instrument/insurer, consistent with insurer-permitted amounts, and unrelated to a charge-off (16.3). *A4-2.1-07 (10/11/2023):* for loans with a non-interest-bearing (NIB) balance the servicer must "attempt to contact" the borrower and send the *Borrower Notification of Non-Interest-Bearing Balance* "as early as 180 days but no later than 150 days prior to the maturity date or the projected date of payoff," and if no contact, "as early as 75 days but no later than 60 days before the maturity date"; an unaffordable balloon goes to SF CPM. *D2-3.2-04 / F-1-27 (00a §3.8–3.9):* deferred and forborne principal are non-interest-bearing and "due at maturity/sale/refinance/payoff" — every payoff figure includes them.

**State payoff-statement overlays (verified today unless marked).** *California Civ. Code §2943:* 21 days; $30 fee cap; $300 forfeiture; reliance through close of escrow (7.6). *Florida §701.04(1):* "Within 10 days after receipt of the written request of a mortgagor" an estoppel letter itemizing principal, interest and other charges plus "interest accruing on a per-day basis"; the servicer "may not qualify, reserve the right to change, or condition or disclaim the reliance of others"; a corrected letter "supersedes all prior estoppel letters" only if received by 3 p.m. at least one business day before payment; prevailing-party attorney fees. *New York RPL §274-a:* on a bona fide written demand by an "authorized individual" (mortgagor, mortgagor's attorney, or the attorney for a lending bank) the mortgagee must deliver the payoff statement "within thirty days"; no charge for the first, "not more than twenty dollars" for each subsequent statement; the statement must show "the balance of the mortgage, including principal, interest and other charges" and "a per diem rate for interest accruing after the date to which the balance has been calculated"; liability for actual damages. *Texas Fin. Code §343.106:* title-insurance-company requests on the Finance Commission's standard form must be answered "within a time specified by finance commission rule, which must allow the mortgage servicer at least seven business days after the date the request is received"; the form states the proposed closing date and "a payoff amount that is valid through that date," and the servicer "may not, on or before the proposed closing date, demand that a mortgagor pay an amount in excess of the payoff amount specified" — the implementing rule 7 TAC §155.2 (7-business-day delivery, Texas Payoff Statement Form) could not be fetched **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. *Connecticut §49-10a:* deliver "on or before the date specified in such request, provided such request date is at least seven business days after the date of receipt of the written request"; no fee for the first payoff or reinstatement statement in a calendar year (expedite fee only by agreement); if late, "the mortgagee shall not be entitled to the payment of any interest on the mortgage loan" accruing after the request date. *North Carolina G.S. 45-36.7:* "Within 10 days after the effective date of a notification" the secured creditor must issue the payoff statement showing the date prepared, the amount by type of each fee/charge, "the per diem interest amount," "the payment cutoff time, if any, the address or place where payment must be made, and any limitation as to the authorized method of payment"; one free per six months, $25 for additional; corrected statements free. *Massachusetts G.L. c.183 §54D:* "within 5 business days of receipt of the request," an "amount certain, as of the payment date specified," a per-diem or formula for later payment, escrow disbursement details, a validity period "of no less than 30 days from issuance," one free per 6-month period, and liability for "the greater of $500 or the mortgagor's actual damages" plus attorney fees. *Virginia §55.1-339:* payoff letters must state "the amount outstanding and required to be paid to satisfy the obligation" and an address for notification (no delivery deadline found). *Georgia §44-14-3:* the holder may "add to the pay-off amount the costs of recording a cancellation." Illinois, New Jersey, Ohio, Maryland, Washington, Arizona, Colorado, Minnesota, Michigan, Pennsylvania: no payoff-statement deadline located today **[UNVERIFIED — compile with counsel]**.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) State deadlines can be *shorter* than 7 business days (MA 5 BD) and several states attach reliance or forfeiture consequences (CT interest forfeiture, FL non-disclaimable reliance, TX binding-through-closing-date, CA $300, MA $500) — `jurisdiction_rules.payoff` is a hard input, not a note. (2) Fannie Mae's interest rule is *up to but not including* the receipt date, with full months on a 360-day basis and partial months on 365 — the 7.6 worked example (`7.6-T8`) counted 20 days for a Nov 20 good-through with a Nov 1 accrual start; the engine rule below yields 19 days, and 7.6-T8 is re-based in the section-level test plan. (3) The row omits NIB balances (A4-2.1-07/D2-3.2-04), buydown credits, HSA references and the non-business-day receipt rule. (4) Automation class stays (a); the only human touchpoints are `human_agent` on request and `officer` approval of a "similar circumstances" reasonable-time category (7.6).

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `payoff.request.received{channel, written}` (7.6 intake) → engine run; `payoff.request.requester_verified`.
- `payoff.quote.requested{mode=oral|portal|api|internal}` — oral/portal quotes (no §1026.36(c)(3) clock) and internal quotes (reinstatement comparison in 13.x, short-sale evaluation in 12.9, transfer-out cut-off 17.x, A4-2.1-07 projected-payoff notices).
- Recalculation triggers (accuracy when issued): `payment.posted`, `payment.reversed`, `fee.assessed`, `fee.waived`, `advance.posted`, `escrow.disbursement.posted` (advance creating negative escrow), `arm.rate_change.effective`, `lossmit.deferral.completed`, `scra.rate_cap.applied/released`, `bankruptcy.case.opened/closed` — any of these on a loan with an open statement whose `good_through ≥ today` → `payoff.quote.recompute`.
- Schedules: nightly 02:00 local re-validation of open statements (rate-change lookahead, expiring good-through), A4-2.1-07 maturity-notice sweep (180/150 and 75/60 days before maturity for NIB loans).
- Inbound: title-company/closing-agent requests via the verification portal or ALTA-style e-mail-in with borrower authorization attachments.

#### State machine
Request/notice states are 7.6's (`received → written_confirmed | oral_only → requester_verified → calculating → rendered → checked → sent → superseded | closed`). The engine adds per-quote states: `computing` → `gated` (accuracy gate failed: unposted items, pending reversal, missing firm/BK figures) → `computed` → `rendered` → `active` (good_through ≥ today) → `superseded` (recompute produced a different total) | `expired` (good_through passed without funds) | `relied_upon` (funds received citing this quote — 16.2) | `void` (loan transferred out, paid by other means). Transitions by the `payoff-release` agent; `gated → computed` may be forced by the agent only with a best-evidence figure when the federal/state deadline would otherwise breach (7.6 rule); `human_agent` may annotate but not alter figures.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Accrual basis.** `accrual_start = paid_through_date + 1 day` where `paid_through_date` is the last day of the interest period covered by the LPI installment (for a loan due on the 1st with LPI 09/01, interest is paid through 08/31 and `accrual_start = 09/01`). Interest is computed on `upb_cents` (interest-bearing principal only) using the loan's `rate_segments`.
2. **Day count (F-1-09).** For `accrual_method = monthly_30_360_partial_365`: count whole calendar months from `accrual_start` to the last full month-end before the payoff date as `months_full`, each accruing `UPB × rate ÷ 12`; the remaining days (`days_partial = payoff_date − first_day_of_partial_month`, i.e., **up to but not including the day funds are received**) accrue `UPB × rate ÷ 365 × days_partial`. For `daily_simple_365`: `UPB × rate ÷ 365 × (payoff_date − accrual_start)` (IRM: "DSI loans accrue daily on a 365-day year"). A statement with `good_through = G` is computed as if funds were received on `G` (interest through `G − 1`).
3. **Rounding.** Each interest component is computed with `decimal.js` (precision 20) and rounded half-up to cents **once, per component** (full-month block, partial block, each rate segment); the displayed `per_diem_cents = round(UPB × rate ÷ 365)`; the statement's post-good-through instruction is "add `per_diem_cents` × days"; the engine accepts the borrower's per-diem arithmetic as full satisfaction when it differs from the exact recomputation by ≤ $0.01 × days (difference absorbed to `payoff_rounding_expense`).
4. **Rate segments.** ARM (7.2) or SCRA (13.9) changes effective inside the accrual window split the window; each segment uses its own rate on the same UPB; a change effective *after* the statement date but before `good_through` is applied as noticed (7.2's notice is the source of truth) and a Δ ≠ 0 recompute triggers an updated statement.
5. **Non-business-day rule (F-1-09).** If the installment due date is a non-business day and funds arrive on the next business day, `payoff_date` is deemed the due date (no interest for the intervening days).
6. **Components.** `total = UPB + interest + NIB deferred + NIB forborne + unpaid late charges + NSF/other borrower-payable fees + recoverable corporate advances + escrow advance (negative escrow balance) + third-party release/recording fee (only where C-1.2-05 conditions and `jurisdiction_rules.release.fee_pass_through` allow) + MI premium due for the current coverage month if borrower-paid and not in escrow (10.5) − buydown credit − suspense/unapplied credit`. Prepayment premium is asserted 0. Late charges are included only if assessed under 2.7 and not barred by state law or a workout; fees under dispute (NoE open) are shown but the borrower is told they are under review (statement is still "accurate" as to what is claimed). Escrow balance is **not netted** (3.5 default; feature flag `escrow.payoff.net_against_shortfall`), and the statement says the escrow balance will be refunded within 20 business days of payoff (§1024.34(b)(1)) unless the borrower elects a 3.5 new-loan credit.
7. **Alternative figures.** When a scheduled installment falls between `calc_at` and `good_through` (or an autodraft is scheduled), the statement prints (a) the figure assuming the installment posts as scheduled and (b) the figure if it does not (7.6 example); the engine's `alt_figures` are reconciled at funds receipt (16.2).
8. **HSA note.** If `hsa_note_flag`, the statement carries the C-1.2-03 language (sale/transfer: due in full; refinance: continues, not required for release).
9. **Fees for the statement.** $0 default; a repeat/expedite fee only where `jurisdiction_rules.payoff.fee_repeat_cents > 0` and 7.6 decision 4 is reversed (NY $20 subsequent; NC $25 beyond one per six months; MA "reasonable" beyond one per six months; CA ≤ $30; CT first free per calendar year).
10. **Statement validity / reliance.** `valid_until = good_through` (≥ issue + 30 CD in MA). In FL, TX and CA the figure is binding as to the requester through `good_through`/closing date; any later discovery that the figure was low is absorbed by Supermortgage (16.2 shortage disposition `servicer_absorbed`), never demanded from a party that relied on it; in NY/NC/MA a corrected statement is sent and the difference may be requested only for funds received after the corrected statement's delivery.
11. **Oral quotes.** The AI voice/chat agent may give the same computed figure orally after identity verification; it must state the good-through date, per diem, that escrow is refunded separately, and offer the written statement (one-click). Oral quotes are recorded as `payoff_quotes{quote_type=oral}` and are not binding unless followed by a written statement (state law may differ — FL treats only the written estoppel as binding).

**Worked example A (fixed-rate, monthly accrual, Ohio).** Loan: original $260,000 at 6.500% fixed, 360 months, P&I $1,643.38; UPB $248,310.55; LPI 09/01/2026 (paid through 08/31/2026; `accrual_start` 09/01); an unpaid late charge of $82.17 (5% × $1,643.38) from the August installment; escrow balance $2,412.90; no NIB balance; Ohio county recording fee $34.00 for the release (illustrative; Form 3036 §23 permits third-party release costs) **[county amount UNVERIFIED]**. Written request e-mailed by the borrower Mon 09/14/2026 with good-through Thu 10/15/2026. Federal deadline: day 7 = **Wed 09/23/2026** (servicer open weekdays); Ohio has no state payoff-statement deadline. Interest: September is a full month → 248,310.55 × 0.065 ÷ 12 = 1,345.0155 → **$1,345.02**; October partial = days 10/01–10/14 = 14 days (funds deemed received 10/15) → 248,310.55 × 0.065 ÷ 365 × 14 = 619.0756 → **$619.08**; displayed per diem = 44.2197 → **$44.22**. Total = 248,310.55 + 1,345.02 + 619.08 + 82.17 + 34.00 = **$250,390.82** good through 10/15/2026; "after 10/15 add $44.22 per day"; escrow $2,412.90 refunded separately within 20 business days. Rendered and sent Tue 09/15 (portal + e-mail as requested); `REGZ_1026_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT_7BD` satisfied. *Funds arrive early* (Fri 10/09 by wire, $250,390.82): exact interest through 10/08 = 8 days = $353.76 → overage $265.32 (= 6 × $44.22) → `payoff.funds.over` → refund within 10 BD (6.5 `SM_OVERPAYMENT_REFUND_10BD`) — the loan is still paid in full on 10/09. *Funds arrive late* (Tue 10/20, statement amount only): exact interest through 10/19 = 19 days = $840.17, statement carried $619.08, per-diem instruction yields 5 × $44.22 = $221.10 (exact Δ $221.09) → shortage $221.10 > tolerance → 16.2 short-payoff path (Ohio has no binding-through-date rule; the closing agent is asked for $221.10 within 5 BD).

**Worked example B (payment deferral).** Interest-bearing UPB $180,000.00 at 5.000%, NIB deferred balance $12,000.00 (D2-3.2-04), LPI 11/01/2026 (`accrual_start` 11/01), good-through 11/20/2026: partial interest 11/01–11/19 = 19 days → 180,000 × 0.05 ÷ 365 × 19 = 468.4932 → **$468.49**; per diem **$24.66**; total = 180,000.00 + 12,000.00 + 468.49 = **$192,468.49**. The NIB line is printed separately ("Deferred principal — non-interest-bearing — due at payoff"), and 16.2 reports LAR principal of $192,000.00 (5.3-T5).

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Notices (7.6 codes): `NTC_REGZ_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT` with state variants `_CA_2943`, `_FL_701_04` (no disclaimers; corrected-letter rule), `_NY_274A` (per diem line; fee rule), `_TX_343106` (Finance Commission form fields **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**), `_CT_4910A`, `_NC_45367` (cutoff time, payment place, method limits), `_MA_54D` (amount certain; validity ≥ 30 days); `NTC_PAYOFF_UPDATED_STMT`; `NTC_FNMA_NIB_BALANCE_NOTICE` (A4-2.1-07 "or its equivalent"). Required-content checklist = 7.6 rule 4 + NIB line, buydown credit, HSA language, verification token, wire fraud warning ("we will never change wire instructions by e-mail; verify at the portal or by calling the number on your statement"), per-diem instruction, escrow refund sentence, alternative figures when applicable.
- Records: `payoff_quotes`, `payoff_statements`, `loan_events` `payoff.quote.computed/recomputed`, `payoff.statement.sent/superseded/expired`, `agent_decisions`. Ledger: none (fees, if any, via 2.7). Investor events: none.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `payoff-release` computes and validates every figure through the deterministic `PayoffCalculator` tool (no LLM arithmetic); the LLM layer classifies requests, resolves requester type and good-through intent, chooses the state variant, explains the figure to the borrower (`borrower-comms` for voice/chat), and drafts authorization requests. Tools: `computePayoffQuote`, `assertAccuracyGate`, `renderStatement`, `mintVerificationToken`, `sendNotice`, `scheduleRecompute`, `openShortagePath` (16.2), `recordDecision`, `escalate`.
- **Decision record:** `{request_id, quote_id, loan_id, good_through, accrual_method, rate_segments, components{...}, total_cents, alt_figures, state_variant, deadlines{federal, state}, gate_results, recipients, sent_at, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`.
- **Guardrails:** figures only from `payoff_quotes`; wire instructions only from the vault's active version; no statement to an unverified third party without a borrower-of-record copy (7.6); no oral figure before identity verification; the agent cannot waive fees > $100 without `officer` approval (2.7 waiver policy); every recompute Δ is explained in the updated statement.
- **Escalations:** `attorney`/firm for foreclosure fee schedules; `bankruptcy-ops` (+`attorney` where a court order governs payoff terms) for post-petition components; `officer` for "similar circumstances" reasonable-time categories and for any request to send a statement with a non-vault wire instruction (never approved — logged as a fraud signal to `security-records`); `human_agent` on request; `licensed_specialist` not applicable.
- **Disclosure/consent:** AI voice/chat disclose automation; e-mail delivery per 7.6 decision 2; inbound-driven, so no TCPA consent gating for the quote itself (outbound callbacks to cell phones follow 4.x consent rules).
- **AI-off path:** the same calculator behind an ops-console "Compute payoff" form; queue with the same timers.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in/out:** open requests carry the original clock (7.6 decision 3); a transfer-out effective before `good_through` voids the statement, the requester is redirected to the transferee within 1 BD, and 17.x's cut-off quote (interest to the transfer date) is produced for the transferee.
- **Bankruptcy:** post-petition fees/costs only as allowed (Rule 3002.1 notices, 14.x); Chapter 13 payoff through the trustee's plan requires the trustee's figures — reasonable-time path with `bankruptcy-ops`; statements to debtor's counsel where represented.
- **Foreclosure:** firm fees/costs from 13.6; reinstatement quote (13.x) offered alongside; sale-date proximity flagged; figures never include non-recoverable costs.
- **SCRA:** capped-rate segments (13.9); the statement notes the protection and any refund due.
- **Disaster:** `officer`-declared operational disaster → reasonable-time path; target ≤ 10 BD (7.6).
- **Successor in interest:** confirmed successor = consumer; potential successor = agent path (4.4).
- **Deferred/forborne balances:** always included; a Flex Mod forbearance with a maturity-date balloon is described as due at payoff.
- **Negative escrow (advance):** included as `escrow_advance_cents`; if the advance was Fannie Mae-funded it is excluded from the payoff and repaid by special remittance (16.2).
- **Pending autodraft:** alternative figures; 2.3 suspends the draft once payoff funds post.
- **Charged-off / matured loans:** statement still issued; interest per note terms to payoff.
- **Requester fraud (fake title company):** verification portal, callback to an independently sourced number, and no statement e-mail to look-alike domains; suspected → `security-records`.
- **Vendor outage:** portal down → voice/e-mail; print/mail down → e-delivery with consent, else next-day mail with evidence.
- **Recompute storms:** a fee reversal and a payment reversal on the same day produce one updated statement (debounced to end of business day unless a deadline forces earlier).

#### Audit and evidence
`payoff_quotes` (immutable, hashed, with ledger snapshot id and rule set), `payoff_statements` with delivery evidence and verification-token access logs, `agent_decisions`, timer histories (federal and state), authorization documents, oral-quote transcripts, and the recompute chain — sufficient to defend §1024.35(b)(6) NoEs, FL/CA/TX reliance claims and MORA sampling of payoff accuracy.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Per-diem display vs exact recompute: accept borrower/closing-agent arithmetic within $0.01 × days? **Default: yes; absorb to `payoff_rounding_expense`.**
2. Third-party release/recording fee on the statement (C-1.2-05) — pass through where law/instrument permit, or absorb and claim from Fannie Mae only where prohibited (F-1-05)? **Default: pass through where permitted, exact county fee, no markup.**
3. Include unpaid late charges on a payoff during an active forbearance/repayment plan? **Default: exclude while the plan is performing (12.x); include otherwise.**
4. Quote-to-statement window: written request with a good-through > 30 days ahead? **Default: statement good through 30 days with per-diem instruction (7.6 decision 1).**
5. Texas rule form (7 TAC §155.2) mechanics **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** — counsel to confirm form fields and whether the rule's clock applies to non-title-company requesters. **Default: 7 BD for all Texas requests.**
