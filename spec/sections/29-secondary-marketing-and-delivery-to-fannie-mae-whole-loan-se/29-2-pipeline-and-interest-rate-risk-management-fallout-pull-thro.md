### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Best-efforts execution carries no rate risk to the seller; closed loans become mandatory — Selling Guide C1-1-01 (02/05/2025), C2-1.2-02/-03 (03/03/2021); PE–WL FAQ and job aids — VERIFIED (quoted in 29.1).** "If the loan does not close, the lender does not have to pay a fee."; "Closed loans are considered a mandatory obligation to be delivered to Fannie Mae."; a pair-off fee "will be assessed for commitments where the fallout event is due to a failure, for any reason, to deliver the closed and committed loan to Fannie Mae"; "Early funding is available for loans delivered in fulfillment of a best efforts commitment." (C2-1.2-03). Fannie Mae "may impose volume limitations at any time, in its sole discretion" (C1-1-01) — a best-efforts pipeline is therefore not free of counterparty capacity risk.

**Mandatory commitments as forward sales — C2-1.1-02/-03/-04; PE–WL Mandatory Committing guide (Feb 2025) — VERIFIED (quoted in 29.1).** "The lender agrees to deliver a specified dollar amount of loans, within certain tolerances, to Fannie Mae by a specified future date." (C1-1-01); tolerance "the greater of $10,000 or +/- 2.5% of the original commitment amount"; over-delivery "maximum … 25 percent of the original commitment amount"; extension per diem = remaining balance × lowest PTR ÷ 360; pair-off/over-delivery priced on "Whole loan prices captured at commitment and again at the time of the overdelivery or pair-off"; cash back when the price at commitment exceeds the price at pair-off "solely as a result of market fluctuations"; "Commitments to deliver most loan products can be taken for 1 to 90 days."; "$200 million in aggregate commitment volume per day"; "Lenders can obtain commitments on any weekday, with the exception of bond market observed holidays as defined by … (SIFMA)."

**TBA market mechanics — SIFMA Uniform Practices Chapter 7 (Pool Notification Process) and SIFMA MBS Notification and Settlement Dates; CME Group TBA primer — VERIFIED (dates) / PARTIALLY VERIFIED (class definitions).** Chapter 7: "the seller must notify the buyer with the requisite pool information … at least 48 hours before delivery"; EPN cutoff "3:00:00 p.m."; "delivery cannot be made until the third business day after the notification unless agreed to by the buyer". A TBA trade fixes "issuing agency (FNMA, FHLMC), maturity, coupon, face value, price and SIFMA TBA Settlement Day" (CME primer); "Two Exchange Business Days before TBA Settlement Day, seller informs buyer of details of MBS pools to be delivered." Class A is the 30-year UMBS/Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac class (CME primer; SIFMA's class-product mapping page was not readable — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**). SIFMA 2026–2027 dates (Class A notification/settlement): Oct 8/Oct 13, 2026; **Nov 9/Nov 12, 2026**; **Dec 8/Dec 10, 2026**; **Jan 12/Jan 14, 2027**; Class B (15-year): Nov 13/Nov 17, Dec 11/Dec 15, 2026; Jan 14/Jan 19, 2027. Because the partner sells whole loans for cash and never delivers pools, every TBA short must be rolled or paired off before the notification date of its settlement month; the good-delivery variance and pools-per-million rules never apply to it.

**FINRA Rule 4210 Covered Agency Transaction margin — FINRA Regulatory Notice 23-14 (Aug 18, 2023), amendments effective May 22, 2024 — VERIFIED.** Covered Agency Transactions are "(1) To Be Announced transactions, inclusive of adjustable rate mortgage transactions, (2) Specified Pool Transactions and (3) transactions in Collateralized Mortgage Obligations". The amendments "eliminate the two percent maintenance margin requirement"; members "collect margin for each counterparty's excess net mark to market loss" — the loss "to the extent it exceeds $250,000"; the "small cash counterparty" exemption applies when "the absolute dollar value of all of such counterparty's open Covered Agency Transactions … is $10 million or less"; uncollected losses trigger net-capital deductions when they persist beyond "the close of business on the next business day" and further action when uncollected within five business days; the former language treating "mortgage bankers" as exempt accounts was deleted; counterparties without a written margin agreement are "non-margin counterparties" subject to capital deductions and strict monitoring by the member. Consequence: the partner's broker-dealer will require a written margin agreement (MSFTA with margin annex) and will call variation margin on the partner's TBA shorts when the excess net mark-to-market loss exceeds the threshold the agreement sets (the $250,000 de minimis is the rule's floor; dealers may contract lower — **[UNVERIFIED — dealer terms]**).

**Interest-rate lock commitments and hedges in the accounts — SEC SAB 105 (2004) and SAB 109 (Nov 5, 2007) — VERIFIED (sec.gov); ASC paragraph numbers — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (FASB Codification is login-gated).** SAB 105 restates FASB Statement 149: "loan commitments that relate to the origination of mortgage loans that will be held for sale … shall be accounted for as derivative instruments by the issuer" (now ASC 815-10-15-71). SAB 109, Question 1: "In measuring the fair value of a derivative loan commitment accounted for under Statement 133, should Bank A include the expected net future cash flows related to the associated servicing of the loan?" — "Yes. The staff believes that, consistent with the recently issued guidance in FASB Statement No. 156 … the expected net future cash flows related to the associated servicing of the loan should be included in the fair value measurement."; applied "on a prospective basis to derivative loan commitments issued or modified in fiscal quarters beginning after December 15, 2007"; SAB 109 supersedes SAB 105 on that point; SFAS 159 (ASC 825-10) "permits fair value election for written loan commitments". TBA forward sales are derivatives at fair value through earnings (ASC 815-10); mortgage loans held for sale are carried at the lower of cost or fair value (ASC 948-310-35-1) unless the fair value option (ASC 825-10) is elected; hedge accounting (ASC 815-20 designation, effectiveness) is optional and is not used by default — both legs run through earnings as an economic hedge. The platform produces the numbers; the partner's accountants own the elections (open question 6).

**Seller financial eligibility and liquidity — FHFA/Enterprise minimum financial eligibility requirements (Aug 2022; effective Sept 30, 2023 for most items) — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (FHFA page returned 404; MBA NewsLink summary read).** Net worth "$2.5 million plus 25 bps" of Enterprise servicing UPB; tangible net worth/total assets 6%; an "origination liquidity requirement" that exempts "smaller lenders with less [than] $1 billion in origination"; liquidity buffers only for large institutions. Consequence: the partner's pipeline (IRLCs + loans held for sale) may drive an origination-liquidity requirement once volume exceeds $1 billion — the liquidity report below tracks it as a partner obligation (31.1 verifies the primary text).

**LL-2026-04 AI/ML governance (Apr 8, 2026; effective Aug 6, 2026) — VERIFIED (00a-fnma §1).** Applies to "All Fannie Mae Single-Family Sellers and Servicers"; vendor flow-down "no less protective"; owner "reviews the policies and procedures at least annually". A machine-learned pull-through model used to size the partner's hedges is within its scope; a lookup-table model is model risk under the partner's policy either way.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "C1-2 (mandatory delivery obligations)" is mis-cited: the mandatory obligations are C2-1.1-01…-06 (mandatory commitments) and C2-1.2-02/-03 (closed best-efforts loans); C1-2 covers delivery data and eMortgages.
2. The row's "Daily" trigger understates the clocks: SIFMA notification dates (roll gate), FINRA 4210 variation-margin timing, PE–WL 5:00 p.m. ET pair-off/extension cutoff on the expiration date, the 8:15 a.m. ET committing open, and Fannie Mae's automatic 1-day/5-day extension and auto pair-off rules all bind intraday.
3. The row omits the FINRA 4210 margin regime (effective May 22, 2024) that turns a TBA hedge into a liquidity exposure, and the FHFA origination-liquidity requirement.
4. "ASC 815 at output level" is right, but SAB 109 makes the servicing value part of the IRLC fair value even though the servicing stays with the partner — the outputs must separate the servicing component (partner MSR economics) from the sale-price component (the Grander pass-through economics).
5. "Hedge advisor/broker-dealer feeds (reference)" — in the operating model the partner, not SM, is the counterparty of record; SM's adapter is read-only for prices/confirmations and the trade orders are the partner's trader's, so the `hedge-feed` adapter never carries an executable order in v1.
6. Best-efforts mode still needs the process: residual exposures (uncommitted locks, undeliverable closed loans, DPA re-commits, extension carry) and fallout statistics exist without any hedge.

#### State machine

`hedge_positions.status`: `open` (trade confirmed) → `rolled` (roll_close executed and a new `open` position in the next settlement month created) | `paired_off` (buy-back executed; realized P&L) | `closed` (Fannie Mae mandatory commitment fulfilled, paired off or expired — mirrors 29.1's commitment status). Transitions are performed by the partner's trader (dealer trades) or the `fnma_portal_operator` (commitments) and recorded by the `secondary` agent from confirmations; a position never transitions on an AI decision alone.

Pipeline lock exposure state (derived per lock, not stored as a status): `locked_uncommitted` → `committed_be` (29.1 best efforts) | `mandatory_pipeline` (covered by the hedge program) → `closed`/`funded_unsold` → `delivered` → `purchased` (exits the position) | `fallout` (exits with a `fallout_events` row) | `closed_undeliverable` (pair-off risk until resolved).

Margin call: `open` → `funded` (wire released under 26.3's dual control pattern — the `funding_approver` role is reused for margin wires) | `disputed` (marks differ by more than the tolerance; `officer` engages the dealer).

Rebalance recommendation: `recommended` → `authorized` (partner `officer`/trader) → `executed` (confirmation) | `declined` (reason recorded) | `expired` (price moved; re-recommended).

#### Business rules and calculations

Money in cents; prices in percent of par to 3 dp (TBA prices quoted in 32nds are converted to decimal at 5 dp); probabilities to 4 dp; durations to 4 dp; rounding half-up to cents at each dollar conversion. Illustrative market levels below are fixtures, not verified prices.
1. **Execution mode.** `execution.mandatory_enabled=false` (default): every lock is committed best efforts (29.1); the position report covers residual exposures only and no hedge exists. When true, the partner's policy assigns locks to the hedge program by product (default: 30-year and 15-year fixed conforming; ARMs and low-volume products stay best efforts).
2. **Pull-through.** `p(lock) = model(stage, days_to_expiry, rate_move_bps, transaction_type, occupancy, ltv_bucket, channel, state)`; v1 lookup defaults (until calibrated on the partner's own data): locked 0.72, DU-approved 0.80, clear-to-close 0.92, CD delivered 0.97, consummated 0.995 (rescission/funding fallout), funded 1.00; rate-move adjustment −0.02 per −12.5 bps (rally) and +0.01 per +12.5 bps (sell-off), floored at 0.50 and capped at 1.00; refinance loans are 5 points below purchase loans at the same stage. `expected_deliverable = Σ p × amount`. Realized pull-through by cohort is compared monthly (`SM_BE_FALLOUT_REVIEW_MONTHLY`).
3. **Hedge ratio and coverage.** `duration_factor = Δprice_whole_loan / Δprice_hedge_instrument` for the hedge coupon (estimated from the last 20 business days of PE–WL and TBA prices; default 1.00 for a Fannie Mae mandatory commitment on the same product/PTR, 0.90–1.05 for the nearest TBA coupon); `target_hedge_face = expected_deliverable ÷ duration_factor`; `coverage_ratio = hedge_face × duration_factor ÷ expected_deliverable`; rebalance when outside the band; rebalance size = `target_hedge_face − hedge_face`, rounded to the dealer's minimum lot ($250,000 default — **[UNVERIFIED — dealer terms]**; Fannie Mae commitments are in multiples of $1).
4. **Instrument choice.** Fannie Mae mandatory commitment (29.1) when the loans will be delivered inside 90 days, the daily $200M limit is not binding and the pair-off/extension economics beat the TBA carry; TBA UMBS 30-year (Class A) or 15-year (Class B) short with the dealer otherwise, in the coupon whose price tracks the target PTR (rule 3), for the settlement month after the expected delivery month (never the current month within 3 business days of notification); no options, swaps or specified pools in v1.
5. **Marks.** Whole-loan mark = PE–WL live price for the product/PTR/remaining period at 5:00 p.m. ET (last live price before close-of-business display), stored in `pewl_price_captures{purpose='mark'}`; TBA mark = dealer 5:00 p.m. ET run; commitments marked against the live price for the same terms; positions without a price are marked at the last price with a stale flag.
6. **ASC 815/948/825 outputs (output level).** Per lock: `irlc_sale_price_component = (market_price − lock_base_price) × amount × p` (the 20.4 quote's base price is the price at which the borrower's economics were fixed); `irlc_servicing_component = servicing_value_multiple × servicing_fee_bps/10,000 × amount × p` (SAB 109; the partner supplies its MSR multiple — default 4.5× of the 25 bps strip — **[partner input]**); `irlc_fv = Σ (sale_component + servicing_component)` — the two components are reported separately because the servicing value is the partner's MSR economics while the sale-price component is the Grander pass-through pool. Held-for-sale loans: `hfs_fv = upb × (net_price_forecast_market − 100)/100 + …` versus cost basis; LOCOM or FVO per the partner's election. Hedges: `hedge_fv = face × (trade_price − mark_price)/100` for a short. All through earnings; no ASC 815-20 designation by default.
7. **Rate shock.** For each shock s ∈ {−100, −50, −25, 0, +25, +50, +100} bp: reprice the pipeline with `Δprice ≈ −duration × s/100` (duration 4.0 default for 30-year at par — recomputed from the price runs), re-estimate p under the shock, recompute expected deliverable, coverage, net value, projected margin call (= max(0, hedge MTM loss − dealer threshold)) and projected pair-off cost of the over/under-hedge; compare with `rate_shock_limit_cents` per shock in the policy.
8. **Liquidity.** `liquidity_need = projected margin calls (−100 bp) + pair-off fees due within 5 days + extension carry accrued + warehouse curtailments due (27.1) + FHFA origination-liquidity requirement (if ≥ $1B origination; primary text per 31.1)`; compare with the partner's reserve; the report states the shortfall date if any.
9. **Roll calendar.** For every TBA position, `roll_due_on = businessDaysBefore(notification_date, roll_lead_business_days, 'fannie_sifma')`; the agent recommends a roll to the next month (close the front month, open the back month at the roll price) or a pair-off when the covered loans will have been delivered; the front-month position must be flat by `roll_due_on` 12:00 p.m. ET.
10. **Swapping the hedge into the sale.** As covered loans close, the agent requests Fannie Mae mandatory commitments (29.1) for the closed balance in short periods (default 10 days) and simultaneously recommends pairing off the equivalent TBA face, so the hedge migrates into the actual forward sale; the 29.1 tolerance band is sized to the closed balance, not the pipeline.
11. **Pair-off economics.** TBA pair-off P&L = `face × (trade_price − pair_off_price)/100` (short); Fannie Mae mandatory pair-off per 29.1 rule 12 (fee or cash back); the daily decision compares extension carry (per diem × days) against the expected pair-off cost given the pull-through path.
12. **Best-efforts residual exposure.** `uncommitted_exposure_cents = Σ locked_uncommitted × duration × shock` (rising rates hurt); `pair_off_risk_cents = Σ closed_undeliverable × max(0, market − commitment price)` (falling rates hurt); `dpa_exposure` from 29.1; extension carry accrual per 29.1 rule 10. All appear on the daily report even in best-efforts mode.
13. **Reporting to the partner.** Daily position pack (by product, note-rate coupon 0.125% buckets, PTR, lock status, expirations ≤ 7 days, uncommitted, undeliverable, hedge positions, coverage, MTM, day change), daily rate-shock table, weekly liquidity stress, monthly fallout/pull-through and hedge effectiveness, quarterly policy compliance; every report is a `documents` row with the snapshot ids it was built from.
14. **Model governance.** Pull-through model versions are registered in the 31.2 model inventory with validation evidence; a change in method (lookup → logistic/ML) needs `officer` approval and an 31.2 review before use; every hedge recommendation records the model version (LL-2026-04 flow-down).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`secondary` agent (pipeline/hedge tools: `takePipelineSnapshot`, `estimatePullThrough`, `estimateDurationFactor`, `computeCoverage`, `recommendRebalance`, `prepareTradePackage`, `recordTradeConfirmation`, `scheduleRoll`, `requestMandatoryCommitment` (→ 29.1), `runMarkToMarket`, `runRateShock`, `computeLiquidityNeed`, `recordMarginCall`, `analyzeFallout`, `recalibratePullThrough`, `publishPartnerReport`, `openEscalation`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: it builds the 7:00 a.m. ET position from `locks`, `commitments`, `fundings`, `deliveries` and `hedge_positions`, estimates pull-through per lock, computes expected deliverable, coverage and the residual exposures, recommends rebalances and rolls with a one-screen package (what, how much, why, policy checks, price expectation, alternatives with costs), records confirmations, marks at 5:00 p.m. ET, exports the GL file, publishes the rate-shock and liquidity reports, watches margin statements, runs the monthly fallout review and proposes recalibrations. Decision record: `{snapshot_id, model_id, expected_deliverable_cents, hedge_face_cents, duration_factor, coverage_ratio, band, recommendation{instrument, side, face_cents, settlement_month, price_expectation}, alternatives[{cost_cents}], policy_checks[], escalation_id, rationale, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails (never alone): never place, roll or pair off a dealer trade — the partner's trader executes on the partner's authorization (`hedge.auto_execute_within_band=false` in v1); never request a Fannie Mae mandatory commitment without the 29.1 `officer` authorization; never use an instrument, counterparty or size outside `hedge_policies`; never let a TBA position stand within 3 business days of its notification date; never mark to a close-of-business PE–WL display price or a stale run without flagging; never change the pull-through model version without `officer` and 31.2 approval; never net SM's economics against the partner's hedge P&L. Escalations: partner `officer`/trader (every trade package; coverage outside band; margin calls; liquidity shortfall; model changes; policy breaches), `fnma_portal_operator` (mandatory commitments/pair-offs/extensions through 29.1), `funding_approver` (margin wires), `compliance-sentinel` (policy or LL-2026-04 governance breaches), `qc-audit` (quarterly sampling of packages vs executions). No borrower contact. Human path: the secondary console shows the same position, packages and reports; the trader can record trades and overrides directly (logged as `recorded_by=user` with reason).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Fannie Mae imposes a volume limitation or the $200M day limit binds while the pipeline is hedged with TBAs: the TBA hedge stays; delivery migrates to later commitments; the roll calendar extends; `officer` engages the account team.
- Dealer marks and vendor marks differ by more than 4/32: dispute path; the conservative mark is used for the liquidity report; the dealer's mark governs the margin call unless disputed within the agreement's window.
- Rally-driven fallout wave (relock elsewhere): pull-through drops faster than the model; intraday trigger snapshots; rebalance in tranches; the 21.4 float-down policy (one per lineage) is the retention tool — its cost is reported here as pipeline value given up.
- Sell-off with rising pull-through (borrowers cling to their locks): under-hedged; the agent adds coverage in the back month; the negative-convexity loss is reported, not hidden.
- Loans closed but undeliverable (29.1 pair-off risk) in mandatory mode: they cannot fill the commitment; the agent sizes the pair-off within tolerance and pairs off the equivalent TBA.
- Margin call on a SIFMA early-close day or federal holiday: due dates follow the margin agreement's business-day definition (`business_days_fannie_et` default); wires on early-close days go before 2:00 p.m. ET.
- Broker-dealer default or failed settlement: positions with that dealer are re-hedged with the second dealer; the partner's legal team handles the MSFTA close-out; the agent reports the unhedged window.
- Pull-through model drift (realized outside ±10 points): hedge ratios move to the conservative band edge (under-hedge in a rally-prone month is preferred to over-hedge) until recalibrated.
- Best-efforts commitment queue exceeds one business day (PE–WL outage, 21.4 edge case): `SM_UNCOMMITTED_POSITION_5BD` counts; the agent quantifies exposure daily; the `officer` may authorize a temporary TBA hedge only if the policy allows (default: no).
- Partner switches `execution.mandatory_enabled` mid-pipeline: existing best-efforts commitments stay best efforts (cannot convert); only new locks enter the hedge program; the position report shows both books until the best-efforts book runs off.
- GL period close falls on a day with a disputed mark: export with the flagged mark and a reconciling item; re-export on resolution.
- SIFMA calendar change (a class date moved): reload; recompute `roll_due_on`; positions inside the new lead window are escalated immediately.

#### Audit and evidence

Per day: the snapshot, the model version and per-lock probabilities, the marks with sources and timestamps, every recommendation with its package, the authorization (`officer`/trader identity, time), the confirmation document, the realized P&L, margin statements and call funding evidence, the GL export and reconciliation, the reports as published. Exam/partner packs: hedge-policy compliance (bands, limits, instruments, counterparties, authorizations), FINRA 4210 margin history (calls, funding times), SIFMA roll discipline (no position past `roll_due_on`), model governance (LL-2026-04: inventory entry, validation, annual review), fallout and pull-through back-testing, SOX-style evidence of segregation between recommendation (AI), authorization (partner) and execution (trader/operator). Fannie Mae MORA: mandatory commitment fulfilment history, pair-off/extension fees and their causes (29.1).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Whose P&L is the hedge.** Default: the partner's — it owns the loans, the commitments, the dealer relationships and the MSR; SM's economics remain the Grander cost-recovery/pass-through model computed on best-efforts-equivalent pricing; any mandatory-execution pickup net of hedge costs is the partner's unless the term sheet allocates it to the borrower pass-through pool.
2. **Launch mode and the switch.** Default: best efforts (`execution.mandatory_enabled=false`) for at least the first six months of production; the switch requires the partner policy, two dealer margin agreements, the calibrated pull-through model and an 31.2 review.
3. **Instrument preference when enabled.** Default: Fannie Mae mandatory commitments first (no dealer margin, delivery tolerance, cash back), TBA only for horizons beyond 90 days or when the daily limit binds; no options.
4. **Coverage band and limits.** Default: 85–110% of expected deliverable; max uncommitted 5 business days; rate-shock limit at −100/+100 bp = 1.5% of the hedged pipeline; margin liquidity reserve = the −100 bp projected call plus 25%.
5. **Pull-through model.** Default: lookup v1 with the stage/rate-move defaults above until three months of the partner's data exist; then a logistic model reviewed by 31.2; ML only with an LL-2026-04-compliant validation.
6. **Accounting elections.** Default outputs assume FVO for held-for-sale loans and no hedge-accounting designation; the partner's accountants choose; the platform can produce LOCOM instead.
7. **Dealer margin threshold and lot size.** Default modeled: $25,000 threshold, $250,000 minimum lot; replace with the executed margin annex terms.
8. **Roll lead.** Default 3 business days before the SIFMA notification date; the partner's trader may set 2 with the dealer's consent.
