### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**The blueprint's premise no longer exists.** Servicing Guide topic **C-4.1-02, "Suspending Credit Bureau Reporting"** (last dated 06/13/2018 in the Feb. 13, 2019 Guide) was removed by **Announcement SVC-2020-03 (July 15, 2020)**: "We are removing the requirement to suspend credit reporting for borrowers impacted by a disaster event," with the reminder that "servicers remain responsible for complying with applicable law, including when reporting to the four major credit repositories." The announcement's updated-topics list names C-4.1-01, C-4.1-02 and (then) F-1-24 "Reporting to Third Parties"; the Aug. 12, 2026 Guide's Section C-4.1 contains only C-4.1-01, and the procedure is now F-1-23 **[retirement PARTIALLY VERIFIED — inferred from the 2019 vs. 2026 tables of contents and the SVC-2020-03 text; the retired topic's full text was not retrievable today]**. Fannie Mae today imposes **no** credit-reporting suspension for disaster, bankruptcy, SCRA or any other event; C-4.1-01 instead requires reporting forbearances and other statuses monthly "in compliance with all applicable laws." D1-3-01 (disaster, 04/08/2026), D2-3.2-01 (forbearance, 04/08/2026), D2-3.2-04 (payment deferral, 08/13/2025) and E-2.1-01 (bankruptcy, 06/10/2020) contain no credit-reporting language (verified today). **Therefore blueprint rows 8.3 and 14.4 become one overlay engine**: 8.3 = "Credit-reporting overlays, suppressions and status flags," and 14.4 = "Bankruptcy credit-reporting overlay (Consumer Information Indicators)" as a consumer of 8.3 with no Fannie Mae suspension duty. "Suspension" survives only where **applicable law** requires or accuracy demands it, as follows.

**Reg X §1024.35(i)(1) / RESPA §6(e)(3)** (verified via 4.1 and law.cornell.edu): 60 days after receipt of an NoE/QWR relating to a payment dispute, no adverse information "regarding any payment that is the subject of the notice of error"; RESPA: no "information regarding any overdue payment ... relating to such period or qualified written request." This is a *scoped* bar, not a whole-account suspension. **FCRA §1681s-2(a)(1)(B)/(a)(3)** (verified): after notice at the designated address that specific information is inaccurate (and it is), no furnishing; any consumer dispute must be flagged (XB). **FCRA §1681c-2 / §1681s-2(a)(6)** (verified): a CRA must block identity-theft information within 4 business days of receiving an identity theft report and "promptly notify the furnisher" that the information "may be a result of identity theft," that a report has been filed, that "a block has been requested," and of "the effective dates of the block"; the furnisher must have "reasonable procedures to respond" and must not re-furnish blocked information, and after a consumer submits an identity theft report to the furnisher's designated address "may not furnish such information ... unless the person subsequently knows or is informed by the consumer that the information is correct." **SCRA 50 U.S.C. 3919** (verified at law.cornell.edu, as amended Dec. 22, 2023): a servicemember's application for or receipt of a stay, postponement or suspension under the SCRA "may not itself ... provide the basis for" (1) a determination of inability to pay, (2) a denial/revocation/change of credit terms, (3) "an adverse report relating to the creditworthiness of the servicemember by or to a person engaged in the practice of assembling or evaluating consumer credit information," (4) an insurer's refusal, or (5) "an annotation in a servicemember's record by a creditor or a person engaged in the practice of assembling or evaluating consumer credit information, identifying the servicemember as a member of the National Guard or a reserve component" (or Space Force). **Bankruptcy** (11 U.S.C. §362(a)(6), §524(a)(2), §1322(b)(5), §1328(a)(1) — **[statute text from general knowledge; not fetched today]**): the automatic stay bars acts to collect; a discharge enjoins collection of the discharged personal liability; a home mortgage cured and maintained under §1322(b)(5) is excepted from the Chapter 13 discharge. No federal rule *requires* suspending credit reporting in bankruptcy; accurate reporting with the CRRG's Consumer Information Indicators is the industry standard, and courts have treated reporting used as leverage as a stay/discharge violation **[case law characterization — UNVERIFIED]**. **Reg F §1006.30(a)** (verified): no furnishing by a debt collector before speaking with the consumer or mailing/sending a communication and waiting a reasonable period (commentary: 14 days) for undeliverability. **CARES Act §1681s-2(a)(1)(F)** (verified): accommodations made in the covered period (ended Aug. 8, 2023) and still in effect keep the "current / frozen" rule. **Deceased consumers:** Metro 2 ECOA code `X` (the 2026 CRRG clarified ECOA "X" reporting expectations — CDIA summary via Bridgeforce; text **[UNVERIFIED — CRRG 2026]**). **Successors in interest:** Reg X §1024.30(d) makes a confirmed successor a "borrower" for the servicing rules but does not create liability; Reg V accuracy requires that furnished information "identifies the appropriate consumer" and reflects "liability" — a non-assuming successor is not furnished (4.4).

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint.** (1) Governing source is not C-4.1-02 (retired) but applicable law + CRRG; Fannie Mae's only instruction is "comply with all applicable laws." (2) Bankruptcy and disaster are not "suspensions": bankruptcy is a per-consumer CII overlay with status rules; disaster is a Special Comment (AW) and, if a disaster forbearance is granted, the 8.1 forbearance rule. (3) The blueprint omits the legally mandatory overlays: the 60-day NoE bar, dispute flags, identity-theft blocks, SCRA, FDCPA pre-furnishing contact, deceased/successor handling. (4) "Flag" as the artifact is right but must be per consumer and per payment scope, with a mechanism and an expiry.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers

#### Data model
- `credit_reporting_suppressions` (extended from 4.1's minimal shape; this is the authoritative schema): `id`, `loan_id`, `borrower_id?` (null = all consumers on the loan), `case_id?`, `reason` ∈ {regx_1024_35_i, respa_6e3_qwr, fcra_a1b_inaccuracy_notice, fcra_dispute_open, identity_theft_block, identity_theft_report, bankruptcy_active, bankruptcy_discharged, scra_relief, scra_stay, disaster, forbearance, cares_accommodation, deceased, fdcpa_1006_30, boarding_unreconciled, transfer_out_final, qc_hold, litigation_hold}, `mechanism` ∈ {report, flag_only, as_if_paid_projection, freeze_status, omit_account, delete_consumer, delete_account}, `codes jsonb` (e.g., `{cii:'D', ecoa:'X', special_comment:'AW', ccc:'XB'}`), `scope jsonb` (installment due dates or `all`), `starts_at`, `ends_at?`, `evidence_document_id`, `created_by` (agent/human), `agent_decision_id`, `status` ∈ {active, expired, released}, `released_reason`.
- `metro2_overlay_decisions` (8.1): the per-cycle resolution of all active suppressions for a loan/consumer with the chosen mechanism and codes.
- `bankruptcy_reporting_state` — `loan_id`, `borrower_id`, `chapter` ∈ {7, 11, 12, 13}, `phase` ∈ {petition, confirmed, discharged, dismissed, withdrawn, closed, reaffirmed}, `petition_date`, `confirmation_date`, `discharge_date`, `dismissal_date`, `reaffirmation_date`, `debt_discharged bool` (false for §1322(b)(5) maintained mortgages), `status_at_petition`, `post_petition_payment_cents`, `plan_cures_arrears bool`, `cii_current char(2)`.
- `accuracy_program` (Appendix E as code): `controls` (id, citation, check implementation, frequency, threshold, owner), `control_runs` (run_at, result, metrics, exceptions, escalation_id), `sample_verifications` (cycle_id, loan_id, borrower_id, fields_compared jsonb, match bool, verified_by), `policy_versions` (document_id, approved_by `officer`, effective_at, next_review_at).

#### State machine
Suppression: `active → expired` (timer) | `→ released` (agent/human with reason and evidence, e.g., dismissal order, block rescission, dispute closed) — never deleted. Overlay evaluation per loan/consumer per cycle (pure function): collect active suppressions → choose the most restrictive mechanism (`delete_account > delete_consumer > omit_account > freeze_status > as_if_paid_projection > flag_only > report`) → merge codes with priorities (CII per consumer; ECOA X per consumer; Special Comment priority `CP > AW > AC > CO/CN > AZ`; CCC per dispute state) → write `metro2_overlay_decisions`. Bankruptcy phase machine: `petition → confirmed → (discharged | dismissed | withdrawn) → closed`; `reaffirmed` is a flag on Chapter 7. Actors: `credit-reporting` agent (all), `officer` (delete_account/delete_consumer, release of identity-theft or litigation holds), Timer Engine (expiries).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **NoE/QWR 60-day bar — mechanism (resolves 4.1-Q4).** For each installment in the suppression's `scope`: compute the Account Status, Amount Past Due and DOFD *as if that installment had been received on its due date* (`as_if_paid_projection`); render the PHP positions for the disputed months as `D` (no history available this month — a neutral value that furnishes no adverse information without asserting the payment was made); report any *undisputed* delinquency accurately; set CCC **XB**. The projection is applied at transmission time: a file built on the 1st but transmitted on the 3rd uses the suppression state on the 3rd. On expiry or NoE closure: error found → the correction (4.1) makes the "as-if-paid" state the true state, PHP restored with `0`, XR; no error → actual status and PHP restored in the next furnishing, XH (or XC if the borrower states continued disagreement). Worked example (from 4.1): NoE received **2026-09-04** disputing the March-1-2026 payment; bar through **2026-11-03**. The Sept-30 file transmitted Oct 5 and the Oct-31 file transmitted Nov 3 are inside the bar → both render the March 2026 PHP position as `D`; if the loan is otherwise current the status is 11 with XB. The Nov-30 file transmitted Dec 3 is outside the bar → if the NoE closed "no error" on Oct 20, the March position reverts to `1` and CCC = XH. If the servicer prefers not to furnish a projected status, the alternative mechanism `omit_account` is permitted (PHP `D` for the omitted month) but is dispreferred because it withholds accurate positive data for other months (8.3-Q1).
2. **FCRA (a)(1)(B) inaccuracy notice.** When a borrower notifies the designated address that specific information is inaccurate and the agent's *preliminary* check (same day) finds it likely inaccurate, create `fcra_a1b_inaccuracy_notice` with `as_if_paid_projection` or `freeze_status` for the affected field(s) pending 8.2's full investigation; if the check finds it likely accurate, XB only (`flag_only`).
3. **Bankruptcy overlay (per filing consumer; non-filing co-borrowers are unaffected).** Default matrix (codes † per CRRG as recalled; the 2026 CRRG modified its bankruptcy FAQs — confirm before go-live):
   - **Petition (Ch. 7/11/12/13):** CII `A`/`B`/`C`/`D` on the filer's segment; Account Status: freeze at the petition-date status (`freeze_status`) until plan confirmation/discharge/dismissal (default; alternative: continue contractual aging — 8.3-Q3); Amount Past Due frozen; Current Balance = total UPB (incl. arrears); no Special Comment; no collection language anywhere in the record.
   - **Ch. 13 plan confirmed** (`plan_cures_arrears=true`): Account Status per **post-petition contractual performance** (11 if post-petition payments are current); Amount Past Due = post-petition past-due only; Scheduled Monthly Payment = post-petition PITI; CII `D` retained; pre-petition arrears remain in Current Balance and are reduced as trustee payments post (14.2 payment-change/3002.1 events keep the scheduled payment accurate).
   - **Ch. 7 discharge, no reaffirmation:** CII `E`; Current Balance **0**, Amount Past Due **0**, Scheduled Monthly Payment **0**, Date Closed = discharge date, Account Status = the frozen pre-discharge status †; then `final_reported` — no further furnishing even if the borrower keeps paying (personal liability is discharged; furnishing a balance/payments would misstate liability) — 8.3-Q4 records the alternative (positive-only reporting on request) as **not** adopted pending CRRG/counsel.
   - **Ch. 7 reaffirmation:** CII `R`; normal reporting continues; rescission → `V`.
   - **Ch. 13 completion/discharge:** if the mortgage was surrendered/discharged → CII `H`, balances 0, Date Closed; if maintained under §1322(b)(5) (debt not discharged) → CII `Q` (remove bankruptcy indicator) and normal reporting resumes †.
   - **Dismissal / withdrawal:** CII `I/J/K/L` (dismissed) or `M/N/O/P` (withdrawn) for one cycle †, then `Q`; the freeze is released and the true contractual status is reported (which may jump several buckets — the anomaly gate in 8.1 whitelists this transition with the dismissal order as evidence).
   - **Deceased debtor / co-debtor stay (§1301):** ECOA `X` and the co-debtor's own status; no CII on the non-filer.
   Worked example: SM-1001's borrower files Chapter 13 on **2027-03-10** with the Feb-1 and Mar-1 installments unpaid (both pre-petition; Feb-28 status was 11 at 27 days). Mar-31 snapshot: the contractual count is 58 days (71), and the freeze anchors at the petition-date status — on 03-10 the loan was 37 days past due from Feb-1 → also **71**; CII `D`; Amount Past Due frozen at the pre-petition arrears 2 × 2,459.55 = 4,919.10 → 000004919; DOFD 02012027; Current Balance 000293063 (UPB; under 8.1 rule 2 accrued interest on the unpaid installments is not part of Current Balance — the "total amount owed" alternative would give 293,063.94 + 4,919.10 = 297,983.04 → 000297983 †). Plan confirmed **2027-06-15**, curing the $4,919.10 pre-petition arrears through the trustee over 60 months; post-petition payments Apr–Jun made on time → Jun-30 snapshot: **11**, CII `D`, Amount Past Due 0 (post-petition current; pre-petition arrears are being paid under the plan), Scheduled Monthly Payment 000002459, DOFD zero-filled, Current Balance 000293063. Case dismissed **2028-01-20** with the Nov-1 and Dec-1-2027 post-petition payments unpaid → Jan-31-2028 snapshot: CII `L`, contractual status from the earliest unpaid installment (Nov-1-2027 → 91 days → **80**), DOFD 11012027 (a new delinquency; the cured Feb-2027 delinquency does not re-open), Amount Past Due = Nov + Dec + Jan installments plus any uncured pre-petition arrears the trustee had not yet paid; Feb-29-2028 snapshot: CII `Q`, then normal reporting.
4. **SCRA overlay.** While a `scra` relief case is active: the contractual payment is the reduced payment under the 6% cap (13.9) — Scheduled Monthly Payment = reduced PITI; interest forgiven under 50 U.S.C. 3937 is not a receivable and never appears in Current Balance/Amount Past Due; a stay/postponement/suspension granted under the SCRA (13.8; Fannie Mae Military Indulgence, D2-3.4) is reported like a forbearance freeze (no adverse change "solely by reason of" the stay) with **no** Special Comment `AI` (recalled to active duty) or any other annotation that identifies Guard/reserve status (§3919(5)) — 8.3-Q5; delinquency that pre-dates the relief or arises from non-payment of the *reduced* amount is reported accurately, but only after `officer` review during the gate (documented "not solely by reason of" rationale). Worked example (loan SM-1001 current through April 2027; UPB after payment #26 = $292,096.58; 334 payments remain): P&I at 6.25% = $1,847.15; SCRA 6% cap effective with the 2027-05-01 payment → P&I = 292,096.58 × 0.005 ÷ (1 − 1.005^−334) = **$1,800.91** (round-half-up at the payment step); forgiven interest for May = 292,096.58 × (0.0625 − 0.0600)/12 = **$60.85**, written off (never a receivable); the borrower pays $1,800.91 + $612.40 escrow = **$2,413.31** → Actual Payment 000002413, Scheduled Payment 000002413, status 11, Current Balance reduced by the full scheduled principal.
5. **Disaster overlay.** On `disaster.case.opened` (D1-3-01 evaluation, property in a FEMA-declared area): Special Comment **AW** † while the case is open and the loan is not in a forbearance (CP takes precedence in the single Special Comment field); no suspension (SVC-2020-03). A disaster forbearance follows 8.1's forbearance rule (freeze + CP); Disaster Payment Deferral follows the deferral rule. The overlay expires with the case (max 12 months from the declaration unless extended by `officer`).
6. **Deceased.** On `borrower.deceased.confirmed` (death certificate/SSA/obituary evidence per 4.4): ECOA `X` on that consumer's segment from the next cycle; the account continues to be furnished for surviving obligors with their own ECOA codes; a sole deceased obligor keeps the account reported with `X` (the bureaus suppress deceased consumers' files); confirmed successors are never added; an erroneous deceased marker (living borrower) is corrected by AUD within 2 BD with the borrower's proof of life (a well-known harm case — `officer` notified).
7. **Identity theft.** On a CRA Block notification (e-OSCAR `/notification` type Block) or a consumer identity theft report (FTC Identity Theft Report or police report) at the designated address: immediately `omit_account` for the affected consumer (or `delete_consumer` via ECOA `Z` if the consumer was never liable), open a `fraud` case (19.x; Fannie Mae fraud reporting through `officer`), investigate origination documents; outcomes: (a) fraud confirmed → `DF` delete account (whole loan fraudulent) or ECOA `Z` (co-borrower identity stolen) with `officer` approval, and the loan's `credit_reporting_status='never_report'` for that consumer; (b) not fraud, consumer's liability confirmed → do not resume furnishing until the CRA rescinds the block (§1681c-2(c)) — submit a BRR with evidence (8.2) and resume only on rescission; (c) inconclusive → keep omitted and review every 30 days.
8. **FDCPA pre-furnishing gate.** For loans boarded in default (`fdcpa_debt_collector=true`, 11.4): `omit_account` until `contact.live` with the consumer about the debt, or a validation notice/letter is mailed or e-messaged and 14 calendar days pass with no undeliverability notice; the gate opens automatically on the event; months omitted show `D` in the PHP thereafter.
9. **Forbearance and CARES legacy.** 8.1's forbearance rule writes a `forbearance` suppression (`freeze_status`, CP) so that the overlay ledger is complete; `cares_accommodation` (accommodation made ≤ 2023-08-08 and still in effect) applies "current / frozen" per §1681s-2(a)(1)(F).
10. **Transfer, boarding, QC and litigation holds.** `transfer_out_final` after the 05 record; `boarding_unreconciled` omits for at most one cycle; `qc_hold` omits records within the scope of a suspected systemic error (18.1) — furnishing suspect data would be furnishing with "reasonable cause to believe" it is inaccurate; `litigation_hold` never suppresses (accuracy still governs) but routes any change on the loan to `officer` before furnishing.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Suppression rows and per-cycle overlay decisions; Metro 2 codes applied (CII, ECOA X/Z, AW/CP, XB/XC/XH/XR, DA/DF); urgent AUDs; the accuracy-program control runs and the monthly Appendix E evaluation; `agent_decisions` for every overlay that changes furnished data.
- Notices: none specific to overlays beyond 8.1/8.2's (identity-theft outcomes are communicated through the 8.2 results letter and the `fraud` case; deceased handling communicates through 4.4). Records: `bankruptcy_reporting_state`, `accuracy_program.*`.
- Ledger: none. Investor events: none (bankruptcy/SCRA/disaster reporting to Fannie Mae is 5.7/14.x/13.x).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `credit-reporting` (overlay mode) — subscribes to the events above, creates/expires suppressions with evidence, resolves conflicts, orders urgent AUDs, runs the accuracy controls, and writes the monthly evaluation. Tools: `credit.suppression.create/release`, `bankruptcy.state.get`, `scra.case.get`, `disaster.case.get`, `borrowers.get`, `documents.search`, `eoscar.aud.submit`, `eoscar.notification.*`, `accuracy.control.run`, `escalation.file`.
- **Decision record:** `{loan_id, borrower_id?, trigger_event_id, reason, mechanism, codes, scope, evidence_document_id, rule_set_version, model_version, rationale, confidence, human_review_reason[], reviewer_id?, outcome}`.
- **Guardrails:** no suppression without a triggering event and evidence; `delete_account`/`delete_consumer` and identity-theft releases require `officer`; a Chapter 7 discharge zero-balance overlay requires the discharge order document; SCRA adverse changes during the gate require `officer`; the agent may not create "courtesy" suppressions on request (a borrower asking "please don't report me" is not a legal basis — accuracy governs; the request is logged and answered).
- **Human touchpoints:** `officer` for deletions, identity-theft outcomes, SCRA adverse determinations, litigation-hold releases and the annual policy review; `attorney` where a bankruptcy court order's effect on reporting is unclear (e.g., a plan that treats the mortgage unusually) — package: docket documents, proposed CII/status, rationale. AI-off path: the ops console overlay worklist.
- **Disclosure/consent:** none (no consumer interaction in 8.3).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Bankruptcy filed by one of two borrowers:** CII and freeze only on the filer's segment; the co-borrower's segment reports contractual status — but §1301 co-debtor stay (Ch. 13, consumer debt) restricts *collection* against the co-debtor, not accurate reporting; `attorney` consult if contested.
- **Serial filings / refiling after dismissal:** new petition → new CII; the freeze anchors at the new petition date.
- **Conversion Ch. 13 → Ch. 7:** CII changes from D to A; state resets to petition phase with the original petition date.
- **Discharge order later vacated:** correction with AUD; officer.
- **Bankruptcy monitor outage:** overlays lag; `SM_CR_SUPPRESSION_REVIEW_30` plus a daily PACER-reconciliation exception report catch missed events; a filing learned late is applied retroactively via AUD and correction record.
- **SCRA relief discovered retroactively (DMDC batch shows active duty starting before adverse marks were furnished):** correction of the affected months if the adverse status arose "solely by reason of" the relief context; otherwise no change; `officer` decides; 13.9 interest refund.
- **Disaster declaration expanded after the cycle:** apply AW in the next cycle; no AUD needed (AW is not adverse).
- **Deceased marker on the wrong consumer:** urgent AUD (2 BD), officer notice, root cause into the accuracy program.
- **Identity-theft report later withdrawn / block rescinded:** resume with BRR and officer approval; never automatically.
- **Successor who later assumes:** add as obligor from the assumption date; the deceased borrower stays `X`.
- **60-day bar and a foreclosure sale in the window:** the bar restricts furnishing only; foreclosure proceeds per 4.1/13.x rules.
- **Overlapping overlays** (e.g., bankruptcy + NoE + SCRA): the mechanism priority resolves the record; codes co-exist (CII + XB + ECOA); the decision record lists all reasons.
- **Transfer-out with active suppressions:** the hand-off file carries the suppressions and evidence; the transferee is told which overlays are live.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **8.3-T1 (NoE bar):** Given the 4.1 NoE received 2026-09-04 disputing the March-2026 payment on an otherwise current loan, then the files transmitted 2026-10-05 and 2026-11-03 show status 11, XB, and PHP position for March 2026 = `D`; the file transmitted 2026-12-03 restores `1` with XH after a "no error" closure on 2026-10-20.
- **8.3-T2 (bar at transmission time):** Given a file built 2026-11-01 and transmitted 2026-11-04 (after expiry), then the projection is not applied.
- **8.3-T3 (partial scope):** Given an NoE disputing only the June installment on a loan also unpaid for July, then the June installment is treated as paid and July's delinquency is reported (status by July's days past due) with XB.
- **8.3-T4 (Ch. 13 petition/confirmation/dismissal):** Given the worked example, then Mar-31 = 71/CII D frozen; Jun-30 = 11/CII D with Amount Past Due 0; Jan-31-2028 = 80/CII L with DOFD 11012027; Feb-29-2028 = CII Q.
- **8.3-T5 (Ch. 7 discharge):** Given discharge 2027-09-20 without reaffirmation, then the Sep-30 record carries CII E, balances 0, Date Closed 09202027, and no record is furnished in October; given a reaffirmation agreement filed, CII R and normal reporting.
- **8.3-T6 (SCRA):** Given relief from 2027-05-01 and payments of $2,413.31 monthly, then status 11, Scheduled Payment 000002413, no AI code, no forgiven interest ($60.85 in May) in balances; given a missed reduced payment in July, the resulting 71 at Aug-31 is held for `officer` review (documented "not solely by reason of the relief") before furnishing.
- **8.3-T7 (disaster):** Given a FEMA declaration covering the property on 2027-08-20 and no forbearance, then the Aug-31 record carries AW; given a disaster forbearance from 2027-09-01, CP replaces AW.
- **8.3-T8 (deceased):** Given confirmed death of co-borrower B on 2027-04-12, then B's J1 segment carries ECOA X from the Apr-30 cycle and A's segment is unchanged; given the successor daughter confirmed under 4.4, no segment is created for her.
- **8.3-T9 (identity theft):** Given a Block notification on 2027-05-03 for borrower C, then C is omitted from the May cycle, an AUD removing C is sent by 2027-05-05, a `fraud` case opens, and resumption requires `officer` + BRR.
- **8.3-T10 (FDCPA gate):** Given boarding in default on 2027-02-10 and a validation notice mailed 2027-02-12 with no undeliverability by 2027-02-26, then the Feb-28 cycle includes the loan; given the notice returned undeliverable on 2027-02-20, the loan is omitted until live contact.
- **8.3-T11 (stale suppression):** Given a bankruptcy dismissed 2027-10-02 with no monitor event, then the 30-day review on 2027-10-10 flags the docket mismatch and the agent applies CII L/Q with the dismissal order.
- **8.3-T12 (no courtesy suppression):** Given a borrower asks the AI voice agent not to report a late payment, then no suppression is created, the request is logged, and the borrower is told about the direct-dispute process.
- **8.3-T13 (sample verification):** Given a cycle of 5,000 records, then ≥250 are re-derived by `qc-audit`; a match rate of 99.2% escalates to `officer` with field-level variances.
- **8.3-T14 (priority resolution):** Given bankruptcy freeze + open dispute + SCRA on one consumer, then mechanism = freeze_status, codes = {CII D, XB}, and the decision record lists three reasons.

#### Audit and evidence
Every suppression with its trigger event, evidence document hash, agent decision, reviewer, start/end and release reason; per-cycle overlay decisions; urgent AUD control numbers; bankruptcy reporting state history with docket document ids; SCRA certificates; deceased evidence; identity-theft case records; accuracy-program control runs, sample verifications and the annual policy approval — retained `fcra_furnishing_5y`/life-of-loan + 4 years; exportable for MORA, state exams (furnisher-accuracy modules), bankruptcy-court disputes and FCRA/RESPA litigation.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **8.3-Q1 NoE-bar mechanism.** Default: `as_if_paid_projection` + PHP `D` for disputed months + XB (answers 4.1-Q4). Alternative: omit the account for the window.
2. **8.3-Q2 Special Comment precedence and CO persistence.** Default: `CP > AW > AC > CO/CN > AZ`; CO reported every cycle while modified terms are in force. Confirm CRRG.
3. **8.3-Q3 Bankruptcy pre-confirmation status.** Default: freeze at petition-date status with CII. Alternative: contractual aging with CII. Counsel + CRRG 2026 bankruptcy FAQs.
4. **8.3-Q4 Post-Chapter 7 "ride-through" reporting.** Default: cease furnishing after the discharge record (zero balance, CII E). Alternative: positive-only reporting at the borrower's written request — not adopted pending CRRG/counsel.
5. **8.3-Q5 SCRA special comment.** Default: never report `AI` or any military annotation (§3919(5) risk). Alternative: report AI for active-duty (non-Guard/reserve) servicemembers only — rejected as unworkable.
6. **8.3-Q6 Deceased sole obligor.** Default: continue reporting the account with ECOA X. Alternative: final-report and stop. Confirm the 2026 CRRG ECOA X clarification.
7. **8.3-Q7 Retired C-4.1-02 text.** Obtain the 2018/2019 topic text for the file (historical only) — not needed for the build.
