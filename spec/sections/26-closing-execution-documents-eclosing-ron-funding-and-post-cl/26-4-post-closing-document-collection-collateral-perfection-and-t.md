### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Note endorsement — B8-3-04, *Note Endorsement* (last updated 10/02/2019).** "The mortgage seller must endorse the note in blank and without recourse." Format (as extracted): "PAY TO THE ORDER OF ______ WITHOUT RECOURSE [LENDER'S NAME]" with the authorized signature, signer name and title. "The endorsement should be signed only by those persons specifically authorized to execute documents in the lender's behalf." "The mortgage seller may not delegate to an attorney-in-fact its authority to execute an endorsement." Chain: "The note must be endorsed to each subsequent owner of the mortgage unless one or more of the owners endorsed the note in blank. The last endorsement on the note should be that of the mortgage seller." Facsimile: "Fannie Mae accepts a lender's facsimile endorsement of notes for those jurisdictions in which the lender has determined that such endorsements are valid and enforceable," provided the lender maintains (a) "legal opinions related to the legality and enforceability of facsimile signatures for each jurisdiction," (b) a board resolution authorizing specific officers, (c) "the corporate secretary's certification," and (d) "a notarized certification of facsimile signature." Allonge: "The endorsement must appear on the note. An allonge may be used for the endorsement as long as the following requirements are met: The form and content of the allonge used must comply with all applicable state, local, or federal law governing the use of allonges and result in an enforceable and proper endorsement to the note. The allonge must be permanently affixed to the related note and must clearly identify the note by referencing at least the name of the borrower(s), the date of the note, the amount of the note, and the address of the security property. The note must clearly reference the attached allonge. Fannie Mae's status as a 'holder in due course' must not be impaired." eNotes are not endorsed — ownership moves by Transfer of Control on the eRegistry (B8-8-02/C1-2-04; 26.2/29.4).

**Custodial documents — E-2-01, *Required Custodial Documents* (05/04/2022); A3-3-04, *Document Custodians* (05/04/2022); A3-3-05, *Custody of Mortgage Documents* (05/04/2022).** E-2-01: "Custodial documents are the legal documents pertaining to a mortgage that the document custodian takes into…" custody (sentence truncated in extraction). The note row: "The original note endorsed 'in blank' and without recourse and there is no break in the chain of endorsements." "For MERS registered loans, the MIN should be added to the upper right-hand corner of the note." Other rows (as extracted): "A copy of the applicable power of attorney, if an attorney-in-fact signed the mortgage note…"; "A name affidavit, if the borrower signed under an 'also known as (AKA) name' or used a signature…"; "Originals of any instruments that modify the terms and conditions of the mortgage note (such as a modification…"; trust loans: "If the loan indicates that it is a part of a trust, it must be signed by the borrower as both…" (individually and as trustee — E-2-04, 26.1); "Facsimile signature language, as applicable. If a mortgage (or deed of trust) note is endorsed by facsimile…"; New York: Form 3172 "for a New York mortgage transaction that uses Form 3172." Packaging: "Do not staple one document to another and do not include any mortgages, deeds of trust, title insurance policies, or appraisals." **No row lists an assignment of the mortgage — to Fannie Mae, to MERS, or intervening — and no row lists a lost note affidavit.** A3-3-05: custodial documents are the "Original note and note addenda" and "Other documents needed for certification of whole loans or certification for inclusion in an MBS pool such as powers of attorney, and interest rate buydown agreements"; "The document custodian must not release custodial documents for either whole loans or MBS loans unless it receives a written request" with the content of Form 2009; recertification on custodian or servicing transfer. A3-3-04: "Each custodian arrangement must be evidenced by the execution of a Master Custodial Agreement (Form 2017)"; for non-eNote loans "the seller/servicer must use one of Fannie Mae's active document custodians"; for eNotes "Fannie Mae is the document custodian but has designated certain third parties to certify eNotes"; "The document custodian is acting on behalf of the seller/servicer and Fannie Mae when certifying loan documents and data at the time of acquisition by Fannie Mae"; "The seller/servicer must pay all fees and charges of the document custodian"; annual internal-audit review and quarterly financial-rating review of the custodian; "The roles and responsibilities of the document custodian are described in the Fannie Mae Requirements for Document Custodians (RDC guide)." Form 2017's text could not be read (download page only) — **[UNVERIFIED — form content]**.

**Requirements for Document Custodians v15.0 (August 2025) — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (extraction-quality quotes; re-read before build).** Cover: "Fannie Mae Requirements for Document Custodians Version 15.0 August 2025." Revision history: "Removed mortgage assignments from required documents." Document review (as extracted): "The note must be endorsed by the Seller or lender's authorized officer"; "Endorsements must be made 'in blank' and 'without recourse'"; "Facsimile signatures are permitted if authorized by corporate resolution"; "There must be no break in the chain of endorsements from the original payee"; "An allonge may be used if there is insufficient space on the note for endorsements"; "allonges…must be permanently affixed to the note"; "The Document Custodian must verify that any Power of Attorney was dated and valid at the time the note was signed"; "For MERS-registered mortgages, the Mortgage Identification Number (MIN) must appear on the mortgage or note." Lost notes (as extracted): "A Lost Note Affidavit may be certified only when the original note cannot be located. The LNA must be executed by the Seller, notarized, and include: (1) indemnification language protecting Fannie Mae; (2) a detailed description of the lost note including loan amount, borrower name, and note date; and (3) a copy of the note." The extraction also asserts an "SFC 159" for LNAs, but the Special Feature Codes list dated 09.09.2026 contains **no** code 159 and no lost-note code — **[UNVERIFIED — treat LNA certifiability as a per-loan confirmation with the custodian/Fannie Mae]**. Bailee letters: "Document Custodian either receives with the mortgage delivery documents a Bailee letter notifying it about a warehouse lender's security interest"; "Upon receipt and opening of a Mortgage Loan package containing a Bailee letter, the Document Custodian must immediately perform a loan inventory." The Loan Delivery warehouse-lender guide (00b-orig F6): the custodian "compares the actual Bailee letterhead text entered by the warehouse lender user to the Loan Delivery Letter Name field. The name entered in Loan Delivery must exactly match the Bailee letterhead text in order to pass the Bailee validation"; Form 2004A releases the warehouse interest only "if the proceeds … are delivered to the disbursement agent in accordance with the delivery instructions in Form 2004A." C2-2-03 (05/04/2022; 00a-fnma §5.4): "any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note."

**Assignments — B8-6-01, *Authorized Use of Intervening and Blanket Assignments* (05/04/2022).** "If the originating lender is not servicing the loan at the time of the sale to Fannie Mae, then unless the loan has been registered with MERS or the loan is secured by a property in Puerto Rico, the seller of the loan must cause to be recorded one or more intervening assignments of mortgage to ensure there are effective mortgage assignment(s) from the originating lender to the servicer." "Copies of all such assignments must be retained in the loan file." "Lenders may use blanket assignments for any intervening assignments that take place before the mortgage is sold to Fannie Mae, as long as this type of assignment is acceptable to the applicable recording jurisdiction… Each blanket assignment must relate to a single recording jurisdiction." "The lender may be required to indemnify Fannie Mae… for any losses incurred by Fannie Mae due to the lender's failure to obtain, record (if applicable), and retain the necessary intervening assignments." Puerto Rico: "Assignments of mortgages generally are not recordable in Puerto Rico. Therefore, no intervening assignments need to be prepared, recorded, or retained." Chapter B8-6 has a single topic; there is no "Mortgage Assignment to Fannie Mae" topic in the current Guide.

**MERS — B8-7-01, *Mortgage Electronic Registration Systems (MERS), Inc.* (05/01/2024); MERS System Procedures Manual Rel. 26.1 (effective May 17, 2026).** B8-7-01: MERS "is the mortgagee of record (either by being named as an assignee in a recorded assignment of the security instrument or as nominee for the beneficiary in the security instrument)"; the MOM instrument must "show MERS as the nominee for the seller/servicer, define and name the originating seller/servicer, and obtain the borrower's acknowledgment of MERS' role"; "For each MERS-registered loan delivered to a document custodian, the seller/servicer must indicate the MIN on the security instrument and related documents"; "the seller/servicer is not required to include the MIN on the note"; "If a seller/servicer registers a loan on the MERS system before delivering it to Fannie Mae, the seller/servicer must ensure that the MIN is registered in MERS and names itself as the investor"; "After Fannie Mae purchases or securitizes the mortgage, Fannie Mae notifies MERS to update its records to reflect Fannie Mae's ownership interest in the loan"; a loan registered after purchase must "name Fannie Mae as the investor during registration and notify MERS of Fannie Mae's ownership interest"; "the seller/servicer is solely responsible for any failure to comply with the provisions of its MERS Member Agreement, Rules, and Procedures"; "Sellers/servicers must also take all reasonable steps to ensure that information pertaining to MERS is updated and accurate at all times"; Montana, Oregon and Washington require the MERS Rider (Form 3158); "In the state of Maine, sellers/servicers must use the MERS Mortgage Assignment (Form 3749)." MERS Procedures Rel. 26.1: "A MOM loan must be registered no later than seven (7) calendar days after the Note Date, or Funding Date for refinance loans or loans in escrow states, even if the rights to the loan are immediately sold to a non-Member." "A Non-MOM loan must be registered no later than seven (7) calendar days after the Assignment to MERS or UCC-3 was executed." "Loans are generally registered on MERS® System post-closing, but the registration can be initiated before closing using the Pre-Closing Registration transaction. A Pre-Closing MIN Record is not active, so it cannot be updated, transferred, or deactivated, but it can be reversed if it was registered in error or a correction is required." "A Registration may be reversed if it was registered in error or a correction is required." "A MIN consists of a three-part, 18-digit string": "seven (7) digit Org ID," "ten (10) digit Sequence Number," "one (1) digit Check Digit"; "The Member generates the Check Digit for component 3 of the MIN." "The MOM Security Instrument or UCC-1 must be prepared and executed in accordance with the applicable document requirements… and must be sent for Recordation." Interim Funder: membership "For Warehouse Lenders and Interim Funders that intend to have their Interim Funding Interest reflected on MERS® System"; on resignation "the Interim Funder must remove its Org ID from the Interim Funder field"; the procedure "Removing an Interim Funding Org ID" exists. The mechanics of *adding* the Interim Funder Org ID (at registration or by MIN update), the Transfer-of-Beneficial-Rights confirmation timing after sale, the Registration Reversal time limit and deactivation reason codes are in the member-only Integration Handbook/quick-reference guides — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. eRegistry (00b-orig F9): registration "no later than one business day after the final tamper sealing"; registration fee "$0.00" if the loan is also on the MERS System, "$8.95" otherwise — an economic reason to keep MOM registration for eNote loans.

**Title policy and recorded instrument (verified in 24.4/26.2; referenced).** B7-2-01 (04/06/2022): "Each mortgage loan purchased by Fannie Mae must have a title insurance policy in place or an attorney title opinion letter that meets Fannie Mae's requirements." B7-2-03 (07/06/2022): for loans originated on or after Jan 1, 2024 the policy must be "the 2021 American Land Title Association (ALTA) Loan Policy"; "The amount of title insurance coverage must at least equal the original principal amount of the loan"; 2006/2021 ALTA policies "may be effective as of loan closing," other forms "no earlier than the later of the date of the final disbursement of loan proceeds or the date the mortgage was recorded"; "Under no circumstances may MERS be named as the insured of a title policy"; ALTA 8.1 required; no 1990 creditors'-rights exclusion. B7-2-04 (05/03/2023) endorsements by property/loan type (24.4 `required_endorsements`). 26.2: `recordings` (`recorded_at`, `instrument_number`, `book_page`, `recorded_image_document_id`, `gap_days`), Covered Risk 14 gap treatment, `SM_O72_ERECORD_SUBMIT_1BD`, `SM_O72_PAPER_FALLBACK_5BD`. Downstream need (Servicing Guide E-1.1-02, *Required Referral Documents*, 11/12/2014): at foreclosure referral the servicer furnishes "the original note, including any allonge; or" "a true, correct, and complete copy of the note, including any allonge" or "a lost note affidavit," plus the recorded mortgage/deed of trust, unrecorded executed assignments and the title policy; "Lost note affidavits must only be used after a thorough and diligent search has been made for the original note"; the servicer must "institute a process to request the necessary documents from the document custodian no later than the 95th day of delinquency." Records: A2-4.1-02 (12/19/2017; 00a-fnma §8) — records are Fannie Mae's property, retained "at least four years" after liquidation. No Fannie Mae deadline exists for receipt of the recorded security instrument or the final policy — **[VERIFIED as absent in the topics read; the timers below are policy]**; county recording turnaround figures are industry experience — **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) "For non-MERS loans the unrecorded assignment to Fannie Mae" is stale: E-2-01 lists no assignment to Fannie Mae, the RDC's revision history removed mortgage assignments from required documents, and B8-6 has no assignment-to-Fannie-Mae topic — the platform never prepares one. (2) The MERS 7-day anchor is the note date *only* for purchase loans outside escrow states; for refinances and escrow-state loans it is the funding date (Rel. 26.1) — the fixture (AZ refinance) has a hard deadline of Nov 19, not Nov 13; SM keeps Nov 13 as its policy target. (3) "B8-6 (assignments)" covers only *intervening* and *blanket* assignments where the originator is not the servicer — not applicable when the partner originates and services, and moot for MERS-registered loans. (4) "MIN on the security instrument" is required; the MIN on the note is "not required" (B8-7-01) but "should be added" (E-2-01) and the RDC checks "the mortgage or note" — SM prints it on both. (5) The blueprint's "note to warehouse custodian ≤ 5–10 business days" is facility policy (27.1: `SM_WH_WET_NOTE_DELIVERY_5BD`, 5 `business_days_servicer` from the advance date); Fannie Mae's only timing rule is delivery-side (C2-2-01 first-morning delivery before commitment expiry; 29.4). (6) A lost-note affidavit is not in E-2-01 and no SFC exists for it; its acceptability for purchase must be confirmed per loan — the blueprint treats the LNA path as routine. (7) B8-8 "eNote transfer of control" is 29.4's (delivery); 26.4 only maintains custody (Controller/Location/Secured Party unchanged) until delivery and reconciles. (8) The recorded security instrument and final policy are not purchase gates; the blueprint's "collateral perfection" for whole loans is satisfied by the note in the custodian's possession under Fannie Mae's certification — recording perfects the lien against third parties, not the sale. (9) The eNote path adds a custody obligation the blueprint omits: eVault Location integrity and monthly eRegistry reconciliation (MERS QA); the paper path adds the bailee-letterhead exact-match rule.

### Operational prerequisites
- **Partner**: MERS membership with Org ID; SM's staff/system designated under the partner's Org ID (MERS user administration) or SM's own MERS membership as a vendor/Interim Funder with the partner's authorization to register on its behalf (**[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — MERS vendor-registration mechanics member-only]**); corporate resolutions naming `signing_officer`s for endorsements, allonges, assignments (Maine 3749), LNAs; facsimile-endorsement file per B8-3-04 (jurisdiction legal opinions, board resolution, corporate secretary certification, notarized facsimile certification) if `endorsement_method = printed_facsimile`; Form 2017 with an active FCC; FCC's 9-313(c) acknowledgment that it holds paper notes for SM as bailee (27.1) — weeks.
- **SM**: MERS membership (Interim Funder; Subservicer role after purchase), MERS System integration (XML/flat file — Integration Handbook member-only; web fallback), MERS QA plan; eVault integrated with the eRegistry (26.2); carrier accounts with API tracking; eRecording vendor status feeds (26.2); title-underwriter/settlement-agent document exchange for final policies (24.4 `title` adapter); custodian exception-notice mailbox adapter (00b-svc N8); `jurisdiction_rules` rows: `recording_turnaround_days` (paper), `rerecording_method`, `assignment_to_mers_required` (Maine), `mers_rider_required` (MT/OR/WA), `escrow_state` (MERS funding-date anchor), `release_of_lien_days` (informational, prior lien) — counsel matrix (**[UNVERIFIED for most states]**).
- **Both**: closing instructions (26.1) that direct the settlement agent to (i) affix the pre-executed allonge or verify the printed endorsement, (ii) ship the original note by tracked overnight courier to the address SM designates (the FCC as bailee by default), (iii) eRecord and return recorded images, (iv) order the final policy and deliver it to SM within 60 days.
- **Rule sets**: `fnma.selling.2026-09-02` (B8-3-04, B8-6-01, B8-7-01, E-2-01, A3-3-04/-05, B7-2-03), `fnma.rdc.15.0`, `mers.proc.26.1`, `mers.eregistry.16.25`, `sm.postclosing.v1` (policy timers), `sm.warehouse.v1` (27.1).

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `closing.consummated{consummation_at}` (26.2) → MIN registration clock (note date) and expected-document list; `loan.funded{disbursement_date}` (26.3) → funding-date anchor for refinances/escrow states; `warehouse.advance.funded` / `warehouse.advance.approved` (27.1) → Interim Funder designation; `warehouse.advance.repaid` (27.2) → Interim Funder removal is 27.2's (`SM_WH_INTERIM_FUNDER_RELEASE_2BD`), 26.4 verifies.
- `closing.documents.released` (26.1) with the MIN and, if elected, `mers.min.pre_closing_registered`.
- `custody.paper_note.shipped{tracking_ref}` / `custody.paper_note.received` (26.2) — the shipment and receipt events 26.4 tracks against 27.1's `SM_WH_WET_NOTE_DELIVERY_5BD` and `SM_WH_BAILEE_LETTER_GATE`.
- `recording.submitted`, `recording.confirmed{recorded_at, instrument_number}`, `recording.rejected`, `recording.paper_fallback` (26.2).
- `title.final_policy.received` (24.4 `title` adapter) and `subordination.recorded`, POA recording evidence (24.4/26.2).
- `mi.activated` (24.6), `flood.determination.received{life_of_loan}` (24.5) — trailing-document closure inputs.
- `enote.registered`, `enote.secured_party.set` (26.2); eRegistry notifications (Transfer of Control at delivery — 29.4; status changes).
- `funding.cancelled`, `rescission.exercised`, `funding.unwind.opened` (26.3/25.3) → MIN reversal/deactivation and document voiding.
- `custody.certified`, `loan.purchased` (29.4) → custody role change (FCC now holds for Fannie Mae; eNote Controller/Location = Fannie Mae).
- Schedules: 18:00 local daily MERS registration batch; 07:00 daily trailing-document sweep; monthly (1st) eVault↔eRegistry and SOR↔MERS System reconciliations; carrier tracking polls hourly for open shipments.
- Inbound documents: custodian trust receipts and exception letters, recorded images/certified copies, final policies/AOLs, corrected policies, county rejection notices.

#### State machine

`mers_registrations.status`: `reserved` (MIN generated at 26.1 rendering) → `pre_closing` (optional Pre-Closing Registration at document release) → `active` (Registration accepted with Servicer = partner, Investor = partner, Subservicer = SM; `post-closing` agent) → `active` with `interim_funder_org_id = SM` (MIN update on `warehouse.advance.funded`, or set at registration when the advance precedes it) → (after purchase) `active` with Investor = Fannie Mae (Fannie Mae's update, verified by 30.1) and Interim Funder removed (27.2) → (servicing life) 1.5 owns. Side: `reversed` (registered in error; loan cancelled before funding; `SM_O74_MIN_REVERSAL_2BD`) [terminal]; `deactivated{reason}` (funded loan unwound/rescinded/paid) [terminal]. Guards: `active` requires a rendered MOM security instrument (or an executed Assignment to MERS for `non_mom_assignment`) with the MIN and the partner's Org ID as component 1; the Investor at registration is always the partner (B8-7-01) — never SM, never Fannie Mae before purchase.

`custody_records` (paper note): `settlement_agent` (seeded at signing, 26.2) → `courier` (`custody.paper_note.shipped`; bailee letter issued — 27.1 gate) → `fcc_bailee` (delivered + trust receipt; `holding_for_party_id = SM`) → `fnma_custodian` (`custody.certified` and `loan.purchased`; `holding_for_party_id = Fannie Mae`; 29.4) → servicing 1.4. Alternates: `endorsement_desk` (when `endorsement_method = wet_post_closing`: settlement agent → partner/SM desk → FCC); `sm_designated_custodian` (27.1-Q5 alternative); `released` (Form 2009 release for payoff/foreclosure — servicing); `lost` (→ `lost_note_affidavits`) → `re_executed` or `lna_certified` → back to `fcc_bailee`; `returned` (cancelled loan: note voided and returned to the settlement agent/borrower per closing instructions). eNote: `enotes.status` per 26.2 (`registered` → `secured_party_set` → `edelivered` → `control_transferred`); 26.4 maintains `custody_reconciled_at` monthly and records any Location change.

`trailing_documents.status`: `expected` (created at `loan.funded` from the document-set profile) → `open` (due date set) → `received` → `reviewed` (`passed`) → `closed` | `defect_open` (`defects`) → (cure) `received` → … ; `waived` only by `officer` with a reason (e.g., county that never returns originals — certified copy substitutes). Guards: `closed` for `recorded_security_instrument` requires `recorded_instrument_reviews.result = pass`; for `final_title_policy` requires `final_policy_reviews.result = pass`.

#### Business rules and calculations

Calendar-day arithmetic for the MERS clocks (day 7 = anchor + 7, end of day, property local time); business-day timers name their unit; `business_days_servicer` and `business_days_creditor` both exclude weekends and the federal holiday list (baseline).

**MERS registration (`registerMin`).** Prerequisites: the security instrument is a MOM instrument (MERS nominee language; MERS Rider in MT/OR/WA) or a Maine Form 3749 assignment exists; the MIN (component 1 = partner Org ID; component 3 check digit generated by SM per the Integration Handbook — **[UNVERIFIED — member-only]**) is printed on the security instrument, riders and (per E-2-01/SM policy) the note's upper right corner. Anchor selection: `anchor_kind = note_date` if `transaction_type = purchase` and `jurisdiction_rules.escrow_state = false`; otherwise `funding_date` (refinances of any kind; any loan in an escrow state); `registration_due_at = anchor_date + 7 calendar days`; `policy_target_at = note_date + 7`. Timing: Pre-Closing Registration at document release is optional (`mers.pre_closing_registration`, default **off** — consistent with 26.1-Q5's concern about reversals on re-draws; the eRegistry needs only a valid MIN, not a prior MERS System record); the active registration is submitted in the 18:00 local batch on the note date or the next `business_days_creditor` (`SM_O74_MOM_REGISTER_TARGET_1BD`) — not "at funding", because the MERS anchor for purchases is the note date and dry-state funding can consume six of the seven days. Payload: MIN, borrower(s), property, note amount/date, Servicer = partner Org ID, Investor = partner Org ID ("names itself as the investor"), Subservicer = SM Org ID, Custodian = FCC Org ID (if a MERS member), Interim Funder = SM Org ID when `warehouse_advances` exists at submission (else `SM_O74_INTERIM_FUNDER_1BD` adds it on `warehouse.advance.funded`). Rejections (data mismatch, duplicate MIN, invalid check digit) are cured and resubmitted the same day; a rejection never extends the MERS deadline. After purchase: Fannie Mae updates the Investor (B8-7-01) — 30.1 verifies within its window; 27.2 removes the Interim Funder within 2 BD of proceeds; 26.4's monthly reconciliation catches any stale field. Registration after purchase (should never happen): Investor = Fannie Mae per B8-7-01.

**Endorsement (`ensureEndorsement`).** Exactly one endorsement: by the partner, in blank, without recourse (B8-3-04; E-2-01 "no break in the chain of endorsements" — the partner is the original payee, so the chain is one link). Methods by policy `endorsement_method`: (a) **`printed_facsimile`** (default where the partner has the B8-3-04 four-document file for the property's jurisdiction): 26.1 renders the endorsement block with the officer's facsimile signature on the note's last page, so the note leaves the closing already endorsed — no post-closing handling; (b) **`allonge_pre_executed`** (default elsewhere): 26.1 renders the allonge (`closing_documents.kind = allonge`) carrying the borrower names, note date, note amount and property address; the partner's `signing_officer` wet-signs it before the package ships to the settlement agent; the closing instructions require the settlement agent to affix it permanently to the note (staple through the signature page) and the note's rendered signature page carries "SEE ATTACHED ALLONGE FOR ENDORSEMENT" so that "the note must clearly reference the attached allonge" is met (**[open question 2 — counsel to confirm state enforceability of pre-executed allonges]**); (c) **`wet_post_closing`**: the note routes to the endorsement desk; `signing_officer` endorses within 1 `business_days_creditor` of receipt; adds 2–3 days to custody (29.4 worked example A's Nov 13 endorsement). Never: an SM signature, an attorney-in-fact, an endorsement naming SM or Fannie Mae, or a second endorsement. The custodian's review (RDC §8) is the acceptance test; an exception (`custodian_exception_codes`: missing endorsement, wrong entity name, unsigned allonge, allonge not affixed, name mismatch without affidavit, POA invalid at signing, MIN missing) opens `SM_O74_CUSTODIAN_EXCEPTION_CURE_5BD` and 27.1's defect clock; cures are executed only by the partner's `signing_officer` (corrected allonge) or by document production (name affidavit, POA copy).

**Assignments (`decideAssignments`).** MOM loans (default everywhere except Maine's Form 3749 flow): no assignment of any kind is prepared, recorded or delivered — not to MERS, not to Fannie Mae, not intervening (B8-6-01 applies only when the originating lender is not the servicer at sale, and is moot for MERS-registered loans; E-2-01 lists no assignment). Maine: Form 3749 MERS Mortgage Assignment executed at closing by the partner's `signing_officer` (26.1 renders), recorded promptly after the mortgage (B8-7-01 Nov 2025 instruction — 26.1), MIN registered within 7 calendar days of the assignment's execution date (`MERS_PROC_NON_MOM_REGISTER_7`); the recorded assignment is a trailing document (`recorded_assignment_to_mers`). Any state where MERS-as-nominee is unavailable (none in scope today; `jurisdiction_rules.mom_available = false` would trigger the same non-MOM path) — **[UNVERIFIED]**. Post-purchase: Fannie Mae's ownership is reflected by the MERS Investor update, never by a recorded assignment to Fannie Mae.

**Paper-note custody chain (`routeNote`).** Default topology (`custody.paper_note_routing = direct_to_fcc_bailee`; 27.1-Q5/29.4-Q2): the settlement agent ships the endorsed original note (plus any custodial documents: POA copy, name affidavit, note addenda, Form 3172, buydown agreement per A3-3-05) by tracked overnight courier within 1 `business_days_creditor` of signing (26.2 `SM_O72_PAPER_NOTE_HANDOFF_1BD`) to the partner's FCC, which holds it for SM under SM's bailee letter (issued by 27.1 for the shipment; `SM_WH_BAILEE_LETTER_GATE`) and the 9-313(c) acknowledgment; the FCC's trust receipt (`custody.trust_receipt.received` → 27.1 `warehouse.note.received`, `collateral_status = secured_possession`). At delivery (29.4) the note is already in the vault ("pre-positioned"); the delivery cover letter and bailee loan list go electronically the same day the operator submits. After purchase the FCC holds for Fannie Mae (`holding_for_party_id = Fannie Mae`; `holder_role = fnma_custodian`); SM's interest is released by the proceeds (C2-2-03; 27.2). Alternate topology (`via_endorsement_desk`) when method (c) applies or when a custodial document must be added. Dry states: the note ships before funding (26.2) — the closing instructions state the note is delivered in trust subject to funding and must be returned/voided if funding fails (`to_role = settlement_agent_return`); wet states: the note ships after signing/disbursement and 27.1's 5-BD wet clock runs from the advance date. **Never**: shipping without a bailee letter, shipping to an address not on the closing instructions, or accepting a copy in place of the original.
   *Facility arithmetic:* wet advance funded Thu Nov 12, 2026 → `SM_WH_WET_NOTE_DELIVERY_5BD` (`business_days_servicer`): Fri Nov 13 (1), Mon Nov 16 (2), Tue Nov 17 (3), Wed Nov 18 (4), **Thu Nov 19 (5)** — the note must be in the FCC's hands (trust receipt) by Nov 19. Purchase fixture: advance Wed Nov 18 → Nov 19 (1), 20 (2), 23 (3), 24 (4), **Wed Nov 25 (5)** (Thanksgiving excluded). Dry-state fixture default: note shipped Mon Nov 9, delivered Tue Nov 10, trust receipt Tue Nov 10 — before funding, so the advance is `secured_possession` from day 0.

**Lost note (`handleLostNote`).** Trigger: no delivery scan by `SM_O74_NOTE_TRANSIT_3BD` + 2 days, or the custodian reports the package received without the note. Steps: carrier trace and claim; settlement-agent written attestation of shipment contents; custodian search; `note.lost_in_transit` when the "thorough and diligent search" (E-1.1-02 standard, applied by policy) is documented. Decision (`officer`, within `SM_O74_LNA_DECISION_5BD`): **re-execution first** — the borrower signs a replacement note identical in terms (marked "REPLACEMENT NOTE — original lost; this note replaces and does not add to the obligation"), with the partner's counsel confirming no new consummation/rescission event arises (**[UNVERIFIED legal position — open question 3]**) and the lost original covered by an LNA; **LNA alone** only if the borrower cannot or will not re-execute — executed by the partner's `signing_officer`, notarized, with indemnification protecting Fannie Mae, the note description (loan amount, borrower name, note date) and a copy of the note (RDC as extracted), and its certifiability confirmed with the FCC/Fannie Mae *before* delivery (no LNA row in E-2-01; no SFC on the 09.09.2026 list). Warehouse effect (27.1): `unsecured_wet` until cured; repurchase at day 60 if not. Servicing hand-off: `custody_records.lost_note = true` and the LNA in the file for 13.3/E-1.1-02.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`post-closing` agent (tools: `computeMersAnchor`, `registerMin` / `preCloseRegisterMin` / `updateMin` / `reverseMin` / `deactivateMin` (MERS adapter), `ensureEndorsement`, `requestBaileeLetter` (27.1), `prepareShipment`, `trackShipment`, `ingestTrustReceipt`, `parseCustodianNotice`, `openCustodianException`, `handleLostNote`, `expectTrailingDocuments`, `ingestRecordedImage`, `reviewRecordedInstrument`, `requestRerecording`, `reviewFinalPolicy`, `requestPolicyCorrection`, `followUp`, `reconcileEregistry`, `reconcileMersSystem`, `voidCollateral`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: on `closing.consummated` it computes the MERS anchor and due dates, submits (or activates) the registration in the next batch, creates the expected-document list from the document-set profile; on `warehouse.advance.funded` it sets the Interim Funder; for paper notes it verifies the endorsement, obtains the bailee letter, monitors the shipment scan-by-scan, ingests the trust receipt and any exception, and drives cures; on every recording/policy/certificate receipt it reviews against the executed set and the commitment and closes or opens defects; it runs the follow-up ladder and the monthly reconciliations; on cancellation it voids the collateral trail. Decision record: `{loan_id, min, anchor{kind, date, due_at, target_at}, registration{batch_ref, response, fields}, endorsement{method, authority_ids, custodian_result}, shipment{tracking_ref, scans[], trust_receipt_at, exceptions[]}, trailing_documents[{kind, due_at, received_at, review{checks, result, defects}}], reconciliations[], rationale, confidence, rule_set_versions, model_version, prompt_version}`. Guardrails: never signs or applies an endorsement, allonge, assignment, LNA or affidavit (the partner's `signing_officer` does; a facsimile endorsement is rendered only from the partner's authority file); never registers a MIN with an Investor other than the partner (pre-purchase) or on an Org ID other than the partner's; never names SM or Fannie Mae as endorsee; never ships a paper note without a bailee letter or to an unlisted address; never accepts a copy for an original; never closes a trailing document without a passed review; never waives a document (`officer` only); never submits an eRegistry Transfer of Control (29.4/partner authority); never converts an eNote to paper. Escalations: `signing_officer` (endorsement/allonge cures, Maine 3749, LNAs, replacement-note packages; SLA 2 `business_days_creditor`), `officer` (LNA decisions, waivers, releases of notes, 120-day escalations, reconciliation variances, cancelled-loan releases; SLA 2 BD), `settlement_agent` (shipments, recorded images, policies; SLA 2 BD), `fnma_portal_operator` (custodian/MERS portals where no interface exists), `human_agent` (borrower re-execution outreach). Human path (`origination.ai_postclosing = off`): the ops console exposes the same queues (registration batch review, custody exceptions, trailing-document aging) with manual actions.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Funding slips after registration (dry state): the MERS anchor moves with `funding_date`; the registration already made stays valid; `registration_due_at` recomputed for reporting only.
- Loan funded on a Friday in an escrow state with the seventh day on a weekend: the deadline is calendar (Sat/Sun count) — the batch runs the preceding business day; never rely on Monday.
- MIN check-digit or Org-ID mismatch between the recorded instrument and the registration → registration rejected; if the instrument is already recorded with a wrong MIN, re-recording is usually unnecessary (the MIN is an identifier, not a term) — counsel/MERS guidance per state (**[UNVERIFIED]**); register the MIN as printed and correct the SOR.
- Settlement agent affixes the allonge to a copy or forgets it → custodian exception; `signing_officer` executes a replacement allonge; the FCC affixes only under the partner's written instruction (custodian policy) — otherwise the note returns to the endorsement desk.
- Facsimile endorsement in a state without a legal opinion on file → `SM_O74_ENDORSEMENT_BEFORE_SHIP_GATE` fails; fall back to the allonge or wet endorsement.
- Trust in inter vivos revocable trust loans: the note signed "individually and as Trustee" (E-2-04) — custodian exception if either capacity is missing → name affidavit cannot cure; re-execution path.
- POA-signed note: the custodian verifies the POA was valid at signing; a copy of the POA is a custodial document (E-2-01) — ship it with the note; recorded POA (where required) tracked as `recorded_poa`.
- Custodian receives the note before the bailee letter → holds without acknowledging SM's interest; 27.1's advance stays `unsecured_wet` until the letter is acknowledged — the platform prevents this by gating the shipment.
- eNote loan where the partner's eVault vendor changes mid-warehouse → Transfer of Location by the Controller/Delegatee; SM as Secured Party approves; `location_change_history` recorded; 27.1 collateral status unchanged.
- County rejects the recording for a formatting defect after the loan funded (wet state) → 26.2 cures within 2 BD; the title policy's gap coverage (2021 form) and CPL protect; the trailing item stays `open`.
- Recorded instrument returned for a county that retains originals → recorded image/certified copy closes the item with an `officer` waiver of the original.
- Final policy names MERS as insured → defect (B7-2-03 prohibition); corrected policy required; QC exposure logged if delivery has occurred.
- Final policy amount below the loan amount (loan amount increased after the commitment) → defect; endorsement/corrected policy for the full original principal.
- Prior lien not released within the state's statutory period (informational item): SM as subservicer opens a borrower-assistance task (servicing 16.x/7.x) — the title policy insures priority regardless.
- Lost note discovered only at delivery (custodian reports no note in the package) → immediate trace; 29.4 withdraws the loan from the delivery batch if the commitment cannot wait; LNA/re-execution path.
- Partner's MERS membership lapses or SM's user access is revoked → registrations fail; sev 1 to `officer`; MERS deadlines cannot be extended.
- Purchase fixture with a Community Second (subordinate financing): the subordinate lien's recording is the DPA provider's; SM tracks `recorded_subordination` only when 24.4 required a subordination agreement.

#### Audit and evidence
- Per loan: MERS registration payloads/responses with the anchor computation and due dates; Interim Funder set/removal times; endorsement method, signer, authority documents; shipment scans and trust receipts; custodian exceptions and cures; recorded-instrument review checklists with hashes; final-policy review against the commitment; reconciliation snapshots; cancelled-loan clean-up trail.
- For Fannie Mae QC/MORA/custodian audits: B8-3-04/E-2-01 compliance (endorsement, allonge, MIN, custodial documents), B8-7-01 (MIN on the security instrument; Investor at registration; MERS accuracy), C2-2-03 warehouse release evidence (with 27.2), A2-4.1-02 record completeness, E-1.1-02 readiness (note/copy, recorded mortgage, policy) for the servicing file.
- For MERS QA: registration timeliness by anchor, reconciliation results, reversal/deactivation reasons.
- For the partner: monthly collateral report (registrations, custody aging, trailing-document aging, exceptions by settlement agent/county/underwriter), and every `signing_officer`/`officer` touchpoint with SLA compliance.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Custodian of record for paper notes (26.4-Q1; = 27.1-Q5/29.4-Q2).** Default: the partner's Form 2017 FCC holds paper notes for SM as bailee from post-closing (bailee letter + 9-313(c) acknowledgment), so notes are pre-positioned for certification; SM-designated custodian only if the FCC will not sign the acknowledgment. Confirm with the FCC that pre-positioned notes satisfy "first morning delivery" (29.4) and that the FCC will accept bailee letters loan-by-loan.
2. **Endorsement method.** Default: `printed_facsimile` where the partner holds the B8-3-04 four-document file for the state (build the file for the launch states first), otherwise `allonge_pre_executed`; `wet_post_closing` only as a fallback. Counsel to confirm the enforceability of a pre-executed allonge affixed by the settlement agent and the "note must clearly reference the attached allonge" legend in each state.
3. **Lost-note remedy.** Default: re-execution by the borrower (replacement note) plus an LNA for the lost original; LNA-only requires `officer` approval and the FCC's/Fannie Mae's confirmation of certifiability (no E-2-01 row; no SFC on the 09.09.2026 list). Counsel to confirm that re-execution creates no new consummation/rescission event and how the replacement note is described on the eRegistry/MERS records.
4. **MERS registration timing policy.** Default: no Pre-Closing Registration (opt-in switch `mers.pre_closing_registration`; 26.1-Q5), active registration in the next daily batch after the note date, Interim Funder at advance — well inside every anchor. Alternative (register only at funding, as 26.1-Q5 phrased it) rejected because the purchase anchor is the note date and dry-state funding slips would push loans toward the MERS limit; a post-signing re-draw is handled by reversal plus re-registration.
5. **Trailing-document SLAs.** Defaults: eRecorded image 5 BD; paper recording 90 days (county override); final policy 60 days from recording; 120-day `officer` escalation; 180-day underwriter/CPL escalation and certified-copy substitution. No Fannie Mae deadline exists; tune from observed county/underwriter performance after 90 days of production.
6. **MERS deactivation reason for unwound funded loans and the Registration Reversal window.** Default: reversal when the loan never funded or the registration was in error; deactivation with the MERS-specified reason otherwise; confirm codes in the member-only procedures.
7. **Reconciliation cadence.** Default monthly for both the eRegistry and the MERS System (MERS QA plan); daily hash checks for eNotes in Location.
