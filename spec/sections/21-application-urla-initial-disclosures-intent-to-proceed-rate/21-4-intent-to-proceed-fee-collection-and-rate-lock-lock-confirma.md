### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Fee restriction and intent to proceed — §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026; 00a-fed §1.4) — VERIFIED.** "Except as provided in paragraph (e)(2)(i)(B) of this section, neither a creditor nor any other person may impose a fee on a consumer in connection with the consumer's application for a mortgage transaction subject to paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section before the consumer has received the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section and indicated to the creditor an intent to proceed with the transaction described by those disclosures." Two conditions, both required: (1) receipt of the LE and (2) an indication of intent. "Received" incorporates the mailbox presumption: §1026.2(a)(6) lists §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A) among the provisions that use the *specific* business-day definition ("all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays"; 00a-fed §1.2), so absent evidence of earlier receipt the LE is received three specific business days after delivery or mailing (§1026.19(e)(1)(iv); 21.2's `REGZ_1026_19E1IV_LE_MAILBOX_3SBD` derives `effective_receipt_date`).

**Credit-report exception — §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(B) — VERIFIED.** "A creditor or other person may impose a bona fide and reasonable fee for obtaining the consumer's credit report before the consumer has received the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section."

**Pre-LE written estimates — §1026.19(e)(2)(ii) — VERIFIED.** Any consumer-specific written estimate given before the LE must state at the top of the first page in ≥ 12-point type: "Your actual rate, payment, and costs could be higher. Get an official Loan Estimate before choosing a loan." (20.4 owns the statement template `NTC_REGZ_1026_19E2II_QUOTE_DISCLAIMER` and the gate `REGZ_1026_19E2II_QUOTE_DISCLAIMER_GATE`; a pre-LE *lock quote* is such an estimate.)

**No verifying documents before the LE — §1026.19(e)(2)(iii) — VERIFIED.** "The creditor or other person shall not require a consumer to submit documents verifying information related to the consumer's application before providing the disclosures required by paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section." The TRID FAQ (page modified Mar 15, 2024; 00a-fed §1.10) adds that conditioning the LE on verifying documents "could be analyzed as an unfair, deceptive, or abusive act" (12 U.S.C. 5531, 5536). Consequence for this process: the lock request may not be conditioned on documents either, because a lock presupposes the LE.

**Manner and record of intent — Official Interpretation comment 19(e)(2)(i)(A)-2 — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (Supplement I to Part 1026 exceeds every fetch path available today; the operative phrases were confirmed through the eCFR search API against the Supplement I version effective March 1, 2026: "consumer may indicate an intent to proceed with a transaction in any manner the consumer chooses" and "a consumer's silence is not indicative of intent because it cannot be documented").** The comment, as the CFPB Small Entity Compliance Guide v5.2 (May 2018, §13.5) restates it: the consumer "may indicate an intent to proceed in any manner the consumer chooses, unless the creditor requires a particular manner of communication"; oral communication in person or by phone, written communication (e-mail) and signing a pre-printed form all suffice if they occur *after* receipt of the LE; the creditor must document the communication to satisfy the §1026.25 record requirement (three years after consummation or the date the action was required — §1026.25(c)(1)(i), 00a-fed §15). Comments 19(e)(2)(i)(A)-3 to -5 (a fee is "imposed" when the person requires the consumer to provide a method of payment even if the payment is not made at that time — e.g., a post-dated check or a card number held for later; and "in connection with" covers appraisal, application, rate-lock and similar fees paid to any party) and 19(e)(2)(i)(B)-1 (the credit-report fee must be bona fide and reasonable — e.g., not exceed the actual cost of the report) are **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — same reason; content per the CFPB guide and the Bureau's 2013 preamble as remembered; 25.1 must re-read the comments on eCFR before treating them as test oracles]**.

**Rate-lock disclosure — §1026.37(a)(13) (CFPB eRegs, current version) — VERIFIED.** The LE states whether the interest rate is locked; if it is, "the date and time (including the applicable time zone) when that period ends" ((a)(13)(i)); and the statement that "the interest rate, any points, and any lender credits may change unless the interest rate has been locked" together with "the date and time (including the applicable time zone) at which estimated closing costs expire" ((a)(13)(ii)). Comments 37(a)(13)-1 to -4 (read on the same page): the rate "is locked for a specific period of time if the creditor has agreed to extend credit to the consumer at a given rate, subject to contingencies described in any rate lock agreement"; the closing-costs expiration disclosure "is required regardless of whether the interest rate is locked"; the creditor determines and discloses the time zone; and "once the consumer indicates an intent to proceed within the time specified by the creditor" the expiration date and time "are left blank on any subsequent revised disclosures" (21.2 owns the 10-business-day expiration timer `REGZ_1026_37A13_COSTS_EXPIRE_10BD`; 21.5 owns the (e)(3)(iv)(E) reset).

**Revised LE on lock — §1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(D) (eCFR 9/08/2026) — VERIFIED.** "The points or lender credits change because the interest rate was not locked when the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section were provided. No later than three business days after the date the interest rate is locked, the creditor shall provide a revised version of the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section to the consumer with the revised interest rate, the points disclosed pursuant to § 1026.37(f)(1), lender credits, and any other interest rate dependent charges and terms." The three days are *general* business days (§1026.2(a)(6) first sentence — (e)(3)(iv)(D) is not in the specific-definition list; 00a-fed §1.2). The receipt limit is separate: §1026.19(e)(4)(ii) — "The creditor shall not provide a revised version of the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section on or after the date on which the creditor provides the disclosures required under paragraph (f)(1)(i) of this section. The consumer must receive any revised version of the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section not later than four business days prior to consummation. If the revised version ... is not provided to the consumer in person, the consumer is considered to have received such version three business days after the creditor delivers or places such version in the mail" — specific business days for both the four-day limit and the presumption ((e)(4)(ii) is in the §1026.2(a)(6) list). Comment 19(e)(3)(iv)(D)-1 (existence confirmed via the eCFR search API: "19(e)(3)(iv)(D) Interest rate dependent charges. 1. Requirements. If the interest…") — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — text not readable]**; the design below issues the revised LE on every lock, relock and float-down whether or not points or credits moved, because the Rate Lock block itself changes and the conservative reading costs nothing.

**Fannie Mae best-efforts commitments — Selling Guide C2-1.2-01 (last updated Aug 06, 2025), C2-1.2-02 (Mar 03, 2021), C2-1.2-03 (Mar 03, 2021); 00a-fnma §5.3; 00b-orig F7 — VERIFIED (topic text).** C2-1.2-01: lenders "may use Fannie Mae's whole loan committing application ... or may contact the Capital Markets Pricing and Sales Desk"; "Fannie Mae confirms its acceptance of the lender's request for a best efforts delivery commitment by providing a confirmation to the lender that the loan has been committed." C2-1.2-03: "Best efforts commitments are for a specific borrower, address, and commitment period. Loan substitution is not allowed."; "Lenders must notify Fannie Mae via the whole loan committing application within one business day of any change to the key data or product type that occurs during the commitment period."; where the revised loan remains eligible "the acquisition price for the committed loan may be changed to reflect the revised information (using worse case pricing when applicable)"; "Lenders may not change a best efforts commitment to a mandatory commitment (or vice versa)". C2-1.2-02: "best efforts commitment periods may be as short as one day and as long as 90 days"; extensions "up to a maximum of 30 days for a fee", in multiple steps "as long as the total extension period is not longer than 30 days"; a closed loan "will automatically extend for five calendar days until the loan is purchased or the commitment has been extended for a maximum of 60 calendar days from the original expiration date"; no pair-off fee when fallout is "cancellation of an applicant's rate lock, as long as the cancellation results from an applicant withdrawal or lender declination" or a product/key-data change that makes the loan ineligible; a pair-off fee "will be assessed for commitments where the fallout event is due to a failure, for any reason, to deliver the closed and committed loan to Fannie Mae"; a "duplicate commitment price adjustment will be assessed" when a loan is delivered against a duplicate commitment for the same borrower and property "committed prior to or within 30 days of the original commitment's fallout or expiration date". The Loan Pricing API returns "a single pricing response with a quote ID" and the Loan Committing API confirms "the lender's loan commitment at the specified price" using that quote ID "within a specified timeframe" (00b-orig F7; committing windows Mon–Fri 8:15 a.m.–5 p.m. ET, extended best-efforts window 5–11:30 p.m. ET; **the quote-ID validity window is login-gated — [UNVERIFIED]**). Mandatory commitments, pair-offs and extensions are UI-only (00b-orig Part (b) 3).

**SAFE Act / Reg H — 12 CFR 1008.103 and Appendix A (eCFR 9/08/2026; 00a-fed §12) — VERIFIED.** "Offers or negotiates terms" includes "Presenting for consideration by a borrower or prospective borrower particular loan terms, whether verbally, in writing, or otherwise" and "Communicating directly or indirectly with a borrower or prospective borrower for the purpose of reaching a mutual understanding about prospective residential mortgage loan terms". A lock is the paradigm case of presenting particular terms; no regulator statement on AI systems doing so exists (00a-fed §12, UNVERIFIED-negative). Hence the `mlo_of_record` approval before any lock is offered to the borrower.

**State lock-agreement laws — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (LII/Justia mirrors of the NYCRR and NJAC; Massachusetts Division of Banks industry letter read on mass.gov).**
- New York, 3 NYCRR §38.6(b): lock-in fees and points "must be refunded in full" if "the property appraisal report is not favorable for the product locked in" or if the applicant, having supplied complete and correct credit information, "is rejected as not creditworthy"; "The lock-in agreement shall be binding on both the applicant and the lender when such agreement is signed by the applicant and the lender" ((b)(3)); a separate written notice of lock-in expiration must be mailed or transmitted "not less than 12 business days nor more than 20 business days prior to the expiration" when the expiration is more than 12 business days from the lock date ((b)(4)); signatures may be "hand-written or digital" where recognized under New York law ((b)(5)); a copy must be promptly delivered ((b)(6)); electronic agreements and expiration notices must be followed by a hard copy within three business days for applicants who cannot download or print ((b)(7)–(8)). §38.4 (commitments): the commitment must be irrevocable and acceptable by the borrower for "not less than seven calendar days" ((a)(2)(i)) with an expiration date that "must be a reasonable time for a consumer to arrange for a closing date" ((a)(2)(iii)); commitment fees refundable on an unfavorable appraisal or a not-creditworthy rejection ((c)(7)–(8)); separate written expiration notice "not less than 12 business days nor more than 20 business days prior to the expiration" ((d)).
- Massachusetts, Division of Banks industry letter "Rate Lock Commitments" (July 31, 2003; 209 CMR 42.02, 42.11A): only licensed/authorized lenders (not brokers) may issue rate locks; a licensed lender must maintain "a net worth of at least $500,000" to do so and notify the Division within one business day if it falls below; rate-lock commitments "which, through no fault of the borrower, expire before the closing takes place, must be honored at any closing subsequent to the expiration", including delays caused by the lender's "agents, servicers, or employees".
- New Jersey, N.J.A.C. 3:1-16.4: a written lock-in agreement must state "The expiration date of the lock-in, if any", "The interest rate locked in, if any", "The discount points locked in, if any", "The commitment fee locked in, if any", "The lock-in fee, if any" and the advisory statement; the lender "shall make a good faith effort to process the mortgage loan application ... before the expiration date of the lock-in agreement and any extension thereof"; "In the event a lock-in agreement is executed and the loan applied for is denied, the lender shall promptly refund any lock-in fee paid"; mailed/broker-delivered agreements require the lender's signature to become effective and the borrower may rescind until receiving the signed copy.
- Other states with lock statutes (e.g., Pennsylvania Mortgage Licensing Act lock-in provisions, Connecticut, Minnesota) — **[UNVERIFIED — not fetched; 31.1 maintains `jurisdiction_rules.lock_agreement`]**.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. The Selling Guide citation "C1-1/C1-2 (best efforts / mandatory commitments)" is wrong: best-efforts commitments are C2-1.2-01/-02/-03, mandatory commitments C2-1.1-01…-06, execution options C1-1-01; C1-2-02/-04 are delivery topics (00a-fnma §5.3).
2. The row treats "lock expiration vs closing date" as a hard stop. Massachusetts requires an expired lock to be honored when the delay is not the borrower's fault; the design makes the lender-caused-delay rule national policy.
3. The row omits §1026.2(a)(6): "received" in (e)(2)(i)(A) runs on the specific business-day presumption, while the (e)(3)(iv)(D) three days run on general business days and the (e)(4)(ii) four-day limit on specific days — three calendars in one process.
4. The row does not say that the fee rule binds "any other person" — SM cannot collect, hold or capture a payment method for any fee (other than the credit-report fee) before receipt plus intent, whatever the partner's instructions.
5. The row's "revised LE on lock" hides the interaction with (e)(4)(ii): a lock within four specific business days of consummation, or after the CD is provided, cannot be disclosed on a revised LE — it goes on the CD/corrected CD (25.2), possibly with a new waiting period if the APR moves beyond §1026.22 tolerance.
6. Fixture: the 45-day lock from Wed Oct 7, 2026 expires Sat Nov 21, 2026; the lock policy must say what a weekend expiration means (default below: roll to the next creditor business day at no cost). Fixture P&I is $3,402.62, not $3,402.63 (21.2 discrepancy 5; same arithmetic here).
7. A best-efforts commitment is not a borrower lock and has different clocks (1–90 days, 30-day extension cap, 60-day closed-loan auto-extension, one-business-day key-data notice); the row conflates them.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `disclosure.le.received` / `disclosure.le.mailed` (21.2) with `effective_receipt_date` on `disclosures`; `disclosure.le.revised` (21.5) for later LE versions.
- Borrower actions: intent expressed in the app (button), by chat/voice (AI-recorded utterance), by e-mail, by signing the LE's optional signature line (21.2 `NTC_REGZ_1026_37_LE` — the signature line only confirms receipt unless the form also records intent, §1026.37(n)), or in person at the partner; lock/relock/extension/float-down requests by the same channels.
- Pricing: `pricing_quotes` (20.4) with `quote_id_fnma` from the Loan Pricing API where a commitment will be requested; rate-sheet change events.
- Timers: `REGZ_1026_37A13_COSTS_EXPIRE_10BD` (21.2) expiry; lock expiry warnings; MLO SLA.
- Fee commands from other processes: `imposeFee`, `capturePaymentMethod`, `orderAppraisal` (24.1), `orderTitle` (24.4), `orderFloodDetermination` (24.5), `orderPropertyDataCollection` (24.1) — each calls `assertGateOpen(application_id, REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE)`.
- Downstream needs: 28.3 (`rate_set_date`), 23.4/25.1 (APOR as of the rate-set date), 29.1 (commitment), 29.2 (pipeline), 25.2 (CD Rate Lock block).

Event `intent.to_proceed.received` (baseline) carries `{intent_id, disclosure_id, channel}`; `intent.to_proceed.rejected_premature` when a statement arrives before receipt (the agent re-solicits after receipt).

`fee_gate_checks` (new, append-only): `check_id`, `application_id`, `command` ∈ {impose_fee, capture_payment_method, order_appraisal, order_title, order_flood, order_pdc, order_credit_report}, `fee_item_id null`, `amount_cents`, `checked_at`, `result` ∈ {open, closed_no_receipt, closed_no_intent, exempt_credit_report}, `basis jsonb` (`le_effective_receipt_date`, `intent_id`), `actor`. Event `fee.gate.checked`; `fee.imposed{fee_item_id, amount_cents, method}` on success (ledger posting to `origination_fees_receivable`, 21.2 note).

`locks` (baseline §3; columns added here): `lock_id uuid`, `application_id`, `lineage_id uuid` (all versions of the lock for one application), `version int`, `kind` ∈ {initial, extension, relock, float_down, renegotiation}, `supersedes_lock_id null`, `status` (state machine below), `requested_at`, `quote_id` (`pricing_quotes`), `quote_id_fnma text null`, `mlo_approval_escalation_id`, `approved_at`, `locked_at timestamptz` (= execution time; the "date the interest rate is locked" for (e)(3)(iv)(D)), `rate_set_date date` (creditor time zone; HMDA/APOR anchor), `note_rate numeric(6,4)` (four decimals, e.g., 6.1250), `price numeric(9,5)` (percent of par, five decimals), `points_cents bigint` (≥ 0; §1026.37(f)(1)), `lender_credit_cents bigint` (≥ 0), `lock_period_days int`, `expires_on date`, `expires_at timestamptz` (5:00 p.m. creditor time zone by default), `expiry_roll_applied boolean`, `time_zone text`, `product_code`, `loan_amount_cents`, `worst_case_pricing_applied boolean`, `extension_fee_cents`, `extension_payer` ∈ {borrower, lender_delay, lender_goodwill}, `float_down_fee_cents`, `commitment_id null` (29.1 `commitments.commitment_id`), `revised_le_disclosure_id null`, `state_agreement_variant text null` (NY/NJ/MA), `cancelled_reason` ∈ {borrower_withdrawal, lender_declination, product_change_ineligible, expired, superseded}. Child `lock_extensions`: `extension_id`, `lock_id`, `days int`, `fee_cents`, `payer`, `granted_at`, `new_expires_on`. Events: `lock.requested`, `lock.approved` (MLO), `lock.rejected{reason}` (MLO returns the terms; satisfies `SM_LOCK_MLO_APPROVAL_SLA_30MIN` without a lock), `lock.executed` (baseline; the canonical event other sections consume; payload `{lock_id, lineage_id, version, note_rate, price, points_cents, lender_credit_cents, lock_period_days, expires_at, rate_set_date, quote_id_fnma}`), `lock.extended` (baseline), `lock.relocked`, `lock.float_down.applied`, `lock.expiry.warned`, `lock.expired`, `lock.cancelled`, `lock.commitment.linked{commitment_id}` (29.1 emits `commitment.executed`; this process records the link).

`changed_circumstances` (baseline; 21.5 owns semantics): this process inserts a row with `kind='rate_lock'`, `discovered_at = locked_at`, `revised_le_due_at` (computed by the timer below) for every `lock.executed`, `lock.relocked`, `lock.float_down.applied` and any `lock.extended` whose fee is charged to the borrower (that one is `kind='borrower_request'`, (e)(3)(iv)(C)).

`notices`: `NTC_SM_RATE_LOCK_CONFIRMATION` (new, owned here; the "lock agreement": borrower names, property, product, note rate, points/credits in dollars, lock period, expiration date and time with time zone, extension/relock/float-down terms, fee refund rules, conditions, partner as lender, MLO name and NMLSR ID; NY variant adds §38.6 content and signature blocks for applicant and lender; NJ variant adds the §3:1-16.4(a) items and advisory statement; MA variant adds the honor-after-expiration statement); `NTC_NY_3NYCRR_38_6_LOCK_EXPIRY_NOTICE` (new, NY only). `NTC_REGZ_1026_37_LE_REVISED` is 21.5's and is referenced.

`escalations`: `kind='mlo_lock_approval'` with SLA timer `SM_LOCK_MLO_APPROVAL_SLA_30MIN`. `agent_decisions`: schema in the AI section.

#### State machine

`locks.status`: `requested` → (`pricing` agent obtains quote, runs guardrails) → `pending_mlo_approval` → (`mlo_of_record` approves within SLA) → `executed` (at `locked_at`; emits `lock.executed`; opens `REGZ_1026_19E3IVD_LOCK_REVISED_LE_3BD`; requests the commitment) → `confirmed` (confirmation delivered) → {`extended` (child row; status stays `confirmed`), `superseded` (a relock/float-down/renegotiation creates a new version whose `supersedes_lock_id` points here), `expired` (expiry passed without consummation and without extension — the loan may still close at policy/MA rules via a `relock` or an `extension` decision), `cancelled` (borrower withdrawal, lender declination, product change to ineligible, application denied), `consummated` (closing occurred inside the lock; terminal for the borrower lock; the commitment continues in 29.1)}. `pending_mlo_approval` → `quote_expired` when the SLA lapses or the rate sheet changes before approval (the borrower is re-quoted; no lock exists). Guards: `executed` requires a valid `intent_records` row for the current LE version (a lock is itself an expression of intent only if recorded after LE receipt — the agent records the intent first, then the lock), `mlo_approval`, a non-stale rate sheet, `loan_amount_cents` and product within the quote, and no open `decisions.kind='denial'`. A lock may be executed before the LE is received (the borrower may lock at pre-application on some lenders' policies), but then the LE itself discloses the lock and no revised LE is needed — this design does not offer pre-LE locks (open question 2).

`intent_records.valid` is computed at insert: `received_at::date ≥ le_effective_receipt_date` in the creditor time zone; an invalid record is kept (evidence) and flagged.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Fee gate.** `gate_open = (today_creditor_tz ≥ le_effective_receipt_date) ∧ (∃ intent_records.valid)`. `le_effective_receipt_date = min(received_at::date with evidence, deemed_receipt_date)` from 21.2. The credit-report exemption applies only to `fee_items.kind='credit_report'` and only up to the vendor's invoiced cost (comment 19(e)(2)(i)(B)-1 as understood — flagged above): `charge_cents ≤ credit_vendor_invoice_cents`. "Impose" includes capturing a payment method: the app collects a card/ACH token for any non-credit fee only after the gate opens; a token collected for the credit-report fee is single-use and may not be re-used for later fees without a fresh authorization after the gate opens.
2. **Intent validity.** `valid = received_at::date ≥ le_effective_receipt_date` where dates are in the creditor time zone; intent against LE version *n* remains valid for later versions unless the borrower withdraws it; a statement received before receipt is stored with `valid=false` and the agent re-asks after receipt ("Now that you have the Loan Estimate, do you want to proceed?").
3. **Revised-LE due date.** `revised_le_due = addBusinessDays(locked_at::date, 3, calendar='creditor')` end of day. Receipt limit: `latest_receipt = subtractBusinessDays(consummation_date, 4, calendar='regz_specific')`; if `locked_at::date > latest_receipt − 3 specific business days` and delivery is not in person/e-confirmed, the revised LE cannot be presumed received in time — the agent uses e-delivery with confirmed receipt (actual receipt counts) or reflects the lock on the CD; if `locked_at ≥ cd_provided_at` (25.2), no revised LE is permitted at all and the terms go on the CD/corrected CD.
4. **Lock expiration.** `expires_on = locked_at::date + lock_period_days` (calendar days); if `expires_on` is not a `creditor` business day, `expires_on := nextBusinessDay(expires_on, 'creditor')` and `expiry_roll_applied=true` (default policy; borrower-favorable); `expires_at = expires_on 17:00 creditor time zone` (Phoenix: MST all year; disclosed as "5:00 p.m. MST"). The LE/CD Rate Lock block shows `expires_at` with the time zone (§1026.37(a)(13)(i)).
5. **Points and credits in dollars.** `points_cents = round_half_up(loan_amount_cents × points_pct / 100)`; `lender_credit_cents` likewise; premium/discount is expressed on `price`; the LE shows points in §1026.37(f)(1) and credits in section J; the SM economics keep the SM-borne-cost credit (21.2 rule 6) separate from rate-dependent credits — both are zero-tolerance for 21.5.
6. **Extensions.** `extension_fee_cents = round_half_up(loan_amount_cents × extension_bps / 10,000)` per the partner's schedule (default 12.5 bps per 7 days, 25 bps per 15 days — policy, open question 3); payer = borrower when the delay is the borrower's (documented cause), lender when the delay is the lender's/agents' (MA mandatory; national policy); a borrower-paid extension fee is a borrower-requested change under (e)(3)(iv)(C) → 21.5 revised LE within 3 creditor business days (`REGZ_1026_19E4_REVISED_LE_3BD`) or on the CD; a lender-paid extension is not a consumer charge and needs no revised LE unless another term changes.
7. **Relock after expiry.** `relock_price = min(current_price, original_price)` ("worst-case") unless the lender-delay rule applies; a relock is a new lock version → `lock.relocked` → revised LE within 3 creditor business days ((e)(3)(iv)(D) — "the date the interest rate is locked" is the relock date).
8. **Float-down.** Offered once per lineage if the partner policy permits: `new_rate = current_market_rate + float_down_margin`, `float_down_fee_cents` as scheduled; produces `lock.float_down.applied` → revised LE; the reduced rate lowers P&I and APR (a decrease never causes a tolerance issue but the CD APR test in 25.1 still runs).
9. **Commitment linkage (best efforts default).** On `lock.executed`, `pricing` requests a best-efforts commitment through `secondary` (29.1) for the same borrower and property with the commitment expiry 29.1 computes — `expires_on = nextBusinessDay(lock.expires_on + 14, fannie_sifma)`, capped at 90 days and at the six-month loan-age gate (29.1-Q1; this supersedes the earlier "lock period + 15" buffer — both give Mon Dec 7, 2026 on the fixture) — using the `quote_id_fnma` when the quote is still valid, else a fresh Loan Pricing call; `commitment.executed` → `lock.commitment.linked`. Extensions/relocks/float-downs emit the key-data-change event for 29.1 to update the commitment within one business day (worst-case pricing on the acquisition price is Fannie Mae's, not the borrower's). Lock cancellation for borrower withdrawal or lender declination → 29.1 records fallout without pair-off; failure to deliver a closed loan → pair-off (29.1). Mandatory execution (`execution.mandatory_enabled=true`) decouples locks from commitments (29.2 hedges the pipeline) — nothing changes in this process except that `commitment_id` stays null.
10. **Rate-set date.** `rate_set_date = locked_at::date` for HMDA rate spread (§1003.4(a)(12): APOR "as of the date the interest rate is set" — VERIFIED rule text; the commentary on which date governs after relocks — the final rate-set before final action — is **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — comment not readable]**; design: the `rate_set_date` of the latest executed lock version at consummation) and for APOR in 23.4/25.1.
11. **MLO approval.** Every `lock.requested` opens an `mlo_lock_approval` escalation with the quote, guardrail results and the borrower's statement; the AI presents terms to the borrower only after `lock.approved`; approval is recorded with the MLO's NMLSR ID on `locks` and reproduced on the confirmation and the revised LE (§1026.36(g)).

**Worked example 1 — refinance fixture.** LE v1 delivered Mon Oct 5, 2026 16:10 MST via the e-sign portal; borrower views and acknowledges 17:42 → `effective_receipt_date` 2026-10-05 (21.2). Tue Oct 6, 09:14 MST the borrower taps "I want to proceed with this Loan Estimate" in the app → `intent_records` row (channel `app_button`, `valid=true`), `intent.to_proceed.received`, `REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE` opens at 09:14. At 09:20 the `valuation` agent (24.1) calls `orderAppraisal` with the $650 AMC fee → `fee_gate_checks.result='open'`, the fee is authorized on the borrower's card (`fee.imposed` $650.00 → `origination_fees_receivable`). Had the same order been attempted Mon Oct 5 at 18:00 (LE received, no intent), the result would be `closed_no_intent` and the appraisal would not be ordered; the $75 credit-report fee collected Oct 5 at 10:50 before the LE existed was allowed (`exempt_credit_report`; vendor invoice $68.50 ≤ $75 — **note**: under the reading flagged above the charge may not exceed actual cost, so the engine caps the collected amount at the invoiced $68.50 and the LE's $75 estimate remains a zero-tolerance ceiling). Wed Oct 7, 2026 10:05 MST the borrower asks to lock; the `pricing` agent prices 6.125 % at 100.000 (no points, no rate-dependent credit; the −$2,617 SM-borne-cost credit is unchanged), 45 days; escalation to the MLO of record 10:06; approved 10:19 (within the 30-minute SLA); `lock.executed` 10:19 MST → `locked_at` 2026-10-07, `rate_set_date` 2026-10-07. `expires_on` = Oct 7 + 45 = **Sat Nov 21, 2026** → rolled to **Mon Nov 23, 2026, 5:00 p.m. MST** (`expiry_roll_applied=true`). Revised-LE due date: Thu Oct 8 (1), Fri Oct 9 (2), Mon Oct 12 is Columbus Day and the partner is closed, Tue **Oct 13, 2026** (3) → `REGZ_1026_19E3IVD_LOCK_REVISED_LE_3BD` due 23:59 MST Oct 13 (if the partner were open on Oct 12 the due date would be Oct 12 — the calendar is data). 21.5 issues LE v2 Thu Oct 8 15:02 with Rate Lock "YES, until 11/23/2026 at 5:00 p.m. MST", closing-costs expiration blank (intent already given), rate 6.125 %, points $0, lender credits −$2,617, P&I $3,402.62. Commitment: `secondary` executes a best-efforts commitment Oct 7 10:25 MST (12:25 p.m. ET, inside the 8:15–5:00 window) for 60 days (45 + 15) → expires Sun Dec 6, 2026 → `lock.commitment.linked`. Consummation Fri Nov 6 and disbursement Thu Nov 12 fall inside the lock; the closed-loan status is set in PE–WL within one business day of funding (29.1); delivery Mon Nov 16 and purchase Thu Nov 19 fall inside the commitment. HMDA: `rate_set_date` 2026-10-07 → 28.3 rate spread uses the APOR for the week containing Oct 7 (23.4 verifies the APOR table cadence).

**Worked example 2 — extension and late lock against the 4-day rule.** (a) Extension: suppose the appraisal (24.2) is late and the closing moves from Nov 6 to Tue Dec 1, 2026, past the Nov 23 expiry. `delay_attribution='lender_agent'` (the AMC is the partner's/SM's agent) → the lock is honored at 6.125 % through Dec 1 with `extension_payer='lender_delay'` (MA rule applied nationally); `lock_extensions` row +8 days (Nov 23 → Tue Dec 1), fee 12.5 bps = 560,000 × 0.00125 = $700.00 borne by the lender — no consumer charge, no revised LE. If instead the borrower had asked to move the closing to Dec 1 for personal reasons, the $700.00 extension fee is a borrower-requested change under (e)(3)(iv)(C): `changed_circumstances.kind='borrower_request'` dated the request day; the revised LE is due within 3 creditor business days — but the CD was provided Mon Nov 2, so no revised LE may issue; 25.2 reflects the fee on a corrected CD (received at or before consummation; a new three-day wait only if the APR changes by more than 1/8 percentage point — $700 of prepaid finance charge on this loan moves the APR by roughly 0.012 points, so no new wait). (b) Late lock: a borrower who floated until Tue Nov 3, 2026 and locks that day (6.250 %, 0.125 points = $700.00) cannot receive a revised LE: the latest receipt date is 4 specific business days before Fri Nov 6 = Thu Nov 5 (1), Wed Nov 4 (2), Tue Nov 3 (3), **Mon Nov 2 (4)** — already past, and the CD went out Nov 2. The lock terms go on a corrected CD (25.2); the three-day (e)(3)(iv)(D) clock (due Fri Nov 6 = Wed 4, Thu 5, Fri 6) is satisfied by `disclosure.cd.corrected` with `reflected_on='cd'`. Because the APR rises from 6.125 % to about 6.262 % (rate +0.125 plus $700 points on $560,000), the change exceeds the 1/8-point tolerance → new three-specific-business-day waiting period from receipt of the corrected CD: received Tue Nov 3 in person/e-confirmed → consummation no earlier than Sat Nov 7 (Wed 4, Thu 5, Fri 6 = 3 days, consummate on the day after) — the Fri Nov 6 closing slips to Mon Nov 9 (Sunday excluded) — 25.2 owns that computation; this process only warns the borrower before executing a lock that late ("locking today moves your closing to Nov 9").

**Worked example 3 — purchase fixture with a New York overlay (illustrative; the fixture property is in Ohio).** Application Mon Oct 19, 2026; LE delivered Thu Oct 22 (21.2) and received the same day (e-sign); intent Fri Oct 23; lock Mon Oct 26 for 30 days → `expires_on` Wed Nov 25, 2026 (a creditor business day; no roll); revised LE due Thu Oct 29 (Tue 27, Wed 28, Thu 29). Closing Wed Nov 18 sits inside the lock. Were the property in New York: `expires_on − locked_on` = 21 creditor business days > 12 → the §38.6(b)(4) expiration notice window is 20 to 12 business days before Nov 25 = **Tue Oct 27 through Fri Nov 6, 2026** (counting back on the creditor calendar with Nov 11 Veterans Day closed: Nov 24 = 1 … Nov 12 = 9, Nov 10 = 10, Nov 9 = 11, Nov 6 = 12 … Oct 27 = 20; recomputed 2026-09-11 — the earlier "Oct 28–Nov 9" window had counted Veterans Day as open) → `NTC_NY_3NYCRR_38_6_LOCK_EXPIRY_NOTICE` scheduled Thu Oct 29 with the revised LE; the confirmation is countersigned by the partner's signing officer or e-signed by the MLO (open question 4) because §38.6(b)(3) makes the agreement binding only when "signed by the applicant and the lender".

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`pricing` agent (tools: `getQuote`, `checkFeeGate`, `recordIntent`, `requestLock`, `openMloEscalation`, `executeLock`, `renderLockConfirmation`, `requestCommitment` (via `secondary`), `recordChangedCircumstance` (21.5 hook), `quoteExtension`, `extendLock`, `relock`, `applyFloatDown`, `expireLock`, `sendNotice`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: the agent listens for intent statements in every channel, validates them against `effective_receipt_date`, records them, opens the gate, and answers fee questions; on a lock request it prices, runs guardrails (rate sheet freshness, product eligibility per DU findings if any, loan amount within tolerance of the LE, no denial in flight, state variant), opens the MLO escalation with a one-screen package (terms, borrower statement, price vs rate sheet, APOR spread preview from 23.4), and on approval executes, confirms, requests the commitment, inserts the changed-circumstance row and monitors the revised LE. It runs the expiry playbook 7 days before expiry (closing scheduled? extension needed? who caused the delay?) and executes extensions/relocks under the same MLO approval. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{lock_id, application_id, quote_id, rate_sheet_id, llpa_version, guardrail_results[], intent_id, le_disclosure_id, mlo_escalation_id, mlo_nmlsr_id, terms{note_rate, price, points_cents, lender_credit_cents, period_days, expires_at}, revised_le_due_at, commitment_request_id, rationale, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails (never alone): never impose or capture payment for any fee before the gate opens (hard code path, not a prompt); never present particular lock terms to the borrower before `lock.approved`; never change note rate, points or credits without a new lock version and a revised LE/CD; never let a lock lineage carry two open Fannie Mae commitments; never treat silence, an unanswered e-mail or the signature line on the LE alone as intent; never quote "no cost to lock" when a lock fee exists; never infer or use a prohibited-basis characteristic in pricing (31.2 monitoring). Escalations: `mlo_of_record` (every lock/relock/float-down/borrower-paid extension; SLA 30 minutes in-hours), `human_agent` (borrower request), `compliance-sentinel` (gate incident, late revised LE), `licensed_specialist` where a state requires an individual for lock issuance (MA: lender only — the partner). Borrower-facing rules: the AI states that it is automated, that the MLO named on the LE approves the terms, and reads the lock terms back (rate, points/credits, expiration date/time/time zone, what happens at expiry); consent to e-delivery per 21.1. Human path: the lock desk console shows the same queue with manual execute/extend/relock; the MLO can lock directly from the console (still logged as `locks` with `recorded_by=user`).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Intent received on the LE delivery day before the e-sign view (e.g., borrower calls at 15:00, LE viewed at 17:42): `valid=false` (received before receipt); the agent re-confirms after the view; the fee gate stays closed until then.
- LE mailed (no e-consent): receipt presumed 3 specific business days after mailing; an intent by phone before that date is invalid unless the borrower confirms actual earlier receipt (recorded statement "I received it on …" → `received_at` evidence on `disclosures`, 21.2).
- Borrower changes loan amount or product after locking: new lock version with `kind='renegotiation'`; 21.5 evaluates (e)(3)(iv)(C); commitment key-data change to 29.1 within one business day.
- Rate sheet changes between quote and MLO approval: `quote_expired`; re-quote; never execute at a stale price.
- Lock request after the CD is provided: allowed only with the 25.2 corrected-CD path and a borrower warning about a possible new waiting period.
- Lock expires while the file waits on a lender-caused item (late appraisal, title curative caused by the settlement agent chosen from the lender's list): honor at locked terms (`lender_delay`); expiry caused by the borrower's late documents: extension at the borrower's cost or relock at worst-case, borrower's choice.
- Application denied (21.6) after lock: `lock.cancelled{lender_declination}`; commitment fallout without pair-off (C2-1.2-02); NY/NJ: lock fees refunded (§38.6(b)(2); N.J.A.C. 3:1-16.4(c)); the refund posts as a reversal.
- Appraisal comes in low so the locked product/LTV is ineligible: NY §38.6(b)(1) refund; new lock version if the borrower proceeds on a different product.
- Two intents from co-borrowers: one valid intent from any applicant opens the gate (the rule speaks of "the consumer"); the agent records all.
- Withdrawal of intent ("I'm not sure anymore"): no fees may be imposed after `withdrawn_at`; fees already imposed stand; a later renewed intent is a new record.
- PE–WL outage or after-hours lock: lock executes; commitment queued; 29.2 carries the uncommitted position; if the queue exceeds 1 business day the `secondary` agent escalates to `fnma_portal_operator`.
- Time-zone edge: a lock executed 23:30 MST Oct 7 is "locked" Oct 7 in the creditor time zone even though it is Oct 8 in ET; the (e)(3)(iv)(D) anchor is the creditor's date.

- **21.4-T7** Given the CD was provided Mon Nov 2, 2026 and the borrower locks Tue Nov 3 at 6.250 % with $700.00 points, then no `disclosure.le.revised` is produced; a `changed_circumstances{kind='rate_lock', reflected_on='cd'}` row is created; 25.2 receives the terms; and the borrower was warned before execution that the closing may move to Mon Nov 9.
- **21.4-T8** Given the closing slips from Nov 6 to Tue Dec 1, 2026 because the appraisal was late (`delay_attribution='lender_agent'`), then the lock is extended 8 days at 6.125 % with `extension_payer='lender_delay'`, fee $700.00 borne by the lender, no consumer charge and no revised LE/CD change for the fee.
- **21.4-T9** Given the same slip requested by the borrower on Fri Nov 13, then `extension_payer='borrower'`, `changed_circumstances{kind='borrower_request'}` is inserted, and — because the CD was provided Nov 2 — the fee is reflected on a corrected CD (25.2) rather than a revised LE.
- **21.4-T10** Given a New York property, a lock on Mon Oct 26 expiring Wed Nov 25, 2026, then the §38.6(b)(4) notice window is Tue Oct 27–Fri Nov 6 (creditor calendar; Veterans Day closed) and `NTC_NY_3NYCRR_38_6_LOCK_EXPIRY_NOTICE` is sent inside it (a send on Mon Nov 9 would be only 11 business days before expiry and fails the gate); the confirmation carries applicant and lender signature blocks and `state_agreement_variant='NY'`.
- **21.4-T11** Given a best-efforts commitment executed for a lock lineage, when a relock creates version 2, then exactly one open commitment remains, `lock.relocked` triggers the 29.1 key-data update, and a second `commit` call for the same lineage is refused by the adapter.
- **21.4-T12** Given the application is denied Fri Oct 23, 2026 after a lock with a $500 NY lock-in fee, then `lock.cancelled{lender_declination}`, the fee is refunded (ledger reversal) and 29.1 records fallout with `pair_off_expected=false`.

#### Audit and evidence

Per application: the LE receipt evidence chain (21.2) → `intent_records` with the verbatim statement/recording → every `fee_gate_checks` row (including refusals) → ledger postings; the `locks` lineage with quotes, rate-sheet and LLPA versions, MLO approval (NMLSR ID, timestamp), confirmation document hash, commitment number and its status history; `changed_circumstances` and the revised LE/CD that satisfied `REGZ_1026_19E3IVD_LOCK_REVISED_LE_3BD` with delivery/receipt evidence; NY notices with mailing/transmission proof; `agent_decisions` with model/prompt versions (LL-2026-04). Exam packs: CFPB/state "fees before intent" sample (all applications with any fee imposed, joined to receipt and intent timestamps), "revised LE within 3 business days of lock" sample, MA honor-after-expiration cases, NJ/NY agreement content checks; Fannie Mae QC/MORA: commitment-to-lock reconciliation and fallout reasons.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Lock without a prior valid intent.** Some lenders treat a lock request as the intent. Default: the agent records a distinct intent first (a lock request after LE receipt is captured as `intent_records.channel` = the request channel with the borrower's words), so the evidence stands on its own.
2. **Pre-LE locks.** Not offered (default). If Doug wants "lock at pre-qualification", the LE itself would disclose the lock and the pre-LE lock quote must carry the (e)(2)(ii) statement; the fee gate still bars any lock fee before receipt plus intent.
3. **Lock policy economics.** Defaults: periods 15/30/45/60 days; extension 12.5 bps per 7 days, 25 bps per 15 days; relock at worst-case after a 30-day cooling period, otherwise market; one float-down per lineage at 25 bps with a 0.125 % margin; weekend/holiday expiry rolls forward at no cost; lender-caused delay is always the lender's cost (MA rule applied nationally). Grander economics: extension fees charged to borrowers are pass-through (cost recovery), not revenue.
4. **Who signs the NY lock agreement for the lender.** Default: the MLO of record e-signs under the partner's authorization (§38.6(b)(5) digital signatures); the partner's `signing_officer` is the fallback where the partner's corporate policy requires an officer.
5. **After-hours locks.** Default: locks accepted 24/7 on SM's rate sheet; commitments placed in the next PE–WL window; SM (not the borrower) bears the overnight basis risk within a cap set by the partner's policy; above the cap the lock is queued.
6. **NY "business days" unit for §38.6(b)(4).** Default: creditor calendar; confirm with NY DFS guidance (31.1).
