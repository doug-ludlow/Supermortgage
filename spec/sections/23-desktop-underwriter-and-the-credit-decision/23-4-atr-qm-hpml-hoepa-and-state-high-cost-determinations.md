### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**ATR — 12 CFR 1026.43 (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026).** §1026.43(c)(1): "A creditor shall not make a loan that is a covered transaction unless the creditor makes a reasonable and good faith determination at or before consummation that the consumer will have a reasonable ability to repay the loan according to its terms." The eight factors, §1026.43(c)(2): (i) "The consumer's current or reasonably expected income or assets, other than the value of the dwelling, including any real property attached to the dwelling, that secures the loan"; (ii) "If the creditor relies on income from the consumer's employment in determining repayment ability, the consumer's current employment status"; (iii) "The consumer's monthly payment on the covered transaction, calculated in accordance with paragraph (c)(5)"; (iv) "The consumer's monthly payment on any simultaneous loan that the creditor knows or has reason to know will be made, calculated in accordance with paragraph (c)(6)"; (v) "The consumer's monthly payment for mortgage-related obligations"; (vi) "The consumer's current debt obligations, alimony, and child support"; (vii) "The consumer's monthly debt-to-income ratio or residual income in accordance with paragraph (c)(7)"; (viii) "The consumer's credit history." §1026.43(c)(3): "A creditor must verify the information that the creditor relies on in determining a consumer's repayment ability under §1026.43(c)(2) using reasonably reliable third-party records, except that…"; §1026.43(c)(4) lists acceptable income/asset records (tax returns filed with the IRS or a State taxing authority; IRS Form W-2s; payroll statements including military Leave and Earnings Statements; financial institution records; records from the employer or a third party; government-agency records; check-cashing and funds-transfer receipts).

**General QM — §1026.43(e)(2).** (i) regular periodic payments that are substantially equal, with no negative amortization, deferral of principal or balloon; (ii) "For which the loan term does not exceed 30 years"; (iii) "For which the total points and fees payable in connection with the loan do not exceed the amounts specified in paragraph (e)(3)"; (iv) underwritten using the maximum rate in the first five years with payments that repay the loan; (v)(A) the creditor "Considers the consumer's current or reasonably expected income or assets other than the value of the dwelling (including any real property attached to the dwelling) that secures the loan, debt obligations, alimony, child support, and monthly debt-to-income ratio or residual income"; **(v)(B): "Verifies the consumer's current or reasonably expected income or assets other than the value of the dwelling (including any real property attached to the dwelling) that secures the loan using third-party records that provide reasonably reliable evidence of the consumer's income or assets, in accordance with [paragraph (c)(4)]" and "Verifies the consumer's current debt obligations, alimony, and child support using reasonably reliable third-party records in accordance with [paragraph (c)(3)]."** (vi) APR limits: "(A) For a first-lien covered transaction with a loan amount greater than or equal to $110,260 (indexed for inflation), 2.25 or more percentage points"; "(B)… greater than or equal to $66,156 (indexed for inflation) but less than $110,260 (indexed for inflation), 3.5 or more percentage points"; "(C)… less than $66,156 (indexed for inflation), 6.5 or more percentage points"; "(D)… secured by a manufactured home with a loan amount less than $110,260 (indexed for inflation), 6.5"; "(E)… subordinate-lien… greater than or equal to $66,156… 3.5"; "(F)… subordinate-lien… less than $66,156… 6.5." **2026 tiers (90 FR 57890, eff. Jan 1, 2026; 00a-fed §5.1): first lien ≥ $137,958 → 2.25; ≥ $82,775 and < $137,958 → 3.5; < $82,775 → 6.5; manufactured home < $137,958 → 6.5; subordinate ≥ $82,775 → 3.5; < $82,775 → 6.5.** Points and fees, §1026.43(e)(3)(i): "For a loan amount greater than or equal to $100,000 (indexed for inflation): 3 percent of the total loan amount"; "≥ $60,000… but less than $100,000…: $3,000 (indexed for inflation)"; "≥ $20,000… but less than $60,000…: 5 percent of the total loan amount"; "≥ $12,500… but less than $20,000…: $1,000 (indexed for inflation)"; "less than $12,500…: 8 percent of the total loan amount"; the amounts "shall be adjusted annually on January 1 by the annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U)." **2026 (90 FR 57890; 00a-fed §5.2): 3% for loans ≥ $137,958; $4,139 for ≥ $82,775 and < $137,958; 5% for ≥ $27,592 and < $82,775; $1,380 for ≥ $17,245 and < $27,592; 8% for < $17,245.** Cure, §1026.43(e)(3)(iii): the cure paragraph opens "For covered transactions consummated on or before January 10, 2021" (eCFR current as of 9/8/2026, re-read 2026-09-11) — only such loans remain QMs if the creditor or assignee pays the excess plus interest "within 210 days after consummation and prior to the occurrence of" the listed events (consumer action, written notice of the excess, or 60 days past due), with the (e)(3)(iv) policies-and-procedures condition. **The cure is therefore unavailable for every loan this platform originates: a post-consummation points-and-fees excess is `not_qm`, full stop (VERIFIED).**

**Safe harbor vs presumption — §1026.43(e)(1) and (b)(4).** (e)(1)(i): "A creditor or assignee of a qualified mortgage complies with the repayment ability requirements of paragraph (c) if: (A) The loan is a qualified mortgage as defined in paragraph (e)(2), (4), (5), (6), or (f) of this section that is not a higher-priced covered transaction"; (e)(1)(ii): a QM "that is a higher-priced covered transaction, is presumed to comply with the repayment ability requirements of paragraph (c)" (rebuttable). (b)(4) "higher-priced covered transaction": "a covered transaction with an annual percentage rate that exceeds the average prime offer rate for a comparable transaction as of the date the interest rate is set by 1.5 or more percentage points for a first-lien covered transaction, other than a qualified mortgage under [paragraph (e)(5)], [(e)(6)], or [(f)]"; "by 3.5 or more percentage points for a first-lien covered transaction that is a qualified mortgage under [paragraph (e)(5)], [(e)(6)], or [(f)]"; "or by 3.5 or more percentage points for a subordinate-lien covered transaction."

**Seasoned QM — §1026.43(e)(7) (00a-fed §5.5).** Fixed-rate, fully amortizing loans meeting (e)(2)(i)–(v), with "no more than two delinquencies of 30 or more days and no delinquencies of 60 or more days at the end of the seasoning period," held in portfolio for "36 months beginning on the date on which the first periodic payment is due after consummation" (limited transfer exceptions). Informational only: whole-loan sale to Fannie Mae within the seasoning period defeats it. The temporary GSE QM ("patch") is no longer in the text.

**HPML — §1026.35 (eCFR 9/03/2026; 00a-fed §5.7).** (a)(1): first lien "by 1.5 or more percentage points"; first lien exceeding the Freddie Mac conforming limit "by 2.5 or more percentage points"; subordinate "by 3.5 or more percentage points" — each "as of the date the interest rate is set." Escrow, (b)(1): "a creditor may not extend a higher-priced mortgage loan secured by a first lien on a consumer's principal dwelling unless an escrow account is established before consummation for payment of property taxes and premiums for mortgage-related insurance"; cancellation (b)(3)(i) "only upon the earlier of: (A) Termination of the underlying debt obligation; or (B) Receipt no earlier than five years after consummation of a consumer's request to cancel," and (b)(3)(ii) only if UPB is "less than 80 percent of the original value" and the consumer is not delinquent or in default. Small-creditor exemption (b)(2)(iii): rural/underserved lending, ≤ 2,000 first-lien transfers, assets below **$2,785,000,000 (2026)**, and "(D) no escrow accounts maintained" — the partner/SM escrow, so the exemption is unavailable. Appraisals, (c)(3): "written appraisal… performed by a certified or licensed appraiser who conducts a physical visit of the interior"; flips, (c)(4)(i): second appraisal when the seller acquired the property "90 or fewer days prior to the date of the consumer's agreement to acquire the property and the price… exceeds the seller's acquisition price by more than 10 percent" or "91 to 180 days prior… by more than 20 percent"; exemptions (c)(2)(i) "qualified mortgages, as defined pursuant to 15 U.S.C. 1639c," and (c)(2)(ii) loans at or below **$34,200 (2026)**. 24.2 owns the appraisal mechanics; 23.4 only flags applicability.

**HOEPA — §1026.32 (eCFR 9/04/2026; 00a-fed §5.8).** (a)(1)(i): APR exceeds APOR "by more than 6.5 percentage points" (first lien) or "more than 8.5 percentage points" (first lien on a personal-property dwelling < $50,000, and subordinate liens); (a)(1)(ii): points and fees exceed "5 percent of the total loan amount for a transaction with a loan amount of $20,000 or more; the $20,000 figure shall be adjusted annually on January 1" or "The lesser of 8 percent of the total loan amount or $1,000 for a transaction with a loan amount of less than $20,000" — **2026: $27,592 / $1,380 (90 FR 57890)**; (a)(1)(iii): prepayment penalty "more than 36 months after consummation… or… more than 2 percent of the amount prepaid." Points and fees, §1026.32(b)(1): "the following fees or charges that are known at or before consummation": (i) "All items included in the finance charge under §1026.4(a) and (b), except that the following items are excluded" — (A) interest; (B) federal/state agency MI premiums; (C) private MI premiums: the portion not exceeding the FHA §203(c)(2)(A) amount if paid at consummation, and the entire amount if paid after consummation; (D) bona fide third-party charges not retained by the creditor, loan originator or an affiliate; (E) up to two bona fide discount points if the undiscounted rate does not exceed APOR by more than 1 point; (F) up to one bona fide discount point if none are excluded under (E) and the undiscounted rate does not exceed APOR by more than 2 points; (ii) loan originator compensation "paid directly or indirectly by a consumer or creditor" attributable to the transaction, except compensation paid by a consumer to a broker already counted, by a broker to its employee, "by a creditor to a loan originator that is an employee of the creditor," or by a manufactured-home retailer to its employee; (iii) real-estate-related charges under §1026.4(c)(7) unless "the charge is reasonable, the creditor receives no direct or indirect compensation," and the charge is "not paid to an affiliate"; (iv) credit-insurance premiums payable at or before consummation; (v) the maximum prepayment penalty; (vi) any prepayment penalty on a refinance by the same creditor/affiliate. (b)(3) bona fide discount point: 1% of the loan amount that reduces the rate per an "industry-standard calculation." (b)(4)(i) total loan amount (closed-end): the amount financed under §1026.18(b) minus any (b)(1)(iii), (iv) or (vi) item that is both included in points and fees and financed.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) B2-1.5-03 is about lien position and property rights, not ATR "legal requirements"; the ATR/QM/HOEPA/state rules are in B2-1.5-02 with A3-2-02. (2) There is no Fannie Mae "HPML allowed with conditions" sentence; HPML status is invisible to the Guide except through the 2.25 spread cap. (3) The points-and-fees base is the Reg Z "total loan amount," not the loan amount; the section-note figure "3% = $12,360" on a $412,000 loan is the naive base — the correct cap is 3% of the amount financed net of financed (iii)/(iv)/(vi) items ($12,128.24 in the fixture below). (4) The verification safe harbor references the June 3, 2020 Selling Guide edition and secondary sources add a "substantially similar" revision rule — the current Guide's 2026 renumbering must be mapped, not assumed. (5) "Escrow ≥ 5 years" is a *cancellation* restriction (no cancellation on request before five years after consummation), not a promise that the account will exist for five years — the loan can pay off. (6) The small-creditor exemptions are unavailable to an escrowing lender regardless of size. (7) Fannie Mae's state list (12 states, 14 definitions) does not include California, Illinois's "rate spread" or NC's § 24-1.1F; the representative table in the section notes (NY, NJ, MA, NC, IL, GA, CA) mixes Fannie Mae-ineligible definitions with state compliance-only statutes. (8) A2-2-07 makes ATR/HOEPA/state-law compliance a life-of-loan representation — no DU waiver, no Day 1 Certainty, no 36-payment relief covers it.

#### State machine

Per application, `qm_determinations` progress `le` → `lock` → `cd` → `consummation` → `post_closing`; each stage row is immutable; the *current* determination is the latest stage. Outcomes: `general_safe_harbor` (QM and not HPCT) → proceed; `general_rebuttable` (QM and HPCT) → proceed with HPML obligations (escrow; appraisal rules waived for QM) and a policy flag (open decision 23.4-Q2); `not_qm` → blocked: restructure (23.2 loop: reduce points/fees, re-price, bona fide discount analysis), or decline (no cure path: (e)(3)(iii) applies only to covered transactions consummated on or before Jan 10, 2021 — a post-consummation excess is `not_qm` permanently, with a remediation refund and 28.4 self-report evaluation). `high_cost_determinations.is_hoepa = true` or `is_state_high_cost = true` with `fnma_ineligible_if_fail` → hard block (never consummated as a Fannie Mae loan). Transitions performed by the `compliance-tester` agent; `underwriting_reviewer` only when the block forces a counteroffer/denial (23.3); `officer` approves a cure payment.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Stages and the rate-set date.** LE stage uses the estimated APR from 25.1 and the APOR table current on the LE date (informational). Lock stage fixes `rate_set_date = locks.locked_at::date` and snapshots the APOR row for the loan's term/amortization type (`fixed` table for fixed-rate; `adjustable` table by initial fixed period for ARMs). A relock or float-down resets `rate_set_date` to the new set date (**[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — comment 35(a)(2)-3]**). CD and consummation stages recompute the APR (25.1) on final fees but keep the lock-stage APOR unless the rate was re-set.
2. **General QM tests (2026 rule set).** `apr_tier` from `loan_amount_cents` (note amount): ≥ $137,958 → 2.25; $82,775–$137,957 → 3.5; < $82,775 → 6.5; manufactured home < $137,958 → 6.5. `apr_test_pass = spread < apr_threshold_pts` where `spread = round(apr − apor, 3)` ("2.25 or more percentage points" fails, so equality fails). `hpct = spread ≥ 1.5` (first lien; 3.5 subordinate). `qm_type = general_safe_harbor` if all product tests pass, `pf_cents ≤ cap_cents`, `consider_verify` complete, `apr_test_pass` and `not hpct`; `general_rebuttable` if the same but `hpct`; else `not_qm`. Fannie Mae eligibility additionally requires `spread ≤ 2.25` (B2-1.5-02 — identical to the QM tier-1 test for our loan sizes; for loans < $137,958 the QM tier is looser than Fannie Mae's flat "2.25%… or a different amount as specified in the Revised General QM Rule" — read as the QM tier; open decision 23.4-Q3).
3. **Points and fees.** `total_loan_amount_cents = amount_financed_cents − Σ(financed items in (b)(1)(iii),(iv),(vi) that are included in points and fees)`; `pf_tier` from `total_loan_amount_cents`: ≥ $137,958 → 3%; $82,775–$137,957 → $4,139; $27,592–$82,774 → 5%; $17,245–$27,591 → $1,380; < $17,245 → 8%. `pf_cents = Σ included items`: creditor-retained origination/underwriting/processing/doc-prep fees (finance charges, no exclusion); discount points not excluded under (E)/(F); LO compensation attributable at rate-set other than creditor-to-employee; real-estate fees that fail any of the three (iii) conditions (unreasonable, creditor compensated, or paid to an affiliate); credit-insurance premiums; the maximum prepayment penalty (always $0 — A3-2-02 ineligible). Excluded with reason codes: interest, agency MI, BPMI paid after consummation (monthly plans) or the at-consummation portion ≤ the FHA amount, bona fide third-party charges not retained (credit report, appraisal by an unaffiliated appraiser/AMC, flood, title to an unaffiliated agent), government recording/transfer taxes (not finance charges). Bona fide discount exclusion: compute `undiscounted_rate` from the pricing engine's rate sheet at the rate-set date (20.4 `pricing_quotes`); if `undiscounted_rate − apor ≤ 1.00` exclude up to 2.00 points; else if `≤ 2.00` exclude up to 1.00 point; each excluded point must satisfy (b)(3) (rate reduction "consistent with established industry practices" — the pricing engine's buydown table is the evidence). Rounding: cents; percentages at four decimals; the cap is `floor(total_loan_amount_cents × 3 / 100)` for the 3% tier.
4. **Consider and verify.** The eight (c)(2) factors are populated from 22.3 (income, employment status), 25.2/20.4 (payment on the covered transaction per (c)(5) — fixed-rate fully amortizing at the note rate; ARMs at the maximum rate in the first five years for QM (e)(2)(iv)), 22.5 (simultaneous loans, debts, alimony/child support), 30.3/24.5 (mortgage-related obligations: taxes, insurance, HOA, MI), 22.5 (DTI), 22.2 (credit history). Verification standard: each factor's evidence cites the Selling Guide standard applied and its June 3, 2020 chapter equivalence (`verification_standard_ref`, e.g., `SG-2020-06-03/B3-3.1-01 ≡ SG-2026-09-02/B3-3.2-01`). DU validation-service reports are third-party records under (c)(4) (financial institution records / employer-sourced records) but are not themselves named in the safe harbor — the underlying record type is cited.
5. **HPML.** `threshold_pts = 1.5` for first liens with `loan_amount ≤ conforming limit for the units/county` (`fnma.limits.2026`; the rule text says the Freddie Mac limit — identical values), 2.5 above it, 3.5 subordinate. `is_hpml = spread ≥ threshold_pts`. If HPML on a first-lien principal dwelling: `escrow_required = true`; `REGZ_1026_35B1_HPML_ESCROW_GATE` requires the account established before consummation (initial deposit on the CD; 30.3), `escrow_min_cancel_date = consummation + 5 years`. Appraisal: `appraisal_rules_apply = is_hpml AND qm_type = not_qm AND loan_amount > $34,200` — a General QM (safe harbor or rebuttable) is exempt under (c)(2)(i); 24.2 still applies Fannie Mae appraisal policy and the Reg B copy rules. Flip second-appraisal check is 24.2's; 23.4 passes `is_hpml` and `appraisal_rules_apply`.
6. **HOEPA.** `apr_fail = spread > 6.5` (first lien; "more than" — equality passes) or `> 8.5` (subordinate / personal-property dwelling < $50,000); `pf_fail = pf_cents > 5% of total_loan_amount` when `loan_amount ≥ $27,592`, else `> min(8% × total_loan_amount, $1,380)`; `ppp_fail` never (no prepayment penalties). `is_hoepa = apr_fail OR pf_fail OR ppp_fail` → gate. HOEPA's points-and-fees definition is the same §1026.32(b)(1) set as QM's.
7. **State tests.** For the property state, load the applicable definitions: `applies_by_size` (NY § 6-l/§ 6-m and GA: principal ≤ Fannie Mae conforming limit for a comparable dwelling; NC: ≤ min(conforming limit, $300,000); NJ: ≤ $350,000 as adjusted (**figure [UNVERIFIED]**); MA and IL: no cap); reference series: Treasury yield "on securities having comparable periods of maturity" (NY/MA — series and "as of" date per statute; the 15th of the month preceding the month of application under the HOEPA-era convention — **[UNVERIFIED — quote each statute's timing rule before enabling NY/MA]**), APOR (IL), PMMS northeast (NY § 6-m), HOEPA reference (NJ, NC, GA → the federal 6.5/8.5 result). Points-and-fees thresholds: NY 5% (≥ $50,000); NJ 4.5% (≥ $40,000); MA greater of 5% or $400-adjusted; NC/GA/IL 5% (≥ $20,000). Each state's points-and-fees definition may differ from Reg Z (e.g., IL cites 12 CFR 226.32 (2000) as amended by Dodd-Frank; some states include prepayment penalties or all broker fees) — `jurisdiction_rules` carries the per-state item mapping; unknown states default to the Reg Z set plus a `state_pf_definition_unverified` flag that blocks CD until counsel signs off (`officer`). `fnma_ineligible_if_fail = true` for the 12 states in B2-1.5-02's table.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Stage rows in `qm_determinations`, `hpml_determinations`, `high_cost_determinations`; `apor_tables` snapshots; the points-and-fees itemization (borrower-safe version available on request; not a required disclosure); the ATR evidence map; the cure ledger (`ledger_entries`: cure refund from `origination_fees_receivable`/partner cost allocation — open decision).
- Records retention: `regz_atr_3y` on the determination set (plus the loan-file class); the APOR snapshot is part of the evidence.
- Reports: QM type mix, HPML incidence, near-threshold loans (spread within 0.25 of 1.5 or 2.25; points and fees within 10% of cap), state-gate blocks, cure events, APOR staleness.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`compliance-tester` agent (tools: `getApor`, `computeTotalLoanAmount`, `classifyFeeItems`, `evaluateBonaFideDiscount`, `runQmTests`, `runHpmlTests`, `runHoepaTests`, `runStateHighCostTests`, `assembleAtrEvidence`, `writeDecision`, `openEscalation`). Deterministic rule execution with a narrative rationale; the LLM component only explains and flags anomalies (e.g., a fee whose payee/affiliate status is ambiguous) — it never overrides a computed result. Decision record: `{application_id, stage, apr_calculation_id, apor_table_id, rate_set_date, spread, tiers, pf_items[], excluded_items[], bona_fide_analysis, product_tests, consider_verify[], qm_type, hpml, hoepa, state_tests[], fnma_eligible, rule_set_versions, model_version, prompt_version, rationale}`. Guardrails: never mark a fee "bona fide third-party" without payee evidence; never exclude discount points without the undiscounted-rate evidence; never accept a stale APOR at lock/CD; never override a state test to "pass" — only `officer` may accept a non-Fannie-Mae state risk; never treat DU findings as ATR evidence (they are not third-party records). Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` (restructure/decline consequences), `officer` (state-law risk acceptance; cure refund), `compliance-sentinel` (rule-set drift, annual threshold update due). Borrower-facing: none (QM/HPML/HOEPA status is not a consumer disclosure, except the HPML escrow itself and HOEPA disclosures that never occur because HOEPA loans are not made).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **APR changes at the CD** (fees moved, tolerance cures): re-run at `cd`; a spread crossing 1.5 or 2.25 late forces a restructure and, if the APR rises > 1/8, 25.2's new waiting period.
- **Relock at a higher rate** after the lock-stage APOR snapshot: `rate_set_date` moves; APOR may fall or rise; recompute.
- **Seller-paid or lender-credit offsets**: lender credits reduce prepaid finance charges (amount financed rises) but do not remove creditor-retained fees from points and fees unless the fee itself is not charged.
- **Affiliate title agency** (partner-owned): all (iii) items to that agency count; the fee engine must know the affiliate map; a change of title agent mid-file changes the result.
- **SM's compensation** (open decision 23.4-Q1): until resolved, the platform computes points and fees both ways (with and without SM's transaction-attributable compensation) and blocks if the inclusive figure exceeds the cap.
- **Loan amount just under a tier boundary** (e.g., $137,900): tier 2 (3.5 points; $4,139 cap) applies — different from the fixture; near-boundary loans are flagged.
- **Manufactured home < $137,958**: 6.5-point APR tier; MH Advantage in scope (24.3).
- **Escrow waiver requested on an HPML**: refused ("may not extend… unless an escrow account is established"); 30.3's waiver logic checks `hpml_determinations`.
- **Post-closing discovery of points-and-fees excess**: no cure — (e)(3)(iii) is limited to loans consummated on or before Jan 10, 2021; `qm_type = not_qm` on the `post_closing` row; the remediation refund (excess + interest) goes to the consumer; record in the ledger; report in 28.2 QC and hand 28.4 the A2-2-07/QM-representation self-report decision.
- **State statute references 12 CFR 226.32**: apply the 1026.32 successor definitions as of the state's referenced version — counsel mapping in `jurisdiction_rules`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **23.4-Q1 — Is SM's compensation "loan originator compensation" under §1026.32(b)(1)(ii)?** Default: obtain a written legal position before launch; structure SM's fees as at-cost pass-through of bona fide third-party charges plus a subservicing fee earned post-closing; until resolved, compute points and fees both ways and block on the inclusive figure.
2. **23.4-Q2 — Originate rebuttable-presumption QMs (HPML QMs)?** Default: yes, with the escrow requirement enforced and a monitoring flag; the partner may set `policy.hpml_allowed=false` in `rule_sets`.
3. **23.4-Q3 — Fannie Mae's "2.25% or a different amount as specified in the Revised General QM Rule" for small loans.** Default: apply the QM tier for the loan amount (3.5/6.5) as the Fannie Mae limit for loans below $137,958; confirm with the partner's Fannie Mae account team.
4. **23.4-Q4 — State coverage at launch.** Default: enable only states whose statute text has been quoted into `jurisdiction_rules` (AZ, OH, and the 12 Fannie Mae-listed states where verified: NY, NJ, MA, NC, GA; IL and CA pending primary-source verification); others blocked at CD.
5. **23.4-Q5 — Who funds a post-consummation points-and-fees remediation refund** (not a QM cure — (e)(3)(iii) sunset for loans consummated after Jan 10, 2021). Default: the creditor (partner) pays; SM reimburses where the excess arose from SM-retained fees, per the term sheet; `officer` approves.
