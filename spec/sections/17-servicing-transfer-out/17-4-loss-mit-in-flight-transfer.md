### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**§1024.41(k)** (eCFR current as of Sept 4, 2026; text in 1.7) binds the transferee, but it fixes what the transferor's file must prove: **(k)(1)(i)** the transferee complies "within the timeframes that were applicable to the transferor servicer based on the date the transferor servicer received the loss mitigation application"; **(k)(1)(ii)** "the transfer date is the date on which the transferee servicer will begin accepting payments … as disclosed on the notice of transfer" — so 17.2's payment-start date is the loss-mitigation transfer date; **(k)(2)(i)** if the §1024.41(b)(2)(i)(B) acknowledgment period "has not expired as of the transfer date and the transferor servicer has not provided such notice," the transferee has 10 business days (federal) from the transfer date; **(k)(2)(ii)** the transferee may not make the first foreclosure notice/filing until after the reasonable date the transferor disclosed; **(k)(3)** a complete application pending at transfer is evaluated within 30 days of the transfer date; **(k)(4)** appeals pending or timely made after transfer are decided within 30 days of the later of transfer date and appeal date, or treated as a pending complete application; **(k)(5)** offers with unexpired acceptance periods must be honored. **Comments** (CFPB interpretations, verified 2026-09-09): 41(k)(1)(i)-1 — "the transferor servicer must timely deliver documents and information that borrowers submitted … coordinating with the transferee servicer per established policies under §1024.38(b)(4)," and when borrowers submit completion documents to the transferor after the transfer, "the transferor must promptly deliver these to the transferee"; 41(k)(1)(i)-2 — documents received by the transferor count as of the transferor's receipt date, and "an application remaining facially complete under the transferor remains so under the transferee as of its original facially complete date"; 41(k)(1)(i)-3 — the transferee need not repeat notices the transferor sent; 41(k)(4)-1 and 41(k)(5)-1 — appeals, acceptances and rejections given to the transferor after transfer must be "timely"/"promptly" transferred. **§1024.38(b)(4)(i)** and comment 38(b)(4)(i)-2 (17.3): status of discussions, agreements and recovery analysis must move. **Bulletin 2020-02 Appendix A §VII**: current status of discussions, agreements, recovery analysis, complete and incomplete applications with supporting documents, acknowledgment and denial notices, option dates, new loan/payment amounts and terms, denial dates and reasons; "borrowers may continue to provide loss mitigation-related documents and information to the transferor servicer after their loans have transferred," so transferors "must work with transferees." Nothing in §1024.41 pauses the transferor's own clocks before the transfer date: every 12.1–12.3 timer keeps running until T and a breach before T is Supermortgage's.

**Foreclosure and dual-tracking overlays that must be handed off intact:** §1024.41(f)(1) 120-day gate (13.1) — computed from the original delinquency, which continues across the transfer; §1024.41(g) (13.2) — no sale while a complete application received more than 37 days before the sale is pending; §1024.41(k)(2)(ii)(B) — a complete application received by the reasonable date but ≤37 days before a sale still gets (c), (d) and (g) treatment. **§1024.39/§1024.40**: early-intervention and continuity-of-contact duties attach to whoever is the servicer on the relevant day; the transferor's contact history and EI-notice dates (11.1/11.2) and the continuity episode (4.3) are delivered so the transferee can evaluate its own 36/45-day duties on the continuing delinquency. **Fannie Mae**: A2-7-03 — identify loans "in foreclosure, bankruptcy, or subject to a workout option" and the transferee must "honor any forbearance agreements or other arrangements"; F-1-11 — deliver "status information on loans with pending workout options," foreclosure/bankruptcy records "with law firm names and addresses," litigation records, and notify law firms; LL-2026-01 (eff. May 1, 2026) — forbearance increments ≤3 months, 12-month cumulative cap from the initial start, so the forbearance history must travel; D2-3.2-06/F-1-27 — trial period plans (a trial payment not received by the last day of its due month fails the trial) and Form 3179 (12.8); SMDU holds every workout case under the partner's servicer number (HSSN retired Dec 1, 2025; research/00b F1). **SMDU case continuity on a change of servicer number** (servicing sales, Fannie Mae-directed transfers): **[UNVERIFIED — no public procedure found; the SMDU UI User Guide is login-gated]**; for `sub_to_sub`/`sub_to_master` the case stays under the partner's number and only access changes. **Bankruptcy** (14.x): FRBP 3002.1 payment-change notices (21 days), fee notices and proofs of claim are filed in the creditor's name (Fannie Mae/partner) by counsel — a servicer change needs a notice of change of servicer/payment address to the trustee and debtor's counsel **[UNVERIFIED — local practice varies]**. **Colorado AI Act / LL-2026-04**: determinations issued before T carry their reviewer records in the file.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint:** (1) "Preserve prior deadlines" understates the transferor's job: its own §1024.41 clocks keep running until T, and after T it owes "prompt" forwarding of documents, appeals and acceptances for as long as borrowers send them (comments 41(k)(1)(i)-1, (k)(4)-1, (k)(5)-1); (2) "If pending" is broader than pending applications — appeals, unexpired offers, trial plans, forbearance/repayment plans, deferrals in process and denials with open appeal windows are all in scope; (3) "Systems: SMDU" omits the counsel hold instructions and the DRA/attorney network; (4) the same engine must hand off every other open case type, which the blueprint does not mention.

### Operational prerequisites
- Handoff data contract with the transferee for case files (MISMO MSTC loss-mitigation/foreclosure/bankruptcy sections or agreed CSV + document bundles) and an acknowledgment format — Supermortgage/transferee; before T-30.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `transfer.loan_list.attested` (17.1) → open-case inventory across `cases` (lossmit, appeal, forbearance, deferral, modification, shortsale, dil, foreclosure, bankruptcy, insurance_claim, fpi, pmi_cancel, payoff, noe, rfi, complaint, sii, scra, fraud, qc_finding) for listed loans.
- Case events until T-1: `lossmit.*`, `foreclosure.*`, `bankruptcy.*`, `case.*` continue to drive 12.x/13.x/14.x/4.x timers.
- `transfer.cutover.frozen` (17.3) → final case snapshot as of COB T-1.
- Post-transfer inbound addressed to Supermortgage: `lossmit.document.received{loan.status='transferred_out'}`, `lossmit.appeal.received`, `lossmit.offer.accepted/rejected`, borrower correspondence, counsel/trustee correspondence, insurer claim checks → forwarding.
- Transferee acknowledgments and requests (`transferee_requests{kind=lossmit_document}`).

#### Data model
- `case_handoffs`: `id`, `batch_id`, `loan_id`, `case_id`, `case_type`, `snapshot_as_of timestamptz`, `checklist jsonb` (`TO-*` results), `deadline_table jsonb` (every open timer: code, anchor, due_at, status, owner_after_transfer ∈ {transferee, supermortgage}), `package_document_id` (bundle hash), `delivered_at`, `acked_at`, `status` enum {inventoried, packaged, delivered, acked, deficient, resolved}.
- `lossmit_handoff_checks` (append-only; mirror of 1.7 `lossmit_carryover_checks`): `case_id`, `check_code` (`TO-01 application present with received date`, `TO-02 completeness status and dates (incomplete/facially complete/complete)`, `TO-03 acknowledgment sent? copy + reasonable date`, `TO-04 determination sent? copy, options, reasons, reviewer record`, `TO-05 appeal received? date, status, reviewer separation`, `TO-06 offer terms and acceptance deadline`, `TO-07 trial plan schedule and payments received`, `TO-08 forbearance history (initial start, increments, cumulative months)`, `TO-09 repayment/deferral plan terms`, `TO-10 SMDU case id and status`, `TO-11 foreclosure gate state (120-day, dual-tracking, (k)(2)(ii) reasonable date)`, `TO-12 counsel hold instruction sent/acked`, `TO-13 recovery analysis`, `TO-14 EI/continuity history`, `TO-15 rule-set version`), `result`, `evidence_document_id`.
- `post_transfer_forwardings`: `loan_id`, `received_at`, `kind` enum {lossmit_document, appeal, acceptance, rejection, correspondence, counsel_notice, trustee_payment, insurer_check, other}, `forwarded_at`, `transferee_ack_at`, `document_id`.
- `cases` additions for listed loans: `handoff_status`, `transferred_out_at`; `timers.owner_after_transfer`.
- Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y` (Fannie Mae workout records) and `transfer_out_archive`; borrower financial documents PII-encrypted.

#### State machine
Per case: `inventoried` (at attestation) → `pre_transfer_active` (12.x/13.x/14.x/4.x processing continues; timers run) → `snapshot` (COB T-1) → `packaged` (all `TO-*` pass or deficiencies documented) → `delivered` (T+1 BD) → `acked` → `closed_transferred` (terminal for Supermortgage's processing; forwarding continues). Branch: `deficient` → `resolved` (Supermortgage cures within 5 BD). Special: `retained` for NoE/RFI/complaint cases received before T — Supermortgage completes them itself (owner stays Supermortgage) and copies the transferee. Transitions by the `lossmit-underwriter` (loss-mit), `foreclosure-ops`, `bankruptcy-ops`, `claims-reo`, `case` and `transfer` agents; determinations before T need `lossmit_reviewer`; counsel instructions are sent by the agent and acknowledged by the firm.

#### Business rules and calculations
- Inventory rule: a case is in scope if it is open at attestation or opened afterwards for a listed loan; a loss-mit case is "pending" per comment 41(k)(1)(i)-1 if subject to §1024.41 and not fully resolved (a denial with an expired appeal window is not pending but its history is still delivered).
- Work-down rule (policy): Supermortgage completes every action it can complete before T (acknowledgments, determinations with `lossmit_reviewer`, appeal decisions with a different reviewer, trial-payment reporting) and never rushes a determination to beat T at the expense of the evaluation quality; any action deliberately left for the transferee is documented with the unexpired period.
- Deemed dates exported: `received_at`, `facially_complete_at`, `complete_at`, `ack_sent_at`, `reasonable_date`, `determination_sent_at`, `appeal_received_at`, `offer_sent_at`, `acceptance_deadline`, `trial_start`, `trial_payments`, `forbearance_history` — the same fields 1.7 expects on the inbound side.
- Worked dates (T = Tue Dec 1, 2026; federal holidays Nov 26, Dec 25): (a) application received Wed Nov 25 → Supermortgage's 5-BD ack due **Thu Dec 3** (Nov 27, Nov 30, Dec 1, 2, 3) — the platform sends it by Mon Nov 30; if not sent, `TO-03` records it and the transferee's (k)(2)(i) deadline is **Tue Dec 15** (10 federal BD: Dec 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15). (b) Complete application received Nov 10 → Supermortgage's determination due **Dec 10**; determined and mailed Nov 30 → no transferee obligation beyond honoring it; not determined → (k)(3) gives the transferee to **Dec 31**. (c) Denial mailed Nov 12 (appeal window to Nov 26); appeal received Nov 20 → Supermortgage's decision due **Dec 20** — decided by a different reviewer before T if the file allows, else handed off with the transferee's deadline = later of Dec 31 and Dec 20 = **Dec 31**. (d) Trial offer mailed Nov 20 with acceptance through **Dec 4** → the transferee must honor an acceptance received by Supermortgage on Dec 3, which is forwarded by Dec 4. (e) Trial plan with payments due Oct 1, Nov 1, Dec 1: Oct/Nov received; the Dec 1 payment mailed to Supermortgage arrives Dec 3 → misdirected, protected, forwarded with receipt date Dec 3; the trial fails only if no December payment is received by Dec 31 — the transferee's determination. (f) Foreclosure sale set for Tue Dec 22 with a complete application received Nov 10 (42 days before the sale, >37) → §1024.41(g) hold: counsel instructed by Nov 30 to hold the sale; the transferee inherits the hold; a complete application received Nov 20 (32 days before) would not trigger (g), but (k)(2)(ii)(B) still requires the transferee to evaluate. (g) Forbearance history: initial start Mar 1, 2026, increments 3+3+3 months = 9 → the transferee may grant at most one further 3-month increment (LL-2026-01) — `TO-08`.
- Counsel instructions: for every loan with a foreclosure referral, a bankruptcy matter or litigation — hold/proceed status keyed to 13.1/13.2/(k)(2)(ii) gates, the transferee's contact, file-transfer/substitution instructions per the partner's law-firm arrangements (13.6; A4-2.2-04 governs matter transfers between firms), and the note-location instruction (return to custodian or hold for the transferee's counsel; Form 2009 in 17.3).
- NoE/RFI/complaints opened before T: Supermortgage answers within the 4.1/4.2 clocks using its own records and copies the transferee; items concerning the transferee's future servicing are referred.
- Post-transfer forwarding: any borrower loss-mit submission, appeal, acceptance or rejection received by Supermortgage is forwarded within 1 business day with its Supermortgage receipt timestamp (comments 41(k)(1)(i)-1, (k)(4)-1, (k)(5)-1); Supermortgage never evaluates, acknowledges or decides anything on a transferred loan.
- SMDU: trial-payment and plan status reported through T-1; for partner-number cases the transferee continues in the same case; for servicer-number changes the Fannie Mae request package (loan list, SMDU case IDs, transfer date, D-Code) is filed by the `fnma_portal_operator` **[UNVERIFIED mechanics]**; any Fannie Mae approval/exception (e.g., LL-2026-01 forbearance exception) is delivered as a document.
- Money: offers, trial payments and plan amounts are exported in cents with rate/term; nothing is re-underwritten; capitalization amounts computed for a modification not yet effective are delivered as the analysis, not booked.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`lossmit-underwriter` agent (tools: `inventoryOpenCases`, `runHandoffChecks`, `exportDeadlineTable`, `completePreTransferAction`, `draftDetermination` (with `lossmit_reviewer`), `packageCase`, `forwardPostTransfer`, `writeDecision`) owns loss-mit handoffs; `foreclosure-ops`, `bankruptcy-ops`, `claims-reo` and `case` agents package their case types with the same schema; `transfer` agent sequences deliveries and acknowledgments. End-to-end: builds the inventory at attestation, keeps every case moving to T-1, decides what can responsibly be completed before T, produces the packages with exported dates and gates, sends counsel and trustee instructions, and forwards post-transfer submissions within one business day. Decision record: `{case_id, handoff_status, checks, actions_before_T, deadline_table, forwarded_items, rationale, reviewer, model_version, rule_set_version}`. Escalations: `lossmit_reviewer` for any denial/appeal determination issued before T (Colorado AI Act, LL-2026-04; reviewer ≠ evaluator per §1024.41(h)(3)); `attorney` for substitution of counsel, court notices of servicer change and any filing; `fnma_portal_operator` for SMDU UI-only actions and the servicer-number request; `officer` for deficiencies that cannot be cured before T and for the sale-risk report; `licensed_specialist` where a pre-T negotiation is MLO activity; `human_agent` on borrower request. Human path: the same queues in the ops-console with identical `TO-*` codes.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Application received T-1 evening: no ack possible; `TO-03` shows "not sent; period unexpired"; transferee ack due within 10 federal BD of T.
- Determination due after T but the file is complete: decide before T only if the reviewer approves in time; otherwise hand off (never issue an unreviewed adverse decision to beat T).
- Offer accepted to Supermortgage on the acceptance deadline after T: forward same day; the transferee must honor (comment 41(k)(5)-1).
- Borrower keeps sending documents to Supermortgage for months: forward every time; no time limit on the forwarding duty while Supermortgage's address remains on the borrower's paperwork.
- Foreclosure sale within days after T with (g) protection: hold instruction and confirmation from counsel before T; if the firm will change, both firms receive the hold.
- Trial payment forwarded late by Supermortgage (breach of the 1-BD forwarding): the receipt date still protects the borrower; Supermortgage logs the breach and notifies the transferee in writing.
- Bankruptcy plan payments by the trustee to Supermortgage after T: forward with dates; trustee notice repeated; counsel informed.
- SMDU case cannot be accessed by the transferee at T: escalation; Supermortgage supplies the decision outputs as documents; rep-and-warrant risk noted to the partner.
- NPRM switch (`regx.lossmit.2024nprm`): packages carry the rule-set version and review-cycle state instead of completeness; the deadline table maps to the new definitions.
- Successor in interest mid-confirmation: `TO-*` includes the §1024.36(i) request status and documents received; the transferee's confirmation clock continues from the original request.
- Disaster forbearance or LL-2026-01 exception pending with Fannie Mae: exception request and response delivered; the transferee resubmits under its access if Fannie Mae requires.
- Transfer cancelled after packages were delivered: packages withdrawn; cases continue on the platform; timers were never cancelled, so nothing is lost.
