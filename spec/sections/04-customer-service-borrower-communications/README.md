# Section 4 — Customer Service & Borrower Communications

## Overview

Section 4 is the borrower-facing "front door" of the Supermortgage platform and the legal machinery behind it. It covers the five processes in which a borrower (or a person standing in the borrower's shoes) initiates something the servicer must answer on a clock: **Notices of Error** (Reg X §1024.35), **Requests for Information** (§1024.36), **continuity of contact** for delinquent borrowers (§1024.40), **successors in interest** (§§1024.30(d), .31, .32(c), .36(i), .38(b)(1)(vi); Reg Z §§1026.2(a)(11), .20(f), .39(f), .41(g)), and **complaint handling / UDAAP** (Dodd-Frank §§1031/1036, 12 U.S.C. 5531/5536; CFPB complaint portal; state regimes such as 3 NYCRR 419.6 and Cal. Civ. Code §2923.7; Fannie Mae A4-1-03 and A4-2.1-04). These processes are enforceable against the servicer directly: §§1024.35/.36 carry RESPA §6(f) private liability (actual damages, up to $2,000 statutory damages per borrower for a pattern or practice, class caps of the lesser of $1,000,000 or 1% of net worth, plus fees), which is why the architecture baseline's litigation-defense posture ("build to the rule, not to the enforcement climate", research/00a §6.6) governs every design choice below.

**Architecture fit.** Two agents own this section: `borrower-comms` (all inbound/outbound channels — AI voice, chat, SMS, email, secure message, scanned mail — with mandatory automation disclosure and human warm-transfer) and `case` (NoE/RFI/complaint/SII investigation, determination, response generation). `compliance-sentinel` owns the timers; `security-records` owns records access and the §1024.38(c)(2) servicing-file assembly; `default-collections` and `lossmit-underwriter` supply the loss-mitigation facts that §1024.40(b) personnel must be able to state. Core services used: **Timer Engine** (a new unit `business_days_federal` on the baseline's `federal` calendar is introduced in 4.1 because §§1024.35/.36 count "days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays)", not Reg X "business days"), **Notice Registry** (every acknowledgment, response, extension, exception and successor notice is a `notice_template`), the **ledger** (read tools for investigation; correction commands post reversing entries), `cases`/`case_events`, `parties`, `contacts`, `consents`, `documents`, `agent_decisions`. A shared **Intake Router** (defined in 4.1 and reused by every process) classifies free-text communications from any channel into `cases` — the CFPB's 2023 chatbot spotlight faulted systems that "only [recognize a dispute when] specific words or syntax" are used and that trap consumers in "doom loops"; the router is designed to over-recognize and to escalate rather than deflect.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** Supermortgage performs every step (intake, investigation, corrections, responses, contact assignment, successor confirmation, complaint responses). The partner (master servicer of record) is liable to Fannie Mae for all of it (A2-1-07), so the partner receives a daily case digest, co-signs the exclusive-address notice and complaint policy, and is the named respondent on CFPB/state-regulator portals unless the portal is registered in Supermortgage's own name (open decision 4.5-Q1). Regulator/exam responses that require an officer certification route to `officer`.

**Dependencies.** 1.3 (RESPA hello notice carries the exclusive NoE/RFI address), 7.1 (periodic statement carries it and the §1024.38(b)(5) procedures pointer), 7.6/16.1 (payoff statements — §1024.35(b)(6) errors and the 7-day clock), 8.1/8.2 (60-day adverse-reporting bar; FCRA direct disputes), 11.1–11.3 (early-intervention notice date drives the §1024.40 assignment; QRPC), 12.x (loss-mit information the assigned personnel must give; potential-successor applications), 13.1–13.3 (foreclosure gates — (b)(9)/(10) errors must be resolved before sale), 17.x and 1.x (one-year untimeliness after transfer; prior-servicer records), 19.1 (retention).

**Changes in flight.** The 2024 Reg X NPRM remains unfinalized (00a §1.1): it would make loss-mitigation determinations subject to §1024.35, mandate Spanish/other-language translations, and add taglines — all designed here behind `rule_sets` flags, off by default. No substantive §1024.40 changes were proposed. LL-2026-04 (AI/ML governance, effective Aug. 6, 2026) makes decision records and monitoring for the `case` and `borrower-comms` agents a contractual condition. Cal. Civ. Code §2923.7 was amended effective Jan. 1, 2026 (Stats. 2025, ch. 200). NY 3 NYCRR 419.6 imposes tighter deadlines than Reg X (15 business days for foreclosure-related complaints; 7-day extension only).
