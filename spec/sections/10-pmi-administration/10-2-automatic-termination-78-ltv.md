### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**12 U.S.C. 4902(b) (verified 2026-09-09).** A BPMI requirement "shall terminate—(1) on the termination date if, on that date, the mortgagor is current on the payments required by the terms of the residential mortgage transaction; or (2) if the mortgagor is not current on the termination date… on the first day of the first month beginning after the date that the mortgagor becomes current on the payments." *Termination date* (4901(18)): fixed rate — "the date on which the principal balance of the mortgage, based solely on the initial amortization schedule for that mortgage, and irrespective of the outstanding balance for that mortgage on that date, is first scheduled to reach 78 percent of the original value"; ARM — "based solely on the amortization schedule then in effect." 4902(d): after a modification "the cancellation date, termination date, or final termination shall be recalculated to reflect the modified terms and conditions." 4902(e)(2): no PMI payments "more than 30 days after the termination date or applicable date." 4902(g): Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac may define high-risk categories exempt from (a) and (b) but not from (c); non-conforming lender-defined high-risk loans terminate at 77% scheduled. 4904(a): written notice within 30 days after termination that PMI "has terminated" and "no further premiums, payments, or other fees shall be due." 4904(b)(2): if the loan does not meet the automatic-termination requirements, notice of grounds "not later than 30 days after the scheduled termination date" (10.6). 4905(b)–(c): LPMI is excluded from 4902–4904, but "not later than 30 days after the termination date that would apply in the case of borrower paid mortgage insurance, the servicer shall provide to the mortgagor a written notice indicating that the mortgagor may wish to review financing options that could eliminate the requirement for private mortgage insurance."

**CFPB Bulletin 2015-03.** "Servicers may not require a borrower to pay for a property valuation as a condition of automatic termination of PMI"; the date "is not dependent on fluctuations in property value"; "a borrower cannot advance the 'termination date' by making additional payments"; examiners cited failures to terminate for both current borrowers and borrowers who later cured, and an unlawful "two-year seasoning requirement to automatically terminate PMI."

**Fannie Mae B-8.1-04, "Automatic Termination of Conventional Mortgage Insurance" (05/15/2019).** For loans "closed on or after July 29, 1999" secured by "a one-unit principal residence or second home," "the applicable termination date is the date the principal balance of the mortgage loan is first scheduled to reach 78% of the original value of the property, or the first day of the month following the date the mid-point of the mortgage loan amortization period is reached, if the scheduled LTV ratio for the mortgage loan does not reach 78% before the mid-point." Loans closed before July 29, 1999, and loans on "a one- to four-unit investment property or a two- to four-unit principal residence" terminate only "on the first day of the month after the date that is the mid-point of the original amortization period, provided the borrower's payments are current on that date" (10.3). "The borrower's payments are considered current if the payment due in the month preceding the scheduled termination date, or the mid-point of the amortization period, as applicable, was paid by the end of the month in which the payment was due." If not current, the servicer "must not terminate the MI, even if the other eligibility criteria for automatic termination are met," must "notify the borrower within 30 days after the termination date the MI was not automatically terminated because the payments were not current, and terminate the MI immediately if the borrower's payments are current at the time of a subsequent review." "The servicer must not charge the borrower a fee for processing an automatic termination." "The servicer must determine the original value of the property in accordance with applicable law." Modified loans: eligibility "must be based on the terms and conditions of the modified mortgage loan, including the amortization schedule of the modified mortgage loan, and must comply with applicable law." Finalizing: the servicer "must not collect MIPs as part of the borrower's mortgage loan payment more than 30 days after the later of the date the servicer received the borrower's request for termination (or the scheduled termination date or the mid-point of the amortization period, as applicable, for an automatic termination), or the date all eligibility criteria for termination were satisfied"; must reduce the payment by the MIP (unless financed), notify the borrower within 30 days of termination, forward the unearned MIP refund "no later than 45 days after MI termination date," and "submit discontinuance of MI with next investor reporting after termination." Fannie Mae's Guide names no high-risk exemption category; action code 54 is used for insurer-initiated rescissions/cancellations (F-1-02) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Fannie Mae's 1999 HPA high-risk guidelines were not retrieved; treat all subserviced conventional loans as non-exempt]**.

**Investor Reporting Manual §3-04 (Apr. 8, 2026), pp. 31–32.** LAR 89 "must be reported by the last reporting day of the reporting period in which the effective date of the termination occurs"; action code **53** = "automatic termination (regardless of whether the termination is based on the scheduled LTV ratio or the date that is the mid-point of the amortization period)"; **54** = "termination due to high risk"; action date MMDDYY = effective date. LL-2026-05 lists "mortgage insurance cancellations" as a future servicing event; no schema/go-live is published (Servicing Changes Reference Guide v1.0 omits it) **[UNVERIFIED future event]**.

**State overlay.** N.Y. Ins. Law §6503(d): the mortgagor "shall not be required to pay… the cost of continuing mortgage guaranty insurance" once the UPB is 75% or less of the appraised value at the time the loan was made — an actual-balance stop on borrower-paid premium with no request or payment-history condition. Its interaction with Fannie Mae's coverage requirement is open question 10.2-Q2.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) "on amortization date" is the *scheduled* date on the initial schedule (ARM: schedule then in effect), never the actual balance, and it moves on rate changes and modifications; (2) the current test is Fannie Mae's operational one (prior month's payment paid within its month) and the fallback is the first day of the month after cure; (3) Fannie Mae limits the 78% rule by property type and closing date; (4) SMDU is not involved in automatic termination (the SMDU FAQ addresses borrower-initiated requests only; a bulk "preliminarily eligible" API exists for original-value lists) — the trigger is the platform's own schedule engine; (5) the row omits the 30-day not-terminated notice, the 30-day termination notice, the 45-day refund, LAR 89 code 53, and the LPMI notice.

### Operational prerequisites
- Initial amortization schedule (or the inputs to rebuild it exactly: original principal, note rate, first payment date, term, IO/balloon/buydown features, ARM index/margin/caps and reset dates) boarded and reconciled to the transferor's schedule (Section 1.1/1.6); schedule reconstruction tolerance ±$0.05 per row.
- `original_value_cents` populated with evidence (sales contract/appraisal/CD from the origination file) — Owner: `boarding`; missing values create escalation `MI_ORIGINAL_VALUE_MISSING` with a 60-day SLA.
- Insurer cancellation channels, LAR 89 submission path, escrow interim-analysis capability (Section 3.2), refund rails (10.5), notice templates `NTC_HPA_4904A_CANCELLED`, `NTC_HPA_4904B_AUTO_NOT_CURRENT`, `NTC_HPA_4905C2_LPMI_OPTIONS`.
- Holiday calendars `fannie_et` for the LAR 89 period close.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Nightly `mi-auto-termination-sweep` (00:30 loan-calendar time) selects `mi_policies` with `status='active'`, `premium_plan` in BPMI plans, and `scheduled_78_date ≤ today` (or `midpoint_date ≤ today`, 10.3), plus loans in `auto_deferred` awaiting cure.
- Events: `loan_terms.rate_changed` (ARM) → rebuild `arm_reset` schedule and recompute `scheduled_78_date`; `lossmit.modification.effective` → `modification` schedule; `loan.boarded`/`mi.original_value.corrected` → recompute; `payment.applied`/`payment.reversed` → cure detection for deferred terminations; `mi.data.corrected`.
- For LPMI policies: `lpmi_equiv_termination_date ≤ today` → 4905(c)(2) notice.

#### Data model
Uses 10.1's engine. Additional fields: `mi_policies.auto_status` ∈ {pending, deferred_not_current, terminated, not_applicable_midpoint_only}, `auto_deferred_since`, `not_current_notice_id`, `became_current_on`; `mi_schedules.derived_78_date`, `derived_80_date`, `derived_midpoint_date` per version (so the audit trail shows why the date moved). `mi_terminations` (append-only): `id`, `loan_id`, `type`, `effective_date`, `basis_schedule_id`, `lar89_action_code`, `investor_event_id`, `insurer_message_id`, `notice_id`, `escrow_analysis_id`, `refund_id`, `created_by` (agent run).

#### State machine
`mi_policies.auto_status`: `pending` → (sweep finds date reached ∧ current) `terminated` (effective = scheduled date) | (date reached ∧ not current) `deferred_not_current` → (cure detected) `terminated` (effective = first day of the first month after cure) ; `pending` → `not_applicable_midpoint_only` when the 78% rule does not apply (2–4 unit/investment/pre-1999) — those loans use 10.3. Any `terminated` triggers the finalization pipeline (below). Cancelled earlier by 10.1/10.3/insurer → `not_applicable`.

#### Business rules and calculations
- **R1 — Applicability.** `rule_78_applies = consummation_date ≥ 1999-07-29 ∧ units = 1 ∧ occupancy_at_origination ∈ {principal, second_home}`; otherwise midpoint-only (10.3). HPA path additionally requires principal residence (second homes are Fannie Mae-only). Occupancy is fixed at origination for these rules; later occupancy changes do not remove the right.
- **R2 — Scheduled date.** From the active schedule version: `scheduled_78_date = min{ due_date : scheduled_upb_after_payment ≤ 0.78 × original_value }` (cents; `≤` comparison; original value × 78 / 100 rounded half-up to cents). Fixed-rate: initial schedule, never rebuilt for curtailments, SCRA caps, or temporary payment changes. ARM: on each rate change, build a new schedule from the *scheduled* balance on the change date (per the prior version), the new rate and the remaining term; the termination date is recomputed and may move earlier or later; the borrower's annual disclosure (10.4) shows the current projection. Interest-only: schedule rows hold the IO balance until amortization begins. Modification (4902(d)): schedule from the modified terms; scheduled principal includes principal forbearance as a constant component.
- **R3 — Current test on date T.** `is_current(T) = (all installments due on or before the first day of the month preceding T were received and applied by the last day of that month)` = `fnma_delinquency_status(as of last day of month preceding T) = current`. Cure: `became_current_on = first date on which no installment is past due` (`regx_days_delinquent = 0`); effective termination = first day of the following month.
- **R4 — Finalization pipeline (shared by 10.1/10.2/10.3; "R-F").** On `mi.terminated`/`mi.cancelled` with effective date E: **R-F1** set `mi_policies.status`, `terminated_on=E`, `lar89_action_code`; **R-F2** stop premium: mark `mi_premium_installments` after E `cancelled`; instruct `escrow` to close the MI line as of E and run an interim analysis (Section 3.2) whose new payment is effective with the first installment due after the analysis notice and in all cases no later than the installment due ≥ 31 days after E — the Section 3 policy gate `ESC_NEW_PAYMENT_NOTICE_MIN_30` is bypassed for a decrease caused by MI termination; **R-F3** send `NTC_HPA_4904A_CANCELLED` within 30 days; **R-F4** notify the insurer (10.1 integration) within 2 BD; **R-F5** emit `investor_events` LAR 89 (51/52/53/54) with action date E; **R-F6** open the refund workflow (10.5). Financed single premium: no payment reduction (B-8.1-04); refund handled per insurer schedule.
- **R5 — No fee, no valuation.** Automatic termination never requires a valuation, a request, or a fee (CFPB 2015-03); the sweep runs without borrower action.
- **R6 — Reporting deadline (Section 5.1 owns; do not restate a different time here).** LAR 89 is due by the close of the reporting period containing E, i.e. `FNMA_IRM_LAR89_PERIOD_END` = **BD2 of the month after E's month, 17:00 ET** (`FNMA_IRM_PERIOD_CLOSE_BD2_1700`). The 15:00 ET figure is the **LSDU bulk-file cutoff**, not the deadline: after 15:00 the bulk channel closes and single-LAR entry continues until the 17:00 period close. Supermortgage's internal next-BD-20:00 buffer is a policy target (`SM_MI_LAR89_INTERNAL_TARGET_NEXTBD_2000`), not a Fannie Mae obligation. Once the LL-2026-05 MI-cancellation event is live (`investor_event_types.channel_mode='event'`), the deadline becomes 03:00 ET next business day (`FNMA_LL202605_EVENT_NEXTBD_0300`, 5.1).
- **Worked example (fixed rate).** Worked loan (10.1): initial schedule reaches 78% ($312,000.00) with payment 135 due **2035-07-01** (UPB $311,913.25 after the payment) → `scheduled_78_date = 2035-07-01`. Scenario A: the 2035-06-01 installment was received 2035-06-12 → `is_current(2035-07-01)=true` → terminated effective 2035-07-01; termination notice due 2035-07-31; premium stop by 2035-07-31 (the 2035-08-01 installment must exclude MI); refund due 2035-08-15; LAR 89 code 53 action date `070135`, due by the July 2035 period close (BD2 August 2035, **17:00 ET**; the bulk file must be in by 15:00, single-LAR entry thereafter); insurer notified by 2035-07-03. Scenario B: the June installment arrives 2035-07-08 with July's on 2035-07-20 → not current on 07-01 → `NTC_HPA_4904B_AUTO_NOT_CURRENT` by 2035-07-31 (grounds: June 2035 payment not received by June 30); `became_current_on = 2035-07-20` → terminated effective **2035-08-01**; notice by 2035-08-31; refund by 2035-09-15; LAR 89 action date `080135`. Scenario C: June paid 2035-07-08 but July paid 2035-08-03 → cure 2035-08-03 → effective **2035-09-01**.

At the first reset (payment 61, 2029-05-01) the rate becomes 7.50%: scheduled balance $351,350.17 re-amortized over 300 months (P&I $2,596.45) → new schedule reaches 78% with payment 137 due **2035-09-01**; `scheduled_78_date` moves from 2034-07-01 to 2035-09-01 and the next annual disclosure shows the new date.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `NTC_HPA_4904A_CANCELLED` (12 U.S.C. 4904(a); B-8.1-04): checklist — statement that PMI has terminated/been cancelled and the borrower no longer has PMI; effective date; "no further premiums, payments, or other fees shall be due"; the new payment or that a revised escrow statement follows; refund amount/timing or that a refund will follow within 45 days; servicer contact. Channel: e-delivery with `esign` consent, else mail.
- `NTC_HPA_4904B_AUTO_NOT_CURRENT` (4904(b)(2), B-8.1-04): grounds (which installment was unpaid at month-end), that termination will occur on the first day of the month after the borrower becomes current, what "current" means, contact — always sent within 30 days after the scheduled termination date (see 10.6 for the template family).
- `NTC_HPA_4905C2_LPMI_OPTIONS` (4905(c)(2)): the BPMI-equivalent termination date has passed; the borrower "may wish to review financing options that could eliminate the requirement for private mortgage insurance"; no cancellation right under LPMI; contact.
- Ledger: no premium postings after E (Section 3.7 disbursement stop); refund entries in 10.5; investor event LAR 89 code 53/54 (54 only for insurer-initiated events, 10.5 edge cases).
- Records: `mi_terminations`, `mi_schedules` versions with derived dates, timers.
- Event naming: `mi.cancelled` (borrower-initiated, 10.1) and `mi.terminated` (automatic 78%, midpoint, state-law stop) are the canonical loan events consumed by Sections 3 and 5; Section 3.9 refers to the same event as `pmi.terminated` — the event bus publishes that name as an alias until Section 3 is aligned.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
The `pmi` agent supervises the nightly sweep (a deterministic job) and acts on its exceptions: builds and validates schedule versions after ARM resets/modifications (checks the rebuilt row against the servicing system's own amortization within ±$0.05), confirms the current test, executes the finalization pipeline, drafts the not-current notice text with the exact installment reference, and reconciles LAR 89 acks. Tools: `pmi.schedule.rebuild`, `pmi.terminate`, `notices.*`, `escrow.interim_analysis.request`, `investor_events.emit`, `mi_insurer.cancel`, `ledger.post`. Decision record: `{loan_id, schedule_version, scheduled_78_date, is_current, test_month, effective_date, actions[], model_version, rule_set_version}`. Guardrails: cannot delay a termination for value, seasoning or fee reasons (hard-coded reason whitelist: `NOT_CURRENT`, `MIDPOINT_ONLY_PROPERTY`, `ORIGINAL_VALUE_MISSING`, `POLICY_NOT_ACTIVE`); cannot edit schedule rows manually; must escalate schedule rebuild variances > $0.05 to `officer` with both schedules attached; `ORIGINAL_VALUE_MISSING` past 60 days → `officer` sev-2 with the loan's origination documents request. Human touchpoints: `fnma_portal_operator` for LAR 89 single-entry on reject; `officer` for corrections of prior erroneous non-termination (refund of premiums back to the true termination date). AI-off path: the sweep still runs; exceptions queue to the ops console.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Borrower prepays heavily: actual UPB far below scheduled — the automatic date does not move (4901(18) "irrespective of the outstanding balance"); the annual disclosure and agent invite an original-value request (10.1).
- Negative amortization/option ARMs: not eligible for Fannie Mae delivery today; if boarded legacy, treat the schedule then in effect per rate/payment recast; escalate `officer`.
- Balloon (e.g., 30/7): amortization period = 30 years; 78% date likely after maturity → payoff ends MI.
- Temporary buydown: does not change the note schedule; ignore.
- Deferral (payment deferral): scheduled 78% date on the original schedule is unchanged (a deferral is not a rate/term modification) — but 4902(d) applies to modifications; a Fannie Mae payment deferral is documented as a modification of terms? Default: treat payment deferral as *not* recalculating the scheduled dates (deferred principal is excluded from the schedule; actual-UPB path includes deferred principal) — open question 10.2-Q1.
- Transfer-in with unknown schedule: rebuild from note terms; if the transferor terminated MI but did not report, report LAR 89 with the transferor's effective date.
- Transfer-out: the MI record, schedule version and `auto_status` go in the transfer file; a termination effective on/after the transfer date belongs to the transferee.
- Bankruptcy: automatic termination proceeds when the contractual current test is met; a debtor whose plan cures arrears is "current" only when the contractual arrears are paid (10.1-Q4).
- Insurer refuses/ignores cancellation: the MI is terminated for the borrower regardless; premium stops; the servicer bears any premium the insurer keeps billing until the policy is cancelled (corporate expense) — escalate `officer`.
- Erroneous late termination discovered (e.g., wrong original value): terminate retroactively to the true date, refund all premiums collected after it with interest per state law if required, report LAR 89 correction through the Investor Reporting representative, and log a self-identified HPA exception for the Compliance Sentinel.
- Original value corrected upward after termination (MI should not have terminated yet): do not reinstate against the borrower; treat as servicer error; MI coverage gap is a corporate risk — escalate `officer` (insurer reinstatement is possible for the servicer's account).
- NY 75% gate hits before Fannie Mae criteria are met (e.g., poor payment history): stop charging the borrower; carry the premium as corporate expense; attempt 10.1 evaluation monthly; escalate `officer` (10.2-Q2).

| 10.2-T6 | Given the sweep did not run (job failure) on the scheduled date, then `HPA_4902B_AUTO_TERMINATE_0` breaches next sweep and `officer` sev-1 opens with the affected loan list. |

#### Audit and evidence
Schedule versions with derivation inputs and hashes; sweep run logs (selected loan IDs, decisions); `mi_terminations` linked to notice IDs with delivery proof, insurer acks, LAR 89 accept/reject records, escrow analysis IDs and refund IDs; timer histories for the 0/30/30/45-day clocks; `agent_decisions` for every exception; monthly reconciliation report of "loans past scheduled 78% date still insured" (should be only `deferred_not_current` and midpoint-only loans) for the Compliance Sentinel and STAR/MORA evidence.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Payment deferral and 4902(d) (10.2-Q1):** default — a Fannie Mae payment deferral does not rebuild the scheduled dates (the schedule is unchanged; deferred principal is a separate balance); obtain counsel confirmation.
2. **NY §6503(d) vs Fannie Mae coverage (10.2-Q2):** default — stop borrower premium at 75% of original appraised value; evaluate cancellation under 10.1 monthly; carry premium at corporate expense until eligible; confirm with partner/Fannie Mae.
3. **ARM schedule basis (10.2-Q3):** default — re-amortize the scheduled (not actual) balance at each reset; document in the annual disclosure.
4. **Bulk SMDU original-value list (10.2-Q4):** default — monthly reconciliation only, not a trigger.
