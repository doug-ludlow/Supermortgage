# Supermortgage Origination Platform — Process Inventory (the "blueprint")

**Version 0.1 · 2026-09-10 · Fannie Mae conventional conforming, 1–4 unit first liens only · whole-loan cash execution, servicing retained by the partner, subserviced by Supermortgage**

This inventory is the origination-side counterpart of the 114-process subservicing blueprint. There was no source PDF; every row below was derived from the lifecycle itself (Selling Guide Parts A–E, Reg Z/TRID, Reg B, Reg C, Reg X, FCRA, FDPA, HPA, SAFE Act, BSA/AML, MERS, Fannie Mae delivery technology) and must be researched against primary sources exactly as the subservicing rows were. Section numbers carry an `O` prefix (`23.2`) so they never collide with servicing sections 1–19; cross-references to the servicing spec use the bare number (`1.1`, `3.1`, `6.1`).

## Operating model (fixed by Doug, 2026-09-10)

| Item | Partner (lender of record, Fannie Mae seller/servicer, "LoR") | Supermortgage ("SM") |
|---|---|---|
| Fannie Mae seller/servicer approval, 9-digit seller/servicer number, Lender Contract reps & warrants, MI master policies, custodian Form 2017 | Holds | Fannie Mae-approved servicer (already required for subservicing) and Technology Service Provider; not the seller |
| NMLS company license, state lender licenses, MLO of record (SAFE Act) | Holds; employs or sponsors the MLOs of record | AI performs every step; a licensed MLO of record appears where the SAFE Act or a state requires an individual; SM holds any third-party processor/underwriter/servicer licenses a state requires of it (31.1) |
| Warehouse capacity | Borrower under the SM warehouse facility (or SM-arranged line) | Provides the facility, controls collateral (bailee/interim funder), is repaid from Fannie Mae purchase proceeds (§27) |
| Loan Delivery, commitments, purchase proceeds, rep & warrant liability | In the partner's name | Prepares every package and submission; operates under partner-scoped Fannie Mae credentials (Form 101) and its own TSP identity |
| Servicing after purchase | Master servicer of record; MSR (including recaptured MSR) stays with the partner | Subservicer from the first payment (Sections 1–19 of the subservicing spec) |
| Economics (Grander term sheet) | — | SM bears third-party origination costs; gain-on-sale recovers only those costs; the remainder is returned to the borrower as a lower rate; platform fee 12.5 bps/yr on UPB |

**Product scope.** Purchase, limited cash-out and cash-out refinance; fixed-rate and ARM (SOFR-indexed 5/6, 7/6, 10/6 and any other DU-eligible plan); 1–4 unit; detached, attached, condo, PUD, manufactured (incl. MH Advantage); HomeReady; high-balance; primary residence, second home, investment; temporary buydowns; Community Seconds/DPA subordinate financing; inter vivos trust vesting; Texas 50(a)(6) and New York CEMA variants. **Out of scope**: HomeStyle Renovation/Energy, construction-to-permanent, co-op share loans, manufactured homes not titled as real property, RefiNow/high-LTV refinance (status to be confirmed by research), any non-Fannie Mae execution, MBS swap execution (whole-loan cash only), manual underwriting (DU Approve/Eligible is a delivery gate — open decision 23.2-Q1), servicing-released execution.

**Default path decisions.** eNote + RON first with hybrid and wet-ink fallback; SM boards the loan to its own servicing platform at funding (no interim servicer of record other than the partner, no RESPA transfer notice unless a payee change occurs after closing); AI-first with the human touchpoints listed in the architecture-baseline addendum.
