### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Source verified:** 12 CFR 1024.39(b), (c), (d) and Appendix MS-4 (eCFR current Sept. 4, 2026; CFPB eRegs mirror for MS-4 text); Supplement I comments 39(b)(1)-1 to -5, 39(b)(2)-1 to -3, 39(b)(2)(iii)-1/-2, 39(b)(2)(iv)-1, 39(c)-1/-2, 39(c)(1)-1, 39(c)(1)(ii)-1/-2, 39(c)(1)(iii)-1, 39(c)(2)-1, 39(d)-1/-2, 39(d)(2)-1; §1024.32(a)(1) (electronic disclosures subject to E-SIGN consent); CFPB interpretive rule, Safe Harbors from Liability under the FDCPA (81 FR 71977, Oct. 19, 2016) — confirmed **not** on the May 12, 2025 withdrawal list; Fannie Mae D2-2-04 (09/13/2017), D2-2-05 (06/09/2021), D2-1-01 (12/12/2018); Forms 710 and 745 (existence and titles verified on the Fannie Mae forms index; current form dates **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — PDFs are behind a download page]**).

**§1024.39(b)(1) — timing.** "A servicer shall provide to a delinquent borrower a written notice with the information set forth in paragraph (b)(2) … no later than the 45th day of the borrower's delinquency and again no later than 45 days after each payment due date so long as the borrower remains delinquent. A servicer is not required to provide the written notice … more than once during any 180-day period. If a borrower is 45 days or more delinquent at the end of any 180-day period after the servicer has provided the written notice, a servicer must provide the written notice again no later than 180 days after the provision of the prior written notice. If a borrower is less than 45 days delinquent at the end of any 180-day period after the servicer has provided the written notice, a servicer must provide the written notice again no later than 45 days after the payment due date for which the borrower remains delinquent." Comment 39(b)(1)-1: a January 1 payment unpaid through February 14 requires the notice by **February 15**; paid February 1 → no notice. Comment 39(b)(1)-2 (dates): delinquent March 1, notice April 15; April 1 also missed — no further notice is required until **October 12** (180 days after April 15) and then only if the borrower is 45+ days delinquent on that date; if the borrower was current and missed October 1 instead, the notice is due by November 15. Comment 39(b)(1)-4: the written notice is required even if loss-mitigation/foreclosure information was given orally under (a). Comment 39(b)(1)-5 (transfers): the transferee must comply "regardless of whether the transferor servicer provided a written notice … within the preceding 180 days," except that if the transferor sent the notice **within 45 days before the transfer date**, the transferee's first notice is due 45 days after the first post-transfer payment due date — example: notice April 10, transfer April 12, May 1 due → transferee notice by **June 15**. Comment 39(b)(1)-3: the notice may go to the borrower's authorized representative (verification per comment 39(a)-5).

**§1024.39(b)(2) — content (minimums; comment 39(b)(2)-1).** (i) "A statement encouraging the borrower to contact the servicer"; (ii) "the telephone number to access servicer personnel assigned pursuant to §1024.40(a) and the servicer's mailing address"; (iii) "if applicable, a statement providing a brief description of examples of loss mitigation options that may be available from the servicer"; (iv) "if applicable, either application instructions or a statement informing the borrower how to obtain more information about loss mitigation options from the servicer"; (v) "the website to access either the Bureau list or the HUD list of homeownership counselors or counseling organizations, and the HUD toll-free telephone number." Comment 39(b)(2)-2: any color, paper, print and reproduction method if each statement is clear and conspicuous; -3: may be combined with other notices in one mailing if each statement stays clear and conspicuous; (iii)-1: no fixed number of examples, but borrowers benefit from examples of home-retention options and of options that end ownership; a generic list plus "not all borrowers qualify" is permitted; (iii)-2: one or more sentences per example, and one "loan modification" description may cover several programs; (iv)-1: "contact us for instructions on how to apply" suffices, and adding representative documents, an evaluation timeline or an application form is encouraged. **Appendix MS-4 model clauses** (safe-harbor wording): MS-4(A) encouragement/contact ("Call us today to learn more about your options and instructions for how to apply"; bracketed "The longer you wait, or the further you fall behind…"; servicer name, address, telephone, website/email); MS-4(B) options ("If you need help, the following options may be possible (most are subject to lender approval)": refinance, modify loan terms, "Payment forbearance temporarily gives you more time to pay your monthly payment," and options ending ownership such as short sale/deed-in-lieu); MS-4(C) counselors (CFPB or HUD counselor list website and the HUD telephone number); MS-4(D) FDCPA opening ("This is a legally required notice. We are sending this notice to you because you are behind on your mortgage payment. We want to notify you of possible ways to avoid losing your home. We have a right to invoke foreclosure based on the terms of your mortgage contract. Please read this letter carefully.").

**Bankruptcy (§1024.39(c)).** (c)(1): while any borrower is a debtor under title 11 (comment 39(c)(1)-1: from petition filing; 39(c)(1)(iii)-1: joint obligors — bankruptcy of any borrower), the servicer is exempt from (a) and, per (c)(1)(ii), from (b) **only if** no loss-mitigation option is available (comment 39(c)(1)(ii)-1: an option is "available" if the owner/assignee offers an alternative to foreclosure through the servicer for which the borrower may apply, even if the borrower would not qualify — always true for Fannie Mae loans) **or** any borrower on the loan has sent an FDCPA §805(c) cease notice and the servicer is subject to the FDCPA (comment 39(c)(1)(ii)-2). Otherwise (c)(1)(iii): the notice is required, modified — (A) if the borrower is delinquent when the petition is filed, provide it "not later than the 45th day after" the filing; if the borrower becomes delinquent after filing, within 45 days of the delinquency — "regardless of" the 180-day limitation; (B) "may not contain a request for payment"; (C) "not more than once during a single bankruptcy case" (comment 39(c)(2)-1: a case dismissed and later reinstated/reopened is the same case — no second notice). Comment 39(c)-1: the modified notice may be provided to the borrower's representative; "bankruptcy counsel is the borrower's representative," verified by confirming counsel's name on the petition or a court filing. Comment 39(c)-2: nothing requires a communication inconsistent with bankruptcy law or a court order; requirements may be adapted. (c)(2)(i): after dismissal, closure or reaffirmation, resume (a) and (b) "with respect to the next payment due date." (c)(2)(ii): after a discharge under 11 U.S.C. 727, 1141, 1228 or 1328, (a) never resumes; (b) resumes "if the borrower has made any partial or periodic payment on the mortgage loan after the commencement of the … case."

**FDCPA (§1024.39(d)).** For a servicer "subject to the FDCPA with respect to a borrower's mortgage loan" (11.4 determination) that receives a §805(c) cease notice: (d)(1) exempt from (a); (d)(2) exempt from (b) if no loss-mitigation option is available (comment 39(d)-1 — never, for Fannie Mae loans) or while any borrower is in bankruptcy (comment 39(d)(2)-1; example of spouses); (d)(3) otherwise the notice is required, modified — (i) it must add "a statement that the servicer may or intends to invoke its specified remedy of foreclosure" (MS-4(D)); (ii) no request for payment; (iii) not more than once in any 180-day period; if the borrower is 45+ days delinquent at the end of the 180 days, resend "no later than 190 days after the provision of the prior written notice"; if less than 45 days delinquent, resend by the later of 45 days after the due date for which the borrower remains delinquent or 190 days after the prior notice. Comment 39(d)-2 and the 2016 interpretive rule: providing the modified notice, and responding to borrower-initiated loss-mitigation communications, do not violate FDCPA §805(c) — but "a servicer subject to the FDCPA must continue to comply with all other applicable provisions of the FDCPA."

**Electronic delivery.** §1024.32(a)(1): Subpart C disclosures may be provided electronically "subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions" of E-SIGN (15 U.S.C. 7001(c)) — Notice Registry channel rule (baseline §6): electronic only with a valid unrevoked `esign` consent for notice class `regx_ei`; otherwise first-class mail.

**Fannie Mae D2-2-04 (09/13/2017) — Borrower Solicitation Package (BSP).** Two triggers: (1) "if at any time during the delinquency the servicer has achieved QRPC and has not obtained a resolution to the delinquency, the servicer must send the borrower a Borrower Solicitation Package, if a Borrower Solicitation Package has not previously been sent"; (2) "if the servicer has not achieved QRPC or obtained a resolution to the delinquency by the 45th day of delinquency, the servicer must send either a Borrower Solicitation Letter (Form 745), or equivalent, or a Borrower Solicitation Package." The BSP = Form 745 (or equivalent) + Form 710 Mortgage Assistance Application (or an equivalent requesting "the same financial information, documentation, hardship information, and borrower attestations"); the servicer "is authorized to include" IRS IVES Form 4506-C. A customized Form 745 must be available to Fannie Mae on request and "must retain references to the Homeowner's HOPE™ Hotline Number (1-888-995-HOPE)." Delivery: mail, fax, electronic transmission, or download from a secure servicer website. D2-2-05 (06/09/2021) defines the complete BRP and the 5-business-day acknowledgment / incomplete-information notice (12.1 owns). D2-1-01: no workout solicitation of borrowers current or <30 days delinquent (the day-45 trigger is inside the permitted range).

**Rule changes in flight.** 2024 NPRM (00a §1.2 item 7): would add the owner/assignee name, a description of each option type generally available from that owner, a website listing all options, Spanish translations/taglines, and a forbearance-end live contact + notice — implemented behind `rule_sets.regx.ei.2024nprm` (off).

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) "Again ≤45 days each cycle" overstates the duty: after the first notice the rule requires at most one notice per 180 days, with the two 180-day-end tests above (190 days in the FDCPA variant). (2) The row omits the five content items, MS-4, the bankruptcy and FDCPA variants, the transferee rule and E-SIGN. (3) The row omits Fannie Mae's Form 745/BSP requirement at day 45 and after QRPC. (4) Class "a" stands (deterministic generation), with an `officer` breach escalation only.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `loan.delinquency.window_opened{due_date}` (11.1) — creates the notice leg of `regx_ei_windows`.
- `payment.applied` / `payment.reversed` — cancel/reinstate legs.
- `notice.sent{template ∈ EI variants}` — starts the 180-day cycle.
- `bankruptcy.petition.filed{case_id, counsel}`, `.dismissed`, `.closed`, `.reaffirmed`, `.discharged{chapter}`, `.reopened` (14.x).
- `fdcpa.cease.received` / `fdcpa.cease.withdrawn` (11.4); `fdcpa_debt_collector_flag` on the loan.
- `contact.qrpc.established{resolution: none}` (11.3) → BSP trigger; `loan.delinquency.day_reached{45}` → Form 745/BSP trigger if no QRPC and no resolution.
- `loan.boarded{regx_days_delinquent>0, transferor_ei_notice_sent_at?}` (1.1) — transferee rule.
- `party.successor.confirmed` (4.4) — address the successor as a borrower.

#### Data model
- `regx_ei_windows` (11.1) notice-leg fields: `notice_status` ∈ {open, sent, satisfied_by_prior_180, cancelled_paid, exempt_bk_no_option, exempt_bk_cease, exempt_fdcpa_no_option, exempt_fdcpa_bk, deferred_transferee, not_applicable}, `notice_id`, `notice_variant`.
- `ei_notice_cycles` (new): `id`, `loan_id`, `notice_id`, `variant` ∈ {standard, fdcpa, bk, bk_fdcpa}, `provided_at date` (mail date or electronic delivery date), `cycle_end_at` (= provided_at + 180), `next_required_by` (computed nightly per rule 3), `bk_case_id` (for once-per-case), `fdcpa_cycle_end_190` (provided_at + 190 when variant ∈ {fdcpa, bk_fdcpa}), `status` ∈ {active, superseded, closed}.
- `solicitation_packages` (new): `loan_id`, `kind` ∈ {form745_letter, bsp}, `trigger` ∈ {day45_no_qrpc, qrpc_no_resolution, resolicit}, `sent_at`, `channel`, `form_745_version`, `form_710_version`, `includes_4506c bool`, `hope_hotline_present bool` (checklist), `document_ids uuid[]`, `fnma_action_event_id`, `previous_bsp_id` (D2-2-04 "if not previously sent").
- `notices` (baseline) rows carry `required_content_checklist jsonb` results: `encouragement_statement`, `assigned_contact_phone`, `mailing_address`, `lossmit_examples_retention`, `lossmit_examples_disposition`, `apply_instructions_or_how_to_learn_more`, `counselor_website`, `hud_phone`, `continuity_block_present` (4.3), `exclusive_noe_rfi_address` (4.1, comment 35(c)-2), `no_payment_request` (bk/fdcpa variants), `fdcpa_foreclosure_statement` (fdcpa variant), `fdcpa_1006_18e_disclosure` (DC loans), `hope_hotline` (Form 745).
- Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y` (also satisfies `regx_1y_post_transfer`).

#### State machine
Notice leg per window: `open → sent | satisfied_by_prior_180 | cancelled_paid | exempt_* | deferred_transferee → (terminal)`; `open` past `notice_due_at` → `breached`. Cycle: `active → superseded` (a new notice is provided) | `closed` (delinquency ends). Bankruptcy overlay: on petition, all open legs → `exempt_bk_*` or a single `bk_modified_required` task per case (`REGX_1024_39C_BK_MODIFIED_NOTICE_45`); on dismissal/closure/reaffirmation → legs re-open from the next due date; on discharge → legs `exempt_discharge` until `payment.applied{after_petition}` re-arms them. FDCPA overlay: on cease (DC loan) → the active cycle switches to the 190-day variant.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Variant selection** (evaluated at send): `bk_active` (any borrower) → `bk` (+`_fdcpa` if a DC loan with an active cease from any borrower — but then §1024.39(c)(1)(ii)/(d)(2) make the notice *exempt*, so the combined variant is only used where policy elects to send a courtesy informational notice, default off); `fdcpa_debt_collector_flag ∧ cease_active` (no bankruptcy) → `fdcpa`; else `standard`. DC loans without a cease receive the standard variant plus the §1006.18(e) disclosure and no overshadowing content during a validation period (11.4).
2. **Day-45 computation**: `notice_due_at = due_date + 45` (Nov 1, 2026 → **Dec 16, 2026**). "Provide" = the mail date evidenced by the vendor manifest or the electronic-delivery timestamp **[interpretation PARTIALLY VERIFIED — no comment defines "provide"; policy uses mail date with a 1-day buffer]**.
3. **180-day cycle (worked example).** Notice mailed 2026-12-14 (day 43 of the Nov 1 window) → `cycle_end_at` = **2027-06-12**. Nightly from 2027-06-12: if `regx_days_delinquent ≥ 45` on 2027-06-12 → `next_required_by` = 2027-06-12 (the platform therefore schedules the repeat for **2027-06-10**, two days early, when the loan is ≥45 days delinquent on June 10 and no cure is pending — a notice provided before day 180 is permitted and starts a new 180-day cycle); if `< 45` on 2027-06-12 (e.g., the borrower caught up to the June 1 installment) → due 45 days after the earliest due date that remains unpaid (June 1 → **2027-07-16**), recomputed each night as installments are paid. FDCPA variant: 190 days → **2027-06-22**, and the "<45" branch uses the later of the two dates.
4. **Windows inside an active cycle** are `satisfied_by_prior_180` at creation (a second missed payment on Dec 1, 2026 needs no notice by Jan 15, 2027 because the Dec 14 notice covers it) — the audit record cites the covering `ei_notice_cycles.id`.
5. **Bankruptcy modified notice (worked example).** Standard notice 2026-12-14; petition (Ch. 13) 2027-01-20 with Nov/Dec/Jan installments unpaid → `REGX_1024_39C_BK_MODIFIED_NOTICE_45` due **2027-03-06** regardless of the December notice (§1024.39(c)(1)(iii)(A)); variant `bk`; addressed to the borrower c/o bankruptcy counsel listed on the petition (comment 39(c)-1) with a copy to the borrower unless counsel or the court directs otherwise; once per case; contains no amount due or payment request. Case dismissed 2027-05-03 → windows resume from the June 1, 2027 due date (live by July 7, notice by July 16 — unless the Mar 6 notice's 180-day cycle (to Sept 2, 2027) covers it: it does, so the next standard notice is due per rule 3 from Sept 2). Case reopened 2027-06-10 → no second bk notice (comment 39(c)(2)-1).
6. **Discharge**: Ch. 7 discharge 2027-04-15 → (a) never resumes; (b) resumes only when a payment received on/after the petition date is applied — then the next window's notice leg is `open` with the standard rules (variant `bk` while the case remains open; standard after closure) **[design choice on variant after discharge — counsel]**.
7. **Transferee rule**: transferor notice within 45 days before transfer → `deferred_transferee`; first notice due 45 days after the first post-transfer due date if the borrower is still delinquent then (Apr 10 / Apr 12 / May 1 example → June 15). Transferor notice older than 45 days → the transferee's own windows govern (a window already past day 45 at boarding is created `breached_at_boarding` and the notice is sent within 1 BD).
8. **Content assembly**: (i) MS-4(A) sentence; (ii) the 4.3 assigned team's direct number + Supermortgage mailing address + exclusive NoE/RFI address (comment 35(c)-2 requires it on any §1024.39 notice that includes contact information); (iii) examples from `rule_sets.fnma.workout_hierarchy` rendered as one generic sentence each — retention: repayment plan, payment deferral, forbearance, loan modification (Flex Modification); disposition: short sale, Mortgage Release (deed-in-lieu) — with "not all borrowers qualify"; (iv) "contact us for instructions on how to apply" plus the Form 710 enclosure/upload link and the representative document list; (v) CFPB counselor list URL, HUD counselor list URL and the HUD toll-free number from `rule_sets`; plus the Fannie Mae HOPE hotline (Form 745 requirement) and the Fannie Mae consumer-resource reference (D2-2-03 language reused). Clear-and-conspicuous: 12-pt minimum, headings, the five items on page 1.
9. **Form 745/BSP packaging**: at day 45 with no QRPC → default send the **full BSP** (Form 745 + Form 710 + document checklist + return envelope + portal upload link; 4506-C included only when 12.x's rule set requires it — default off); after QRPC without resolution → BSP within 3 BD if none sent before; re-solicitation is at 12.x's discretion (e.g., deferral solicitation by the 15th of the following month per 12.6). The EI notice and the BSP are mailed in one envelope by default (comment 39(b)(2)-3) with the EI notice on top; when the variant is `fdcpa` or `bk`, the BSP omits payment amounts.
10. **Multiple borrowers**: one notice addressed to all borrowers at the property/mailing address; a separate copy to any borrower with a different mailing address of record; confirmed successors in interest are borrowers (§1024.30(d)); potential successors get 4.4 handling only.
11. **Channel**: electronic only with `esign` consent for class `regx_ei` (E-SIGN 15 U.S.C. 7001(c) via §1024.32(a)(1)); electronic delivery evidence = portal/email delivery receipt; bounce → mail same day. Mail returned undeliverable → re-mail to any newer address within 1 BD, address research, note on the window (obligation is to "provide," evidenced by mailing to the address of record **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**).
12. **Spanish/other languages**: policy copy in the borrower's stated language with the English original (NPRM flag off → optional; on → mandatory Spanish for all).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`default-collections` runs the notice pipeline end-to-end: nightly `windows.evaluate` → variant selection → `notice.render` (Handlebars template version bound to `rule_sets.regx.ei.2013`) → `checklist.verify` (machine-checkable; any false item blocks the send) → channel selection → `print.request`/`edeliver.send` → `notice.sent` event → cycle creation → `decision.record`. Tools: `windows.list`, `cycle.compute`, `bk.status`, `fdcpa.status`, `continuity.assignment.get`, `lossmit.options.list`, `notice.render`, `checklist.verify`, `print.request`, `edeliver.send`, `fnma.action.emit`, `decision.record`, `escalate`. Decision record: `{window_ids[], cycle_id, variant, variant_rationale{bk_case_id?, cease_id?, options_available:true}, provided_at_target, channel, consent_id?, checklist_results, template_version, rule_set_version, model_version}`. Guardrails: never send a payment request in bk/fdcpa variants (template-level, not prompt-level); never send a second bk notice in the same case; never send electronically without consent; never combine with a state pre-foreclosure notice unless the jurisdiction flag allows. Escalations: `officer` on any breach or vendor failure within 2 days of a deadline; `attorney` when a bankruptcy court order restricts communications (comment 39(c)-2) — the agent drafts an adapted notice for counsel review; `human_agent` none. Disclosure/consent: E-SIGN consent for electronic; §1006.18(e) on DC-loan notices. AI-off path: the same deterministic pipeline runs as a batch job with a human release queue (checklist visible), timers unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Notice sent, then the installment is paid on day 44**: the notice stands (no harm); the cycle stays active; later windows within 180 days are covered.
- **Reversal (NSF) after a `cancelled_paid`**: leg reinstated with the original `notice_due_at`; if already past, send within 1 BD and record the reason.
- **Borrower in loss mitigation (comment 39(a)-1: performing under an option = not delinquent)**: legs `not_applicable` while performing; a failed plan re-opens windows; an active cycle continues to count.
- **Joint obligors, one in bankruptcy**: exemption for the loan; modified notice may go to either borrower (comment 39(c)(1)(iii)-1) — default: to the non-debtor borrower directly and to the debtor c/o counsel.
- **Joint obligors, one FDCPA cease (DC loan), the other in bankruptcy**: exempt from (b) (comment 39(c)(1)(ii)-2).
- **Court order barring contact**: adapt (comment 39(c)-2) — `attorney` review, adapted notice or documented non-delivery.
- **Deceased borrower**: notice to the estate/confirmed successor (4.4); potential successors receive the 4.4 information notice, not the EI notice.
- **Transfer-out**: the transfer file carries `ei_notice_cycles` (the transferee's comment 39(b)(1)-5 rule depends on it) and copies of notices.
- **Vendor outage**: fail over to the secondary print vendor; if a deadline cannot be met by mail, electronic delivery only where consented; otherwise breach recorded and the notice mailed at the earliest opportunity.
- **Address unknown / returned**: re-mail on new address; skip-trace; the obligation is evidenced by mailing to the last known address; escalate to `officer` if no address after 30 days.
- **Non-principal-residence**: no Reg X notice; Fannie Mae Form 745/BSP still required at day 45.
- **NPRM switch-on**: template adds owner name (Fannie Mae), option descriptions per owner, website list, Spanish; forbearance-end notice timer added (12.4).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **11.2-Q1 "Provide" = mail date.** Default: mail (vendor manifest) or electronic delivery by day 45, hand-off by day 43; counsel to confirm.
2. **11.2-Q2 Courtesy notice when exempt (bk + cease).** Default: none (send nothing that could be read as collection); revisit with counsel.
3. **11.2-Q3 BSP default at day 45.** Default: full BSP rather than Form 745 alone (one touch, faster BRP); 4506-C excluded by default.
4. **11.2-Q4 Combined mailing.** Default: EI notice + BSP together; statements and state pre-foreclosure notices separate.
5. **11.2-Q5 Spanish copies.** Default: on for borrowers whose stated language is Spanish (English original enclosed), pending the NPRM.
