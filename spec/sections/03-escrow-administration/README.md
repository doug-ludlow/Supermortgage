# Section 3 — Escrow Administration

## Overview

Escrow administration is the part of the servicing lifecycle in which Supermortgage holds borrower funds in trust for taxes, property/flood insurance, borrower-paid mortgage insurance, HOA/condo assessments and ground rents; projects those disbursements a year ahead; sets the escrow portion of the monthly payment inside the RESPA ceiling; pays every bill before its penalty date; refunds surpluses; collects shortages and deficiencies; pays state-mandated interest; and, from December 1, 2026, tells Fannie Mae about every escrow deposit and disbursement the same day it is posted. The governing federal rule is 12 CFR 1024.17 (escrow accounts) plus 1024.34 (timely disbursement, 20-day refund at payoff); the Fannie Mae layer is Servicing Guide B-1-01 (Aug 12, 2026 edition; topic last revised 09/11/2024), F-1-05 (reimbursement of escrow advances), D2-3.2-06/F-1-27 (escrow at modification) and Lender Letter LL-2026-05 (escrow event reporting). Reg Z 1026.35(b) constrains escrow cancellation on higher-priced mortgage loans, and 12+ states require interest on escrow balances.

Architecture fit: the `escrow` package owns the **Escrow Analysis Engine** (deterministic aggregate-analysis math per 1024.17(d) and Appendix E), the **Disbursement Scheduler** (penalty-avoidance timers per bill), the **Waiver Lifecycle**, the **Interest Accrual** service driven by `jurisdiction_rules`, and the **Escrow Event Emitter** that writes `investor_events` for the `fnma-escrow-reporting` adapter. The `escrow` agent orchestrates these services end-to-end; the `cashiering` agent (Section 2) posts escrow deposits; the `insurance-property` and `pmi` agents (Sections 9–10) own the insurance and MI lines' bill data; the `investor-reporting` agent (Section 5) owns transport, acks and the BD2 period close; `payoff-release` (Section 16) triggers the payoff refund and short-year statement; `transfer` (Sections 1 and 17) triggers transfer analyses and short-year statements; `compliance-sentinel` watches every timer below. All money is `bigint` cents; rate math uses `decimal.js`; rounding is stated per rule.

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: Supermortgage performs every escrow function and holds the T&I custodial account established under the partner's authorization (Form 1014 via CBAM — Section 6.2; open decision in the architecture baseline §1). Every Fannie Mae escrow event carries the partner's 9-digit servicer number. Interest on escrow is a Supermortgage cost (B-1-01: Fannie Mae does not reimburse it, "whether required by law or voluntary"); escrow advances are Supermortgage advances reimbursable under F-1-05 only after the loan becomes delinquent and per the claim rules in Section 15.2. The partner signs nothing in this section; the only human touchpoints are the monthly escrow attestation in the Servicing Platform UI (`fnma_portal_operator`, Technology Guide bans automation), borrower-requested human handoffs, and policy dual-control on unusual disbursements.

Dependencies: 1.1/1.6 (boarding data: escrow flags, computation year, lines, balances, initial-statement evidence), 2.1/2.2 (deposit posting order and suspense), 5.x (`fnma-servicing-events` transport, sequence numbers, attestation package), 6.2/6.4 (T&I custodial account and Form 496A reconciliation), 7.1 (periodic statement must show the new escrow payment), 7.4 (E-SIGN consent for electronic statements), 9.1–9.6 (insurance lines, force-placed premiums, flood), 10.x (PMI lines and termination dates in projections), 12.4–12.8 (workout escrow analyses, 60-month shortage spread), 14.2 (Rule 3002.1 payment-change notices when the escrow payment changes in Chapter 13), 16.1–16.2 (payoff refund), 17.x (transfer-out short-year statement).

Changes in flight: LL-2026-05 escrow reporting is optional now and **mandatory December 1, 2026** (setup events for all active and inactive escrowed loans, then same-day deposit/disbursement events, monthly attestation BD3–BD2); the CFPB has made no change to 1024.17/1024.34 (00a §1.3); the OCC's 2026 escrow-interest preemption reaches only national banks and federal savings associations, so state interest laws apply to this nonbank platform (00a §5.5); Wisconsin eliminated escrow interest for loans originated on/after April 18, 2018, while Connecticut's floor rate (1.5%) binds for 2026. Blueprint corrections found in this section: the "retain 5 yrs" note on 3.1 is a legacy HUD rule that no longer exists in 1024.17 (retention is now 1024.38(c) plus Fannie Mae life-of-loan rules), Chapter B-1 has a single topic (B-1-01), and the 60-month post-modification shortage spread lives in B-1-01, not F-1-27.
