### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Fannie Mae (Guide Aug. 12, 2026).** *C-1.2-04 (05/15/2019):* "Once payoff funds are received, the servicer must take all actions necessary to satisfy a mortgage loan, including recording a release of lien in the real property records, in a timely manner"; for an electronic mortgage the servicer "must determine and execute the appropriate satisfaction documents ... based on the requirements of the state in which the security property is located"; when Fannie Mae is mortgagee of record, "The servicer that does not have a LPOA (or that has a power of attorney that does not cover release and satisfaction documents) must send any required release or satisfaction documents to Fannie Mae for execution, whether or not Fannie Mae is holding the custody documents"; when Fannie Mae is not mortgagee of record, "The servicer must execute any required satisfaction documents in its own name (or in MERS' name, if applicable for a MERS-registered mortgage loan)." *A2-1-04 (09/09/2020):* the servicer may request an LPOA when Fannie Mae is owner of record; it covers "full satisfaction or release of a mortgage or the request to a trustee for a full reconveyance of a deed of trust," partial releases, modifications, subordinations, foreclosure documents, conveyances to insurers and "assignments or endorsements of mortgages, deeds of trust, or promissory notes"; otherwise "the servicer must send the documents requiring execution ... by email, when permitted. If, however, an original document must be executed by Fannie Mae, the servicer must send the document by regular or overnight mail." *F-1-10 (05/15/2019):* one LPOA form per jurisdiction with a cover letter stating the jurisdictions, any changes and recording instructions; "Have the executed LPOA from Fannie Mae recorded in the proper jurisdiction" — "The servicer is authorized to submit the LPOA for recordation immediately upon its receipt"; documents for Fannie Mae execution go to the SF CPM Servicing Documents mailbox (`sfcpm.servicingdocuments@fanniemae.com`) or "Fannie Mae, Attn: SF CPM, Documents, 5600 Granite Parkway VII, Plano, TX 75024" with the loan number, reason, executable documents and a return shipping label; custody documents are obtained with "Request for Release/Return of Documents (Form 2009)" to the document custodian and returned when no longer needed. *F-4-02 (05/13/2026)* confirms the SF CPM Documents channel for "Satisfactions/Discharges of paid off mortgage loans, Servicer LPOA requests." *F-1-09 (10/19/2016):* "Prepare and execute the appropriate release or satisfaction documents identified by the Fannie Mae loan number and, if applicable, the MERS MIN"; eNote payoff — update the eRegistry, deactivate the MERS registration and, where the state requires return of a paper note, give the borrower a paper copy of the eNote marked "Copy" and "Paid-In-Full" with an explanatory letter; "The servicer must not pass on to the borrower or to Fannie Mae any penalty fee it has to pay because it failed to process the release and satisfaction documents within the required time frame." (F-1-09 still prints a Herndon, VA "Vendor Oversight, Legal Document Execution" address — superseded by the F-1-10/F-4-02 Plano channel.) *C-1.2-05 (05/15/2019)* fee conditions and *F-1-05 (06/11/2025)* reimbursement (16.1/16.2).

**MERS (Rules of Membership eff. June 30, 2025; Procedures Manual Release 24.2).** Rule 2 §7: "When circumstances warrant that a lien should be released ... any Member servicing or identified as the Servicer, Subservicer, or Investor ... shall be responsible ... to execute a release of the Security Instrument (a 'Lien Release') duly executed by a MERS Signing Officer." Rule 3 §3: MERS furnishes "a duly authorized MERS Corporate Resolution" granting "MERS Signing Officers the limited authority to take action as officers of MERS," and "All actions required to be taken by Members under these Rules to release, discharge, assign, or convey a Mortgage ... shall be taken by and through a MERS Signing Officer or a MERS Corporate Officer in the name of MERS." Rule 2 §4: discrepancies with the land records must be "promptly correct[ed]." Lien Release via Simplifile is priced at $1.00 (MERS pricing, 00b N1).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) "Commonly 30–90 days" understates the spread: Maryland requires delivery within **7 days**, New Jersey a 10-day notice and 30 days from fee receipt, and several statutes key the clock to a *request* rather than payoff (CT, WA, MN) — the engine anchors at payoff (earliest) and records the statutory anchor for penalty exposure. (2) Florida's own release deadline is **45 days** under §701.03 (the "60 days" commonly cited is §701.04(2)). (3) Texas has no direct deadline. (4) The blueprint's "county recorder, title vendor" omits the three signatory paths (MERS Signing Officer, LPOA, Fannie Mae execution), the custody-document dependency (Form 2009), and the F-1-09 penalty non-pass-through rule. (5) Automation class (b) is confirmed: the `signing_officer` execution is a legally required human touchpoint; everything else is agent-run.

### Operational prerequisites
- **MERS Signing Officers:** Supermortgage employees certified under the partner's MERS Corporate Resolution (CRMS entries) *and* under Supermortgage's own resolution for MINs where Supermortgage is Servicer — partner MERS administrator + Supermortgage; before first release (2–4 weeks); annual re-certification and MERS Annual Report evidence (1.5).
- **Fannie Mae LPOAs** per jurisdiction where Fannie Mae is (or may be) mortgagee of record on the boarded book, requested with cover letters (F-1-10), recorded on receipt, indexed in `lpoas` — Supermortgage (or partner as grantee, with Supermortgage sub-delegation permitted only if the LPOA text allows **[UNVERIFIED]**); 4–8 weeks.
- **Partner officer signatures** for non-MERS loans where the partner is mortgagee of record: a partner corporate resolution/POA naming Supermortgage signing officers, or a partner `officer` SLA — partner; before boarding non-MERS loans.
- **Notary capacity:** commissioned notaries (including RON commissions where state law allows remote acknowledgment of a signing officer's signature) and a RON platform with tamper-evident audit trails; `jurisdiction_rules.release.ron_accepted`, `.erecord_accepted`, `.esign_accepted` per county — Supermortgage; 4 weeks.
- **eRecording vendor accounts** (Simplifile primary; CSC/ePN backup) with API credentials, recording-fee ACH, county coverage tables (≈2,400 of 3,600+ offices, 00b N15); paper-recording print/mail path for the rest — Supermortgage; 4–12 weeks.
- **Document custodian** interface for Form 2009 requests (custodian portal/API or e-mail; Fannie Mae's custodian for eNotes) with an SLA for original-document return **[custodian SLA UNVERIFIED]** — partner/Supermortgage.
- **Templates:** state/county-specific release instruments with recorder formatting rules (margins, cover sheets, PRIA indexing), MIN and Fannie Mae loan number fields, counsel-approved — 6–8 weeks.
- `jurisdiction_rules.release`: `instrument_type`, `deadline_days`, `deadline_anchor` ∈ {payoff, request, fee_receipt}, `penalty jsonb`, `original_note_required` (CA trustee delivery; MD; NY on request), `borrower_delivery_required` (FL recorded release; GA via clerk), `fee_pass_through_allowed`, `fee_cap_cents` (CA $45 presumption), `title_self_release_mechanism` (TX/AZ/VA), `public_trustee` (CO), `trustee_reconveyance` (CA/WA) — compliance; counsel review.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `loan.paid_in_full` (16.2) → open `release_tasks` for the loan (one per recorded security instrument/county; multi-parcel or multi-county loans get one task each).
- `custody.documents.received`, `lpoa.recorded`, `signing.completed`, `notarization.completed`, `erecord.accepted/rejected/recorded`, `recorder.paper.returned`, `trustee.reconveyance.recorded` (CA/WA third-party trustee), `fnma.execution.returned` (SF CPM), `payoff.reversed` (cancel/void the task before execution; after recording → `attorney`).
- Also: charge-off release (D1-1-02, Section 15/13) and partial release/subordination requests reuse the same pipeline with `release_kind` ∈ {full, partial, subordination, charge_off} (only `full` is specified here).

#### State machine
`opened` → `awaiting_custody_docs` (only if `original_note_required` or the recorder requires the original instrument) → `prepared` (instrument generated, checklist passed) → `awaiting_execution` (escalation to `signing_officer`; or `sent_to_fnma` / `sent_to_partner` paths) → `executed` → `notarized` → `submitted` (eRecord or paper) → `recorded` | `rejected` (→ `prepared` after fix) → `borrower_notified` → `mers_deactivation_pending` (16.4) → `closed`. Trustee path (CA/WA third-party trustee): `executed` (request for reconveyance) → `delivered_to_trustee` → `trustee_recorded` → `borrower_notified`. Public-trustee path (CO): `submitted_to_public_trustee` → `recorded`. Side states: `void` (payoff reversed before execution), `post_recording_reversal` (`attorney`), `penalty_exposure` (deadline breached — exposure computed, `officer` informed).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Instrument and signatory selection** (`selectReleaseInstrument`): read `jurisdiction_rules.release.instrument_type` for the state and security-instrument type (mortgage vs deed of trust vs security deed), then the mortgagee of record from the title chain in `documents` (recorded assignments, 1.4/1.5): (a) MIN active and MERS is mortgagee/beneficiary of record (MOM or assigned to MERS) → execute in MERS' name by a MERS Signing Officer under the partner's resolution (Rule 2 §7 / Rule 3 §3); the instrument recites MERS as nominee, the MIN and the Fannie Mae loan number; (b) Fannie Mae is mortgagee/assignee of record → execute as attorney-in-fact under a recorded LPOA for that state (`SM_LPOA_RECORDED_GATE`), else send to SF CPM Documents for execution; (c) partner (or a predecessor whose rights the partner holds by recorded assignment) is mortgagee of record → partner officer (or Supermortgage under the partner's POA); (d) prior lender of record with an unrecorded/missing assignment → obtain and record the assignment first (MERS assignment if the loan is registered; otherwise from the prior lender) or an attorney-approved alternative → `attorney`; (e) deed-of-trust states with a third-party trustee → request for full reconveyance to the trustee with the original note and DOT (CA §2941(b)(1)), or substitution of trustee plus full reconveyance where practice and the DOT permit **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED practice]**; (f) Colorado → request for release to the public trustee with the original canceled note (or lost-instrument bond), DOT copy, release fee and the grantor's address (§38-39-102).
2. **Originals.** Request custody documents (Form 2009) on payoff day when `original_note_required` (CA third-party trustee, CO, MD, NY on request, any recorder demanding the original) or when the borrower asks for the note; otherwise leave documents with the custodian until the release is recorded, then request return/cancellation per the custodial agreement. Lost originals → lost-note/lost-instrument affidavit executed by the `signing_officer` under the applicable authority (LPOA scope includes endorsements/assignments; a lost note affidavit on Fannie Mae's behalf requires Fannie Mae execution unless the LPOA covers it **[UNVERIFIED]**) and `attorney` review for state form requirements.
3. **Content checklist** (machine-checked before execution): correct instrument title; MIN and Fannie Mae loan number (F-1-09); original recording reference (book/page/instrument number, recording date); legal description where the county requires it; borrower/trustor names as recorded; property address/APN; statement of full satisfaction; signatory block matching the authority (MERS as nominee for the original lender and its successors and assigns, by Supermortgage as Subservicer for the partner; or Fannie Mae by attorney-in-fact); notary acknowledgment in the recording state's form; PRIA cover sheet/indexing data; return-to address; fees computed from the recorder's schedule.
4. **Execution and notarization.** The `signing_officer` reviews the package (payoff evidence, checklist result, authority certificate) and signs; wet-ink or e-signature and in-person or remote online notarization per `jurisdiction_rules.release.esign_accepted/ron_accepted` for the *recording* state (state RON law and county acceptance govern; SVC-2026-04's RON video-retention change concerns loan closings and does not change release practice). Audit trail (signer identity, IP, timestamps, notary journal) stored as `documents`.
5. **Recording.** eRecord through the primary vendor when the county participates (PRIA XML package: document type, parties, MIN/loan references, image, fees); rejections are fixed within 2 BD; paper packages (printed instrument, check for fees, self-addressed return) for non-participating counties via `print-mail`, tracked by mail-tracking barcode; walk-in only via a local agent for imminent statutory breaches.
6. **Deadline computation.** `deadline_at = payoff_date + deadline_days` in calendar days with no weekend/holiday roll-forward (conservative; statutes are silent); statutes anchored to a request (CT/WA/MN) or fee receipt (NJ) also compute their statutory date for exposure, but the operational timer starts at payoff. Where the statute is satisfied by *delivery* rather than recording (MA within 45 days to the closing attorney; MD within 7 days to the disbursing agent), delivery with evidence satisfies the timer and a separate recording monitor continues.
7. **Fees.** Third-party recording/notary/trustee/public-trustee fees are charged to the borrower only when C-1.2-05's four conditions and `jurisdiction_rules.release.fee_pass_through_allowed` hold (CA ≤ $45 presumption; MD trustee ≤ $15; NJ service fee ≤ $25) and were disclosed on the payoff statement (16.1); otherwise corporate expense with the F-1-05 claim where eligible. Statutory penalties, if incurred, are corporate expense and never billed to the borrower or Fannie Mae (F-1-09).
8. **Borrower notification.** Always send `NTC_LIEN_RELEASE_RECORDED` with the recorded image (FL: required; GA: the clerk transmits the original — we still send our copy); return the original note marked "Paid in Full" where state law or the borrower's request requires (NY 45 days; MD; the F-1-09 eNote paper copy).
9. **Penalty exposure model.** `penalty_exposure_cents` is computed daily for breached tasks from the matrix (e.g., NY tiered, CT $200/week capped $5,000, OH $250 then $100/day capped $5,000) and reported by `compliance-sentinel`.

**Worked example 1 (Ohio, MERS MOM, eRecording county).** Payoff posted Fri 10/16/2026 → `release_tasks` opened; instrument `release_of_mortgage`; MERS is mortgagee of record; `signatory_path = mers_signing_officer`; no originals required. Deadline: 10/16 + 90 = **Thu 01/14/2027** (R.C. §5301.36). Prepared Wed 10/21 (checklist passed; MIN 1000123-0000456789-0 and Fannie Mae loan number printed) → `signing_officer` e-signs with RON acknowledgment Fri 10/23 → Simplifile package submitted the same day → county accepted and recorded Mon 10/26 (instrument no. returned; recording fee $34.00 paid by vendor ACH and matched to the borrower-collected `recording_fee_payable`) → `NTC_LIEN_RELEASE_RECORDED` with the image e-delivered (consent on file) Tue 10/27 → `SM_BORROWER_RELEASE_NOTICE_5BD` satisfied; 16.4 deactivation due 60 days after recording (Fri 12/25/2026; policy target Mon 11/02). Timers `SM_RELEASE_PREPARE_5BD` (due 10/23), `SM_RELEASE_EXECUTE_3BD` (due 10/28) and `SM_RELEASE_SUBMIT_21` (due 11/06) all satisfied.

**Worked example 2 (California deed of trust, third-party trustee).** Same payoff date; `original_note_required = true` → Form 2009 to the custodian Fri 10/16 (`FNMA_F110_FORM2009_CUSTODY_REQUEST_1BD` satisfied); originals received Fri 10/23. Beneficiary duty: deliver the original note, DOT and request for full reconveyance to the trustee within 30 calendar days → **Sun 11/15/2026** (no roll-forward; operational target Fri 11/13). MERS Signing Officer executes the request for full reconveyance Tue 10/27; package couriered to the trustee 10/28 (delivery evidence) → `CA_CC2941_TRUSTEE_DELIVERY_30` satisfied; trustee's 21-day clock runs to 11/18 (`CA_CC2941_TRUSTEE_RECORD_21`); reconveyance recorded 11/05; borrower notified 11/09. Where the DOT names a trustee the servicer controls (or substitution is permitted), the `substitution_of_trustee_and_full_reconveyance` variant is executed and eRecorded directly (target 10/29), removing the trustee dependency.

**Worked example 3 (Maryland).** Settlement agent wires the payoff Fri 10/16. `MD_RP_7106_RELEASE_DELIVERY_7` due **Fri 10/23**: the release is prepared 10/19, executed and notarized 10/20, and the executed original is delivered to the settlement agent 10/21 with delivery evidence (the agent records it); the custody request for the original note runs in parallel and the note marked "paid" follows when received. Recording is monitored (`SM_RECORDING_CONFIRM_30`).

- **`erecording`** adapter (Simplifile API primary; CSC/ePN secondary): package create/submit, status polling or webhooks, reject reasons, recorded image and reference download, fee statements; PRIA 2.4.x/3.0 XML; idempotency by `(release_task_id, attempt)`; county coverage table refreshed monthly; sandbox with recorder-reject simulations. Also carries the MERS Lien Release transaction where offered ($1.00; 16.4).
- **`print-mail`**: paper recording packages with fee checks (positive pay, 6.4) and borrower notices; mail tracking.
- **`custodian`**: Form 2009 requests (custodian portal/API/e-mail per custodian), document tracking, return of documents; Fannie Mae eVault for eNotes (16.4).
- **`mers`** (16.4): deactivation after recording; MIN data validation before release drafting (Rule 2 §4).
- **Fannie Mae SF CPM Documents** (e-mail/mail, not a portal): package = loan number, reason ("Satisfaction — no LPOA for <state>"), executable PDF, cover letter, return label; tracked in `integration_messages` with a 15-BD follow-up.
- **RON/e-sign platform**: signing sessions for `signing_officers` and notaries; audit-trail export to `documents`.
- **`attorney-network`**: chain-of-title cures, lost-instrument affidavits, contested releases.
- **Recorder outage / vendor outage:** fall back to the secondary vendor or paper; timers unaffected; statutory breach risk escalates to `officer` at 70%.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`payoff-release` agent: `selectReleaseInstrument`, `requestCustodyDocuments`, `draftReleaseInstrument` (template merge — the LLM may resolve name/legal-description discrepancies against the recorded instrument image and flag, never invent), `runReleaseChecklist`, `routeForExecution` (escalation to the `signing_officer` role with the complete package: payoff evidence, authority certificate, instrument, recorder rules), `scheduleNotarySession`, `submitRecording`, `handleRecorderReject`, `notifyBorrower`, `computePenaltyExposure`, `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{release_task_id, state, county, instrument_type, mortgagee_of_record, signatory_path, evidence[], deadline_at, checklist_results, submission_channel, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: no execution without a passed checklist and proof of payoff; the agent never signs; no borrower charge outside C-1.2-05; no release for a loan with `payoff_reversed` or open `fnma_liquidated_in_error` without `officer` approval; confidence < 0.9 on mortgagee-of-record → hold and `attorney`/title review before deadline − 15 days. Escalations: `signing_officer` (execution — always), `attorney` (chain gaps, lost instruments, contested releases, post-recording reversal), `officer` (partner) for partner-executed documents and penalty write-offs, `human_agent` for borrower questions, no `fnma_portal_operator` (SF CPM is e-mail/mail). Disclosure: none beyond 4.x for any calls. AI-off path: ops-console release workbench generating the same packages; recorder submissions via the vendor UI.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-out after payoff but before recording:** the release obligation stays with the entity that received the payoff (Supermortgage) — task continues; the transferee is informed; MERS deactivation follows.
- **Payoff reversed after execution/recording:** stop if unrecorded; if recorded, `attorney` for a re-recording/reinstatement of lien (state-specific) and the loan is serviced as unsecured pending cure; `officer` informed.
- **Bankruptcy discharge without payoff:** no release (lien survives) — out of scope; charge-off release per D1-1-02 (Section 15).
- **SCRA/disaster:** no effect on the release duty.
- **Successor in interest / deceased borrower:** release runs to the trustor of record; notices to the confirmed successor (4.4).
- **Multi-county / multi-parcel:** one task per recording office; all must record.
- **Recorder rejects for legal-description or margin defects:** fix within 2 BD; repeated rejects → `attorney`.
- **Lost note / lost security instrument:** affidavit path; CO public trustee requires a lost-instrument bond **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Assignment gap discovered at payoff:** record a corrective assignment first (MERS or prior lender); statutory clock keeps running — `attorney` engaged immediately; exposure logged.
- **eNote:** no paper original; release executed normally; eRegistry status + paper copy where required (16.4).
- **Title company self-release (TX/AZ/VA) or settlement-agent release (VA/MD):** if a third party records a release first, verify and close the task with the third-party instrument; still deactivate MERS.
- **Vendor outage:** secondary vendor/paper; statutory dates unchanged.

### Open questions / decisions
1. LPOA grantee: partner (as servicer of record) with sub-delegation to Supermortgage signers, or Supermortgage directly (A2-1-04 permits "the servicer")? **Default: request LPOAs in the partner's name naming Supermortgage officers as additional attorneys-in-fact where Fannie Mae's form allows; otherwise partner officers sign [UNVERIFIED form flexibility].**
2. California: substitution of trustee + full reconveyance (fast) vs request to the original trustee (statutory path)? **Default: substitution + reconveyance where the DOT permits; otherwise trustee request within 30 days.**
3. Calendar-day deadlines falling on weekends/holidays: roll forward? **Default: no roll-forward (conservative).**
4. Borrower release fee pass-through where permitted: charge or absorb? **Default: charge exact third-party cost where permitted (16.1 decision 2).**
5. Custodian SLA for Form 2009 returns and Fannie Mae SF CPM execution turnaround **[UNVERIFIED]** — negotiate ≤ 5 BD and ≤ 10 BD respectively; timers set to monitor at 10/15 BD.
