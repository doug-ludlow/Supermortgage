### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Uniform instruments — which forms.** Selling Guide B8-2-01, *Security Instruments for Conventional Mortgages* (last updated 02/06/2019): "Security instruments for regularly amortizing mortgages include the Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac Uniform Mortgages, Deeds of Trust, and Security Deeds"; lenders must use the instrument for "the applicable jurisdiction, mortgage type, lien type, property type, and transaction type"; "The current versions of the Fannie Mae standard first mortgage security instruments are posted on Fannie Mae's Legal Documents website"; "If...a security instrument is modified in any way, Fannie Mae will consider it to be a nonstandard document"; authorized changes may be made "at lender's option" but "lenders must make sure that any changes they make...comply with all applicable laws"; riders modify the standard instrument ("a rider is needed for an adjustable-rate mortgage as well as for a security property" of certain types); Master/Short Forms let a lender "record a Master Form in a given recording jurisdiction, and then subsequently record a Short Form" with "Any applicable riders...attached to the Short Form." B8-3-01, *Notes for Conventional Mortgages* (09/02/2020): "Lenders should use the note that is correct for the applicable mortgage type, lien type, property type, and product type"; "The multistate note can be used in most jurisdictions unless the security property is located in a jurisdiction for which Fannie Mae publishes a state-specific mortgage note"; fixed-rate "Authorized changes that must or may be made to these documents are set out in the instructions that accompany each document"; for ARMs without a state-specific note "the lender must use the multistate note for that particular ARM plan and amend it as necessary to satisfy the requirements of the jurisdiction" by "reprinting the amended documents, adding addenda to the documents, or making the changes on the documents themselves." The Fannie Mae Notes library page (fetched 2026-09-10) lists Form 3200 (Multistate Fixed-Rate Note) with state-specific fixed-rate notes 3202 Alaska, 3210 Florida, 3220 Maine, 3230 New Hampshire, 3233 New York, 3239 Pennsylvania, 3246 Vermont, 3247 Virginia, 3249 West Virginia, 3250 Wisconsin, 3253 Puerto Rico; 30-day Average SOFR ARM notes Form 3441 (adjustable) and 3442 (fixed/adjustable) with the same state variants (3441.02…3441.53; 3442.02…3442.53); and Texas Home Equity notes 3244.1 (fixed) and 3442.44 (fixed/adjustable). The Security Instruments library lists standard, Master and Short forms per state — Arizona Form 3003, California 3005, Florida 3010, New York 3033, Ohio 3036, Texas 3044 (and Texas Home Equity 3044.1 per B5-4.1-03) — and the MERS Rider (Form 3158); "Users may begin using revised legal documents immediately" and must use them "by no later than six months after the revision date." The January 2023 Uniform Instruments Fact Sheet: "Lenders are required to use these updated security instruments, notes, riders and addenda... for loans delivered to Fannie Mae with note dates on or after Jan. 1, 2023"; "These updated instruments cannot be used with prior versions of any instrument"; e.g., "A Michigan Mortgage with a footer date of '07/2021' cannot be used with a Planned Unit Development Rider with a footer date of '1/01.'" Form 3200 (footer "MULTISTATE FIXED RATE NOTE—Single Family—Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac UNIFORM INSTRUMENT Form 3200 07/2021", fetched 2026-09-10) provides: §1 "In return for a loan in the amount of U.S. $ ***_***** (the "Principal") that I have received"; "The Lender or anyone who takes this Note by transfer and who is entitled to receive payments under this Note is called the "Note Holder.""; §3(A) "I will make my Monthly Payment on the ***_***** day of each month beginning on ***_******_******_, *****." and "If, on ***_*_, *****_, I still owe amounts under this Note, I will pay those amounts on that date, which is called the "Maturity Date.""; §4 "I may make a full Prepayment or partial Prepayments without paying a Prepayment charge."; §6(A) "If the Note Holder has not received the full amount of any Monthly Payment by the end of ******_**** calendar days after the date it is due, I will pay a late charge to the Note Holder. The amount of the charge will be __***% of my overdue Monthly Payment."; §6(B) "If I do not pay the full amount of each Monthly Payment on the date it is due, I will be in default."; §10 "This Note is a uniform instrument with limited variations in some jurisdictions."; signature block "WITNESS THE HAND(S) AND SEAL(S) OF THE UNDERSIGNED. [Sign Original Only]". **Note terms — late charge and prepayment.** B8-3-02, *Special Note Provisions and Language Requirements* (06/03/2020): "The late charge must be a minimum of 0% and up to 5% of the principal and interest (P&I) payment," for payments not received by the 15th day after the due date, "subject to applicable state law restrictions"; condo/unit properties must include the unit number in the property address on the note; mandatory-arbitration clauses make the loan ineligible unless the provision automatically voids on transfer to Fannie Mae (lender notifies borrowers within 60 days using specified language). Prepayment: the uniform note carries no prepayment charge (§4 above); B8-3-02 "contains no information about prepayment penalties" — the platform never renders a prepayment provision. State variation example (verified): NY Real Property Law §254-b — the late charge "shall not exceed and shall only be enforced to the extent of two percent of such delinquent instalment" and "no charge shall be imposed on any instalment paid within fifteen days after the due date" for one- to six-family owner-occupied residences. California Civ. Code §2954.4 could not be fetched (robots) — **[UNVERIFIED]**; Arizona and Ohio late-charge caps for first-lien conventional loans are `jurisdiction_rules` items **[UNVERIFIED]** — the default fill is 5%/15 days capped by the state row.

**Signatures.** B8-3-03, *Signature Requirements for Notes* (02/07/2024): "An individual whose credit (credit history, income, assets, liabilities, etc.) is used in qualifying for the loan must sign the note"; "An individual whose credit was not used in qualifying for the loan, but who does have an ownership interest in the property must be named in and sign the security instrument, but is not required to sign the note"; "An individual who is a co-signer or guarantor and who does not have an ownership interest in the property must sign the note, but is not named in (or required to sign) the security instrument"; "An attorney-in-fact may sign the note"; guardians with "unlimited power over the ward's affairs"; "A borrower's signature should not contradict the name typed below the signature line"; significant variations "are not acceptable unless the lender obtains a name affidavit." B8-2-03, *Signature Requirements for Security Instruments* (10/22/2013): "Each person who has an ownership interest in the security property" must sign; "The spouse or domestic partner of any person who has an interest in the property" must sign where state law requires it to waive marital/domestic-partnership rights; an attorney-in-fact may sign if the lender obtains a copy of the POA and "the lender must ensure that recordation has been effected" where required. Reg Z §1026.36(g) (00a-fed §12, eCFR 9/08/2026): the organization's "name and NMLSR ID" and "the name of the individual loan originator (as the name appears in the NMLSR) with primary responsibility for the origination and... that NMLSR ID" must appear on "the credit application; the disclosures required by §1026.19(e) and (f); the note or loan contract; and the security instrument."

**MERS as original mortgagee.** B8-7-01, *Mortgage Electronic Registration Systems (MERS), Inc.* (05/01/2024): "A seller/servicer that wants to register a newly originated loan with MERS may prefer to designate MERS as the nominee for the beneficiary in the security instrument"; the instrument must "show MERS as the nominee for the seller/servicer, define and name the originating seller/servicer, and obtain the borrower's acknowledgment of MERS' role"; "For each MERS-registered loan delivered to a document custodian, the seller/servicer must indicate the MIN on the security instrument and related documents"; "If a seller/servicer registers a loan on the MERS system before delivering it to Fannie Mae, the seller/servicer must ensure that the MIN is registered in MERS and names itself as the investor"; "After Fannie Mae purchases or securitizes the mortgage, Fannie Mae notifies MERS to update its records to reflect Fannie Mae's ownership interest" (30.1 consumes); Montana, Oregon and Washington require the MERS Rider (Form 3158) and "post-closing assignments to MERS are prohibited" there; "In the state of Maine, sellers/servicers must use the MERS Mortgage Assignment (Form 3749)" (Nov 2025 instruction change: record the assignment promptly after the mortgage). MERS System Procedures Manual Rel. 26.1 (00b-orig N1): "A MOM loan must be registered no later than seven (7) calendar days after the Note Date, or Funding Date for refinance loans or loans in escrow states" — 26.4 owns `MERS_PROC_MOM_REGISTER_7`; "The Member generates the Check Digit for component 3 of the MIN" — the MIN is generated here, before rendering.

**Riders.** B8-4-01 (06/05/2018): "The Multistate PUD Rider (Form 3150) or the Multistate Condominium Rider (Form 3140)" for project units; "The Multistate 1–4 Family Rider (Form 3170)" for investment properties or 2–4 unit principal residences; "The Multistate Second Home Rider (Form 3890)"; "The applicable Multistate Adjustable Rate Rider (and, if applicable, an addendum to the rider)" for all ARMs (30-day Average SOFR riders 3141/3142 with state variants, e.g., 3141.53/3142.53 for Puerto Rico per the July 2021 archive entry); leasehold estates need "A rider that includes a cross-default provision" ("Fannie Mae does not publish a standard rider"); a revocable-trust rider may be used (B8-5-02 — optional; Fannie Mae sample for California).

**eNote form.** B8-8-02 (12/13/2023): "eMortgages must be originated using the Uniform Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac form of eNote"; "Fannie Mae requires creation of eNotes in the MISMO SMART Doc® format"; the uniform eNote "modification includes addition of a specific provision (Section 11, or Section 12 in case of ARM eNotes) that addresses electronic nature"; "all eNotes must be registered on the MERS eRegistry and contain valid, unique 18-digit Mortgage Identification Number"; for trade names the "legal entity name must also be included on eNote (example: ABC Financial, Inc., dba XYZ Mortgage)"; "borrower signs only eNote at closing and does not also sign paper version of Uniform note"; errors "typically require new note executed; original should be de-activated via Registration Reversal," while clerical errors in Exhibit E-2-06 may be fixed by "eNote correction agreement, including name affidavit." E-2-07 (12/13/2023): header "Note (For Electronic Signature)"; footer "MULTISTATE FIXED RATE eNOTE--Single Family -- Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac Uniform Instrument Form 3200e"; clause text: "The only copy of this Electronic Note that is the Authoritative Copy is the copy identified by the Note Holder named in the Note Holder Registry"; "This Electronic Note will be registered in the Note Holder Registry operated by MERSCORP Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation"; "I agree that the Note Holder has the right, at any time to transfer the registration of this Electronic Note to another Note Holder Registry"; "I agree that the Note Holder has the right, at any time, to convert this Electronic Note into a Paper Note"; "Electronic Note means an electronically created note" used as a Transferable Record under UETA/E-SIGN. eMortgage Technical Requirements v3.2 (February 2026): "eNotes and Addenda delivered to Fannie Mae in SMART Doc format must be in Category One SMART Doc format as specified by MISMO's SMART Document Implementation Guide V. 1.0 or later version"; "SMART Doc eNotes must be created to the SMART Doc V. 1.0.2 specification using the Closing DTD 2.3.1"; "The hashing algorithm used to apply the tamper-evident seal must be SHA-256 or greater"; "For every required data point within the DATA element of the SMART Doc eNote, an ARC must be present that validates the corresponding data point in the VIEW element." #### State machine

`closing_document_sets.status`: `pending_data` → `snapshot_taken` (final CD received + CTC + 24.4 gates; `closer`) → `generated` (all `closing_documents` rendered; `closer`) → `qc_failed` (any `document_qc_checks.result='fail'`; back to `pending_data` on data fix or `generated` on template fix) | `qc_passed` → `released` (`closing.documents.released` to the settlement agent/eClosing platform; `closer`, blocked by the gates below) → `executed` (26.2 sets every required `closing_documents.execution_status ≥ signed`) → `closed` (26.3 `loan.funded`). Side transitions: `released` → `superseded` (re-draw: new set with `redraw_reason` ∈ {cd_corrected, rate_change, date_change, vesting_change, template_retired, qc_defect_found, borrower_request}) — every release after signing has begun requires `closing.documents.redrawn` and 25.2's corrected-CD logic; `voided` (application withdrawn/denied). Guards: no transition to `released` unless `qc_passed`, `SM_O71_TEMPLATE_VERSION_GATE`, `SM_O71_DOC_QC_PASS_GATE`, and the TX gates (if applicable) are open; `executed` only from `released`.

#### Business rules and calculations

**Document-set profile selection (`selectDocumentSet`).** Inputs: state, product (fixed/ARM plan), transaction type, occupancy, property type, project type, vesting (individual/trust), signer capacities (POA, guardian, non-borrowing title holder, spouse-waiver state), buydown, TX 50(a)(6) flag, NY CEMA flag, `closing.enote_default=true`, partner eMortgage approval, RON authorization for the property state (26.2 matrix), settlement-agent eClosing eligibility (26.2). Rules: (1) note form = state-specific note if the library has one for the state and product, else the multistate form (B8-3-01); ARM → 3441/3442 family for the SOFR plan plus the matching rider (3141/3142); (2) security instrument = the state form (mortgage/deed of trust/security deed) with MOM language, plus Form 3158 in MT/OR/WA and Form 3749 in ME; (3) riders per B8-4-01: condo → 3140; PUD → 3150; investment or 2–4 unit → 3170; second home → 3890; ARM → rider; leasehold → cross-default rider (counsel-drafted; nonstandard-document review); trust → optional revocable-trust rider per state matrix; (4) eNote (3200e/ARM eNote) only if the partner is eMortgage-approved and the loan is not TX 50(a)(6), NY CEMA, Puerto Rico, HomeStyle, co-op or single-close C-to-P; POA and buydown loans stay eNote-eligible with supplemental eDelivered documents; (5) TX 50(a)(6) → Form 3244.1/3442.44 note, Form 3044.1 security instrument, Form 3185 affidavit and agreement, fair-market-value acknowledgment with the appraisal attached, closing receipt, §50(g) notice on file, T-42/T-42.1 in the closing instructions, `closing_type=wet` (no electronic signing), closing location per §153.15; (6) NY CEMA → Form 3172 with exhibits, consolidated note, new-money security instrument (Form 3033) when new funds are advanced, §255 affidavit, `closing_type ∈ {wet, hybrid}` (paper note); (7) NMLSR block (organization name/ID, individual MLO name/ID) rendered on the note, security instrument, final 1003 (LE/CD carry it in 21.2/25.2); (8) signers: note = every borrower whose credit was used (+ co-signers/guarantors); security instrument = every title holder + spouses/domestic partners in `jurisdiction_rules.spousal_signature_required` states; trusts per E-2-04 (trustee line on both; settlor acknowledgment on the security instrument; "individually and as Trustee" on the note when the trustee is a credit applicant); POA → "by [agent], as attorney-in-fact" lines and the POA copy in the set.

**Note terms (`computeNoteTerms`).** `principal_cents` = final CD loan amount. `note_rate` = `locks.note_rate` (3-decimal precision; DU final must match within B3-2-10 tolerances). `term_months` from the product. `first_payment_date` = the first day of the second month after the scheduled disbursement month when disbursement is after the 1st (26.3 computes per-diem; the note only carries the date); `maturity_date` = first payment date + (term − 1) months. `pi_cents` = round_half_up(P × r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n), 2) with r = note_rate/12 at full precision. Late charge: `pct = min(5.0, jurisdiction_rules.late_charge_cap_pct)`, `days = max(15, jurisdiction_rules.late_charge_min_days)` (B8-3-02 fixes 15 days; a state may require a longer grace, never shorter). Prepayment: none. ARM: index (30-day Average SOFR), margin, first change date, caps from the plan matrix; the qualifying rate stays in DU, not on the note.

**Worked example 1 — fixture refinance (Phoenix, AZ; consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026).** Snapshot: $560,000.00 limited cash-out refinance, 30-year fixed, note rate 6.125%, 1-unit primary residence, individual vesting (borrower and non-borrower spouse both on title), escrowed, no MI, no buydown, MERS MOM, eNote eligible (AZ RON-authorized; partner eMortgage-approved). First payment **Jan 1, 2027**; maturity = Jan 1, 2027 + 359 months = **Dec 1, 2056**. Late charge 5% after 15 days → $170.13 on an overdue P&I payment ($3,402.62 × 0.05 = 170.131 → $170.13). First-month split for the QC cross-check: interest $2,858.33, principal $544.29; 360 rounded payments amortize to −$1.00, so the final payment is adjusted by the servicer (not a note term). **Worked example 3 — 2-1 temporary buydown on the Columbus, OH purchase fixture.** $412,000 loan (90% LTV; price ≈ $457,778), assumed note rate 6.500% (illustrative; the fixture's rate is not fixed by the brief): P&I at 6.500% = $2,604.12; year 1 at 4.500% = $2,087.54 (subsidy $516.58/month, $6,198.96/year); year 2 at 5.500% = $2,339.29 (subsidy $264.83/month, $3,177.96/year); total subsidy **$9,376.92**, funded by the seller at closing into SM's T&I custodial account (servicer-held per SVC-2026-02). The note and the CD show 6.500% and $2,604.12; the buydown agreement (signed by the seller as provider and the borrower; `signing_officer` counter-signs where the partner is a party) states the schedule, that the borrower remains obligated for the full note payment if funds are unavailable, and that funds credit the payoff or return per the agreement; the agreement and the funds evidence are eDelivered with the eNote (B8-8-01 supplemental documents). **Data-to-document QC (`runDocumentQc`).** Every rule compares three sources: the snapshot, the rendered document (parsed back from the SMART Doc DATA section or from the template's field map for paper), and the upstream record. Hard rules (fail = no release): loan amount, note rate, term, P&I, first payment and maturity dates, late-charge terms (note ↔ CD ↔ DU final ↔ lock); borrower legal names and capacities (note/security instrument ↔ 1003 ↔ title commitment vesting ↔ trust/POA reviews); property address incl. unit number (B8-3-02) and legal description (security instrument ↔ title commitment); MIN check digit (MERS algorithm) and MIN present on the security instrument and each rider; NMLSR organization and individual IDs identical across 1003/LE/CD/note/security instrument (§1026.36(g)); riders required = riders rendered; template family revision identical across every instrument (no mixing); eNote ARC/VIEW consistency and SMART Doc validation against the v1.0.2 schema; TX: 80% and 2% tests, one-year rule, location, itemization timing; CEMA: consolidated amount = Σ unpaid principal of prior notes + new money, exhibit lists complete. Soft rules (warn, `officer` waiver): signature name variants (B8-3-03 name affidavit), cosmetic formatting.

**Closing-instruction letter (`NTC_SM_CLOSING_INSTRUCTIONS`).** Rendered in the partner's name with SM as the fulfillment contact and, per the facility, as an additional addressee; content: transaction identifiers; document list with signing/notary/witness requirements per `closing_documents.signers`; "no changes to any document" rule and the re-draw hotline; funding conditions (all documents executed as instructed, CPL received, title date-down clear, no disbursement before the funder's written authorization; TX: rescission expiry; dry states: record-then-fund per 26.3); wire instructions reference verified in 24.4 (`wire_verifications.match_result='verified'`, never re-typed in the letter); disbursement/settlement statement reconciliation to the CD; recording instructions (instrument order, MERS Rider, Maine assignment, eRecording via the vendor or paper fallback with tracking); document return requirements (original wet note to the warehouse custodian by overnight courier within the 26.4 timer; recorded security instrument and final title policy as trailing documents); e-closing capability confirmation (platform, session ID, RON provider, notary commission state); TX 50(a)(6) items (closing at the agent's permanent office; T-42/T-42.1; Form 3185 recording; closing receipt); acknowledgment request with SLA.

#### Integrations
- **Template library / renderer** (in-house, `origination/closing`): deterministic rendering from `closing_data_snapshots` into PDF (paper) and SMART Doc v1.0.2 Category One XML+PDF view (eNote) — every artifact carries `template_revision_date`, `data_hash`, `render_hash`; idempotent by `(set_id, template_id, data_hash)`.
- **`eclosing` platform** (00b-orig N8; DocMagic/DocuSign/Snapdocs-class): outbound package (documents, signer manifest, tagging of signature/initial/notary fields, eNote SMART Doc), inbound signed artifacts and audit trails (26.2); idempotent by `set_id`; rejects → `qc_failed{platform_validation}`.
- **`title`/settlement exchange** (24.4 adapter; portal/e-mail fallback): closing instructions, document package (paper/hybrid), acknowledgment; settlement statement for CD reconciliation.
- **MERS** (`evault`/MERS adapter shared with 1.4/1.5): MIN generation (check digit locally), optional Pre-Closing Registration (member-only spec **[UNVERIFIED]**); registration itself in 26.2 (eNote) / 26.4 (paper).
- **Print/e-delivery** (shared): `NTC_TX_50A6_12DAY`, `NTC_TX_50F2_REFI_NOTICE` and the itemization (when not the CD) with receipt evidence; paper on borrower request (A2-4.1-03).
- **eRecording** vendor pre-check (26.2): document formatting rules per county (margins, cover sheets) applied at render time from `jurisdiction_rules.recording_format`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`closer` — the closing-execution runtime of the `title-closing` agent package (`origination/closing`; defined here, reused by 26.2–26.4). Tools: `evaluateDocGenGates`, `takeClosingSnapshot`, `selectDocumentSet`, `computeNoteTerms`, `generateMIN`, `renderDocument`, `buildSmartDocENote`, `runDocumentQc`, `runTxHomeEquityReview`, `buildCemaPackage`, `draftBuydownAgreement`, `draftClosingInstructions`, `releaseToPlatform`, `releaseToSettlementAgent`, `requestAcknowledgment`, `scheduleRedraw`, `writeDecision`. End-to-end behavior: opens the set at clear-to-close, waits for the final CD, takes the snapshot, chooses the profile and closing type, computes note terms, generates the MIN, renders every instrument from the active templates, runs QC, drafts and sends the closing instructions, releases the package at least one business day before signing, and re-draws on any change (routing corrected-CD questions to 25.2). Decision record: `{set_id, application_id, snapshot_hash, profile, closing_type, closing_type_reasons, templates[{form, revision, mandatory_from}], note_terms, signers, riders, qc_results, tx_review, cema, buydown, instructions_version, rationale, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails — the agent may never: alter uniform-instrument text beyond the state's authorized changes; render an instrument from a retired or not-yet-approved template; mix revision families; produce an eNote for TX 50(a)(6), NY CEMA or another excluded product; generate a prepayment-penalty or arbitration clause; fill a late charge above the state cap or 5%/15 days; release a set with a failed hard QC rule; type wire instructions into a letter; schedule a TX closing before the 12-day/1-day/one-year dates or outside a permitted office; treat an e-consent as covering documents the borrower asked to receive on paper. Escalations (each an `escalation` with SLA): `signing_officer` — lender-executed documents (TX FMV acknowledgment, buydown agreement where the partner is a party, closing instructions where a state requires a wet lender signature, Maine Form 3749) SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`; `officer` (partner) — nonstandard-document decisions (leasehold rider, counsel-modified instruments), QC waivers, template retirements affecting scheduled closings, TX classification disputes, SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`; `settlement_agent` — acknowledgment and document questions, SLA same day; `licensed_specialist`/closing attorney — attorney-state document preparation where `closing.attorney_required` (24.4); `human_agent` on borrower request. Borrower-facing: the final-1003 review and every TX notice go through the borrower app with automation disclosure; paper is provided on request without delay. Human path: ops console shows the same set with per-document regeneration, QC results and manual release under dual control.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Corrected CD after release (25.2): note terms unchanged → re-render only the CD-bearing documents; note terms changed → `superseded`, new snapshot, new set, new MIN not required (same loan) but eNote must be re-created if already tamper-sealed (Registration Reversal — 26.2).
- Lock extension moves the note date past a template `retired_after` date (e.g., a Virginia closing slipping past June 30, 2026 needs the revised Form 3047) → gate fails → regenerate on the new revision.
- Borrower declines electronic records for the closing package (A2-4.1-03) → `closing_type=wet`, paper set, no eNote; the loan remains eligible.
- Signature-name variance at the signing (B8-3-03) → name affidavit rendered on demand by the `closer`; significant variances block funding until executed.
- Non-borrowing spouse in a spousal-signature state refuses to sign the security instrument → escalation to `officer`; the loan cannot close.
- Trust vesting changes at title (trust amendment) → `trust_reviews` re-run (24.4) and signature blocks re-rendered.
- POA presented for a cash-out refinance → ineligible; the `closer` offers rescheduling for personal signing or a limited cash-out restructure through O4.
- TX: DU classifies the loan as limited cash-out but Texas law treats it as 50(a)(6) (e.g., paying off a prior 50(a)(6) lien with any cash back) → `is_50a6=true` by the TX rule engine, partner counsel confirmation on ambiguity; SFC 304.
- TX: fee test headroom consumed by a late third-party charge → the partner absorbs the excess (SM-borne under the operating model) or the closing moves; the file records the cure.
- NY CEMA: a prior lender refuses to assign the existing mortgage → the CEMA cannot be built; fall back to a new mortgage with full mortgage tax (rate quote and CD re-issued via 21.5/25.2).
- Maine: the MERS assignment must be recorded promptly after the mortgage (Nov 2025 instruction change) → 26.4 trailing-document timer seeded from the set.
- eNote SMART Doc validation fails at the platform → `qc_failed{platform_validation}`; never fall back to a paper note on the same set without a new snapshot and profile decision (paper notes cannot later become eNotes).
- Template source drift: nightly hash check of the Fannie Mae library pages detects a revised form → `document.template.retired` proposal; sets already released are unaffected only if the note date precedes the mandatory date.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Nonstandard documents.** Should SM ever accept counsel-modified instruments (leasehold cross-default rider, local-law addenda) that make the loan a "nonstandard document" delivery? Default: no — only authorized changes; nonstandard documents require `officer` approval and a Fannie Mae nonstandard-document review before generation.
2. **Late-charge fills by state.** Populate `jurisdiction_rules.late_charge` from partner counsel's matrix before go-live in each state (NY verified 2%/15 days; CA, AZ, OH unverified). Default until verified: 5%/15 days only in states with a counsel-confirmed absence of a cap; otherwise the state cap.
3. **TX 50(a)(6) scope.** Offer TX cash-out at all in the first release? Default: yes, wet-only, title-company office closings, with `officer` sign-off per loan for the first 25 loans; 50(f)(2) refinances enabled with the notice workflow.
4. **NY CEMA.** Default: build the CEMA path for refinances where the prior lender assigns; fall back to a new mortgage on refusal; Form 3172 exhibit structure to be conformed to the .docx (unread) before release.
5. **Pre-Closing MERS Registration.** Default: generate the MIN at snapshot time and register at funding (26.4) rather than pre-closing, to avoid reversals on re-draws.
6. **Closing-instruction signature.** Default: the letter is electronically signed by the `closer` under the partner's delegation with the `signing_officer` wet-signing only where a state or settlement agent requires it.
