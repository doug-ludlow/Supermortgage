### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Guide.** F-1-03 (05/13/2026): the servicer must "Reconcile its cash book to the custodial account(s) every month using the applicable Custodial Account Reconciliation Form" — Form 496 for each P&I account — reflecting "the composition of the cash book balance" at period close, and "Retain each month's reconciliation in its records in the event that Fannie Mae requests the submission of a specific Form 496 or Form 496A to Fannie Mae." A2-1-01 (12/17/2025): "The servicer must maintain monthly records of all P&I advances for delinquent mortgage loans and perform appropriate monthly reconciliation activities"; the servicer acts "as a fiduciary for, Fannie Mae." **The 45-day deadline is not in F-1-03 or F-1-20; it is in the Form 496 instructions** ("Completing the Principal and Interest (P&I) Custodial Account Reconciliation (Form 496)," © 2026): "The reconciliation must be completed within 45 days after month end." Same document: "A separate Form 496 must be prepared monthly for each P&I custodial account and for each remittance type (remittance types may not be commingled in the same reconciliation)"; "Each form must be retained with the related accounting records and provided to Fannie Mae upon request"; "All reconciling items should include the Fannie Mae loan number, root cause of the variance, dollar amount, and the age of the item"; deposits in transit "Include credits posted to the borrower's loan and not yet deposited into the bank account. Funds are to be deposited within one to two business days of receipt in accordance with Fannie Mae requirements"; disbursements in transit "Include debits that have been processed and not yet debited from the bank account. Funds are to be debited within a few business days of processing"; Section I line 7 "Difference … should always total $0.00"; Section II line 12 "The total for Composition of Cashbook should match the servicer's system of record and applicable Fannie Mae receivable totals"; for S/S "download Fannie Mae's Remittance Principal and Interest Detail Report from Fannie Mae Connect, selecting the same month that you are reconciling." Supporting documentation: bank statements, servicer cashbooks, reports used for Section II, applicable portfolio test reports. (URL: https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/23531/display, verified 2026-09-09.) SVC-2026-01 (Feb. 18, 2026): Forms 496/496A "have been converted to Excel format, supporting a streamlined reconciliation process"; F-1-03 updated; "Servicers are encouraged to implement these changes immediately."

**Form 496 line structure (verbatim line names, © 2026 instructions).** Section I: 1 Depository Balance; 2 Add Deposits in Transit; 3 Less Disbursements in Transit; 4 Total Depository Adjustments; 5 Adjusted Depository Balance; 6 Cashbook Balance; 7 Difference. Section II: 1 Net Principal and Interest (A/A only — "fixed installment net of servicing fees collected during the month reconciled but not remitted to Fannie Mae (full monthly installments only)"); 2 Principal from Current Month Installment Collections (S/A only); 3 Net Prepaid Principal and Interest Installments (S/A, S/S MRS, S/S MBS); 4 Curtailment/Liquidation Principal (S/A, S/S); 5 Net Interest Fundings on Curtailments (S/S); 6 Interest Gains/Losses (S/A — payoff-related, not yet removed/funded by cutoff); 7 Net Fixed Installment on Payoffs (S/S); 8 Net Delinquent Principal & Interest (S/S — "enter as negative"); 9 Fannie Mae P&I Receivable total (S/S — from the Remittance P&I Detail Report); 10 Adjustments to Fannie Mae P&I Receivable (S/S); 11 Other (S/A, S/S — loan number, root cause, amount, aging); 12 Total (all). Section III: itemised Deposits in Transit, Disbursements in Transit and Depository Adjustment(s), each with "Fannie Mae loan number, root cause, aging, and amount."

**Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026) §1-02 "Performing Monthly Reconciliations."** Monthly loan-level reconciliation of "balances, LPI dates, interest rates, and pass-through rates" to Fannie Mae's records using Schedule 1 (Form 473), Schedule 2 (Form 473A), Schedule 3 (Form 472) and, for S/S MBS/MRS, Form 512; "Unreconciled shortages are payable to Fannie Mae. The servicer must remit the amount of the unreconciled shortage immediately"; for surpluses unexplained beyond 90 days "Fannie Mae may adjust the servicer's shortage/surplus account to zero out the surplus." The loan-level schedules are owned by 5.1; the shortage/surplus schedule is the bridge between Form 496 line 10 and Fannie Mae's cash position. LL-2026-05 will "eliminate the monthly shortage/surplus balance reconciliation using Schedule 3 – Reconciliation of Shortage Surplus (Form 472)" for A/A once auto-drafting is live and "settle-up any outstanding shortage/surplus balances"; "There are no remitting or draft date changes for any other remittance types." FAQ Q17: "The pre-draft notification will be available no later than the next business day after the event is successfully processed by Fannie Mae. We will draft the P&I remittance amount from the servicer's custodial account two business days after successfully processing payment events."

**Fannie Mae-side data sources.** LSDU Cash Position Search by servicer number × remittance type (current + 11 prior periods; "data as of the prior day"; Adjustment Amount and Draft Amount drill-downs) — LSDU User Guide (Feb. 2026). Fannie Mae Connect "Remittance Principal and Interest Detail Report" (Form 496 line 9). Loan-Level Draft Notifications API (consolidates CRS Draft Notifications, MBS P&I Draft Notifications, MBS Guaranty Fee Draft Notifications, Committing/Delivery Fee Draft Notifications, DLRS Disbursement Notifications; inputs "filing date and draft type"; returns "loan, commitment, and seller-level details associated with amounts to be drafted, reimbursed, or adjusted" — response schema behind login **[UNVERIFIED]**). CRS: bank instructions by 8 p.m. ET one day before the effective date; drafts entered before 4 p.m. ET settle next BD; "Draft Request Report" lists requests for the past 100 days; batch import `.txt` ≤ 100 KB / 1,000 transactions (Lender ID 1–9, Remittance Code 10–13, Draft Amount 14–28, Loan Number 29–38, Settlement Date 39–48). CRS User Guide (March 2021) contains **no** text on returned/NSF drafts **[UNVERIFIED handling]**.

**Control expectations (Fannie Mae Servicer Self-Assessment, 2022–2026 form).** "Comparison of bank statement to cashbook"; "Identification of reconciling items, including Fannie Mae loan number, root cause, amount, and aging of adjustment"; "Timely follow-up and resolution of reconciling items"; "Supervisory review and approval of reconciliations"; "Segregation of duties between payment posting and account reconciliation" and between "remitting funds and account reconciliation"; "Reconciliations are completed monthly using Forms 496/496A (or an equivalent)." A4-1-01 (02/12/2025): QC must "ensure the mortgage loans are serviced in accordance with sound mortgage banking and accounting principles," report results to senior management, and be available to Fannie Mae on request. Benchmark only: Freddie Mac's custodial guide (Nov. 2023) requires reconciliation "within 45 days" and that servicers "fund any shortages and clear all variances within 90 days of the accounting cycle, even if you have not identified the root cause."

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (a) Governing source is F-1-03 + Form 496 instructions (+ IRM §1-02 for shortage/surplus), not F-1-20 (which governs remitting). (b) "Systems" must add Fannie Mae Connect (line 9 source), the Draft Notifications API/CRS reports, and the bank statement feed; LSDU cash position is a cross-check, not the line-9 source. (c) The 45-day rule is a Fannie Mae *form instruction*, so it is contractual through F-1-03's "applicable … Form" reference — treat as hard. (d) Automation class "b" understates: the Guide requires no human signature on Form 496; the only human touchpoints are policy (supervisory approval; shortage funding above threshold) and the portal pull of the Remittance Detail report if the Connect API is not yet enabled.

### Operational prerequisites
- Active P&I accounts with executed Forms 1013 (6.1) and statement feeds (BAI2 or camt.053 prior-day, intraday feed for draft coverage) — Supermortgage treasury.
- Fannie Mae Connect access to the "Remittance Detail – P&I" report category and (preferred) Insights & Reporting API credentials (Loan-Level Draft Notifications) under the partner's servicer numbers via Form 101/Related-Party System ID — partner Corporate Administrator + Supermortgage TSP onboarding (2–6 months).
- LSDU roles (External View Only at minimum) for cash position downloads; CRS user IDs for the Draft Request Report — partner CA.
- Official Form 496 Excel template downloaded from Fannie Mae's forms library, hashed and registered in `form_templates` with a validated cell map **[template URL/cell map UNVERIFIED — obtain from the Selling & Servicing Guide Forms page]** — Supermortgage; 1 week.
- Written custodial reconciliation policy (approval thresholds, segregation of duties between the `cashiering` agent/credentials and the `custodial-recon` agent/credentials, human approval flag) approved by the officer and shared with the partner — before first month-end.
- Corporate funding account (operating account) with pre-authorized transfer rail to each custodial account for shortage funding; dual control above threshold — treasury.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Daily: `custodial.statement.received` (prior-day BAI2/camt.053 per account, expected by 08:00 local), `custodial.intraday.received` (camt.052/BAI2 intraday snapshots, hourly 09:00–17:00 ET), `fnma.cash_position.imported` (LSDU download/API, prior day), `fnma.draft_notification.received` (Draft Notifications API, daily 06:00 ET), `crs.draft_request.report_imported`, ledger day-close `ledger.day.closed` (cashiering cut-off).
- Monthly: `ledger.period.closed` (month-end cut-off), `fnma.reporting_period.closed` (BD2 17:00 ET), `fnma.remittance_detail.report_received` (Connect), `custodial.statement.month_end_received` → `custodial.reconciliation.monthly_started`.
- Remittance calendar: `remittance.draft.expected` (from 5.2: S/S by the 18th, S/A by the 20th, A/A per CRS/LL-2026-05), `remittance.draft.settled` (bank debit matched).
- Exceptions: `payment.returned` (2.1), `remittance.adjustment.received` (LSDU adjustment), `custodial.shortage.identified`.

#### State machine
Daily reconciliation (`kind = daily_three_way`): `awaiting_feeds` → (statement + ledger close + Fannie Mae feeds, or 10:00 local cutoff) `matching` → `exceptions_open` | `balanced` → (all items ≤ tolerance cleared or carried) `closed_day`. Missing statement by 10:00 → `statement_missing` item, day still closes at 17:00 with the item carried.

Monthly Form 496: `not_started` → (`ledger.period.closed` ∧ month-end statement) `composing` → (Remittance Detail report received for S/S; LSDU period closed) `drafted` (xlsx generated, difference computed) → `under_review` (`qc-audit` agent run) → (`difference = 0` ∧ every open item itemised with loan/root cause/aging ∧ review passed) `approved` (human `officer` approval when flag `custodial.form496.human_approval = on`) → `completed` (document hashed, timer satisfied, package assembled) → terminal. `drafted` → `rework` on review failure. `completed` → `amended` (new version) if a later correction changes the period; the original is never edited.

Reconciliation item: `open` → `proposed` (agent proposes entries) → `posted` → `cleared`; `open` → `funded` (corporate funding) → `cleared` (recovery) ; `open` → `escalated` → …; `written_off` only via `officer` with reason (amounts ≤ $25.00 rounding-class only).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Bank balance of record** = closing *ledger* balance (BAI2 015 / camt.053 `CLBD`) on the as-of date; available balance (045 / `CLAV`) is used only for draft coverage. Control totals: Σ 16-record credits must equal the 03-record 100 summary and Σ debits the 400 summary (or camt `TtlNtries`); mismatch → `control_total_mismatch`, statement not used until the bank re-sends.
2. **Matching order** (deterministic, before any AI step): (i) exact reference match (lockbox batch id, ACH batch trace number, CRS draft settlement date + remittance code + amount, wire IMAD/OMAD); (ii) amount + value date within ±1 BD, one-to-one; (iii) amount + date many-to-one against a ledger batch (e.g., one bank credit = one lockbox deposit ticket = N ledger entries); (iv) residuals → exception with category inferred from type code (BAI2 165/142/168 ACH credits, 115/116 lockbox, 195 incoming wire, 555 deposited item returned, 451/455/469 ACH debits, 475 check paid, 495 outgoing wire, 560–568 fees; camt `BkTxCd` equivalents mapped per bank) **[bank-specific type-code usage UNVERIFIED — mapping table per depository]**.
3. **Auto-clear rules**: `deposit_in_transit` and `disbursement_in_transit` auto-clear when the bank line arrives ≤ 2 BD (deposit) / ≤ 3 BD (disbursement) after the ledger date; `timing_difference` across month-end is carried into Section III with aging; `interest_credit` posts to `interest_due_corporate` (P&I) — F-1-03 lets the servicer remove it; `bank_fee` debits are reimbursed from corporate ≤ 1 BD (custodial funds may not bear servicer expenses) and the fee is then re-billed to corporate ops; `returned_item` posts the payment reversal (2.1 command `payment.reverse`) the same day and re-runs draft coverage.
4. **Draft variance**: expected draft `E` (from `fnma_remittance_payable:<type>` at the draft date, corroborated by the Draft Notifications API / CRS Draft Request Report) vs bank debit `B`. If `B = E` → matched. If `B ≠ E`: pull LSDU cash position adjustments for the period; each adjustment with a loan number becomes a `draft_variance` item with that loan; the net goes to `fnma_shortage_surplus:<type>` (Form 472 basis) and, for a Fannie Mae over-draft, the agent files a documented refund claim per C-3-01 ("submit a documented claim for a refund"); for an under-draft the shortage is remitted via CRS within 1 BD (`FNMA_IRM102_SHORTAGE_REMIT_1BD`).
5. **Shortage funding** (cash in bank < cashbook after in-transit items are validated): the agent must fund from corporate within 2 BD regardless of root cause (fiduciary rule; policy adopting the Freddie Mac benchmark at day 90 as the outside bound). Approval tiers: ≤ $1,000.00 → agent auto-funds and logs; $1,000.01–$25,000.00 → `officer` approval ≤ 1 BD; > $25,000.00 or any suspicion of unauthorized debit → `officer` + partner notice + `fraud` case (18.5) + bank claim. Recovery entries reverse the funding when the root cause is found; write-offs need `officer` sign-off (≤ $25.00 rounding class only).
6. **Composition (Section II) mapping** from `remittance_components` at period close, in cents, per remittance type:
   - A/A: L1 = Σ full installments (P&I gross − servicing fee) received in the month and not yet drafted at month-end (ledger `fnma_remittance_payable:A/A`); L11 = other; L12 = L1 + L11.
   - S/A: L2 = Σ principal portions of current-month installments collected (remitted on the 20th of the next month); L3 = prepaid installments net of fees; L4 = curtailments/liquidation principal collected, unremitted; L6 = payoff interest gains (−) / losses (+) not yet removed/funded; L11; L12 = Σ.
   - S/S (separate forms for MBS and MRS): L3 prepaid net; L4 curtailments; L5 interest funded on curtailments net of fees; L7 fixed installments net of fees on loans paid off in the month; L8 = −Σ delinquent installments net of fees (delinquency advances outstanding, i.e., the servicer has remitted or will remit scheduled P&I not collected — negative because the cash is not in the account; loans in Stop Delinquency Advance after four consecutive months (C-3-01/F-1-20) are excluded because Fannie Mae stops drafting them, cross-ref 5.4); L9 = Fannie Mae P&I Receivable from the Remittance P&I Detail Report; L10 = documented variances between L9 and the servicer's scheduled P&I net of fees (`fnma_remittance_payable:S/S` computed); L11; L12 = Σ.
   The identity checked before drafting the form: `L12 = cashbook balance = ledger custodial_pi_cash:<account>` at period close; and Section I `L5 (adjusted depository) − L6 (cashbook) = 0`.

Deadline: 2026-09-30 + 45 days = Sat 2026-11-14 → internal `due_at` Fri 2026-11-13 17:00; warning day 30 = 2026-10-30.
8. **Worked example — draft variance.** A/A account, expected draft (ledger) 4,821,033 cents; bank debit 4,841,033 cents on 10/07; LSDU adjustments for the period show +2,000,000 cents tagged to loan 1234567890 ("LAR correction, interest"). Item `draft_variance` −$200.00, loan 1234567890, root cause "Fannie Mae adjustment for corrected LAR (5.1 correction 10/02)", aging 0 → posted to `fnma_shortage_surplus:A/A`; if the correction was Fannie Mae's error the agent prepares the refund claim.
9. **Rounding**: all composition amounts are sums of cent-exact ledger postings — no rounding occurs in this process; servicing-fee rounding lives in 2.1/5.2.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- **Form 496 workbook** per account × remittance type (`documents`, xlsx from the official template + PDF render), status `completed`, SHA-256, retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y`, indexed by servicer number, account, period.
- **Section II/III support**: loan-level listings per line (CSV + PDF), reconciling-items register with loan number, root cause, amount, aging.
- **Ledger postings**: reclassifications, interest to corporate, fee reimbursements, returned items, shortage fundings and recoveries, `fnma_shortage_surplus` entries — each a balanced `ledger_entries` set linked to a `loan_event`/`custodial_account_event`.
- **Investor events**: none emitted by this process itself; it *consumes* 5.1/5.2 outputs. CRS shortage remittances are `remittances` rows (5.2) triggered from here.
- **Notices**: none to borrowers. Internal notices `CUST-SHORTAGE-FUNDED-v1`, `CUST-DRAFT-COVERAGE-ALERT-v1`, partner monthly package cover memo `CUST-496-PKG-v1` (citation F-1-03 / Form 496 instructions).
- **MORA evidence package** (monthly zip, WORM): manifest (SHA-256 per file), Form 496 xlsx/pdf, bank statement (raw + rendered), cashbook export, composition support, Remittance P&I Detail report, LSDU cash position export, items register, funding approvals, `agent_decisions` (preparer and reviewer runs), officer approval record, timer history (`FNMA_F496_PI_RECON_45` start/due/satisfied), policy version, template hash.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Preparer**: `custodial-recon` agent (tools: `bank.read_statement`, `ledger.read`, `ledger.post_reclass` (restricted to custodial ↔ corporate reimbursement, suspense, shortage/surplus and in-transit accounts), `fnma.read_cash_position`, `fnma.read_draft_notifications`, `crs.prepare_batch`, `form496.generate`, `documents.write`, `escalation.create`, `timer.*`, `partner.notify`). Runs the daily engine, classifies residuals (structured output: `{line_id, category, loan_id?, root_cause, amount_cents, confidence, evidence_refs, proposed_entries[]}`), posts only when `confidence ≥ 0.95` **and** both sides carry documentary evidence, otherwise leaves the item open with a research task; never posts a balancing "plug."
- **Reviewer**: `qc-audit` agent, separate credentials and prompt, re-derives Section II from the ledger independently, re-parses the statement, checks every Section III item has loan/root cause/aging/evidence, checks segregation (preparer run ≠ any run that posted payments that day — credentials are distinct packages), and signs the review record. This implements the Self-Assessment's "supervisory review" with a second, independent AI run; feature flag `custodial.form496.human_approval` (default **on** for the first six months and any month with an open item > 60 days or a funding > $25,000) adds an `officer` approval step. Human approval here is a *policy* touchpoint (Fannie Mae contractual expectation), not a legal signature; when the flag is off the `officer` still receives the package.
- **Escalations**: `fnma_portal_operator` for Connect/LSDU/CRS pulls and uploads (package: report name, period, servicer numbers, expected row counts, upload file + fixture check results); `officer` for funding tiers, aged items, breaches, write-offs, and package approval; partner treasury (not a platform role — external notice) for > $25,000 shortages; `fraud` case for unidentified debits.
- **Human path when AI is off**: ops-console "Reconciliation Workbench" exposes the same matching engine, exception queue and Form 496 generator; the command handlers enforce identical guards (difference = 0, itemised items, evidence) so a human preparer cannot skip controls.
- LL-2026-04: this agent is in the AI inventory with a pre-deployment evaluation suite (synthetic statements with seeded exceptions; accuracy ≥ 99.5% classification, 0 false auto-posts) and monthly monitoring of auto-post reversals.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Month-end on a weekend** (e.g., Sat 2026-10-31): statement as-of 10/31 arrives Mon 11/2; lockbox receipts of 10/31 posted 11/2 appear as deposits in transit dated 10/31 — allowed within the 2-BD rule; cutoff policy documented ("reasonable daily work cut-offs," F-1-03).
- **Fannie Mae drafts on the 18th when the 18th is a Saturday** → draft on Fri the 17th; coverage timer anchors on the preceding fannie_et business day.
- **A/A auto-draft go-live (LL-2026-05)**: switch L1 logic from "collected not remitted" to "events processed, draft pending ≤ 2 BD"; Form 472 shortage/surplus lines retire for A/A; Fannie Mae "settle-up" of outstanding balances must be matched as a one-time draft/credit with its own item category.
- **Multiple MBS pools in one account** → pool-level sub-ledger (`pool_id` on `remittance_components`) so L9/L10 can be defended per pool (A4-1-02 detailed accounting).
- **Stop Delinquency Advance** loans: exclude from L8 from the month Fannie Mae stops drafting; re-include when drafting resumes (5.4 events).
- **Transfer-in/out mid-month**: partial-month composition split by transfer date; transferor/transferee cutoff statements; boarding cash reconciliation (1.6) feeds opening items.
- **Bankruptcy/SCRA/disaster**: no effect on custody; forbearance changes the delinquency-advance line (L8) via 5.4.
- **Bank feed outage > 2 days** → fall back to bank-portal PDF statements uploaded by treasury (manual `bank_statements` ingestion with OCR + control totals); reconciliation of record still uses the electronic file when it arrives.
- **Fannie Mae API/Connect outage** → line 9 waits (timer keeps running); at day 30 warning the agent uses the LSDU cash position as provisional L9 with an L10 explanation and marks the form "provisional," replacing it when the report arrives (versioned).
- **Duplicate statement files / restated statements** → BAI2 group status 3 (correction) or camt.053 with a later `CreDtTm` supersedes; earlier file retained.
- **Returned Fannie Mae draft (NSF at the custodial bank)** → treated as a critical breach: immediate CRS re-remittance same day, `officer` + partner notice, Fannie Mae custodial team contact; root-cause item and post-mortem (handling with Fannie Mae **[UNVERIFIED]**).
- **Retro-corrections** (payment reversals after Form 496 completion) → new version of the affected period's items in the *next* month's Section III with aging from the original date; the completed form is never altered.

#### Audit and evidence
Every daily and monthly reconciliation run: inputs (statement hashes, ledger snapshot id, Fannie Mae report hashes), matching results, item lifecycle with timestamps and actor, decision records (model/prompt/rule-set versions, confidence), reviewer record, approvals, funding approvals with tier, timer history, and the final package manifest. Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y`; WORM copy; exportable by period for MORA (18.2), STAR (18.3), state exams and the partner's Reg AB/USAP attestation (18.6, which tests custodial reconciliations as a servicing criterion).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Human approval of the monthly Form 496 package — default: on via flag for six months, then risk-based (see AI design).
2. Shortage-funding tiers — default $1,000 / $25,000 as above; partner may set lower.
3. Whether to seek Fannie Mae Connect/Insights API access for the Remittance Detail report at TSP onboarding or rely on the portal pull — default: request API in the TSP intake; portal pull as bridge.
4. Retention of raw BAI2/camt files — default: 7 years corporate (beyond life-of-loan+4 for loan-linked items).
5. Whether the partner wants a co-review of every month or exceptions only — default: package delivery monthly; co-review on breach/funding.
