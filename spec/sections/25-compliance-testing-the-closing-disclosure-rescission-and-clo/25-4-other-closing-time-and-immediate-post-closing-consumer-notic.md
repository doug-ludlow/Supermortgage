### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Initial escrow account statement — Reg X §1024.17(g) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026; 00a-fed §6.8; servicing 3.1).** (g)(1): "The servicer shall submit an initial escrow account statement to the borrower at settlement or within 45 calendar days of settlement" for escrow accounts "established as a condition of the loan"; the statement may be incorporated into the settlement statement or delivered separately; (g)(2): escrow established after settlement → within 45 calendar days of establishment. Content per (g)(1)(i) (servicing 3.1, verified 2026-09-09): the monthly mortgage payment and the escrow portion; an itemization of "the estimated taxes, insurance premiums, and other charges that the servicer reasonably anticipates to be paid from the escrow account during the escrow account computation year and the anticipated disbursement dates of those charges"; "the amount that the servicer selects as a cushion"; a trial running balance. §1024.17(c)(1)(ii): cushion "no greater than one-sixth (1/6) of the estimated total annual payments"; (c)(4): "All servicers must use the aggregate accounting method". **Settlement — §1024.2(b) (eCFR 9/09/2026):** "Settlement means the process of executing legally binding documents regarding a lien on property that is subject to a federally related mortgage loan. This process may also be called 'closing' or 'escrow' in different jurisdictions." The anchor is therefore the consummation date (Fri Nov 6, 2026 on the fixture), not the disbursement date. Servicing 3.1 owns the obligation, the timer `REGX_1024_17G_INITIAL_STMT_45` (anchor `settlement_date` or `escrow_accounts.established_at`, 45 `calendar_days`) and the template `NTC_REGX_1024_17G_INITIAL_ESCROW_STMT`; 3.1's boarding rule: "an origination-delivered statement satisfies (g)(1) only if the boarding file contains the document and a delivery date ≤ settlement_date + 45 days." 30.3 computes the initial analysis; 25.4 decides whether the statement rides in the closing package and evidences its delivery. The CD's escrow section (§1026.38(l)(7), 25.2) discloses the first-year escrowed costs, the initial escrow payment and the monthly escrow payment; 3.1's decision (3.1-Q2): "Treat the TRID Closing Disclosure as a substitute for the initial statement? Default: no."

**Escrow waivers — Fannie Mae Selling Guide B2-1.5-04, Escrow Accounts (last updated 04/01/2020; fetched 2026-09-10) — VERIFIED.** "First mortgages generally must provide for the deposit of escrow funds to pay as they come due, including taxes, ground rents, premiums for property insurance, and premiums for flood insurance." "Unless required by law, lenders may waive escrow account requirements for an individual first mortgage, provided the standard escrow provision remains in the mortgage loan legal documents." "Lenders must have a written policy governing the circumstances under which escrow accounts may be waived." The written policies "must provide that the waiver not be based solely on the LTV ratio of a loan, but also on whether the borrower has the financial ability to handle the lump sum payments of taxes, insurance, and other items described above." "Lenders cannot waive an escrow account for certain refinance transactions (see above) or for the payment of premiums for borrower-purchased mortgage insurance (if applicable)"; the "(see above)" passage: "For certain refinance transactions where the borrower is financing real estate taxes in the loan amount, an escrow account is required, subject to applicable laws or regulations" (cross-reference B2-1.3-03). Condo/co-op/PUD units under a blanket policy: "Fannie Mae does not require an escrow deposit for property or flood insurance premiums for an individual unit … when the project … is covered by a blanket insurance policy." No escrow-waiver fee, LLPA or borrower disclosure appears in the topic. **LLPA Matrix (version 09.09.2026; fetched 2026-09-10) — VERIFIED as absent:** the words "escrow" and "impound" "do not appear anywhere in this document"; the only "waiver" text is the LLPA-waiver section for first-time homebuyers ≤ 100% AMI/Duty to Serve and the High LTV Refinance cap. Federal overlay: an HPML first lien must have an escrow account "established before consummation" with the five-year cancellation floor (§1026.35(b)(1), (b)(3); 23.4 `REGZ_1026_35_HPML_ESCROW_5Y`); a General QM is exempt from HPML appraisal rules but not from the escrow rule (00a-fed §5.7). Flood-premium escrow under 42 U.S.C. 4012a(d) binds "regulated lending institutions", which a non-bank partner is not; the Fannie Mae flood-escrow expectation flows from B2-1.5-04's "premiums for flood insurance" sentence and Servicing Guide B-1-01 (not fetched — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**).

**Notice of ownership transfer — Reg Z §1026.39 (eCFR current as of 9/09/2026; CFPB eRegs and Official Interpretations read 2026-09-10) — VERIFIED.** (a)(1): a "covered person" is "any person … that becomes the owner of an existing mortgage loan by acquiring legal title to the debt obligation, whether through a purchase, assignment or other transfer, and who acquires more than one mortgage loan in any twelve-month period"; "a servicer of a mortgage loan shall not be treated as the owner of the obligation if the servicer holds title to the loan … solely for the administrative convenience" of servicing. Comment 39(a)(1)-2: "a person must become the owner of an existing mortgage loan by acquiring legal title to the debt obligation"; comment 39(a)(1)-3.i: the section does not apply to "a party that acquires only a beneficial interest or a security interest in the loan"; comment 39(a)(1)-1: providing the disclosure "does not by itself make that person a 'creditor'". (b)(1): the covered person "shall mail or deliver the disclosures required by this section to the consumer on or before the 30th calendar day following the date of transfer"; disclosures "shall be provided clearly and conspicuously in writing, in a form that the consumer may keep" and "may be provided to the consumer in electronic form, subject to compliance with … E-Sign Act". (b)(2): "the date of transfer to the covered person may, at the covered person's option, be either the date of acquisition recognized in the books and records of the acquiring party, or the date of transfer recognized in the books and records of the transferring party." Comment 39(b)(1)-1 example: "if a covered person acquires a mortgage loan on March 15, the disclosure must be mailed or delivered on or before April 14." (b)(3): "a covered person may mail or deliver the disclosures to any consumer who is primarily liable." (b)(4): where a loan is acquired and "subsequently sold, assigned, or otherwise transferred to another covered person, a single disclosure may be provided on behalf of both covered persons"; comment 39(b)(4)-1: "each has a duty to ensure that disclosures related to its acquisition are accurate and provided in a timely manner." (b)(5): joint acquisitions — "a single disclosure must be provided on behalf of all covered persons." Exceptions (c): (1) the covered person "sells, or otherwise transfers or assigns legal title to the mortgage loan on or before the 30th calendar day following the date that the covered person acquired" it (comment 39(c)(1)-1 example: acquired March 15, transferred April 1 → no disclosure); (2) a transfer "in connection with a repurchase agreement that obligates the transferor to repurchase the loan"; (3) the covered person "acquires only a partial interest in the loan and the party authorized to receive the consumer's notice of the right to rescind and resolve issues concerning the consumer's payments on the loan does not change." Content (d): (1) "The name, address, and telephone number of the covered person" — "The party identified must be the covered person who owns the mortgage loan, regardless of whether another party services the loan" (comment 39(d)(1)-1; an e-mail or web address is optional); (2) "The date of transfer"; (3) "The name, address and telephone number of an agent or party authorized to receive notice" of the right to rescind "and resolve issues concerning the consumer's payments" (not required if the (d)(1) information can be used for that purpose; comment 39(d)(3)-2: an agent's e-mail/web address may be added); (4) "Where transfer of ownership of the debt to the covered person is or may be recorded in public records", or alternatively that "the transfer of ownership has not been recorded in public records at the time the disclosure is provided" (comment 39(d)(4)-1: the disclosure "can state where the transfer may later be recorded"); (5) partial-payment policy for closed-end dwelling-secured loans, in the H-25 format (comment 39(d)(5)-1): (i) the covered person "may accept partial payments and apply such payments", or (ii) "may hold partial payments in a separate account" until the consumer pays the remainder, or (iii) "does not accept any partial payments", plus (iv) "a statement that, if the loan is sold, the new covered person … may have a different policy". (e): optional additional information; (f): no disclosure to a confirmed successor in interest "unless and until" assumption or an executed acknowledgment (Reg X §1024.32(c)(1)(iv)). The text read today contains no provision addressing a servicer sending the notice as the covered person's agent; it requires only that the covered person be the party identified. **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — agent delivery is neither authorized nor prohibited by the text read; comments 39(a)(1)-1 through 39(d)(5)-1 and the 2010 Federal Reserve final rule (75 FR 58489, fetched with truncation) do not address it.]**

**GLBA — Reg P §1016.4 (eCFR 9/04/2026; 00a-fed §14) and §1016.13 (eCFR 9/09/2026) — VERIFIED.** §1016.4(a)(1): initial notice to "An individual who becomes your customer, not later than when you establish a customer relationship, except as provided in paragraph (e)"; (c)(1): a customer relationship is "a continuing relationship"; the loan rule: the relationship is established "when you originate or acquire the servicing rights to a loan to the consumer for personal, family, or household purposes"; example (c)(3)(i): the consumer "obtains credit from you"; (e)(1): later delivery only where establishing the relationship "is not at the customer's election" or notice "would substantially delay the customer's transaction". §1016.13(a)(1): the opt-out requirements do not apply to disclosures to a nonaffiliated third party that performs services or functions on the institution's behalf if the institution does "(i) Provide the initial notice in accordance with § 1016.4" and "(ii) Enter into a contractual agreement with the third party that prohibits the third party from disclosing or using the information other than to carry out the purposes for which you disclosed the information". 21.3 owns `NTC_GLBA_1016_4_PRIVACY_INITIAL` (delivered with the application package); 25.4 owns the consummation gate `GLBA_1016_4_INITIAL_PRIVACY_GATE`.

**First-payment letter and autopay — Reg E §1005.10 (eCFR 9/08/2026, quoted in servicing 2.x, verified 2026-09-09) and Reg Z §1026.41 (servicing 7.1).** §1005.10(e)(1): "No financial institution or other person may condition an extension of credit to a consumer on the consumer's repayment by preauthorized electronic fund transfers."; (b): preauthorized transfers "may be authorized only by a writing signed or similarly authenticated by the consumer" and the person obtaining the authorization "shall provide a copy to the consumer"; (d)(1): variable-amount notice "at least 10 days before the scheduled date". No federal rule requires a first-payment letter; the periodic statement (§1026.41; servicing 7.1; comment 41(b)-1 "four days after the close of the courtesy period" as the "reasonably prompt" benchmark) is a separate, mandatory artifact. **RESPA transfer notice at settlement — §1024.33(b)(3)(iii) (eCFR 9/08/2026; 00a-fed §6.3).** "Notices of transfer provided at settlement by the transferor servicer and transferee servicer, whether as separate notices or as a combined notice, satisfy the timing requirements." §1024.33(b)(2)(i) excludes transfers "between master servicers without changing the subservicer" and transfers with no change in "the payee, address to which payment must be delivered, account number, or amount of payment due". Design consequence (00a-fed §6.3): if the closing documents name the partner as payee and SM later becomes the payment address, that is a transfer of servicing requiring notice unless delivered at settlement.

**ARM initial adjustment — §1026.20(d) (eCFR 9/08/2026) — VERIFIED, servicing 7.2 owns.** "The disclosures shall be provided to consumers at least 210, but no more than 240, days before the first payment at the adjusted level is due" for "the initial interest rate adjustment pursuant to the loan contract"; (c) subsequent adjustments "at least 60, but no more than 120, days before"; (c)(1)(ii) exempts ARMs with terms of one year or less and a first adjustment whose first adjusted payment is due within 210 days after consummation where the rate disclosed at consummation was not an estimate. 30.4 seeds 7.2 with the first change date.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Servicer as agent" for §1026.39 is not Fannie Mae's stated practice: Fannie Mae's consumer page says the borrower receives "a letter from us" and that notifying the borrower is "a legal requirement for us". The default flips — Fannie Mae sends; the platform's obligations are accurate servicer identity/contact data at delivery, readiness to answer, and evidence capture. Sending "on Fannie Mae's behalf" becomes a contractual fallback **[UNVERIFIED — no Servicing Guide instruction located]**.
2. "≤ 30 days after acquisition date" understates the rule: "on or before the 30th calendar day following the date of transfer", where the date may at the covered person's option be the acquirer's or the transferor's books-and-records date; calendar days with no holiday or weekend adjustment (the second fixture's due date is Christmas Day).
3. The assignment's "Fannie Mae escrow-waiver LLPA cross-reference" points at nothing: the 09.09.2026 LLPA Matrix contains no escrow or impound adjustment. Any escrow-waiver price is partner rate-sheet pricing (20.4) and, if charged as a fee, the CD's "Escrow Waiver Fee" line (§1026.38(l)(7); 25.2).
4. The 45-day escrow clock is anchored on "settlement" = execution of the loan documents (§1024.2(b)), i.e., consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 — not the Nov 12 disbursement; and the obligor is "the servicer" (partner as servicer of record, SM as subservicer), which is why the timer and template are servicing 3.1's.
5. §1026.20(d) is a window (210–240 days), not a floor; it is a servicing 7.2 artifact seeded at boarding.
6. HPA: the §4905 LPMI disclosure is due at commitment, not at closing; only the §4903 initial disclosure is a closing-package item (24.6).
7. The blueprint omits: Reg E §1005.10(e)(1) (autopay may not be a loan condition), the RESPA "payment address named at settlement" design that avoids a transfer notice, Fannie Mae B2-1.5-04's written escrow-waiver policy and its two non-waivable cases, the state escrow-election notices (Utah at or before closing; California written statement), and the closing-year 1098 consequence of prepaid interest.

#### State machine

Closing package: `composing` → `gated` (all required items rendered from the final CD version; `GLBA_1016_4_INITIAL_PRIVACY_GATE`, `HPA_4903_INITIAL_DISCLOSURE_GATE` (24.6), `SM_O63_NOTICE_AT_SIGNING_GATE` (25.3) and the state gates open) → `delivered` (signing session or paper hand-off; per-item evidence) → `evidenced` (every required item has receipt evidence) | `exception` (an item refused/undeliverable → `officer`). Post-closing run: `scheduled` (at `loan.funded`) → `sent` → `evidenced`. Ownership notice: `not_applicable` (exception (c)(1)–(3) or no acquisition) | `expected` (at `loan.purchased`) → `evidenced` (copy or borrower confirmation) | `overdue_unconfirmed` (day 45 without evidence → inquiry to Fannie Mae's account team) ; `sm_warehouse_assignee` path: `expected` → `sent` by day 30 when the loan has not been sold. Transitions are performed by the `disclosure` agent (package/run), `boarding` agent (seeds), `escrow` agent (3.1) and `officer` (exceptions).

SM warehouse advance structured as a loan secured by the note → security interest only → not a covered person (comment 39(a)(1)-3.i). Fannie Mae purchase → covered person; `date_of_transfer = purchase_date` (acquirer's books; the purchase advice date); `due_date = date_of_transfer + 30 calendar_days`; exceptions never apply (Fannie Mae holds; no repurchase agreement; 100% interest). If 27.1's legal form is instead an assignment to SM at funding (table funding — 00a-fed §6.7), SM becomes a covered person on the funding date: sold to Fannie Mae on or before day 30 → exception (c)(1) (fixture: funded Nov 12, purchased Nov 19 → 7 days → no notice); aged beyond day 30 → SM must send `NTC_REGZ_1026_39_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER` by day 30 (a Nov 12 assignment → Sat Dec 12, 2026) and, on the later sale, Fannie Mae's letter follows — (b)(4) would permit a single combined disclosure only if the timing lines up.

**`NTC_REGZ_1026_39_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER` content (fallback / assignee use), per §1026.39(d):** (d)(1) the covered person's name, address and telephone number (Fannie Mae's when sent on its behalf — sourced from Fannie Mae's instruction, never invented; SM's when SM is the assignee); (d)(2) the date of transfer; (d)(3) the agent for rescission notices and payment issues: Supermortgage, servicer for [Partner]/[covered person], address, phone, e-mail and portal (comment 39(d)(3)-2); (d)(4) for MERS-registered loans: "The transfer of ownership of your loan has not been recorded in public records at the time this notice is provided" (the MERS System registration is not a public land record; the security instrument names MERS as nominee — 26.4); where the transfer may later be recorded, name the county recorder (comment 39(d)(4)-1); (d)(5) the partial-payment policy in the H-25 format: SM holds partial payments in a separate (suspense) account until the remainder is paid (servicing 2.1/7.1 rule) → alternative (ii), plus "If this loan is sold, your new lender may have a different policy" (comment 39(d)(5)-1 permits format-appropriate wording); (e) optional: "Fannie Mae is not your servicer; send all payments to Supermortgage" (mirrors Fannie Mae's own letter). Delivery per (b)(1) in writing; electronic only under a valid E-SIGN consent scoped to `servicing_notices`; (b)(3): the platform addresses all borrowers jointly.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`disclosure` agent (tools: `composeClosingPackage`, `evaluateEscrowWaiver`, `renderStateEscrowNotice`, `checkPrivacyNotice`, `runCdEscrowConsistency` (25.2 check codes), `deliverPackage` (via 26.2), `recordPackageEvidence`, `schedulePostClosingRun`, `evaluateOwnershipTransfer`, `renderOwnershipNotice`, `writeDecision`) and `boarding` agent (tools: `seedTaxReporting`, `seedEscrowElection`, `seedFirstPaymentLetter` (30.2), `writeDecision`). End-to-end: at `closing.scheduled` the disclosure agent composes the package from the final CD, runs the consistency checks, evaluates every gate, renders the state notices for the property state, delivers through the signing session, captures evidence, schedules the post-closing run for funding day + 2 business days, and at purchase records the Fannie Mae letter expectation; the boarding agent hands the seeds to servicing at `loan.boarded`. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{run_id, cd_disclosure_id, items[], gates{glba, hpa, flood, escrow_stmt, state[]}, consistency_results, escrow_election, ownership_transfer{covered_person, date_of_transfer, due_date, sender}, seeds_hash, rationale, model_version, prompt_version}`. Guardrails: never mark an item delivered without signing-session or manifest evidence; never render the escrow statement from figures other than 30.3's approved analysis; never approve an escrow waiver outside the partner's written policy or where B2-1.5-04/HPML forbid it; never send a §1026.39 notice in Fannie Mae's name without a written Fannie Mae instruction on file; never present autopay as a condition or pre-check enrollment; never invent Fannie Mae contact details. Escalations: `officer` (waiver-policy exception requests; any decision to send on Fannie Mae's behalf; `overdue_unconfirmed` ownership notices after the account-team inquiry); `settlement_agent`/`notary` (paper package hand-off and manifest); `human_agent` on request. Borrower-facing: the agent explains each notice only by reading its own text; automation disclosure at the start of every interaction; a borrower who calls about "a letter from Fannie Mae" is told it is expected, informational, and that payments continue to Supermortgage. Human path: ops console with the same package view, per-item evidence upload (for paper closings) and a read-only view of the §1026.39 expectation.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- 30.3's initial analysis not approved before consummation (late tax figures): the statement is dropped from the package; servicing 3.1's `REGX_1024_17G_INITIAL_STMT_45` governs (fixture: Dec 21, 2026); the CD escrow section still shows 30.3's best figures and any post-funding change is a 3.1 short-year/initial reset, not a CD correction, unless an amount actually paid at closing changed ((f)(2)(iii), 25.2).
- Escrow waived after the CD was issued (borrower changes election at the table): the CD escrow section and cash-to-close change → corrected CD at or before consummation (no new waiting period — no APR/product/prepayment change) unless a waiver fee that is a finance charge pushes the APR beyond 1/8 (25.1 re-run); Utah/California notices re-rendered.
- HPML determined at consummation figures (23.4 re-run at CD): escrow cannot be waived; `escrow_elections` forced to `escrow_full` with reason `hpml`; state notices adjusted.
- Property state with an unverified escrow-notice statute (IL, NY, MA, MN, WI, MD): the composer refuses to originate there until 31.1 verifies the row — a `jurisdiction_rules` row without `verified_at` is a hard block.
- Partner insists its own address be the payee at closing: servicing 1.3's combined MS-2 notice is delivered at settlement ((b)(3)(iii)) naming SM as the servicer to which payments go from the first due date; `loans.payment_address_named_at_closing = false` and the 60-day misdirected-payment protection (§1024.33(c)) is armed by 1.3.
- Fannie Mae letter arrives before the borrower's first payment and confuses the borrower into paying Fannie Mae: servicing 2.x misdirected-payment handling; the first-payment letter and portal already state that payments go to Supermortgage.
- Warehouse aging beyond 30 days under an assignment structure: SM must send the notice by day 30 (see the applicability rule); the timer arms automatically when `warehouse_advances.assignment = true`.
- Loan not purchased (repurchase, fallout, partner keeps the loan): no §1026.39 notice; if the partner later sells to another investor, that investor is the covered person.
- Successor in interest confirmed after purchase: no §1026.39 disclosure "unless and until" assumption or acknowledgment ((f)); servicing 4.4 records.
- E-SIGN consent withdrawn before the post-closing run: paper for every item; the first-payment letter still meets the 5-day timer by mail.
- Non-borrower spouse (refinance): receives the CD and rescission notices (25.2/25.3) but is not a "customer" for GLBA and not a Form 1098 payer of record; the first-payment letter is addressed to the borrowers.
- Points seeded on a purchase where the CD labels the charge "loan discount" instead of "points": still Box 6-eligible if computed as a percentage of principal and designated as points on the CD — the composer normalizes the CD label to "Discount Points (0.500%)" so the 1098 condition is met.
- Texas 50(a)(6): 26.1's 12-day notice and one-day itemization precede everything here; the constitutional rescission runs with 25.3's.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Who sends the §1026.39 notice.** Fannie Mae's consumer page says Fannie Mae sends its own letter; the Servicing Guide instruction was not located. Default: Fannie Mae sends; the platform records an expectation, keeps servicer contact data accurate, and never sends in Fannie Mae's name without a written Fannie Mae instruction; the partner's account team is asked to confirm the practice and the timing before the first delivery.
2. **Initial escrow statement at settlement vs within 45 days.** Default: deliver at settlement inside the closing package whenever 30.3's analysis is approved ≥ 1 creditor business day before consummation; otherwise servicing 3.1's 45-day path; never treat the CD escrow section as the statement (3.1-Q2).
3. **SM's own GLBA initial notice.** SM is a subservicer without the MSR and a §1016.13 service provider under the partner's notice. Default: no separate SM initial notice; the partner's notice names SM; the subservicing agreement carries the §1016.13(a)(1)(ii) clause; revisit if SM ever acquires servicing rights or markets to the borrowers in its own name (31.3).
4. **Payment address at closing.** Default: the note's place of payment, the CD contact page and the first-payment letter all name "Supermortgage, as servicer for [Partner]" so no RESPA transfer occurs; fallback: servicing 1.3's combined MS-2 at settlement under §1024.33(b)(3)(iii).
5. **Form 1098 "date of origination".** The instructions say "the date of the origination of the mortgage" without defining it. Default: the note/consummation date (Nov 6, 2026 on the fixture), consistent with the origination date reported to Fannie Mae/HMDA; disbursement date retained as a separate field; counsel to confirm.
6. **Escrow-waiver pricing.** Fannie Mae has no escrow-waiver LLPA. Default: no escrow-waiver fee; if the partner's rate sheet carries a waiver adjustment it is priced into the rate through 20.4, disclosed on the CD as required, and treated as a finance charge if charged as a fee (25.1).
7. **State escrow-notice matrix.** Default: originate only in states whose closing-time escrow-notice rows are verified (UT, CA today); IL/NY/MA/MN/WI/MD rows verified by 31.1 before enabling; interest-on-escrow computations belong to servicing 3.x.
8. **First-payment letter timing.** Default: with the post-closing run (funding + 2 servicer business days), hard deadline funding + 5, and never later than 20 calendar days before the first due date; the first periodic statement (7.1) is not replaced by it.
