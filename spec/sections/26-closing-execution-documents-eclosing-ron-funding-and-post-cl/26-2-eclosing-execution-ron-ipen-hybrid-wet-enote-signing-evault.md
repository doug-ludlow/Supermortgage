### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Electronic records and eClosing scope — Fannie Mae.** A2-4.1-03, *Electronic Records, Signatures, and Transactions* (last updated 05/06/2026; SEL-2026-05): "All documents used to originate, service or modify a loan, may be generated, signed, processed, stored or transmitted electronically" except promissory notes "unless the seller has received special approval to deliver electronic notes (eNotes)"; "Any form of electronic signature on an electronic record that is valid under applicable law" is permitted; "All electronic signatures must be attributable to an identified signer"; the seller retains evidence of the authenticated signer identity, attribution, the signer's consent to e-sign, the date, and the method/vendor, "sufficient to enable Fannie Mae to conduct a thorough quality control review of the loan"; electronic notarizations are accepted "either in person or remotely using real-time, two-way audio/video communication" when "performed in accordance with and is legally valid under the laws and regulations of the state"; "A lender may not require a borrower to use remote notarization and must have other notary options available"; "Under no circumstances may a borrower be required to use electronic records and electronic signatures"; the seller must fulfill "all requirements of Section 101(c) of ESIGN to create a binding electronic record" and "must retain evidence of its compliance"; the file must evidence "Any required disclosures made before obtaining the borrower's consent," the "Borrower's consent to receiving subsequent disclosure electronically," and "how the servicer 'reasonably demonstrated' the borrower's ability to receive the disclosures"; "Paper notes may not be converted into eNotes." E-SIGN 15 U.S.C. 7001(c)(1) consent elements (00a-fed §8): affirmative consent not withdrawn; prior clear and conspicuous statement of the paper option, the scope of consent, withdrawal procedures and paper-copy procedures/fees; hardware/software statement with consent "in a manner that reasonably demonstrates that the consumer can access information in the electronic form"; re-disclosure on material change. eClosing FAQs (updated May 14, 2026): "Fannie Mae will accept wet-signatures and electronic signatures on the same document, except on the Promissory Note"; hybrid is "a hybrid process in which certain key documents, such as the security instrument, are printed to paper and wet-signed, while other documents are signed electronically"; "In order to be truly paperless, the mortgage or security instrument must be electronically notarized prior to recording" (RON is not required); "Texas 50(a)(6) loans are also not eligible to be signed electronically and sold to Fannie Mae."

**RON — Fannie Mae acceptance (SEL-2026-05).** Announcement SEL-2026-05 (May 6, 2026): "removed the requirement for sellers and servicers to maintain, or cause to be retained, the video recording of the remote notarial ceremony"; the "RON platform used for loan closings complies with industry standards pursuant to the MISMO Remote Online Notarization Standards Version 2.0 (Draft), except for the Identification and Authentication section"; "lenders may verify identity using: technical methods (for example, credential analysis of government-issued identification and knowledge-based authentication), or non-technical methods (for example, a credible witness)"; the "lender must also retain the RON audit trail as part of the electronic loan file delivered to Fannie Mae and transfer it to the servicer"; "These changes are effective immediately and apply to loans closed using RON on or after May 6, 2026." A2-4.1-03 RON section: the property must be in a listed state, or a state that "adopts a law that expressly permits the use of remote online notarization," or a state that will "accept (either through state law or through the application of an express federal law) remote online notarizations performed out-of-state in accordance with the laws of the state in which the notarial act is performed"; the list as displayed on 2026-09-10 — Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming — 48 states + DC; **Georgia and Mississippi do not appear** (the extraction tool's own count differed; treat the per-state presence as **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — re-read before enabling a state]**); California footnote: "For a subject property located in California, a remote online notarization may be taken by a notary outside of the State of California in accordance with the laws of the state in which the notarial act was performed." Identity: non-technical — "Notary's personal knowledge of the borrower or a credible witness as identified through technical means"; technical — "Capture of one or more images of the borrower's government-issued identification credential and a validation of the authenticity" (credential analysis) and a "Process to verify that the borrower matches the validated identity (for example, knowledge-based authentication)". Technology: "Compliance with the MISMO RON Standards Version 2.0" (draft); once final, the "System used to perform RON must comply with the final standards in their entirety." "The lender must store the tamper-sealed audit trail produced by the RON system until such time that it can provide the audit trail to the servicer for inclusion in the individual mortgage loan file." "The notary public must be licensed and physically located in the state in which the notarial act is performed and, where required by law or regulation, is specifically licensed to perform electronic notarizations." "County recorder in the state and county where the property is located must accept the remotely notarized document for recording"; "If the remotely notarized document is a security instrument or amendment to the security instrument, the RON must comply with the title requirements" and the "Title insurance company may not take any exception with respect to any element of the loan being remotely notarized"; "If the remotely notarized document is a security instrument or an amendment to a security instrument, the loan must be delivered with the Remote Notarization Indicator flag"; "The mortgage loan is not a Texas Section 50(a)(6) loan"; all selling representations and warranties "continue" (clear title, first-lien enforceability, compliance with laws, title insurance). A2-4.1-04, *Notarization Standards* (06/02/2021) covers remote ink-signed notarization (RIN: "audio-visual aided ink-signed method of notarization of paper documents") with "review of two forms of government-issued photo ID" and recording storage "for the minimum period required by applicable law, or if no period is specified...for 10 years"; TX 50(a)(6) excluded. Federal: the SECURE Notarization Act of 2025 (H.R. 1777/S. 1561) is not law (00a-fed §8 — PARTIALLY VERIFIED via LegiScan); MISMO RON Standards V2 is a draft ("re-release in 2026"), V1 remains the published standard (00a-fed §8). State survey (secondary, NotaryLive, updated 09/10/2026): "49 states and Washington, DC have passed remote online notarization (RON) laws"; California's SB 696 "is rolling out in phases through 2030" — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**RON — state statutes read (load-bearing states).** *Arizona* A.R.S. §41-263 (fixture state): identity by "Personal knowledge under section 41-255, subsection A," by "oath or affirmation from a credible witness," or "by using at least two different types of identity proofing"; "The notary public, or a person acting on behalf of the notary public, creates an audiovisual recording of the performance of the notarial act"; "The recording must be retained for a period of at least five years after the recording is made"; the certificate "must indicate that the notarial act was performed using communication technology"; "A notary public located in this state may perform a notarial act using communication technology for a remotely located individual." *Ohio* R.C. 147.64 (eff. **eNote signing, sealing, eVault, eRegistry.** B8-8-02 (12/13/2023): "eNotes registered in MERS eRegistry as soon as possible after tamper-evident seal applied, but no later than one business day" after signing; "record must reflect originating lender as first Controller"; "all subsequent transfers—changes of Control—must be reflected in MERS eRegistry"; "borrower signs only eNote at closing and does not also sign paper version of Uniform note"; signing records ("name of person (and related entity) who signed each document," "date of signature," "method by which document was signed," "identifying information to verify electronic signature and attribution") "must be retained for life of loan plus seven years"; "Lenders must provide Fannie Mae any audit trail files requested at any time in format compatible"; on servicing transfer the "lender required to provide new servicer with all attribution evidence and audit trails"; errors → new note and "Registration Reversal," clerical errors → correction agreement reported to the eRegistry and eDelivered to Fannie Mae's eVault. B8-8-01 (11/05/2025): the eNote signature must be "legal and enforceable under applicable law and the electronic signature was not effected by means of audio or video recording"; the process must be "clearly identifying the electronic record being signed, clearly displaying the borrower's name and requiring the borrower to take action to initiate the electronic signature"; "A single electronic signature cannot be applied to multiple electronic records simultaneously"; the lender must use an "eNote technology provider that has completed integration testing with Fannie Mae" and systems "fully operational, including, systems integration among the lender, Fannie Mae and MERS"; the lender must be "named the Controller (the person having control of the Transferable Record) on the MERS eRegistry"; "There has been, at all times, one and only one Authoritative Copy of the eNote in existence" and it "has not been altered"; the eNote must be "a valid and enforceable Transferable Record Pursuant to the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act ('UETA'), or the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act ('ESIGN')"; "A lender cannot use any other software technology or any other technology provider without the express written prior approval of Fannie Mae" beyond the eClosing Technology Service Provider List. **Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) The eNote clock is "no later than one business day after the final tamper sealing" (MERS) — the blueprint's "same/next business day" resolves to next-business-day as the hard limit with same-day as SM policy. (2) The blueprint cites "MERS Procedures (MOM registration ≤ 7 calendar days after note date)" — the current rule is Rel. 26.1's "Note Date, or Funding Date for refinance loans or loans in escrow states," which for the fixture (AZ refinance) anchors on the funding date. (3) The "federal SECURE Notarization Act" has no operative status — the design relies solely on state law plus Fannie Mae's acceptance rule. (4) "State recording deadlines" do not exist as statutory mortgage-recording deadlines in the sources read; the timers below are contractual/policy. (5) The blueprint omits: the Remote Notarization Indicator and eNote Indicator at delivery; the RON audit-trail retention duty (video retention retired May 6, 2026); the Secured Party role (eNote analogue of Interim Funder — no Interim Funder on the eRegistry); the county-recorder and title-underwriter acceptance conditions for RON; TX 50(a)(6)'s electronic-signing ban and NY CEMA's eNote ban (26.1). (6) "eVault (SM's or partner's)" — Fannie Mae does not require the lender to own an eVault, but the Location Participant must hold and validate the Authoritative Copy, and SM as warehouse provider must use an eVault "tested with Fannie Mae" under the eNote Transfer of Control and Location and Custodial Agreement. (7) Georgia and Mississippi are not on Fannie Mae's RON list even though secondary surveys count them — the platform must follow Fannie Mae's list plus the "adopts a law that expressly permits" clause with counsel confirmation.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `closing.documents.released` (26.1) with `closing_document_sets.closing_type` and the signer manifest; `closing.scheduled` with appointment time, settlement agent, notary/RON provider, location (TX: permitted office).
- Gates from other processes that must be open before a session may start: `REGZ_1026_19F1_CD_3SBD_GATE` (25.2), `SM_O61_COMPLIANCE_PASS_CONSUMMATE_GATE` (25.1), `SM_O63_NOTICE_AT_SIGNING_GATE` (25.3), `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD_GATE` (24.2), `SM_O71_INSTRUCTIONS_ACK_GATE`, `TX_50A6_12DAY_CLOSING_GATE`/`TX_50A6_ITEMIZATION_1BD_GATE` (26.1), `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD` (22.3, satisfied within 10 business days before the note date), `SM_MI_COMMITMENT_EXPIRY_GATE` (24.6), lock validity (21.4).
- Platform webhooks: session created/started, consent captured, identity-proofing results, document signed, notarial act completed, tamper seal applied, audit trail available, session failed/abandoned.
- eRegistry acknowledgments and notifications (HTTPS callbacks): registration accepted/rejected, Change Data (Secured Party) accepted, eDelivery receipt, transfer confirmations.
- eRecording vendor callbacks: accepted for recording, rejected (reason codes), recorded (instrument number, book/page, recording timestamp), fees.
- Courier tracking events for paper notes and wet packages; settlement-agent confirmations of execution and shipment.
- Borrower actions: closing-type election (RON/IPEN/hybrid/wet — always with a paper option), e-consent (or its withdrawal), rescheduling.

#### State machine

`closings.status`: `scheduled` → `package_released` (26.1) → `pre_session_checks_passed` (all gates open; consent verified; eligibility confirmed) → `session_in_progress` → `signed` (every required signature applied; `consummation_at` set when the note/eNote is fully signed) → `notarized` (every notarial act complete; witnesses applied) → `sealed` (eNote tamper-sealed; audit trail received) → `execution_reviewed` (`closer` post-signing review passed) → `awaiting_funding` (26.3) → `funded` → `recorded` (all recordable instruments confirmed) → `complete`. Side states: `session_failed{reason}` (identity failure, platform outage, signer no-show, document defect) → `rescheduled` or `converted_to_paper_path`; `voided` (rescission exercised — 25.3; or unwind — 26.3) with `enote.registration.reversed` and release/reconveyance recorded when applicable. Guards: `session_in_progress` requires `pre_session_checks_passed` in the last 24 hours; `sealed` requires `enotes.tamper_sealed_at`; `execution_reviewed` requires zero blocking `execution_defects`; `recorded` requires `recordings.recorded_at` for every `closing_documents.recordable=true`.

`signing_sessions.status`: `created` → `consent_captured` → `identity_proofed` (each signer) → `in_session` → `documents_signed` → `notarial_acts_complete` → `tamper_sealed` (eNote/eSigned docs) → `audit_trail_received` → `completed`; failures → `failed{reason}` (KBA failure after the permitted retries, credential analysis failure, connectivity loss, notary unavailable, signer withdrew consent) or `abandoned`. Only the `notary` performs the notarial act; only the signer applies a signature; the platform records, never signs.

`enotes.status`: `created` (26.1 SMART Doc built) → `signed` → `tamper_sealed` → `registered` (eRegistry Registration accepted; Controller = partner, Location = SM/vendor eVault, Delegatee = SM) → `secured_party_set` (before the warehouse advance) → `edelivered` (to Fannie Mae's eVault at delivery — 29.4) → `control_transferred` (29.4) ; `reversed` (Registration Reversal on re-draw/rescission/unwind); `converted_to_paper` (never by SM's choice; Fannie Mae/Controller right).

#### Business rules and calculations

**Closing-type decision (`decideClosingType`).** Start from 26.1's profile. `ron` if: property state RON-authorized and on Fannie Mae's list (or counsel-confirmed), county accepts remotely notarized instruments for eRecording or paper recording, title underwriter takes no exception, settlement agent/RON provider eligible, every signer consents to electronic records and can complete identity proofing, no TX 50(a)(6), no signer requiring a credible witness who cannot attend remotely, and any witness requirement satisfiable remotely under state law. `ipen` if RON fails but in-person electronic notarization is valid and the agent is IPEN-capable. `hybrid` (eNote + paper security instrument wet-signed and notarized in person; or paper note where eNote is excluded + eSigned ancillaries) if the eNote is eligible but eNotarization/eRecording is not, or the product excludes the eNote but e-signatures are otherwise acceptable. `wet` for TX 50(a)(6), borrower election, consent withdrawal, or any failure at the session. The note is always single-method: eNote fully electronic or paper note fully wet.

**Identity proofing (`proofIdentity`).** Technical path = credential analysis pass + KBA pass; KBA parameters from `jurisdiction_rules.kba_params` (Texas verified: ≥5 questions, 2 minutes, ≥80%, one retake within 24 hours with ≥60% new questions; other states default to the same parameters until verified). Fail → one retake where the state allows; second fail → `session_failed{identity}`; alternative non-technical path (credible witness or notary personal knowledge) only where the state and Fannie Mae permit; SM never overrides a notary's refusal.

**Consummation and note date.** `consummation_at` = timestamp of the final required signature on the note/eNote (the borrower becomes contractually obligated — 25.3 uses the local date); note date printed = the scheduled signing date; if the session slips past local midnight the set is re-drawn (26.1) with the new date.

**eNote seal → register → Secured Party.** After the last eNote signature the platform applies the tamper-evident seal (SHA-256) and deposits the Authoritative Copy in the Location eVault; SM validates the hash, submits the digitally signed Registration XML (Controller = partner Org ID; Location = SM/vendor eVault Org ID; Delegatee for Transfers = SM; Servicing Agent = SM; MIN = the 26.1 MIN), receives the acceptance, then submits Change Data naming SM as Secured Party before any warehouse advance. `registration_due_at` = end of the next `business_days_federal` day after `tamper_sealed_at` (SM target: within 2 hours). eDelivery to Fannie Mae's eVault and Transfer of Control and Location happen at delivery (29.4), with SM as Secured Party approving or the partner transferring per the Custodial Agreement.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`closer` (the closing-execution runtime of the `title-closing` package — defined in 26.1). Additional tools here: `runPreSessionChecks`, `decideClosingType`, `verifyEsignConsent`, `checkRonEligibility`, `assignNotary`, `openSigningSession`, `monitorSession`, `validateAuthoritativeCopy`, `registerENote`, `setSecuredParty`, `reverseRegistration`, `ingestAuditTrail`, `reviewExecution`, `submitERecording`, `handleRecordingReject`, `dispatchPaperRecording`, `trackPaperNote`, `seedCustodyRecord`, `writeDecision`. End-to-end behavior: confirms every gate and eligibility item before the appointment, sends the borrower instructions, opens and monitors the session (reading platform events, never acting as notary), validates the seal and registers the eNote within minutes, names SM as Secured Party before the advance, reviews execution, submits recordings (or paper fallback), tracks paper notes into custody, and hands 26.3 a funding-ready closing. Decision record: `{closing_id, session_ids, closing_type, closing_type_reasons, gates_checked, eligibility, identity_results (no PII beyond method/result), consummation_at, tamper_sealed_at, registration{txn, due_at, registered_at}, secured_party, audit_trail_hash, execution_review, recordings, custody_chain, rationale, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails — the agent may never: open a session with a closed gate; require electronic records or RON of any borrower; apply, complete or "assist" a signature or notarial act; accept a KBA/credential override; register an eNote whose hash does not match the eVault copy; fund-related actions before the audit trail is on file; convert a paper note to an eNote; submit a recording with a handwritten instrument change; disable the paper fallback; use a notary outside the state of the notarial act; treat Georgia/Mississippi (or any unlisted state) as RON-eligible without counsel confirmation. Escalations: `notary` (external; session tasks, SLA session window); `settlement_agent` (execution defects, shipments, recording rejections; SLA same day); `signing_officer` (eRegistry transactions not delegated to SM; Maine Form 3749; lost-in-transit note affidavits with 26.4; SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`); `officer` (registration-late incidents, RON legal questions, waiver of an execution defect; SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`); `licensed_specialist`/closing attorney in attorney states; `human_agent` on borrower request. Borrower-facing: automation disclosed in the appointment notice and at session start (the platform's own scripts); the borrower may stop and switch to paper at any time before signing without penalty. Human path: ops console with the same session monitor, manual registration submission under dual control, and manual recording dispatch.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- KBA failure after the permitted retries → session fails; offer IPEN/wet within the lock and CD validity; 25.2 re-checks the consummation date.
- Notary loses connectivity mid-act → the act is void; the notary restarts the act; the platform logs both; no partial notarizations are accepted.
- Tamper seal applied but the eRegistry rejects the registration (MIN mismatch, digital-signature failure) → fix and resubmit within the 1-BD window; if the eNote itself is defective → Registration Reversal is not needed (never registered); re-draw and re-sign (26.1).
- Registration accepted but the eNote must be replaced (data error found in execution review) → new eNote executed; original "de-activated via Registration Reversal" (B8-8-02) after the replacement registers; both events recorded.
- Rescission exercised (25.3) after registration → Registration Reversal (or Change Status per MERS procedures — **[UNVERIFIED which transaction]**), Secured Party release, and if recorded, release/reconveyance recorded at the partner's cost (SM-borne under the operating model).
- Session runs past midnight local time → note date changes → re-draw (26.1) before the note is signed; if the note was signed before midnight and ancillaries after, the note date stands and the CD/rescission dates are re-evaluated (25.2/25.3).
- Borrower withdraws e-consent at the session → wet path; the platform preserves the un-signed package; a paper set is issued (26.1) and the appointment rescheduled.
- Signer located outside the notary's state (or outside the U.S.) → allowed only where the notary's state law permits (NY: record must relate to a U.S. matter) → `jurisdiction_rules` check; otherwise wet/IPEN.
- County eRecording outage/rejection for formatting → cure within 2 business days or paper fallback; the CPL and Covered Risk 14 protect the gap; 26.3 dry-state funding waits for recording confirmation only where the partner's policy requires (decision 26.2-Q3).
- Paper note lost in transit → lost-note affidavit path (13.3/26.4) with the courier claim; funding holds in dry states; Fannie Mae delivery requires the original or an approved lost-note affidavit.
- Hybrid with a POA signer → the eNote is eligible with the certified POA eDelivered (B8-8-01); the POA original goes to the custodian where state law requires (B8-5-05).
- Witness state with RON → remote witnessing only where the state allows; otherwise IPEN/wet with witnesses present.
- The RON provider ceases business → the contract's recording escrow/transfer clause moves recordings to a successor custodian; SM holds the audit trails regardless.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **26.2-T1** Given an eNote tamper-sealed Fri Nov 6, 2026 14:41 MST, when `registration_due_at` is computed, then it is Mon Nov 9, 2026 23:59 ET; registration accepted at 14:46 MST satisfies `MERS_PROC_ENOTE_REGISTER_1BD`; an acceptance at Tue Nov 10 00:30 ET breaches it.
- **26.2-T2** Given a tamper seal at Wed Nov 25, 2026 16:10 EST (day before Thanksgiving), then `registration_due_at` = Fri Nov 27, 2026 23:59 ET (Thu Nov 26 excluded).
- **26.2-T3** Given a Texas 50(a)(6) closing, when `decideClosingType` runs, then `closing_type=wet`, `SM_O72_RON_STATE_AUTH_GATE` reports `product_excluded`, and the location must be a lender/attorney/title-company office.
- **26.2-T4** Given a Georgia property, then RON is refused with reason `state_not_on_fnma_list` unless a counsel confirmation record exists.
- **26.2-T5** Given a borrower who fails KBA twice within the session in Texas, then the session status is `failed{identity}` and no retake is offered with the same notary for 24 hours; a wet/IPEN reschedule is proposed.
- **26.2-T6** Given the eVault copy hash ≠ the platform seal hash, then `SM_O72_ENOTE_LOCATION_GATE` stays closed and no Registration XML is sent.
- **26.2-T7** Given a warehouse advance requested on an eNote loan before `enote.secured_party.set`, then the advance is refused with reason `secured_party_missing`.
- **26.2-T8** Given the fixture RON session, when the audit trail has not arrived by the funding request on Thu Nov 12, then `funding.authorize` is refused until `closing.audit_trail.received`.
- **26.2-T9** Given a deed of trust notarized Fri Nov 6 in a dry state under the record-after-signing default, then `SM_O72_ERECORD_SUBMIT_1BD` is satisfied by a submission on Mon Nov 9 and breached on Tue Nov 10.
- **26.2-T10** Given an eRecording rejection on Mon Nov 9 10:30 for a missing cover sheet, then a corrected submission accepted by Wed Nov 11 close satisfies `SM_O72_RECORDING_REJECT_CURE_2BD` (Veterans Day is not a creditor business day — the due date is Thu Nov 12); a paper fallback dispatched instead starts `SM_O72_PAPER_FALLBACK_5BD`.
- **26.2-T11** Given a wet-signed paper note on Wed Nov 18, then a courier scan by Thu Nov 19 satisfies `SM_O72_PAPER_NOTE_HANDOFF_1BD` and `custody_records.chain` shows settlement_agent → courier with the tracking reference.
- **26.2-T12** Given the same session signs the eNote at 23:58 local and the deed of trust at 00:04 the next day, then `consummation_at` and the note date remain the first day and 25.3's period is anchored there; an eNote signed after midnight triggers a re-draw.
- **26.2-T13** Given an executed set with a handwritten change to the interest rate on a paper note, then `reviewExecution` fails with `instrument_altered`, funding is blocked, and a re-draw is scheduled.
- **26.2-T14** Given a RON closing delivered to Fannie Mae, then the ULDD carries `remote_notarization_indicator=true` and `enote_indicator=true`, and the audit trail is in the electronic loan file handed to servicing.

#### Audit and evidence

For Fannie Mae QC/MORA and eMortgage reviews: per-session identity method and results, consent evidence with the E-SIGN §101(c) statements, tamper-sealed audit trails (life of loan + 7 years), eRegistry transaction log with acceptance timestamps against `registration_due_at`, Authoritative Copy hash chain, Secured Party history, eDelivery/transfer records, recording receipts with instrument numbers, custody chains for paper notes, execution-review reports, and `agent_decisions`. For state notary regulators/exams: notary commission and physical-location attestations, journal references, recording custodian and retention period per state. For litigation: proof that one and only one Authoritative Copy existed at all times; the platform's event timeline for consummation; witness manifests.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **eVault ownership.** SM's own eRegistry-integrated eVault as Location vs the eClosing vendor's eVault. Default: vendor eVault at launch with SM as Location Participant of record only if the vendor's eVault is operated under SM's eRegistry Org ID; migrate to an SM eVault after the first 500 eNotes.
2. **Business-day unit for the MERS 1-BD rule.** MERS defines no unit. Default: `business_days_federal` with same-day registration as the operating target; confirm with MERS.
3. **Record-after-signing vs record-at-disbursement in dry-state refinances.** Default: record after signing (fixture Mon Nov 9) so the lien is of record before funds move, accepting the release cost on a rescission; the partner may elect record-at-disbursement per state.
4. **Georgia/Mississippi.** Default: no RON until Fannie Mae lists the state or partner counsel issues a written "expressly permits" opinion.
5. **RON video retention contract.** Default: the RON provider retains recordings for the longer of the state period or 10 years with an escrow/successor clause; SM keeps only audit trails.
6. **MERS transaction on rescission of a registered eNote.** Default: Registration Reversal after Secured Party release; confirm with MERS eRegistry support before go-live.
