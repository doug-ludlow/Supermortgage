# Section 18 — QC, Audit & Regulatory Reporting

## Overview

Section 18 is the platform's second and third line of defense and its window to the people who examine it. Everything the other seventeen sections do is produced by AI agents against versioned rule sets; this section is what proves — to Fannie Mae, to the partner (master servicer of record, "SoR"), to state examiners, to the CFPB and to a RESPA plaintiff's lawyer — that the work was done correctly, and what catches it when it was not. Seven processes: the **internal QC program** (Servicing Guide A4-1-01, 02/12/2025, plus the A2-1-01/A2-1-07 subservicer-oversight duties and Reg X 1024.38 policies-and-procedures objectives) implemented as **QC-as-code**; the **Fannie Mae review/exam response engine** (A2-4-01 Quality Control Reviews with its 30-day file-delivery rule, the Servicing Compliance Review — formerly the STAR Operational Assessment — A1-1-03 servicer reviews, and CFPB/state/partner examinations); the **STAR** metric pipeline (2026 program: Transition to 60+, 60+ to Cure, Retention Efficiency, 6-Month Mod/Deferral Performance, Transition to Beyond Timeframe; Master vs. Acting Servicing views); **Form 582** and the other Selling Guide A4-1-02/A4-1-03 filings (AFS within 90 days of fiscal year-end, Form 1002 quarterly, 5-business-day and 60-day organizational-change notices); **mortgage fraud reporting** (Selling Guide A3-4-03, 12/10/2025: 30 days via Loan Quality Connect; OFAC matches in 24 hours); the **Reg AB / USAP** support package (Items 1122/1123 — inapplicable to the Fannie Mae book because Fannie Mae MBS are exempt securities, 12 U.S.C. 1719(d), but required by the partner's other investors and financing counterparties); and **FHFA financial eligibility** monitoring ($2.5M + 25 bps, 6% tangible-net-worth ratio, 7/3.5 bps liquidity; subserviced UPB excluded from the subservicer and counted by the master). Guide citations are to the **August 12, 2026** Servicing Guide and the Selling Guide as of **August 5, 2026** (SEL-2026-07, which changed nothing in the financial-eligibility numbers); eCFR text current as of **September 2–8, 2026**.

**Architecture fit.** The section is owned by the **`qc-audit`** agent (`agents/qc-audit`) with `compliance-sentinel` (timers, Guide/Lender Letter watch) and `security-records` (retention, incident runbook) as collaborators, and it defines the one governance program the whole platform depends on: the **LL-2026-04 AI governance program** (contractual since **August 6, 2026**), including the `ai_systems` inventory, risk tiers, evaluation suites, monitoring and the disclosure package Fannie Mae may demand "promptly." Core services used: Timer Engine (every filing and response deadline), the append-only `loan_events`/`agent_decisions`/`timers` tables (the evidence base), `documents` (hashed artifacts), the `fnma-connect` adapter (STAR scorecards, report pulls), a new `gl` adapter (financial eligibility), and `human_portal_task` escalations for the portal-only islands: Loan Quality Connect (review files, self-reports), ECRM/Form 582, WebMB/Form 1002 and the privacy_office@fanniemae.com incident mailbox. Independence is structural: `qc-audit` runs a separate model/prompt lineage from every producing agent, has read-only access to operational tables, and its cycle reports are signed by a human QC officer (`officer` role, designation `qc_officer`) who reports outside operations.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** Supermortgage is itself a Fannie Mae-approved servicer (A2-1-07), so **both entities file**: each submits its own Form 582, AFS and Form 1002, each meets FHFA eligibility on its own book, and each may be reviewed by Fannie Mae directly ("Fannie Mae may enforce any rights and remedies it may have against the subservicer"). The partner remains "fully liable," is scored on STAR's Master Servicing view, signs officer certifications for the subserviced book (Form 582 subservicing confirmation, Reg AB/USAP, exam responses) and must "conduct audits and QC reviews on subservicer/outsource vendor for contracted servicing activities" (A2-1-01) — Supermortgage's QC program is built to be that audit's evidence. Supermortgage prepares every package; the partner's officers sign what only the SoR can sign.

**Dependencies.** Every section feeds 18.1's sampling populations; 5.x (investor-reporting rejects, repurchase demand/appeal timers `FNMA_A1302_*`), 6.x (Forms 496/496A, custodial controls), 8.3 (tradeline re-derivation), 10.6 (PMI denial sampling), 12.x (loss-mitigation decisions and Form 183 adverse-action certification), 13.x/14.x (law-firm oversight A4-2.2-02, foreclosure timelines for STAR), 17.x (transfer QC), 19.1/19.2 (retention, Safeguards Rule, 36-hour incident runbook), 1.2 (Form 629/Form 101 registry used by Form 582).

**Changes in flight.** LL-2026-04 (AI governance, Aug. 6, 2026) and the Information Security and Business Resiliency Supplement (Sept. 2, 2025 edition; annual officer attestation) are the two new contractual programs. STAR 2026 moved investor-reporting metrics to supplemental status, added two disaster metrics and suppresses scores where a metric's denominator is under 30 loans. Fannie Mae's Servicing Compliance Review is now risk-based (about every two years). LL-2026-05 event-based reporting will change the STAR "reporting quality" evidence base in 2027. The Colorado AI Act (June 30, 2026) makes AI loss-mitigation decisioning a "consequential decision" with impact-assessment, notice and appeal duties that 18.1's AI governance program absorbs. CSBS prudential standards (12 states) add board-oversight, internal/external-audit and risk-management duties once a servicer has 2,000+ loans in 2+ states.
