### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**1. Legal form and RESPA (Reg X §1024.2(b), §1024.5(b)(7); eCFR current as of 9/08/2026 per 00a-fed §6.7 — VERIFIED).** "Lender means, generally, the secured creditor or creditors named in the debt obligation and document creating the lien. For loans originated by a mortgage broker that closes a federally related mortgage loan in its own name in a table funding transaction, the lender is the person to whom the obligation is initially assigned at or after settlement." "Table funding means a settlement at which a loan is funded by a contemporaneous advance of loan funds and an assignment of the loan to the person advancing the funds. A table-funded transaction is not a secondary market transaction (see § 1024.5(b)(7))." §1024.5(b)(7) (eCFR 9/09/2026, re-read 2026-09-11): "A bona fide transfer of a loan obligation in the secondary market is not covered by RESPA and this part, except with respect to RESPA (12 U.S.C. 2605) and subpart C of this part (§§ 1024.30-1024.41). In determining what constitutes a bona fide transfer, the Bureau will consider the real source of funding and the real interest of the funding lender. Mortgage broker transactions that are table-funded are not secondary market transactions." HUD's RESPA warehouse-lending solicitation (75 FR 71725, Nov 24, 2010; federalregister.gov, fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED) restates the 1992 rule: "[i]n determining what constitutes a bona fide transfer, HUD would consider the real source of the funding and the real interest of the settlement lender" (57 FR at 49609; "settlement lender" changed to "funding lender" by the Feb 10, 1994 final rule, 59 FR at 6509), and asked the industry whether ownership "ever transfer[s] to the warehouse lender," whether "the obligation to repurchase a loan … [is] absolute or conditional," and "[t]o what extent is the warehouse lender involved in the loan-level credit approval decision … Do warehouse lenders approve the funding of individual loans before settlement?" No HUD or CFPB guidance answering the 2010 solicitation was located **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — absence]**. *Consequence*: the facility must be a **loan to the partner secured by the mortgage loans** (the partner funds settlement with money it borrowed; no assignment to SM at settlement; the partner bears full recourse), and the fulfillment agents' loan-level work must be performed as the partner's agent under the Services Agreement, not as the warehouse lender's credit approval — see the structure decision below and 31.1.

**2. UCC Article 9 (uniform text, law.cornell.edu, fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED unless flagged).** Collateral classes: "Instrument" (9-102(a)(47)) "means a negotiable instrument or any other writing that evidences a right to the payment of a monetary obligation, is not itself a security agreement or lease, and is of a type that in ordinary course of business is transferred by delivery with any necessary indorsement or assignment"; "Promissory note" (9-102(a)(65)) "means an instrument that evidences a promise to pay a monetary obligation, does not evidence an order to pay, and does not contain an acknowledgment by a bank"; "Chattel paper" (9-102(a)(11)) "means a record or records that evidence both a monetary obligation and a security interest in specific goods" — a note secured by real property is therefore an instrument (paper) and never chattel paper; "General intangible" (9-102(a)(42)) is the residual class and "Payment intangible" (9-102(a)(61)) "a general intangible under which the account debtor's principal obligation is a monetary obligation" — the Article 9 class of an eNote (not a "writing"). Perfection: 9-312(a) "A security interest in chattel paper, negotiable documents, instruments, or investment property may be perfected by filing"; 9-313(a) a secured party "may perfect a security interest in … instruments … by taking possession of the collateral"; 9-313(c) possession through a third person exists when that person "authenticates a record acknowledging that it holds possession of the collateral for the secured party's benefit" or "takes possession of the collateral after having authenticated a record acknowledging that it will hold possession"; 9-313(d) "perfection occurs no earlier than the time the secured party takes possession and continues only while the secured party retains possession"; 9-313(f) "A person in possession of collateral is not required to acknowledge that it holds possession for a secured party's benefit." Priority: 9-330(d) "a purchaser of an instrument has priority over a security interest in the instrument perfected by a method other than possession if the purchaser gives value and takes possession of the instrument in good faith and without knowledge that the purchase violates the rights of the secured party" — filing alone does not protect SM against a partner double-pledge; possession by a bailee does. 9-312(e): a security interest in instruments "is perfected without filing or the taking of possession or control for a period of 20 days from the time it attaches to the extent that it arises for new value given under an authenticated security agreement" — the wet-funding window is protected for 20 days even before the note reaches the custodian. 9-309(4): "a sale of a promissory note" is "perfected when [it] attach[es]" (relevant to the master-repurchase alternative). 9-105 (2010 text as displayed): "(a) A secured party has control of electronic chattel paper if a system employed for evidencing the transfer of interests in the chattel paper reliably establishes the secured party as the person to which the chattel paper was assigned. (b) A system satisfies subsection (a) if the record or records comprising the chattel paper are created, stored, and assigned in such a manner that: (1) a single authoritative copy exists which is unique, identifiable and unalterable …; (2) the authoritative copy identifies the secured party as the assignee …; (3) the authoritative copy is communicated to and maintained by the secured party or its designated custodian; (4) copies or amendments that add or change an identified assignee … can be made only with the consent of the secured party; (5) each copy … is readily identifiable as a copy that is not the authoritative copy; (6) any amendment … is readily identifiable as authorized or unauthorized." **This section governs electronic chattel paper, not eNotes.** 2022 UCC amendments, Article 12 §12-102(a)(1) (Delaware enactment, delcode.delaware.gov, fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED): "'Controllable electronic record' means a record stored in an electronic medium that can be subjected to control under Section 12-105. The term does not include … an electronic copy of a record evidencing chattel paper, … or a transferable record." Filing mechanics (9-307 debtor location = state of organization for a registered organization; 9-502 contents; 9-515 five-year effectiveness with a six-month continuation window) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — sections not re-read today; standard text]**.

**3. eNotes — E-SIGN 15 U.S.C. §7021 (uscode.house.gov, text current through Sept 9, 2026 — VERIFIED).** (a) a "transferable record" is an electronic record that "would be a note under Article 3 of the Uniform Commercial Code if the electronic record were in writing," whose issuer "expressly has agreed is a transferable record," and that "relates to a loan secured by real property." (b) "A person has control of a transferable record if a system employed for evidencing the transfer of interests in the transferable record reliably establishes that person as the person to which the transferable record was issued or transferred." (c) the six-element test: "a single authoritative copy … unique, identifiable, and … unalterable"; the copy identifies the controlling person "as the person to which the transferable record was issued" or most recently transferred; it is "communicated to and maintained by the person asserting control or its designated custodian"; copies or revisions "made only with the consent of the person asserting control"; copies "readily identifiable as a copy that is not the authoritative copy"; revisions identifiable "as authorized or unauthorized." (d) the person with control "is the holder … of the transferable record and has the same rights and defenses as a holder of an equivalent record or writing under the Uniform Commercial Code." (f) on request the enforcing party "shall provide reasonable proof that the person is in control of the transferable record." MERS eRegistry Procedures Manual (Release 16.25, effective Feb 10, 2020; mersinc.org, fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED): the eRegistry exists to "identify the Participant that has Control of the eNote" under "Section 201(c) of the E-SIGN Act and Section 16(c) of the UETA with respect to a transferable record" (p. 1); "Secured Party" = a Participant that has been "sold, pledged, assigned, or granted a security interest in the eNote by the Controller" (p. 6); "Add Secured Party" is submitted only by the **Controller** (p. 10) after reciprocal relationships ("Secured Party Controller relationship" and "Controller Acknowledges Secured Party relationship," p. 40); a Participant in the Secured Party field can "Perform transactions to its eNote Records to protect and release its secured interest," "Initiate any Transfer transaction except a stand-alone Transfer of Servicing Agent … and must confirm any of these transfers that it or its Secured Party Delegatee did not initiate" (pp. 7, 29); "Release Secured Party" is submitted by the Secured Party only (p. 10); the Transfer Effective Date is "typically the same day … but it can be future-dated within 30 calendar days" and "cannot be retroactive" (p. 28); a Transfer of Control and Location "is accepted or rejected in full" (p. 27); and processing a transfer "Removes any Secured Party or Secured Party Delegatee if the Transfer transaction included a Transfer of Control" (p. 33). Fannie Mae eClosing/eMortgage FAQs (singlefamily.fanniemae.com, fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED): the eNote Transfer of Control and Custodial Agreement / Funding Agreement "Acts as a master bailee agreement for eNotes transferred to us by the warehouse provider on behalf of the lender," "Allows us to control the eNotes as designated custodian for the warehouse bank as a secured party and designee for the lender until purchased," "Requires us to return control to the warehouse bank (or add them back to the eNote as the Secured Party), should we decide not to purchase the loans," and "Specifies the release of the warehouse's interest when Fannie Mae pays the purchase price for the loan"; "Warehouse lenders: Must register with MERS® and have access to an eVault for communicating with the MERS eRegistry®"; "Fannie Mae requires warehouse banks to utilize an eVault which has been tested with Fannie Mae, and to execute our eNote Transfer of Control and Location and Custodial Agreement prior to delivery of eNotes to Fannie Mae." Selling Guide C1-2-04 (12/13/2023; 00b-orig F9): the Transfer of Control and Location request "must include an 'Effective Date' of the same day as the request."

**5. Fannie Mae warehouse-lender mechanics (all fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED unless flagged).**
- *Selling Guide C1-2-03, Ownership of Mortgage Loans Prior to Purchase or Securitization and Third-Party Security Interests (04/06/2022):* "Fannie Mae recognizes that the legal rights of the mortgage seller delivering the notes (or, as applicable, of its warehouse lender) are unaffected by the certification processes until Fannie Mae has remitted purchase proceeds"; "Effective upon the remittance of the purchase proceeds, Fannie Mae and/or its MBS trusts obtains and holds ownership and title to the notes"; "If the transaction fails to close as contemplated by the contract between Fannie Mae and the mortgage seller and purchase proceeds are not wired, Fannie Mae claims no ownership interest in the notes"; "Fannie Mae has established procedures for resolving title issues presented by its receipt of a bailee letter. See C1-2-05, Bailee Letters."
- *C1-2-05, Bailee Letters (04/06/2022 per page title):* "The term 'bailee letter' includes any communication that notifies a document custodian of any of the following" — that notes are pledged to a third party; that "the recipient holds the notes as a 'bailee' (or in trust) for the third party until the third party either receives the proceeds (cash or securities) or receives back the notes for any mortgages that were not purchased or securitized"; that "the lien of the third party's security interest will be released only if the proceeds from the transfer of the mortgages to Fannie Mae are delivered to the third party in accordance with the delivery instructions in the letter"; or any other security interest, lien or ownership claim that would prevent Fannie Mae from acquiring "clear, unencumbered, and exclusive title." "Fannie Mae will undertake to identify mismatches and to achieve matches in delivery instructions promptly, in good faith, and in accordance with reasonable business practices"; "Fannie Mae will not be liable for any losses"; the seller must align its delivery instructions with its warehouse lender's before delivery and bears "full responsibility for any trading or settlement delays" from mismatches; Fannie Mae's handling does not waive the seller's title warranty.
- *C2-2-03, General Information on Whole Loan Purchasing Policies (05/04/2022):* "When Fannie Mae purchases a mortgage, the mortgage seller represents and warrants that title to the mortgage note is free and clear of any security interest, lien, pledge, or other encumbrance, which means that any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note"; "Fannie Mae will send the proceeds from the whole loan sale via a wire transfer into the lender's designated account on the 'Purchased and Funded' date (as reflected in Loan Delivery) if: the designated payee code is valid; the loan is eligible for purchase; and the document custodian received a complete, accurate mortgage document submission package and loan delivery data by the applicable cutoff times"; "Fannie Mae accepts delivery of any mortgage up to 45 days from the due date of the reported last paid installment" (timer `FNMA_C2_2_DELIVERY_LPI_45` is 29.4's); "Each lender is responsible for establishing and maintaining controls and procedures that ensure the confidentiality of all transfer instructions"; "A lender is solely liable for transfers … as the result of a breach in its security arrangements or … failure to give Fannie Mae timely notice."
- *C2-2-04, Timing of Distribution of Whole Loan Purchase Proceeds (05/04/2022):* "Fannie Mae will fund the purchase proceeds for whole loan deliveries of regularly amortizing mortgages provided the following procedure has already occurred": the "lender has electronically submitted clean, error-free loan delivery data through Loan Delivery by 9:00 p.m. (Eastern time)"; "the document custodian has received a complete and accurate mortgage document submission package from a lender by first morning delivery the following day"; "the document custodian has certified the mortgage document submission package"; otherwise "Fannie Mae will disburse the purchase proceeds on the business day following the date it receives these materials, assuming they are in order and the mortgage is eligible for purchase"; "Following the funding of the purchase proceeds the loan status in Loan Delivery will be 'Purchased and Funded.'" *Loan Delivery User Guide (May 2026), p. 64 (00b-orig F6):* "If Fannie Mae's document custodian receives the complete loan documentation package by 7:30 a.m. ET on the day following an error-free transmission of loan data, Fannie Mae will purchase the loan and wire your proceeds by the next business day." (27.2 owns the proceeds-side timers.)
- *C2-2-07, Purchase Payee Codes (05/01/2024):* "Lenders must specify a valid and appropriate payee code at delivery to have the purchase proceeds for a specific mortgage or group of mortgages transferred to the designated account"; "Fannie Mae will call the authorized representative who signed the Form 482 request to confirm validity prior to processing"; "To change or delete an existing payee code, lenders must submit a new Form 482"; "Fannie Mae will make the requested change within three business days after receiving the valid and completed Form 482; however, Fannie Mae cannot guarantee that this will occur in time to prevent a previously authorized transfer of funds for a mortgage that is in its purchase pipeline or to stop a wire transfer that is in process"; changes to a payee code "created in connection with the execution of a Triparty Wiring Instruction Agreement must be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Agreement." *C2-2-06 (05/01/2024):* no proceeds "until the lender has provided Fannie Mae with an executed Certificate of Authority, Incumbency, and Specimen Signatures" (Form 360); "Fannie Mae is authorized to conclusively rely on the accuracy, genuineness, and good faith of any written communication related to transfer instructions."

**Structure decision (recommendation).** *Master repurchase agreement (MRA)*: the partner "sells" each loan to SM against the advance with an obligation to repurchase; SM gets the §559 safe harbor and automatic perfection under 9-309(4); but ownership passes to SM at funding — exactly the fact HUD flagged ("Does ownership … ever transfer to the warehouse lender?") — and, combined with SM's loan-level fulfillment role, invites recharacterization of every closing as **table funding** with SM as the RESPA "lender" and the partner as a "mortgage broker" (§8/AfBA/servicing-disclosure consequences, state lender-licensing exposure for SM, and a creditor question on every LE/CD). *Loan-and-security agreement (LSA)*: the partner borrows and funds with borrowed money; SM holds a perfected first-priority security interest (possession through the custodian-bailee for paper; Secured Party/eVault control plus the Fannie Mae Funding Agreement for eNotes; UCC-1 filing as belt-and-braces and for proceeds); no ownership transfer; full recourse. **Recommend the LSA.** Reasons: (1) it is the "bona fide warehouse line of credit" pattern that keeps the partner as the Reg X lender — the operating model's foundation; (2) Fannie Mae's mechanics (bailee letter, Form 2004A, Interim Funder, Secured Party, "release … no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note") are all written for a *security interest*; (3) the loan stays on the partner's books as held-for-sale, so the partner's sale to Fannie Mae is a clean ASC 860 sale and the MSR is unambiguously the partner's (27.2); (4) exposure is short (fixture: 7 days) and fully collateralized, so the incremental protection of §559 is modest against the RESPA/licensing cost. The MRA remains the fallback if counsel concludes the partner's credit requires it (27.1-Q1). Either way, the LSA/MRA must **separate the two hats**: the Services Agreement (SM as the partner's fulfillment agent — DU, underwriting, closing, funding wires on the partner's behalf) and the facility (SM as lender whose only loan-level act is a *collateral-eligibility* check against objective criteria).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Control of eNotes under UCC 9-105" is mis-aimed: 9-105 governs *electronic chattel paper*; a mortgage note is not chattel paper (9-102(a)(11) requires a security interest in *goods*); eNote control is E-SIGN §7021(b)–(c)/UETA §16, the eRegistry is the §7021 "system," and the 2022 UCC Article 12 expressly excludes "a transferable record." The six-part tests are nearly identical, so the design is unchanged, but the citation and the perfection analysis (payment intangible → filing; holder status → control) are corrected.
2. "Form 482/2004/2004A": Form 482 (Jan 2025) is the *seller's* payee-code designation and must "match the bailee letter on file"; Form 2004A (Jan 2018) is the warehouse lender's release used only when proceeds go to *different* (disbursement-agent) instructions; a plain "Form 2004" was not located on Fannie Mae's pages **[UNVERIFIED]**. The default path uses Letter Type "Bailee" with SM's collection account as the partner's payee code — no Form 2004A.
3. "Selling Guide C2-1.2 (delivery deadlines)": C2-1.2 is *best-efforts commitments*. Delivery timing lives in C2-2-01 (custodian receipt by first morning delivery the day before commitment expiry), C2-2-03 (45 days from LPI), C2-2-04 (9:00 p.m. ET data / first-morning-delivery custodian rule) and C1-1-01 (six months from first payment date to Purchase Ready).
4. "Bailee letter forms": Fannie Mae publishes none; the only mechanical rules are the exact-match Letter Name (Warehouse Lender User Guide p. 5) and the Form 482 match. The Selling Guide's definition of "bailee letter" (C1-2-05) covers "any communication" of a pledge, so SM's letter must contain the release condition verbatim.
5. "Typ. 5–10 BD note delivery; typ. 30–60 day aging" are industry practice (00b-orig N10, unverified) — set here as facility policy (5 business days; 45/60/90).
6. The blueprint omits the automatic deletion of the Secured Party on Transfer of Control (eRegistry p. 33) — the reason Fannie Mae requires the Funding Agreement — and the Interim Funder's absence from the eRegistry (Secured Party is the eNote analogue).
7. The blueprint omits the RESPA legal-form question that decides whether SM can be the warehouse provider at all without becoming the "lender" (item 1 above; 31.1).
8. The MERS System Procedures Manual is Release 26.1 (May 17, 2026), not 24.2 (00b-orig N1); the rule set is `mers.proc.26.1`.

#### State machine

`requested` → `approved` (eligibility + borrowing base pass; `warehouse` agent) | `rejected` (any hard-fail criterion; reasons to 26.3) → `funded` (outbound wire released by `funding_approver`; `advance_date` set) → collateral sub-state `unsecured_wet` (paper note not yet with the bailee, or eNote without Secured Party) → `secured_possession` (trust receipt) / `secured_control` (Secured Party confirmed) → `delivered` (`delivery.submitted` with SM as warehouse lender on the wire) → `transferred_pending_payment` (eNote: Transfer of Control to Fannie Mae accepted, Secured Party removed by the registry, Funding Agreement protection; paper: custodian certified, holds as bailee) → `repaid` (terminal; `repaid_from=purchase_proceeds` — 27.2 performs) | `repurchased` (terminal; `repaid_from=partner_repurchase`) | `curtailed` (partial; returns to the prior state with a lower balance) | `kicked_out` (day-90 or incurable defect: removed from the borrowing base; remedies) | `returned` (Fannie Mae declines: notes returned to the custodian as SM's bailee / Control returned to SM; back to `secured_*`). Guards: no state may move to `delivered` unless `collateral_status ∈ {secured_possession, secured_control}`; `repaid` only when `proceeds_matches.status=matched` (27.2) or the repurchase wire cleared; `kicked_out` and `repurchase demanded` require `officer{sm}` acknowledgment (no automatic enforcement wires). Facility: `active` → `suspended` (covenant breach, event of default, limit breach) → `active` (cure/waiver) | `terminated`.

#### Business rules and calculations

Money in cents; rates as basis points (integer) for the facility and 5-dp decimals in formulas; interest computed on an actual/360 basis; rounding half-up to the cent only at posting, using the cumulative method below; `aged_days = as_of_date − advance_date` in calendar days (advance date = day 0).
1. **Eligibility (collateral criteria — all objective; evaluated by the `warehouse` agent from platform records, never re-underwritten).** Hard criteria: (a) DU `recommendation = approve_eligible` on the final submission whose data match the closing documents (`du_submissions`); (b) `SM_UW_CTC_GATE` satisfied and `clear_to_close.issued` (23.3); (c) `compliance.gate.opened{gate=disbursement}` (25.1: TRID, ATR/QM general QM, HPML, HOEPA/state high-cost, licensing); (d) a live, unexpired best-efforts (or mandatory) PE–Whole Loan commitment covering the loan (29.1); (e) `wire_verifications.match_result = verified`, `expires_at > now`, `blocks_disbursement = false` for the settlement-agent beneficiary (24.4); (f) for refinances `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE` open (25.3) and, in dry states, 26.3's recording/doc-review condition; (g) note amount ≤ `max_loan_cents` for the unit count (2026: $832,750 baseline / $1,249,125 ceiling for one unit; by county — F11 table); (h) `loan_terms` within program scope (no HomeStyle, C-to-P, co-op, non-Fannie; §20 scope); (i) first payment date ≤ 2 months after the disbursement date (C2-2-01; 26.3's `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M`); (j) no open `qc_reviews{kind=prefunding}` with blocking findings (28.1); (k) MI certificate active when LTV > 80% (24.6); (l) flood/insurance evidence gates (24.5); (m) title commitment with CPL naming the partner (24.4); (n) no duplicate advance for the `loan_id`; (o) partner not suspended, facility `active`. Soft criteria (flag but fund): appraisal `expires_at` within 30 days of advance; lock extension count > 1; commitment expiry < 15 days after the advance date (29.1 extends or re-commits; the fixture's Nov 21 expiry is flagged, not blocking). `eligibility_snapshot` records every criterion with the evidence ids and versions.
2. **Advance amount.** `advance_cents = min(round_half_up(advance_rate_bps/10,000 × note_amount_cents), net_disbursement_cents)`; `partner_contribution_cents = net_disbursement_cents − advance_cents` (funded from `partner_haircut_reserve`; gate `SM_WH_HAIRCUT_RESERVE_GATE`). The outbound wire to the settlement agent equals `net_disbursement_cents` (one wire, SM's funding account, on the partner's behalf per 26.3), split in the ledger into the advance and the partner contribution. Fixture refinance: $560,000 × 0.98 = **$548,800.00** advance.
3. **Borrowing base and availability.** For each open advance `collateral_value_cents = round_half_up(advance_rate × min(note_amount, commitment_price/100 × note_amount))` (mark-to-commitment; premium commitments cap at par so the base never exceeds 98% of UPB); ineligible if `aged_days > kickout_day`, `collateral_status = unsecured_wet` beyond its deadline, an open incurable defect, or a concentration overflow (excess only). `borrowing_base = Σ eligible collateral_value`; `availability = min(facility_limit, borrowing_base + pending eligible advances) − outstanding`; `wet_availability = wet_sublimit_pct × facility_limit − wet_outstanding`. An advance is approved only if both availabilities cover it. If `outstanding_principal > collateral_value` for any advance (commitment re-priced below par, curtailment unpaid), `warehouse.margin_call.issued` for the difference (`SM_WH_MARGIN_CALL_1BD`). Concentration limits (policy defaults, policy version `sm.warehouse.v1`): wet ≤ 40% of the limit; single loan ≤ `max_loan_cents`; per state ≤ 50%; ARM ≤ 25%; investment ≤ 15%; 2–4 unit ≤ 15%; Texas 50(a)(6) ≤ 10%; aged > 45 days ≤ 10%; paper notes ≤ 40% (eNote default).
4. **Collateral control by note form and funding type.** `enote`: control passes to SM when the Controller (partner) submits "Add Secured Party" naming SM's Org ID (target: within the registration transaction or ≤ 1 BD after `enote.registered`; `SM_WH_ENOTE_SECURED_PARTY_1BD`); until then the advance is `unsecured_wet` (counts against the wet sublimit even in a dry state); at delivery the Transfer of Control and Location to Fannie Mae (29.4) is initiated by the partner as Controller and **confirmed by SM as Secured Party** (or initiated by SM); the registry removes SM as Secured Party on acceptance and the Funding Agreement governs until proceeds arrive (27.2). `paper` in a dry state: 26.3 funds only after the signed note is in the custodian's (or SM-designated bailee's) hands or in transit under SM's bailee letter — the advance is `secured_possession` on the trust receipt, otherwise `unsecured_wet` with the 5-BD clock; 9-312(e) gives 20 days of automatic perfection for new value in the meantime; Interim Funder Org ID on the MIN within 7 days (26.4 registers; 27.1 verifies). `paper` in a wet state: advance at signing; `SM_WH_WET_NOTE_DELIVERY_5BD` runs. Custodian = the partner's Form 2017 custodian holding for SM under SM's bailee letter (default) or SM's designated custodian (26.4-Q1); either way a 9-313(c) acknowledgment is on file.
5. **Bailee letter content and issuance.** One letter per shipment (may list many loans), on SM letterhead whose text equals `warehouse_facilities.bailee_letter_name` character-for-character; addressed to the custodian and Fannie Mae; loan list; the pledge statement; the C1-2-05 release condition ("the lien of [SM]'s security interest will be released only if the proceeds from the transfer of the mortgages to Fannie Mae are delivered to [SM] in accordance with the delivery instructions in this letter"); wire instructions identical to the partner's Form 482 payee code for SM (hash equality enforced); the return instruction for unpurchased loans; expiry; e-signature of SM's `officer` (wet-ink where the custodian requires — 27.1-Q4). In Loan Delivery the partner's operator selects Receiver Type "Warehouse Lender" → SM, Letter Type "Bailee" and leaves the Letter Name blank; SM's `fnma_portal_operator{party=sm}` approves the instruction once ("Pending" → "Active") and enters the Letter Name. Form 2004A is generated only when proceeds must go elsewhere (disbursement agent) and is signed by SM's `officer`, mailed in original to the custodian, copied to the partner. Corrections follow the Bailee Correction Reminders (cancel certification; new Fannie Mae loan number on resubmission; corrected physical letter to the custodian) and are logged as `warehouse.bailee_letter.corrected`.
6. **Interest.** `all_in_rate = max(index_rate, floor) + spread + (dwell_stepup if aged_days ≥ aging_curtail_day + 1) + (wet_overdue_stepup 100 bps if applicable)`. `index_rate` for accrual date *d* = SOFR published on the last `business_days_federal` on or before *d* − 1 business day (one-business-day lookback; weekends/holidays reuse the last published value). Daily cumulative interest `C(n) = Σ_{k=1..n} P_k × r_k / 360` computed unrounded; the posting for day *n* is `round_half_up(C(n)) − Σ previous postings`, so the sum of postings always equals the rounded cumulative amount. Capitalization on the 1st: `capitalized_interest_cents += Σ postings of the prior month` (or invoice if `interest_treatment=invoice_monthly`); capitalized interest accrues at the same rate from the 1st (simple within the month, compounding monthly) and is subject to the borrowing base; at payoff the payoff statement = principal + capitalized interest + accrued-not-capitalized interest through the day before repayment + fees.
7. **Fees.** `wire_fee_cents` per outbound advance and per inbound proceeds match (pass-through of bank cost; $25.00 default), `custodian_fee_cents` per paper note when SM's designated custodian is used (pass-through), no commitment, non-usage or unused-line fee in v1, `wet_overdue` and dwell step-ups are rate, not fee. Fees post to `warehouse_fees_receivable{loan}` / `warehouse_fees` and are collected at payoff.
8. **Aging, curtailment, repurchase, kick-out.** Buckets from `aged_days`: `d0_30`, `d31_45`, `d46_60`, `d61_90`, `d90_plus`. At `aged_days = 45`: curtailment due = `curtail_pct × outstanding_principal` (10% → $54,880.00 on the fixture) payable next business day if day 45 is not a business day; from day 46 the dwell step-up applies. At day 60: `warehouse.repurchase.demanded` (full payoff within 5 `business_days_servicer`). At day 90 (or on an incurable defect): `warehouse.kickout.issued` — the advance leaves the borrowing base, the facility is suspended for new advances until repaid, and LSA remedies apply (`officer{sm}` decision; no automated liquidation). The partner may substitute collateral only with SM's consent (`officer{sm}`) and only with a loan meeting every criterion in rule 1 that is already funded and unpledged — the default answer is repurchase.
9. **Defect handling.** A defect is anything that prevents purchase on the current path (custodian exception, Loan Delivery fatal edit / "Purchase Error", EarlyCheck fatal, compliance failure discovered post-funding, QC finding, missing trailing document that blocks certification, eRegistry/MERS mismatch). The `warehouse` agent records it, opens `SM_WH_DEFECT_CURE_10BD`, and hands the cure to the owning agent (`secondary`, `post-closing`, `compliance-tester`, `title-closing`). Cured → resubmission (new Fannie Mae loan number if certification was cancelled). Not cured → repurchase from the partner's own funds (never a new advance); the loan returns to the partner as portfolio/held-for-sale; SM's cost recovery and platform economics for that loan are settled per 27.2's kick-out branch.
10. **Covenants (policy defaults; partner `officer` certifies quarterly).** Tangible net worth ≥ max($2,500,000, the FHFA minimum for the partner's UPB serviced) **[UNVERIFIED FHFA figure — 31.1]**; liquidity ≥ 30 days of haircut contributions + curtailments outstanding; leverage ≤ 15:1; maintain Fannie Mae seller/servicer approval, eMortgage approval, MERS membership, state licenses, E&O and fidelity coverage (A3-3-04/A4 levels); notify SM within 2 business days of any Fannie Mae repurchase/indemnification demand, regulatory action, or change of control; deliver quarterly financials ≤ 45 days and audited annuals ≤ 90 days; no other pledge of program loans (negative pledge); haircut reserve maintained. Breach → `warehouse.covenant.breached` → facility `suspended` unless waived by `officer{sm}` within 5 business days.
11. **Daily report to the partner (by 09:00 ET).** Position (outstanding, availability, wet usage), per-advance table (advance date, aged days, bucket, collateral status, accrued interest, all-in rate, expected purchase date, exceptions), curtailments due, defects, covenant status, index history; delivered as PDF + JSON (`warehouse_daily_reports`); the partner's GL mirror lines ride the 27.2 export.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`warehouse` agent (tools: `evaluateEligibility`, `computeBorrowingBase`, `approveAdvance`, `prepareWire` (hands to `funding_approver`), `issueBaileeLetter` (renders; routes to `officer{sm}` e-signature), `trackCollateral` (trust receipts, Secured Party, Interim Funder), `confirmTransferOfControl` (eRegistry, as Secured Party), `accrueInterest`, `capitalizeInterest`, `assessFee`, `ageAdvances`, `issueCurtailment`, `recordDefect`, `demandRepurchase`, `issueKickout` (requires `officer{sm}` acknowledgment), `testCovenants`, `renderDailyReport`, `writeDecision`). Behavior: on `funding.authorized` it evaluates every criterion from platform records, computes availability, approves or rejects with reasons within 2 business hours, prepares the wire, and thereafter watches collateral events, accrues daily, ages, curtails, and reports; the LLM is used only to draft partner-facing narratives (exception explanations, repurchase-demand cover letters) and to classify unstructured custodian exception e-mails; every amount is computed by deterministic code. Decision record: `{advance_id, eligibility_snapshot, availability_before/after, wet_check, advance_cents, partner_contribution_cents, collateral_plan, rationale, policy_version, rule_set_versions, model/prompt versions where the LLM drafted text, outcome, reviewer}` (LL-2026-04). Guardrails — the agent may never: fund without `funding_approver` release; approve an advance failing a hard criterion; re-underwrite or contact the borrower; alter a payee code or Letter Name; sign a bailee letter or Form 2004A (human `officer{sm}` signature); liquidate collateral or set off partner funds without `officer{sm}` acknowledgment; waive a covenant; read `applicant_demographics`. Escalations (each an `escalation` with an SLA timer): `funding_approver` (every wire, 2-hour SLA within the cut-off window), `officer{sm}` (bailee-letter signature same day; kick-outs, waivers, substitutions, Form 2004A — 1 business day), `officer{partner}` (repurchase funding, covenant certification, haircut reserve top-up — per the timers above), `signing_officer{partner}` (Add Secured Party / MERS Interim Funder transactions where a Controller/Member signing officer must act — same day), `fnma_portal_operator{party=sm}` (Loan Delivery wire approval, Letter Name — same day), `post-closing`/`secondary` (defect cures). Human path: warehouse console with the same eligibility checklist, borrowing base and payoff calculator; manual advance entry requires two SM users and produces the same decision record.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Secured Party never added (eNote)** — the partner's eVault fails or the Controller's signing officer is unavailable: advance stays `unsecured_wet` (wet sublimit), 1-BD timer breaches → escalation; if not cured by day 5, treated as `wet_overdue` (+100 bps, repayment at day 10).
- **Transfer of Control accepted, then Fannie Mae declines to purchase**: the Funding Agreement "Requires us to return control to the warehouse bank (or add them back to the eNote as the Secured Party)"; the agent watches for the returned Control/Secured Party and moves the advance back to `secured_control`; defect recorded.
- **Bailee validation fails at the custodian** (Letter Name ≠ letterhead; Form 482 ≠ letter): certification blocked; the partner's operator cancels certification, corrects, resubmits (new Fannie Mae loan number); SM issues a corrected physical letter; settlement date slips — aging continues to run (partner bears the interest).
- **Payee-code change attempt**: any Form 482 change touching SM's payee code requires SM's approval in Loan Delivery ("Pending" → "Active") and a fresh bailee letter; the agent blocks delivery of SM-funded loans on a mismatched payee code.
- **Partner double-pledge / prior lien found in the UCC search**: no advances until released; 9-330(d) makes possession/control, not filing, the protection — the agent refuses `delivered` without `secured_*` status.
- **Note lost in transit**: lost-note affidavit path (26.4); the advance is `unsecured_wet`; Fannie Mae will not purchase without the original note or an accepted lost-note affidavit — repurchase at day 60 unless cured.
- **Fedwire outage / bank holiday**: advances roll to the next `business_days_federal`; 26.3 re-computes per-diem/interest credits and rescission-independent dry-funding dates; wet closings already signed wait (settlement agent holds documents in escrow).
- **SOFR not published (holiday) or negative**: reuse the prior rate; floor 0%.
- **Commitment expires while the loan is on the line**: 29.1 extends or re-commits; if the price falls below par the mark-to-commitment triggers a margin call.
- **Partner insolvency**: facility suspended; no new advances; existing advances continue to be repaid from Fannie Mae proceeds under the bailee letters (proceeds go to SM's account); counsel path per LSA remedies; the MRA alternative's §559 rights are noted in 27.1-Q1.
- **Two hats conflict**: a defect discovered by SM's fulfillment agents must be recorded for the warehouse ledger on the same day (no information asymmetry in SM's favor); the LSA's information-barrier clause and the audit log evidence this.
- **Texas 50(a)(6) loan**: eligible only with 26.1's constitutional checklist passed; concentration ≤ 10%; no other change.
- **Partial-month purchase on the 1st**: interest through the day before repayment; capitalization job and payoff on the same day are ordered (capitalize first) so no interest is double-counted.

- **27.1-T7** Given the fixture advance unpurchased at day 45 (Sun Dec 27, 2026), then a curtailment of $54,880.00 is due Mon Dec 28, the step-up applies from Dec 28 (day 46), and the payoff computed for a Tue Jan 5, 2027 purchase is $499,514.71; unpaid at day 60 (Mon Jan 11, 2027), then a repurchase demand of $500,115.64 is issued with `SM_WH_REPURCHASE_PAYMENT_5BD.due_at` = Tue Jan 19, 2027.
- **27.1-T8** Given an eNote advance where the partner initiates the Transfer of Control and Location to Fannie Mae on Nov 17, when SM confirms as Secured Party and the registry accepts, then `collateral_status = transferred_pending_payment`, `enotes.secured_party_released_at` is set from the registry notification, and the advance remains in the borrowing base (Funding Agreement); when Fannie Mae later returns Control, then `secured_control` again and a defect is recorded.
- **27.1-T9** Given facility limit $50,000,000, wet sublimit 40%, wet outstanding $19,800,000, when a $403,760 wet advance is requested, then it is rejected for `wet_sublimit_exceeded` while an eNote advance with Secured Party confirmed of the same size is approved.
- **27.1-T10** Given a best-efforts commitment re-priced to 99.500 for a pledged loan with outstanding $548,800, when the borrowing base runs, then `collateral_value_cents` = 98% × 99.5% × $560,000 = $546,056.00, a margin call of $2,744.00 is issued, and `SM_WH_MARGIN_CALL_1BD` is opened.
- **27.1-T11** Given the partner's quarterly package arrives 50 days after quarter end, then `SM_WH_COVENANT_QUARTERLY_45` breaches, `warehouse.covenant.breached{reporting}` fires, the facility is `suspended` for new advances, and existing advances continue to accrue and repay normally.
- **27.1-T12** Given any attempt by the `warehouse` agent to call the bank wire API without a `funding_approver` approval record, or to issue a kick-out without an `officer{sm}` acknowledgment, then the platform refuses the call and logs a guardrail violation.

#### Audit and evidence

Per advance: the eligibility snapshot with evidence ids and rule-set versions; the funding-approver dual-control record and bank wire confirmation; collateral chain (trust receipt / Secured Party confirmation / Interim Funder confirmation / Transfer of Control acceptance / Funding Agreement reference / release); every accrual row with the SOFR publication date; curtailments, defects and cures; repurchase and kick-out decisions with `officer` acknowledgments. Per facility: the LSA, UCC-1 and continuations, lien searches, custodian acknowledgment, bailee-letter register with Letter Name evidence, Form 482 match log, covenant tests and waivers, daily reports as delivered. For Fannie Mae (MORA/QC): proof that "any interest held by a warehouse lender [was] released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquire[d] the note" (bailee release + proceeds receipt timestamps; eRegistry transfer acceptance). For examiners (RESPA/state): the legal-form memo, the two-hats information barrier, and evidence that the partner funded each settlement with borrowed money in its own name.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Facility legal form.** Default: loan-and-security agreement (partner borrower, SM secured lender) per the structure decision; MRA only on a written counsel opinion that RESPA/state-licensing risk is acceptable and §559 protection is required. Owner: SM counsel with 31.1; before first advance.
2. **Interest treatment.** Default: capitalize monthly into the advance (compounding monthly) rather than invoicing, so a thinly capitalized partner never has an unpaid interest bill; reversible per partner by `interest_treatment`.
3. **Aging/curtailment/repurchase days.** Default 45/10%/60/90 with +50 bps dwell; sized to a 7–14-day expected dwell and the Fannie Mae windows (45 days from LPI; six months from first payment to Purchase Ready). Revisit after 90 days of production data.
4. **Bailee-letter signature and form acceptance.** Default: e-signed PDF on SM letterhead; wet-ink originals where the custodian's procedures require; confirm with the partner's FCC whether a per-loan bailee letter is also required for eNote deliveries (Funding Agreement path) — default: generate and archive it, send only when the custodian is a party.
5. **Custodian of record for paper notes (26.4-Q1).** Default: the partner's Form 2017 FCC holding for SM under SM's bailee letter and a 9-313(c) acknowledgment; SM-designated custodian if the partner's FCC will not sign the acknowledgment.
6. **Interim Funder removal timing.** MERS publishes no deadline; default `SM_WH_INTERIM_FUNDER_RELEASE_2BD` after payoff (defined in 27.2) so the partner's investor update (30.1) is never blocked by SM's field.
7. **Haircut funding mechanism.** Default: partner-funded haircut reserve at SM's bank, replenished daily to cover the next business day's expected contributions; alternative: per-advance pre-funding wire (slower).
8. **Commercial-lender licensing for SM as warehouse lender** (California Financing Law and any similar statute). Default: 31.1 obtains the analysis before the facility funds a California-domiciled partner; no California advances until resolved **[UNVERIFIED]**.
