## §2 Credit scores and credit reports

**2.1 Eligible models — B3-5.1-01, General Requirements for Credit Scores (last updated April 22, 2026). VERIFIED.**
- Classic FICO versions: "Equifax Beacon® 5.0; Experian®/Fair Isaac Risk Model V2SM; and TransUnion FICO® Risk Score, Classic 04".
- VantageScore 4.0 from all three bureaus "eligible for use by approved lenders" (topic text as of 04/22/2026; LL-2026-06 extends to all sellers as of 09/09/2026 — the topic will be updated in a later Guide edition).
- FICO 10T (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) listed for future delivery; Credit Score Models page: "FICO® Score 10T is not yet available for loans delivered to Fannie Mae. We'll provide advance notice and implementation guidance before launch."
- Tri-merge: "The lender must request these FICO credit scores for each borrower from each of the Fannie Mae-approved score providers when they order the three in-file merged credit report." Credit Score Models page: "VantageScore 4.0 must be requested from each of the three credit bureaus; the same credit score model must be used for all borrowers on a single loan."
- **Minimum score.** Manually underwritten: "620 — fixed-rate loans" and "640 — ARMs" (representative score for one borrower; average median score for multiple borrowers). **DU: "A minimum credit score is not required for DU loan casefiles"**; "Credit scores are not an integral part of DU's risk assessment because DU performs its own analysis of the credit report data". Confirmed by the LLPA Matrix change log: "11/17/25 (Announcement SEL-2025-09): Removed reference to minimum required credit score of 620 for DU submissions."
- Exceptions exist for borrowers without traditional credit and HomeReady low-score cases (not extracted).

**2.2 Representative and loan-level score — B3-5.1-02, Determining the Credit Score for a Mortgage Loan (04/22/2026). VERIFIED.**
- Per borrower: three scores → "choose the middle score"; two → "choose the lower score"; one → that score.
- Multiple borrowers: "If there are multiple borrowers, determine the applicable credit score for each individual borrower and select the lowest applicable score from the group as the representative credit score for the loan."
- Use: "The representative credit score for the loan is determined based on the credit scores of each borrower and is used to determine loan eligibility for manually underwritten loans with only one borrower and for pricing purposes (i.e., assessing LLPAs) on all loans."
- Average median: "For certain manually underwritten transactions, the average median credit score is used in place of the representative credit score to determine whether the minimum credit score requirement is met." (Examples in the topic: "(605+693)/2 = 649", "(590+693)/2 = 642".)
- No score: "If there is a borrower who does not have a credit score, determine the representative credit score for the loan based on the credit scores of the other borrowers on the loan."
- LLPA Matrix (09.09.2026): "Credit score requirements are based on the 'representative' credit score for the mortgage loan as defined in the Selling Guide." and "Loans delivered without any credit score will be charged under the lowest credit score range shown in each of the applicable LLPA tables."
- *Build note:* pricing therefore keys off the **lowest** representative score across borrowers (not an average) for both DU and manual loans; DU eligibility no longer has a score floor; the DU engine's own credit-data analysis and the June 2026 "minimum credit risk standards" change (§3.1) are the actual gate.

**2.3 Credit report requirements — B3-5.2-01, Requirements for Credit Reports (11/05/2025). VERIFIED (partial extraction).**
- "Lenders are required to request a three in-file merged credit report from one of the credit information providers listed on Fannie Mae's website."
- "The report must list all inquiries that were made in the previous 90 days."
- "All data must be presented in a format that is easy to read and that is understandable without the need for code translations."
- Age (from B1-1-03, 04/02/2025): "Credit documents must be no more than four months old on the note date." Applies to credit reports and employment/income/asset documentation; "the most recent document is used to determine whether it meets the age requirement" for consecutive documents. DU-validated income: "lenders may rely on the age of tax transcript methodology provided by the service."
- Not extracted (re-fetch B3-5.2-01/-02 before citing): DU credit-report reissue/refresh mechanics, frozen-credit and fraud-alert handling. **UNVERIFIED.**

## §10 Surprises — what a 2024-based spec would get wrong
1. **No minimum credit score for DU loans** since SEL-2025-09 (11/05/2025); the LLPA grid's bottom row is "≤639". The 620 floor survives only for manual underwriting (640 for ARMs). Eligibility logic and adverse-action reason codes built around "620 minimum" are wrong for DU.
2. **VantageScore 4.0 is live for every seller as of Sept 9, 2026 (LL-2026-06)** — optional, tri-merge, SFC 067, DU-only, one model per loan, with its own LLPA grids. FICO 10T is approved but not deliverable. **Bi-merge has not happened.** The credit adapter needs `score_model` per loan and the pricing engine needs two grid families.
3. **DU 12.1 tightened its "minimum credit risk standards" on June 26–27, 2026** — fewer Approve/Eligible outcomes; a spec tuned to DU 11.x/12.0 approval rates is off.
4. **Chapter B3-3 was renumbered in 2026** (SEL-2026-02/-08): VVOE is B3-3.1-04, self-employment is B3-3.5, rental income is B3-3.8 (mandatory for applications on/after Nov 1, 2026). Citations to B3-3.1-07 / B3-3.2 / B3-3.4 are stale.

6. **Property insurance:** 1–4 unit policies no longer need roof replacement-cost coverage or RCV documentation (LL-2026-03, immediate); master-policy inflation guard retired; $50,000 per-unit master deductible cap and new HO-6 rules (mandatory July 1, 2026); servicer monitoring/annual reminder mandatory Jan 1, 2027.
7. **Post-closing QC is a 90-day cycle measured from the month of disbursement** (D1-3-01, 04/01/2026) with streamlined reverification (SEL-2026-03) — not the older "select within 30 days, review within 60 days" pairing.

9. **UAD 3.6 / redesigned URAR broad production ends Nov 2, 2026**, with DU messaging already citing that deadline; Form 1004/1073/1025-era templates and UAD 2.6 parsing are end-of-life inside the build window.
