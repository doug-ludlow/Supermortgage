### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Sources verified:** Fannie Mae D2-3.2-06 (04/08/2026; SVC-2026-03/LL-2026-01, SVC-2025-05, SVC-2024-06/LL-2024-02, SVC-2025-02), F-1-27 (08/13/2025), Fannie Mae Modification Interest Rate exhibit (current 6.625% effective Aug. 14, 2026; 6.750% effective Sept. 15, 2026), D2-3.1-02 (01/20/2021), A1-3-06, B-1-01 (09/11/2024), F-1-22 (10/11/2023), F-2-02 ($1,000), F-2-06, D2-1-01, E-3.4-01, C-1.1-02 (2.x), Form 3179 (revised SVC-2025-05, mandatory for agreements sent after Nov. 1, 2025 — 00a §6.4); 12 CFR 1024.41(c), (d), (e)(2)(ii), (g)(3); comment 41(d)-1.

**Eligibility (D2-3.2-06).** Conventional first lien (property may be vacant/condemned); the modified loan must be fixed-rate and fully amortizing (ARMs/interest-only convert; no biweekly or daily-simple-interest); originated **≥12 months** before evaluation; **≥60 days delinquent**, *or* current/<60 days delinquent and in **imminent default** per D2-1-01 (which requires a complete BRP, principal residence, reserves <$25,000 and a qualifying hardship); **not modified three or more times previously** (any program; payment deferrals do not count); no failed Flex Mod Trial Period Plan within **12 months**; no prior Flex Mod that became **60+ days delinquent within its first 12 months** without reinstatement; not subject to recourse/indemnification, an approved liquidation workout, an active repayment plan, a current retention offer or an active TPP. **Disaster (LL-2026-01/SVC-2026-03):** disaster-related hardship with insured loss/FEMA IA area/employment basis; loan current or <2 months delinquent at the disaster; at least 3 months delinquent at evaluation **[as summarized from D2-3.2-06 — PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; prior modifications do not disqualify; payments received after evaluation that reduce the delinquency do not affect eligibility if the loan is at least 30 days delinquent before the TPP begins. **Escrow:** revoke any escrow waiver and establish an escrow account before the trial (unless T&I are confirmed current); analyze the escrow account before offering the TPP; shortage spread over **60 months** unless the borrower prepays (B-1-01/F-1-27). MI: delegated insurers per F-2-06.

**Solicitation without a BRP (D2-3.2-06).** For loans 90+ days delinquent, solicit **between the 90th and 105th day of delinquency** if no complete BRP was received before day 90, or the borrower was found ineligible for any option, or rejected all alternatives; previously modified step-rate loans with a rate adjustment within 12 months: **days 60–75**; after forbearance expiry without QRPC and deferral-ineligible: **within 15 days**; after a repayment-plan failure without QRPC and deferral-ineligible: **by the 15th of the following month**; after a payment deferral, if 60+ days delinquent within 6 months of the deferral effective date without QRPC: **by day 75**. No solicitation if a foreclosure sale is scheduled within **60 days (judicial)** or **30 days (non-judicial)** of evaluation. Documents: "Flex Modification Solicitation Cover Letter" and the "Flex Modification Trial Period Plan Solicitation Offer — Not Based on an Evaluation of a BRP" Evaluation Notice.

**Trial Period Plan.** **4 months** if current or <31 days delinquent at evaluation; **3 months** if 31+ days. First trial payment due the first day of the month after the Evaluation Notice if sent **on or before the 15th**; the month after next if sent after the 15th. "If the borrower fails to make a Trial Period Plan payment by the last day of the month in which it is due, the borrower is considered to have failed the Trial Period Plan and the servicer must not grant the borrower a permanent Fannie Mae Flex Modification." Foreclosure must be suspended during a performing TPP (E-3.4-01). Late charges may be assessed during the trial but "the servicer must waive all late charges, penalties, stop payment fees, or similar charges" on conversion; no administrative fees. The modification is not binding until (1) the TPP is completed, (2) the borrower executes and returns Form 3179 and (3) the servicer or Fannie Mae executes and dates it. Trial payment = **estimated modified P&I plus escrow** (F-1-27). Trial payments are applied per C-1.1-02 (2.x).

**Terms — F-1-27 waterfall.** *Step 1 capitalize:* accrued interest; out-of-pocket escrow advances to third parties paid before the effective date; servicing advances paid to third parties in the ordinary course, not retained, paid before the effective date, where state law permits; any outstanding non-interest-bearing balance from a prior modification or deferral. **Excluded:** late charges (waived on completion) and projected escrow shortages; if state law prohibits capitalizing an item, collect it over ≤60 months unless prepaid. *Step 2 rate:* fixed-rate loans (and ARMs/step-rates already at their final rate) keep the contractual rate in effect for the payment due in the evaluation month; other ARMs/step-rates take the greater of the contractual rate and the **Fannie Mae Modification Interest Rate** (posted at singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/fannie-mae-modification-interest-rate; "the servicer must use the current … rate … when evaluating"), capped at the lifetime cap/final step rate. *Step 3:* if post-modification MTMLTV (gross UPB incl. capitalized arrearages ÷ current value) **≥50%**, reduce the rate in **0.125% increments** until the earlier of the 20% target or the Modification Interest Rate (a partial increment may land exactly on the floor). *Step 4:* extend the remaining term in monthly increments until the earlier of the target or **480 months from the modification effective date** (leasehold must run ≥5 years beyond the new maturity); the remaining term is first computed from the interest-bearing pre-modification UPB and the contractual P&I (SVC-2025-02). *Step 5:* if MTMLTV **>50%**, forbear principal equal to the **lesser of** the amount reaching the target, the amount producing a **50% MTMLTV on the interest-bearing UPB**, or **30% of gross post-modification UPB**; forborne principal is non-interest-bearing, due at the earliest of maturity, sale/transfer, refinance or payoff of the interest-bearing UPB. **Target:** the P&I reduction "must exceed but is as close as possible to 20% (for example, 20.01%)" of the pre-modification P&I. **Exhaustion:** if the steps run out, offer the resulting terms provided the new P&I is **less than** (current/<31 days delinquent) or **less than or equal to** (31+ days) the pre-modification P&I. Valuation: exterior BPO, appraisal, SMDU AVM, Freddie Mac AVM, third-party AVM, or a regulator-reviewed internal AVM, **not more than 90 days old at evaluation**, per the Interagency Appraisal and Evaluation Guidelines; an AVM without a reliable confidence score must be replaced by a BPO/appraisal. Buydown funds on deposit are applied to reduce capitalized arrearages (SVC-2025-05).

**Effective date, documents, recording (F-1-27, D2-3.2-06).** Effective the first day of the month after the TPP is completed, or — under a written policy — the first day of the second month after the final trial payment (processing month; no additional trial payment). Form 3179 (Loan Modification Agreement) with the eligibility cover letter; electronic documents, signatures and notarizations acceptable where compliant with Fannie Mae requirements **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Fannie Mae e-signature policy topic not re-verified]**; borrower signature required (documented exceptions, e.g., incapacity/deployment); the servicer must ensure the modified loan **retains first-lien position and is enforceable**; record when recordation is necessary for that purpose or when the modification includes an assignment of leases and rents; document custodian: recorded — certified copy within **25 days** of receipt from the borrower and the original within **5 business days** of receipt from the recorder; unrecorded — fully executed original within **25 days**. Post-modification: add capitalized amounts to UPB on the capitalization date (typically one month before the effective date), revise payment records, apply remaining buydown/borrower/third-party funds, and assess servicing-fee changes (A2-3-02). **MBS loans (D2-3.1-02, A1-3-06):** the agreement must be executed after the loan is removed from the MBS pool; a modification "does not become effective while it remains in an MBS pool"; reclassification follows A1-3-06 (SMDU TPP reporting triggers it; the servicer updates the Officer Signature Date after reclassification — F-1-22).

**Reporting and incentive (F-1-22, F-2-02).** Submit the TPP case with the valuation to SMDU; report loan-level data on receipt of the first and each subsequent trial payment; after all trial payments and any reclassification, update the Officer Signature Date to close the modification; loan-data changes (rate/payment/term/NIB) via the Investor Reporting Manual mechanics (5.x). Incentive **$1,000** if the modification closes within **two months of the last day of the month** in which the final trial payment is due (retention cap $1,000/loan).

**Reg X interplay.** A Flex Mod trial offer on a complete application is an offer under (c)(1) with the 14/7-day window; the borrower's first trial payment is acceptance (D2-2-05; (e)(2)(ii) reasonable time for other acceptance items); a TPP denial is a modification denial with (d) content and appeal rights (12.3); performing under a TPP is a (f)(2)(iii)/(g)(3) protection; trial failure ends it. Streamlined solicitations without a BRP are servicer-initiated (comment 41(c)(2)(i)-1; 12.2 open question 2).

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) LL-2024-02 is now Guide policy (SVC-2024-06; mandatory Dec. 1, 2024) and has since been clarified by SVC-2025-02 (remaining term), SVC-2025-05 (buydown funds; Form 3179) and SVC-2026-03/LL-2026-01 (disaster) — cite the current topics. (2) The cropped nuance is reconstructed above; note the exact ordering (rate reduction only when MTMLTV ≥50%; forbearance only when >50%) and the three-way "lesser of" cap. (3) The row omits the 3-vs-4-month trial rule, the 15th-of-month first-payment rule, the month-end trial failure rule, the solicitation windows, the MBS reclassification prerequisite, custodian/recording clocks and the incentive deadline. #### Business rules and calculations
1. **Inputs at evaluation (all `bigint` cents; rates in basis points; amortization with `decimal.js` precision 20; payments rounded half-up to cents):** IB UPB (ledger `principal`), accrued unpaid interest (interest portions of unpaid installments; for an amortizing loan with no principal paid, each month's interest = UPB × rate/12 rounded half-up), eligible escrow/servicing advances, prior NIB, late charges (excluded), buydown funds (reduce arrearages), valuation, contract rate, remaining term = months to amortize IB UPB at the contract rate with the contractual P&I (SVC-2025-02), pre-mod P&I, MIR in effect on the evaluation date.
2. **Target P&I** = the largest cent amount strictly below 80% of the pre-modification P&I (so the reduction "exceeds" 20% and is as close as possible to it).
3. **Waterfall** (deterministic module `flexmod.waterfall`, versioned `fnma.flexmod.2025-08`): Step 1 gross UPB; MTMLTV = gross UPB ÷ value (decimal, 4 places); Step 2 rate; Step 3 only if MTMLTV ≥ 50% *and* rate > MIR: iterate rate −12.5 bps, recompute P&I over the current term, stop when P&I ≤ target (then back-solve a partial increment only to land exactly on the MIR floor — never to land on the target); Step 4 extend term by 1 month per iteration up to 480 from the effective date, stop when P&I ≤ target; Step 5 if MTMLTV > 50%: forbearance = min(a, b, c) with a = gross − IB UPB that yields P&I = target at the Step-2/3 rate over the Step-4 term (solve, round IB UPB down to cents, verify P&I ≤ target, adjust by cents), b = gross − 50% × value, c = 30% × gross (rounded half-up); P&I recomputed on IB UPB; if the target is still not met, offer anyway if the P&I rule holds (exhaustion), else **ineligible** (reason `INV_FNMA_F127_PI_RULE`).
4. **Worked example ($250,000 / 6.5% / 8 months delinquent).** 30-year fixed, first payment 2021-11-01, P&I **$1,580.17**; LPI 2026-01-01; unpaid 2026-02-01…2026-09-01 (8 installments; `fnma_delinquency_status` = 8 months; `regx_days_delinquent` = 221 on 2026-09-09). IB UPB after 51 payments **$236,765.47**; monthly interest $236,765.47 × 0.065/12 = **$1,282.48**; accrued interest 8 × $1,282.48 = **$10,259.84**; escrow advances to third parties **$4,200.00**; servicing advances (inspections) **$180.00**; late charges 8 × $63.21 = $505.68 (excluded; waived at conversion); escrow shortage $1,860.00 (excluded; $31.00/month over 60 months); T&I $520.00/month; SMDU AVM (confidence reliable, as of 2026-09-02) **$290,000.00**; evaluation 2026-09-09; MIR **6.625%** (effective 2026-08-14). *Step 1:* gross post-mod UPB = 236,765.47 + 10,259.84 + 4,200.00 + 180.00 = **$251,405.31**; MTMLTV = 251,405.31 ÷ 290,000 = **86.69%**. *Step 2:* fixed-rate → **6.500%**. *Step 3:* MTMLTV ≥ 50% but 6.500% < MIR 6.625% → no reduction (rate cannot go below the floor; it is already below). Target P&I = largest cent < 0.80 × 1,580.17 = 1,264.136 → **$1,264.13** (reduction 20.0004%). *Step 4:* remaining term 309 months; extend to **480 months** from the effective date 2027-01-01 (maturity **2066-12-01**); P&I on $251,405.31 at 6.5%/480 = **$1,471.87** (reduction 6.85%) → target not met. *Step 5:* MTMLTV > 50% → (a) IB UPB for P&I $1,264.13 at 6.5%/480 = **$215,921.99** → forbear **$35,483.32**; (b) 251,405.31 − 145,000.00 = $106,405.31; (c) 0.30 × 251,405.31 = $75,421.59 → lesser = **$35,483.32**. **Result:** rate 6.500%, term 480, IB UPB $215,921.99, forborne principal (NIB) $35,483.32, P&I **$1,264.13** (−20.0004%), post-mod IB MTMLTV 74.46%. **Trial:** 8 months delinquent → 3 months; Evaluation Notice sent 2026-09-11 (≤15th) → trial payments due **2026-10-01, 2026-11-01, 2026-12-01**, each **$1,264.13 + $520.00 + $31.00 = $1,815.13**, each due by the last day of its month; modification effective **2027-01-01** (or 2027-02-01 with a January processing month under the written policy); capitalization date 2026-12-01; Form 3179 sent by 2026-12-01; incentive deadline 2027-02-28. At conversion the waterfall is re-run on the capitalization date with interest accrued through the trial and the trial payments applied per C-1.1-02 (the rate is locked at 6.500%; term/forbearance may shift by small amounts) — the Form 3179 figures come from the conversion run, and the difference from the evaluation-run figures is logged.
5. **ARM illustration of Step 3:** contract 7.250% ARM (not at final rate), MTMLTV 86.69%, MIR 6.625% → Step 2 sets max(7.250, 6.625) = 7.250%; Step 3 iterates 7.125 → 7.000 → 6.875 → 6.750 → 6.625 (floor) over the 309-month remaining term, testing the target at each step; then Step 4/5 as above.
6. **Trial payment application (2.x, C-1.1-02):** trial payments are held/applied as partial payments to the pre-modification loan per Fannie Mae's rule; a payment of at least the trial amount received by month-end = met; any shortfall = missed (no tolerance unless partner policy allows a de-minimis under C-1.1-02 — default none).
7. **Conversion:** on `servicer_executed` and the effective date: post capitalization (debit `principal`/`forborne_principal`; credit `interest_due`, `escrow_advances`, `corporate_advances`, prior NIB accounts), waive late charges/fees (contra), version `loan_terms`, set next due date = effective date, reset delinquency counters, establish the escrow payment incl. the 60-month shortage, report loan-data changes (5.x), claim the $1,000 incentive.
8. **Recording decision:** `recording_required = jurisdiction_rules.mod_recording(state, {term_extended, ib_upb_gt_original, forbearance_added, leasehold, assignment_of_rents})` **[UNVERIFIED state matrix — default: record when the term is extended beyond the original maturity or IB UPB exceeds the original principal, plus any state where counsel/title requires]**; notarization per state; RON permitted where allowed (SVC-2026-04 audit-trail rule).
9. **Denials:** any failed criterion or P&I-rule failure is a modification denial (12.2 reviewer + (d) notice + 12.3 appeal).

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `NTC_FNMA_D23206_TPP_OFFER` (Evaluation Notice, BRP-based; §1024.41(c)(1) content; NY grant-notice items): trial amounts/dates, the month-end rule, estimated modified terms (rate, term, forborne amount, P&I), that the modification is not binding until the three conditions are met, escrow establishment, late-charge waiver at conversion, acceptance by first payment, appeal rights for any denied option, SPOC/counselor blocks.
- `NTC_FNMA_D23206_SOLICIT_STREAMLINED` (cover letter + "TPP Solicitation Offer — Not Based on an Evaluation of a BRP"): same terms; incomplete-application disclosures where an application is open.
- `NTC_FNMA_D23206_TPP_REMINDER` (monthly), `NTC_FNMA_D23206_TPP_FAILED` (consequences; other options), `DOC_FNMA_FORM_3179` (+ cover letter, state riders, notarization page), `NTC_FNMA_D23206_MOD_COMPLETED` (new terms, NIB, first payment), Reg Z §1026.20(c)/(d)-style change notices are not required for a modification (7.x confirms), 7.x periodic statement shows new terms.
- **Events — 12.8 is the producer of modification completion, and it emits exactly two (both canonical; every downstream section consumes one of these and no other spelling).**
  - **`lossmit.modification.effective`** — fires when the **modification effective date arrives** (`modifications.terms.effective_date`, 00:05 loan tz), i.e. the instant the modified terms govern the loan. Preconditions: the trial (if any) is satisfied, the conversion waterfall has been re-run on the capitalization date, and the new `loan_terms` version is activated. Payload: `{loan_id, modification_id, effective_date, rate_bps, term_months, ib_upb_cents, forborne_cents, new_pi_cents, maturity_date, prior_terms_version, new_terms_version, mbs}`. It is the **ledger/servicing moment**: it drives re-amortization (2.6), the late-charge waiver at conversion (`FNMA_D23206_LC_WAIVE_ON_CONVERSION_0`), the ARM/payment-schedule rebuild (7.x), escrow re-analysis (3.x) and the credit-reporting change flag (8.x). It fires **even if the executed agreement has not yet come back** — Fannie Mae's effective date does not wait on the mail — so nothing that requires a returned or recorded document may key on it.
  - **`lossmit.modification.completed`** — fires when the modification is **fully done as a file**: the executed Form 3179 (or state equivalent) has been **returned by the borrower and executed by the servicer**, **recorded** where `recording_required` is true (recording evidence in hand, `recorded_at` set), delivered to the custodian where required, and the modification **booked** — i.e. `modifications.status='completed'` with `loan_terms` active, `borrower_executed_at`, `servicer_executed_at`, `recorded_at?` and `custodian_delivered_at?` all set and the case closed. Payload: the `…effective` payload plus `{completed_at, borrower_executed_at, servicer_executed_at, recorded_at?, custodian_delivered_at?, incentive_claim_id?}`. It is the **case-closure moment**: it is the milestone that starts **15.2's severity-1 60-day final expense-claim clock** (`FNMA_E501_EXPENSE_FINAL_60`) and the reclass trigger in 5.x, because both need the file complete, not merely the terms in force.
  - **Ordering:** `…effective` always fires first or simultaneously, never after. The gap is normally days to weeks (execution + recording) and is bounded by the Form 3179 timers above; a modification that goes effective but never completes is an exception queue item, not a silent stall — see Edge cases.
  - **Retired aliases (do not emit; accepted on ingest for one release, then removed):** `lossmit.modification.executed` (7.x) and `loan.modification.completed` (2.x) are aliases of **`lossmit.modification.effective`** — both were named for the terms taking effect, not the file closing.
- Records: `modifications`, `trial_plans`, `loan_terms` version, ledger postings, `smdu_cases`, executed/recorded documents with hashes, custodian receipts, investor events, incentive claim, `agent_decisions{kind=lossmit.flexmod}`, `foreclosure_holds{kind=fnma_trial_performing}`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `lossmit-underwriter` sub-role `modification`. Tools: `flexmod.waterfall` (deterministic), `valuation.order/get`, `mir.lookup(date)`, `escrow.analysis.run`, `smdu.case.submit/poll`, `trial.schedule.create`, `notice.render_send`, `form3179.generate`, `esign.send`, `erecording.submit`, `custodian.deliver`, `ledger.post_conversion`, `investor.loan_data_change`, `timers.*`, `attorney.instruct`.
- **Decision record:** `{modification_id, basis, eligibility[{criterion, value, pass}], valuation, mir_version, waterfall_inputs, waterfall_steps, terms, pi_rule, exhaustion_offer, trial{months, first_due, amount}, smdu_case_ids, rep_warrant_relief, recording_decision, notices, rationale}`; conversion run recorded as a second decision with the delta.
- **Guardrails:** the LLM never computes terms — it calls the waterfall; terms in notices must equal the waterfall/SMDU output; no TPP offer without a fresh valuation and escrow analysis; no permanent modification without the three binding conditions; no Form 3179 changes outside the template/riders; denials require `lossmit_reviewer`; solicitation blocked near a sale.
- **Escalations:** `signing_officer` (execute Form 3179 for the servicer/MERS; notarization), `fnma_portal_operator` (SMDU UI fallback; reclassification issues), `attorney` (foreclosure suspension; contested lien-position questions), `licensed_specialist` (states treating term discussions as licensed activity), `officer` (MIR feed confirmation; policy elections), `human_agent` on request.
- **AI-off:** human underwriters use the same waterfall/SMDU/templates.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **MIR changes between evaluation and conversion:** the evaluation-date MIR governs (rate locked at trial); log the version.
- **Valuation confidence missing:** order BPO; (c)(4) delay handling if on a complete application.
- **Borrower makes a partial trial payment:** month missed → failure (policy: no tolerance); funds handled per 2.x (`SM_TRIAL_FAILED_FUNDS_RESOLVE_30`).
- **Borrower pays all trial payments but does not return Form 3179:** (e)(2)(ii) reasonable time; processing-month re-dating; partner policy on trial extension **[open question 3]**.
- **MBS reclassification delayed:** execution waits; if the delay pushes past the effective date, re-date under the processing-month policy and re-run conversion.
- **Bankruptcy:** Chapter 13 modification may need court approval; Chapter 7 discharged borrowers may sign with "no personal liability" riders (E-2.x).
- **Successor in interest:** confirmed successor may assume and modify (D1-4.1-02; 4.4).
- **Texas 50(a)(6), leaseholds, condos with rents assignment:** riders/recording rules.
- **Trial failure then new hardship:** no new Flex Mod TPP for 12 months; deferral/liquidation paths.
- **Transfer-out mid-trial (17.4):** trial schedule, SMDU case ids and executed documents shipped; transferee must honor (CA §2924.11(e)); incentive to the closing servicer.
- **State law prohibits capitalizing interest:** collect over ≤60 months; waterfall variant flag.
- **Retro-corrections:** waterfall input error discovered post-conversion → re-run, amend via a new agreement, reversing entries; borrower notified; NoE handling.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Effective-date policy.** Default: first day of the month after the trial (no uniform cut-off); processing-month re-dating only when documents/reclassification slip — written policy signed by the partner.
2. **Recording matrix by state.** Default rule stated in business rule 8 pending counsel's 50-state matrix.
3. **Trial completed but Form 3179 unsigned.** Default: one processing-month extension with a reminder; then treat as not accepted (D2-3.2-06 binding-conditions rule) with a denial-style notice and appeal offer — counsel confirmation.
4. **Title updates before conversion.** Default: order a title update when IB UPB exceeds the original principal by >10% or recording is required (policy, cost-benefit).
5. **De-minimis trial shortfalls.** Default: none (strict).
