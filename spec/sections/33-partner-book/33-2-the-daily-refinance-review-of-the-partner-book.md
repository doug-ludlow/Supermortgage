# 33.2 — The daily refinance review: every monitored loan reviewed each day by the refinance analyst

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Section | 33 — The partner book: monitored loans, real accounts and refinance readiness |
| Automation class | a — the engine decides, the analyst explains, the offer goes out; no human starts it; the officer approves the partner's campaign once |
| Capacity | Lender (the partner's refinance program, 20.1 rule 6); Supermortgage runs the engine and the analyst on the partner's book |
| Trigger & frequency | Daily at 07:00 America/New_York after 20.1's 06:30 run, over every monitored loan; on every re-upload the next morning's run reads the new facts |
| Governing source | 20.1 rules 1–9 (the universe, the candidate, the benefit, the fire rule, the fair-lending extract, the investor-blind view, the selection allowlist); Reg B §1002.4 / §1002.5(b) (no prohibited-basis input); FCRA §604 and §615(d) (no report for selection; a firm offer only through 20.2's prescreen path); GLBA §1016.13; 32.11 (the borrower-facing surface); 18.1 (the analyst's model governance) |
| Key deadlines | The review by 07:30 ET each day; an offer delivered within 2 servicer business days of `offer_ready` (20.1's `SM_REFI_OFFER_SLA_2BD`); an offer expires at 30 days (`SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30`) |
| Timers | `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_REVIEW_DAILY` |

### Blueprint row
The daily refinance run already exists for serviced loans (20.1 through `refi-daily`): the day's rate sheet, the investor-blind universe, one opportunity row per loan per day with the candidate terms, the benefit and the fire rule, a decision row. Three things stop it from working the partner book: monitored loans are not in the universe and the run derives balances, delinquency and value from ledger, installments and origination rows a monitored loan does not have; a scheduled offer never reaches the homeowner because nothing plans or sends 20.2's touch, so the OfferCard waits for a status that never comes; and no one writes the review a homeowner or an examiner could read. This process puts the partner's facts into the universe every morning, adds the analyst who reads each loan's facts and writes the reason in plain words, and runs the two passes that were missing — offer delivery and expiry.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-14)
**The engine is 20.1's; the analyst never decides.** Candidacy is 20.1's fire rule over 20.1's universe row: at least 25 basis points off the rate, a positive 84-month NPV, a positive seven-year total-cost delta, the prescreen (LTV by occupancy, product, current), the state rule and no suppression (exclusions, cooldown, frequency cap, recapture window). The analyst's turn reads the engine's facts through tools and writes a rationale and flags; it cannot change the verdict, the rate or any figure (`ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES`). **[VERIFIED against src/domain/leads-pricing/ops-20-1.ts fireRule/loadUniverse and the 20.1 spec rule 5.]**

**Selection reads the allowlist and nothing else (20.1 rule 8; Reg B §1002.4).** The partner's tape carries name, ZIP, DTI, FICO, agency and remittance data; the universe row built from it carries only the allowlisted facts — the partner's value as `value_estimate` (source `partner_fmv` / `partner_bpo` / `partner_appraisal`, confidence by age: within 12 months `medium`, older `low`), the partner's FICO as `representative_score` with `score_source = partner_file` (pricing only), occupancy, property type, state, balances, rate, payment, remaining term, delinquency days from the tape's next due date, and the flags. DTI, ZIP, name, age and the investor columns never reach it (`assertInvestorBlind`, `PROHIBITED_SELECTION_FIELDS`). **[VERIFIED in source; the confidence policy for a partner value is this process's own default.]**

**No consumer report is obtained for selection (FCRA §604; 20.1 `NO_CREDIT_PULL`).** The review uses the partner's score as a fact of the partner's file; a report is ordered only after the homeowner's own Yes and authorization (32.11 §3, 33.3).

**An offer that reaches the homeowner is a 20.2 touch (CAN-SPAM; TCPA).** E-mail and the in-app card are the default channels; a text or an AI voice call needs the marketing consent 20.2 gates on. The campaign and the creative are approved by the officer once per program; each touch is scheduled through 20.2's gates and `marketing.touch.sent` turns the opportunity `offered`, which is what lets 32.11 place the OfferCard. **[VERIFIED against src/app/tools/section20-2.ts and flows/11-rate-watch.ts.]**

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) 20.1's run reads value from an AVM or the origination file; here the partner's value is the primary source with its own confidence policy. (2) 32.11 §1's "no proactive watching line" stands: the review is never announced in the thread; it is on the record's rate-watch block only as 32.11 renders it, and in the situation the turn reads so the assistant can answer a homeowner who asks. (3) 20.1 writes one decision per opportunity by the `intake` agent; this process adds the analyst's review row and decision beside it, never in place of it.

### Operational prerequisites
- A `partner_programs` row for the partner (33.1 registers it), the day's rate sheet (20.1's run publishes it from the FRED or FAKE feed), 20.4's LLPA matrix and a cost schedule for the property states in the book.
- The officer's one-time approval of the partner's refinance campaign and creative (20.2 `planChannels{approve_campaign}`, `renderCreative{approve}`); the FAKE officer approves them in nonprod (DELTA-30 pattern).
- The analyst's model: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in deploy through `@anthropic-ai/sdk`; the scripted client in every test; an `ai_systems{code=refi-analyst}` row and a version per prompt/model pair (18.1).

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- The sweep at or after 07:00 America/New_York once per calendar day (`review.run`): every `loans{status=monitored}` with a `partner_book_facts` row.
- `refi.opportunity.offer_ready` on a monitored loan with a party → `offer.deliver` on the same pass; `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30` breached → `offer.expire`.
- `partner_book.review.run_completed{as_of_date, reviewed, candidates, watching, not_now, excluded, analyst_turns, analyst_skipped, origination}` (global); `partner_book.review.written{loan_id, party_id, as_of_date, verdict, opportunity_id, origination}` per loan (loan-scoped).
- A homeowner's question about refinancing → the turn reads the situation's `partner_book.review` (32.16; no new command).

#### Data model
- **`partner_book_reviews`** (new; append-only): `id uuid pk`, `loan_id` → `loans`, `party_id` → `parties`, `as_of_date date`, `run_id text`, `opportunity_id text` (20.1's `opp-<loan>-<date>-<program>`), `verdict text` ∈ {candidate, watching, not_now, excluded}, `reasons jsonb` (codes from the engine: the fire rule's misses, the exclusion, the suppression), `facts jsonb` (the review's inputs as the engine saw them: the note rate and the day's candidate rate, the value with source and as-of, the LTV, the UPB, the remaining term, the current and candidate payments, the monthly delta, the NPV, the breakeven, the seven-year delta, the delinquency days, the flags), `analyst jsonb` (`{model_version, prompt_version, turn_id, rationale, flags[], confidence}` or `{skipped: reason}`), `decision_id uuid`, `created_at`. One row per loan per day.
- Baseline tables written: `entity_records` (`refi_universe` rows per monitored loan from the facts — `20.1 loadUniverse{op=load_row}`; `refi_opportunities`, `refi_trigger_runs`, `fair_lending_extracts` by 20.1's run), `agent_decisions` (`review.write` by `refi-analyst`; 20.1's own by `intake`), `agent_turns` (the analyst's turn, `channel = analyst`), `ai_systems` / `ai_system_versions` (`refi-analyst`), `marketing_campaigns` / `marketing_creatives` / `marketing_touches` (20.2), `loan_events`, `timers`. `v_refi_universe` includes `monitored` loans (33.1's migration); the run's universe builder takes a monitored loan's balance, next due date, payment history, foreclosure/bankruptcy/modification flags and value from the latest `partner_book_facts` row, never from ledger, installments or transfer rows.

#### State machine
Per loan per day (`partner_book_reviews.verdict`): the engine's opportunity status maps to `candidate` (offer_ready, offered, engaged, converted), `watching` (suppressed with reason `no_benefit` or a rate delta under the floor: the loan is current and eligible, the numbers are not there today), `not_now` (suppressed by cooldown, frequency cap, recapture window, marketing suppression, an implausible-value exception on the facts, or a stale value beyond 24 months), `excluded` (not current, bankruptcy, foreclosure, loss mitigation, transfer pending, not in the universe). Per opportunity (20.1's): `offer_ready —(offer.deliver: touch sent)→ offered —(32.11: the homeowner's Yes)→ engaged —(33.3 refi.open)→ converted`; `offer_ready | offered —(30 days)→ expired` by `offer.expire`. Terminal for the day: the review row. No human performs a transition; the officer's approvals are per program, not per loan.

#### Timers and gates
| Timer code | Kind | Trigger event | Anchor | Offset & unit | Satisfied by | Breach action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_REVIEW_DAILY` | recurring | `partner_book.review.run_completed` | `as_of_date` | +1 calendar_days | `partner_book.review.run_completed` | sev 3 → `compliance-sentinel` (the book was not reviewed today) |

Jurisdiction overrides: 20.1's borrower's-interest rule per state, unchanged.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **The facts into the universe.** For every monitored loan the pass builds 20.1's row from the latest facts: `upb_cents` = interest-bearing UPB (deferred UPB as `deferred_principal` on the terms, excluded from the candidate); `note_rate_pct` = the current rate; `pi_cents`; `next_due_date` and `regx_days_delinquent` = days from the tape's next due date to the as-of date when it is past, else 0 (the MBA status and the pay string corroborate: a `3`/`6`/`9` in the latest pay-string month or an MBA status other than current marks the loan delinquent); `remaining_term_months` from the maturity date; `value_estimate` = the newest of Current FMV and Most Recent BPO by their dates (source `partner_fmv` / `partner_bpo`), else the original appraised value (source `partner_appraisal`); confidence `medium` within 12 months of the as-of date, `low` beyond; `representative_score` = Current FICO (`score_source = partner_file`); `mi_status` = `bpmi_active` when the PMI flag is set, `mi_monthly_cents` from the PMI rate on the UPB when given; `bankruptcy_active`, `foreclosure_referred` (referral date or an active flag), `lossmit_plan_active` (modification in process), `transfer_out_pending` from the servicing status; `escrowed` and `escrow_monthly_cents` from T&I; `arm_first_adjustment_date` from the ARM fixed period; `product_code` `FRM<term>` or `ARM`. The row is loaded through `20.1 loadUniverse{op=load_row}` so 20.1's own investor-blind and prohibited-basis checks refuse a bad row before anything else reads it.
2. **The engine runs as it does today.** 20.1's scheduled run evaluates monitored and serviced loans alike under the partner's program; monitored loans count in `loans_in_universe`. The daily review then reads each loan's opportunity row of the day and writes the review (rule 3). A loan whose facts carry an `implausible_value` exception or whose value is older than 24 months is loaded with `refi_do_not_solicit` unset but marked `not_now` by this process and its opportunity is not delivered.
3. **The review.** Per loan per day one `partner_book_reviews` row: the verdict by the state-machine mapping; `reasons` = the engine's suppression reasons or, for a candidate, the fire rule's satisfied conditions; `facts` = the engine's inputs and outputs for the day (the figures are the engine's, copied); for `watching`, the rate the loan would need to see on the sheet to fire (the current rate minus 25 basis points, rounded down to the 0.125 grid) as `watch_rate_pct`. Then the analyst's turn (rule 4). `review.write` records the decision `{loan_id, as_of_date, verdict, opportunity_id, rule_set_version: sm.refi_trigger.v1 + partner_book.review.v1, model_version, prompt_version, confidence, rationale}`.
4. **The analyst.** One model turn per loan per day (`refi-analyst`, prompt `33.2-p1`, effort low, at most two tool calls): `review.facts` returns the day's review facts as tokens (`{{facts.rate_now}}`, `{{facts.candidate_rate}}`, `{{facts.monthly_delta}}`, `{{facts.value}}`, `{{facts.ltv}}`, `{{facts.days_delinquent}}`…) and the engine's verdict and reasons in words; `review.write` takes `{rationale, flags[]}` where `flags` ∈ {value_stale, value_low_confidence, arm_reset_within_12m, prepayment_penalty, recent_modification, pay_string_late, bankruptcy_or_foreclosure, high_ltv, tape_exception} and the rationale is one to three sentences in the homeowner's language that state the verdict's reason using only tokens for figures. The guard's provenance rule refuses a rationale with a digit or spelled-out amount outside a token; one regeneration, then the engine's own explanation text stands in and `analyst.skipped = provenance`. The turn is skipped (never the review) when the model is off, rate-limited, or the pass's cap (`PARTNER_BOOK_ANALYST_MAX_PER_DAY`, default 500) is reached — the review row carries `analyst.skipped`. Every turn writes an `agent_turns` row and counts under `ai_systems{refi-analyst}`.
5. **Offer delivery.** For each opportunity `offer_ready` today on a monitored loan whose party has an e-mail: the partner's campaign and creative (one per program; created by the pass under `planChannels{create_campaign}` / `renderCreative` when absent; approved by the officer — the FAKE officer in nonprod); a touch per party through `20.2 scheduleTouch` (channel `email`, the party's informational consent for the servicing relationship, the scrub and suppression facts, the sheet current) and `scheduleTouch{op=send}` with the 20.1 benefit disclosure payload and the party's name and e-mail; then `20.1 emitOfferReady{op=offered}` — 32.11's flow places the OfferCard and the rate-watch block reads `offer_open`. A party without an e-mail gets the portal channel only (the card). A text or an AI voice call is never scheduled without `consents{kind=tcpa_sms|tcpa_voice, purpose=marketing}`.
6. **Expiry.** Every breached `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30` on a monitored loan runs `20.1 emitOfferReady{op=expired}`; 32.11 closes the card; the next day's review reads the loan afresh under 20.1's cooldown and frequency-cap gates.
7. **What the homeowner hears.** Nothing unprompted. The situation the turn reads carries `partner_book.review {as_of_date, verdict, reasons_copy_keys, watch_rate_token?, offer_card_instance_id?}`; when the homeowner asks whether refinancing makes sense, the assistant answers from it in plain words (a candidate: the offer card is where the terms are; watching: the rate is not there yet and we check every morning; not now: the reason in the copy library's words — never a figure the model wrote, never a DU or credit word). The record's rate-watch block shows the current rate and the best rate on the day's sheet (32.11), and the offers list any open offer.

**Worked example A (loan 1 of the fixture on the first review, 2026-09-15).** Note rate 7.250%, UPB **$441,366.13** (the tape's interest-bearing balance; 23 of 360 payments made, 337 remaining), current P&I **$3,069.79**, T&I $612.50, value **$605,000.00** (Current FMV, 2026-08-31, source `partner_fmv`, confidence `medium`), LTV 441,366.13 ÷ 605,000.00 = 0.7295 (4 dp); FICO 748 for pricing only; current (next due 2026-10-01, pay string all zeros); no flags. The day's FAKE sheet prices the 30-year fixed candidate at 6.125%: rate delta 112.5 basis points; the engine's candidate loan amount, candidate P&I, monthly delta, NPV over 84 months and seven-year delta are the engine's figures, asserted by 33.2-T2 from the opportunity row, and the verdict is `candidate` with reasons `rate_delta`, `npv_positive`, `seven_year_delta_positive`, `prescreen`, `state_rule`. For loan 9 (5.875%, current), the same sheet gives no rate reduction: verdict `watching`, `watch_rate_pct` 5.625.

#### Integrations
- **`rate-feed`** (20.1's daily sheet: FRED or the FAKE grid); **`llm`** (`@anthropic-ai/sdk`, the analyst's turn; scripted client in tests; idempotency per loan-day; a 429 or an outage skips the turn, never the review).
- **20.1** `loadUniverse{op=load_row}`, `emitOfferReady{op=run|offered|expired}`, `writeDecision`; **20.2** `planChannels`, `renderCreative`, `scheduleTouch`; **32.11** unchanged.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Rows: `partner_book_reviews`, `refi_universe` rows, 20.1's opportunity/run/extract rows, `agent_decisions`, `agent_turns`, 20.2's campaign/creative/touch rows.
- Events: `partner_book.review.run_completed`, `partner_book.review.written`; 20.1's `refi.opportunity.*`; 20.2's `marketing.touch.*`.
- The examiner's daily report per partner: reviewed, candidates, watching, not now, excluded, offers delivered, expired, analyst turns and skips, the fair-lending extract id.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`refi-analyst` agent (tools: `review.run`, `review.facts`, `review.write`, `offer.deliver`, `offer.expire`). End-to-end: `review.run` is the daily pass — the facts into the universe, 20.1's run (as `intake`), one review per loan with the analyst's turn, then `offer.deliver` and `offer.expire`; `review.facts` and `review.write` are the model's two tools; the pass is idempotent per day (`run_id = review-<as_of>-<program>`) and reports once. Decision record schema `{loan_id, as_of_date, opportunity_id, verdict, reasons, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, confidence, rationale, flags}`. Guardrails: `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES` (the verdict, the rate and every figure are the engine's; `review.write` refuses a verdict or a number), `NO_CREDIT_PULL` and `NO_PRESCREEN` (never a consumer report for selection), `NO_INVESTOR_FIELDS` and `NO_PROHIBITED_BASIS` on the row (20.1's), `NO_UNCONSENTED_TEXT_OR_VOICE` (20.2's gates), `PROVENANCE` on the rationale (no figure outside a token). Escalations: `compliance-sentinel` on a missed day; `officer` for the campaign and creative approvals; `ops_analyst` when a loan's facts carry an implausible value.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- No sheet in force → 20.1's run is skipped and so is the review; the clock breaches at 07:00 tomorrow.
- A loan re-uploaded mid-day → the next morning's row reads the new facts; the day's review stands.
- A loan whose party has no e-mail and no activated session → a candidate with the card placed and nothing sent; the reminder path of 33.1 carries the invitation.
- The model refuses or times out → `analyst.skipped`, the engine's explanation stands, the review is written.
- The engine reuses the day's opportunity on a second pass → the review row is not duplicated (one per loan-day).
- A homeowner says "never" on the card → 20.1's marketing suppression; the review reads `not_now` with reason `marketing_suppression` every day after.

#### Audit and evidence
What an examiner is shown: per loan per day the review row (verdict, reasons, the engine's facts, the analyst's rationale and flags, the decision id), 20.1's opportunity row and decision, the touch and its notice, the turn's `agent_turns` row (model, prompt, tokens, guard result), the fair-lending extract of the run, the timer history of the daily clock, the campaign and creative approvals. Exported through the console's partner-book view (per loan) and the standard evidence pack.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should a partner value older than 12 months be `low` confidence (suppressing offers above 90% LTV) or block the review? **Default: `low` within 24 months, `not_now` beyond.**
2. Should the analyst run on every loan or only on candidates and watchers? **Default: every reviewed loan, capped per pass.**
3. May the partner's FICO price a proactive offer? **Default: yes for the benefit shown after the homeowner's Yes; counsel confirms the prescreen posture before a real book.**
