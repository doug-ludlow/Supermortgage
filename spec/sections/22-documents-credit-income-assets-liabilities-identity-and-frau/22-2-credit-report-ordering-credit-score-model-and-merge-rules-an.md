### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Credit-score models and eligibility — B3-5.1-01, General Requirements for Credit Scores (04/22/2026); LL-2026-06 (Sept. 9, 2026); Fannie Mae Credit Score Models page (fetched 2026-09-10). VERIFIED.** Eligible Classic FICO versions: "Equifax Beacon® 5.0; Experian®/Fair Isaac Risk Model V2SM; and TransUnion FICO® Risk Score, Classic 04." VantageScore 4.0 versions: "Equifax VantageScore 4.0, Experian VantageScore 4.0, TransUnion VantageScore 4.0." "The lender must request these FICO credit scores for each borrower from each of the Fannie Mae-approved score providers when they order the three in-file merged credit report." LL-2026-06 ("VantageScore® 4.0 Broad Lender Availability", to all sellers): "These changes are effective immediately. Lenders may begin originating and delivering eligible loans using VantageScore 4.0 as of the date of this Lender Letter."; "the same credit score model must be used for all borrowers on a single loan."; "For manually underwritten loans, lenders must continue to only use Classic FICO."; "For any loan originated using VantageScore 4.0, lenders must deliver Special Feature Code 067." Credit Score Models page: "VantageScore 4.0 must be requested from each of the three credit bureaus" on "a three in-file merged credit report"; "VantageScore 4.0 is not currently eligible for manually underwritten loans"; "FICO® Score 10T is not yet available for loans delivered to Fannie Mae." No bi-merge: the page and LL-2026-06 speak only of the three in-file merged report (00a-fnma §2.5: "bi-merge is not implemented"). **Minimum score:** "A minimum credit score is not required for DU loan casefiles."; "Credit scores are not an integral part of DU's risk assessment because DU performs its own analysis of the credit report data."; manual underwriting "620 — fixed-rate loans; 640 — ARMs" (representative score; average median for multiple borrowers); exceptions include "loans where no borrower has a credit score" and manually underwritten HomeReady loans with a low-score borrower. "Loan-level price adjustments (LLPAs) are assessed based on the representative credit score for the loan." Frozen credit: "If the borrower's credit information is frozen at one of the credit repositories for borrowers who have traditional credit, the credit report is still acceptable as long as credit data is available from two repositories, and the lender requested a three in-file merged report."; "Loans for borrowers with credit data frozen at two or more of the credit repositories will not be eligible whether underwritten manually or in DU."

**Representative score — B3-5.1-02, Determining the Credit Score for a Mortgage Loan (04/22/2026) (00a-fnma §2.2). VERIFIED.** Per borrower: three scores → "choose the middle score"; two → "choose the lower score"; one → that score. "If there are multiple borrowers, determine the applicable credit score for each individual borrower and select the lowest applicable score from the group as the representative credit score for the loan." It "is used to determine loan eligibility for manually underwritten loans with only one borrower and for pricing purposes (i.e., assessing LLPAs) on all loans." "If there is a borrower who does not have a credit score, determine the representative credit score for the loan based on the credit scores of the other borrowers on the loan." LLPA Matrix (09.09.2026): "Credit score requirements are based on the 'representative' credit score"; "Loans delivered without any credit score will be charged under the lowest credit score range shown"; lowest row "≤639"; separate Classic FICO and VantageScore 4.0 grid families (values not verified — 20.4/29.1 load them).

**Credit report requirements — B3-5.2-01, Requirements for Credit Reports (11/05/2025); B3-5.2-02, Types of Credit Reports (11/05/2025). VERIFIED.** "Lenders are required to request a three in-file merged credit report from one of the credit information providers listed on Fannie Mae's website." Content: "The report must include all discovered credit and legal information that is not considered obsolete under the Fair Credit Reporting Act"; "The report must be an original report, with no erasures, white-outs, or alterations"; "An automated credit report or one that is transmitted by fax is considered to be an 'original' report"; per debt: creditor name, opening date, highest credit, current status, required payment, unpaid balance, payment history — "The preferred format is '0 x 30, 0 x 60, 0 x 90 days' late" and "Statements such as 'current,' 'satisfactory,' or 'as agreed' are not satisfactory by themselves"; "The report must include all available public records information, identify the sources of the public records information"; "The report must generate an inquiry that will appear on future credit reports and must list all inquiries that were made in the previous 90 days"; "The credit report used by DU in the final loan casefile submission must be maintained in the mortgage loan file"; "The version of the credit report received by DU must meet the requirements in this topic and be one that supports trended credit data" ("expanded information on a borrower's credit history at a tradeline level on several monthly factors, including: amount owed, minimum payment, and payment made"). B3-5.2-02: an in-file/merged report "should include all information from three different credit repositories, or two repositories, if: that is the extent of the data available for the borrower, or the borrower's credit information is frozen at one credit repository"; "If the report lists accounts that were not checked with the creditor within 90 days of the date of the in-file report, the lender must obtain an updated credit report or a separate written verification for those accounts"; "The report cannot be provided by a credit reporting agency that is affiliated with the lender in any way"; RMCR: at least two national repositories for each locality in the last two years, with a certification. **Age — B1-1-03 (04/02/2025):** "Credit documents must be no more than four months old on the note date" (22.1). The Fannie Mae DU undisclosed-liabilities page uses the term "credit report expiration date" without defining it; the only age rule is B1-1-03's four months.

**Credit history — B3-5.3-09, DU Credit Report Analysis (08/05/2026); B3-5.3-07 (08/07/2019); B3-5.3-03 (07/25/2017); B3-5.3-04 (04/01/2009); B3-5.3-06 (10/30/2009). VERIFIED.** DU disputed tradelines: "DU will first assess the risk of the loan casefile using all tradelines, including those disputed. If DU issues an Approve recommendation using the disputed tradelines, no further documentation or action is necessary."; otherwise "DU will re-assess the risk without using the disputed tradelines" and the lender must investigate "whether the borrower is responsible for the accounts or if the account information is accurate or complete" — not responsible → document, eligible; responsible but adverse information disproved → document, eligible; responsible and accurate → "loan is not eligible for delivery as a DU loan"; "Tradelines reported as medical debt are not shown in the disputed tradeline message. Therefore, lenders are not required to investigate disputed medical tradelines." Collections/charge-offs: one-unit principal residence — "borrowers are not required to pay off outstanding collections or non-mortgage charge-offs—regardless of the amount"; 2–4 unit owner-occupied and second homes — "collections and non-mortgage charge-offs totaling more than $5,000 must be paid in full prior to or at closing"; investment — "individual collection and non-mortgage charge-off accounts equal to or greater than $250 and accounts that total more than $1,000 must be paid in full prior to or at closing." Judgments/liens: "Open judgments and all outstanding liens that are in the Public Records section of the credit report will be identified in the Underwriting Findings report, and must be paid off at or prior to closing." Mortgage delinquency: "If any borrower's credit report contains a mortgage tradeline that is 60 or more days past due when the account was last reported by the creditor and the account was reported within the 12 months prior to the credit report date, the loan casefile will receive an Ineligible recommendation"; excessive prior mortgage delinquency = "one or more 60-, 90-, 120-, or 150-day delinquency reported within the 12 months prior to the credit report date" (B3-5.3-03 uses the same definition for manual loans). Inquiries: "The lender should examine inquiries to determine whether they represent potential sources of undisclosed credit. If new debt was obtained, the lender may need to correct the loan application and resubmit it." (B3-5.3-04: "When the credit report indicates that recent inquiries took place, the lender must confirm that the borrower has not obtained any additional credit that is not reflected in the credit report or the mortgage application.") Waiting periods (DU): "Because DU does not have the disbursement date of the subject loan, DU uses the date of the credit report to measure whether or not the applicable waiting period has been met. However, because the credit report date may not result in an accurate calculation of the waiting period (it is earlier than the disbursement date), the lender may use the disbursement date to confirm that the waiting period has been met." Authorized users (DU): "DU takes credit report tradelines designated as authorized user tradelines into consideration as part of the DU credit risk assessment. No additional investigation of the borrower's credit history is required by the lender unless instructed by DU." (SEL-2026-06 clarification; B3-5.3-06's 12-month sole-payer documentation applies only to manual underwriting.) B3-5.3-07 waiting-period table (manual measurement "commences on the completion, discharge, or dismissal date ... and ends on the disbursement date of the new loan"): Chapter 7/11 — 4 years (2 with extenuating circumstances); Chapter 13 — 2 years from discharge, 4 years from dismissal (2 with extenuating circumstances for dismissal); multiple filings within 7 years — 5 years (3); foreclosure — 7 years (3, with maximum 90% LTV, purchase of a principal residence or limited cash-out refinance); deed-in-lieu, preforeclosure sale, mortgage charge-off — 4 years (2); "If a mortgage debt was discharged through a bankruptcy, the bankruptcy waiting periods may be applied if the lender obtains the appropriate documentation ... Otherwise, the greater of the applicable bankruptcy or foreclosure waiting periods must be applied."

**Undisclosed liabilities — B3-6-01, General Information on Liabilities (05/04/2022); A2-2-04 (04/01/2026); Fannie Mae DU "Undisclosed Liabilities" page. VERIFIED.** "If the borrower discloses, or the lender discovers, additional liabilities after the underwriting decision has been made, up to and concurrent with closing, the lender must recalculate the borrower's debt-to-income ratio."; "If a current liability appears on the credit report that is not shown on the loan application, the borrower should provide a reasonable explanation for the undisclosed debt."; "If the credit report does not contain a reference for each significant open debt shown on the loan application ... the lender must provide separate credit verification." **FCRA — 15 U.S.C. 1681b (current through Sept. 8/9, 2026); 1681c-1(i) (00a-fed §7). VERIFIED.** Permissible purpose §1681b(a)(3)(A): the user "intends to use the information in connection with a credit transaction involving the consumer on whom the information is to be furnished and involving the extension of credit to, or review or collection of an account of, the consumer"; (a)(3)(F): "legitimate business need ... in connection with a business transaction that is initiated by the consumer" (20.3 soft pulls). §1681b(f): a person may not use or obtain a report unless "(1) the consumer report is obtained for a purpose for which the consumer report is authorized to be furnished under this section; and (2) the purpose is certified in accordance with section 1681e of this title by a prospective user of the report through a general or specific certification." Security freeze (§605A(i), 15 U.S.C. 1681c-1(i)): a freeze "prohibits a consumer reporting agency from disclosing the contents of a consumer report ... to any person requesting the consumer report"; removal on consumer request within "1 hour" (toll-free telephone or secure electronic) or "3 business days" (mail); (i)(3)(E) temporary removal "for the period of time specified by the consumer"; when a third party seeks access for credit purposes and the consumer refuses to lift, the party "may treat the application as incomplete" ((i)(3)(D) as displayed); exception for a person with which the consumer "has or had prior to assignment an account or contract" for account review/collection. Consumer notice: a freeze "may delay, interfere with, or prohibit the timely approval of any subsequent request or application ... regarding a new loan, credit, mortgage." §605B (15 U.S.C. 1681c-2) is the identity-theft block ("a consumer reporting agency shall block the reporting of any information in the file of a consumer that the consumer identifies as information that resulted from an alleged identity theft, not later than 4 business days"). Score disclosures (§609(g) "Notice to the Home Loan Applicant", Reg V §1022.74(d) H-3; §615(a) adverse-action content with "a numerical credit score") are 21.3's/21.6's notices; this process supplies the score, range, date, key factors and CRA identity.

**Reg B §1002.6(b)(6) (CFPB eRegs, current). VERIFIED.** In considering credit history the creditor shall consider "(i) accounts designated for joint use or joint contractual liability; (ii) On request, applicant-presented information suggesting the creditor's historical data inaccurately reflects creditworthiness; (iii) On request, spouse or former spouse account history demonstrating applicant creditworthiness" (as displayed).

**Reg Z — credit report fee (eCFR §1026.19 current as of 9/08/2026; §1026.37 current as of 9/08/2026). VERIFIED (rule text) / PARTIALLY VERIFIED (commentary not read).** §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A): no fee before the LE is received and intent to proceed is indicated, except "A creditor or other person may impose a bona fide and reasonable fee for obtaining the consumer's credit report before the consumer has received the disclosures." The fee is disclosed under §1026.37(f)(2) "Services You Cannot Shop For" ("an itemization of each amount, and a subtotal of all such amounts, the consumer will pay for settlement services for which the consumer cannot shop"). Because §1026.19(e)(3)(ii)'s 10-percent category requires that the consumer be "permitted to shop," a credit-report fee falls under the (e)(3)(i) general rule — zero tolerance — by the rule text; comment 19(e)(3)(i)-1 was not readable today.

**HMDA (Reg C §1003.4(a)(15)) — 00a-fed §4.2. VERIFIED (data point exists).** The credit score relied on and the scoring model name are LAR data points; 28.3 consumes `credit_reports.representative_score`, the per-borrower score relied on, and `score_model`.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. **"Refresh ≤ 10 business days before closing" is not a rule.** No Fannie Mae or federal rule sets a credit-refresh window; the 10-business-day figure belongs to the VVOE (B3-3.1-04, 22.3). The binding facts are: the report must be ≤ 4 months old on the note date (B1-1-03); liabilities discovered "up to and concurrent with closing" must be re-underwritten (B3-6-01); relief for undisclosed non-mortgage debt needs closing "by the credit report expiration date" (A2-2-04). The refresh/UDM cadence below is SM policy.
2. **No minimum credit score for DU casefiles** (SEL-2025-09, B3-5.1-01); the 620/640 floors are manual-only and manual underwriting is out of scope (23.2-Q1). Eligibility and adverse-action logic must not encode a 620 floor.
3. **VantageScore 4.0 is live for all sellers as of Sept. 9, 2026** (LL-2026-06): optional, tri-merge, one model per loan, DU-only, SFC 067, own LLPA grids; **FICO 10T is not deliverable; bi-merge has not happened.** The row's "FHFA credit-score/merge policy status" resolves to: tri-merge only, two eligible model families.
4. **Security freezes are FCRA §605A(i)** (15 U.S.C. 1681c-1(i)); §605B is the identity-theft information block.
5. **B3-6-05 is "Monthly Debt Obligations"** (08/05/2026); the undisclosed-liability duties are in **B3-6-01 (05/04/2022)** and the relief in **A2-2-04 (04/01/2026)**.
6. **Authorized-user tradelines** need no lender investigation on DU casefiles (B3-5.3-09, SEL-2026-06); the manual 12-month sole-payer test (B3-5.3-06) does not apply.
7. **DU measures waiting periods from the credit report date**, and the lender may confirm with the disbursement date — the row's "credit history" item hides a date-basis rule that matters when a waiting period ends between the pull and disbursement.
8. The row omits the **90-day inquiry listing** and **trended data** report requirements, the **two-repository** acceptance rule for a single freeze, the ineligibility with two or more freezes, and the **medical-debt** carve-out from disputed-tradeline investigation.
9. The credit-report fee is the **only fee** collectable before intent to proceed and is **zero-tolerance** (rule text); the row does not mention the LE linkage.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `application.trid_received` (six items incl. SSN "to obtain a credit report") + borrower authorization + credit-report fee handling (21.2/21.4) → `orderCreditReport` (hard tri-merge).
- 20.3 soft pre-qualification report (`report_type = soft_prequal`, permissible purpose (a)(3)(F)) → never used for DU; used only for pre-qualification.
- `du.submitted` (23.1) → reissue reference (`credit_reference_number`, `du_credit_provider_code`) supplied to the DU submission.
- Reseller UDM alerts (webhook/poll) → `credit.udm.alert.received`.
- `closing.scheduled` / reschedule → freshness re-check (four-month rule) and pre-closing refresh scheduling.
- Borrower events: freeze-lift confirmation, dispute documentation, inquiry explanation (via 22.1 needs list).
- 22.5 liability changes and 23.1 resubmission tolerances (B3-2-10) consume this process's outputs.

#### Data model

`credit_reports` (baseline; extended here): `report_type text` ∈ {tri_merge_infile, rmcr, soft_prequal, soft_refresh, udm_snapshot}, `reseller text`, `du_credit_provider_code text null`, `credit_reference_number text null` (DU reissue key), `borrower_ids uuid[]` (joint report), `repositories_requested text[]` (efx, exp, tu), `repositories_returned text[]`, `frozen_repositories text[]`, `fraud_alerts jsonb` (initial/extended/active-duty per bureau), `score_model text` ∈ {classic_fico, vantagescore_4} (baseline column; enum fixed here), `scores jsonb` (per borrower: {efx, exp, tu} with model version and key factors), `borrower_applicable_scores jsonb` (middle/lower/single per B3-5.1-02), `representative_score int null`, `representative_score_borrower_id uuid null`, `no_score_borrowers uuid[]`, `key_factors jsonb` (up to four reason codes per borrower per bureau + inquiries flag for §615(a)), `inquiries_90d jsonb`, `disputed_tradelines jsonb`, `public_records jsonb`, `mortgage_tradelines jsonb`, `trended_data boolean`, `permissible_purpose text` ∈ {credit_transaction_604a3A, consumer_initiated_604a3F, account_review_604a3A}, `certification_ref text`, `pulled_at timestamptz` (baseline), `report_date date`, `expires_at date` (baseline: `pulled_at + 4 months`; computed per 22.1 R1 as `add_months(report_date, 4)`), `fee_cents int`, `fee_item_id uuid` (→ `fee_items`, `tolerance_class = zero`), `document_id uuid` (→ `documents{doc_class=credit_report}`), `supersedes_report_id uuid null`.

`applications.score_model text` (new column, owned here): set on the first hard pull from `credit.score_model_default`; immutable for the loan except by a full re-pull of every borrower under the other model before any DU submission relying on the new report (`credit.score.model.selected` event). Feeds 20.4 (grid family), 23.1 (DU submission), 29.3/29.4 (SFC 067), 28.3 (HMDA model name).

`credit_alerts` (new): `alert_id`, `application_id`, `borrower_id`, `source` ∈ {udm_vendor, refresh_compare, inquiry_review}, `alert_type` ∈ {new_tradeline, inquiry, secondary_reissue, bankruptcy, judgment, lien, collection, late_payment, balance_increase}, `payload jsonb`, `received_at`, `status` ∈ {open, explained, verified_new_debt, false_positive, resolved}, `resolution jsonb`, `dti_impact_cents`.

`credit_freeze_actions` (new): `action_id`, `application_id`, `borrower_id`, `repository`, `detected_at`, `borrower_notified_at`, `lift_requested_by_borrower_at`, `lift_window_start/end` (temporary removal period), `re_pull_at`, `status` ∈ {open, lifted, re_pulled, borrower_declined, ineligible_two_or_more}.

`inquiry_explanations` (new): `inquiry_id`, `application_id`, `borrower_id`, `creditor_name`, `inquiry_date`, `explanation text`, `new_credit_obtained boolean`, `new_liability_id uuid null` (→ `application_liabilities`, 22.5), `explained_at`, `evidence_document_id`.

Events: `credit.report.ordered`, `credit.report.received` (baseline), `credit.report.superseded`, `credit.report.expired`, `credit.score.model.selected`, `credit.representative_score.computed`, `credit.freeze.detected`, `credit.freeze.lifted`, `credit.fraud_alert.detected`, `credit.dispute.detected`, `credit.dispute.resolved`, `credit.inquiry.explained`, `credit.udm.alert.received`, `credit.udm.alert.resolved`, `credit.refresh.received`, `credit.undisclosed_debt.found`.

Retention: `credit_report` documents carry `regb_25m` then `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` (22.1 R9); FCRA use limitation flag `pii_flags ⊇ {consumer_report}` blocks reuse outside the transaction (20.1 must not read origination reports for marketing).

#### State machine

Credit report: `ordered` → `received` → `parsed` → (`usable` | `freeze_blocked` | `two_repository` | `no_score` | `error`) → `du_reissued` → `relied_upon` (final DU submission) → (`superseded` | `expired`). Guards: `usable` requires three repositories or two with exactly one frozen (B3-5.1-01/B3-5.2-02); `freeze_blocked` (two or more frozen) blocks DU submission and starts the freeze workflow; `no_score` routes to the nontraditional path (23.2) and cash-flow assessment (12-month asset report, 22.4); `expired` when `expires_at < scheduled_note_date`.
Undisclosed-debt loop: `monitoring_active` (from `received` to `closing.consummated`) with alerts `open` → `explained`/`verified_new_debt`/`false_positive` → `resolved`; `verified_new_debt` triggers 22.5 liability update and 23.1 tolerance check.
Freeze action: `open` → (`lifted` → `re_pulled`) | `borrower_declined` (→ 21.6 incompleteness path) | `ineligible_two_or_more` (until lifted).

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — Ordering.** Order a three in-file merged report (`repositories_requested = {efx, exp, tu}`) for every borrower whose credit will be considered, joint where spouses/co-borrowers apply jointly (Reg B §1002.7(d) joint intent evidenced at application, 21.1), with score requests for the loan's `score_model` from all three bureaus. Requesting `vantagescore_4` requires `applications.score_model = vantagescore_4` for every borrower on the loan (LL-2026-06). The order carries the partner's subscriber code and the certification reference; SM's operator id is recorded as the ordering agent. No hard pull before the six TRID items exist and the borrower has authorized the pull; the credit-report fee may be charged before the LE (§1026.19(e)(2)(i)(B)); the fee is disclosed under "Services You Cannot Shop For" with `tolerance_class = zero`.

**R2 — Applicable and representative score (B3-5.1-02).** Per borrower: `applicable = median(three) | min(two) | the one`; loan: `representative = min(applicable over borrowers with a score)`; if no borrower has a score → `representative_score = null` and pricing uses the lowest LLPA band. Worked example (refinance fixture, two borrowers, Classic FICO): Borrower A 742 / 751 / 760 → 751; Borrower B 698 / 712 / 705 → median 705; representative = 705 → LLPA band 700–719 for the Classic FICO LCOR grid (20.4 loads the value). If Borrower B's Experian file is frozen and the report returns only 698 (EFX) and 705 (TU) → `min = 698` → band 680–699 — a different price, which is why the freeze workflow runs before the lock is priced as final (21.4). With VantageScore 4.0 on the same loan, the same arithmetic applies to the VantageScore 4.0 triples and the VantageScore grid family (SFC 067).

**R3 — Freshness.** `expires_at = add_months(report_date, 4)`. Refinance fixture: pulled Mon Oct 5, 2026 → expires Fri Feb 5, 2027; note date Fri Nov 6, 2026 → open. Purchase fixture: pulled Mon Oct 19, 2026 → expires Fri Feb 19, 2027; closing Wed Nov 18, 2026 → open. A refinance whose closing slips to Mon Feb 8, 2027 breaches: `SM_CREDIT_EXPIRY_WARN_21` fires Fri Jan 15, 2027; the re-pull is scheduled for ≤ Fri Jan 29, 2027 (10 days before the new note date), DU is resubmitted and, if the representative band changed, 20.4 re-prices before the revised LE/CD.

**R4 — Freeze handling (B3-5.1-01; §605A(i)).** `frozen_count = |frozen_repositories|` per borrower. `0` → normal. `1` → report acceptable if the other two repositories returned data and a tri-merge was requested; representative score uses `min(two)`; the agent tells the borrower that lifting the freeze may change the price (never that it will improve it) and offers a re-pull after a lift. `≥ 2` → ineligible until lifted; the agent sends the borrower the bureau lift instructions (1-hour electronic removal; temporary removal "for the period of time specified by the consumer" — the agent suggests a window covering the re-pull day plus 7 days to allow DU reissue), records `lift_window_start/end`, re-pulls inside the window, and re-runs R2/R3. If the borrower declines to lift, the application is treated as incomplete for the missing item (statute: the user "may treat the application as incomplete") → 21.6 NOIA. Worked example: freeze at EXP and TU detected Tue Oct 6, 2026 14:10; borrower lifts electronically Wed Oct 7 09:30 with a window Oct 7–14; re-pull Wed Oct 7 10:45 (three repositories) → `report_date` Oct 7, `expires_at` Sun Feb 7, 2027; the original Oct 5 report is `superseded` and DU is submitted with the Oct 7 reference.

**R5 — Fraud alerts and identity mismatches.** Initial/extended fraud alerts and active-duty alerts (FCRA §605A(a)–(c)) require the contact step before extending credit — the identity verification and contact are performed by 22.6 (`fraud-risk`) before DU submission; this process only flags (`credit.fraud_alert.detected`) and blocks `submitDu` until 22.6 clears. Name/SSN/address mismatches between the report header and the application route to 22.6 as well.

**R6 — Disputed tradelines, collections, public records, mortgage lates.** Parse DU messages (23.2 maps ids) and apply B3-5.3-09: (a) disputed tradeline message → open a needs-list item for the borrower's documentation and a written agent determination (responsible? accurate?); medical-debt disputes are exempt; (b) collections/charge-offs: one-unit principal residence → no payoff condition; 2–4 unit principal or second home → condition "pay in full prior to or at closing" when aggregate > $5,000 (500,000 cents); investment → individual ≥ $250 (25,000 cents) or aggregate > $1,000 (100,000 cents); (c) open judgments/liens in Public Records → PTF condition to pay at or before closing (and title clearance, 24.4); (d) mortgage tradeline 60+ days past due at last report within 12 months of the report date → DU Ineligible (out of scope for delivery) — flag early so 21.6 can act within Reg B's 30 days. Worked example (purchase fixture, second home): collections of $3,200.00 and $2,150.00 → aggregate 535,000 cents > 500,000 → PTF payoff condition; on a one-unit principal residence the same accounts require no payoff.

**R7 — Waiting periods.** For each derogatory event, compute `end = event_date + waiting_period` (calendar years) and test against both dates: DU tests `report_date`; the lender confirms with `scheduled_disbursement_date` when DU's test fails. Worked example: Chapter 7 discharged Nov 20, 2022 → standard 4 years → eligible on/after Fri Nov 20, 2026; report date Oct 5, 2026 fails DU's test; the refinance fixture's disbursement Thu Nov 12, 2026 also fails (8 days short); the purchase fixture's dry-state funding Thu Nov 19, 2026 fails by one day — the agent recommends rescheduling disbursement to Fri Nov 20, 2026 or later, and 23.1 resubmits with a report dated after Nov 20 only if a re-pull is otherwise needed (DU uses the report date, so a resubmission with the Oct 5 report still fails DU's test; the lender's disbursement-date confirmation is the documented basis). With extenuating circumstances (2 years, B3-5.3-08 documentation) → eligible from Nov 20, 2024.

**R8 — Inquiries (B3-5.3-04/-09).** Every inquiry in the last 90 days not matching the partner's own pull or a known DU reissue opens an `inquiry_explanations` item; borrower answers in the portal (structured: creditor, purpose, whether credit was obtained, terms). `new_credit_obtained = true` → 22.5 adds the liability with documentation and 23.1 checks tolerance (B3-2-10: DTI > 45% or +3 points → resubmit). Worked example: report Oct 5, 2026 shows an auto-lender inquiry Sept 12, 2026; borrower reports a $612.40 (61,240 cents) monthly auto loan. With qualifying income 1,350,000 cents ($13,500.00) and obligations 513,000 cents (38.0%), the new obligations are 574,240 cents → DTI 42.5% (+4.5 points) → ≥ 3 points, so 23.1 must resubmit under B3-2-10 even though 45% is not exceeded; a $150.00 (15,000 cents) payment instead → 528,000 / 1,350,000 = 39.1% (+1.1 points) → no resubmission required by tolerance, but the liability is still added to the file and the final DU submission reflects it (23.1 final-submission accuracy).

**R9 — Undisclosed-debt monitoring and refresh.** Monitoring runs from `report_date` through `closing.consummated`; each alert is triaged: `false_positive` (matches a known tradeline/reissue), `explained` (borrower explanation + evidence; no new obligation), `verified_new_debt` (→ 22.5/23.1). Pre-closing gate: a clean soft refresh or UDM snapshot dated ≤ 3 `business_days_creditor` before consummation. Refinance fixture: consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 → refresh no earlier than Tue Nov 3, 2026 (Nov 5, 4, 3 counting back three creditor business days; Wed Nov 11 Veterans Day is outside the window); the agent orders it Tue Nov 3 after the CD (delivered Mon Nov 2). Purchase fixture: closing Wed Nov 18 → refresh no earlier than Fri Nov 13 (Nov 17, 16, 13; Sat/Sun excluded). Rep-and-warrant relief for undisclosed non-mortgage debt (DU message 3941) is recorded in `rep_warrant_relief` (23.3) with the condition "close by the credit report expiration date" — Feb 5, 2027 for the refinance fixture — and the monitoring evidence is retained regardless.

**R10 — Score disclosures and HMDA feed.** On `credit.report.received` the process emits to 21.3 the per-borrower data for `NTC_FCRA_609G_CREDIT_SCORE` / `NTC_REGV_1022_74_RBP_EXCEPTION` (score, range, date, key factors, CRA name/contact — each borrower receives only their own scores, §1022.75(c)), and to 21.6 the §615(a) content when a denial relies on credit. 28.3 receives `score_model` and the score relied upon (representative score and per-applicant applicable scores).

**R11 — Model selection invariants.** `score_model` is set once per application; a DU casefile is created only after `applications.score_model` is set; the delivery pipeline (29.3) asserts `SFC 067 present iff score_model = vantagescore_4`; manual underwriting is out of scope, so no Classic-FICO fallback is needed, but `credit.bi_merge = false` blocks any two-repository *request* (a two-repository *result* is only acceptable under R4).

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `credit_reports` rows and the stored report documents (`doc_class = credit_report`; the report used in the final DU submission is flagged `relied_upon` and retained in the loan file per B3-5.2-01).
- `representative_score` and `score_model` to pricing, DU, delivery (SFC 067) and HMDA.
- Freeze/dispute/inquiry/UDM work items and their resolutions (needs-list items via 22.1: `NTC_SM_NEEDS_LIST` entries "lift security freeze", "dispute documentation", "inquiry explanation").
- Fee: `fee_items{type=credit_report, tolerance_class=zero}` posted at order (`origination_fees_receivable` if charged to the borrower per 21.4 policy; otherwise `third_party_costs` SM-borne per the term sheet).
- Conditions to 23.3 (collections payoff, judgments/liens, dispute resolution) and liabilities to 22.5.
- Reports: freeze incidence, UDM alert rates, re-pull rates, model mix (Classic FICO vs VantageScore 4.0) for the partner and 31.2 fair-lending monitoring.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`verification` agent (credit tools: `orderCreditReport`, `parseCreditReport`, `computeScores`, `detectFreezes`, `detectFraudAlerts`, `mapDuCreditMessages`, `openInquiryItems`, `triageUdmAlert`, `orderRefresh`, `scheduleRepull`, `emitScoreDisclosureData`, `writeDecision`, `escalate`). End-to-end: orders on `application.trid_received` once authorization and fee handling are satisfied; parses; computes applicable/representative scores; detects freezes/alerts/disputes; emits disclosure data; manages the borrower loop for freezes, disputes and inquiries through 22.1's needs list; runs monitoring; orders the pre-closing refresh; re-pulls before expiry. Decision record: `{report_id, order_inputs, permissible_purpose, certification_ref, repositories_returned, frozen, scores, applicable_scores, representative_score, model, du_messages_mapped, conditions_proposed, alerts_triaged, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails — never: pull without a recorded permissible purpose and borrower authorization; pull a hard report before the six items and the credit-report-fee handling; change `score_model` mid-loan without a full re-pull and a written decision; request a two-repository report; use a soft pre-qualification report for DU; advise the borrower on how to raise a score or which model to choose based on price (only factual statements: the loan's model, that a freeze must be lifted, that pricing is based on the representative score per Fannie Mae's rule); contact a creditor to "fix" a tradeline (the borrower disputes with the furnisher/CRA — the agent may give the statutory rights); reuse origination reports for marketing; disclose one borrower's score to another. Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` when credit findings drive a denial/counteroffer/NOIA (21.6, SLA 2 `business_days_creditor`) and when a dispute investigation concludes "responsible and accurate" (loan not deliverable as DU); `fraud-risk` handoff (22.6) on fraud alerts/identity mismatches; `human_agent` on request or after three unanswered freeze reminders. Borrower-facing: every credit-related message names the partner as the creditor and the CRA, discloses automation, and includes the FCRA dispute rights language supplied by 21.3/21.6 templates. Human path: ops console with manual order/re-pull, manual alert triage and manual condition proposals, all logged as human decisions.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Borrower with no score on any bureau → `no_score`; DU nontraditional path (23.2), 12-month asset report for cash-flow assessment (22.4), representative score null, lowest LLPA band; manual nontraditional rules do not apply (manual out of scope).
- Mixed: one borrower with scores, one without → representative from the scored borrower (B3-5.1-02); DU handles the no-score borrower's cash-flow/rent history.
- Freeze at one bureau plus a thin file at another → only one repository with data → not acceptable (needs two) → treat as freeze workflow plus RMCR consideration (B3-5.2-02) — escalate to `underwriting_reviewer`.
- Report returned with a repository "no hit" (not frozen) → two-repository report acceptable only "if that is the extent of the data available"; the agent documents the no-hit response.
- Credit report affiliated with the lender → prohibited; the reseller list check is a startup assertion.
- Reseller outage at application → order retried; the LE clock (21.2) does not wait for credit; DU submission waits.
- Same-name/junior-senior file merges (mixed files) → identity mismatch → 22.6; the agent requests a corrected report from the reseller, never edits tradelines.
- Borrower obtains new credit after the final DU submission → B3-6-01 recalculation; if over tolerance → 23.1 resubmission and possibly a revised CD (25.2); rep-and-warrant relief for undisclosed non-mortgage debt still requires closing by `expires_at`.
- Report expires between CD and consummation because of a delay → re-pull, DU resubmit, re-price; if the representative band worsens, 21.4/21.5 handle the changed circumstance and a corrected CD (25.2) may need a new three-day wait if APR moves beyond §1026.22 tolerance.
- Waiting period satisfied by disbursement date but not by report date → documented lender confirmation (B3-5.3-09); if DU returns Ineligible on the derogatory event, no delivery path (manual out of scope) → 21.6.
- Bankruptcy discharge of a mortgage → bankruptcy waiting period if the discharge documentation is obtained; otherwise the greater of the two.
- Active-duty alert or extended fraud alert → 22.6 contact requirement before credit is extended; DU submission blocked until cleared.
- Borrower disputes the report *after* closing → furnisher/CRA process (servicing 8.x); nothing for origination.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **22.2-T1 (representative score).** Given Borrower A scores 742/751/760 and Borrower B 698/712/705, when computed, then applicable scores are 751 and 705 and `representative_score = 705` with `representative_score_borrower_id = B`.
- **22.2-T2 (single freeze).** Given Borrower B's Experian file is frozen and EFX 698 / TU 705 are returned on a requested tri-merge, when parsed, then the report is `usable`, B's applicable score is 698, `representative_score = 698`, and `credit.freeze.detected` is emitted with a borrower notice offering a lift and re-pull.
- **22.2-T3 (two freezes).** Given freezes at EXP and TU, when parsed, then the report is `freeze_blocked`, `submitDu` is refused, and after `credit.freeze.lifted` on Wed Oct 7, 2026 the re-pull supersedes the Oct 5 report with `expires_at = Feb 7, 2027`.
- **22.2-T4 (expiry gate).** Given `report_date` Oct 5, 2026 and scheduled note date Mon Feb 8, 2027, when `assertGateOpen('FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_REPORT_EXPIRY_4M')` runs, then it fails (expires Feb 5, 2027), `SM_CREDIT_EXPIRY_WARN_21` fired Jan 15, 2027, and a re-pull is scheduled no later than Jan 29, 2027.
- **22.2-T5 (model invariant).** Given `applications.score_model = vantagescore_4`, when an order for Borrower B requests Classic FICO codes, then the order is rejected before transmission; and at delivery `SFC 067` is asserted present.
- **22.2-T6 (collections by occupancy).** Given collections of $3,200.00 and $2,150.00, when occupancy is second home, then a PTF condition "pay $5,350.00 in full prior to or at closing" is proposed; when occupancy is one-unit principal residence, then no payoff condition is proposed.
- **22.2-T7 (waiting period date basis).** Given a Chapter 7 discharge Nov 20, 2022 and disbursement Nov 19, 2026, when evaluated, then the loan is not eligible; with disbursement Nov 20, 2026 the lender-confirmed basis passes while DU's report-date test (Oct 5, 2026) is recorded as failing with the written confirmation.
- **22.2-T8 (inquiry → new debt).** Given qualifying income 1,350,000 cents, obligations 513,000 cents, an inquiry dated Sept 12, 2026 and a borrower-reported new $612.40 monthly payment, when recorded, then `application_liabilities` gains the debt, DTI moves 38.0% → 42.5% (+4.5 points), and 23.1's tolerance check returns "resubmission required"; with a $150.00 payment DTI moves to 39.1% and the check returns "no resubmission required" while the final submission still includes the debt.
- **22.2-T9 (pre-closing refresh window).** Given consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026, when the refresh is dated Tue Nov 3, 2026 with all alerts resolved, then `SM_CREDIT_REFRESH_PRECLOSE_GATE` is open; dated Mon Nov 2, 2026 → not open.
- **22.2-T10 (UDM verified new debt).** Given a UDM alert "new tradeline" received Thu Oct 29, 2026 that the borrower confirms as a $410.00 installment, when triaged as `verified_new_debt`, then 22.5 recalculates DTI, 23.1 evaluates B3-2-10, and the relief record notes the debt was disclosed before closing.
- **22.2-T11 (credit-report fee).** Given the LE not yet delivered, when the credit-report fee is charged Mon Oct 5, 2026, then the charge is permitted (§1026.19(e)(2)(i)(B)) and the `fee_items` row has `tolerance_class = zero` under "Services You Cannot Shop For"; any other fee on that date is refused (21.4 gate).
- **22.2-T12 (disclosure data).** Given the report received Oct 5, 2026, when scores are computed, then 21.3 receives per-borrower payloads containing only that borrower's scores, key factors, range, date and the CRA identity.

#### Audit and evidence

Per report: order request/response XML, permissible purpose and certification reference, operator/agent identity, repositories returned, freeze/alert flags, parsed scores with model versions, applicable/representative computation, DU reissue reference and the DU submission numbers that relied on it, expiry computation with the note date used, supersession chain. Per application: freeze workflow log with borrower notices and lift windows, dispute investigations with documentation and the written determination, inquiry explanations, UDM alerts and resolutions, refresh reports and the gate result at consummation, score-disclosure payloads sent, HMDA feed values. Exportable for Fannie Mae QC (D1-3 reverification of credit — the report itself and, if requested, a QC re-pull), CFPB/state FCRA exams (permissible-purpose evidence per pull), and the partner's fair-lending monitoring (model mix and outcomes by group, 31.2).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Default score model.** Default `credit.score_model_default = classic_fico` at launch with VantageScore 4.0 enabled per partner by flag; evaluate a model-selection policy (which model prices better is loan-specific; a "best-of" pick per loan raises fair-lending and consistency questions) with 31.2 before allowing per-loan selection.
2. **Refresh cadence and product.** Default: UDM monitoring from pull to closing plus one soft refresh within 3 `business_days_creditor` before consummation; alternative: refresh only on UDM alert.
3. **Who pays the credit-report fee.** Default: charged to the borrower on the LE (zero tolerance) only if the partner's fee schedule says so; otherwise SM-borne under the term sheet (`third_party_costs`) and not disclosed as a borrower charge.
4. **Two-repository acceptance when the missing bureau is a no-hit rather than a freeze.** Default: accept only with the reseller's no-hit evidence; otherwise treat like a freeze and ask the borrower to check the bureau.
5. **Freeze-lift reminders cadence.** Default: +2 days, then every 3 days, NOIA evaluation at +10 (21.6).
6. **Retention of soft pre-qualification reports.** Default: 25 months under Reg B if an application follows; otherwise 12 months, then purge (FCRA use limitation).
