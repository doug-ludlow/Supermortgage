### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Bankruptcy Code.** *Automatic stay* — 11 U.S.C. §362(a) stays, among other things, "any act to obtain possession of property of the estate ... or to exercise control over property of the estate" (a)(3), "any act to create, perfect, or enforce any lien against property of the estate" (a)(4), the same as to property of the debtor for pre-petition claims (a)(5), and "any act to collect, assess, or recover a claim against the debtor that arose before the commencement of the case" (a)(6). Duration: acts against estate property are stayed until the property leaves the estate (c)(1); all other stays last until the case is closed, dismissed, or discharge is granted or denied (c)(2). *Repeat filers*: one prior case pending and dismissed within the preceding year → the stay "shall terminate with respect to the debtor on the 30th day after the filing of the later case" unless extended after a hearing completed before the 30 days expire (c)(3); two or more such cases → the stay "shall not go into effect upon the filing," and a party may move within 30 days to impose it (c)(4). *Relief*: for cause "including the lack of adequate protection" (d)(1); no equity and not necessary to an effective reorganization (d)(2); in rem relief where the filing was "part of a scheme to delay, hinder, or defraud creditors" involving a transfer or multiple filings, binding for 2 years if recorded (d)(4). *Hearing clocks*: the stay terminates 30 days after a request for relief unless the court continues it after a preliminary hearing (e)(1); in an individual Chapter 7/11/13 case it terminates **60 days** after the request unless a final decision is rendered or the parties/court extend (e)(2). §362(j): comfort order that the stay has terminated. §362(k): actual damages, costs, attorney's fees and, in appropriate circumstances, punitive damages for a willful violation. §342(g)(1)–(2): notice given other than per §342 "shall not be effective notice until such notice is brought to the attention of such creditor," and no monetary penalty may be imposed for stay violations before effective notice — which is why the designated §342(f) "preferred address" and the EBN registration below are load-bearing controls. *Co-debtor stay* — §1301(a): in Chapter 13 "a creditor may not act, or commence or continue any civil action, to collect all or any part of a consumer debt of the debtor from any individual that is liable on such debt with the debtor, or that secured such debt," until the case is closed, dismissed or converted; relief on the grounds in (c); a (c)(2) request (plan does not propose to pay the claim) is granted automatically 20 days after filing absent a written objection (d). *Chapter 13 plan* — §1322(b)(2): the plan may modify secured claims "other than a claim secured only by a security interest in real property that is the debtor's principal residence"; (b)(3) cure or waive any default; (b)(5) "notwithstanding paragraph (2)," the plan may "provide for the curing of any default within a reasonable time and maintenance of payments while the case is pending" on a claim whose last payment is due after the final plan payment; (c)(1) a default on the principal residence may be cured "until such residence is sold at a foreclosure sale that is conducted in accordance with applicable nonbankruptcy law"; (c)(2) a principal-residence mortgage whose last payment falls due before the final plan payment *may* be modified (short-term/balloon loans — cramdown exposure); (d) plan length 3 or 5 years, never more than 5; (e) the cure amount "shall be determined in accordance with the underlying agreement and applicable nonbankruptcy law" (interest on arrears only if the note/state law provide it). §1325(a)(5): each allowed secured claim is treated by (A) acceptance, (B) lien retention "until the earlier of the payment of the underlying debt determined under nonbankruptcy law; or discharge under section 1328," value distributed "not less than the allowed amount of such claim," periodic payments "in equal monthly amounts," or (C) surrender — this is the cramdown path for non-principal-residence collateral (investment properties, E-2.3-04) and §1322(c)(2) loans; the hanging paragraph after (a)(9) covers personal property only. §1328(a): the Chapter 13 discharge excepts "any debt provided for under section 1322(b)(5)" — a cured-and-maintained mortgage survives discharge; §1328(f) bars a discharge if a Chapter 7/11/12 discharge was received in a case filed within 4 years, or a Chapter 13 discharge within 2 years. *Chapter 7* — §521(a)(2): the statement of intention (retain/surrender; exempt/redeem/reaffirm) is due "within thirty days after the date of the filing of a petition ... or on or before the date of the meeting of creditors, whichever is earlier," and must be performed "within 30 days after the first date set for the meeting of creditors"; §521(a)(6) and §362(h) (automatic stay termination for unperformed intentions) apply to **personal property only** — a home mortgage debtor may "ride through" without reaffirming. §524(a)(2): the discharge "operates as an injunction against ... an act, to collect, recover or offset any such debt as a personal liability of the debtor"; §524(c): a reaffirmation agreement is enforceable only if made before discharge, with the (k) disclosures, filed with the court, with counsel's certification (or court approval if unrepresented), and the debtor may rescind "at any time prior to discharge or within sixty days after such agreement is filed with the court, whichever occurs later"; §524(j): a creditor holding "a security interest in real property that is the principal residence of the debtor" may act "in the ordinary course of business between the creditor and the debtor" to seek "periodic payments associated with a valid security interest in lieu of pursuit of in rem relief" — the statutory basis for post-discharge statements and payment acceptance. All statute text verified at law.cornell.edu on 2026-09-09.

**Bankruptcy Rules (restyled Dec. 1, 2024; 3002.1 amended Dec. 1, 2025; verified at law.cornell.edu 2026-09-09).** Rule 3001(a): a proof of claim must "substantially conform to Form 410"; (b) only the creditor or its agent may sign; (c)(1) a claim based on a writing must attach the writing or explain its loss; (c)(2) in an individual debtor's case (A) "an itemized statement of the principal amount and any interest, fees, expenses, or other charges incurred before the petition" claimed in addition to principal, (B) the amount necessary to cure any default as of the petition date where a security interest in the debtor's property is claimed, (C) for a claim secured by the debtor's principal residence, Form 410A and "an escrow-account statement, prepared as of the date the petition was filed," and (D) sanctions — the court may "preclude the holder from presenting the information" or "award other appropriate relief, including reasonable expenses and attorney's fees"; (d) evidence of perfection; (e) transferred claims — the transferor gets notice with "21 days after the notice is mailed to file an objection"; (f) a compliant claim is "prima facie evidence of the claim's validity and amount." Rule 3002(a): secured creditors must file ("a lien that secures a claim is not void solely because an entity failed to file a proof of claim"); (c): in voluntary Chapter 7, 12 and 13 cases the claim is due "70 days after the order for relief or entry of an order converting the case"; (c)(7) principal-residence mortgages: the claim with the Rule 3001(c)(2)(C) attachments (Form 410A and escrow statement) is timely if filed within **70 days** after the order for relief, and the Rule 3001(c)(1)/(d) attachments (the note/mortgage writing and perfection evidence) may be filed "as a supplement" within **120 days** after the order for relief **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the 70/120 split of which attachments is stated from the rule as recalled; the retrieved summary confirms the 70- and 120-day figures]**; (c)(6) the court may extend for insufficient notice. Chapter 11 claims follow Rule 3003(c)(3) (court-set bar date). Rule 4001(a): relief-from-stay motions are contested matters under Rule 9014; "an order granting a motion for relief from the automatic stay ... is stayed for 14 days after it is entered" unless the court orders otherwise (a)(3); (d) agreed orders/stipulations need notice with "14 days after the notice is mailed" to object. Rule 9006(a): periods in days "exclude the day of the event that triggers the period," "count every day, including intermediate Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays," include the last day unless it "is a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday," in which case "the period continues to run until the end of the next day that is not"; the "next day" is counted "forward when the period is measured after an event and backward when measured before an event" (a)(5); the last day ends at midnight in the court's time zone for electronic filing (a)(4); state holidays count only for forward periods (a)(6). Rule 9006(f): 3 days are added only for service by mail, leaving with the clerk, or consented means — **not** for electronic (CM/ECF) service. Rule 9037(a): filings may show only the last four digits of SSNs/TINs and financial-account numbers, birth year and a minor's initials (the POC and 410A must be redacted accordingly). Official Forms in force: **B 410** Proof of Claim, **B 410A** Mortgage Proof of Claim Attachment (rev. 12/23), **B 410S-1** Notice of Mortgage Payment Change (updated 12/25), **B 410S-2** Notice of Postpetition Mortgage Fees, Expenses, and Charges (12/16), **B 410C13-M1/M1R**, **B 410C13-N/NR**, **B 410C13-M2/M2R** (effective 12/1/2025), **B 113** Chapter 13 Plan, **B 427** reaffirmation cover sheet, **B 2400A/B ALT** reaffirmation agreement (uscourts.gov forms list, verified 2026-09-09).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) "FRBP 3002" is right but incomplete: the POC content rules are in Rule 3001(c)(2) and Form 410A, the 70/120-day split is Rule 3002(c)(7), and Chapter 11 uses Rule 3003. (2) "Per court claims-bar deadlines" understates a rule-fixed clock (70 days after the order for relief) that the platform can compute the day the petition is verified. (4) "PACER, counsel" omits EBN/BNC and monitoring vendors, DRA (attorney-entered), SMDU (cramdown/workouts), Property 360 (expense claims), and Form 20. (5) Automation class "c" is right only for the filing act itself — everything up to the signature is automated here. (6) "Sub coordinates": Supermortgage does far more than coordinate — it computes the claim, prepares every package and is the operational party; the partner holds liability.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- **Detection sources (all produce `bankruptcy.notice.received` with `source`):** (a) `ebn.notice.received` — BNC Electronic Bankruptcy Noticing e-mails with PDF + XML case data (§341 notices, plan/confirmation notices, discharge/dismissal orders); (b) `pcl.scrub.completed` — nightly PACER Case Locator batch party search (`POST /pcl-public-api/rest/parties/download`, `jurisdictionType:"bk"`, `role:["db"]`, `lastName` + `ssn4`/`ssn` per borrower, `dateFiledFrom` = last scrub date − 3 days) for every active borrower and co-borrower, plus a weekly full-portfolio re-scrub; (c) `bkvendor.alert.received` — monitoring-vendor case alerts/docket events; (d) borrower/attorney/trustee contact — `contact.*` with intent `bankruptcy_notice` (voice, chat, e-mail, letter, fax) captured by `borrower-comms`; (e) court mail scanned by the mailroom; (f) `foreclosure.attorney.event` (13.6) or DRA sweep showing a bankruptcy event; (g) `loan.boarded` with a transferor bankruptcy flag (1.1); (h) `payment.received` with `payer_type='trustee'` on a loan with no open case (an orphan trustee payment is itself a detection trigger).

#### State machine
Case (`bankruptcy_cases.status`): `notice_received` → `verifying` (PCL/vendor/EBN cross-check; ≤1 BD) → `active` (stay gates on; sub-state by chapter) | `unverified_hold` (no case found: gates on provisionally for 5 BD while counsel/borrower confirms; then `closed_no_case`) → chapter sub-states: **Ch. 7**: `active.pre_341` → `active.soi_pending` → `active.retain` | `active.surrender` (MFR path) | `active.reaffirm_pending` → `discharged` → `closed`; **Ch. 13/12**: `active.pre_confirmation` (POC, plan review, objections) → `active.confirmed` (`performing` | `postpetition_delinquent` → `referral_mfr` → `mfr_filed` → `relief_granted` | `agreed_order` → (`agreed_order_default` → `stay_terminated_stipulation`)) → `plan_completed` (trustee 410C13-N; response) → `discharged` → `closed`; **Ch. 11**: `active.pre_plan` → `active.plan_confirmed` (cramdown branch) → `closed`/`dismissed`. Cross-cutting transitions: `dismissed` (any state; stay ends; gates off after counsel confirmation; §362(c)(3)/(c)(4) flags set for the next filing), `converted` (new chapter sub-state; petition date retained; conversion date drives the 70-day claims clock per Rule 3002(c)), `stay_relief_granted` (foreclosure/collections gates off for the property; statements continue per 14.3), `post_sale_void_review` (E-2.3-06), `reopened` (comment 41(e)(5)-4 and stay resume), `transferred_out` (17.x with the E-2.1-05 court/trustee notice). Terminal: `closed`, `closed_no_case`, `dismissed` (until a new petition), `transferred_out`. Actors: `bankruptcy-ops` (all automated transitions), `attorney` (confirms legal effect of orders where ambiguous; every filing), `signing_officer` (signatures), docket events (external truth), Timer Engine (expiries), `officer` (repurchase/Form 20 decisions).

Stay-gate propagation (evaluated on every transition): `in_effect` → all gates on; `not_in_effect_362c4` → gates **off** but `contact_route=counsel_only` and `foreclosure_blocked=true` until counsel confirms in writing that no stay exists (E-2.1-03 requires counsel's express advice before continuing collection — the platform treats §362(c)(4) cases as "continue only on counsel's written advice"); `terminated_362c3` on day 31 absent an extension order → same counsel-confirmation rule; `relief_granted` → `foreclosure_blocked=false` after the Rule 4001(a)(3) 14-day stay of the order (unless waived), `collections_blocked` stays true for the debtor's personal liability while the case is open (in rem only); `ended_discharge` → discharge-injunction mode (14.3: informational communications only; no personal-liability demands; in rem enforcement allowed); `ended_dismissal` → all gates off, contractual status resumes (2.7 decides suspended late charges — default waived), foreclosure resumes per 13.x with the breach letter (E-2.2-01/04); `ended_closed` (Ch. 13 without discharge, e.g., hardship) → as dismissal unless the plan completed.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Detection, matching and verification.** A notice from any source creates `bankruptcy.notice.received` and applies gates **immediately** (E-2.1-03; §362(k)); verification runs in parallel, not before. Matching: PCL `parties/find` by `lastName` + `ssn4` (or full SSN with last name — the API rejects SSN-only queries), `dateFiledFrom` ≥ boarding date − 8 years; a hit is accepted when SSN4 + last name + (first name or property address in the case address) match, or when the case number from an EBN/mail notice resolves via `cases/find{caseNumberFull}`; the debtor's schedules (from EBN PDF, vendor coversheet or PACER docket purchase through counsel) are parsed for the creditor listing and treatment. A borrower-only assertion with no PCL/vendor hit within 1 BD is held for 5 BD (`unverified_hold`) with gates on; the agent asks for the case number through `borrower-comms` (informational script) and asks counsel to check the district. False positives (same name, different SSN) are rejected with a decision record. Joint cases set `joint_flag` and both filers; a non-filing co-obligor on a Chapter 13 consumer debt gets `codebtor_stay_applies=true`.
2. **Prior-filing analysis (E-2.1-02/E-2.3-01; §362(c)(3)–(4)).** Within 14 days of the petition the agent assembles `prior_cases` from PCL (`dateDismissedFrom` ≥ petition − 1 year), the vendor, and internal `cases`; classification: one case pending within the preceding year and dismissed → `one_prior_dismissed_1y` (stay ends day 30 unless extended; `USC_362C3_SERIAL_STAY_30`); two or more → `two_plus_prior_dismissed_1y` (no stay; counsel confirmation before any act); any pattern of filings around foreclosure sales or transfers of interests in the property → `abusive_suspected` → immediate referral marked "repeat filer"/"possible bankruptcy abuse" with a request that counsel evaluate §362(d)(4) in rem relief and dismissal with prejudice. Exception (c)(3)/(c)(4) applies "with respect to the debtor" — the co-debtor stay and the estate-property stay analysis are left to counsel; the platform keeps `foreclosure_blocked=true` until counsel's written confirmation.
3. **Referral decision (F-2-01 / E-2.2-0x).** Referral type and clock: Chapter 11 → full referral within 14 days of filing regardless of status, plus Form 20; Chapter 7/12/13 with `fnma_delinquency_status ≥ 60` at filing or an open foreclosure → full referral within 14 days of filing; < 60 days delinquent → `poc_only` referral (POC preparation and, for 12/13, plan review) sent immediately (policy: the POC clock runs from day 1 anyway), converting to full referral within 14 days of the loan reaching 60 days delinquent (contractual counter pre-confirmation; post-petition ledger after confirmation); cramdown/abuse → immediate. The referral package (E-1.1-02): note copy from the custodian (or original/lost-note affidavit — Form 2009 request to the custodian where the original is needed, 1.x), mortgage/assignments/MERS milestones, the case coversheet, prior-case list, `bankruptcy_ledger_views` snapshot, collection and foreclosure history, workout history, property value, escrow statement as of the petition date (3.x), the computed POC package (rule 5) if ready, and the requested actions (NOA, POC, plan review, MFR, objection). The firm's acknowledgment (2 BD) and document requests (3 BD) are timed; every request/response is a `case_event` with document hashes.
4. **Stay-gate matrix (what each engine does while `in_effect`).** Collections (11.x): no outbound collection contact to the debtor (§362(a)(6)); D2-2-02 attempt cadence suspended; QRPC suspended; `contact_route=counsel_only` where a debtor's attorney appears (Reg F §1006.6(b)(2) for FDCPA-covered loans; policy for all loans); inbound borrower calls answered with the informational script (14.3). Foreclosure (13.x): `foreclosure_blocked=true` — no referral, no first legal, no sale, no publication; scheduled sales cancelled through counsel; SCRA/other holds unaffected. Late charges/NSF (2.7): `bankruptcy_active` overlay — post-petition late charges accrue to memo only where `jurisdiction_rules.bankruptcy.postpetition_late_charges_permitted` and the plan/local rule allow (default off, 14.1-Q6); pre-petition late charges are claim items; NSF fees not assessed. Autodraft (2.3): paused unless the debtor's counsel or the plan authorizes continued drafting for post-petition payments (written authorization on file). Escrow (3.x): analyses continue; Chapter 13 → post-petition analysis mode (rule 6) and 14.2 gate; disbursements continue (lien protection). Insurance/preservation (9.x): continue; post-petition advances become 3002.1(c) exposure items. Loss mitigation (12.x): applications accepted and evaluated (Reg X §1024.41 has no bankruptcy exemption **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — 2016 rule preamble not retrieved]**); offers routed through counsel; court approval where required (E-2.1-09). Payoff/reinstatement (7.6/16.1): `reasonable_time_bk` path; figures include post-petition components; quotes to counsel. Statements (14.3); credit reporting (14.4); Fannie Mae reporting (rule 11).
5. **Proof of claim computation (Rule 3001(c)(2), Form 410A rev. 12/23).** All figures as of the petition date from the ledger, `bigint` cents, no rounding beyond posted cents. *Part 2 (total debt)*: `principal_balance` = interest-bearing UPB + deferred/forborne principal; `interest_due` = Σ scheduled interest of unpaid installments due on or before the petition date (installment loans — no per-diem accrual beyond the last due installment, 14.1-Q4); `fees_costs_due` = pre-petition late charges, NSF fees, inspection fees, attorney fees/costs incurred pre-petition (foreclosure fees actually incurred and recoverable under the note/state law), recording/title costs, non-escrow tax/insurance advances; `escrow_deficiency_for_funds_advanced` = max(0, −escrow balance at petition) (the amount the servicer advanced for escrow disbursements beyond the account balance); `funds_on_hand` = suspense/unapplied balance at petition; `total_debt` = principal + interest_due + fees − funds_on_hand + escrow_deficiency. *Part 3 (arrearage)*: `principal_due` = Σ scheduled principal of unpaid pre-petition installments; `interest_due` as above; `prepetition_fees_due`; `escrow_deficiency`; less `funds_on_hand`; `total_prepetition_arrearage`. The missed **escrow portions** of installments are not claimed as such (they are either embodied in the negative escrow balance or handled by the post-petition escrow analysis and a 14.2 notice) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Form 410A (12/23) has no "projected escrow shortage" line; the form instructions were not retrievable today; counsel confirms the district's practice]**. *Part 4*: current P&I + monthly escrow (post-petition analysis result if available, else current) + PMI (10.x) = total. *Part 5*: the loan payment history from the **first date of default** (the due date of the earliest unpaid installment that was never cured) through the petition date, generated from `ledger_entries` with the 17 columns A–Q (date, contractual payment amount, funds received, amount incurred, description, contractual due date, prin/int/esc past-due balance, amounts to principal/interest/escrow/fees, unapplied funds, and running principal/accrued-interest/escrow/fees/unapplied balances); every row references the `ledger_entries` ids so the history is provable. The escrow statement "prepared as of the date the petition was filed" is the 3.x analysis output with the petition-date balance. Rule 9037 redaction: last four digits of the loan number/SSN only; Rule 3001(c)(1)/(d) writings (note with allonges, recorded mortgage, assignments, MERS report) attached or filed as the 120-day supplement. Amendments: any correction (e.g., a pre-petition payment posted late with an effective date before the petition) produces a `poc_amendment` package within 5 BD.

6. **Pre-/post-petition ledgers and payment application (E-2.2-04; §1322(b)(5)).** At `bankruptcy.petition.filed` (ch. 12/13, and ch. 11 with a plan) the engine freezes the contractual receivable for installments due on or before the petition into `bk_prepetition_arrearage` (components = POC Part 3) and opens the post-petition schedule starting with the first installment due after the petition (fixture: 2026-10-01). Application rules: (a) **Trustee disbursements** (`payer_type='trustee'`, voucher parsed: case number, claim number, "arrearage"/"post-petition"/"conduit" designations): post to `bk_trustee_clearing`, then split per the voucher — arrearage amounts reduce `bk_prepetition_arrearage` in the order of the claim's components (principal & interest first, then fees, then escrow deficiency — policy 14.1-Q5; the contractual installments are marked "cured" FIFO as the P&I components are covered), conduit post-petition amounts apply to the oldest unpaid post-petition installment in contractual order (interest, principal, escrow — C-1.1-01); unlabelled trustee funds are applied per the confirmed plan's designation, else to post-petition first (conduit districts) or arrearage first (non-conduit), with the decision recorded. (b) **Debtor direct payments** post-petition apply to the oldest unpaid **post-petition** installment; they never touch the pre-petition arrearage unless the debtor's counsel directs it in writing or the plan pays arrears directly. A partial post-petition payment goes to `bk_postpetition_suspense` (`bankruptcy_hold`), released when a full installment accumulates; no 30-day return clock (6.5). (c) **Pre-petition-dated receipts** (effective date ≤ petition, e.g., lockbox items) reduce the arrearage and trigger a POC amendment if the claim was already filed. (d) **Chapter 7**: no plan, no separate ledger — payments apply contractually FIFO to the oldest unpaid installment; the loan's contractual delinquency continues to age. (e) **Post-petition fees** (inspection, attorney fees, advances) are memo items in `bk_postpetition_fees_memo` until noticed under 14.2 and allowed/unchallenged; only then do they become receivables. (f) **Three views** are maintained as projections: plan-terms (arrearage + post-petition), contract-terms (installments FIFO as if no bankruptcy — used for Fannie Mae reporting comparisons and dismissal reversion), and `fnma_scheduled_interest` (S/S remittance obligations, 5.x). (g) Cramdown: a confirmed §1325(a)(5)(B) treatment opens `bk_unsecured_cramdown` for the unsecured portion and re-amortizes the secured portion at the plan rate/term in the plan-terms view only — the note is never modified (E-2.3-03); receipts are split secured/unsecured per plan; A/A unsecured receipts remitted via CRS code 001 (5.x).

7. **Plan review and objections (E-2.1-06).** On `plan_filed`/`plan_amended` the agent parses the Form 113 (or local form) treatment of the claim and compares: arrearage proposed vs. Part 3 (tolerance $50 or 0.5% → otherwise objection); interest on arrears vs. note/state law; cure period vs. local rule/plan length (≤ 60 months); modification of rate/term/principal/maturity on a principal residence (§1322(b)(2) → object; §1322(c)(2)/investment property → cramdown path, rule 9); surrender (→ MFR/`bankruptcy.surrender`); "pay outside the plan" vs. conduit rules; omission of attorney fees; lien avoidance. It produces an **objection recommendation** with a decision record and the objection package (`CRT_OBJ_CONFIRMATION`; fee $700) for `attorney` **before** the objection deadline in the confirmation notice (docket-parsed; timer `SM_BK_PLAN_OBJECTION_DEADLINE` from the notice, warning at 70%). No-objection decisions are also recorded (the plan binds the creditor on confirmation, §1327 — a silent non-objection is a decision).
8. **Monitoring and the MFR decision engine.** Post-petition delinquency is measured on the post-petition ledger (pre-confirmation: contractual post-petition installments; post-confirmation: plan terms), in Fannie Mae's month buckets: `postpetition_fnma_bucket=60` when two post-petition installments are unpaid on the day after the second's due date. In conduit districts the agent confirms status with the trustee within 5 BD (E-2.2-04) before referral. The engine then scores the path: **MFR** (default when ≥ 2 post-petition installments unpaid and no cure/agreed order in place; Ch. 7 when the SOI is surrender or the debtor is delinquent and the property is not otherwise protected; investment property with no equity and not necessary to reorganization (§362(d)(2))), **motion to dismiss** (counsel's recommendation under local practice), **agreed order/stipulation** (debtor proposes a cure of the post-petition default over ≤ 6 months — accepted by policy when the cure ≤ 6 months and the debtor is otherwise performing; post-stipulation defaults follow the $125/$250 notice-of-default path), **adequate protection** (conduit district with confirmation > 45 days after the 341 meeting), **sequestration of rents** (investment property). The MFR package: the post-petition payment history declaration (`CRT_MFR_DECL`, from `bankruptcy_ledger_views`, signed by `signing_officer`), the note/mortgage, the POC, the plan, escrow statement, property value, and the recommended relief; it goes to `attorney` within the 14-day referral clock (fee $1,350 ch. 12/13; $1,225 ch. 7). Docket monitoring tracks §362(e) (30/60 days), the hearing, the order and the 14-day Rule 4001(a)(3) stay; relief → `bankruptcy.stay.relief_granted` (foreclosure resumes per 13.x; 3002.1 duties cease under Rule 3002.1(a) unless the court orders otherwise — the platform keeps filing payment-change notices while the case remains open, policy 14.2-Q2).
9. **Cramdowns, Chapter 11 and non-routine litigation (E-2.3-03; E-2.2-02; SVC-2025-06).** Any plan provision or motion that modifies rate, term, principal (§506 bifurcation), maturity or lien on a Fannie Mae loan is `bankruptcy.cramdown.requested`: within 1 BD the agent prepares the Form 20 package (case facts, plan terms, valuation comparison from an ordered valuation (12.x/SMDU valuation API), recourse/indemnification status from `loan_terms.recourse`, MBS/portfolio status, recommended strategy) → `attorney` review → `officer` submits to Fannie Mae Legal (F-4-02 contact) and, where the loan carries recourse/indemnification, the partner's `officer` decides the **voluntary repurchase** (A1-3-01) that must precede implementation. Fannie Mae directs strategy; the agent tracks the valuation hearing, appeal decision (5 BD counsel/Fannie Mae consult) and, on confirmation, (a) reports the cramdown terms through the **SMDU UI** (`human_portal_task` for `fnma_portal_operator` with the pre-/post-cramdown UPB, LPI, secured/unsecured split, rate, term, payment; the Cramdown FAQ warns not to book the treatment locally until Fannie Mae confirms booking, so the plan-terms view is held `pending_fnma_booking`), (b) opens `bk_unsecured_cramdown`, (c) never modifies the note or files a Form 3179, (d) routes A/A unsecured receipts to CRS code 001 and notifies ETM_DelMods@fanniemae.com (5.x), (e) sends the confirmed plan/order to the document custodian, (f) reports the modification in the next delinquency status transmission. Chapter 11 individual cases: full referral within 14 days, Form 20 within 1 BD, plan/disclosure-statement review, §1111(b) elections only with Fannie Mae approval. Late charges are never capitalized in a cramdown (FAQ Q2).
10. **Discharge, dismissal, closure, conversion.** *Chapter 13 discharge (§1328(a))*: the mortgage debt is not discharged when it was cured and maintained under §1322(b)(5) → `debt_discharged=false`; the case leaves bankruptcy mode after the 14.2 end-of-case procedure resolves (the trustee's 410C13-N and the response); if the response conceded a post-petition default, the residual is collected contractually after the case closes (with the 14.2 evidence). *Chapter 7 discharge*: `debt_discharged=true` unless reaffirmed → discharge-injunction mode for the life of the loan (communications per 14.3; credit reporting per 8.3 rule 3; foreclosure in rem only). *Dismissal*: gates off; suspended late charges resolved per 2.7 (default waived); `bk_prepetition_arrearage` is dissolved back into the contractual installments (the contract-terms view becomes the ledger of record — cured amounts reduce the oldest installments FIFO, exactly as they were applied); 13.x resumes with the breach letter (E-2.2-01/-04); `serial_filer_class` for a re-filing is pre-computed; 14.3/8.3 revert. *Closing without discharge* (e.g., failure to certify the §1328(g) course): treated as dismissal for collections after counsel confirms the stay ended (§362(c)(2)(A)). *Conversion 13 → 7*: the case record keeps the petition date; chapter and gates switch; a new 70-day claims window runs from the conversion order (Rule 3002(c)); the Chapter 13 ledgers are frozen and payments revert to contractual FIFO; a new referral type `conversion` goes to counsel. *Reopening*: gates re-applied on the docket event; comment 41(e)(5)-4 for statements.
11. **Fannie Mae reporting, DRA and expense hooks.** On verification the agent sets `fnma_status_code` (65 Ch. 7; 66 Ch. 11; 67 Ch. 13; 59 Ch. 12; 69 Ch. 13 post-petition after confirmation; 3L Ch. 7 asset case when the trustee administers assets; 3M when the plan/SOI surrenders) for 5.7's next transmission ("first delinquency status information ... after it learns," E-2.1-01) — including for loans that are current. DRA: milestones are entered by counsel; the agent reconciles the firm's milestone feed and the DRA read-only view daily against `bankruptcy_docket_events` and opens exceptions for missing/late entries (the servicer is accountable for the firm's delays, E-2.1-10). Expense reimbursement (15.2): every counsel invoice line is matched to the Allowable Bankruptcy Attorney Fees exhibit (11/12/2025) and the milestone; excess fees require the Excess Attorney Fee/Cost Guidelines approval; the final claim is filed within 60 days of the milestone (`FNMA_E5_01_BK_EXPENSE_CLAIM_60`); amounts recovered from the debtor through a 14.2 fee notice are netted against/refunded to Fannie Mae per F-1-05 **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — netting rule to be confirmed in 15.2]**. MBS: E-2.1-11 repurchase/reclassification eligibility (four consecutive missed payments or a term-modifying confirmed plan) is surfaced to 5.x/the partner's `officer` as a decision item — never automatic.
12. **Post-sale filings (E-2.3-06).** A petition dated on or before a completed foreclosure sale that is discovered afterwards → `bankruptcy.post_sale.identified`: gates on, `attorney` engaged immediately (the sale may be void as a stay violation), the Bankruptcy Notification Template (xlsx) completed and sent to SF CPM within 2 BD (with the REOgram status), the REOgram/P360 status updated per Fannie Mae's direction (15.1), and eviction/REO activity frozen.
13. **Servicing transfers.** Transfer-in with an open case (1.1): the case is created from the transferor's data, verified against PCL within 1 BD, counsel substituted (or retained) and the **E-2.1-05 notice to the court and the Chapter 12/13 trustee** filed (with a Rule 3001(e)-style transfer-of-claim/notice of servicing change as local practice requires) within 10 BD of the transfer date (policy). Transfer-out (17.x): the outbound file carries the case record, both ledgers, the POC/410A, all 3002.1 filings and timers; the transferee's notice duty is flagged.

Tools: `bk.case.read/write`, `pcl.search`, `ebn.inbox.read`, `bkvendor.*`, `docket.classify` (LLM), `ledger.snapshot`, `bk.claim.compute`, `bk.ledger.apply_trustee`, `bk.ledger.apply_postpetition`, `documents.render/search`, `timer.*`, `attorney.send_package/request`, `escalation.file`, `notice.send` (informational templates only), `smdu.package.prepare`, `p360.claim.prepare`, `fnma.form20.prepare`.
- **Decision record** (`agent_decisions`): `{case_id, loan_id, decision_type ∈ {verify, gate, prior_filing_class, referral, claim_compute, plan_review, objection, payment_application, mfr_path, agreed_order, cramdown_strategy, dismissal_close, post_sale}, inputs (event ids, ledger snapshot hash, docket document hashes), rule_set_version (frbp.2025-12, fnma.guide.2026-08-12, fnma.bk_fees.2025-11-12), model_version, prompt_version, rationale, confidence, outcome, human_review_reason[], reviewer_id?, package_document_ids[]}`.
- **Guardrails:** no court paper leaves without `attorney` acceptance and, where the form carries a declaration, a `signing_officer` signature; the agent may not release a stay gate without a docket order or counsel's written confirmation; money figures come only from `bk.claim.compute`/ledger services with a reproducible computation log; docket classifications below confidence 0.90 are human-verified before they change state (dismissal/discharge/relief always human-verified by counsel or `human_agent` against the PDF); no outbound collection communication to a debtor while gates are on (template allowlist enforced in `notice.send`); Form 20 and repurchase decisions require `officer`.
- **Escalations:** `attorney` — every filing, plan objection, MFR, agreed order, reaffirmation, cramdown strategy, ambiguous orders, post-sale filings, §362(c)(3)/(4) confirmations; `signing_officer` — Forms 410/410A/410S-1/410S-2/410C13-*, MFR declarations, reaffirmation execution; `fnma_portal_operator` — SMDU cramdown reporting, DRA reconciliation export; `officer` — Form 20 submission, voluntary repurchase, excess-fee approvals over policy limits, E-2.1-11 purchase/reclassification decisions; `lossmit_reviewer` — adverse workout decisions in bankruptcy (12.x); `human_agent` — on the debtor's or counsel's request; `licensed_specialist` — where a state treats negotiating modification terms as licensed activity (12.x). Each escalation carries the complete package, the decision record and the deadline timer.
- **Disclosure/consent:** debtor-facing voice/chat interactions start with the automation disclosure and the "informational only" bankruptcy script; no TCPA outbound campaigns to debtors while gates are on (payment reminders are collection communications and are suppressed).
- **AI-off path:** ops-console "Bankruptcy worklist" runs the same deterministic pipeline (detection queue, claim calculator, package builder, MFR scorer) with staff performing classification and decisions; timers unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Notice arrives after a foreclosure act** (sale, publication, filing) dated after the petition: treat as a potential stay violation — counsel same day; unwind per counsel; §342(g) evidence (when effective notice reached the preferred address) preserved for any §362(k) defense.
- **PACER/vendor outage:** EBN and inbound contacts still gate; nightly scrub resumes with a widened `dateFiledFrom`; exception report lists loans not scrubbed > 3 days.
- **Wrong-debtor match** (common surname): SSN4 + name + address rule; a mismatch discovered later reverses gates with a decision record and, if credit reporting was affected, an 8.3 AUD correction.
- **Joint debtors with different chapters / one spouse filing:** gates apply to the loan (the estate includes the residence interest); §1301 protects a non-filing co-obligor from collection for a consumer debt; statements per 14.3 (f)(4)/(4)-2.
- **Deceased debtor** during the case: counsel advises on continuation; 4.4 successor process runs in parallel; no collection.
- **Successor in interest in bankruptcy:** a confirmed successor who is the debtor gets modified statements (CFPB FAQ); claims are filed on the loan, not the person.
- **Trustee pays after dismissal / overpays the arrearage:** refund to the trustee (not the debtor) per voucher instructions; excess post-petition funds after case closure apply contractually.
- **Escrow deficiency claimed in the POC and then also spread in a post-petition analysis** (double recovery): the 3.2 post-petition analysis starts from a zero pre-petition balance when the deficiency is in the claim — cross-check rule `BK_ESCROW_NO_DOUBLE_COUNT`.
- **Claim objection / Rule 3001(c)(2)(D) motion:** package to counsel within 3 BD with the computation log and ledger tie-out; if a component cannot be substantiated, amend rather than litigate.
- **Chapter 13 plan pays the mortgage "outside the plan" while the debtor is delinquent** (no cure): counsel evaluates objection/MFR; statements show contractual amounts.
- **Post-petition ARM or escrow change before confirmation:** 14.2 applies from the petition date, not confirmation.
- **SCRA overlay:** SCRA protections continue (13.8/13.9); an MFR against a servicemember needs the SCRA affidavit path.
- **Disaster/forbearance during bankruptcy:** LL-2026-01 forbearance offered through counsel; plan modification (§1329) may be needed — counsel; trial-period plans allowed with court/trustee approval where required.
- **Transfer-out mid-case:** 17.x hand-off carries timers; the E-2.1-05 notice duty passes to the transferee; the last 3002.1 filing before transfer is a notice of the servicing change (local practice).
- **Retro-corrections:** a reversed pre-petition payment (NSF after the petition) re-opens the arrearage → POC amendment; a mis-posted trustee voucher is reversed by new entries, never edited.
- **Conversion, reopening, hardship discharge, §1329 modifications, motions to sell/refinance (payoff statements to counsel and the trustee), motions to incur debt:** each is a docket event type with a counsel task and, where money is involved, a 16.1 payoff/reinstatement figure.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **14.1-Q1 Creditor name on court papers.** Default: claims and notices filed in the name "Fannie Mae, c/o [Partner] as servicer, by Supermortgage as subservicer/authorized agent," consistent with counsel's limited retention agreement and the partner's resolution — confirm with counsel per district; alternative: partner as creditor of record.
2. **14.1-Q2 Monitoring stack.** Default: PCL API nightly scrub + EBN + one monitoring vendor (AACER or BankruptcyWatch) for docket alerts; alternative: vendor-only.
3. **14.1-Q3 `poc_only` referral for loans < 60 days delinquent.** Default: refer immediately (the 70-day clock does not wait); alternative: prepare the POC in-house and use a filing vendor with counsel review.
4. **14.1-Q4 Interest in the claim.** Default: scheduled interest of unpaid installments only (no per-diem accrual to the petition date on installment loans); confirm with counsel; state-law prejudgment interest excluded.
5. **14.1-Q5 Arrearage cure allocation order and partial post-petition applications.** Default: cure receipts to P&I components first, then fees, then escrow deficiency; post-petition shortfalls recorded against the escrow line rather than suspense when P&I is covered. Alternative: strict suspense until a full installment.
6. **14.1-Q6 Post-petition late charges.** Default: not assessed (memo only) unless `jurisdiction_rules.bankruptcy.postpetition_late_charges_permitted` and the plan/local rule allow, and then only via 14.2 notice.
7. **14.1-Q7 Agreed-order policy.** Default: accept post-petition cures ≤ 6 months without Fannie Mae approval (counsel's recommendation); longer cures → workout path (12.x) and Fannie Mae approval where required.
8. **14.1-Q8 Form 20 / Bankruptcy Notification Template channel.** Confirm the F-4-02 contact and submission method; default e-mail with `officer` sender for Form 20.
