### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Reg Z 12 CFR 1026.20(c) (eCFR current Sept. 3, 2026; CFPB interactive regulation verified today).**
- **Coverage (c)(1)(i):** an ARM is "a closed-end consumer credit transaction secured by the consumer's principal dwelling in which the annual percentage rate may increase after consummation." Comment 20(c)-1: "Creditors, assignees, and servicers that own either the applicable adjustable-rate mortgage or the applicable mortgage servicing rights or both are subject to the requirements" — the partner (MSR owner) and Supermortgage (acting servicer) both carry it. **Exemptions (c)(1)(ii):** "(A) ARMs with terms of one year or less; (B) the first interest rate adjustment to an ARM if the first payment at the adjusted level is due within 210 days after consummation and the new interest rate disclosed at consummation pursuant to § 1026.20(d) was not an estimate; or (C) ... when the servicer on the loan is subject to the [FDCPA] with regard to the loan and the consumer has sent a notification pursuant to FDCPA section 805(c)." Comment 20(c)-2: loss-mitigation modifications are exempt, but later adjustments under the modified contract are not; comment 20(c)-3: ARM-to-fixed conversions that change the rate and payment require the (c) notice.
- **Timing (c)(2):** "at least 60, but no more than 120, days before the first payment at the adjusted level is due"; "at least 25, but no more than 120, days ... for ARMs with uniformly scheduled interest rate adjustments occurring every 60 days or more frequently and for ARMs originated prior to January 10, 2015 in which the loan contract requires the adjusted interest rate and payment to be calculated based on the index figure available as of a date that is less than 45 days prior to the adjustment date"; "as soon as practicable, but not less than 25 days before the first payment at the adjusted level is due, for the first adjustment to an ARM if it occurs within 60 days of consummation and the new interest rate disclosed at consummation pursuant to § 1026.20(d) was an estimate." Comment 20(c)(2)-1: the servicer "must deliver the notice or place it in the mail within that timeframe, excluding any grace or courtesy periods."
- **Content (c)(2)(i)–(vii):** statement that the rate/payment is changing with the effective date and future adjustment schedule; a **table** of "the current and new interest rates; the current and new payments and the date the first new payment is due; for interest-only or negatively-amortizing payments, the amount of the current and new payment allocated to principal, interest, and taxes and insurance in escrow"; "the specific index or formula ... and a source of information about the index"; "the type and amount of any adjustment to the index, including any margin and an explanation that the margin is the addition of a certain number of percentage points to the index, and any application of previously foregone interest rate increases"; "any limits on the interest rate or payment increases at each interest rate adjustment and over the life of the loan ... including the extent to which such limits result in the creditor, assignee, or servicer foregoing any increase in the interest rate and the earliest date that such foregone interest rate increases may apply"; how the new payment is determined — index/formula, adjustments, "the loan balance expected on the date of the interest rate adjustment," "the length of the remaining loan term expected on the date of the interest rate adjustment and any change in the term"; interest-only/negative-amortization statements (and the fully amortizing payment when negative amortization results); prepayment-penalty circumstances, period and a statement that the consumer may contact the servicer for the maximum amount. **Format (c)(3):** a table "in the same order as, and with headings and format substantially similar to, forms H-4(D)(1) and (2)"; the rate/payment table nested inside the larger table. Comment 20(c)(3)(i)-1: forms may be modified for payment-option ARMs and similar circumstances.
- **Electronic delivery:** §1026.17(a)(1) — subpart C disclosures "may be provided to the consumer in electronic form, subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the E-Sign Act" (only §§1026.17(g), 1026.19(b) and 1026.24 are exempt) → E-SIGN-grade consent (7.4, class `arm_notices`) is mandatory for an electronic (c) notice.

**Fannie Mae Servicing Guide (Aug. 12, 2026 edition).**
- **C-2.1-01 (11/12/2014):** "Enforce each ARM loan according to the terms specifically described in its mortgage loan instrument"; "use its own funds to satisfy any shortage resulting from untimely interest rate or payment adjustments"; reimburse Fannie Mae for negative-amortization errors.
- **C-2.1-02 (08/13/2025, SVC-2025-05):** "The servicer of an ARM loan must notify the borrower before the effective date of any change to the mortgage loan interest rate or monthly payment in accordance with applicable law and the mortgage loan security instrument"; "All ARM notices must comply with the disclosure requirements set forth in the TILA and the individual mortgage loan documents"; "When the interest rate and the monthly payment change at different intervals, separate notices are required for each change"; **temporary buydowns:** "The servicer must send notification detailing the pending interest rate increase of a mortgage loan subject to a temporary interest rate buydown plan to the borrower 90 days prior to the payment change"; payment-cap/negative-amortization content items for legacy plans.
- **F-1-01, Servicing ARM Loans (12/20/2023):** new payment = "the amount required to repay the UPB of the ARM loan in substantially equal payments, over the remaining term of the mortgage loan, and at the new ... interest rate"; the payment "becomes effective in the month following the month in which the new ... interest rate goes into effect"; verification steps "use the correct index value applicable for each adjustment date," "apply any per-adjustment interest rate caps," "round the resulting rate as specified in the mortgage loan instrument," never exceed the lifetime cap; conversion timetable ("twenty-five days before the effective date of the new payment" borrower notice; Fannie Mae reporting by "the third business day of the month following the effective date for the new payment"); re-amortization method for errors.
- **C-2.2-01 (11/12/2014):** on a confirmed adjustment error the servicer must "make arrangements within 60 days to correct the error(s) in its and Fannie Mae's records," send an interim response if a borrower inquiry cannot be resolved "within 20 days," "notify the borrower about the effect of the correction," not report the correction "until it has discussed the specifics ... with its Fannie Mae Investor Reporting Representative," re-amortize "from the date of the first erroneous adjustment through the date the LPI was applied"; "If the net effect of correcting an adjustment error is an undercharge, it cannot be collected from the borrower, nor can the UPB of the mortgage loan be changed to offset it." **C-2.2-03 (11/12/2014) / F-1-01:** overcharges are refunded in cash or credited (reallocation or UPB reduction); credits permitted where the servicer advanced for an escrow overdraft, the loan is delinquent, or the error involves only a rate or only a payment change; combined rate-and-payment net overcharges over $1.00 → cash refund; current loans without advances get the borrower's choice (refund vs curtailment treatment).
- **Selling Guide B2-1.4-02 (12/10/2025) and the Standard ARM Plan Matrix (Oct. 2021):** "A Fannie Mae ARM plan must be tied to the Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR) Index" — "a 30-day average of the SOFR index as published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York"; "ARM instruments provide for each new interest accrual rate to be calculated by adding the mortgage margin to the most recent index figure available 45 days before the interest change date. Fannie Mae uniform instruments for all standard ARM plans provide for rounding to the nearest one-eighth"; "Lifetime interest rate change limitations apply to interest rate increases only. Per-adjustment interest rate change limitations apply to interest rate increases and decreases"; "Mortgage interest rates may never decrease to less than the ARM's margin, regardless of any downward interest rate cap"; maximum margin 300 bps; fallback language for an unavailable index. Plans: **4926** (3/6; initial cap ±2%, subsequent ±1%, lifetime +5%; Forms 3441/3141), **4927** (5/6; ±2/±1/+5; Forms 3442/3142; TX 50(a)(6) variants 3442.44/3142.44), **4928** (7/6; ±5/±1/+5), **4929** (10/6; ±5/±1/+5); all 30-day Average SOFR, 6-month adjustments, 45-day look-back, floor = margin, no conversion option. The Selling Guide adds: "Unless specific product terms provide otherwise, if the index plus gross margin equals a number that is equidistant between the higher and lower one-eighth percent, Fannie Mae rounds down to the nearest one-eighth percent" — stated in the fully-indexed-rate discussion; the uniform note says only "nearest one-eighth," so exact-midpoint handling is decision 2 below (default: round down, matching Fannie Mae's stated convention and favoring the borrower).
- **Investor reporting:** LAR 83 (rate/payment change) is due "prior to 8 PM eastern ... on the 5th Business Day after the Scheduled Interest Rate Calculation Date (Rate Effective Date minus the Look Back Period)" (Investor Reporting Manual, as verified in 5.1); under LL-2026-05 the rate/payment change becomes a servicing event (5.1 dual-rail). Lender pass-through rate = new note rate − servicing fee (− guaranty fee for MBS) recomputed per 5.2.
- **Legacy index transition:** 1-year USD LIBOR ARMs were transitioned under the Adjustable Interest Rate (LIBOR) Act and Regulation ZZ to the Board-selected replacement (12-month CME Term SOFR plus the statutory tenor spread adjustment, with a one-year consumer-loan phase-in) on the first change date after June 30, 2023; Fannie Mae's transition playbook governs servicer implementation **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — statutory framework known; Fannie Mae's specific spread/phase-in instructions not re-fetched today]**. The engine stores each loan's replacement-index history as effective-dated rows and the notice must describe the current index and source (1026.20(c)(2)(iii)).
- **Index source (verified live today):** New York Fed Markets Data API — `GET https://markets.newyorkfed.org/api/rates/secured/sofrai/last/{n}.json` and `.../sofrai/search.json?startDate=&endDate=` return `refRates[]` with `effectiveDate`, `type` "SOFRAI", `average30day`, `average90day`, `average180day`, `index`, `revisionIndicator`; five-decimal values; published each business day (no publication Sept. 5–7, 2026 — weekend and Labor Day). Values retrieved: 30-day average **3.64883** on 2026-09-09, 3.64817 on 2026-09-08, 3.64586 on 2026-09-04; 3.64381 on 2026-04-20.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** (1) the row cites only Reg Z; Fannie Mae C-2.1-02 requires a notice for *any* rate or payment change (including rate-only changes and temporary-buydown steps 90 days ahead), and C-2.2-01/F-1-01 impose a 60-day error-correction and 20-day interim-response regime; (2) the 25–120 day rule also covers pre-2015 ARMs with a look-back under 45 days, not only ≤60-day adjusters; (3) the FDCPA cease-communication exemption and the loss-mitigation modification exemption are missing; (4) "Systems: Core" understates the need for an index feed adapter and the LAR 83/servicing-event hand-off.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `loan.boarded` with `loan_terms.product = ARM` → build `arm_schedule` (all change dates to maturity) and schedule timers for the next change.
- `arm.index.captured` (daily feed) → when `effective_date = change_date − lookback_days` (or the most recent publication on/before it) → `arm.adjustment.calculated`.
- `arm.adjustment.verified` (independent second computation) → notice rendering.
- `loan_terms.version.activated` (from the adjustment on the change date) → 2.1 accrues at the new rate; 7.1 statements show the new rate/payment; 5.1 emits LAR 83/event on calculation.
- `arm.error.suspected` (borrower inquiry via 4.2, QC sample, index revision, boarding audit) → correction workflow.
- `buydown.step.scheduled` (temporary buydown schedule from 1.1) → 90-day Fannie Mae notice.
- `lossmit.modification.effective` (12.8; canonical — the retired spelling `lossmit.modification.executed` was an alias of this event, not of `…completed`) → new ARM schedule if the modified note remains adjustable (comment 20(c)-2); Flex Mods produce fixed rates, so this is rare.

#### State machine
`scheduled` → `index_pending` (T−45) → `calculated` → `verified` (dual computation match) | `discrepancy` (→ hold, ops review) → `notice_rendered` → `notice_sent` (within window) → `effective` (change date; `loan_terms` version activated) → `reported` (LAR 83/event accepted, 5.1) → `closed`. Side branches: `corrected` (C-2.2-01 workflow producing new versions and a correction notice); `exempt_fdcpa` (805(c) notice on file — Fannie Mae C-2.1-02 notice still sent as a non-collection informational notice? see decision 4). Actors: `disclosures` agent; `investor-reporting` (reported); `human_agent`/ops on discrepancy; `officer` on portfolio-wide index substitution.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Index selection:** `index_date = change_date − lookback_days`; `index_value` = the value with the latest `effective_date ≤ index_date` ("most recent index figure available 45 days before the interest change date"); revisions published later for that date are ignored once the adjustment is `verified` (note fixes the "available" figure), but a revision before verification re-triggers calculation.
2. **Rate:** `unrounded = index_value + margin` (decimal.js, 8 dp); `rounded = round_to_eighth(unrounded, rounding_rule)`; `periodic_limit = [prior_rate − periodic_cap, prior_rate + periodic_cap]` (initial cap on the first change date; caps apply both directions); `lifetime_limit = initial_note_rate + lifetime_cap` (increases only); `floor = margin`; `new_rate = max(floor, min(lifetime_limit, clamp(rounded, periodic_limit)))`; record which limit bound. No "foregone increase" carryover exists in current plans (`cap_test.foregone = 0`; comment 20(c)(2)(iv)-1 applies only to carryover loans — the notice omits the carryover sentence).
3. **Payment:** `expected_upb` = scheduled UPB after the payment due on the change date assuming all contractual payments through the change date are made (F-1-01: "UPB ... expected"; the note: "unpaid principal that I am expected to owe at the Change Date"); `remaining_term_months = months from first_new_payment_due to maturity inclusive`; `new_pi = round_half_up(UPB × r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n))`, `r = new_rate / 1200`; IO loans: `new_payment = round_half_up(UPB × r)` until IO expiry; payment-cap plans: apply the cap unless it perpetuates an error (F-1-01) and compute the negative-amortization amount for the C-2.1-02 items.
4. **Timing:** `first_new_payment_due` = first payment due date after the change date (payment effective "in the month following"); `notice_window = [first_new_payment_due − 120, first_new_payment_due − 60]`; for 25-day loans `[−120, −25]`. Because the index is fixed only at `change_date − 45`, the sendable window for a standard plan is `[change_date − 45, first_new_payment_due − 60]`, i.e., **about 16 days**; the engine targets send within 3 business days of verification.
5. **Content assembly:** current rate/payment from the active `loan_terms`; new values from `arm_adjustments`; escrow shown separately (the (c)(2)(ii) table shows P&I; escrow/total is shown as additional information outside the mandated table); index description and source ("30-day Average SOFR published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, newyorkfed.org"); margin explanation ("your margin of 2.750 percentage points is added to the index"); caps and whether a cap limited the increase; expected balance, remaining term; prepayment penalty ("none" for conforming loans); the "next adjustment" date (`change_date + adjustment_period_months`).
6. **Fannie Mae-only notice:** if `new_rate ≠ prior_rate` but `new_pi == prior_pi` (theoretically only under payment caps) or vice versa, send `NTC_FNMA_C2_1_02_RATE_CHANGE` before the effective date (policy: ≥ 25 days); if neither changes, no notice (feature `arm.no_change_courtesy_notice`, default off).
7. **Reporting:** on `verified`, emit `investor_events` type `rate_payment_change` {effective_with_payment_due MMYY, index_value, new_rate, pass_through_rate, new_payment} → 5.1 (LAR 83 by BD5 after the calculation date, or the servicing event when live).
8. **Error correction (C-2.2-01/F-1-01):** re-amortize from the first erroneous change date using the correct rate at each change date and the actual payments made; `net_effect = reamortized_UPB − actual_UPB` (positive = overcharge); overcharge > $1.00 combined → cash refund (borrower may elect curtailment treatment when current with no advances); rate-only or payment-only errors, delinquent loans, or escrow-advance situations → credit by reallocation/UPB reduction; undercharge → absorbed (never collected, UPB unchanged; servicer books the shortfall to `servicer_advance_receivable` write-off per 5.x if Fannie Mae was under-remitted); corrections reported to Fannie Mae only after the IRR discussion (5.1 correction LAR/event); borrower correction notice explains the effect.

**Worked example (Plan 4927, 5/6 SOFR ARM, 2/1/5 caps).** Note rate 5.750%, margin 2.750%, original $400,000.00, 360 months, first payment Dec 1, 2021, first change date **Nov 1, 2026**, first new payment due **Dec 1, 2026**. Index date = Nov 1 − 45 = **Sept 17, 2026** (the value published Sept 17, or the latest earlier publication). Illustrating with today's latest print (30-day Average SOFR **3.64883** on Sept 9, 2026 — the actual Sept 17 print will be used): unrounded 3.64883 + 2.750 = **6.39883%** → nearest 1/8 = **6.375%** (6.39883 − 6.375 = 0.02383 < 6.500 − 6.39883 = 0.10117). Caps: initial cap ±2.000% around 5.750 → [3.750, 7.750]; lifetime 5.750 + 5.000 = 10.750; floor 2.750 → **new rate 6.375%**, no cap applied. Expected UPB after the Nov 1, 2026 payment (60 scheduled payments of $2,334.29 at 5.750%): **$371,048.86**; remaining term 300 months; `r = 0.053125%`; new P&I = 371,048.86 × 0.00053125 / (1 − 1.00053125^−300) = **$2,476.44** (vs $2,334.29). With escrow $612.50 the total payment shown outside the mandated table is $3,088.94 (vs $2,946.79). Notice window: Aug 3 – Oct 2, 2026; sendable Sept 17 – **Oct 2, 2026** (mail by); next change date May 1, 2027 (subsequent cap ±1.000% → [5.375, 7.375]). LAR 83 due by 8 p.m. ET on the 5th Fannie Mae business day after Sept 17 = **Thu Sept 24, 2026** (5.1). If the Sept 17 index were instead 5.10000: 5.10 + 2.75 = 7.85 → 7.875 → capped at 7.750 → P&I $2,802.64; the notice states "your rate would have been 7.875% but the 2.000% initial cap limits it to 7.750%" — no carryover ("foregone increase" does not apply because the note has no carryover clause).

#### Outputs and artifacts
- Notices: `NTC_REGZ_20C_ARM_ADJ` (1026.20(c); checklist: date/effective-date statement; nested table with current/new rate, current/new payment, first new payment due date, IO/neg-am allocation when applicable; index + source; margin explanation; cap statement incl. foregone increases when applicable; balance and remaining term; amortization statements; prepayment penalty statement; H-4(D)(2) order/headings), `NTC_FNMA_C2_1_02_RATE_CHANGE` (rate-only/payment-only changes), `NTC_FNMA_C2_1_02_BUYDOWN_STEP_90`, `NTC_FNMA_C2_2_01_ARM_CORRECTION` (effect of correction, refund/credit election), `NTC_ARM_INQUIRY_INTERIM_20`. Channel: `esign_or_mail` (class `arm_notices`); never SMS-only.
- `loan_terms` new version effective on the change date (rate, P&I, next change date); `investor_events` `rate_payment_change`; `loan_events` `arm.index.captured`, `arm.adjustment.calculated/verified/noticed/effective/corrected`; `documents` (notice PDF); `arm_adjustments`, `arm_corrections`. Ledger: none at adjustment (accrual under the new rate posts in 2.1; refunds/credits post as reversing `ledger_entries` linked to `arm.correction.completed`).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `disclosures`, using deterministic `computeArmAdjustment` (engine A) and an independently implemented `verifyArmAdjustment` (engine B, different code path/library) — both must agree to the cent before a notice renders. The agent schedules, captures the index, runs both engines, renders, runs the checklist, sends within the window, emits the investor event, and writes the decision record `{loan_id, change_date, index_date, index_value, publication_date, unrounded, rounded, cap_test, new_rate, expected_upb, remaining_term, new_pi, notice_window, sent_at, consent_id, channel, versions}`.
- **Guardrails:** the LLM never computes rates or payments; it handles exception triage (index missing, boarding data conflict, borrower dispute intake), drafts the internal correction memo for the IRR discussion (C-2.2-01) from engine output, and explains the notice to borrowers via `borrower-comms` using only recorded numbers. Any adjustment with `cap_test.applied ≠ none`, a term change, or a discrepancy is sampled to `qc-audit`.
- **Escalations:** `officer` — portfolio-wide index substitution/fallback (note fallback language) and any decision to absorb undercharges above a threshold (policy $10,000 aggregate); `human_agent` — borrower request; `attorney` — none (unless litigation); `fnma_portal_operator` — LSDU single-LAR correction screens when the file path rejects (5.1 package: loan number, corrected LAR 83 values, IRR discussion log); `licensed_specialist` — not applicable (no negotiation).
- **Disclosure/consent:** electronic notices only with `arm_notices` E-SIGN consent; borrower calls about adjustments handled with the AI disclosure; no TCPA-covered outbound calls are needed (mail/email notice suffices).
- **AI-off path:** ops-console "ARM cycle" runs the same engines; exceptions worked by ops with the same gates.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in shortly before a change date:** if boarding occurs inside the notice window, verify from the transfer file whether the transferor sent the (c) notice (17.3/1.7 checklist item `ARM_NOTICE_SENT`); if not and ≥ 60 days remain, send; if < 60 days remain, send immediately, log the transferor breach, and (decision 5) defer the payment change to the next cycle at the transferor's cost — the rate still changes on the change date per the note; the shortage is the servicer's (C-2.1-01).
- **Transfer-out after notice, before effective date:** transferee applies the noticed terms; the transfer file carries `arm_adjustments` and the notice document.
- **Index unavailable/delayed (NY Fed outage, publication holiday):** use "most recent available" value on/before the index date (that is the note's rule); if the API is down, vendor feed or manual dual-control entry with evidence; flag for QC.
- **Index discontinued:** fallback per note (Note Holder chooses a replacement based on comparable information) — `officer` decision, `replacement_index` row, borrower notice describing the new index and source; Reg ZZ mechanics for LIBOR loans already applied.
- **Bankruptcy:** adjustments continue under the note; the Chapter 13 payment change also requires a Rule 3002.1(b) notice at least 21 days before the new payment (14.2) — the engine emits `payment.change.scheduled` early enough (notice sendable ≥ 60 days ahead satisfies 21 days).
- **SCRA 6% cap (13.9):** the ARM engine still computes the contractual rate; the effective accrual rate is `min(contract, 6%)` while the cap applies; the (c) notice is still sent for the contractual change and a SCRA credit explanation accompanies it.
- **Loss-mitigation modification to a fixed rate (12.8):** schedule cancelled with reason `modified_fixed`; step-rate mods (legacy) emit `NTC_FNMA_C2_1_02_RATE_CHANGE`-type notices and, being contractual, the (c) notice (comment 20(c)-2: adjustments under the modified contract are covered).
- **FDCPA 805(c) cease request on file** (loan boarded in default, 11.4): Reg Z (c) notice exempt; Fannie Mae still requires notice of the change — send the Fannie Mae notice marked as required-by-contract information, not a collection communication (decision 4).
- **Successor in interest:** notice to the borrower(s) and to a confirmed, acknowledged successor (4.4).
- **Payment-cap/neg-am legacy plans:** additional content per C-2.1-02 and 1026.20(c)(2)(vi); fully amortizing payment disclosed.
- **Retro corrections:** an index revision after `verified` does not change the loan (the note fixes the "available" figure); a boarding-data error (wrong margin) triggers the C-2.2-01 correction path with re-amortization, refund/credit, notice and IRR-coordinated reporting.
- **Reject of LAR 83/event:** 5.1 retries; if unresolved by the change date, the loan still adjusts (borrower terms are governed by the note), and the investor mismatch is worked as a 5.1 exception.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Send (c) notices by email when consent exists, or mail all ARM notices? **Default: e-delivery permitted with `arm_notices` consent** (E-SIGN compliant), mail otherwise.
2. Exact-midpoint rounding under "nearest one-eighth." **Default: round down at an exact midpoint** (Fannie Mae's stated convention in B2-1.4-02; borrower-favorable), per-loan override if the note states a tie rule; flag midpoints for QC. Confirm with partner counsel.
3. Escrow on the (c) table: show total payment inside the mandated table? **Default: P&I inside the table; escrow and total as adjacent additional information** (1026.20(c)(3) "substantially similar" headings).
4. FDCPA-exempt loans: send the Fannie Mae rate-change notice? **Default: yes**, as required-by-contract information, reviewed by counsel for FDCPA compliance.
5. Late (c) notice remedy: defer the payment change by one cycle (servicer absorbs the shortage) vs proceed. **Default: defer the payment change, not the rate change**, and fund the shortage (C-2.1-01).
6. Dual-engine verification: implement engine B in a different language/library? **Default: separate TypeScript module with `decimal.js` replaced by BigInt fixed-point arithmetic.**
