## Overview

**Scope.** Section 24 covers everything the platform must establish about the collateral and its protections between intent to proceed and delivery: how the property is valued and by whom (24.1), how the valuation is validated, delivered to the applicant and defended (24.2), whether the property and any condo/PUD project are eligible for sale to Fannie Mae and what repairs or holdbacks are required (24.3), title, vesting, settlement-agent vetting, payoffs and subordinations (24.4), hazard and flood insurance including the FDPA notice (24.5), and mortgage insurance ordering, activation and HPA disclosures (24.6). Product scope follows the inventory: Fannie Mae conventional conforming 1–4 unit first liens — detached, attached, condo, PUD, manufactured (including MH Advantage), HomeReady, high-balance, second homes and investment properties; co-ops, HomeStyle, construction-to-permanent and manufactured homes not titled as real property are out of scope. Every process is written to the Selling Guide edition of Sept 2, 2026 and the eCFR pages current as of Sept 3–8, 2026.

**Agents.** The `valuation` agent owns 24.1–24.3 end-to-end (method selection and ordering; receipt, UCDP/CU review, Reg B/HPML copy delivery and ROV; property and project eligibility, condition routing and holdbacks). The `title-closing` agent owns 24.4–24.6 (title and settlement, insurance and flood, MI). Shared agents appear where the baseline names them: `compliance-sentinel` for fair-lending monitoring of appraisal outcomes and ROV decisions, `qc-audit` for independent sampling, `underwriter` for consuming values and eligibility facts into DU. Humans appear only at enumerated touchpoints: external `appraiser`/`property_data_collector`; `underwriting_reviewer` as the designated ROV subject-matter expert (feature-flagged default); `fnma_portal_operator` for UCDP overrides and Condo Project Manager certification, both UI-only; `officer` (partner) for §1026.42(g)/AIR §7 misconduct and discrimination referrals, PERS submissions and holdback breaches; `licensed_specialist` where a state licenses valuation management or project review; `human_agent` on borrower request; `settlement_agent`, `notary` and `signing_officer` in 24.4–24.6 and O7.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the creditor under Reg B §1002.14, Reg Z §1026.35(c)/§1026.42 and TRID, the "Seller" under the Appraiser Independence Requirements, the registered UCDP lender whose Doc File ID is delivered, the seller making every property, project, title, insurance and MI representation to Fannie Mae, and the insured under the MI master policies. SM performs each step under partner-scoped credentials (UCDP lender-agent, Property Data API, Technology Manager/CPM, MI company accounts), selects and pays appraisers and AMCs as the Seller's "third party specifically authorized" under AIR §4.2, bears third-party origination costs under the operating model (the appraisal fee is SM-borne by default and, when borrower-paid, is a zero-tolerance charge because the consumer cannot shop for it), holds holdback funds in a custodial account, and delivers every borrower-facing artifact — appraisal copies, ROV disclosure, HPML copies, flood notice, HPA notices — in the partner's name. SM's `intake`, `pricing` and `disclosure` agents and the `mlo_of_record` are AIR "restricted parties" and are architecturally separated from the `valuation` agent.

**Dependencies.** Inbound: 21.2/21.4 (LE, intent to proceed and the fee gate; lock expiry pressure from valuation turn-times), 21.3 (the Reg B appraisal notice and the HPML §1026.35(c)(5) statement, both due within three business days of application), 23.1 (DU findings carrying the value-acceptance/VA+PD/hybrid/desktop offers and the resubmission with the appraised value), 23.4 (HPML and QM determinations that switch the §1026.35(c) rules on or off), 24.4 (title chain for the flip test, PACE/UCC filings, leasehold and multiple-parcel facts, ALTA 7 for manufactured homes), 20.1 (Loan Lookup for the Fannie Mae-to-Fannie Mae condo waiver). Outbound: 25.2 (CD: appraisal fee paid by others, holdback line, MI, insurance), 25.3 (rescission recomputation when the Reg B copy gate moves consummation), 26.3 (holdback funding, per-diem shifts), 29.3/29.4 (ULDD: Doc File ID, Property Data ID, SFC 801/774/859, Project Type Code, CPM ID SID 39/Phase ID SID 49.2, UCDP "Successful" gate on delivery), 28.1/28.2 (QC on appraisal, project and holdback evidence), 28.4 (self-reports when a delivered loan proves ineligible), 31.1 (state AMC and licensing matrices), 31.2 (fair-lending monitoring and LL-2026-04 governance). Servicing: 3.x administers post-closing holdbacks and escrow, 9.x inherits insurance and flood tracking, 6.x defines the custodial account criteria, 1.4/7.x the document custody of appraisal and title artifacts.

**Changes in flight that shape the build.** (1) UAD 3.6/redesigned URAR: broad production since Jan 26, 2026; mandatory for all new UCDP submissions on/after Nov 2, 2026 (fatal edit FNM0391); UAD 2.6 revisions allowed through May 3, 2027; the combined 1004D becomes two UAD 3.6 reports — the platform engages UAD 3.6 for every order from launch. (2) Project standards: Limited Review retired for applications on/after Aug 3, 2026; waiver of review for ten-or-fewer-unit projects (master-association exception); 50% investor concentration and Florida PERS retired; reserve-study "baseline" funding prohibited; reserves rise from 10% to 15% for Full Review applications on/after Jan 4, 2027 (LL-2026-03/SEL-2026-07). (3) Rental income rules (B3-3.8, incl. ADU rent capped at 30%) mandatory for applications on/after Nov 1, 2026. (4) The ROV framework (B4-1.3-12) now requires the borrower disclosure only with the appraisal copy (SEL-2025-07), one ROV per appraisal, none after closing. (5) Reg Z §1026.42(i) AVM quality-control standards are effective (Oct 1, 2025) for SM's own AVM use; DU value acceptance remains Fannie Mae's determination. (6) Property insurance rewrite (LL-2026-03/SEL-2026-07): replacement cost except roofs, 5% deductible cap, master-policy and HO-6 changes mandatory for applications on/after July 1, 2026; servicer monitoring from Jan 1, 2027. (7) The FEMA SFHDF (FF-206-FY-21-116) OMB approval expires Sept 30, 2026 — the flood form version must not be hard-coded. (8) 2026 dollar thresholds: HPML appraisal exemption $34,200; QM/HOEPA tiers per 00a-fed §5. (9) VantageScore 4.0 (Sept 9, 2026) and DU 12.1 releases (Sept 25, 2026) change DU offers and messages; the valuation adapter must read offers from findings, never infer them. (10) Fannie Mae's TLS cipher cutover (Oct 23, 2026) and the Consolidated Technology Guide's ban on UI automation fix the portal-only islands (UCDP overrides, CPM, Loan Quality Connect) as human-operated steps with AI-prepared packages.

### Section-level test plan and sequencing

### Build order
1. **Shared foundations (Stage O-1).** `application_properties`/`purchase_contracts` data, the `documents` pipeline with classification, the Timer Engine units used in this section (`business_days_creditor`, `business_days_regz_specific`, `business_days_federal` for the §1024.34(b) reference, `calendar_days`), `jurisdiction_rules` seeds (attorney/escrow/dry-funding state, TX 50(a)(6), NY CEMA, POA recording, trust certification, flood state notices, `partner.regulated_lending_institution`), and the Notice Registry templates owned here: `NTC_REGB_1002_14_VALUATION_COPY`, `NTC_FNMA_B4_1_3_12_ROV_DISCLOSURE`, `NTC_REGZ_1026_35C_HPML_APPRAISAL_COPY`, `NTC_REGB_1002_14_COPY_NOT_CONSUMMATED` (24.2), `NTC_FDPA_4104A_FLOOD_NOTICE` (24.5), `NTC_HPA_4903_INITIAL_FIXED`, `NTC_HPA_4903_INITIAL_ARM`, `NTC_HPA_4905_LPMI` (24.6). Rule sets `fnma.selling.2026-09-02`, `fnma.uad.3.6`, `fnma.llpa.2026-09-09`, `fnma.insurance.2026-08` (shared with servicing 9.1), `regb.2013`, `regz.hpml`, `regz.1026.42`, `fdpa.4012a`, `nfip.44cfr61.2026`.
2. **24.1 → 24.2 (valuation rail).** AMC/PDC ordering, UCDP/CU adapters (`fnma-ucdp`, `fnma-cu`, `fnma-property-data`), the AIR separation of the `valuation` agent, appraisal-age and value-acceptance timers, Reg B/HPML copy gates and ROV. These are on the critical path for every purchase and most refinances and depend on the TSP integration window (00b-orig Part (a)).
3. **24.3 (eligibility and projects).** Property-type rules, project review (Full Review only after Aug 3, 2026; CPM portal step), condition/quality routing, holdbacks — consumes 24.1/24.2 outputs and 24.4's title facts (parcels, leasehold, PACE/UCC, MH title).
4. **24.4 (title and settlement).** `title` adapter (or document intake), exception classifier, curative workflow, settlement-agent vetting, `wire-verification` adapter, payoff channels (external and the servicing 16.1 bridge), subordinations, trust/POA reviews, CPL/date-down gates. Can be built in parallel with 2–3 once the property record exists; it is the gate-heavy process for 25.2/26.1/26.3.
5. **24.5 (insurance and flood).** `flood` adapter with LOL, the flood notice and its 10-day gate, the insurance requirement/adequacy engine shared with 9.1, evidence intake, mortgagee-clause checks, project insurance, escrow-line seeding for 30.3. Depends on 24.3 project facts and 24.4 vesting.
6. **24.6 (mortgage insurance).** LTV rounding, coverage lookup, minimum-coverage LLPA grids (both score models), `mi/*` adapters (quote → order → commitment → activation → remittance), HPA disclosure rendering with the immutable initial amortization schedule, boarding mapping to 10.1. Depends on 23.1 findings, 24.1/24.2 value basis, the pricing engine (20.x/21.4) and the LE/CD engine (21.2/25.2).
7. **Section-wide governance.** LL-2026-04 inventory entries for the `valuation` and `title-closing` agents (model versions, evaluation suites), fair-lending monitoring hooks (`compliance-sentinel`) on appraisal outcomes, ROV decisions and MI plan elections, and the QC sampling feeds (28.1/28.2).

### Section open questions

1. (24.1-Q1) Appraisal fee economics — SM-borne by default; borrower-paid allowed per partner program with zero tolerance and the intent-to-proceed gate.
2. (24.1-Q2) Direct appraiser panel vs AMC-only — AMC-only at launch; `valuation.direct_panel=false` pending 31.1's state matrix.
3. (24.1-Q3) UAD 3.6 engagement start — every order on/after Sept 8, 2026.
4. (24.1-Q4) Customary-and-reasonable fee benchmark source — licensed third-party survey refreshed annually plus §1026.42(f)(2) adjustments with logged reasons.
5. (24.1-Q5) "Reasonable period" for §1026.42(g)(2) referrals — 30 calendar days.
6. (24.1-Q6) AVM quality-control (§1026.42(i)) scope — SM's own AVMs in scope; DU reliance documented in the partner's policy; confirm in 31.2.
7. (24.2-Q1) Designated ROV subject-matter expert — `underwriting_reviewer` approves on AI-prepared analysis; revisit under LL-2026-04 evidence.
8. (24.2-Q2) Reg B mailbox assumption — mailed copies "provided" on mailing for the prompt test; received 3 business days later for the 3-day gate.
9. (24.2-Q3) Business-day unit for §1002.14/§1026.35(c) — `business_days_creditor` (recorded in `rule_sets.regb.2013`).
10. (24.2-Q4) DU value-acceptance messages as "valuations" — no; AVM outputs and staff value memos are.
11. (24.2-Q5) Enhanced review triggers on CU — CU ≥ 4.0 or Overvaluation flag; field review only when value is unsupported; second appraisal only for HPML flips/unreliability.
12. (24.2-Q6) Interagency ROV guidance after the May 12, 2025 withdrawals — treat as best practice; B4-1.3-12 is contractual.
13. (24.3-Q1) Project document age for lender Full Reviews — ≤ 120 calendar days; reserve study ≤ 3 years.
14. (24.3-Q2) CPM certification validity — use the CPM expiry, never beyond B4-2.1-01's one-year/180-day limits.
15. (24.3-Q3) Special-assessment installments in PITIA — include (conservative); 23.1 to confirm.
16. (24.3-Q4) Environmental/disaster topics unread — post-disaster inspection policy; professional reports for hazards.
17. (24.3-Q5) Holdback custodial account form — segregated custodial account meeting servicing 6.x criteria.
18. (24.3-Q6) State condo super-lien priority — `jurisdiction_rules.condo_lien_priority` with legal review per state.
19. (24.4-Q1) Attorney opinion letter program — `title.aol_enabled=false` at launch; revisit after counsel and warehouse acceptance.
20. (24.4-Q2) Commitment age / date-down window — 30 calendar days; date-down within 5 business days before funding in dry states.
21. (24.4-Q3) Settlement-agent E&O/fidelity minimums and re-vetting — E&O ≥ $1,000,000; fidelity ≥ $500,000 or CPL; annual re-vetting; Best Practices attestation above 10 closings/year.
22. (24.4-Q4) Same-servicer escrow credit default — offer credit-to-new-loan with explicit consent; refund path absent consent by the CD date.
23. (24.4-Q5) Wire-change freeze window — 48 hours; dual callback and `funding_approver` release inside the window.
24. (24.4-Q6) Payoff per-diem cover in dry states — good-through = planned disbursement + 5 calendar days.
25. (24.5-Q1) Short-period flood notice path — allow only with a pre-signing acknowledgment and a rescheduling offer; never for purchase contracts signed post-determination without the notice.
26. (24.5-Q2) CBRS/OPA properties — ineligible pending Fannie Mae confirmation.
27. (24.5-Q3) Flood-premium escrow on non-escrowed loans — require as SM policy; waivable only by written election on non-HPML loans where the partner is not a regulated lending institution.
28. (24.5-Q4) Minimum policy term/effective date — effective on/before disbursement; 12-month term paid at closing on purchases; ≥ 30 days remaining on refinances.
29. (24.5-Q5) Private flood compliance-aid statement — accept with the B7-3-01 rating check; else vendor term comparison.
30. (24.6-Q1) MI coverage-option policy — present both; default standard; election monitored by `compliance-sentinel`.
31. (24.6-Q2) Insurer panel and delegated authority — all six private insurers on the approved list; delegated where the master policy grants it.
32. (24.6-Q3) §4905(c) "loan commitment" anchor — the conditional-approval letter; re-issue after the disclosure if LPMI is elected later.
33. (24.6-Q4) Projected-payments MI termination for non-HPA loans — show MI through the 78% scheduled date; confirm with 21.2/25.2 counsel.
34. (24.6-Q5) Upfront MI premium remittance window — 10 calendar days from funding; monthly reconciliation.
35. (24.6-Q6) State MI disclosure overlays at origination — state addendum where `jurisdiction_rules.mi.state_notice` is set; legal review.
