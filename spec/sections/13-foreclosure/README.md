# Section 13 — Foreclosure

## Overview

Section 13 is the platform's **Foreclosure Engine**: the gated, attorney-executed, AI-run path from the 120th day of delinquency to a completed foreclosure sale (and the hand-off to Section 15 for REO/claims), plus the two SCRA obligations that sit astride it. Nine processes: the Reg X **120-day pre-foreclosure prohibition** (13.1) and **dual-tracking restriction** (13.2), which are implemented as Timer Engine `not_before_gate`s that every foreclosure command must clear; Fannie Mae's **prereferral review** (13.4) and **foreclosure referral** (13.3) with the referral package, MERS assignment and law-firm hand-off; **allowable time frames / compensatory fees** (13.5), the LPI-to-sale clock with the state table and delay credits; **default-related law-firm management** (13.6), Form 200 retention, oversight, milestone invoicing and DRA reconciliation; **environmental hazard / non-routine litigation** (13.7) escalation to Fannie Mae Legal via Form 20; and **SCRA foreclosure protection** (13.8) and the **6% interest cap** (13.9) with DMDC verification, affidavits and Fannie Mae Form 1022 reimbursement. The sale itself (bidding instructions, certification, sale results, third-party sale proceeds) is specified inside 13.3/13.5/13.6 because the Guide splits it across E-3.3/E-3.5, and REOgram confirmation is Section 15.1.

**Architecture fit.** The `foreclosure-ops` agent owns every process here end-to-end, reading from `default-collections` (QRPC, breach letter, solicitation — Section 11), `lossmit-underwriter` (application state that drives 13.2 — Section 12), `bankruptcy-ops` (stay overlays — Section 14), `insurance-property` (hazard claims, PFPIP — Sections 9/11), `investor-reporting` (delinquency status codes that earn allowable-delay credit — 5.4) and `claims-reo` (REOgram — 15.1). Core services: the Timer Engine (gates and deadlines below), the ledger (advances, fees, SCRA forgiven interest, third-party-sale proceeds), the Notice Registry (state pre-foreclosure notices, SCRA correspondence), `jurisdiction_rules` (judicial/non-judicial, first-notice document, pre-foreclosure notice rules, allowable days, redemption), and the adapters `attorney-network`, `dmdc`, `mers`, `erecording`, `custodian`, `fnma-smdu` (valuation/reserve-price orders), `fnma-p360`, and the portal-only islands DRA (read-only), HomeTracker, quatro.fanniemae.com (Form 20), DMDC batch upload and REOgram — all executed by a `fnma_portal_operator` from an AI-prepared package (Consolidated Technology Guide, Apr 21, 2026; baseline §0/§7).

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** The partner (SoR) retains the law firms (Form 200 is filed under its servicer number), is the mortgagee of record after the MERS assignment and the party in whose name foreclosure is brought (open decision 13.3-1), grants the limited power of attorney under which Supermortgage's `signing_officer`s execute assignments, substitutions of trustee and affidavits, and signs any Fannie Mae certification. Supermortgage performs everything operational: gates, prereferral review, referral packages, attorney oversight, invoice review, bidding, sale management, SCRA verification and rate relief, Form 20/Form 1022 preparation, and the foreclosure file that defends RESPA §6(f) suits and MORA reviews. Fannie Mae may proceed against either (A2-1-07; research/00a §4.1).

**Dependencies.** 1.1/1.3 (boarded delinquency dates, referral/foreclosure status carried on transfer, `REGX_1024_41K2_NO_FIRST_FILING_GATE`); 2.7 (late-charge suppression on referral and under SCRA); 4.4 (successor in interest as "borrower" under 1024.30(d)); 5.1/5.4 (Form 1022/Transaction 83 rate changes; status codes 43/71/94/95/33/31/32/BE/BG that earn delay credits); 7.1 (statement "first notice or filing" flag); 8.x (`SCRA_3919_NO_ADVERSE_GATE`, foreclosure reporting); 9.x (hazard claims before bidding, insurance cancellation after sale); 11.x (breach letter, solicitation package, outreach cut-offs 60/30 days before sale, PFPIP at 90 days); 12.x (complete-application state, offers, appeals, trial plans — the (f)(2)/(g) inputs); 14.x (automatic stay, relief from stay, E-1.2-01 referral-back); 15.1/15.2 (REOgram within 1 BD, expense claims within 60 days); 16.x (payoff/reinstatement during foreclosure); 17.x (transfer-out of loans in foreclosure).

**Changes in flight.** Reg X 1024.41(f)/(g) are unchanged since 2016 (eCFR current as of Sept. 8, 2026); the 2024 NPRM would replace them with the "review cycle + two safeguards" model and a fee freeze — built here behind `regx.lossmit.2024nprm` (13.1/13.2). Fannie Mae Guide citations are to the **Aug. 12, 2026** edition: LL-2026-01/D1-3-01 (Apr. 8, 2026) added the **disaster foreclosure prior-written-approval gate** (eff. May 1, 2026); the **Foreclosure Time Frames and Compensatory Fee Allowable Delays** exhibit (06.18.25) applies to sales on/after **July 1, 2025** (LL-2025-01); E-3.2-08 was revised Aug. 13, 2025 (buydown funds in reinstatements); LL-2026-05 moves delinquency and liquidation reporting to the Servicing Platform/Property 360 in Feb–Mar 2027 (5.4/15.1); LL-2026-04 (Aug. 6, 2026) makes the `foreclosure-ops` decision records contractual. State law is the fastest-moving layer (NY FAPA, CA HBOR amendments eff. Jan. 1, 2025, NJ FFA 2019 amendments, MN §582.043) and is isolated in `jurisdiction_rules` with a ~12-state seed matrix (13.3).
