Civ. Code §2923.6(c)–(g); 3 NYCRR 419.7(e)–(g); Colorado SB 24-205 as delayed by SB25B-004 (effective June 30, 2026).

**Evaluate and notify within 30 days (§1024.41(c)(1)).** If a complete application is received **more than 37 days before a foreclosure sale** (or no sale is scheduled), the servicer must, **within 30 days** of receiving it, (i) evaluate the borrower for **all** loss mitigation options available to the borrower and (ii) provide a written notice stating the servicer's determination of which options, if any, it will offer, the amount of time the borrower has to accept or reject (per (e)), and, if applicable, that the borrower may appeal a denial of a loan modification option, the time to appeal and the requirements for appealing (per (h)). Comment 41(c)(1)-1: how the evaluation is conducted is in the servicer's discretion — making an eligibility determination satisfies the rule; borrowers cannot enforce investor agreements (§1024.41(a)). Comment -2: "available options" are those the owner/assignee (Fannie Mae) offers, including pilots and geographically limited programs; if an owner requirement causes denial the borrower must be told. Comment -3: a non-retention offer (short sale) may be conditional on later third-party information (appraisal/title) — offering a listing/marketing period satisfies (c)(1). Comment -4: other legally required notices (e.g., a Reg B adverse action notice) may be combined with the (c)(1) notice.

**Incomplete applications (§1024.41(c)(2)(i)–(ii)).** The servicer may not evade the complete-application evaluation by offering options on an incomplete application, *but* (comment 41(c)(2)(i)-1) nothing prohibits offering options to borrowers who have not applied, or to incomplete applicants, where the offer is **not based on any evaluation of information submitted by the borrower in connection with an application** (example: trial modifications offered to all borrowers 150+ days delinquent). (c)(2)(ii): after reasonable diligence, if the application "remains incomplete for a significant period of time under the circumstances without further progress," the servicer may in its discretion evaluate it and offer an option; such an offer is outside §1024.41 and does not count under (i). Comment 41(c)(2)(ii)-1: "significant period" is judged against foreclosure timing (15 days is more significant when <50 days before a sale than at 120 days delinquent). Comment -2: §1024.38(b)(2)(v) may still require evaluation per owner requirements.

**Third-party information (§1024.41(c)(4)).** The servicer must exercise reasonable diligence to obtain information not in the borrower's control (request promptly and early enough to finish within 30 days; heighten efforts after 30 days — comments 41(c)(4)(i)-1/-2); may not deny solely because such information is missing, except where after reasonable diligence it remains unavailable "for a significant period of time" after the 30 days and the owner's requirements make determination impossible without it ((c)(4)(ii)(A)(2)); if it cannot determine within 30 days it must send a notice stating that third-party information was not received, which items, that it was requested, and that evaluation will finish on receipt ((c)(4)(ii)(B)); it must not send the (c)(1)(ii) denial notice until the information arrives (except under (A)(2)) and must then promptly notify ((c)(4)(ii)(C)). Comment 41(c)(4)(ii)-1: all other evaluation steps must still be completed within 30 days (e.g., determine eligibility subject only to MI approval).

**Denial of modification options (§1024.41(d); comments 41(d)-1..-3).** When a complete application is denied for any trial or permanent loan modification option, the notice must state the **specific reason or reasons for each such option** and, if applicable, that the borrower was not evaluated on other criteria. Investor requirement → identify the owner/assignee and the specific requirement (a bare "investor guidelines" statement is insufficient); where the owner's waterfall ranks options and the borrower qualified for an offered option, the notice may say the lower-ranked options were not offered because of the ranking. NPV denial → disclose the inputs used. A decision not to offer an available modification is a denial even if another option is offered. If the system stops at the first failed criterion, disclose the reasons actually evaluated plus that other criteria were not considered.

**Borrower response (§1024.41(e)).** Acceptance window: complete application received **≥90 days** before a sale (or no sale) → the servicer may require a response **no earlier than 14 days** after it *provides* the offer; received **<90 but >37 days** before a sale → **no earlier than 7 days**. (e)(2)(i): a borrower who has not accepted within the deadline may be deemed to have rejected. (e)(2)(ii): a borrower who makes the required trial-plan payments within the deadline but has not satisfied other acceptance requirements must be given a reasonable period to do so. (e)(2)(iii): an appeal extends the acceptance deadline of the original offer to 14 days after the (h)(4) notice.

**Foreclosure consequences (§1024.41(f)(2), (g); comments 41(g)-1..-5, 41(g)(3)-1).** A complete application received before the first notice/filing bars the first notice/filing; one received after the first filing but >37 days before a sale bars moving for judgment/order of sale and conducting the sale — until (1) the servicer sent the denial notice and the appeal period expired without appeal or the appeal was denied, (2) the borrower rejected all offers, or (3) the borrower failed to perform under an agreement. The servicer must promptly instruct counsel not to file dispositive motions, to seek to avoid rulings on pending motions and to prevent any sale; counsel's failure does not excuse the servicer (41(g)-3); publication/mediation/arbitration steps may continue if they do not directly cause a judgment or sale (41(g)-2); a sale conducted by a trustee/third party is still the servicer's violation (41(g)-5); during a short-sale listing period the borrower is performing (41(g)(3)-1). Late applications (≤37 days) still get an owner-required evaluation under §1024.38(b)(2)(v) (41(g)-4). These gates are implemented in 13.1/13.2 and *fed* from here through `foreclosure_holds`.

**ECOA/Reg B analysis (design position).** Reg B "adverse action" (§1002.2(c)(1)) includes a refusal to grant credit in substantially the amount or terms requested and an unfavorable change in terms; §1002.2(c)(2)(ii) *excludes* "any action or forbearance relating to an account taken in connection with inactivity, default, or delinquency as to that account." A modification request is an "application" (§1002.2(f)) for a change in terms. Position: (a) denial of a modification to a **delinquent** borrower falls within the (c)(2)(ii) exclusion — no Reg B §1002.9 notice is *required*, though comment 41(c)(1)-4 anticipates combined notices where one is needed; (b) denial of a modification to a **current** borrower (imminent default) is not taken "in connection with default" and is adverse action → §1002.9 written notice within 30 days of a completed application stating the action, the ECOA notice and the specific principal reasons (or the right to request them) — exactly what Fannie Mae's Form 182 implements; (c) FCRA §615(a) borrows the ECOA definition, so consumer-report disclosures follow the same split, but the platform includes the FCRA disclosure whenever a consumer report was used (harmless over-inclusion). Default: **every** denial notice carries the ECOA nondiscrimination statement and the FTC/CFPB agency block; current-borrower denials use the Form 182 content as a distinct template. **[Design position — not a regulator statement; counsel sign-off required (open question 3).]**

**Colorado AI Act (C.R.S. 6-1-1701 et seq.; effective June 30, 2026 per SB25B-004).** An AI system that makes or is a substantial factor in a consequential decision in financial/lending services or housing is high-risk; the deployer must use reasonable care, maintain a risk-management program and impact assessments (annual; within 90 days of a substantial modification), notify the consumer before the decision, and for an adverse decision provide the principal reasons, the degree to which the AI contributed, the type and source of data, an opportunity to correct data and to appeal to human review (if technically feasible), and disclose discovered algorithmic discrimination to the AG within 90 days; banks and credit unions examined by prudential regulators are exempt — a nonbank subservicer is not. **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — from the bill summaries; statutory subsection cites not confirmed.]** Design: the pre-decision notice rides in the acknowledgment (12.1); every adverse determination includes the Colorado explanation block; `lossmit_reviewer` approval and the 12.3 appeal supply human review.

**State overlays.** CA §2923.6(c): no NOD/NOS/sale while a complete first-lien modification application is pending until a written determination and the appeal period expire, the borrower fails to accept within 14 days, or the borrower accepts and defaults; (d) at least **30 days** from the written denial to appeal; (e) no NOD/NOS until the later of 31 days after the denial or, if appealed, 15 days after the appeal denial or 14 days after a post-appeal offer is declined/breached; (f) denial content: appeal timeframe and procedure, the specific investor disallowance reason, NPV inputs (monthly gross income, property value) if NPV-based, prior modification payment failures, and other foreclosure-prevention alternatives with steps; (g) no re-evaluation absent a documented material change in financial circumstances. NY 419.7(e)–(g): 30-day evaluation; initial denials reviewed by supervisory personnel not involved; grant notice must disclose the option, response timeframe, material terms/costs/risks, balance breakdown, fees and capitalized amounts; denial must give per-option reasons, appeal rights, other options, NPV results on request and the DFS complaint statement; acceptance **no earlier than 30 days** after the offer when received ≥90 days before sale (7 days at 45–90); respond to borrower information requests within 5 business days.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) The 30-day (c)(1) duty is *both* evaluate and notify; Fannie Mae adds the 5-days-after-decision notice rule. (2) The blueprint's ">37 days" trigger omits the "no sale scheduled" case (treated as ≥90) and the 90-day tier that drives 14- vs 7-day response windows and appeal availability. (3) Denial content ((d)), third-party delay ((c)(4)), deemed rejection ((e)), the Reg B/Form 182 current-borrower notice, and the CA/NY overlays are absent from the row. (4) Automation class "b" → AI-first with a mandatory human approval of adverse determinations (baseline §8 item 5).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Available options** = Fannie Mae options for a conventional first lien (F-2-10) filtered by loan data: `reinstatement` (always), `forbearance` (12.4), `repayment_plan` (12.5), `payment_deferral` (12.6), `disaster_payment_deferral` (12.7, disaster flag), `flex_mod` (12.8), `short_sale`, `mortgage_release` (12.9), `military_indulgence` (SCRA orders), `qma` (transfer pending). Options excluded by loan data alone (e.g., three prior modifications, loan <12 months old) are `not_evaluated_ineligible_by_loan_data` with the specific Fannie Mae criterion cited — this is still a "denial" for (d) purposes when the option is a modification.
2. **Hierarchy walk** (F-2-10): (a) can the borrower reinstate (funds available ≥ total arrears incl. late charges)? → reinstatement; (b) hardship temporary and unresolved → forbearance; (c) resolved, cannot reinstate, can afford repayment plan (12.5 test) → repayment plan; (d) resolved, cannot afford repayment plan → payment deferral (12.6 eligibility) ; (e) permanent/long-term hardship or deferral-ineligible → Flex Mod (12.8) → short sale → Mortgage Release; borrower's stated liquidation request → evaluate that option first, then continue the retention path so the (c)(1) "all options" duty is met and every modification option gets a stated determination. Disaster: D1-3-01 order.
3. **Affordability inputs** (from Form 710 + docs): gross monthly income (grossed up 25% for non-taxable/bank-statement income per D2-2-05), PITIA, non-retirement cash reserves; housing expense-to-income ratio = PITIA / gross income. Used by the repayment-plan test, D2-1-01 (40% ratio; $25,000 reserves), short-sale/DIL cash-contribution rules (12.9). All money in `bigint` cents; ratios `decimal(7,4)` truncated to 4 places for comparison.
4. **SMDU submission** for every retention and liquidation determination that SMDU decisions (imminent default, payment deferral, disaster deferral, Flex Mod TPP, short sale, Mortgage Release, charge-off, second-lien): decisions are taken from SMDU (`rep_warrant_relief=true`); if SMDU returns "refer" or the case is non-delegated, `fnma_referral_pending` (F-1-24 package). Forbearance and repayment plans are decided locally until `smdu.plan_cases` is on (Q1 2027).
5. **Decision assembly:** every option gets a determination row; offered options carry terms; denied modification options carry specific reasons and the owner's name; hierarchy-ranking reason allowed only when an offered option outranks the denied one (comment 41(d)-1). No NPV for Flex Mod (Fannie Mae's Flex Mod has no NPV test — an NPV reason code is disabled for `fnma.workout.2026-08`).
6. **Reviewer gate:** any determination containing a denial, an ineligibility, a duplicative exception, a (c)(2)(ii) discretionary evaluation, or a Reg B adverse action goes to `lossmit_reviewer` with the full package (inputs, SMDU outputs, rationale, draft notice, Colorado explanation, reason codes). The reviewer approves, edits reason codes, or returns for re-evaluation; identity is stored and excluded from the 12.3 appeal pool for that application.
7. **"Provides the offer" date** = `notices.provided_at` = mailing date (proof of mailing) or e-delivery timestamp under a valid E-SIGN consent. Acceptance window: `accept_by = provided_at + 14` (or 7 / NY 30). Worked example: complete 2026-10-07 → 30-day deadline **2026-11-06**; decision 2026-10-30; notice mailed Mon 2026-11-02 → Fannie Mae 5-day check ✓ (≤2026-11-04); accept-by **2026-11-16** (14 days); appeal-by 2026-11-16 (12.3); NY loan → accept-by 2026-12-02. Deemed rejection recorded 2026-11-21 (after the 5-day policy grace), which closes `foreclosure_holds{kind=lm_offer_pending}` and, if all options were rejected, satisfies (g)(2).
8. **Acceptance by payment:** for trial plans, receipt of the first trial payment by its due date is acceptance ((e)(2)(ii); D2-2-05 "receipt of payment"); for deferral solicitations, contact/executed agreement/other evidence (12.6).
9. **Third-party delay notice:** if at day 30 a valuation or MI decision is outstanding, send `NTC_REGX_41C4IIB_THIRD_PARTY_DELAY` *with* every determination that could be made (comment 41(c)(4)(ii)-1) and keep `foreclosure_holds{kind=lm_third_party_pending}`; on receipt, complete and notify within 5 days (policy).
10. **Late-stage applications (≤37 days):** no (c)(1) clock; run the Fannie Mae expedited review; `lossmit_reviewer` decides on sale postponement requests to counsel (E-3.4-01: retention offer inside the 14-day window → delay).
11. **Offers to non-applicants (comment 41(c)(2)(i)-1):** eligibility screens use only loan-level data (delinquency, seasoning, prior workouts, property state, MI); the borrower's "hardship resolved / can afford" confirmation is captured as an *acceptance condition* on the solicitation, not as an evaluation input; if the loan has an open incomplete application, diligence and acknowledgments continue in parallel and the offer letter includes the (c)(2)(iii)-style disclosures (incomplete application; other options may be available; may complete the application). **[Design position; see open question 2.]**
12. **Incentive-fee claim:** on completion events, `investor_events{kind=incentive_claim}` per F-2-02 (5.x/15.2 own the claim rails).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `lossmit-underwriter` (sub-role `evaluate`). Tools: `lossmit.evaluation.*`, `hierarchy.walk` (deterministic rules engine — the LLM orchestrates and explains; eligibility arithmetic is code), `smdu.case.submit/poll`, `valuation.order`, `mi.decision.get`, `credit.report.pull`, `notice.render_send` (checklist-enforced), `foreclosure_holds.set/release`, `attorney.instruct`, `timers.*`, `escalation.file`.
- **Decision record** (`agent_decisions.kind='lossmit.evaluation'`): `{evaluation_id, basis, rule_set, fnma_rule_set, model_version, prompt_version, inputs_hash, hierarchy_trace[{option, rank, inputs_used, result, reason_codes, smdu_decision_id, rep_warrant_relief}], offers[], denials[], colorado_block{ai_contribution_degree, data_types, data_sources, correction_path, appeal_path}, regb_applicable, notice_template_versions, rationale, confidence, reviewer{id, decision, at, edits}}`.
- **Guardrails:** eligibility outcomes must equal the rules engine/SMDU output (the model may not override a decision); reason codes only from the catalog; no denial notice without reviewer approval; no offer terms outside SMDU-returned or calculator-produced values; borrower-facing text only from templates; PII minimization in prompts; every SMDU submission carries the partner servicer number.
- **Escalations:** denials/ineligibility → `lossmit_reviewer` (package above); non-delegated/mitigating-circumstances → `fnma_portal_operator` only if B2B cannot carry the F-1-24 request; foreclosure holds → `attorney` instruction; state-licensed negotiation → `licensed_specialist` (term negotiation conversations in flagged states are conducted by the specialist with AI-prepared scripts); borrower requests human → `human_agent`; suspected fraud/misrepresentation → `qc-audit` + `officer` (Fannie Mae fraud reporting).
- **Disclosure/consent:** AI disclosure at conversation start; Colorado pre-decision notice; TCPA consents for outbound.
- **AI-off path:** `lossmit.evaluate.ai=false` → human underwriters use the same rules engine, SMDU adapter and templates; reviewer independence still enforced.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Sale <37 days but no first filing (non-judicial states):** (f)(2) applies (first notice/filing bar) even if a "sale" date is hypothetical; tier logic uses the earliest of scheduled sale or none.
- **Judicial state with a pending dispositive motion:** counsel instructed within 1 BD to withdraw/seek continuance; evidence retained (41(g)-1).
- **Application completes during a performing (c)(2)(iii) forbearance:** full evaluation proceeds; forbearance continues; holds continue.
- **Transfer-out with evaluation pending (17.4):** transferee gets `complete_date`, tier, partial determinations; 30-day clock continues from the original date ((k)(1)) — flagged in the goodbye package.
- **Transfer-in (1.7):** (k)(3): comply with (c)(1)/(c)(4) within 30 days of the transfer date if the application was complete at transfer.
- **Bankruptcy:** evaluation continues; offers routed through debtor's counsel; trial plans may require court approval in Chapter 13 (E-2.x); no Reg X exemption from evaluation.
- **SCRA borrower:** Military Indulgence (D2-3.4-01) evaluated first when orders are on file; 6% cap (13.9).
- **Successor in interest confirmed mid-stream:** application dated per 4.4 decision; Form 710 must be from the successor.
- **SMDU outage:** portal fallback (Integrations); if both fail with <2 days on the clock, `lossmit_reviewer` issues the notice with determinations made and a (c)(4) delay statement for SMDU-dependent options — recorded as an incident.
- **Valuation returns with no confidence score:** order exterior BPO (F-1-27); (c)(4) delay handling.
- **Borrower disputes inputs (income/property value):** treated as an NoE (4.1) and as new information for appeal (12.3); Colorado data-correction path.
- **Retro-corrections:** if a determination is found wrong after notice, re-open, re-evaluate, send a corrected notice with a fresh acceptance window; never edit the original decision record.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **12.2-T1:** Given completion 2026-10-07 and no sale scheduled, when the evaluation runs, then all options have determinations, the notice is provided by 2026-11-06, `accept_by=provided_at+14`, and appeal rights appear for every denied modification option.
- **12.2-T2 (7-day tier):** sale 2027-01-15, complete 2026-11-01 (75 days before) → acceptance 7 days; no appeal rights statement (tier <90) but Fannie Mae D2-2-07 appeal availability check also negative.
- **12.2-T3 (denial content):** Flex Mod denied because "loan has been modified three times previously" → notice names Fannie Mae, quotes the criterion, states other criteria not evaluated; `lossmit_reviewer` approval recorded before mailing; mailing blocked without approval.
- **12.2-T4 (ranking reason):** Payment Deferral offered; Flex Mod not offered "because Fannie Mae's hierarchy places the deferral first" → allowed only because deferral outranks Flex Mod; the reverse ordering is rejected by the checklist.
- **12.2-T5 (third-party delay):** BPO outstanding at day 30 → delay notice plus all determinable results sent by day 30; on BPO receipt day 38, short-sale determination sent within 5 days; `foreclosure_holds{kind=lm_third_party_pending}` held throughout.
- **12.2-T6 (current borrower, Fannie Mae declines):** imminent-default case declined in SMDU → Form 182-based notice within 30 days; Reg B timer satisfied; counteroffer accepted → timers cancelled with reason.
- **12.2-T7 (deemed rejection):** no response by day 14+5 → `deemed_rejected`; hold released only if no other pending offer/appeal.
- **12.2-T8 (appeal extends acceptance):** appeal filed day 10 → original offer `accept_by` becomes appeal notice + 14.
- **12.2-T9 (NY):** NY loan, tier ge_90 → `accept_by=provided_at+30`; supervisory reviewer recorded for the denial.
- **12.2-T10 (CA):** denial → `CA_CIV_2923_6E_NOD_NOS_HOLD_31`; 13.x NOD command refused on day 20; appeal window 30 days shown in notice.
- **12.2-T11 (SMDU outage):** B2B down 6 hours at day 24 → portal task filed with package; decision recorded from the operator's SMDU result; notice on time.
- **12.2-T12 (counsel):** hold set on a loan with a pending summary-judgment motion → instruction sent within 1 BD and acknowledged; a sale conducted anyway is detected by the 13.x sale-event reconciliation and raises a sev-1 NoE-risk incident.
- **12.2-T13 (streamlined offer with open incomplete application):** day-90 Flex Mod solicitation issued while an incomplete application is open → letter includes incomplete-application disclosures; diligence follow-ups continue; no (c)(1) clock started by the solicitation.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Deemed-rejection grace.** Default: 5 calendar days after `accept_by` (3 for the 7-day tier) before `deemed_rejected`; documented as the (e)(2)(i) "reasonable period".
2. **Servicer-initiated (no-application) offer doctrine** (comment 41(c)(2)(i)-1) for Fannie Mae streamlined deferrals/Flex Mods while an application is open. Default: loan-data-only screens; borrower confirmation as an acceptance condition; parallel diligence; counsel opinion before go-live (the NPRM flag would moot this).
3. **Reg B position.** Default as stated (current-borrower denials = adverse action with Form 182 content; delinquent-borrower denials carry ECOA/FCRA blocks voluntarily). Counsel sign-off.
4. **Reviewer scope.** Default: human approval for every adverse determination (not only Colorado loans) — simpler, satisfies LL-2026-04 and baseline §8.
5. **Policy definition of (c)(4) "significant period"** after day 30. Default: 30 more days with heightened efforts, then reviewer decision.
6. **Licensed-negotiation states.** Default list from `jurisdiction_rules` seeded empty pending the 00a §5.1 state survey; until populated, AI presents terms but does not "negotiate" (fixed Fannie Mae terms are not negotiable anyway).
