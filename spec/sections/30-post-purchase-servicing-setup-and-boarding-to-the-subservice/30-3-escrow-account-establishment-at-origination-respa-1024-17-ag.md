### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-11)

**Reg X §1024.17 (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026) [VERIFIED].** (b): "Escrow account computation year is a 12-month period that a servicer establishes for the escrow account beginning with the borrower's initial payment date." (c)(1)(i) (settlement deposit): the servicer may charge amounts "attributable to the period from the date such payment(s) were last paid until the initial payment date" (00a-fed §6.8); "In addition, the servicer may charge the borrower a cushion that shall be no greater than one-sixth (1⁄6) of the estimated total annual payments from the escrow account." (c)(1)(ii): monthly "one-twelfth (1/12) of the total annual escrow payments" plus the same cushion. (c)(2): "Before establishing an escrow account, the servicer must conduct an escrow account analysis to determine the amount the borrower must deposit into the escrow account (subject to the limitations of paragraph (c)(1)(i) of this section), and the amount of the borrower's periodic payments into the escrow account (subject to the limitations of paragraph (c)(1)(ii) of this section)" (3.1/00a-fed §6.8); "Pursuant to paragraph (k) of this section, the servicer must use a date on or before the deadline to avoid a penalty as the disbursement date for the escrow item and comply with any other requirements of paragraph (k) of this section." (c)(3): annual analysis at the end of each computation year (3.2). (c)(4): "All servicers must use the aggregate accounting method in conducting escrow account analyses." (c)(5): "the cushion must be no greater than one-sixth (1/6) of the estimated total annual disbursements from the escrow account." (c)(6): "A servicer must not practice pre-accrual." (c)(7): "If the servicer knows the charge for an escrow item in the next computation year, then the servicer shall use that amount in estimating disbursement amounts. If the charge is unknown to the servicer, the servicer may base the estimate on the preceding year's charge, or the preceding year's charge as modified by an amount not exceeding the most recent year's change in the national Consumer Price Index for all urban consumers (CPI, all items)." (c)(8): loan documents with a lower cushion control; (c)(9): multi-year items. (d)(2) and Appendix E: the three arithmetic steps (trial running balance; add the amount that brings the lowest balance to zero; add the cushion) yielding maximum target balances, with the lowest target balance ≤ 1/6 of annual disbursements (3.2/3.4). (g)(1): "After conducting the escrow account analysis for each escrow account, the servicer shall submit an initial escrow account statement to the borrower at settlement or within 45 calendar days of settlement for escrow accounts that are established as a condition of the loan"; content (g)(1)(i): the borrower's monthly payment and the escrow portion, itemized anticipated disbursements and dates, the cushion, and a trial running balance; (g)(1)(ii)/(h): may be incorporated into the settlement statement or delivered separately — the TRID CD is not a HUD-1, so a separate document is used (3.1 interpretation). (g)(2): accounts established after settlement → 45 calendar days from establishment. Retention: §1024.17 has none; §1024.38(c)(1) applies (00a-fed §6.8/§15). Statute: 12 U.S.C. 2609(c)(1)/(d) ($50 per failure; $100,000 per 12-month cap) (3.1).

**"Settlement" — Reg X §1024.2(b) (current as of 9/09/2026) [VERIFIED].** "Settlement means the process of executing legally binding documents regarding a lien on property that is subject to a federally related mortgage loan." The (g)(1) anchor is therefore the consummation/signing date (fixture Fri Nov 6, 2026), not the dry-state disbursement date.

**Reg X §1024.34(b) (current as of 9/03/2026) and Supplement I [VERIFIED via 3.5, 2026-09-09].** (b)(1): "within 20 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays) of a borrower's payment of a mortgage loan in full, a servicer shall return to the borrower any amounts remaining in an escrow account that is within the servicer's control"; (b)(2): if the borrower agrees, the servicer may credit the remaining funds to the escrow account of a new federally related mortgage loan from the same lender/owner/assignee or serviced by the same servicer as of the new loan's settlement date; comment 34(b)(1)-1: (b)(1) "does not prohibit a servicer from netting any remaining funds in an escrow account against the outstanding balance"; comment 34(b)(2)-1: a refund is always permissible; comment 34(b)(2)-2: the borrower "may agree either orally or in writing" to the credit.

**Reg Z §1026.35(b) (current as of 9/03/2026) [VERIFIED via 00a-fed §5.7].** (b)(1): "a creditor may not extend a higher-priced mortgage loan secured by a first lien on a consumer's principal dwelling unless an escrow account is established before consummation for payment of property taxes and premiums for mortgage-related insurance"; (b)(3)(i): cancellation "only upon the earlier of: (A) Termination of the underlying debt obligation; or (B) Receipt no earlier than five years after consummation of a consumer's request to cancel"; (b)(3)(ii): UPB "less than 80 percent of the original value of the property" and not delinquent/in default. 23.4 owns the HPML determination and `REGZ_1026_35_HPML_ESCROW_5Y`; SM/partner are not small creditors (they escrow — 00a-fed §5.7).

**Reg Z §1026.38 — CD escrow disclosures (current as of 9/09/2026) [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — items paraphrased by the fetch; 25.2 quotes the labels].** (g)(3) "Initial escrow payment at closing": itemization of the §1026.37(g)(3) escrow items with the number of months and the amount per month, the aggregate adjustment (12 CFR 1024.17(d)(2)), and the total. (l)(7)(i)(A): (1) "Escrowed Property Costs over Year 1" — the amount the consumer will pay into escrow over year 1, calculated as the monthly escrow payment ((l)(7)(i)(A)(4)) × the number of periodic payments in the year; (2) "Non-Escrowed Property Costs over Year 1" — estimated property costs not paid from escrow; (3) "Initial Escrow Payment" — the (g)(3) total, described as a cushion for the account; (4) "Monthly Escrow Payment" — the amount paid into escrow with each periodic payment during year 1; (l)(7)(ii): the "In the future" statement that property costs may change and the escrow payment may change with them.

**Fannie Mae escrow policy — Selling Guide B2-1.5-04, Escrow Accounts (04/01/2020; edition Sept. 2, 2026) [VERIFIED].** "First mortgages generally must provide for the deposit of escrow funds to pay as they come due, including taxes, ground rents, premiums for property insurance, and premiums for flood insurance." "However, escrow deposits for the payment of premiums for borrower-purchased mortgage insurance (if applicable) are mandatory." "Unless required by law, lenders may waive escrow account requirements for an individual first mortgage, provided the standard escrow provision remains in the mortgage loan legal documents." "Lenders cannot waive an escrow account for certain refinance transactions (see above) or for the payment of premiums for borrower-purchased mortgage insurance (if applicable)" — the referenced transactions: "For certain refinance transactions where the borrower is financing real estate taxes in the loan amount, an escrow account is required, subject to applicable laws or regulations." "Lenders must have a written policy governing the circumstances under which escrow accounts may be waived"; the policy "must provide that the waiver not be based solely on the LTV ratio of a loan, but also on whether the borrower has the financial ability to handle the lump sum payments." Fannie Mae waives escrow for blanket policies in condos/co-ops/PUDs when the HOA maintains coverage; unpaid special assessments require accumulation before due. **LLPA Matrix (09.09.2026) [VERIFIED]:** a full-text search finds "no mentions of 'escrow,' 'escrow waiver,' 'impound,' or 'waived escrow'" — there is no Fannie Mae escrow-waiver LLPA; any waiver pricing is the partner's/SM's own pricing-engine decision (20.4; open question 3). Flood-premium escrow: neither Guide requires it for a non-bank seller (24.5 rule 4; 12 CFR 22.5 reaches "regulated lending institutions") — the flood line is escrowed by SM policy when the loan is escrowed and is waivable only under 24.5-Q3's default. Servicing Guide B-1-01 (09/11/2024): the servicer "must not solicit borrowers to waive escrow requirements" (3.8) — an origination-time waiver is the borrower's election under the partner's policy, never a solicitation.

**Arizona property-tax timing — A.R.S. §42-18052 [VERIFIED].** "one-half of the amount of the taxes on real property and personal property is due and payable on October 1" and is delinquent "after November 1 at 5:00 p.m."; "the remaining one-half of the taxes is due and payable on the following March 1" and is delinquent "after the following May 1 at 5:00 p.m."; "If the total amount of taxes is one hundred dollars or less: The entire amount of the taxes is due and payable on October 1" with delinquency "after December 31 at 5:00 p.m." Arizona offers no early-payment discount **[UNVERIFIED-negative — no discount provision located in §42-18052]**; Arizona has no interest-on-escrow statute (3.9's 13-state table) **[VERIFIED-negative via 3.9]**.

**Interest on escrow.** 3.9 owns the state matrix (NY, CA, MN, UT, ME, CT, MD, MA, OR, VT, RI, NH, WI; verified 2026-09-09) and the accrual engine; 30.3 seeds `escrow_accounts.interest_rule_code` from `jurisdiction_rules.escrow_interest` at establishment and, where a state requires a closing-time election notice (Utah 7-17-4 per 3.1/3.8), 21.3/26.1 deliver it.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "B2-1.5-04?" is confirmed as the topic (04/01/2020): waiver allowed "unless required by law" under a written policy that looks beyond LTV; BPMI monthly and tax-financing refinances cannot be waived; **no Fannie Mae escrow-waiver LLPA exists** — the row's "LLPA" is a pricing-engine matter, not a Fannie Mae adjustment.
2. The 45-day anchor is *settlement* = execution of the loan documents (§1024.2(b)); for the dry-funding fixture that is Nov 6, 2026 (deadline Mon Dec 21, 2026), not the Nov 12 disbursement.
3. The row's "cushion ≤ 1/6" is right but the operative arithmetic is (c)(1)(i)/(d)(2)/Appendix E — the deposit is the amount that zeroes the lowest projected balance plus the cushion, not "1/6" by itself.
4. Arizona's semiannual "due" dates (Oct 1/Mar 1) are not the disbursement dates the analysis must use — (c)(2)/(k) require a date on or before the penalty-avoidance deadline (Nov 1/May 1), which changes the trial balance.
5. §1024.17 has no record-retention period (00a-fed §15); the servicing retention is §1024.38(c)(1) plus Fannie Mae life-of-loan + 4 years.
6. The "same-servicer refi netting" in the row conflates two permissions: (b)(1) netting against the payoff (comment 34(b)(1)-1) and (b)(2) crediting to the new loan's escrow with the borrower's agreement; the design uses (b)(2) by default (rule 8).
7. §1026.38(l)(7)'s "Escrowed Property Costs over Year 1" is the *deposits* over year 1 (monthly escrow × payments), not the disbursements — the two differ whenever the first year's disbursements ≠ 12 × the monthly amount.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `escrow.election.recorded` (25.4 owns `escrow_elections`; recorded at application/underwriting — escrowed by default; waiver requested → rule 7) and `disclosure.cd.prepared{version}` (25.2 — every CD draft, initial or corrected) → run the initial analysis (rule 1–4) and the CD consistency check.
- Line data events: `flood.determination.received`, `insurance.policy.verified` (24.5), `mi.certificate.issued` (24.6), `tax.parcel.resolved` / `tax.installments.received` (tax service), `project.review.completed` (HOA dues — non-escrowed by default), `title.commitment.received` (delinquent taxes to be paid at closing).
- `closing.scheduled` (26.2) and the final CD reaching `consummation_ready` (25.2) → freeze the analysis version used for the statement so 25.4 can compose the closing package; `closing.consummated` (26.2) → confirm the frozen version; re-run if any line changed since the CD (corrected CD path, 25.2).
- `loan.funded` (26.3) → establish `escrow_accounts` (status `active`), post the deposit (30.2 ledger), hand off to the `escrow` agent (3.x).
- `loan.purchased` (30.1) → Escrow Setup event (30.1 owns the timer).
- Same-servicer refinance: `payoff.funds.received` on the prior loan (16.2's canonical three-segment name) with `consents{kind=escrow_credit_to_new_loan}` → credit transfer (rule 8); otherwise 3.5's refund.
- Borrower requests: waiver request (any channel) → rule 7; questions → `borrower-comms` with the analysis as ground truth.

#### Data model

`escrow_accounts` (3.1 owns; columns set here): `establishment_reason='origination'`, `established_at` = funding date, `computation_year_start` = `first_payment_date`, `computation_year_end` = start + 12 months − 1 day, `cushion_months` (policy 2.00 capped by instrument/state — 3.4), `cushion_cap_source`, `monthly_escrow_payment_cents`, `custodial_account_id` (`custodial_ti_prepurchase` until purchase; Fannie Mae T&I after 30.1), `interest_rule_code` (3.9), `hpml_escrow_min_cancel_date` (new column, written from 23.4's `hpml_determinations`; null when not HPML), `origination_waiver_id` (→ `escrow_waivers`), `initial_statement_delivered_at`, `initial_statement_delivery_basis` ∈ {at_settlement, within_45_days}.

`escrow_lines` (3.2 owns; enum additions): `source` gains `origination`; `line_type` as 3.2; `estimate_basis` ∈ {known_bill, prior_year, prior_year_cpi, quote, contract}; `payee_id`, `payee_reference` (APN; policy number; MI certificate), `frequency`, `installment_count`, `estimated_annual_cents`, `terminates_on` (MI: scheduled 78% date from 24.6; flood multi-year term), `effective_from` = first payment date.

`escrow_bills` (3.2): projected installments with `due_date` and `penalty_date` from the tax service/insurer/MI schedule, `status='projected'`.

`escrow_analyses` (3.2 owns; columns added): `source` ∈ {origination, servicing} (new), `analysis_type='initial'`, `cd_version_id` (the CD version that carried the figures), `settlement_date`, `disbursement_date`, `first_payment_date`, `annual_disbursements_cents`, `base_payment_cents`, `cushion_cents`, `low_point_month`, `required_start_balance_cents`, `target_at_start_cents` (= initial deposit), `single_item_lines jsonb` (the CD (g)(3) months/amount per item), `aggregate_adjustment_cents` (= target_at_start − Σ single-item lines), `escrowed_costs_year1_cents` (12 × base), `non_escrowed_costs_year1_cents`, `status`, `frozen_at` (consummation), `supersedes_analysis_id`. `escrow_analysis_lines` per 3.2.

`escrow_waivers` (3.8 owns; origination use): `origin='origination'`, `scope` ∈ {full, partial}, `waived_line_types`, `decision`, `denial_reasons` (3.8 codes + `PARTNER_POLICY_LTV`, `PARTNER_POLICY_RESERVES`, `PARTNER_POLICY_PAYMENT_HISTORY`, `REFI_FINANCING_TAXES`), `policy_version`, `basis_document_id` (decision worksheet — B2-1.5-04 file requirement), `borrower_election_document_id` (written election; UT notice where applicable), `pricing_adjustment_bps` (20.4 value; 0 by default).

`escrow_credit_transfers` (new): `id`, `old_loan_id`, `new_loan_id`, `agreement_consent_id`, `old_balance_after_final_disbursements_cents`, `credited_cents`, `refunded_remainder_cents`, `posted_at`, `ledger_entry_ids`, `cd_line_reference`.

Baseline tables written: `fee_items` (escrow initial deposit lines, tolerance class `unlimited` — §1026.19(e)(3)(iii)(C) "Amounts placed into an escrow, impound, reserve, or similar account"), `disclosures{kind='initial_escrow_stmt'}`, `notices`, `documents` (statement PDF; decision worksheet), `timers`, `agent_decisions`, `escalations`.

#### State machine

Analysis: `lines_pending` (waiting for tax/insurance/MI data) → `computed` → `anomaly_review` (3.2 R10 thresholds; missing penalty date; parcel mismatch) → `approved` (agent) → `disclosed_on_cd` (25.2 issued the CD with these figures) → `frozen` (when the final CD version reaches 25.2's `consummation_ready` — before signing, so the initial escrow statement 25.4 puts in the closing package is rendered from this version; confirmed unchanged at `closing.consummated`) → `established` (at funding: `escrow_accounts.status='active'`, deposit posted) → hand-off to 3.2's annual cycle. `superseded` when a line changes before consummation (new analysis; corrected CD path if figures move — 25.2) or after funding (3.2 interim analysis + 3.3 short-year statement, not a second "initial"). Waiver: `requested` → `evaluated` → `approved` (account `waived`; `escrow_lines` retained inactive for monitoring — 3.7 rule 10) | `denied` (reasons) | `partial` (mandatory lines escrowed). Credit transfer: `agreement_recorded` → `posted_at_settlement` | `refunded` (no agreement — 3.5). Actors: `escrow` agent (all analysis transitions), `disclosure` agent (CD), `payoff-release` (credit), `officer` only for a waiver outside the partner's written policy.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Line construction at origination.** Taxes: from the tax-service parcel record — current-year installments (known bills) or, before the bill exists, the prior year's charge (optionally CPI-adjusted per (c)(7); default on for taxes — 3.2-Q5); new construction uses a comparable assessment. Hazard/flood/HO-6: the first-year premium is paid at closing (prepaid, 25.2) so the escrow line carries the *renewal* premium at the policy's expiration date (24.5 `premium_paid_through`); until a renewal quote exists the estimate basis is the current premium (no CPI). MI (monthly BPMI): contractual premium from `mi_certificates`, `terminates_on` = scheduled 78% date (24.6), first premium with the first payment (B-8.1-02: borrower-paid renewals from escrow). HOA/condo dues: not escrowed by default (open question 4). Ground rents, special assessments: escrowed when the title/project package shows them. Disbursement dates = `scheduled_pay_date` (3.7): the earlier of a discount deadline (if any) and the penalty-avoidance date, never before the bill exists — for Arizona: **Nov 1** (first half) and **May 1** (second half), not the Oct 1/Mar 1 due dates.
2. **Base payment and cushion.** `annual_cents = Σ projected installments in the computation year (first payment date → +12 months)`; `base_payment_cents = round_half_up(annual / 12)`; `cushion_cents = floor_cents(annual × cushion_months / 12)` with `cushion_months = min(2.00, instrument, state)` (3.4) → for 2 months this equals floor(annual/6).
3. **Trial running balance and deposit (Appendix E Steps 1–3).** Start at 0 on the day before the first payment; per month `+base`, `−disbursements`; `required_start = −min(balance)` (0 if never negative); `target_at_start = required_start + cushion` = the **initial deposit at closing**; targets = zeroed balances + cushion; assert `min(target) ≤ floor(annual/6)` ((d)(2)(ii)). The (c)(1)(i) "attributable to the period since last paid" amount is inherent in Step 2 — no separate add-on.
4. **CD single-item lines and aggregate adjustment ((g)(3)).** For each item `i`: `months_i = ceil((installment_i − deposits_before_first_disbursement_i) / monthly_i) + cushion_months`, floored at `cushion_months`; line amount = `months_i × monthly_i`; `aggregate_adjustment = target_at_start − Σ line amounts` (≤ 0). (l)(7): monthly escrow payment = `base_payment_cents`; escrowed property costs over year 1 = `base × payments in year 1` (12); initial escrow payment = (g)(3) total; non-escrowed property costs over year 1 = HOA dues and any non-escrowed item known to the creditor (comment 38(l)(7)-? — 25.2).
5. **Initial escrow statement at settlement.** Rendered by the `escrow` agent from the `frozen` analysis using 3.1's template and (g)(1)(i) checklist (total monthly payment with the escrow portion; itemized disbursements with dates; cushion; trial running balance table; computation year Jan 1–Dec 31, 2027; servicer contact block "Supermortgage, servicer for [Partner]"); delivered in the consummation package as a separate document (paper or electronic under the E-SIGN scope `origination_disclosures` — it is an origination-delivered record), recorded in `disclosures{kind='initial_escrow_stmt'}`, `notices` and `escrow_accounts.initial_statement_delivered_at`. Boarding (3.1 rule 1 / 1.1) finds the evidence and creates no timer instance; if the closing package omitted it, the 3.1 timer runs from settlement and SM sends it within 45 calendar days.
6. **Establishment and hand-off.** At `loan.funded`: `escrow_accounts.status='active'`, `computation_year_start = first_payment_date`; the closing deposit is posted (30.2 ledger) into `custodial_ti_prepurchase`; `escrow_lines.active=true`, `escrow_bills` projected; the `escrow` agent takes over (3.2 annual lead 45 days before Dec 31, 2027 → Nov 16, 2027; 3.7 disbursement scheduling from the first projected bill). At `loan.purchased`: 30.1's Escrow Setup event reports the T&I balance.
7. **Waiver at origination (borrower-requested; never solicited).** Evaluate in order: (a) law: HPML → refuse (§1026.35(b)(1)); state law requiring escrow → refuse; (b) Fannie Mae: BPMI monthly → MI line cannot be waived (partial at most); refinance financing real-estate taxes → refuse; (c) partner written policy (`rule_sets.partner.escrow_waiver`): default parameters LTV ≤ 80% (the policy may not rest on LTV alone), verified reserves after closing ≥ 2 months of T&I, no mortgage 30-day late in the last 12 months, DTI ≤ 45%, and a documented "financial ability to handle the lump sum payments" finding (B2-1.5-04); (d) flood line: waivable only per 24.5-Q3 (SM policy: escrow flood on SFHA loans unless the borrower elects otherwise in writing on a non-HPML loan where the partner is not a regulated lending institution). Decision within 3 creditor business days; the LE/CD "escrow waiver fee" line shows `pricing_adjustment_bps` (default 0 — open question 3); the decision worksheet is retained (B2-1.5-04 "basis"); the standard escrow covenant stays in the security instrument; 3.8 governs any later revocation/re-establishment.
8. **Same-servicer refinance escrow transfer.** When SM subservices the loan being paid off: during 20.3/21.4 the borrower is offered the (b)(2) credit and, if agreeing (orally on a recorded call or in writing — comment 34(b)(2)-2), a `consents{kind=escrow_credit_to_new_loan}` row is captured before the new loan's settlement date. At payoff (funding date, dry state), 3.7 cancels/pays disbursements due before payoff; the remaining balance `B` is credited to the new loan's escrow as of the settlement date: Dr old-loan `escrow` B / Cr new-loan `escrow` B, with cash moved from the Fannie Mae T&I account (an authorized withdrawal — "remove funds due borrowers", F-1-03) to `custodial_ti_prepurchase`; the borrower's cash requirement at closing for escrow is `target_at_start − B` (the CD's (g)(3) still shows the full initial deposit; the credit is disclosed by 25.2's CD engine — open question 5); the initial escrow statement shows the transferred balance as part of the opening deposit. Any excess (`B > target_at_start`) is refunded under (b)(1). Without an agreement, 3.5's 20-day refund applies and the full deposit is collected at closing. Netting against the payoff (comment 34(b)(1)-1) is the fallback only when the borrower requests it.
9. **HPML.** `escrow_accounts.hpml_escrow_min_cancel_date = consummation_date + 5 years` (same month/day) from `hpml_determinations`; 3.8 refuses cancellation before that date and enforces < 80% UPB/original value and no delinquency thereafter (§1026.35(b)(3)); the origination waiver engine refuses any waiver.
10. **Rounding and precision.** Cents exact; `decimal.js` for rates; base payment round-half-up; cushion floored; single-item months are integers; the CD's aggregate adjustment absorbs rounding so that the (g)(3) total equals the deposit to the cent.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`escrow` agent in origination mode (tools: `buildEscrowLines`, `lookupParcel`, `readPolicyDeclarations`, `runEscrowAnalysis(type='initial', source='origination')`, `approveAnalysis`, `overrideLineEstimate(reason, evidence_doc)`, `computeCdFigures`, `renderStatement`, `evaluateWaiver` (deterministic rule engine), `recordCreditAgreement`, `postCreditTransfer`, `establishEscrowAccount`, `escalate`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: assembles lines as soon as the parcel/policies/MI are known, runs the engine, resolves anomalies with evidence (bill images, declarations), publishes the CD figures, freezes the version at consummation, renders the statement into the closing package, establishes the account at funding and hands off. Decision record: `{application_id, analysis_id, lines[{type, basis, amount, disbursement_date, evidence}], annual, base, cushion, low_point, deposit, cd_lines, aggregate_adjustment, consistency_result, statement{notice_id, delivered_at, basis}, waiver{decision, reasons, policy_version}, credit_transfer?, rule_set='regx.escrow.2013', model_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: the engine's arithmetic and the Reg X option set are immutable to the agent; a line amount may be overridden only with a cited document; the agent may not solicit a waiver (B-1-01) nor approve one outside the partner's policy (that needs `officer`); it may not treat a spoken "yes" as an escrow-credit agreement unless the call is recorded and the scripted agreement language was used (comment 34(b)(2)-2 permits oral agreement; evidence is required); no statement leaves without the (g)(1)(i) checklist. Escalations: `officer` (policy exception waivers); `human_agent` on request; Colorado SB 26-189: an escrow-waiver denial is a consequential-decision adjacent outcome — the notice carries reasons and a human-review path (3.8 pattern). AI-off path: ops-console "Initial escrow analysis" with the same engine and checklists.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Tax bill unknown at CD time** (new construction; parcel split): prior-year/CPI or comparable basis with an anomaly flag; re-analysis when the bill arrives (3.2 interim + short-year statement if material — never a second "initial").
- **Lines change between CD and consummation** (insurance premium requote; MI plan change): superseding analysis; if the (g)(3)/(l)(7) figures change, 25.2's corrected-CD rules decide whether a new 3-business-day wait applies (escrow changes do not by themselves — 25.2); the statement is rendered from the frozen version at consummation.
- **Delinquent taxes paid at closing** (refinance): paid from proceeds per the title commitment; the analysis starts from a clean parcel; the loan cannot waive escrow (B2-1.5-04).
- **Supplemental/special assessments known at closing**: added as lines; unpaid special assessments accumulate before due (B2-1.5-04).
- **Multi-year flood premium (NFIP 1-year) / 3-year private policy**: (c)(9) spread; annual statement explains the low point (3.3).
- **HOA/condo blanket coverage**: no hazard line (B2-1.5-04 waiver of hazard escrow when the HOA maintains coverage); HO-6 escrowed when required (24.5).
- **Biweekly payment plan elected at origination**: 26-period projection (§1024.17(a) "modified accordingly"; 3.1/3.2).
- **Temporary buydown**: buydown funds are not escrow lines (ledger `buydown_funds`); the statement excludes them.
- **HPML with a waiver request**: refused; if the borrower withdraws, 21.6 handles.
- **Borrower revokes the escrow-credit agreement before settlement**: refund path (3.5); if after posting, the credit stands (funds are in the new account) and the borrower may request a refund from the new account only via 3.5's rules.
- **Prior loan not subserviced by SM** (different servicer): no credit mechanism; the borrower receives the prior servicer's refund; the full deposit is collected.
- **Vendor outage at statement time**: the statement is in the closing package; if the package route fails, 3.1's 45-day path with in-house print.
- **Wet-funding state with same-day funding**: settlement = funding date; identical logic.
- **Post-closing discovery that the cushion exceeded the cap** (engine bug guard): interim analysis, surplus refund per 3.5, corrected statement, `qc_finding`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Cushion at origination** — 2 months (default; matches 3.4) vs a lower cushion to reduce cash to close; the borrower may request less (the reg permits a lower or no cushion) — default: offer 2 months, honor a written request for less.
2. **Escrow-credit default for same-servicer refinances** — (b)(2) credit with recorded agreement (default) vs always refund under (b)(1); the credit lowers cash to close and avoids a check.
3. **Escrow-waiver pricing** — no adjustment (default; the rate-pass-through model funds costs, and Fannie Mae has no LLPA) vs an 20.4 adjustment (industry practice 0.125–0.25 point) — partner decision; disclosed on the LE/CD as "Escrow Waiver Fee" when non-zero.
4. **HOA/condo dues** — not escrowed (default; not a lien-priority item Fannie Mae requires escrowed) vs escrowed on request; super-priority-lien states may justify escrowing (3.7 rule 9).
5. **CD rendering of the escrow credit** — 25.2 decides the line (credit in the borrower's favor) — default: a separate credit line; 30.3 supplies the amount.
