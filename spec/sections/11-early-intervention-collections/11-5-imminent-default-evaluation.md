### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Source verified:** Fannie Mae Servicing Guide (Aug. 12, 2026 edition) D2-1-01 "Determining if the Borrower's Mortgage Payment is in Imminent Default" (12/12/2018), D2-2-05 (06/09/2021), D2-2-04 (09/13/2017), D2-3.2-06 Flex Modification eligibility (via research/00a §3.8, 04/08/2026), F-1-21 forbearance imminent-default indicator (via Section 5.7), A4-2.1-06 (referenced by D2-1-01; not fetched **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**); Form 182 Adverse Action Notice and Form 710 (titles verified on the Fannie Mae forms index; PDFs behind download pages **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**); 12 CFR 1024.41(b)–(c) (12.1/12.2); 12 CFR 1002.9 (ECOA adverse-action timing — applicability to modification requests **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**); Colorado AI Act SB 24-205 (00a §5.6); LL-2026-04; SMDU role and interfaces (00b F1 — B2B spec portal-gated **[UNVERIFIED field-level]**).

**D2-1-01 — what imminent default is and when to evaluate.** The topic defines imminent default by reference to "events or factors that are expected to cause the borrower's monthly payment to be in default within the next 90 days" and requires the servicer, on notification or awareness of such conditions, to "evaluate the borrower's financial and hardship condition" and "evaluate the condition of and the circumstances affecting the property securing the mortgage loan by consulting with the borrower." The servicer must **not solicit** borrowers who are "current or less than 30 days delinquent" for workout options — the evaluation is borrower-initiated (a hardship reported in QRPC, an inbound request, a portal "I need help" flow or a BRP), never an outbound solicitation.

**Eligibility — conventional loan modification for imminent default (all of the following):** (1) "mortgage loan is current or less than 60 days delinquent as of the evaluation date"; (2) "property securing the mortgage loan is occupied as a principal residence by at least one borrower"; (3) "borrower submits a complete BRP" (D2-2-05); (4) "borrower's non-retirement cash reserves are less than $25,000 based on information provided in the Mortgage Assistance Application (Form 710), or equivalent"; (5) "borrower has a hardship as documented in accordance with Form 710, or equivalent." **Plus one of:** (a) *credit-based* — FICO ≤ 620 **and** either two or more 30-day delinquencies on the mortgage loan in the preceding six months **or** a "pre-modification housing expense-to-income ratio greater than 40%" calculated per F-1-12; the FICO score "must be no more than 90 days old as of the date of evaluation," and with multiple scores per borrower the servicer "must select a representative credit score using the lower of two or the middle of three credit scores"; or (b) *hardship-based* — one of: "death of a borrower or death of either the primary or secondary wage earner in the household"; "long-term or permanent disability, or serious illness of a borrower, co-borrower, or dependent family member"; "divorce or legal separation"; "separation of borrowers unrelated by marriage, civil union, or similar domestic partnership under applicable law"; "increased monthly P&I payment occurred as result of an interest rate adjustment within the last 12 months for a mortgage loan previously modified with a step-rate feature."

**Eligibility — Fannie Mae Short Sale or Mortgage Release for imminent default:** the same five base criteria, with a **PCS exception**: for a servicemember with permanent-change-of-station orders "greater than 50 miles one way," the property "must have been or currently be the servicemember's principal residence" and the cash-reserve test is waived; the credit path is identical; the hardship list replaces the step-rate item with "distant employment transfer/relocation, including a PCS order, greater than 50 miles one way from the property."

**Approval and notices.** The servicer "must request Fannie Mae's approval of a conventional mortgage loan modification through Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system" — since HSSN's retirement (Dec. 1, 2025) that system is **SMDU** (00b F1: retention decisions, trial-period reporting, "imminent-default determination" with rep-and-warrant relief for SMDU decisions). When Fannie Mae declines a modification request for a **current** borrower, the servicer must send **Form 182 (Adverse Action Notice) within 30 days** unless it offers a counteroffer the borrower accepts; the notice must state that "Fannie Mae, as the owner of the mortgage loan, reviewed the mortgage loan modification request," give Fannie Mae's address ("Midtown Center, 1100 15th Street, NW, Washington, DC, 20005"), the reason for the decline, and "name and contact information of the credit reporting agency used to make the denial decision, if applicable" (A4-2.1-06 certification referenced). D2-2-05: complete BRP = Form 710 fully completed and signed by all obligated parties whose income was used (unless deceased/divorced), income documentation "no more than 90 days old" at the completeness determination, hardship documentation per Form 710, IRS Form 4506-C when reconciliation is needed or Fannie Mae requests; the decision is communicated "within five days after making the decision, but no later than 30 days following the receipt of a complete BRP" with a 14-day acceptance window (12.2 owns the Evaluation Notice). Flex Modification (00a §3.8; D2-3.2-06): eligibility is "≥60 days delinquent **or imminent default**"; current/<31-day borrowers receive a **4-month trial**; origination ≥12 months before evaluation; prior-modification limits.

**Reg X / ECOA / state overlay.** A current borrower's request accompanied by information the servicer requires is a "loss mitigation application" (§1024.31) — §1024.41's 5-day acknowledgment (the rule's own count is "5 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays)" = `business_days_federal`, **not** the §1024.2(b) servicer-office calendar), reasonable-diligence, 30-day evaluation and appeal rules apply (12.1–12.3); §1024.39/.40 do not (the borrower is not delinquent) except that the platform still assigns a contact team by policy. A modification request is a request for an extension/renewal of credit under ECOA/Reg B — adverse-action notice within 30 days of a completed application (12 CFR 1002.9(a)(1)) with the statement of reasons or the right to request them **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Reg B applicability to modification denials is the CFPB's long-standing position but the rule text was not re-fetched]**; Fannie Mae's Form 182 satisfies the content in practice when combined with the Reg B/FCRA (score disclosure, §1681m) elements. Colorado AI Act (eff. June 30, 2026): an AI-influenced ineligibility decision on a Colorado loan is a consequential decision → pre-decision notice, explanation of principal reasons, human review/appeal (00a §5.6; baseline §8 item 5) — hence the `lossmit_reviewer` gate on every adverse outcome platform-wide. LL-2026-04: evaluation-model inventory, decision records, monitoring.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) "Trigger: on hardship" is incomplete — the process is borrower-initiated (no solicitation below 30 days delinquent) and requires a complete BRP; (2) "Deadlines: on event" omits the real clocks: the §1024.41(b)(2) 5-day acknowledgment (federal calendar — excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays and Sundays), 30-day evaluation, the 90-day FICO/income-document freshness limits, the 60-day delinquency ceiling "as of the evaluation date," and the 30-day Form 182/Reg B adverse-action notice; (3) "Systems: SMDU" is right but HSSN references in older material are obsolete; (4) class "b" becomes AI-first with `lossmit_reviewer` approval of any ineligible/declined outcome and `fnma_portal_operator` for SMDU UI steps.

#### State machine
`requested → brp_pending (12.1 acknowledgment/missing-items loop) → evaluating (complete BRP; evaluation_date set) → eligible{path} → smdu_submitted → smdu_approved (→ 12.8 Flex Mod trial / 12.9 liquidation flows) | smdu_declined → reviewer_pending → adverse_notice_sent | counteroffer → borrower_response (14 days) ; evaluating → ineligible → reviewer_pending → adverse_notice_sent ; any → rerouted_delinquent (≥60 days at evaluation) | withdrawn`. Guards: `evaluating` requires `brp_complete_at` and all documents ≤90 days old at that date; `eligible` requires the five base tests and one path; `smdu_submitted` requires an SMDU ack (B2B) or a completed portal task; `adverse_notice_sent` requires `reviewer_id` (human) — no adverse notice issues without it.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Counter.** All D2-1-01 date tests use `regx_days_delinquent` as of `evaluation_date` (actual days since the oldest unpaid due date — the same count Fannie Mae's Flex Mod day thresholds use); `fnma_delinquency_status` is recorded for reporting consistency (open decision 11.5-Q1 to align with Section 12).
2. **Evaluation date** = the date the servicer first determines the BRP complete (`brp_complete_at`), re-set if the borrower supplements after a missing-items notice; the 60-day ceiling and the 90-day freshness tests are evaluated on that date. If `regx_days_delinquent ≥ 60` on that date → `rerouted_delinquent` (the borrower is evaluated on the standard delinquent track — no imminent-default finding is needed; Flex Mod's "≥60 days" branch applies).
3. **Base tests** (all must pass): principal residence occupied by ≥1 borrower (properties + Form 710 occupancy attestation; QRPC occupancy status corroborates); complete BRP (12.1 completeness engine); `cash_reserves_cents < 2,500,000` from Form 710's non-retirement liquid assets (checking, savings, money market, CDs, stocks/bonds — retirement accounts excluded); hardship documented per Form 710 (documentation requirements per the form's hardship table — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — current Form 710 documentation matrix not retrieved; the rule set stores the matrix as a versioned parameter]**).
4. **Hardship path**: `hardship_type ∈ {death_of_borrower_or_wage_earner, disability_or_illness (long-term/permanent or serious), divorce_or_legal_separation, separation_unmarried, step_rate_increase_last_12m (modification track), distant_transfer_or_pcs_gt_50mi (liquidation track)}` with the evidence document classified (death certificate/obituary; divorce decree/separation agreement; recorded quitclaim or binding agreement; physician statement/disability award; PCS orders or employer letter with distance computed from the property address to the new duty station/employer ≥ 50.0 miles one way — geocoded driving distance, rounded to 0.1 mi, ties fail).
5. **Credit path**: representative score per borrower = lower of two / middle of three (single score used as is); the loan-level score for the ≤620 test = the lowest representative score among borrowers whose income is used (policy; Fannie Mae text says "the borrower's FICO" **[design choice — 11.5-Q2]**); `fico_pass = score ≤ 620 ∧ (evaluation_date − fico_date) ≤ 90`; `delinquency_pass = count(30-day-or-worse delinquencies in the 6 months before evaluation_date) ≥ 2` from `loan_installments` history (an installment unpaid 30+ days past due counts once); `hti_ratio = PITIA_monthly_cents / gross_monthly_income_cents` computed in `decimal.js` (precision 20), compared unrounded to 0.40 (strictly greater passes), displayed to two decimals; PITIA per F-1-12 = contractual P&I + escrowed taxes/insurance (or 1/12 of annual bills if non-escrowed) + HOA/condo dues + MI; gross income per D2-2-05 grossing-up rules (non-taxable income +25 %). `credit_pass = fico_pass ∧ (delinquency_pass ∨ hti_pass)`.
6. **Worked example A (hardship path).** Borrower calls 2026-10-12 (current; last unpaid due date none → `regx_days_delinquent = 0`) reporting the co-borrower spouse's death on 2026-09-28. Form 710 e-signed 2026-10-15; death certificate and two pay stubs (2026-09-26, 2026-10-10) uploaded 2026-10-20 → `brp_complete_at = evaluation_date = 2026-10-20` (oldest income doc 24 days old ✓; 12.1 acknowledgment was sent 2026-10-16, 1 `business_days_federal` after the 10-15 receipt, within the 5-day limit). Occupancy principal ✓; reserves $8,400.00 (840,000 cents < 2,500,000 ✓); hardship = death of a borrower ✓ (evidence: death certificate) → `eligible_hardship`; no credit pull needed. SMDU case submitted 2026-10-21 with `imminent_default_indicator = true`, hardship "Death of borrower," reason code 001 (5.7), financials; SMDU decision 2026-10-21: Flex Modification trial (current borrower → 4-month trial; 12.8 computes terms per F-1-27). Evaluation Notice due by the earlier of decision + 5 days (2026-10-26) and complete BRP + 30 days (2026-11-19) → sent 2026-10-23 with the 14-day acceptance window (to 2026-11-06).
7. **Worked example B (credit path).** Same loan but hardship "reduction in income" (not on the D2-1-01 hardship list) → credit path: soft pull 2026-10-20 returns 601/612/620 (three scores) → representative 612 ≤ 620 ✓, age 0 days ✓; delinquencies in the last 6 months: one 30-day (June) → ✗; HTI: P&I $1,650.00 + escrow T&I $612.50 + HOA $150.00 + MI $0.00 = PITIA $2,412.50 = 241,250 cents; gross monthly income $5,600.00 = 560,000 cents → 241,250 / 560,000 = 0.43080357… > 0.40 ✓ (display 43.08 %) → `eligible_credit`. Had income been $6,031.25, the ratio = 0.40000000 → **fails** (not "greater than 40 %").
8. **Adverse outcomes.** `ineligible` (a base test or both paths fail) or `smdu_declined` → `reviewer_pending`; the `lossmit_reviewer` sees the full decision record (inputs, tests, evidence, model rationale) and approves/corrects; then the notice: for a borrower current at evaluation and declined by Fannie Mae → Form 182 within 30 days of the decline (example: decline 2026-10-21 → due 2026-11-20) combined with the Reg B statement of reasons (due 30 days from `brp_complete_at` = 2026-11-19 → the combined notice is scheduled for the **earlier** date, 2026-11-19), and FCRA score disclosure when a score was used; for a delinquent-but-<60-day borrower the 12.2 denial notice (with §1024.41(d) reasons and appeal rights for modification denials) carries the same content. A counteroffer accepted within the 14-day window cancels the Form 182 timer.
9. **No solicitation** below 30 days delinquent: the Contact Engine may *inform* (comment 39(a)-4 style, generic) but may not send Form 745/BSP or "you may qualify" messaging unless the borrower asked; the borrower's request is logged as the basis for the BSP (D2-2-04 QRPC trigger).
10. **Forbearance for imminent default** (D2-3.2-01/F-1-21 pos 49): where 12.4 grants a forbearance based on the imminent-default finding, the evaluation id is linked so 5.7 reports `imminent_default_ind = 1` on the forbearance record.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`lossmit-underwriter` runs the evaluation end-to-end: `intake.link` (from `default-collections`/`borrower-comms` handoff), `brp.completeness` (12.1), `documents.classify`, `credit.pull` (only when the hardship path fails — data minimization), `hti.compute`, `eligibility.evaluate` (deterministic rule set `fnma.imminent_default.2018-12` — the model explains, the rules decide), `smdu.submit`/`smdu.decision.get` or `portal_task.create`, `notice.render`, `decision.record`, `escalate`. `borrower-comms` handles the intake conversation (hardship discovery per 11.3, the "we can evaluate you now" path when the borrower asks, Form 710 walkthrough, document requests) and status updates. Decision record: `{evaluation_id, loan_id, rule_set_version, evaluation_date, counters{regx, fnma}, base_tests{occupancy, brp_complete, reserves{cents, pass}, hardship_documented{type, evidence_id}}, path_tests{hardship{qualifying, evidence_id}, credit{scores[], representative, fico_date, age_days, delinquencies_6m, hti{pitia_cents, income_cents, ratio_dec, pass}}}, result, ineligibility_reasons[], smdu{case_id, submission_id, decision, decided_at}, reviewer{id, action, notes}, notices[], model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails: eligibility is computed by code, not by the model; the model may not alter inputs (every input has a document or ledger provenance); no adverse notice without `reviewer_id`; no terms quoted to the borrower before SMDU's decision; no credit pull without a recorded permissible purpose; no outbound solicitation below 30 days delinquent. Escalations: `lossmit_reviewer` (every ineligible/declined outcome; Colorado explanation), `fnma_portal_operator` (SMDU UI), `licensed_specialist` (presenting/negotiating modification terms in licensed states), `human_agent` (borrower request; bereavement conversations by policy offer a human), `officer` (timer breaches). AI-off path: the same rule set runs as a deterministic job; a human underwriter completes the package in the console; reviewer and notice steps unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Delinquency crosses 60 days during document collection**: evaluation date governs; if the BRP completes at ≥60 days → delinquent track (no imminent-default finding); the borrower is told nothing changes in what they must submit.
- **PCS servicemember**: liquidation-track reserve waiver and occupancy variant; SCRA overlay (14.x/13.x) for any foreclosure-related statement.
- **Death of a non-borrower wage earner**: qualifies ("primary or secondary wage earner in the household") with evidence of the household relationship (policy: obituary/death certificate + Form 710 household statement).
- **Step-rate modification borrowers**: the rate-adjustment hardship needs the prior modification agreement and the payment-change notice (7.x ARM/step notices) — pulled from `loan_terms` history, not requested from the borrower.
- **Bankruptcy**: schedules ≤90 days old in lieu of Form 710 (D2-2-05); communications via counsel; SMDU submission unaffected.
- **Disaster-impacted borrower**: 180-day income-document freshness; disaster forbearance/deferral paths (12.4/12.7) usually pre-empt an imminent-default modification.
- **Investment property / second home**: fails the principal-residence test → ineligible for imminent default (reviewer + notice); other options per 12.x.
- **Flex Mod seasoning/limits** (origination <12 months; three prior modifications; failed trial within 12 months): checked by 12.8 after the imminent-default finding; an imminent-default-eligible borrower may still be Flex-Mod-ineligible → 12.2 denial notice with reasons.
- **Credit report unavailable / frozen file**: the hardship path is evaluated first; if the credit path is needed and no score is obtainable → `ineligible_credit_unavailable` with reviewer review and a borrower request to lift the freeze (documented).
- **Multiple borrowers with different scores**: lowest representative score (policy); reviewer may apply Fannie Mae guidance if it differs (11.5-Q2).
- **SMDU reject (schema/eligibility)**: parse, correct, resubmit same day; three rejects → portal task.
- **SMDU counteroffer**: presented through 12.2 with the 14-day window; Form 182 timer cancelled on acceptance.
- **Transfer-in with an open evaluation**: boarded documents (1.7) reused; evaluation re-dated to Supermortgage's completeness determination; timers inherited from the transferor's receipt date where §1024.41 requires (12.1).
- **Transfer-out**: evaluation, documents and SMDU case ids in the transfer file.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **11.5-Q1 Delinquency counter for D2 eligibility dates.** Default: `regx_days_delinquent` (actual days since the oldest unpaid due date) for "as of the evaluation date" tests; align with Section 12 before build; record both counters.
2. **11.5-Q2 Multi-borrower FICO.** Default: lowest representative score among income-contributing borrowers (conservative for the ≤620 test — which *helps* eligibility); confirm against Fannie Mae's intent with the Servicing Solutions team.
3. **11.5-Q3 Form 710 hardship documentation matrix.** Load from the current Form 710 (version to be pulled) as a versioned rule-set parameter; until then require documentary evidence for every D2-1-01 hardship-path item.
4. **11.5-Q4 Credit pull policy.** Default: pull only when the hardship path fails; soft inquiry; one pull per evaluation.
5. **11.5-Q5 Reg B applicability.** Default: treat modification denials for current borrowers as ECOA adverse actions (30-day notice with reasons); counsel to confirm and to approve the combined Form 182/Reg B/FCRA template.
