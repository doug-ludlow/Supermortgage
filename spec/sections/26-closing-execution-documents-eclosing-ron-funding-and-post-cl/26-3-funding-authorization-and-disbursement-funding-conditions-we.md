### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Consummation, the CD's dates, and prepaid interest (Reg Z).** §1026.2(a)(13) (eCFR "up to date as of 9/09/2026"): consummation is "the time that a consumer becomes contractually obligated on a credit transaction." §1026.2(a)(17)(i): the creditor is "A person who regularly extends consumer credit that is subject to a finance charge or is payable by written agreement in more than four installments (not including a down payment), and to whom the obligation is initially payable, either on the face of the note or contract, or by agreement when there is no note or contract." §1026.38(a)(3)(ii) (eCFR 9/09/2026): the Closing Disclosure shows "The date of consummation, labeled 'Closing Date.'" §1026.38(a)(3)(iii): "The date the amount disclosed under paragraph (j)(3)(iii) (cash to close from or to borrower) or (k)(3)(iii) (cash from or to seller) of this section is expected to be paid in a purchase transaction under § 1026.37(a)(9)(i) to the consumer or seller, respectively, as applicable, except as provided in comment 38(a)(3)(iii)-1, or the date some or all of the loan amount disclosed under paragraph (b) of this section is expected to be paid to the consumer or a third party other than a settlement agent in a transaction that is not a purchase transaction under § 1026.37(a)(9)(i), labeled 'Disbursement Date.'" §1026.38(g)(2): "Under the subheading 'Prepaids' and in the applicable column as described in paragraph (g) of this section, an itemization of each amount for charges described in § 1026.37(g)(2), the name of the person ultimately receiving the payment or government entity assessing the property tax… and the total of all such itemized amounts that are designated borrower-paid at or before closing." §1026.37(g)(2)(iii) (Cornell LII text, last amended 90 FR 2502, Jan 10, 2025 — eCFR §1026.37 returned 503 on 2026-09-10): the prepaid-interest line is labeled "Prepaid Interest ( ***_*_ per day for **** days @____ %)". The Closing Disclosure form H-25 shows the same line as "per day from __ to **" — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — form H-25 not fetched]**. The 2017 TRID amendments clarified that "prepaid interest that is disclosed as a negative number must be included as a negative value when calculating the Total Interest Percentage" (secondary summary of the rule; comment text not read — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**) — the regulatory basis for disclosing an interest credit as negative prepaid interest; 25.2 owns the disclosure.

**Rescission and disbursement (Reg Z §1026.23 — 00a-fed §2, eCFR 9/08/2026).** §1026.23(c): "Unless a consumer waives the right of rescission under paragraph (e) of this section, no money shall be disbursed other than in escrow, no services shall be performed and no materials delivered until the rescission period has expired and the creditor is reasonably satisfied that the consumer has not rescinded." The period runs "until midnight of the third business day following consummation, delivery of the notice required by paragraph (b) of this section, or delivery of all material disclosures, whichever occurs last" on the specific business-day definition ("all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays," §1026.2(a)(6)). Purchase-money transactions are exempt (§1026.23(f)(1)); a same-creditor refinance is rescindable only as to new money (§1026.23(f)(2)). 25.3 owns `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE`, the satisfaction sweep and unwinds; 26.3 asserts the gate before `disburse` and may wire *into escrow* before expiry only under the `escrow_prefund` exception below.

**First payment date and loan age (Selling Guide).** C2-2-01, *General Requirements for Good Delivery of Whole Loans* (last updated 08/07/2018): "For whole loans delivered to Fannie Mae, the first payment date must be no later than two months from the final disbursement date of the loan proceeds." "In the case of a single-close construction-to-permanent loan, the two month period begins at the time of the conversion to permanent financing." The topic's example table: "January final disbursement → March first payment (latest)"; "February final disbursement → April first payment (latest)"; "November final disbursement → January first payment (latest)." Documents must "reach the Fannie Mae-approved document custodian no later than the first morning delivery, the day prior to the expiration date of the commitment" (29.4). B2-1.5-02, *Loan Eligibility* (06/05/2024): "To be eligible for purchase by Fannie Mae on a flow basis, a loan must be no more than six months old measured from the first payment date to the 'Purchase Ready' date"; "Seasoned loans are loans that are more than one year old from the first payment date to: the loan purchase date for whole loans"; "For loans that are more than four months old from the date of the note to the date the loan is sold to Fannie Mae, the current value of the property cannot be less than the original value"; "The term of a first mortgage may not extend more than 30 years beyond the date that is one month prior to the date of the first payment. The minimum original term is 85 months." C2-2-03 (05/04/2022; 00a-fnma §5.4): "Fannie Mae accepts delivery of any mortgage up to 45 days from the due date of the reported last paid installment" and "any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note." B2-1.3-03 (12/10/2025; 00a-fnma §4): cash-out seasoning is measured to "the disbursement date"; D1-3-01 (04/01/2026): the post-closing QC cycle runs "within 90 days from the month of the disbursement date" — the `disbursement_date` set here is the anchor both use. No Selling Guide topic on interest credits or a per-diem convention was found (00a-fnma §5.7) — **[UNVERIFIED — treated as lender policy constrained by C2-2-01]**.

**Table funding (Reg X — 00a-fed §6.7, eCFR §1024.2 9/08/2026; §1024.5 9/09/2026).** §1024.2(b): "Lender means, generally, the secured creditor or creditors named in the debt obligation and document creating the lien. For loans originated by a mortgage broker that closes a federally related mortgage loan in its own name in a table funding transaction, the lender is the person to whom the obligation is initially assigned at or after settlement." "Table funding means a settlement at which a loan is funded by a contemporaneous advance of loan funds and an assignment of the loan to the person advancing the funds. A table-funded transaction is not a secondary market transaction (see § 1024.5(b)(7))." "Mortgage broker means a person (other than an employee of a lender) that renders origination services and serves as an intermediary between a borrower and a lender in a transaction involving a federally related mortgage loan, including such a person that closes the loan in its own name in a table-funded transaction." §1024.5(b)(7): "A bona fide transfer of a loan obligation in the secondary market is not covered by RESPA and this part, except with respect to RESPA (12 U.S.C. 2605) and subpart C of this part (§§ 1024.30-1024.41). In determining what constitutes a bona fide transfer, the Bureau will consider the real source of funding and the real interest of the funding lender. Mortgage broker transactions that are table-funded are not secondary market transactions." (Third sentence confirmed on the eCFR page 2026-09-11; it is part of the paragraph, not only the digest's rendering.) The analysis and design consequence are in rule 9 below; 31.1 fixed the legal form.

**Wet-settlement statutes (state; example verified).** Va. Code § 55.1-902, *Duty of lender* (law.lis.virginia.gov, fetched 2026-09-10; as extracted): lenders must "cause disbursement of loan funds to the settlement agent" at or before loan closing; for loans with a federal rescission right, "within one business day after the expiration of the rescission period"; and lenders "shall not be entitled to receive or charge any interest on the loan until disbursement of loan funds and loan closing has occurred." § 55.1-903: "The settlement agent shall cause recordation of the deed, the deed of trust, or the mortgage or other documents required to be recorded and shall cause disbursement of settlement proceeds within two business days of settlement." The wet/dry state lists are customary practice from a secondary source (00a-fed §16.3, LegalClarity Aug 9, 2026): dry-funding states "Alaska, Arizona, California, Hawaii, Idaho, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, and Washington" (all others wet) — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — secondary]**; each `jurisdiction_rules.wet_dry` row must cite the state's wet-settlement/good-funds statute or the custom.

**Fedwire.** Federal Reserve Financial Services operating-hours page (fetched 2026-09-10): the Fedwire Funds Service opens at "9:00 p.m. ET on the preceding calendar day"; cutoffs of 5:00 p.m. ET (tax payments), 6:45 p.m. ET (customer transfers) and "7:00 p.m. ET" (bank transfers/close); the Reserve Banks "observe the following holidays: all Saturdays, all Sundays" plus the Federal Reserve holiday list; extensions are granted "to facilitate special market needs." Access is through the funding bank (FedLine Advantage/Direct; bank wire APIs with dual approval are bank-specific — 00b-orig N14, **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**). Veterans Day (Wed Nov 11, 2026) is a Federal Reserve holiday, so no Fedwire funds transfer is possible that day.

**Wire-fraud advisories.** FinCEN FIN-2016-A003 (Sept 6, 2016), *Advisory to Financial Institutions on E-Mail Compromise Fraud Schemes*, red flags (quoted): "E-mailed transaction instructions contain different language, timing, and amounts than previously verified and authentic transaction instructions."; "Transaction instructions originate from an e-mail account closely resembling a known customer's e-mail account; however, the e-mail address has been slightly altered."; "E-mailed transaction instructions direct payment to a known beneficiary; however, the beneficiary's account information is different from what was previously used."; "E-mailed transaction instructions direct wire transfers to a foreign bank account that has been documented in customer complaints as the destination of fraudulent transactions."; "E-mailed transaction instructions direct payment to a beneficiary with which the customer has no payment history or documented business relationship."; "E-mailed transaction instructions include markings, assertions, or language designating the transaction request as 'Urgent,' 'Secret,' or 'Confidential.'"; "E-mailed transaction instructions are delivered in a way that would give the financial institution limited time or opportunity to confirm the authenticity."; "E-mailed transaction instructions originate from a customer's employee who is a newly authorized person on the account."; "A customer e-mails transaction requests for additional payments immediately following a successful payment to an account not previously used."; and the control: "Financial institutions may verify the authenticity of suspicious e-mailed transaction payment instructions by using multiple means of communication or by contacting others authorized to conduct the transactions." FIN-2019-A005 (July 16, 2019), *Updated Advisory on Email Compromise Fraud Schemes Targeting Vulnerable Business Processes*: "Real estate transactions have been a particularly lucrative target for BEC schemes. The large dollar volumes involved in such transactions, whether for down payments on a property or the final transfer of proceeds upon closing, are an attractive target of opportunity for criminals."; "The red flags from the 2016 BEC Advisory remain relevant." **Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) The blueprint's "first payment date ≤ 62 days after disbursement" is wrong in form: C2-2-01 counts *months* ("no later than two months from the final disbursement date"), which yields 59–62 days depending on the month; the gate computes by month arithmetic, never by 62 days. (2) "Fannie Mae B2-1.5-04?" is *Escrow Accounts*; the first-payment rule is C2-2-01 and the loan-age rules are B2-1.5-02 — the ownership-map code `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M` is therefore mis-cited; it is **kept as the canonical code** because 25.3, 26.1, 26.2, 27.1 and 29.4 already reference it, with the source recorded as C2-2-01 in `timer_definitions.source_citation`. (3) "C1-2 (note date to delivery timing)" — the rules are B2-1.5-02 (six months from first payment date to Purchase Ready; four months from note date to sale triggers the value test) and C2-2-03 (45 days from the LPI due date); 29.4 owns those timers; 26.3 only supplies `disbursement_date`, `first_payment_date` and `lpi_date`. (4) "§1026.19(f) consummation vs disbursement" — the definitions live in §1026.2(a)(13) and §1026.38(a)(3)(ii)–(iii), not §1026.19(f). (5) "Dry states fund on recording" is a custom, not a rule, and varies (fund-then-record vs record-then-fund); SM's default is 26.2-Q3 (eRecording submitted after notarization, before funding) with the funding trigger being the executed-package review plus, where the partner elects, recording confirmation. (6) "State wet/dry funding laws": the *dry* list is custom; the *wet* obligations are statutory in some states (VA § 55.1-902 adds a one-business-day post-rescission disbursement duty and a no-interest-before-disbursement rule) — modeled as `jurisdiction_rules` overrides. (7) The blueprint omits interest credits, the per-diem convention, and the fact that the Federal Reserve holiday (Nov 11) — not the rescission rule — is what pushes the fixture's funding to Thu Nov 12. (8) "Nacha/Fedwire": ACH is unsuitable for loan funding (next-day settlement, return risk, same-day ACH per-entry cap — **[UNVERIFIED figure]**); funding is Fedwire-only; ACH appears only for post-closing refunds (21.5/25.2). (9) The FBI BEC advisories were not read; FinCEN's 2016 red-flag list is the operative control set. (10) The table-funding analysis was resolved by 31.1 (Form A, `secured_loan_to_partner`); this process owns the operational consequences and restates the open counsel decision.

#### Inputs and triggers
- `closing.execution_review.passed` and `closing.audit_trail.received` (26.2) → open or refresh the `funding_conditions` checklist; `closing.consummated{consummation_at}` sets `fundings.note_date`/`consummation_at`.
- `rescission.period.started{expires_at}` / `rescission.confirmed_not_rescinded` / `rescission.waiver.accepted` / `rescission.notice.received` (25.3).

- `disclosure.cd.consummated{version}` (25.2) — the CD version whose figures the funding worksheet must reconcile to; `disclosure.cd.corrected` after consummation re-opens the worksheet.
- Settlement-agent inputs: executed package (scan) and the settlement agent's balanced settlement statement / ALTA settlement statement with the requested net funding amount; disbursement confirmation and recording confirmation (`recording.confirmed`, 26.2).
- Bank inputs: wire acceptance (IMAD), settlement confirmation (OMAD), returns/rejects; inbound credits (returned funds).
- Schedules: 07:30 ET daily funding-calendar run (what funds today, what is blocked, holiday shifts); 12:30 ET cutoff sweep (anything not released by 13:00 ET rolls — 27.1 `SM_WH_ADVANCE_CUTOFF_GATE`); nightly recomputation of `first_payment_latest_allowed_date` and per-diem for every `fundings` row whose `scheduled_funding_date` moved.
- Borrower/partner actions: borrower's written waiver of rescission (25.3 `officer` acceptance); partner `officer` holds; `funding_approver` release/refusal.

#### State machine

`fundings.status`: `pending_conditions` (opened at `closing.scheduled`; checklist items `pending`) → `conditions_met` (every item `pass`/`n/a`/`waived`; `earliest_funding_date` ≤ today; `funder` agent) → `authorized` (`funding.authorized` emitted; advance request to 27.1; worksheet reconciled; `funder` agent) → `advance_approved` (`warehouse.advance.approved`; 27.1) → `wire_pending_release` (wire prepared; four-eyes checks pass; escalation to `funding_approver`) → `wire_released` (human release; `funding.wire.released`) → `wire_accepted` (IMAD) → `funds_at_agent` (OMAD/settled or agent confirmation) → `disbursed` (`funding.disbursement.confirmed`; `disbursement_date` set; `loan.funded`) [terminal] . Side states: `held{reason}` from any state before `wire_released` (failed condition, fraud flag, partner hold, wire-instruction change inside the freeze window, date slip that changes the first payment date) → back to `pending_conditions`; `cancelled{reason}` from any state before `wire_released` (terminal; `funding_unwinds` with `funds_position=not_released`); `returned` from `wire_released`/`wire_accepted`/`funds_at_agent` when the agent returns funds (advance reversed by 27.1; then `cancelled`); `unwinding` from `disbursed` (rescission exercised post-disbursement, 25.3; fraud) → `unwound` (terminal; `funding.unwind.completed`). Guards: `authorized` requires every gate in rule 1 open at evaluation time *and* re-asserted at release (`assertGateOpen` for `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE`, `SM_O61_COMPLIANCE_PASS_DISBURSE_GATE`, `SM_WIRE_VERIFICATION_GATE`, `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M`); `wire_released` requires a `funding_approver` distinct from any human who edited the wire or the worksheet; `disbursed` requires a disbursement confirmation source; no transition may skip `wire_pending_release`.

**Per-diem interest (convention `365_rounded_per_diem`, policy `sm.funding.v1`).** `annual_interest_cents = round_half_up(gross_loan_cents × note_rate)`; `per_diem_cents = round_half_up(gross_loan_cents × note_rate / 365)`; `prepaid_days = days from disbursement_date through the last day of that month, inclusive`; `prepaid_interest_cents = per_diem_cents × prepaid_days`. The per-diem is rounded to the cent *before* multiplying so that the disclosed "per day × days" product equals the disclosed total (§1026.37(g)(2)(iii)/H-25 present a per-day amount, a day count and a total; a consumer must be able to reproduce the total). This overrides 25.1-Q2's proposed "exact per diem × days, round once" default: 25.1's APR/TIP engine and 25.4's `tax_reporting_seeds.prepaid_interest` consume `fundings.per_diem_interest_cents` (see the section closing for the reconciliation of 25.4-T11's $1,785.48 and INT-O6-6's $2,913.15 to this convention). The note itself accrues monthly at `note_rate/12` on the unpaid principal (Form 3200 §2–3; 26.1); the 365-day per-diem is a closing convention for the odd days only. Leap years: 366-day denominators are not used (policy; §1026.17(c)(4) permits disregarding); `per_diem_basis = 360` is available only where a state form or the partner's counsel requires it (**[UNVERIFIED — no state identified]**).
   *Fixture:* `annual_interest_cents = 56,000,000 × 0.06125 = 3,430,000` ($34,300.00); `per_diem = 3,430,000 / 365 = 9,397.26…` → **$93.97**; disbursement Thu Nov 12, 2026 → days Nov 12–30 inclusive = 30 − 12 + 1 = **19**; prepaid interest = 19 × 9,397 = **178,543 cents = $1,785.43** (the unrounded product would be $1,785.48 — not used).

**Interest mode, first payment date, LPI and maturity.** Payments are due on the first of the month and interest is paid in arrears (Form 3200). Given `disbursement_date = D`: (a) *Prepaid mode* (default): `interest_accrual_start_date = D`; `first_payment_date = first day of the second month after D's month` (Nov 12 → Jan 1); prepaid interest per rule 2. (b) *Interest-credit mode* (allowed only when `day(D) ≤ interest_credit_window_days` — policy default 7, open question 2 — and the borrower elected it on the CD): `interest_accrual_start_date = first day of D's month`; `interest_credit_days = day(D) − 1`; `interest_credit_cents = per_diem_cents × interest_credit_days` disclosed as negative prepaid interest (25.2); `first_payment_date = first day of the month after D's month` (Nov 3 → Dec 1). (c) *None*: `day(D) = 1` → no prepaid interest, no credit, `first_payment_date = first day of the next month`. Gate: `first_payment_latest_allowed_date = add_months(D, 2)` (same day-of-month; clamped: Dec 31 → Feb 28, 2027); `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M` passes iff `first_payment_date ≤ first_payment_latest_allowed_date`. Consistency: the note's printed first-payment and maturity dates (26.1 `closing_data_snapshots`) must equal the computed values — if a funding slip moves `first_payment_date` (e.g., a November note funded in December), the agent either (i) elects interest-credit mode when the slipped D is within the window, so the note's first payment date still holds, or (ii) opens an 26.1 re-draw (`redraw_reason=date_change`) and the borrower re-signs the note; funding waits. `lpi_date = first_payment_date − 1 month` (the "interest paid through" date reported at delivery; 29.4's `FNMA_C2_2_DELIVERY_LPI_45` anchors on it — convention for new loans **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — Investor Reporting Manual not read]**). `maturity_date_expected = first_payment_date − 1 month + 360 months` (B2-1.5-02: "may not extend more than 30 years beyond the date that is one month prior to the date of the first payment"): first payment Jan 1, 2027 → maturity **Dec 1, 2056**. Monthly interest check for the first installment (30/360 note accrual): `56,000,000 × 0.06125 / 12 = 285,833.33…` → $2,858.33 interest, principal = $3,402.62 − $2,858.33 = **$544.29**.

**Funding worksheet (`buildFundingWorksheet`).** Gross loan amount (CD §1026.38(b)) minus lender-retained items (prepaid interest to the partner as interest income; initial escrow deposit per 30.3's initial analysis; any lender fees the CD shows as paid to the lender and withheld, which are zero under the SM model where SM bears third-party costs and the partner charges no retained origination fee — 20.4) plus lender credits (§1026.38(h)(3), including the borrower rate pass-through delivered as a credit where 20.4 chose that form) equals `net_wire_cents`. The settlement agent's balanced statement (ALTA settlement statement/seller CD for purchases) must request the same net figure; `variance_cents` must be 0 (≤ $1.00 rounding explained) before `FC_FIGURES_RECONCILED` passes. Third-party payoffs (existing liens), title charges, recording fees, and cash to/from borrower are disbursed *by the settlement agent* from the gross funds and appear on the worksheet as informational lines checked against the CD (payoff amount = 24.4 `computed_total_at_disbursement_cents` for the disbursement date).
   *Fixture (figures consistent with 27.1 worked example A; escrow deposit and lender credit are illustrative pending 30.3/20.4):* gross $560,000.00 − prepaid interest $1,785.43 − initial escrow deposit $1,665.00 + lender credit $700.00 = **net wire $557,249.57** (55,724,957 cents). Warehouse split (27.1 rule 2): advance 98% × $560,000.00 = $548,800.00; partner contribution $8,449.57.

**Wet vs dry behavior (`decideFundingType`).** From `jurisdiction_rules.wet_dry` and the closing type. *Wet* (default outside the nine dry states; VA/OH fixture): `disbursement_authorization_mode = table_funds_then_authorize` — the pre-signing subset of the checklist (everything except `FC_DOCS_EXECUTED_QC`, `FC_CD_ACK`, `FC_ID_VERIFIED`, `FC_AUDIT_TRAIL`, `FC_ENOTE_REGISTERED_SECURED`) must pass by the morning of closing; the wire (value date = closing date) is released by 10:00 local into the settlement agent's escrow under closing instructions that forbid disbursement until SM's authorization; after signing, 26.2's execution review (`SM_O72_POST_SIGNING_REVIEW_4H`) completes the remaining items and the agent issues the disbursement authorization (funding number) through the settlement-agent portal/verified channel; the agent disburses the same day (VA: within two business days by statute); `disbursement_date = funding_date` normally. Refinances in wet states: the wire waits for rescission expiry and goes out the next Fedwire business day (`SM_O73_POST_RESCISSION_FUNDING_1BD`; statutory in VA). *Dry* (AK, AZ, CA, HI, ID, NV, NM, OR, WA; fixture AZ): `review_then_fund` — the executed package is reviewed (26.2), all items pass, `earliest_funding_date` = max(rescission expiry + 1 Fedwire day, review completion, recording confirmation where elected), the wire is released the morning of `scheduled_funding_date`, the escrow agent confirms recording status and disburses that day; `disbursement_date` = the agent's disbursement date. *Escrow pre-fund exception* (`escrow_prefund=true`; `officer` approval per loan): §1026.23(c) permits money "in escrow" before expiry; SM may wire on the last business day before a holiday-lengthened gap only when the settlement agent has signed a hold-in-escrow undertaking naming SM's return instructions and the CPL covers the agent's escrow — default off because the warehouse advance would accrue interest while funds sit idle and the rescission unwind would then require a return wire.

**Wire preparation, dual control and the fraud playbook (`prepareWire`, `releaseWire`).** The beneficiary is always the settlement agent's escrow/trust account verified under 24.4 (vendor match + independent callback), never a borrower, never a third party, never an account first seen by e-mail. Four-eyes checks recorded in `funding_wires.four_eyes_check`: (i) `wire_instructions_hash` equals the verified record's hash; (ii) verification unexpired; (iii) no change inside the 48-hour freeze (any change → hold, re-verify by callback to a number sourced from the ALTA Registry/underwriter — never from the e-mail — and `funding_approver` sign-off; a change on the funding day itself → funding moves at least one Fedwire day: **no same-day changes**); (iv) OFAC screen of the beneficiary name/bank; (v) amount equals `net_wire_cents` on the reconciled worksheet; (vi) beneficiary agent equals the CPL's covered agent. BEC indicators (FIN-2016-A003 list above) are scored by the agent on every inbound instruction/e-mail: altered sender domain or display name, "urgent/confidential" markings, changed account at a known beneficiary, foreign beneficiary bank, new payee without history, instructions arriving with "limited time or opportunity to confirm", requests for additional payments after a first wire — any hit → `funding.held{reason=bec_indicator}` and a callback; two hits → `officer` and a fraud case (28.4). Release: the `funder` agent may prepare but never release; the `funding_approver` reviews the four-eyes record and releases in the bank channel (bank-side dual approval applies as a second, independent control); the approver must not be the person who edited any instruction. After release: IMAD captured within 30 minutes; the settlement agent is told the amount and IMAD through the verified channel; the agent confirms receipt; any "wire not received" claim after IMAD → bank trace with OMAD, never a replacement wire without `officer` approval; misdirected-wire response: immediate recall request to the bank and notification to the beneficiary bank (FBI IC3 kill-chain filing — **[UNVERIFIED program details]**), fraud case, CPL/E&O notice.

**Fedwire/ACH mechanics.** Funding uses Fedwire only: same-day final settlement, irrevocable once accepted by the Reserve Bank (recall is a request to the beneficiary bank, not a reversal), operating window 9:00 p.m. ET prior day to the 6:45 p.m. ET customer cutoff, no Saturday/Sunday/Federal Reserve holiday value dates; SM's internal release cutoff is 13:00 ET (27.1) so that receipt, recording and disbursement happen the same day in Pacific/Mountain time zones. Messages carry the partner's name and loan number in the originator-to-beneficiary field (ISO 20022 pacs.008 since the July 14, 2025 Fedwire migration — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**). ACH is not used for funding (prefunding/settlement timing and return rules make it unsuitable); ACH credits are used only for post-closing borrower refunds (tolerance cures under 21.5/25.2) and for partner settlement (27.2) under 00b-svc N10.

**RESPA table-funding analysis (operational form).** Test: does the settlement involve "a contemporaneous advance of loan funds *and an assignment of the loan to the person advancing the funds*"? Under `origination.warehouse_legal_form = secured_loan_to_partner` (31.1; Form A): the note and security instrument name the partner; SM advances under a credit agreement with recourse to the partner and a partner contribution (haircut); the partner grants SM a security interest (E-SIGN §7021/UETA §16 "control" of the eNote through the eRegistry Secured Party entry plus Fannie Mae's Funding Agreement — 27.1 corrects the blueprint's UCC 9-105 citation, which governs electronic chattel paper, not notes; possession through the bailee for paper notes; Interim Funder on the MIN) — a pledge, not an assignment; repayment comes from the partner's bona fide secondary-market sale to Fannie Mae (§1024.5(b)(7)), to which SM is never a party; SM's economics are warehouse interest/fees and a services arrangement, not a purchase price. Conclusion: the partner is the RESPA "lender" ("the secured creditor… named in the debt obligation and document creating the lien") and the TILA creditor ("to whom the obligation is initially payable"); the transaction is not table-funded; SM is neither "lender" nor "mortgage broker". The contrary form (Form B — assignment to SM at settlement) would make SM the RESPA lender and the partner a "mortgage broker that closes the loan in its own name", changing the LE/CD creditor question, §8/AfBA exposure and Fannie Mae seller-of-record integrity — refused by the platform absent an `officer` + `attorney` decision record (31.1-T9). Operational consequences enforced here: the wire is an advance to the partner's settlement (originator-to-beneficiary text names the partner as lender); closing instructions and the CPL name the partner; no assignment, allonge or endorsement to SM is ever generated (26.4: endorsement in blank to Fannie Mae's benefit only); `funding_wires.kind=funding` is booked to `warehouse_advance_receivable`, never to a loan-purchase account. **Open legal decision** (26.3-Q1 = 31.1-Q3): counsel must opine that the "real source of funding and the real interest of the funding lender" test is satisfied by a recourse credit facility with a genuine partner contribution and interest, given that SM also bears the third-party origination costs and receives the platform fee — the economics must not be restructured into a purchase price without re-running this analysis.

**Funding cancellation and unwind (`openUnwind`).** (a) *Rescission exercised before release* (`rescission.exercised` during the period or at the sweep): `funding.cancelled{rescinded_before_funding}`; warehouse request withdrawn (27.1 `rejected`/never funded); 26.1 set `voided`; eNote `enote.registration.reversed` (26.2); MIN reversal/deactivation (26.4); recorded security instrument (dry state, record-before-fund) → release/reconveyance prepared by the settlement agent and recorded (26.4 tracks); borrower refunds of any fees per 25.3's 20-day clock; 29.1 fallout; HMDA action taken 'withdrawn'/'approved not accepted' per 28.3's rule. (b) *Rescission exercised after disbursement* (mailed notice postmarked within the period): 25.3 `rescission_exercises` drives; 26.3 opens `funding_unwinds{funds_position=disbursed}` — recover the escrow deposit and any undisbursed funds from the settlement agent, request the payoff-lender refund where the prior lien was paid (or accept that the new loan stands as a refinance of a refinance only under counsel's direction — **[open question 3]**), partner repurchase of the advance (27.1 `partner_repurchase`), de-board (30.2), reverse ledger entries. (c) *Conditions failed after signing* (dry): `held` → cure within the lock/commitment window or `cancelled{conditions_failed}` with the same void/reverse steps; the borrower receives an 21.6 notice where the failure is a credit decision (e.g., VVOE reveals job loss → adverse action by `underwriting_reviewer`). (d) *Documents not returned* (dry): no wire; day 2 escalation to the settlement agent and the title underwriter (CPL); day 5 cancellation and re-schedule. (e) *Agent failed to disburse* (wet, funds at the table): `SM_O73_DISBURSEMENT_CONFIRM_1BD` → return demand (`SM_O73_FUNDS_RETURN_2BD`) → CPL claim; the advance is reversed on return. (f) *Fraud discovered after release*: recall, fraud case, `officer`. Every unwind step is owned, timed and evidenced in `funding_unwinds.steps`; money movements after disbursement require `officer` approval.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`funder` agent (tools: `evaluateFundingConditions`, `computeDates` (rescission/Fedwire/holiday arithmetic), `computePerDiem`, `decideInterestMode`, `buildFundingWorksheet`, `reconcileToSettlementStatement`, `scoreBecIndicators`, `prepareWire`, `runFourEyesChecks`, `requestWarehouseAdvance`, `notifySettlementAgent` (verified channel), `confirmDisbursement`, `postLedger`, `openUnwind`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: on `closing.scheduled` it computes the funding calendar (earliest date, prepaid days, first payment gate) and starts the checklist; on each upstream event it re-evaluates; when all items pass and the date has arrived it authorizes funding, requests the advance, builds and reconciles the worksheet, prepares the wire, runs the four-eyes checks, files the `funding_approver` escalation, and after release tracks acceptance, agent receipt, disbursement and boarding; on any failure it holds with a precise reason and the cure owner. Decision record: `{funding_id, checklist_snapshot, dates{consummation, rescission_expires_at, earliest_funding_date, scheduled_funding_date, disbursement_date, first_payment_date, latest_allowed, lpi_date}, interest{mode, per_diem_cents, days, amount_cents}, worksheet{version, net_wire_cents, variance_cents}, wire{beneficiary_verification_id, instructions_hash, four_eyes, bec_score}, advance{advance_id, amount_cents}, gates_asserted[], rationale, confidence, rule_set_versions, model_version, prompt_version}`. Guardrails: never releases a wire; never accepts wire instructions from e-mail; never waives a regulatory gate; never funds before `rescission_expires_at` unless a waiver was accepted by the `officer` or the escrow pre-fund exception is approved; never changes the first payment date or note figures — it re-draws through 26.1; never funds a loan whose worksheet does not reconcile; never sends a second wire for a "missing" wire without `officer` approval; never funds under Form B economics. Escalations: `funding_approver` (every release; SLA `SM_O73_DUAL_CONTROL_RELEASE_1H`; also 48-hour-window instruction changes and the three waivable items), `officer` (escrow pre-fund, unwinds moving money, replacement wires, fraud), `settlement_agent` (returns, confirmations, disbursement failures), `signing_officer` (none here — endorsements are 26.4), `human_agent` (borrower questions about funding date/prepaid interest; AI discloses automation), `underwriting_reviewer` (failed PTF conditions that are credit decisions). Borrower-facing: the funding date, prepaid interest or interest credit and first payment date are explained in plain language before consummation (25.2 CD walkthrough) and again in 30.2's first-payment letter; no borrower is asked to wire funds to any account not verified by 24.4 (borrower wire-fraud warning is part of the closing instructions and the 25.2 CD delivery). Human path (`origination.ai_funding = off`): the ops console presents the same checklist and worksheet for a human funder; the release step is unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Rescission notice arrives after release but postmarked within the period → valid exercise (25.3): `unwinding`; funds still in escrow → return; disbursed → rule 10(b).
- Settlement agent's requested net differs from the worksheet by more than $1.00 → hold; the agent reconciles (usually a payoff per-diem or a recording-fee change); a CD figure change → corrected CD (25.2) before release.
- Wire instructions "updated" by e-mail on the funding morning → BEC score high; hold; callback to an independently sourced number; funding moves to the next Fedwire day even if the change is genuine (no same-day changes).
- Fedwire/bank outage or bank OFAC hold → re-date; refinance in VA → statutory one-business-day exposure logged (`VA_55_1_902_REFI_DISBURSE_1BD`).
- Hazard policy effective date later than the slipped disbursement date → `FC_HAZARD_EVIDENCE` fails; 24.5 obtains an endorsement; funding waits.
- VVOE older than 10 business days because the note date moved (re-draw) → 22.3 re-verifies before the new signing; the funding checklist re-tests against the new note date.
- Payoff statement good-through passes because funding slipped → `SM_PAYOFF_GOOD_THROUGH_GATE` (24.4) requests a refresh; the worksheet informational line updates; the wire amount is unchanged (payoff is disbursed by the agent from gross funds) unless the CD's cash-to-close changes.
- Interest-credit request when `day(D)` > window → refused; prepaid mode with the two-month gate.
- Disbursement in dry state slips past the CD's disclosed Disbursement Date → 25.2 evaluates a corrected CD (post-consummation event) and 26.3 recomputes prepaid interest; the borrower is told before the wire.
- Escrow agent disburses partially (e.g., pays the prior lien but holds cash-to-borrower pending a lien release) → `disbursement_date` = first disbursement date; the remainder tracked as an exception with the agent; boarding proceeds.
- Loan funded on the last business day of the month with `day(D)=31` → prepaid 1 day; first payment the first of the second following month; two-month gate clamps (Dec 31 → Feb 28).
- Partner places a hold (`partner_hold`) after conditions are met → `held`; if it persists past lock/commitment expiry → 21.4/29.1 economics; borrower communication by `human_agent`.
- Wire returned by the beneficiary bank (account closed) → `returned`; instructions re-verified from scratch (new callback, vendor); the advance is reversed and re-requested.
- eNote loan whose Secured Party change is rejected by the eRegistry → `FC_ENOTE_REGISTERED_SECURED` fails; 26.2 cures; wet-state closings already signed proceed as `unsecured_wet` under 27.1's 1-BD clock only with `funding_approver` acknowledgment.
- Texas 50(a)(6): the later of the federal and Texas rescission expiries governs; funding at a permitted office is 26.1's; 26.3 adds no Texas per-diem variation.
- Borrower dies between consummation and funding (refinance) → hold; `officer` and counsel; rescission/estate rules — no automated funding.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **RESPA lender status / table-funding opinion (= 31.1-Q3).** Default: Form A (`secured_loan_to_partner`) with a recourse facility, partner haircut contribution and interest; counsel opinion required before the first funding; the platform refuses Form B without an `officer`+`attorney` record. Re-open if the partner's economics are ever restructured so that SM's return looks like a purchase price.
2. **Interest-credit window and default election.** Default: allowed when the disbursement day-of-month ≤ 7; offered (not defaulted) to the borrower at CD preparation; the first payment then falls on the first of the next month. Fannie Mae publishes no cap; the only hard constraint is C2-2-01's two-month rule and the compressed LPI-45 delivery window (29.4).
3. **Post-disbursement rescission where the prior lien was paid.** Default: treat as a full unwind (rescission voids the security interest; the borrower must tender; the partner repurchases the advance); counsel to define the tender/settlement mechanics per §1026.23(d) and whether the prior lienholder's refund can be compelled — **[UNVERIFIED — §1026.23(d) mechanics not fetched here; 25.3 owns]**.
4. **Escrow pre-fund exception.** Default: off; permitted per loan by `officer` when a holiday gap ≥ 2 days follows rescission expiry and the settlement agent signs the hold-in-escrow undertaking; warehouse interest for idle days is SM's cost.
5. **Record-before-fund in dry states.** Default: not required for funding (26.2-Q3 has eRecording submitted after notarization, so recording usually precedes funding anyway); `FC_RECORDING_CONFIRMED` becomes required where the partner's policy or the title underwriter's CPL/gap position demands it.
6. **Per-diem basis.** Default: 365 with rounded per-diem × days (this process's convention); 360 only where a state/investor form requires; 25.1 and 25.4 consume `fundings` values — their earlier "exact × days" figures are superseded.
7. **`funding_approver` thresholds.** Default: every outbound funding wire requires human release (no de minimis); backup approver within 1 hour; bank-side dual approval retained.
