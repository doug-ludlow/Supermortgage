# Section 31 — Cross-cutting: licensing and approvals, AI governance and fair lending, records/privacy/security

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 31 holds the three programs every origination process leans on but none owns. **31.1 Licensing, approvals and the partner boundary** keeps the registries and gates that decide whether the platform may act at all in a given state or on a given rail: the partner's Fannie Mae seller/servicer standing and FHFA-derived eligibility (with the origination-only liquidity inputs the servicing spec's 18.3 test needs), the eMortgage special approval, SM's Technology Service Provider certification and Technology Manager authorizations, the SAFE Act analysis of who must be a licensed MLO (an AI is not "an individual"; the partner-employed `mlo_of_record` presents terms and approves LEs and locks; SM's human processors/underwriters are licensed where a state requires), the nine-state verified matrix of third-party processing/underwriting licensing (AZ, OH, TX, NC, IL, WA, FL, CA, CT) with the rest fail-closed, the NMLS renewal season (Nov 1–Dec 31; CE 8 hours; reinstatement to Feb 28), and the RESPA table-funding analysis resolved in favor of a secured warehouse loan to the partner. **31.2 AI governance, model risk and fair lending** implements LL-2026-04 for the origination agents: inventory, pre-deployment assessment, evaluation and bias tests as a deploy gate, decision records that produce §1002.9(b)(2)-grade reasons, monthly/quarterly outcome monitoring of underwriting, pricing, exceptions, steering and valuation review by prohibited basis, less-discriminatory-alternative searches, the human-review touchpoints, Colorado SB 26-189 developer duties, the California ADMT posture, and the governance evidence pack for the partner and Fannie Mae. **31.3 Records, retention, privacy and security** loads the origination retention matrix into the servicing spec's 19.1 engine (Reg Z/B/C/X, FDPA, BSA, OFAC, TCPA, E-SIGN, Colorado, Fannie Mae life-of-loan + 4 years, eNote signing records life + 7, state RON custody), settles the Fannie Mae ownership wording, adds the §1016.13 service-provider and vendor flow-down gates, the restricted-PII access regime for `applicant_demographics`, the FCRA/Technology Guide data-use limits, and the origination alignment of the 19.2 incident runbook (36-hour Fannie Mae/Form 101, 24-hour partner, 30-day FTC, 72-hour NYDFS, state clocks).

**Agents.** `compliance-sentinel` (licensing persona in 31.1; timers, rule-version watch and deploy-gate evaluation in 31.2/31.3), `qc-audit` (independent evaluation, bias tests and fair-lending monitoring in 31.2), and `security-records` (evidence custody, retention lifecycle, vendor gates, PII monitoring, incident scoping in 31.3; custody of the demographic enclave in 31.2). The deterministic parts — readiness predicates, retention arithmetic, statistics, gates, syncs — are code; the language model drafts assessments, matrix proposals, root-cause hypotheses, packages and notices inside approved templates and never approves, files, deletes, releases a hold, opens a gate on inference, or reads restricted demographic content into a prompt. Human touchpoints are the enumerated ones: `officer` (partner and SM — approvals, attestations, dispositions, regulatory and Fannie Mae notices, disposal attestations), `attorney` (matrix verification, AI-intake positions, table-funding opinion, privilege, subpoenas, breach-matrix gaps), `fnma_portal_operator` (Technology Manager, ECRM/Form 582, file-transfer portals), `underwriting_reviewer` and second-level reviewers (decisions, Colorado reconsideration, appeals), `mlo_of_record` (terms), `signing_officer` (records certifications).

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the Fannie Mae seller/servicer and Lender Contract obligor (LL-2026-04, A3-2-01, A4-1-01 eligibility, Form 582), the NMLS-licensed lender in every property state and the employer/sponsor of every MLO of record, the ECOA creditor, the Colorado "deployer", the GLBA financial institution with the customer relationship, and the Reg Z/B/C/X record-keeper. SM is the Fannie Mae-approved servicer and TSP operating under partner-scoped credentials, the holder of whatever processing/underwriting credential a state requires of a non-employee fulfillment company, the warehouse lender as secured party (never the creditor), the Colorado "developer", the partner's service provider under §1016.13, an FTC-jurisdiction financial institution and Form 101 Subordinate Subscriber in its own right, and the operator of the evaluation, monitoring, records and security machinery. Records of loans sold to Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property held "solely for the benefit of Fannie Mae"; records of applications that never fund are the partner's.

**Dependencies.** Origination: 20.2/20.3/21.1 consume `SM_LICENSE_STATE_GATE`, the `mlo_roster` and `jurisdiction_rules.licensing`; 20.3 owns the AI-interaction disclosure gate that satisfies Utah and the Colorado pre-use notice trigger; 21.6 owns the Colorado consumer artifacts (`NTC_CO_SB26_189_ADMT_NOTICE`, the 30-day explanation and human-review timers, `co_admt_3y`) and the `adverse_actions` reasons that 31.2 tests for accuracy; 22.x/23.3 route human touchpoints through `licensed_specialist`/`underwriting_reviewer` per 31.1; 25.1 keeps per-loan `license_checks` and `pricing_exception_reviews`; 26.2/26.3/27.1 depend on the eMortgage gate and the warehouse legal form; 28.3 supplies HMDA outcomes and demographics to the monitor; 29.x depends on TSP certification gates; 30.2 delivers the fair-lending elements to 19.4. Servicing: 18.3 (`FHFA_ELIG_QUARTERLY_TEST`, Form 1002), 18.4 (`FNMA_A4102_FORM582_FYE_90`, org-change notices), 19.1 (`retention_classes`, `record_objects`, `legal_holds`, disposal and productions), 19.2 (incident tables, 36-hour/72-hour/30-day timers, control tests), 19.3 (`ai_systems`, `vendors`, `contract_clauses`, Form 101, `FNMA_LL2026_04_*`, `FNMA_A2101_*`), 19.4 (`ai_bias_tests`, `restricted_fl`, methodology) — all referenced, never redefined.

### Section open questions

1. **(31.1-1) SM entity credential strategy per state** — default: obtain the specific processing/underwriting credential where one exists (OH exemption letter, NC registration, IL entity license, TX company license) and a broker/lender license where that is the only way to sponsor individual licensees (WA, AZ, CT, CA); route human touchpoints to partner employees elsewhere; unverified states stay closed.
2. **(31.1-2) Is SM a "third-party originator" under A3-3-01; any SFC?** — default: run the TPO program over SM; deliver no SFC 211/212; obtain the account team's written view before the first delivery.
3. **(31.1-3) Warehouse legal form** — default: secured loan to the partner (no table funding); counsel opinion on §1024.2(b)/§1024.5(b)(7) before go-live.
4. **(31.1-4) AI-intake position per state** — default `assisted_required` everywhere; `autonomous` only on a written state memo reviewed annually.
5. **(31.1-5) SM employees as MLOs of record** — default: never; every MLO of record is partner-employed and partner-sponsored.
6. **(31.1-6/7) NMLS B2B Access vs manual lookups; A/A remittance assumption in the eligibility arithmetic** — defaults: B2B if available, else the 25.1 30-day manual procedure; A/A, confirm C2-1.1.
7. **(31.2-1) Materiality thresholds and small-cell/pooling rules** — default: 5 pp / 10 bps / OR outside 0.80–1.25; cell 10; pooling 30; revisit after two quarters.
8. **(31.2-2) Counsel-directed monitoring for privilege** — default: yes; findings still acted on within 30 days.
9. **(31.2-3) California CCPA/ADMT applicability to mortgage decisions** — default: treat as applicable from Jan 1, 2027 for California applicants pending counsel's memo.
10. **(31.2-4/5) Partner approval of each high-risk deployment; 10-business-day version-change notice** — defaults: yes; 10 days (1 day for emergency fixes with post-hoc assessment).
11. **(31.2-6/7) BISG for "not provided" applicants; approvals without reviewer action treated as "substantially replaces human decisionmaking"** — defaults: yes (monitoring only); yes (conservative).
12. **(31.3-1) HMDA 3-year anchor** — default: the March 1 submission date; confirm the §1003.5 sentence.
13. **(31.3-2) Unfunded-file purge at the effective (max) date** — default: yes; no defensive over-retention of non-customer PII.
14. **(31.3-3) RON recordings — provider custody with access right vs copies** — default: provider custody with quarterly access tests.
15. **(31.3-4/5) State origination-record and breach-notice matrices** — defaults: longest known class for unknown states; breach clocks fail-closed to `attorney` until loaded (AZ, OH, TX, CA, FL, NY first).
16. **(31.3-6) Biometric/voice data** — default: none retained by SM; vendors under statutory duties with flow-downs.
17. **Cross-section:** servicing 19.4's Colorado impact-assessment timers and `impact_assessments` should be re-based to SB 26-189 (no assessments; 3-year records; developer/deployer roles); 21.3's `NTC_REGX_1024_33A_SDS` retirement (00a-fed §17) is unrelated to §31 but noted for the orchestrator.
