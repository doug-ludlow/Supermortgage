# Section 14 — Bankruptcy

## Overview

Section 14 is the platform's **Bankruptcy Engine**. When a borrower becomes a debtor under title 11, almost every other section changes behaviour: collections stop (11 U.S.C. §362(a)(6)), foreclosure freezes (§362(a)(4)–(5)), post-petition fees become noticed memo items (Fed. R. Bankr. P. 3002.1(c)), the periodic statement switches to a Reg Z §1026.41(f) variant or is exempted, credit reporting gets a Consumer Information Indicator overlay, payments split between a pre-petition arrearage ledger and a post-petition "maintain" ledger (§1322(b)(5)), and the servicer acquires court-facing duties whose deadlines are enforced by sanctions (Rules 3001(c)(2)(D), 3002.1(h)). Four processes: **14.1 Bankruptcy monitoring & Proof of Claim** — detection (PACER Case Locator API, Electronic Bankruptcy Noticing, monitoring vendor, borrower/attorney notice), the case record, automatic-stay propagation, referral to retained counsel on Fannie Mae's clocks (Guide Chapter E-2, Exhibit F-2-01), the pre-/post-petition payment-application engine, the Form 410/410A claim package, plan review, the motion-for-relief decision engine, cramdowns (SVC-2025-06), reaffirmation, discharge/dismissal handling, Fannie Mae reporting and expense hooks; **14.2 Payment change notices (Rule 3002.1)** — the full rule as amended December 1, 2025 (Form 410S-1 ≥21 days ahead, Form 410S-2 within 180 days, mid-case status responses, the 28-day end-of-case response on Form 410C13-NR, untimely-notice and sanctions rules); **14.3 Periodic statement in bankruptcy** — the §1026.41(e)(5)/(f) decision engine plus the stay/discharge-safe communications matrix (Reg X §1024.39(c), Reg F §1006.6(b)(2), §524(j)); **14.4 Credit reporting suspension in BK** — rebuilt, since Guide topic C-4.1-02 is retired, as the bankruptcy feed into Section 8.3's overlay engine.

**Architecture fit.** The `bankruptcy-ops` agent (baseline §8) owns the section end-to-end: it verifies filings, maintains the `bankruptcy` case (`cases` plus the `bankruptcy_cases` extension), drives the Timer Engine (every Fannie Mae and FRBP deadline is a `timer_definition`; a new `roll_rule` attribute implements Rule 9006(a) forward/backward counting on `court:<district>` calendars), computes claim and ledger figures deterministically from `ledger_entries`, drafts every court paper from versioned Official Forms in the Notice Registry, and escalates to **retained counsel (`attorney`)** for anything docketed, to `signing_officer` for declarations under penalty of perjury, to `fnma_portal_operator` for the SMDU cramdown-reporting UI, and to `officer` for Form 20 and voluntary-repurchase decisions. Integrations: `pacer`, `ebn`, `bk-monitor`, `attorney-network`, `fnma-smdu`, `fnma-p360`; governance via `agent_decisions` (LL-2026-04); retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y` plus a `court_record_7y` class for filed papers.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** The partner is the Fannie Mae servicer of record; counsel is selected under A4-2.2-01 (Form 200; Fannie Mae "no objection" within 15 business days) under a limited retention agreement with Fannie Mae and the servicer. Supermortgage performs every operational step, appears in filings as the servicer/authorized agent (creditor naming per 14.1-Q1), signs declarations through its own `signing_officer` under the partner's resolution, monitors counsel and the read-only DRA, and prepares Fannie Mae submissions; the partner stays liable for timelines, including counsel's delays (E-2.1-10). Chapter 11 cases and cramdowns are non-routine litigation (Form 20) decided by Fannie Mae.

**Dependencies.** 1.1/1.6; 2.1–2.3/2.7 (holds, suspense, autodraft pause, late-charge overlay); 3.2/3.3 (`BK_3002_1_PAYMENT_CHANGE_21`); 4.x; 5.7 (status codes 65/66/67/59/69/3L/3M); 7.1–7.4; 8.3; 9.x; 11.x; 12.x; 13.x; 15.2 (fees within 60 days of milestone); 16.1; 17.x.

**Changes in flight (verified 2026-09-09).** Rule 3002.1 was restyled Dec. 1, 2024 and **substantively amended Dec. 1, 2025** (HELOC annual notices, untimely-notice effects, new Forms 410C13-M1/M1R/N/NR/M2/M2R; trustee notice within 45 days and creditor response within **28 days** — the blueprint-era 21-day response is gone). The Dec. 1, 2026 rules package touches Rule 9006 only conformingly. SVC-2025-06 (Nov. 12, 2025) updated the Allowable Bankruptcy Attorney Fees exhibit and requires voluntary repurchase of recourse/indemnification loans before a cramdown (E-2.3-03, mandatory Feb. 1, 2026); the Bankruptcy Cramdown FAQs (Sept. 8, 2025) route cramdown reporting through the SMDU UI. The PACER PCL API guide is the August 2026 version. LL-2026-04 applies to the agent's decision records.
