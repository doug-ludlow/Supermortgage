# Supermortgage Origination Platform — Build Specification: Master Index

**Version 1.0 · 2026-09-11 · Fannie Mae conventional conforming, 1–4 unit first liens · whole-loan cash execution, servicing retained by the partner, subserviced by Supermortgage**

This is the index to the origination-side counterpart of the Supermortgage Subservicing build specification. It covers the loan's life from the moment a refinance opportunity is detected on the subserviced book (or an organic lead arrives) through application, verification, Desktop Underwriter, valuation, title and insurance, compliance testing, the Closing Disclosure, closing and funding on the Supermortgage warehouse facility, quality control, delivery to and purchase by Fannie Mae, and boarding onto the subservicing platform. There was no source blueprint for this side; the 51-process inventory was derived from the lifecycle itself and then every row was researched against primary sources as of September 10–11, 2026 and written up as a full engineering spec: data model, state machine, timers, business rules with worked arithmetic, integration contracts, AI agent design, edge cases, numbered test cases, audit evidence, open questions with defaults, and sources.

## Corrections to the blueprint

The inventory was written to sources as they stood in the research digests on September 10, 2026. Process-level research found the following material corrections, all now reflected in the specs.

**Fannie Mae policy is more current than a 2024-era spec would assume.** VantageScore 4.0 went live for every seller on September 9, 2026 (LL-2026-06) — optional, tri-merge, one model per loan, DU-only, SFC 067, with its own LLPA grids — and there is no bi-merge and no deliverable FICO 10T. DU casefiles have no minimum credit score (SEL-2025-09); the 620/640 floors survive only for manual underwriting, which the specs do not use. DU 12.1's June 2026 tightening and its September 25, 2026 update key on submission, not casefile creation. Chapter B3-3 was renumbered (VVOE is B3-3.1-04; self-employment B3-3.5; rental income B3-3.8, mandatory for applications on or after November 1, 2026, with a 30% ADU cap and a 12-month management-experience test). Limited Review for condo projects ended for applications on or after August 3, 2026, and Full Review reserves rise from 10% to 15% on January 4, 2027. Property insurance moved to a replacement-cost basis with a roof exception (LL-2026-03), and no Guide mandates flood-premium escrow for a non-bank seller — only monthly BPMI escrow is non-waivable (B2-1.5-04). The 09.09.2026 LLPA matrix has no escrow-waiver adjustment. LCOR cash back is the greater of 1% or $2,000 (SEL-2025-08). The application-stage ROV disclosure was removed by SEL-2025-07. Post-closing QC is a 90-day cycle measured from the month of disbursement (D1-3-01), and fraud self-reporting has a hard 30-day clock through Loan Quality Connect (A3-4-03). B2-1.3-04 has no "general advertising" carve-out and bars pre-arranged refinance terms, so the self-improving mortgage is a standing portfolio-wide program, never a covenant.

**Committing, delivery and purchase work differently from the inventory's sketch.** Commitments are C2-1.1 (mandatory) and C2-1.2 (best efforts), not "C1-1/C1-2": a best-efforts commitment is not a borrower lock, runs 1–90 days with a 30-day extension cap and a 60-day closed-loan auto-extension, requires a one-business-day key-data-change notice, needs a DU Approve/Eligible no more than 60 days old, and closes within one business day of funding. Custodian receipt is due by first-morning delivery the day before expiration; loans may be no more than six months old from first payment to Purchase Ready; the 45-day rule runs from the reported LPI date. Whole-loan funding is event-driven (data by 9:00 p.m. ET, documents by first morning, certification by 4:00 p.m. ET, proceeds the next business day), and the bank credit — not the advice — triggers settlement. Purchase-month interest is settled in the price (C2-1.1-06). Whole-loan wires go by Form 482 payee code matching the bailee letter's exact Letter Name (ULDD SID 642/650.1); the integration digest's "SID 398.3" is MBS-only. Loan Delivery has no API or direct integration — every submission ends in a human operator action from a frozen, hashed package — and EarlyCheck is a Direct Integration XML service whose clean run does not guarantee a clean import.

**Consumer regulation changed in 2025–2026.** The RESPA Servicing Disclosure Statement (§1024.33(a)) is reverse-mortgage-only; forward loans carry the servicing statement on the Loan Estimate (§1026.37(m)(6)). CFPB Circulars 2022-03 and 2023-03 on AI adverse-action reasons were withdrawn May 12, 2025; comments 9(b)(2)-1 to -9 govern. The CFPB's Reg B final rule (effective July 21, 2026) removed the effects test from §1002.6(a). Colorado repealed and re-enacted its AI Act as SB 26-189 (signed May 14, 2026): pre-use notice, a 30-day adverse-outcome explanation, correction, commercially reasonable human review, three-year records, ECOA/FCRA deemed compliance — operative for decisions on or after January 1, 2027, not June 30, 2026. The 2026 QM loan-amount tiers ($137,958/$82,775), QM points-and-fees tiers ($4,139/$1,380), HOEPA thresholds ($27,592/$1,380) and the HPML appraisal exemption ($34,200) are current; §1026.43(e)(3)(iii)'s points-and-fees cure applies only to loans consummated on or before January 10, 2021, so the inventory's 210-day cure path was dead and has been replaced. The TCPA one-to-one consent rule was vacated; AI voices are "artificial" (FCC 24-17); revocation must be honored within 10 business days. FinCEN's 2024 AML program proposal was withdrawn (an April 2026 proposal is pending) and CIP remains reserved for non-bank originators; OFAC records run 10 years from unblocking. "12 CFR 1022.90" does not exist — the Red Flags Rule for a non-bank is 16 CFR 681. Reg X §1024.17 has no escrow retention period. The SFHDF's OMB approval expires September 30, 2026, so the form version is a parameter.

**Technology facts that moved.** MERS Procedures are Release 26.1 (May 17, 2026): MOM registration within 7 calendar days of the note date; eNote registration within one business day of the tamper seal; the eRegistry deletes the Secured Party on Transfer of Control and has no Interim Funder role of its own. UAD 3.6 is required November 2, 2026 (UAD 2.6 retires May 3, 2027). ULDD Phase 5 (5.2.0) is current with no future mandate; the UCD v2.0 mandate date is deferred to a Q4 2026 announcement. The PE–Whole Loan API covers best-efforts pricing and committing only; mandatory commitments, pair-offs and extensions are UI or Sales Desk. The public rails are real and free: the FFIEC HMDA Filing API, the Rate Spread API, OFAC SLS, IRS IVES A2A, SSA CBSV, FinCEN SDTM.

**Arithmetic and citations.** The fixture P&I is $3,402.62, not $3,402.63; a 45-day lock from October 7, 2026 expires Saturday November 21; the 45-day initial-escrow deadline from a November 6 settlement is December 21; the prepaid-interest convention is 365-day rounded per diem ($1,785.43 for November 12–30); the fixture escrow figures are 30.3's ($6,400 taxes + $1,850 hazard; $687.50 monthly; $2,062.50 initial deposit). Guide topics that the inventory mis-cited or that were renumbered (B3-3.x, B3-6-05/-06/-07, B7-1-03/-04, E-2-01, F-1-03/A4-1-02, C1-3-01, A3-3-01) are corrected in each process's "Discrepancies vs blueprint."

## The structural decisions that changed the design

The subservicing spec's defining question was whether an AI voice satisfies "live contact." The origination spec has three analogues, each built behind a flag with a human path rather than assumed away.

**Who takes the application.** The SAFE Act and Reg H define a loan originator as an individual who takes an application or offers or negotiates terms; the clerical exemption is written for a person, not software, and §1026.36(g) puts the MLO's name and NMLSR ID on the application, LE, CD, note and security instrument. The specs therefore run every interview, quote and lock through an `mlo_of_record` — a partner-employed, partner-sponsored licensed individual attributed on every particular-terms screen and document, who approves LE terms and every lock — with `origination.ai_mlo_intake='assisted'` as the default and `autonomous` permitted only on a written state-specific memo reviewed annually. Supermortgage staff are never MLOs of record unless Supermortgage itself holds a sponsoring license in that state.

**Who is the lender when Supermortgage funds the closing.** RESPA's table-funding definition (§1024.2(b)) makes the party that advances funds and takes assignment at settlement the lender. The warehouse is therefore structured as a recourse secured loan to the partner (a loan-and-security agreement, not a purchase at closing), with Supermortgage perfected by possession or control and named Secured Party on the eRegistry; the purchase-at-closing form is refused by configuration without an officer-and-counsel record, and no California advance is made until Financing Law licensing is resolved.

**What Supermortgage itself is.** Whether Supermortgage is a third-party originator under A3-3-01, a §1026.36(d) loan-originator organization whose flat per-loan fee counts toward points and fees, and a "loan or finance company" needing its own §1029.210 AML program are unresolved; the specs run the TPO program over Supermortgage regardless, compute points and fees both ways and block on the inclusive figure, and give Supermortgage its own AML program with joint SAR filing.

## Build sequence

**Stage O-1 — Foundation.** The ULAD data model and `applications` aggregate, the Timer Engine's origination units (`business_days_creditor`, `business_days_regz_specific`) and definitions seeded from the registry, the fee/tolerance engine and the LE/CD engine with an exact Appendix J APR, the Notice Registry's origination templates, the compliance-test engine, and the document templates for the Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac uniform instruments. Sections §21, §25, 26.1 and the pricing core of 20.4.

**Stage O-2 — Rails.** Fannie Mae TSP onboarding and the two adapters (`fnma-di` for DU, EarlyCheck, UCDP and UCD over signed XML; `fnma-api` for Property Data, appraisal findings, AMI, pricing/committing and purchase advice over OAuth), credit and verification vendors, AMC and property-data collection, MI, flood, title and wire verification, the eClosing/RON platform, eVault and eRegistry, the warehouse bank and the document custodian. Sections §22, 23.1, §24, 26.2–26.4, §27, O10. TSP onboarding (2–6 months) and eMortgage approval are on the critical path.

**Stage O-3 — Judgment.** The underwriting agent and credit decision, DU findings interpretation, valuation review and ROV, fraud, adverse action, prefunding and post-closing QC, secondary execution. Sections 21.6, 23.2–23.4, 24.2, 22.6, §28, 29.1–29.2.

**Stage O-4 — Hand-off.** Post-purchase setup, boarding at funding, escrow establishment and the first-90-day timer seeding into the subservicing platform. Section 30, which consumes servicing sections 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10.

**Cross-cutting from day one.** The licensing program (state processor/underwriter matrix, MLO roster, NMLS renewals, Form 582 inputs), the LL-2026-04 AI governance program with fair-lending monitoring, and records/privacy/security. Section 31. The AI governance program is contractual under the partner's Lender Contract and flows down to Supermortgage; it cannot be retrofitted.

## Open decisions requiring a call

Each process carries its open questions with a recommended default so the build can proceed. Consolidated:

**Commercial and structural.** Program framing for the self-improving mortgage (standing program; 25 bps benefit floor plus positive 84-month NPV; no Supermortgage-product marketing from servicing data). The economics of the borrower rate pass-through: residual premium above the 12.5 bps credit cap, variance allocation (execution variance to future credits; post-closing price/LLPA variance to the partner; third-party cost variance to Supermortgage within 10%), 25 bps servicing strip with A/A remittance and no buy-up/buy-down. Lock policy (15/30/45/60-day periods, cost-pass-through extensions, one float-down, no pre-LE locks). Cure and fee allocation between the parties. Warehouse terms (monthly interest capitalization, 45/60/90-day aging ladder, daily haircut reserve) and a best-efforts-only first six months. Custody (the partner's Form 2017 custodian as Supermortgage's bailee; vendor eVault at launch). Who the humans are: reviewers, QC officers and Loan Delivery operators as partner employees or Supermortgage staff under written delegation and licensing.

**Legal positions to take.** The `assisted` AI-intake model and the state memos that would permit `autonomous`. The warehouse's legal form and the counsel opinion on table funding. Supermortgage's status under §1026.36(d), §1026.32, A3-3-01 and the BSA. No affiliated business arrangement and no separate Supermortgage GLBA notice. The application and disclosure boundaries (the soft-pull authorization as the sixth TRID item; prefilled data counts on confirmation; video is not "in person"; consummation is note-signing time; only e-sign, courier or signed receipt rebuts the mailbox presumption). Rescission (two copies per consumer regardless of channel; H-9 loans hold the entire disbursement). Fannie Mae sends the §1026.39 letter; no at-settlement §1024.33 notice; the note, CD and first-payment letter name "Supermortgage, as servicer for [Partner]." Texas 50(a)(6) closings are wet-ink only; no RON in Georgia or Mississippi. Colorado SB 26-189 applied by residence or property from January 1, 2027, with the pre-use notice from December 1, 2026; California's ADMT regulation treated as applicable from January 1, 2027.

**Policy elections.** Calendars (`business_days_creditor` = Monday–Friday excluding federal holidays for the LE, Reg B, Reg X, §1026.19(b)/(g), §1002.14, §1026.35(c) and VVOE clocks; `regz_specific` holidays on their statutory dates; SIFMA calendar for commitments). E-sign portal receipt as delivery evidence; waivers never solicited; a general section-J lender credit for Supermortgage-borne costs; 365-day prepaid interest; the conservative 30-day Reg B clock from the receipt date; a 14-day NOIA; combined C-4 counteroffers. DU Approve/Eligible only — no manual underwriting, overlays or variances; Supermortgage-initiated term changes are counteroffers; HPML QMs allowed with escrow. UAD 3.6 from launch; AMC-only valuation with human ROV decisions; flood premiums escrowed on SFHA loans; both MI options shown with standard coverage as default. UCD v2.0 with v1.5 fallback; same-servicer escrow credited to the new loan with consent; every funding wire human-released; officer approval for mandatory execution. Marketing: click-to-dial only to established-relationship customers with an MLO on the call; prescreening off; SMS and AI voice disabled in the two-party-consent and mini-TCPA states pending the matrix.

**Technical confirmations with Fannie Mae and vendors.** Whether a TSP System ID can create DU casefiles under the partner's seller number; whether any Loan Delivery direct-integration channel exists for TSPs; whole-loan wire-nickname import and ULDD Appendix D coverage of the newer identifiers; the Purchase Advice API schema and the partial-month interest convention; the UCD v2.0 mandate date; the §1026.39 sending practice; subservicing identification and the Escrow Setup deadline basis for newly acquired loans; the eRegistry Master Servicer field (29.4 and 30.1 default differently — reconcile before the first delivery); MERS one-business-day unit and reversal-on-rescission mechanics; custodian acceptance of loan-by-loan bailee letters and pre-positioned notes; NMLS B2B Access; CBSV/eCBSV enrollment.

## Unresolved legal questions that shape the design
1. Can an AI take an application and present particular terms under the SAFE Act? — `mlo_of_record` model; `autonomous` only on a written state memo (20.3, 21.1, 25.1, 31.1).
2. Is Supermortgage the RESPA lender when it funds the closing from its warehouse? — secured loan to the partner; purchase-at-closing refused by configuration; counsel opinion before the first advance (26.3, 27.1, 31.1).
3. Must Supermortgage and its staff hold processor/underwriter licenses, and may they act as reviewers? — credential per state; human touchpoints routed to partner employees elsewhere; unverified states stay closed (21.6, 23.3, 28.1, 31.1).
4. Is Supermortgage a third-party originator under A3-3-01? — TPO program run regardless; no SFC 211/212 delivered; account-team view before the first delivery (28.2, 29.3, 31.1).
5. Who sends the §1026.39 notice? — Fannie Mae, per its consumer-facing statement; sending in Fannie Mae's name only on written instruction (25.4).
6. Can delivery be automated without a Loan Delivery API? — human operator with evidence capture; any channel found replaces the import task, not the evidence model (29.3, 29.4).
7. When and to whom do Colorado SB 26-189 and California's ADMT rule apply? — pre-use notice from December 1, 2026; explanations and human review for decisions from January 1, 2027 (21.6, 31.2).
8. What is Supermortgage under Reg Z and the BSA? — flat per-loan fee; points and fees computed both ways; own AML program; written positions through 31.1 before launch (22.6, 23.4, 25.1, 28.4).

## How to read a section spec

Each process follows one structure: the blueprint row as written, then what the primary sources actually require as of the verification date with every discrepancy called out, then operational prerequisites with owner and lead time, then the build spec proper — inputs and triggers, data model, state machine, timers and gates, business rules with worked arithmetic on the fixture calendar (refinance application October 5, 2026; consummation November 6; disbursement November 12; purchase November 19; first payment January 1, 2027), integrations, outputs, AI agent design and escalation conditions, edge cases, numbered test cases, and audit evidence — then open questions with recommended defaults, then sources with verification dates.

Names are canonical across sections and shared with the subservicing spec: tables and columns in `snake_case`, events as `domain.entity.action` in past tense, timer codes as `SOURCE_SECTION_SUBJECT_DAYS`, notice templates as `NTC_` plus citation plus subject, process IDs `ON.M`, tests `ON.M-Tk`. Servicing-spec names (sections 1–19) are referenced by bare number and never redefined; the ownership map in the writer brief says which origination process defines each shared timer and notice. The architecture baseline addendum defines the shared vocabulary; a section that needs a new name defines it once in its own data-model subsection.
