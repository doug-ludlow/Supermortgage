### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**D1-1-01, Lender Quality Control Programs, Plans, and Processes (last updated 04/01/2026) — VERIFIED (extracted quotes).** "Fannie Mae requires the lender to develop and implement a QC program that provides a structure for identifying the deficiencies in the loan manufacturing process and for implementing plans to quickly remediate those deficiencies and underlying issues." "The lender's QC program must include a documented QC plan that establishes standards for quality and incorporates systems and processes for achieving those standards." The plan must contain "quality standards and measures, including: a general overview of the QC philosophy; QC plan objectives; specific risks to be measured, monitored, and managed; and methods used to ensure the QC program is an independent and unbiased function including program governance (targets, sampling)"; "detailed operating and reporting procedures for all employees involved in or affected by the QC process"; a file-review process confirming "compliance with the Selling Guide and the Lender Contract" and "compliance with applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations"; "a process for identifying a representative sample of loans for QC file reviews for both prefunding and post-closing QC"; "written procedures for reporting the results of the QC file reviews, including: the method of monthly reporting of review findings"; "a process for establishing a plan for specific corrective action to be taken when trends are identified"; "a process for reviewing and reporting on the QC work performed by the lender's QC vendors"; "maintaining all QC-related documentation for at least three years"; and "an audit process to ensure that the lender's QC policies, processes and procedures are followed by the QC staff." Quality standards: "A target defect rate must be established that is as reasonably low as possible"; "Fannie Mae may assess how the lender's chosen target defect rate affects Fannie Mae's risk and may provide input on a more appropriate target." Monitoring of third parties: "The lender's QC process must include procedures for monitoring the quality of work performed by employees, contractors, vendors, and other third parties involved in loan origination, property appraisal, processing, underwriting, valuation, and closing functions." Reporting: "no less than a monthly basis"; when trends are identified the lender must "establish a written action plan for specific corrective action to be taken, including the expected resolution and the time frame for implementation and completion." Records: "The lender must retain all written and electronic records that are created as part of the QC review process for at least three years" and "provide Fannie Mae with a copy of its records upon request." Audit: "The lender must have an independent audit process to ensure that its QC process and procedures are followed by QC staff, and that assessments and conclusions are recorded and applied consistently"; "Results of the QC audit must be distributed to the lender's management." Self-reporting: the lender must notify Fannie Mae within 30 days of confirming defects that make a loan ineligible, "using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect," and must "maintain records of all loans self-reported to Fannie Mae and provide upon request" (28.2 owns the self-report; a prefunding defect on an unclosed loan is corrected, not self-reported).

**D1-1-02, Lender Quality Control Staffing and Outsourcing of the Quality Control Process (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED (extracted quotes).** "all QC employees (including those related to establishing, monitoring, and enforcing procedures) must be independent of the production, underwriting, and closing departments"; "The lender must establish minimum requirements for the skill set and expertise of the staff managing and performing the QC file review process"; "The lender is responsible for ensuring that all individuals conducting QC reviews are adequately trained and have sufficient experience levels." Outsourcing: "Fannie Mae holds the lender fully accountable for its overall QC program … regardless of whether the work is performed by the lender itself or by an outsourced QC vendor"; "The lender's contract for services is not a substitute for the lender establishing and maintaining its own QC plan"; "The lender must ensure the QC vendor conducts file reviews in accordance with the lender's QC plan"; "The lender must perform a monthly review of at least 10% of the post-closing QC sample reviewed by the vendor to validate the accuracy and completeness of the vendor's work"; "This review must be performed by the lender itself and may not be contracted out"; vendor-review reports must include "a description of the sample selected for review, concurrence rates, and discrepancies identified by the lender." (The 10% vendor re-review is written for the *post-closing* sample; its application to prefunding work performed by SM is a partner policy decision — open question 2.)

**D1-1-03, Lender Quality Control Reporting (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED (extracted quotes).** Reports must "be in writing and shared with management monthly," "summarize results into a comprehensive, summary report of all QC findings," "communicate defects to responsible parties (that is, lender departments or business units)," and "use consistent methodology and terminology, including defect categories and severity levels," with "a description of each sample selection, including the sampling criteria, number of loans reviewed, and percentage of total eligible loans reviewed." Prefunding reports must include "defect trending for at least three months" and be "shared with management within 30 days of completion." Post-closing reports must carry "data for the highest severity classification defect rate, benchmarked against its defined target rate, at least quarterly" and distinguish "between defects related to compliance with applicable law and underwriting and eligibility defects" (28.2). The page does not define "gross" or "net" defect rates and does not use "senior management" — see 28.2 discrepancy 4.

**D1-2-01, Lender Prefunding Quality Control Review Process (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED (extracted quotes).** "The lender must maintain and implement a written prefunding QC plan that outlines requirements for reviewing a sample of its loans prior to closing or, in the case of loans acquired from a delegated third-party, prior to acquisition." Independence: "The lender's prefunding QC process must operate independent of the lender's production process, if practical"; "At a minimum, prefunding QC must be conducted by individuals who have no involvement in the origination, processing or underwriting functions of the loan being reviewed." Timing: "Prefunding QC reviews must be conducted early enough in the origination process to allow adequate time to make loan selections, complete the reviews, and properly inform the loan production staff so that corrections can be made prior to loan closing or acquisition"; "The results of prefunding file reviews provide important and timely feedback, allowing the lender to identify loans with defects … prior to closing or acquiring the loan." Selection: "The lender must establish and document a monthly process for selecting loans for its prefunding QC reviews"; "The process should take into account the lender's assessment of the risks inherent in its origination processes" (business sources, production channels, volume, product mix); "The monthly sample must include selections from each of the lender's production channels"; "Fannie Mae reserves the right to require the lender to make adjustments or impose minimum sampling requirements if a lender's approach does not provide adequate representation or effectively identify risk." Scope: "The lender's prefunding QC plan must contain requirements for full-file reviews and analysis of data and documents prior to funding"; "Full-file reviews are comprehensive reviews of an underwriting decision" and must assess "data entered into an automated underwriting system," "borrower Social Security number(s)," "income calculations and supporting documentation," "employment documentation, including verbal verification of employment," "assets needed to close or meet reserve requirements," "appraisal or other eligible collateral data or documentation," "adequate mortgage insurance coverage," and "occupancy"; "Component reviews focus on specific elements of a loan and typically can be completed more efficiently than full-file reviews" and "may be completed … in areas the lender believes pose unique or elevated risk."

**SEL-2026-03 (April 1, 2026) — VERIFIED.** "We reinstated flexibility in prefunding QC by eliminating the fixed 10% minimum sampling requirement"; "Removed requirements related to targeting areas with higher potential for errors, misrepresentation, or fraud, and the requirement for a minimum sample size"; "Clarified lenders may include randomly selected loans"; "Lenders are encouraged to implement these changes immediately but must do so for all QC reviews conducted on or after July 1, 2026." Updated topics: D1-1-01, D1-1-02, D1-1-03, D1-2-01, D1-3-01, D1-3-02, D1-3-03, B3-2-01, C3-7-04, A2-2-04. The superseded rule, for the record: Fannie Mae's Prefunding and Post-Closing QC FAQs (February 24, 2023) stated "The Selling Guide requires lenders to review the lesser of 10% or 750 based on the prior month's total closings."

**A3-4-03, Preventing, Detecting, and Reporting Mortgage Fraud (12/10/2025) — VERIFIED (extracted quotes; 00a-fnma §7).** The seller must "aggressively sample loans that have a high risk for fraud as part of the quality control process"; screen contractors/vendors against the FHFA Suspended Counterparty Program list, the GSA Excluded Parties List and the HUD LDP list; "A record of activity under the internal audit and management control systems must be maintained and made available to Fannie Mae upon request." The reporting duty ("within 30 days using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect") is 28.4's. **A3-4-02, Data Quality and Integrity (08/07/2018) — VERIFIED:** "All data entered into DU must be verifiable"; "The lender must have adequate procedures in place to validate the integrity of specific data for each underwriting recommendation"; "Fannie Mae may require the lender to correct data as needed."

**LL-2026-04 (April 8, 2026; effective August 6, 2026) — VERIFIED (00a-fnma §1).** AI/ML policies must "be transparent and communicated to appropriate personnel," "reflect risk management activities based on the lender's risk tolerance," and "have an owner(s) that implements, maintains and reviews the policies and procedures at least annually"; the seller must "Manage risks and appropriate governance of subcontractor and vendor use of AI/ML that is no less protective of these requirements" and "promptly disclose the types of AI/ML used, the purpose and manner for such use, the safeguards…" on request. Applied here: an AI QC reviewer is itself AI/ML use that the partner must govern; the QC agent's independence is a "safeguard" the partner can be asked to disclose.

**Records.** D1-1-01 three years for QC records; A2-4.1-02 (12/19/2017): loan records are "Fannie Mae's property," retained "at least four years" after liquidation (00a-fnma §8). Retention class for QC artifacts: `fnma_qc_3y` (defined here) layered under the loan file's `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` where the artifact is placed in the loan file.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.**
1. The row cites D1-1-01 for independence and staffing; the operative staffing/outsourcing text is **D1-1-02** and the reporting text is **D1-1-03** (both 04/01/2026). The row omits both.
2. **A3-4-02 is "Data Quality and Integrity," not the fraud-prevention program**; the fraud program and the "aggressively sample" duty are in A3-4-03 (12/10/2025).
3. The row's "selection, review scope, reverifications" implies a prescribed prefunding reverification set; D1-2-01 prescribes eight full-file assessment areas and permits component reviews, and **no minimum sample size or mandatory risk-targeting exists since SEL-2026-03** (mandatory for reviews on/after July 1, 2026). Reverification "directly from the source" is a post-closing requirement (D1-3-03, 28.2); prefunding reverification is a lender design choice.
4. "Monthly reporting" is right but incomplete: D1-1-03 requires prefunding results "shared with management within 30 days of completion" with "defect trending for at least three months," consistent categories and severity levels, and the sample description.
5. "Review completed before closing/funding" understates the rule: reviews must be timed so "corrections can be made prior to loan closing," i.e., selection must precede clear-to-close with slack for correction — `FNMA_D1_2_01_PREFUNDING_PRIOR_TO_CLOSING_GATE` (owned here) is asserted by 23.3 before clear-to-close (its `ctc_checklists` item `SM_QC_PREFUNDING_HOLD`).
6. Independence has two layers the row collapses: reviewer-level ("no involvement in the origination, processing or underwriting functions of the loan being reviewed") and organizational ("independent of the production, underwriting, and closing departments"; process independence "if practical").

### Operational prerequisites
- **Partner:** adopt the written QC plan (SM-drafted; D1-1-01 contents) including the prefunding plan, the target defect rate, the defect taxonomy and severity levels, and the designation of the `qc_officer`; a written delegation if the `qc_officer` is SM personnel (lead time: 2–4 weeks; before the first closing).
- **Partner:** vendor-oversight terms in the SM services agreement treating SM as the "outsourced QC vendor" (D1-1-02) — the partner's own review of SM's QC work (default 10% monthly of the post-closing sample, 28.2; prefunding per open question 2), concurrence reporting, and LL-2026-04 flow-down covering the `qc-audit` agent (with the SM agreement).
- **SM:** `origination/qc` package deployed as a separate service with its own model deployment, prompts, evaluation suite, credentials and a read-only replica of the origination schema; write access limited to the `qc_*` tables, `escalations`, `agent_decisions`, `loan_events{qc.*}` (Stage O-3; must exist before the first application reaches `ptd_cleared`).
- **SM:** `qc_officer` console (finding review, sign-off, monthly report), and a `qc_officer` roster with training records (D1-1-02 "adequately trained"); rule set `fnma.selling.2026-09-02` (D1 chapters), `fnma.qc.taxonomy.v1` (defect categories aligned to Loan Quality Connect's category/sub-category/defect drop-downs so that a post-closing self-report needs no re-mapping).
- **SM:** reverification vendor access for prefunding component checks (SSA CBSV, IRS IVES/DU transcript suppliers, VVOE sources — 22.3/22.6 adapters, called under a QC purpose code so production and QC calls are distinguishable in vendor logs).
- **Both:** the QC plan is available to Fannie Mae on request; the audit of the QC process (D1-1-01) is scheduled annually (policy) with results to partner management.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- Daily schedule `SM_QC_PREFUNDING_SELECTION_DAILY` (06:00 partner time zone) over every application in `applications.disposition ∈ {conditional_approval, approved}` whose 23.3 sub-status is `ptd_cleared` or later and which has no `qc_reviews{kind=prefunding}` row; plus event-driven selection on `ptd.cleared` (23.3), `closing.scheduled` (26.1) and `du.findings.received` with red-flag/observation messages (23.2).
- Risk features read from: `du_submissions.risk_factors`, `du_message_rules` outcomes (23.2), `document_integrity_checks` (22.1), `verifications`, `credit_reports`/`credit_alerts` (22.2), `income_calculations`/`employment_verifications`/`tax_transcript_requests` (22.3), `application_assets` large-deposit flags (22.4), `appraisals.cu_score`/`rov_requests` (24.2), `title_orders` (24.4), `mi_certificates` (24.6), `decisions.risk_assessment` (23.3), `agent_decisions` (model/prompt version, overrides), `escalations` (any human override in the file), `fraud_cases` (28.4/22.6), `locks`/`pricing_quotes` (exceptions), `jurisdiction_rules` (TX 50(a)(6), NY CEMA).
- Production status feeds for the hold: `ctc_checklists` items (23.3 includes the QC hold as a checklist item), `closing.scheduled`, `closing.consummated` (a review still open at consummation is a breach event).
- Human inputs: `qc_officer` decisions on findings and sign-off; partner management acknowledgement of the monthly report; borrower contact only for reverification where a source requires borrower authorization already on file (never for QC "re-interviews" without disclosure — see AI agent design).

#### State machine

`qc_reviews.status` (prefunding): `selected` (row created by the selection job; `production_hold_applied = true`; 23.3 receives `qc.review.opened{kind=prefunding}` and asserts `FNMA_D1_2_01_PREFUNDING_PRIOR_TO_CLOSING_GATE` before clear-to-close) → `in_review` (agent run started) → [`awaiting_reverification`]* (a `qc_reverifications` request is outstanding) → `findings_drafted` (agent conclusions written; findings in `draft`) → `officer_review` (any finding of severity ≤ 2, or the sampled no-defect case) → `findings_released` (findings `released`; `qc.finding.released` → the production side reopens conditions or reverses the decision under 23.3 rules) → `rebuttal` (production agent/`underwriting_reviewer` rebuts with evidence within the rebuttal window) → `closed` with `outcome` ∈ {no_defect, defect_corrected} (`hold_released_at` set; `qc.review.closed`) | `closed{defect_uncorrected}` (defect sustained and not correctable before closing — the loan cannot close as approved: 23.3 `decision.reopened`/21.6 adverse process; hold persists) | `cancelled` (application withdrawn/denied/expired before completion) | `unable_to_complete` (a reverification source fails and the `qc_officer` accepts the documentation of the attempt — the hold is released only by the officer). Guards: `selected → in_review` requires the reviewer identity to be the `qc-audit` agent (never a production agent run); `findings_released` requires `officer_review` when `min(severity) ≤ 2`; `closed{no_defect}` on an agent-only path is allowed only when no finding of severity ≤ 2 exists and the review is not in the officer's random sample; `hold_released_at` may be set only by `closed{no_defect|defect_corrected}` or by an `unable_to_complete` officer decision. Terminal: `closed`, `cancelled`, `unable_to_complete`.

| `FNMA_D1_1_03_PREFUNDING_REPORT_30` | deadline | last review of the month's sample closed (`sample_plan` period end + all reviews terminal) | `completion_date` = max(`closed_at`) for the period | +30 `calendar_days` | `qc.report.issued{prefunding_monthly}` and `qc.report.signed` | sev 1 → partner `officer`; report issued immediately with the gap noted |
| `SM_QC_REPORT_SIGNOFF_SLA_3BD` | deadline | `qc.report.issued` | `issued_at` | +3 `business_days_creditor` | `signed_by_qc_officer_at` | sev 2 → partner `officer` |
| `FNMA_D1_1_01_QC_AUDIT_ANNUAL` | recurring | schedule | each Jan 15 (policy) | annual; `calendar_days` | `qc.report.issued{qc_audit_annual}` distributed to management | sev 2 → partner `officer` |

Retention (not a timer): `fnma_qc_3y` on every `qc_*` row and report; the class is enforced by `security-records` (31.3). No jurisdiction overrides: prefunding QC is a Fannie Mae contractual requirement; state exam expectations are met by the same records.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Population and eligibility.** The daily job builds the prefunding population as every application that reached `ptd_cleared` in the last 24 hours or has a scheduled closing within 10 calendar days without a prior selection; a loan already selected is never re-selected; loans denied/withdrawn leave the population (`cancelled` if already selected). The monthly `qc_sample_plans` row is created on the first calendar day of the month from the prior month's eligible population and the forecast for the current month.
2. **Sample design (policy; no Guide minimum since July 1, 2026).** Random component: `random_target = max(ceil(0.05 × forecast_closings_month), 10)`, allocated proportionally across strata (`channel` ∈ {refi_trigger, organic, referral} × `transaction_type` × `occupancy`) with at least one selection per stratum with volume ("selections from each of the lender's production channels"); the daily draw uses a seeded uniform random over the day's new population with the acceptance probability `random_target_remaining / expected_remaining_population` so that the target is met evenly through the month. Risk-trigger component (unbounded; every hit is selected): any of `du_red_flag_message`, `document_integrity_checks.result = warn|fail` unresolved by 22.1 override, `credit_alerts` (fraud alert, SSN discrepancy, address mismatch), `income_source_self_employed` with a calculation variance > 5% between the Income Calculator and the agent, `large_deposit_unsourced_flag` cleared by human override, `gift_funds_pct_of_funds_to_close > 50%`, `dti ≥ 45%` or `ltv > 95%`, `transaction_type = cash_out`, `occupancy ∈ {second_home, investment}` with an existing primary within 50 miles of the subject, `property_type ∈ {condo, manufactured, 2_4_units}`, `non_arms_length`, `escalations` count ≥ 1 for a human override of an agent decision, first 90 days after any `model_version` or `prompt_version` change of `underwriter`/`verification`/`valuation` (model-monitoring stratum, LL-2026-04), `appraiser_license` or `settlement_agent_party_id` first seen in the last 90 days, `applications.score_model = vantagescore_4` during the first 6 months of use, TX 50(a)(6), NY CEMA, temporary buydown, Community Seconds. Each selection stores `selection_reason_codes[]`; a random draw that also hits a trigger is recorded as `risk_trigger` with `random_hit = true` so that random-sample statistics remain unbiased.
3. **Review type.** Random selections and fraud-family triggers get `full_file`; other triggers get `component` reviews scoped to the trigger's area plus `aus_data` and `ssn` (always). A component review escalates to full-file when any finding of severity ≤ 2 is found.
4. **Full-file checklist (D1-2-01's eight areas, executable).** (a) `aus_data`: recompute every DU input from `application_*` tables and compare with the last `du_submissions` request payload; any field difference outside B3-2-10 tolerances → finding `data_integrity/du_input_variance` severity 1 (DU limited waiver at risk, A2-2-04); a difference inside tolerance → severity 3 with a data correction. (b) `ssn`: SSA CBSV result present and `match` for every borrower (22.6); absent → order under `purpose_code = qc_prefunding`. (c) `income`: independent recomputation from source documents with the 22.3 formula ids; variance > 2% of qualifying income or any DTI crossing 45%/50% → severity 1 or 2. (d) `employment_vvoe`: VVOE present and within B3-3.1-04 windows (22.3 timer `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD`), the phone number independently sourced; if the note date is > 10 business days away the check is scheduled, not failed. (e) `assets`: funds-to-close and reserves recomputed; every large deposit sourced; gift letter elements present; asset documents pass `document_integrity_checks`. (f) `collateral`: appraisal/SSR/CU messages reconciled (24.2), value-acceptance offer age ≤ 4 months on the note date (24.1), UAD 3.6 form where required; a CU score > 2.5 with unreconciled messages → severity 2. (g) `mi`: coverage percent equals B7-1-02 for the LTV/term; certificate issued (24.6). (h) `occupancy`: declared occupancy consistent with distance-to-work, other REO, insurance type, mailing address; contradictions → `occupancy` finding severity 1 with a red-flag investigation (22.6). Always added: `compliance` (25.1 test results current; TRID clocks; Reg B decision date) and `fraud` (OFAC re-screen date, identity result, `fraud_cases` open).
5. **Severity and hold logic.** Severity 1 = the loan would be ineligible for sale or the decision is unsupported (must be corrected or the decision reversed); 2 = a material underwriting/eligibility deficiency correctable before closing; 3 = data/documentation deficiency with no eligibility effect; 4 = best-practice observation. `hold_released` requires no open severity-1/2 finding. The `qc-audit` agent may close a review `no_defect` alone only when the review is not in the officer's sample (rule 6) and has no severity ≤ 2 findings.
6. **Officer sampling of AI work (policy).** Every severity-1/2 finding goes to `officer_review` before release; additionally the `qc_officer` reviews a uniform random 10% of reviews the agent closed `no_defect` (the same proportion Fannie Mae requires a lender to re-review of a vendor's post-closing work, D1-1-02, adopted by analogy) and 100% of `unable_to_complete` proposals; the officer's concurrence rate is a monthly metric; a concurrence rate below 90% in a month triggers an 31.2 model review and doubles the officer sample for the following month.
7. **Monthly metrics (D1-1-03).** `prefunding_defect_rate_by_severity = findings(sev≤2, sustained) / reviews_completed`; `sample_pct = reviews_completed / eligible_population`; trend over the trailing 3 months; findings by category/sub-category/defect code; time from selection to release; officer concurrence; the corrective-action register with owners and due dates; a channel/product breakdown; the model-monitoring stratum's defect rate versus the random stratum (LL-2026-04 evidence). A "trend" (policy) = the same defect code in ≥ 3 loans or ≥ 10% of reviews in a month → mandatory written corrective-action plan with "the expected resolution and the time frame."
8. **Report timing arithmetic.** `completion_date` = the `closed_at` date of the last review in the month's sample; `report_due = completion_date + 30 calendar days`. Worked: October 2026 sample — last review closed Fri Oct 30, 2026 → report due Sun Nov 29, 2026; issued by policy on the last business day before, Fri Nov 27, 2026 (the day after Thanksgiving is a business day for the partner unless its calendar says otherwise). November 2026 sample — last review closed Mon Nov 30 → due Wed Dec 30, 2026.

**Worked example 1 — refinance fixture (Phoenix, AZ; $560,000 LCOR; consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026).** Application Mon Oct 5; DU Approve/Eligible Tue Oct 6; conditional approval Wed Oct 7; PTD cleared Tue Oct 27 (23.3). The daily selection run at 06:00 MST Wed Oct 28 evaluates the loan: triggers — none (DTI 38%, LTV 70%, primary, W-2 income validated by the DU validation service, no document-integrity warnings); the random draw accepts it (`random_target_remaining = 7` of a 12-loan October target; expected remaining population 96 → acceptance probability 0.073; the seeded draw 0.041 < 0.073 → selected). `qc_reviews` row `selected` at 06:00:12, `production_hold_applied = true`; `qc.hold.applied{kind=prefunding}` is emitted and 23.3 sets the CTC checklist item `qc_prefunding` = fail (23.3-T8 mirrors this). The agent opens a full-file review at 06:01: (a) recomputes the 41 DU inputs from the ULAD snapshot — all match the Oct 22 resubmission; (b) CBSV `match` on Oct 6 for the borrower; (c) income recomputed at $14,850.00/month from the two most recent paystubs and 2025 W-2 versus the underwriter's $14,850.00 — variance 0; (d) VVOE window: note date Nov 6 → the 22.3 VVOE is scheduled for Oct 26–Nov 5, one is on file dated Tue Oct 27 (8 business days before the note date, inside the 10-business-day window); (e) funds to close $4,212.80 against verified assets $61,940.11 (statements pass integrity checks; no deposit ≥ 50% of qualifying income); (f) value acceptance exercised — offer Oct 6, note date Nov 6, age 31 days ≤ 4 months; (g) MI n/a at 70% LTV; (h) occupancy: mailing address = subject, homeowner's policy is an HO-3 owner-occupied form, no other REO. Compliance: 25.1 tests current, `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` satisfied Oct 7. One severity-4 observation: the 2025 W-2 employer name is abbreviated differently from the paystub ("ACME Semicond." vs "Acme Semiconductor LLC") — recorded, no action. Review `closed{no_defect}` Thu Oct 29 10:42 MST (`SM_QC_PREFUNDING_REVIEW_SLA_2BD` due Fri Oct 30 — satisfied); not in the officer's 10% sample; `hold_released_at` set; 23.3 issues CTC Fri Oct 30; CD delivered Mon Nov 2; consummation Fri Nov 6. The review's `agent_decisions` row carries `rule_set_version = fnma.selling.2026-09-02`, `model_version`, `prompt_version` and the checklist evidence hashes.

**Worked example 2 — purchase fixture (Columbus, OH; $412,000 at 90% LTV with BPMI; HomeReady; closing Wed Nov 18, 2026, wet-funded same day).** Application Mon Oct 19; DU Approve/Eligible Tue Oct 20 with a large-deposit observation message (deposit of $14,600 on Sept 30 exceeding 50% of monthly qualifying income $7,000 → $3,500 threshold); PTD cleared Fri Nov 6. Selection run Mon Nov 9 06:00 EST: triggers `du_red_flag_message` (large deposit) and `gift_funds_pct_of_funds_to_close = 62%` (gift $28,000 of $45,320.55 funds to close) and `ltv > 95%`? no (90%) — selected as `risk_trigger`, `full_file` (fraud-family trigger). Review Mon Nov 9–Tue Nov 10: (e) assets — the gift letter on file states the amount and that no repayment is expected but omits the donor's relationship to the borrower and the donor's contact information, and the $14,600 deposit was accepted on the strength of a "bonus" note in the file without the employer's bonus statement; finding F1 `assets/gift_letter_incomplete` severity 2 (B3-4.3-04 elements), F2 `assets/large_deposit_unsourced` severity 1 (funds to close would fall to $30,720.55 if the deposit were excluded — short by $14,600); (h) occupancy consistent; (f) appraisal CU score 1.8, no messages; (g) MI certificate 25% coverage for 90.01–95%? no — LTV 90.00% → standard coverage 25% (B7-1-02 for > 20-year fixed at 85.01–90% is 25%) matches. Metrics: selection-to-release 1 business day; correction 3 business days; the November report lists two findings, one severity 1, defect code `assets/large_deposit_unsourced`, with a corrective action (the `verification` agent's deposit-sourcing prompt is tightened; 31.2 change record).

**Worked example 3 — cycle statistics.** October 2026: eligible population 118 closings; random target `max(ceil(0.05 × 118), 10) = 10`; risk-trigger selections 23 (of which 4 also random hits); component reviews 15, full-file 18; reviews completed 33 (28.0% of eligible); sustained severity-1/2 findings 3 → `prefunding_defect_rate_by_severity = 3/33 = 9.1%`; officer concurrence 100% on 3 released findings and 3 sampled no-defect reviews; trend check: `income/calculation_variance` in 2 loans (below the 3-loan trend rule) → watch list.

#### Integrations
- **Internal (read-only replica):** the `qc-audit` agent reads every origination table through a replica role `qc_reader`; writes only to `qc_*`, `escalations`, `agent_decisions`, `loan_events`. Production reacts to `qc.finding.released` through 23.3 (`reopenCondition`, `decision.reopened`) — QC never calls production commands.
- **SSA CBSV, IRS IVES/DU transcript suppliers, VVOE sources, credit refresh (22.2 soft refresh), OFAC (22.6):** existing adapters invoked with `purpose_code = qc_prefunding`; idempotent by `reverification_id`; retries per adapter; outage → `awaiting_reverification` with the SLA timer; portal fallbacks per 22.3/22.6.
- **Loan Quality Connect:** not used in prefunding (no self-report on unclosed loans); a prefunding finding that survives to a closed loan becomes an 28.2 item.
- **Print/e-delivery:** none to the borrower; the monthly report is an internal document (`documents`, class `fnma_qc_3y`) delivered to partner management through the partner console/e-mail with a read receipt.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `qc_reviews`, `qc_findings`, `qc_reverifications`, `qc_sample_plans` rows; per-review evidence bundle (checklist with evidence hashes, reverification responses, agent decision record); the release note to production (`qc.finding.released` payload with defect code, evidence and Guide citation).
- `qc_reports{prefunding_monthly}` (PDF + JSON metrics; retention `fnma_qc_3y`), the corrective-action register, the annual QC-process audit report; the written prefunding QC plan (versioned partner document).
- Ledger: reverification fees (`third_party_costs`, SM-borne under the Grander economics; D1-3-03's "The lender must pay any applicable fees charged by employers, financial institutions, or other third-parties" applies to post-closing, adopted here too).
- Feeds: 28.2 (prefunding findings as a discretionary post-closing trigger), 31.2 (model-monitoring stratum results), 29.3 (a sustained severity-1 finding blocks `submitDelivery` until corrected — via 23.3's decision state), 28.4 (fraud-family findings open `fraud_cases`).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`qc-audit` agent, prefunding profile (tools: `buildPopulation`, `planSample`, `selectLoans`, `openReview`, `recomputeDuInputs`, `recomputeIncome`, `recomputeAssets`, `checkCollateral`, `checkMi`, `assessOccupancy`, `runComplianceSnapshot`, `orderReverification`, `draftFinding`, `routeToOfficer`, `releaseFindings`, `evaluateRebuttal`, `closeReview`, `computeMetrics`, `draftMonthlyReport`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end behavior: it runs the daily selection, opens reviews, recomputes every underwriting figure from source documents with the same formula libraries as production but a separate deployment (the formulas are shared code; the model weights, prompts, credentials and runtime are not), orders reverifications, drafts findings with evidence and citations, routes severity-1/2 findings to the `qc_officer`, releases findings, evaluates rebuttals, closes reviews, computes the monthly metrics and drafts the report for signature. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{review_id, application_id, selection_basis, reason_codes[], checklist:[{area, tests:[{test_id, observed, expected, result, evidence_refs[]}]}], findings:[finding_id], reverifications:[…], rule_set_versions, model_version, prompt_version, inputs_hash, rationale, confidence, officer_review:{required, escalation_id, decision, at}, outcome}`.
Guardrails: the agent never writes to production tables, never clears or opens a production condition directly, never contacts a borrower except through an existing verification channel with a QC purpose disclosed, never uses `applicant_demographics`, never suppresses a finding on volume or closing-date pressure (the closing-date feature is not an input to the finding model), and never self-approves a severity-1/2 finding; independence is enforced by identity (`agent_id = qc-audit`), by database roles, and by a deployment boundary; the same rules of D1-2-01 that bar a reviewer "involved in the origination, processing or underwriting functions of the loan" are implemented as a check that no `agent_runs` row on the reviewed application carries the QC deployment's identity. Escalations: `qc_officer` (findings sev ≤ 2; no-defect sample; unable-to-complete; report sign-off), partner `officer` (report acknowledgement; hold-release disputes; breach of the prior-to-closing gate), `compliance-sentinel` (missed runs, SLA breaches), `underwriting_reviewer` (production side, when a finding reverses a decision — 21.6/23.3). Borrower-facing: none; a reverification call to an employer identifies the partner as lender and states the QC purpose; AI voice discloses automation. Human path: the `qc_officer` console allows a fully manual review with the same checklist and the same records.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Closing scheduled within 2 business days of `ptd_cleared` (rush): selection still runs; the review SLA compresses to the closing date minus 1 business day; if it cannot finish, 26.1 receives `closing.at_risk{reason=qc}` — the closing moves; the hold is never lifted for schedule pressure.
- Loan consummated with the gate closed (a gate bypass or a race with a wet closing): `qc.breach.closed_with_open_review` sev 1; the review completes as `post_closing_discretionary` (28.2); any sustained severity-1 finding blocks delivery (29.3) and, if the defect makes the loan ineligible, triggers 28.2's self-report clock.
- A reverification source refuses or does not respond (employer policy; bank): document request date and non-response (D1-3-03 practice), use an alternative (paystub + bank deposit pattern; The Work Number); `unable_to_complete` only by the officer.
- Finding rebutted with new evidence produced by the production agent: the QC agent evaluates; if accepted the finding is `withdrawn` with the rationale; if the rebuttal is itself a new document, `document_integrity_checks` run on it first.
- Application withdrawn/denied during review → `cancelled`; the partial review is kept (records) and counted in the report as cancelled, not in the defect rate denominator.
- Fraud-family finding (identity, occupancy, fabricated documents): the QC agent opens a `fraud_cases` row via 28.4's `openFraudCase` and stops further borrower contact; 28.4's timers start on the 28.4 detection rules, not on the QC finding.
- Model/prompt version change in the QC agent itself: the change is recorded (31.2), the officer sample doubles for 30 days, and the prior month's no-defect reviews are re-run in shadow mode to measure drift.
- Data-integrity finding inside DU tolerance but affecting HMDA/ULDD fields: severity 3 with a data correction routed to 28.3/29.3; no hold.
- Fannie Mae imposes "minimum sampling requirements" on the partner: `qc_sample_plans.random_target` becomes the greater of the policy and the imposed floor; the plan version records the Fannie Mae letter.
- Officer unavailable (holiday, Nov 11/26): the SLA rolls to the next business day; a second designated `qc_officer` is on the roster; no agent-only release of severity-1/2 findings.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **28.1-T1** Given the refinance fixture reaches `ptd_cleared` Tue Oct 27, 2026 and the Oct 28 06:00 MST draw selects it, then a `qc_reviews{kind=prefunding, selection_basis=random}` row exists, `production_hold_applied = true`, 23.3's checklist item `qc_prefunding` fails, and `SM_QC_PREFUNDING_REVIEW_SLA_2BD` is due Fri Oct 30, 2026.
- **28.1-T2** Given the review closes `no_defect` Thu Oct 29 10:42 MST with no severity ≤ 2 findings and the review is not in the officer sample, then `hold_released_at` is set by the agent, `qc.review.closed` is emitted and 23.3 can issue CTC Fri Oct 30.
- **28.1-T3** Given the purchase fixture is selected Mon Nov 9, 2026 by `du_red_flag_message` and `gift_funds_pct > 50%`, then `review_type = full_file`, and a severity-1 finding cannot reach `released` without a `qc_officer` decision (`SM_QC_OFFICER_FINDING_SLA_1BD` due Thu Nov 12 because Wed Nov 11 is a holiday).
- **28.1-T4** Given findings F1 (sev 2) and F2 (sev 1) are released Tue Nov 10 09:41, then 23.3 reopens conditions within the same transaction, `applications.qc_prefunding_status = defect_open`, and `assertGateOpen(FNMA_D1_2_01_PREFUNDING_PRIOR_TO_CLOSING_GATE)` returns closed until both findings are `corrected`.
- **28.1-T5** Given F2 is corrected Fri Nov 13 08:10 and the review closes `defect_corrected`, then CTC may issue and the CD delivered Fri Nov 13 supports consummation Wed Nov 18 (three Reg Z specific business days: Nov 14, 16, 17).
- **28.1-T6** Given a production agent run attempts `closeReview` or a QC agent run attempts `clearCondition`, then both are refused by role and identity checks and logged as sev 2 to `compliance-sentinel`.
- **28.1-T7** Given the DU request payload of Oct 22 shows monthly income $14,850.00 but the recomputation from the paystubs yields $13,900.00 (−6.4%), then a `data_integrity/du_input_variance` finding of severity 1 is drafted with both values and the evidence refs, and the loan cannot close until a DU resubmission (23.1) resolves it.
- **28.1-T8** Given a closing consummated Fri Nov 6 while a prefunding review is `in_review`, then `qc.breach.closed_with_open_review` fires at sev 1, the review continues as `post_closing_discretionary`, and 29.3's delivery gate reads the sustained-finding state.
- **28.1-T9** Given the October 2026 sample's last review closes Fri Oct 30, then `FNMA_D1_1_03_PREFUNDING_REPORT_30` is due Sun Nov 29 and the report is issued and signed by Fri Nov 27 with a three-month trend (Aug–Oct) and the sample description (33 of 118 eligible; 28.0%).
- **28.1-T10** Given the same defect code appears in 3 loans in one month, then the report contains a corrective-action plan with owner, expected resolution and due date, and `compliance-sentinel` tracks its completion.
- **28.1-T11** Given the officer's concurrence rate for a month is 85%, then 31.2 receives a model-review escalation and the officer sample rate for the next month is 20%.
- **28.1-T12** Given a reverification request sent Mon Nov 9 with no response by Thu Nov 12 (+3 BD) and Mon Nov 16 (+5 BD), then a second request is logged and the officer decides `unable_to_complete` or keeps the hold; the file records both request dates.
- **28.1-T13** Given any QC review, then its `agent_decisions` row contains `rule_set_versions`, `model_version`, `prompt_version`, `inputs_hash`, per-test evidence refs, and no field derived from `applicant_demographics` (access log shows zero reads by `qc-audit`).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Sample floor.** Default: `max(5% of forecast monthly closings, 10)` random plus unbounded risk-trigger selections, all channels represented; revisit quarterly against the defect rate; accept any Fannie Mae-imposed floor.
2. **Partner re-review of SM's prefunding QC work.** D1-1-02's 10% monthly lender re-review is written for the post-closing vendor sample. Default: the partner's `qc_officer` sample of 10% of no-defect prefunding reviews plus 100% of severity-1/2 findings satisfies the "lender itself" review; confirm with the partner's Fannie Mae QC Specialist whether Fannie Mae expects a separate partner-performed re-review of prefunding vendor work **[UNVERIFIED]**.
3. **Identity of the `qc_officer`.** Default: a partner employee; an SM person only under a written delegation, with organizational independence documented (no reporting line into SM origination operations) and 31.1 licensing where a state treats QC as licensed activity.
4. **Officer sampling rate of AI no-defect conclusions.** Default 10% (analogy to D1-1-02); 20% for 30 days after any QC model/prompt change.
5. **Hold waiver.** Default: none — a selected loan does not close until the review is terminal; the only release paths are `no_defect`, `defect_corrected`, or an officer's `unable_to_complete`.
6. **Prefunding reverification depth.** Default: CBSV always; VVOE present per 22.3; written income/asset reverification only when a trigger or integrity warning exists; tax transcripts only when tax returns were relied upon (mirrors D1-3-03's post-closing rule).
7. **Audit of the QC process.** Default: annual, performed by SM internal audit or a third party not involved in QC, results to partner management by Jan 31 for the prior year; Fannie Mae states no frequency **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — "an audit process" is required; frequency not stated]**.
