# Section 6 — Custodial Account Management

## Overview

Custodial account management is the cash-control spine of the platform. Every borrower dollar the platform touches — P&I, escrow, partial payments, loss drafts, buydown funds — must sit in a depository account that Fannie Mae has approved through a Letter of Authorization (Form 1013 for P&I, Form 1014 for T&I), be reconciled every month on Fannie Mae's Excel Forms 496/496A within 45 days of month-end, and be available on demand for Fannie Mae's ACH drafts. The Servicing Guide makes the servicer "responsible for the safekeeping of custodial funds at all times" and holds it liable for any loss "even if the servicer has complied with all of the requirements" (A4-1-02, 07/12/2023). Because the master servicer "remains fully liable to Fannie Mae for the performance of all servicing obligations" (A2-1-07, 05/13/2026), the partner will push every one of these controls onto Supermortgage contractually.

**Architecture fit.** The section is owned by the `custodial-recon` agent (`agents/custodial-recon`), with `cashiering` (deposits and posting), `escrow` (T&I composition), `investor-reporting` (remittances, LSDU cash position, draft notifications), `qc-audit` (independent review) and `compliance-sentinel` (timers, Guide watch) as collaborators. Core services used: the double-entry `ledger` (per-custodial-account `custodial_pi_cash`, `custodial_ti_cash`, `fnma_remittance_payable`, `servicer_advance_receivable`), the Timer Engine, the `integrations/framework` adapters `custodial-bank` (BAI2 / ISO 20022 camt.053 prior-day statements, camt.052/BAI2 intraday), `lockbox`, `nacha`, `fnma-lsdu` (cash position), `fnma-connect` (Remittance Detail – P&I report; Loan-Level Draft Notifications API), and `fnma-crs` (draft instructions/requests). Fannie Mae's Custodial Bank Account Management application (CBAM) is portal-only and therefore runs as `human_portal_task` escalations to `fnma_portal_operator`, with DocuSign signature by an `officer`.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** A2-1-07 is unambiguous: "Each subservicer must establish custodial accounts for all Fannie Mae mortgage loans that it subservices for a master servicer," separate from any other investor's funds and from loans it services directly for Fannie Mae. Supermortgage therefore opens and owns the depository relationships, titles the accounts as "(Supermortgage) as subservicer for (Partner), as agent, trustee, and/or bailee for the benefit of Fannie Mae …", executes Forms 1013/1014 in CBAM under the partner's 9-digit servicer numbers (Form 101 grants that access), reconciles, and funds shortages. The partner retains the Fannie Mae relationship, remains the "Lender ID" on every CRS draft and LSDU cash position, confirms the arrangement annually on Form 582, and receives every Form 496/496A package and every shortage notice. This resolves the architecture baseline §1 open decision in favor of Supermortgage-established accounts (the Guide leaves no alternative for a subservicer).

**Dependencies.** 1.2 (Form 629 — Fannie Mae requires evidence that custodial accounts exist before a subservicing transfer), 1.6 (boarding cash reconciliation), 2.1–2.4 (deposits, posting, ACH), 2.2 (partial payments feed 6.5), 3.2/3.5/3.7/3.9 (escrow balances, refunds, disbursements and state escrow interest feed 6.2/6.4), 5.1/5.2/5.4 (LAR/event reporting, remittance computation, Stop Delinquency Advance feed the P&I composition in 6.3), 9.7 (loss drafts held in T&I), **16.1/16.2** payoffs (escrow refunds within 20 days excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays and Sundays; compliance layer 7.6), 18.1/18.2 (QC and MORA evidence), 19.1 (retention).

**Changes in flight.** (1) CBAM became mandatory for Forms 1013/1014 on Aug. 1, 2026 (SVC-2026-04); the legacy PDF form URL now redirects to the CBAM page. (2) Forms 496/496A were converted to Excel (SVC-2026-01, Feb. 18, 2026) with a new Section III for itemised reconciling items. (3) LL-2026-05 will have Fannie Mae automatically draft A/A P&I two business days after payment-event processing, with a pre-draft notification the next business day, and will eliminate the Schedule 3 (Form 472) shortage/surplus reconciliation — the daily engine must be able to verify draft coverage before Fannie Mae pulls. (4) Escrow event reporting with a monthly UI attestation of escrow balances is mandatory Dec. 1, 2026 — the T&I reconciliation must tie to what is attested. (5) LL-2026-04 requires the reconciliation agent to be inventoried and governed as an AI system.
