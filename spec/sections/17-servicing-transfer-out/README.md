# Section 17 — Servicing Transfer-Out

## Overview

Section 17 is the exact mirror of Section 1. It covers everything Supermortgage must do when a Fannie Mae loan it subservices leaves the platform: the partner replaces Supermortgage with another subservicer (`sub_to_sub`), takes servicing in-house (`sub_to_master`), sells the servicing to a third party whose servicer or subservicer takes over (`servicing_sale`), or Fannie Mae terminates/transfers the partner's portfolio and rescinds recognition of the subservicing arrangement (`fnma_directed`; A1-2-02, A1-4.1-02, A2-1-07). In every case Supermortgage is the RESPA "transferor servicer" (it is the entity that receives payments, 12 U.S.C. 2605(i)(2)) and the outgoing "acting servicer" for Fannie Mae reporting (LL-2026-05 FAQ, research/00a §3.1), while the partner remains the Fannie Mae servicer of record that files Form 629 in Quick Exchange as "the Transferor" (Servicing Transfers User Guide: "This form must be completed by the Transferor"). A master-servicer-only change in which Supermortgage stays subservicer with no change of payee, address, account number or payment amount is **not** a transfer-out (§1024.33(b)(2)(i)(C)); it is handled as a Form 629 servicing transfer with continuity and is noted in 17.1.

The section is executed by the `transfer` agent (batch orchestration, Form 629 package, counterparties, notices, post-transfer support window), with `custodial-recon` (funds, final accounting, advances settlement), `escrow` (short-year statement, escrow interest through T-1), `investor-reporting` (final reporting period, BD2 close, shortage/surplus adjustment), `lossmit-underwriter` (in-flight applications and case handoff), `security-records` (document packaging, custodian shipment, retention and de-identification), `case` (NoE/RFI/complaints during the one-year post-transfer window), `borrower-comms` ("who do I pay" handling) and `compliance-sentinel` (timers, breach reporting). It uses the same tables as Section 1 — `transfer_batches` (new column `direction ∈ {in,out}`), `transfer_batch_loans`, `boarding_tapes` (now outbound), `misdirected_payments`, `custody_records`, `custody_recerts`, `mers_transactions`, `reconciliations`, `recon_variances`, `transfer_funds_receipts` — plus the transferor-side tables defined once in 17.3 (`transfer_out_deliverables`, `counterparty_notifications`, `transferee_requests`). Timer codes are reused from Section 1 wherever the legal obligation is identical (the `timer_definitions.trigger_event` filter becomes `transfer.batch.*{direction=out}`); new codes are introduced only where the transferor's duty differs. Event names: the canonical batch events are `transfer.batch.proposed/approved/loan_list_frozen/cutover_completed/closed` with `direction=out`; the projections already used by other sections — `transfer_out.approved` (5.1), `transfer_out.effective` (7.x), `loan.transfer_out.completed` (8.1), `payment_holds{transfer_out_cutover}` (2.2), `release_reason=transfer_out` (4.3) — are aliases emitted by the same commands.

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: the partner owns the Fannie Mae relationship (Form 629 submission and attestation in Quick Exchange, acceptance of approval conditions, Form 101 termination signature, the annual Form 582 confirmation, MERS Servicer-of-record status, the servicer number carried on the custodial account titles, any A2-1-01 technology-provider notices to Fannie Mae), and remains liable to Fannie Mae as master servicer of record for the funds throughout. **Custodial accounts are Supermortgage's, not the partner's** — A2-1-07 requires that "each subservicer must establish custodial accounts for all Fannie Mae mortgage loans that it subservices for a master servicer," so Supermortgage opened and holds the depository relationships, is the account holder of record on the Forms 1013/1014 executed in CBAM under the partner's 9-digit servicer numbers, and is the party that signs, reconciles and closes them (6.1 resolves the architecture baseline's open decision this way; 2.1 and 6.x build on it). Transfer-out therefore does **not** hand an account over: Supermortgage's Fannie Mae-only custodial accounts for the transferred population are **settled and closed by Supermortgage after the final reconciliation** (see 17.3 — balances wired to the transferee's own custodial accounts, `FNMA_F1_11_CUSTODIAL_RECON_5BD` cleared, the 30-day adjustment window run, then Forms 1013/1014 withdrawn in CBAM and the depository accounts closed to a zero balance); the transferee establishes its own accounts under its own servicer number. Supermortgage prepares every package, performs every operational step, sends the goodbye notice in its own name, reports the final period, wires the custodial balances out of its own accounts, delivers the data/document/funds package with a signed data-quality attestation, hands off every in-flight case, runs the 60-day misdirected-payment window and the one-year NoE/RFI window, and retains records. Fannie Mae portal steps (Quick Exchange, CBAM, Fannie Mae Connect downloads) are `human_portal_task` escalations with an AI-prepared package — no browser automation (Consolidated Technology Guide, Apr 21, 2026).

Dependencies: 17.1 is triggered by the partner's instruction or a Fannie Mae termination notice and gates every other 17.x process; 17.2 consumes the frozen loan list and the print-mail adapter (1.3 templates); 17.3 consumes 2.x (cashiering cut-off, `payment_holds`), 3.x (escrow balances, interest on escrow through T-1, short-year statement), 5.1/5.2 (final period close, TT32/Quick Exchange, remittance), 6.3–6.5 (custodial reconciliations, unapplied funds), 8.1/8.2 (final Metro 2 cycle status 05, ACDVs in flight), 9.x/10.x (insurer, MI notifications), 14.x/13.x (trustee and counsel notices), 16.x (payoff requests received before T), 19.1 (retention); 17.4 consumes 12.x, 13.x, 14.x, 9.7, 4.1/4.2/4.4/4.5 case state and hands it to the transferee using the 1.7 `CO-*` checklist in reverse.

Changes in flight that shape the build: Servicing Guide edition Aug 12, 2026 (A2-7-03, F-1-11, A2-1-07 last changed 05/13/2026); eTransfers retired Oct 31, 2025 — from Nov 1, 2025 "all servicing and internal transfers must be submitted via a 629 form in Quick Exchange" with loan additions by CD10, deletions and attestation by CD25 and processing "on Business Day 3" (Servicing Transfers Transition Guide); the Investor Reporting Manual (Apr 8, 2026) still carries Transaction Type 32 (IRM 3-01, 10/13/2021) — treated as superseded by the Quick Exchange loan list **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; LL-2026-05 event-based reporting (3:00 a.m. ET next business day; BD2 5:00 p.m. ET close) applies to the final period; Form 101 must be re-executed at termination of the subservicing arrangement and CBAM governs custodial account changes (SVC-2026-04); the CFPB's 2024 Reg X proposal is not final, so 17.4 hands off `regx.lossmit.2013` state with the NPRM behind the `regx.lossmit.2024nprm` flag; CFPB Bulletin 2020-02 remains the supervisory yardstick for transferor planning, data validation and post-transfer cooperation.
