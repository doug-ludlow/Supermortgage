### Blueprint row
Every month the servicer's books close in a fixed order: the last day's cash cut-off, the daily custodial three-way close for that day, the Metro 2 snapshot as of month-end, the LARs for the period, Fannie Mae's BD2 period close, the ledger period close per custodial account, the Form 496/496A reconciliations, the QC cycle, the STAR metrics, and at quarter-end the eligibility test; in January the same close also ends the tax year and starts the 1098, 1099-INT and 1099-A/C runs. Today every one of those pieces exists as a tool or a domain function — 5.1's `investor_reporting_periods.closed` (src/domain/investor/ops-5-1.ts:253), 6.3's `close_period`/`close_day` ops (src/app/tools/section6-3.ts:61-75), `form496.generate` (src/app/tools/section06.ts:113), 7.1's `closeTaxYear` (src/domain/notices/ops-7-1.ts:240-246), 3.9's (src/domain/escrow/ops-3-9.ts:80-90), `furnishForm1098` (src/runtime/servicing.ts:249) — but nothing on the hosted runtime calls them in order, nothing records that a month closed, and no one attests that the custodial ledger, the composition and the bank tie at month-end. The gap assessment lists this as blocking: "nothing on the runtime runs … a 6.x custodial reconciliation, an 8.x Metro 2 furnishing, an ARM change or a month-end close — those sections are compute tools an operator would have to invoke by hand with hand-fed state. The sweep's own body is refi/partner-book dailies, controls, FAKE reviewers, timer breach → escalation, and an outbox count" (evidence src/runtime/servicing.ts:205, 249; src/runtime/app.ts:246-300; src/runtime/main.ts:61-66 — each verified at HEAD, line numbers as of ee08b4b (2026-09-15): the sweep body at app.ts:246-300 runs the refinance check, the partner-book review and readiness, the daily reports, the kill-switch expiry, the FAKE reviewers and the breach pass, and nothing else; `ledger.month.ended` has no emitter anywhere under src/). This process is the month-end and year-end close as a measured thing: a `close_periods` row per period, one `close_period_steps` row per step with its dependencies, receipts recorded as they arrive from the owning sections' own events, a balance attestation to the cent, a reopen that leaves the original attestation untouched, and the tax-year close that turns 31 December into the 1098/1099 runs.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-15)
**Fannie Mae period close and finality (5.1; IRM 2-01, 11/13/2024, and 4-08, 04/08/2026 — re-read 2026-09-15).** The prior period's payment (non-removal) activity and its corrections must reach Fannie Mae "no later than 8 p.m. eastern time on the first business day of the month following the reporting period"; removal transactions processed on that day and removal corrections must reach Fannie Mae "by 5 p.m. eastern time on the second business day of the month following the reporting period" (IRM 2-01, p. 11; LL-2026-05 is not in the verification bundle); after period close "Fannie Mae will not accept any update to Action Code 60 after the applicable reporting period has closed" (IRM 4-08) — a closed investor-reporting period is final, and a later correction is a new period's item, never an edit. The close chain therefore treats `investor_reporting_periods.closed{checklist_complete=true}` as a receipt that a reopen never resets (rule 9).

**Form 496 identity and deadline (6.3, Servicing Guide F-1-03 and the Form 496 instructions, verified by 6.3 on 2026-09-09; F-1-03 re-read 2026-09-15 — the Guide requires the monthly Form 496/496A reconciliation and its retention, and the 45-day completion is the form instructions', which are not in the verification bundle).** One Form 496 per P&I custodial account per remittance type, "complete within 45 days after month-end"; the identity checked before the form is drafted is `L12 = cashbook balance = ledger custodial_pi_cash:<account>` at period close and Section I `L5 (adjusted depository) − L6 (cashbook) = 0`; "every reconciling item needs loan number, root cause, amount and aging"; the preparer "never posts a balancing 'plug'". 6.3's worked example (S/S MBS account, September 2026) is the figure set this process attests: adjusted depository $1,266,750.55, L12 $1,266,750.55, difference $0.00. The T&I form (6.4, F-1-03 Form 496A) closes on the same 45-day clock per T&I account.

**Month-end credit snapshot (8.1, Servicing Guide C-4.1-01, 07/15/2020, verified by 8.1).** The tradeline file reports the loan's status as of the last calendar day of the month; 8.1 builds it "00:05 ET on the 1st" as of `as_of_date` = month-end and transmits within 3 servicer business days (8.1's schedule; C-4.1-01 sets no transmission deadline).

**QC cycle and STAR (18.1 — Servicing Guide A4-1-01 sets no cycle dates, so BD3/BD20 are 18.1's policy; 18.3, verified by their files).** The monthly QC cycle "opens on the 3rd business day of each month for the prior month's population, after the investor-reporting close" and closes by BD20 with `qc.cycle.signed`; STAR metrics are computed by BD5 (`business_days_fannie_et`) after Fannie Mae's period close (`SM_STAR_COMPUTE_MONTHLY_BD5`); the quarterly eligibility test (18.7, `FHFA_ELIG_QUARTERLY_TEST`) computes by BD10 for Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec period ends.

**Tax-year information returns (7.1, 26 U.S.C. 6050H and 26 CFR 1.6050H-2; 3.9, 26 U.S.C. 6049; the sections' verification stands — this file re-read Pub. 1179 §4.6.3, Form 1098 (f1098) and Servicing Guide C-4.2-01 / F-1-23 on 2026-09-15, no other IRS page).** Form 1098 for each payer of record from whom $600 or more of interest was received in the calendar year, furnished by 31 January (7.1's `IRS_6050H_1098_FURNISH_0131` holds the calendar date — Sun 2027-01-31 for tax year 2026, no business-day roll, src/domain/notices/timers.ts:21 and 7.1-T13; Pub. 1179 §4.6.3 would treat a statement posted by the first business day after as timely, a relaxation that is 7.1's to adopt) and e-filed by 31 March of the following year (the General Instructions' date, not in the verification bundle); box 1 = interest received from borrower funds in the calendar year, box 2 = UPB as of 1 January; 7.1's default is to furnish below $600 and file only at or above $600 (7.1 open question 5). 1099-INT for escrow interest of $10.00 or more (3.9-T9: $24.88 → form; $8.40 → none), same furnish/file dates. 1099-A/1099-C are filed by the servicer — Supermortgage — electronically on Fannie Mae's behalf: Fannie Mae's name on the first Lender/Creditor line followed by the servicer's address and contact information, Fannie Mae's TIN 52-0883107 in the Lender's/Creditor's TIN field and the servicer loan number in the account-number field, for portfolio loans (participation pools at 50% or more) and MBS loans not serviced under the regular servicing option (and for a regular-servicing-option MBS loan the servicer did not purchase out of the pool before the property was acquired); in the owner of record's name for the other regular-servicing-option MBS loans and sub-50% participations; followed by a Summary of IRS Form 1099-A and 1099-C Filing (Form 1100) to Fannie Mae; and never a 1099-C for a loan Fannie Mae has assigned to a collection vendor (its list arrives by e-mail no later than January 5 of the year after the foreclosure sale) (Servicing Guide C-4.2-01, 06/09/2021, pp. 233–234; F-1-23, 06/09/2021, pp. 672–673 — re-read 2026-09-15). The tax-year close therefore opens 1099-A/C furnish and file steps beside the 1098 and 1099-INT steps, never a hand-off list.

**The cut-off (2.1, Reg Z 1026.36(c)(1), verified by 2.1).** A payment's `received_on` is set by the written cut-off per channel (`cashiering.eod.cutoff`); the month's last day therefore has a definite set of receipts once 35.5's daily cashiering cycle for that calendar day has run, and every downstream step (custodial day close, snapshot, LAR) reads that set, never a later one.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) The §35 plan (written at d1238cc) named the chain's period-close receipt `fnma.reporting_period.closed`; at HEAD 5.1's close emits `investor_reporting_periods.closed` (src/domain/investor/ops-5-1.ts:253, src/app/tools/section5-2.ts:13) and 6.3's `SM_FNMA_CONNECT_REMIT_DETAIL_PULL_2BD` still names `fnma.reporting_period.closed` as its trigger (spec/registry/timers.json) — the chain keys on the event the code emits, and 5.1's close appends the 6.3 alias in the same transaction until 6.3's row is re-registered (open question 1). (2) The plan had the stall clock run from `close.step.completed` to the next `close.step.started`; a blocked step is not stalled, it is waiting, and the Close board shows it blocked immediately (rule 4) — the clock here runs from a step's start to its completion, which is the thing that can actually go wrong unnoticed. (3) `human_approval_on` is a per-call input of `form496.generate` today (src/app/tools/section06.ts:79); a caller could omit it. Here the flag is read from configuration (`custodial.form496.human_approval`, default on) by the attestation tool, never from the request (rule 7). (4) The plan dated the seam's migration 0135; migrations run to 0141 (src/infra/db/db.test.ts:40 still counts 776 base tables — 0138–0141, added by 2.7, 34.1, 34.5 and 25.3 between a1ecc95 and ee08b4b, create no table), so this process's tables land at 0142 or later, append-only, after 35.1's. (5) The plan named three tables; the attestation and the reopen are money-bearing evidence an examiner reads on their own, so they are append-only tables of their own (`close_attestations`, `close_reopens`) rather than JSON on a mutable period row, and the tax-year close gets `tax_year_closes` because 7.1 and 3.9 each close a tax year per loan and nothing today records the year as a whole. (6) The plan and this file's first draft named the chain's cycles by step (`custodial_day_close`, `lar`, `period_close`, `form496`, `form1098`) and the daily receipt `cashiering.cycle.run_completed`; 35.3's registry is the sole code registry ("a cycle is added by adding a def"), so rule 1's table names 35.3's `cycle_code`s and receipt literals verbatim (`cashiering_daily` / `cashiering.daily.run_completed`, `custodial_recon_daily`, `metro2_monthly`, `lar_daily` / `investor.lar.run_completed`, `investor_period_close`, `form_496_monthly`, `form_1098` / `tax_form.1098.run_completed`) and the three cycles only this chain needs — `ledger_period_close`, `form_496a_monthly`, `star_monthly` — are rows of 35.3's table with this process as runner owner; the step codes (`close_period_steps.code`) stay this process's. (7) The trigger's payload is 35.3's `{period_key, period_end}` (35.3 rule 4, 35.3-T15), not the plan's `{period, period_end, as_of, source}`, and it fires on the planner's first pass in the new month, not on a 00:05 gate of this file's own. (8) The plan and this file's first draft had the tax-year close hand a 1099-A/C candidate list to Fannie Mae for Fannie Mae's own filing; Servicing Guide C-4.2-01 / F-1-23 (re-read 2026-09-15) put the filing on the servicer, on Fannie Mae's behalf, so the kind `tax_year` period carries `form_1099_ac_furnish` / `form_1099_ac_file` steps and `tax_year_closes.form_1099_ac_loans` counts filings, never candidates (rule 10, open question 4). (9) The plan and the first draft keyed the `lar` step on the period's last business day; IRM 2-01 closes the period's non-removal LARs and corrections at BD1 20:00 ET, so the step's unit is the BD1 `lar_daily` run of the following month (rule 1; 35.3's `investor_period_close` row).

### Operational prerequisites
- 35.3 (cycles, jobs and receipts): the planner lock, `cycles.run_unit`, per-cycle `<cycle>.run_completed` receipts, and the planner's emission of `ledger.month.ended` — this process plans steps as 35.3 jobs and reads receipts; it runs no unit of its own except the attestation and the tax-year close.
- 35.5 (the daily cashiering cycle): `cashiering.daily.run_completed{as_of_date}` for every calendar day — the literal 35.3's `cashiering_daily` registry row owns (35-3-*.md rule 2, the first row of its table) and 35.5 emits — the `eod_cutoff` step's receipt.
- 35.1 (typed rows): `investor_reporting_periods`, `reconciliations`, `remittance_components`, `custodial_accounts`, `ledger_lines`, `qc_cycles`, `tax_forms_1098` written by their sections' commands so the attestation can read them with SQL, not by hydrating entity JSON.
- 35.7 (roles): `qc-audit` reviewer runs under `reviewer_roles` credentials disjoint from the preparer's; a human `officer` and `qc_officer` who can sign in; FAKE reviewers approve both in every build stage before go-live.
- 6.3's Fannie Mae Connect Remittance P&I Detail report (line 9's source) pulled by `fnma_portal_operator` within 2 BD of BD2 — the form496 step cannot draft without it; the attestation does not wait for it (rule 5).
- The Fannie Mae investor reporting calendar as the kernel carries it: `business_days_fannie_et` is a code constant (src/kernel/calendar/business.ts:63 `FNMA_PUBLISHED_CLOSURES`, `fannieMaeObservedCalendar`), not a table — no `holiday_calendars` table exists in db/migrations (only `court_calendars` and `creditor_calendars`); every BDn in this file counts on it.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `ledger.month.ended{period_key: "2026-09", period_end: "2026-09-30"}` — the payload exactly as 35.3 rule 4 and 35.3-T15 define it, emitted once per period on the global subject by 35.3's planner on its first pass whose `as_of` civil date (America/New_York) is in a new month (idempotent by `period_key`; the event no code emits today). Starts `close.open`, which keys `close_periods.period` on the event's `period_key`.
- Receipts the chain waits on, each the owning section's own event (never re-emitted here): `cashiering.daily.run_completed{as_of_date}` (35.5 — the `cashiering_daily` registry literal); `custodial.reconciliation.daily_completed{custodial_account_id, as_of_date}` (6.3 `close_day`); `credit.cycle.snapshot_completed{as_of_date}` (8.1); `investor.lar.run_completed{as_of_date}` (35.3's receipt for the `lar_daily` run of BD1 of the following month — the LAR pass over every active loan through period end, due 20:00 ET on BD1 under IRM 2-01); `investor_reporting_periods.closed{servicer_number, period, checklist_complete: true}` (5.1); `ledger.period.closed{custodial_account_id, account_kind, period_end, recon_due_on}` (6.3 `close_period`, one per custodial account); `custodial.reconciliation.completed{kind: monthly_form_496 | monthly_form_496a, period, reconciliation_id}` (6.3/6.4); `qc.cycle.signed{period}` (18.1); `star.metrics.computed{period}` (18.3); `eligibility.computed{quarter}` (18.7, quarter-end periods only); `tax_year.closed{tax_year, source}` (7.1 per reportable loan; 3.9 per 1099-INT loan); `tax_form.1098.furnished{tax_year}` / `tax_form.1098.filed{tax_year}` (7.1); `escrow_interest_1099.furnished` / `.filed` (3.9); `tax_form.1099ac.furnished{tax_year}` / `tax_form.1099ac.filed{tax_year, form_1100_submitted: true}` (15.x — the 1099-A/C owner 7.1 and 8.1 assign; no emitter at HEAD, its rule and T-id are 15.x's to add).
- Schedules: the sweep (every minute) — the planner plans the steps whose dependencies are met and records receipts; the step `metro2_snapshot` carries `not_before` 00:05 ET on the 1st (8.1's schedule); `tax_year_close` carries `not_before` 2 January 00:05 ET (7.1's "Jan 2, 00:05 ET").
- Human commands: `close.attest` with an `officer` approval record while `custodial.form496.human_approval` is on; `close.reopen{period, reason}` by an `officer`; `close.step.skip{step, reason}` by an `officer` for a step the period cannot have (no T&I account → `form496a`; no eligibility quarter → `eligibility`) — skipping records a reason, never a receipt.
- Reads: `close.board{period?}` for the Close view (35.8/35.11) and the evidence pack (34.4).

#### State machine
Per period (`close_periods.status`): `open` —(`close.attest`: every step in `balance_attestation.depends_on` completed, variance 0, review passed, officer approval when the flag is on)→ `attested` —(every remaining step `completed` or `skipped`)→ `closed`. `attested` or `closed` —(`close.reopen` by an `officer` with a reason; from `closed` also a `compliance` escalation)→ `reopened` —(the reset steps complete again and `close.attest` passes at variance 0 with `supersedes_attestation_id` set)→ `attested` → `closed`. Terminal: `closed` (until a reopen; a period can be reopened at most until its successor period is `attested`, after which corrections belong to the successor's Section III — 6.3 "retro-corrections"; open question 2). A kind `tax_year` period has no `attested` state from balances: `open` —(the tax-year attestation: counts and sums tie)→ `attested` —(`form_1098_file`, `form_1099_int_file` and `form_1099_ac_file` completed with IRS acceptance, the last with `form_1100_submitted: true`)→ `closed`.

Per step (`close_period_steps.status`): `blocked` —(every `depends_on` step `completed` or `skipped`, and `now ≥ not_before`)→ `planned` (a 35.3 job per unit exists; `close.step.planned`) —(the first unit claimed, or for a receipt-only step the first matching receipt)→ `running` (`close.step.started{step_id, started_at}`; `SM_CLOSE_STEP_STALLED_2BD` armed) —(`received = expected_receipts`)→ `completed` (`close.step.completed{step_id}`; the clock satisfied). `running` —(2 fannie_et BD without completion)→ `stalled` (the clock's breach; the step keeps running, the status is a label and clears on completion). `running` —(35.3 `job.unit.dead` on every unit)→ `failed` → `planned` again on `cycles.retry`. `blocked` | `planned` —(`close.step.skip` by an `officer`)→ `skipped`. `completed` —(a reopen that resets it)→ `blocked`; a final step (`lar`, `period_close`, `metro2_snapshot`) is never reset: on reopen it is relabelled `pre_reopen` and still counts as completed for dependencies. Who: the planner (system) plans, starts and completes; the agent attests and proposes reopens; `officer` approves attestations under the flag, approves reopens, skips steps; `qc-audit` reviews; `qc_officer` reviews variances.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **The chain is data, not code.** `close.open` writes the period and its steps from the table below; the planner reads `depends_on`, `not_before` and the receipts and never consults a hard-coded order. A step is `planned` only when every dependency is `completed` or `skipped`; a blocked step is visible as `blocked` with its unmet dependencies named from the moment the period opens (the Close board, 35.8/35.11), so nobody waits for a stall clock to learn the chain is stuck. 2. **Receipts are the sections' own events.** A step completes when `received = expected_receipts` matching `receipt_event_type` and `receipt_filter` have been observed on the bus (35.1's commit hook delivers them; a receipt that commits while the planner is not running is picked up by the next sweep from `loan_events`). 35.4 never emits a receipt on a section's behalf; a step with no receipt after its unit ran is a 35.3 failure, not a completion.
3. **The balance attestation, to the cent.** For each P&I custodial account × remittance type at `as_of = period_end`: `adjusted_depository = bank_closing_ledger + deposits_in_transit − disbursements_in_transit + depository_adjustments` (6.3 Section I/III, cents, no rounding); `cashbook = Σ ledger_lines(custodial_pi_cash:<account>) through as_of` (bigint); `composition_l12 = Σ L1..L11` from `remittance_components` for the period (6.3 rule 6); `variance = adjusted_depository − cashbook`. The attestation is `attested` only when `variance = 0` **and** `composition_l12 = cashbook` **and** the preparer's confidence ≥ 0.95 with evidence on both sides (the statement document and the items register). Otherwise the row is written with `outcome = variance` and a `qc_officer` escalation, and nothing else changes.
4. **Never a plug.** No tool in this process writes `ledger_lines`, `payments`, `reconciliations`, `reconciliation_items` or any `*_cents` column of another section; a variance is explained by the owning section's own corrections (2.1 reversal, 6.3 item, 5.1 correction) and then re-attested. There is no `force`, `override` or `tolerance` input on `close.attest`; a request carrying one is refused `NO_PLUG` before any read.
5. **The attestation does not wait for Fannie Mae's report.** Line 9 (Fannie Mae P&I receivable) enters the composition from 5.2's computed `fnma_remittance_payable:S/S` at BD2 close; the Remittance P&I Detail report (line 9's documentary source) is a `form496` step prerequisite, not an attestation one. If the report later differs, 6.3 records the difference as L10 and the form496 step's own review handles it; the attestation is not reopened for an L10 item that nets to the same L12.
6. **Independent review.** `close.review` must be run by `qc-audit` under credentials disjoint from the preparer's (35.7 `reviewer_roles`); it re-derives `cashbook` and `composition_l12` from `ledger_lines` and `remittance_components` with its own query, re-reads the statement's closing ledger from the stored document (35.2 bytes, not the preparer's number), and writes its own decision. Same run id, same credentials or same model session as the preparer → `REVIEWER_NOT_INDEPENDENT`.
7. **Officer approval under the flag.** `custodial.form496.human_approval` (configuration; default on for the first six months and any month with an item aged > 60 days or a corporate funding > $25,000 — 6.3's rule) is read by `close.attest` from configuration at call time and recorded on the row as `human_approval_flag`; when on, the attestation needs an `officer` approval record (`agent_decisions` by a `human` actor with role `officer`, action `close.attest.approve`, naming the period and account) or it is refused `OFFICER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`; when off, the `officer` still receives the package (6.3).
8. **BDn counts on the Fannie Mae calendar.** Period-open, stall and attestation clocks count `business_days_fannie_et` from `period_end` or `started_at` (the sections they wrap are Fannie Mae clocks); the tax-year close counts `business_days_federal` (an IRS clock). For September 2026 (period_end Wed 2026-09-30): BD1 = Thu 2026-10-01, BD2 = Fri 2026-10-02, BD5 = Wed 2026-10-07. For October 2026 (period_end Sat 2026-10-31): BD1 = Mon 2026-11-02, BD5 = Fri 2026-11-06. For tax year 2026: 3 federal business days after Thu 2026-12-31 (Fri 1 Jan 2027 is a federal holiday) = Wed 2027-01-06.
9. **Reopen.** Only an `officer` reopens, with a reason and the triggering event (a restated statement, a reversal, a 5.1 correction). The reopen resets to `blocked` every step downstream of the change in the dependency graph except the final steps — `lar`, `period_close` and `metro2_snapshot` are Fannie Mae's and the bureaus' record of the period (IRM 4-08; C-4.1-01) and are relabelled `pre_reopen`, still satisfying dependencies; their corrections are the next period's items. The original attestation row is never edited; the re-attestation is a new row with `supersedes_attestation_id`. A reopen from `closed` also opens a `compliance` escalation (the package may already be with the partner). A period whose successor is `attested` cannot be reopened (rule: the correction is the successor's Section III item, 6.3 "retro-corrections").
10. **Tax-year close.** On the December period's `tax_year_close` step: the reportable set is every loan with `interest_due` credits from borrower funds in the calendar year (7.1 rule 11), the 1099-INT set is every loan with escrow interest ≥ $10.00 (3.9), and the 1099-A/C set is every loan with a 15.x acquisition by Fannie Mae or a third party (the redemption period ended without redemption, the Mortgage Release accepted, or the third-party foreclosure sale), a known abandonment (with C-4.2-01's three-month deferral when foreclosure is expected within three months), or a discharge of debt in the year — filed by Supermortgage on Fannie Mae's behalf under C-4.2-01/F-1-23 (Fannie Mae's name and TIN 52-0883107, servicer loan number as the account number, Form 1100 to Fannie Mae); Fannie Mae's no-1099-C list arrives by January 5, after this 2 January close, so the `form_1099_ac_furnish` step, not the close, drops the listed loans from the 1099-C set. The unit calls 7.1's `closeTaxYear{tax_year, reportable_loans}` and 3.9's `closeTaxYear` (their `tax_year.closed` events arm the IRS clocks), writes `tax_year_closes` and the 1099-A/C filing-list document, opens the kind `tax_year` close period with its six steps, and emits `close.tax_year.closed{tax_year, reportable_loans, ioe_1099_loans, form_1099_ac_loans}`. The tax-year attestation ties counts and sums: `furnished_count = reportable_loans` (7.1 furnishes below $600 too), `filed_count = loans with box1 ≥ $600.00`, `box1_sum = Σ tax_forms_1098.box1` over the year `= ledger_interest_sum`, variance 0.
11. **Idempotency and concurrency.** `close.open` is idempotent per (`kind`, `period`, `servicer_number`); the planner runs under 35.3's session-level advisory lock, so two sweeps in a minute plan once; receipts are recorded once per `source_event_id` (unique index on `close_period_events(source_event_id)` where not null); the demo clock's per-day advance runs the planner inline per crossed day (35.3-T9), so a two-month advance produces two periods with receipts in dependency order.
12. **Money never moves here.** Every figure on an attestation is read; the only writes are this process's own tables, events, timers and decisions. An agent proposing a reopen is a proposal; the `officer` command is the act.

- Reports: the Close board (35.8/35.11) — per period every step with status, dependencies, receipts, the owner's clock and due date, the attestation summary and the reopen history.
- Notices: none. §35 owns no notice code; the forms and statements the chain drives are 7.1's (its Form 1098 notice template) and 3.9's.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`custodial-recon` agent (tools: `close.open`, `close.plan`, `close.step.start`, `close.step.complete`, `close.step.skip`, `close.board`, `close.attest`, `close.review`, `close.reopen`, `close.tax_year`, `writeDecision`), with `qc-audit` as the independent reviewer running `close.review` under separate credentials (35.7) and `investor-reporting` unchanged as the owner of `lar` and `period_close` (its own units emit their receipts). End-to-end: the planner (35.3, system actor) calls `close.open` on `ledger.month.ended`, `close.plan` every sweep (plans unblocked steps as 35.3 jobs, records receipts, completes steps, relabels stalls), and `close.tax_year` for December; the agent prepares the balance attestation unprompted once `balance_attestation` is planned — reads the statement document, the items register and the ledger, computes the three balances, writes the preparer decision and calls `close.attest`; `qc-audit` reviews before the attestation is written; the agent proposes a reopen when a restated statement, reversal or correction changes a closed period's balances and never performs it. Humans: `officer` approves attestations under the flag, approves reopens and skips; `qc_officer` disposes variances; `compliance` receives missed attestations and reopens from `closed`. Decision record schema `{period, servicer_number, custodial_account_id?, remittance_type?, action, attestation_id?, composition_snapshot_id?, adjusted_depository_cents, cashbook_cents, composition_l12_cents, attestation_variance_cents, confidence, evidence_document_ids, reviewer_decision_id?, officer_approval_id?, human_approval_flag, rule_set_version: close.v1, model_version, prompt_version: 35.4-v1, rationale}`. Guardrails: `NO_PLUG` (no ledger, payment, reconciliation or `*_cents` write; no force/tolerance input), `RECEIPTS_ARE_OWNERS_EVENTS` (never emits a section's receipt), `REVIEWER_NOT_INDEPENDENT`, `OFFICER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` (flag on), `OFFICER_REOPEN_ONLY`, `FINAL_STEPS_NEVER_RESET` (`lar`, `period_close`, `metro2_snapshot`), `SUCCESSOR_ATTESTED_NO_REOPEN`, `FLAG_FROM_CONFIG` (never from the request), `NO_CLOCK_EDIT` (the owners' timers are read, never satisfied or cancelled here). Escalations: `qc_officer` for a variance or confidence < 0.95; `ops_analyst` for a missed period open or a stalled step; `compliance` for a missed BD5 attestation or a reopen from `closed`; `officer` for a late tax-year close, and as the approver of attestations under the flag, reopens and skips; `fnma_portal_operator` through 6.3's own Connect pull and for the Form 1100 submission after the `form_1099_ac_file` step's e-file (C-4.2-01 / F-1-23).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Month-end on a weekend (Sat 2026-10-31) → `period_end` stays 10/31; 35.5's cashiering cycle runs for every calendar day, so `eod_cutoff`'s receipt is the 10/31 run; the statement as of 10/31 arrives Mon 11/2; BD1 = 11/2, BD5 = 11/6 (rule 8); the `lar` step's unit is the BD1 (Mon 11/2) `lar_daily` run, due 20:00 ET (IRM 2-01).
- No T&I custodial account for the servicer number → `form496a` is `blocked` with dependency `ledger_period_close`, an `officer` skips it with reason `no_ti_account`; the period can still close.
- A P&I account with two remittance types (S/S MBS and S/S MRS) → one `balance_attestation` and one `form496` unit per (account × remittance type); the period is `attested` only when every P&I attestation is `attested`.
- `ledger.month.ended` arrives twice (a second planner run after a crash before commit) → `close.open` returns the existing period; the second event is journaled as a duplicate with `source_event_id`, no new steps, no new clock.
- A receipt arrives before its step is planned (5.1's close ran by hand on BD1) → recorded against the step while `blocked`; the step completes the moment its dependencies do, with `started_at = completed_at`; the stall clock never arms.
- The Remittance P&I Detail report never arrives → `form496` stalls (this clock) and 6.3's day-30 warning fires; the attestation stands on L9 from 5.2's computed payable (rule 5).
- Variance caused by a bank fee debited to the custodial account → the attestation shows the variance; 6.3 rule 3 reimburses from corporate and re-bills; re-attest — this process posts nothing.
- A reopen requested while the successor period is `attested` → refused `SUCCESSOR_ATTESTED_NO_REOPEN`; the correction is written up as the successor's Section III item by 6.3.
- A `close.attest` call carrying `force: true`, `tolerance_cents` or any `*_cents` input → refused `NO_PLUG` before any read; the attempt is a decision row and an `ops_analyst` escalation.
- 35.3's unit for a step dies three times (`job.unit.dead`) → the step is `failed`, an `ops_analyst` escalation names the step and period, the period stays `open`; `cycles.retry` re-plans it.
- The demo clock advances 60 days in one request → each crossed day runs the planner inline; the periods, steps and receipts come out in dependency order with `period_key` from the persisted offset (35.3-T9); FAKE reviewers approve the officer and reviewer roles.
- Production with FAKE reviewers off and no staffed `qc_officer` → the variance escalation waits on the role queue and 35.7's `SM_ROLE_QUEUE_UNSTAFFED_1BD` raises it; no attestation is written by default.
- A loan transferred out mid-year (17.x) → it is a reportable loan for the interest Supermortgage received (7.1 "the 1098 for the partial year is Supermortgage's"); the tax-year close counts it once with its `interest_due` credits through the transfer date.
- The reportable set is empty (a servicer number with no loans) → `close.tax_year` writes `tax_year_closes{reportable_loans: 0}` and `close.tax_year.closed`; no `tax_year.closed` is emitted (7.1's `closeTaxYear` refuses an empty list, src/domain/notices/ops-7-1.ts:242) and the kind `tax_year` period opens with every step `skipped{reason: no_reportable_loans}` by the system.
- No acquisition, abandonment or discharge of debt in the year (`form_1099_ac_loans = 0`) → the kind `tax_year` period opens with `form_1099_ac_furnish` and `form_1099_ac_file` `skipped{reason: no_1099_ac_loans}` by the system; a loan on Fannie Mae's January 5 no-1099-C list is dropped from the 1099-C set by `form_1099_ac_furnish`, never by the 2 January close (rule 10).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should 5.1's close also emit 6.3's `fnma.reporting_period.closed` until 6.3's timer row is re-registered on `investor_reporting_periods.closed`? **Default: yes — 5.1's `closeReportingPeriod` appends both in the same transaction; the chain keys on `investor_reporting_periods.closed`.**
2. How long can a period be reopened? **Default: until its successor period is `attested`; after that the correction is the successor's Section III item (6.3 "retro-corrections").**
3. Should the balance attestation wait for the Remittance P&I Detail report so L9 is documentary at attestation time? **Default: no — the attestation stands on 5.2's computed payable at BD2 close; the report is `form496`'s prerequisite, and an L10 difference that nets to the same L12 does not reopen the attestation.**
4. Resolved (Servicing Guide C-4.2-01 / F-1-23, re-read 2026-09-15): Supermortgage files Forms 1099-A/1099-C electronically on Fannie Mae's behalf (Fannie Mae's name and TIN 52-0883107, the servicer loan number as the account number) and submits Form 1100 to Fannie Mae; the tax-year row keeps the filing list as its document and the `fnma_portal_operator` submits Form 1100.
5. Should the December period's `tax_year_close` step block December's `closed` status until the 1098s are filed on 31 March? **Default: no — December closes on its month chain; the kind `tax_year` period carries the furnish and file steps to their own close.**
6. Should a period attest per P&I account or once per servicer number? **Default: one attestation row per (P&I account × remittance type) and the period is `attested` when all are; the board shows the roll-up.**
