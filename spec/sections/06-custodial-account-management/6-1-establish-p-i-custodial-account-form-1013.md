### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Who establishes.** Servicing Guide A2-1-07, Subservicing (05/13/2026): "Each subservicer must establish custodial accounts for all Fannie Mae mortgage loans that it subservices for a master servicer." "Funds for MBS pools and for portfolio mortgage loans cannot be commingled in the same custodial account." "A subservicer's custodial accounts related to mortgage loans it is servicing for the master servicer must be separate from any other accounts it maintains for mortgage loans it services directly for Fannie Mae or for any other investor, including other mortgage loans (which are not Fannie Mae mortgage loans) that it services for the master servicer." "The subservicer must submit the applicable custodial account form—Letter of Authorization for P&I Custodial Account (Form 1013) … The completed forms must be submitted electronically." "The master servicer and the subservicer must execute and submit the Data Access Authorization Form (Form 101) at the inception of the subservicing arrangement" and again at termination. A4-1-02 adds that subservicing custodial accounts "do not relieve master servicers of responsibilities for establishing required accounts and ensuring compliance." (URL: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/a2-1-07/subservicing, verified 2026-09-09.)

**Eligible depository (A4-1-02, Establishing Custodial Bank Accounts, 07/12/2023).** A Federal Reserve Bank, a Federal Home Loan Bank, or a depository whose accounts are FDIC- or NCUSIF-insured, that is rated "well capitalized" by its federal/state regulator, and that meets the rating thresholds: ≥ $30 billion in assets — S&P short-term issuer "A-3" or better (or long-term "BBB-" if no short-term rating) or Moody's "P-3" (or "Baa3"); < $30 billion — IDC Financial Publishing 125 or better, or KBRA Financial Intelligence C+ or better; for accounts holding only actual/actual or scheduled/actual funds, IDC 75 / KBRA C suffice. "The servicer must monitor these ratings based on the frequency used by the ratings agency for publishing and updating rating changes." "The servicer must notify Fannie Mae within three business days when its custodial depository fails to meet Fannie Mae's eligibility requirements"; remedies include transferring funds to an eligible depository, a trust account at Fannie Mae's direction, keeping balances fully insured, or more frequent remittances as Fannie Mae directs. Fannie Mae "may mandate fund transfers or accelerated timelines" regardless of ratings. "All funds in a custodial account must be immediately available on demand, without the servicer (or Fannie Mae) having to provide advance notice of its intent to withdraw funds or pay a penalty fee for early withdrawals." "Investment of custodial funds is not permitted." Interest-bearing P&I accounts are permitted; the servicer pays any penalties on accounts that limit withdrawals (money-market). (URL: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/a4-1-02/establishing-custodial-bank-accounts, verified 2026-09-09.)

**Account structure (A4-1-02; F-1-03, 05/13/2026).** "Maintain separate P&I accounts for each remittance type"; for scheduled/scheduled, "establish minimum two P&I accounts separating MBS pool funds from portfolio/other funds"; multiple accounts per remittance type are allowed; if several MBS pools share an account the servicer must keep pool-level accounting. "P&I and T&I funds must never commingle regardless of remittance type." Eligible P&I deposits: borrower P&I, buydown applications, servicer advances, curtailments, payoffs, third-party foreclosure proceeds, repurchase proceeds, guaranty fees and related fees, special remittances, and excess servicing fees securitized in MBS. Clearing accounts (general-ledger or operating accounts) may be used only at an eligible depository, titled with "for the benefit of Fannie Mae," with deposits recorded in the custodial account "within one business day (including any period during which funds were in a clearing account or general ledger account) of receipt from the borrower," and with audit trails. F-1-03 requires the servicer to "deduct the servicing fees and late charges before the servicer transfers P&I payments from its clearing account to the custodial account" or, if full payments are deposited, remove the servicer's share within one business day; to designate "one P&I account per remittance type as the drafting account" (all funds moved there before a draft; a consolidated drafting account may not hold other investors' funds); and to "advise Fannie Mae by submitting Form 1013 electronically" when an existing account becomes the special drafting account. Titling (F-1-03, verbatim): "(Name of servicer), as agent, trustee, and/or bailee for the benefit of Fannie Mae and/or payments of various mortgagors and/or various owners of interests in mortgage-backed securities (Custodial Account)." The 2019 Form 1013 job aid instructs subservicers to enter the name as "(Name of Subservicer) as subservicer for (Name of Master Servicer)." "Subservicers must execute separate Form 1013 or Form 1014 for each custodial account established. The servicer, subservicer, and depository must each retain copies of executed forms." (URLs: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/f-1-03/establishing-and-implementing-custodial-accounts; https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/16526/display, both verified 2026-09-09.)

**Deposit timing (C-1.1-01, 04/12/2023).** "Deposit all funds into a custodial account in a financial institution that meets Fannie Mae's rating requirements for custodial depositories … within 24 hours of receipt." With a lockbox agent, "the payments must be deposited into the collection clearing account no later than the 1st business day after they are received by the lockbox agent … the funds must be deposited into the applicable custodial account no later than the 2nd business day after the servicer's lockbox agent receives them." (URL: https://servicing-guide.fanniemae.com/svc/c-1.1-01/servicer-responsibilities-processing-mortgage-loan-payments, verified 2026-09-09.) F-1-03 separately requires payments collected between delivery and purchase to be deposited "no later than one business day after the servicer or its designee receives Fannie Mae's purchase proceeds" (boarding of newly delivered loans; cross-ref 1.6).

**CBAM (SVC-2026-04, May 13, 2026; CBAM User Guide dated 5.27.26).** "Servicers are encouraged to implement the changes immediately but must do so by Aug. 1, 2026." "A new Data Access Authorization Agreement (Form 101) may be required to grant any sub-servicer access to the master servicer's custodial account management data." The User Guide confirms: Technology Manager role "Custodial Bank Account Management - External General User Role"; a Form 1013 "allows single remittance type selection"; the app performs "real-time validation of depository eligibility" against A4-1-02 from the ABA number (errors if "inactive, ineligible, or not found"; contact custodial_account@fanniemae.com); fields are effective date, custodial account number, branch name and physical address (no PO boxes), servicer representative and depository representative contacts; DocuSign routing is servicer representative first, then depository; statuses are In Draft → Pending Signatures → (Signatures Declined) → Fully Signed → In Effect → Pending Replacement; "Change/Replace" may add/remove 9-digit servicer numbers under the same 5-digit ID or fix typos but "Form Number" and "ABA #" cannot change (a new depository = a new form); "Report Account Closed" is irreversible; the executed document is stored in the Files section; "Subservicers can view, create new forms and Change/Replace existing forms for any of the displayed 9-digit Servicer Numbers" once Form 101 is on file. (URLs: https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/46396/display; https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/single-family-custodial-bank-account-management-user-guide, verified 2026-09-09.)

**Drafting authorization (C-3-01, 03/11/2026; F-1-20, 03/11/2026).** Servicers "must authorize Fannie Mae to draft custodial accounts via ACH"; CRS bank instructions are due "by 8 p.m. ET, one day prior to the effective date"; A/A drafts are initiated "on the next business day after loan activity is reported"; S/A funds must be available "by the 20th calendar day of each month (or the preceding business day)"; S/S portfolio "by the 18th calendar day of the month (or the preceding business day)." LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026): "we will automatically initiate a draft of the Principal and Interest (P&I) remittance amount from the servicer's custodial account two business days after successfully processing payment events"; "There are no remitting or draft date changes for any other remittance types." (URLs in Sources.)

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (a) Blueprint cites only A2-1-07 and C-3-01; the operative rules are A4-1-02 (eligibility, structure, clearing, interest) and F-1-03 (titling, CBAM, drafting account) plus C-1.1-01 (24-hour deposit rule). (b) "Before servicing" is correct but incomplete: Form 629 (1.2) requires evidence of established custodial accounts at least 30 days before the subservicing transfer date, so the account must be In Effect before Form 629 is filed. (c) The blueprint lists the CBAM app as the only system; the depository onboarding, ACH debit-block whitelisting, statement feed and CRS drafting instructions are equally prerequisite. (d) Class "c" is right for the signature and portal steps, but everything else (eligibility monitoring, registry, gating of deposits) is fully automatable.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `subservicing.arrangement.created` (from 1.2) with the partner's 9-digit servicer numbers and the remittance types expected in the transferred portfolio → plan one P&I account per remittance type (`A/A`, `S/A`, `S/S-MBS`, `S/S-MRS`) plus one clearing account and the T&I set (6.2).
- `loan.boarded` with a remittance type that has no active P&I account → blocking exception (gate below).
- `custodial.depository.ineligible_detected` (rating monitor) → replacement account workflow.
- `custodial.form.change_requested` (entity-name change, add/remove servicer number, account-number correction, close account).
- Scheduled: `custodial.depository.rating_check` (monthly; quarterly for IDC/KBRA publication cycles), `custodial.depository.fdic_status_check` (quarterly, after Call Report release).

#### State machine
Account (`custodial_accounts.status`): `planned` → (`depository_eligibility_passed` ∧ bank account opened) `form_pending` → (`custodial.form.in_effect`) `active` → (`custodial.form.change_requested`) `pending_replacement` → `active`; `active` → (`custodial.account.close_requested` ∧ balance = 0 ∧ no open reconciling items) `closing` → (`Report Account Closed` in CBAM + final Form 496 retained) `closed` (terminal). Guards: no transition to `active` unless `custodial_forms.status = in_effect` **and** `debit_whitelist_confirmed_at` set **and** `statement_feed_id` receiving test files. Actors: agent for all transitions except `pending_signatures` → `fully_signed` (DocuSign by `officer` and the depository) and portal actions (`fnma_portal_operator`).

Form (`custodial_forms.status`) mirrors CBAM exactly: `in_draft` → `pending_signatures` → {`signatures_declined` → `in_draft`} | `fully_signed` → (effective date reached) `in_effect` → `pending_replacement` → `in_effect`; any → `closed_reported` (irreversible).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Eligibility evaluation** (pure function `evaluateDepositoryEligibility(depository, ratings[], accountUse)`): `insured` ∧ `well_capitalized` ∧ ratingTest, where ratingTest = if `total_assets_cents ≥ 3_000_000_000_000` (= $30,000,000,000.00 in cents) then (S&P ST ≥ A-3, else S&P LT ≥ BBB-) ∨ (Moody's ST ≥ P-3, else LT ≥ Baa3); else (IDC ≥ 125 ∨ KBRA ≥ C+) or, if `accountUse ∈ {A/A, S/A}` only, (IDC ≥ 75 ∨ KBRA ≥ C). Rating scale ordinals are stored in `rule_sets.fnma.custodial.2023-07` so a Guide change is a data change.
2. **Account plan**: required P&I accounts = distinct `remittance_type × pool_class` in the portfolio, with S/S always split {mbs, portfolio_mrs}; exactly one `is_drafting_account` per remittance type; a clearing account is optional but, if used, must itself satisfy rule 1 and the title rule.
3. **Title string** generated from the template and compared byte-for-byte to the depository's signature card/statement header at activation and monthly (OCR of the statement header; mismatch = exception `title_mismatch`).
4. **Servicing-fee split**: for full payments landing in clearing, the sweep to custodial is `sum(payment_allocations.principal + interest_gross) − servicing_fee − late_charges_retained`, where `servicing_fee = round_half_up(UPB_prior × servicing_fee_rate / 12)` in cents per loan (rule stated in 2.1/5.2; this section only enforces that the sweep happens ≤ 1 BD).
5. **Worked example (3-BD notice)**: IDC publishes a downgrade of Depository X from 128 to 118 on Thursday 2026-10-15; the rating monitor ingests it the same day → `ineligible_detected` 2026-10-15. Fannie Mae ET business days: Fri 10-16 (1), Mon 10-19 (2), Tue 10-20 (3) → `due_at = 2026-10-20T17:00-04:00`. The agent drafts the notice, moves the account to `watch`, computes fully-insured exposure (`balance − $250,000` per ownership category **[FDIC coverage rule — general knowledge, verify per account structure]**) and proposes remedy: open replacement at Depository Y (eligible) and migrate by the next remittance date.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`custodial-recon` agent, tools: `depository.evaluate`, `fdic.lookup`, `ratings.read`, `custodial.plan_accounts`, `cbam.prepare_package`, `documents.write`, `escalation.create`, `timer.start/satisfy`, `email.send` (allowlisted recipients only). The agent: builds the account plan from the remittance-type mix; evaluates eligibility; drafts the CBAM package; opens the `human_portal_task` (role `fnma_portal_operator`) and the DocuSign signature escalation (role `officer` — signing a Letter of Authorization that binds the company and directs a bank is an officer certification under baseline §8(3)); verifies the executed PDF against the plan (account number, title, ABA, remittance type, effective date — any mismatch reopens the task); flips the gate; starts monitors. Decision record: `{account_plan, eligibility_inputs, rating_sources, decision, confidence, rule_set_version, model_version, package_hash, reviewer}`. Guardrails: it can never transmit an account number outside the allowlisted CBAM/depository/partner channels; it cannot mark a form `in_effect` without the executed document hash. Human path when AI is off: ops-console wizard produces the same package and the same gate checks run as command validators.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Transfer-in with a remittance type not yet covered → boarding blocked for those loans (gate), Form 629 date slips; agent recommends the partner keep those loans one more cycle.
- Depository declines the DocuSign (e.g., bank wants its own custodial agreement wording) → `signatures_declined`; agent attaches the Guide title language and Fannie Mae's contact; `officer` negotiates; no funds move.
- Depository merges/changes ABA → new form required (ABA cannot be changed); plan parallel accounts and a cut-over date on a remittance boundary; keep both reconciled until zero.
- Fannie Mae moves the servicer number to a new master (partner exits) → A2-1-07 Form 101 at termination; accounts closed only after the final Form 496 and Fannie Mae's final draft.
- Money-market or notice accounts offered by the bank → reject (funds must be on demand).
- Interest-bearing P&I account: interest is the servicer's to remove (F-1-03 authorized withdrawal), swept within the month so it never inflates the P&I composition.
- Vendor outage (FDIC API down) → eligibility check uses the last result ≤ 35 days old; older → `unknown` and the account is not activated.
- Entity-name change of the partner → Change/Replace not allowed for name; new forms required (CBAM guide).

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- 6.1-T1 Given a portfolio with A/A, S/A and S/S (MBS + MRS) loans, when the plan is built, then four P&I accounts + one T&I are planned, exactly one drafting account per remittance type, S/S split by pool class.
- 6.1-T2 Given a $12B bank with IDC 120 and KBRA C, when evaluated for S/S use, then ineligible; for A/A-only use, then eligible; decision record lists the rule applied.
- 6.1-T3 Given a $40B bank with S&P ST A-3 and no Moody's, when evaluated, then eligible under the ≥ $30B test.
- 6.1-T4 Given a form in `pending_signatures`, when a deposit command targets the account, then the gate rejects it with `FNMA_F103_FORM1013_IN_EFFECT_GATE`.
- 6.1-T5 Given `ineligible_detected` on Thu 2026-10-15, then `due_at` = Tue 2026-10-20 17:00 ET; when `fnma_notified` on 10-19, then satisfied; when not by 10-20 17:00, then breached with `officer` critical escalation.
- 6.1-T6 Given a lockbox receipt on Fri 2026-11-06, then the custodial deposit deadline is Tue 2026-11-10 (Wed 11-11 is a federal holiday but the servicer calendar is used; if the servicer is closed 11-11 the deadline is still 11-10).
- 6.1-T7 Given the executed PDF shows account number differing by one digit from the plan, then the form is not marked `in_effect` and the portal task reopens.
- 6.1-T8 Given a DocuSign declined event, then status `signatures_declined`, `officer` escalation created, and no timer is deleted (cancelled with reason).

#### Audit and evidence
Events `custodial.account.*`, `custodial.form.*`, `custodial.depository.*` with actor (agent run id / user id); decision records with rating inputs; executed form hashes; DocuSign certificate of completion; bank signature card and statement header image proving title; ratings evidence per check; timer history for the 3-BD notice; partner acknowledgements. Packaged into the MORA "Custodial Accounts" evidence set (see 6.3 audit section).

### Open questions / decisions
1. Depository choice: one bank for all custodial accounts vs. two (concentration/BCP). Default: one primary eligible bank with a pre-approved secondary already onboarded (dormant accounts, forms not executed).
2. Clearing account: use a titled clearing account (simpler lockbox posting) vs. deposit directly to custodial by remittance type. Default: one titled clearing account per depository with same-day automated sweep.
3. Interest-bearing P&I accounts. Default: yes where the bank offers demand-deposit interest with no withdrawal limits; interest swept monthly to corporate.
4. Signature authority: `officer` = Supermortgage officer only, or partner co-sign? Default: Supermortgage officer signs (the form's servicer representative); partner receives copy within 1 BD.
5. Whether Fannie Mae requires the *partner* to also hold a P&I account per remittance type for the same servicer number. Default: no (A2-1-07 puts the duty on the subservicer), but confirm with the Fannie Mae custodial team in writing before Form 629.
