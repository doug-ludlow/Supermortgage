### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**When MI is required and who may insure.** Selling Guide B7-1-01, *Provision of Mortgage Insurance* (04/02/2025): "the lender must obtain a primary mortgage insurance policy for a conventional first mortgage loan that has an LTV ratio greater than 80%"; "Conventional mortgages may be insured by private mortgage insurers or state or local insuring agencies that have been approved under Fannie Mae's Qualified Mortgage Insurer Approval Requirements"; the definitive list is "Approved Mortgage Insurers and Related Identifiers" on Fannie Mae's website; "The form of mortgage insurance policy, including any endorsements, must be acceptable to Fannie Mae"; "For a purchase money loan, the value used in determining the LTV ratio is the lower of the sales price or the appraised value of the security property"; "A mortgage is insurable if a mortgage insurer would not decline to insure it by reason of any fraud, misrepresentation, negligence, or dishonest, criminal, or knowingly wrongful act"; the lender must ensure MI is "in place" at purchase/securitization (as extracted — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED wording]**). Approved insurers (July 2025 list): Arch Mortgage Insurance Company (MI code 38), Enact Mortgage Insurance Corporation (01), Essent Guaranty, Inc. (43), Mortgage Guaranty Insurance Corporation (06), MassHousing Mortgage Insurance Fund (37), National Mortgage Insurance Corporation (44), Radian Guaranty Inc. (33), United Guaranty Residential Insurance Company (12); codes 95/97 denote "MI not required" ("MI Code 97 is now valid for negotiated (bulk) transactions only").

**Coverage table.** B7-1-02, *Mortgage Insurance Coverage Requirements* (08/07/2019) — standard coverage (^) / minimum coverage (*) by LTV band:

| Transaction | 80.01–85.00% | 85.01–90.00% | 90.01–95.00% | 95.01–97.00% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-rate, term ≤ 20 years (all property types except standard MH) | 6% | 12% | 25%^ / 16%* | 35%^ / 18%* |
| Fixed-rate, term > 20 years; all ARMs | 12%^ / 6%* | 25%^ / 12%* | 30%^ / 16%* | 35%^ / 18%* |
| HomeReady, fixed ≤ 20 years | 6% | 12% | 25%^ / 16%* | 25%^ / 18%* |
| HomeReady, fixed > 20 years; all ARMs | 12%^ / 6%* | 25%^ / 12%* | 25%^ / 16%* | 25%^ / 18%* |
| Standard manufactured homes (any term) | 12%^ / 6%* | 25%^ / 12%* | 30%^ / 16%* | Not applicable |

"For certain transactions, Fannie Mae offers two mortgage insurance coverage level options: standard coverage for the transaction type (noted with ^) and minimum coverage (noted with *) with corresponding LLPAs." "Lenders who choose less than standard coverage (but no lower than minimum coverage) will be assessed an LLPA based on the LTV ratio and representative credit score." "The minimum mortgage insurance LLPAs can be found in the Loan-Level Price Adjustment (LLPA) Matrix, and are in addition to any other LLPAs that may apply to the transaction." LLPA Matrix (version 09.09.2026), "Minimum Mortgage Insurance Coverage Option" grid as extracted — Classic FICO ≥ 740: 0.125% / 0.375% / 0.500% / 1.000% across the four LTV bands; 720–739: 0.125 / 0.625 / 0.875 / 1.250; 700–719: 0.125 / 0.750 / 0.875 / 1.250; 680–699: 0.125 / 0.750 / 0.875 / 1.750; 660–679: 0.750 / 1.250 / 1.750 / 2.125; 640–659: 1.250 / 1.750 / 2.000 / 2.375; lower rows to 3.000% at 95.01–97% (620–639: 1.750 / 2.000 / 2.250 / 2.750; < 620: 2.000 / 2.250 / 2.500 / 3.000); a separate VantageScore 4.0 grid with bands shifted one band (top row "> 760" = Classic FICO "> 740" values; bottom row "< 640" = Classic FICO "< 620" values). **[VERIFIED 2026-09-11 — both minimum-MI grids read cell-by-cell from the 09.09.2026 PDF; the PDF text layer renders the top band as "> 740" / "> 760"; because the next band ends at 739/759, a score of exactly 740/760 falls in the top band.]**

**LTV rounding.** B2-1.2-01, *Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios* (06/01/2022): purchase — "Divide the original loan amount by the property value. (The property value is the lower of the sales price or the current appraised value.)"; refinance — "(The property value is the current appraised value.)"; financed MI — "Divide the original loan amount plus the financed mortgage insurance by the property value"; "The result of these calculations must be truncated (shortened) to two decimal places, then rounded up to the nearest whole percent" (examples: 94.01% → 95%; 80.001% → 80%).

**Lender-purchased MI.** B7-1-03, *Lender-Purchased Mortgage Insurance* (05/27/2010): Fannie Mae accepts "lender-purchased mortgage insurance coverage for all loan types except adjustable-rate mortgages that can be converted to fixed-rate mortgages"; the lender must "pay for the mortgage insurance coverage as a corporate obligation with an initial premium and renewal premiums for each subsequent period of coverage, which may be a month or a year"; "Lump-sum premium plans that provide coverage for the life-of-the-mortgage loan also are acceptable"; the lender must "keep the mortgage insurance coverage in effect until the mortgage is paid in full," "increase the servicing compensation it would otherwise be required to retain for the mortgage loan ... by at least the amount of the mortgage insurance renewal premium" (not for lump-sum life-of-loan plans), "make any and all disclosures to the borrower that are either required by law, including the Homeowners Protection Act of 1998, or are otherwise appropriate for lender-purchased mortgage insurance coverage," and "deliver the loan with SFC 019." The Guide does not state that LPMI must be reflected in a higher note rate — that is the lender's economics (partner/SM pricing, 20.x) — and it does not address the minimum-coverage option for LPMI (**[UNVERIFIED — default: standard coverage only for LPMI]**).

**Financed borrower-purchased MI.** B7-1-04, *Financed Borrower-Purchased Mortgage Insurance* (12/15/2021): "All or a portion of the borrower-purchased mortgage insurance premium (split and single-premium plans) is included in the loan amount"; "The mortgage insurance coverage amount is determined based on the base (or net) LTV ratio – the LTV ratio calculated without the financed premium"; the "gross LTV ratio – the LTV ratio calculated with the financed premium – is used to determine the maximum LTV ratio permitted for the transaction. The LTV ratio may never exceed the LTV ratio allowed per the Eligibility Matrix"; "The loan amount including the financed mortgage insurance premium cannot exceed the applicable maximum Fannie Mae loan limit"; "The loan purpose is purchase, construction, or limited cash-out refinance" (cash-out excluded); "The Financed MI Amount and MI Financed Indicator must be delivered" and "The loan must be delivered with SFC 281."

**Master policies and rescission relief.** FHFA news release (Dec 2, 2013): the GSE master-policy overhaul covered loss mitigation, claims-processing timeframes, "when, and under what circumstances, coverage under the mortgage insurance policy must be maintained and when it may be revoked," and information sharing, with master policies "to go into effect in 2014." "Amended and Restated GSE Rescission Relief Principles for Implementation of Master Policy Requirement #28 (Rescission Relief/Incontestability)" (Sept 10, 2018; revising the Dec 21, 2017 principles; guidance "for re-structuring those provisions of their 2014 master policies"): 36-month relief when "Payments are timely and consecutive from the loan's origination based on the first payment due date, except for (i) no more than two 30-day delinquencies and (ii) no 60-day or greater delinquencies" and "The loan is current as of the date which is 36 months following the due date of the Borrower's first payment"; 60-month relief when "Either the loan is current at the time the 60th payment is made, or if the loan is not current at such time, it subsequently becomes current"; "Upon the completion of an Independent Validation that does not identify any Significant Defect, Rescission Relief must be granted"; QC-review relief; automated-tool relief for delegated and non-delegated loans; life-of-loan exclusions for single-loan fraud, pattern activity (3+ loans), data inaccuracies (5+ loans), non-compliance with laws impairing enforceability, non-first-lien, and unacceptable products. The 2020 master-policy generation (MI-company FAQs, e.g., Essent "New Master Policy" 2020) implements these principles — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — MI-company documents only; the partner's executed master policies govern]**.

**HPA disclosures.** 12 U.S.C. 4903 (current through Sept 3, 2026): "at the time at which the transaction is consummated, the mortgagee shall provide" — fixed-rate (a)(1)(A): "(i) a written initial amortization schedule; and (ii) written notice" that (I) the "mortgagor may cancel the requirement in accordance with section 4902(a)" on the cancellation date, (II) the "mortgagor may request cancellation in accordance with section 4902(a) earlier than" that date based on actual payments, (III) the "requirement for private mortgage insurance will automatically terminate on the termination date" and what that date is, and (IV) "there are exemptions to the right to cancellation and automatic termination of a requirement" (high-risk loans) and whether the exemptions apply; ARM (a)(1)(B): notice that the "mortgagor may cancel the requirement in accordance with section 4902(a) on the cancellation date," that the "requirement for private mortgage insurance will automatically terminate on the termination date," and that "on the termination date, the mortgagor will be notified of the termination or that the requirement" continues (because the loan is not current); (a)(2) high-risk loans: notice that "in no case may private mortgage insurance be required beyond the date that is the midpoint of the amortization period"; (a)(3) annual disclosure (servicing 10.4); (c): disclosures "may be provided on the annual disclosure relating to the escrow account"; (d): "The mortgagee or servicer may use standardized forms." Definitions, 12 U.S.C. 4901: (2) cancellation date — fixed rate: the date the principal balance "is first scheduled to reach 80 percent of the original value" (initial amortization schedule) or "based solely on actual payments, reaches 80 percent"; ARM: "amortization schedule then in effect"; (18) termination date — "first scheduled to reach 78 percent of the original value ... irrespective of the outstanding balance"; (12) original value — "lesser of the sales price of the property ... or the appraised value at the time," and for a refinance of the principal residence "only the appraised value relied upon by the mortgagee"; (5) initial amortization schedule; (7) midpoint of the amortization period; (15) residential mortgage transaction — a "single-family dwelling that is the principal residence" (consummated on/after July 29, 1999) — so second homes, investment properties and 2–4 unit properties are outside §4902/§4903(a) and receive only Fannie Mae's B-8.1-04 rules (10.x). 12 U.S.C. 4905 (00a-fed §11): (a) LPMI is PMI "payments for which are made by a person other than the borrower"; (b) "Sections 4902 through 4904 of this title do not apply in the case of lender paid mortgage insurance"; (c) disclosures "not later than the date on which a loan commitment is made" that LPMI "may not be canceled by the mortgagor, while borrower paid mortgage insurance could be cancelable," "usually results in a residential mortgage having a higher interest rate than it would in the case of borrower paid mortgage insurance," and "terminates only when the residential mortgage is refinanced, paid off, or otherwise terminated"; plus a notice "not later than 30 days after the termination date that would apply in the case of borrower paid mortgage insurance" (servicing 10.x `NTC_HPA_4905C2_LPMI_OPTIONS`).

**TRID MI lines.** 12 CFR 1026.37 (read on the Cornell LII mirror after eCFR returned 503; eCFR page date 9/08/2026 per 00a-fed): (c)(1)(i)(C) requires a separate projected-payments column when "The creditor must automatically terminate mortgage insurance or any functional equivalent under applicable law"; (c)(2)(ii): "The maximum amount payable for mortgage insurance premiums corresponding to the principal and interest payment disclosed pursuant to paragraph (c)(2)(i) of this section, labeled 'Mortgage Insurance'"; (c)(4)(ii): estimated taxes, insurance and assessments are "The sum of the charges identified in § 1026.43(b)(8), other than amounts identified in § 1026.4(b)(5), expressed as a monthly amount" (i.e., excluding MI); (g)(2)(ii) Prepaids: "the number of months for which mortgage insurance premiums are to be paid by the consumer at consummation and the total dollar amount to be paid by the consumer at consummation for such premiums, labeled 'Mortgage Insurance Premium ( ***_ months).'"; (g)(3) initial escrow payment at closing: "Mortgage Insurance ***_ per month for ____ mo." The CD mirrors these under §1026.38 (25.2). **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — mirror source; §1026.38 not re-read.]**

**Escrow of BPMI.** B2-1.5-04 (04/01/2020): "escrow deposits for the payment of premiums for borrower-purchased mortgage insurance (if applicable) are mandatory"; Servicing Guide B-1-01 (09/11/2024): "The servicer may not waive the individual escrow requirement for MIPs when the premiums are paid monthly."

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. Topic numbering: B7-1-03 is *Lender-Purchased MI* and B7-1-04 is *Financed Borrower-Purchased MI*; the row's "LPMI (B7-1-04)" is reversed. B7-1-05 (government mortgage insurance/loan guaranty — title not read today, **[UNVERIFIED]**) is out of scope for conventional loans.
2. "MI certificate before closing" — the Guide requires MI to be in place at purchase; the pre-closing commitment/certificate and the note-date activation are SM/partner policy and master-policy mechanics, not a Selling Guide deadline.
3. The row omits Fannie Mae's LTV rounding rule (B2-1.2-01), which decides the coverage band and the LLPA cell.
4. The row omits SFC 019 (LPMI) and SFC 281 (financed MI), the "MI Financed Indicator," the loan-limit cap on gross loan amount, and the cash-out exclusion for financed MI.
5. The HPA initial disclosure for fixed-rate loans must *embed the initial amortization schedule* — the row says "HPA disclosures at consummation" without this content requirement; the §4905 LPMI disclosure is due at **commitment**, not consummation.
6. HPA §§4902–4903(a) reach only principal-residence single-family loans consummated on/after July 29, 1999; second-home, investment and 2–4 unit loans get Fannie Mae's rules only — the row does not scope this.
7. "Approved MI companies list" — the July 2025 list includes MassHousing and United Guaranty (code 12) in addition to the six insurers the row names.
8. No Fannie Mae "rescission relief 2014/2020 framework" topic exists in the Selling Guide; the framework lives in the GSE Rescission Relief Principles (2017/2018) and the insurers' master policies — the platform records the master-policy version per insurer rather than citing a Guide topic.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `pricing_quotes` / `locks` (20.x/21.4) with LTV > 80% → MI rate quotes for the pricing engine (BPMI monthly/single/split; LPMI rate adjustment).
- `du.findings.received` (23.1): DU MI messages (required coverage per B7-1-02, HomeReady flag, `mi_requirement` in `du_submissions`), representative score and score model (Classic FICO vs VantageScore 4.0 → LLPA grid), property value basis (24.1/24.2 `value_basis`), transaction type, term, product (fixed/ARM), MH flag.
- `decision.issued{conditional_approval}` (21.6/23.x) → MI order (delegated) or submission (non-delegated).
- `application.*` changes affecting LTV, loan amount, property value, score, product → re-quote/re-order; `lock.executed` → premium plan fixed for the LE/CD.
- `closing.scheduled` (26.1) → certificate in file, HPA disclosure rendered into the closing package; `closing.consummated` → activation request; `loan.funded`/`loan.purchased` → activation confirmed, remittance of upfront premium, hand-off to 10.x.
- Borrower elections: premium plan choice (monthly vs single vs split vs LPMI) captured with the rate/cost comparison.

#### State machine

`mi_certificates.status`: `quoted` → `plan_selected` (borrower election; LE issued/revised) → `ordered` (delegated: DU Approve/Eligible + data submitted; non-delegated: full package to the insurer's underwriter) → `committed` (commitment/certificate received; `commitment_expires_at` set) → `docs_ready` (HPA disclosure rendered; certificate matches final terms) → `activation_requested` (note date) → `active` (insurer confirmation; premium remitted where upfront) [terminal for origination; boarded to 10.1] | `declined` (non-delegated decline → underwriter/`officer` path; loan restructured or denied via 21.6) | `cancelled_pre_closing` (loan withdrawn/denied; LTV ≤ 80% after value change) [terminal] | `expired` (commitment expired before closing → re-order) → `ordered`. Guards: `committed → docs_ready` requires `coverage_pct` = B7-1-02 requirement for the *final* LTV, `premium_plan` matches the CD, and `sfc_codes` computed; `active` requires `activation_effective_date = note date` (or the insurer's activation-date convention **[UNVERIFIED]**).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **LTV for MI** (B2-1.2-01): `ltv_raw = loan_amount ÷ value_basis` where `value_basis` = lower of sales price or appraised value (purchase) or appraised value (refinance); `ltv_pct_rounded = ceil(trunc(ltv_raw × 100, 2))`. Financed MI: `gross_ltv_pct_rounded = ceil(trunc((loan_amount + financed_premium) ÷ value_basis × 100, 2))`. Coverage is looked up on the **base** LTV; eligibility (Eligibility Matrix maximum) on the **gross** LTV; the gross loan amount must be ≤ the 2026 limit ($832,750 1-unit baseline; 00a-fnma §4.2).
2. **Coverage lookup** (B7-1-02): keyed by `(product ∈ {fixed_le_20, fixed_gt_20_or_arm}, homeready, standard_mh, ltv_band)`; `coverage_option='minimum'` allowed only where the table shows a * value, with `llpa_min_coverage_bps` from the matrix grid for the loan's score model (Classic FICO vs VantageScore 4.0) and representative score; the LLPA is passed to the pricing engine (20.x/21.4) and is "in addition to any other LLPAs."
3. **Premium arithmetic** (rates from the insurer's quote; never computed from an internal table): monthly BPMI `monthly_premium_cents = round_half_up(loan_amount_cents × rate_bps ÷ 10000 ÷ 12)`; annual plans divide by 12 for the escrow line; single premium `upfront = round_half_up(loan_amount_cents × rate_bps ÷ 10000)`; split plans = upfront portion + monthly renewal; financed single premium increases the note amount by `financed_premium_cents`. Insurer rounding conventions (some compute on the original balance for "constant" renewals, some re-rate at year 11 for "declining" renewals) are taken from the quote response and stored in `renewal_type`.
4. **Worked example 1 — purchase fixture (Columbus, OH; HomeReady-eligible; application Mon Oct 19, closing Wed Nov 18, 2026; first payment Fri Jan 1, 2027)**: sales price $457,800.00, appraised $460,000.00 → `value_basis = 45,780,000` cents; loan $412,000.00 → `ltv_raw = 0.899956…` → trunc 89.99 → **90%** → band 85.01–90.00% → standard coverage **25%** (30-year fixed; HomeReady also 25%); minimum option 12% with LLPA 0.375% (Classic FICO ≥ 740 row as extracted) = 412,000 × 0.00375 = **$1,545.00** — the agent presents both: e.g., illustrative BPMI monthly rates 0.38%/yr at 25% coverage vs 0.30%/yr at 12% coverage → $130.47 vs $103.00 per month; the $27.47 monthly saving costs $1,545.00 up front (break-even ≈ 56 months) — the borrower's election is recorded and the LE shows the selected plan. Selected: standard 25%, monthly BPMI at 0.38% (illustrative) → `monthly_premium_cents = round(41,200,000 × 38 ÷ 10000 ÷ 12) = 130.47` → **$130.47**; LE/CD projected payments "Mortgage Insurance $130.47" in every column until the termination column; escrow initial deposit line "Mortgage Insurance $130.47 per month for N mo." per 30.3's aggregate analysis; Prepaids "Mortgage Insurance Premium (0 months)" for a monthly plan.
5. **Worked example 1 (continued) — HPA dates from the initial amortization schedule** (illustrative note rate 6.375%, 360 months; P&I by the standard formula `P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)^−360)`, r = 0.06375/12 → **$2,570.34**): original value $457,800.00; 80% = $366,240.00; 78% = $357,084.00. Schedule (interest rounded half-up to cents each month, principal = payment − interest): payment 1 (due Jan 1, 2027) interest $2,188.75, principal $381.59, balance $411,618.41; … payment 94 (due **Oct 1, 2034**) balance $365,633.53 — first scheduled balance ≤ 80% → **cancellation date Oct 1, 2034**; payment 108 (due **Dec 1, 2035**) balance $356,532.66 — first ≤ 78% → **termination date Dec 1, 2035**; midpoint = payment 180 (due Dec 1, 2041) → final termination **Jan 1, 2042** (first day of the following month, §4902(c)). `NTC_HPA_4903_INITIAL_FIXED` states these three dates, the actual-payments alternative (§4901(2)(A)(ii)), the good-payment-history/current/no-decline conditions of §4902(a), and that no high-risk exemption applies; the LE's projected-payments table gets a new column beginning with payment 109 (year 10) showing "Mortgage Insurance —/0" (§1026.37(c)(1)(i)(C)) — 21.2/25.2 consume `termination_date`.
6. **Worked example 2 — financed single premium on the same loan**: quote 1.60% single premium (illustrative) → `upfront = 41,200,000 × 160 ÷ 10000 = 6,592,000` cents (**$6,592.00**) financed → note amount **$418,592.00**; base LTV 90% → coverage 25% (unchanged); gross LTV = 418,592 ÷ 457,800 = 91.4355… → trunc 91.43 → **92%** ≤ 97% HomeReady maximum → eligible; gross amount ≤ $832,750 → eligible; delivery: SFC 281, Financed MI Amount 6,592.00, MI Financed Indicator; LE: loan amount $418,592, Prepaids "Mortgage Insurance Premium (360 months) $6,592.00" paid from loan proceeds (**[PARTIALLY VERIFIED placement]**), no monthly MI line; HPA: financed single-premium BPMI is still borrower-paid PMI — the §4903 initial disclosure applies and 10.x handles refund on cancellation per the refund schedule (`refundable`); the P&I rises to $2,611.47 (418,592 × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)^−360)) — recomputed by the amortization engine, not hand-entered. Cash-out refinances cannot use this plan.
7. **Worked example 3 — LPMI**: partner prices LPMI as +0.250% to the note rate (illustrative; B7-1-03 requires the premium to be a lender corporate obligation and the servicing compensation retained to rise by at least the renewal premium — 30.x/10.x); `NTC_HPA_4905_LPMI` delivered with the conditional approval on Mon Oct 26, 2026 (before any commitment letter) stating LPMI "may not be canceled by the mortgagor," "usually results in a residential mortgage having a higher interest rate," and "terminates only when the residential mortgage is refinanced, paid off, or otherwise terminated"; SFC 019 at delivery; `mi_policies.lpmi_equiv_termination_date = Dec 1, 2035` (BPMI-equivalent termination date) so 10.x sends the §4905(c)(2) notice by Dec 31, 2035; no §4903 initial disclosure (§4905(b)).
8. **Delegated vs non-delegated**: if the insurer's program accepts DU Approve/Eligible with the partner's delegated authority (`du_reliance=true`), the agent submits the order with the DU casefile ID and the 1003/1008 data and receives a commitment automatically; otherwise the full credit package goes to the insurer's underwriter (external human; SLA tracked as an `escalation` of role `settlement_agent`-class external party with 2 `business_days_creditor` follow-up). A decline triggers `underwriting_reviewer` review before any adverse action (21.6).
9. **Re-underwriting triggers**: any change in loan amount, value basis, LTV band, product, term, occupancy, score model or representative score, or DU recommendation → re-quote; if the coverage band changes, a revised LE (21.5 changed circumstance) and a re-order are required; certificate must match the final CD.
10. **Activation and premium flow**: monthly BPMI — no premium at closing; first premium is due with the first payment and remitted by the subservicer (10.x); single/split upfront — collected on the CD (borrower-paid) or added to the note (financed) and remitted by SM to the insurer within the master-policy window; LPMI — partner corporate obligation remitted by SM on the partner's behalf (27.2 settlement economics). Activation effective on the note date; the ULDD carries MI company code, certificate number, coverage percentage and premium data (29.3).
11. **HPA scope**: `hpa_covered = (units = 1) ∧ (occupancy = primary) ∧ (premium_plan ∉ lpmi_*)`; otherwise render the Fannie Mae-only notice variant (B-8.1-04 midpoint/current-value rules, 10.x) — no §4903(a) content.

#### Integrations
- **`mi/*` adapters** (per insurer): `rateQuote` (MISMO 3.5 JSON/XML; idempotent by payload hash; cache until `quote_expires_at`), `submitOrder` (delegated/non-delegated; DU casefile reference), `getCommitment`/`getCertificate`, `activate` (note date), `cancel`, `remitPremium`/`premiumFile`; acks/rejects per insurer; retries with idempotency keys; outage → alternate approved insurer with a re-quote (never a manual certificate). Portal fallback (insurer web tools such as Rate Express/RateStar) is an SM operator step, not a Fannie Mae portal island.
- **DU (23.1)**: MI requirement messages consumed; DU casefile ID passed to the insurer.
- **LE/CD engine (21.2/25.2)**: projected-payments MI amounts and termination column, prepaids/escrow lines, loan amount (financed MI).
- **Pricing (20.x/21.4)**: minimum-coverage LLPA, LPMI rate adjustment.
- **Closing package (26.1)**: HPA initial disclosure and amortization schedule rendered with the note data; **29.3/29.4**: SFC 019/281, MI data points, activation gate; **30.4 → 10.1**: `mi_policies` seeding.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`title-closing` agent (tools: `computeLtvRounded`, `lookupCoverage`, `lookupMinCoverageLlpa`, `requestMiQuotes`, `compareMiPlans`, `recordPlanElection`, `submitMiOrder`, `parseCommitment`, `verifyCertificateMatchesTerms`, `buildInitialAmortizationSchedule`, `computeHpaDates`, `renderHpaDisclosure`, `renderLpmiDisclosure`, `requestActivation`, `remitPremium`, `evaluateGates`, `seedMiPolicy`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: computes the rounded LTV and the coverage requirement at DU findings; obtains quotes from at least two approved insurers; presents plan options with cost comparisons (never steering — fair-lending monitoring by `compliance-sentinel`, 31.2); orders on approval (delegated where available); verifies the certificate against final terms; renders the HPA disclosure with the immutable initial schedule; activates at the note date; remits; seeds 10.1. Decision record: `{application_id, ltv_raw, ltv_rounded, gross_ltv_rounded, coverage_required_pct, coverage_option, llpa_bps, quotes[{insurer, plan, rate_bps, premium}], election, order{type, du_reliance, casefile_id}, certificate{number, coverage_pct, expires}, hpa{original_value, cancellation_date, termination_date, midpoint}, gates, rationale, rule_set_version, model_version}`. Guardrails: never derive a premium from an internal rate table; never select the minimum-coverage option without the recorded borrower/partner election and the LLPA in pricing; never render an HPA schedule from anything but the final note terms; never activate before the note date or deliver without `active`; never place a financed premium on a cash-out or above the loan limit; never use an insurer absent from the approved list. Escalations: insurer underwriter (external) for non-delegated files; `underwriting_reviewer` on MI decline before any adverse action; `officer` for coverage-option policy exceptions and master-policy questions (SLA 2 `business_days_creditor`); `human_agent` on request. Borrower-facing: plan comparison discloses automation and that MI protects the lender; HPA disclosures are in the partner's name as mortgagee.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Appraised value below the sales price → value basis drops; LTV may cross a band (e.g., 89.99% → 90.01% → 91% → 30% coverage) → re-quote, revised LE, re-order.
- DU Approve/Eligible withdrawn on resubmission → delegated order invalid; re-order or non-delegated path.
- Insurer declines (non-delegated) → `underwriting_reviewer`; alternative insurer allowed; if none, restructure (lower LTV) or adverse action with the specific reason (collateral/LTV, not "MI declined" alone).
- Commitment expires before a delayed closing → re-order with the same insurer; new certificate number recorded; HPA dates unchanged unless terms change.
- Lock changes note rate (ARM ↔ fixed, term ≤ 20 vs > 20) → coverage band changes for fixed ≤ 20-year loans (6/12/25/35).
- HomeReady eligibility lost (income > 80% AMI) → coverage rises from 25% to 35% at 95.01–97%; LLPA waivers change (20.x).
- Manufactured home at 95.01–97% → MI "Not applicable" → loan ineligible at that LTV (Eligibility Matrix governs).
- Financed premium pushes the gross amount over the loan limit or the Eligibility Matrix maximum → decline the plan; offer monthly/split.
- LPMI selected after the LE → §4905(c) disclosure must precede the commitment; if a commitment letter already issued, treat the plan change as a new commitment with the disclosure delivered first.
- ARM: the initial disclosure uses the ARM variant; 10.x recomputes the 78% date at each reset from the "amortization schedule then in effect."
- Second home/investment/2–4 unit: `hpa_covered=false` → no §4903(a) notice; Fannie Mae B-8.1-04 rules only (10.x); TRID projected payments still show MI and its Fannie Mae termination assumption (open question 24.6-Q4).
- Insurer API outage at activation → activate by the insurer's portal (SM operator) within the 1-business-day policy; never ship to delivery without confirmation.
- Refund-schedule mismatch (single premium marked non-refundable by the insurer but refundable on the CD) → correct the CD before consummation (25.2).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Coverage option policy** — offer the minimum-coverage option (lower premium, LLPA) by default or only on borrower request? Recommended default: present both with the break-even comparison; default selection = standard coverage; `compliance-sentinel` monitors election patterns for disparate outcomes.
2. **Insurer panel and delegated authority** — which insurers and whether SM orders under delegated authority relying on DU Approve/Eligible? Recommended default: all six private insurers on the approved list with delegated authority where the partner's master policy grants it; non-delegated only when the insurer requires it.
3. **"Loan commitment" anchor for §4905(c)** — the statute does not define the commitment; recommended default: the conditional-approval letter (first written commitment to lend) — deliver the LPMI disclosure with, and dated no later than, that letter; if LPMI is elected later, re-issue the approval after the disclosure.
4. **Projected-payments MI termination for non-HPA loans** (second home/investment/2–4 unit) — use Fannie Mae's midpoint/B-8.1-04 assumptions for the TRID column? Recommended default: show MI through the 78% scheduled date (consistent with the automatic-termination policy Fannie Mae applies to these loans under B-8.1-04) — confirm with 21.2/25.2 counsel.
5. **Upfront premium remittance window** — master-policy terms (typically within a stated number of days of closing) not read; recommended default: remit within 10 calendar days of funding and reconcile monthly with each insurer.
6. **State MI disclosure overlays at origination** (MN/NY/CA identified in 10.x) — recommended default: include a state addendum to the HPA notice where `jurisdiction_rules.mi.state_notice` is set; legal review before go-live.
