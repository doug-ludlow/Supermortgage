### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**12 CFR 1026.41(e)(5) (eCFR current as of Sept. 3, 2026; verified 2026-09-09).** *(i) Exemption*: a servicer is exempt from §1026.41 for a loan while (A) any consumer on the loan "is a debtor in bankruptcy under title 11" or "has discharged personal liability for the mortgage loan pursuant to 11 U.S.C. 727, 1141, 1228, or 1328," **and** (B) one of: "(1) The consumer requests in writing that the servicer cease providing a periodic statement or coupon book; (2) The consumer's bankruptcy plan provides that the consumer will surrender the dwelling ..., provides for the avoidance of the lien ..., or otherwise does not provide for, as applicable, the payment of pre-bankruptcy arrearage or the maintenance of payments due under the mortgage loan; (3) A court enters an order in the bankruptcy case providing for the avoidance of the lien ..., lifting the automatic stay ... with regard to the dwelling ..., or requiring the servicer to cease providing a periodic statement or coupon book; or (4) The consumer files with the court overseeing the bankruptcy case a statement of intention identifying an intent to surrender the dwelling ... and a consumer has not made any partial or periodic payment on the mortgage loan after the commencement of the ... case." *(ii) Reaffirmation or request*: the exemption ends "if the consumer reaffirms personal liability for the loan or any consumer on the loan requests in writing that the servicer provide a periodic statement or coupon book, unless a court enters an order ... requiring the servicer to cease." *(iii) Exclusive address*: a servicer may designate an address for (i)(B)(1) and (ii) requests but must use the same address for both. *(iv) Timing*: on any of the events (A)(1) the loan becomes subject to (f), (2) ceases to be subject to (f), or (3) the servicer ceases to qualify for the (i) exemption, the servicer "is exempt ... with respect to the next periodic statement or coupon book that would otherwise be required but thereafter must provide modified or unmodified periodic statements." *(f) Modified statements* for a consumer who is a debtor in bankruptcy or has discharged personal liability (and no (e)(5) exemption applies): (1) the statement "may omit the information set forth in paragraphs (d)(1)(ii) [late fee] and (d)(8)(i), (ii), and (v) [delinquency start date, risk-of-foreclosure warning, first-notice statement]," and the "shown more prominently" requirement for the amount due does not apply; (2) must include "(i) A statement identifying the consumer's status as a debtor in bankruptcy or the discharged status of the mortgage loan; and (ii) A statement that the periodic statement is for informational purposes only"; (3) Chapter 12/13 debtors: (i) may also omit (d)(8)(iii), (iv), (vi) and (vii) (account history, loss-mitigation program, amount to bring current, counselor reference); (ii) the amount due "may be limited to the date and amount of the post-petition payments due and any post-petition fees and charges"; (iii) the explanation limited to the post-petition breakdown, post-petition fees/charges and post-petition past-due amounts; (iv) transaction activity "must include all payments the servicer has received since the last statement, including all post-petition and pre-petition payments and payments of post-petition fees and charges, and all post-petition fees and charges"; (v) pre-petition arrearage: total pre-petition payments received since the last statement, since the beginning of the case, and "the current balance of the consumer's pre-petition arrearage"; (vi) additional disclosures — that the amount due includes only post-petition payments; if applicable, that the consumer should pay the trustee; that the statement may not reflect payments made to the trustee and may not be consistent with the trustee's records; encouragement to contact the attorney or trustee; and, if the consumer is more than 45 days delinquent on post-petition payments, "that the servicer has not received all the payments that became due since the consumer filed for bankruptcy"; (4) the modified statement may go "to any or all of the primary obligors, even if a primary obligor ... is not a debtor in bankruptcy"; (5) coupon-book analog. **Official interpretations (verified 2026-09-09):** 41(e)(5)-1 (a request by the consumer's bankruptcy counsel or agent "is deemed to be submitted by the consumer"), -2 (the most recent written request controls), -3 (effective on receipt), -4 (a revived case re-triggers (e)(5)), -5 (any one joint obligor's status triggers the exemption), -6 (either spouse's written request for statements must be honored); 41(e)(5)(i)(B)(2)-1 (the most recently filed plan, confirmed or not); (i)(B)(4)-1 (most recent statement of intention); 41(e)(5)(iv)(A)-1/-2 ((f) applies when any consumer becomes a debtor or discharges liability; ceases on reaffirmation or dismissal without discharge; the exemption ends when the case is dismissed without discharge, the plan is amended to retain/pay, or the consumer pays despite a surrender filing); 41(e)(5)(iv)(B)-1 (single-statement examples: event before the end of the 15-day courtesy period and no statement yet provided for the next cycle → skip one statement, comply for the following cycle; event after the courtesy period when the statement was already sent → no correction and skip one more); -2/-3 (coupon books; the exemption "might apply more than once"); 41(f)-1 ("after the debtor exits bankruptcy, § 1026.41(f) continues to apply if the consumer has discharged personal liability ... but ... does not apply if the consumer has reaffirmed"); -2 (alternative terminology such as "payment amount"/"account history"); -3 (all other §1026.41 requirements continue unless expressly modified); -4 (further modification "to facilitate compliance with title 11 ..., court orders, and local rules"); -5 (commence per (e)(5)(iv)); -6 (a reaffirming consumer is not a debtor in bankruptcy); 41(f)(3)-1 (pre-petition payments cure pre-bankruptcy defaults; post-petition payments satisfy periodic payments as they come due; the plan controls; where the plan reduces the balance or rate the servicer may show original terms, adjusted terms, or refer to the trustee/attorney); -2 (post-petition fees are those imposed after filing; fees the court requires in an amended POC may be treated as pre-petition arrearage); -3 (first statement after an exemption ends may limit activity to the period since the last due date during the exemption); 41(f)(3)(ii)-1 and (iii)-1 (amount due/explanation need not include pre-petition arrearage or trustee payments; other agreed amounts may be added if also in the explanation); 41(f)(3)(v)-1 (disputed/undetermined arrearage may be acknowledged as unresolved and omitted until the servicer "has had a reasonable opportunity to determine" it, but must appear after the claims bar date); 41(f)(4)-1/-2 (one modified statement to all obligors; obligors in different chapters — servicer's discretion on (f)(3)). CFPB Mortgage Servicing FAQs (Mar. 20, 2018; verified in 7.1): trustee payments actually received are transaction activity; using H-30(E)/(F) is "unlikely to violate the automatic stay"; statements may go to counsel where bankruptcy law bars direct contact; confirmed successors in bankruptcy get (f)-modified statements.

**Communications overlays owned here.** *Reg X §1024.39(c)* (eCFR current Sept. 4, 2026; verified): while any borrower is a debtor, the servicer is exempt from live contact (a); exempt from the written notice (b) "if no loss mitigation option is available, or if any borrower on the mortgage loan has provided a notification pursuant to" FDCPA §805(c); otherwise it must send the written notice with three changes — "(A) If a borrower is delinquent when the borrower becomes a debtor in bankruptcy, a servicer must provide the written notice ... not later than the 45th day after the borrower files a bankruptcy petition"; "(B) The written notice ... may not contain a request for payment"; "(C) A servicer is not required to provide the written notice ... more than once during a single bankruptcy case"; (c)(2) compliance resumes "after the next payment due date that follows the earliest of" dismissal, closure or reaffirmation; after a discharge the servicer need not resume live contact but must resume the written notice if the borrower makes "any partial or periodic payment." Comments 39(c)-1 (the notice may go to the borrower's bankruptcy counsel; confirming counsel by the court filings is reasonable), 39(c)(1)-1 (exemption from the petition), 39(c)(1)(ii)-1 (a loss-mitigation option is "available" when the owner offers an alternative to foreclosure through the servicer for which the borrower may apply), 39(c)(1)(iii)-1 (joint obligors — notice to any borrower), 39(c)(2)-1 (revived case; still once per case), 39(d)-2/(d)(2)-1 (FDCPA cease + bankruptcy interplay). *Reg F §1006.6(b)(2)* (eCFR current Sept. 4, 2026): "A debt collector must not communicate or attempt to communicate with a consumer in connection with the collection of any debt if the debt collector knows the consumer is represented by an attorney" unless the attorney fails to respond within a reasonable period or consents; (c) cease-communication rules. *§524(j)*: post-discharge periodic-payment communications "in the ordinary course of business" are permitted for the principal-residence lender in lieu of in rem relief; *§362(a)(6)* bars collection acts during the case; *Fannie Mae E-2.1-03*: suspend "any and all debt collection efforts" on notice unless counsel advises otherwise; *D2-2-03* payment reminders are collection communications (suppressed); *A4-2.1-04* channels "as permitted by law."

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) "Per modified statement rules" hides two distinct regimes — exemption (no statement) and modification (H-30(E)/(F)) — plus the single-statement transition rule and the resumption triggers, all of which are deadline-bearing. (2) The blueprint has no row for the bankruptcy-specific early-intervention notice (45th day after the petition; no payment request; once per case) or for the Reg F represented-consumer rule — both are specified here. (3) Automation class "a" holds for rendering, but the addressing and cease/resume decisions depend on docket facts and counsel instructions that 14.1 must supply. (4) The row's "Core" omits print/mail proof-of-delivery and the counsel-addressing channel.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `statement.cycle.opened` (7.1) for every loan with an open `bankruptcy` case or `debt_discharged=true`; `bankruptcy.status.changed` (any phase change: petition, plan filed/amended/confirmed, SOI filed, relief order, reaffirmation filed/approved/rescinded, discharge, dismissal, closure, reopening, conversion) → re-evaluate the variant and the single-statement exemption; `bankruptcy.statement_request.received{kind ∈ {cease, resume}, from ∈ {debtor, counsel, agent}, received_at, document_id}`; `court.order.received{kind ∈ {cease_statements, lien_avoidance, stay_relief}}`; `bankruptcy.plan.treatment.changed`; `payment.received` after a surrender SOI (ends the (i)(B)(4) exemption); `regx.days_delinquent.crossed_45_postpetition` (post-petition ledger; for (f)(3)(vi)(E)); `loan.boarded` with bankruptcy flags (1.1 — the transferor's exemption/cease status carries over); early-intervention triggers from 11.2 (`delinquency.day45.reached`) and the petition event (for (c)(1)(iii)(A)).

#### Data model
- `bk_statement_status` (per loan; history in `case_events`): `loan_id`, `case_id`, `mode` ∈ {standard, modified_ch7_11, modified_ch12_13, exempt_cease_request, exempt_plan_surrender, exempt_court_order, exempt_soi_surrender, exempt_charged_off_n_a, single_statement_skip}, `basis_document_id` (written request image, plan, order, SOI), `basis_event_id`, `exclusive_address_used bool`, `last_request jsonb` {kind, from, received_at} (most recent controls), `addressing` ∈ {debtor, counsel, counsel_and_debtor, trustee_copy}, `addressing_basis`, `single_statement_used_for_cycle date?`, `resume_from_cycle date?`, `debt_discharged bool`, `reaffirmed bool`, `updated_at`.
- `statement_snapshots.bk` (7.1, extended): `{chapter, petition_date, discharge_date?, status_text ('debtor in bankruptcy' | 'discharged'), postpetition_due {date, amount_cents, breakdown {principal, interest, escrow}}, postpetition_past_due_cents, postpetition_fees_since_last_cents, postpetition_fees_unpaid_cents, transactions[] (tagged pre/post-petition; trustee flag), prepetition_received_since_last_cents, prepetition_received_since_filing_cents, prepetition_arrearage_balance_cents, arrearage_undetermined bool, trustee_pays_postpetition bool, trustee_pays_arrearage bool, postpetition_days_delinquent, more_than_45 bool, plan_modified_terms bool, omitted_items[]}` — every figure from `bankruptcy_ledger_views` (14.1) with the snapshot hash.
- `bk_early_intervention` — `case_id`, `loan_id`, `required bool` (loss-mit available and no FDCPA cease), `deadline date` (45th day after petition when delinquent at filing; else the ordinary 11.2 clock), `sent_at?`, `recipient` ∈ {borrower, counsel}, `notice_id?`, `once_per_case_satisfied bool`.
- `communications_matrix` (rule table, versioned): `communication_code` × `mode` ∈ {stay_in_effect, stay_relief_granted, discharged_no_reaffirm, dismissed, codebtor_protected} → `action` ∈ {send, send_modified, send_via_counsel, suppress}, `legend_required bool`, `citation`.

#### State machine
`bk_statement_status.mode`: `standard` → (petition/discharge event) `single_statement_skip` (one cycle, optional) → `modified_ch7_11` | `modified_ch12_13` → (cease request / surrender plan / court order / SOI surrender + no payment) `exempt_*` → (written request for statements / reaffirmation / plan amended to pay / payment after surrender SOI / dismissal) `single_statement_skip` → `modified_*` or `standard` (dismissal without discharge, reaffirmation) ; `discharged_no_reaffirm` keeps `modified_ch7_11` (or `modified_ch12_13` while a Chapter 13 case remains open) for the life of the loan unless an exemption condition applies. Actors: `disclosures` agent (7.1) renders; `bankruptcy-ops` sets `mode`/`addressing` from docket facts; `attorney` when an order/instruction conflicts with (f); `human_agent` on request.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Variant decision (runs before 7.1's variant order, feeding step 5 of 7.1).** Inputs: case facts (chapter, phase, plan treatment, SOI, orders), requests (most recent controls), `debt_discharged`, `reaffirmed`. Decision: (a) any consumer a debtor or discharged (§727/1141/1228/1328)? if not → `standard`; (b) exemption test in order — court order to cease (exempt), most recent plan surrenders/avoids lien/does not provide for arrearage or maintenance (exempt), Chapter 7 SOI surrender with no post-petition payment (exempt — ends on the first payment), written cease request at the exclusive address or from counsel (exempt; overridden by a later written request for statements from any obligor unless a court order requires cessation); (c) otherwise `modified_ch12_13` for Chapter 12/13 debtors, `modified_ch7_11` for Chapter 7/11 debtors and discharged consumers; obligors in different chapters → `modified_ch12_13` at the servicer's discretion (comment 41(f)(4)-2; default: use it). (d) Single-statement exemption: on each (e)(5)(iv)(A) event the platform records whether the next required statement is skipped — default **skip** when the event falls before that cycle's `courtesy_period_end` and the statement has not been rendered; otherwise send the already-rendered statement unmodified and skip the following one (comment (iv)(B)-1 examples); the skipped cycle is logged with the event id. (e) Reaffirmation: `modified_*` continues until the §524(c)(4) rescission window lapses (14.1 gate) — then `standard`. (f) Dismissal without discharge → `standard` after one skippable cycle. (g) Revived/reopened case → (e)(5) re-applies (comment -4).
2. **Content computation — Chapter 12/13 (`NTC_REGZ_41_STMT_BK12_13`).** From `bankruptcy_ledger_views`: amount due = post-petition installment due this cycle + post-petition past-due installments (unpaid, FIFO on the post-petition ledger) + unpaid post-petition fees that have been noticed under 14.2 and allowed (memo-only fees are never shown as due — comment 41(f)(3)-2 and Rule 3002.1(c) alignment); suspense funds disclosed under (d)(3)/(d)(5), never netted; explanation = post-petition P&I/escrow breakdown (scheduled split, 14.1 rule 6), post-petition fees since last statement, post-petition past-due; transaction activity = every receipt in the period tagged pre-/post-petition and "trustee" where applicable, plus post-petition fees imposed; pre-petition arrearage block = receipts since last statement, since filing, and the arrearage balance (`bk_prepetition_arrearage`), or the "amount not yet determined" acknowledgment until the POC is computed, and always after the bar date (comment (v)-1); (f)(3)(vi) statements selected by `trustee_pays_postpetition`/`trustee_pays_arrearage` and `postpetition_days_delinquent > 45`; late-fee line, delinquency box and foreclosure language omitted; "debtor in bankruptcy"/"informational purposes only" statements present; counselor block retained (policy; may be omitted). Cramdown/plan-modified terms → show the plan-terms amounts with the trustee/attorney referral sentence (comment 41(f)(3)-1.ii).
   **Worked example (fixture BK-13-A, direct-pay example B; statement for the Feb 1, 2027 due date, statement date Sunday 2027-01-17, mailed by Wed 2027-01-20).** Post-petition installment = $2,054.22 P&I + $752.50 escrow = **$2,806.72** (escrow change noticed under 14.2). As of the 2027-01-17 cut-off: Oct 1 paid 10-05, Nov 1 paid 11-20, Dec 1 and Jan 1 unpaid, $1,500.00 received 01-15 in post-petition suspense. **Amount due (Feb 1, 2027): $8,420.16** = 2,806.72 + past-due post-petition 5,613.44; explanation: Feb installment principal $368.63 / interest $1,685.59 / escrow $752.50 (scheduled split for payment #43), post-petition fees since last statement $0.00, past-due post-petition $5,613.44; past payments: since last statement $1,500.00 (unapplied $1,500.00), YTD 2027 $1,500.00, unapplied held $1,500.00; transaction activity: "01/15/2027 Payment received — $1,500.00 — held in unapplied funds (post-petition)"; (d)(5): "We need $1,306.72 more to apply a full post-petition payment"; pre-petition arrearage: received since last statement $0.00, since filing $0.00, current pre-petition arrearage **$14,241.94**; disclosures: amount due includes only post-petition amounts; the statement may not reflect payments made to the trustee (the trustee pays the arrearage); contact your attorney or trustee; and, because the Dec 1 installment is 47 days past due on the post-petition ledger, "we have not received all the payments that became due since you filed for bankruptcy"; no late-fee line, no delinquency box, no foreclosure language; "You are a debtor in a Chapter 13 bankruptcy case (No. 4:26-bk-31234) — this statement is for informational purposes only." Timing: courtesy for Jan 1 ended Jan 16 → due by Jan 20 (`REGZ_1026_41B_STATEMENT_PROMPT_4`).
3. **Content computation — Chapter 7/11 and discharged loans (`NTC_REGZ_41_STMT_BK7_11`).** Contractual figures (no plan): amount due = current installment + past-due installments + unpaid pre-petition late charges/fees; no new late charges post-petition or post-discharge (14.3-Q4); the late-fee line and (d)(8)(i)/(ii)/(v) omitted; account history, loss-mitigation program (if any), amount to bring current and counselor reference retained; status sentence "debtor in bankruptcy" or "you received a discharge ... this statement is for informational purposes only and is not an attempt to collect a debt from you personally." **Worked example (Chapter 7 example C; statement for the Jan 1, 2027 due date, statement date 2026-12-17, discharge entered 2026-12-15).** Unpaid installments Jun 1–Dec 1, 2026 (7 × $2,699.22 = $18,894.54; the Oct 5 payment cured May 1); pre-petition late charges $410.84; **amount to bring current $19,305.38; amount due Jan 1, 2027 $22,004.60**; account history: Jul–Dec 2026 each "$2,699.22 remaining"; `regx_days_delinquent` on Dec 17 = 199 (from Jun 2) — the "date delinquency began" and risk language are omitted under (f)(1), the history/amount-to-cure retained; first-notice statement omitted; discharge legend present. No single-statement exemption arises at discharge (the loan was already subject to (f) since the petition — comment 41(f)-1).
4. **Addressing.** Order of precedence: (i) court order/local rule → follow; (ii) counsel's written instruction (cease → exemption; "send to my office" → `counsel`; "send to client" → `debtor`); (iii) `fdcpa_debt_collector=true` (11.4) and a debtor's attorney of record → `counsel` with a debtor copy only if counsel consents (Reg F §1006.6(b)(2), policy — the statement carries (f)(2) disclaimers but is routed conservatively); (iv) otherwise `debtor` at the address of record with a courtesy copy to counsel on request. Co-obligors: one modified statement may serve all primary obligors ((f)(4)); a non-filing co-obligor protected by §1301 receives the same modified statement (informational) — no separate unmodified statement (comment 41(f)(4)-1). Trustee: no statement (trustee vouchers are reconciled in 14.1), unless local practice asks for copies.
5. **Requests.** A written cease request (any obligor, counsel or agent) at the exclusive address — or received anywhere from counsel — is effective on receipt: the current cycle's statement is held if not yet mailed; the request image is the exemption evidence; a later written request for statements from any obligor resumes them from the next cycle (single-statement exemption available; the first statement may limit activity to the period since the last due date while exempt — comment 41(f)(3)-3). Requests received elsewhere than the exclusive address are honoured anyway (policy) and logged.
6. **Early intervention in bankruptcy (§1024.39(c)).** On the petition: live contact suspended; if the loan was delinquent at the petition and a loss-mitigation option is available (Fannie Mae's options always are, comment 39(c)(1)(ii)-1) and no FDCPA §805(c) cease is on file → the modified written notice (no payment request; owner = Fannie Mae; option types; counselor info) by the 45th day after the petition (fixture: 2026-09-08 + 45 = **2026-10-23**), sent to the borrower or to counsel (comment 39(c)-1), once per case; a later delinquency during the case starts the ordinary 45-day clock (once per case). After dismissal/closure/reaffirmation, 11.1/11.2 resume after the next due date; after discharge, live contact never resumes but the written notice resumes if any payment is received (both notices without a payment request while `debt_discharged`).
7. **Communications matrix (informational vs. collection).** `stay_in_effect`: send — periodic statements (modified), escrow statements (3.3, legend), ARM notices (7.2/7.3), privacy notices (7.5), insurance/FPI notices (9.x, legend), PMI notices (10.x), Form 1098, payoff/reinstatement statements on request (via counsel where represented), NoE/RFI responses (4.1/4.2; to counsel where represented), SCRA notices, modified early-intervention notice, loss-mitigation communications through counsel (E-2.1-09; 12.x), the 14.1 payment-instructions letter; **suppress** — D2-2-03 payment reminders, collection calls/texts/e-mails, breach/acceleration letters, late-charge notices, foreclosure notices, debt-validation follow-ups (Reg F validation notice already sent stays), autodraft solicitations. `discharged_no_reaffirm`: as above plus the in rem breach letter variant (13.x) and property-inspection/preservation contacts (9.x), every document with the discharge legend; no personal-liability demands ever; payment acceptance and statements permitted under §524(j). `stay_relief_granted`: foreclosure communications resume in rem; statements continue per (e)(5)/(f) (relief order is an (i)(B)(3) exemption ground only if the servicer elects it — default: keep sending modified statements, 14.3-Q3). `dismissed`: full resumption after the next due date. `codebtor_protected` (§1301): no collection communications to the co-obligor for the consumer debt; informational statements permitted.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agents:** `bankruptcy-ops` decides `mode`/`addressing` and supplies the ledger views; `disclosures` (7.1) renders, checks and sends; `borrower-comms` handles inbound questions with the informational script and routes written requests. Tools (bankruptcy-ops): `bk.statement_mode.set`, `bk.ledger_views.read`, `docket.read`, `requests.classify` (LLM classification of inbound letters as cease/resume/other with confidence; human verification < 0.90), `escalation.file`.
- **Decision record:** `{loan_id, case_id, cycle_due_date, mode, exemption_basis {kind, document_id, event_id}, single_statement_skip bool, addressing, addressing_basis, early_intervention {required, deadline, recipient}, ledger_view_hash, rule_set_version (regz.periodic_statement.2018; regx.early_intervention.2025-07), model_version, rationale, reviewer_id?}`.
- **Guardrails:** no exemption without a linked evidence document; no `standard` variant while any consumer is a debtor or discharged (unless reaffirmed and the rescission window lapsed); the LLM never alters figures; a cease request is never inferred from a phone call (writing required — a call is answered with instructions for a written request, and the agent may take the request through an authenticated portal message as "in writing"); statements are never suppressed for returned mail; no payment request in bankruptcy early-intervention notices (checklist `block`).
- **Escalations:** `attorney` (via 14.1) when an order/instruction conflicts with (f) or when local rules restrict debtor contact; `human_agent` on request; `officer` for a portfolio-wide suppression decision; none to Fannie Mae.
- **AI-off path:** the 7.1 ops worklist with a bankruptcy queue; staff set modes from docket documents with the same evidence rule.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Petition learned late** (statement already sent with collection language after the petition date): send a corrected modified statement next cycle; log the §342(g) notice timing; no retroactive "violation" self-report is required but the event is in the audit trail.
- **Cease request from one spouse, request for statements from the other:** the most recent written request controls; either spouse's request for statements must be honoured (comment -6).
- **Plan amended from surrender to retain:** exemption ends (comment (iv)(A)-2); resume with the single-statement exemption.
- **Payment received after a surrender SOI (Chapter 7):** the (i)(B)(4) exemption ends on the payment; resume statements.
- **Chapter 13 case dismissed then re-filed:** `standard` after dismissal (one skippable cycle), then `modified_ch12_13` again on the new petition (another skippable cycle).
- **Arrearage disputed / POC objection pending:** show the acknowledgment sentence and the servicer's figure only after the bar date (comment (v)-1).
- **Trustee pays post-petition but the debtor also pays directly** (conduit confusion): both receipts appear as transaction activity with their source tags; the amount due follows the post-petition ledger.
- **Successor in interest who is the debtor:** modified statements to the successor (FAQ); a non-debtor confirmed successor without an assumption gets no statement (7.1(g)).
- **Transfer-in with an unknown cease status:** default `modified_*` (send) unless the transferor's file shows a cease request or order — a missed cease request is corrected on the first inbound complaint; a wrongly suppressed statement is the worse error.
- **Statement returned undeliverable / debtor address changes to counsel's office:** address research (4.x); counsel-addressed copy continues.
- **Discharge injunction complaints** (debtor alleges statements are collection): the (f)(2) legends, §524(j), the CFPB FAQ and the addressing basis form the response package for `attorney`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **14.3-Q1 Skip or send on the transition cycle?** Default: skip the next statement when the event precedes the courtesy end and nothing was rendered; otherwise send unmodified and skip the following cycle (comment (iv)(B)-1).
2. **14.3-Q2 Addressing for FDCPA-covered loans with counsel of record.** Default: counsel-addressed; debtor copy only with counsel's consent. Alternative: debtor plus counsel copy (relying on the informational character of (f) statements).
3. **14.3-Q3 Treat a stay-relief order as an exemption ground?** Default: no — keep sending modified statements unless the order says otherwise.
4. **14.3-Q4 Post-discharge late charges.** Default: not assessed on discharged loans (informational statements never show new late charges); alternative: memo accrual for in rem reinstatement figures — requires counsel review per state.
5. **14.3-Q5 Counselor block on Chapter 12/13 statements.** Default: retain (permitted omission not exercised).
