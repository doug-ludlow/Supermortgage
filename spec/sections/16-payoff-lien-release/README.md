# Section 16 — Payoff & Lien Release

## Overview

Section 16 closes the loan. It owns the **Payoff Engine** — request intake from any channel (portal, AI voice, chat, email, fax, mail, title/closing-agent APIs), requester authorization, the deterministic quote-computation service, statement generation and delivery with state variants, and the fraud controls around wire instructions (16.1); the receipt and application of payoff funds to a zero ledger, short/over-payoff handling, the Fannie Mae remittance and action-code-60 removal reporting with its period-close finality rule, and the post-payoff housekeeping tasks (escrow refund and short-year statement, MI cancellation, insurance/tax-service termination, credit reporting, Form 1098, records) (16.2); the lien-release pipeline — instrument selection by state and mortgagee-of-record status, document generation, `signing_officer` execution (MERS Signing Officer, Fannie Mae LPOA attorney-in-fact, or Fannie Mae's own execution), notarization including RON where permitted, eRecording through PRIA-standard vendors, recording confirmation, custody-document retrieval, and borrower notification under a state-by-state deadline/penalty matrix (16.3); and the MERS "Paid in Full" deactivation and eNote eRegistry status update (16.4).

Architecture fit (architecture baseline §2–§9): the `payoff-release` agent owns the section end-to-end and calls the shared `ledger`, `escrow`, `timers`, `notices` and `integrations/*` packages. It hands off to `cashiering` (posting, 2.x), `investor-reporting` (LAR 60 and CRS, 5.2/5.3), `escrow` (refund and short-year statement, 3.3/3.5), `pmi` (10.5), `insurance-property` (9.1), `borrower-comms` (oral quotes, delay explanations, human handoff), `case` (NoE §1024.35(b)(6), 4.1), `security-records` (wire-fraud events) and `qc-audit`. Section 7.6 already defined the payoff statement as a compliance-notice layer (timer `REGZ_1026_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT_7BD`, templates `NTC_REGZ_36C3_PAYOFF_STMT*`, requester-verification rules); 16.1 builds the engine those notices consume and does not restate the 7.6 rules except where the engine changes them. Section 5.3 defined the removal event `removal.payoff` and its clock (`FNMA_IRM_PAYOFF_AC60_NEXTBD_2000`); 16.2 supplies the payoff facts, the cash, and the housekeeping and refers to 5.3 for the LAR mechanics.

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: Supermortgage performs every step under its own state servicer licenses and MERS membership (named Subservicer on MINs). The partner is the Servicer on the MIN and the Fannie Mae servicer of record: releases are executed in MERS' name by Supermortgage employees certified as MERS Signing Officers under the partner's MERS Corporate Resolution (Rule 3 §3), or under a recorded Fannie Mae Limited Power of Attorney (A2-1-04/F-1-10) when Fannie Mae is mortgagee of record; documents outside those authorities go to Fannie Mae's SF CPM Documents mailbox for execution. Every LAR and CRS transaction carries the partner's servicer number.

Dependencies: 1.1 (accrual terms, NIB balances, MIN), 1.4/1.5 (custody, MERS state), 2.1–2.4 (allocation and reversal engines), 3.3/3.5/3.7 (escrow statement, refund, disbursement cut-off), 4.1 (NoE), 4.4 (successors), 5.2/5.3 (remittance and removal reporting), 6.4/6.5 (custodial T&I, unapplied funds, escheat), 7.6 (notice layer), 8.1 (tradeline status 13), 9.1 (mortgagee-interest removal), 10.5 (MI refund), 12.x (deferral/forbearance balances), 13.x/14.x (foreclosure/bankruptcy payoff components), 17.x (transfer-out with open payoff cases).

Changes in flight (verified 2026-09-09 unless noted): Servicing Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026 — the payoff topics are **C-1.2-03 Processing Payments in Full, C-1.2-04 Satisfying the Mortgage Loan and Releasing the Lien, C-1.2-05 Charging for a Release of Lien, C-3-02 Remitting Payoff Proceeds**, with procedures in **F-1-09** (interest: full months on a 360-day basis, partial months on 365; conventional interest "up to, but not including, the day the payoff funds were received"), **F-1-10** (LPOA, SF CPM Documents mailbox, Form 2009 custody release) and **F-1-05** (recording-cost reimbursement, no netting from proceeds). **SVC-2026-03** (eff. July 1, 2026): a paid-off loan cannot be reactivated after the reporting period closes. **LL-2026-05**: liquidation/loan-data events move to event-based reporting in Feb–Mar 2027; A/A P&I auto-draft is a later phase — CRS code 001 remains the A/A payoff rail today. **MERS Procedures Manual Release 24.2**: Paid-in-Full deactivation within 60 calendar days after the lien release is recorded **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. State statutes verified today give a release matrix from **7 days (MD delivery) to 90 days (VA, OH, CO)** with statutory damages up to $5,000 (CT) and a payoff-statement matrix with deadlines as short as **5 business days (MA)** — both shorter than the blueprint's "7 business days / 30–90 days" framing.
