### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Recommendations.** B3-2-05, Approve/Eligible Recommendations (01/27/2015): "Satisfies Fannie Mae's credit risk standards/assessment? Yes"; "Satisfies Fannie Mae's mortgage loan eligibility criteria? Yes"; "Eligible for sale to Fannie Mae? Yes, if all approval conditions have been met"; "Eligible for Fannie Mae's limited waiver of certain mortgage loan eligibility and underwriting representations and warranties? Yes, as long as the mortgage loan satisfies the applicable requirements related to limited waivers." B3-2-06, Approve/Ineligible Recommendations (02/01/2023): credit risk "Yes"; eligibility "No, the loan does not meet Fannie Mae's stated eligibility requirements"; limited waiver "No"; eligible for sale "Yes, if the lender has negotiated terms (such as variances, exceptions, or special requirements)"; ineligibility arises when "The loan does not comply with stated product requirements or policies that apply to DU loans" or "has a combination of product features or risk factors that place the loan outside of Fannie Mae's current acquisition preferences"; the lender's response is to "Update the loan application data (if applicable) and resubmit the loan casefile to DU for an updated recommendation" or "manually underwrite the loan." B3-2-07, Refer with Caution Recommendations (02/01/2023): "Does not meet Fannie Mae's credit risk standards for DU loans"; "Does not meet Fannie Mae's stated eligibility requirements for DU loans"; eligible for sale "Not as a DU loan"; "The layering and degree of risk factors that result in a Refer with Caution recommendation represent a greater risk of serious delinquency"; "The lender may manually underwrite the loan in accordance with this Selling Guide if the loan product or transaction otherwise allows for sale of manually underwritten loans." B3-2-08, Out of Scope Recommendations (04/01/2009): "DU is unable to underwrite the particular product, mortgage, or borrower described in the submission"; "Any mortgage that receives an Out of Scope recommendation must be manually underwritten." An "Error" outcome is a processing result (casefile could not be underwritten), not a recommendation topic — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — DU Error Codes document is login-gated]**.

**Findings structure.** B3-2-11 (09/07/2022): the report "summarizes the overall underwriting recommendation and eligibility component of the loan casefile," "lists certain steps necessary for the lender to complete the processing of the loan file," and includes "potential red flag" messages; "Neither the presence nor absence of these messages alters the lender's responsibility to ensure accurate information." *DU Potential Red Flag Messages* matrix (7.22.24): "The appearance of these messages does not affect the underwriting recommendation from DU, but the messages are designed to help lenders detect inconsistencies and potentially fraudulent transactions"; named messages — Frozen Credit Report ("a borrower has frozen their account with one of the credit repositories"), Potential Casefile ID Reuse ("the subject property address, Doc File ID, occupancy, or loan purpose have been modified"), Excessive Resubmissions ("Excessive submissions can indicate improper manipulation"), Occupancy Modified ("The occupancy was modified from investor to primary"). New PDF format (DU release notes June 15, 2026): a Summary section with "the recommendation, key expiration-related closing dates"; "Rep and Warrant Relief Eligibility"; a "Priority Actions Section" that "helps lenders quickly focus on the most important steps needed to resolve outstanding items" — "Not all DU messages will have a condensed version shown in the Priority Actions section. Lenders remain responsible for ensuring all requirements specified on the DU Underwriting Findings report are satisfied"; then "Detailed DU Messages." The Sept 25, 2026 update names the message families this process maps: Potential Red Flag section, "occupancy Verification messages," "Collateral Underwriter Eligibility Messages" (Lava Zone confirmation; Ineligible for appraiser on the No Longer Accepted list; Ineligible for condition rating C6), DU validation-service messages (fixed base income "At least 3 months of current employment, and At least 12 months of total employment history"; close-by date "using business days"), and "Observation" messages (RefiNow loan number; attachment type). JSON v2 carries "Borrower-specific message associations" (May 27, 2026 notes).

**Documentation and waiver.** B3-2-04 (03/31/2015): "DU indicates the minimum verification documentation requirements necessary for the lender to process the loan application"; "a more comprehensive level of documentation is always acceptable and in some instances should be required by lenders when circumstances in the loan file warrant it." A2-2-04 (04/01/2026): limited waiver requires "All Verification Messages/Approval Conditions… must be satisfactorily resolved" and "SFC 127"; DU validation relief per component requires the validated message and closing "by the 'Close by Date'"; undisclosed non-mortgage-debt relief requires the DU message and closing "by the credit report expiration date."

**HomeReady.** B5-6-01 (06/04/2025): "the total annual qualifying income may not exceed 80% of the AMI for the property's location"; "lenders must refer to the AMIs that Fannie Mae uses in Desktop Underwriter or on Fannie Mae's website and may not rely on other published versions"; "The lender must count the income from all borrowers who will sign the note, to the extent that the income is considered in evaluating creditworthiness for the loan"; "A HomeReady mortgage is a first mortgage, purchase money, or limited cash-out refinance transaction for one- to four-unit properties used as the borrower's principal residence"; "Homeownership education is required for HomeReady purchase loans when all occupying borrowers are first-time homebuyers"; borrowers "who complete housing counseling within 12 months prior to loan closing may be eligible for a loan-level price adjustment credit"; high-balance "Not permitted for LTV ratios of 95.01-97%"; "For transactions with LTV, CLTV, or HCLTV ratios of 95.01–97%, DU only." B5-6-02 (11/05/2025): "If the lender does not select HomeReady as the community lending product, DU will provide a message when the total qualifying income entered in DU appears to be within the applicable AMI limits for the property's location"; "For DU loan casefiles, DU will determine the reserve requirement." B2-2-06, Homeownership Education and Housing Counseling (11/05/2025): required "if none of the borrowers on a purchase transaction underwritten in DU has at least one credit account or installment account reported on their credit report," for "HomeReady purchase transactions when all occupying borrowers are first-time homebuyers," and for "purchase transactions with LTV, CLTV, or HCLTV ratios greater than 95%, when all borrowers are first-time homebuyers"; "at least one borrower on the loan must complete homeownership education prior to loan closing"; providers: "Any qualified third-party provider, independent of the lender" whose "content must be aligned with NIS or HUD standards"; counseling "must be provided by a HUD-approved agency"; the lender "must retain a copy of the certificate of course completion in the loan file"; for the counseling credit "the Housing Counseling data must be submitted to DU, and the loan must be delivered with SFC 184." HomeView (00b-orig F11): a certificate after "scoring 80% or higher on the final assessment," "There is no expiration date," and "no lender verification portal or API" — document intake only. AMI Lookup and HomeReady Evaluation API (Developer Portal, 00b-orig F11): "Identify HomeReady-eligible borrowers earlier"; web equivalent `ami-lookup-tool.fanniemae.com`. Pricing (00a-fnma §4.11/§5.1): SFC 900; LLPA waivers for first-time homebuyers ≤ 100% AMI (120% in high-cost areas); the HomeReady very-low-income first-time-homebuyer credit runs for whole loans purchased through Feb 28, 2027.

**MI coverage.** B7-1-02 (08/07/2019) — standard coverage, fixed-rate > 20 years and ARMs: 80.01–85% 12%; 85.01–90% 25%; 90.01–95% 30%; 95.01–97% 35%; fixed ≤ 20 years: 6% / 12% / 25% / 35%; HomeReady, > 20 years and ARMs: 12% / 25% / 25% / 25% (≤ 20 years: 6% / 12% / 25% / 25%); minimum-coverage option 6% / 12% / 16% / 18% "+ MI LLPA." The DU findings state the required coverage for the casefile (MI message) — 24.6 orders the certificate.

**Value acceptance.** B4-1.4-10 (06/03/2026) / B4-1.4-11 (12/10/2025) (00a-fnma §4.12): exercised only when "the final submission of the loan casefile to DU resulted in a value acceptance offer," no appraisal is obtained, and the offer "is not more than four months old on the date of the note and the mortgage"; SFC 801 / SFC 774; VA+PD property data "must be successfully submitted to Fannie Mae's Property Data API" before closing; ineligible lists (2–4 units, manufactured homes, Texas 50(a)(6), ≥ $1,000,000, DU Ineligible, manual underwriting, and others).

**Eligibility inputs.** Eligibility Matrix (DU 12.1, effective Aug 5, 2026; 00a-fnma §4.1, partially extracted): principal residence 1-unit purchase/LCOR FRM 97% / ARM 95%; cash-out 1-unit 80%; second home 90% / 75%; investment purchase 85% / LCOR 75% / cash-out 75%; HomeReady 97/95. DU maximum DTI 50% (B3-6-02, 04/02/2025). 2026 loan limits: 1-unit $832,750 baseline / $1,249,125 ceiling (LL-2025-04). High-balance (B5-1-01, 07/02/2025): "All loans must be underwritten through DU"; limits "established for each county (or equivalent)"; SFC 808. Multiple financed properties (B2-2-03, 11/05/2025): second home/investment "DU - 10"; "If the Number of Financed Properties field is completed, DU will use that as the number of financed properties"; HomeReady maximum 2 financed properties (as extracted); reserve add-ons per B3-4.1-01 (not read — **[UNVERIFIED]**). No minimum credit score for DU casefiles (SEL-2025-09).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) Topic numbers: Approve/Eligible is B3-2-05, Approve/Ineligible B3-2-06, Refer with Caution B3-2-07, Out of Scope B3-2-08. (2) The blueprint lists "conditions issued within internal SLA" as the only deadline; the binding Fannie Mae clocks are the DU close-by date (employment validation; business days for casefiles created on or after Sept 26, 2026, calendar days for older casefiles — 22.3), the credit-report expiration date for undisclosed-debt relief (22.2), the value-acceptance offer's four months at the note date (24.1), and the pre-closing education requirement (this process). (3) Homeownership education is not only a HomeReady rule: it also applies to > 95% LTV purchases with all first-time homebuyers and to DU purchase casefiles with no tradelines (B2-2-06). (4) "Education completion before closing (HomeReady)" is correct but the counseling LLPA credit is a distinct 12-month rule with SFC 184 and DU data entry. (5) HomeView has no verification API; the blueprint's "HomeView" system is a certificate intake, not an integration. (6) HomeReady requires 25% coverage for every band above 85% (not only "> 90%"); the difference from standard coverage is 25% vs 30%/35% at 90.01–97%. (7) The June 27 and Sept 26, 2026 DU policy generations (23.1) change the Approve/Eligible rate; recommendation policy must be evaluated per generation.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `du.findings.received{submission_id}` (23.1) — every submission, including resubmissions and the final closed-loan match.
- `homeready.candidate.flagged` (from the intake or pricing agents when income appears ≤ 80% AMI, or from DU's HomeReady message when HomeReady was not selected).
- `document.classified{kind=education_certificate}` (22.1 intake of a HomeView or provider certificate).
- `valuation.received` / `appraisal.cu_score.received` (24.2) — re-evaluate CU-driven eligibility messages.
- `pricing.repriced` (20.4) and `changed_circumstance.recorded` (21.5) — restructuring loop feedback.
- Schedules: none beyond 23.1's clocks; the education gate is evaluated at `clear_to_close` (23.3) and at `consummate` (§25/§26).

#### State machine

`du_findings_interpretations.policy_outcome` per submission: `approve_eligible` → `proceed` (23.3 conditional approval/CTC path); `approve_ineligible` → `restructure_required` (structural reasons enumerated) — if no restructure is feasible within policy → `decline_candidate` (23.3/21.6 denial with `underwriting_reviewer`); `refer_with_caution` → `restructure_required` when a specific risk driver can lawfully be changed (e.g., liability payoff, larger down payment) else `decline_candidate`; `out_of_scope` → `out_of_policy_manual` → decline/counteroffer path (no manual underwriting in policy); `error` → back to 23.1 retry. `conditions.status`: opened here as `open`; lifecycle owned by 23.3. `homeownership_education_records.status`: `required_open` → `received` (certificate classified) → `verified` (provider/date/borrower checks pass) → gate satisfied; `not_required` when the basis disappears (e.g., a non-first-time co-borrower added).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Recommendation policy (partner credit policy, open decision 23.2-Q1).** Only `approve_eligible` proceeds. `approve_ineligible`: enumerate the eligibility messages (structural reasons) and attempt a restructure; a loan may not be delivered under "negotiated terms (such as variances…)" because the partner has none. `refer_with_caution` and `out_of_scope`: no manual underwriting; the only lawful paths are a borrower-accepted restructure that yields `approve_eligible` on resubmission, or a Reg B decision (counteroffer/denial via 21.6 with `underwriting_reviewer`). Consequence recorded on every decision record: the reasons must be specific (§1002.9(b)(2)); DU findings are a reason *source*, never the reason text ("failed to achieve a qualifying score… insufficient" — 00a-fed §3.1).
2. **Message-to-condition mapping.** For each message in the JSON v2 findings: look up `du_message_rules` by `message_id` and `du_release`; apply `action`. Unknown IDs go to a triage queue (never dropped) and generate a generic `COND_DU_UNMAPPED_MESSAGE` PTD condition so that "all requirements specified on the DU Underwriting Findings report are satisfied" remains provable. 1. **Borrower-facing wording.** Conditions are rendered to the borrower as plain needs-list items (22.1 loop) with the partner named as lender; DU wording, message IDs and the findings are never shown (Fannie Mae-confidential; also avoids implying that DU, not the creditor, made the decision — the creditor is the partner).
2. **HomeReady evaluation.** `qualifying_income_annual_cents = Σ(monthly qualifying income of all note signers) × 12`; `income_limit_annual_cents = floor(ami_annual_cents × 80 / 100)`; `eligible = qualifying_income_annual_cents ≤ income_limit_annual_cents`. AMI comes from DU's message or the AMI Lookup and HomeReady Evaluation API for the property's location (county/tract as the API keys it); a web-tool lookup is the portal fallback. Never use HUD/other published AMIs ("may not rely on other published versions"). Non-borrower household income is no longer a DU income type (removed enumeration `NonBorrowerHouseholdIncome`, Sept 25, 2026) and is never counted. *Worked example (purchase fixture, Columbus, OH):* application Mon Oct 19, 2026; two occupying first-time homebuyers; qualifying income $8,200.00/month → $98,400.00/year. Illustrative AMI returned by the API for the property's county: $124,000.00 (illustrative input — the real value is whatever DU/the API returns) → limit $99,200.00 → eligible; DU (HomeReady selected) returns Approve/Eligible with SFC 900; `FNMA_B2_2_06_HOMEOWNERSHIP_ED_GATE` opens (all occupying borrowers are first-time homebuyers) — one borrower completes HomeView and uploads the certificate Fri Oct 30, 2026 (`completed_on` 2026-10-30, provider HomeView, no expiration) → verified the same day → gate satisfied before the Wed Nov 18 closing. Counseling credit: none (education, not HUD counseling) → no SFC 184. Fail case: income $8,400.00/month → $100,800.00 > $99,200.00 → not HomeReady-eligible; DU (HomeReady selected) returns Approve/Ineligible with an income-limit eligibility message → `restructure_proposals{kind=remove_homeready}` → resubmit as standard conventional 30-year fixed at 89.96% LTV (sales price $458,000.00; loan $412,000.00): MI coverage stays 25% (85.01–90% band is 25% under both tables), LLPAs change (standard grid; first-time-homebuyer ≤ 100% AMI waiver still evaluated by 20.4 — at $100,800 vs illustrative AMI $124,000 the borrower is ≤ 100% AMI, so the waiver applies), P&I unchanged at $2,570.34 (6.375%), DTI (P&I $2,570.34 + MI $137.33 illustrative + taxes $520.00 + insurance $95.00 + debts $410.00 = $3,732.67) / $8,400.00 = 44.44% → Approve/Eligible on resubmission; the product change is a borrower-consented change → 21.5 revised LE (product line, LLPA-driven pricing) within 3 business days.
3. **MI coverage message.** `mi_coverage_pct` = DU's stated coverage for the band (B7-1-02); the pricing engine may select the minimum-coverage option (6/12/16/18%) with its LLPA if it lowers the borrower's all-in cost; 24.6 orders the certificate to the selected percentage; the final DU submission must carry the coverage actually obtained (B3-2-10 "mortgage insurance coverage" is inside the loan-amount tolerance condition).
4. **Value acceptance.** If the final-match submission (23.1 rule 5) carries a value-acceptance or VA+PD offer and `valuation.value_acceptance_default=true`, 24.1 decides whether to exercise it (LTV/eligibility tables live in DU); an offer on an earlier submission that disappears on the final submission cannot be exercised (B4-1.4-10). Offer age: four months at the note date (24.1 timer). Refinance fixture: initial findings Tue Oct 6, 2026 carry a value-acceptance offer on the $800,000.00 estimate (LTV 70.00%); note date Fri Nov 6, 2026 is 31 days later → exercisable; SFC 801 at delivery; A2-2-06 relief on value.
5. **High-balance.** If `loan_amount_cents > baseline_limit(county, units)` and `≤ ceiling(county, units)` (Loan Limit Look-Up Table 2026, `fnma.limits.2026`): flag high-balance, SFC 808, LLPA attribute (20.4), HomeReady not permitted at 95.01–97%; DU is mandatory (already policy). Above the ceiling → structural ineligible (jumbo is out of scope).
6. **Multiple financed properties.** Copy the borrower's count (22.4/22.5 REO reconciliation) into the DU "Number of Financed Properties" field; DU applies the limit (10 for second home/investment) and the reserve add-ons; a count correction is a resubmission trigger (23.1). HomeReady: maximum two financed properties (as extracted — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**).
7. **Restructuring loop.** For `approve_ineligible`/`refer_with_caution`, the agent enumerates lawful levers: loan amount (larger down payment / smaller cash-out), term, product (remove HomeReady, ARM→fixed), liability payoff at or before closing (22.5), additional verified assets/reserves (22.4), MI option, occupancy correction with evidence, co-borrower change at the borrowers' request. Each lever produces a `restructure_proposals` row with the expected outcome computed from the current data (DTI/LTV arithmetic) before any resubmission. If SM *offers* different terms because the requested terms cannot be approved, it is a Reg B counteroffer: `regb_treatment = counteroffer` → 21.6 (`NTC_REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER`, `REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER_90`) with `underwriting_reviewer` approval before the offer is communicated; if the borrower initiates the change, it is `borrower_initiated` and only 21.5's changed-circumstance rules apply. Every accepted restructure flows to 21.5 (revised LE when a fee/APR/product changes; loan amount, term and product are always changed-circumstance events) and 20.4 (repricing/LLPAs) before 23.1 resubmits. *Worked example (purchase fixture, DTI over 50%):* income $7,000.00/month; at $412,000.00 the obligations are $3,732.67 → DTI 53.32% → DU 50% cap exceeded (`ineligible_change`, 23.1). Levers: (a) reduce the loan to $380,000.00 (LTV 82.97%): P&I $2,370.71 + MI $126.67 + $615.00 + $410.00 = $3,522.38 → 50.32% — still over; (b) reduce to $360,000.00 (LTV 78.60%, no MI): $2,245.93 + $615.00 + $410.00 = $3,270.93 → 46.73% → expected Approve/Eligible; the proposal is a counteroffer (SM proposes a lower amount than applied for) → `underwriting_reviewer` approves the counteroffer notice; borrower accepts Thu Oct 29, 2026 → 21.5 records `borrower_request` (loan amount and the disappearance of MI change the LE) → revised LE by Tue Nov 3; 23.1 resubmits Oct 29 → Approve/Eligible.
8. **Recommendation drift on resubmission.** If a resubmission returns a worse recommendation with no data change other than a DU policy generation (June 27 / Sept 26, 2026), record `du.recommendation.changed{cause=du_policy}` and treat it as `restructure_required`; the decision record must cite the DU release.
9. **SFC assembly.** From the interpretation: always 127; 067 if `applications.score_model = vantagescore_4_0`; 900 for HomeReady; 184 counseling credit; 801/774 if value acceptance/VA+PD exercised; 808 high-balance; 118 Community Seconds; 009/014 temporary buydown; 168 inter vivos trust; 304 Texas 50(a)(6); 007 limited cash-out. 29.3 owns the ULDD; 23.2 emits the required set.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`underwriter` agent (tools: `parseFindings`, `mapMessages`, `openCondition`, `openInvestigation`, `evaluateHomeReady` (AMI API), `verifyEducationCertificate`, `computeRestructure`, `quote` (20.4), `proposeRestructure`, `writeDecision`, `openEscalation`). End-to-end: within the SLA it interprets every submission, opens/supersedes conditions (a resubmission supersedes conditions whose message disappeared, unless evidence already cleared them), runs the HomeReady evaluation whenever DU or the intake flags it, verifies education certificates (provider on list, borrower name match, completion date before closing, counseling ≤ 12 months for SFC 184), computes restructures with full arithmetic, and hands counteroffers/denials to `underwriting_reviewer`. Decision record: `{submission_id, recommendation, policy_generation, messages[{id, section, action, condition_id|investigation_id}], structural_reasons[], homeready{ami, limit, income, eligible, source}, education{basis, status}, mi{required_pct, selected_pct}, value_acceptance{offer, exercisable_until}, sfc[], restructure_proposals[], regb_treatment, rationale, model_version, prompt_version, rule_set_versions}`. Guardrails: never drop an unmapped message; never re-word a DU requirement into something weaker; never present DU output to the borrower; never propose a restructure that changes the borrower's stated occupancy or income; never send a counteroffer or denial without `underwriting_reviewer`; never rely on non-Fannie Mae AMI data; never mark education verified without the certificate document. Escalations: `underwriting_reviewer` (counteroffer/denial; recommendation downgrade within 3 BD of closing; unmapped critical message older than 1 BD), `qc_officer` (red-flag investigations sampled by 28.1), `human_agent` on borrower request. Borrower-facing disclosure: needs-list messages state that the lender (partner) is reviewing the application; Colorado SB 26-189/California ADMT notices are 21.1/21.6's.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Message present on submission n, absent on n+1** (e.g., liability paid): supersede the condition with a link to the new submission; evidence already attached is retained for QC.
- **DU HomeReady message but borrower declines HomeReady** (e.g., prefers a different product): record the offer and the borrower's choice (fair-lending evidence that the lower-cost option was offered — A3-2-02 "lowest-cost product").
- **Education certificate from a provider not on the lists**: status stays `received`; `underwriting_reviewer` decides provider acceptability (NIS/HUD alignment); default reject if the provider is the lender or an affiliate.
- **All-first-time-homebuyer status changes** (a co-borrower with prior ownership is added): the requirement basis flips to `none`; the gate closes as not required; record why.
- **Value-acceptance offer only on an interim submission**: not exercisable; 24.1 orders an appraisal or the final submission is re-run after data correction to see whether the offer returns.
- **CU Ineligible (C6/No Longer Accepted appraiser) after CTC**: reopen the decision (23.3), block closing docs, 24.2 handles a new appraisal/appraiser.
- **AMI API outage**: DU's HomeReady message is authoritative when present; otherwise the web tool via `fnma_portal_operator`; never proceed on cached data older than the current dataset version.
- **Approve/Ineligible caused solely by data entry error** (e.g., wrong occupancy code): correct and resubmit (23.1) — not a restructure and not a Reg B event.
- **Counteroffer not accepted within 90 days**: 21.6's `REGB_1002_9_COUNTEROFFER_90` governs; 23.2 marks the proposal expired.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **23.2-Q1 — DU Approve/Eligible only, no manual underwriting, no variances.** Default: adopt. Consequences: Refer with Caution/Out of Scope loans are restructured or declined; Approve/Ineligible loans are restructured or declined; the partner cannot use A2-2 "negotiated terms." Revisit only if the partner obtains variances and a manual-underwriting QC capability.
2. **Minimum-coverage MI option.** Default: allowed when the all-in borrower cost (LLPA vs premium) is lower and 24.6 can place it; requires the final DU submission to reflect the option.
3. **Counteroffer trigger.** Default: any SM-initiated proposal of terms different from those applied for (amount, term, product, rate) is a counteroffer under §1002.9; borrower-initiated changes are not. `underwriting_reviewer` approves all counteroffers.
4. **Education provider acceptance.** Default: HomeView, HUD-approved agencies, and providers on a maintained NIS-aligned list; anything else needs `underwriting_reviewer`.
5. **AMI figures in worked examples.** Illustrative only; the platform must never hard-code AMI values — the AMI API/DU dataset version is the source of truth.
