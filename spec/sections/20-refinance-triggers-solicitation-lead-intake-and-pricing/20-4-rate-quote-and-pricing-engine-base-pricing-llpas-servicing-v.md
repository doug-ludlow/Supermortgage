### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `priceCandidate` (20.1), `quoteForLead` (20.3), `quoteForLock`/`repriceForRelock` (21.4), `repriceForChangedCircumstance` (21.5), `finalPricingForCommitment` (29.1).
- Rate feed: Loan Pricing API responses (per product/note-rate/lock period; quote IDs and expiry) at 06:35 ET and on demand; Browse Prices export; partner rate sheet upload (`rate_sheets.source=partner_rate_sheet`).
- Reference: `llpa_tables`, SFC list, loan limits, AMI API, `sm_cost_schedules`, `fee_schedules`, APOR (23.4), holiday calendars (PE–WL committing days).
- Events consumed: `credit.report.received` (representative score/model), `valuation.received` (LTV), `du.findings.received` (HomeReady/MI/value acceptance affect costs), `lock.executed`, `rate_sheet.published`.

#### Data model
- `rate_sheets` (baseline; columns): `rate_sheet_id`, `partner_id`, `source` ∈ {pe_whole_loan_api, browse_prices_export, partner_rate_sheet, manual_ui_read}, `published_at`, `effective_from`, `expires_at` (next publish or committing close), `execution` ∈ {best_efforts, mandatory_indicative}, `servicing_fee_bps` (25), `prices jsonb` [{product_code, term_months, amortization ∈ {fixed, arm_5_6, arm_7_6, arm_10_6}, note_rate (5 dp), pass_through_rate, lock_period_days, price (3 dp, % of par), pe_wl_quote_id, pe_wl_quote_expires_at}], `status` ∈ {active, superseded, withdrawn}, `published_by` (agent run / operator), `raw_response_document_id`.
- `llpa_tables` (baseline; columns): `llpa_table_id`, `matrix_version` ('09.09.2026'), `effective_from`, `effective_to null`, `grid` ∈ {purchase_fico, purchase_vs4, lcor_fico, lcor_vs4, cashout_fico, cashout_vs4, attr_purchase, attr_lcor, attr_cashout, min_mi_fico, min_mi_vs4, waivers, credits}, `rows jsonb` (score band → LTV band → pct, 3 dp), `source_document_id`, `verified_by text[]`, `notes` (flags for partially verified cells).

#### State machine

Rate sheet: `active` → `superseded` (next publish) | `withdrawn` (API/UI discrepancy). Quote: `draft` → `presented` (after `mlo.review.completed` where personalized — 20.3) → `locked` (21.4 `lock.executed`) | `superseded` (new sheet or input change) | `expired` (`SM_QUOTE_VALIDITY_GATE`). LLPA table: `staged` → `verified` (two reviewers) → `active` (effective date) → `retired`. Transitions by the `pricing` agent; `officer` for policy/exception approvals; `fnma_portal_operator` for manual UI reads.

#### Business rules and calculations

Money in cents; prices to 3 decimals (% of par); rates 5 dp on the 0.125% grid; LLPA percentages 3 dp; rounding half-up to cents at each dollar conversion.
1. **Base price.** From the active `rate_sheets` for (product, term, lock period): `price(r)` per note rate r; pass-through = r − 0.00250 (servicing fee 25 bps retained by the partner). For lock periods not on the sheet, use the next longer period. Mandatory-execution indicatives are display-only (`execution.mandatory_enabled=false`).
2. **LLPA computation.** Select the purpose grid by `transaction_type` and `score_model`; row by representative score (lowest across borrowers; "no score" → lowest row); column by gross LTV (2 dp, `ltv = loan_amount / min(value, price)` for purchase; `/ value` for refinance); add attribute rows for occupancy (investment/second home), property type (condo, MH), units (2–4), high-balance (loan amount > baseline limit for the county/units — high-cost counties per table), ARM, subordinate financing (CLTV > LTV), cash-out attribute table; minimum-MI LLPA by base LTV if the minimum-coverage option is elected; then apply waivers: HomeReady (SFC 900) or FTHB ≤ 100% AMI (120% high-cost) or Duty to Serve → `llpa_total_pct = min_mi_pct` only; credits: HomeReady very-low-income FTHB −$2,500 if `expected_purchase_ready_date ≤ 2027-02-28`. `llpa_cents = round_half_up(llpa_total_pct × loan_amount_cents)` — the forecast; Fannie Mae's actual calculation on the Purchase Ready date on UPB is reconciled by 27.2.
3. **Third-party cost schedule.** `third_party_costs_cents = Σ sm_cost_schedules.items` for the state/transaction/valuation method (value acceptance → appraisal item $0; VA+PD → PDC fee; traditional → appraisal fee); MI premiums are borrower costs (not SM-borne) unless the program says otherwise **[UNVERIFIED term]**.
4. **Pass-through solve (price-to-rate conversion).** For each candidate r on the sheet ascending: `net_premium(r) = round_half_up((price(r) − 100)/100 × loan_amount_cents) − llpa_cents + credits_cents`; choose `r* = min{r : net_premium(r) ≥ third_party_costs_cents}`; `residual = net_premium(r*) − third_party_costs_cents`; `lender_credit = min(residual, residual_credit_cap_bps × loan_amount / 10,000)` (default cap 12.5 bps); `sm_retained = residual − lender_credit`. Points charged to the borrower = 0 (program); a borrower may *elect* to pay third-party costs in cash to obtain a lower rate ("buy the rate down by paying costs") — then the solve uses `third_party_costs = 0` and the fees are borrower-paid on the LE (21.2). If no r on the sheet satisfies the inequality (deep discount market), the quote returns `not_priceable` and the program offers the lowest rate with borrower-paid costs.
5. **Payment, MI, escrow, prepaid interest, APR estimate.** `pi = round_half_up(L × r/12 ÷ (1 − (1 + r/12)^−n))`; BPMI monthly from the MI rate-quote API (MISMO; N9) at the coverage from B7-1-02 for the LTV band (HomeReady 25% at 95.01–97%); escrow estimate from 30.3's aggregate-analysis routine with the tax/insurance figures on file or vendor estimates; prepaid interest = `L × r / 365 × days` from the assumed disbursement date to month-end (Fannie Mae uniform note accrues monthly; the per-diem convention is the partner's closing policy — **[UNVERIFIED which of 360/365 the partner uses; default 365]**); `apr_estimate` by the 25.1 Appendix J engine with the quote's finance charges (prepaid interest, MI, any borrower-paid finance charges; SM-paid third-party fees are not finance charges to the consumer under the `sm_paid_by_others` treatment — open question 2).
6. **Quote validity.** `valid_until = min(rate_sheet.expires_at, quoted_at + 24 h)`; presentation text states the expiry; a lock (21.4) freezes the price and starts the revised-LE clock; after expiry the borrower is re-quoted (up or down) — never silently held.
7. **Written quote.** `NTC_SM_RATE_QUOTE`: disclaimer at the top (gate); "estimate" language (§1026.17(c)(2)(i)); rate, estimated APR, term, P&I, estimated MI/escrow separately, "payments do not include taxes and insurance" if a total is shown, lock period and expiry, third-party cost treatment sentence, no points, lender credit if any, MLO name and NMLSR ID, partner name/NMLSR ID, "not a commitment." Layout must not resemble H-24 (no "Loan Estimate" title, no LE section structure).
8. **Fair-lending pricing controls.** The note rate is a pure function of the inputs in `pricing_quotes.inputs`; no user may adjust price except through `pricing_exceptions` with a reason code; `relationship` exceptions are disabled; every exception is reviewed by 31.2 quarterly for disparities; MLO compensation is not a function of any term (§1026.36(d)); the engine's inputs exclude any prohibited basis and proxies (no ZIP-level cost tables below county except recording/transfer-tax tables, which are jurisdictional facts).
9. **Version control.** Every quote records `rate_sheet_id`, `llpa_table_id`, `cost_schedule_id`, `fee_schedule_id`, rule-set version and engine version; re-running the quote with the same ids reproduces the same numbers (property-based test).
10. **VantageScore 4.0 loans.** `score_model=vantagescore_4` selects the VS grids and adds SFC 067 to the delivery data (29.3); mixed models across borrowers are rejected (LL-2026-06).
11. **Hand-off to 21.2.** The quote's fee items (from `fee_schedules` and `sm_cost_schedules`) are emitted as `fee_items` with `tolerance_class` and `paid_by`; 21.2 owns the LE presentation and the tolerance baseline.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`pricing` agent (tools: `fetchPeWlPrices`, `publishRateSheet`, `loadLlpaTable` (staged, requires two human verifications), `computeLlpa`, `solvePassThrough`, `computePayment`, `estimateApr` (25.1), `buildFeeItems`, `renderQuote` (disclaimer gate), `explainQuote`, `requestException`, `writeDecision`). The engine is deterministic code; the LLM (i) drafts the plain-language explanation of the quote (why the rate is 6.125%, how costs are recovered, the same-term alternative) from computed fields and (ii) classifies vendor fee documents when a fee source is unstructured. Decision record: `{quote_id, inputs, rate_sheet_id, llpa_table_id, llpa_items, solve_trace:[{rate, price, premium, net}], r_star, residual, credit, fees, apr_estimate, validity, explanation, versions}`. Guardrails: never invent a price not on the sheet; never apply a waiver without an AMI/HomeReady evaluation or DU HomeReady message; never present a personalized quote before MLO review (20.3); never render a written quote without the disclaimer; never alter price outside `pricing_exceptions`; never read prohibited-basis fields. Escalations: `officer` (policy, exceptions, cost schedules), `fnma_portal_operator` (UI reads), `mlo_of_record` (presentation), `compliance-sentinel` (disparity alerts). Human path: pricing console with the same solve trace; manual sheet upload with dual entry.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **No sheet rate covers costs** (discount market): `not_priceable` for the no-cost program; offer the lowest rate with borrower-paid costs (LE shows the fees borrower-paid).
- **Score band change between quote and lock** (soft 768 → tri-merge 758): re-quote; if the borrower already locked, 21.4 handles as a changed circumstance (LLPA change is a valid reason under §1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(A) — 21.5).
- **Value comes in lower** (LTV band moves): re-price; disclose.
- **High-balance by county** (e.g., a $900,000 loan in a high-cost county under the ceiling): high-balance LLPA applies although conforming; the engine keys off the county table.
- **HomeReady lost at DU** (income above AMI after verification): waiver removed; re-quote; 21.5 changed circumstance.
- **VantageScore for one borrower, FICO for another**: rejected; 22.2 re-orders under one model.
- **Matrix version unread cells** (second home; VS bands): quotes in those cells are blocked until the table's `notes` flag is cleared by the verification log.
- **PE–WL maintenance windows / weekends**: Friday's last sheet carries through the weekend with `stale=true` and a shortened validity; locks on weekends are held for Monday's commit (21.4).
- **API vs UI discrepancy**: sheet `withdrawn`; sev 2; operator reconciliation.
- **Premium recapture** (loan paid off within 120 days of purchase): pricing reserve line in 27.2; not a borrower cost.
- **Rate-sheet republish after presentation**: quotes `superseded`; the 20.3 conversation re-quotes; no adverse bait-and-switch (UDAAP) — the prior presentation is shown with the change explained.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Residual premium above the 12.5 bps credit cap.** Default: retained by SM (rounding); alternative: uncapped lender credit to the borrower (term sheet **[UNVERIFIED]**).
2. **LE/CD treatment of SM-paid third-party costs.** Default: `sm_paid_by_others` (disclosed on the CD in "Paid by Others"; not imposed on the consumer on the LE) — 21.2/25.2 must confirm against §1026.37(f)/(g) and §1026.38(f)/(g) commentary; alternative: disclose fees with an offsetting general lender credit (zero-tolerance protection of the credit).
3. **Servicing fee and buy-up/buy-down.** Default: 25 bps retained, no buy-up/buy-down until the whole-loan mechanics are verified with Fannie Mae's Capital Markets desk.
4. **Prepaid-interest day count.** Default: 365-day per diem; confirm the partner's closing policy and state rules.
5. **Matrix-change exposure on locked loans.** Default: borrower's locked price honored; LLPA delta absorbed per the term sheet.
6. **Escrow-waiver pricing.** Default: none (no LLPA exists); the partner may add a rate-sheet adjustment — requires fair-lending review.
