### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Timing and receipt — §1026.19(f)(1) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026).** (f)(1)(ii)(A): "the creditor shall ensure that the consumer receives the disclosures required under [paragraph (f)(1)(i)] of this section no later than three business days before consummation." (f)(1)(iii): "If any disclosures required under paragraph (f)(1)(i) of this section are not provided to the consumer in person, the consumer is considered to have received the disclosures three business days after they are delivered or placed in the mail" (00a-fed §1.7). Business day for both (f)(1)(ii) and (f)(1)(iii) is the **specific** definition — §1026.2(a)(6) second sentence (eCFR 9/04/2026): "for purposes of rescission under §§1026.15 and 1026.23, and for purposes of §§ 1026.19(a)(1)(ii), 1026.19(a)(2), 1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B), 1026.19(e)(1)(iv), 1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A), 1026.19(e)(4)(ii), 1026.19(f)(1)(ii), 1026.19(f)(1)(iii), 1026.20(e)(5), 1026.31, and 1026.46(d)(4)" the term means "all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays" (the eleven federal legal public holidays in 5 U.S.C. 6103(a), including Juneteenth; per comment 2(a)(6)-2 the four date-specific holidays are counted on their statutory dates and an observed substitute date "is a business day" — see decision 25.2-Q4). Unit: `business_days_regz_specific`. Electronic delivery: §1026.17(a)(1) (eCFR 9/04/2026): disclosures "may be provided to the consumer in electronic form, subject to compliance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-Sign Act)"; §1026.17(a)(1) also requires the disclosures "clearly and conspicuously in writing, in a form that the consumer may keep". Multiple consumers: §1026.17(d): "If there is more than one consumer, the disclosures may be made to any consumer who is primarily liable on the obligation. If the transaction is rescindable under § 1026.23, however, the disclosures shall be made to each consumer who has the right to rescind." Actual receipt earlier than the presumption: the Official Interpretation permits the creditor to rely on evidence of actual receipt (e.g., an e-mail acknowledgement) to establish an earlier receipt date **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — comments 19(f)(1)(iii)-1/-2 could not be fetched today; the rule text above and the CFPB Small Entity Compliance Guide v5.1 (Oct 2017) headings were read; 21.2's treatment of comment 19(e)(1)(iv) is the analogue]**.

**Waiver — §1026.19(f)(1)(iv).** "the consumer may modify or waive the three-business-day waiting period … after receiving the disclosures required under [paragraph (f)(1)(i)]" if "the extension of credit is needed to meet a bona fide personal financial emergency"; "the consumer shall give the creditor a dated written statement that describes the emergency … Printed forms for this purpose are prohibited."

**Settlement agent — §1026.19(f)(1)(v).** "A settlement agent may provide a consumer with the disclosures required under [paragraph (f)(1)(i)] of this section, provided the settlement agent complies with all relevant requirements"; "The creditor shall ensure that such disclosures are provided in accordance with all requirements of this [paragraph (f)]".

**Changes before consummation — §1026.19(f)(2)(i)–(ii).** (i): when no new waiting period is required "the creditor shall provide corrected disclosures reflecting any changed terms to the consumer so that the consumer receives the corrected disclosures at or before consummation." (ii): a new waiting period under (f)(1)(ii)(A) when "(A) The annual percentage rate disclosed under [§ 1026.38(o)(4)] becomes inaccurate, as defined in [§ 1026.22]. (B) The loan product is changed, causing the information disclosed under [§ 1026.38(a)(5)(iii)] to become inaccurate. (C) A prepayment penalty is added, causing the statement regarding a prepayment penalty required under [§ 1026.38(b)] to become inaccurate." TRID FAQ (content May 14, 2021; corrected-CD section Jan. 25, 2019): "If overstated APR is accurate under Regulation Z, creditor may provide corrected CD at consummation without new waiting period" (citing (f)(2)(i) and §1026.22(a)(4)). The accuracy verdict itself comes from 25.1 (`APR_1026_22_ACCURACY`).

**After consummation — §1026.19(f)(2)(iii)–(v).** (iii): for an event "in connection with the settlement" occurring during the 30-day period after consummation that causes the disclosures to become inaccurate and results in a change to an amount actually paid, "the creditor shall deliver or place in the mail corrected disclosures not later than 30 days after receiving information sufficient to establish that such event has occurred." (iv): non-numeric clerical errors — "the creditor delivers or places in the mail corrected disclosures no later than 60 days after consummation." (v): "the creditor refunds the excess to the consumer no later than 60 days after consummation" and delivers corrected disclosures reflecting the refund within the same 60 days (21.5 owns `REGZ_1026_19F2V_TOLERANCE_REFUND_60` and `tolerance_cures`).

**Charges — §1026.19(f)(3).** (i): "The amount imposed upon the consumer for any settlement service shall not exceed the amount actually received by the settlement service provider for that service"; (ii) average-charge exception on four conditions — "(A) The average charge is no more than the average amount paid for that service … for a class of transactions; (B) The creditor or settlement service provider defines the class … based on an appropriate period of time, geographic area, and type of loan; (C) … uses the same average charge for every transaction within the defined class; (D) … does not use an average charge for any type of insurance, for any charge based on the loan amount or property value, or if doing so is otherwise prohibited by law."

**Seller — §1026.19(f)(4).** (i): "the settlement agent shall provide the seller with the disclosures in [§ 1026.38] that relate to the seller's transaction reflecting the actual terms"; (ii): "no later than the day of consummation"; (iv): "the settlement agent shall provide to the creditor (if the creditor is not the settlement agent) a copy of the disclosures provided to the seller". **No fee — (f)(5):** "No fee may be imposed on any person, as a part of settlement costs or otherwise, by a creditor or by a servicer for the preparation or delivery of the disclosures required under [paragraph (f)(1)(i)]".

**Content — §1026.38 (eCFR 9/08/2026).** Form H-25 layout (pages 1–5) with, among the items the assignment singles out: (a)(5)(v) "Loan ID #", (a)(5)(vi) "MIC #"; (l)(7) escrow: "a statement of whether the creditor has established or will establish (at or before consummation) an escrow account", and for the first year "Escrowed Property Costs over Year 1", "Non-Escrowed Property Costs over Year 1", "Initial Escrow Payment", "Monthly Escrow Payment", or for no escrow "Property Costs over Year 1" and "Escrow Waiver Fee"; (m) the AP table — "the table required to be disclosed by § 1026.37(i)" (when payments can change); (n) the AIR table — "the table required to be disclosed by § 1026.37(j)" (when the rate can change); (o)(2) finance charge with the $100/overstatement accuracy standard; (o)(4) APR "expressed as a percentage" with "Your costs over the loan term expressed as a rate. This is not your interest rate."; (o)(5) TIP "expressed as a percentage" with "The total amount of interest that you will pay over the loan term as a percentage of your loan amount."; (r) contact information for the creditor, mortgage broker, consumer's and seller's real estate brokers and settlement agent — "name, address, NMLS ID or state license ID, contact name, contact NMLS ID, email, phone" [as summarized from the eRegs page]; (s) signature statement ("By signing, you are only confirming that you have received this form…" per the H-25 form; text not rendered by the fetch [PARTIALLY VERIFIED]); (t)(4) rounding — percentages to "three decimal places" ((t)(4)(ii); dollar rounding sentence not rendered [PARTIALLY VERIFIED]). §1026.36(g)(2)(ii): the partner's name/NMLSR ID and the `mlo_of_record` name/NMLSR ID on the CD (25.1 tests it). Retention: §1026.25(c)(1)(ii) — CD and related records "for five years after consummation" (00a-fed §15).

**Consummation — §1026.2(a)(13) (eCFR 9/04/2026).** "Consummation means the time that a consumer becomes contractually obligated on a credit transaction." Comment 2(a)(13)-1 (CFPB Official Interpretations, read 2026-09-10): "When a contractual obligation on the consumer's part is created is a matter to be determined under applicable law; Regulation Z does not make this determination." and "A contractual commitment agreement, for example, that under applicable law binds the consumer to the credit terms would be consummation."; the platform records `closings.consummation_at` as the time the note is signed (eNote tamper-sealed or wet signature witnessed), not the disbursement or recording date (decision 25.2-Q1).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "(1/4 irregular)": the 1/4 tolerance never applies merely because of an odd-days first period (§1026.22(a)(3)); the fixture uses 1/8 (25.1).
2. "Corrected CD ≤ 30 days post-consummation" is imprecise: (f)(2)(iii) covers events occurring within 30 days *after consummation* and requires the corrected CD within 30 days *after the creditor receives information sufficient to establish the event* — two different 30-day windows.
3. "Fannie Mae UCD requirements (MISMO 3.3)" — the schema is MISMO v3.3.0299; the UCD must embed the borrower CD PDF; the seller CD is **not** required in the XML when separate CDs are issued; a revised CD before delivery requires a re-submission; the v2.0 mandate date is unknown (Q4 2026 announcement) so v1.5 and v2.0 must both be producible.
4. The blueprint omits (f)(1)(iv) (waiver; no printed forms), (f)(1)(v) (settlement-agent delivery; creditor still responsible), (f)(3) (actual charges/average charge), (f)(4) (seller CD by the settlement agent no later than the day of consummation; copy to creditor), (f)(5) (no fee for the CD), and §1026.17(d) (each rescinding consumer must receive the CD).
5. The fixture: CD e-sign-confirmed Mon Nov 2 permits consummation on Thu Nov 5; the fixture closes Fri Nov 6 — one day of slack, which the platform's policy target (`SM_O62_CD_TARGET_4SBD`) makes deliberate.
6. Ownership overlap: the brief assigns "UCD gates" to 25.2 and "EarlyCheck/UCD/UCDP pre-delivery gates" to 29.3; resolved here as 25.2 defining `FNMA_UCD_ACCEPTED_GATE` (UCD accepted with the final CD), which 29.3 composes into its pre-delivery gate set and does not redefine.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `decision.issued{kind=approval}` with all PTD conditions cleared (23.3) and `closing.scheduled` (26.2) → prepare the initial CD.
- Settlement-agent figures (title/settlement fees, recording, payoffs, prorations, seller credits, seller CD data) via the `title` adapter, versioned in `cd_figure_sources`.
- Locked terms (`locks`), fee items (`fee_items` with 21.5 tolerance classes), escrow analysis inputs (30.3 initial escrow computation for §1026.38(l)(7)), MI (24.6), flood/insurance (24.5), payoffs (24.4), APR/finance charge/TIP (25.1 `apr_calculations`), HPML/QM (23.4), rescission applicability (25.3).
- Change events after the initial CD: any of the above changes; `closings.scheduled_at` change (odd days); borrower-requested changes; `tolerance_tests` results (21.5).
- Post-consummation: `fundings.disbursement_date` set; settlement-agent final disbursement ledger; recording confirmation with actual recording fees; borrower/agent reports of clerical errors; `tolerance_cures` (21.5).
- E-SIGN consent state (`consents`) per consumer; consumer channel preferences.

#### State machine

CD version: `drafting` → `gated` (25.1 CD gate open, figures reconciled, consistency checks pass) → `delivered` → `received` (every required consumer has an `effective_receipt_date`) → `waiting` (until `earliest_consummation_date`) → `consummation_ready` → `consummated` (on `closing.consummated`) → `final` (no further pre-consummation change) / `superseded` (a newer version delivered). Post-consummation versions: `corrected_post_consummation` → `delivered` → `final`. UCD: `generated` → `submitted` → {`accepted`, `accepted_with_warnings`} → `final` | `rejected` → `regenerated`. Transitions are performed by the `disclosure` agent; `received` for in-person delivery is recorded from the settlement agent's signed receipt; `waiver` moves `waiting` → `consummation_ready` only after `officer` acceptance.

#### Business rules and calculations

**Business-day arithmetic (`regz_specific` calendar).** `addSpecificBusinessDays(d, n)`: step forward one calendar day at a time, counting a day iff it is not a Sunday and not a federal legal public holiday (list from `holiday_calendars{code=regz_specific}`: New Year's Day, Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr., Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas). Saturdays count. `earliest_consummation_date = addSpecificBusinessDays(effective_receipt_date, 3)`. For mail/e-mail without confirmation: `presumed_receipt_date = addSpecificBusinessDays(mailed_at.date, 3)`. `effective_receipt_date = min(actual_receipt_at.date, presumed_receipt_date)` when actual evidence exists, else `presumed_receipt_date`; in-person delivery: the delivery date. With several required consumers (all rescinding consumers in a refinance; the primarily liable consumer otherwise, §1026.17(d) — platform policy: every borrower), the gate uses the **latest** `effective_receipt_date`.

**Worked example 1 — refinance fixture, e-delivery.** CD version 1 e-delivered Mon Nov 2, 2026 at 09:14 MST to both borrowers; both e-sign the acknowledgement the same day (`receipt_evidence = esign_confirmed`, `actual_receipt_at = Nov 2`). Count: Tue Nov 3 (1), Wed Nov 4 (2), Thu Nov 5 (3) → `earliest_consummation_date = Thu Nov 5, 2026`. Scheduled consummation Fri Nov 6 → gate open with one day of slack. Had the borrowers not acknowledged, the mailbox presumption would apply from the e-mail date: presumed receipt Thu Nov 5 → earliest consummation Mon Nov 9 (Fri 6 (1), Sat 7 (2), Sun 8 excluded, Mon 9 (3)) — the Nov 6 closing would have to move.

**Worked example 2 — same CD mailed.** Placed in the mail Mon Nov 2 → presumed received Thu Nov 5 (Tue 3, Wed 4, Thu 5) → consummation no earlier than Mon Nov 9, 2026. If the borrower calls on Wed Nov 4 to say the package arrived, the call is not "evidence" the platform accepts (decision 25.2-Q2: only a signed/e-signed acknowledgement, courier signature or portal acknowledgement rebuts the presumption); the Nov 9 date stands.

**Worked example 3 — Veterans Day.** CD received Mon Nov 9, 2026: Tue Nov 10 (1), Wed Nov 11 Veterans Day (excluded), Thu Nov 12 (2), Fri Nov 13 (3) → earliest consummation Fri Nov 13. CD received Tue Nov 10: Wed 11 excluded, Thu 12 (1), Fri 13 (2), Sat 14 (3) → earliest consummation Sat Nov 14 (a Saturday closing is lawful; the settlement agent's calendar decides).

**Worked example 4 — purchase fixture, counting backward.** Closing Wed Nov 18, 2026 (Columbus, OH): the CD must be received no later than Sat Nov 14 (Tue 17 (1), Mon 16 (2), Sun 15 excluded, Sat 14 (3)). By mail that means placed in the mail no later than Tue Nov 10 (Thu 12 (1) — Wed 11 excluded — Fri 13 (2), Sat 14 (3) → presumed receipt Sat Nov 14). The policy target `SM_O62_CD_TARGET_4SBD` sets e-delivery by Fri Nov 13; the settlement-agent figures are due by Thu Nov 12 (`SM_O62_SETTLEMENT_FIGURES_5SBD`: five specific business days before Nov 18 = Tue 17, Mon 16, Sat 14, Fri 13, Thu 12).

**Redisclosure decision (`evaluateRedisclosure(prev, new)`).** `new_wait = APR_inaccurate(prev.apr_disclosed, new.apr_actual) ∨ product_changed(prev, new) ∨ prepayment_penalty_added(prev, new)`, where `APR_inaccurate` is 25.1's `APR_1026_22_ACCURACY` result (1/8 for regular transactions; (a)(4)/(a)(5) finance-charge-derived relief applied), `product_changed` compares the §1026.38(a)(5)(iii) product description (loan type/features, e.g., "Fixed Rate" → "5/6 Adjustable Rate") and `prepayment_penalty_added` compares the (b) statement. If `new_wait`: render `NTC_REGZ_1026_38_CD_CORRECTED` with `cd_reason = pre_consummation_new_wait`, deliver, recompute `earliest_consummation_date` from the new receipt, reschedule closing. Else: `cd_reason = pre_consummation_no_wait`, deliver so that receipt is at or before consummation (in-person at the table is acceptable; the platform prefers e-delivery the day before). Worked example 5: on Wed Nov 4, 2026 the fixture borrower buys down to 5.875% (25.1 worked example 2): actual APR 5.979 vs disclosed 6.159 → inaccurate (Δ 0.180) → corrected CD e-signed Wed Nov 4 → earliest consummation Sat Nov 7 (Thu 5, Fri 6, Sat 7); the borrower chooses Mon Nov 9 → rescission period recomputed by 25.3 (ends midnight Thu Nov 12; disbursement Fri Nov 13). Worked example 6: on Thu Nov 5 the title agent's recording fee estimate rises from $30 to $42 — no APR effect (recording fees are excluded under §1026.4(e)(1)), no product change → corrected CD delivered Thu Nov 5 for receipt before the Fri Nov 6 signing; 21.5 runs `runToleranceTest` (10-percent category).

**Post-consummation regimes.** Fixture consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 → (f)(2)(iii) event window: Sat Nov 7–Sun Dec 6, 2026 (30 calendar days after consummation; day 1 = Nov 7). Worked example 7: on Mon Nov 16 the recorder returns the deed of trust with an actual recording fee $12.00 higher than disclosed (an event in connection with the settlement, within the 30-day window; the amount actually paid by the consumer changed) → `REGZ_1026_19F2_CORRECTED_CD_30` due Wed Dec 16, 2026 (Nov 16 + 30); the platform issues the corrected CD Tue Nov 17 with the UCD resubmitted the same day because Loan Delivery is scheduled Mon Nov 16 → if the correction post-dates delivery, no UCD resubmission is required (FAQ: resubmission applies "if the loan has closed, but has not been delivered"), but the corrected CD is retained in the loan file. Worked example 8: a borrower's middle initial is wrong on page 1 (non-numeric clerical) → `REGZ_1026_19F2_CLERICAL_CD_60` due Tue Jan 5, 2027 (Nov 6 + 60). Tolerance refunds (21.5): also Jan 5, 2027.

**Waiver.** A consumer-authored, dated, signed statement describing the emergency (never a platform-supplied form; the portal may only provide a blank text box with no template text — decision 25.2-Q3); all consumers entitled to the disclosure sign; `officer` accepts; `earliest_consummation_date` is then the date of acceptance but never before receipt of the CD ("after receiving the disclosures").

**Content assembly.** Figures come from versioned sources (`cd_figure_sources`), never typed: loan terms from `locks`/`loan_terms`; projected payments from 25.1's stream (P&I $3,402.62 on the fixture) plus escrow ($ from 30.3's initial analysis); loan costs/other costs from `fee_items` with 21.5's tolerance classes and the "Paid by Others"/"Seller-Paid" columns from the purchase contract and settlement figures; calculating cash to close (h)–(i) reconciled against the LE; summaries of transactions (j)/(k) or the (e)/(k) alternative for transactions without a seller (refinance uses the alternative "Payoffs and Payments" table — §1026.38(t)(5)(vii)(B) [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — text not re-read]); loan disclosures (l) incl. (l)(7) escrow with the 30.3 figures (escrowed property costs over year 1 = 12 × monthly T&I; initial escrow payment = 30.3's aggregate-analysis deposit; monthly escrow payment) — the CD-escrow-section consistency check compares these to 30.3's initial escrow statement inputs (25.4); AP/AIR tables only for ARMs/payment-change loans; loan calculations (o) from `apr_calculations` (total of payments per the TRID FAQ "Total of Payments" method, finance charge $668,793.20, amount financed $556,150.00, APR 6.159%, TIP 119.059%); other disclosures (p) incl. appraisal, contract details, liability after foreclosure (state law), refinance, tax deductions; (q) questions notice; (r) contact information with NMLSR IDs; (s) confirm receipt.

**CD-to-note consistency checks (`cd_consistency_checks`, feeding 26.1).** `CD_NOTE_LOAN_AMOUNT`, `CD_NOTE_RATE`, `CD_NOTE_TERM`, `CD_NOTE_FIRST_PAYMENT_DATE` (Jan 1, 2027 on the fixture), `CD_NOTE_MATURITY_DATE` (Dec 1, 2056), `CD_NOTE_PI` ($3,402.62), `CD_NOTE_LATE_CHARGE` (state cap from `jurisdiction_rules`; Fannie Mae note late charge), `CD_NOTE_PREPAY` (none), `CD_NOTE_ARM_TERMS` (index, margin, caps, first change date — AIR table vs the ARM rider), `CD_BORROWER_NAMES_VESTING` (borrowers and non-borrower title-holders vs the security instrument and 25.3's rescission list), `CD_PROPERTY_ADDRESS`, `CD_NMLSR_IDS`, `CD_ESCROW_VS_O11_3`, `CD_MI_VS_CERT` (Ohio fixture: BPMI monthly premium vs `mi_certificates`), `CD_PAYOFF_VS_DEMAND` (refinance payoff vs `payoff_demands` good-through date), `CD_CASH_TO_CLOSE_VS_SETTLEMENT_LEDGER`. Any `mismatch` blocks 26.1's `generateClosingDocuments`.

**UCD.** After the CD reaches `consummated` (or earlier for a pre-closing dry run in the test environment), `generateUcd` builds the UCD XML (v2.0 by default; v1.5 on flag `fnma.ucd.version=1.5`) from the same figure sources, embeds the final CD PDF (base64), sets the DU Casefile ID linkage, validates against the schema and the Critical Edits Matrix (v2.0, updated Jul. 27, 2026), and submits via `fnma-ucd` DI; the returned UCD Casefile ID is stored on `ucd_submissions.casefile_id_ucd` and copied to `deliveries`. Critical-edit failures are fixed at the source (figures or CD) and the corrected CD is re-delivered if the change affects the consumer's disclosures. Before delivery, the embedded PDF must be the most recent CD version; a post-consummation correction before purchase triggers `SM_O62_UCD_RESUBMIT_ON_CORRECTION`. The seller CD is not embedded when separate; the seller data points required by the spec are populated from the settlement agent's seller CD copy (f)(4)(iv) to the extent the Critical Edits Matrix requires them [PARTIALLY VERIFIED — matrix not read].

#### Integrations
- **E-delivery/e-sign vendor** (`e-delivery`; 00b-svc N9/N15): send CD package; capture `sent_at`, `opened_at`, `acknowledged_at`, signer identity, IP/UA, E-SIGN consent id; idempotent by `disclosure_id + consumer_id`; outage → fall back to print/mail with the mailbox rule and recompute the earliest consummation date.
- **Print/mail** (`print-mail`): mailing manifest with USPS acceptance date as `mailed_at`; retries next collection.
- **Settlement agent** (`title` adapter; MISMO Title & Closing datasets where the agent's system supports them, otherwise portal upload): inbound fee sheet/settlement statement versions, seller CD copy, signed in-person receipt, final disbursement ledger; outbound closing instructions (26.1) and the CD version to be used at the table; every inbound version is hashed and reconciled; unreconciled variances block the CD gate.
- **UCD Collection Solution** (`fnma-ucd`, Direct Integration XML over the legacy DI platform; UI fallback by `fnma_portal_operator`): request = UCD XML with embedded PDF; response = feedback messages, casefile ID, status; idempotency by `(application_id, cd_version, ucd_version)`; retry on transport errors with the same payload; a `rejected` status with critical edits is a business failure, not a retry.
- **25.1**: `assertGateOpen(SM_O61_COMPLIANCE_PASS_CD_GATE)` before render/deliver; `APR_1026_22_ACCURACY` verdicts for redisclosure.
- **21.5**: `runToleranceTest` at every CD version; `tolerance_cures` (lender credits on the CD; 60-day refunds).
- **26.1/26.2**: consistency checks; `closing.consummated` event returns `consummation_at`.
- **29.3/29.4**: `ucd_submissions.casefile_id_ucd` and `FNMA_UCD_ACCEPTED_GATE`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`disclosure` agent (tools: `assembleCdFigures`, `reconcileFigureSources`, `renderCd`, `deliverDisclosure`, `recordReceipt`, `computeEarliestConsummation`, `evaluateRedisclosure`, `runToleranceTest` (21.5), `assertGateOpen` (25.1), `runCdConsistencyChecks`, `generateUcd`, `submitUcd`, `scheduleCorrectedCd`, `openEscalation`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: at clear-to-close it requests figures from the settlement agent, reconciles, renders, passes the 25.1 gate, delivers to every required consumer over the channel their consent allows, records receipt evidence, computes and publishes `earliest_consummation_date`, watches for changes, decides redisclosure, re-delivers, and after consummation builds and submits the UCD and runs the post-consummation correction clocks. Decision record: `{disclosure_id, cd_version, figure_source_versions, gate_run_id, receipts[], earliest_consummation_date, redisclosure_evaluation{triggers, new_wait}, ucd_submission_id, rationale, model_version, prompt_version}`. Guardrails: never edit a figure without a versioned source; never record `actual_receipt_at` without evidence of the enumerated kinds; never treat an oral confirmation as receipt; never shorten the waiting period except through a `cd_waivers` row accepted by `officer`; never issue a "no-wait" corrected CD when 25.1's APR verdict is `fail`; never charge a fee for the CD ((f)(5)); never embed a superseded CD in the UCD. Escalations: `settlement_agent` (figures late, seller CD missing, in-person receipt unrecorded) with SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`; `officer` (waiver acceptance; breach of the 30/60-day correction clocks; UCD rejected within 2 `business_days_fannie_et` of a delivery deadline); `fnma_portal_operator` (UI fallback for UCD); `human_agent` on borrower request. Borrower-facing: every e-delivery e-mail/portal message discloses automation and offers a human; the CD itself is never explained by the agent beyond the form's own text unless the borrower asks (then plain-language answers with a referral to the `mlo_of_record` for terms questions — SAFE Act boundary). Human path: ops console with the same actions; no "mark received" without an uploaded evidence document.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Borrower e-signs the acknowledgement at 11:58 p.m. local on Nov 2 — receipt date is the calendar date in the borrower's time zone as captured by the vendor; the platform stores UTC and the local date.
- Two borrowers, one acknowledges Nov 2, the other Nov 4 → gate uses Nov 4 → earliest consummation Sat Nov 7.
- Non-borrower spouse in a refinance (rescinding consumer) — must receive the CD (§1026.17(d)); add to `cd_receipts` required set from 25.3's rescission list.
- In-person delivery at the table (hybrid closing without prior e-delivery) — consummation cannot occur for three specific business days; the platform never schedules a same-day table delivery for the initial CD.
- CD delivered, then the closing slips a week — no new CD required unless figures change; odd days change with the disbursement date → 25.1 re-run; corrected CD at/before consummation if within tolerance.
- Rate lock expires before the new consummation date (after a new waiting period) → 21.4 relock/extension; if the rate changes, APR re-tested; possibly another new waiting period.
- Settlement agent substitutes its own CD version at the table with different figures → the signed version must be ingested; the platform re-evaluates redisclosure against the last delivered version; if the table version required a new waiting period, the closing is void for TRID purposes — escalate `officer`; corrected CD and, if consummated, treat as post-consummation correction plus a compliance incident.
- UCD critical edit on a data point derived from a CD field (e.g., loan purpose) — fix source, regenerate; if the CD itself was wrong, corrected CD under (f)(2)(iii)/(iv).
- UCD DI outage on the delivery deadline — `fnma_portal_operator` uploads through the UI; same XML, same hash.
- v2.0 mandate announced (Q4 2026) with a date inside a pipeline — flag `fnma.ucd.version` flips per the announced date; loans delivered after the mandate must be v2.0 even if first submitted in v1.5 (FAQ).
- E-SIGN consent withdrawn between versions — later versions by mail; recompute clocks.
- Texas 50(a)(6) (26.1) requires the final itemization one business day before closing and a 12-day notice; the CD schedule must respect both (26.1's timers gate `consummate` too).

- **25.2-T2** Given CD v1 placed in the mail Mon Nov 2 with no acknowledgement, then `presumed_receipt_date = 2026-11-05`, `earliest_consummation_date = 2026-11-09`, and `consummate` on Fri Nov 6 is refused.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Consummation timestamp.** Default: `consummation_at` = note signing time (eNote tamper seal or wet-ink witnessed) in the property state's time zone; legal review to confirm per state that contractual obligation attaches at signing (comment 2(a)(13)-1 leaves it to state law).
2. **What rebuts the mailbox presumption.** Default: only an e-sign/portal acknowledgement, courier signature or the settlement agent's signed receipt; e-mail "opened" events and phone confirmations do not.
3. **Waiver intake.** Default: accept only a consumer-authored statement (scan, photo or free-text portal field with no template language); `officer` approval required; the platform never suggests wording.
4. **Observed federal holidays.** Resolved by comment 2(a)(6)-2 (CFPB Official Interpretations, read 2026-09-10): "Four Federal legal holidays are identified in 5 U.S.C. 6103(a) by a specific date: New Year's Day, January 1; Independence Day, July 4; Veterans Day, November 11; and Christmas Day, December 25." and "When one of these holidays (July 4, for example) falls on a Saturday, Federal offices and other entities might observe the holiday on the preceding Friday (July 3). In cases where the more precise rule applies, the observed holiday (in the example, July 3) is a business day." Default (now VERIFIED): the `regz_specific` calendar excludes only the statutory dates (and the Monday-designated holidays on their statutory Mondays); an observed substitute date is a business day. Worked consequence: Sat Jul 4, 2026 is a holiday (not a business day) and Fri Jul 3, 2026 is a business day for the CD and rescission clocks even though the federal government is closed; none of the fixture-window holidays (Columbus Day Mon Oct 12, Veterans Day Wed Nov 11, Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26, Christmas Fri Dec 25, 2026; New Year's Day Fri Jan 1, 2027) fall on a weekend. 21.2 owns the `holiday_calendars{code=regz_specific}` rows and must encode exactly this.
5. **UCD version default.** Default: generate v2.0 now (accepted since Sept 29, 2025), keep v1.5 generation for fallback until the Q4 2026 mandate announcement fixes the date; flag `fnma.ucd.version`.
6. **Average charges.** Default: none — every settlement charge is the actual amount ((f)(3)(i)); revisit only if a settlement partner insists on average charges for a class.
7. **Settlement-agent CD delivery in escrow states.** Default: SM delivers the CD to the consumer directly (e-delivery) and the settlement agent delivers only the table copy and seller CD; where an agent insists on delivering the consumer CD under (f)(1)(v), SM requires the agent to use SM's delivery rails so evidence is captured.
