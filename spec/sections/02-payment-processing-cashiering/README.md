# Section 2 — Payment Processing & Cashiering

## Overview

Section 2 is the cash-intake spine of the platform: every dollar a borrower (or a third party paying for a borrower) sends must be received, identified, credited as of the right date, allocated in the order the note and security instrument prescribe, posted as balanced double-entry ledger entries, deposited into the correct Fannie Mae custodial account within the Guide's clock, disclosed on the periodic statement, and reported to Fannie Mae as an investor event. The section owns seven processes: periodic-payment posting (2.1), partial payments and the suspense engine (2.2), ACH auto-draft enrollment and the authorization lifecycle (2.3), additional principal/curtailments (2.4), biweekly payments from third-party contractors (2.5), payments received while a modification trial is pending (2.6), and the late-charge engine (2.7). Two cross-cutting engines are defined here and reused everywhere: the **Allocation Engine** (deterministic, rule-set-versioned, instrument-order allocation with overlays for forbearance, trial, bankruptcy, SCRA, transfer and dispute states) and the **Reversal Engine** (returned items, misapplications, corrections — always new balanced entries, never edits).

Architecture fit (architecture baseline §2–§7): the `ledger` package holds the per-loan and per-custodial-account accounts; `core` holds `payments`, `payment_allocations`, `suspense_items`, `fees`, `consents` and the new tables defined below; the `cashiering` agent runs intake, allocation, reversal and late-charge decisions end-to-end, with `custodial-recon` (Section 6) owning aging/return/escheat of unapplied funds, `investor-reporting` (Section 5) owning transport of the events emitted here, `escrow` (Section 3) consuming escrow deposits, `borrower-comms` handling every borrower conversation (partial-payment commitments, ACH enrollment by phone/chat, revocations) and `compliance-sentinel` watching timers. Adapters: `lockbox`, `nacha`, `custodial-bank`, `fnma-servicing-events`/`fnma-lsdu` (via Section 5), `fnma-smdu`, `print-mail`/`e-delivery`, `telephony/voice`.

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: Supermortgage performs every step and — per Section 6.1's resolution of the baseline's open decision — owns the custodial depository relationships, is the ACH **Originator** under its own ODFI agreement (not a Third-Party Sender), and is the payee named in the RESPA hello notice and on statements. The partner (master servicer of record, A2-1-07) remains liable to Fannie Mae for prompt crediting, custodial deposits and investor reporting, receives the daily cashiering exception digest, and is the servicer number on every investor event. No Fannie Mae UI is automated: SMDU trial-payment reporting runs through B2B or a `human_portal_task`.

Dependencies: 1.1/1.6 (note terms, inherited suspense), 1.3 (60-day misdirected-payment window; `REGX_1024_33C1_LATE_FEE_PROTECTION_60`), 3.x (escrow deposits), 4.1 (NoE corrections via `payment.reapply`/`fee.reverse`/`late_charge.waive`), 5.1/5.2 (event transport, remittance math, CD22 no-payment sweep), 6.1–6.5 (custodial accounts, deposit evidence, unapplied-funds aging), 7.1 (statement breakdown and unapplied-funds disclosure), 8.1 (credit reporting reads `credited_as_of`), 11.x (payment reminder by the 20th), 12.4–12.8 (forbearance/repayment/trial overlays), 13.x/14.x (foreclosure and bankruptcy holds; Rule 3002.1 fee notices), 16.1/16.2 (payoffs are out of scope here), 17.x (transfer-out in-transit payments).

Changes in flight and blueprint corrections (verified 2026-09-09): the Servicing Guide (Aug. 12, 2026 edition) defers the application order to the security instrument (C-1.1-01) and states it in F-1-09 — interest → principal → escrow → late charges for instruments dated March 1999 or later; C-1.1-02 (08/13/2025) carries the four-condition partial-payment rule, the $50 rule and the trial-period suspense rule; C-1.1-03 and C-1.1-04 are one-sentence topics; C-1.2-01 (11/13/2024) adds the deferral/modification curtailment order and Form 181 re-amortization; late-charge terms live in A2-3-04 + Selling Guide B8-3-02 + Form 3200 (07/2021) — there is no dedicated Servicing Guide late-charge topic. The blueprint's Reg X 1024.34 citation for partial payments is wrong (1024.34 is escrow disbursement/refund); the operative rules are Reg Z 1026.36(c)(1)(ii), 1026.41(d)(3)/(d)(5) and Reg X 1024.35(b)(1)–(3). LL-2026-05 makes payment, curtailment, reversal and no-payment events reportable by 3:00 a.m. ET next business day once the payment-event phase goes live (Section 5 runs LAR and event rails in parallel until then). Nacha's fraud-monitoring rule reached all originators on June 19, 2026. The CFPB's 2022 pay-to-pay advisory opinion was withdrawn May 12, 2025 and not reissued; FDCPA §808(1) and state law still govern convenience fees.
