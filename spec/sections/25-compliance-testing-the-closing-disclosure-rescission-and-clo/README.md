## Overview

**Scope.** Section 25 is the platform's last line before a consumer is bound and money moves: the compliance-test engine that must be green before the LE, lock, CD, consummation, disbursement and delivery commands run (25.1); the Closing Disclosure — preparation from versioned figure sources, delivery and receipt evidence per consumer, the three-specific-business-day waiting period, the three redisclosure triggers, the 30/60/60-day post-consummation regimes, the seller's CD and the UCD submission (25.2); the right of rescission for refinances of a principal dwelling — who holds it, H-8 vs H-9, the period, the waiver, confirmation of non-rescission, the funding gate and the unwind (25.3); and the closing-day and immediate post-closing notices that are not the CD — initial escrow statement delivery, escrow elections and state escrow notices, the §1026.39 ownership-transfer notice as Fannie Mae actually practices it, the GLBA privacy gate, the first-payment letter and autopay offer, the HPA/flood cross-references, the state matrix and the Form 1098 seeds (25.4). Every rule is written to the eCFR pages current as of Sept 3–9, 2026, the CFPB Official Interpretations read Sept 10, 2026, the Selling Guide edition of Sept 2, 2026 and the LLPA Matrix of Sept 9, 2026; the fixture calendar (CD e-signed Mon Nov 2 → consummation Fri Nov 6 → rescission expires midnight Tue Nov 10 → disbursement Thu Nov 12 → purchased Thu Nov 19, 2026) is worked in every process.

**Agents.** The `compliance-tester` agent owns 25.1 end-to-end: it assembles the canonical input snapshot, runs every test deterministically (the LLM never sits in the arithmetic path), derives the six gates and drafts cure plans. The `disclosure` agent owns 25.2, 25.3 and 25.4: CD assembly, delivery, receipt evidence and redisclosure; rescission applicability, notices, period computation, channel sweeps and unwind; the closing-package and post-closing notice runs, escrow elections and the §1026.39 expectation. The `funder` agent (26.3) consumes `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE` and the 25.1 disbursement gate; the `boarding` agent hands the escrow election, first-payment-letter and 1098 seeds to servicing; `compliance-sentinel` and `qc-audit` receive every decision record. Humans appear only where the law or policy names them: the partner `officer` (blocking-failure review, policy-only waivers, acceptance of a consumer's bona fide emergency waiver, rescission unwinds, escrow-policy exceptions), the `mlo_of_record` (individual licensing failures; informed of rescissions), the external `settlement_agent`/`notary` (seller CD, table copies, paper packages), the `fnma_portal_operator` (UCD UI fallback), the `licensed_specialist` where a state requires one, and `human_agent` on request.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the creditor for every obligation in this section — the "person" liable under TILA/Reg Z (APR accuracy, the CD, rescission), RESPA §8 and §1024.17, the SAFE Act and state licensing (its NMLS company/branch records and its `mlo_of_record` are what 25.1 checks), GLBA (the customer relationship is the partner's), and the Fannie Mae seller whose eligibility representations the tests protect and whose Technology Manager credentials the UCD submission uses. Every consumer artifact names the partner as lender with its NMLSR ID and the MLO's. SM designs and runs the tests, prepares, delivers and evidences every disclosure, computes every clock, holds funding through the rescission period as warehouse provider, submits the UCD under the partner's TSP Product, is the subservicer named as payment address from day one, and is itself bound by §1026.36 as a loan-originator organization to the extent its AI takes the application and presents terms (25.1-Q3) and by state third-party processor/underwriter licensing (31.1). Fannie Mae becomes the §1026.39 "covered person" at purchase and — per its own consumer page — sends the ownership letter itself; SM's warehouse interest is a security interest and never makes SM a covered person unless the facility takes an assignment.

**Dependencies.** Inbound: 21.2/21.4/21.5 (LE, intent to proceed and fee gate, revised LEs; the tolerance engine `tolerance_tests`/`tolerance_cures`/`runToleranceTest` and `REGZ_1026_19F2V_TOLERANCE_REFUND_60` that 25.1 invokes and 25.2 relies on), 21.6 (`adverse_actions` — no CD on a denied file), 23.3 (clear-to-close), 23.4 (`qm_determinations`, `hpml_determinations`, `high_cost_determinations`, `apor_tables`, `FFIEC_APOR_TABLE_REFRESH_WEEKLY`, `REGZ_1026_35_HPML_ESCROW_5Y` — re-executed on CD and consummation figures, never redefined), 24.2 (Reg B copy gate on a different calendar), 24.4 (`title_orders`, `settlement_agents`, payoffs, vesting for the rescission list), 24.5 (`NTC_FDPA_4104A_FLOOD_NOTICE`), 24.6 (`hpa_disclosures`, `HPA_4903_INITIAL_DISCLOSURE_GATE`), 26.1/26.2 (document generation consuming `cd_consistency_checks`; the consummation timestamp and signing-session evidence), 30.3 (initial escrow analysis).
