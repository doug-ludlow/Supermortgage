### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**E-5-01, Requesting Reimbursement for Expenses (11/09/2016).** Servicers may request reimbursement of advances for taxes, insurance, applicable HOA dues and other out-of-pocket expenses. The servicer must submit its **final** request for expense reimbursement within **60 days** after: "the completion of a workout option (including a mortgage loan modification or short sale)"; "a third-party sale is completed in accordance with E-3.5-02"; "the date the mortgage loan is reinstated or paid off"; or "Fannie Mae disposes of a property acquired through foreclosure sale or a Fannie Mae Mortgage Release" (government loans: "the date the final FHA, VA, or RD claim proceeds are submitted to Fannie Mae"). "If the servicer submits a request for reimbursement of advances after the date Fannie Mae specifies for the submission of the final request for expense reimbursement, Fannie Mae will deny the request." Procedures: F-1-05.

**F-1-06 (05/13/2026) tightens the clock for MI-insured liquidations:** after a short sale or third-party sale the servicer must submit a final expense reimbursement request "no later than 30 days following the settlement of the third-party sale or short sale," and after a Mortgage Release or foreclosure sale "no later than 30 days following the settlement of the Mortgage Release or foreclosure sale date, or, in states that have redemption periods, within 30 days after expiration of the redemption period" (court-order jurisdictions use the docket-entry date). The procedure's wording "through the CRS" is legacy; the operative channel is Property 360 (Job Aid below) — **discrepancy noted**.

**F-1-05, Expense Reimbursement (06/11/2025; SVC-2025-03).** Subsections: General Expense Reimbursement Requirements; Reimbursement for Escrow Advances; HOA Assessments; Expenses Associated with Workout Options; Reimbursing Fannie Mae for a Workout Incentive Fee Related to a Cancelled Modification or Payment Deferral; Foreclosure Attorney Fee Milestones or Prorated Bankruptcy Attorney Fees; Property Inspections and Property Preservation Expenses; Recording Costs Associated with a Satisfaction or Charge-Off; Mortgage Insurance Premium Expenses; Defined Expense Reimbursement Limits; Other Reimbursable Default-Related Legal Expenses. Operative rules: advances must be "necessary to protect Fannie Mae's interest in the property" and made on a portfolio, participation-pool or special servicing option MBS loan; "If Fannie Mae reimburses the servicer for an advance, and the mortgage loan is subsequently reinstated or subject to a completed retention workout option, the servicer must reimburse Fannie Mae **within 60 days** of the date of the reinstatement or the completed retention workout option all recoverable advances collected from the borrower" (special remittance — CRS **353** "Re-instated Advance Recoverable Proceeds" / **352** "Payoff/Repurchase Advance Recoverable Proceeds" / **571** "REO Related Servicer Disbursements" per the Oct. 15, 2025 code list); escrow advances (taxes, property and flood premiums) are reimbursable "even if the expenses were advanced prior to the mortgage loan becoming delinquent" provided the loan "subsequently become[s] delinquent," and insurance/HOA advances only "for a period of up to **14 days** after the date of the foreclosure sale or acceptance of an executed Mortgage Release, the closing date for a short sale, or for a third-party sale the later of the date the sale is completed (including funds received), or … the date the court confirms or ratifies the sale"; HOA reimbursement is "limited to the lowest of the actual advances paid, maximum limit provided in the project declaration, or state statutory maximums" with late fees/interest/attorney fees excluded where they "directly result from the servicer's failure to monitor"; MI renewal premiums "paid after the date of default and through the month that any of the following events occurs: a third-party sale, a short sale, a foreclosure, or a Mortgage Release," and an MI premium refund received after reimbursement must be remitted "through the CRS using the **336** receipt code within 30 days"; deferral-related expenses "within 60 days of the completion of the payment deferral"; Mortgage Release document preparation "$650 each, upon completion"; a workout incentive fee for a modification/deferral cancelled and not re-entered within 30 days is repaid "within 60 days of the cancellation date"; foreclosure attorney fees "at established foreclosure milestones" and bankruptcy fees when a case closes early, "even if the full applicable allowable fee is not incurred"; satisfaction recording costs only where law/documents bar charging the borrower and never netted from payoff proceeds; property inspections/preservation per the Defined Expense Reimbursement Limits (9.9 `allowable_matrix`, e.g., exterior inspection $30, interior $45, insured-loss $60, code violations $1,000 each / $3,000 life); other legal expenses per E-5-07 (court filing costs, trustee sale guarantees, posting/publication, postponement announcements, BPOs, recording, statutory certified mail). **No provision pays interest on servicer advances** (verified absent in E-5-01/F-1-05/E-5-05).

**E-5-02…E-5-07 (attorney fees and costs; 02/12/2020, 12/18/2024, 11/12/2014).** Before requesting reimbursement the servicer must "review and approve the attorney fees and costs to ensure they comply with Fannie Mae guidelines," verify "all costs be actual, reasonable, and necessary," that services were performed lawfully, that fees charged to borrowers are permitted by the note/security instrument/law, that invoices "describe each expense item in sufficient detail," that milestone billing follows E-5-05, and it must "pay the fees and costs and retain paid invoices." **Milestone schedule (E-5-05):** judicial — title requested 30%, title reviewed 40%, complaint filed 50%, service started 60%, service complete 70%, affidavit/judgment prepared 80%, judgment to court 90%, bid reviewed/confirmed 95%, sale held/documents recorded 100%; non-judicial — title requested 30%, title reviewed 65%, notices started 75%, first legal 85%, sale package 95%, sale held/recorded 100%; "must not prorate … between milestones." Uncollected fees/advances are reimbursed only when "it was not legally permissible to collect" them from the borrower at reinstatement/payoff/workout, and never if the servicer simply failed to include them in the reinstatement/payoff figure. **Allowable Foreclosure Attorney Fees Exhibit (Dec. 18, 2024)** — e.g., TX $2,300 non-judicial ($3,800 judicial 50(a)(6)), FL $5,400 ($6,900 trial), NY $6,800 judicial ($2,000 co-op), CA $2,300, GA $2,225, IL $4,100, NJ $6,700, OH $4,000, PA $4,150, VA $2,600, WA $2,400 ($4,150 judicial), AZ $2,250, NV $2,650, MI $2,550, MA $4,700, CT $4,400/$5,000, MD $3,900, MN $2,375 (+$1,100 registered land), CO $2,800, NC $2,950; costs (title, filing, recording, process server, publication) are outside the fee; fees above the exhibit require excess-fee approval "from Fannie Mae's SF CPM division" obtained by the **law firm** through the **Property 360 Excess Fees and Costs** portal (Job Aid; E-5-04); overhead items (travel, copies, notary, secretarial, correspondence) are non-reimbursable; a cured-then-re-defaulted loan earns a new allowable fee. **E-5-03/Allowable Bankruptcy Attorney Fees Exhibit** (12/18/2024; SVC-2025-06 updated the exhibit) with proration "to reasonably relate to the amount of work actually performed." **E-5-06:** technology fees reimbursable up to "$25.00 per loan for the life of a default"; e-invoice submission fees "$10.00 for the life of the loan" ($5.00 foreclosure + $5.00 bankruptcy); no per-loan/click/data-entry charges to attorneys.

**Servicer Expense Reimbursement Job Aid (05/01/2026) and Property 360 Expense Reimbursement User Guide v11.0 (07/10/2025).** Claim types: **571** "business as usual," **FNMA Mod** (modification expenses), **NPL** (non-performing/re-performing loan sales), **HECM**, **RECON** (servicer reconciliation), **SOL** ("claims with incurred expenses that predate a statute of limitations"). "All expense reimbursement claims must be filed within 60 days" of the E-5-01 milestones; later claims "may be denied for late claim filing" (dispute via IRT when caused by circumstances beyond the servicer's control). Submission: single-claim entry or **bulk upload** — an Excel template (Claims / Error / Configurations tabs) validated locally and uploaded as a ZIP containing the .XLSX and a generated .JSON; required fields include Fannie Mae loan number, servicer loan number, claim type, claim number, action, expense type/subtype, service start date, quantity, unit price, paid date, payee/seller-servicer ID, request date, submitter POC, transaction ID; up to 100 line items per claim, 5 attachments per line, file names matching the "Attachment Names" column; a **Non-Recoverable Indicator** (blank = recoverable at reinstatement/payoff; "Non-recoverable"; "Not-yet recovered"). Statuses: Hold, Submitted, Approved (auto-paid), **Pending Submitter Action** (documentation request; **60-day** countdown to auto-denial), Rejected (system error), Void, plus Paid/Denied/Curtailed; "Claims are processed based on claim age … determined by the submission date … Updating a claim after it has been submitted changes the submission date"; processing "generally … within 30 days"; **ACH within 3 business days** of Paid (checks 10 days); landscaping, taxes, insurance and attorney fees "should always be itemized"; quantity aggregates identical recurring items (whole numbers); NPI must be removed from attachments; property-registration bonds are not reimbursable; capitalized advances on modifications closed on/after Oct. 1, 2023 must be claimed (no auto-generation); workout incentive claims remain auto-generated (`P360AutoGenerated`, $1,000 cap); the "Third Party Sales Reimbursement" line was retired May 1, 2026 (itemize); post-payoff payment-deferral reimbursements use "General Services – Post Payoff PD Reimbursement"; missing ACH/check → `FannieMae_REO_Disbursements@fanniemae.com`; the FMConnect "Expense Reimbursement Reconciliation Report" (monthly) lists disbursements, advances and refunds; **B2B system integration** exists for interested servicers via a discovery call, and the Developer Portal lists an **Expense Claims** API (spec not public — research/00b F5/F6) **[UNVERIFIED — schema at credentialing]**.

**Inquiry Response Tool (IRT) Submitter User Manual (07/24/2025).** Categories include Expense Denied, Expense Curtailed, Expense Pending, Manual Claim Request, System Issue, Unclear Guidelines; single or CSV batch (one category per batch); statuses Draft → New → In Progress → Pending → Completed → Re-Opened → Final Review → Closed; "the submitter … has seven calendar days ('seven days') to respond. If a response is not received within seven days, the inquiry closes and automatically changes to a 'Completed' status"; a Completed inquiry may be reopened once, the second reopening enters Final Review (last rebuttal). Portal only (no API); Job Aid expects Fannie Mae responses "within 5 business days."

**Reg X / other law.** Reg X does not govern investor claims, but it constrains what can be *charged to the borrower* and therefore the Non-Recoverable Indicator: fees must be authorized by the contract and law (§1024.35(b)(5) error resolution for improper fees; the 2024 NPRM's fee restriction during a review cycle — behind the versioned rule set, research/00a §1.2). 1099-MISC for attorney payments (E-4.3-04). State laws cap recoverable attorney fees/late charges (`jurisdiction_rules`). Bankruptcy: fees charged to the debtor must be noticed under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 3002.1(c) (Section 14) — a fee not noticed is non-recoverable from the borrower but may still be claimable from Fannie Mae if otherwise allowable.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** deadlines are concrete (60 days; 30 days for MI-insured liquidations; 60-day PSA; 7-day IRT; 60-day repayments; 30-day 336 refund); the system is Property 360 (bulk/API), not a generic "Servicer Expense Reimbursement" tool, and Form 571 "Cash Disbursement Request" survives only as the claim-type name; the row omits the recoverable-advance repayment obligation, milestone billing, the excess-fee process split (servicer → HomeTracker for preservation; law firm → P360 Excess Fees portal for legal), the reset-on-edit claim-age rule and the absence of interest on advances; automation is (a) for claim preparation/validation/reconciliation and (b) only for the portal steps (bulk upload if the API is not credentialed, PSA responses, IRT).

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Every `advances` row (kind ∈ {escrow_tax, escrow_hazard, escrow_flood, hoa, mi_premium, inspection, preservation, registration, utilities, attorney_fee_fcl, attorney_fee_bk, legal_cost, recording, valuation, mediation, technology_fee, einvoice_fee, workout_expense, other}) with `paid_at`, invoice document, allowable code and `borrower_recoverable` flag (from Sections 3, 9, 13, 14, 12, 16).
- Milestone events that start the final-claim clock: **`lossmit.modification.completed`** — declared by 12.8, which is its only producer; it fires when the modification is complete **as a file** (executed agreement returned and countersigned, recorded where required, delivered to the custodian where required, and booked), which is the instant F-1-05's 60-day window should run from because the expense population is final only then. **Do not key this clock on `lossmit.modification.effective`** (12.8's other event, fired when the modification effective date arrives, terms in force but the file possibly still open) — using it would start the severity-1 clock weeks early and expire claims that are still accruing recording and execution costs — `shortsale.closed`, `lossmit.deferral.completed`, `tps.completed` (final payment + confirmation), `loan.reinstated`, `payoff.funds.cleared`, `reo.disposed_by_fnma` (from Fannie Mae Connect REO reports / P360), `mortgage_release.completed`, `foreclosure.sale.held` (30-day F-1-06 clock when MI-insured), `mi.claim.settled` (government loans: final claim proceeds submitted).
- Inbound: P360 claim status updates (API/B2B where available; else operator export), PSA requests, payment details (check #/date/amount), FMConnect Expense Reimbursement Reconciliation Report (monthly), ACH credits on the bank feed, IRT responses, excess-fee approvals (firm-supplied), HomeTracker bid decisions (9.9), insurer premium refunds (10.5), unearned hazard refunds (9.5).

#### State machine
`draft` (lines accumulate from `advances`) → `validated` (allowable checks pass or warnings acknowledged) → `package_ready` (bulk ZIP/API payload built; attachments manifest) → `submitted` (P360 Submitted/Hold) → {`approved` → `paid` (ACH matched) | `psa` (60-day clock; respond → `submitted`) | `denied`/`curtailed` (→ `disputed` via IRT → `paid`/`closed`) | `rejected` (system error; fix and resubmit — claim age resets)} → `closed` (all lines final; credits reconciled; recoveries booked). `void` from any pre-payment state. Interim claims (`interim = true`) follow the same path; the final claim is the one submitted against `final_due_at`. Actors: `claims-reo` agent (all transitions except portal clicks), `fnma_portal_operator` (bulk upload, PSA upload, IRT), Fannie Mae (status/payment), `custodial-recon` (ACH match).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Claim-line eligibility.** A line is claimable iff the advance was necessary to protect Fannie Mae's interest, the loan is portfolio/participation/special servicing option MBS (regular servicing option MBS loans: the servicer bears the loss — lines are `ineligible_regular_servicing` unless the loan was reclassified/repurchased), the expense is within an allowable schedule or carries an approval id, it was **paid** (`paid_date` present, invoice retained), and it is not borrower-recoverable-and-uncollected (E-5-05): at reinstatement/payoff/workout the line must have been included in the reinstatement/payoff figure; if collected → `advance_recoveries` (no claim); if legally non-recoverable → `nonrecoverable_indicator = non_recoverable` with the legal basis (`jurisdiction_rules`/contract) in the comment; if recoverable but uncollected by our error → not claimable (`fees.nonreimbursable_reason = 'e505_not_included'`). Liquidation claims (REO/TPS/short sale/Mortgage Release) leave the indicator blank.
2. **Allowable checks (per line).** Foreclosure attorney fee ≤ exhibit amount for the state/track × milestone %; costs itemized separately; bankruptcy fees ≤ exhibit per service and prorated; technology fee ≤ $25 per default, e-invoice ≤ $5 FCL + $5 BK (life of loan $10); inspections $30/$45/$60; preservation ≤ `allowable_matrix` caps with life-of-loan accumulators (9.9) unless `hometracker_bid_id` approved; HOA ≤ min(actual, declaration max, state statutory max) with late fees/interest/attorney fees excluded when caused by our monitoring failure; taxes actual (penalties/interest itemized and excluded when caused by our late payment); hazard/flood premiums actual through the 14-day post-event cut-off; MI premiums after the default date through the month of the liquidation event; Mortgage Release doc prep ≤ $650; registration actual cost (bonds excluded); code violations ≤ $1,000 each / $3,000 life; overhead items rejected. Each check writes `validation_messages` mirroring P360's "edit" vocabulary so proactive-feedback denials are anticipated.
3. **Milestone and deadline selection.** `final_due_at = min(E-5-01 milestone + 60 CD, F-1-06 date + 30 CD if mi_insured)`; for REO the E-5-01 anchor is Fannie Mae's disposition (observed from REO reports) — unknown at sale, so the MI-insured 30-day rule or the internal `SM_EXPENSE_CLAIM_TARGET_20` drives filing; for uninsured REO the platform files the "final" claim within 60 days of the sale as policy (open question 2) and a supplemental 571 for post-sale items (e.g., late-arriving tax bills within the 14-day window) before disposition + 60.
4. **Post-event cut-offs.** Insurance/HOA advances dated > event + 14 days → `post_sale_nonreimbursable`; preservation after a Fannie Mae acquisition → non-reimbursable unless SF CPM directed (15.1 rule 6); TPS: inspections/preservation reimbursable through completion date.
5. **Itemization and quantity.** Taxes, insurance, landscaping, attorney fees are one line per invoice/bill; identical recurring items (monthly HOA, monthly inspections) may use `quantity = n` with a service date range; quantities are integers; unit price × quantity = amount (cents, no rounding needed).
6. **Claim age.** Submit once, complete; edits after submission are avoided — a PSA response is an attachment/comment, not a re-submission; if a re-submission is unavoidable, `age_reset_count++` and the deadlines are re-evaluated against the new submission date.
7. **Credits.** Unearned hazard/flood premium refunds, MI refunds, escrow balances, rents and insurance proceeds received before the final claim are `claim_credits` netted on the claim; received after payment → remit (318 hazard, 336 MI within 30 days, 571 other) — the FMConnect reconciliation report is matched to both.
8. **Recovered-advance repayment.** When a reinstatement/retention workout completes after Fannie Mae reimbursed advances, `advance_recoveries` with `source = borrower_reinstatement` create a `fnma_repay_due_at = completion + 60 CD` and a CRS 353 (or 352 for payoff/repurchase) batch; the amount is the collected portion of each reimbursed advance, FIFO by advance.
9. **Worked example (fixture L15, REO, MI-insured; sale Tue Oct 5, 2027).** Lines: taxes $4,820.00 (paid Jan 31, 2027; 2026 bill); hazard premium $1,450.00 (paid Mar 15, 2027; term Mar 20, 2027–Mar 20, 2028); MI premiums Dec 2026–Oct 2027 = 11 × $124.54 = $1,369.94 (quantity 11); exterior inspections Jan–Sep 2027 = 9 × $30.00 = $270.00; preservation (vacant Jul 9, 2027): lock $60.00, initial cut $125.00, re-cuts 2 × $80.00 = $160.00, debris 2 CY × $50.00 = $100.00 → $445.00; foreclosure attorney fee TX non-judicial $2,300.00 (milestone 100%, invoices at 30/65/75/85/95/100%); costs: title $350.00, posting/filing/recording $215.00 → $565.00; technology fee $25.00 + e-invoice $5.00 = $30.00. Gross = 4,820.00 + 1,450.00 + 1,369.94 + 270.00 + 445.00 + 2,300.00 + 565.00 + 30.00 = **$11,249.94**. Credit: unearned hazard premium for Oct 5, 2027–Mar 20, 2028 = 166/365 × $1,450.00 = $659.45 (received Nov 2, 2027) → net **$10,590.49**. `final_due_at` = min(disposition + 60 [unknown], Oct 5 + 30 = **Thu Nov 4, 2027**) → the package is ready by Oct 25 (`SM_EXPENSE_CLAIM_TARGET_20`) and submitted Nov 3 after the refund posts; P360 auto-approves the itemized lines; Paid Nov 20; ACH expected by Wed Nov 24, 2027 (3 BD; Nov 25 holiday tolerance to Nov 29). If the refund had arrived after payment, the claim would carry the E-4.4-02 comment and the $659.45 would be remitted with code 318 on receipt. Delinquency (P&I) advances are **not** on this claim (15.4).
10. **Curtailment/denial triage.** Each denied/curtailed line maps to a reason code; if the reason is a validation we can cure (missing invoice, wrong subtype), file an IRT inquiry with evidence within 5 BD; if the reason is policy (over allowable without approval, late claim), book the write-off to the partner's loss account and record the root cause for 9.9/13.x process fixes; late-claim denials caused by Fannie Mae system issues are disputed with outage evidence.
11. **Interest on advances.** None claimable; the carrying cost is a funding-facility cost (5.2 open decision) and reported to the partner monthly.

#### Integrations
- **Property 360 claims.** Preferred: `fnma-p360` Expense Claims API / B2B (OAuth ROPC per 00b F14; idempotency by `claim_number`; JSON payload mirroring the bulk template fields; status callbacks or polling) **[UNVERIFIED — spec at credentialing; sandbox `api-clve`]**. Fallback: the agent generates the bulk ZIP (.XLSX populated from `expense_claim_lines` + generated .JSON + attachments named per the manifest) and a `human_portal_task` `p360.claims.bulk_upload` for `fnma_portal_operator` (validate → upload → capture batch id/errors → paste the Error-tab export back, parsed into `validation_messages`). Single-claim entry only for ≤3 lines when the bulk path is down.
- **PSA/edits:** `p360.claims.psa_response` task with the requested documents pre-attached; **payments:** P360 File Details (check #/date/amount) via API/export + bank ACH match; **FMConnect** Expense Reimbursement Reconciliation Report (API where exposed; else monthly operator pull) reconciled to `expense_claims`, `claim_credits` and `advance_recoveries`.
- **IRT:** portal/CSV batch only → `irt.inquiry.create`/`irt.inquiry.respond` tasks with a 7-day SLA timer; responses parsed from the Repository export.
- **HomeTracker** (9.9) and **P360 Excess Fees and Costs** (law firms; we ingest approval ids with invoices) — the claim validator requires the approval id on any line above the schedule.
- **`attorney-network`:** e-invoices (LEDES or vendor JSON **[vendor-specific, UNVERIFIED]**) with milestone codes, excess-fee approval ids, 1099 data; **`preservation`/`tax-service`/`insurance-tracking`/`hoa` feeds** supply invoices and paid dates; **`fnma-crs`** for 336/353/352/571/318/350 remittances (5.2 batch task).
- Failure: API outage → bulk fallback before `final_due_at − 5 CD`; bulk validation errors → fix and re-upload the same day (age not yet started); P360 down on the deadline day → single-entry attempt, screenshots, IRT late-filing dispute.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `expense_claims`/`expense_claim_lines`/`claim_credits`/`irt_inquiries`/`advance_recoveries`; claim packages (ZIP + manifest, hashed); P360 evidence; ACH matches; monthly reconciliation report; partner loss report (denials/curtailments/write-offs by root cause).
- Ledger: on submission no entry (memo `claims_receivable_fnma` per line); on Paid: Dr bank (partner/advance account) Cr `servicer_advance_receivable` / `corporate_advances` / `escrow_advances` per line; denied/curtailed: Dr `partner_loss_expense` Cr the advance account (with reason); credits remitted: Dr the advance account Cr `fnma_remittance_payable` → CRS.
- Investor events: none (claims are not investor-reporting events); CRS special remittances as above.
- No borrower notices; borrower-facing fee itemization lives in payoff/reinstatement statements (Section 16) and periodic statements (Section 7).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`claims-reo` agent. Tools: `sweepClaimCandidates`, `validateLine` (allowable schedules, cut-offs, recoverability), `assembleClaim`, `buildBulkPackage`/`submitClaimApi`, `pollClaimStatus`, `answerPsa`, `matchPayment`, `fileIrtInquiry`, `bookWriteOff`, `scheduleRepayment`, `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{claim_id, loan_id, claim_type, milestone, final_due_at, lines[{advance_id, code, amount, cap, validation, indicator, evidence_ids}], credits[], gross, net, channel, submitted_at, confidence, exceptions[]}`. Guardrails: never submit a line without a paid invoice and allowable basis; never mark `non_recoverable` without a cited legal basis; never re-submit a claim that would push the age past `final_due_at`; never attach documents with NPI (automated redaction check must pass); denials > $5,000 per loan or systematic denial patterns → `officer` briefing. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (bulk upload, PSA, IRT, CRS); `attorney` (fee disputes with firms, excess-fee approvals); `officer` (write-offs above threshold; A1-3-02 make-whole demands). Toggle-off: the validator runs; a human approves each claim in `ops-console` before upload.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in with open claims** (Section 1): board the transferor's claim history from the Reconciliation Report; claims for pre-transfer advances are the transferor's unless the agreement assigns them; avoid duplicate claims (P360 rejects duplicates by loan/expense/date).
- **Transfer-out**: freeze new submissions after the cut-off; hand the open-claim ledger to the transferee (Form 629 acquired-property list).
- **Bankruptcy overlays**: fees not noticed under 3002.1 are non-recoverable from the debtor; still claimable if allowable; proration for closed cases.
- **Modification capitalized advances** (post-10/1/2023): claim the capitalized amounts on an `fnma_mod` claim within 60 days of completion; watch the incentive $1,000 cap and the cancelled-modification 60-day repayment.
- **Late-arriving invoices** (tax bills, HOA) after the final claim: supplemental 571 only if before the milestone + 60 and, for MI-insured, before the MI claim's supplemental window (15.3).
- **Refund refusals**: E-4.4-02 comment on the final claim; Fannie Mae otherwise treats the computed refund as due.
- **Reinstatement after reimbursement**: 60-day repayment of the collected portion (353); partial collections repaid pro-rata.
- **Property 360 auto-generated incentive claims**: reconcile, never duplicate.
- **Regular servicing option MBS loans**: no Fannie Mae reimbursement of default expenses; costs recovered from proceeds/borrower or borne by the servicer (5.6 repurchase interplay).
- **Disaster inspections** for current loans (F-1-05 allows) → 571 lines with the disaster code.
- **P360 rejects the ZIP** (schema change after a release) → refresh the template mapping from the Configurations tab; release-note watch job.

#### Audit and evidence
Per claim: line-level validation records (schedule version, cap, evidence hashes), submission payload/ZIP hash and channel, P360 ids and status history, PSA/IRT threads, payment details and bank matches, reconciliation-report matches, write-off approvals and root causes, repayment remittances, `agent_decisions`, timer histories — retained `life_of_loan_plus_4y`; supports Fannie Mae post-payment reviews (P360 PPR tile), STAR/MORA expense testing and partner loss reporting.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Channel** — default: pursue Expense Claims API/B2B credentialing at TSP onboarding; launch on the bulk-upload package with the operator queue.
2. **Uninsured REO "final" claim timing** — default: file within 60 days of the sale as a "final" and treat later items as supplementals until Fannie Mae's disposition + 60 (needs Servicing Manager confirmation that supplementals after a "final" are accepted).
3. **Which bank account receives ACH reimbursements** (partner vs. Supermortgage advance facility) — default: the funding account that advanced (partner line), with Supermortgage reconciling.
4. **Write-off authority thresholds** — default: agent ≤ $500/line, `officer` above; partner sign-off monthly.
