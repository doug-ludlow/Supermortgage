### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-11)

**Servicer of record and the subservicing arrangement — Servicing Guide A2-1-07, Subservicing (05/13/2026; Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026) [VERIFIED].** "The master servicer may not enter into new subservicing arrangements or extend existing arrangements to include newly originated mortgage loans, unless both the master servicer and the subservicer are Fannie Mae-approved servicers in good standing." "Each mortgage loan that is subject to a subservicing arrangement must be identified in Fannie Mae's records." "The master servicer and the subservicer must execute and submit the Data Access Authorization Form (Form 101) at the inception of the subservicing arrangement." "Each subservicer must establish custodial accounts for all Fannie Mae mortgage loans that it subservices for a master servicer." "Even if a subservicing arrangement is known, approved of, or consented to by Fannie Mae, the master servicer remains fully liable to Fannie Mae for the performance of all servicing obligations." The *mechanism* by which a newly originated loan delivered under the partner's servicer number is "identified in Fannie Mae's records" as subserviced by SM (arrangement-level registration vs a per-loan Form 629 entry) is **[UNVERIFIED — confirm with Fannie Mae's servicing-transfer/subservicing team at onboarding; default: the approved arrangement covers every loan delivered under the partner's servicer number]**. The LL-2026-05 FAQ v1.6 treats a subservicer as the "acting servicer" that completes the Implementation Readiness Tracker and reports on the same schedule (5.1; 00a-fnma §9).

**Servicer duties — Servicing Guide A2-1-01, General Servicer Duties and Responsibilities (12/17/2025) [VERIFIED].** The topic enumerates the duties ("monitoring and paying property taxes, HOA assessments, and related expenses"; "maintaining adequate property insurance…"; "establishing and maintaining accounts for the deposit of borrowers' funds"; "responding to borrowers' inquiries…"; "maintaining accurate mortgage loan servicing and accounting records"; "collecting and promptly remitting any and all amounts due Fannie Mae"; "advancing reasonable amounts, if necessary, to cover expenses…") but "does not explicitly state a specific date when the servicer's obligations commence for purchased mortgage loans." Operationally the obligations attach on the purchase date — the day Fannie Mae becomes owner and the day from which it purchases/deducts interest (C2-1.1-06) and expects reporting (below).

**Remittance type for whole loans — Selling Guide C1-3-01, General Information on Remittance Types (08/26/2014) [VERIFIED].** "The Actual/Actual (A/A) remittance type is available for whole loans"; "The Scheduled/Scheduled (S/S) remittance type is available on a negotiated basis for whole loans"; "The Scheduled/Actual (S/A) remittance type is available for whole loans." "When taking down a commitment to sell whole loans to Fannie Mae, lenders must select a remittance type that identifies both the amount and timing for sending the borrowers' mortgage payments to Fannie Mae." "After Fannie Mae purchases a mortgage, it generally will not change the remittance type the lender has selected." S/A: "Lenders remit scheduled interest (net of servicing fees) regardless of actual collections from the borrower and actual principal collected the month following receipt of funds." The remittance type is therefore fixed at commitment (29.1) and flows to 30.1 from `commitments.remittance_type` and the Purchase Advice. Pass-through rate: C2-1.1-02 (06/07/2023) defines "the pass-through rate, defined as the difference between the gross note rate and the servicing fee" [VERIFIED]; the minimum whole-loan servicing fee was not found on the topic (the PE–Whole Loan FAQ says a 25 bps "servicing strip" is required for ARMs — 00b-orig F7) **[UNVERIFIED for fixed-rate minimum; 25 bps used illustratively]**.

**Purchase-month interest — Selling Guide C2-1.1-06 (06/28/2011) [VERIFIED via 00a-fnma §5.5].** A/A: Fannie Mae "purchases accrued interest from the last paid installment date for the mortgage up to, but not including, the purchase date" on the UPB "at the time it is submitted for purchase and the designated pass-through rate"; if the loan is paid ahead, Fannie Mae deducts accrued interest from proceeds. S/S and S/A: "from the first day of the purchase month up to, but not including, the purchase date." The day-count convention is not stated **[UNVERIFIED — 27.2 reconciles the Purchase Advice figure]**. Consequence for 30.1: the purchase-month interest is settled inside the purchase price (27.2 books the interest adjustment); no P&I is remitted for the purchase month and the first LAR carries zero interest and principal.

**Purchase Advice — Selling Guide C2-2-05, Whole Loan Purchasing Process (10/04/2023) [VERIFIED].** The Purchase Advice lists "each loan, identified by a Fannie Mae loan number (which the servicer should enter into its records immediately because it must be used in reporting all subsequent activity related to the mortgage to Fannie Mae)" and is available "on the business day Fannie Mae funds the purchase proceeds"; "The lender must submit a post-purchase adjustment request to provide notice of any error, omission, or irregularity in the transfer of funds or in processing the request for the repetitive transfer within 30 days of the date of the Purchase Advice" (27.2/29.4 own the 30-day window). Loan Delivery: "The eligible loan is submitted successfully and is assigned a Fannie Mae Loan Number" (Loan Delivery User Guide, May 2026) [VERIFIED]; "Proceeds are typically wired to the lender 24 hours after the purchase of the loan" (Loan Delivery FAQs, Sept 2025 — 00b-orig F6). C2-2-03 (05/04/2022): "any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note" (00a-fnma §5.4) — the bailee/Form 2004A mechanics are 27.2's.

**First reporting cycle — Investor Reporting Transaction Type 96 User Guide (Mar. 27, 2025) and Servicing Guide C-4.3-01 (04/08/2026) [VERIFIED].** TT96 guide: "Newly acquired A/A loans and MBS S/S loans will be established in Fannie Mae's investor reporting system the business day after they are acquired. Servicers are required to report LARs in the same month that the loans are acquired." Loans become available for reporting "the business day following settlement", and LARs referencing loans not yet in the LSDU database produce invalid transactions. C-4.3-01: non-removal transactions "by 8 p.m. eastern time on the next business day after the servicer processes the transaction" and "by 8 p.m. eastern time on the 22nd calendar day of the month for all summary reporting mortgage loans, if no payment is received" (5.1 quotes the full text); the Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026) fixes the IRED as the preceding business day when the 22nd is not a business day and the period close at 5 p.m. ET BD2 with bulk cut-off 3 p.m. ET BD2 (5.1). The IRM itself contains no "newly acquired" rule (a targeted read on 2026-09-11 found none) — the same-month rule rests on the TT96 guide **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — user guide, not Guide/IRM text; LSDU's "Missing LAR – New Acquisition" exception (5.1) corroborates]**. LAR 81: "Transaction Type 81 (Lender Loan I.D. Change Record) must be used to add, delete, or change the information Fannie Mae is carrying in its records for the servicer's unique identifier for the mortgage loan" (IRM p. 29) [VERIFIED].

**Escrow Setup event for newly acquired loans — Lender Letter LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026) [VERIFIED].** "For newly acquired loans, servicers must establish escrow balances when the loan is onboarded to the Fannie Mae Servicing Platform by reporting an Escrow Setup event for each applicable escrow item category type." "Servicers may begin reporting escrow events to the Fannie Mae Servicing Platform on July 18, 2026, but must comply with escrow reporting requirements by Dec. 1, 2026." General event clock: "Report standardized loan-level borrower and servicer events to Fannie Mae Servicing Platform the same day as the servicing events are processed in the servicer's systems, but no later than 3:00 a.m. eastern time on the next business day." "Report a 'no payment' event if a borrower payment is not received by the 22nd calendar day of the month." "The reporting period will close at 5:00 p.m. eastern time on the second business day of the month." The letter does not say when a newly purchased loan becomes visible on the Servicing Platform; the fatal rule "valid loan number / servicer association" (Reference Guide v1.0; 3.7) means a Setup event cannot be accepted before the loan exists there **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — anchor inferred; see timers]**.

**Initial custodial deposits — Servicing Guide F-1-03, Establishing and Implementing Custodial Accounts (05/13/2026) and A4-1-02 (07/12/2023) [VERIFIED].** F-1-03: "Deposit any monthly payments collected after the mortgage loan was submitted to Fannie Mae for purchase or securitization, including any payments due from an interest rate buydown plan account, into the P&I account no later than one business day after the servicer or its designee receives Fannie Mae's purchase proceeds." "Deposit the borrower's escrow account balance and any funds for an interest rate buydown plan account that are not scheduled for application toward the monthly payment into the T&I custodial account no later than one business day after the servicer or its designee receives Fannie Mae's purchase proceeds for a mortgage loan." "Fannie Mae may waive its next-day-deposit requirement when a servicer uses a computer service bureau or a third party as a collection agent if adequate controls exist." Titling (F-1-03, verbatim): T&I — "(Name of servicer), as agent and/or trustee for the benefit of Fannie Mae and payments of various mortgagors, respectively (Custodial Account)"; P&I — "(Name of servicer), as agent, trustee, and/or bailee for the benefit of Fannie Mae and/or payments of various mortgagors and/or various owners of interests in mortgage-backed securities (Custodial Account)"; subservicers title as "(Name of Subservicer) as subservicer for (Name of Master Servicer)" (6.1). SVC-2026-02 (03/11/2026): the servicer, not Fannie Mae, holds temporary buydown funds in the T&I account (00a-fnma §9).

**MERS after purchase — Selling Guide B8-7-01, Mortgage Electronic Registration Systems (MERS), Inc. (05/01/2024) [VERIFIED].** "If a seller/servicer registers a loan on the MERS system before delivering it to Fannie Mae, the seller/servicer must ensure that the MIN is registered in MERS and names itself as the investor." "After Fannie Mae purchases or securitizes the mortgage, Fannie Mae notifies MERS to update its records to reflect Fannie Mae's ownership interest in the loan." "If a seller/servicer registers a mortgage with MERS after Fannie Mae has purchased or securitized the loan, the seller/servicer must name Fannie Mae as the investor during registration and notify MERS of Fannie Mae's ownership interest in the loan." "Additionally, the seller/servicer must include the MIN in the delivery data." "For each MERS-registered loan delivered to a document custodian, the seller/servicer must indicate the MIN on the security instrument and related documents." "MERS will notify Fannie Mae about the deactivation of any loan in which it has an interest." "All actions that MERS takes with respect to a loan are based on the instructions initiated by the originating seller, Fannie Mae, or the servicer." No timing for Fannie Mae's investor update is stated. Fannie Mae's 7-digit MERS Org ID is not published in the Guide **[UNVERIFIED — loaded from the MERS member directory at onboarding into `rule_sets.mers.fnma_investor_org_id`]**. MERS System Procedures Manual Release 26.1 (effective May 17, 2026): a "Procedure: Removing an Interim Funding Org ID" exists and "For active MIN Records where the Resigning Member is named as Interim Funder, the Interim Funder must remove its Org ID from the Interim Funder field"; no post-sale timing is stated **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — public manual; the Integration Handbook is member-only]**; "The Servicer of a MERS Loan is responsible for complying with the requirements set forth in the Governing Documents. These responsibilities remain with the Servicer even if a Subservicer or Vendor is named on the Servicer's MIN Records."

**eNotes after purchase — Selling Guide C1-2-04, Delivering eMortgages to Fannie Mae (12/13/2023) [VERIFIED].** "Fannie Mae requires that the MERS eRegistry records, for loans transferred to Fannie Mae, contain the Servicer's MERS Org ID (or sub-servicer, if applicable) in the 'Master Servicer' field on the eRegistry in order to enable that party to perform certain transactions within the MERS eRegistry." "The transfer request must include an 'Effective Date' of the same day as the request. The Effective Date is used by the MERS eRegistry to denote the date that the transfer must be complete." "Fannie Mae provides in-house automated document certification (auto-certification) for eligible eNotes." Warehouse providers: "the warehouse provider must transfer Control and Location of the related eNote to Fannie Mae, on the lender's behalf" or "must approve a Transfer of Control and Location to Fannie Mae that has been initiated by the lender"; "In connection with delivery of funded eMortgages to Fannie Mae, warehouse providers must enter into an eNote Control Transfer and Custodial Agreement with Fannie Mae." A3-3-04 (05/04/2022): "For whole loans or MBS loans delivered as eNotes, Fannie Mae is the document custodian" (00b-orig F8).

**Document custodian — Selling Guide A3-3-04 (05/04/2022) [VERIFIED via 00b-orig F8]** and Loan Delivery: the certifying custodian is the seller's Form 2017 custodian (FIN in the Seller Profile); after purchase the paper note stays with that custodian on Fannie Mae's behalf; 1.4 owns `custody_records`.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. The remittance-type rule lives in **C1-3-01 (08/26/2014)**, not "C2-1.1"; C2-1.1-02 only defines the pass-through rate.
2. The MERS investor update after purchase is **Fannie Mae's** action ("Fannie Mae notifies MERS to update its records"), not the seller's; the seller's duties are to register naming itself as investor before delivery and to include the MIN in the delivery data. The platform verifies the update; it does not perform a TOB.
3. The first LAR is required **in the acquisition month** ("report LARs in the same month that the loans are acquired"), not "in the reporting period following purchase"; the loan is established in Fannie Mae's investor reporting system "the business day after" acquisition.
4. The Escrow Setup event's anchor is "when the loan is onboarded to the Fannie Mae Servicing Platform" — not literally the purchase day; the next-business-day 03:00 ET clock is the general event rule.
5. "Servicer loan number (LAR 81)" is avoidable: deliver SM's servicing loan number as the Loan Delivery Lender Loan Number (29.3) so Fannie Mae's "servicer's unique identifier" is right from purchase; LAR 81 is a correction path only.
6. The custodial deposit rule is **F-1-03/A4-1-02** (one business day after purchase proceeds), not F-1-20 (which governs remittances — 5.2).
7. The blueprint's A1-1/A2-1 citation is generic; the operative provisions are A2-1-07 (subservicing extended to "newly originated mortgage loans"; Form 101; custodial accounts) and A2-1-01 (duties).
8. The purchase-month interest is settled in the purchase price (C2-1.1-06) — there is no purchase-month remittance to schedule.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `purchase_advice.received` (baseline; from `fnma-purchase-advice` daily JSON or Connect report — 29.4/27.2) carrying `fnma_loan_number`, `purchase_date`, `settlement/funding date`, price, `interest_adjustment_cents`, `net_proceeds_cents`, remittance type, pass-through rate, servicing fee, payee code.
- `loan.purchased` (baseline; emitted by 29.4 when Loan Delivery shows *Purchased and Funded* and the Purchase Advice is matched).
- `warehouse.advance.repaid` (baseline; 27.2) → Interim Funder removal.
- `custody.certified` (baseline; 29.4/1.4) → custody record finalization.
- Inbound Fannie Mae data: LSDU trial-balance/position extract or Connect Loan Activity Summary showing the loan (`loan.fnma_established` is derived); Servicing Platform loan-position response; MERS acknowledgment/MRE snapshot; eRegistry Transfer of Control confirmation.
- Schedules: daily 07:00 ET establishment check for loans purchased the prior business day; 5.1's IRED/BD2 sweeps.

#### Data model

`loans` (baseline; columns added here): `investor` ∈ {partner_warehouse, fnma}, `fnma_loan_number char(10)`, `fnma_servicer_number char(9)` (partner), `purchase_date date`, `purchase_advice_id`, `remittance_type` ∈ {AA, SA, SS}, `pass_through_rate numeric(9,6)`, `servicing_fee_bps int`, `participation_pct numeric(9,6)` (100.000000), `servicing_option` ∈ {regular, special} (S/S only), `fnma_established_at date` (first date the loan is visible in LSDU/Servicing Platform), `investor_setup_status` ∈ {pre_purchase, purchased, established, first_lar_accepted, escrow_setup_acked, reporting_active}, `fnma_first_reporting_period char(7)` (YYYY-MM = purchase month).

`loan_terms` (baseline, effective-dated): `remittance_type`, `pass_through_rate`, `servicing_fee_bps`, `gfee_bps` (0 for whole loans) versioned effective on `purchase_date`.

`investor_loan_positions` (5.1 owns; seeded here): `fnma_lpi_date` = first payment date − 1 month (the installment considered paid because interest was collected through the day before the first accrual month — for the fixture 2026-12-01), `fnma_actual_upb_cents` = original loan amount, `remittance_type`, `fnma_ptr`, `participation_pct`, `source='purchase_advice'`, `as_of=purchase_date`.

`fnma_establishment_checks` (new): `id`, `loan_id`, `checked_at`, `source` ∈ {lsdu_position, connect_loan_activity_summary, servicing_platform_position}, `found boolean`, `fnma_lpi_date`, `fnma_upb_cents`, `fnma_remittance_type`, `fnma_ptr`, `variance jsonb`, `agent_run_id`.

`custodial_transfers` (new): `id`, `loan_id`, `kind` ∈ {ti_escrow_balance, ti_buydown_funds, pi_prepurchase_collections}, `amount_cents bigint`, `from_account_id` (`custodial_ti_prepurchase` | `custodial_pi_prepurchase`), `to_account_id` (Fannie Mae T&I or P&I custodial account of the loan's remittance type), `proceeds_received_at timestamptz`, `due_at`, `transferred_at`, `bank_reference`, `timer_id`, `ledger_entry_id`.

`mers_transactions` (1.5 owns; column added): `subtype` ∈ {interim_funder_removed, investor_verified, none}. `mers_min_snapshots` (1.5) rows with `source='mers_batch'` capturing `investor_org_id` after purchase.

`enotes` (1.4/addendum): `controller_org_id`, `location_org_id`, `master_servicer_org_id` (new column; the C1-2-04 field), `secured_party_org_id` (new; SM's Org ID while the warehouse advance is outstanding — null after purchase), `post_purchase_verified_at` (new).

`custody_records` (1.4 owns): set `certification_status='certified_transferor'`→ for originations use `certified` (enum value added), `code_type='none'`, `note_location` ∈ {custodian, fnma_evault}, `custodian_party_id` = the partner's Form 2017 custodian, `sfc_508` for eNotes.

`investor_events` (5.1): first `payment.none` (LAR 96 action 00) with `activity_period` = purchase month; `escrow.setup` per category (3.7 subtype fields). `remittance_calculations` (5.2): none for the purchase month.

Events emitted: `loan.purchased` (consumed), `loan.investor_updated`, `loan.fnma_established`, `investor.first_lar.submitted`, `investor.first_lar.accepted`, `escrow.setup_event.sent` / `investor_events.acked{type=EscrowSetup}` (3.7/1.1 names), `custodial.prepurchase_funds.transferred`, `warehouse.interim_funder.removed` (consumed — 27.1-defined, emitted by 27.2 when the MIN update is accepted; the former 30.1 alias `mers.interim_funder.removed` is retired), `mers.investor.fnma_verified`, `enote.post_purchase.verified`, `custody.record.finalized`, `loan.reporting_active`. Retention: `life_of_loan_plus_4y` (Fannie Mae accounting records); no new PII.

#### State machine

`loans.investor_setup_status`: `pre_purchase` —(`loan.purchased` + Purchase Advice matched; `boarding` writes investor fields; `custodial-recon` schedules transfers)→ `purchased` —(establishment check `found=true`, values reconcile to `investor_loan_positions`)→ `established` —(first LAR accepted by Fannie Mae in the acquisition month's period)→ `first_lar_accepted` —(all Escrow Setup events acked, or flag off / non-escrowed)→ `escrow_setup_acked` —(custodial transfers matched; MERS investor = Fannie Mae verified; Interim Funder removed; eNote fields verified; custody record finalized)→ `reporting_active` (terminal for 30.1; 5.x cycles take over). Exceptions: `establishment_lag` (loan not found on establishment day + 1 BD → Investor Reporting Representative package), `position_variance` (Fannie Mae's LPI/UPB/PTR ≠ ours → PPA/Loan Data Change package, `officer` if money), `setup_rejected` (Escrow Setup fatal reject → correct and resubmit). Transitions to `established`/`first_lar_accepted`/`escrow_setup_acked` are made only from parsed Fannie Mae responses (5.1 guardrail).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Purchase update (idempotent).** On `purchase_advice.received`, match by the Loan Delivery Lender Loan Number = `loans.servicing_loan_number` (delivered by 29.3 — rule 6); write `fnma_loan_number`, `purchase_date`, `remittance_type`, `pass_through_rate`, `servicing_fee_bps`, `investor='fnma'`; version `loan_terms` effective `purchase_date`; seed `investor_loan_positions` (LPI = first payment date − 1 month; UPB = original amount less any principal already paid). Idempotency key = SHA-256(`fnma_loan_number|purchase_date|advice_id`).
2. **Servicer number.** Every Fannie Mae submission for the loan carries the partner's `fnma_servicer_number` (the seller/servicer number on the commitment); SM's own servicer number is never used for partner loans. The subservicing identification in Fannie Mae's records is arrangement-level (open question 1).
3. **Remittance type.** Taken from `commitments.remittance_type` (29.1) and confirmed against the Purchase Advice; default `AA` (open question 3). Fannie Mae "generally will not change" it after purchase — a mismatch between commitment and advice is a `position_variance` exception, never a silent overwrite.
4. **Purchase-month economics.** No remittance for the purchase month: A/A interest from LPI up to the purchase date is settled in the price; for a loan whose delivered LPI date is *after* the purchase date (all new originations — prepaid interest was collected at closing through the end of the disbursement month), Fannie Mae deducts interest from the purchase date through the day before the LPI date from proceeds. Fixture: purchased Thu Nov 19, 2026; LPI date Dec 1, 2026; PTR 5.875% (6.125% − 0.250% illustrative) → 12 days (Nov 19–30) × $560,000 × 5.875% ÷ 360 = **$1,096.67** deducted (365-basis $1,081.64) — 27.2 books the actual Purchase Advice figure; 30.1 only asserts that `remittance_calculations` for 2026-11 is empty and that the first LAR carries zero interest and principal **[UNVERIFIED day count — 27.2]**.
5. **First LAR (acquisition month).** On `loan.fnma_established`, project `payment.none` for the purchase month: LAR 96 = lender number (partner 9-digit) | `F` | `96` | source `0` | Fannie Mae loan number | LPI `MMYY` = `1226` | UPB `0005600000{` ($560,000.00) | interest `0000000000{` | principal `0000000000{` | action `00` | action date `MMDDYY` = the establishment date (`112026`) | other fees `0000000{`. Submit by the next Fannie Mae BD 20:00 ET after establishment; hard stop 20:00 ET on the last Fannie Mae business day of the purchase month (TT96: LARs "in the same month that the loans are acquired"; the IRM's BD1 20:00 ET prior-period limit applies only when establishment itself falls on BD1). Worked dates (fixture): purchased **Thu Nov 19, 2026** → established **Fri Nov 20** (the business day after) → November IRED is also Fri Nov 20 (CD22 = Sun Nov 22 → preceding BD) → target submission **Mon Nov 23, 2026 20:00 ET** (the next Fannie Mae BD; Nov 26–27 are Fannie Mae holidays but irrelevant) → hard deadline **Mon Nov 30, 2026 20:00 ET** (last business day of November; Dec 1 = BD1 and Dec 2 = BD2 are the following period's correction/close days, not this LAR's deadline). LSDU shows "Missing LAR – New Acquisition" from CD23 (Nov 23) until the LAR is accepted — expected and cleared the same day. The **December** cycle then runs under 5.1: no payment expected (first payment due Jan 1, 2027) → `payment.none` by **Tue Dec 22, 2026 20:00 ET** (CD22 is a Tuesday; the sweep runs 18:00 ET). **January**: the first contractual payment (due Fri Jan 1, 2027 — a federal/Fannie Mae holiday; received e.g. Wed Dec 30 by ACH and processed that day) → LAR by **Thu Dec 31, 2026 20:00 ET** with LPI `0127`, UPB `0005594557A` ($559,455.71 = 560,000.00 − 544.29), interest at PTR `0000027416G` ($2,741.67 = 560,000 × 5.875% ÷ 12), principal `0000005442I` ($544.29 = 3,402.62 − 2,858.33), action `00`, action date `123026`; it belongs to the December activity period (processed by BD2 Jan 5, 2027). A/A remittance: 2,741.67 + 544.29 = $3,285.96 > $2,500 → CRS code 001 request the same day (5.2 `FNMA_F120_AA_REMIT_2500_SAMEDAY`).
6. **Servicer loan number.** `loans.servicing_loan_number` is assigned at boarding (30.2) and delivered as the Loan Delivery Lender Loan Number (29.3) so Fannie Mae's "servicer's unique identifier" equals SM's from purchase — no LAR 81. A LAR 81 (or, from Mar 8, 2027, a Loan Data Change request — 5.1) is generated only if the delivered identifier differs (a `position_variance` of kind `lender_loan_id`) and only through 5.1's channels **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the ULDD data point carrying the identifier is the MISMO `LOAN_IDENTIFIER` container; the UI label is "Lender Loan Number"]**.
7. **Escrow Setup event.** For escrowed loans with the flag on: one `escrow.setup` event per category with a nonzero balance (T&I: the initial deposit; Buy Down: unapplied buydown funds; Loss Draft/Renovation: none at origination) with `item_type='Set up'`, amount = category balance, balance = same, processed date = the event date, sequence 1. Fixture: T&I balance **$2,062.50** (30.3). Timing: the event is queued at `loan.purchased`; submission is attempted on the establishment day and must be accepted by 03:00 ET on the business day after the loan is visible on the Servicing Platform. Worked: purchased Thu Nov 19 → if the loan is visible the same day, due **Fri Nov 20, 2026 03:00 ET** (the brief's date); if visible on the establishment day Fri Nov 20, due **Mon Nov 23, 2026 03:00 ET** — the engine computes the deadline from the observed visibility date and records which applied. Before Dec 1, 2026 a rejection is a CIT/production-window matter; from Dec 1 it is a contractual breach.
8. **Custodial transfers.** During the warehouse period the escrow deposit collected at closing (fixture $2,062.50; posted to the loan's `escrow` liability at funding by 30.2) sits in `custodial_ti_prepurchase`; any P&I received between delivery and purchase sits in `custodial_pi_prepurchase`. On `proceeds.received` (27.2's bank match — typically the business day after purchase: fixture **Fri Nov 20, 2026**), book-transfer each balance to the Fannie Mae T&I custodial account (6.2) / the P&I account for the loan's remittance type (6.1) by the next servicer business day (**Mon Nov 23, 2026**). Ledger: Dr `custodial_ti_cash` (Fannie Mae T&I) / Cr `custodial_ti_prepurchase_cash` 206,250 cents; no borrower-level entry (the loan's `escrow` liability is unchanged); 6.4's Form 496A shows the deposit in November. Buydown funds (temporary buydowns; purchase fixture variants) move to the T&I account and are reported as a `Loan Buy Down` Setup event.
9. **MERS state after purchase.** Expected MIN record: Servicer = partner Org ID; Subservicer = SM Org ID (set at MOM registration, 26.4); Investor = Fannie Mae Org ID (updated by Fannie Mae); Interim Funder = empty (removed by SM after repayment). SM submits `MIN Update` removing its Interim Funder Org ID in the batch of the day after `warehouse.advance.repaid` (fixture: repaid Fri Nov 20 → batch Mon Nov 23). The investor field is verified from the MERS batch acknowledgment/MRE snapshot; if Fannie Mae's update is not reflected within 10 servicer business days (fixture: **Fri Dec 4, 2026** — Nov 20, 23, 24, 25, 27, 30, Dec 1, 2, 3, 4), a query package goes to the partner for Fannie Mae's MERS Program Office; 1.5's `W-016` remains open until verified. SM never submits a TOB.
10. **eNote state after purchase.** After 29.4's Transfer of Control and Location (same-day effective) and the warehouse approval, the eRegistry must show Controller = Fannie Mae, Location = Fannie Mae's eVault, Master Servicer = SM's Org ID (subservicer per C1-2-04; open question 4), Secured Party cleared; `enotes.post_purchase_verified_at` set from the eRegistry inquiry within 1 servicer BD; SFC 508 in `custody_records`.
11. **Custody record.** Paper note: `note_location='custodian'`, `custodian_party_id` = the partner's Form 2017 custodian that certified (Loan Delivery certification status *Certified*/*Qualified Cert*), `certification_status='certified'`, `code_type='none'`; eNote: `note_location='fnma_evault'`. 1.4's recert timers do not apply (no transfer); Form 2009 releases later in life follow 1.4.
12. **Establishment reconciliation.** `fnma_establishment_checks` compares Fannie Mae's LPI, UPB, remittance type and PTR to `investor_loan_positions`; UPB variance > ±$0.05 or LPI ≠ ours → `position_variance` (money field → `officer`), resolved through a PPA/Loan Data Change package before the first LAR (5.1 reject loop).
13. **Interest-rate/PTR consistency.** `servicing_fee_bps = note_rate − pass_through_rate` (both from the advice); if the servicing fee < the commitment's fee (buy-up/buy-down), 29.1's records govern and 27.2 books the price difference; 30.1 only asserts `note_rate − ptr − gfee(0) = servicing_fee`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`investor-reporting` agent (tools: `matchPurchaseAdvice`, `checkFnmaEstablishment`, `seedInvestorPosition`, `projectEvent`, `submitBatch`, `pollFeedback`, `createCorrection`, `openPortalTask`, `recordDecision`) owns rules 5, 7, 12. `boarding` agent (tools: `applyPurchaseUpdate`, `versionLoanTerms`, `finalizeCustodyRecord`, `recordDecision`) owns rules 1–3, 6, 11. `custodial-recon` agent (tools: `scheduleCustodialTransfer`, `executeBookTransfer`, `matchBankFeed`) owns rule 8. `post-closing` agent (tools: `buildMersBatch`, `submitMersBatch`, `snapshotMins`, `verifyERegistry`) owns rules 9–10. Decision record: `{loan_id, fnma_loan_number, purchase_date, established_at, remittance_type, ptr, first_lar{event_id, submitted_at, accepted_at}, escrow_setup{category, amount, deadline_basis, accepted_at}, custodial_transfers[], mers{interim_funder_removed_at, investor_verified_at}, enote{verified_at}, variances[], rule_set_version, model_version}`. Guardrails: no agent may mark an event accepted without a parsed Fannie Mae response; money-field variances (UPB, LPI, PTR, escrow balance) are never "corrected" to Fannie Mae's values without evidence — a PPA package and `officer` approval are required; the Interim Funder removal is submitted only after `warehouse.advance.repaid` is bank-matched; no TOB is ever submitted. AI-off path: the same pipeline runs deterministically; exceptions are worked from the ops console with the same decision schema.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Purchase after CD22 but before month-end (the fixture):** the first LAR still belongs to the purchase month; submit on the next BD after establishment; the December CD22 rule applies to the following period.
- **Purchase on BD1/BD2 of a month** (e.g., Tue Dec 1, 2026): the loan is established Wed Dec 2 (BD2); the first LAR is due within December — target Thu Dec 3 20:00 ET, the December IRED (Tue Dec 22) applies to the `payment.none` projection, and the hard stop is Thu Dec 31, 2026 20:00 ET (last Fannie Mae business day of December); the engine treats the earlier applicable date as the target. **Purchase on the last business day of a month** (e.g., Mon Nov 30, 2026): established Tue Dec 1 (BD1) — the LAR for the November activity period is due that day by 20:00 ET (IRM 2-01 prior-period limit).
- **Loan not established on the expected day** (Loan Delivery *Purchased and Funded* but no LSDU record): hold the LAR (it would be Invalid), escalate at +2 BD with the Purchase Advice as evidence; the hard deadline still runs.
- **Purchase Advice remittance type ≠ commitment**: `position_variance`; do not report until resolved (Fannie Mae "generally will not change" the type — the commitment record is the evidence).
- **Escrow-events flag off or purchase before the cut-over**: no Setup event; when the flag turns on, 3.7's cut-over job sends Setup events for all escrowed loans (Nov 2, 2026 default).
- **Non-escrowed loan**: no Setup event; `escrow_setup_acked` is reached trivially.
- **Temporary buydown**: buydown funds move to the T&I account and produce a `Loan Buy Down` Setup event; the buydown subsidy is applied with each payment (5.2/C-3-01) — no Fannie Mae holding of funds (SVC-2026-02).
- **Payment received between delivery and purchase** (e.g., an early first payment on a loan with a short first period): posts to the loan, sits in `custodial_pi_prepurchase`, moves to the Fannie Mae P&I account within 1 BD after proceeds, and is reported in the acquisition-month LAR with the LPI advanced (interest at PTR); Fannie Mae's purchase-interest adjustment then runs from the advanced LPI (27.2 reconciles).
- **Warehouse advance repaid but Interim Funder removal rejected** (MIN status or data mismatch): 1.5 QA finding; resubmit after reconciliation.
- **Fannie Mae's MERS investor update never appears**: keep `W-016`; do not self-correct; partner query; no effect on servicing.
- **eNote Transfer of Control pending at purchase** (warehouse approval late): 29.4 exception; 30.1's verification timer breaches and blocks eNote-dependent commands until resolved.
- **Purchase Advice adjustment (30-day window)**: 27.2; 30.1 re-versions `loan_terms` only if PTR/servicing fee change.
- **Repurchase before the first LAR** (defect found at certification): 29.4/5.6; `investor_setup_status` → `pre_purchase` with reason; custodial transfers reversed.
- **Bankruptcy/SCRA/disaster overlays at purchase**: none expected on a new origination; if a SCRA notice arrives before purchase, 13.9 applies and the delivery is reviewed (29.4).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **How newly originated loans are identified as subserviced in Fannie Mae's records** (A2-1-07) — arrangement-level registration under the partner's servicer number vs a per-loan notification. Default: arrangement-level; confirm in writing with Fannie Mae before the first delivery; if per-loan, add a `human_portal_task` at purchase.
2. **Pre-purchase custodial accounts** — hold the closing escrow deposit in a partner-titled trust account (default: SM-established `custodial_ti_prepurchase` titled for the partner and borrowers, state-law compliant) vs deposit directly into the Fannie Mae T&I account at funding (rejected: non-Fannie funds cannot sit in the Fannie Mae custodial account — A4-1-02 commingling rule).
3. **Default remittance type** — A/A (default: minimizes servicer advances; aligns with the future LL-2026-05 A/A auto-draft) vs S/A (partner preference for cash-flow timing); set per partner in `commitments` defaults (29.1).
4. **eRegistry "Master Servicer" field** — SM's Org ID (default; C1-2-04 "or sub-servicer, if applicable"; matches 1.4-Q2) vs the partner's Org ID with SM as Delegatee; align with the MERS Subservicer designation (1.5-Q3).
5. **Escrow Setup deadline basis** — treat the purchase day as the processing day (Nov 20 03:00 ET target) even when the loan is not yet visible (retry loop) — default yes as the *target*, with the visibility-based date as the recorded deadline; confirm with Fannie Mae during the escrow CIT.
