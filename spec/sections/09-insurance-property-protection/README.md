# Section 9 — Insurance & Property Protection

## Overview

Section 9 is the collateral-protection layer of the platform: it makes sure every property securing a subserviced Fannie Mae loan carries the hazard (and, where required, flood, wind and project) insurance the loan contract and the Servicing Guide require; that when coverage fails, replacement coverage is obtained only on the strict notice-and-refund choreography of Regulation X §1024.37 (hazard) and the Flood Disaster Protection Act §102(e) (flood); that when a loss occurs the insurance proceeds are captured, held in trust and released against verified repairs under Servicing Guide B-5-01/B-5-02; and that when a borrower stops paying, the property is inspected on Fannie Mae's D2-2-10 clock and, if vacant, secured and preserved within the allowables of the Property Preservation Matrix and Reference Guide (June 2025) and F-1-05 — increasingly by Fannie Mae's own vendors through the Property 360 Pre-Foreclosure Property Inspection & Preservation Program (PFPIP).

Governing sources verified today (2026-09-09): 12 CFR 1024.37 and Supplement I (eCFR current as of Sept. 3/Sept. 2, 2026), 12 CFR 1024.17(k)(5) (owned by Section 3.7), RESPA §6(k)–(m) (12 U.S.C. 2605), 42 U.S.C. 4012a and the OCC's 12 CFR Part 22 as the model interagency flood rule, 44 CFR Part 61 (NFIP limits, deductibles, waiting periods), and the Fannie Mae Servicing Guide edition of **August 12, 2026** — Chapter B-2 as reorganized by SVC-2026-05 (B-2-01 all property types, B-2-02 1–4 unit, B-2-03 project master policies, B-2-04 individual units), **B-3-01** flood (02/14/2024), **B-6-01** lender-placed insurance (10/14/2015), **B-5-01** insured losses (07/12/2023), **B-5-02** uninsured losses (09/09/2020), **D2-2-10** property inspections (05/10/2023), **E-3.2-12** preservation during foreclosure (07/14/2021), **E-3.3-03** pre-sale inspection (04/10/2019), **D1-3-01** disasters (04/08/2026), **F-1-05** expense reimbursement (06/11/2025), plus Selling Guide B7-3-01…B7-3-08 (the Servicing Guide now defers coverage, deductible, perils, rating and mortgagee-clause rules to the Selling Guide; B7-3-02/-03/-04/-07 were updated 08/05/2026 by SEL-2026-07). Lender Letter **LL-2026-03** (Mar. 18, 2026) rewrote coverage sufficiency ("replacement cost basis" is deemed sufficient; the 100%-RCV/80%-insurable-value arithmetic is retired; roofs may be ACV), set project deductibles ($50,000 per-unit max; unit-owner policies at the greater of 5% or $2,500), and imposed new servicer monitoring, borrower-notification, documented-procedure, annual-reminder and LPI duties that are **mandatory January 1, 2027**.

Architecture fit: the `insurance-property` agent (architecture baseline §8) owns every process here, with `escrow` (premium disbursement, (k)(5) gate, escrow events — Section 3.7), `cashiering` (claim-check deposit), `default-collections` (delinquency states, QRPC facts that switch inspections off), `foreclosure-ops` (pre-sale inspection, disaster referral approval), `claims-reo` (REO handoff, E-4.3), `investor-reporting` (loss-draft escrow events, CRS code 332 remittances), `borrower-comms` (all borrower contact with automation disclosure) and `compliance-sentinel` (timers). Core services: the **Insurance Tracking Engine** (policy records, EOI intake with AI document extraction, adequacy rules, expiration/cancellation monitoring), the **Force-Placement State Machine** (one machine with two tracks — Reg X hazard and FDPA flood — and one refund calculator), the **Flood Determination Lifecycle**, the **Loss-Draft Workflow**, the **Inspection Scheduler**, the **Preservation Work-Order Engine** with the allowable-cost matrix, and the Timer Engine, Notice Registry, ledger and `integrations/*` adapters (`insurance-tracking/lpi`, `flood`, `preservation`, `fnma-p360`, `print-mail`, `e-delivery`).

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: the partner (master servicer of record) is the mortgagee named on borrower policies ("[Partner], its successors and/or assigns, c/o Supermortgage" per Selling Guide B7-3-08; never MERS), is the named insured under the LPI program written by an unaffiliated carrier (B-6-01), and is the servicer of record in Property 360, HomeTracker and the Expense Reimbursement claim (partner's 9-digit servicer number). Supermortgage performs everything: tracking, notices, placement and refunds, claims, inspections, preservation and claim preparation. Human touchpoints are limited to (i) physical endorsement of loss-draft instruments under a limited power of attorney from the partner (`signing_officer`), (ii) Fannie Mae portal-only steps — Property 360 PFPIP submission/reconciliation where the servicer-direct APIs are not available to Supermortgage, HomeTracker over-allowable bids, Form 176 e-mail packages, Expense Reimbursement uploads (`fnma_portal_operator`), (iii) `officer` sign-off for B-2-03 indemnification elections and Fannie Mae LPI documentation requests, (iv) `attorney` for Form 20 litigation, entry during bankruptcy stays, and state entry-law questions, and (v) `human_agent` whenever a borrower asks.

Dependencies: 1.1/1.6 (boarding policy, flood and claim data; warnings W-005/W-007), 3.7 (premium disbursement, the `REGX_1024_17K5_LPI_PURCHASE_GATE`, escrow events incl. the `loss_draft` category), 3.2 (LPI premiums change the escrow analysis), 5.x (Fannie Mae events and CRS 332 remittance of proceeds), 6.2/6.4 (T&I custodial account holding loss drafts), 7.1 (LPI charges on the periodic statement), 10.x (MI premiums share the escrow lines; MI claims need hazard/inspection evidence), 11.x (delinquency and QRPC facts), 12.x (workout evaluation on delinquent loss drafts; disaster deferral/forbearance), 13.x (foreclosure referral, disaster approval gate, HOA super-lien monitoring, bid instructions on damaged property), 14.x (bankruptcy stay overlay on inspections/entry), 15.2 (expense reimbursement of LPI premiums, inspections, preservation — 60-day filing rule), 16.x (payoff cut-offs; proceeds applied to UPB), 17.x (transfer-out of open FPI cycles, claims and program loans), 19.x (retention, AI governance inventory for the extraction models).

Changes in flight: LL-2026-03 items mandatory **2027-01-01** (feature flag `insurance.ll2026_03=on` from day one — the new rules are simpler and Fannie Mae encourages early adoption); LL-2026-05 escrow events (Dec. 1, 2026) cover loss-draft deposits/disbursements; LL-2026-01 disaster foreclosure approval (May 1, 2026); Property 360 release notes 05/01/2026; no CFPB change to §1024.37 since 2013 (00a §1.3). Blueprint corrections found: the blueprint cites "FNMA B-2" for LPI — the LPI topic is **B-6-01**, and flood is **B-3-01** (not part of B-2); "Reg X 1024.37(c)" first notice is correct but the row omits the escrowed-borrower prohibition in 1024.17(k)(5) and the flood carve-out in 1024.37(a)(2)(i); D2-2-10's "continue monthly" is actually "every 20–35 days" with occupancy-based exceptions and a 35-day pre-sale inspection; the Property Preservation Matrix imposes a **14-calendar-day** initial-securing deadline (not "per matrix" open-ended); and inspection/preservation cost is largely shifted to Fannie Mae for PFPIP-enrolled loans.
