# Section 12 — Loss Mitigation

## Overview

Section 12 is the judgment core of the platform: it takes a delinquent (or imminently defaulting) borrower from a request for help to a Fannie Mae workout or a liquidation, under two overlapping rulebooks that must be satisfied simultaneously — **Regulation X §1024.41** (the 2013/2016 "complete application" framework, as amended through the May 16, 2025 COVID rescission IFR, 90 FR 20791, eCFR current as of Sept. 8, 2026) and the **Fannie Mae Servicing Guide** (Aug. 12, 2026 edition: D2-2-04/05/07, D2-3.1-01, D2-3.2-01/02/04/05/06, D2-3.3-01/02, D2-3.4, F-1-21/22/24/27, F-2-02/06/10, LL-2026-01). Reg X gives the borrower procedural rights (acknowledgment, evaluation, denial reasons, appeal, dual-tracking protection) that are privately enforceable under RESPA §6(f); the Guide dictates *which* options exist, their eligibility and terms, and how each is decisioned and reported in **SMDU** with rep-and-warrant relief. State overlays (California HBOR, New York 3 NYCRR Part 419, Colorado AI Act, mediation states) are modelled as `jurisdiction_rules`, never as forks of the engine.

**Architecture fit.** The `lossmit-underwriter` agent owns every process here end-to-end: intake classification (with the Section 4 Intake Router), application assembly and document extraction, completeness determinations and acknowledgments, evaluation through the Fannie Mae hierarchy (Exhibit F-2-10) via the `fnma-smdu` adapter, option calculators, notices, trial-period management, liquidation paths and reporting. Humans appear only at the legally-required touchpoints in architecture baseline §8: **`lossmit_reviewer`** approves every denial/ineligibility determination before the notice issues (Colorado AI Act, LL-2026-04, ECOA/Reg B where applicable) and reviews appeals with personnel different from the original evaluator (§1024.41(h)(3)); `signing_officer` executes Form 3179 and deeds; `fnma_portal_operator` performs SMDU-UI-only steps from an AI-prepared package (Consolidated Technology Guide bans automation against Fannie Mae UIs); `attorney` handles foreclosure counsel instructions; `licensed_specialist` where a state treats negotiation of modification terms as licensed activity; `human_agent` on request. Core services used: Timer Engine (every deadline below is a `timer_definition`), Notice Registry (every acknowledgment, offer, denial, appeal decision and agreement is a versioned template with a required-content checklist), ledger (capitalization, deferral and forbearance postings to `deferred_principal`/`forborne_principal`), `cases` (`lossmit`, `appeal`, `forbearance`, `deferral`, `modification`, `shortsale`, `dil`), `agent_decisions`, `rule_sets`, `jurisdiction_rules`, integrations (`fnma-smdu`, `fnma-servicing-events`/`fnma-lsdu`, `erecording`, `custodian`, `print-mail`, `e-delivery`, `telephony/voice`).

**Rule-set versioning (mandatory design).** Two Reg X rule sets exist from day one: **`regx.lossmit.2013`** (production: current §§1024.31, .38(b)(2), .41 with (c)(2)(vi) and (f)(3) removed) and **`regx.lossmit.2024nprm`** (behind flag `lossmit.rule_set=2024nprm`, default off) implementing the July 24, 2024 proposal's "request for loss mitigation assistance → review cycle → two procedural safeguards" logic (research/00a §1.2). The Fannie Mae bundle is **`fnma.workout.2026-08`** (Guide Aug. 12, 2026 + LL-2026-01 + the Fannie Mae Modification Interest Rate exhibit as a dated input, 6.625% effective Aug. 14, 2026; 6.750% effective Sept. 15, 2026). The state machine is built so the *trigger* of protections (complete application vs. request for assistance) and the *foreclosure safeguards* ((f)/(g) vs. NPRM (f)(1)–(3)) are strategy objects selected by rule set — no re-architecture on switch-over.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** Supermortgage performs everything: intake, evaluation, SMDU case submission under the partner's 9-digit servicer number (Related-Party/TSP credentials, Form 101), notices, agreements, reporting and incentive-fee claims. The partner (master servicer of record) is liable to Fannie Mae for all of it (A2-1-07), signs the AI-governance program and the written policy elections the Guide requires (uniform modification cut-off date, processing-month policy, delegated-authority elections), and is named in every notice as the servicer where required. Fannie Mae retains investor-level approvals for non-delegated cases (F-1-24), forbearance exceptions (Forbearance Exception Request Template), disaster foreclosure referrals (LL-2026-01) and current-borrower modification declines (Form 182).

**Dependencies.** 4.1 (Intake Router, NoE for §1024.35(b)(7)/(9)/(10) errors), 4.3 (§1024.40 continuity of contact, CA §2923.7 SPOC), 4.4 (successors in interest — comment 41(b)-1), 11.1–11.3 (early intervention, QRPC — the entry point for most workouts), 11.5 (imminent default, D2-1-01), 2.x (trial-payment cashiering, C-1.1-02 funds during pending modification), 3.x (escrow analysis before any trial plan or deferral, 60-month shortage spread, B-1-01), 5.x (LAR/loan-data-change after deferral or modification; delinquency status codes 09/12/BF/28/17/15/44/H5 per F-1-21), 8.x (credit reporting of plans), 10.x (MI delegations, F-2-06), 13.1/13.2 (the foreclosure gates this section feeds), 13.4 (pre-referral review), 1.7/17.4 (§1024.41(k) transfers), 19.x (retention).

**Changes in flight.** RIN 3170-AB04 remains unfinalized (00a §1.1) — build to the current rule, keep the NPRM flag. Forbearance and repayment-plan case management moves into SMDU in **Q1 2027** per Fannie Mae's Servicing Platform FAQ (Q64) — the delinquency-status-code path (F-1-21) stays until then behind flag `smdu.plan_cases`. LL-2026-04 (AI/ML governance, effective Aug. 6, 2026) and the Colorado AI Act (effective June 30, 2026 per SB25B-004) make the reviewer/appeal design and decision records contractual and statutory requirements, not options. Fannie Mae's Modification Interest Rate changes roughly monthly and is a versioned input. **Day-counting conventions used throughout.** §1024.41's 5-day windows ((b)(2)(i)(B), (c)(3)(i), (k)(2)(i)) are "days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays)" → Timer Engine unit `business_days_federal` (the unit introduced in 4.1). Its 7/14/30/37/90/120-day periods are calendar days (§1024.31 "day" = calendar day). Fannie Mae "business days" (D2-2-05 5-business-day acknowledgment; D2-3.3-01 offer acknowledgment) → `business_days_servicer`. Where both apply, both timers run and the earlier due date governs. Delinquency: Reg X gates ((f)(1) 120 days, comment 41(b)(2)(ii)-2 milestones) use `regx_days_delinquent`; Fannie Mae eligibility windows ("2–6 months delinquent", "≥60 days", "day 90–105") use `fnma_delinquency_status` months/days (MBA method, next-due-date based). Every rule below states which counter it uses.
