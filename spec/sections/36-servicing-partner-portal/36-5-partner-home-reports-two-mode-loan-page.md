### Blueprint row
Three landings tie the portal together. Home is the partner's morning: the last tape as-of and when the next is due, a late badge, the three bucket counts, how many members are in flight, and a link to the latest daily report — or, before the first tape, "Upload a tape to open the book." Reports is the examiner's daily report per partner that 34.3 already produces, listed for this partner only and exportable by the roles 36.1 names. The loan page is one page with two modes on the same row: a monitored loan reads "Monitored — {partner} remains servicer" with its facts as of the latest tape, its review history with the analyst's rationale rendered exactly as `/ops` renders it, its readiness gaps and its offers, and no Pay, Escrow or Draft control anywhere; a loan in refinance reads "In refinance — origination in progress" with its pipeline stage; the boarded new loan reads "Active — Supermortgage subservicing" with a Serviced tab that is visible, disabled and answers 36.6's one code in V1. Every number on the three pages is a stored row's field or a count of rows; nothing is computed about a loan here.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-16)
**Home is counts of rows the owners already keep (33.1; 34.3 rules 3 and 6; 36.3 rule 4; 36.4 rule 5).** The book line is 33.1's status (`partnerBookStatus`: the last as-of date, the next expected date on `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_TAPE_EXPECTED_7`, its late state, the hold count) and 34.3's counts for the tenant (`loans{status=monitored}`, on hold); the eligibility line is 36.3's counts for the board's `as_of_date`; the pipeline line is the count of 36.4's items in a non-terminal stage and of those `boarded` in the month; the report link is the newest `partner_book_daily_reports` row of the tenant. None of it is stored again and none of it is estimated. **[VERIFIED against src/runtime/partner-book.ts partnerBookStatus, src/runtime/book-ops/loans.ts bookLoans counts and src/runtime/book-ops/report.ts latestDailyReport / listDailyReports.]**

**The daily report is 34.3's row, read for one tenant (34.3 rule 6; 33.2 and 33.3 Outputs; GLBA §1016.13).** `partner_book_daily_reports` holds one row per partner per day — reviewed, candidates, watching, not now, excluded, offers delivered, expired, the analyst's turns and skips, the fair-lending extract id, readiness checked / ready / not ready by item, applications opened, DU runs reached, the book summary with `last_as_of_date` and `next_expected` — produced by the sweep after 33.3's pass (and on demand by staff), the newest row of a day winning. The partner reads that row by `as_of` and lists the tenant's rows, with `partner_party_id` from the session; the same ids and the same counts 34.3 shows staff, and no other partner's row exists on this door. The hashed document export (a `documents` row) stays `compliance`'s on `/ops` (34.3-T5); the partner's export is the stored row answered as a file. **[VERIFIED against src/runtime/book-ops/report.ts bookDailyReport, latestDailyReport, listDailyReports and exportDailyReport, and 34.3-T5.]**

**The loan page is 34.3's, masked partner-grade, with the banner as the row's state (34.3 rule 4; 33.1; 30.2; 35.10; 36.1).** 34.3's `book.loan` already answers facts by as-of date, terms history, reviews by day with the analyst's rationale, readiness by day, offers with how they were delivered and when they expire, the resolutions and the clocks; this page takes that answer for the tenant's loan, drops what the partner grade does not show (raw facts and raw columns, invitations, contact beyond the masked forms), and adds the three things the brief names: `banner`, `bucket` and `pipeline_stage` — all computed from `loans.status`, the refinance application's state (33.3, 21.6) and 36.3's and 36.4's projections, and stored nowhere. `loans.status` is `monitored` from 33.1's import, `paid_off` or `transferred_out` from the partner's tape, `book.resolve` or 35.10's closeout, and `active` for the loan 30.2 boarded from the refinance application (`origination_application_id` set). **[VERIFIED against src/runtime/book-ops/loan.ts BookLoan and bookLoan, db/migrations (the `loan_status` enum with `monitored`, `paid_off`, `transferred_out`, `active`; `loans.origination_application_id`), 35.10's state machine and 30.2's.]**

**The rationale is rendered, never re-written (33.2 rule 4; 33.2-T4; `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES`, `PROVENANCE`).** The analyst's rationale on a review row carries its figures only as `{{facts.<key>}}` tokens and the surface resolves them from that review's own `facts`; when the turn was skipped the engine's explanation text stands in. The partner page renders the stored rationale the way `/ops` does and never runs a turn: no model call from the portal, no new rationale, no figure the analyst did not tokenize. **[VERIFIED against src/runtime/partner-book-analyst.ts (review_facts tokens, validateAnalystWrite, the provenance guard) and src/runtime/book-ops/loan.ts LoanReview.analyst.]**

**No servicing control on any row (33.1 `LOAN_MONITORED`; 36.6).** Sections 2–19 refuse a monitored loan and nothing of theirs is armed on one; the page shows no Pay, Escrow, Draft, Statement, ACH or Payoff-request control on a monitored row, and none on the `active` row either in V1 — the serviced pane is 36.6's contract and answers its one code. **[VERIFIED — 33.1-T9 and the brief's rules 8 and 9.]**

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) The brief's Home shape carries no date; 36.5-T1 compares Home with 36.3's counts and 36.4's in-flight "for the same `as_of`", so Home carries `as_of_date` (36.3's board date) beside the counts. (2) The brief's `serviced` field allows `null`; this page answers `{ available: false, code: "SERVICED_PANE_NOT_BUILT" }` for a monitored row and for an `active` row in V1 (36.5-T3, 36.5-T4) and `null` only for a `paid_off` / `transferred_out` row, which will never have a pane. (3) The brief lists `in_refinance` among the row's `bucket` values while 36.3 keeps a member in motion on Eligible now (36.3 rule 1 and its open question 2, which hands `in_refinance` and `serviced` to this page): here the page answers `bucket = in_refinance` for a monitored loan whose refinance application is open, and the board answers `eligible_now` for the same loan — the board's `bucket` names the list the loan is on, the page's names the row's state (rule 5; open question 1). (4) The brief's three banner sentences have none for the retired prior loan: DELTA-03 — `banner` null with `status` and `refinanced_by_loan_id` shown until accepted (rule 4). (5) 34.3's loan page offers `ops_analyst` the resolve control on a held loan; this page shows the hold and offers nothing (36.2; `book.resolve` stays on `/ops`).

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `GET /v1/partner/home` → `{ as_of_date, partner: { legal_name, nmlsr_id }, book: { loans_monitored, on_hold, last_as_of, next_tape_due, late }, eligibility: { eligible_now, likely_soon, not_near }, pipeline: { in_flight, boarded_mtd }, latest_report_id }` (rule 1). All three roles.
- `GET /v1/partner/reports/daily?as_of=` → 34.3's daily report row for the tenant and that day (`id`, `partner_party_id`, `partner_legal_name`, `as_of_date`, `review`, `readiness`, `book`, `produced_by`, `document_id`, `created_at`, `decision_id` — the shape `book.daily_report` returns); without `as_of`, the tenant's rows newest first (34.3's list for the partner). `partner_admin` and `partner_auditor`; a session holding `partner_ops` only answers `403 ROLE_REQUIRED{role: partner_auditor, act_as: []}` before any read (36.1 rule 3).
- `GET /v1/partner/reports/daily/export?as_of=` → the same stored row answered as a file (JSON; a CSV of its counts beside it) — a read, no `documents` row (rule 3). `partner_admin` and `partner_auditor`.
- `GET /v1/partner/loans/:id` → `PartnerLoanDetail` = `{ banner, bucket, pipeline_stage, loan: PartnerLoanRow, facts_history: [{ as_of_date, change }], reviews: [{ as_of_date, verdict, reasons, watch_rate_pct, analyst_rationale_tokens }], readiness: { ready, missing, items[] }, offers: [{ status, delivered_at, expires_at }], serviced }` (rules 4–8). All three roles; the full servicer loan number for `partner_admin` and `partner_ops`, the last four for `partner_auditor`. Another tenant's loan or an unknown id → `404 NOT_FOUND` (36.1 rule 4). `GET /v1/partner/loans/:id/serviced` is 36.6's.
- The rows: 34.3's `book.loan` with the tenant filter; 33.1's `partnerBookStatus` for the book line; 34.3's `book.loans` counts and 36.3's bucket projection for the eligibility line and the row's `bucket`; 36.4's projection for `in_flight`, `boarded_mtd` and `pipeline_stage`; 34.3's report reads for the tenant.
- Events: none of its own; 36.1's `partner_actions` row per request with `partner_portal.viewed`, `view = home`, `report`, `report.export` or `loan` (`subject_id` = the report or loan id), no homeowner field.

#### Data model
New tables: none — `banner`, `bucket` and `pipeline_stage` are computed on every read and stored nowhere (brief §4.6 and §10); the report row is 34.3's; every other row is 33.1's, 33.2's, 33.3's, 20.1's, 30.2's or 35.10's.
- Baseline tables read: `partner_book_daily_reports` (34.3: the newest row per tenant-day), `partner_book_imports` and `timers` (33.1's status line and `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_TAPE_EXPECTED_7`), `partner_book_facts` (facts by as-of date, for `facts_history` and the row's figures), `loan_terms`, `partner_book_reviews` (`verdict`, `reasons`, `facts.watch_rate_pct`, `analyst.rationale`, `analyst.skipped`), `readiness_checks` (`ready`, `missing`, `items`), the `refi_opportunities` entity and 20.2's `marketing_touches` (the offers: `status`, the touch's `sent_at`, `offer_valid_until` — through 34.3's row), `loans` (`status`, `servicer_loan_number`, `partner_party_id`, `refinanced_by_loan_id`, `origination_application_id`), `applications` (`prior_loan_id`, `disposition`, `loan_id`), `properties` (the state), `parties` (the tenant's legal name and NMLSR id; the homeowner's name for the mask), `borrowers`, `loan_borrowers`, `partner_sessions` and `partner_users` (36.1).
- Baseline tables written: `partner_actions` (36.1; one row per request, no PII). Nothing else — no report row, no document, no loan row.

#### State machine
None of its own — a projection. The loan page's modes follow `loans.status` and the refinance application: `monitored` (banner Monitored) —(33.3 `refi.open`: an application with `prior_loan_id` opens)→ `monitored` in refinance (banner In refinance, `bucket = in_refinance`, `pipeline_stage` set) —(21.6 terminal disposition other than `originated`)→ `monitored` (banner Monitored; the board under 33.2's cooldown) | —(35.10 `refinance.prior_loan.retired`, `partner_book.loan.paid_off`; the partner's tape; `book.resolve`)→ `paid_off` / `transferred_out` (banner per DELTA-03, `bucket` null, `serviced` null); the new loan —(30.2 `loan.boarded`, `loan.active`)→ `active` (banner Active, `bucket = serviced`, `serviced` = 36.6's refusal object in V1). Every transition is 33.1's, 33.3's, 21.6's, 30.2's or 35.10's; none is performed here.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Home is counts of the tenant's rows and nothing more.** `partner` is the tenant's `parties` row (`legal_name`, `nmlsr_id`); `book` is 33.1's status for the tenant — `last_as_of` (the latest import's as-of date), `next_tape_due` (33.1's `next_expected`: the as-of date plus 7 calendar days, the due date of `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_TAPE_EXPECTED_7`), `late` (that clock breached) — with 34.3's `loans_monitored` and `on_hold` counts; `eligibility` is 36.3's `counts` (`eligible_now`, `likely_soon`, `not_near`) for the board's `as_of_date`, carried on the answer; `pipeline.in_flight` is the count of 36.4's items whose current stage is neither `boarded` nor terminal, and `boarded_mtd` the count of items `boarded` with `entered_at` in the calendar month of the read (America/New_York); `latest_report_id` is the newest `partner_book_daily_reports` row of the tenant (null before the first). Home never counts another tenant's row and never computes a figure (36.5-T1: Home's eligibility counts equal 36.3's and `in_flight` equals 36.4's for the same `as_of_date`). Before the first import every count is 0, the dates are null and the page says "Upload a tape to open the book."
2. **The daily report is 34.3's row for the tenant.** `GET …/reports/daily?as_of=` answers the newest `partner_book_daily_reports` row for `partner_party_id = session.partner_party_id` and that day — the same id, the same `review`, `readiness` and `book` counts, the same `fair_lending_extract_id` and `decision_id` 34.3 shows staff (36.5-T2); the list without `as_of` is the tenant's rows newest first and no other partner's row is listed or reachable (`404 NOT_FOUND` for a day the tenant has no row, the same answer another tenant's day gets). The partner never produces a row: production is 34.3's sweep after 33.3's pass (and staff's on demand); a day the sweep has not reached answers `404 NOT_FOUND`.
3. **The export is a read.** `GET …/reports/daily/export?as_of=` answers the stored row as a file — the row's JSON, and a CSV of its counts — under `partner_admin` or `partner_auditor` (36.1 rule 2: the auditor exports; the admin acts on everything the tenant may); it writes no `documents` row and no newer report row (34.3's hashed export stays `compliance`'s on `/ops`, and a partner who needs the hashed document asks staff); the log row reads `view = report.export` with the report id.
4. **The banner is the row's state, one of three sentences.** `banner` is exactly one of `Monitored — {partner_legal_name} remains servicer` (`loans.status = monitored` and no open refinance application; `{partner_legal_name}` is the tenant's `parties.legal_name`, never a Supermortgage name — for the demo tenant "Monitored — Northlight Mortgage Servicing (FAKE partner) remains servicer"), `In refinance — origination in progress` (`loans.status = monitored` and a refinance application with `prior_loan_id` = the loan whose `disposition` is not terminal — from 33.3's `partner_book.refinance.opened` until 21.6's terminal disposition or 30.2's board), or `Active — Supermortgage subservicing` (`loans.status = active` and `origination_application_id` set: the new loan 30.2 boarded from the refinance application). A `paid_off` or `transferred_out` row has none of the three: until DELTA-03 is accepted its `banner` is null and the page shows `loans.status` and, when set, `refinanced_by_loan_id` as the link to the new loan's page (36.4-T4's "paid off / refinanced"). The sentence is computed from those fields on every read and stored nowhere.
5. **`bucket` and `pipeline_stage` on the page.** `bucket` is `serviced` for an `active` row (never on the board, 36.3 rule 4); `in_refinance` for a monitored row whose refinance application is open (the state 36.3's open question 2 hands to this page — on the board the same loan is listed on Eligible now with `bucket = eligible_now` and `pipeline_stage` set, because its review reads `candidate` while the offer is continued, 33.2 rule 5; the board's value names the list, the page's names the state); otherwise 36.3 rule 1's value for a monitored row (`eligible_now`, `likely_soon`, `not_near`, computed by the same pure function with the same precedence — a held loan reads its verdict's bucket here with `on_hold = true` and the hold's last as-of date); null for a `paid_off` or `transferred_out` row. `pipeline_stage` is the loan's 36.4 item's current stage when an item exists (offered or later, terminal and `boarded` included), else null; the new `active` loan's `pipeline_stage` is null and its page links back to the member through `origination_application_id` → `applications.prior_loan_id`.
6. **Reviews are read; the rationale is rendered, never written.** `reviews[]` newest first, one per 33.2 review row: `as_of_date`, `verdict`, `reasons` (the engine's codes, each with 33.2's words — 36.3 rule 6), `watch_rate_pct` (the review's `facts.watch_rate_pct` when the verdict is `watching`, else absent) and `analyst_rationale_tokens` — the stored `analyst.rationale` with its `{{facts.<key>}}` tokens as 33.2 rule 4 wrote it, which the page resolves from that review row's own `facts` exactly as `/ops` resolves them (34.3 rule 4), or the engine's explanation text when `analyst.skipped` is set. No model turn runs from the portal, no rationale is regenerated, no figure appears that the analyst did not tokenize (`PROVENANCE`), and no control writes a verdict, a flag or a note (`ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES` holds for the partner as for the analyst).
7. **Facts history without raw facts.** `facts_history[]` has one entry per import that carried the loan, newest first: `as_of_date` and `change` ∈ `created | updated | unchanged` (33.1 rule 2's word for that import's row) and, for `updated`, the names of the facts that differed (UPB, note rate, P&I, T&I, next due, last payment, status, value — 34.3 rule 2's list) as names, never before/after values and never a raw tape column; the row's current figures are on `loan` (36.3 rule 5: decimal-string cents and the tape's percent strings, as 34.3 returns them). No SSN, TIN, DOB, FICO or investor column is on the page in any form.
8. **Readiness, offers and the serviced field.** `readiness` is the latest `readiness_checks` row for the loan: `ready`, `missing` (33.3 rule 1's item names in the order they are asked) and `items[{ item, status }]` (`present | stale | missing | not_applicable`), with no source id, no document and no ask (33.3's asks reach the homeowner through their own cards; the partner requests nothing); absent before the first check. `offers[]` is 34.3's list of the loan's opportunities with `status` (20.1's word), `delivered_at` (the touch's `sent_at`, null for a portal-only delivery) and `expires_at` (`offer_valid_until`) — no rate, payment or NPV of the offer on the page (those are the homeowner's on the OfferCard, 32.11). `serviced` is `{ available: false, code: "SERVICED_PANE_NOT_BUILT" }` for a monitored row (36.5-T3) and for an `active` row in V1 (36.5-T4), the V2 pane for an `active` row once 36.6 lights it, and null for a `paid_off` / `transferred_out` row.
9. **No servicing control on any row.** The loan page offers navigation only: to the board, the pipeline detail, the holds (36.2) and the new or prior loan's page. No Pay, Escrow, Draft, Statement, ACH, Payoff-request, Resolve, Exclude, Message or Re-offer control exists on a monitored row (`LOAN_MONITORED`; `book.resolve` stays `ops_analyst` on `/ops`; 20.2 stays `officer`) nor on an `active` row in V1 (36.6). The Serviced tab is visible and disabled on every loan page with 36.6's copy.
10. **Tenant, roles and the mask.** Every read is `partner_party_id = session.partner_party_id`; another tenant's loan or report is `404 NOT_FOUND`, logged `result = refused` (36.1 rule 4). Home and the loan page accept all three roles (36.5-T5: a `partner_auditor` reads home, eligibility, the pipeline, reports and loan pages and cannot POST an import — 36.2's route answers `403 ROLE_REQUIRED`); Reports accepts `partner_admin` and `partner_auditor`. The page's `loan` is 36.3 rule 5's PartnerLoanRow: the servicer loan number in full for `partner_admin` and `partner_ops`, its last four for `partner_auditor`; the homeowner's first name and last initial; `email_masked` and `phone_masked` as 34.3's masks produce them for `partner_admin` and `partner_ops` only, never raw, and never on a list.

No money figure is computed here.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`portfolio` agent owns the projection and declares nothing of its own on the bus. End-to-end: on every read the runtime calls 34.3's `book.loan` and `book.daily_report` reads and 33.1's status with the tenant filter, applies 36.3's bucket mapping and 36.4's stage mapping to the row, computes the banner from `loans.status` and the application's state (rule 4), counts the tenant's rows for Home (rule 1), renders the stored rationale from its tokens (rule 6), masks partner-grade (36.1) and logs the look (36.1); the report it lists is produced by 34.3's own sweep after 33.3's pass; nothing runs unprompted, nothing is proposed for a human, nothing is written but the log row. Decision record: none of its own — the report's decision is 34.3's, the review's 33.2's. Guardrails: `READ_ONLY` and `NO_COMPUTED_FIGURE` (34.3), `NO_DESTINATION` (34.3 — no invitation destination, no raw contact), `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES` and `PROVENANCE` (33.2 — the rationale is rendered from its tokens and never regenerated), `LOAN_MONITORED` (33.1 — no servicing control on a monitored row), `NO_CREDIT_PULL`, `NO_INVESTOR_FIELDS` and `NO_PROHIBITED_BASIS` (20.1 and 33.2, inherited by the row), `ROLE_MASK` (34.2's principle at 36.1's partner grade), `NO_PII_IN_LOG` (34.1, reused by 36.1). Escalations: none of its own — a late tape is 33.1's `ops_analyst` escalation and a missing report receipt 34.3's; Home shows the late badge and the last report meanwhile.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- No import yet → Home counts 0, dates null, `latest_report_id` null, "Upload a tape to open the book."; Reports lists nothing; no loan page exists for the tenant.
- The next tape is late (33.1's clock breached) → Home's `late` badge; nothing else changes; the board and the pages stay as of the last tape.
- No report yet for a day (the sweep has not reached it, or readiness did not run) → `404 NOT_FOUND` for that `as_of`; the list shows the days that exist; a report produced with readiness marked absent (34.3) is shown as 34.3 stores it.
- The report was re-produced later the same day (a receipt arrived) → the newest row, as 34.3's state machine says; the earlier row is not listed twice.
- A `partner_ops`-only session opens Reports → `403 ROLE_REQUIRED{role: partner_auditor, act_as: []}` before any read; Home and the loan page still open.
- A held loan's page → banner Monitored, `on_hold = true` with the hold's last as-of date and the link to Book > Holds (36.2); no resolve control.
- A loan the partner's tape marked paid or transferred (no refinance) → `status = paid_off` / `transferred_out`, `banner` null (DELTA-03), no `refinanced_by_loan_id`, `bucket` null, `serviced` null; the page still opens with its history.
- The retired prior loan of a refinance (35.10) → the same, with `refinanced_by_loan_id` linking to the new loan's page (36.4-T4).
- The new `active` loan's page → banner Active, `bucket = serviced`, `pipeline_stage` null, the link back to the prior loan, `serviced.code = SERVICED_PANE_NOT_BUILT` (36.5-T4); its Serviced tab disabled like every other.
- The analyst's turn was skipped (provenance, the cap, the model off) → `analyst_rationale_tokens` is the engine's explanation text as 33.2 stored it; nothing is regenerated.
- A review row whose `facts` lack a token the rationale names → the token renders as absent, never as a figure the page made up.
- A homeowner asks the partner for a figure the page does not show → the partner has no control to fetch it; the figure is 33.2's on the homeowner's own record (32.11).
- Another tenant's loan or report id → `404 NOT_FOUND`, logged `result = refused`, no field of the row on the log.

#### Audit and evidence
What an examiner is shown: the daily report per partner and day as 34.3 stores it (the review counts, the fair-lending extract id, the readiness counts, the book summary, the decision id) and, for the hashed document, 34.3's `compliance` export; 36.1's action-log rows for every Home, report, export and loan-page look with the role and no PII; for any loan, 33.1's facts rows by as-of date, 33.2's review rows with the engine's facts and the analyst's tokenized rationale, 33.3's readiness rows, 20.1's opportunity rows, and — for the two rows a refinance leaves — 35.10's closeout journal and 30.2's boarding validations; the code that computes the banner, the bucket and the stage (pure, unit-tested), so any page can be reproduced for any day from the rows. Exported through 34.4's evidence pack (staff); the partner reads the pages and the report.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should the page's `bucket` echo the board's `eligible_now` for a loan in refinance instead of `in_refinance`? **Default: no — the brief lists `in_refinance` as a row value and 36.3's open question 2 hands it to this page; the page's `bucket` is the row's state, the board's is the list, both from the same rows and neither stored; `pipeline_stage` and the banner say the same thing either way, and the counts on Home are 36.3's and 36.4's, not the page's.**
2. What formats does the export answer? **Default: the row's JSON and a CSV of its counts, as a read with no `documents` row; the hashed document is 34.3's `compliance` export on `/ops`, handed to the partner by staff when asked.**
3. Should the partner be able to produce the day's report on demand? **Default: no — production is 34.3's sweep after 33.3's pass; the partner reads what exists and a missing day is a 404, not a trigger.**
4. What is the retired prior loan's banner? **Default: DELTA-03 — null with `status` and `refinanced_by_loan_id` shown until the sentence is accepted; never a sentence minted here.**
5. Should `facts_history` show before/after values for an updated fact, as 34.3 rule 2 shows staff? **Default: names only in V1 — the current figures are on the row and the tape is the partner's own file; before/after values are a later extension once the partner grade for them is decided.**
