# Section 26 — Closing execution: documents, eClosing/RON, funding, and post-closing collateral

## Overview

**Scope.** Section 26 takes a loan from clear-to-close and a final Closing Disclosure to a funded, recorded, collateral-perfected loan in the warehouse: generating the closing documents from Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac uniform instruments, riders, the uniform eNote and the state variants, with the closing instructions and the data-to-document QC (26.1); executing the closing by RON, IPEN, hybrid or wet signing, sealing and registering the eNote on the MERS eRegistry, eRecording the security instrument and moving paper notes into custody (26.2); authorizing and disbursing funds with wet/dry-state, rescission, wire-control and per-diem logic (26.3); and collecting, perfecting and shipping the collateral — endorsements/allonges, assignments where MERS is not the original mortgagee, MERS MOM registration with the interim-funder designation, recorded instruments, final title policy, note custody and shipment to the warehouse/document custodian (26.4). Product scope follows the inventory (Fannie Mae conforming 1–4 unit first liens, fixed and SOFR ARMs, HomeReady, high-balance, buydowns, trusts, POA, Texas 50(a)(6) and New York CEMA), with two product-driven closing constraints established here: Texas 50(a)(6) loans cannot be signed electronically or sold as eMortgages, and New York CEMA loans cannot be eNotes. Every process is written to the Selling Guide edition of Sept 2, 2026, MERS System Procedures Rel. 26.1 (May 17, 2026), MERS eRegistry Procedures Rel. 16.25, the eMortgage Technical Requirements v3.2 (Feb 2026) and the state statutes read on 2026-09-10 (Arizona, Ohio, Texas, New York for RON; Texas Constitution art. XVI §50 and 7 TAC ch. 153 for home equity).

**Agents.** The `closer` — the closing-execution runtime of the `title-closing` agent package (`origination/closing`), defined in 26.1 and reused by 26.2–26.4 — prepares, QC-checks and releases the document set, orchestrates the signing session, validates and registers the eNote, names SM as Secured Party, submits recordings and tracks paper notes into custody. The `funder` agent (26.3) evaluates funding conditions and prepares wires under `funding_approver` dual control; the `post-closing` agent (26.4) drives trailing documents, MERS MOM registration and shipping; `warehouse` (§27) consumes the Secured Party/Interim Funder facts; `compliance-tester` re-runs the pre-funding tests (25.1); `qc-audit` samples closings. Humans appear only at the enumerated touchpoints: external `notary` and `settlement_agent`/closing attorney (the platform never signs or notarizes); `signing_officer` for lender-executed documents (Texas fair-market-value acknowledgment, buydown agreements where the partner is a party, Maine Form 3749, non-delegated eRegistry transactions, allonges/assignments in 26.4); `funding_approver` on every outbound wire; `officer` (partner) for nonstandard documents, QC waivers, late-registration incidents and RON legal questions; `licensed_specialist` in attorney states; `human_agent` on request.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the Lender and Note Holder on every instrument, the creditor under Reg Z (including §1026.36(g)'s NMLSR block on the note and security instrument), the MERS Member whose Org ID anchors the MIN, the first Controller of every eNote (B8-8-02), the eMortgage-approved seller whose eClosing vendors must come from Fannie Mae's list, and the party making the B8/A2-4.1 representations at delivery. SM generates every document from the partner-approved, version-controlled uniform-instrument library; issues closing instructions in the partner's name; operates the eClosing orchestration and the eVault (own or vendor) as Location and Delegatee for Transfers; becomes Secured Party on the eRegistry (and Interim Funder on the MERS System, 26.4) when it advances on the warehouse line under the eNote Transfer of Control and Location and Custodial Agreement with Fannie Mae; submits eRecordings; and takes paper notes into its warehouse custody. Consumer-facing artifacts (Texas §50(g) notice, closing-appointment instructions, the paper option) name the partner; the borrower can never be required to use electronic records, RON or an eNote.

**Dependencies.** Inbound: 23.3 clear-to-close and PTD conditions; 25.1 compliance gates (`SM_O61_COMPLIANCE_PASS_CD_GATE`/`_CONSUMMATE_GATE`/`_DISBURSE_GATE`); 25.2 final CD and `REGZ_1026_19F1_CD_3SBD_GATE`; 25.3 rescission notices and `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE`; 24.4 title, vesting, `settlement_agents`, `wire_verifications`, `trust_reviews`, `poa_reviews`, `SM_CPL_BEFORE_FUNDING_GATE`, `SM_TRUST_POA_REVIEW_GATE`; 24.6 `mi_certificates`/`hpa_disclosures`; 24.5 insurance/flood evidence; 24.2 `REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_COPY_3BD_GATE`; 22.3 `FNMA_B3_3_1_04_VVOE_10BD`; 21.4 lock validity; 21.1 `NTC_SM_ESIGN_CONSENT`. Outbound: 27.1 warehouse advances (Secured Party/Interim Funder, note custody), 29.3/29.4 delivery data (eNote Indicator, Remote Notarization Indicator, SFC 009/014/168/304/508, recording data, eDelivery and Transfer of Control and Location, custodian certification), 30.1 (Fannie Mae's post-purchase MERS investor update), 30.2 boarding at funding (`loans` created), 30.3 initial escrow statement, 28.1/28.2 QC evidence, 31.1 (eMortgage approval, MERS roles, eNote sub-servicer and warehouse-bank listings, state licensing), 31.3 (retention classes incl. `fnma_enote_signing_life_plus_7y` and `ron_recording_state_<n>y`).
