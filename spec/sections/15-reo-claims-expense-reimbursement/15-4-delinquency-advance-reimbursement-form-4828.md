### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**What "Form 4828" is — and is not.** Form 4828 does **not** exist in the single-family Selling/Servicing Guide forms library (verified 2026-09-09 at https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/selling-servicing-guide-forms — the library lists Form 571 "Cash Disbursement Request," Form 1022 SCRA Disbursement Request, Form 1095, Form 3179, etc., but nothing numbered 4828), is not referenced anywhere in the Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026), F-1-20, F-1-05 or the Servicer Expense Reimbursement Job Aid, and Section 5's agent reached the same conclusion (5.x open question 13). It is a **Multifamily** (DUS) form: **Form 4828, "Delinquency Advancing Stop and Advanced Payments Reimbursement Request Form"** (Excel, https://multifamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/xlsx/4828), listed on the Fannie Mae Multifamily forms library page 5 next to **Form 4829, "Servicing Advances Payment Reimbursement Request Form"** (verified 2026-09-09 at https://multifamily.fanniemae.com/doing-business-us/forms?page=4). Under the Multifamily Guide a DUS servicer *requests* permission to stop advancing and *requests* reimbursement of advanced payments on that form; single-family servicing has **no request step**: the Stop Delinquency Advance status is set by Fannie Mae from reported delinquency, and reimbursement of P&I delinquency advances is triggered automatically by the servicer's own investor-reporting transactions. The blueprint imported a multifamily artifact; this process is therefore **re-based** on the single-family mechanics below. Form **571** ("Cash Disbursement Request") — the single-family expense-reimbursement form that survives as Property 360 claim type 571 — covers *expenses* (15.2) and is **never** used for P&I delinquency advances (the Job Aid and F-1-05 contain no P&I-advance line items).

**Servicing Guide C-3-01 (03/11/2026) and F-1-20 (03/11/2026), "Stop Delinquency Advance process."** For MBS and portfolio S/S loans under the special servicing option the servicer remits scheduled P&I "until the mortgage loan becomes four consecutive months delinquent," after which Fannie Mae "will suspend drafting delinquency advances from the servicer's custodial account"; regular servicing option loans are remitted "until the mortgage loan is removed from Fannie Mae's active accounting records." While in the process the servicer keeps reporting and advances excess servicing fees, but not guaranty fees (SVC-2026-02). Contractual payments collected during the process are drafted by Fannie Mae "and first appl[ied] … to recover advances made by Fannie Mae on behalf of the servicer. Once Fannie Mae has recovered all its advances, the servicer may then retain subsequent contractual payments to recover delinquent P&I advances it has made." Exits (F-1-20 table): removed from the trust/reclassified to A/A → "Fannie Mae will reimburse the servicer for any outstanding delinquency advances"; loan becomes current → scheduled drafting resumes; brought current through a completed payment deferral → Fannie Mae reimburses delinquency advances made up to that point; paid off or repurchased → Fannie Mae drafts its own outstanding P&I; **liquidated → "Fannie Mae will reimburse the servicer for any outstanding delinquency advances."** The Guide states the obligation, not the operational mechanism.

**Investor Reporting Manual (Apr. 8, 2026).** p. 25: "Upon completion of a foreclosure and acceptance of the foreclosure LAR, Fannie Mae will reimburse the servicer for advanced P&I for scheduled/scheduled remittance type mortgage loans for which Fannie Mae bears the foreclosure loss risk or those which are shared risk with Fannie Mae responsible for marketing the property." p. 26: for S/A loans "the servicer is authorized to recover its advances for delinquent interest during the fourth month of delinquency … by report[ing] a negative interest remittance amount … when reporting the Transaction Type 96 LAR for the month in which the mortgage loan becomes four months delinquent"; pp. 26–27: "Fannie Mae will reimburse the servicer for the additional month of interest it advances (fourth month) after the servicer reports the loan liquidation (as Action Code 70, 71, or 72)." p. 35: "Generally, servicer P&I advances will be reimbursed within three to four business days after a payment deferral has been accepted in Fannie Mae's investor reporting system." p. 37: on reclassification of a delinquent S/S special servicing option MBS loan to A/A, "Fannie Mae will reimburse the servicer its delinquency advances for scheduled P&I once Fannie Mae completes the reclassification." The manual names no form, no claim and no report line for these reimbursements.

**Mechanics and visibility (Stop Delinquency Advance webinar deck, 2020; SDA Form 496 job aid 10/4/2023; CRS codes Oct. 15, 2025).** Reimbursements and recoveries surface on the Fannie Mae Connect **Remittance Detail – P&I** and **Remittance Detail – Cash Adjustments** reports and the LSDU **Cash Position** downloads as credits/adjustments against the servicer's drafts ("standard settlement procedures"; no business-day timeline is published for liquidations — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**). Reclassification reimbursements ride the reclass purchase advice (A1-3-06/F-1-25; 5.4). The CRS code list carries **208 "S/S Cash DelMod/PD P&I Advance Reimbursement,"** which by its title relates to delinquency-modification/payment-deferral P&I advance reimbursements on portfolio (cash) S/S loans — direction and usage **[UNVERIFIED — confirm with the Investor Reporting Representative]**. **E-3.5-01 (10/13/2021):** "the servicer of an MBS mortgage loan must ensure that the mortgage loan is removed from the MBS pool prior to completion of the foreclosure" (repurchase A1-3-01/02 or reclassification A1-3-06) — for regular servicing option loans that removal, not a reimbursement, ends the advance obligation. **No Guide provision pays interest on delinquency advances** (verified absent: C-3-01, F-1-20, F-1-05, E-5-01).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** the artifact ("Form 4828") is multifamily-only; the system is investor reporting plus Fannie Mae Connect/LSDU cash reports and the reclass purchase advice, not a form; the deadlines that exist are ours (report the triggering transaction on the 5.1/5.3 clocks) and Fannie Mae's (3–4 BD after deferral acceptance; "upon acceptance" of the liquidation LAR; on reclass completion); the row omits S/A fourth-month recovery, the regular-servicing-option pre-foreclosure removal, and the recovery order during Stop Delinquency Advance. Automation is (a) — nothing here is portal-bound except pulling reports Fannie Mae Connect does not expose by API and the Investor Reporting Representative escalation.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Trigger transactions (ours): `removal.liquidation.uninsured|insured|third_party` accepted (LAR 70/72/71; later P360 liquidation events), `reclass.purchase_advice.received`, `lossmit.deferral.completed` accepted in Fannie Mae's investor reporting system (IRM 4-01/4-02), `lossmit.modification.completed` (→ reclass), `payoff.funds.cleared`, `repurchase.approved`, `payment.applied` constituting full contractual payments on Stop Delinquency Advance loans (5.4 recovery order), `loan.reinstated`.
- Positions: `advances` rows `kind = delinquency_pi` (S/S) and `kind = delinquency_interest_sa` (S/A), `sda_status`, `gfee_relief_status` (5.5), `remittance_calculations`.
- Inbound: BD3 draft notifications, Remittance Detail – P&I and Cash Adjustments reports, LSDU Cash Position P&I/Adjustment downloads, reclass purchase advices, Loan-Level Draft Notifications API payloads (00b F3) **[coverage of cash adjustments UNVERIFIED]**.
- Schedules: daily position recompute (post `timer-sweep`), BD3 reconciliation, monthly unreimbursed-advance aging report to the partner.

#### Data model
- `delinquency_advance_positions` (new, one row per loan per day; latest row projected onto `loans`): `loan_id`, `as_of`, `remittance_type`, `servicing_option`, `servicer_pi_advances_outstanding_cents`, `periods jsonb` ([{activity_period, principal_cents, interest_cents, drafted_at, funded_from, advance_id, status}]), `fnma_sda_receivable_cents` (5.4), `sa_interest_advanced_cents`, `sa_month4_interest_cents`, `gfee_advanced_cents` (5.5), `expected_recovery_event` ∈ {liquidation_lar, reclass_pa, deferral_acceptance, payoff, repurchase, borrower_contractual, pre_fcl_removal}, `expected_by`, `matched_cents`, `variance_cents`, `status` ∈ {accruing, sda_active, recovery_expected, matched, variance, escalated, closed}.
- `advance_recoveries` (shared with 15.2): `advance_id`, `source` ∈ {fnma_liquidation_reimb, fnma_reclass_pa, fnma_deferral_reimb, fnma_sda_recovery_credit, sa_negative_interest_lar, payoff_proceeds, repurchase_price, borrower_contractual, write_off}, `amount_cents`, `recovered_at`, `report_line_ref` (report id + row hash / purchase advice id / LAR id), `crs_code?`, `notes`.
- Reuse `advances`, `sda_status`, `draft_adjustments` (5.4: add `type` values `delinquency_advance_reimbursement`, `sa_interest_reimbursement`), `liquidation_facts` (5.3), `remittances`, `investor_events`, `agent_decisions`, `timers`. Ledger: `servicer_advance_receivable` (per custodial account/corporate), memo `fnma_pi_receivable_sda`. Retention `life_of_loan_plus_4y`. No PII beyond loan identifiers.

#### State machine
`accruing` (S/S special servicing: months 1–4 advanced; S/A: months 1–3 interest advanced) → `sda_active` (Fannie Mae Stop Advance status; 5.4) → `recovery_expected` (trigger transaction accepted; `expected_recovery_event` and `expected_by` set) → `matched` (reimbursement/recovery lines matched to `advances` FIFO, ledger relieved) → `closed`. Branches: `borrower_contractual` recoveries during SDA (Fannie Mae's receivable first, then ours) loop between `sda_active` and partial `matched`; `variance` (amount/timing mismatch beyond tolerance) → `escalated` (Investor Reporting Representative package; `officer` at 60 days) → `matched`/`write_off`. Regular servicing option loans: `accruing` → `pre_fcl_removal` (repurchase/reclass before sale; 5.6) → `closed`. A/A loans: `not_applicable`. Actors: agent (all), Fannie Mae (report lines), `fnma_portal_operator` (report pulls), `officer` (write-offs/disputes).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Scope by remittance type and servicing option.** S/S special servicing option (MBS or portfolio): months 1–4 advanced (scheduled principal + interest at PTR on scheduled UPB), Stop Delinquency Advance from month 5, reimbursement at the exits below. S/S regular servicing option MBS: advance until removal; the loan must be repurchased or reclassified before foreclosure completion (E-3.5-01; A1-3-06 six-month selection; 5.6) — advances are recovered through the purchase price/reclass, never through a liquidation reimbursement. S/A portfolio: interest advanced months 1–3, recovered by the month-4 negative-interest LAR; any fourth-month interest advanced is reimbursed after the liquidation LAR (IRM pp. 26–27) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — exact month-4 mechanics; defer to 5.2's S/A calculator]**. A/A: nothing advanced.
2. **Position arithmetic.** `servicer_pi_advances_outstanding = Σ advances.kind = delinquency_pi with status = outstanding`; each period's advance = `remittance_calculations.scheduled_pi_cents` actually drafted (principal and PTR interest components stored). `fnma_sda_receivable` is Fannie Mae's, from its reports (5.4) — it is never ours to recover and, on liquidation, is Fannie Mae's loss.
3. **Expected recovery event and amount.** Liquidation (LAR 70/71/72 or P360 liquidation event accepted; S/S special servicing) → Fannie Mae reimburses the full `servicer_pi_advances_outstanding`; reclass → full amount on the purchase advice; deferral accepted → full amount within 3–4 BD; payoff/repurchase → Fannie Mae drafts its SDA receivable from the proceeds (payoff calculator includes it) while our advances are recovered from the borrower's/seller's delinquent P&I in the payoff (16.2/5.6) — Fannie Mae reimburses nothing; borrower contractual payments during SDA → Fannie Mae's recovery credits first, then our retention (5.4 rule 4).
4. **Matching.** Reimbursement lines (Cash Adjustments/draft-notification credits; purchase-advice lines) are matched to `advances` FIFO by activity period; tolerance $0.05 per line; a matched line posts Dr `custodial_pi_cash` (or corporate cash where Fannie Mae disburses separately) Cr `servicer_advance_receivable`. Amount variance beyond tolerance or timing beyond the expectation window → `variance` with a package (loan, activity periods, drafted amounts, LAR ids/acks, the Fannie Mae report lines) for the Investor Reporting Representative; unresolved 60 days after the exit → `officer` (write-off authority) and the partner's monthly report.
5. **No claims, no interest.** The engine never creates a 571 line for P&I advances (validator in 15.2 rejects `advances.kind ∈ {delinquency_pi, delinquency_interest_sa}`); carrying cost is not reimbursable and is reported as funding cost (5.2).
6. **Guaranty fee and excess servicing.** G-fees for months 1–4 were drafted on the 7th (5.5); reimbursement of g-fee advances on liquidation is 5.5's open question — the position row carries `gfee_advanced_cents` for reconciliation only. Excess servicing strip advanced during SDA is reconciled per 5.4 rule 7.
7. **Worked example (fixture L15 — S/S MBS special servicing, PTR 6.00%).** Installments due Dec 1, 2026–Mar 1, 2027 unpaid; scheduled P&I at PTR drafted Jan 18, Feb 18, Mar 18 and Fri Apr 16, 2027 (Apr 18 is a Sunday): $1,476.00 + $1,476.10 + $1,476.19 + $1,476.29 = **$5,904.58** outstanding (interest component ≈ $1,245.44 declining slightly with scheduled UPB, principal ≈ $230.6–$230.9 rising; the sum rises because the 0.25% servicing-fee strip deducted from note-rate interest shrinks as UPB amortizes). Fannie Mae sets Stop Advance at BD2 of May 2027 (start date May 1, 2027); activity periods Apr–Sep 2027 are credited (net $0 drafts) and Fannie Mae's receivable grows to ≈ 6 × $1,476.4–$1,476.9 ≈ **$8,860** — Fannie Mae's loss at liquidation. Foreclosure sale Tue Oct 5, 2027; LAR 72 (MI-insured, Fannie Mae acquiring) submitted Oct 5, accepted Wed Oct 6, 2027 → `expected_recovery_event = liquidation_lar`, `expected_by` = two draft cycles (Nov 18, 2027 and Fri Dec 17, 2027, since Dec 18 is a Saturday). The Cash Adjustments report for the October activity period (available at the early-November BD3 notification) is expected to show a delinquency-advance reimbursement of **$5,904.58**; matched FIFO to the four `advances` rows; ledger Dr `custodial_pi_cash` $5,904.58 Cr `servicer_advance_receivable` $5,904.58, then the corporate transfer that returns the funds to the partner's advance line. Fannie Mae's own $8,860 receivable is closed on its side. Nothing about the $5,904.58 goes on the 571 claim (15.2) or the MI claim (15.3 — MI claims cover note-rate interest and expenses, not investor advances). If the Dec 17 cycle passes unmatched → Investor Reporting Representative package; Feb 4, 2028 (60 days after Oct 6) unresolved → `officer`.
   *Deferral variant:* had a payment deferral been accepted in Fannie Mae's investor reporting system on Fri Mar 12, 2027 (three installments deferred), reimbursement of the three outstanding advances ($4,428.29) is expected by Thu Mar 18, 2027 (4 `fannie_et` BD) — the same day as the March draft, so the BD3 notification/Cash Adjustments lines are matched that cycle.
   *S/A variant (portfolio S/A, UPB $249,088.61):* interest advanced months 1–3 = 3 × $1,245.44 = $3,736.32; the LAR 96 for the month the loan becomes four months delinquent reports −$3,736.32 interest (recovery); a fourth-month interest advance of $1,245.44, if drafted, is reimbursed after the liquidation LAR — expected within two draft cycles.
8. **Reversals and corrections.** If a liquidation LAR is corrected/withdrawn before BD2 close (5.3) the expectation is cancelled and re-created on the superseding acceptance; an elimination/rescission (15.1) after reimbursement reverses the position: the loan re-enters `sda_active` or `accruing`, and any reimbursement Fannie Mae claws back appears as a debit adjustment matched to a reversing `advance_recoveries` row.
9. **Rounding.** All amounts are `bigint` cents as drafted/reported; no recomputation of Fannie Mae's amounts — variances are investigated, not rounded away.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`custodial-recon` agent (positions, matching, ledger) with `investor-reporting` (trigger transactions, LAR ids/acks) and `claims-reo` (liquidation context; ensures P&I advances never leak into 571/MI claims). Tools: `recomputeAdvancePosition`, `setRecoveryExpectation`, `parseCashAdjustments`, `matchRecoveries` (FIFO, tolerance), `postRecoveryEntries`, `buildIrrPackage`, `agingReport`, `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{loan_id, as_of, remittance_type, servicing_option, outstanding_cents, periods[], expected_event, expected_by, matched[], variance_cents, action, evidence_ids[]}`. Guardrails: never fund an advance on a Stop Advance loan (5.4); never book a recovery without a report-line reference; never write off without `officer` approval; never net Fannie Mae's SDA receivable against our advances; flag any S/S special servicing loan liquidated with a zero expectation (data error). Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (report pulls); `officer` (write-offs > $500/loan; disputes at 60 days; regular-servicing-option removal decisions with 5.6). Toggle-off: positions and expectations still computed; a human approves matches in `ops-console`.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-in with outstanding advances**: board the transferor's advance schedule and Fannie Mae's Stop Advance data (1.1/5.4); the transfer agreement decides who is reimbursed for pre-transfer advances (F-1-11: transferee reimburses transferor after final accounting) — recoveries received for the transferor's advances are passed through.
- **Transfer-out mid-SDA**: expectation transfers with the loan; document outstanding advances on the final accounting.
- **Bankruptcy (cramdown/Chapter 13 plan)**: no liquidation; advances persist until reclass (24 months special servicing) or exit — the aging report shows long-dated positions; confirm treatment of plan payments as contractual (Section 14).
- **Disaster forbearance (LL-2026-01)**: advances continue months 1–4; SDA unaffected (5.4).
- **Elimination/rescission after reimbursement**: reverse per rule 8.
- **Loan liquidated in error (post-close payoff error, 5.3)**: no reimbursement expectation; partner remits/advances to liquidate; positions frozen pending re-add.
- **Reclass while SDA active then modification cancels**: purchase-advice reimbursement stands; later cancellation follows 5.6/15.2 incentive repayment, not an advance clawback.
- **Fannie Mae reimburses more than outstanding** (duplicate credit): book a payable and notify the Investor Reporting Representative; never keep the excess.
- **LL-2026-05 payment-event/auto-draft phase**: when S/S remittance mechanics change (later phase; 00a §3.1), the expectation engine keys off the accepted event rather than the LAR — feature-flagged.

#### Audit and evidence
Daily position history, advance-funding trail (funding source, draft ids), trigger transaction ids/acks (LAR/event/PA/SMDU), report lines matched (report ids, row hashes), ledger entries, variance packages and Fannie Mae responses, write-off approvals, `agent_decisions`, timer histories — retained `life_of_loan_plus_4y`; supports STAR custodial/investor-reporting reviews, FHFA/partner liquidity reporting on advance exposure, and transfer final accountings.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Operational mechanism and timing of liquidation reimbursements** (draft credit vs separate disbursement; days after LAR acceptance) — default: expect a Cash Adjustments credit within two S/S draft cycles (5.4) and confirm at onboarding with the Investor Reporting Representative.
2. **CRS code 208 usage** ("S/S Cash DelMod/PD P&I Advance Reimbursement") — default: not used by us unless Fannie Mae instructs; parse it if it appears on inbound drafts.
3. **S/A fourth-month interest mechanics** — default: implement per 5.2's S/A calculator; reconcile the post-liquidation reimbursement as a separate expectation.
4. **Blueprint row title** — default: keep the row id/name for traceability but label the process "Delinquency (P&I) advance recovery" in the ops console; no Form 4828.
