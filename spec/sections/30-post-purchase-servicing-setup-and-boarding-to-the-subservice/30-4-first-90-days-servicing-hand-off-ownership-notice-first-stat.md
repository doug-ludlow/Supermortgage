### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-11)

**Reg Z §1026.39 (eCFR current as of 9/09/2026) [VERIFIED].** (a)(1): "A 'covered person' means any person, as defined in § 1026.2(a)(22), that becomes the owner of an existing mortgage loan by acquiring legal title to the debt obligation". (b): "Each covered person...shall mail or deliver the disclosures required by this section to the consumer on or before the 30th calendar day following the date of transfer." (c) exceptions: sale/transfer within 30 days of acquisition; repurchase agreements; partial-interest acquisitions where the party authorized to receive notices does not change. (d) content: the covered person's name, address and telephone number; the date of transfer; the agent/party authorized to receive rescission notices; whether the transfer is or may be recorded; the partial-payment policy. 25.4 owns `REGZ_1026_39_OWNERSHIP_NOTICE_30` and `NTC_REGZ_1026_39_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER` (fallback template).

**Fannie Mae practice [VERIFIED — consumer page; Servicing Guide instruction UNVERIFIED].** "Understanding your loan purchase letter" (yourhome.fanniemae.com/own/notice): "You may have received a letter from us notifying you that we purchased your loan."; the letter "Identifies Fannie Mae as the owner/investor for your loan. Identifies your mortgage servicer, who provides customer service."; "It is a legal requirement for us to notify the borrower of the purchase."; "It does not change the terms or conditions of your mortgage loan, deed of trust, or note."; "Send all payments to your mortgage servicer. Fannie Mae is not your mortgage servicer."; "Fannie Mae is not your servicer. We do not service mortgage loans." The page states no timeframe. Consequence: Fannie Mae sends the §1026.39 notice itself; the platform must not send a duplicate, must be able to explain the letter to the borrower, and must keep the servicer identity Fannie Mae prints current (29.4 delivery data; 30.1 servicer records). Searches of the Servicing Guide (A2-1-01, D1-4, F-1-11, F-1-17) found no instruction directing servicers to send the notice on Fannie Mae's behalf.

**Reg Z §1026.41 (eCFR current as of Sept. 3, 2026; servicing 7.1) [VERIFIED via 7.1].** (b): "The periodic statement must be delivered or placed in the mail within a reasonably prompt time after the payment due date or the end of any courtesy period provided for the previous billing cycle." Comment 41(b)-1: "Delivering, emailing or placing the periodic statement in the mail within four days of the close of the courtesy period of the previous billing cycle generally would be considered reasonably prompt." For a newly originated loan there is no previous billing cycle before the first due date; the first statement is therefore governed by policy (30.2 `SM_ORIG_FIRST_STATEMENT_LEAD_15`: first due date − 15 calendar days) and the second statement by 7.1's `REGZ_1026_41B_STATEMENT_PROMPT_4` from the first courtesy-period end.

**Reg X §1024.38(c) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026) [VERIFIED].** (c)(1): "A servicer shall retain records that document actions taken with respect to a borrower's mortgage loan account until one year after the date a mortgage loan is discharged or servicing of a mortgage loan is transferred by the servicer to a transferee servicer." (c)(2): "A servicer shall maintain the following documents and data on each mortgage loan account serviced by the servicer in a manner that facilitates compiling such documents and data into a servicing file within five days:" (i) "A schedule of all transactions credited or debited to the mortgage loan account, including any escrow account as defined in § 1024.17(b) and any suspense account"; (ii) "A copy of the security instrument that establishes the lien securing the mortgage loan"; (iii) "Any notes created by servicer personnel reflecting communications with the borrower about the mortgage loan account"; (iv) "To the extent applicable, a report of the data fields relating to the borrower's mortgage loan account created by the servicer's electronic systems in connection with servicing practices"; (v) "Copies of any information or documents provided by the borrower to the servicer in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 1024.35 or § 1024.41". "Five days" is unqualified — treated as calendar days.

**Servicing Guide C-4.1-01, Notifying Credit Repositories (07/15/2020) [VERIFIED via servicing 8.1].** Monthly furnishing to the four "major credit repositories" (Equifax, Experian, Innovis, TransUnion) in Metro 2® format; statuses to report include "new originations; current mortgage loans; delinquent mortgage loans, including 30–, 60–, 90+ day delinquencies, forbearances, repayment plans, Trial Period Plans, modifications; and liquidated mortgage loans…". `as_of_date` = last calendar day of the month (8.1 `FNMA_C41_01_METRO2_SNAPSHOT_EOM`; transmission by 8.1's `SM_METRO2_TRANSMIT_ALL4_BD3`, hard stop `SM_METRO2_TRANSMIT_HARD_CD10`). FCRA §1681s-2(a)(7) negative-information notice: 8.1's `NTC_FCRA_1681S2A7_B1` is embedded in 30.2's first-payment letter (30.2 rule 9), so no separate first-cycle notice is due.

**Servicing Guide C-4.3-01 / IRM / LL-2026-05 [VERIFIED — 30.1].** First LAR in the acquisition month (TT96 guide: loans "acquired" are reported with "LARs in the same month that the loans are acquired"); LL-2026-05 Escrow Setup event for newly acquired loans (mandatory Dec 1, 2026; 1.1's `LL_2026_05_ESCROW_SETUP_ACQUIRED_BD1` instantiated by 30.1 with `anchor_event=loan.purchased`); period close BD2 17:00 ET (5.1 `FNMA_IRM_PERIOD_CLOSE_BD2_1700`). 30.4 consumes 30.1's `loan.reporting_active` as hand-off evidence; it defines no investor timer.

**Selling Guide D1-3-01, Lender Post-Closing Quality Control Review Process (04/01/2026) [VERIFIED].** "The lender must establish a process for selecting loans for its discretionary post-closing QC. While the lender may include randomly targeted reviews, the process must target areas the lender identifies as having a higher potential for errors and loans that may have a high risk of misrepresentation or fraud, including early payment defaults, as described in A3-4-03, Preventing, Detecting, and Reporting Mortgage Fraud." "The entire QC cycle (selection, review, rebuttal, and reporting) must be completed within 90 days from the month of the disbursement date" (28.2 `FNMA_D1_3_QC_CYCLE_90`). D1-3-01 contains no numeric EPD definition; A3-4-03 (12/10/2025) as extracted contains none either.

**Selling Guide Exhibit E-2-05, Mortgage Loan Servicing Purchase and Sale Agreement (12/15/2021), §7.4(b)(iii) [VERIFIED — the only Fannie Mae numeric EPD definition located].** "In the event that for any Mortgage Loan for which Servicer has purchased the Servicing Rights, the first, second or third monthly payment due Servicer becomes sixty (60) or more days delinquent (each such Mortgage Loan, an 'EPD Mortgage Loan')" — the seller reimburses the servicer the purchase price plus a $1,500 fee within seven business days. The definition governs Fannie Mae's servicing-rights sales, not the seller's QC plan; the platform adopts it as the "Fannie Mae-usage" flag and carries 28.2's broader policy flag alongside it.

**Selling Guide A2-4.1-02, Ownership and Retention of Loan Files and Records (12/19/2017) [VERIFIED].** "All records related to loans (including all data and materials representing, based on, or compiled from such records) sold to or serviced for Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property"; "If the seller does not service the loan, it must transfer the loan file to the servicer."; the servicer and responsible party "must keep all of the individual loan records and all servicing records for the time it serviced the loan."; "The servicer must maintain permanent mortgage account records for each loan it services for Fannie Mae."; "After a loan is liquidated, the servicer must keep the individual loan records for at least four years, unless the local jurisdiction requires longer retention"; "The time frame from loan liquidation is measured from the date of the loan payoff or the date that any applicable claim proceeds are received, whichever is later."; "the servicer may destroy any accounting reports 18 months after such reports are filed with Fannie Mae."; sellers/servicers must "ensure the accuracy, security, confidentiality, integrity, completeness and legibility of the individual loan file" and "periodically review changes in technology to make sure that all records continue to be obtainable and readable in the future." Retention class `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` (baseline addendum §10) is therefore correctly worded.

**Origination retention (00a-fed §15) [VERIFIED].** Reg Z §1026.25(c)(1)(i) LE/§1026.19(e) evidence "for three years after the later of the date of consummation, the date disclosures are required to be made, or the date the action is required to be taken"; (c)(1)(ii) CD "for five years after consummation" (an owner/servicer receiving a copy "shall retain such disclosures for the remainder of the five-year period"); (c)(2) originator compensation three years after receipt/payment; (c)(3) ATR/QM "for three years after consummation"; (a) other Reg Z evidence two years. **Discrepancies vs blueprint.**
1. The blueprint row implies the servicer sends the §1026.39 notice within 30 days of purchase; Fannie Mae sends its own loan purchase letter — 25.4 records an expectation and evidence (`SM_O64_FNMA_1026_39_EVIDENCE_45`); SM sends only as a fallback or when it is itself the covered person (warehouse assignment — 25.4).
2. "Lender's EPD review in D1-3" — D1-3-01 requires EPDs to be targeted in discretionary QC but defines none; the only numeric Fannie Mae definition is E-2-05 (payments 1–3, 60+ days) for servicing-rights sales. 28.2's policy definition (payments 1–6, 60+ days) is broader and stays; 30.4 computes both flags.
3. Blueprint retention "5 yrs" for the initial escrow statement — §1024.17 sets no period; servicing records fall under §1024.38(c)(1) (one year after discharge/transfer) and Fannie Mae's A2-4.1-02 (life of servicing + four years after liquidation); the platform applies the longest applicable class.
4. C-4.1-01 lists "new originations" among statuses to report — confirmed; the first Metro 2 cycle is the first month-end after boarding, before the first payment is due (policy default; see Q3).
5. "First periodic statement reasonably prompt after due date" — for cycle 1 there is no prior cycle; 30.2's 15-day lead applies and 7.1's four-day clock begins with cycle 2.
6. The "first 90 days" window is an SM operating construct, not a rule; its closure is a checklist gate, not a regulatory deadline.

#### State machine

`servicing_handoffs`: `open` (all items pending; opened at boarding) → `complete` (every item `satisfied`/`not_applicable`) or `complete_with_exceptions` (≥ 1 `breached`/`waived`) → `closed` at `SM_ORIG_HANDOFF_CLOSE_90` (or earlier when `complete` and the third statement cycle has run) → `reopened` when a post-closure exception references the hand-off (e.g., Fannie Mae letter evidence never arrived, an EPD flag before closure is later cleared as a posting error) → `closed`. Terminal: `closed` with `close_basis`; a loan paid off or transferred inside the window closes with that basis and open items marked `not_applicable{reason=loan_terminated}`.
`epd_flags`: `raised` → `cleared` (payment brings days past due below the threshold; posting error reversed) | `confirmed_to_qc` (28.2 review opened; the flag stays for history). `vendor_activations`: `requested` → `confirmed` | `rejected` → `requested` (retry with corrected data) | `not_applicable`. Actors: `boarding` agent (all transitions), vendor adapters, `officer` (override closure), `compliance-sentinel` (read-only reporting).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`boarding` agent, hand-off profile (tools: `openHandoff`, `verifyTimerSeeding`, `repairTimer`, `requestVendorActivation`, `confirmVendorActivation`, `reconcileFirstStatement`, `evaluateEpd`, `compileServicingFile`, `classifyRetention`, `explainFnmaLetter` (portal content), `closeHandoff`, `requestOfficerOverride`). Decision records for every non-mechanical step (vendor reject resolution, first-statement discrepancy triage, EPD clearing with `posting_error`, closure with exceptions) carry inputs, evidence ids, rule version and confidence. Guardrails: the agent cannot send a consumer notice from this process; cannot clear an `epd_flags` row without a ledger event; cannot mark HO-018 `not_applicable` when `mi_certificates` exists; cannot close a hand-off with breached statutory items (HO-004 initial escrow statement, HO-009 when SM is the sender, HO-019/`REGX_1024_38C2_SERVICING_FILE_5` breaches) without an `officer` override. `compliance-sentinel` composes the daily report from queries only (no writes to loan data). Human path when AI is off: the same commands in `ops-console` with the checklist enforced; `fnma_portal_operator` is not involved (no portal work in this process).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Purchase after the first payment** (delayed delivery into January): HO-010…HO-014 anchor on the later purchase date; the first Metro 2 and first statement cycles run on boarding-based clocks regardless; 30.1 handles the LPI shift and 27.2 the interest.
- **Loan not sold / repurchased inside the window**: the hand-off continues (SM services for the partner or the warehouse); §1026.39 flips to the 25.4 covered-person analysis; HO-010…HO-014 `not_applicable{reason=not_purchased}`; EPD flags still feed 28.2.
- **Borrower calls about the Fannie Mae letter** ("is my loan sold? who do I pay?"): 4.x script from `explainFnmaLetter`; no change to payee; if the borrower reports never receiving a letter by Jan 3, 2027, 25.4's evidence timer handles the inquiry to Fannie Mae.
- **Vendor activation rejected for identity mismatch** (name spelling, APN): the agent corrects from 30.2's canonical data; if the origination record was wrong, an 30.2 correction (`boarding.correction.applied`) precedes the retry.
- **MI activation not confirmed** (purchase fixture): sev-1; the loan has been delivered — the partner `officer` is told the same day; 10.x escalates with the insurer; premium remittance is held until active status to avoid remitting on a non-existent certificate.
- **First statement sent before the initial escrow statement** (late analysis path): allowed — 7.1 renders from the boarded escrow figures; 30.3/3.1's 45-day timer still governs the initial statement.
- **First payment misdirected to the closing agent or the partner**: 2.x misdirected-payment handling; `sm_watch_p1_6_30` fires only if the payment is not located; the agent checks 30.2's payee data on the first-payment letter before any collections contact.
- **EPD raised then reversed by a posting correction**: flag cleared `posting_error`; 28.2 withdraws the selection if not started; the report shows both events.
- **Servicing-file request on day 1** (e.g., a QWR under §1024.36 arriving at boarding): compile from the opening ledger; item (ii) uses the executed security instrument pending recording; the five-day clock runs from receipt.
- **Retention rule change** (`rule_sets.retention` version bump): `retention_schedule` recomputed for all open loans; shorter periods never shorten an existing `retention_until` without compliance sign-off.
- **Paid off or transferred inside 90 days**: hand-off closes with that basis; 30.3's same-servicer credit rules or 16.x/17.x take over; retention anchors move to the liquidation/transfer date.
- **Purchase fixture (OH, wet, MI)**: boarding Wed Nov 18, 2026; vendor activations due Fri Nov 20; MI confirmation due Fri Nov 20; first statement lead Thu Dec 17; first Metro 2 as of Nov 30; first-payment letter by Fri Nov 27 (Thanksgiving excluded — 25.4-T7); purchase Wed Dec 2 (27.2 example C) → §1026.39 expected by Fri Jan 1, 2027; hand-off closes by Tue Feb 16, 2027.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **EPD definition alignment** — 30.4 computes the E-2-05 flag (payments 1–3, 60+) and 28.2's policy flag (payments 1–6, 60+); default: 28.2 selects on either; confirm with the partner's Fannie Mae QC Specialist whether Fannie Mae's Quality Insider "EPD" reporting uses a different window.
2. **"Five days" in §1024.38(c)(2)** — calendar (default, conservative) vs business days; counsel to confirm; the compile capability is designed to complete in minutes so the choice affects only the timer.
3. **Report new originations to the bureaus before the first due date** — default yes (status 11, PHP `B`, Date Opened = note date) per 8.1; alternative: first report after the first due month; CRRG guidance on pre-first-payment tradelines [UNVERIFIED].
4. **Tax-service contract type and payer** — life-of-loan contract at boarding (default; fee on the CD as a borrower-paid origination charge where the partner's pricing includes it, else SM-absorbed) vs annual monitoring; multi-parcel handling.
5. **Hand-off closure basis** — 90 days from boarding (default) vs completion of the disbursement-month QC cycle (end of the third month) — the latter would extend the window to ~110 days for late-month fundings.
6. **Fixture reconciliation** — 25.4's worked examples use annual taxes $4,800 (monthly escrow $525.00, deposit $1,875.00); the orchestrator's fixture and 30.3 use taxes $6,400 + hazard $1,850 (monthly $687.50, deposit $2,062.50). 30.3 is authoritative for the escrow figures; 25.4's examples should be updated by its writer (no §30 file is changed by this note). Likewise 29.4/27.2 use `loans.fnma_purchase_date` where 30.1 wrote `loans.purchase_date` — treat as one column named `fnma_purchase_date`.
