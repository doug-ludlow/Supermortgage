# Section 36 — The servicing partner portal: partner identity, the tape drop, the eligibility board, the refinance pipeline, home and reports, and the serviced pane contract

## Overview

The servicing partner portal is the tenanted B2B surface for the servicer partner whose book section 33 already imports, reviews and readies and whose operations section 34 already shows to Supermortgage's own staff. It is one portal whose unit is a loan row with a state — `monitored` → `in_refinance` → `active` (Supermortgage subservicing the new loan), or `paid_off` / `transferred_out` / on hold — and two modes on the same row: pre-refi the loan is `monitored`, the partner's incumbent subservicer keeps servicing it and the partner sees the tape drop, the eligibility buckets and the live refinance feed; post-refi the new loan boarded through 30.2 / 35.10 is `active`, Supermortgage services it, and the same row flips its banner while its serviced pane stays a dark stub in V1. V1 is the recapture command center: the partner uploads its servicing book, its current subservicer keeps servicing, the platform receives only the updated tape, the portal shows which loans are eligible now, likely soon and not near, and a feed of members actively in a refinance; after close and board Supermortgage becomes the active subservicer of the new loan, and the banner and the stub are designed so V2 attaches without a rewrite. Nothing here rebuilds refinance scoring, tape parsing, invitations or servicing: the section projects 33.1–33.3 and 34.3 through a new `/v1/partner/*` prefix and a new app `apps/partner` on `/partners`, and never puts a partner user on `/ops`, `/app` or `/video`.

Six processes. 36.1 puts partner users, the doors (a code to the e-mail, then a password or passkey — 34.1's mechanics, copied and never shared), the three roles (`partner_admin`, `partner_ops`, `partner_auditor` — not staff roles, not `StaffRole`), one action-log row per request and the tenant scope in place: every query is `partner_party_id = session.partner_party_id` and anything outside the tenant is `404 NOT_FOUND`, never `403`. 36.2 is the partner tape drop: a `partner_admin` posts the tape and the supplement and the runtime dispatches 33.1's `book.import` with the partner taken from the session and the partner user as actor — the one partner write in V1; imports, the import report, the book status with 33.1's next-tape clock, and the holds the partner can see and cannot resolve. 36.3 is the eligibility board: three buckets that are a projection of 33.2's stored `verdict` and nothing else — Eligible now (`candidate`), Likely soon (`watching`, with `watch_rate_pct`), Not near (`not_now` ∪ `excluded`, with the engine's reason codes) — with no filter that changes who appears. 36.4 is the refinance pipeline feed: the members who left the static board and entered motion, projected from `refi_opportunities`, the application, DU, disclosures, closing and boarding rows that already exist, newest first. 36.5 is home, the daily report (34.3's `bookDailyReport` shape for the tenant) and the two-mode loan page with its banner. 36.6 is the post-refinance serviced pane contract, dark in V1: the modules V2 will read and the one refusal every one of them answers until then.

Pre-refi the monitored book is a GLBA service-provider data feed — not a post-delivery servicing transfer and not subservicing of the old loan: no Form 629, no Form 101, no RESPA hello/goodbye, no Supermortgage P&I or T&I custodial for those loans. Post-refi, after 30.2 / 35.10 board the new loan Supermortgage originated, the partner remains master servicer, Fannie seller and MSR owner and Supermortgage is subservicer of the new loan; Selling Guide A2-1-07 then applies (both parties Fannie-approved, Form 101, separate 1013/1014 custodials, the master remains fully liable), and A2-7-03 applies if Supermortgage is later appointed onto already-delivered Fannie loans. V1 does not implement those forms and must not claim they are done. GLBA also fixes what the portal is for: Supermortgage may not market its own products from the partner's servicing data; the portal is the partner's book, branded with the partner's legal name and NMLSR id, never a Supermortgage acquisition funnel. FCRA holds as it holds in 33.2: no consumer report is pulled to populate Eligible now, Likely soon or Not near; the partner's tape FICO is a file fact for pricing after a homeowner's own Yes (20.4), never a selection input.

Capacity: Supermortgage as platform operator and, after board, as subservicer; the partner as master servicer / MSR owner / Fannie seller / program lender. Pre-refi the platform is a GLBA service provider holding a monitored book — the partner is servicer of record of every monitored loan; post-refi it is the Fannie subservicer of the new loan (V2). Nothing in this section changes a money field, a verdict or a borrower record on its own; the portal shows, and it dispatches 33.1's `book.import` with the partner user as actor. Partner users cannot waive money fields, approve campaigns, resolve holds, message a homeowner or pull credit; `book.resolve` stays `ops_analyst` on `/ops`, campaign approval stays `officer`, invitations stay 33.1's system and `portfolio` agent. Monitored loans refuse servicing commands (`LOAN_MONITORED`): no clock, ledger, statement or draft of sections 2–19 arms on a monitored row, and the portal exposes no Pay, Escrow or Draft control on one. Masking is tighter than staff: a list row carries the servicer loan number's last four, the homeowner's first name and last initial and the state — no raw e-mail or phone, no SSN, TIN or DOB, no investor column, no DTI, and FICO never as a filter; the action log carries ids and the role only. Every vendor is the in-repo FAKE and `INTEGRATIONS=fake` in every build stage; the demo tenant is 33.1's fixture partner, Northlight Mortgage Servicing (FAKE partner), NMLSR 1234567, with its 12 monitored loans.

Dependencies: 33.1 (`book.import`, `book.report`, `book.resolve`, `account.provision`, `account.invite`; `partner_book_imports`, `partner_book_facts`, `partner_book_invitations`; the holds; `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_TAPE_EXPECTED_7` and `SM_PARTNER_BOOK_INVITATION_REMINDER_14`, its clocks, shown here and never re-triggered), 33.2 (`partner_book_reviews` and the verdict `candidate | watching | not_now | excluded`, `watch_rate_pct`, `ANALYST_NEVER_DECIDES`), 33.3 (`readiness_checks`, `applications.prior_loan_id`), 34.1 (the door mechanics and the roles pattern, copied), 34.3 (the read tools `book.history`, `book.loans`, `book.loan`, `book.day`, `book.daily_report`, always called with the tenant as `filter.partner`; `partner_book_daily_reports`), 20.1 (the investor-blind universe, the fire rule, `refi_opportunities` statuses `offer_ready | offered | engaged | expired`, `SM_REFI_OPPORTUNITY_EXPIRY_30`), 30.2 (the boarding that makes the new loan `active`), 35.10 (the refinance closeout in `monitored_partner` mode, `partner_book.loan.paid_off`, `SM_REFI_PARTNER_CONFIRM_21` — its clock, never re-triggered here). Consumers: none in V1; V2's serviced pane (36.6) will read sections 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16 and 18 through their own tools. The gap log is docs/partner-portal/BACKEND-DELTAS.md: a name the portal needs that no owning section has is a numbered delta there, never a new table, timer, notice, command, role, verdict, bucket, stage or filter minted in this section.

Changes in flight that shape the build: the section owns no timer code (36.1's session expiry is two session columns, as 34.1's) and one notice (`NTC_SM_PARTNER_USER_INVITE`, 36.1 — the invitation of a partner user, never a borrower notice); the worked figures are 33.2-T2's, quoted by 36.3 and never recomputed; `bucket` and `pipeline_stage` are computed, never stored on `loans`; the partner never chooses `partner_party_id` or the tape profile; there is no OIDC door in V1.

## Closing

Build order: 36.1 (nothing else is safe without a partner actor and the tenant scope) → 36.2 (the tape drop is the one write and the operational need) → 36.3 and 36.4 (the pure projections `buckets` and `pipeline` with their tests, then the reads) → 36.5 and 36.6 (the landings, the loan page, the dark pane) → `apps/partner` (the V1 screens against the live `/v1/partner/*` API, driven from `node:test` with Playwright as section 32's shell suites are). Shared fixtures: 33.1's 12-loan Northlight book (`src/domain/partner-book/fixtures/partner-book-demo.ts`, seeded by `seedPartnerBookDemo`), a seeded `partner_admin` of the demo partner, a second partner for the cross-tenant tests, and 33.2's daily run and offer passes driven through the existing runtime functions exactly as `33-2.spec.test.ts` drives them — never a verdict written by raw SQL. The tests live in `src/domain/servicing-partner-portal/36-*.spec.test.ts`, one `node:test` per T-id titled exactly as the spec's row; the scaffold and the audit need this section's test directory named where sections 32–35 name theirs (`tools/scaffold_spec_tests.py`, `tools/audit.py`) before the first `npm run spec:scaffold`.

Section-level acceptance: a `partner_admin` of the demo partner signs in through `/partners/sign-in` without ever touching `/ops`; sees Home counts that match the latest 33.2 run; sees every monitored loan in exactly one of Eligible now, Likely soon, Not near or Holds; sees any member with an open offer or an open application on Pipeline; uploads the fixture tape (or receives `already_loaded`) and reads the 33.1 import report; opens a monitored loan and reads "Monitored — Northlight Mortgage Servicing (FAKE partner) remains servicer"; cannot pay, draft, waive, resolve a hold or pull credit; cannot see another partner's loans; and every one of those looks and acts is on `partner_actions` with their identity and role and no homeowner's name. In code: `LOAN_MONITORED` still refuses servicing commands on the 12 loans, `/ops` still refuses partner cookies, `/v1/partner-book/*` still works with the machine token for staff, and the section's progress is the fraction `npm run audit` prints for each 36.x row — never a sentence.
