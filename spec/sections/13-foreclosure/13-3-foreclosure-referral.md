### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Timing — E-1.2-02 (05/10/2023).** "For all mortgage loans secured by a principal residence, the servicer must refer the mortgage loan to foreclosure no earlier than the 121st day of delinquency unless applicable law permits earlier referral." Non-principal residence: "Depending on the outcome of the prereferral review ..., the mortgage loan must be referred to a law firm for foreclosure no later than the 120th day of delinquency, except in circumstances where the borrower submits a BRP shortly before the foreclosure referral" (E-3.2-04 postponements, 13.4). "Foreclosure is considered to have begun on the date when the servicer refers the matter to a law firm. The servicer must maintain a record of the date of the referral in the mortgage loan file." The Guide never states a "no later than" day for principal residences; the practical deadline is the allowable time frame (13.5). E-1.2-02 also carries the environmental-hazard hook: contact the Servicing Representative "if it becomes aware of environmental hazards" and report environmental litigation "immediately" on Form 20 (13.7).

**Referral mechanics — E-1.1-01 (05/08/2024).** Send the referral "to a law firm that has been selected and retained for the jurisdiction under the requirements set forth in A4-2.2-01"; "Submit a complete referral package to the law firm and work with the law firm to determine the documents needed in that particular jurisdiction"; "Advise the law firm ... that Fannie Mae owns or securitizes the mortgage loan being referred"; "Manage referrals to law firms to avoid any conflicts of interest"; bulk-transfer notice "at least 5 business days prior to transferring any default-related matter that results in an aggregate of 30 or more transfers within a 6-month period from a single law firm to another law firm in the same state"; "If a foreclosure sale has already occurred, the servicer must obtain Fannie Mae's prior approval before transferring"; a bankruptcy after referral goes to the same firm and "the matter must be referred back to the law firm that originally received the foreclosure referral if foreclosure is still necessary" (E-1.2-01); "Fannie Mae may deny reimbursement of fees and out-of-pocket expenses for any referrals to law firms that have not been selected and retained under these requirements."

**Referral documents — E-1.1-02 (11/12/2014).** At referral provide "a true, correct, and complete copy of the note, including any allonge, produced from the original held by the document custodian," or the original note with allonges, or a lost-note affidavit (after "a thorough and diligent search"); Fannie Mae does not reimburse the cost of obtaining originals/affidavits. The servicer must have a process to request documents from the document custodian "no later than the 95th day of delinquency." Failure to provide documents or to respond "within three business days" to firm requests exposes the servicer to "indemnification," "make whole," "repurchase, or compensatory fees." Assignments: identify the mortgagee of record "by the 90th day of delinquency" and ensure the mortgage is "validly assigned (that is, is legally effective and enforceable)" before referral; where MERS is mortgagee, "prepare and execute (and record, if necessary)" the assignment to the foreclosing party; record before foreclosure where the state requires, otherwise "as soon as possible"; include an executed-but-unrecorded assignment in the package. Manufactured homes: flag the property type and include collateral documents. Bankruptcy packages: additional items incl. repeat-filer flag (E-2.3-01; Section 14).

**Referral data — E-1.1-03 (11/12/2014).** Servicer identity and Fannie Mae ID; property (address, APN, type, units, occupancy, owner-occupied vs investment, "whether the property is Native American land (tribal trust, allotted, restricted fee)", HOA/co-op contacts); borrower (names, phones, email, mailing address, SSN/TIN, "borrower's current military status (if any)"); loan (servicer loan ID, Fannie Mae loan number, MIN, lien priority, original/current amounts, "total amount past due (reinstatement)", payoff, "itemization of fees, costs, and other charges", 12-month servicing history incl. prior referrals/workouts, MI company, special characteristics such as Texas 50(a)(6)); bankruptcy add-ons.

**Party and name — A2-1-05 (06/21/2017) and E-3.2-09 (10/13/2021).** "Fannie Mae is at all times the owner of the mortgage note"; "Fannie Mae temporarily gives the servicer possession of the mortgage note whenever the servicer, acting in its own name, represents the interests of Fannie Mae in foreclosure actions" — automatically on commencement, reverting at conclusion; physical possession via Form 2009 from the custodian; eNotes per F-1-26. E-3.2-09: "In most states, the law firm must initiate the proceedings in the servicer's name when the servicer is the mortgagee of record"; "The servicer must conduct the foreclosure in Fannie Mae's name when Fannie Mae is the mortgagee of record" (except full-risk/shared-risk loans); transfer-tax states: "an assignment of the mortgage or deed of trust to Fannie Mae must be prepared and recorded in a timely manner" where proceedings must be in Fannie Mae's name to avoid tax; "The servicer must not name MERS as a plaintiff or foreclosing party"; "When MERS is the mortgagee of record, the servicer must prepare an assignment from MERS to the servicer and bring the foreclosure in its own name unless Fannie Mae specifically allows the foreclosure to be brought in the name of Fannie Mae"; MERS-to-Fannie Mae assignments run "in care of the servicer"; no reimbursement for MERS assignment costs; re-registration with MERS after reinstatement is at the servicer's discretion and expense; substitutions of trustee under the LPOA (A2-1-04/F-1-10; E-3.1-03 address). Deficiency: "The servicer must proceed non-judicially in jurisdictions where the preferred or routine method of foreclosure is non-judicial, even if doing so means waiving Fannie Mae's right to pursue a deficiency judgment" unless benefits outweigh time/cost; deficiency waivers may be granted by the servicer on conventional loans to resolve delays, with MI consent (E-3.3-07, 01/18/2017). Method: the Time Frames exhibit lists the "preferred method of foreclosure for each jurisdiction"; "Fannie Mae's Regional Counsel must approve the use of a different methodology prior to foreclosure initiation" via Form 20 (exhibit 06.18.25).

**During foreclosure.** E-3.2-05 (04/10/2019): firm acknowledges the package "within two business days"; servicer supplies missing items "within three business days"; firm notifies the sale date (or estimated court-order docket date); servicer "provide[s] the law firm with bidding instructions at least five business days before the scheduled sale date" (no reimbursement of continuance costs caused by late instructions); advance requests answered "within ten business days"; Louisiana deficiency election communicated. E-3.2-06: outreach ends "60 days prior to foreclosure sale" (judicial) / "30 days" (non-judicial); firm notified within 2 BD of a workout/reinstatement. E-3.2-08 (08/13/2025): "The servicer must accept a full reinstatement of a first lien mortgage loan even if foreclosure proceedings have already begun" — delinquent payments "bearing interest at the rate applicable on the date they became due," late charges, advances, the pre-foreclosure inspection cost, "All expenses, including attorney fees; that were actually incurred"; buydown funds not applied unless the agreement requires; partial reinstatement allowed "if the borrower would qualify for a workout option after application of the partial reinstatement funds"; return the original note via Form 2009; report per F-1-21. E-3.2-10 (11/12/2014): use escrow funds for T&I and security-protection costs, advance if short (F-1-05). E-3.1-04: contact the firm "within one business day after it learns of a bankruptcy filing" on a referred loan. E-3.2-13 (12/16/2015): correct title defects "as part of the foreclosure process when possible"; title-insurer delays "will not excuse the servicer ... or prevent the imposition of compensatory fees."

**Sale.** E-3.3-01 (04/10/2019): pre-sale review "at least 30 days prior to the scheduled foreclosure sale date"; E-3.3-02: certification 7–15 days before sale (13.2); E-3.3-03: inspection "within 35 days prior to foreclosure sale" (D2-2-10/A4-2.1-02); E-3.3-04/F-1-08: use the Foreclosure Sale Marketing and Auction Services Exhibit vendors where listed (Fannie Mae pays engaged vendors). **E-3.3-05 (05/10/2023)** bidding: "The servicer must issue bidding instructions to the law firm for all mortgage loans referred for foreclosure"; uninsured conventional first liens — bid "the lesser of: 100% of the reserve price obtained from Fannie Mae, or 100% of the total mortgage indebtedness minus any outstanding property or flood insurance claims"; in non-redemption, transfer-tax states without exemption "enter an initial bid of $100 (or any other minimum amount the state requires) and continue bidding until it either wins ... or bids" that lesser amount; insured loans — "bid an amount approved by the mortgage insurer" (else the uninsured rule); property valuation ordered "through Fannie Mae's servicing solutions system as soon as it is aware of the foreclosure sale date, but no earlier than 90 days prior"; results "within 10 calendar days"; reserve price requested "between 30 and 90 days before the scheduled foreclosure sale date" with reason code "Reserve Price Bid Instructions"; "always use an unexpired reserve price"; if not available in time, "bid the total indebtedness amount minus any outstanding property or flood insurance claim(s)"; do not issue bids where significant uninsured hazard damage is found — contact the Servicing Representative; resale-restriction cases to the Servicing Representative. **E-3.5-02 (07/12/2017)** third-party sales: "Collect the gross sale proceeds and remit the amount Fannie Mae is due within five business days after it receives the final payment from the third-party bidder"; failed sale — remit the bidder's deposit "within five business days after discovery"; cancel insurance within 14 days of the later of sale completion or confirmation; keep inspecting until then; "Any excess sales proceeds must be distributed in accordance with applicable law"; F-1-08: "Remove the mortgage loan from Fannie Mae's active accounting records by reporting Action Code 71" in the sale month, special remittance per F-1-20, closing statement to SF CPM the same day. Fannie Mae acquisitions: REOgram in Property 360 confirmed within 1 BD (E-4.1-01; 15.1; research/00b F5).

**Reg X / other federal.** 13.1/13.2 gates; 1024.39/.40 continue; FDCPA (if Supermortgage is a "debt collector" for loans boarded in default — Section 11.4) affects validation and communications, not the filing; SCRA 13.8 (DMDC before referral, affidavit before default judgment, no sale during service + 1 year); Bankruptcy Code §362 (14.x); MERS Rules (assignment from MERS by a certifying officer before foreclosure; MERS never the foreclosing party — research/00b N1).

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) The row cites "Property 360" as a referral system — referral runs through the attorney network; Property 360 is used for PFPIP and delinquent-property inspections (9.8), expense claims (15.2) and REOgram (15.1). (2) The row omits the document/data package (E-1.1-02/-03), the 95th-day custodian request and 90th-day mortgagee-of-record identification, the MERS assignment, the name-of-party rules and the deficiency/method rules. (3) "Refer by 120th day" applies to non-principal residences only; principal residences are "no earlier than day 121" with no Guide "no later than." (4) Section 2.7 uses `foreclosure.referred`; the canonical event here (and in 5.4/8.x/9.x) is `foreclosure.referral.sent` — 2.7 should alias.

#### State machine
`foreclosure_cases.status`: `prereferral` (13.4) → `referred` (package sent; `foreclosure.referral.sent`) → `acknowledged` (firm ack ≤2 BD) → `pre_first_legal` (title, assignment recording, state notices maturing) → `first_legal` (complaint filed / NOD recorded / NOS posted; `foreclosure.first_notice.filed`) → `in_process` (service, publication, mediation, answer/contest) → `judgment` (judicial: judgment entered) → `sale_scheduled` → `sale_held` → `post_sale` (confirmation/ratification, redemption period, deed) → terminal `closed_fnma_acquired` (REOgram accepted, 15.1) | `closed_third_party` (proceeds remitted) | `closed_reinstated` | `closed_paid_off` | `closed_workout` | `closed_cancelled` (charge-off, assignment to MI, rescission). Holds (13.2) overlay any state; `on_hold_bankruptcy` (14.x) freezes and refers back on relief; `rescinded` re-enters `sale_scheduled`. Actors: `foreclosure-ops` (all transitions on evidence), `attorney` (court steps by milestone report), `signing_officer` (assignment/affidavit execution events), `fnma_portal_operator` (REOgram, valuation portal fallback).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Referral eligibility** = 13.4 review complete with outcome `refer` ∧ all gates open for step `refer`: `REGX_1024_41F1_120_DAY_GATE` (principal residence), `REGX_1024_41F2_PRE_FILING_APP_GATE`, `REGX_1024_41K2_NO_FIRST_FILING_GATE`, `FNMA_E1202_REFER_NO_EARLIER_121`, `BK_362_STAY_GATE`, `SCRA_3953C_FC_PROTECTION_GATE` + DMDC ≤30 days old, `FNMA_D1301_DISASTER_FC_APPROVAL_GATE`, `LITIGATION_HOLD`/`ENVIRONMENTAL_HOLD` (13.7), `STATE_MN_582_043_DUAL_TRACK_GATE`, `TITLE_HOLD` (uncured defect blocking the action), and package readiness (note copy/original or LNA, assignment status, data set complete).
2. **Foreclosing party** (open decision 13.3-1, default): MERS→partner assignment; foreclosure in the partner's name; Supermortgage prepares/executes as attorney-in-fact under the partner's LPOA and as MERS certifying officer; where the state requires Fannie Mae's name to avoid transfer tax (`jurisdiction_rules.transfer_tax_exemption=false ∧ fnma_name_required`), MERS→Fannie Mae "c/o [partner]" per E-3.2-09/E-3.1-03 with a Form 20 only if a non-preferred method is used.
3. **First-notice authorization**: after referral the firm may complete title and prepare the first legal, but must not file/record/publish until `foreclosure.first_notice.authorized` — which requires 13.1/13.2/state gates open on that date (re-evaluated, not cached) and assignment recorded where the state requires pre-foreclosure recordation.
4. **Reinstatement quote during foreclosure** (E-3.2-08; 16.x engine): `reinstatement = Σ delinquent P&I (interest at the contractual rate in effect on each due date) + late charges (unless suppressed) + escrow advances + corporate advances (inspection, preservation) + attorney fees earned to the last completed milestone + costs actually incurred`; quotes are good-through-dated; full tender before sale must be accepted; partial reinstatement only if it makes the borrower eligible for a workout (12.x). Example: 6 payments × $1,420.11 = $8,520.66 + late charges $284.02 + escrow advances $3,105.40 + inspections $60.00 + attorney fees at "service complete" milestone (70% of allowable $A) + costs $612.00 ⇒ quote; on receipt: `loan.reinstated`, firm notified ≤2 BD, Form 2009 return of the original note if pulled, status code update (F-1-21).
5. **Bid** (E-3.3-05): `total_indebtedness = UPB + accrued interest at the note rate from LPI due date to sale date + escrow advances + corporate advances + allowable attorney fees/costs incurred + late charges where permitted − outstanding property/flood insurance claims`. Worked example: UPB $250,000.00; note 6.50%; LPI due Sept. 1, 2025; sale Nov. 3, 2026 (428 days): interest = 250,000 × 0.065 × 428/365 = **$19,054.79**; escrow advances $6,842.17; corporate advances $1,975.00; attorney fees $2,150.00; costs $1,487.50; no insurance claim ⇒ total **$281,509.46**. Reserve price from Fannie Mae $310,000 (expires Nov. 20) ⇒ bid = min(310,000.00, 281,509.46) = **$281,509.46**. If the state has a transfer tax without exemption and no redemption: open at $100 (or statutory minimum) and bid up to $281,509.46. If the reserve price had been $270,000: bid $270,000.00 (lesser). Interest is computed with `decimal.js`, rounded half-up to cents once at the end; per-diem shown on the instruction for the firm to extend to a postponed date.
6. **Third-party sale accounting**: winning bid $290,000.00 > $281,509.46 ⇒ surplus **$8,490.54** held in `tps_surplus_payable` pending distribution "in accordance with applicable law" (junior liens/borrower; court or trustee in many states); Fannie Mae due = UPB + interest at the pass-through rate + advances per F-1-20 (5.x computes the special remittance); remit within 5 BD of final payment; Action Code 71 in the sale month; closing statement to SF CPM same day; MI claim for any shortfall (15.3). If the winning bid is below indebtedness, shortfall = deficiency basis (E-3.3-07 policy).
7. **Fannie Mae acquisition**: DRA sale event → REOgram auto-created in Property 360 → confirmation within 1 BD (15.1); Action Code 70/72 (5.x); insurance/flood cancellation timers; eviction referral to the same firm (E-4.3-04; Section 15).
8. **Rounding/day counts**: calendar days for statutory windows; Fannie Mae SLAs in `business_days_servicer` except remittances in `business_days_fannie_et`; interest per diem = UPB × rate / 365 (loan terms dictate 360/365 — use `loan_terms.interest_basis`).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`foreclosure-ops` assembles the package (calls `documents.get`, `custodian.request`, `mers.assignment.prepare`, `erecording.submit`, `jurisdiction.rules.get`, `notices.render/send`, `attorney.referral.send`), tracks milestones, prepares bids (`fnma.valuation.order`, `fnma.reserve_price.request`, `ledger.balances.get`, `payoff.compute`), issues instructions and reconciles sale results. Model use: extracting data from the note/allonge images (verify endorsement chain), reading firm correspondence/dockets into milestones, drafting Form 20/exception memos. Decision record: `{case_id, step, gates[], package_manifest_hash, jurisdiction_rule_version, computed_amounts{reinstatement, indebtedness, bid}, basis, firm_id, rationale, model_version}`. Guardrails: money figures computed by deterministic code; the model never edits amounts; any bid below total indebtedness without an unexpired reserve price is refused; any deviation from the preferred method needs Form 20 (13.7) and `officer` approval. Escalations: `signing_officer` (assignments, substitutions of trustee, lost-note and other affidavits — package: instrument, evidence, notary/RON slot); `attorney` (all filings; deficiency and method questions; contested matters); `fnma_portal_operator` (reserve price/valuation portal fallback, REOgram, DRA view, HomeTracker); `officer` (foreclosing-party exceptions, deficiency waiver above delegation, Fannie Mae approvals); `human_agent` on borrower request. Disclosure/consent: outbound contact during foreclosure follows 11.x (TCPA consent for AI voice; state AI-disclosure). AI-off path: same commands from an ops-console queue.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Firm rejects the package as incomplete: missing items due in 3 BD; the referral date stands (E-1.2-02) — track in 13.5.
- Assignment recording rejected by the county (legal description mismatch): fix and re-record; if pre-foreclosure recordation is required, `first_notice` stays closed; status BE (title) for delay credit.
- Note image shows a broken endorsement chain: `TITLE_HOLD`; escalate to `attorney`; lost-note affidavit only after a documented diligent search.
- MI company issues its own bidding instructions: basis `mi_instruction`; conflict with Fannie Mae reserve ⇒ MI controls (E-3.3-05) — record both.
- Reserve price expires before a postponed sale: refresh (30–90-day window) or fall back to indebtedness; never bid on an expired reserve.
- Significant uninsured hazard damage found at the pre-sale inspection: no bid; Servicing Representative contact; 9.x claim.
- Sale postponed for the servicer's late bid instructions: continuance costs non-reimbursable; 13.6 root-cause entry.
- Bankruptcy filed the morning of sale: firm notified within 1 BD (target: immediately by phone); sale held in violation of the stay ⇒ 14.x/E-2.3-06 rescission.
- Transfer-in of a loan already in foreclosure (1.3): case boarded with milestones, firm assignment (Form 200 coverage for the *partner*), referral date from the transferor; gaps flagged.
- Transfer-out (17.x): package, milestones and firm hand-off in the transfer file; firm transfer counts toward the 30-in-6-months rule if the same state.
- Redemption states (MN, MI, etc.): post-sale redemption timer; payoff during redemption per state formula (16.x).
- Eviction/possession (15.x) never started before confirmation/redemption expiry.

- 13.3-T4 Given NY, Then `NTC_STATE_PREFC_NY_1304` renders with ≥5 county agencies, certified + first-class mail evidence, §1306 filing within 3 BD; first notice refused before day 90.
- 13.3-T5 Given the worked bid example, Then bid = $281,509.46 with reserve $310,000; = $270,000.00 with reserve $270,000; transfer-tax state ⇒ incremental bidding from $100.
- 13.3-T6 Given a third-party sale at $290,000, Then surplus $8,490.54 booked, proceeds remitted within 5 BD of final payment, Action Code 71 in the sale month, closing statement sent same day.
- 13.3-T7 Given a full reinstatement tendered 2 days before sale, Then accepted, firm notified ≤2 BD (target same day), sale cancelled, note returned via Form 2009.
- 13.3-T8 Given the firm requests a document, When 3 BD pass without response, Then sev-1 escalation and comp-fee exposure flagged.
- 13.3-T9 Given a bankruptcy filed after referral, Then firm notified within 1 BD, case `on_hold_bankruptcy`, referral-back on relief to the same firm.

- 13.3-T12 Given a transfer-in of a case with `first_notice_filed_at` evidenced, Then no state pre-foreclosure notice is re-sent and 13.5 uses the transferor's LPI date.
