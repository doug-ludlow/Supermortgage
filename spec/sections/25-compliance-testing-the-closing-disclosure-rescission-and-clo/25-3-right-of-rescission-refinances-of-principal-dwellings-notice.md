### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Who may rescind — §1026.23(a)(1) (eCFR current as of 9/08/2026; 00a-fed §2).** "each consumer whose ownership interest is or will be subject to the security interest shall have the right to rescind the transaction, except for transactions described in paragraph (f)." §1026.2(a)(11) (eCFR 9/04/2026): "for purposes of rescission under §§ 1026.15 and 1026.23, the term also includes a natural person in whose principal dwelling a security interest is or will be retained or acquired, if that person's ownership interest in the dwelling is or will be subject to the security interest." Comment 23(a)(1)-2 (CFPB Official Interpretations, read 2026-09-10): "To be a consumer within the meaning of § 1026.2, that person must at least have an ownership interest in the dwelling that is encumbered by the creditor's security interest" and "if only one spouse signs a credit contract, the other spouse is a consumer if the ownership interest of that spouse is subject to the security interest". §1026.17(d) (eCFR 9/04/2026): "If the transaction is rescindable under § 1026.23, however, the disclosures shall be made to each consumer who has the right to rescind."

**Which transactions — §1026.23(f).** Exempt: "(f)(1) A residential mortgage transaction." (§1026.2(a)(24): a security interest "created or retained in the consumer's principal dwelling to finance the acquisition or initial construction of that dwelling" — purchase-money); "(f)(2) A refinancing or consolidation by the same creditor of an extension of credit already secured by the consumer's principal dwelling. The right of rescission shall apply, however, to the extent the new amount financed exceeds the unpaid principal balance, any earned unpaid finance charge on the existing debt, and amounts attributed solely to the costs of the refinancing or consolidation."; (f)(3) state-agency creditor; (f)(4) subsequent advances in a series if the notice and material disclosures were given; (f)(5) renewal of optional insurance. Comment 23(f)-4: "The exemption in § 1026.23(f)(2) applies only to refinancings (including consolidations) by the original creditor."; "If the refinancing involves a new advance of money, the amount of the new advance is rescindable."; "In a merger, consolidation or acquisition, the successor institution is considered the original creditor". Because the right belongs to consumers whose *principal dwelling* is encumbered, loans on second homes and investment properties carry no right; the transaction type does not matter (a cash-out refinance of a rental is not rescindable; a rate-and-term refinance of a principal dwelling by a new creditor is).

**How and when — §1026.23(a)(2)–(3).** (a)(2): the consumer notifies the creditor "by mail, telegram or other means of written communication. Notice is considered given when mailed, when filed for telegraphic transmission or, if sent by other means, when delivered to the creditor's designated place of business." (a)(3)(i): the consumer may exercise "until midnight of the third business day following consummation, delivery of the notice required by paragraph (b) of this section, or delivery of all material disclosures, whichever occurs last." Business day = §1026.2(a)(6) second sentence ("all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays"; the observed date of a weekend holiday is a business day — comment 2(a)(6)-2, 25.2). Comment 23(a)(3)-1: "The period within which the consumer may exercise the right to rescind runs for 3 business days from the last of 3 events" with the example "If a transaction is consummated on Friday, June 1, and the disclosures and notice of the right to rescind were given on Thursday, May 31, the rescission period will expire at midnight of the third business day after June 1 - that is, Tuesday, June 5." Extended right, (a)(3)(i) second sentence: "If the required notice or material disclosures are not delivered, the right to rescind shall expire 3 years after consummation, upon transfer of all of the consumer's interest in the property, or upon sale of the property, whichever occurs first." Comment 23(a)(3)-3: "When the creditor has failed to take the action necessary to start the three-business day rescission period running, the right to rescind automatically lapses on the occurrence of the earliest of the following three events". Material disclosures, (a)(3)(ii): "the required disclosures of the annual percentage rate, the finance charge, the amount financed, the total of payments, the payment schedule, and the disclosures and limitations referred to in §§ 1026.32(c) and (d) and 1026.43(g)." Comment 23(a)(3)-2: "Failure to provide information regarding the annual percentage rate also includes failure to inform the consumer of the existence of a variable rate feature." Accuracy for this purpose is judged under (g): the finance charge and affected disclosures are accurate if the finance charge "Is understated by no more than 1⁄2 of 1 percent of the face amount of the note or $100, whichever is greater" ((g)(1)(i)) or overstated; for a refinancing by a new creditor with no new advances, "Is understated by no more than 1 percent of the face amount of the note or $100, whichever is greater" ((g)(2)(i)); after foreclosure is initiated, (h): the right survives if "A mortgage broker fee that should have been included in the finance charge was not included" or "The creditor did not provide the properly completed appropriate model form", and the tolerance becomes "Is understated by no more than $35". §1026.22(a)(4) ties the APR's accuracy for rescission to these (g)/(h) tests (25.1).

**Notice — §1026.23(b).** (b)(1): "A creditor shall deliver two copies of the notice of the right to rescind to each consumer entitled to rescind (one copy to each if the notice is delivered in electronic form in accordance with the consumer consent and other applicable provisions of the E-Sign Act)." Content: the retention or acquisition of a security interest in the consumer's principal dwelling; the consumer's right to rescind; "How to exercise the right to rescind, with a form for that purpose, designating the address of the creditor's place of business"; the effects of rescission; the date the rescission period expires. (b)(2): "the appropriate model form in appendix H of this part or a substantially similar notice." Comment 23(b)(1)-1: "Each consumer entitled to rescind must be given two copies of the rescission notice and the material disclosures." and, for spouses, "both must receive the notice of the right to rescind and disclosures". Appendix H (eCFR 9/03/2026) — H-8 "NOTICE OF RIGHT TO CANCEL": "You have a legal right under Federal law to cancel this transaction, without cost, within three business days from whichever of the following events occurs last: (1) the date of this transaction, which is ***_******_*; or (2) the date you received your Truth in Lending disclosures; or (3) the date you received this notice of your right to cancel."; how to cancel "not later than midnight of *****_"; "Within 20 calendar days after we receive your notice of cancellation, we must take the steps necessary to reflect the fact that the [mortgage/lien/security interest] [on/in] your home has been cancelled."; signature block "I WISH TO CANCEL". H-9 (refinancing with the original creditor): "You are entering into a new transaction to increase the amount of credit previously provided to you."; "You have a legal right under Federal law to cancel this new transaction, without cost, within three business days from whichever of the following events occurs last: (1) the date of this new transaction, which is ******_***__; or (2) the date you received your new Truth in Lending disclosures; or (3) the date you received this notice of your right to cancel."; "Within 20 calendar days after we receive your notice of cancellation of this new transaction, we must take the steps necessary to reflect the fact that your home does not secure the increase of credit."

**Delay of performance — §1026.23(c).** "Unless a consumer waives the right of rescission under paragraph (e) of this section, no money shall be disbursed other than in escrow, no services shall be performed and no materials delivered until the rescission period has expired and the creditor is reasonably satisfied that the consumer has not rescinded." Comment 23(c): "the creditor must not, either directly or through a third party: Disburse loan proceeds to the consumer"; "The creditor must wait until it is reasonably satisfied that the consumer has not rescinded"; "The creditor may disburse loan proceeds during the rescission period in a valid escrow arrangement"; one way to be reasonably satisfied is "Waiting a reasonable time after expiration of the rescission period to allow for delivery of a mailed notice". **The rule contains no requirement that the disbursement day be a business day** — after midnight ending the third business day, disbursement is lawful on any day on which the creditor is reasonably satisfied; the practical constraint is the Federal Reserve holiday calendar (frbservices.org, read 2026-09-10: 2026 holidays include Columbus Day October 12, Veterans Day November 11, Thanksgiving November 26, Christmas December 25; "For holidays falling on Saturday, Federal Reserve Banks and Branches will be open the preceding Friday"; Sunday holidays are observed "the following Monday").

**Waiver — §1026.23(e).** "The consumer may modify or waive the right to rescind if the consumer determines that the extension of credit is needed to meet a bona fide personal financial emergency. To modify or waive the right, the consumer shall give the creditor a dated written statement that describes the emergency, specifically modifies or waives the right to rescind, and bears the signature of all the consumers entitled to rescind. Printed forms for this purpose are prohibited." Comment 23(e)-1: "the consumer must have a bona fide personal financial emergency that must be met before the end of the rescission period"; "Each consumer entitled to rescind must sign the waiver statement".

**Effects — §1026.23(d).** (d)(1): "The security interest giving rise to the right of rescission becomes void and the consumer shall not be liable for any amount, including any finance charge." (d)(2): "Within 20 calendar days after receipt of a notice of rescission, the creditor shall return any money or property that has been given to anyone in connection with the transaction and shall take any action necessary to reflect the termination of the security interest." (d)(3): the consumer may retain possession until the creditor performs, then tenders the money or property (or its reasonable value); (d)(4): "The procedures outlined in paragraphs (d)(2) and (3) of this section may be modified by court order." Comment 23(d)(2)-1: "Any amounts of this nature already paid by the consumer must be refunded. 'Any amount' includes finance charges already accrued, as well as other charges, such as broker fees".

**Fannie Mae interplay (00a-fnma §5.4).** C2-2-01 (08/07/2018): "The first payment date must be no later than two months from the final disbursement date of the loan proceeds." The rescission-driven disbursement date therefore bounds the first payment date; 26.3 owns `FNMA_B2_1_5_FIRST_PAYMENT_2M`. Texas 50(a)(6) home-equity loans carry a separate constitutional three-day rescission (26.1) — Tex. Const. art. XVI §50(a)(6)(Q)(viii), read on statutes.capitol.texas.gov 2026-09-11: "the owner of the homestead and any spouse of the owner may, within three days after the extension of credit is made, rescind the extension of credit" — **VERIFIED** (the constitution does not say "business days"; 26.1 owns the day-count rule under 7 TAC ch. 153; the platform takes the later expiry).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Disbursement no earlier than the next business day": §1026.23(c) imposes no business-day condition on the disbursement day; the platform's `disburse` command is gated on period expiry plus the reasonable-satisfaction step, and the Fedwire calendar (not Reg Z) pushes the fixture's disbursement from Wed Nov 11 (Veterans Day) to Thu Nov 12, 2026.
2. The period is measured from the **latest** of consummation, delivery of the notice and delivery of all material disclosures — the blueprint anchors it on consummation only.
3. Copies: two per consumer on paper, **one** per consumer when delivered electronically under E-SIGN — omitted by the blueprint.
4. Same-creditor refinances: under the operating model many refinances are of partner-originated loans → (f)(2) applies and only the new advance is rescindable (H-9); refinances of other lenders' loans are fully rescindable (H-8). The blueprint does not distinguish.
5. The blueprint omits the 3-year extended right, the (g)/(h) accuracy tolerances that decide whether the material disclosures were "delivered", and §1026.17(d) (each rescinding consumer must receive the CD).
6. Fixture calendar: the brief's "consummation Thu Nov 25" is wrong — Nov 25, 2026 is a **Wednesday**; the Thanksgiving example below uses Wed Nov 25.
7. Fannie Mae C2-2-01's two-month first-payment rule is a hidden constraint on how late a rescission-delayed disbursement may fall.

#### State machine

`not_applicable` (purchase-money; not a principal dwelling; same creditor with no new advance) — terminal. Otherwise `pending_consummation` → (on `closing.consummated` with notices and material disclosures delivered) `running` → (at `expires_at`, no valid exercise) `expired_not_rescinded` → (after the sweep) `funding_released` (event `rescission.confirmed_not_rescinded`; `fundings` may proceed). `running` → `waived` (accepted waiver; funding may proceed immediately). `running` | `expired_not_rescinded` (before satisfaction) | `extended_3y` → `rescinded` on a valid exercise → `unwinding` → `closed`. `running`/`expired_not_rescinded` → `extended_3y` when a post-closing review (28.1/28.2, 25.1 delivery re-run) finds the notice or a material disclosure defective; `extended_3y` lapses at the earliest of consummation + 3 years, transfer of all the consumer's interest, or sale (comment 23(a)(3)-3) — servicing 9.x/16.x observe those events. Transitions are performed by the `disclosure` agent (notices, period), the `funder` agent (release), and the `officer` (waiver acceptance, unwind sign-off).

#### Business rules and calculations

**Applicability (`determineRescindability`).** `not_principal_dwelling` if `occupancy ≠ primary` for every consumer with an ownership interest (a co-owner who lives in the property as a principal dwelling makes the loan rescindable as to that consumer even if the borrower does not — evaluate per consumer). `exempt_purchase_money` if `transaction_type = purchase` (residential mortgage transaction). Refinance: if `original_creditor_id = partner_id` (including a predecessor merged into the partner — comment 23(f)-4): `rescindable_amount = amount_financed − (UPB_existing + earned_unpaid_finance_charge_existing + costs_of_refinancing)`; if `rescindable_amount ≤ 0` → `exempt_same_creditor_no_new_money`; else `rescindable_new_advance` with form H-9. If the original creditor is anyone else (including a loan the partner merely subservices for another lender's origination, or a loan Fannie Mae owns that another lender originated) → `rescindable_full`, form H-8. Fannie Mae's ownership of the existing loan does not make Fannie Mae the "creditor" of the new loan and does not change the original-creditor test.

**Worked example A — fixture, H-8 (other-lender loan).** The $560,000.00 limited cash-out refinance pays off a loan originated in 2021 by a different lender (subserviced by SM). Both spouses are on title; only one is a borrower. `consumers` = {borrower, non-borrower spouse}. Notices: two paper copies each at the RON signing on Fri Nov 6, 2026 (the RON platform renders two copies per consumer; e-delivered H-8 with E-SIGN consent needs one copy each — decision 25.3-Q1 keeps two copies even electronically). Material disclosures: CD v1 received Mon Nov 2 by both (25.2). `period_start_date` = max(consummation Fri Nov 6; notice Fri Nov 6; CD Mon Nov 2) = Fri Nov 6. Count: Sat Nov 7 (1), Sun Nov 8 (excluded), Mon Nov 9 (2), Tue Nov 10 (3) → `expires_at` = Tue Nov 10, 2026 24:00 MST (stored as 2026-11-11T07:00:00Z). Wed Nov 11 is Veterans Day: not a Reg Z business day, but Reg Z does not care about the disbursement day; the Federal Reserve is closed, so the wire cannot move. `SM_O63_SATISFACTION_SWEEP` runs Wed Nov 11 08:00 MST (mail room, e-mail, portal, fax: nothing received; USPS does not deliver on Veterans Day — the 2-day allowance keeps watching through Thu Nov 12); `reasonably_satisfied_at` = Nov 11 08:05; `funder` releases the wire Thu Nov 12 at Fed open → disbursement Thu Nov 12, 2026 (fixture). Interest starts Nov 12 (19 odd days to Dec 1; 25.1); first payment Fri Jan 1, 2027 is within two months of disbursement (C2-2-01).

**Worked example B — Thanksgiving.** Consummation Wed Nov 25, 2026 (the brief's "Thu Nov 25" does not exist), notices and CD delivered on or before that date. Count: Thu Nov 26 Thanksgiving (excluded), Fri Nov 27 (1), Sat Nov 28 (2), Sun Nov 29 (excluded), Mon Nov 30 (3) → expires midnight Mon Nov 30 → sweep Tue Dec 1 08:00 → disbursement Tue Dec 1, 2026. First payment no later than two months later → Feb 1, 2027 at the latest; the platform sets Jan 1, 2027 (per-diem interest for Dec 1–31 = 31 days at $93.97 = $2,913.15, or Feb 1 with an interest credit — 26.3 decides).

**Worked example C — corrected CD moved the closing (25.2 worked example 5).** Consummation Mon Nov 9, 2026: Tue Nov 10 (1), Wed Nov 11 (excluded), Thu Nov 12 (2), Fri Nov 13 (3) → expires midnight Fri Nov 13 → the disbursement could lawfully occur Sat Nov 14, but Fedwire is closed on weekends → Mon Nov 16, 2026 (the delivery date in the fixture then slips; 29.4 re-plans).

**Worked example D — late notice.** Consummation Fri Nov 6 but the non-borrower spouse's H-8 copies were not delivered until Mon Nov 9 (she was not at the signing; the courier delivered Monday, signed receipt). Her period: max(Nov 6, Nov 9, Nov 2) = Mon Nov 9 → Tue 10 (1), Wed 11 (excluded), Thu 12 (2), Fri 13 (3) → expires midnight Fri Nov 13; the loan's `expires_at` is the latest across consumers → Fri Nov 13; disbursement Mon Nov 16. Had the copies never been delivered, `status = extended_3y` with `extended_expires_at` = Tue Nov 6, 2029 (or earlier on sale/transfer).

**Worked example E — H-9 new-advance arithmetic.** Suppose instead the existing loan was originated by the partner: UPB $548,200.00; earned unpaid interest through the disbursement date $2,100.55; costs of the refinancing (all closing costs financed, whether or not finance charges) $3,850.00 + $4,100.00 = $7,950.00; amount financed $556,150.00 (25.1). `rescindable_amount` = 556,150.00 − (548,200.00 + 2,100.55 + 7,950.00) = **$−2,100.55 → ≤ 0 → exempt_same_creditor_no_new_money**: no notice, no waiting period; disbursement may follow consummation immediately (dry-state recording permitting). With $2,000.00 cash back to the borrower the sum becomes: amount financed unchanged but the payoff smaller — say UPB $544,000.00 → rescindable_amount = 556,150.00 − (544,000.00 + 2,100.55 + 7,950.00) = **$2,099.45** → H-9 for the increase of $2,099.45 only; the platform still holds the *entire* disbursement until expiry (decision 25.3-Q3: the payoff and the new advance cannot be separated in practice without an escrow disbursement of the exempt portion, which the platform does not do).

**Material-disclosure accuracy for the period start.** The period does not start for a consumer until "all material disclosures" are delivered: the CD's APR, finance charge, amount financed, total of payments and payment schedule (projected payments), plus §1026.32(c)/(d) (HOEPA — never present) and §1026.43(g) (prepayment penalty — never present). Delivery is measured by 25.2's `effective_receipt_date` of the CD version whose figures are accurate under (g): finance charge overstated or understated by ≤ max(0.5% × face amount, $100) = max($2,800.00, $100) = **$2,800.00** on the fixture (1% = **$5,600.00** if a new-creditor refinance with no new advance — the fixture has a $2,000 cash-back, so 0.5% applies); APR accurate under §1026.22(a)(4) if it results from that finance charge. 25.1 stores the (g) result on `apr_calculations.accuracy_basis` and 25.3 copies it to `material_disclosures_accurate`; a `false` at post-closing review flips the period to `extended_3y`.

**Exercise.** A written notice from any consumer entitled to rescind, on the form or otherwise, is valid if `given_at ≤ expires_at` (mailed: postmark date; other: delivery to the designated place of business — the notice "is considered given when mailed"). One consumer's rescission rescinds the transaction for all. Unwind within 20 calendar days of receipt: if not yet disbursed — cancel the wire, void the eNote (MERS eRegistry Change Status/Registration Reversal — 26.2/26.4), record the release/reconveyance of the security instrument if it was recorded, refund every amount paid by any consumer (including finance charges accrued, appraisal/credit fees paid in cash — comment 23(d)(2)-1 "Any amount"), and return the partner's/SM's third-party costs to the SM cost ledger (SM bears them under the Grander economics); if disbursed (extended-right case, or a mailed notice arriving after funding) — reverse the payoff where possible or treat the paid-off lender's release as the consumer's tender problem per (d)(3), refund all amounts paid, terminate the security interest, and coordinate with the warehouse (`warehouse_advances.repaid_from = partner_repurchase` if Fannie Mae has purchased — 27.1/29.4). `refund_due_at` = `received_at` + 20 calendar days: notice received Mon Nov 9, 2026 → due Sun Nov 29 → the platform completes by Fri Nov 27.

**Waiver.** Accepted only on a consumer-authored, dated, signed statement describing the emergency, signed by every consumer entitled to rescind; the portal offers a blank field with no template wording; `officer` accepts or rejects within 4 hours (escalation SLA); on acceptance `status = waived` and `disburse` may proceed. Expected volume: near zero; the agent never suggests a waiver.

**Confirmation of non-rescission practice.** The rule requires the creditor to be "reasonably satisfied", not a signed confirmation. Platform practice: (i) channel sweep at 08:00 local the day after expiry (mail with postmarks, e-mail, portal, fax, voicemail transcriptions flagged as possible rescission); (ii) optional borrower e-acknowledgement "I have not cancelled" sent at expiry — never a condition of funding and never described as a waiver; (iii) 2-day allowance for mailed notices. `satisfaction_basis` records which applied.

#### Integrations
- **eClosing/RON platform** (26.2): renders and delivers the H-8/H-9 copies to each consumer at signing (paper copies printed by the notary/settlement agent for hybrid/wet; electronic copies with E-SIGN consent for RON), returns per-consumer delivery evidence and the consummation timestamp.
- **Print/mail and courier**: copies for consumers not present at signing (non-borrower owners); signed receipt required; mailbox rule is not used for the rescission notice (delivery is what counts — the platform requires evidence).
- **Inbound channels**: mail-room scanning with postmark OCR; e-mail inbox; borrower portal message classifier; fax gateway — all feed the document AI classifier `rescission_notice_candidate` with a human-review fallback (`human_agent`) for ambiguous documents.
- **`funder` agent / warehouse bank** (26.3, 27.1): `fundings.rescission_expires_at`; wire release only after `rescission.confirmed_not_rescinded`; Fedwire holiday calendar from the bank adapter.
- **MERS eRegistry / eVault; eRecording** (26.2, 26.4): unwind operations (eNote status change/reversal; release recording).
- **Servicing 2.x/9.x/16.x** (30.4 hand-off): `loans.rescission_extended_until`; payoff and lien-release processes check it; sale/transfer events lapse it.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`disclosure` agent (rescission tools: `determineRescindability`, `renderRescissionNotice`, `deliverRescissionNotice`, `computeRescissionPeriod`, `sweepInboundForRescission`, `classifyInboundDocument`, `writeDecision`) and `funder` agent (`assertGateOpen(REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE)`, `releaseFunding`) with `closer` agent (`unwindClosing`). End-to-end: applicability decided at `closing.scheduled` from title/vesting and payoff data; notices rendered per consumer with the computed expiry date; delivered at signing with evidence; period computed the moment `closing.consummated` arrives and re-computed on any later delivery; daily sweeps; funding released after satisfaction; exercises processed within 20 days with a full unwind checklist. Decision record: `{rescission_id, applicability, form, rescindable_amount_cents, consumers[], deliveries[], period_start_date, expires_at, satisfaction{at, basis, channels_checked}, waiver_id, exercise_id, rationale, model_version, prompt_version}`. Guardrails: never mark a notice delivered without per-consumer evidence; never compute the period from consummation alone; never release funding before `expires_at` unless a `rescission_waivers` row is accepted by `officer`; never draft or suggest waiver language; never classify an inbound "I want to cancel" message as anything other than a rescission candidate (even if oral — an oral statement is not a valid exercise, but it triggers a human contact to tell the consumer how to exercise in writing before midnight); never separate the new-advance and payoff disbursements for an H-9 loan. Escalations: `officer` (waiver acceptance within 4 hours; every exercise; any late-notice period extension that moves funding beyond the lock/commitment expiry); `settlement_agent`/`notary` (paper copies at hybrid/wet closings); `human_agent` on request; `mlo_of_record` informed (not approving) of any rescission. Borrower-facing: the agent explains the right in plain language only by reading the notice's own text and answering procedural questions ("how do I cancel" → "sign and send the form to the address on it by midnight of {date}"); automation disclosure at the start of every interaction.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Non-borrower owner not at signing: courier copies with signed receipt; period runs from her delivery date; if refused, the loan cannot fund (period never starts) — escalate `officer`; consider re-closing.
- Inter vivos trust vesting (24.4): the settlor-beneficiary who occupies the dwelling is a consumer with an ownership interest — notices to the settlor(s) individually and as trustee.
- Consummation after 5 p.m. or on a Saturday: the transaction date is the local calendar date of signing; a Saturday consummation counts day 1 on Monday only if Sunday intervenes (Sat Nov 14 → Mon 16 (1), Tue 17 (2), Wed 18 (3)).
- Notice with the wrong expiry date printed: the notice is not "properly completed" — comment/(h)(1)(ii) risk; re-deliver a corrected notice (restarts a 3-business-day period) before funding; if discovered after funding → `extended_3y`.
- CD corrected after the notice with a material change: the period restarts from delivery of the corrected material disclosures; the printed expiry date on the H-8 becomes stale → re-deliver the notice with the new date.
- Rescission notice arrives by mail postmarked on the expiry date but delivered two days after funding: valid; unwind within 20 days of receipt.
- Oral cancellation call at 11:30 p.m. on day 3: not a valid exercise; the agent immediately e-mails the form and instructions and logs the contact; funding is held for the 2-day mail allowance anyway.
- Borrower rescinds, then asks to "un-rescind": no mechanism; a new transaction requires new disclosures and a new period.
- Lock/commitment expiry collides with a rescission-extended funding date (21.4/29.1): the agent requests extension before the notice re-delivery; never funds early.
- Extended-right loan reaches servicing: payoff, modification and lien-release processes (servicing 9.x/16.x) must treat the (a)(3) three-year flag; sale or transfer of all interest lapses it.
- Texas 50(a)(6): both the constitutional and Reg Z rights run; the later expiry governs.
- Same-creditor determination error discovered post-funding (H-9 used but the prior creditor was different): notice defective as to the exempt-portion statement → `extended_3y` and legal review.

#### Audit and evidence
- Per loan: applicability decision with the original-creditor evidence and the new-advance arithmetic; per-consumer notice copies with delivery evidence (signing-session audit trail, courier receipts, e-delivery certificates); the period computation with the calendar used and the three anchor dates; the sweep log (channels, timestamps, results); the funding release timestamp against `expires_at`; waiver statements and `officer` acceptance; every inbound candidate document and its classification; unwind checklists with recording/MERS/refund evidence.
- For Fannie Mae QC/MORA and the warehouse: proof that no loan was disbursed before expiry; for CFPB/state exams: the period arithmetic per loan and the (g) accuracy test; for litigation: the H-8/H-9 as delivered with the printed expiry date, and the material-disclosure receipt evidence (the three-year exposure turns on exactly these).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Copies when delivered electronically.** Rule: one copy suffices under E-SIGN. Default: deliver two copies per consumer regardless of channel (costless electronically; removes a litigation argument).
2. **Designated place of business on the notice.** Default: the partner's principal office address plus SM's processing center as an additional "creditor's designated place of business" only if the partner appoints SM in writing as its agent for receipt; e-mail/portal channels are accepted as "other means of written communication" delivered to the designated place when the notice lists them.
3. **H-9 partial disbursement.** Default: hold the entire disbursement (payoff and new advance) until expiry; do not use an escrow disbursement of the exempt portion.
4. **Cure of a defective notice after funding.** Default: re-deliver a compliant notice and accurate material disclosures, which starts a fresh three-business-day period (the creditor may "cure" — general understanding; comment not fetched **[UNVERIFIED]**), and keep the `extended_3y` flag until the fresh period expires without exercise; legal review.
5. **Mailed-notice allowance.** Default: 2 calendar days after expiry before the wire, only where the disbursement day would otherwise be the first Fed business day after expiry (fixture: no delay because Nov 11 is a holiday anyway); the allowance may be shortened to zero by `officer` policy for e-delivery-only borrowers who confirmed non-rescission electronically.
6. **Occupancy per consumer.** Default: rescission applies if the dwelling is the principal dwelling of *any* consumer with an ownership interest subject to the security interest, even where the borrower's occupancy is "second home".
