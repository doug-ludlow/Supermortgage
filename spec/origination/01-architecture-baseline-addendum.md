# Supermortgage Origination Platform — Architecture Baseline Addendum (v0.1, 2026-09-10)

This addendum extends the subservicing Architecture Baseline (v0.1, 2026-09-09; project doc `claude/01-architecture-baseline.md`) to the origination side. Everything in the subservicing baseline stays in force — stack, Timer Engine, ledger, Notice Registry, integration framework, AI-first policy, governance, records — and section specs reference both documents by name. Where this addendum names a new table, event, agent, role, or unit, that name is canonical for every origination spec (§20–§31). Anything here can be overridden by Doug; treat it as the default.

## 0. Ground truth that shapes the origination build (to be re-verified by research/00a-orig and every section)
- **Operating model.** The partner is the lender of record and Fannie Mae seller/servicer; Supermortgage (SM) is the AI fulfillment/technology layer, the warehouse provider, and the subservicer from the first payment. Every Fannie Mae submission (DU, UCDP, UCD, EarlyCheck, Loan Delivery, PE–Whole Loan) is made under the partner's seller/servicer number through partner-scoped credentials (Form 101 / Related Party) and/or SM's TSP identity. Every consumer disclosure names the partner as creditor/lender; SM appears as the servicer/payment address where a subservicer is named at closing.
- **Execution.** Whole-loan cash sale to Fannie Mae (PE–Whole Loan best-efforts or mandatory), servicing retained by the partner, funded on the SM warehouse facility. MBS swap, servicing-released, and non-Fannie executions are out of scope.
- **AI-first, human where the law names a person.** The origination human touchpoints are enumerated in §8 below; the analogue of the subservicing "AI live contact" question is **"can an AI take an application and offer or negotiate terms under the SAFE Act without a licensed MLO doing so?"** — the specs build the `mlo_of_record` touchpoint in, gated by feature flag `origination.ai_mlo_intake` (default: MLO of record reviews and approves at LE and at lock), and treat removal of that touchpoint as an open legal decision.
- **Consumer-facing timing rules are governed by three different "business day" definitions** — Reg Z general (§1026.2(a)(6) first sentence: creditor's offices open for substantially all business functions), Reg Z specific (§1026.2(a)(6) second sentence: all calendar days except Sundays and federal legal public holidays — used for the LE 7-day, CD 3-day, revised-LE 4-day, and rescission clocks), and Reg X (§1024.2(b)). Units: `business_days_creditor` (Reg Z general; calendar `creditor`), `business_days_regz_specific` (calendar `regz_specific`), `business_days_servicer` and `business_days_federal` (from the subservicing baseline), `business_days_fannie_et`, `calendar_days`. Every timer states its unit.
- **Fannie Mae technology under active change** (verify dates in 00a/00b): DU 12.x credit assessment; VantageScore 4.0 acceptance and merge-policy status; UAD 3.6 / redesigned URAR limited → broad → required; UCD critical edits and casefile requirements; ULDD current phase; Loan Delivery and EarlyCheck APIs; Value Acceptance + Property Data; LLPA matrix version; 2026 loan limits; Servicing Platform events for newly acquired loans (Escrow Setup mandatory Dec 1, 2026).
- **LL-2026-04 AI/ML governance** (effective Aug 6, 2026) binds the partner as seller and flows down to SM; the Consolidated Technology Guide forbids scraping/RPA against Fannie Mae UIs; the Information Security and Business Resiliency Supplement requires 36-hour incident notice. These are inherited unchanged from the subservicing baseline.

## 8. AI agent roster and human touchpoints (origination)

Agents (packages under `agents/`): `intake` (refi trigger, lead, conversational 1003 interview, consents), `pricing` (quotes, locks, LLPAs, rate pass-through), `disclosure` (LE/CD/companion notices; shared code name with 7.x `disclosures` — one package), `verification` (documents, credit, income, assets, liabilities), `fraud-risk` (identity, OFAC, fraud, SAR preparation), `underwriter` (DU orchestration, conditions, credit decision, ATR/QM), `valuation` (ordering, UCDP/CU review, ROV, project review), `title-closing` (title, insurance, MI, closing docs, eClosing orchestration), `compliance-tester` (all regulatory tests), `funder` (funding conditions, wires, rescission), `warehouse` (advances, collateral, payoffs, gain on sale), `post-closing` (trailing docs, custody, MERS), `secondary` (commitments, hedging, ULDD/EarlyCheck/Loan Delivery, purchase advice, remedies), `qc-audit` (shared; independent tooling), `hmda`, `boarding` (shared), `compliance-sentinel` (shared), `security-records` (shared).

Legally required or policy-mandated human touchpoints (the agent prepares the complete package and files an `escalation` with an SLA timer):
1. **`mlo_of_record`** — licensed/registered MLO employed or sponsored by the partner; SAFE Act "takes an application / offers or negotiates terms": reviews and approves the LE terms and every lock/relock; named with NMLSR ID on the 1003/LE/CD. Feature flag `origination.ai_mlo_intake` (default `assisted`: AI conducts, MLO approves; `autonomous` requires a written legal position).
2. **`underwriting_reviewer`** — approves every denial, counteroffer, and NOIA before the Reg B notice issues (LL-2026-04; Colorado AI Act human review; ECOA reason accuracy); appeals/second reviews by different personnel.
3. **`notary`** (RON/IPEN/in-person; vendor) and **`settlement_agent`/`closing_attorney`** — external licensed parties; the platform orchestrates but never signs or notarizes.
4. **`appraiser`/`property_data_collector`** — external; AIR bars SM production agents from influencing value.
5. **`signing_officer`** — partner officer under corporate resolutions: note endorsement/allonge, assignments, closing instructions where a wet signature is required, bailee-letter counter-signature; MERS signing officer for eRegistry/Interim Funder transactions where required.
6. **`funding_approver`** — dual control on every outbound funding wire (policy; fraud control); the agent prepares and verifies, a human releases above the threshold.
7. **`officer`** (partner) — certifications: HMDA LAR, QC plan and reports, Form 582, Fannie Mae attestations, SAR sign-off (`bsa_officer`), fraud reports, incident notices, warehouse facility covenants.
8. **`fnma_portal_operator`** — portal-only Fannie Mae steps (Technology Guide bars automation).
9. **`qc_officer`** — independent of production per D1-1-01; reviews AI QC output, signs monthly reports.
10. **`human_agent`** — on borrower request; AI discloses automation at the start of every voice/chat interaction; TCPA consent gating for outbound AI voice.
11. **`licensed_specialist`** — where `jurisdiction_rules` require a licensed individual for an activity (e.g., a state treating processing/underwriting as licensed activity for non-employees).

Governance (LL-2026-04): every `agent_decisions` row persists inputs, rule-set version, model version, prompt version, rationale, confidence, outcome, reviewer; pre-deployment evaluation suites per agent; fair-lending monitoring of underwriting and pricing outcomes; disclosure on request.

## 12. Conventions for origination specs

Identical to the subservicing baseline §12, plus: process IDs are `ON.M`; test cases `ON.M-Tk`; cross-references to servicing processes use bare numbers (`1.1`, `3.1`, `5.1`, `6.1`, `7.x`, `9.x`, `10.x`); never redefine a servicing timer, notice, table or event — reference it; state the business-day unit on every timer; mark anything not verified against a primary source **[UNVERIFIED]** or **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; cite the Selling Guide topic with its "last updated" date and the eCFR "current as of" date.
