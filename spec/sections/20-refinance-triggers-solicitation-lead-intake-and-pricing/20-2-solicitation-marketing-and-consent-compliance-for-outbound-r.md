### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**TCPA — 47 CFR 64.1200 (eCFR up to date as of 9/08/2026).** (a)(1)(iii): no call using an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice "To any telephone number assigned to a paging service, cellular telephone service, specialized mobile radio service, or other radio common carrier service, or any service for which the called party is charged for the call" without prior express consent. (a)(2): may not "Initiate, or cause to be initiated, any telephone call that includes or introduces an advertisement or constitutes telemarketing, using an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice, to any of the lines or telephone numbers described in paragraphs (a)(1)(i) through (iii) … other than a call made with the prior express written consent of the called party." (a)(3): may not "Initiate any telephone call to any residential line using an artificial or prerecorded voice to deliver a message that includes or introduces an advertisement or constitutes telemarketing without the prior express written consent of the called party." (f)(9) "prior express written consent": "An agreement, in writing, bearing the signature of the person called that clearly authorizes the seller to deliver or cause to be delivered to the person called advertisements or telemarketing messages using an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice," which must include a clear and conspicuous disclosure that "By executing the agreement, such person authorizes the seller to deliver or cause to be delivered to the signatory telemarketing calls using an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice" and that "The person is not required to sign the agreement (directly or indirectly), or agree to enter into such an agreement as a condition of purchasing any property, goods, or services"; "The term 'signature' shall include an electronic or digital form of signature, to the extent that such form of signature is recognized as a valid signature under applicable federal law or state contract law." FCC 24-17 (Feb 8, 2024): "the TCPA's restrictions on the use of 'artificial or prerecorded voice' encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices" (00a-fed §13.7) — every outbound AI-voice marketing call is an (a)(2)/(a)(3) call. (b): artificial/prerecorded messages must "At the beginning of the message, state clearly the identity of the business, individual, or other entity that is responsible for initiating the call," state "During or after the message … the telephone number … of such business," and "Provide an automated, interactive voice- and/or key press-activated opt-out mechanism for the called person to make a do-not-call request … within two (2) seconds of providing the identification information." (c)(1): "No person or entity shall initiate any telephone solicitation to any residential telephone subscriber before the hour of 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. (local time at the called party's location)." (c)(2): no telephone solicitation to "A residential telephone subscriber who has registered his or her telephone number on the national do-not-call registry," with a safe harbor that requires "employing a version of the national do-not-call registry obtained from the administrator of the registry no more than 31 days prior to the date any call is made"; exception (c)(2)(ii): "It has obtained the subscriber's prior express invitation or permission. Such permission must be evidenced by a signed, written agreement between the consumer and seller which states that the consumer agrees to be contacted by this seller and includes the telephone number to which the calls may be placed." (d): entities making artificial/prerecorded-voice or telemarketing calls "must have a written policy, available upon demand, for maintaining a do-not-call list"; "A do-not-call request must be honored for 5 years from the time the request is made"; requests must be honored "within a reasonable time … This period may not exceed ten (10) business days from the receipt of such request." (f)(5) established business relationship: "a prior or existing relationship formed by a voluntary two-way communication … on the basis of the subscriber's purchase or transaction with the entity within the eighteen (18) months immediately preceding the date of the telephone call or on the basis of the subscriber's inquiry or application regarding products or services offered by the entity within the three months immediately preceding the date of the call"; "The subscriber's seller-specific do-not-call request … terminates an established business relationship for purposes of telemarketing and telephone solicitation." (f)(15) "telephone solicitation" excludes a call "(i) To any person with that person's prior express invitation or permission; (ii) To any person with whom the caller has an established business relationship." (a)(10)–(12) revocation: "A called party may revoke prior express consent, including prior express written consent … by using any reasonable method"; "stop," "quit," "end," "revoke," "opt out," "cancel," or "unsubscribe" by text are per-se reasonable; "must be honored within a reasonable time not to exceed ten business days from receipt"; (a)(11) other means create "a rebuttable presumption that the consumer has revoked consent"; (a)(12) one confirmation text is permitted. **One-to-one consent:** *Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC*, No. 24-10277 (11th Cir. Jan 24, 2025) vacated Part III.D of the 2023 order — never in effect (00a-fed §13.7). **VERIFIED.**

**TSR — 16 CFR 310 (eCFR up to date as of 9/08/2026).** §310.2: "telemarketing" = "A plan, program, or campaign which is conducted to induce the purchase of goods or services … by use of one or more telephones and which involves more than one interstate telephone call"; "established business relationship" = purchase/financial transaction "within the 540 days immediately preceding the date of a telemarketing call" or inquiry/application "within the 90 days immediately preceding." §310.4(b)(1)(iii)(A)/(B): no calls to a person who "previously has stated that he or she does not wish to receive an outbound telephone call made by or on behalf of the seller" or whose number "is on the 'do-not-call' registry," unless "the seller has obtained the express agreement, in writing, of such person … [which] shall include the telephone number to which the calls may be placed and the signature of that person" or "the seller has an established business relationship with such person, and that person has not stated that he or she does not wish to receive outbound telephone calls." §310.4(b)(3) safe harbor: "employing a version of the 'do-not-call' registry obtained from the Commission no more than thirty-one (31) days prior to the date any call is made." §310.4(b)(1)(v): prerecorded messages only with "an express agreement, in writing" obtained "only after a clear and conspicuous disclosure" and "without requiring … as a condition of purchasing any good or service." §310.4(c): no calls "to a person's residence at any time other than between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. local time." §310.4(d): outbound calls must disclose "The identity of the seller; That the purpose of the call is to sell goods or services; The nature of the goods or services." **VERIFIED.** Applicability: the TSR exempts banks but not non-bank mortgage lenders or their telemarketers [statement from the TSR's jurisdictional structure — PARTIALLY VERIFIED; §310.6 not fetched].

**CAN-SPAM — 15 U.S.C. 7704 (current through Sept 8, 2026).** (a)(1) no "header information that is materially false or materially misleading"; (a)(2) no subject heading "likely to mislead a recipient"; (a)(3)(A) a "functioning return electronic mail address or other Internet-based mechanism, clearly and conspicuously displayed"; (a)(4)(A) after an opt-out request it is unlawful "to initiate the transmission … more than 10 business days after the receipt of such request"; (a)(5)(A) "clear and conspicuous identification that the message is an advertisement or solicitation," notice of the opt-out opportunity, and "a valid physical postal address of the sender." **VERIFIED.**

**Reg N (MAP Rule) — 12 CFR 1014 (eCFR up to date as of 9/08/2026).** §1014.3: "It is a violation of this part for any person to make any material misrepresentation, expressly or by implication, in any commercial communication, regarding any term of any mortgage credit product," including (a) interest, (b) APR, (c) fees, (e) taxes/insurance, (f) prepayment penalties, (g) variability ("fixed"), (h) rate/payment comparisons to temporary rates, (k) required payments, (m) debt reduction/elimination claims, (n) government association, (o) "Source of communication" (e.g., that the sender is the consumer's current lender or servicer), (q) likelihood of obtaining credit/preapproval, (r) likelihood of refinancing/modification, (s) counseling services. §1014.4: no waivers. §1014.5: retain "for 24 months from the last date a commercial communication regarding any mortgage credit product term was made or disseminated" copies of "all materially different commercial communications, sales scripts, training materials, and marketing materials," documentation of "all mortgage credit products available to consumers during each communication period," and of additional products offered with them. **VERIFIED.** AI prompts, voice scripts and creative variants are "sales scripts"/"commercial communications."

**Reg Z advertising — 12 CFR 1026.24 (eCFR up to date as of 9/08/2026) and §1026.2(a)(2).** "Advertisement means a commercial message in any medium that promotes, directly or indirectly, a credit transaction"; comment 2(a)(2)-1 covers "Electronic advertisements, such as on the Internet" and "Telephone solicitations," and excludes "Direct personal contacts, such as follow-up letters, cost estimates for individual consumers, or oral or written communication relating to the negotiation of a specific transaction" and "Communications about an existing credit account (for example, a promotion encouraging additional or different uses)"; comment 2(a)(2)-2: "All persons must comply with the advertising provisions in §§ 1026.16 and 1026.24, not just those that meet the definition of creditor." §1026.24(a): "If an advertisement for credit states specific credit terms, it shall state only those terms that actually are or will be arranged or offered by the creditor." (b) clearly and conspicuously. (c): "If an advertisement states a rate of finance charge, it shall state the rate as an 'annual percentage rate,' using that term"; a simple annual rate may appear only with less prominence than the APR. (d)(1) triggering terms — amount/percentage of downpayment, number of payments or period of repayment, amount of any payment, amount of any finance charge — require (d)(2) the downpayment, terms of repayment reflecting the full obligation, and the APR and whether it may increase. (f) dwelling-secured: each rate with the period it applies and the APR "with equal prominence and in close proximity"; payment advertising must state each payment amount and period and "the fact that the payments do not include amounts for taxes and insurance premiums, if applicable, and that the actual payment obligation will be greater." (i) prohibited acts: misleading "fixed," misleading comparisons to temporary rates, government endorsement claims, (i)(4) misleading use of the consumer's current lender's name unless the advertiser is identified and states that it "is not associated with, or acting on behalf of, the consumer's current lender," debt-elimination claims, "counselor," and foreign-language partial disclosures. **VERIFIED.** Design consequence: the trigger offer (letter/email/portal card/telephone solicitation) *is* an advertisement; the individual benefit conversation and written quote are "cost estimates for individual consumers" (not advertisements) but carry the §1026.19(e)(2)(ii) disclaimer (20.4).

**GLBA — 12 CFR 1016 (20.1 analysis, VERIFIED).** The partner's own program marketed by SM as service provider needs no opt-out; the annual/initial privacy notice must describe service-provider sharing. SM may not use the data for its own products (§1016.11(d) and the §1016.13(a)(1)(ii) contract).

**FCRA prescreening — 15 U.S.C. 1681b(c), 1681a(l), 1681m(d) (00a-fed §7; uscode.house.gov current through Sept 9, 2026) and 16 CFR 642 (eCFR up to date as of 8/20/2026).** A prescreened list may be used only for a "firm offer of credit" — "any offer of credit … that will be honored if the consumer is determined, based on information in a consumer report on the consumer, to meet the specific criteria used to select the consumer for the offer," conditionable only on application-based creditworthiness criteria "established before selection," re-verification of the criteria, and collateral. §1681m(d) written solicitations must carry the statement; 12 CFR 1022.54(c)–(d) (Reg V — the CFPB rule that governs non-dealer users; 16 CFR 642.3 is textually identical but §642.1(b) limits Part 642 to motor vehicle dealers excluded from CFPB jurisdiction under 12 U.S.C. 5519): a short notice "on the front side of the first page of the principal promotional document," ≥ 12-point type, "set apart from other text," stating "that the consumer has the right to opt out of receiving prescreened solicitations, and shall provide the toll-free number," and a long notice headed "PRESCREEN & OPT-OUT NOTICE" (≥ 8-point) with the §1681m(d) content, the toll-free number, the possible-denial statement and the 5-year/permanent election. Reg B §1002.12(b)(7): prescreened-solicitation records retained 25 months (00a-fed §3.6). **VERIFIED.** Default: prescreening disabled (`marketing.prescreen_enabled=false`).

**Reg V §1022.21 (20.1, VERIFIED structure).** Not applicable between the nonaffiliated partner and SM; applies if either shares "eligibility information" with an affiliate for that affiliate's marketing.

**UDAAP — 12 U.S.C. 5531 (20.1, VERIFIED).** Governs benefit claims ("no cost," "guaranteed savings"), pressure tactics, and AI-voice conversations that a consumer may mistake for a human.

**Fannie Mae B2-1.3-04 (20.1, VERIFIED).** Creatives and selection may not target Fannie Mae borrowers or treat investor classes differently; no "arrangement for special terms … for a future refinance."

**State solicitation and licensing rules.** Maryland COMAR 09.03.06.06 (Advertising and Solicitation; Justia mirror current through Register Vol. 52 No. 12, June 13, 2025): "A licensee may not advertise under any name or address other than a name or address that appears on its license"; "A licensee shall disclose its NMLS unique identifier in every advertisement"; no "false, misleading, or deceptive statements regarding the making, brokering, or servicing of mortgage loans" — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — mirror source]**; no current-lender or "not affiliated" rule found in that section. New Jersey N.J.A.C. 3:15-8.3 (Justia mirror, current through Register Vol. 57 No. 12, June 16, 2025): "No advertisement shall contain false, misleading or deceptive claims or misrepresentations"; unlicensed persons "shall not offer residential mortgage loans in this State through direct or indirect solicitation or advertisement"; check-like solicitations and misleading terminology prohibited — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. Massachusetts 209 CMR 42 advertising/license-number requirements, and state "mini-TCPA" statutes (Florida FTSA, Oklahoma TSA, Washington CEMA), state call-recording consent laws — **[UNVERIFIED — carried in `jurisdiction_rules.solicitation_rules`; servicing 11.1 flags the same cites]**. SAFE Act: an advertisement that "offers" particular loan terms is not itself "taking an application" or "negotiating" (Reg H App. A), but the AI voice conversation that follows is governed by 20.3/21.1's `mlo_of_record` model; every creative carries the partner's NMLSR ID and, where a state requires, the MLO of record's name and NMLSR ID.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "Do-not-call scrubs every 31 days (TSR)" is right but incomplete: the identical 31-day version rule is in the FCC rule (§64.1200(c)(2)(i)(D)) and the *company-specific* list has its own 5-year honoring period and 10-business-day processing rule (§64.1200(d)).
2. The row's "TCPA consent before AI-voice call" understates: marketing AI-voice/ATDS/SMS to cell phones needs prior express **written** consent (§64.1200(a)(2), (f)(9)); artificial-voice marketing to residential landlines also needs PEWC ((a)(3)); the EBR does not substitute for either.
3. The one-to-one consent rule is vacated — consent design targets PEWC naming the partner as seller (and SM on its behalf) plus the (a)(10) revocation mechanics (00a-fed §17.11).
4. "GLBA opt-out" is not required for the partner's own program run by SM as service provider (§1016.13); the row implies an opt-out that would be wrong to offer for that use.
5. "FCRA prescreen if any" — default off; if enabled, the *firm offer* obligation (offer must be honored subject only to §1681a(l) conditions) is the real constraint, not just the notice.
6. The row omits the MAP Rule 24-month record rule for scripts/prompts and Reg Z §1026.24 payment/rate content rules, both of which drive template checklists.
7. Reg Z's "existing credit account" exclusion does not cover a refinance offer to a servicing customer; the refinance offer is an advertisement.

#### State machine

Campaign: `draft` → `approved` (partner compliance) → `live` → `paused` | `ended`. Touch: `queued` → (`suppressed` | `scheduled`) → `sent` → `delivered`/`answered_ai`/`answered_human`/`voicemail_no_message`/`bounced`/`opted_out`/`replied`. Guards on `queued → scheduled`: all gates in the timers table pass and the creative is `approved` and not `superseded`. Only the `intake` agent transitions touches; humans can only `pause`/`end` campaigns and approve creatives. Consent (7.4 machine) adds `pending_written_confirmation` for `voice_recording_interim` → `active` on written confirmation within 24 h, else `expired`.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Channel eligibility matrix (per touch).** Email: allowed unless `email_optout`/`all_marketing`; must carry ad identification, opt-out link, partner postal address, `NTC_REGZ_1026_24_REFI_OFFER` content when rate/payment terms appear. Portal card: allowed for enrolled borrowers (no CAN-SPAM; §1026.24 applies). Mail: allowed unless `mail_optout`; state disclosures per property state. SMS: PEWC (marketing) required; 10DLC; STOP handling (11.1). AI voice: PEWC required for cell *and* landline; (b) identification script; opt-out mechanism within 2 s; quiet hours; national DNC gate (EBR or written permission) — note PEWC that names the seller and includes the number also satisfies (c)(2)(ii) when `national_dnc_written_permission=true`. Human click-to-dial (no ATDS, no artificial voice): no TCPA consent needed under 227(b); national DNC gate; company DNC; quiet hours; TSR §310.4(d) disclosures at the start; recorded per state consent law.
2. **EBR computation.** `ebr_basis = transaction_18m` if any `payment.received` or loan transaction with the partner within 18 months (servicing customers with a current loan always qualify while paying); `inquiry_3m` if the consumer inquired within 3 months; `none` otherwise; any company-specific DNC request sets `none` regardless (f)(5).
3. **Consent evidence model (PEWC).** A consent is `marketing`-valid only if: (i) the text shown contains both (f)(9) disclosures verbatim-equivalent and names the partner as seller and SM as caller on its behalf; (ii) the phone number was entered/confirmed by the consumer; (iii) a signature exists — `esign_click_typed_name`, `checkbox_with_text`, `sms_keyword_double_optin` (the keyword reply is the signature; the disclosure text was in the invitation), or `wet_ink`; a `voice_recording_interim` capture (oral "yes" during an inbound call) is stored as evidence but is `pending_written_confirmation` until a written channel confirms within 24 h [FCC 2012 guidance that E-SIGN-compliant voice recordings can be PEWC — **PARTIALLY VERIFIED; conservative default**]; (iv) evidence stored: disclosure version hash, timestamp, IP/user agent or message-id/call-id, session id, the number as entered; (v) consent is per number, per purpose, per seller. Revocation by any reasonable method sets `status=withdrawn` at commit and propagates to every list within one minute (11.1 SLA), well inside the 10-business-day ceiling.
4. **Advertising content rule (`NTC_REGZ_1026_24_REFI_OFFER`).** If the creative states a rate, it states "annual percentage rate"/APR with equal prominence; if it states a payment or term (triggering terms), it includes the terms of repayment and the APR and whether it may increase; for dwelling-secured payment ads: "Payments do not include amounts for taxes and insurance premiums; your actual payment obligation will be greater." No "fixed" for ARMs; no comparison with a temporary rate; no "no-cost" claim — the approved phrasing is "no lender fees and no third-party closing costs charged to you; those costs are paid by Supermortgage and reflected in the rate offered"; no "guaranteed" or "pre-approved" (MAP (q)/(r)); "not a commitment to lend"; partner name, NMLSR ID, and "[Partner] is your current lender/servicer; Supermortgage services your loan for [Partner]" (accurate source, MAP (o)); for organic creatives: never a third party's lender name (§1026.24(i)(4)).
5. **Quiet hours.** `local_time = now() at tz(called_party)`; tz = the stricter of the tz implied by the area code and the tz of the property/mailing address; window 09:00–20:00 (policy) inside the 08:00–21:00 rule; Sundays and federal holidays: no voice/SMS (policy).
6. **Frequency.** Max 1 voice attempt per opportunity per 7 days, max 3 total per opportunity; max 2 emails per opportunity; no re-contact after `declined` for 90 days (20.1 cooldown); Reg F frequency rules do not apply (not debt collection) but 11.1's 7-day post-conversation rule is adopted as policy for consistency.
7. **Investor blindness in creatives.** Creatives cannot merge any investor field; the template variable schema has no such variable; B2-1.3-04 checklist item `no_investor_reference=true` is machine-checked (regex on rendered output for "Fannie", "MBS", pool numbers).
8. **MAP archive.** Every rendered variant (content hash), every prompt version used by the AI voice/chat, training material and the product list in force are archived; `map_rule_archive_until` = last dissemination + 24 months.
9. **Prescreen (flag off).** If enabled: selection criteria frozen before the list pull; the offer is honored for every consumer meeting the criteria subject only to §1681a(l) conditions; short/long notices per 12 CFR 1022.54 (Reg V; 16 CFR 642.3 is the motor-vehicle-dealer twin); opt-out numbers honored; records 25 months (Reg B §1002.12(b)(7)).
10. **State overlays.** MD: NMLS unique identifier in every advertisement; NJ: no check-like solicitations; every state: license/NMLS display per `jurisdiction_rules`; unlicensed-state check — no solicitation into a state where the partner lacks a lender license (`SM_LICENSE_STATE_GATE`, 31.1 owns the licensing matrix).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`intake` agent (campaign/touch orchestration) with `borrower-comms` (voice/SMS/chat execution). Tools: `planChannels`, `checkGates` (every gate above via `assertGateOpen`), `renderCreative` (approved templates only; variable schema enforced), `runContentChecklist`, `scheduleTouch`, `placeCall`/`sendSms`/`sendEmail`/`queueMail`, `recordResponse`, `recordSuppression`, `writeDecision`. Behavior: for each `offer_ready` opportunity the agent computes channel eligibility (rule 1), schedules the permitted touches, executes them, handles replies (a reply about refinancing → `lead.created`; a STOP/opt-out → suppression; a question → `borrower-comms` conversation under 20.3 disclosure rules), and closes the loop to 20.1. The LLM may draft variant copy only inside approved templates' free-text slots, each variant re-run through the checklists and archived; it never changes rates, APRs, payments or legal text. Decision record: `{touch_id, channel_plan, gates:[{code,result,basis}], creative_version, rendered_hash, schedule_local_time, outcome}`. Guardrails: never dial a number without a passing gate record; never use an artificial voice without PEWC; never state "pre-approved," "guaranteed," or a rate not on the current sheet; never reference investors; never pressure ("limited time" only when true and stated in the sheet's validity terms); disclose automation at the start of every voice/chat interaction (20.3 `NTC_SM_AI_INTERACTION_DISCLOSURE`). Escalations: partner `officer`/compliance for creative approval and any complaint; `human_agent` on request or on any "is this a real person?" question (Utah/CA/CO overlay in 20.3); `licensed_specialist`/`mlo_of_record` when the consumer wants to negotiate terms (SAFE Act — 20.3/21.1). Human path: ops console campaign board with the same gates; human click-to-dial lists generated only from gate-passing numbers.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Number reassigned** (RND hit or "wrong number" outcome): stop AI voice/SMS to it; consent flagged `suspect`; human verification before reuse.
- **Consent captured for a different seller** (e.g., a prior lender's PEWC boarded with a transferred loan): not valid for the partner — `seller_named` mismatch → gate fails; invite fresh consent.
- **Borrower revokes on a servicing call**: revocation applies to marketing and, if the borrower says "all calls," to informational calls too (11.1 handles; UDAAP).
- **Co-borrowers with different consents**: each number/party evaluated separately; the offer may go to one and not the other; mail can go to the household address.
- **Rate sheet changes after send**: email states "rates change daily"; the 20.3 conversation re-quotes from the current sheet; no "bait and switch" — if the rate rose, the agent explains before any application step.
- **Bounce/complaint**: complaint → `email_optout` immediately; hard bounce → address flagged.
- **State without partner license**: no solicitation (31.1 gate).
- **Prescreen list in use while flag off**: impossible by construction — no consumer-report adapter is wired to campaigns.
- **Registry download stale > 31 days**: telephone solicitations stop (email/portal/mail continue).
- **Fannie Mae ownership leaks into a template**: regex check fails the creative; sev 2.
- **Voice-model says "I am a person"**: prohibited output (prompt guard + post-call transcript scan); any occurrence escalates to `officer` and the number is moved to human-only.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Accept E-SIGN-compliant voice-recorded PEWC as final consent?** Default: **no** — interim only, written confirmation within 24 h; revisit with counsel citing the FCC 2012 order (**[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**).
2. **Use human click-to-dial marketing calls at all?** Default: **yes**, limited to EBR customers and gate-passing numbers, by partner-employed/sponsored staff under the `mlo_of_record` team (SAFE Act: the call may present terms only when an MLO is on it — 20.3).
3. **Enable FCRA prescreening for organic acquisition?** Default: **off**; if on, firm-offer honoring and 12 CFR 1022.54 notices as specified.
4. **State mini-TCPA and call-recording matrix source.** Default: compile from statutes with counsel before launching voice/SMS in FL, OK, WA and two-party-consent states; until verified, SMS/AI-voice marketing disabled in those states.
5. **Sunday/holiday voice and SMS.** Default: none (policy stricter than federal rule).
