### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Sources verified:** 12 CFR 1024.31 and 1024.41 on eCFR ("up to date as of 9/08/2026"; last amended 90 FR 20792, May 16, 2025); CFPB Official Interpretations to §1024.41 (consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/interp-41 — note this page still displays the 41(f)(3) COVID comments that the IFR removed; eCFR governs); 12 CFR 1024.38(b)(2), (b)(4), (c)(2); Fannie Mae D2-2-04 (09/13/2017), D2-2-05 (06/09/2021), D2-3.1-01 (11/12/2014); Cal. Civ. Code §§2924.10, 2923.6(h); 3 NYCRR 419.7(d).

**What is an application (§1024.31; comments 41(b)(1)-2, -3).** A loss mitigation application is "an oral or written request for a loss mitigation option that is accompanied by any information required by a servicer for evaluation for a loss mitigation option." The term is construed expansively: when a borrower expresses interest in an option *and* provides information the servicer would evaluate (income, hardship, expenses), the inquiry becomes an application; "prequalification" requests (servicer evaluates against preliminary criteria) are applications. A call that only asks what options exist or how to apply, with no evaluative information, is not an application (comment 41(b)(1)-3) — but it is a "request for assistance" for D2-2-04 solicitation, CA §2923.7 SPOC assignment and the NPRM trigger. Comment 41(b)-1: an application from a potential successor in interest may be evaluated before confirmation; if not, it must be preserved and evaluated on confirmation (principal residence), with stale documents identified in the (b)(2) notice.

**Complete application and reasonable diligence (§1024.41(b)(1); comments 41(b)(1)-1, -4, -5).** An application is complete when the servicer has received all the information *it requires from the borrower* to evaluate for the options available to the borrower; the servicer "shall exercise reasonable diligence in obtaining documents and information to complete" it. Diligence includes (comment -4): promptly contacting the applicant after receipt to obtain missing items; on a transfer, reviewing transferred documents before asking again; when a short-term forbearance/repayment plan is offered on an incomplete application with the (c)(2)(iii) notice, diligence may be suspended while the borrower performs and requests nothing more, but must resume immediately on breach or request, and near the end of the plan the servicer must contact a still-delinquent borrower about completing the application (the CFPB page's -4.iv "COVID-19 forbearance … 30 days before end" text was revised by the 2025 IFR — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** as to residual wording; the platform applies a 30-day pre-expiry contact regardless because D2-3.2-01 requires it). Comment -1: the servicer may stop collecting documents for an option once it has confirmation the borrower is ineligible for it under owner criteria, but not merely because the borrower prefers another option; comment -5: an application is complete when the borrower has provided everything required *from the borrower*, regardless of third-party information the servicer must still obtain (§1024.41(c)(4)). Comment 41(b)(2)(i)(B)-1: if, during evaluation, the servicer discovers it needs more or corrected information, it must promptly request it.

**Acknowledgment (§1024.41(b)(2)(i)).** If an application is received **45 or more days before a foreclosure sale** (no sale scheduled = treated as ≥45 days, comment 41(b)(2)(i)-1), the servicer must (A) promptly review it for completeness and (B) **within 5 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays)** after receipt notify the borrower in writing that the application is complete or incomplete; if incomplete, state the additional documents and information needed, the **reasonable date** by which they should be submitted ((b)(2)(ii)); and that the borrower should consider contacting servicers of other liens on the property. **Reasonable date (comments 41(b)(2)(ii)-1..-3):** 30 days after the notice is generally reasonable; it must not be later than the earliest of (i) the date documents become stale/invalid under the option's requirements, (ii) the **120th day of delinquency**, (iii) **90 days before** a foreclosure sale, (iv) **38 days before** a foreclosure sale; and never less than **7 days** from the notice.

**Protections attach on completeness (§1024.41(b)(3); comments -1, -2).** Whether a complete application was received ≥90 days, >37 days, or ≤37 days before a sale is fixed "as of the date a complete loss mitigation application is received"; no scheduled sale = ≥90 days; protections persist even if a sale is later scheduled or rescheduled.

**Facially complete applications (§1024.41(c)(2)(iv); comments -1, -2).** Once the borrower has submitted everything listed in the (b)(2) notice (or the notice listed nothing, or a (c)(3) notice is due), the application is *facially complete*. If the servicer later needs more or corrected information, it must promptly request it and treat the application as complete for (f)(2) and (g) until the borrower has had a reasonable opportunity to comply; if the borrower complies, the application is deemed complete as of the facially-complete date for (d), (e), (f)(2), (g), (h), but as of the actual completion date for the (c) 30-day clock. Failure to complete in the time given renders it incomplete (comment -2).

**Notice of complete application (§1024.41(c)(3)).** Within **5 days (excluding legal public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays)** after receiving a complete application, written notice stating: (A) it is complete; (B) the date received complete; (C) evaluation expected within 30 days of that date; (D) the foreclosure protection — no first notice/filing before evaluation if not yet initiated, or no sale before evaluation if initiated; (E) additional information may later be needed, the borrower will get a reasonable opportunity, evaluation may then take longer, and protections end if the information is not provided; (F) state/federal protections may also apply. Exceptions ((c)(3)(ii)): the (b)(2) notice already said "complete" and nothing was requested since; the application was not complete/facially complete more than 37 days before a sale; or the (c)(1) determination notice was already sent. Comments 41(c)(3)(i)-1/-3: disclose the *most recent* completion date and send a new notice each time the application becomes complete again.

**Short-term options on an incomplete application (§1024.41(c)(2)(iii); comments -1..-6).** A short-term payment forbearance (payments forborne for **no more than 6 months**, regardless of the repayment period) or short-term repayment plan (**no more than 3 months** of arrears repaid over **no more than 6 months**) may be offered on an incomplete application; promptly (≤5 federal business days after the offer, comment -6) the servicer must send written notice of the specific payment terms and duration (amounts, due dates, whether the loan will be current at the end; estimates flagged where escrow or rate changes are unknown), that the offer was based on an incomplete application, that other options may be available, and that the borrower may complete the application for full evaluation. While the borrower performs, no first notice/filing, no motion for judgment/order of sale, no sale. All other §1024.41 duties (completeness review, diligence, (b)(2) notice) continue (comments -2, -3).

**Duplicative requests (§1024.41(i); comments -1, -2).** A servicer must comply with §1024.41 for an application unless it previously complied for a *complete* application from the borrower and the borrower "has been delinquent at all times since." A borrower who became current and delinquent again gets the full process, including a new (c)(3) notice. Transferor and transferee are different servicers: a transferee must comply even if the transferor already evaluated (comment -2).

**Related duties.** §1024.38(b)(2): policies reasonably designed to give borrowers accurate information about available options, identify with specificity the documents and information required, make that list available, give assigned personnel prompt access to submitted documents, and properly evaluate for all options per owner requirements. §1024.38(c)(2): servicing-file assembly within 5 days includes loss-mitigation submissions. §1024.35(b)(7): failure to provide accurate loss-mitigation information is a covered error (4.1). §1024.40(b)(1)(ii)–(iv) (4.3): assigned personnel must be able to state the application's status, missing documents and deadlines.

**Fannie Mae layer (D2-2-04, D2-2-05).** The Borrower Solicitation Package (Borrower Solicitation Letter **Form 745** or equivalent + **Form 710** Mortgage Assistance Application + optional IRS **Form 4506-C**) must be sent by the **45th day of delinquency** if QRPC has not been achieved or no resolution obtained; Form 745 must keep the Homeowner's HOPE™ Hotline (1-888-995-HOPE). A complete **BRP** (D2-2-05) is: Form 710 completed in its entirety by every borrower who signed the note and whose income qualified the loan (unless deceased or divorced); income documentation **no more than 90 days old** (180 for disaster-impacted borrowers), per income type; hardship documentation as Form 710 specifies; Form 4506-C when needed to reconcile inconsistencies or when Fannie Mae requests it; bankruptcy schedules (≤90 days old) may substitute for Form 710. Non-borrower income may be included if voluntarily provided and verified; non-taxable and bank-statement income are grossed up/adjusted 25%. **Acknowledge receipt within 5 business days** stating complete/incomplete, the evaluation process and response timeframe, and an explanation that foreclosure may continue unless a complete BRP is reviewed or an offer extended; for BRPs received **≤37 days** before a sale, the plan to evaluate and suspend the sale if appropriate. **Incomplete Information Notice** within 5 business days of determining items are missing: list of missing items, toll-free number, HUD-approved counselor reference, warning that failure to submit may mean ineligibility and continued foreclosure, and that timing gives no guarantee of evaluation or suspension (may be combined with the acknowledgment). D2-2-05 also authorizes third-party verification vendors (with borrower authorization, retained reports).

**State overlays.** Cal. Civ. Code **§2924.10** (owner-occupied principal-residence first liens per §2924.15): written acknowledgment within **5 business days** of *any* document received in connection with a first-lien modification application; for a complete application the first acknowledgment must describe the process (estimate of decision timing and the borrower's time to consider an offer), all deadlines including document-expiration dates, and any deficiencies; §2923.6(h): "complete" = all documents the servicer required, within the reasonable timeframes it specified. **3 NYCRR 419.7(d):** reasonable diligence; if received ≥45 days before sale, notify within 5 business days whether complete, identify missing items with a reasonable date and the consequences. Other states (e.g., mediation states, NV/WA/MD/CT pre-foreclosure mediation) impose program-specific document lists — loaded per state in `jurisdiction_rules.lossmit` **[UNVERIFIED — not enumerated]**.

**2024 NPRM (flag only).** Would delete most of (b), make protections attach on any oral/written "request for loss mitigation assistance," and require determination notices for every option (00a §1.2). The acknowledgment content changes to a "request received / what happens next" notice; the reasonable-date and completeness machinery becomes advisory.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) The 5-day clock excludes Saturdays, Sundays and federal legal holidays — it is not "servicer business days"; the Fannie Mae 5-business-day acknowledgment is a second, servicer-calendar clock. (2) The blueprint omits the ≥45-days-before-sale condition, the reasonable-date rules, the (c)(3) notice of complete application, the facially-complete rule, the duplicative-request rule and the CA/NY overlays — all built below. (3) "Systems: SMDU" is inaccurate for acknowledgment; SMDU is used from 12.2 onward (the acknowledgment is a Notice Registry artifact). (4) Automation class "a" is upgraded to AI-first with structured completeness determinations recorded in `agent_decisions`.

#### State machine
`lossmit_applications.status`:
- `rfa_only` (request for assistance, no evaluative info) → on evaluative info: `received`; on 2024nprm rule set: `review_cycle_open`.
- `received` → completeness review (≤1 business day policy) → `incomplete` (ack sent with missing list + reasonable date) | `complete` (ack says complete; (c)(3) notice due unless exception) | `duplicative` (§1024.41(i) exception documented; borrower still gets Fannie Mae evaluation under §1024.38(b)(2)(v)) | `pending_sii_confirmation` (4.4).
- `incomplete` → `facially_complete` (all listed items received) → `complete` (verified; `complete_at` set) | `incomplete` again (new request; reasonable opportunity; deemed date preserved) ; `incomplete` → `expired` (reasonable date passed without progress → (c)(2)(ii) discretionary evaluation path in 12.2, or closure with `NTC_REGX_41B2_INCOMPLETE_CLOSED`).
- `complete` → `evaluating` (12.2) → `determined` → `offer_pending` | `denied` → `appeal_pending` (12.3) → `closed{accepted, rejected, deemed_rejected, denied_final, withdrawn, current, transferred, paid_off}`.
- Guards: `assertReceived45DaysBeforeSale` decides whether (b)(2) applies (else `le_37_no_ack_required` path — Fannie Mae still requires the D2-2-05 explanation-of-plan notice); `assertNotDuplicative`; `assertRuleSet`.
- Transitions are by `lossmit-underwriter` (agent), `case.sii.confirmed` (4.4), timers (expiry), or external (transfer).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Receipt date.** `received_date` = the servicer-local calendar date the communication arrived at any Supermortgage channel (mail: postmark not used; date of receipt in the mailroom scan; e-channels: timestamp; voice: call date). Comment 41(k)(1): documents received by the transferor before the transfer date keep the transferor's date.
2. **Application vs. RFA classification** (Intake Router, 4.1, `rule_set=regx.lossmit.2013`): an RFA becomes an application when the communication contains *any* evaluative information (hardship reason, income/expense/asset figure, statement about ability to pay, a Form 710 page, a bank statement). The classifier is tuned to over-recognize; the agent's structured output `{is_rfa, is_application, evidence_spans[], confidence}` is persisted in `agent_decisions`. Confidence <0.80 → treated as an application (conservative).
3. **45-day test.** If a foreclosure sale is scheduled and `received_date > sale_date − 45 days`, (b)(2) does not apply; the D2-2-05 "explanation of plan" acknowledgment (`NTC_FNMA_D2205_LATE_BRP_PLAN`) is still sent within 5 servicer business days, and 12.2's expedited review applies.
4. **Completeness determination.** Build the requirement set from `lossmit_requirement_catalog` for *all options available to the borrower* (F-2-10 hierarchy; excluding options for which owner criteria already establish ineligibility from loan data, e.g., Flex Mod excluded when the loan has three prior modifications — comment 41(b)(1)-1), less items already in the servicing file (prior-servicer submissions, 1.7; recent Form 710 ≤90 days), less third-party items (never counted against the borrower, comment 41(b)(1)-5). Complete ⇔ every `source=borrower` requirement is `received` or `verified` and not `stale`. Document AI extracts fields; an item is `received` when the document class and the borrower's identity match and the date test passes; `verified` after vendor/IVES reconciliation where required.
5. **Reasonable date computation** (worked example): application received Thu 2026-09-10; ack sent Tue 2026-09-15 → default reasonable date 2026-10-15. Milestones: earliest unpaid due date 2026-08-01 → day 120 of delinquency = 2026-11-28 (day 1 = Aug 1); sale scheduled 2027-01-15 → sale−90 = 2026-10-17, sale−38 = 2026-12-08; paystubs dated 2026-08-20 become stale 2026-11-18. Earliest cap = 2026-10-15 (the 30-day date itself is earliest) → reasonable date **2026-10-15**; floor check: ≥ 2026-09-22 ✓. Had the sale been 2026-11-20, sale−38 = 2026-10-13 would cap the date at 2026-10-13. Documented in `reasonable_date_basis`.
6. **Federal-business-day arithmetic.** Received Thu 2026-09-10 → 5 days excluding weekends/holidays → due **Thu 2026-09-17** (Sept 11, 14, 15, 16, 17). If received Fri 2026-11-20 → Nov 23, 24, 25, 27, 30 (Nov 26 Thanksgiving excluded) → due **Mon 2026-11-30**.
7. **Facially complete.** When the last item listed in the (b)(2) notice arrives, set `facially_complete_at`; run verification within 2 business days; if anything further is needed, send `NTC_REGX_41B2_SUPPLEMENTAL_REQUEST` with a new date (≥7 days; milestones apply) and keep `foreclosure_holds` active; if nothing further, `complete_at = facially_complete_at`.
8. **Duplicative test (§1024.41(i)).** `duplicative` only if a prior application by the same borrower on this loan reached `complete` and was fully processed by *Supermortgage* (not a transferor) and `regx_days_delinquent` never reset to zero since; otherwise full process. Even when duplicative, evaluate under §1024.38(b)(2)(v)/Fannie Mae and send a courtesy notice (`NTC_REGX_41I_DUPLICATIVE`) stating that §1024.41 procedural rights were exhausted by the prior application — reviewed by `lossmit_reviewer` because it removes protections.
9. **Successor in interest** (comment 41(b)-1): default policy = evaluate potential successors' applications after confirmation only, preserving the application and documents; on confirmation ≥45 days before sale, the (b)(2) notice identifies stale items (4.4-Q3 governs the alternative "evaluate now" policy).
10. **NPRM strategy (flag).** Under `regx.lossmit.2024nprm`, `lossmit.assistance.requested` alone opens `review_cycle_open`, sets `foreclosure_holds{kind=lm_review_cycle}` when received >37 days before a sale, and the acknowledgment becomes `NTC_REGX_41_NPRM_RFA_RECEIVED` (what was received, what will happen, how to reach the SPOC, translations/taglines). The completeness engine keeps running for Fannie Mae's BRP requirements.

#### Integrations
- **Intake channels:** `telephony/voice` (transcripts + structured QRPC capture), `e-delivery`/portal uploads (virus scan, OCR), `print-mail` inbound scanning (mail/fax), email-in; all produce `documents` with SHA-256 and `communication.inbound.received`.
- **Document AI:** Anthropic Messages API with vision for classification/extraction; outputs stored with confidence; no PII leaves the vault except to the model provider under the LL-2026-04 vendor clauses.
- **Third-party verification:** IVES vendor for Form 4506-C (API/SFTP, vendor-specific **[UNVERIFIED]**), consumer report pull (FCRA permissible purpose: review of an existing account), employment/income verification vendors — all modelled as `lossmit_requirements{source=third_party}` with `integration_messages` outbox rows and retry.
- **SMDU:** not used in 12.1 except an optional early "imminent default / eligibility pre-screen" read (12.2). 
- **Fannie Mae reporting:** delinquency status code **H5 "Complete BRP"** (F-1-21) when a complete BRP is on hand; via the F-1-21 file today and the Servicing Platform Delinquency Reporting event ("Borrower Solicitation Package" action type) after the Feb–Mar 2027 go-live (5.x owns the channel).
- **Print/mail & e-delivery:** acknowledgment channel per Notice Registry rule (E-SIGN consent for the "loss mitigation notices" class, else first-class mail; CA/NY overlays mail by default).
- Failure modes: if document AI is down, the completeness review falls back to a human `lossmit_reviewer` checklist task within the 5-day window (never breach the clock for a tooling outage); if print-mail rejects, re-render and re-send with a new proof.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `lossmit-underwriter` (sub-role `intake`), invoked by the Intake Router. Tools: `lossmit.application.open/update`, `documents.classify_extract`, `requirements.compute`, `calendar.federal_bd`, `notice.render_send`, `contacts.log`, `timers.start`, `foreclosure_holds.set`, `fnma.status_code.report`, `sii.lookup`, `transfer.lossmit_file.get`.
- **Decision record** (`agent_decisions.kind='lossmit.completeness'`): `{application_id, rule_set, model_version, prompt_version, received_date, sale_date?, forty_five_day_test, options_considered[], requirements[{code, status, evidence_doc_id, extracted_fields, confidence}], determination ∈ {complete, incomplete, duplicative}, reasonable_date, reasonable_date_basis, notice_template, rationale}`.
- **Guardrails:** may not classify an RFA as "not an application" when confidence <0.80; may not mark an item `waived` without a catalog waiver rule; never asks for documents already in the file; never issues an ack after the clock without an `escalations` row; every borrower-facing sentence comes from the approved template (no free-text legal statements).
- **Escalations:** potential successor → 4.4 flow; duplicative determination → `lossmit_reviewer` approval (removes rights); application from a borrower in active bankruptcy → `bankruptcy-ops` notification (no change to Reg X duties; comment 41(f)-? not applicable — the automatic stay affects foreclosure, not evaluation); disaster overlay → D1-3-01 requirement set (180-day docs); borrower asks for a human → `human_agent`.
- **Disclosure/consent:** AI voice/chat sessions open with the automation disclosure (baseline §8); TCPA consent required for outbound diligence calls to cell phones (`consents.tcpa_voice`); SMS diligence reminders require `tcpa_sms`.
- **AI-off path:** feature flag `lossmit.intake.ai=false` routes completeness reviews to a human queue with the same tools and the same timers; templates and calculations are unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Application ≤45 days before sale:** no (b)(2) duty; D2-2-05 plan notice + expedited review (12.2); if the sale is postponed so that the complete application is now >37 days before the new date, protections do not retroactively attach (comment 41(b)(3)-2 fixes the tier at receipt) — but Fannie Mae E-3.4-01 review still applies.
- **Sale scheduled after receipt:** tier fixed at receipt (no sale = ≥90 tier).
- **Transfer-in mid-application (1.7):** transferor dates carry; if the transferor sent no ack, `REGX_1024_41K2_TRANSFEREE_ACK_10`; documents received by the transferor after transfer keep the transferor date.
- **Transfer-out mid-application (17.4):** freeze timers with statuses; ship `lossmit_file` (application, requirements, notices, holds) in the goodbye package.
- **Bankruptcy:** intake continues; communications go through debtor's counsel where represented (E-2.x); the (c)(3) foreclosure statement is adapted to "subject to the automatic stay".
- **SCRA:** military-orders documents trigger 13.8/13.9 checks; Military Indulgence (12.9 note).
- **Disaster:** 180-day income documents; disaster forbearance without a BRP (12.4).
- **Partial/illegible documents:** treated as received-but-deficient → supplemental request; never silently ignored (CFPB chatbot "doom loop" finding, 4.1).
- **Vendor outage (IVES/consumer report):** third-party items never block completeness; (c)(4) handling in 12.2.
- **Borrower withdraws:** `withdrawn` with written confirmation; holds released; a later application is a new application (not duplicative unless prior was complete and processed).
- **Same-day multiple submissions:** consolidate into one application; the earliest receipt date governs.
- **Language access:** non-English requests routed to interpreter services; NPRM translations behind flag.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **12.1-T1 (happy path):** Given a borrower's portal submission Thu 2026-09-10 with Form 710 and two paystubs, when classified, then `NTC_REGX_41B2_ACK_INCOMPLETE` (missing bank statements, co-borrower signature) is sent by 2026-09-17 with reasonable date 2026-10-15 and the basis recorded.
- **12.1-T2 (holiday arithmetic):** received Fri 2026-11-20 → ack due Mon 2026-11-30 (Thanksgiving excluded); timer due_at equals 2026-11-30 23:59 servicer-local.
- **12.1-T3 (milestone cap and floor):** (a) sale 2026-11-20, ack sent 2026-09-15 → reasonable date = min(2026-10-15, sale−38 = 2026-10-13) = **2026-10-13**; (b) same sale, ack sent 2026-10-08 → cap 2026-10-13 is earlier than the 7-day floor 2026-10-15 → the floor wins (comment 41(b)(2)(ii)-3; comment 41(k)(2)-1 logic) → **2026-10-15**, and `milestone_conflict=true` routes the file to `lossmit_reviewer` for expedited handling.
- **12.1-T4 (facially complete):** all listed items received 2026-10-01 → `facially_complete_at=2026-10-01`; verification finds a stale paystub → supplemental request 2026-10-02 with date ≥2026-10-09; `foreclosure_holds{kind=regx_f2_prefiling}` active throughout; borrower complies 2026-10-07 → `deemed_complete_date=2026-10-01`, `complete_at=2026-10-07`; (c)(3) notice by 2026-10-14.
- **12.1-T5 (≤45 days):** sale 2026-10-20, received 2026-09-10 → (b)(2) not applicable; `NTC_FNMA_D2205_LATE_BRP_PLAN` within 5 servicer BD; expedited review queued.
- **12.1-T6 (duplicative):** prior complete application evaluated 2026-03-15, borrower delinquent continuously → new application 2026-09-10 flagged duplicative; `lossmit_reviewer` approves; Fannie Mae evaluation still performed; no (b)(2)/(c)(3) timers started; courtesy notice sent.
- **12.1-T7 (current in between):** same but loan brought current 2026-06-01 → full process.
- **12.1-T8 (RFA only):** call "what programs do you have?" with no financial info → `rfa_only`, no ack timer, CA SPOC assignment (4.3) and solicitation package sent; when the borrower later says "my income dropped by half," application opens with that date.
- **12.1-T9 (AI outage):** document AI unavailable for 3 days → human checklist task completes the determination on day 4; ack on time; incident logged.
- **12.1-T10 (CA per-document ack):** each of three separate uploads on a CA loan receives an acknowledgment within 5 business days.
- **12.1-T11 (NPRM flag):** with `2024nprm`, an oral RFA received 40 days before a sale opens a review cycle and sets a `foreclosure_holds{kind=lm_review_cycle}` hold; with `2013`, it does not.
- **12.1-T12 (breach):** ack not produced by day 5 (simulated print failure) → escalation `officer` sev-1 created at 00:05 day 6, ack re-sent, NoE-risk flag set on the loan.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Conservative application threshold.** Default: any evaluative information converts an RFA into an application (over-recognition). Cost: more 5-day clocks. Recommended: keep.
2. **Successor applications before confirmation.** Default: preserve and evaluate on confirmation (comment 41(b)-1); alternative "evaluate now" per 4.4-Q3 requires partner sign-off.
3. **Reasonable-date policy.** Default: 30 days capped by milestones, 7-day floor, with `milestone_conflict` review when the cap is earlier than the floor.
4. **Colorado-style AI pre-decision notice in every acknowledgment nationwide.** Default: yes (one template; satisfies the strictest regime; supports LL-2026-04 transparency).
5. **Third-party verification vendor set** (IVES, VOE/VOI). Default: one IVES vendor + consumer report; VOE optional.
