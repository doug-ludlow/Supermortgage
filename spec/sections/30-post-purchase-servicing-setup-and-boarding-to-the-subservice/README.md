## Overview

**Scope.** Section 30 is where an origination becomes a serviced loan. It has two clocks that the blueprint collapsed into one. The first runs from **funding**: the loan is boarded onto the Supermortgage servicing platform the day it disburses (30.2), its escrow account is established from the origination-side aggregate analysis that already fed the Closing Disclosure and the initial escrow statement delivered at settlement (30.3), and every servicing timer is seeded from that day (30.4). The second runs from **purchase**: when Fannie Mae funds the purchase price the loan must be established in Fannie Mae's investor-reporting records, reported on a LAR in the acquisition month, given its Escrow Setup event under LL-2026-05, moved from the pre-purchase custodial cash into the Fannie Mae custodial accounts, and shown in MERS with Fannie Mae as investor (30.1). 30.4 then carries the loan through its first three billing cycles — the Fannie Mae loan purchase letter that satisfies §1026.39, the first periodic statement, the first Metro 2 cycle, vendor activations (tax service, flood life-of-loan, insurance tracking, MI), early-payment-default monitoring for 28.2, the §1024.38(c)(2) servicing-file capability, origination record retention classes — and closes the hand-off at 90 days. Every rule is written to the eCFR pages current as of Sept 3–9, 2026, the Servicing Guide topics dated through May 13, 2026, LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026), the Selling Guide edition of Sept 2, 2026 and the LLPA Matrix of Sept 9, 2026; the fixture calendar (consummation Fri Nov 6 → disbursement/boarding Thu Nov 12 → Loan Delivery Mon Nov 16 → custodian certified Wed Nov 18 → purchased Thu Nov 19, 2026 → first payment Fri Jan 1, 2027) is worked in every process.

**Agents.** The `boarding` agent owns 30.2 (mapping, the DQ gate OB-001…OB-022/OW-001…OW-012, the opening ledger, documents and consents, the first-payment letter) and 30.4 (the hand-off checklist HO-001…HO-022, vendor activations, first-cycle reconciliations, EPD flags, the servicing-file compile, retention classification, closure). The `investor-reporting` agent owns 30.1 (Fannie Mae loan number and servicer number, establishment verification, the first LAR, the Escrow Setup event, the MERS/eNote/custody post-purchase checks) with `custodial-recon` for the pre-purchase-to-Fannie-Mae custodial transfers and `post-closing` for the MERS Interim Funder release. The `escrow` agent owns 30.3 with the deterministic Escrow Analysis Engine (3.2/3.4) and hands the account to 3.x's annual cycle; the `disclosure` agent consumes 30.3's figures for the CD (25.2) and the initial statement (25.4/3.1); `payoff-release` handles the same-servicer escrow credit. `compliance-sentinel` publishes `CS_BOARDED_ORIGINATIONS_DAILY`. Humans appear only where law or policy names them: the partner `officer` for escrow waivers outside the written policy and for hand-off closure with open statutory items; `fnma_portal_operator` for Investor Reporting Representative packages when establishment fails; `human_agent` on borrower request. The subservicing specs' human-boarding roles (transfer-in reconciliation) do not exist here — there is no transferor.

**Partner/SM boundary.** The partner is the creditor, the Fannie Mae seller and servicer of record (Fannie Mae's servicer number, Form 101 already executed, the written escrow-waiver policy B2-1.5-04 requires, the QC plan that must target EPDs — D1-3-01) and the owner of the origination record on Fannie Mae's behalf (A2-4.1-02 — "Fannie Mae's property"). SM is the subservicer named as servicer and payee at closing, so it is the servicer from payment 1 under §1024.2(b): no RESPA transfer notice exists at boarding (§1024.33(b)), and SM in its own name owes the initial escrow statement (§1024.17(g)), the periodic statement (§1026.41), the furnisher duties (FCRA/C-4.1-01), the servicing file (§1024.38(c)) and every investor-reporting act. Fannie Mae is the §1026.39 covered person and sends its own letter — the platform expects and evidences it, never duplicates it. MERS ownership updates after purchase are Fannie Mae's action (B8-7-01); SM verifies.

**Dependencies.** Inbound: 26.3 `loan.funded` with the settlement figures and prepaid interest; 26.1/26.2 closing documents, consents and the eNote; 26.4 recording/trailing documents; 25.2 CD versions and `cd_figure_sources`; 25.3 `rescission_extended_until`; 25.4 closing-package notices, escrow elections, `tax_reporting_seeds` and the §1026.39 expectation; 24.4 APN; 24.5 `flood_determinations`/`insurance_policies`; 24.6 `mi_certificates`/`hpa_disclosures`; 23.4 `hpml_determinations`; 21.1 `applicant_demographics`; 20.3 `consents.scope`; 29.3/29.4 delivery data, custodian certification and `loan.purchased`; 27.2 matched purchase advice, PTR, remittance type and Interim Funder release; 28.2 QC selections. **Changes in flight that shape the build.** (1) LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026): the Escrow Setup event for newly acquired loans is mandatory Dec 1, 2026 — the fixture is purchased Nov 19, so the loan is in the last voluntary window; the platform sends the event from day one behind `investor_reporting.escrow_events`. (2) Servicing Guide C-4.3-01 (04/08/2026) and the TT96 guide: the first LAR is due in the acquisition month, not the month after — 30.1 targets Mon Nov 23, hard deadline Wed Dec 2, 2026 17:00 ET. (3) A2-1-07 (05/13/2026) extends subservicing to "newly originated mortgage loans" and requires the subservicer's custodial accounts; F-1-11 makes the five fair-lending data elements queryable. (4) No escrow-waiver LLPA exists on the 09.09.2026 matrix; the waiver is a partner-policy decision. (5) SVC-2026-04 removed Fannie Mae's RON video-retention requirement (loans closed on/after May 6, 2026); state rules still govern `ron_recording_state_<n>y`. (6) The LL-2026-04 AI governance regime applies to every agent decision record in this section.
