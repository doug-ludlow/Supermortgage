### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Reg X §1024.38(b)(4)(i)** (eCFR current as of Sept 8, 2026): policies and procedures reasonably designed to ensure that, "as a transferor servicer, [the servicer can] timely transfer all information and documents in the possession or control of the servicer relating to a transferred mortgage loan to a transferee servicer in a form and manner that ensures the accuracy of the information and documents transferred and that enables a transferee servicer to comply with the terms of the transferee servicer's obligations to the owner or assignee of the mortgage loan and applicable law." Comment 38(b)(4)(i)-1: electronic transfer is permitted if "reasonably designed to ensure the accuracy" and the transferee can board and identify the documents; comment 38(b)(4)(i)-2: the transfer must include "information reflecting the current status of discussions with a borrower regarding loss mitigation options, any agreements entered into with a borrower on a loss mitigation option, and any analysis by a servicer with respect to potential recovery from a non-performing mortgage loan." **§1024.35(b)(8)**: "failure to transfer accurately and timely information relating to the servicing of a borrower's mortgage loan account to a transferee servicer" is a covered error. **§1024.38(c)(1)**: records documenting actions taken on the account are retained "until one year after the date a mortgage loan is discharged or servicing of a mortgage loan is transferred by the servicer to a transferee servicer"; (c)(2): a servicing file (transaction schedule incl. escrow and suspense, security instrument, contact notes, data-field report, borrower-submitted documents) compilable within five days. **§1024.35(g)(1)(iii)** and **§1024.36(f)(1)(v)**: a notice of error or information request is untimely only if delivered "more than one year after … servicing for the mortgage loan … was transferred from the servicer receiving the [notice/request] to a transferee servicer" — so Supermortgage must keep answering NoEs/RFIs about its own servicing for one year after T (5/7/30/10 business-day clocks unchanged; 4.1/4.2).

**CFPB Bulletin 2020-02** (not withdrawn in May 2025): transfer plan with communications, testing, milestones and escalation; "tailored testing protocols" and quality control "after a transfer of preliminary data to validate that the data on the transferee's system matches the data submitted"; "transferors must work with transferees to ensure that the new information and documents are transferred" when borrowers keep sending materials to the transferor; transferors should "respond to requests from transferees for missing documents and information"; a "post-transfer review or de-brief"; Appendix A data categories I–X (foundational loan data, investor, escrow, PMI, FHA, hazard/flood, loss mitigation, foreclosure, bankruptcy, signing authority), with PMI cancellation dates and loss-mitigation documents flagged as heightened risk.

**Servicing Guide A2-7-03 (05/13/2026).** The transferor must "deliver to the transferee servicer the complete individual mortgage loan file," "identify to the transferee servicer any mortgage loans that are in foreclosure, bankruptcy, or subject to a workout option," "turn over the complete books and records to the transferee servicer of each mortgage loan or acquired property," and "maintain adequate records of the mortgage loans and acquired properties included in the portfolio transfer in their corporate records"; "the transferor or transferee servicer must take certain actions to ensure that all servicing functions that involve third parties will continue uninterrupted (or will be discontinued if that is appropriate)"; the transferor must "advise the transferor document custodian … within 30 days of the transfer effective date" and arrange "the prompt and safe transfer of the custodial documents"; "the transferor servicer must submit the monthly LARs for Fannie Mae's investor reporting system for the month of the transfer date" and "is contractually responsible for all remittances due Fannie Mae for the final monthly accounting period"; "the transferor servicer must prepare and record an assignment, if required, to ensure the chain of assignment is complete."

**F-1-11 (05/13/2026)** — the transferor's deliverables: MI approvals/commitments or master-policy evidence; list of loans with borrower-paid or lender-purchased MI (premium rates, next due dates); list of eMortgages; **trial balances as of close of business the day before transfer** showing remittance type, remittance cycle, participation percentage, pool numbers, delinquencies/foreclosures/bankruptcies/acquired properties, pending transfers/payoffs/modifications, escrow balances, advances, curtailments, unapplied funds, loss drafts and buydown balances; custodial bank reconciliation for each account as of the cutoff date "(or within five business days after transfer if unable to complete by transfer date)"; investor accounting reports for the three months preceding the cutoff; reconciliation of the outstanding shortage/surplus balance; code definitions; escrow analyses; delinquency-management/default-prevention information; copies of all documents including custodian-held items and modification agreements; all customer correspondence, complaints and escalated cases; title policies; foreclosure/bankruptcy lists with law-firm names and addresses; records for loans in foreclosure/bankruptcy/workout and for acquired properties; litigation records; state Address Confidentiality Program enrollment with substitute address; and, for loans originated on/after March 1, 2023, queryable fair-lending data (race, ethnicity, age, gender, preferred language). **Funds:** forward "all P&I and T&I custodial account balances" including unremitted P&I collections, escrow funds, unapplied funds, loss drafts, accruals on deposit and buydown funds; "if the transferor servicer has advanced delinquent interest or scheduled P&I to Fannie Mae, the transferee servicer must reimburse the transferor servicer once it receives a final accounting of all monies from the transferor servicer." **Reporting:** "in the month of the transfer date, the transferor servicer will be contractually responsible for reporting the monthly LAR for all mortgage loan activity processed on the mortgage loans, and ensuring that sufficient funds to satisfy that month's remittance obligation are available for drafting on the scheduled remittance date. However, the transferor and transferee servicers may agree that the transferee servicer will make the actual remittance"; "in the month following the transfer date, the transferee servicer will be responsible for reporting"; the transferor gives the transferee copies of the shortage/surplus reconciliations "for the final monthly accounting period"; any Fannie Mae adjustment "must be requested within 30 days after the transfer date" through the Investor Reporting Representative; "notify the hazard, flood, earthquake, other property insurance carriers" to endorse the transferee into the mortgagee clause and change the premium billing address; "notify any tax or flood service provider and any optional insurance provider" whether the transferee will continue their services; TOS is initiated by the transferor Servicer (1.5). **MGIC Servicing Guide (Mar 12, 2026):** "Notify us within 60 days of acquiring or selling servicing rights"; the notice carries certificate number, borrower name, selling servicer, "new Servicer's name and address," new loan number, "effective date of the change of Servicer"; via MGIC/Link, SFT, e-mail or fax; other MIs' transfer-notice rules **[UNVERIFIED — default: notify before T under each master policy]**. **Retention:** Selling Guide A2-4.1-02 / Servicing Guide A2-5-01 make loan records Fannie Mae's property (life of loan + 4 years after liquidation for the servicer holding them); after transfer the transferor keeps "adequate records" (A2-7-03) with no stated period. **Consolidated Technology Guide (Apr 21, 2026):** Fannie Mae Data belongs to Fannie Mae and may be used only for internal mortgage-related purposes; Escrow Setup events for acquired loans are the transferee's (1.1). **MISMO Servicing Transfer Catalog** (Candidate Recommendation): Implementation Guide, Servicing Transfer Instructions with Portfolio Characteristics, Image Transfer Schedule, Required Reporting and Reconciliation & Balancing templates; the foundation of the ITSD (LBDS Candidate Recommendation Oct 2, 2025; MISMO v3.6) — Fannie Mae's transfer page points to it as the minimum documentation set.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint:** (1) "timely, accurate" is a policies-and-procedures standard; the operative dates come from F-1-11 (trial balance as of COB T-1; custodial reconciliation within 5 BD; 30-day adjustment request; the transferor reports the transfer month), A2-7-03 (custodian notice within 30 days) and the MI master policies (MGIC 60 days); (2) "Systems: Transferee" omits the custodians, Fannie Mae (final period, shortage/surplus), MERS/eRegistry, insurers, MI, tax/flood vendors, taxing authorities/HOAs, law firms, trustees and credit bureaus; (3) the blueprint omits funds transfer, the final accounting and advances reimbursement, the transferee's liability for unresolved Fannie Mae shortages, the one-year NoE/RFI tail and record retention.

### Operational prerequisites
- Subservicing/transfer agreement terms: deliverable schedule (test T-30, preliminary T-14, final T-1 COB delivered T+1 BD, images T+5 BD), wire timing (T+1 BD), advances reimbursement (net within 30 days of final accounting), support window (90 days), transferee acknowledgment SLA (2 BD), transition fees — Partner/transferee + Supermortgage.
- Transferee data-exchange setup: SFTP/PGP keys, MISMO MSTC templates or agreed CSV layouts, image index format, acknowledgment format — Supermortgage; 2–4 weeks.
- Transferee custodian identity and Form 2017 evidence (from Form 629/Custodian Matrix); D-Code (17.1).
- Vendor termination/transfer clauses executed for tax service, flood, insurance tracking/LPI, print-mail, e-delivery, telephony, lockbox, bankruptcy monitoring — Supermortgage.
- MI trading-partner transfer-notice formats (MGIC/Link or SFT; other MIs) — Supermortgage.
- Retention classes `regx_1y_post_transfer` and `transfer_out_archive` (decision 4) configured with legal-hold support (19.1) — Supermortgage.
- Supermortgage `officer` designated to sign the data-quality attestation and the final accounting; partner `officer` for Fannie Mae correspondence.
- **Custodial-account disposition plan — Supermortgage** (with partner countersignature as master servicer of record): the accounts holding P&I and T&I for the transferred population are Supermortgage's own (A2-1-07; Forms 1013/1014 executed by Supermortgage in CBAM under the partner's servicer numbers — 6.1, 2.1). They are **not** assigned to the transferee. Before T, identify per account whether the whole population or only part of it is leaving; after the F-1-11 reconciliation and the T+30 adjustment window, settle to zero and close the fully-vacated accounts (Forms 1013/1014 withdrawn, bank closure letters filed) or file a post-transfer reconciliation certificate for accounts that retain loans — `SM_XFER_OUT_CUSTODIAL_CLOSE_60`. Bank lead time for closure and the CBAM withdrawal step: 2–4 weeks.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `transfer.batch.approved{direction=out}`, `transfer.loan_list.attested` (17.1) → deliverable plan; `transfer.batch.cutover_completed` → final tape, funds and final-period tasks; `fnma.transfer.processed` (BD3) → post-transfer mode.
- Scheduler: nightly deliverable generation from T-30; `cutover_freeze` job at COB T-1 (after the last posting batch); daily forwarding file; monthly final-accounting reconciliation until settled.
- Inbound: transferee acknowledgments and exception files; `transferee_request.received` (missing documents/information); custodian acknowledgments, shipment manifests and exception notices; MI/insurer/vendor acknowledgments; Fannie Mae IR representative correspondence on the shortage/surplus adjustment; NoE/RFI/complaints received after T (4.x); credit disputes (8.2); returned mail.
- Bank feeds: wire confirmations for custodial balance transfers and daily forwarding wires.

#### State machine
Batch 17.3 sub-state: `plan_ready` → `test_tape_sent` (T-30) → `preliminary_sent` (T-14; transferee load report received) → `counterparties_notified` (all `counterparty_notifications` due before T sent) → `frozen` (COB T-1: postings stopped, `payment_holds{transfer_out_cutover}` on every loan) → `final_sent` (final tape + trial balance + histories by T+1 BD) → `funds_wired` (T+1 BD; wires matched to trial-balance totals) → `final_period_closed` (BD2 17:00 ET) → `final_accounting_delivered` (≤ T+30) → `advances_settled` → `custodian_shipped` (≤ 30 days after TED notification) → `support_window` (days 1–90) → `retention` (to T+1 year for NoE/RFI; archive to `retain_until`) → `closed`. Per deliverable: `planned` → `generated` → `validated` → `attested` → `delivered` → `acked` (or `exception` → `resolved`). Transitions by the `transfer`, `custodial-recon`, `investor-reporting` and `security-records` agents; attestation and final accounting require Supermortgage `officer`; Fannie Mae adjustment requests require the partner `officer`.

#### Business rules and calculations
- **Deliverable schedule (T = Tue Dec 1, 2026):** test tape by Nov 1; preliminary by Nov 17 (T-14); preliminary QC by Nov 24; counterparties notified by Mon Nov 30 (T-1 BD); freeze at COB Nov 30 after the last posting batch; final tape, trial balance (as of COB Nov 30) and life-of-loan histories delivered by Wed Dec 2; custodial wires by Dec 2; images with index by Dec 8 (T+5 BD: Dec 2, 3, 4, 7, 8); custodial bank reconciliations as of Nov 30 by Dec 8; final period (November activity) closed Wed Dec 2 17:00 ET; Fannie Mae processes the transfer Thu Dec 3; shortage/surplus adjustment request and final accounting by Thu Dec 31; custodian notified at approval and documents shipped within 30 days of that notice; MGIC notice by Jan 30, 2027 (sent Nov 30 in practice); support window through Mon Mar 1, 2027; NoE/RFI tail and Reg X retention floor to Wed Dec 1, 2027.
- **Outbound data-quality gate:** every deliverable is generated from the system of record via the MISMO v3.6 mapping set (ITSD/LBDS paths; the same `mapping_rules` used in 1.1, reversed), then validated with the 1.1 hard/warning rules in outbound mode (`HF-001`–`HF-020`, `W-001`–`W-016`) plus tie-outs: Σ tape UPB = Σ trial-balance UPB = Σ ledger `principal` = Fannie Mae position after the final period; escrow, unapplied, advances, fees, deferred/forborne balances tie per loan; document counts tie to the image index. Any hard failure blocks attestation; the `officer` attestation states the as-of date, the rule set version, the scorecard and the tie-out results (§1024.38(b)(4)(i) "form and manner that ensures the accuracy").
- **Trial balance content (F-1-11):** per loan — Fannie Mae loan number, servicer loan number, borrower, property, remittance type (A/A, S/A, S/S), remittance cycle, participation %, pool number, UPB, LPI date, next due date, interest rate/P&I, escrow balance, escrow advances, corporate advances, curtailments in period, unapplied/suspense, loss-draft balance, buydown balance, delinquency status (both `regx_days_delinquent` and `fnma_delinquency_status`), foreclosure/bankruptcy/REO flags, pending payoff/modification/transfer flags, MI flag, eNote flag (SFC 508 excluded from the custodian's recert list), custodian, MIN, ACP flag.
- **Funds (worked example, cents; interest-on-escrow state NY at 2% for L1):** L1 — escrow 184,250 + November interest round_half_up(184,250 × 0.02 / 12) = 307.08 → **307** → escrow to wire 184,557; unapplied 32,500; corporate advances 15,000 (receivable); late charges due 6,464 (memo receivable). L2 — S/S loan three installments delinquent, escrow −41,300 (Supermortgage advanced taxes → receivable), scheduled P&I advanced to Fannie Mae under Stop Delinquency Advance rules for 3 months = 3 × 161,603 = **484,809** `servicer_advance_receivable`. L3 — escrow 92,015 (no interest state); a prepaid Dec 1 installment received Nov 28 of 205,800 (P&I belonging to the transferee's period). T&I wire = 184,557 + 92,015 = **276,572**. P&I forwardable wire = unapplied 32,500 + prepaid next-period P&I 205,800 = **238,300**. Retained for the final-period remittance: all P&I collected for installments due ≤ Nov 1 and reported in the November period (drafted by Fannie Mae in December from Supermortgage's custodial account per 5.2 — the parties may instead agree the transferee remits). Final accounting receivables from the transferee: 484,809 (P&I advances) + 41,300 (escrow advance) + 15,000 (corporate advances, recoverable from the borrower per the note) = **541,109**; late charges 6,464 travel as a borrower receivable, not a transferee obligation. Ledger at freeze: Dr `escrow` (liability) 276,572 / Cr `custodial_ti_cash` 276,572 on wire; Dr `suspense/unapplied` 32,500 + Dr `fnma_remittance_payable`(next-period prepaid) 205,800 / Cr `custodial_pi_cash` 238,300 on wire; advances: Dr `due_from_transferee` 541,109 / Cr `servicer_advance_receivable` 484,809, `escrow_advances` 41,300, `corporate_advances` 15,000 when the final accounting is acked; Dr `custodial_pi_cash`(or operating) / Cr `due_from_transferee` on reimbursement. Rounding: escrow interest rounds half-up to cents at the monthly accrual step (3.9).
- **Final-period reporting:** all events processed ≤ T-1 are reported by their 5.1 deadlines (last: events processed Mon Nov 30 due 3:00 a.m. ET Tue Dec 1 event-based / 8 p.m. ET Dec 1 LAR); removals processed Nov 30 by 5 p.m. ET Dec 1; corrections by BD1 8 p.m. ET (non-removal) and BD2 5 p.m. ET (removal); the November period closes Dec 2 17:00 ET with the Form 496/496A trial-balance tie-out; November delinquency status reporting (F-1-21/AMN, BD2) and MI default reports for November (by the 25th) are Supermortgage's; the "no payment" day-22 events for November were due Nov 22 (5.1). No event with an activity date ≥ T is created; a payment received on/after T is a misdirected payment (17.2), never a `payment.received` on the loan.
- **Shortage/surplus:** the A/A Form 472 Schedule 3 balance and the cash position by remittance type are reconciled as of the November period; the transferee receives copies; an adjustment request goes to the Fannie Mae IR representative by T+30 with support; unresolved shortages shift to the transferee (F-1-11), so `officer` must sign the reconciliation showing zero unresolved items or the adjustment request evidence.
- **Counterparty notification content:** MI — certificate number, borrower, Supermortgage as selling/transferring servicer, transferee name/address/loan number, effective date, requestor contact (MGIC list; others by master policy); hazard/flood/earthquake/LPI carriers — mortgagee-clause endorsement to the transferee "ISAOA/ATIMA" and billing address; tax/flood service and optional insurance — continue/discontinue per the transferee's election with contract copies or tape-transfer notice; taxing authorities/HOAs/leaseholders/lienholders/utilities — transferee name/address for escrowed items; law firms — transferee contact, hold/proceed instructions and file-transfer instructions (17.4); trustees/debtor counsel — new payment address and servicer identity (14.x); MERS — partner's MIN Update (Subservicer field cleared or replaced; TOS for sales) and eRegistry Servicing Agent update for eNotes (via the eVault vendor); credit bureaus — final cycle status 05 with date closed = T (8.1); e-OSCAR — respond to ACDVs received before T on Supermortgage's data; after T route disputes about the transferee's period to the transferee (8.2); print/e-delivery — stop statements for cycles due ≥ T (7.1), return-mail routing; telephony — scripts; lockbox — coupon routing; custodial bank — no new drafts after the final-period draft; CBAM — LOA cancellation for the last batch (6.1/6.2).
- **Retention:** `retain_until = max(transfer_date + 1 year [§1024.38(c)(1)], transfer_date + 7 years [policy `transfer_out_archive`, decision 4], any legal hold)`; after `retain_until`, PII is de-identified per 19.1 and the manifest (hashes, dates) is kept indefinitely. Loan-file copies are Fannie Mae's property (A2-4.1-02) — access on Fannie Mae/partner request within 5 business days (§1024.38(c)(2) analog).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`transfer` agent (tools: `planDeliverables`, `generateDeliverable`, `runOutboundDqGate`, `buildTrialBalance`, `buildImageIndex`, `sendDeliverable`, `ingestTransfereeAck`, `answerTransfereeRequest`, `notifyCounterparty`, `verifyMersSnapshot`, `verifyERegistry`, `buildDebrief`, `writeDecision`) orchestrates the schedule end-to-end; `custodial-recon` agent (`freezeLedgers`, `computeWires`, `matchWires`, `buildFinalAccounting`, `reconcileShortageSurplus`, `postSettlement`) owns funds; `investor-reporting` agent closes the final period and drafts the adjustment request; `security-records` agent packages documents, drives the custodian shipment, writes archive manifests and executes de-identification at `retain_until`; `case` agent works NoE/RFI/complaints in the tail; `escrow` agent credits escrow interest through T-1. Decision record: `{batch_id, deliverable_id?, loan_id?, checks_run, tie_outs, exceptions, action, confidence, rationale, model_version, rule_set_version}`. Guardrails: the agent may not alter any balance to make a tie-out pass — differences are categorized (1.6 taxonomy) and resolved with evidence or escalated; attestation, final accounting and any write-off require Supermortgage `officer`; Fannie Mae adjustment requests and MERS/TOS submissions under the partner's credentials require the partner `officer`/`fnma_portal_operator`; `signing_officer` for any assignment, allonge or Form 2009 execution; `attorney` for litigation-file handoffs where counsel of record must substitute (17.4). No borrower contact from 17.3. Human path: ops-console transfer-out workbench with the same deliverable and notification queues.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Payment posted on T-1 but reversed (NSF) after T: reversal is reported by Supermortgage as a final-period correction if before BD2 close; otherwise communicated to the transferee in the forwarding file and the final accounting (the transferee reports it) — Fannie Mae's post-period rules per 5.1.
- Loan pays off between attestation and T: removal reported by Supermortgage (5.3); loan marked `withdrawn_after_attestation`; payoff proceeds remitted by Supermortgage (16.2); Fannie Mae informed.
- Transferee cannot board a loan (their hard fail): Supermortgage supplies corrections within 5 BD; if the transferee refuses the loan, the loan still transferred in Fannie Mae's records on BD3 — escalate to the partner; Supermortgage may not resume servicing without a new Form 629.
- Wire shortfall claimed by the transferee: 1.6 variance process in reverse; Supermortgage's trial balance and bank evidence control; disputes to `officer`.
- Stop Delinquency Advance loans: the advances receivable is billed to the transferee; Fannie Mae reimbursement claims (15.4) after T are the transferee's — Supermortgage must not file them.
- Open insurance claim/loss draft: restricted escrow balance wired with the claim file; contractor payment schedule and inspection results in D23/D34 (9.7).
- Loans in foreclosure with notes out to counsel: Form 2009 stays open; counsel instructed to hold the note for the transferee's counsel or return it to the custodian (17.4).
- eNote: Servicing Agent must be updated before T; if the transferee is not on eRegistry, the partner/Fannie Mae must resolve — Supermortgage cannot leave itself as Servicing Agent past T (unauthorized status) and escalates at T-5 BD.
- Non-MERS loan where Supermortgage was mortgagee of record (not the default): assignment to the transferee/subservicer prepared and recorded by Supermortgage as transferor (A2-7-03) — `signing_officer`, `erecording`.
- Transferee is a non-MERS servicer (`servicing_sale`): assignment from MERS and MIN deactivation "Transfer to Non-MERS Status" by the partner (F-1-11).
- Fannie Mae termination for cause with immediate effect: deliverables compressed to Fannie Mae's instructions; the final-period and funds rules do not change; the platform records instruction dates as anchors.
- Fannie Mae rejects the final-period file after BD2: corrections in the next period are the transferee's; Supermortgage supplies data within 5 BD and records the exposure (F-1-11 "not promptly resolved").
- Transferee requests beyond the support window: still answered (Bulletin 2020-02; §1024.38(c)); SLA relaxes to 10 BD after day 90 (policy).
- Bankruptcy trustee continues to pay Supermortgage after T: forward with receipt dates; trustee notice re-sent.
- Successor in interest confirmed pre-transfer: `parties` records in D30 with confirmation dates; pending SII requests in D34 (17.4).
- Legal hold or open litigation: `legal_hold=true` blocks de-identification; litigation records in D22 with counsel notified.
- Data-use restriction: Fannie Mae Data pulled under the partner's Form 101 is not used after revocation except for retained records required by law — access to `fnma-*` adapters for the partner scope is disabled (17.1).

#### Audit and evidence
Every deliverable with hash, as-of date, DQ scorecard, tie-outs and `officer` attestation; transferee acknowledgments and load reports; wire confirmations matched to trial-balance totals; final-period close evidence, shortage/surplus reconciliation and adjustment correspondence; final accounting and settlement postings; counterparty notices with acknowledgments; custodian notices, manifests, Form 2009s and exception responses; MERS/eRegistry snapshots; transferee-request log with response times; archive manifests, retention and de-identification records; timers and `agent_decisions` — the evidence set for §1024.35(b)(8)/§1024.38(b)(4) claims, Fannie Mae MORA/QC on the transferred period and state exams.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Who makes the final-period remittance — Supermortgage (default; F-1-11 primary rule) vs the transferee by agreement (then unremitted P&I is forwarded and the agreement is on file).
2. Advances settlement mechanics — net settlement 30 days after the final accounting (default) vs gross.
3. Support-window SLAs — transferee requests 5 BD (default) within 90 days, 10 BD after; forwarding daily through day 90, on receipt thereafter.
4. Post-transfer archive retention — default `transfer_out_archive` = T + 7 years (floor §1024.38(c)(1) 1 year; A2-7-03 "adequate records"; state rules to be confirmed), with de-identification thereafter.
5. Whether P360 PFPIP enrollments and DRA firm associations need transferor action — default: statuses current through T-1; transferee re-enrolls; confirm with Fannie Mae.
