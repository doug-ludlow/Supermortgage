### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Fannie Mae.** The blueprint cites A2-1-01, but that topic (12/17/2025) only requires the servicer to "maintain accurate mortgage loan servicing and accounting records"; the operative retention text is **Selling Guide A2-4.1-02, Ownership and Retention of Loan Files and Records (12/19/2017)**, incorporated by **Servicing Guide A2-5-01 (02/14/2024)**, which simply cross-references Selling Guide A2-4.1-01 through A2-4.1-04. Verified operative language (Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026):
- Ownership: "All records related to loans (including all data and materials representing, based on, or compiled from such records) sold to or serviced for Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property"; the seller/servicer "has no right to possess these documents and records except under the conditions specified by Fannie Mae, and must hold these documents solely for the benefit of Fannie Mae."
- Audit: "Fannie Mae may examine and audit, at any reasonable time, all loan records and other information that Fannie Mae considers necessary," including "monitoring all monthly accounting reports submitted to Fannie Mae; conducting periodic procedural reviews during visits to the seller/servicer's office or the document custodian's place of business; conducting in-depth audits of the seller/servicer's internal records and operating procedures; and performing spot-check reviews of loans in the seller/servicer's portfolio on a random sample basis."
- General requirements: "The seller/servicer must ensure the accuracy, security, confidentiality, integrity, completeness and legibility of the individual loan file." "When Fannie Mae sends a written request to a seller/servicer to examine mortgage records, the seller/servicer must deliver all records to Fannie Mae or to whomever Fannie Mae designates within the time frame specified by Fannie Mae." "If the seller/servicer is retaining any of the records in a format other than paper, the seller/servicer must reproduce them at its own expense."
- Retention: "The servicer must maintain permanent mortgage account records for each loan it services for Fannie Mae." "After a loan is liquidated, the servicer must keep the individual loan records for at least four years, unless the local jurisdiction requires longer retention." "If a loan or property is repurchased or a make whole payment remitted, the responsible party must keep the individual loan records for at least four years from loan liquidation." "Unless instructed otherwise, the servicer may destroy any accounting reports 18 months after such reports are filed with Fannie Mae." "A servicer that is also a Fannie Mae document custodian must maintain a copy of Form 2001 for seven years at all locations" (not applicable — Supermortgage is not a custodian).
- **Selling Guide A2-4.1-03 (05/06/2026)**: electronic records are acceptable "provided they are capable of reproduction in paper format"; systems must "be sufficiently secure to preserve the integrity and authenticity of the records"; when converting paper to electronic, the seller/servicer "must retain documentation that explains the process used to convert paper-based records to electronic formats"; electronic signatures must comply with ESIGN/UETA with retained evidence of "the authenticated identity of the signer," attribution and date; paper originals remain required for "the promissory note and any related addenda (unless the seller has received special approval to deliver electronic notes)" and "any unrecorded ink-signed originals of documents that modify or supplement the security instrument."
- Transfers: A2-7-03/F-1-11 (05/13/2026) require the transferor to deliver a complete copy of the individual mortgage loan files and "transaction and payment histories for the life of the mortgage loans" (Section 1.1/17.3). The Guide sets no explicit post-transfer retention period for the transferor; Reg X's one-year floor applies and this spec's policy is four years (below).
- Supplement (Sept. 2, 2025): the code of conduct must address "record management"; annual training must cover "data retention"; the Company must "provide instructions on how to remove information from media such that the information cannot be retrieved or reconstructed."

**Reg X 12 CFR 1024.38(c)** (eCFR current as of Sept. 8, 2026; last amended 81 FR 72372, Oct. 19, 2016):
- (c)(1): "A servicer shall retain records that document actions taken with respect to a borrower's mortgage loan account until one year after the date a mortgage loan is discharged or servicing of a mortgage loan is transferred by the servicer to a transferee servicer." Comment 38(c)(1)-1: retention "does not necessarily mean actual paper copies of documents."
- (c)(2): "A servicer shall maintain the following documents and data on each mortgage loan account serviced by the servicer in a manner that facilitates compiling such documents and data into a servicing file within five days: (i) A schedule of all transactions credited or debited to the mortgage loan account, including any escrow account as defined in § 1024.17(b) and any suspense account; (ii) A copy of the security instrument that establishes the lien securing the mortgage loan; (iii) Any notes created by servicer personnel reflecting communications with the borrower about the mortgage loan account; (iv) To the extent applicable, a report of the data fields relating to the borrower's mortgage loan account created by the servicer's electronic systems in connection with servicing practices; and (v) Copies of any information or documents provided by the borrower to the servicer in accordance with the procedures set forth in § 1024.35 or § 1024.41." Comment 38(c)(2)(iv)-1: the data-field report "means a report listing the relevant data fields by name, populated with any specific data relating to the borrower's mortgage loan account." Comment 38(c)(2)-2: the servicing file "does not confer upon any borrower an independent right to access information" (borrower access runs through §1024.36 — Section 4.2). Failure to keep these records is a §1024.38 policies-and-procedures issue and a RESPA §6(f) litigation exposure via §1024.35(b)(11)-type errors.

**Other federal retention rules verified.** Reg Z §1026.25(a) (eCFR Sept. 4, 2026): a creditor "shall retain evidence of compliance with this part … for two years after the date disclosures are required to be made or action is required to be taken" (binds the creditor; the servicer holds those records for the owner — treated as a floor). Reg B §1002.12(b)(1): 25 months after notifying an applicant of action taken for the application, monitoring information, the notification/statement of reasons and any written allegation; §1002.12(b)(4): retain beyond 25 months while under investigation/enforcement until final disposition (loss-mitigation modification decisions are treated as credit decisions, Section 12). Reg F §1006.100 (eCFR Sept. 3, 2026): "A debt collector must retain records that are evidence of compliance or noncompliance with the FDCPA and this part starting on the date that the debt collector begins collection activity on a debt until three years after the debt collector's last collection activity on the debt"; call recordings "for three years after the date of the call" (applies where `fdcpa_debt_collector_flag = true`, Section 11.4). FCRA/Reg V: no furnisher retention period; 15 U.S.C. §1681p's 2-year/5-year limitations period drives Section 8's `fcra_furnishing_5y` policy class. TCPA: 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(10) (eCFR Sept. 8, 2026) — revocation "within a reasonable time not to exceed ten business days from receipt"; 64.1200(d)(6) — a do-not-call request "must be honored for 5 years"; the federal four-year catch-all limitations period (28 U.S.C. §1658(a)) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — statute not re-fetched]** underlies the baseline `tcpa_consent_4y` class. E-SIGN 15 U.S.C. §7001(d)(1): an electronic record satisfies a retention requirement if it "accurately reflect[s] the information set forth in the contract or other record" and "remains accessible to all persons who are entitled to access … in a form that is capable of being accurately reproduced for later reference"; §7001(d)(3): such a record satisfies "original form" requirements. IRS General Instructions for Certain Information Returns (2025): "keep copies of information returns you filed with the IRS, or have the ability to reconstruct the data, for at least 3 years (4 years for Form 1099-C) from the due date of the returns," and 4 years if backup withholding was imposed — Section 7's `tax_4y` class is therefore conservative and kept. FTC Disposal Rule 16 CFR 682.3 (eCFR Sept. 4, 2026): "reasonable measures to protect against unauthorized access to or use of the information in connection with its disposal" (shredding/pulverizing paper; destroying/erasing media; due diligence on disposal contractors). FTC Safeguards Rule 16 CFR 314.4(c)(6): secure disposal of customer information "no later than two years after the last date the information is used" unless "necessary for business operations or for other legitimate business purposes, is otherwise required to be retained by law or regulation, or where targeted disposal is not reasonably feasible."

**State.** New York 3 NYCRR 419.9 (amended Dec. 18, 2019): loan-level records "for at least three years after making the final entry with respect to any New York mortgage loan being serviced by the servicer, unless a longer period is provided by statute"; "a log of all telephone calls and file of all written correspondence, including fax transmissions and e-mail correspondence" for three years; complaint records with date received, assignee, nature, status and actions taken; books kept "in a manner that will allow the superintendent to determine whether the servicer is complying." 23 NYCRR 500.6: audit-trail records for "not fewer than five years" (the 2017 text distinguished 5 years for transaction-reconstruction records and 3 years for cybersecurity-event audit trails **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the copy read showed a single five-year period]**); 500.17(b): certification support records five years; 500.13(b): periodic secure disposal of nonpublic information no longer necessary except where retention is legally required or targeted disposal is infeasible. Other states' servicer-record rules are carried in `jurisdiction_rules.record_retention` **[UNVERIFIED — to be populated by licensing counsel; default is the longest applicable class]**. Colorado AI Act impact-assessment records (3 years after final deployment) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** are in 19.4.

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) Citation: A2-4.1-02/A2-5-01, not A2-1-01. (2) "Life-of-loan + minimum periods" is right but the Fannie Mae floor is *permanent while serviced, then ≥4 years after liquidation, longer if local law requires*; the Guide is silent on transferor retention after a transfer, so Reg X's one year is the only hard floor there (policy: four years). (3) The row omits the five-day servicing-file capability, the 18-month accounting-report rule, the Reg B/Reg Z/Reg F/IRS/NY category periods, the FTC two-year disposal trigger, legal holds and Fannie Mae's "reproduce at own expense" delivery duty. ### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- Every write to `documents`, `loan_events`, `ledger_entries`, `notices`, `contacts`, `agent_decisions`, `consents`, `cases` carries a `record_type` (see data model) and is enrolled in the lifecycle engine synchronously (same transaction).
- Anchor events: `loan.liquidated` (with `liquidation_kind` ∈ {paid_in_full, foreclosure_sale, short_sale, dil, charge_off, repurchase, make_whole}), `loan.discharged` (= paid in full / satisfied; **not** a bankruptcy discharge), `servicing.transferred_out`, `case.closed`, `collection.activity.last` (computed for Reg F loans), `call.recorded`, `consent.revoked`, `notice.sent`, `lossmit.decision.notified` (Reg B anchor), `tax.form.filed` (IRS anchor), `investor_report.filed` (18-month accounting-report anchor), `contact.last_use` (FTC two-year trigger for non-loan customer information).
- `legal_hold.placed` / `legal_hold.released` (from complaints 4.5, litigation 13.x/14.x, exam notices 18.2, Fannie Mae requests, subpoenas).
- `records.request.received` (Fannie Mae, regulator, partner, transferee, court, MI company, auditor; borrower requests route to 4.2 as RFIs).
- Schedules: `retention-sweep` daily 01:00 America/New_York (computes eligibility); `disposal-run` monthly (first Sunday 02:00 ET); `records-inventory-review` annual; `legal-hold-review` every 180 days per hold; `servicing-file-drill` monthly (control test CTL-REC-01).

#### State machine
Record object: `active` → (anchor event fires) `retention_running` → (sweep finds `today ≥ eligible_for_disposal_at` and `hold_count = 0`) `eligible` → (attested disposal run) `disposed`. Overlay: any state → `held` when `hold_count > 0`; `held` → previous state on release with re-evaluation. Guards: a Fannie Mae-property object cannot reach `eligible` while the loan is `active` (permanent while serviced); `eligible` requires every class in `record_types.retention_class_codes` to be satisfied (max rule) and no open `records_requests` touching the loan; `disposed` requires `disposal_runs.attested_by` (officer) and, for physical media, a vendor certificate. Transitions are made only by the lifecycle engine (`security-records` agent tools) — never by direct SQL; `disposed` is terminal.
Records request: `received` → `scoped` (agent classifies requester, authority, scope, due date; auto-places a `legal_hold` of reason `fannie_mae_request`/`regulator_exam`/`subpoena`) → `compiling` → `qa_redaction` (GLBA/§1024.36(d)(3) redaction where the requester is not entitled to third-party data) → `awaiting_certification` (only when a signed certification is requested) → `delivered` → `closed` (hold released 90 days after delivery unless the matter remains open). Branch `refused` for requests lacking authority (e.g., a third party with no authorization) with an `attorney` review.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Effective retention** for an object = max over all `retention_class_codes` of its record type of (anchor date + offset), then max with any jurisdiction override, then indefinitely extended while `hold_count > 0`. Loan-linked objects also require the loan to be liquidated or transferred out (permanent while active). Anniversary arithmetic: same month/day N years later; Feb 29 anchors roll to Mar 1.
2. **Anchor semantics.** `loan.liquidated` = the Fannie Mae liquidation (payoff, foreclosure/REO conveyance, short sale, Mortgage Release, charge-off, repurchase/make-whole). `loan.discharged` (Reg X) = paid in full/satisfied; a bankruptcy discharge of personal liability does **not** discharge the loan. `servicing.transferred_out` starts both the Reg X 1-year floor and the policy 4-year class. `loan.final_entry` (NY) = the last ledger or servicing entry (typically the escrow refund or final remittance).
3. **Worked example A (payoff).** Loan paid in full Mon 2027-03-15; escrow refund disbursed 2027-03-24 (final entry); property in NY; `fdcpa_debt_collector_flag = true` with last collection activity 2027-03-10. Reg X floor: 2028-03-15. Reg F: 2030-03-10. NY 419.9: 2030-03-24. Fannie Mae: 2031-03-15. Effective `eligible_for_disposal_at` = **2031-03-15**; the first disposal run after that is Sunday 2031-04-06. A Reg B decision notified 2029-01-20 (post-payoff modification request would not exist, but a pre-payoff denial notified 2027-01-20 → 2029-02-20) does not extend the date. If a legal hold is placed 2030-11-01 for litigation and released 2032-02-01, the sweep re-evaluates on release and the object is disposed in the 2032-03-01 run.
4. **Worked example B (transfer-out).** Servicing transferred 2028-10-01 (loan active); final reconciliation entry 2028-10-31 (NY loan). Reg X: 2029-10-01. NY: 2031-10-31. Policy `life_of_loan_plus_4y` (transfer-out anchor): 2032-10-01. Effective = **2032-10-01**. The transferee receives the complete file at T-0 (17.3); Supermortgage keeps a read-only copy under this schedule.
5. **Servicing file compilation (five-day capability).** `compileServicingFile(loanId)` produces a signed bundle: (i) transaction schedule = `ledger_entries` joined to `payments`, `payment_allocations`, `suspense_items`, `escrow_lines` and `disbursements`, ordered by posting timestamp, with running balances (principal, interest, escrow, suspense, fees), rendered as CSV and PDF; (ii) latest `documents` row of type `security_instrument` (plus recorded modifications); (iii) all `contacts` narratives, case notes and `agent_decisions.rationale` for borrower communications (AI-agent notes are "notes created by servicer personnel" for this purpose — the servicer's system created them); (iv) data-field report = every column of `loans`, `loan_terms` (current version), `loan_borrowers`/`borrowers` (redacted SSN), `properties`, `escrow_accounts`, `escrow_lines`, delinquency counters, listed "by name, populated"; (v) all `documents` where `source = borrower` and `case_type` ∈ {noe, rfi, lossmit, appeal, forbearance, deferral, modification, shortsale, dil}. Target: ≤5 minutes wall-clock; hard limit 5 days.
6. **Redaction** in productions: third-party borrower/successor data and NPI not pertaining to the requester are masked per §1024.36(d)(3)-style rules and Reg P (Section 4.2 redaction module); Fannie Mae, regulators, courts and the partner receive unredacted files (Reg P §1016.15 exceptions).
7. **Disposal.** Electronic objects: crypto-shred (destroy the per-object data key) plus object delete after WORM retention expiry; DB rows: hard delete of content columns, tombstone row retained with hash (`disposed` status) — the `loan_events` append-only log is never deleted while any loan-level class is running; after the loan's final class expires, the loan's event partition is archived and crypto-shredded as one object. Paper: vendor certificate required. Every run requires an `officer` attestation before execution; manifests are retained `corporate_7y`.
8. **FTC two-year rule** applies only to customer information not tied to a serviced loan (e.g., prospect contact data, abandoned third-party payment enrollments); loan-linked information is "otherwise required to be retained" (Fannie Mae/Reg X), which is the documented exception.
9. **Records are Fannie Mae's property.** No retention class for a Fannie Mae-linked record type may be shortened below the Guide floor; Fannie Mae may direct return/destruction on termination of the Lender Contract/SSA (19.3) — that instruction is modelled as a `records.request` of format `custom` with `requester_type = fannie_mae` and executed under officer certification.

#### Integrations
- **WORM object store** (S3 Object Lock compliance mode or equivalent; US regions only): every `documents`, recording and audit-log copy; integrity job verifies hashes daily; failure → sev-1 (19.2 CTL-SEC-22).
- **Print/mail** (paper productions when a court or regulator requires paper) — vendor mail-piece ids retained as delivery evidence.
- **Secure file transfer to Fannie Mae**: MORA/records requests typically use Fannie Mae-provided Box workspaces or the File Transfer Portal (research/00b "Other Fannie Mae touchpoints") — **portal-only** → `human_portal_task` for `fnma_portal_operator` with package: production manifest (loan list, record types, hashes), encrypted archive, cover letter, certification if requested, and the timer due date. Email delivery only where the request specifies it.
- **Regulator/court productions**: SFTP or the regulator's portal (state exam portals are not Fannie Mae systems; still human-operated by `officer`); e-discovery load-file format (PDF + metadata CSV) for subpoenas prepared by the agent, reviewed by `attorney`.
- **Transferee export** (17.3): MISMO v3.6-aligned projections plus document bundle — 19.1 supplies the bundle and manifest.
- **Disposal vendors**: certificate PDFs ingested as `documents` and linked to `disposal_runs`.
- Failure handling: productions are idempotent by `records_requests.id`; partial deliveries are re-sent from the manifest; if the WORM store is unavailable, disposal runs halt (never delete without verified WORM copy existence check passing first).

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `servicing_file_snapshots` bundles (PDF + JSON + CSV, hashed); production manifests; certification of records (`NTC_RECORDS_CERTIFICATION` — plain business-records certification signed by a `signing_officer`; not a Notice Registry borrower notice but versioned in the same template store); internal `NTC_LEGAL_HOLD_INTERNAL` (hold notice to custodians of paper records/vendors); `NTC_DISPOSAL_CERTIFICATE` (officer-attested run certificate); annual retention-schedule review memo; `records_inventory` report; NY 419.9 call-log/correspondence extracts.
- Ledger postings: none. Investor events: none. Records written: `record_objects`, `legal_holds`, `disposal_runs`, `records_requests`, `servicing_file_snapshots`, `agent_decisions`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `security-records`. End-to-end: classifies every record at write time (record type → classes), computes anchors from `loan_events`, runs sweeps and disposal planning, triages inbound records requests (mail-scan, email, portal), verifies requester authority (Fannie Mae/partner contacts registry; regulator letterhead + exam notice; subpoena validity check → `attorney`), scopes the request, compiles and redacts, drafts the cover letter and certification, schedules delivery, places holds, and produces the annual schedule review with a rule-version diff (`compliance-sentinel` Guide-watch feed).
- **Tools:** `records.classify`, `records.compileServicingFile`, `records.export(scope, format)`, `records.redact`, `holds.place` (allowed), `holds.release` (**not** allowed — human only), `disposal.plan` (allowed), `disposal.execute` (only after `officer` attestation event), `portal_task.create`, `documents.read`, `timers.read`.
- **Decision record** (`agent_decisions`): `{request_id, requester_type, authority_basis, scope_resolved, record_types[], redaction_rules_applied[], objects_count, hashes_manifest_id, due_at, delivery_channel, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, confidence, escalation?}`; for disposal: `{run_id, class_code, object_count, gates_checked[], holds_checked, exceptions[]}`.
- **Escalations:** subpoena/court order or any request implicating litigation → `attorney` (validity, objections, privilege); Fannie Mae/regulator productions and every disposal run → `officer` attestation/sign-off; sworn certifications → `signing_officer`; Fannie Mae portal uploads → `fnma_portal_operator`; borrower requests → re-routed to the `case` agent as RFIs (4.2). Package attached: manifest, draft cover letter, timer status, redaction log, authority evidence.
- **Guardrails:** the agent can never delete; disposal executes only through the attested run; holds are never auto-released; any request scope > 500 loans or any request from a new requester identity requires `officer` confirmation before delivery; confidence < 0.85 on requester authority → escalate.
- **Human path (AI off):** the ops-console "Records" workspace exposes the same tools to a human `security-records` operator; timers unchanged.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Transfer-out while a Fannie Mae request is open:** the request stays with Supermortgage for records through T-0; post-T-0 records come from the transferee; the hold survives the transfer.
- **Loan reported liquidated in error:** SVC-2026-03 bars reactivation in Fannie Mae reporting after the period closes (Section 5.3), but the internal anchor is reversed by a correcting `loan.liquidation.reversed` event; retention timers are cancelled with reason and re-created on the true liquidation.
- **Bankruptcy discharge vs. loan discharge:** never treated as `loan.discharged`; a bankruptcy case places an automatic `legal_hold` (reason `litigation`) for the life of the case plus 1 year **[policy]**.
- **Successor in interest:** confirmed successor documents (4.4) are loan records (life-of-loan+4); potential-successor documents of persons never confirmed follow `ftc_314_disposal_2y_post_last_use`.
- **Missing security instrument copy at boarding:** the (ii) element is mandatory — `boarding` opens a `documents.gap` exception; the agent orders a recorded copy from the county/e-recording vendor; drill failure flagged until cured.
- **AI-agent notes:** stored as `contacts`/`agent_decisions` — included in element (iii); voice transcripts are stored with the recording and included.
- **Vendor-hosted records** (print/mail proofs, telephony recordings at the carrier): the `records_inventory` marks `vendor_hosted`; contracts require return on request within 5 business days and at termination (19.3 clause checklist); the agent mirrors proofs into `documents` nightly.
- **WORM outage:** writes queue in the transactional store with `worm_pending`; disposal and productions requiring WORM verification pause; sev-1 after 4 hours.
- **Partial/ambiguous requests:** the agent asks the requester for clarification within 1 business day and starts the timer from receipt regardless.
- **Retro-corrections:** superseded documents/notices keep the loan's retention (they are evidence); corrections are new objects linked by `supersedes_id`.
- **Local jurisdiction requires longer retention:** unknown states default to the longest known class; the `jurisdiction_rules.record_retention` entry, once populated, only ever lengthens.
- **Fannie Mae directs destruction or return** (Lender Contract/SSA termination): executed as a certified production/disposal; partner and Supermortgage officer both certify.

#### Audit and evidence
Per object: classification, anchors, gate timers (`timers` rows with definition versions), holds, disposal run id and manifest hash. Per request: authority evidence, scope, compile time, redaction log, delivery proof (portal task completion record with submission id/screenshot, SFTP log, mail-piece id), certification copy, timer history. Monthly: drill results (CTL-REC-01), disposal certificates, WORM integrity reports. Annually: schedule review memo with rule-version diff. All are exportable for MORA (Fannie Mae's "in-depth audits of … internal records and operating procedures"), state exams (NY 419.9 logs) and RESPA §6(f) defense (proof that §1024.38(c) records existed and were retrievable).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Call recordings:** keep `life_of_loan_plus_4y` (default, treats recordings as loan records) or the Reg F/NY 3-year floor? Default: life-of-loan+4 with Opus compression; revisit at 100k loans.
2. **Transferor retention after transfer-out:** policy 4 years (default) vs. Reg X 1-year floor. Default: 4 years.
3. **"Within five days" in §1024.38(c)(2):** calendar days assumed (default); the drill target of 5 minutes makes the distinction moot.
4. **Default Fannie Mae/regulator production SLA when no time frame is stated:** 10 business days (default) — confirm with the partner's account team.
5. **Legal-hold release approvals:** `officer` + `attorney` (default) vs. attorney alone.
6. **Purge of `loan_events` partitions:** crypto-shred whole partitions after the final class (default) vs. keep event skeletons forever (statistics). Default: crypto-shred.
