### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**50 U.S.C. §3937 (verified today, current through Sept. 8, 2026).** (a)(1): an obligation "that bears interest at a rate in excess of 6 percent per year before the servicemember enters military service shall not bear interest at a rate in excess of 6 percent — (A) during the period of military service and one year thereafter, in the case of an obligation or liability consisting of a mortgage, trust deed, or other security in the nature of a mortgage; or (B) during the period of military service, in the case of any other obligation." (a)(2) "Interest at a rate in excess of 6 percent per year that would otherwise be incurred but for the prohibition in paragraph (1) is forgiven." (a)(3) "The amount of any periodic payment due from a servicemember under the terms of the instrument that created an obligation or liability covered by this section shall be reduced by the amount of the interest forgiven under paragraph (2) that is allocable to the period for which such payment is made." (b)(1) the servicemember "shall provide to the creditor written notice and a copy of the military orders ... not later than 180 days after the date of the servicemember's termination or release from military service"; (b)(2) "the limitation ... shall be effective as of the date on which the servicemember is called to military service." (c) A court may grant the creditor relief if "the ability of the servicemember to pay interest ... at a rate in excess of 6 percent per year is not materially affected by reason of the servicemember's military service." (d) "interest" "includes service charges, renewal charges, fees, or any other charges (except bona fide insurance) with respect to an obligation or liability." §3911(2)–(3) define military service and its period (13.8). §3919: an application for or receipt of a stay, postponement or suspension "shall not itself" support an adverse credit report, a determination of inability to pay, denial/revocation of credit, or a National Guard/reserve annotation (8.x). **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED: whether §3937(b)(1) currently also accepts "any other appropriate indicator of military service" in lieu of orders was not confirmed in today's extract — policy below accepts a DMDC certificate.]**

**Fannie Mae D2-3.4-01 (06/13/2018 — verified today).** "The servicer must grant an eligible servicemember a reduction in the interest rate to 6% in accordance with the provisions of the SCRA"; "make the new interest rate effective with the first payment due after the servicemember (i) enters active duty or (ii) is entitled to the new interest rate under the SCRA"; "must not exercise the option provided by the SCRA to apply to a court for relief"; "must reapply any prepaid monthly payments with due dates during the period of active duty at the 6% interest rate"; late evidence — "must reapply any monthly payments made during the period of active duty at the 6% interest rate"; "Installments that are delinquent when the servicemember enters active duty status must be paid at the interest rate that was in effect when the payments came due"; remaining funds — "application as a monthly payment, if sufficient; application as a principal curtailment; or a refund"; written correspondence stating "the new payment amount, the date it becomes effective, and the date it will be discontinued," no document execution required; "must waive any late charges that became due after the servicemember was called to active duty"; identify servicemembers via DMDC and inform them proactively; Form 180/Form 710 documentation; quarterly contact (13.8).

**F-1-19, Processing a Military Indulgence (12/20/2023 — verified today).** Payment during active duty by either the **Standard Amortization Method** ("a recalculated amortization schedule with interest at the rate of 6% and the actual remaining term") or the **Interest Subsidy Amortization Method** ("the sum of the next monthly principal installment called for by the applicable amortization schedule plus monthly interest at the rate of 6%," adjusted "at least annually"); ARMs "must be treated as a fixed-rate mortgage loan bearing interest at 6%, unless the applicable adjustable rate would be lower." Delinquent loans: portfolio/PFP — "Capitalize the delinquent interest by increasing the UPB and advancing the LPI date"; MBS — "Hold the payments collected at the recalculated interest rate of 6% as unapplied funds. Apply the funds when they are sufficient to pay the oldest delinquent installment (P&I) in full, with interest at the rate that was in effect when the installment came due." After active duty: fixed-rate — "The payment they had before the interest rate reduction"; ARM (standard) — re-amortize the scheduled UPB "at the latest applicable interest rate"; ARM (subsidy) — the full payment last reported as of the preceding change date. Servicing fee: "calculate its servicing fee on the UPB of the mortgage loan at the beginning of each month and not use a percentage-of-interest factor." Reporting: portfolio/PFP — complete the **SCRA Reporting and Disbursement Request Form (Form 1022)** when reducing the rate, changing the payment (subsidy method), applying other indulgence, or restoring the rate, and "Send an email with the completed Form 1022 to the Exceptions Transaction Management Unit ... no later than the ninth business day of the month" (F-4-02: militaryindulgence@fanniemae.com); MBS — "Submit the notification via a file upload on the Fannie Mae investor reporting system ... no later than the 15th calendar day of the month"; ARMs — Transaction Code 83 at each scheduled adjustment. Funding: portfolio — "Fannie Mae will adjust its investor reporting system records ... The servicer will not have to advance any interest and no request for reimbursement is necessary"; PFP — Form 1022 by BD9 "to request an adjustment ... to the servicer's shortage surplus account"; MBS — records not adjusted; standard-method MBS loans "Remit a reconciling principal curtailment" for the difference between 6%-schedule principal and pool-scheduled principal; "Fannie Mae will continue to make disbursements for the amount of the interest rate reduction through the military indulgence end date plus one year. The disbursements will be funded two days prior to the end of the month in the custodial account that the servicer has assigned for military indulgence funds." Delinquency reporting: additional indulgence reported in the next delinquency status report (code 32/reason 014 — F-1-21). (2) The row omits the one-year post-service tail for mortgages, the fee-inclusion definition of "interest," the 180-day notice window, and the reapplication/refund options. (4) Retroactivity: statute — call-up date; Fannie Mae — first payment due after entry (the platform applies the more borrower-favorable full-payment rule).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Eligibility**: pre-service obligation (origination < service begin) with a note rate > 6% (or ARM rate > 6% at any time in the period); evidence = written notice (any written channel incl. portal/email; a call is logged and a written confirmation requested) + orders **or** a DMDC certificate/Form 180 (policy); request within 180 days after release is the statutory right; later requests honoured on verified service (policy — Fannie Mae's "in accordance with the SCRA" allows generosity, not stinginess).
2. **Effective date**: statute — call-up date; Fannie Mae — "first payment due after" entry; platform: the capped rate applies to the entire interest period covered by the first payment due after entry (more favourable) — `cap_effective_payment_due`.
3. **Capped rate** = min(6.00%, contractual rate); ARM frozen at 6% "unless the applicable adjustable rate would be lower"; Transaction 83/servicing event at each scheduled adjustment reports the applicable rate.
4. **Method** (decision 13.9-1, default **Interest Subsidy**): payment = scheduled principal (original schedule) + UPB × 6%/12; re-adjusted at least annually; Standard alternative: re-amortize UPB over the remaining term at 6%.
5. **Retroactive recalculation** (`scra_recalculations`): for each installment due on/after `cap_effective_payment_due` and already paid at the note rate: `forgiven = interest_note − interest_capped`; principal unchanged (subsidy) — the excess is a borrower overpayment; delinquent installments due before entry stay at the old rate. Fees/charges assessed in the period count as "interest" (§3937(d)) — late charges waived; other fees suppressed.
6. **Worked example** (subsidy method; `decimal.js`, round half-up to cents per installment). 30-year fixed 7.25%, $300,000, first payment Apr. 1, 2024, P&I **$2,046.53**; after the Mar. 1, 2026 payment (#24) UPB **$293,975.18**. Active duty begins **Mar. 15, 2026** → first payment due after entry = Apr. 1, 2026 (#25). Written notice with orders arrives **Sept. 10, 2026**; payments #25–#29 (Apr.–Aug.) were paid at 7.25%. Recalculation:

| 25 | 2026-04-01 | 293,975.18 | 1,776.10 | 1,469.88 | 306.22 | 270.43 |
| 26 | 2026-05-01 | 293,704.75 | 1,774.47 | 1,468.52 | 305.95 | 272.06 |
| 27 | 2026-06-01 | 293,432.69 | 1,772.82 | 1,467.16 | 305.66 | 273.71 |

| 29 | 2026-08-01 | 292,883.62 | 1,769.51 | 1,464.42 | 305.09 | 277.02 |

Total forgiven interest (5 installments) = **$1,528.30** = borrower overpayment. UPB after #29 = **$292,606.60**. New payment from #30 (Sept. 1, 2026): scheduled principal $278.70 + 6% interest $1,463.03 = **$1,741.73** (Standard method alternative: re-amortize $292,606.60 over 331 months at 6% = **$1,810.42**). Monthly interest reduction funded by Fannie Mae for #30 = 1,767.83 − 1,463.03 = **$304.80** (on an MBS loan with PTR 6.75%: pool interest due $1,645.91; servicer collects $1,463.03 from the borrower + $304.80 from Fannie Mae's disbursement = $1,767.83, covering PTR $1,645.91 + servicing fee $60.96 (0.25% on UPB per F-1-19) + guaranty fee $60.96). Overpayment election: $1,528.30 < $1,741.73 ⇒ "apply as a monthly payment" is not "sufficient" alone — offer curtailment or refund (or apply to #30 with the borrower adding $213.43; policy offers this too). Tail: if service ends Feb. 28, 2027, the 6% cap continues to Feb. 28, 2028; restoration with the first payment due after (Mar. 1, 2028) at $2,046.53 (fixed-rate: "the payment they had before").
7. **Ledger postings** (per installment reallocation, subsidy method): Dr `interest_income`/`interest_due` (loan) 306.22 → Cr `scra_overpayment_payable` 306.22, with a memo entry Dr `scra_interest_forgiven` (contra-income) — i.e., interest recognized is reduced and the cash already received becomes a borrower liability until elected: refund (Dr `scra_overpayment_payable` / Cr `cash`), curtailment (Dr `scra_overpayment_payable` / Cr `principal`; investor curtailment event 5.1), or apply-as-payment (Dr `scra_overpayment_payable` / Cr `suspense/unapplied` then normal application). Investor side (MBS): monthly Dr `fnma_military_indulgence_receivable` 304.80 / Cr `interest_income` at activation of the month's accrual; on receipt in the designated custodial account Dr `custodial_mi_cash` / Cr `fnma_military_indulgence_receivable`; P&I remittance to the pool is unchanged (S/S). Portfolio: no receivable — Fannie Mae adjusts its records on Form 1022 (remittance reflects 6%). Standard-method MBS: `reconciling_curtailment_payable` for the principal difference remitted monthly. Servicing fee: computed on UPB ×0.25%/12 (not a percentage of interest).
8. **Delinquent at entry** (portfolio): capitalize delinquent interest and advance the LPI (loan_terms version + 5.x reporting); (MBS): hold 6% payments unapplied until the oldest installment (at its original rate) is covered — `suspense` handling per 2.2 with the SCRA rule flag.
9. **Restoration**: first payment due after `cap_ends_on`; letter states the end date (sent at activation) and again 60 days before restoration (policy); ARMs per F-1-19. `cap_ends_on` is computed the same way as the §3953(c) foreclosure tail — `addYears(service_end_on, 1)`, inclusive, with the Feb 29 → Feb 28 clamp (13.8 rule 3) — because §3937(a)(1)(A) caps the rate "during the period of military service **and one year thereafter**" for a mortgage obligation. A `+365 calendar_days` offset restores the note rate one day early across any tail containing a Feb 29, which over-collects interest in a §3937 window and forfeits fees the section otherwise suppresses (`SCRA_3937_FEES_IN_CAP_GATE` runs "through `cap_ends_on`", so it inherits the same fix). The two tails share one date helper and one leap-crossing fixture.
10. **Rounding**: interest per installment = round_half_up(UPB × rate / 12); forgiven per installment = difference of rounded interests; totals are sums of rounded installment figures.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Orders show multiple deployments: cap each period; tail computed per period end; overlapping periods merged.
- Request after the 180-day window: honour on verified service (policy; low risk, Fannie Mae-funded).
- Loan modified during service (12.x): the cap applies to the modified rate if >6%; Form 1022 payment-change notification.
- Payoff during the cap: payoff at 6% accrual (16.x); forgiven interest never re-billed.
- Transfer-in with an active cap: board the loan_terms version and the Fannie Mae funding arrangement (custodial account mapping); transfer-out: include the recalculation history and Form 1022 trail (17.x).
- ARM adjusts down below 6%: use the lower rate; adjusts above: stay at 6%; report each adjustment via 83/event.
- Co-borrower non-servicemember: cap applies to the obligation (joint liability) **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — §3937 applies to the servicemember's obligation; joint obligations are covered in practice]**.
- Delinquent at entry (MBS): unapplied-funds rule; borrower informed how funds will be applied (2.2 statement disclosures).
- Fannie Mae disbursement arrives short/late: 6.x reconciliation exception; no borrower impact.
- Late charges already collected after the call-up: refund/credit (waiver) with the reallocation.
