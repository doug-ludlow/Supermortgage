### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**Selling Guide B3-2-01, General Information on DU (last updated 04/01/2026; Guide edition Sept 2, 2026).** DU is one of "two options for conducting a comprehensive risk assessment–automated underwriting through DU or manual underwriting." DU "analyzes the information in the loan casefile to reach an overall credit risk assessment," and "DU does not evaluate a loan's compliance with federal and state laws and regulations… Lenders bear sole responsibility for complying with applicable laws and regulations." The findings report "summarizes the overall underwriting recommendation and lists the steps necessary for the lender to complete the processing of the loan file." Under *General Requirements for Lenders* the lender must: "employ prudent underwriting judgment in assessing whether a loan casefile should be approved"; "confirm the accuracy of the data it submits, making sure that it did not fail to submit any data that might have affected the DU recommendation had it been known"; "ensure that the loan complies with all of the verification messages and approval conditions"; "apply due diligence when reviewing the documentation in the loan file"; "review the credit report to confirm that the data that DU evaluated with respect to the borrower's credit history was accurate and complete"; "determine if there is any potentially derogatory or contradictory information that is not part of the data analyzed by DU"; and "take action when erroneous data in the credit report or contradictory or derogatory information in the loan file would justify additional investigation." *Loan Casefile Archival Policy*: "DU loan casefiles are archived and no longer retained in DU from the earlier of 270 days from the date on which the loan casefile was last updated, or 540 days from the date on which the loan casefile was created." Single-closing construction-to-permanent casefiles "will be archived using 660 days from the date on which the loan casefile was created" (SEL-2026-03; out of scope for this build but the rule must not be hard-coded away). When an archived casefile must be re-underwritten "a new loan casefile must be created and submitted to DU. The loan casefile will be subject to the policies in effect for the current version of DU." Credit: "The credit report must meet the allowable age of documents as of the note date. If the credit report expired prior to the note date and the loan casefile is being resubmitted to DU, a new credit report must be requested."

**B3-2-10, Accuracy of DU Data, DU Tolerances, and Errors in the Credit Report (12/04/2019).** Closed-loan rule: "The data submitted to DU must reflect the loan as it was closed, including occupancy type, product type, amortization, loan term, property type, loan purpose, sales price, and appraised value." Tolerances (each read as a targeted quote on the topic page today): interest-rate decreases — "No resubmission required" unless the decrease is from a permanent buydown, in which case the "Loan casefile must be resubmitted to DU"; interest-rate increases and changes to income, liabilities or assets — "Loan casefile must be resubmitted to DU" when "the result of these changes causes the DTI ratio recalculated by the lender to now exceed 45%, or increase by 3 percentage points or more" ("if the recalculated DTI ratio is 50% or less" — above 50% the loan is ineligible under B3-6-02 and must in any case be resubmitted); income on income-limited products ("Verified income used to qualify the borrower for loans subject to income limits; for example, as with HomeReady loans") — "Loan casefile must be resubmitted to DU" when "Income is greater than the loan application indicates" (a *higher* verified income can breach the AMI limit; a lower one is caught by the DTI test) [corrected 2026-09-11 against the topic page — the earlier "less than" reading was reversed]; refinance loan amount — "The loan amount may increase $500 or up to 1% of the loan amount, whichever is less" and "may decrease 5% of the loan amount", "provided the new LTV/CLTV does not result in changes to mortgage insurance coverage, loan-level price adjustments, or loan eligibility"; the loan-amount tolerance "does not apply to Fannie Mae's requirements regarding the amount of cash back to the borrower on a limited cash-out refinance transaction"; reserves — resubmission is required when verified reserves are less than the "Reserves Required to be Verified" per the findings unless documented reserves equal "at least 90% of the Reserves Required to be Verified." The topic states no loan-amount tolerance for purchase transactions (a purchase loan-amount change is therefore a resubmission trigger). Credit-report errors: "the lender must take action when information not considered by DU would result in a recommendation other than that returned by DU." Non-applicant debts: "DU will include these tradelines in the credit risk assessment, and will also include the accounts in the DTI ratio when provided on the loan application."

**B3-2-09, Erroneous Credit Report Data (01/27/2015).** The lender must "work with the credit repository to correct the data and resubmit the loan casefile to DU for underwriting"; "the lender must obtain written documentation that supports the error," which may be "a supplement to the credit report; a new mortgage credit report; documentation from the credit provider that reported the error." "If there is not enough time to obtain corrected information… the lender may manually underwrite the mortgage" (manual underwriting is outside this build — open decision 23.2-Q1).

**B3-2-11, DU Underwriting Findings Report (09/07/2022).** DU "provides a number of 'potential red flag' messages designed to help the lender detect inconsistencies in the loan casefile," including messages for excessive resubmissions, a frozen credit report and potential casefile ID reuse; "Neither the presence nor absence of these messages alters the lender's responsibility to ensure accurate information." **B3-2-04, DU Documentation Requirements (03/31/2015).** "The final DU Underwriting Findings report" must be "maintained in the permanent loan file"; DU "indicates the minimum verification documentation requirements"; "a more comprehensive level of documentation is always acceptable." **B3-2-02, DU Validation Service (02/05/2025).** "When a lender provides the required reports, all loans submitted to DU will automatically be assessed through the DU validation service"; "lenders must 'opt-in' to validate income and employment using an asset verification report"; "If the lender obtains an updated verification report, the lender must resubmit the loan to DU and receive a message that the component has been validated in order for the representation and warranty enforcement relief to apply" (close-by-date gate `FNMA_B3_2_02_DU_CLOSE_BY_GATE` is 22.3's).

**A2-2-04, Limited Waiver and Enforcement Relief (04/01/2026).** Limited-waiver conditions that this process must make true at delivery: "All data pertaining to the mortgage loan is complete, accurate, and not fraudulent"; the "DU loan casefile ID… reported at the time of delivery"; "SFC 127 is required for all loans underwritten through DU"; "All Verification Messages/Approval Conditions… must be satisfactorily resolved."

**DU Specification and access (00b-orig F1/F12; DU Implementation Guide v2.6.1, Aug 8, 2025).** "The DU Spec is based on the MISMO v3.4 Reference Model Build 324." Resubmissions carry the `AutomatedUnderwritingCaseIdentifier`; joint credit uses the arcrole "SharesJointCreditReportWith"; validation reports are referenced by `DU:VerificationReportSupplierType`, `DU:VerificationReportIdentifier`, `DU:VerificationReportType`. There is **no DU product in the Developer Portal**; DU is reached over the legacy Direct Integration XML platform with a "DU Integration environment"; transport/auth for that rail "is not on public pages" — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**. Lender authorization of a TSP is performed in Technology Manager by the lender's Corporate Administrator: *Manage TSP Product → Available Product → Set Up Product → +Add User/System IDs → +Add Applications → roles/data folders → Complete Set Up*, with a companion "Revoke API System ID Access to TSP Product" job aid; IP Address Restrictions are optional and, if enabled, "you will also need to provide a list of allowable IP address ranges that will apply to the TSP's system(s)." The DU job aid *Associating a Credit Report* (© 2026) defines the credit mechanics used below: a lender may "request a new credit report directly through DU" or "request a reissue of the credit report to bring it into DU" by selecting the credit agency, entering "your account number and password for that credit agency" and "the reference number that appears on the existing credit report"; "A credit report must be available in DU for every borrower and co-borrower on a loan casefile"; the report type is "Joint or Individual"; the submission choice is "Credit and Underwriting" or "Credit Only"; "Once a credit report is retrieved within DU it is always available"; "any changes you make to the borrower last name, suffix, or social security number (SSN) will require you to associate a new credit report." The casefile create/submit choreography, credit-provider codes and the Return File Type value list live in the login-gated DU Spec/FAQ — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**.

**Credit-report age.** B1-1-03 (04/02/2025): "Credit documents must be no more than four months old on the note date" (00a-fnma §2.3) — timer `FNMA_B1_1_03_CREDIT_DOCS_4M` is 22.1's; credit-refresh timers are 22.2's. **Minimum credit score.** "A minimum credit score is not required for DU loan casefiles" (B3-5.1-01, 04/22/2026; SEL-2025-09).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.** (1) B3-2-04 is *DU Documentation Requirements*, not recommendations; the recommendation topics are B3-2-05 (Approve/Eligible), -06 (Approve/Ineligible), -07 (Refer with Caution), -08 (Out of Scope), and -09 is *Erroneous Credit Report Data* — every citation in 23.1/23.2's blueprint rows is shifted by one. (2) "Casefile/credit expiration (credit ≤ 4 months)" conflates two clocks: the casefile archival clock (270/540 days, B3-2-01) and the credit-document age at the note date (B1-1-03); a casefile can be live with an expired credit report, in which case resubmission requires a new report. (3) "DU API (TSP access)" does not exist as a Developer Portal product; DU is a Direct-Integration XML service (00b-orig F1/F12). (4) The tolerance rules are not "at closing" checks — they are per-change tests, and a separate unconditional rule requires eight fields to match the closed loan regardless of tolerance. (5) There is no minimum credit score for DU casefiles (SEL-2025-09) — any "620 floor" logic is manual-underwriting only. (6) The blueprint omits DU version policy generations: the June 27, 2026 credit-risk tightening and the Sept 26, 2026 September-update changes are keyed to casefile creation date, so a casefile's behavior depends on when it was created, not when it was last submitted. (7) DU validation-service reports never transit SM to DU (00b-orig Part (c)(5)); the blueprint's "DU (API/UI)" system line should read "DU DI + supplier-of-record report references." (8) The findings format matters for the build: Return File Types 16/17 retire Nov 30, 2026 and RES/TEXT/XML retire Oct 27, 2028 — JSON v2 is the only durable machine format.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- `application.trid_received` and `credit.report.received{report_id}` for every borrower → eligible for first submission (the `underwriter` agent waits for the 22.2 credit association, never for intent to proceed — DU submission is not a fee).
- `application.data.changed` (any ULAD field diff), `verification.received{kind}`, `credit.refresh.received` (22.2's name for a refreshed report), `valuation.received`, `lock.executed`, `lock.extended`, `changed_circumstance.recorded` (21.5), `mi.certificate.issued`, `project.review.completed` → run `evaluateResubmission`.
- `closing.document_set.opened` (26.1 — the event that starts closing-document generation) and `delivery.uldd.built` (29.3) → run `assertFinalSubmissionMatches` (gate `FNMA_B3_2_10_DU_FINAL_MATCH_GATE`).
- Schedules: nightly archival watch (270/540-day clocks); DU release calendar (`fnma.du` rule-set cutovers; Return File Type retirements); credit-expiry watch fed by 22.2.
- Inbound: DU findings (JSON v2 + PDF) over the DI channel; DU error responses; DU validation-service messages embedded in findings.

#### State machine

`du_casefiles.status`: `draft` (row created, no DU call yet) → `credit_associated` (every borrower has a report reference: order-new or reissue) → `submitted` (request sent, awaiting findings) → `findings_received` → [`resubmission_required` → `submitted`]* → `final` (a `du_submissions` row with `is_final=true` whose `closed_loan_snapshot_hash` equals the closing-data hash) → `delivered` (29.4 confirms the casefile ID was reported at delivery). Side states: `error` (DU error response; returns to `credit_associated` or `findings_received` on retry), `archive_warning` (T−30 days before `archive_due_at`; non-blocking), `archived` (terminal for this row; `superseded_by_casefile_id` set when re-created), `superseded` (identity change or casefile error; terminal). Transitions are performed by the `underwriter` agent; `fnma_portal_operator` may perform `submitted` via the DU UI fallback with the same request artifact. Guards: `submitted` requires `credit_associated` for all borrowers and a non-expired credit report at the projected note date (or a `credit_refresh` reason); `final` requires the 23.2 recommendation to be `approve_eligible`, all eight closed-loan fields matched, and the note rate/loan amount/term/product equal to the CD-final values; `delivered` requires `is_final` and SFC 127 (and SFC 067 when `score_model = vantagescore_4_0`) present in the ULDD (29.3).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Casefile creation and policy generation.** `policy_generation = '2026_09_26'` if `created_at ≥ 2026-09-26`; `'2026_06_27'` if `2026-06-28 ≤ created_at < 2026-09-26`; else `pre_2026_06_27`. `policy_generation` captures only the *creation-keyed* rules (June 27 minimum-credit-risk standards; Sept 26 DU validation service rules — fixed-base-income minimums and the business-day close-by date). Every other change in a DU release is *submission-keyed* ("submitted or resubmitted on or after the evening of" June 26 / Sept 25, 2026), so each `du_submissions` row also records `du_release_applied` (the latest release evening ≤ `submitted_at`, e.g. `2026_09_25`); a casefile created before a release picks up the submission-keyed changes (CU-driven Ineligible recommendations, Lava Zone messages, retired/modified messages) on its first submission after the release evening. The agent records both keys on the decision record so that recommendation drift after a DU update is explainable (LL-2026-04). Archival clocks: `archive_270_due_at = last_updated_at + 270 days`; `archive_540_due_at = created_at + 540 days`; `archive_due_at = min(...)`. *Worked example (refinance fixture):* casefile created Tue Oct 6, 2026 → `archive_540_due_at` = Wed Mar 29, 2028; last update on the final submission Thu Nov 5, 2026 → `archive_270_due_at` = Mon Aug 2, 2027 → `archive_due_at` = Aug 2, 2027. The loan is purchased Nov 19, 2026, so the clock is moot; the row is kept for audit.
2. **Credit association.** For each borrower: if 22.2 ordered a tri-merge from a DU-registered provider, `mode = reissue` with the provider's DU code and the report reference number; joint applicants use `report_type = joint` and the DU Spec arcrole `SharesJointCreditReportWith`. The score model on the report must equal `applications.score_model` for every borrower (VantageScore 4.0 or Classic FICO, never mixed — Sept 9, 2026 release notes). If a borrower's last name, suffix or SSN changes after association, the agent must associate a new report (DU job aid) and open a `DU_JOBAID_IDENTITY_CHANGE` check (also an 22.6 fraud signal).
3. **Submission types.** First pass: `credit_and_underwriting`. `credit_only` is used only to pre-stage a reissue before the 1003 is complete (no findings; no policy effect). `underwriting_only` is the normal resubmission (credit already in the casefile). Every request is the full ULAD snapshot (DU Spec is a whole-file submission), hashed into `request_hash`; two identical hashes in a row are suppressed unless `reason ∈ {error_retry, final_closed_loan_match}`.
4. **Resubmission decision — B3-2-10 as executable logic.** Given baseline submission *B* (last findings) and candidate data *C*:
   - `note_rate`: if `C.rate < B.rate` and the decrease is not from a permanent buydown → `within_tolerance` (record `du.resubmission.waived`); if from a permanent buydown → `resubmission_required`; if `C.rate > B.rate` → evaluate the DTI test below (a rate increase is a resubmission trigger only through DTI, but see rule 5).
   - **DTI test** (applies to rate increases and income/liability/asset changes): `dti_C = round_half_up((C.total_obligations_cents / C.qualifying_income_cents) × 100, 2)`; `resubmission_required` if `dti_C > 45.00` or `dti_C − dti_B ≥ 3.00`; if `dti_C > 50.00` → `ineligible_change` (B3-6-02: "For loan casefiles underwritten through DU, the maximum allowable DTI ratio is 50%") and the 23.2 restructuring loop starts. Rounding: DTI to two decimals, half-up, computed on cents.
   - `loan_amount` (refinance): `max_increase = min(50000 cents, floor(B.amount × 0.01))`; `max_decrease = floor(B.amount × 0.05)`; within tolerance only if `B.amount − max_decrease ≤ C.amount ≤ B.amount + max_increase` **and** the new LTV/CLTV/HCLTV does not change MI coverage (B7-1-02 bands 80.01–85 / 85.01–90 / 90.01–95 / 95.01–97), any LLPA band (`llpa_tables` LTV columns, 20.4) or eligibility (Eligibility Matrix maximums). Purchase: any loan-amount change → `resubmission_required`. LCOR cash-back limits (B2-1.3-02, 10/08/2025: "the greater of 1% of the new refinance loan amount or $2,000" — verified 2026-09-11; 22.4/21.5) are tested separately and never excused by this tolerance.
   - `reserves`: `resubmission_required` if `verified_reserves_cents < 0.90 × reserves_required_cents` (compare in cents; 90% computed as `floor(required × 90 / 100)`); otherwise `within_tolerance` even if below the findings figure.
   - `income` on income-limited products (HomeReady): `resubmission_required` if verified income > application income (B3-2-10: "Income is greater than the loan application indicates"); a decrease is evaluated only through the DTI test above, and the HomeReady AMI test (23.2) is re-run on the verified figure.
   - Closed-loan fields (`occupancy, product, amortization, loan_term, property_type, loan_purpose, sales_price, appraised_value`): any difference between the final submission and the closed loan → `resubmission_required` regardless of tolerance (B3-2-10 first sentence).
   - `credit_report`: if the report's `expires_at` (22.2: pulled_at + 4 months) is earlier than the projected note date → new report required before any resubmission (`B3_2_01_CREDIT_EXPIRED`); a refreshed report always triggers `resubmission_required` with `reason = credit_refresh`.
   - `validation_report`: an updated validation report → `resubmission_required` (A2-2-04 relief requires the post-update validated message).
5. **Policy: the final submission always carries the closed-loan data.** Even where B3-2-10 waives resubmission (e.g., a small rate increase inside the DTI tolerance), SM resubmits once more with `reason = final_closed_loan_match` immediately after the CD-final data are frozen (25.2) and before closing documents are generated, so that `closed_loan_snapshot_hash` matches and A2-2-04's "complete, accurate" condition is provable. Cost: one extra casefile submission per loan (DU pricing is per casefile, not per submission — **[UNVERIFIED]**; confirm in the partner's subscription agreement).
6. **Excessive resubmissions.** After the 10th submission on a casefile the agent must attach a rationale for each further submission; after the 15th, `underwriting_reviewer` review is required before the next one (DU's "excessive resubmissions" red-flag message, B3-2-11). Data may never be changed to obtain a better recommendation; every diff must trace to a `verifications`/`documents` row or a borrower request (`changed_circumstances`).
7. **Worked example 1 — refinance fixture, liability change.** Application Mon Oct 5, 2026; tri-merge (Classic FICO) pulled Oct 5 (expires Feb 5, 2027 — 22.2). First submission Tue Oct 6, 2026 (`credit_and_underwriting`, DU 12.1, policy generation `2026_09_26`): $560,000 LCOR, 30-year fixed, quoted 6.125%, qualifying income $12,000.00/month, P&I $3,402.62 (formula: $560,000 × 0.0051041667 / (1 − 1.0051041667^−360) = $3,402.619 → $3,402.62 half-up; the fixture's $3,402.63 is a round-up convention — 26.1 owns note rounding; results below are identical at either value), taxes $500.00, insurance $120.00, other debts $537.37 → obligations $4,560.00 → DTI 38.00%; Approve/Eligible. Lock Wed Oct 7 at 6.125% (no change). Tue Oct 20 the 22.2 undisclosed-debt monitor reports a new auto loan at $450.00/month: DTI = ($4,560.00 + $450.00) / $12,000.00 = 41.75%; 41.75 ≤ 45.00 but 41.75 − 38.00 = 3.75 ≥ 3.00 → `resubmission_required` (`B3_2_10_DTI_45_OR_3PT`); `SM_DU_RESUBMIT_SLA_1BD` due Wed Oct 21; resubmitted Oct 20 (`underwriting_only`, reason `tolerance_breach`); findings Oct 20 remain Approve/Eligible with an updated liability message → 23.2 conditions. Had the new payment been $300.00: DTI 40.50%, increase 2.50 → `within_tolerance`, `du.resubmission.waived` recorded — but the liability still goes into the final closed-loan submission (rule 5).
8. **Worked example 2 — refinance fixture, loan-amount change.** Thu Oct 22 the borrower asks to raise the loan to $565,600 to finance closing costs. Tolerance: `max_increase = min($500.00, 1% × $560,000 = $5,600.00) = $500.00` → ceiling $560,500; $565,600 exceeds it → `resubmission_required` (`B3_2_10_REFI_AMOUNT_500_1PCT`). Independently, LTV moves from 70.00% to 70.70%, crossing the 70.01–75% LLPA column (LLPA Matrix 09.09.2026; values 20.4), which alone defeats the tolerance. This is a borrower-requested change → 21.5 records `changed_circumstances{kind=borrower_request}` and a revised LE by Tue Oct 27 (21.5's `REGZ_1026_19E4_REVISED_LE_3BD`); 23.1 resubmits the same day the pricing engine reprices. Had the request been $560,400 (+$400, LTV 70.05%): inside the $500 cap but LTV crosses into 70.01–75% → still `resubmission_required` on the LLPA condition.
9. **Worked example 3 — rate increase inside tolerance.** Suppose the Oct 7 lock had been lost and re-locked Mon Nov 2 at 6.250%: P&I = $3,448.02; DTI = ($3,448.02 + $620.00 + $537.37) / $12,000.00 = 38.38%; 38.38 ≤ 45 and +0.38 < 3 → B3-2-10 does not require resubmission; rule 5 resubmits anyway with the closed-loan rate before closing documents are generated on Tue Nov 3, so the final findings show 6.250%.
10. **Worked example 4 — purchase fixture, reserves.** Columbus, OH purchase, application Mon Oct 19, 2026; casefile created Tue Oct 20 (`archive_540_due_at` Wed Apr 12, 2028); findings show "Reserves Required to be Verified" $8,000.00. Verified assets after closing costs leave $7,300.00 (91.25%) → `within_tolerance`; $7,100.00 (88.75%) → `resubmission_required` (`B3_2_10_RESERVES_90PCT`) and 22.4 sources additional funds or 23.2 restructures.
11. **Version cutover example.** A casefile created Thu Sept 24, 2026 and still open on Sept 26 keeps its pre-update behavior only for the DU validation service changes ("apply to loan casefiles created on or after Sept. 26, 2026" — its close-by date stays in calendar days and the old fixed-base-income minimums apply) but, on its first submission or resubmission after the evening of Sept 25, is subject to every other September change ("submitted or resubmitted on or after the evening of Sept. 25, 2026" — CU-driven Ineligible for a C6 rating or a No-Longer-Accepted appraiser, Lava Zone confirmation, the 11/25/7 message changes), exactly as the June 26 notes applied their non-credit-risk changes to "submitted or resubmitted" casefiles. If it is archived (270 days idle) and re-created, the new casefile is "subject to the policies in effect for the current version of DU."

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`underwriter` agent (package `agents/underwriter`; this process uses tools `buildDuRequest`, `associateCredit`, `submitCasefile`, `fetchFindings`, `evaluateResubmission`, `snapshotClosedLoan`, `assertFinalSubmissionMatches`, `writeDecision`, `openEscalation`). End-to-end: on credit receipt it assembles the ULAD snapshot, associates every borrower's report (reissue), submits `credit_and_underwriting`, parses JSON v2 findings into `du_submissions`, and hands messages to 23.2. On every application diff it runs `evaluateResubmission` and either resubmits within the SLA or records a waiver with the arithmetic. Before closing documents it freezes the closed-loan snapshot and performs the final match submission. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{application_id, casefile_id, submission_number, reason, diff[], rule_codes[], arithmetic{dti_before, dti_after, amount_before, amount_after, ltv_before, ltv_after, reserves_required, reserves_verified}, policy_generation, du_version, request_hash, findings_hash, recommendation_before, recommendation_after, rationale, model_version, prompt_version, confidence}`. Guardrails: never edit ULAD data except from a `verifications`/`documents`/`changed_circumstances` source; never suppress a liability or income change to stay within tolerance; never submit without a report for every borrower; never mix score models; never exceed the resubmission cap without `underwriting_reviewer`; never disclose findings to the borrower. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (DI outage), `underwriting_reviewer` (resubmission > 15; erroneous-credit path where correction cannot be obtained; recommendation downgrade on the final match submission), `officer` (Technology Manager/TSP credential changes). Borrower-facing: none directly; DU submission is disclosed in the application's automated-underwriting consent language (21.1). Human path: ops console shows the same request/diff/rule evaluation with a manual "submit" that produces the identical artifact.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Frozen credit** (DU red-flag message): 22.2 obtains the lift; no submission until a full report is associated; if a bureau stays frozen, the tri-merge is incomplete → submission blocked (B3-5.2-01 requires a three in-file merged report).
- **Borrower without a score**: allowed in DU (no minimum score); nontraditional-credit/cash-flow assessment rules (B3-2-03) apply — 22.2 supplies the 12-month asset report; the casefile is still a normal submission.
- **Casefile ID reuse**: DU flags potential reuse; the platform enforces one casefile per application and never re-uses a casefile for a different borrower/property (superseded rows keep their IDs).
- **Archived mid-pipeline** (e.g., a 300-day stalled application): create a new casefile, re-associate credit (a new report is required if the old one is > 4 months at the projected note date), record `du.casefile.superseded`, re-run 23.2/23.3 because the new casefile is "subject to the policies in effect for the current version of DU."
- **DU update between submissions changes the recommendation** (June 27 / Sept 26 generations): the agent records `recommendation_before/after` and, if the loan falls to Approve/Ineligible or Refer with Caution, 23.2's restructuring loop runs; if the closing date is inside 3 business days, `underwriting_reviewer` is notified because the closing may have to move.
- **DI channel outage at the final-match step**: `fnma_portal_operator` uploads the request through the DU UI; the JSON findings are retrieved from the UI export and ingested by hash; the gate stays closed until findings are on file.
- **Identity change** (name/SSN correction from 22.6): new credit association mandatory; the credit refresh restarts the 22.2 4-month clock.
- **Validation report updated after the last submission**: relief is lost unless resubmitted and re-validated — the agent resubmits automatically.
- **Loan amount change on an LCOR that breaches the cash-back cap**: never excused by the $500/1% tolerance; 21.5/22.4 fix the structure first, then resubmit.
- **DU Error recommendation** (casefile could not be processed): stored as `recommendation = error`; retry once after cause fixed; never counted as a credit decision for Reg B purposes (21.6's `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` keeps running from its conservative anchor, `application_date`, regardless).
- **Return File Type retirement** (Nov 30, 2026): requests carrying types 16/17 are refused by the adapter from Dec 1, 2026.
