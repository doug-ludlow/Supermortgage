### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Servicing Guide A2-1-01, "Additional Servicer Duties and Responsibilities for Certain Servicers" (12/17/2025; Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026) — verbatim:** "The following provisions apply to all master servicers and subservicers that own and/or service a total portfolio size greater than or equal to 20,000 mortgage loans at any time during a calendar year. To the extent the servicer relies on any third-party technology provider for the performance of critical servicing functions, the servicer must provide: not less than 180 days' prior written notice to Fannie Mae of its intent to change such third-party technology provider, together with, upon Fannie Mae's written request, the servicer's transition plan for such change; and within 5 business days after its occurrence, written notice to Fannie Mae of any termination, breach, or impairment of rights by servicer or the technology provider of or under such contract. These obligations are in addition to, not in lieu of, the servicer's obligations to notify Fannie Mae under Selling Guide A4-1-01, Maintaining Seller/Servicer Eligibility. Critical servicing functions include services and processes that directly impact the servicing of a mortgage loan (e.g., mortgage loan payment processing, remitting, accounting, and reporting, etc.), providing servicing solutions to a borrower in need, or the timely and accurate reporting to Fannie Mae or other entities. Such transition plan must describe the steps the servicer is undertaking to ensure an orderly transfer of critical servicing functions to a new technology service provider so there is no interruption to borrowers and Fannie Mae and account for continuity of technology service from the original third-party technology service provider. In addition, in all contracts with third-party technology providers for critical servicing functions, the servicer must ensure Fannie Mae is provided within five business days with copies of any termination, notice of default, breach or non-performance or any notice of impairment of rights, in each case sent to the servicer; and such third-party technology provider acknowledges Fannie Mae's ownership interest in the Fannie Mae mortgage loans (including all associated files and data) and agrees to reasonably cooperate with any transfer of such mortgage loans to a new servicer, technology service provider or servicing platform, as may be required by or directed by Fannie Mae, upon payment of reasonable and customary transition fees."

**A2-1-01 Overview (all servicers, no size threshold):** master servicers and subservicers must require contracted vendors to "have policies and procedures for the contracted servicing activities, including for the orderly transfer of any contracted critical servicing activities upon a termination or expiration"; "ensure no disruption of service, service levels, or offerings … which may have a material adverse effect on either the borrower or the critical servicing functions"; "conduct audits and QC reviews on subservicer/outsource vendor for contracted servicing activities, including services performed outside the United States"; "conduct operational assessments and reviews that measure the subservicer/outsource vendor performance"; and "develop, document, and implement a formal vendor risk management program that meets the requirements in the Fannie Mae Information Security and Business Resiliency Supplement." **A2-1-07 (05/13/2026)**: both master servicer and subservicer must be approved servicers in good standing; the master "remains fully liable"; the agreement must acknowledge Fannie Mae's right to rescind recognition of the arrangement; **Form 101** executed and submitted "at inception and again at termination" (research/00a §4.1).

**Consolidated Technology Guide (Apr. 21, 2026; verified):** "Fannie Mae Data" = "All data, materials, and records (including data and materials compiled from records) representing, related to, associated with, or based on loans sold to Fannie Mae, serviced for Fannie Mae, or in which Fannie Mae has an ownership right or interest"; licensees "may only access and use the Licensed Materials for Licensee's internal mortgage-related business purposes"; "Licensee must not disclose Fannie Mae Data (alone or in combination with other data, even if aggregated or anonymized) to third parties or use or permit the use of the same for any purpose other than (a) for Licensee's internal mortgage-related business purposes, (b) for such third party's development, operation, and maintenance of the Licensed Application or a permitted interface or integration with the Licensed Application, or (c) assisting a Fannie Mae seller/servicer or DO Licensee in connection with originating or servicing loans eligible for sale or sold to Fannie Mae"; "If Licensee is not a SF Lender or MF Lender, Licensee may only issue Authentication Credentials to Licensee's employees"; third-party designation only "with the express written permission of Fannie Mae"; the licensee "guarantees full performance by each agent or other third party"; credentials reset at least every 90 days (individuals)/365 days (systems); no scraping/RPA, no identity masking, no API use "other than as intended" or over rate limits; "must not transfer data via an Integration Interface if the Integration Interface is out of compliance for more than 120 days"; Fannie Mae "may audit Licensee's use … [and] access to Licensee's systems, facilities, books, and records"; on termination "return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information … [and] have a duly authorized officer … certify"; equitable relief includes compelling "return or deletion of Fannie Mae Data and all derivatives" and "preservation and production of relevant logs, system images, and artifacts"; §5.8: record-keeping under state/federal law is the licensee's responsibility. No clause addresses AI/ML model training — the disclosure limits above govern any model provider.

**Form 101 / TR101 (12/9/2025; verified):** parties Fannie Mae, Master Subscriber (partner) and Subordinate Subscriber (Supermortgage); authorizes the subordinate to "access, edit, and provide data" for listed applications limited to specified seller/servicer numbers; obligations of confidentiality, security safeguards, need-to-know disclosure, use "solely for Master Subscriber's benefit"; breach notice "to Fannie Mae and Master Subscriber in writing … no later than 36 hours after Subordinate Subscriber becomes aware"; mutual indemnities in Fannie Mae's favour; "may be terminated at any time by Fannie Mae upon written notice to Subscribers or by either Subscriber upon written notice to Fannie Mae and the other Subscriber"; submitted to `Technology_Registration@fanniemae.com`; designatable applications include AMN, CRS, SMDU, CBAM and custom service-bureau applications.

**LL-2026-04 (Apr. 8, 2026; effective Aug. 6, 2026; verified):** written policies covering development, implementation, use, maintenance and risk measurement of AI/ML, "transparent and communicated to appropriate personnel," reflecting "trustworthy and ethical AI/ML," legal/regulatory requirements and risk tolerance, with designated owner(s) reviewing "at least annually"; manage "subcontractor and vendor use of AI/ML that is no less protective"; comply with the Supplement; "Upon request by Fannie Mae, the seller/servicer must promptly disclose the types of AI/ML used, the purpose and manner for such use, the safeguards … implemented to mitigate risks, and such other information as Fannie Mae may require." **Supplement (Sept. 2, 2025)** supply-chain section: vendor controls "at least as protective," risk-based re-assessment, agreements covering security and resiliency, and full responsibility for vendors' failures. **Reg X §1024.38(b)(3)** requires policies to give personnel access to service-provider records, facilitate periodic reviews of service providers, and share loss-mitigation/foreclosure status with service-provider personnel. **Form 582** (annual, within 90 days of fiscal year-end; 18.4) carries the "Contractors/Third Parties" disclosure and the Supplement attestation (research/00b). **Selling Guide A4-1-01**: material changes within five business days (research/00a §4.3; 18.7). **TSP Integration Agreement**: obligations are portal-gated **[UNVERIFIED specifics]** — assume the Technology Guide §5.7 API terms (build to spec, stay within one version, no masking, health checks).

**Discrepancies vs. blueprint row.** (1) The regime is notices + contract clauses + transition planning, not merely "attestations"; the attestation artifacts are Form 582 and the Supplement officer attestation. (2) The 20,000 threshold is measured per entity on its **total** portfolio (owned and/or serviced — read as all loans, not only Fannie Mae's) "at any time during a calendar year"; the partner and Supermortgage are tested separately, and Supermortgage-as-subservicer owes the same notices about **its own** critical technology providers (cloud, AI model provider, payment rails, print/mail, telephony) — a materially broader obligation than the row suggests. (3) The vendor-oversight duties (audits/QC, operational assessments, formal vendor risk program) apply to every servicer regardless of size. (4) Form 101, the Technology Guide's employee-only credential rule and LL-2026-04's vendor flow-down are not in the row.

#### State machine
- Vendor: `proposed → due_diligence → approved → active ⇄ remediation → offboarding → terminated` (guards: Tier 1 needs a completed onboarding assessment, all mandatory clauses `present`, SOC 2/pen-test evidence ≤ 12 months old, BCP evidence and an officer approval; `terminated` requires data return/destruction certification and credential revocation; AI vendors additionally need `ai_systems` linkage and an LL-2026-04 governance attestation).
- Technology-provider change: `intent_declared → notice_sent → plan_ready → (plan_requested → plan_delivered) → gate_open (≥ 180 days after notice) → cutover → post_transition_monitoring (90 days) → closed`; `cutover` is blocked by `assertGateOpen(FNMA_A2101_TECH_PROVIDER_CHANGE_NOTICE_180)` and, when Supermortgage is being replaced, by the Section 17 transfer-out checklist and Form 629 approval (30 days for subservicing transfers).
- Contract event: `occurred → assessed (fnma_notice_required?) → notice_drafted → notice_sent → closed`; if the event is a notice *sent to the servicer* under the technology contract, the copy obligation (5 BD) attaches in parallel.
- Form 101: `drafted → executed → submitted → acknowledged → active → termination_submitted → terminated`; Fannie Mae or either party may terminate at any time → `fnma.access.revoked` triggers the BCP path.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Threshold.** `loan_count(entity, date)` = all loans the entity owns and/or services (all investors) on that date; `year_max` = max over the calendar year; `a2101_regime_active = year_max ≥ 20000`, sticky for the rest of the calendar year (the text is "at any time during a calendar year") and re-tested each January 1. Both entities are tracked; the partner's count comes from the partner's monthly attestation feed (18.7) plus Supermortgage's subserviced count.
2. **What is a "third-party technology provider for critical servicing functions."** For the partner: Supermortgage. For Supermortgage: any vendor whose failure would stop payment processing, remitting, accounting, reporting to Fannie Mae or borrower servicing solutions — default classification: cloud infrastructure (SoR hosting), the AI model provider (runs the servicing agents), lockbox/ACH processor, custodial bank platform, print/mail (statements/notices), telephony/voice platform, IdP. These carry `critical_servicing_function = true`, Tier 1, and the 180-day change regime **[open decision 1]**.
3. **"Change" of provider.** Replacing a provider, moving a critical function in-house from a provider, or moving a critical function to a new provider; a failover to a pre-disclosed secondary provider under an existing contract is treated as continuity, not change, **only if** the secondary was named in the original notice/Form 582 disclosure (open decision 2).
4. **Worked example — 180 days.** Partner declares on Mon 2026-11-02 its intent to replace Supermortgage with cutover 2027-04-01; the notice is sent the same day → earliest permissible cutover = 2026-11-02 + 180 days = **Sat 2027-05-01**; the planned 2027-04-01 cutover is blocked by the gate; the Form 629 (30-day) lead for the subservicing transfer is separately satisfied by filing no later than 2027-04-01 for a May 1 transfer (Section 17.1).
5. **Worked example — 5 business days.** Supermortgage sends the partner a notice of the partner's non-payment (a "notice of default … sent to the servicer") on Fri 2026-11-20. Fannie Mae ET business days: Mon 23, Tue 24, Wed 25, [Thu 26 Thanksgiving], Fri 27, Mon 30 → copies due to Fannie Mae by end of day ET **Mon 2026-11-30**; the partner's own 5-BD "breach or impairment" notice (if the event qualifies) is due the same day. A termination notice occurring Wed 2026-12-23 → BD1 Thu 24, [Fri 25 holiday], BD2 Mon 28, BD3 Tue 29, BD4 Wed 30, BD5 Thu 31 → due 2026-12-31.
6. **Mandatory clause set** (checklist must be `present` or `deviation` with attorney sign-off before `active`): the three A2-1-01 clauses; A2-1-07 rescission acknowledgment (subservicing agreement); Supplement flow-down + LL-2026-04 governance "no less protective"; incident notice ≤ 24 h to Supermortgage (so Supermortgage can meet 36 h); audit rights for Fannie Mae and Supermortgage; Fannie Mae Data use limited to Technology Guide §3 purposes, no disclosure even if aggregated/anonymized, no derivative works; return/destroy at termination with officer certification; records return within 5 BD on request (19.1); US-only processing unless approved; subprocessor notice; no scraping of Fannie Mae UIs; for AI providers: no training on inputs/outputs, zero/limited retention (≤ 30 days abuse-monitoring at most, none preferred), no human review of prompts without notice, model/version change notice, evaluation/assurance reports, cooperation with Fannie Mae disclosure requests.
7. **AI-provider data-handling standard** (CTL-SEC-21 tests it): only tokenized identifiers in prompts (no SSN/TIN, account numbers, DOB beyond age band); never restricted FL data (19.4); Fannie Mae Data sent only to the extent needed for the servicing task (Technology Guide §3(c)); per-call manifest of loan ids and data classes stored in `agent_runs` (supports incident scoping); provider region pinned to US; provider logs retention verified quarterly; kill-switch to a pre-approved secondary provider.
8. **Vendor tiering and cadence.** Tier 1 (critical function or Fannie Mae Data/NPI at scale): onboarding assessment, annual reassessment, SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent independent assessment), pen-test summary, BCP test evidence, subprocessor list, financial viability; Tier 2: biennial; Tier 3: triennial questionnaire. Offshore services require an audit plan (A2-1-01 "including services performed outside the United States") — default: none permitted.
9. **Form 582 package** (to 18.4): contractors/third-parties list (name, function, critical flag, data classes, country), subservicing confirmation, Supplement attestation letter (19.2), LL-2026-04 program summary.
10. **LL-2026-04 disclosure response** (on request): AI types (LLM agents; voice AI; ML scoring if any), purposes by agent, manner of use (tool allowlists, human touchpoints), safeguards (evaluation suites, monitoring, bias testing 19.4, decision records, vendor clauses), model/agent inventory export.

#### Integrations
- **Fannie Mae written notices** (A2-1-01 change/event/copies, transition plan, LL-2026-04 and Supplement responses): letters/emails prepared by the agent, signed and sent by the responsible `officer` through the channel fixed at onboarding **[UNVERIFIED channel]**; acknowledgements stored; no API.
- **Form 101**: PDF prepared from `data_access_authorizations`, e-signed by both subscribers, emailed to Technology_Registration@fanniemae.com by the `officer`; Fannie Mae acknowledgement gates adapter activation for the partner scope.
- **Form 582** (ECRM in Technology Manager — portal-only): 18.4's `human_portal_task`; 19.3 supplies the attachments.
- **Vendor assurance ingestion**: SOC 2 reports/questionnaires as `documents`; structured extraction into `vendor_assessments.findings` by the agent; bridge letters tracked.
- **Contract repository / e-signature**: executed agreements ingested with clause extraction; deviations to `attorney`.
- **Agent runtime**: `ai_systems` rows are written by deployment pipelines (model/prompt versions), enforcing the pre-deploy evaluation gate.
- Failure handling: notices are idempotent by `fnma_notices.id`; if the channel bounces, fallback to courier with proof; portal task escalation per 18.4.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agents:** `security-records` (vendor/contract/AI-inventory custodian) and `compliance-sentinel` (threshold monitor, clocks, Guide-watch on A2-1-01/Technology Guide/Supplement/LL editions). End-to-end: computes threshold status; extracts clauses from executed contracts and scores the checklist; classifies contract events and drafts the 5-BD notices and copies; drafts change notices and transition plans from the 17.x/19.1 export capabilities; runs vendor due diligence (questionnaire dispatch, SOC 2 extraction, control mapping to the Supplement, risk rating, remediation tracking); maintains the LL-2026-04 inventory from deploy metadata; assembles Form 582 and disclosure packages; monitors Fannie Mae integration compliance (schema/version drift) and credential ages.
- **Tools:** `contracts.extractClauses`, `contracts.checklist`, `vendors.assess`, `vendors.setStatus` (approve requires officer event for Tier 1), `notices.draft`, `fnma_notices.recordSent` (after officer), `portfolio.count`, `ai_systems.upsert`, `timers.read`, `portal_task.create`.
- **Decision record:** `{subject (vendor/contract/change), clause_scores[], risk_rating, critical_function_basis, threshold_basis{entity, year_max}, notices_required[], due_dates[], model_version, prompt_version, rule_set_version (Guide edition), confidence, escalation?}`.
- **Escalations:** every Fannie Mae notice and Form 101/582 signature → `officer` (partner's officer for partner obligations; Supermortgage's for its own); clause deviations, subpoena-like data demands, termination disputes → `attorney`; Form 582 upload → `fnma_portal_operator`; Tier 1 vendor approval/termination → `officer`; any vendor with `offshore = true` or `ai_ml_used = true` lacking attestation → `officer` before activation.
- **Guardrails:** the agent cannot activate a Tier 1 vendor, send a Fannie Mae notice, or execute a cutover; it cannot mark a clause `present` without an evidence excerpt; threshold status changes are logged with the count basis.
- **Human path (AI off):** vendor-management workspace in the ops console with the same checklists and timers.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Threshold crossed mid-year** (e.g., 19,800 → 20,300 on Aug 3): the regime applies for the whole calendar year; any provider change already in flight without a 180-day notice is re-planned (notice sent immediately; cutover ≥ 180 days out) and the gap disclosed to Fannie Mae.
- **Partner below 20,000 but Supermortgage above:** Supermortgage's obligations attach to its own providers; the partner still includes the A2-1-01 clauses (contractual best practice and Fannie Mae may treat the arrangement as critical) — default: always include.
- **Fannie Mae terminates Form 101** (at will): adapters running under partner scope stop immediately; reporting continues through Supermortgage's own TSP scope where permitted, else the partner's staff perform portal tasks from AI-prepared packages; Section 5 timers keep running — file a Supplement disruption notice if Fannie Mae reporting is impaired.
- **Fannie Mae suspends APIs on suspected cyber risk** (Technology Guide): treated as 19.2 incident + BCP.
- **Supermortgage's own exit** (voluntary or partner termination): termination notice → 5-BD Fannie Mae notice and copies; 180-day gate; transition plan on request; Form 629 (30 days) and Section 17 transfer-out; Form 101 termination; data return/destruction certification — while continuing full service until cutover ("continuity of technology service from the original … provider").
- **AI provider change/failover:** pre-disclosed secondary provider avoids a "change"; an unplanned switch to an undisclosed provider is a change → notice and 180 days, so the platform must be able to run on the disclosed secondary for at least 180 days.
- **Vendor subprocessor changes/offshoring without notice:** contract breach → reassessment, possible termination; if the vendor touches Fannie Mae Data, consider it an impairment-of-rights event → 5-BD notice.
- **Partner's own breach of the technology contract** (e.g., non-payment): the partner must notify Fannie Mae within 5 BD of its own breach; Supermortgage's copies obligation runs in parallel.
- **Contract event ambiguity** (is a cure notice a "notice of default"?): default to notify; `attorney` confirms within the 5-BD window.
- **Vendor insolvency / cloud region loss:** BCP contingency (19.2) plus the transition plan for the affected function; Fannie Mae disruption notice.
- **Fannie Mae-directed transfer to a new platform** (A2-1-01 clause): executed as a Section 17 transfer with "reasonable and customary transition fees" invoiced per the contract's fee schedule.
