### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**12 CFR 1024.34(a)** (eCFR current as of Sept. 3, 2026): the servicer must make escrow disbursements "on or before the deadline to avoid a penalty, as governed by the requirements in § 1024.17(k)." **12 CFR 1024.17(k)** (current as of Sept. 4, 2026): (1) pay "in a timely manner, that is, on or before the deadline to avoid a penalty, as long as the borrower's payment is not more than 30 days overdue"; (2) "must advance funds to make disbursements in a timely manner as long as the borrower's payment is not more than 30 days overdue," recouping under (f); (3) where a taxing jurisdiction offers annual vs. installment: if it "neither offers a discount for disbursements on a lump sum annual basis nor imposes any additional charge or fee for installment disbursement, the servicer must make disbursements on an installment basis"; if it offers a discount or charges an installment fee, the servicer may (not must) pay annually, and the Bureau "encourages" following a known borrower preference; (4) servicer and borrower may agree individually to a different basis/date, voluntarily and not as a loan condition; (5)(i) for a borrower "more than 30 days overdue" with an escrow account for hazard insurance, the servicer "may not purchase force-placed insurance … unless a servicer is unable to disburse funds from the borrower's escrow account"; (5)(ii)(A) inability exists only with "a reasonable basis to believe either that the borrower's hazard insurance has been canceled (or was not renewed) for reasons other than nonpayment of premium charges or that the borrower's property is vacant"; (5)(ii)(B) insufficient escrow funds are *not* inability; (5)(ii)(C) advances are recoverable; (5)(iii) small-servicer exception (not applicable — Supermortgage will exceed 5,000 loans). Supplement I 17(k)(5)(ii)(A)-1 gives the three "reasonable basis" examples (borrower notice without replacement coverage; insurer cancellation notice before the premium due date; no payment notice by expiration); 17(k)(5)(ii)(C)-1 permits month-to-month premium advances where the insurer accepts them. (b) "Penalty" means a payee's late charge, "not … an additional charge or fee imposed by the payee associated with choosing installment payments as opposed to annual payments." 1024.35(b)(3) makes "failure to make timely payment from escrow" a Notice-of-Error category (Section 4.1).

**Fannie Mae B-1-01 (09/11/2024)**: the servicer must "ensure the timely payment of all escrow and related charges before any applicable penalty or termination date" and "make payments … early enough to take advantage of any discount period to obtain the maximum discounts allowed whenever funds are available in the escrow account"; monitor and keep accurate records on "all escrow and related charges" (taxes, ground rents, HOA/condo assessments, property/flood insurance, MI, "and other charges that could attain priority over Fannie Mae's lien") — for escrowed and non-escrowed loans alike; "the servicer must promptly advance the funds to cover an expense" when escrow is insufficient; advances "must stay in the T&I custodial account until the borrower remits funds sufficient to cure the deficit"; manufactured homes must be taxed as real property with a single bill or the servicer must escrow personal-property tax; HOA/condo super-priority liens: determine the minimum to clear, negotiate, pay "prior to the foreclosure sale date or closing of a Mortgage Release," and report refusals/litigation to Fannie Mae Legal via Form 20. **F-1-05 (06/11/2025)**: tax/insurance advances are reimbursable if the loan subsequently becomes delinquent; "Late charges and penalties are not payable on escrowed mortgage loans"; for non-escrowed loans "the first set of late charges and penalties for each tax type" may be reimbursed; HOA late/attorney fees caused by the servicer's failure to monitor are excluded; property/flood premiums reimbursed up to 14 days after foreclosure sale/DIL/short sale. **B-8.1-02 (05/20/2015)**: borrower-paid MI renewals are paid from escrow; lender-paid MI from servicer funds, never from a custodial account.

**Fannie Mae LL-2026-05 (June 24, 2026)** and companions (Servicing Changes Reference Guide v1.0, Dec 17, 2025; Escrow Reporting CIT Plan v2.2, Sept 1, 2026; FAQ Q51 Jul 22, 2026; Servicing Platform UI Guide Apr 2026): servicers "must comply with escrow reporting requirements" by **December 1, 2026** (optional from July 18, 2026); first "submit a separate Escrow Setup event for each applicable escrow item category type" for "all existing active and inactive mortgage loans with an escrow balance"; then "report escrow deposit and escrow disbursement events on the same day the servicing activity is processed" and "no later than 3:00 a.m. eastern time on the next business day"; the reporting period "will close at 5:00 p.m. eastern time on the second business day of the month." Category types: Loan Taxes and Insurance, Loan Buy Down, Loan Loss Draft, Loan Renovation. Attributes: "Fannie Mae Servicer Number," "Fannie Mae Loan Number," "Loan Escrow Item Category Type," "Loan Escrow Item Type" (examples: "Set up," "Loan Escrow Payment," "Interest on Escrow," "County Tax," "Property Insurance," "Loan Loss Draft Activity," "Modification"), "Loan Escrow Item Amount" (signed: deposits positive, disbursements negative), "Loan Escrow Balance Amount," "Loan Taxes & Insurance Balance Amount" (and per-category balances), "Loan Servicer Transaction Processed Date," "Loan Event Sequence Number," "Loan Escrow Contractual Payment Amount." Fatal rules: prior category balance + item amount must equal the reported balance; processed date may not precede the latest processed date for that category; Loss Draft/Buy Down/Renovation balances may not be negative (T&I may); item amount ≠ 0.00; loan/servicer numbers must be valid and associated. Corrections: resubmit a corrected event; "The previously rejected event is cleared upon acceptance of the new event." Monthly attestation (UI only): confirm "Ending escrow balance," "Loan count," and "Aggregate escrow contractual payment amount for the reporting period" per category; window "Opens on Business Day 3 (BD3)" and "Closes on Business Day 2 (BD2) of the following month" (e.g., March activity: April 3 – May 4). Roles: `LMS_Escrow_View`, `LMS_Escrow_Update`, `LMS_Portal_View`. Subservicers are "acting servicers" and must complete CIT (two consecutive monthly cycles, by Nov 13, 2026). **[UNVERIFIED: the full enumerated "Loan Escrow Item Type" list, reversal/refund item types, and JSON field names — available only in the credentialed API Integration Guide/Swagger and the technical-specifications zip.]**

**Flood**: 12 CFR 22.5 (OCC; parallel FDIC/FRB/NCUA rules; current as of Sept. 3, 2026) requires regulated lending institutions to escrow flood premiums for designated loans made/increased/extended/renewed on/after Jan 1, 2016 (exceptions: business purpose, subordinate lien with coverage, condo/HOA master policy, HELOC, nonperforming ≥ 90 days, term ≤ 12 months; small-lender exception) and to pay them when due; escrow accounting follows RESPA where the loan is RESPA-covered. Section 9.6 owns flood policy tracking; this process pays the premiums.

**State**: Illinois 765 ILCS 910/15 — written notice "within 45 business days after the tax payment" with payment date, amount and property identification (may be combined with other documents or provided annually via phone/email/internet access) (verified via Justia 2026-09-09); Wisconsin 138.052(5m) — borrower options for the escrow agent to pay property taxes by December 20/31 or on the due date on written direction by November 1 if the loan is current; California Civ. Code 2954.8(b) — no impound fees that reduce interest below 2% (3.9). Other state disbursement-timing rules **[UNVERIFIED — 50-state survey in the licensing program]**.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row**: the row's "on tax due dates" trigger understates the scope — the scheduler pays every escrow line (tax, hazard, flood, wind, MI, HOA, ground rent) and must also monitor non-escrowed loans; the row omits the Fannie Mae escrow-event mandate (Dec 1, 2026), the installment-vs-annual rule, advances, and the (k)(5) hazard-insurance restriction.

### Build spec
#### Inputs and triggers
- `escrow.bill.received` (tax service, insurer, MI, HOA feeds) → schedule; `escrow.bill.corrected/supplemental` → reschedule; `escrow.line.created` (boarding/analysis) → expected-bill timers.
- Daily scheduler job (`timer-sweep` at 06:00 servicer TZ): release disbursements whose `release_date = today`.
- `payment.applied` with escrow portion (Section 2.1) → escrow deposit event; `escrow.interest.credited` (3.9) → deposit event; `escrow.refund.received` from payee → deposit event; any `ledger_entries` touching `escrow` → event emitter.
- `loan.boarded` (escrowed) → Escrow Setup event; go-live cutover job on the configured date → Setup events for all active/inactive escrowed loans.
- `insurance.policy.cancellation_notice`, `property.vacancy_confirmed` (Section 9) → (k)(5) inability evaluation.
- Tax service delinquency report (non-escrowed loans) → `escrow.nonescrow.tax_delinquent` case.
- Month-end: attestation package generation on BD3; `human_portal_task` for the UI attestation.

#### State machine
Disbursement: `projected` → `scheduled` (bill received; release_date computed) → `funds_check` (release_date − 1 BD) → `released` | `advance_required` → (advance posted) `released` → `sent` (file/ACH/check issued) → `confirmed` (payee/vendor confirmation or cleared check) [terminal] | `rejected` (vendor/payee reject) → `scheduled` (re-plan within 2 BD) | `returned` → `reissued` → `sent` | `cancelled` (payoff/transfer/duplicate; reason) | `refunded` (payee refund received → escrow deposit). Escrow event: `queued` → `sent` → `accepted`/`accepted_warning` [terminal] | `rejected` → `corrected` (new event) → … ; `closed` (manual close in UI, human) . Attestation: `package_ready` (BD3) → `human_portal_task.open` → `attested_yes` | `attested_no_with_commentary` → `variance_case` → `resolved`. Actors: scheduler, `escrow` agent, adapters, `fnma_portal_operator` (attestation only).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Scheduling**: `must_pay_by = penalty_date ?? due_date`; `release_date = min(discount_date_if_capture, must_pay_by) − lead_days(method)` in servicer business days, never earlier than `bill.received_at` (no pre-accrual, no payment before the bill exists) and never earlier than `must_pay_by − 45 calendar days` unless a discount justifies it.
2. **Discount capture** (B-1-01): pay by the discount date only if `escrow balance − other disbursements due within 30 days ≥ amount` (no advance to capture a discount) and `discount_cents > cost of early release`; log discounts lost with cause.
3. **Installment vs. annual** ((k)(3)–(4)): if the jurisdiction offers no annual discount and no installment fee → installments; if a discount or fee exists → policy `prefer_annual_if_discount ≥ 1% and funds available`; a recorded borrower preference/agreement overrides (voluntary; not a loan condition).
4. **Funds check** at `release_date − 1 BD`: `available = escrow balance (may be negative) − reserved amounts for disbursements releasing within 5 BD`; if `available ≥ amount` → release; else `advance_cents = amount − max(available,0)` → advance (see rule 6) then release. Never delay a tax/insurance/MI/flood/HOA-priority payment for lack of funds.
5. **Delinquency overlay**: Reg X requires timely payment/advance while the borrower is ≤ 30 days overdue; Fannie Mae requires lien protection regardless — policy: advance for all escrowed loans not in `charge_off`/`reo` status; for loans in foreclosure follow Section 13 (taxes and hazard continue; HOA super-priority per B-1-01). Hazard insurance for a borrower > 30 days overdue: continue paying from escrow/advance unless a documented (k)(5)(ii)(A) inability exists (cancellation for non-payment reasons; vacancy); insufficient funds is never inability; month-to-month premium advances allowed where the insurer accepts.
6. **Advance ledger**: Dr `custodial_ti_cash` / Cr `servicer_advance_receivable` (servicer funds into T&I custodial); disbursement Dr loan `escrow` / Cr `custodial_ti_cash` (loan escrow goes negative by `advance_cents`); the advance is recovered only when the loan's escrow balance returns to ≥ 0 (B-1-01); deficiency repayment per 3.6; F-1-05 claim only after the loan becomes delinquent (Section 15.2); penalties caused by servicer/vendor error are booked to corporate expense, never to the borrower and never claimed (F-1-05: not payable on escrowed loans).
7. **Execution methods**: tax_service_bulk (vendor pays authorities from a funded file — funding by ACH debit from the T&I custodial account per the bulk file totals [vendor-specific]); ACH/e-pay to insurers/MI/HOA with validated instructions; check with positive pay; wire for same-day rescue. Idempotency key = (`disbursement.id`, attempt); duplicate prevention by (payee, parcel/policy, period, amount) hash.
8. **Confirmation and rejects**: tax service confirmation/return files update `confirmed`/`rejected`; a reject (wrong parcel, closed account, amount mismatch) must be re-planned within 2 BD; returned checks reissued; refunds from payees posted as escrow deposits (event item type per Fannie Mae allowable values **[UNVERIFIED]**).
9. **Supplemental/delinquent/corrected bills**: paid from escrow when escrowed (advance if needed) with an interim analysis before recoupment (3.2/3.6); for non-escrowed loans, notify the borrower and monitor; if unpaid and lien-threatening (tax sale/ foreclosure by HOA), advance and revoke the waiver (3.8), reimbursable per F-1-05 (first set of penalties reimbursable on non-escrowed loans).
10. **Non-escrowed monitoring**: tax service delinquency search at least twice a year per parcel (aligned to installment dates) plus tax-sale/lien notices; insurance and MI monitoring via Sections 9–10; HOA monitoring via association statements where obtainable.
11. **Escrow event emission** (every `ledger_entries` set that touches a loan's `escrow` account, T&I category unless tagged loss_draft/buy_down/renovation): amount = signed change to the category balance; balance = category balance after posting; `processed_date` = ledger posting date (servicer processing day); `sequence_no` = next per-loan integer; item type mapped from `disbursement_kind`/deposit source (contractual payment → "Loan Escrow Payment" with `contractual_payment_cents`; interest → "Interest on Escrow"; county tax → "County Tax"; hazard → "Property Insurance"; modification balance adjustments → "Modification"; setup → "Set up"; other mappings per the Business & Data Requirements once received); reversals emit an opposite-signed event on the reversal's processed date (not a deletion); events are queued in the outbox with `deadline_at`; the `investor-reporting` agent (Section 5) transports via the `fnma-servicing-events` adapter (API primary; CSV bulk upload via UI as a fallback executed by the `fnma_portal_operator`) and reconciles acks. Balance-equation invariant is enforced locally before sending: `prior_reported_balance + Σ(unsent items in sequence) = ledger balance`.
12. **Setup events**: one per category per loan with a nonzero (or zero, if Fannie Mae requires all escrowed loans) balance on the cutover date and at boarding, before any deposit/disbursement event; category balances come from the ledger snapshot with a reconciliation to the T&I custodial trial balance (Section 6.4).
13. **Attestation package** (BD3): per servicer number and category — ending balance, loan count, aggregate contractual payment amount for the period — computed from `investor_events` accepted in the period and reconciled to the ledger and the Fannie Mae summary; discrepancies become variance cases before the human attests.

Worked example: county tax bill $760.00 for parcel 123-45-678, due 2027-12-10 with penalty after 2027-12-10 (economic loss date = 12/10), 2% discount if paid by 2027-11-30 ($15.20); method tax_service_bulk (lead 10 BD) → `release_date` = 10 servicer business days before 12/10/2027 = **2027-11-26**; discount capture: escrow balance on 11/24 is $810.00 (Nov deposit posted) ≥ $760.00 → pay $744.80 by the discount date; the bulk file goes 11/26, funded by ACH debit $744.80 from T&I custodial on 11/29; vendor confirmation 12/02 → `confirmed`. Ledger: Dr loan escrow 74480 / Cr custodial_ti_cash 74480. Escrow event: category T&I, item "County Tax", amount −744.80, balance 810.00 − 744.80 = 65.20, processed date 2027-11-26 (Fri) → deadline Mon 2027-11-29 03:00 ET; sequence 17. Advance variant: balance $500.00 → advance $260.00: Dr custodial_ti_cash 26000 / Cr servicer_advance_receivable 26000; then disbursement 76000; loan escrow −260.00; escrow event amount −760.00, balance −260.00 (negative allowed for T&I); interim analysis before any repayment demand (3.6). Deposit example: 2027-07-01 payment applied with escrow portion $172.22 → event "Loan Escrow Payment" +172.22, contractual payment 172.22, balance 872.22, sequence 15.

- **Sections 9/10/12/13/15/16**: line data, LPI premium charges to escrow (Section 9.2–9.5 govern the borrower charge; the escrow disbursement to the LPI carrier is executed here), workout adjustments ("Modification" item), foreclosure/REO holds, payoff cutoffs.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `disbursements`, `ledger_entries`, `investor_events` (escrow), `escrow_attestations`, `nonescrow_tax_checks`; notices: `NTC_IL_765_910_15_TAX_PAID` (765 ILCS 910/15: payment date, amount, property identification; mail/electronic; 45 business days), `NTC_SM_ESCROW_ADVANCE` (policy), `NTC_SM_NONESCROW_TAX_DELINQUENCY` (policy: informs borrower of delinquent taxes found; warns of advance/waiver revocation), `NTC_SM_ESCROW_HAZARD_ADVANCE` (policy; when premiums are advanced for a > 30-day delinquent borrower); `loan_events`: `escrow.bill.received/scheduled`, `escrow.disbursement.released/sent/confirmed/rejected/returned/reissued/cancelled`, `escrow.advance.posted/recovered`, `escrow.event.queued/sent/accepted/rejected/corrected`, `escrow.setup_event.sent`, `escrow.attestation.package_ready/submitted`, `escrow.nonescrow.tax_delinquent`.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- Agent: `escrow` (scheduling, funds/advance decisions, rejects, non-escrow monitoring, bill anomaly investigation) with `investor-reporting` (event transport/acks) and `custodial-recon` (496A). Tools: `scheduleDisbursement`, `releaseDisbursement`, `postAdvance`, `expediteDisbursement(method=wire)`, `investigateBill(parcel/policy lookup, prior-year compare, payee validation)`, `recordBorrowerTaxPreference`, `emitEscrowEvent`, `buildAttestationPackage`, `openHumanPortalTask`, `openCase`, `escalate`.
- Decision record: {disbursement id, bill provenance, amount checks (variance vs prior year, duplicate hash), funds check, advance decision and rule basis ((k)(2)/B-1-01), method and lead, discount decision, (k)(5) inability evaluation when relevant, rationale, versions}.
- Guardrails: payee remittance changes require validated evidence and (policy) `officer` dual approval when > $10,000 or a new payee; the agent may not skip a lien-protecting payment; penalties from agent-caused delay are flagged as `servicer_error`; LPI gate enforced by code. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` for attestation and CSV fallback; `officer` for attestation "No" with commentary (contractual representation); `human_agent` on request; `attorney` when an HOA lien litigation/Form 20 referral is needed (Section 13).
- AI-off path: ops-console queues for release, advances, rejects; the event emitter and timers run regardless of AI.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Bill never arrives (vendor miss): estimate from prior year, pay by the penalty date via direct payment to the authority (bill image or online lookup), then reconcile.
- Duplicate bills (vendor and insurer both feed): duplicate hash blocks the second; anomaly review.
- Parcel split/merge, new construction, manufactured home dual taxation (B-1-01): line re-setup with the tax service; escrow both real and personal property taxes.
- Payoff in flight: disbursements with `release_date` after the payoff date are cancelled; bills due before payoff are paid; refund after settlement (3.5).
- Transfer-out: transferor pays bills due through the transfer date, sends the unpaid-bill list (F-1-11), cancels later releases; transferee's setup events **[UNVERIFIED reporting handling on transfer]**.
- Bankruptcy: continue paying taxes/insurance (lien protection); post-petition advances handled under Section 14 rules; escrow events unaffected.
- Foreclosure/REO: taxes and hazard continue until title transfer; after REO conveyance follow Section 15 (Fannie Mae assumes); HOA super-priority payments before sale per B-1-01.
- Disaster: insurer moratoria may move due dates; keep paying on schedule unless the payee confirms deferral.
- Fannie Mae API outage: queue; if the 3:00 a.m. deadline is at risk, fall back to CSV upload via the operator; log late reports.
- Reject storms after a ledger correction: re-sequence and resubmit in order; never send out-of-order.
- Escrow balance negative for LD/BD/Renovation categories (not allowed): block the posting; route to `custodial-recon`.
- Tax service fails to fund/pay after we debited: escalate sev-1; pay directly; reclaim from vendor; penalty cause `vendor_error`.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Tax service vendor (Cotality vs LERETA) and whether to use vendor bulk payment or pay authorities directly. **Default: vendor bulk payment for taxes; direct ACH/check for insurance/MI/HOA.**
2. Advance policy for loans > 30 days delinquent beyond Reg X's minimum. **Default: advance all lien-protecting items until charge-off/REO (B-1-01).**
3. Escrow event channel. **Default: API under Supermortgage's TSP identity; CSV UI fallback.**
4. Discount capture threshold. **Default: capture if funds available; never advance for a discount.**
5. Cutover date for escrow events. **Default: first business day of November 2026** (one full production month before the mandate).
