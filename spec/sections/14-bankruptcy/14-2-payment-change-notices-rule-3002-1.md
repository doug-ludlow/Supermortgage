### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Fed. R. Bankr. P. 3002.1 (restyled Dec. 1, 2024; amended Dec. 1, 2025; text verified at law.cornell.edu 2026-09-09).** *(a) Scope*: "This rule applies in a Chapter 13 case to a claim that is secured by a security interest in the debtor's principal residence and for which the plan provides for the trustee or debtor to make payment on the debt. Unless the court orders otherwise, the requirements of this rule cease when an order terminating or annulling the automatic stay related to that residence becomes effective." *(b)(1) Payment change*: "The claim holder must file a notice of any change in the payment amount, including one resulting from an interest-rate or escrow-account adjustment," filed and served on the debtor, the debtor's attorney and the trustee "at least 21 days before the new payment is due." *(b)(2) HELOC*: the holder may instead file within one year after the petition and then annually, stating the payment due in the month of the notice and a "reconciliation amount to account for any overpayment or underpayment during the prior year," applied to "the first payment due at least 21 days after the annual notice is filed and served," and must still file a (b)(1) notice for any month whose change exceeds $10 — not applicable to this portfolio (closed-end first liens only) but stubbed. *(b)(3) Untimely notice*: if the holder fails to give timely notice, a payment **increase** takes effect on "the first payment due date that is at least 21 days after the untimely notice was filed and served," while a **decrease** takes effect on "the actual payment due date, even if it is prior to the notice." *(b)(4) Objection*: a party in interest may file "a motion to determine the change's validity" "before the day the new payment is due"; "Unless the court orders otherwise, if no motion is filed before the day the new payment is due, the change goes into effect on that date." *(c) Fees, expenses, charges*: the holder must file and serve a notice itemizing all post-petition fees, expenses or charges it "asserts are recoverable against the debtor or against the debtor's principal residence" "within 180 days after the fees, expenses, or charges are incurred." *(d) Form*: "A notice under (b) or (c) must be filed as a supplement to a proof of claim using Form 410S-1 or 410S-2, respectively. The notice is not subject to Rule 3001(f)" (no prima facie effect). *(e) Determination of fees*: on a party in interest's motion "filed within one year after the notice under (c) was served, unless a party in interest requests and the court orders a shorter period," the court determines whether payment "is required by the underlying agreement and applicable nonbankruptcy law." *(f) Mid-case status (new)*: "at any time after the date of the order for relief under Chapter 13 and until the trustee files the notice under (g)(1)," the trustee or debtor may file a motion on **Form 410C13-M1** to determine the status of the claim; the holder must respond on **Form 410C13-M1R** "within 28 days after the motion is served," addressing any disputed facts; the court holds a hearing if facts are disputed, otherwise may enter an order on the motion's facts. *(g) End of case (new)*: "within 45 days after the debtor completes all payments due to the trustee under a Chapter 13 plan" the trustee files **Form 410C13-N** (amount disbursed to cure the default and whether it has been cured; amount disbursed for post-petition payments and whether they are current as of the notice date; the holder's duty to respond), served on the holder, debtor and debtor's attorney; the holder must file **Form 410C13-NR** "within 28 days after its service," "filed as a supplement to the claim holder's proof of claim," stating whether the pre-petition default has been fully cured and whether the debtor is current on all post-petition payments, and itemizing any amounts it contends remain due (other than future regular installments), served on the debtor, debtor's attorney and trustee; the debtor or trustee may then move on **Form 410C13-M2** "within 45 days after service of the response under (g)(3) or after service of the trustee's notice under (g)(1) if no response is filed," the holder responds on **Form 410C13-M2R** within 28 days, and "after notice and a hearing, the court must determine whether the debtor has cured all defaults and paid all required postpetition amounts." *(h) Sanctions*: if the holder "fails to provide any information as required by this rule," the court may "preclude the holder from presenting the omitted information in any form as evidence in a contested matter or adversary proceeding in the case" (unless substantially justified or harmless) and "award other appropriate relief, including reasonable expenses and attorney's fees caused by the failure." The 2025 Committee Note confirms that late payment-increase notices "do not go into effect until the first payment due date after the required notice period (at least 21 days) expires," that decreases may apply retroactively, and that the trustee's notice, response and motion clocks became 45/28/45/28 days.

**Official Forms.** **410S-1 Notice of Mortgage Payment Change (12/25)** — header: "Name of creditor," "Court claim no. (if known)," "Last 4 digits of any number you use to identify the debtor's account," "Date of payment change — Must be at least 21 days after date of this notice," "New total payment: Principal, interest, and escrow, if any"; Part 1 Escrow Account Payment Adjustment ("Will there be a change in the debtor's escrow account payment?", attach the escrow statement and describe the basis, "Current escrow payment"/"New escrow payment"); Part 2 Mortgage Payment Adjustment (interest-rate adjustment; "Attach a copy of the rate change notice"; current/new interest rate and current/new principal-and-interest payment); Part 3 Annual HELOC Notice ("Current HELOC payment," "Reconciliation amount," monthly amounts); Part 4 Other Payment Change (reason; current/new mortgage payment); Part 5 Sign Here — "The person completing this Notice must sign it," "I am the creditor" / "I am the creditor's authorized agent," declaration "under penalty of perjury that the information provided in this claim is true and correct to the best of my knowledge, information, and reasonable belief." **410S-2 Notice of Postpetition Mortgage Fees, Expenses, and Charges (12/16)** — Part 1 line items with "Dates incurred" and "Amount": 1 Late charges, 2 Non-sufficient funds (NSF) fees, 3 Attorney fees, 4 Filing fees and court costs, 5 Bankruptcy/Proof of claim fees, 6 Appraisal/Broker's price opinion fees, 7 Property inspection fees, 8 Tax advances (non-escrow), 9 Insurance advances (non-escrow), 10 Property preservation expenses (specify), 11–14 Other (specify); instruction "Do not include any escrow account disbursements or any amounts previously itemized in a notice filed in this case"; Part 2 declaration (creditor/authorized agent). **410C13-NR (12/25)** — Part 2 Arrearages ("The amount required to cure any prepetition arrearage has been paid in full" / amounts remaining), Part 3 Postpetition Payments ("The debtor is current on all postpetition payments, including all fees, charges, expenses, escrow, and costs" or the due date when the debtor first became delinquent, plus payoff-style data: last payment date received, next payment due date and amount, unpaid principal balance, deferred/accrued interest, escrow balance, unapplied/suspense funds, unpaid fees/charges), Part 4 Itemized payment history (required when disputing cure or asserting post-petition non-payment), Part 5 signature (claim holder / authorized agent, under penalty of perjury). All verified at uscourts.gov 2026-09-09.

**Time computation.** Rule 9006(a): the 21-day period is measured **backward** from the due date — exclude the due date, count back 21 days including weekends/holidays, and if day 21 is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday the deadline moves to the preceding qualifying day ((a)(1)(C), (a)(5)); the 180-day and 28-day periods count forward with the weekend/holiday roll; 3 days are added under 9006(f) only when the triggering paper was served by mail (or other non-electronic consented means) — trustee and counsel service through CM/ECF adds nothing. Rule 3002.1(b)'s "filed and served" means both acts must be complete by the deadline.

**Fannie Mae.** Allowable Bankruptcy Attorney Fees exhibit (11/12/2025): Chapter 13 "Payment Change Notification" **$175** ("allowed for each occurrence of a payment change, even if notices must be given to multiple debtors"); "Notice of Fees, Expenses, and Charges" **$200** (limited to "notices for fees, expenses and charges that aggregate $200 or more"; below $200 the servicer may file at its own cost or seek reimbursement **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — exhibit note as extracted]**); "Response to Motion to Determine Status" $125 (agreed)/$625 (objection); "Response to Trustee's Notice of Disbursements Made" (renamed by SVC-2025-06 from "Response to Final Cure Payment Notice") $125/$625; post-stipulation default $125/$250; plan-payment monitoring and post-discharge audits are servicer-paid. Guide E-2.2-04 requires separate pre-/post-petition accounting and confirmation of plan status with the trustee; E-2.1-04 requires the servicer to supply counsel's requested figures within 3 BD. Reg X §1024.17 escrow rules and Reg Z §1026.20(c)/(d) ARM notices continue to apply in bankruptcy (3.2/7.2/7.3); the platform sends the Reg Z rate-change notice and the escrow statement to the debtor (and counsel) as attachments to the 410S-1.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row.** (1) "21 days before change effective" is only (b)(1); the row omits the 180-day fee notice, the 28-day responses (mid-case and end-of-case), the 1-year fee-challenge window and the sanctions rule. (2) The blueprint pre-dates the Dec. 1, 2025 amendments: the old "notice of final cure → 21-day response" is now the trustee's Form 410C13-N → **28-day** Form 410C13-NR, and there is a new mid-case status procedure. (3) "PACER" is not the filing system — notices are filed in CM/ECF (by counsel or a filer account) as supplements to the POC and served on three parties; PACER is only where the docket is read. (4) Automation class "b" is right for the drafting; the filing act is class "c" (counsel) with a `signing_officer` declaration. (5) The row does not mention that the rule applies only to Chapter 13 principal-residence claims paid through the plan/debtor and ceases on stay relief.

#### State machine
Payment-change notice: `computed` → `package_ready` (form rendered, attachments bundled, checklist pass) → `escalated` (`signing_officer` declaration → `attorney` filing) → `signed` → `filed_served` (NEF + certificate of service + mail proof) → `effective` (on the due date absent a (b)(4) motion) | `objected` → `determined` (court order; payment set accordingly) ; `superseded` (a later notice for the same due date), `withdrawn`. Untimely branch: `filed_served` after the deadline → `effective_date_applied` recomputed per (b)(3) and the 3.2/7.2 effective date pushed (increase) or left (decrease). Fee item: `incurred` → `batched` → `noticed` → `allowed_by_lapse` (day 366 after service without a motion) | `challenged` → `determined_allowed`/`determined_disallowed`; `precluded_not_noticed` if day 180 passes un-noticed (then `waived` unless `officer` approves a late-notice attempt with counsel). Status response: `triggered` → `computed` → `package_ready` → `signed` → `filed_served` → `resolved` (order or lapse).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Scope test.** A notice is required when: chapter = 13 (or Chapter 12 with the local flag); the property is the debtor's principal residence (`bankruptcy_cases.principal_residence`; if unknown, comply); the plan (or, pre-plan, the presumption) provides for the trustee or debtor to pay the claim (treatment ∈ {cure_and_maintain, pay_outside}); no effective stay-relief order (or the court ordered continued compliance / policy 14.2-Q2). Out of scope: surrender plans, Chapter 7/11 cases (informational notices to counsel still go out under 14.3), investment properties (E-2.3-04; payment changes are communicated to counsel/trustee by letter). The decision is recorded per change.
2. **Deadline computation (Rule 9006).** `deadline = effective_due_date − 21 days`, excluding the due date; if the result is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday (federal holidays only for backward periods, (a)(6)), step back to the preceding qualifying day; the deadline time is end of day in the court's time zone. The internal target is `effective_due_date − 35 days` so that counsel review, signature and mail service all complete with margin; `served_at` for the mailed debtor copy is the print-mail manifest date. Worked example (fixture BK-13-A): the post-petition escrow analysis approved 2026-09-25 raises the escrow payment from $645.00 to **$752.50** (base $645.00 + $1,290.00 shortage ÷ 12 = $107.50) effective with the **2026-11-01** installment (a Sunday). Count back: Oct 31 = day 1 … Oct 11 = day 21 (Sunday) → continue backward → **Friday 2026-10-09** is the last day to file and serve; target 2026-09-27 (Sunday → the scheduler uses Friday 2026-09-25); the package (410S-1 with Part 1 completed: current escrow $645.00, new escrow $752.50, new total $2,806.72, escrow statement attached) is signed by the `signing_officer` on 2026-09-28 and filed/served by counsel on **2026-09-30** (CM/ECF to the trustee and debtor's counsel; first-class mail to the debtor the same day) → 32 days' notice ✓ → `effective` on 2026-11-01 absent a (b)(4) motion. Late variant: if the same notice were filed and served on **2026-10-15**, the increase could take effect only on the first due date at least 21 days later — 2026-10-15 + 21 = 2026-11-05 → the **2026-12-01** installment; the November payment stays $2,699.22, the 3.2 effective date is pushed to Dec 1, and the one-month escrow timing loss ($107.50) is left to the next annual analysis (policy — no re-notice; the noticed Dec amount $2,806.72 stands). Decrease variant: an escrow decrease to $2,650.00 noticed late applies on the actual due date (b)(3), and the ledger bills the lower amount from that date.
3. **ARM changes.** The 7.2 engine emits `payment.change.scheduled` when it generates the §1026.20(c) notice (60–120 days before the first payment at the new level: for a 2027-02-01 change, between 2026-10-04 and 2026-12-03); the 410S-1 (Part 2: current/new rate, current/new P&I; the Reg Z notice attached) is filed within 5 BD of the Reg Z notice and in any case by the 9006 deadline — 21 days before 2027-02-01 = **Monday 2027-01-11**. Initial-adjustment notices (7.3, 210–240 days ahead) do not by themselves trigger a filing until the payment is fixed.
4. **Other changes.** PMI termination (10.x), SCRA 6% cap (13.9), modification effective in bankruptcy (12.8 with court approval), buydown step-ups, and escrow re-analyses after a shortage payment all flow through Part 4 (or Parts 1/2 as applicable). Every payment change on the loan while in scope is noticed — increases and decreases alike — including the post-petition escrow analysis run at the petition (3.2 `postpetition_ch13` mode).
5. **Post-petition fees (c).** Each `fee.incurred_postpetition` item is validated against `recoverable_basis` (the note/security instrument and state law; the Fannie Mae exhibit line for attorney fees) and Fannie Mae reimbursement status; items the servicer does not intend to recover from the debtor (policy defaults: no post-petition late charges unless permitted, 14.1-Q6; no fees Fannie Mae has reimbursed unless 15.2 confirms the netting mechanics) are marked `waived` and never noticed. Recoverable items are batched: a 410S-2 is filed when the batch aggregates ≥ $200 (the Fannie Mae $200 reimbursement threshold) or when the oldest item reaches day 90, and always before the oldest item's day 180. Worked example: counsel's POC & plan review ($1,225) and 410A history ($325) rendered 2026-10-20; inspections $20 on 2026-11-05 and 2026-12-05 → aggregate $1,590 ≥ $200 → the agent files on 2027-01-15 (batch day 87), well before the first item's deadline of 2026-10-20 + 180 = 2027-04-18 (Sunday) → **Monday 2027-04-19**; the 410S-2 lists line 5 "Bankruptcy/Proof of claim fees" $1,550.00 with dates 10/20/2026 and line 7 "Property inspection fees" $40.00 with two dates; the items move to `noticed` with a challenge deadline one year after service: 2028-01-15 is a Saturday, Sunday 2028-01-16 and Monday 2028-01-17 (Martin Luther King Jr. Day, the third Monday of January) are excluded under Rule 9006(a)(1)(C)/(a)(6), so the engine records **Tuesday 2028-01-18** (a period "stated in days or a longer unit of time" follows the same roll). Items not noticed by day 180 become `precluded_not_noticed` and are written off (Dr fee expense) unless `officer` approves a late notice attempt with counsel's advice.
6. **Mid-case status (f).** On a served Form 410C13-M1 the engine computes the response from `bankruptcy_ledger_views` as of the motion's stated date: pre-petition arrearage paid vs. claimed (trustee vouchers), post-petition installments paid/unpaid, noticed fees paid/unpaid; if the motion's facts match (tolerance $0.00 — any variance is a disagreement), the response agrees ($125 fee); otherwise it itemizes the disagreement with the payment history attached ($625 fee). Response due 28 days after service (+3 if mail): e.g., motion served by CM/ECF on **Friday 2028-03-03** → due **Friday 2028-03-31**.
7. **End of case (g).** On `bankruptcy.plan.completed` the post-petition and arrearage views are frozen daily for the expected trustee notice (within 45 days). On the trustee's Form 410C13-N (e.g., served **Friday 2031-06-20** electronically → response due **Friday 2031-07-18**; by mail → **Monday 2031-07-21**): the engine tests (a) `bk_prepetition_arrearage` balance = 0 → "paid in full" else itemize the remainder; (b) every post-petition installment due on or before the notice date paid in full and all noticed (c) fees paid → "current" else the first unpaid due date and an itemization (installments by due date and amount, unpaid noticed fees, escrow shortfalls); (c) Part 3 data — last payment received date, next due date/amount (e.g., 2031-07-01, $2,054.22 + then-current escrow), UPB (fixture: $288,625.18 after the 2031-06-01 installment, payment #95), deferred/accrued interest $0.00, escrow balance, unapplied funds, unpaid fees; (d) Part 4 itemized history whenever the response disagrees. The response is filed as a POC supplement and served on the debtor, debtor's attorney and trustee. Consequences: an "agree/current" response ends the bankruptcy accounting — any later claim of a post-petition default from the plan period is precluded; a "not current" response starts the (g)(4) 45-day motion window and the platform keeps the itemized amounts as the only collectible post-case arrears (collected contractually after the case closes, 13.x/16.1).
8. **Service.** Debtor: first-class mail to the address of record (or the §342(c)(2) address the debtor used) plus, where the debtor consented, e-mail; debtor's attorney and trustee: CM/ECF (NEF is the service proof) or mail where not registered; certificate of service generated from the service list and attached to the filing. Every service act is a `notice_deliveries` row (7.1) with proof.
9. **Sanction-proof evidence bundle** per filing: the computation log (inputs, ledger snapshot hash, escrow analysis/ARM evidence), the 9006 deadline computation (calendar version, holidays applied), the signed form (hash), the NEF/docket entry, the certificate of service, the mail manifest, and the decision record — retained `court_record_7y` and exportable for any (h) motion.
10. **Fannie Mae fee handling.** Counsel's invoice for each filing is matched to the exhibit lines ($175 per payment-change occurrence; $200 per qualifying fee notice; $125/$625 responses) and to the case milestone for the 15.2 claim; a payment-change notice for a change that Fannie Mae's exhibit does not cover (e.g., a decrease-only notice) is still filed — the rule, not the fee schedule, governs.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `bankruptcy-ops` (3002.1 module). It detects in-scope changes, computes deadlines (deterministic `timers` service), renders and checks the forms from ledger/analysis data, assembles attachments, produces the service list, escalates for signature and filing, tracks NEFs and service proofs, batches fee items, and computes (f)/(g) responses. Tools: `bk.change.detect`, `bk.notice.render`, `bk.fee_items.read/batch`, `bk.status_response.compute`, `timer.compute_frbp`, `documents.render`, `attorney.send_package`, `notice.send` (debtor service copy), `escalation.file`.
- **Decision record:** `{case_id, filing_type, trigger_event_id, computation_hash, deadline_calc {anchor, offset, calendar_version, holidays_applied, result}, scope_test_result, fee_items[], service_list, rule_set_version (frbp.2025-12; fnma.bk_fees.2025-11-12), model_version, rationale, outcome, reviewer_id?}`.
- **Guardrails:** no notice is filed with figures that do not tie to the ledger/analysis (checklist `block`); the agent cannot waive a filing for a change it detected — only a scope-test failure with a decision record can skip it; a late filing is never back-dated; the (g) response cannot state "current" while any post-petition installment or noticed fee is unpaid; fee items that Fannie Mae reimbursed are not noticed for recovery without the 15.2 netting rule confirmed.
- **Escalations:** `signing_officer` (declarations on 410S-1/410S-2/410C13-*), `attorney` (filing and service through CM/ECF; any (b)(4)/(e)/(f)/(g) contested matter; local-rule questions), `officer` (late-notice attempts; write-offs above $1,000 per case), `human_agent` (debtor/counsel questions about a notice), `fnma_portal_operator` (none).
- **AI-off path:** ops-console 3002.1 worklist with the same calculators; staff perform batching and package assembly.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Change detected fewer than 21 days before the due date** (e.g., a late-discovered escrow item): file immediately; the increase takes effect per (b)(3) on the first due date ≥ 21 days after filing/service; the intervening installment is billed at the old amount; the ledger tracks the shortfall for the next analysis.
- **Multiple changes for the same due date** (ARM + escrow): one 410S-1 with Parts 1 and 2 both completed; a later change to the same due date supersedes the earlier notice (new filing; earlier marked `superseded`).
- **Debtor's counsel withdraws / pro se debtor:** service on the debtor by mail only; `contact_route` updated.
- **Trustee changes or conduit switch mid-case:** service list refreshed from the docket; payment instructions letter re-issued.
- **Relief from stay granted:** rule ceases unless the court orders otherwise; policy keeps filing while the case is open (14.2-Q2) — harmless and avoids disputes if the relief order is later vacated.
- **Case dismissed after a notice is filed but before the effective date:** the change takes effect contractually; no further filings.
- **Fee later reimbursed by Fannie Mae after being noticed and collected from the debtor:** 15.2 netting; if double-recovered, refund to the debtor/trustee within 5 BD.
- **(b)(4) motion or (e) motion filed:** the amount is held at the prior level (increase) pending the order; counsel responds; the order's figures are applied on entry.
- **Trustee's (g) notice never arrives** after plan completion: watch timer; counsel asks the trustee; the accounting stays frozen; no post-case collection of plan-period amounts until resolved.
- **Transfer-out mid-window:** the transferee inherits open deadlines (17.x hand-off lists each filing's deadline and status).
- **Service failure** (returned debtor mail): re-mail to the §342 address; the NEF to counsel stands; a service defect is recorded for the (h) record.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **14.2-Q1 Comply before a plan is filed?** Default: yes — treat every Chapter 13 principal-residence loan as in scope from the petition date (the rule's scope depends on the plan, but the safest reading is to notice from day one).
2. **14.2-Q2 Continue after stay relief?** Default: continue filing (b)/(c) notices while the case is open unless counsel advises the district treats it as improper.
3. **14.2-Q3 Fee-notice batching.** Default: ≥ $200 aggregate or day 90, whichever first; alternative: notice each item within 30 days.
4. **14.2-Q4 Late-increase timing loss.** Default: absorb the one-month escrow timing difference into the next analysis (no re-notice); alternative: re-analyze and re-notice.
5. **14.2-Q5 Filing channel.** Default: counsel files via CM/ECF; alternative: a vendor filer (BankruptcyWatch-type) for 410S-1/410S-2 with counsel oversight — requires counsel's agreement and Fannie Mae fee alignment.
