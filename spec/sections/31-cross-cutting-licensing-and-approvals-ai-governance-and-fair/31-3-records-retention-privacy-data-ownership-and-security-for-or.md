### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-11)

**A. Federal retention rules (rule text).** Reuse of 00a-fed §15 (eCFR pages "current as of" 9/03–9/08/2026), with the anchors that drive the engine:
- *Reg Z §1026.25(c)(1)(i)* — LE and §1026.19(e) evidence: "for three years after the later of the date of consummation, the date disclosures are required to be made, or the date the action is required to be taken" — VERIFIED. *(c)(1)(ii)* — Closing Disclosure: "for five years after consummation"; on sale of the loan "without" retaining servicing, the creditor provides a copy to the owner/servicer, who "shall retain such disclosures for the remainder of the five-year period" — VERIFIED (here the partner retains servicing, so the partner keeps the CD and SM holds it for both). *(c)(2)* — loan originator compensation: "for three years after the date of receipt or payment" — VERIFIED. *(c)(3)* — ATR: "for three years after consummation" — VERIFIED. *(a)* — general Reg Z evidence: "for two years after the date disclosures are required to be made or action is required to be taken" — VERIFIED (19.1's `REGZ_1026_25A_RETENTION_2Y`).
- *Reg B §1002.12(b)(1)* — "For 25 months (12 months for business credit...)" after the date the creditor notifies the applicant of action taken or of incompleteness: the application, information on applicant characteristics (§1002.13 monitoring data), the notification of action taken, the statement of specific reasons, and any written allegation of violation; (b)(4) extended while under investigation/enforcement; (b)(6) self-tests 25 months; (b)(7) prescreened solicitations 25 months — VERIFIED (CFPB eRegs).
- *Reg C §1003.5(a)(1)(ii)-related sentence* — the full LAR retained "for at least three years" — VERIFIED (anchor: the March 1 submission — the section's exact anchor sentence is **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — read as displayed in 00a-fed §4.3]**); modified LAR/disclosure statement availability rules (§1003.5(c)–(e)) are 28.3's.
- *Reg X §1024.15(d)* — AfBA disclosures "retained for 5 years after the date of execution" — VERIFIED; *§1024.14(h)* — Section 8 records five years — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (CFPB eRegs display). *§1024.38(c)(1)* — servicing records "until one year after the date a mortgage loan is discharged or servicing of a mortgage loan is transferred" — VERIFIED (19.1 owns). *§1024.17* — **no retention period** (00a-fed §6.8, §17 item 5).
- *FDPA* — the SFHDF retention sentence is in the banking agencies' regulations, e.g., 12 CFR 22.6(b) (eCFR up to date as of 8/04/2026): the bank "shall retain a copy of the completed standard flood hazard determination form, in either hard copy or electronic form, for the period of time the bank or savings association owns the loan" — VERIFIED (text) but it binds *regulated lending institutions*, not the partner; it reaches this platform through 42 U.S.C. 4012a(b)(3) (Fannie Mae's procedures) and Selling Guide B7-3-06/A2-4.1-01 ("flood insurance policy (if required)" in the loan file) — the design keeps the SFHDF for the life of the loan under the Fannie Mae class (`fdpa_life_of_loan` folds into `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`).
- *BSA 31 CFR 1029.320(c)* — SAR and supporting documentation "for a period of five years from the date of filing the SAR" — VERIFIED. *OFAC 31 CFR 501.601* — "for at least 10 years after the date of such transaction"; blocked property "for the period of time that such property is blocked and for at least 10 years after the date such property is unblocked" — VERIFIED (re-read 2026-09-11).
- *TCPA* — no rule period; the federal catch-all limitations period, 28 U.S.C. 1658(a) (uscode.house.gov, current through Sept 7, 2026): "a civil action arising under an Act of Congress enacted after the date of the enactment of this section may not be commenced later than 4 years after the cause of action accrues." — VERIFIED (basis for servicing 7.4's `tcpa_consent_4y`, which origination reuses for TCPA consents captured by 20.2/20.3).
- *E-SIGN 15 U.S.C. 7001(d)–(e)* — no period; an electronic record satisfies a retention requirement if it "accurately reflect[s] the information" and "remains accessible to all persons who are entitled to access … in a form that is capable of being accurately reproduced for later reference" — VERIFIED (19.1); consent evidence is kept for the life of the relationship (`esign_consent_life`).
- *Colorado SB 26-189, C.R.S. 6-1-1703* — records "not less than three years after the date of a consequential decision" (21.6; class `co_admt_3y`) — VERIFIED.

**B. Fannie Mae ownership and retention.** *Selling Guide A2-4.1-02, Ownership and Retention of Loan Files and Records (last updated 12/19/2017)* — VERIFIED (re-read 2026-09-11): "All records related to loans … sold to or serviced for Fannie Mae are Fannie Mae's property"; the seller/servicer "must hold these documents solely for the benefit of Fannie Mae"; "The servicer must maintain permanent mortgage account records for each loan it services"; **"After a loan is liquidated, the servicer must keep the individual loan records for at least four years"**; for repurchases/make-whole, "the responsible party must keep the individual loan records for at least four years from loan" "liquidation unless applicable law requires longer retention"; "the servicer may destroy any accounting reports 18 months after such reports are filed"; "For eMortgages, the seller/servicer must follow the record retention requirements … and the" "requirements for storing mortgage loan files and records as described in A2-4.1-03". **The topic states no distinct retention period for a seller's origination records** (VERIFIED-negative) — the baseline class `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` is therefore justified by the ownership sentence plus the servicer rule (the partner services; SM subservices), not by a seller-specific sentence; its wording is now settled: *permanent while the loan is active; at least four years after liquidation; longer where applicable law requires*. *A2-4.1-01, Establishing Loan Files (09/04/2024)* — VERIFIED: "The seller must establish the individual mortgage loan file 'loan file' when it originates a loan and clearly identifies each file with Fannie Mae's loan number"; the file must contain "all documents, records and reports used to support the underwriting decision required by the Lender Contract", "underwriting documents, including any DU reports", "property appraisal and inspection orders and reports", "title policy or an attorney title opinion letter, property insurance policy, flood insurance policy (if required)", "the note and any related addenda", "the recorded mortgage or deed of trust, any applicable recorded rider or recorded modification", the conventional "mortgage insurance certificate, if applicable", "third-party asset verification report, in human-readable format", "final settlement statement evidencing all settlement costs paid by the borrower and seller (if applicable)", "the final version of the Closing Disclosure", "all required intervening assignments". *A2-4.1-03 (05/06/2026)* — electronic records acceptable "provided they are capable of reproduction in paper format"; systems "sufficiently secure to preserve the integrity and authenticity of the records"; paper-to-electronic conversion process documented; e-signature evidence of "the authenticated identity of the signer", attribution and date; paper originals required for "the promissory note and any related addenda (unless the seller has received special approval to deliver electronic notes)" (19.1 — VERIFIED). *B8-8-02 (Dec 13, 2023)* — eNote signing records (signer name, signature date, signing method, attribution evidence) kept "for the life of the loan plus seven years" (00b-orig F9 — VERIFIED). *eClosings page* — "Sellers and servicers no longer need to maintain long-term access to video recordings from remote online notarizations (RON)" (VERIFIED, 31.1 fetch). *MERS*: eRegistry "monthly Member Profile reconciliation" and QA program with Controller/Servicing Agent reconciliation (00b-orig F9, MERS eRegistry Procedures Rel. 16.25); MERS System Procedures Rel. 26.1 — members must keep transaction evidence supporting registrations/transfers **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — retention sentence not read]**.

**E. **Discrepancies vs blueprint.**
1. "Reg X §1024.17 (escrow 5 years)" does not exist in the current rule; the Reg X servicing-record floor is §1024.38(c)(1) (1 year post-discharge/transfer), built in 19.1; escrow records of a funded loan are Fannie Mae records (life + 4) anyway.
2. A2-4.1-02 contains **no seller-specific origination-file period**; the class `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` rests on the ownership sentence and the servicer's "at least four years" after liquidation (longer where law requires) — the baseline's "verify wording" note is resolved.
3. FDPA "SFHDF life of loan" is an agency rule for regulated lending institutions (12 CFR 22.6(b): "for the period of time the bank … owns the loan"); it reaches the partner through Fannie Mae's file requirements — retained for the life of the loan under the Fannie Mae class, not as a stand-alone statutory duty of the partner.
4. "State RON retention (5–10 years)" is right for FL (10), AZ (5) and OH (10, journal) — but the duty sits on the notary/RON provider, and Fannie Mae dropped its own 10-year video-access requirement; the seller's obligation is contractual access, not custody.
5. The row omits OFAC's 10-year period, BSA's 5-year SAR period, the TCPA 4-year basis, the Colorado 3-year ADMT records, the eNote signing-record "life of the loan plus seven years", the Reg B 25-month anchor on the *notification* date (not the application date), and the §1026.25(c)(1)(ii) rule that the CD follows the loan to a purchaser only when servicing is not retained.
6. The row's "GLBA §1016 notices" is 21.3/25.4's territory; what belongs here is the §1016.13 service-provider contract condition that lets SM and every vendor receive NPI without opt-out.
7. "MISMO canonical model" is not a legal requirement; it is the 19.1/17.3 export layout (MISMO v3.6-aligned) reused for origination productions — noted, not a rule.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- Every write to `documents`, `disclosures`, `notices`, `consents`, `agent_decisions`, `verifications`, `credit_reports`, `du_submissions`, `appraisals`, `signing_sessions`, `closing_documents`, `enotes`, `hmda_records`, `sars`, `adverse_actions`, `applicant_demographics` → `record_objects` enrolment (19.1) with an origination `record_type`.
- Anchor events (origination): `application.received` (Reg B; prescreen/self-test clocks), `disclosure.le.delivered`/`disclosure.le.required_at` (Reg Z (c)(1)(i)), `notice.adverse_action.sent` / `noia.sent` / `application.withdrawn` / `decision.issued{kind=approved_not_accepted}` (all 21.6's names) → `regb_action_notified_at`, `closing.consummated` (Reg Z (c)(1)(ii), (c)(3); RON), `lo_comp.paid` (Reg Z (c)(2)), `afba.executed` (Reg X), `hmda.lar.accepted` (Reg C; 28.3's `signed_at` submission), `party.screened{role, result}` (OFAC — 22.6's screening event) and `ofac.property.blocked`/`ofac.transaction.rejected` (28.4), `sar.filed` (BSA), `decision.issued{CO consumer}` (Colorado), `enote.signed` (B8-8-02), `loan.funded` (→ `loans` row; Fannie Mae class begins), `loan.purchased`, and the servicing anchors (`loan.liquidated`, `servicing.transferred_out`) from 19.1.
- `legal_hold.placed/released` (19.1) — origination auto-holds: complaint alleging discrimination or UDAAP, regulator inquiry, Colorado AG notice, litigation, QC self-report to Fannie Mae (28.4), any `security.incident.opened` touching the application.
- Vendor events: `vendor.order.requested{vendor_class}` (22.x, 24.x, 26.x) → flow-down gate; `vendor.data.returned`; `vendor.incident.reported` (19.3).
- Access events: every read of `applicant_demographics`, `credit_reports.raw`, `documents{pii_flags ∋ ssn|tax_data|id_number}` with purpose code → `pii_access_log`.
- Schedules: 19.1's `retention-sweep` (daily), `disposal-run` (monthly, first Sunday 02:00 ET), `legal-hold-review` (180 days); origination additions: `denied_withdrawn_purge_sweep` (monthly), `pii_access_review` (quarterly), `vendor_manifest_reconciliation` (weekly), `fannie_data_use_scan` (weekly: no Fannie Mae Data or credit data in analytics/marketing/model-training datasets).

#### State machine

Record object: 19.1's `active → retention_running → eligible → disposed` with the `held` overlay, plus an origination pre-state `pre_funding` (file open; `fnma_property=false`) that becomes `active{fnma_property=true}` on `loan.funded`, or moves to `unfunded_running` on `regb_action_notified_at` (denial/NOIA/withdrawal/approved-not-accepted) where the effective class is `orig_unfunded_policy_25m`. Guards: no origination object of a funded loan may reach `eligible` while the loan is active (permanent while active); an unfunded file's objects become `eligible` only when every applicable class (Reg B, HMDA, OFAC, Colorado, TCPA) has expired and `hold_count = 0`; disposal requires the 19.1 `officer` attestation.
Vendor order: `requested → flowdown_checked (gate) → placed → data_returned → reconciled (manifest) → closed`; `flowdown_checked` fails closed.
Incident: 19.2's machine unchanged; origination adds scoping steps (applications by state from `application_borrowers.mailing_state`; vendor manifests; RON sessions; eVault objects).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Effective retention** = max over applicable classes (19.1 rule 1), then jurisdiction overrides, then holds. For a **funded** loan every origination object is Fannie Mae property → `permanent_while_active` dominates; the federal clocks are recorded (for audit) but never shorten anything. For an **unfunded** file the effective date is `orig_unfunded_policy_25m` = max(Reg B, HMDA, OFAC, Colorado, TCPA where applicable).
2. **Worked example 1 — refinance fixture (funded).** TRID application Mon Oct 5, 2026 (LE required by Thu Oct 8, 2026 — 3 `business_days_creditor`); AfBA executed Oct 5; OFAC screening Oct 5; intent Oct 6; lock Oct 7; CD delivered Mon Nov 2; consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 (RON, eNote, Phoenix AZ); disbursement Thu Nov 12; MLO compensation paid Tue Dec 15, 2026; LAR for 2026 submitted Mon Feb 15, 2027; purchased Nov 19, 2026; boarded at funding. Clocks: `regz_le_3y` → later of Nov 6, 2026 / Oct 8, 2026 → **Nov 6, 2029**; `regz_cd_5y` → **Nov 6, 2031**; `regz_atr_3y` → **Nov 6, 2029**; `regz_locomp_3y` → **Dec 15, 2029**; `regb_25m` (approval notified at consummation; use Nov 6, 2026) → **Dec 6, 2028**; `respa_afba_5y` → **Oct 5, 2031**; `hmda_3y` → **Feb 15, 2030**; `ofac_10y` → **Oct 5, 2036**; RON recording (AZ, provider-held) → **Nov 6, 2031**; eNote signing records → liquidation + 7 years; `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y` → permanent while active, then liquidation + 4 years; `esign_consent_life` → relationship end. Effective: **the Fannie Mae class** — e.g., payoff Mon Mar 15, 2032 → eligible **Sat Mar 15, 2036** for the loan file (every federal clock has already expired), the OFAC screening record to Oct 5, 2036 (later, so the OFAC record's own class holds it), eNote signing records to Mar 15, 2039; first disposal run for the file: Sunday **Apr 6, 2036** (the March run on Sunday Mar 2 precedes eligibility; runs are the first Sunday of each month, 19.1). Retention arithmetic: same month/day N years later; Feb 29 anchors roll to Mar 1 (19.1).
3. **Worked example 2 — denied application (unfunded).** Reg B adverse-action notice sent **Wed Nov 4, 2026** (denial decided within the 30-day window; 21.6) → `regb_action_notified_at` = 2026-11-04 → `REGB_1002_12B_APPLICATION_RETENTION_25M` = **Mon Dec 4, 2028** (Nov 4, 2026 + 25 calendar months). Other clocks on the file: HMDA (LAR row for 2026, submitted Feb 15, 2027) → Feb 15, 2030 — but only the LAR *fields* (in the HMDA store) carry `hmda_3y`; the file's documents do not; OFAC screening record (Oct 19, 2026 application) → Oct 19, 2036 (the screening log row only); Colorado n/a (Ohio property); TCPA consent (if any) 4 years after revocation/last reliance. Effective for the application file (1003, credit report, verifications, notices, reasons, decision record): **Dec 4, 2028**; first disposal run after that: the first Sunday of December 2028 is Dec 3 (before eligibility), so the file is included in the run of **Sunday Jan 7, 2029**; the LAR row and OFAC log survive under their own classes. If a written allegation of an ECOA violation is received on Mon Jun 5, 2028, the file is held under (b)(4) until `investigation.closed`. Credit-report data is never re-used after the Reg B date (FCRA §604(f)); the `credit_reports` row is crypto-shredded in the same run.
4. **Worked example 3 — purchase fixture (Columbus, OH; closing Wed Nov 18, 2026; wet-funded same day):** `regz_cd_5y` → Nov 18, 2031; `regz_le_3y` → Nov 18, 2029 (LE required by Oct 22, 2026); OH RON journal (if RON) → 10 years (Nov 18, 2036, provider-held); Fannie Mae class dominates; HomeReady education certificate and Form 1103 are in the loan file (A2-4.1-01 "all documents … used to support the underwriting decision") → life + 4.
5. **Unfunded-file purge.** Monthly sweep selects `record_objects{funded=false}` whose every class has expired and `hold_count = 0`; the run is attested by an `officer` (19.1); the `applications` row is tombstoned (id, dates, action taken, HMDA-derived fields retained per `hmda_3y`; PII columns crypto-shredded); `agent_decisions` keep hashes and versions, not content (Colorado 3-year class applies to CO decisions; LL-2026-04 evidence for the monitoring program is retained in aggregate under `ai_governance_7y`, not per-applicant).
6. **Ownership and productions.** Fannie Mae requests → 19.1's `records_requests` flow (`FNMA_A2_4_1_02_RECORDS_DELIVERY_REQUESTED`, default 10 `business_days_fannie_et` when unspecified) with the origination file compiled from the A2-4.1-01 list; the partner receives full files on request (its own records); a transferee servicer (17.3) receives origination documents in the Fannie Mae file; borrowers' requests for their own application records are answered under Reg B §1002.14 (valuations — 24.2), TRID copies (25.2), and state law — never through the servicing-file route unless the loan is boarded (4.2).
7. **Data-use limits (executable).** `credit_reports.*`, `verifications.raw`, `du_submissions.findings`, `appraisals` (UCDP/CU outputs), `earlycheck_runs`, `purchase_advices` carry `data_class ∈ {fcra_consumer_report, fnma_data}`; the analytics/marketing/model-training pipelines refuse any dataset containing those classes (CI check + weekly scan); the 20.1 refi trigger uses servicing data under the partner's privacy notice and FCRA account-review prong (20.1), never origination credit data of *other* partners' applicants; no cross-partner data joins exist (partner-scoped tenancy).
8. **`applicant_demographics` protection.** Application-layer encryption under a separate KEK (19.2 rule 4); the only writers are 21.1's intake path; the only readers are 28.3 (LAR build), 30.2 (19.4 boarding export), 31.2's enclave jobs and `security-records` custody tools; every read logged with a purpose code; a CI check fails any build referencing the table outside those packages; SIEM rule on unexpected principals (19.4-T5 pattern).
9. **Vendor flow-downs by class** (executed before the first order): AMC/appraiser — AIR/§1026.42 independence, no borrower NPI beyond what the order requires, UCDP lender-agent terms, `FNMA_DATA_NO_REDISCLOSURE`; credit reseller — FCRA end-user certification (§1681e), permissible-purpose certification per pull, `GLBA_1016_13_USE_LIMIT`, US-IP allow-list; income/asset/verification vendors — FCRA (where consumer reports), borrower-authorization scope, no retention beyond the transaction + audit log (IVES: IRS audit-log elements), `NO_TRAINING_ON_DATA`; identity/fraud/OFAC vendors — GLBA, biometric-data state laws (**[UNVERIFIED — BIPA-class matrix]**), retention limits; flood — GLBA; title/settlement — ALTA Best Practices pillar 3 (privacy/security), wire-fraud controls (24.4), `GLBA_1016_13_USE_LIMIT`; eClosing/RON — state RON retention and access right, MISMO RON audit trail, `RON_RECORDING_ACCESS_RIGHT`, breach notice ≤ 24 h; eVault — MERS eRegistry participant terms, SMART Doc integrity, transfer-of-control cooperation, return/export on termination; eRecording — PRIA, GLBA; MI — master-policy data terms, NPI limited to the certificate; print/mail and e-delivery — GLBA, proof retention (19.1); telephony/voice AI — GLBA, call-recording consent states, retention per `tcpa_consent_4y`/loan file; model provider — 19.3 AI-provider set. 10. **Incident runbook (origination alignment with 19.2).** Same tables, severities and clocks; origination-specific scoping: affected applications by applicant mailing state; vendor manifests (`agent_runs.inputs_manifest`, order payloads) to count consumers; RON sessions and eVault objects as assets; "customer information" for the FTC count includes applicants who never became customers (16 CFR 314.1(b) covers information "of other financial institutions" and consumers — the count uses distinct individuals whose unencrypted information was acquired). **Worked example:** an AMC's SFTP account is used by an unknown party on **Mon Nov 16, 2026 10:00 ET** to download appraisal PDFs (borrower names, addresses, no SSNs; files unencrypted at rest on the vendor side) for 620 applicants in 9 states (AZ 210, OH 180, TX 95, NY 60, others 75). `identified_at` = Nov 16 10:00 ET (vendor notice received 09:40; SOC confirms 10:00) → Fannie Mae Supplement and Form 101 notices due **Tue Nov 17, 2026 22:00 ET** (sent by the `officer` Nov 16 16:30 from the agent's draft); partner notice due Nov 17 10:00 ET (sent 11:15 Nov 16); FTC: unencrypted customer information of ≥ 500 consumers presumed acquired → `FTC_314_4J_NOTIFICATION_EVENT_30D` due **Wed Dec 16, 2026** (internal target Dec 14); NYDFS: 60 NY residents and an FTC notice required → `determined_at` Wed Nov 18 09:00 ET (inside `SM_NYDFS_DETERMINATION_48H`) → `NYDFS_500_17A_INCIDENT_NOTICE_72H` due **Sat Nov 21, 2026 09:00 ET** (Nov 20 target; the platform routes SM's NY-registration obligation and the partner's, if any, separately); NY consumer/AG notices within 30 days (Dec 16); Arizona/Ohio/Texas consumer notices per `jurisdiction_rules.breach_notice` (**[UNVERIFIED matrix — computation refused until loaded; `attorney` escalation within 1 hour]**); legal holds placed on all 620 applications; the AMC's 19.3 `vendor_incidents` row, reassessment and possible termination; lessons learned within 30 days of recovery. Veterans Day (Wed Nov 11) and Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26) do not extend any of these clocks (hour- and calendar-day-based).
11. **NYDFS scope.** The origination stack is within SM's Part 500 program (asset inventory, MFA, 72-hour notice, April 15 certification) because SM is a NY-registered servicer regardless of whether origination itself is NY-licensed; the partner's own Part 500 duties as a NY lender are the partner's, supported by the pack from 19.2.
12. **Technology Guide controls on origination rails.** No scraping/RPA of DU/UCDP/UCD/EarlyCheck/Loan Delivery/PE-WL UIs (adapters use DI/APIs; `fnma_portal_operator` humans use UIs); Fannie Mae credentials are issued only to SM employees and rotated 90/365 days (19.2 control tests); Fannie Mae Data is returned or destroyed with officer certification on termination (19.3 `FNMA_TECHGUIDE_TERMINATION_RETURN_DESTROY_30D`).

- **19.2 SIEM/IdP/KMS**: `pii_access_log` shipped to the SIEM; anomaly rules; KEK separation for `applicant_demographics`.
- **19.3 vendor registry**: `vendor_flowdown_requirements` evaluated at order time (`assertGateOpen(SM_O123_VENDOR_FLOWDOWN_GATE)` inside the adapter layer — every `integrations/*` adapter calls it).

- **Fannie Mae**: records productions (19.1: Box/File Transfer Portal — portal-only → `fnma_portal_operator`); incident notices by email (19.2 `NTC_FNMA_INCIDENT_36H`); Form 101 notices (19.3).
- **FTC / NYDFS / state AGs**: 19.2 `officer` tasks; consumer notices via print/mail with state variants.
- **Partner**: file productions on request; incident notices; quarterly `pii_access_review` and retention-exception reports.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
- **Agent:** `security-records` (records and security persona; shared with 19.x) with `compliance-sentinel` for clocks and rule watch. Tools: `records.classify`, `records.anchor`, `records.compileOriginationFile(application_id)` (A2-4.1-01 checklist), `records.export`, `holds.place` (allowed; release is human), `disposal.plan`, `vendors.checkFlowdown(vendor_id, class)`, `pii.monitor`, `scan.fannieDataUse`, `incident.scope{origination}`, `notices.draft`, `portal_task.create`, `timers.read`.
- **End-to-end behavior:** classifies every origination artifact at write time and anchors it as events arrive; keeps unfunded files on the Reg B/HMDA/OFAC/Colorado clocks and funded files on the Fannie Mae class; blocks vendor orders lacking flow-downs; monitors restricted-table access and Fannie Mae/credit data misuse; compiles productions (Fannie Mae, partner, regulator, court); scopes origination incidents and drafts every notice with the 19.2 checklists; runs the quarterly access review and RON/eVault access tests; drafts the annual schedule review with the `compliance-sentinel` rule diff (Reg Z/B/C/X, Fannie Mae A2-4.1, state RON, breach matrix).
- **Decision record:** `{subject (record | request | order | incident), classes_applied[], anchors{...}, effective_date, gates_checked[], holds, vendor_clause_scores[], scoping{consumers, states, encryption_status}, notices_required[], due_dates[], rule_set_version (retention schedule vN; breach matrix vN), model_version, prompt_version, confidence, escalation?}`.
- **Guardrails:** never deletes (disposal only through the attested run); never releases a hold; never opens a vendor gate on inference; never sends a regulatory or Fannie Mae notice (drafts only); never reads `applicant_demographics` content into a prompt (the custody tools operate on ids and hashes); never re-purposes credit or Fannie Mae data.
- **Escalations:** `officer` — disposal attestations, incident notices/attestations, Fannie Mae/regulator productions, vendor gate deviations; `attorney` — subpoenas, litigation holds, privilege, breach-matrix gaps, law-enforcement delays; `fnma_portal_operator` — Fannie Mae file-transfer uploads; `signing_officer` — records certifications.
- **Human path (AI off):** the 19.1/19.2 ops-console workspaces with the origination record types and gates.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Application funds after a denial was reversed on appeal**: `regb_action_notified_at` is preserved for the original notice; the file becomes `funded` and the Fannie Mae class applies to everything, including the earlier notice and reasons (they are "records related to" the loan).
- **Withdrawn before any notice**: Reg B anchor = the date the creditor records the withdrawal/notifies the applicant (§1002.9(e)/(c) practice — 21.6); the file follows `orig_unfunded_policy_25m`.
- **Same borrower re-applies within the Reg B window**: records are per application; nothing is merged; the earlier file purges on its own clock.
- **Colorado applicant on an unfunded file**: `co_admt_3y` (decision + 3 years) can outlast Reg B (25 months) — effective date = the later.
- **RON provider fails the access test or exits the market**: 19.3 reassessment; recordings/journals are transferred to a secure repository per the state statute (FL §117.245 delegation; AZ depository rules); the platform keeps its session audit trail regardless.
- **eNote converted to paper** (MERS Change Status "Converted To Paper"): signing records retained on the same class; paper note custody per 26.4.
- **Fannie Mae directs destruction/return on Lender Contract termination**: 19.3/19.1 path; the partner's own Reg Z/B/C copies must still be retained by the partner (the creditor's legal duties do not end) — the production to the partner precedes destruction.
- **Vendor retains data beyond the transaction** (e.g., a verification vendor's consumer-permissioned data cache): contract clause requires deletion on request; the platform sends a deletion demand at the Reg B date for unfunded files and logs the certificate.
- **Breach at the partner affecting SM-held origination data**: Form 101 duty still applies to SM; single coordinated Fannie Mae notice by agreement; both liable (19.2).
- **CCPA/state privacy requests from applicants**: GLBA-covered data exempt from CCPA deletion (Civ. Code §1798.145(e), 19.2); respond with the GLBA basis; Colorado data-correction requests are handled by 21.6 (not deletion).
- **Encryption-key compromise**: all data under the KEK treated as unencrypted for the FTC/state counts (19.2 rule); emergency rotation.
- **Legal hold on a file eligible for purge**: held until release (`officer` + `attorney`).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **HMDA 3-year anchor** — default: the March 1 submission date of the LAR year (conservative reading of §1003.5); confirm the section's anchor sentence.
2. **Unfunded-file purge at the Reg B date** vs a longer policy (e.g., 3 years) for defensive purposes — default: purge at the effective (max) date; longer retention of PII of non-customers increases FTC/state exposure and the 16 CFR 314.4(c)(6) two-year disposal expectation for non-loan customer information applies.
3. **RON recordings — obtain a copy into SM's WORM store or rely on the provider's statutory custody?** Default: rely on provider custody with a contractual access right and quarterly access tests; take a copy only where a state's statute makes the *notary* the custodian and the provider cannot warrant retention.
4. **State origination-record rules matrix** (NY, TX, others) — default: unknown states default to the longest known class; counsel populates within 90 days of go-live.
5. **Breach-notice matrix for all origination states** — default: fail-closed to `attorney` (19.2) until loaded; load AZ, OH, TX, CA, FL, NY first.
6. **Biometric/voice data** (RON identity proofing, voice AI) — default: no biometric identifiers retained by SM; vendors retain under their own statutory duties with flow-downs; BIPA-class matrix to counsel.
