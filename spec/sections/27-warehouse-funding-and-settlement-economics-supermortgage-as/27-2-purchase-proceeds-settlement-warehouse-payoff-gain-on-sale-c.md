### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**1. Purchase price, LLPAs and the pass-through rate.** Selling Guide C2-1.1-02 (06/07/2023; verified in 20.4): "Fannie Mae purchases regularly amortizing whole mortgages either at par (100% of the unpaid principal balance), at a discount, or at a premium"; the pass-through rate is "the difference between the gross note rate and the servicing fee." C1-1-01 (02/05/2025; 20.4/20.1): "Whole loans: LLPAs are calculated on the 'Purchase Ready' date based on the unpaid principal balance of the loan"; "All applicable LLPAs will be deducted from the purchase proceeds"; premium recapture — "With respect to any loan that pays off within 120 days from the whole loan purchase date …," Fannie Mae recovers "the premium, or the amount by which the purchase price exceeded the par price (100% of the face value)" multiplied "by the UPB of the loan at the time of purchase," and "At Fannie Mae's sole discretion, the reimbursement may be reduced by the amount equal to the total of all LLPAs paid in connection with delivery of a loan less a processing fee of 50 basis points based on the UPB at acquisition." LLPA Matrix 09.09.2026 (20.4): "All applicable LLPAs for whole loan transactions are calculated on the 'Purchase Ready' date (as reflected in Loan Delivery) based on the unpaid principal balance of the loan and will be deducted from the loan net proceeds." **VERIFIED.** Servicing fee: the topic is C2-1.1-05 "Servicing Fees" (title verified in the C2 index today; body not fetched); the 25 bps figure is **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — see 20.4 discrepancy 2]**; buy-up/buy-down for whole loans **[UNVERIFIED]** — v1 delivers at a fixed 25 bps servicing fee.

**2. Interest at purchase — C2-1.1-06, Accrued Interest Payments for Regularly Amortizing Mortgages (06/28/2011; 00a-fnma §5.5 — VERIFIED as extracted; the topic body did not render today).** A/A remittance: Fannie Mae "purchases accrued interest from the last paid installment date for the mortgage up to, but not including, the purchase date," computed on "the unpaid principal balance of the mortgage at the time it is submitted for purchase and the designated pass-through rate"; where the loan is paid ahead Fannie Mae deducts accrued interest from the proceeds (as extracted). S/S: interest "from the first day of the purchase month up to, but not including, the purchase date" on "the scheduled unpaid principal balance for the mortgage as of the purchase date and the designated pass-through rate." S/A: "from the first day of the purchase month up to, but not including, the purchase date" on the UPB "at the time it is submitted for purchase and the designated pass-through rate." **Day-count convention: not stated in C2-1.1-06 [UNVERIFIED].** Investor Reporting Manual (April 8, 2026; fetched 2026-09-10 — VERIFIED): "(Prior Month's Actual UPB x Pass-through Rate) ÷ 12 x Fannie Mae's Percentage Interest = Pass-through Interest Remittance Amount" (p. 16); in the A/A payoff context, "A full month of interest will be based on a 360-day year, while a partial month's interest will be based on a 365-day year" (p. 21). Design: two candidate conventions (A: 30/360 day-months; B: actual days/365) are computed; the matcher accepts an advice equal to either; the first production advice locks `fnma_interest_convention` for the partner.

**3. Purchase proceeds timing and account.** C2-2-04 (05/04/2022; verified 2026-09-10): Fannie Mae funds "provided the following procedure has already occurred": the lender "has electronically submitted clean, error-free loan delivery data through Loan Delivery by 9:00 p.m. (Eastern time)"; "the document custodian has received a complete and accurate mortgage document submission package from a lender by first morning delivery the following day"; "the document custodian has certified the mortgage document submission package"; otherwise "Fannie Mae will disburse the purchase proceeds on the business day following the date it receives these materials, assuming they are in order and the mortgage is eligible for purchase"; "Following the funding of the purchase proceeds the loan status in Loan Delivery will be 'Purchased and Funded.'" Loan Delivery User Guide (May 2026), p. 64 (verified 2026-09-10): **"If Fannie Mae's document custodian receives the complete loan documentation package by 7:30 a.m. ET on the day following an error-free transmission of loan data, Fannie Mae will purchase the loan and wire your proceeds by the next business day"**; "Once the document custodian has certified the loans and the loans have been purchased by Fannie Mae, the ability to retrieve the Whole Loan Purchase Advice report will be available the next day by using Fannie Mae Connect." Loan Delivery FAQ (Sept 2025; 00b-orig F6): "Proceeds are typically wired to the lender 24 hours after the purchase of the loan." C2-2-03 (05/04/2022): proceeds go "via a wire transfer into the lender's designated account on the 'Purchased and Funded' date (as reflected in Loan Delivery) if: the designated payee code is valid; the loan is eligible for purchase; and the document custodian received a complete, accurate mortgage document submission package and loan delivery data by the applicable cutoff times"; "any interest held by a warehouse lender must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note." C2-2-07 (05/01/2024): payee codes via Form 482; changes "within three business days after receiving the valid and completed Form 482; however, Fannie Mae cannot guarantee that this will occur in time to prevent a previously authorized transfer of funds for a mortgage that is in its purchase pipeline or to stop a wire transfer that is in process." Form 482 (Jan 2025): "For any organization with warehouse creditor(s), instructions on the Form 482 must match the bailee letter on file." eClosing FAQ (verified 2026-09-10): the eNote Funding Agreement "Specifies the release of the warehouse's interest when Fannie Mae pays the purchase price for the loan." **VERIFIED.**

**4. Purchase Advice — C2-2-05, Whole Loan Purchasing Process (10/04/2023; verified 2026-09-10).** The Purchase Advice lists "each loan, identified by a Fannie Mae loan number (which the servicer should enter into its records immediately because it must be used in reporting all subsequent activity related to the mortgage to Fannie Mae)," gives "details about the loan purchase, including loan information, price adjustments, and purchase proceeds," and includes "a report with the applicable payee code for each wire transfer request"; "The Purchase Advice will be available on the business day Fannie Mae funds the purchase proceeds" through Fannie Mae Connect; "The lender should examine each Purchase Advice it receives and reconcile the advice to its books and records to ensure that any transfer of funds is properly credited to the lender's designated bank"; "Fannie Mae expects lenders to have appropriate controls and procedures in place to perform timely and accurate reconciliations of the transfers of funds for all whole loan deliveries"; "The lender must submit a post-purchase adjustment request to provide notice of any error, omission, or irregularity in the transfer of funds or in processing the request for the repetitive transfer within 30 days of the date of the Purchase Advice. Otherwise, Fannie Mae will assume that the information on its Purchase Advice is correct" (request carries the nine-digit seller/servicer number and ten-digit Fannie Mae loan number). **VERIFIED.**

**5. Whole Loan Purchase Advice Seller & Servicer APIs (Fannie Mae Connect fact sheet; verified 2026-09-10) and the Developer Portal (00b-orig F7/F12).** "daily-updated purchase advice details"; "All API responses sent via comprehensive JSON file format"; "The dataset returned by these APIs is available in the existing Fannie Mae Connect report"; Sellers API gives "faster month-ending accounting for investor loans with detailed pricing attributes," Servicers API "faster access to the Fannie Mae purchased loan inventory"; access: "Contact your Sales Engineers or Customer Management Solutions Team representative to get started." The Developer Portal also lists **Committing and Delivery Fee Draft Notifications** ("Retrieve all adjustments made at the loan, commitment, and lender levels") and **Loan-Level Draft Notifications**. **Field-level schema: login-gated [UNVERIFIED]** — the `purchase_advices` columns below are a superset to be mapped at integration.

**6. Post-purchase adjustments (Seller/Servicer-Initiated Post-Purchase Adjustment FAQs, page updated July 1, 2025; verified 2026-09-10 as extracted).** Purpose: "resolve all discrepancies between the servicer's records and the data attributes submitted to Fannie Mae at loan delivery"; submitted through the "Loan Servicing Data Utility (LSDU)"; "$100 minimum (credit or debit) threshold for PPA loan-level price adjustments"; "18-month lookback period"; the "Fannie Mae Connect Post-Purchase Adjustment Notification Report provides transparency on Loan-Level Price Adjustment (LLPA) Fees impacted by data corrections"; a "PPA Data Change Rules Matrix" governs correctable attributes. **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** (deadline for data-correction PPAs other than the 18-month lookback not stated).

**7. **Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "C2-2 (whole loan purchase; price and interest computation)": the interest computation lives in C2-1.1-06 (mandatory-commitment chapter, applied to all whole loans), and no fetched topic states the partial-month day count — the design reconciles rather than replicates. C2-2-05 defines the advice; C2-2-04 the timing; C2-2-07 the payee codes.
2. "C2-1.1 (servicing fee/buy-up-buy-down)": the servicing-fee topic is C2-1.1-05 (not fetched); buy-up/buy-down for whole loans is unverified; the Buy Up Buy Down Daily API is MBS-only (00b-orig F7). v1: fixed 25 bps, no buy-up/down.
3. "Purchase proceeds matched and warehouse repaid same day": correct as SM policy, but Fannie Mae's own timing is "by the next business day" after the 7:30 a.m. ET custodian cut-off (User Guide) / "first morning delivery the following day" after 9:00 p.m. ET data (C2-2-04); the advice may be available the funding day (C2-2-05) or "the next day" (User Guide p. 64) — the bank credit, not the advice, triggers settlement.
4. The blueprint omits: the LPI-anchored A/A adjustment that *deducts* interest on a paid-ahead new loan; the Purchase Ready-date LLPA strike; the 30-day funds-transfer correction window; the $100 PPA threshold and LSDU channel; premium recapture within 120 days; the Sellers vs Servicers API split.
5. "Gain-on-sale booked in period" blurs whose gain: the partner (seller) books the sale; SM books cost recovery, warehouse income and platform fee; SM computes the partner's gain as its agent and exports it.
6. "MSR accounting hand-off": SM hands *data* (UPB, note rate, servicing fee, remittance type, purchase date, first payment, LPI, P&I, term, escrow/MI flags); valuation and the ASC 860-50 entry are the partner's.
7. The inventory's Wire Instruction Reference Identifier (SID 398.3) and Form 482 payee codes are 29.4's delivery-data concern; 27.2 consumes the payee code on the advice to prove the proceeds went to SM's account.

#### State machine

Per loan settlement: `awaiting_purchase` (from `delivery.submitted`; forecast posted to `purchase_proceeds_receivable`) → `advice_received` or `proceeds_received` (whichever first) → `matched` (three-way match within tolerance) | `exception` (variance above tolerance, missing counterpart by the expected date, wrong account, duplicate) → `ppa_requested` → `resolved` → `settled` (waterfall posted; `warehouse.advance.repaid`; releases issued; partner residual scheduled) → `closed` (GOS posted, pass-through reconciled, MSR hand-off acknowledged, GL exported). Terminal alternatives: `not_purchased` (Fannie Mae declines / kick-out — 27.1 branch; forecast reversed), `repurchased_from_fnma` (post-purchase A2-3.2 demand — 29.4; 27.2 reverses GOS on the partner's books and re-books the loan; no warehouse advance is created for a repurchase — policy). Transitions are performed by the `warehouse` agent; `funding_approver` releases residual wires; `officer{sm}` approves set-offs/write-offs; `fnma_portal_operator` files PPAs.

#### Business rules and calculations

Money in cents; prices to 3 dp (% of par); rates 5 dp; LLPA percentages 3 dp; rounding half-up at each dollar conversion; day counts per the stated convention.
1. **Forecast at delivery** (`delivery.submitted`, 29.4): `expected_gross = round_half_up(price/100 × UPB)`; `expected_llpa = Σ round_half_up(pct × UPB)` re-evaluated with the matrix version active on the expected Purchase Ready date (20.4 rule 2); `expected_interest` per remittance type: A/A — if `purchase_date < lpi_date` (paid ahead: new loan with prepaid interest) then **deduction** = UPB × PTR × d/360 (convention A, d = 30/360 days from purchase date to LPI date) or × d_actual/365 (convention B); if `purchase_date > lpi_date` then **addition** on the same basis from LPI date to purchase date; S/S and S/A — from the first day of the purchase month to the day before purchase; `expected_net = expected_gross − expected_llpa ± expected_interest − expected_fees` (fees on the advice, if any; commitment-level fees are drafted separately and are *not* expected on the wire). Both conventions are stored while `fnma_interest_convention = unresolved`.
2. **Three-way match.** Keys: advice `fnma_loan_number`/`seller_loan_number` ↔ `deliveries` ↔ `loan_id` ↔ `warehouse_advances.advance_id` ↔ `fundings.funding_id`; receipt amount ↔ Σ advice net proceeds for the receipt's loans (Fannie Mae may batch several loans in one wire; the reference text and the daily advice set resolve it). Tolerances: |variance| ≤ $1.00 → `matched` (rounding); ≤ $100.00 with a fully explained breakdown → `matched_with_variance`; otherwise `exception`. Explanation order: price (commitment vs advice), LLPA (attribute/pct differences, matrix version), interest (direction, days, convention), fees, unexplained. A receipt without an advice by 14:00 ET is provisionally matched to the loan whose forecast equals the amount within $100 and confirmed when the advice arrives; an advice without a receipt by the expected date triggers `SM_WH_PROCEEDS_EXPECTED_1BD` handling. Duplicate receipt → `suspense` and return per Fannie Mae's instruction. Receipt to an account other than SM's collection account (payee-code error) → sev 1, `ppa.requested{funds_transfer_error}` the same day, bailee-letter enforcement (the letter's release is conditioned on delivery to SM's account — 27.1).
3. **Waterfall (same day as the match; value date = receipt value date).** `received` → (1) advance principal including capitalized interest; (2) accrued interest through the day before the value date (27.1 cumulative method); (3) warehouse fees; (4) SM cost recovery = actual third-party invoices for the loan (`third_party_costs{loan}`), capped at the quote's `third_party_costs_cents` × 1.10 (variance policy; excess is SM's); (5) SM retained residual = `pricing_quotes.sm_retained_cents` (20.4-Q1 disposition); (6) partner residual → `partner_settlement_payable{loan}`, wired next business day (dual control) or netted against the partner's haircut-reserve top-up per the LSA. If `received < (1)+(2)+(3)`: `shortfall_cents` is drafted from the partner within 2 business days and (4)–(5) become receivables. If `received < (1)+(2)+(3)+(4)+(5)`: SM's recovery is short-paid and the shortfall is a partner receivable **[27.2-Q2]**.
4. **Releases.** Paper: bailee letter released to the custodian the same day (`SM_WH_BAILEE_RELEASE_SAME_DAY`), Interim Funder Org ID removal within 2 business days (`SM_WH_INTERIM_FUNDER_RELEASE_2BD`); eNote: the registry already removed SM as Secured Party at Transfer of Control (27.1) and the Funding Agreement releases SM's interest "when Fannie Mae pays the purchase price for the loan" — the agent records `enotes.secured_party_released_at` and closes the collateral chain. C2-2-03 is satisfied because SM's interest is released "no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note" (C1-2-03: ownership passes "upon the remittance of the purchase proceeds").
5. **Gain on sale (program view, computed by SM for the partner).** `gross_premium = round((price − 100)/100 × UPB)`; `net_premium = gross_premium − llpa_total`; `fnma_interest = ± interest adjustment on the advice`; `prepaid_interest_collected` = 26.3's figure (borrower-paid from disbursement to month-end at the note rate); `interest_carry = prepaid_interest_collected + fnma_interest` (positive when the partner keeps more than Fannie Mae deducts); `lender_credit` = final CD lender credit (25.2); `third_party_costs_actual`; `warehouse_interest`, `warehouse_fees` (27.1); `sm_cost_recovery`, `sm_retained_residual` (waterfall). Partner origination result = `net_premium − lender_credit − sm_cost_recovery − sm_retained_residual − warehouse_interest − warehouse_fees + interest_carry`. GAAP view for the partner's GL (data only): gain on sale = net proceeds (gross − LLPA ± interest) − carrying amount of the loan held for sale (UPB + deferred origination costs/credits per ASC 948/310-20 — the partner's accountants decide the deferral policy) with warehouse interest as interest expense; SM exports both views and labels them.
6. **Expected vs actual reconciliation (the term-sheet proof).** Expected (at lock, from `pricing_quotes`): `net_premium_expected`, `third_party_costs_forecast`, `lender_credit`, `sm_retained_expected` — with `net_premium_expected = third_party_costs_forecast + lender_credit + sm_retained_expected` by construction of the 20.4 solve. Actual: from the advice and invoices. Variance lines: (a) price (zero for best efforts unless extension/pair-off re-pricing — 29.1); (b) LLPA (Purchase Ready-date matrix version, attribute re-mapping, waiver differences); (c) costs (invoice vs schedule); (d) interest items (not part of the pass-through — carry); (e) warehouse carry (partner's financing cost — not part of the pass-through). Rule: the borrower's benefit is fixed at closing (note rate and lender credit on the CD); post-closing variances in (a)–(c) are settled between SM and the partner per `sm.economics.v1` — default: LLPA/price variances to the partner (seller risk), cost variances to SM within the 10% cap, surplus above the forecast to the partner **[27.2-Q2]**; no post-closing borrower adjustment (policy off; if turned on, it is a principal curtailment credit disclosed to the borrower). `rate_passthrough_reconciliations.evidence_document_ids` = quote solve trace, lock confirmation, final CD page 2 (lender credit), note, purchase advice, invoices.
7. **Premium recapture contingency.** `recapture_exposure_cents = gross_premium` (Fannie Mae may reduce it by LLPAs less 50 bps of UPB at its discretion) until `purchase_date + 120 days`; if Fannie Mae recaptures, `premium_recapture.assessed` posts a reversal on the partner's GL mirror and the term sheet's allocation applies (default: partner bears it; SM's cost recovery is not clawed back — 27.2-Q4); 20.1's gate already suppresses proactive refinance solicitation in the window.
8. **MSR hand-off.** Within 1 business day of the match, `msr_handoffs.payload` (rule-listed fields) is delivered to the partner's accounting channel with the purchase advice attached; SM performs no valuation; the servicing fee is 25 bps (fixed); the platform fee is disclosed in the payload so the partner's MSR model nets it.
9. **Platform fee (12.5 bps/yr on UPB).** `monthly_fee = round_half_up(UPB_basis × 0.00125 / 12)`; first period prorated: `× days from purchase date to month-end (inclusive of purchase date) / days in month`; subsequent periods use the UPB as of the first of the month from the servicing ledger; last period prorated to the payoff/transfer date; posted to `platform_fee_receivable{loan}` / `platform_fee_income` and billed with the subservicing invoice; accrual begins at the Fannie Mae purchase date (27.2-Q3).
10. **GL export.** Every balanced `ledger_entries` set linked to a `loan_events` row is exported once (idempotent by entry id) in the 20:00 ET batch for `sm_gl` and, for partner-mirror accounts, `partner_gl`; control totals and hash per batch; re-exports carry the same batch id; acknowledgments are recorded; period-end (month) close waits for the batch acknowledgment.
11. **Post-purchase adjustments.** Funds-transfer errors (wrong amount/account) → e-mail request to acquisitions_loan_delivery@fanniemae.com under the partner's seller number within 30 days of the advice date (`FNMA_C2_2_05_PPA_REQUEST_30`); data corrections affecting LLPAs → LSDU by `fnma_portal_operator{party=partner}` with the AI-prepared package; the $100 minimum threshold applies to LLPA PPAs; expected settlement via draft/credit is matched as a new `proceeds_receipts` row (negative for debits) and re-runs the waterfall delta.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`warehouse` agent, settlement tools: `pollPurchaseAdvices`, `ingestReceipts`, `forecastProceeds`, `matchProceeds`, `explainVariance`, `postWaterfall`, `releaseCollateral`, `schedulePartnerResidual` (hands to `funding_approver`), `computeGainOnSale`, `reconcilePassthrough`, `issueMsrHandoff`, `accruePlatformFee`, `exportGl`, `preparePpa` (hands to `fnma_portal_operator`/`officer{partner}`), `writeDecision`. Behavior: the agent runs the forecast at delivery, matches on the first of advice/receipt, explains any variance component-by-component with deterministic code, posts the waterfall and releases the same day, computes and posts the gain and the pass-through reconciliation the next business day, issues the MSR file, accrues the fee monthly and exports the GL nightly; the LLM drafts the partner-facing narrative of a variance and classifies unstructured Fannie Mae correspondence — every number is computed by code. Decision record: `{match_id, keys, expected, advice, received, variance_breakdown, convention_used, waterfall, releases, gos_components, passthrough_status, ppa_decision, rationale, rule_set_versions, model/prompt versions where text was drafted, outcome, reviewer}`. Guardrails — the agent may never: release a partner residual wire without `funding_approver`; net a commitment fee draft against proceeds; change the borrower's terms or post a borrower adjustment; alter SM's cost recovery above the invoice/cap; write off a shortfall or set off partner funds without `officer{sm}`; submit a PPA under the partner's credentials (human `fnma_portal_operator{party=partner}`); accept an advice that fails the loan-number/seller-number key match. Escalations: `funding_approver` (residual wires, same day), `fnma_portal_operator{party=partner}` (LSDU PPA, Connect fallback; 1 business day), `officer{party=partner}` (funds-transfer correction letters; repurchase-related reversals), `officer{party=sm}` (write-offs, set-offs, convention lock override, economics disputes; 1 business day), `secondary` (delivery-data causes of LLPA/price variances), `compliance-sentinel` (pass-through exceptions feeding 31.2's fair-lending/UDAAP review). Human path: settlement console showing the same three-way match, waterfall and variance explanation; manual match requires two SM users and produces the same decision record.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Advice before receipt (or the reverse) across a weekend/holiday**: match state holds the first leg; the expected-proceeds timer counts `business_days_fannie_et`; no interest over-accrual because 27.1 stops accrual at the receipt value date.
- **One wire, many loans**: split by the advice set; a residual difference ≤ $1.00 per loan is absorbed; otherwise suspense and a funds-transfer correction request.
- **Convention flip**: an advice matching convention B after the partner was locked to A → exception `convention_changed`, `officer{sm}` review, both conventions kept until Fannie Mae's practice is confirmed (27.2-Q1).
- **Purchase after the first payment is received** (delayed delivery into January): UPB on the advice is the post-payment scheduled/actual balance; LPI advances to Jan 1, 2027; interest due lender from Jan 1 to the purchase date; the forecast uses the servicing ledger's UPB (30.2 boarded at funding).
- **Purchase date on the 1st of a month**: A/A interest from LPI to the day before; S/S and S/A produce zero days — the forecast handles zero.
- **Proceeds wired to the wrong account**: sev 1; C2-2-05 request the same day; bailee-letter enforcement; 27.1 collateral chain stays open until SM is paid.
- **Fannie Mae declines after certification**: forecast reversed; 27.1 `returned` branch; no GOS.
- **Post-purchase repurchase demand (A2-3.2; 29.4)**: GOS reversed on the partner mirror; the loan re-books as held-for-investment/for-sale; no new warehouse advance; platform fee continues while SM subservices.
- **Premium recapture assessed (payoff within 120 days)**: `premium_recapture.assessed` with Fannie Mae's amount; partner mirror reversal; 20.1 gate evidence attached.
- **Partner residual beneficiary changed**: no wire until `wire_verifications{purpose=partner_residual}` is re-verified by callback to the LSA notice contacts.
- **Platform-fee basis when UPB is corrected by a PPA**: recompute from the corrected UPB at purchase; delta posted in the next accrual.
- **API returns an advice for a loan SM did not fund** (partner's other channel): stored, matched to no advance, reported to the partner, excluded from SM economics.

#### Audit and evidence

Per loan: forecast, advice (raw JSON), bank receipt, three-way match with variance breakdown and convention, waterfall with ledger entry ids, collateral release evidence (custodian release, Interim Funder removal confirmation, eRegistry Secured Party release), residual wire approval and confirmation, gain-on-sale computation (program and GAAP views), pass-through reconciliation with the quote solve trace, lock, CD lender credit, note rate and advice, MSR hand-off payload and acknowledgment, platform-fee accruals, PPA requests and resolutions, recapture exposure. For Fannie Mae (C2-2-05 "appropriate controls and procedures … timely and accurate reconciliations"): the daily settlement report and exception log with timestamps against the 30-day window. For examiners/UDAAP/Reg N (20.2/31.2): the program-level reconciliation proving that SM's gain-on-sale participation equalled documented third-party costs plus the disclosed residual policy, and that each borrower's rate/credit matched the engine's output. For the partner's auditors: GL batches with hashes and acknowledgments, MSR data lineage, and the term-sheet parameter versions (`sm.economics.v1`).

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Fannie Mae's partial-month interest convention at purchase.** Default: compute both, accept either, lock per partner on the first production advice, and confirm with the Fannie Mae account team (or a Purchase Advice field definition) before go-live; escalate any later flip.
2. **Disposition of post-closing variances between expected and actual net premium.** Default: price/LLPA variances to the partner (seller risk); third-party cost variances to SM within a 10% cap over the forecast (excess is SM's); no post-closing borrower adjustment (policy flag `economics.post_closing_borrower_credit = off`). Tied to 20.4-Q1 (residual above the 12.5 bps credit cap).
3. **Platform-fee start date and basis.** Default: accrue from the Fannie Mae purchase date on UPB at purchase, then UPB as of the first of each month, prorated first/last periods on actual days; alternative: from boarding at funding (30.2). Term-sheet clarification needed.
4. **Premium recapture allocation** (C1-1-01, 120 days). Default: the partner bears Fannie Mae's recapture as seller; SM's cost recovery is not clawed back; 20.1's suppression gate is the mitigant.
5. **Partner residual settlement mechanics.** Default: daily wire of residuals net of any haircut-reserve top-up due; alternative: weekly netting statement (fewer wires, more partner float risk).
6. **Purchase Advice API field mapping** (login-gated). Default: implement against the Connect report layout and map the JSON in the integration environment; keep `raw_document_id` for every advice so re-mapping never loses data.
