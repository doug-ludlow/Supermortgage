### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**1. General property eligibility — B2-3-01 (09/03/2025).** Properties must be "residential in nature as defined by the characteristics of the property and surrounding market area", "one to four units", in "the United States (including the District of Columbia), Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, or Guam", and represent "the highest and best use of the property as improved (or as proposed per plans and specifications), and the use of the property must be legal or legal non-conforming". Ineligible: "vacant land or land development properties", "agricultural properties, such as farms or ranches", "units in condo or co-op hotels", "properties that are not secured by real estate such as, houseboats, boat slips, timeshares", "boarding houses", "bed and breakfast properties", properties not suitable for year-round occupancy. "A one-unit property with an accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is defined as a one-unit property and subject to all one-unit requirements, unless otherwise stated." B4-1.3-04, *Site Section* (06/04/2025): the appraiser reports "a legal conforming use", "a legal non-conforming (grandfathered) use", "an illegal use under the zoning regulations" or "no local zoning"; for legal non-conforming uses "the appraisal analysis reflects any adverse effect that the nonconforming use has on the value and the marketability"; illegal uses are ineligible; properties subject to "coastal tideland or wetland laws, that create setback lines" preventing reconstruction are ineligible; "Fannie Mae will only purchase or securitize a loan that represents the highest and best use of the site as improved"; "The appraisal must include the actual size of the site and not a hypothetical portion of the site"; the site should be "of a size, shape, and topography that is generally conforming and acceptable in the market area" (no acreage cap); where there is "market resistance to an area because of environmental hazards" the appraisal must address "the effect of the hazards on the value and marketability". B4-2.1-04/-05/-06 (environmental hazard assessments for projects) were not fetched **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**2. Special property types — B2-3-04 (10/08/2025).** *ADU*: "typically an additional living area independent of the primary dwelling that may have been added to, created within, or detached from a primary one-unit dwelling"; "Only one ADU is permitted on the parcel of the primary one-unit dwelling. ADUs are not permitted with a two- to four-unit dwelling."; kitchen minimum "cabinets; a countertop; a sink with running water; and a stove or stove hookup"; an ADU that violates zoning keeps the property eligible if the lender confirms it will not jeopardize insurance claims and appraisal requirements are met. *ADU rental income* — B3-3.8-02, *Rental Income from the Subject Property* (09/02/2026; mandatory for applications on/after Nov 1, 2026 per SEL-2026-08): "Purchase or limited cash-out refinance transactions only."; documented with "Form 1007" or "Form 1025, as applicable" plus "a copy of the fully executed lease agreement(s)" where one exists; "multiply the lease amount by 75% for the net rental income"; "The qualifying rental income amount from the ADU is limited to 30% of the total qualifying income." (22.3/23.1 consume; 24.3 supplies the ADU eligibility fact and the appraisal's ADU description). *Multiple parcels*: "Each parcel must be conveyed in its entirety."; parcels adjoining unless separated by a road (with a documented non-buildable exception); "Each parcel must have the same basic zoning (for example, residential, agricultural)."; "The entire property may contain only one dwelling unit. Limited additional non-residential improvements, such as a garage, are acceptable."; first lien on each parcel. *Mixed-use*: "The property must be a one-unit dwelling that the borrower occupies as a principal residence."; "The borrower must be both the owner and the operator of the business."; "The property must be primarily residential in nature."; "The dwelling may not be modified in a manner that has an adverse impact on its marketability as a residential property." (appraisal per B4-1.4-07). *Solar*: borrower-owned panels follow standard rules; leased/PPA panels — payment in DTI unless the production-guarantee structure applies, contributory value excluded from LTV, "The property must maintain access to an alternate source of electric power that meets community standards.", third-party owner not a loss payee; separately financed panels with a senior UCC fixture filing must be subordinated, debt in DTI and CLTV; personal-property panels: debt in DTI only, no value; PACE: "Properties with solar panels and other energy efficient items financed with a PACE loan are not eligible for delivery to Fannie Mae if the PACE loan is not paid in full prior to or at closing." *Hawaiian lava zones* 1–2 ineligible. *Leasehold* — B2-3-03 (08/05/2026): "The lease must have an unexpired term that exceeds the maturity date of the loan by five (5) years or more."; "The lease must be recorded in the appropriate land records."; lease "to be assigned, transferred, mortgaged, and subleased an unlimited number of times"; loan "secured by a first lien in the property improvements and the borrower's rights in the leasehold interest in the land"; lender gets "at least 30 days, at the lender's option, to either cure the default, take over the lessee's rights under the lease, or commence foreclosure" and notice of lessee default; leaseholds are ineligible for value acceptance (24.1). *Disaster-affected properties* (B2-3-05) not fetched **[UNVERIFIED — policy default in Edge cases]**.

**3. Condition and quality — B4-1.3-06 (06/04/2025).** C5: "The improvements feature obvious deferred maintenance and are in need of some significant repairs."; C6: "The improvements have substantial damage or deferred maintenance with deficiencies severe enough to affect safety, soundness, or structural integrity."; Q6: "Dwellings are of basic quality and lower cost; some may not be suitable for year-round occupancy." Rules: "Loans secured by properties with a condition rating of C6 are not eligible for sale to Fannie Mae" unless deficiencies affecting safety, soundness or structural integrity are repaired to reach at least C5; C5 is eligible as-is when the conditions are minor and do not affect safety, soundness or structural integrity; "Loans secured by properties with a quality of construction rating of Q6 are eligible for sale to Fannie Mae provided any items affecting safety, soundness, or structural integrity are repaired."; "Any deficiencies impacting the safety, soundness, or structural integrity of the property must be repaired with a resulting minimum condition rating of C5 prior to sale."; "If the appraiser has identified any deficiencies, the property must be appraised 'subject to' completion of specific repairs or alterations." with a Form 1004D completion certification (UAD 3.6: Completion Report); infestation, dampness or abnormal settlement → "the lender must either provide satisfactory evidence that the condition was corrected or submit a professionally prepared report indicating the condition does not pose structural damage threat". B4-1.3-05 (06/04/2025): "The lender must ensure that any issues affecting the safety, soundness, or structural integrity are repaired before delivery."; unpermitted additions: "the appraiser must comment on the quality and appearance of the work and its impact, if any, on the market value".

**4. Completion and postponed improvements — B4-1.2-05 (12/10/2025).** New/proposed construction: "Verification of completion of construction is required before sale of the loan to Fannie Mae, unless the lender complies with the postponed improvements policies." Existing construction: minor deferred maintenance not affecting safety/soundness needs no completion; items affecting safety, soundness or structural integrity require the appraisal "subject to" completion before loan sale. Postponed improvements (eligible items are "part of the sales contract", postponed for a "valid reason, such as inclement weather or shortage of building materials", and "do not affect the ability to obtain an occupancy permit"): "The postponed improvements must be completed within 180 days of the note date."; escrow "Funds equal to 120% of the estimated cost for completing the improvements" or the "full amount of the contract price" under a guaranteed fixed-price contract, held in a custodial account meeting Fannie Mae criteria; after "Form 1004D or an acceptable completion alternative" the "lender must release the final draw from the escrow account". Completion alternatives: "Form 1004D with virtual inspection", "Borrower/builder attestation letter with supporting evidence" (new construction), "Borrower attestation letter with supporting evidence" (existing-construction repairs), "Professionally prepared inspection report" for required inspections — with "visually verifiable exhibits" carrying "metadata and geocode for the subject property". **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the 120%/fixed-price wording and the existing-construction "minor, not safety-related" limitation were extracted as paraphrase; re-quote before final QC.]**

B4-2.1-02, *Waiver of Project Review* (08/05/2026): detached units ("completely detached from other condo units in the project. The unit may share no adjoining walls, ceilings, floors, or other attached architectural elements"), "Project review is waived for new and established condo projects" of two to four units, a "Unit in a five- to ten-unit condo project that is not part of a larger development or master association", PUD units ("except for PUD projects consisting of manufactured homes"), and Fannie Mae-to-Fannie Mae limited cash-out refinances "with a maximum loan-to-value ratio of 80%"; basic requirements still apply: "property eligibility requirements (described in Chapter B2-3, Property Eligibility)", "the project is not a condo hotel or motel, houseboat project, or a timeshare or segmented ownership project", "the project is not terminating and is not involved in insolvency proceedings", plus appraisal and insurance requirements. **Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. "C5/C6 and Q6 ineligible without repairs" is wrong: C6 is ineligible unless repaired to ≥ C5; C5 is eligible as-is when deficiencies are minor and not safety-related; Q6 is eligible once safety items are repaired.
2. Limited Review no longer exists for applications on/after Aug 3, 2026; "Full/Limited Review" and the Florida geographic rules are archive-only (pre-Aug 3 applications).
3. Validity is one year (established) / 180 days (new) before the note date, plus CPM validity at the note date — not "180 days for Full Review".
4. Safety/soundness items on existing construction cannot be handled by a 180-day holdback; they must be completed before sale. The holdback is for minor, weather/material-delayed, contract items.
5. ADU rental income sits in B3-3.8-02 (not B2-3): purchase/limited cash-out only, Form 1007/1025 + lease, 75%, capped at 30% of total qualifying income.
6. The B4-2 chapter was renumbered (B4-2.2-03 FHA-approved review; B4-2.2-04 PERS; B4-2.2-05 special considerations); "unavailable list" is the CPM "Unavailable" status.
7. Form 1076 is optional and its current published edition is March 2016 + Dec 2021 addendum; the addendum questions are the deferred-maintenance/special-assessment evidence.
8. The "$10,000 per unit" figure is an unfunded-repair trigger, not a special-assessment threshold; a special assessment of any size tied to a critical repair makes the project ineligible until the repair is complete.
9. Reserves: 10% now, 15% for Full Review applications on/after Jan 4, 2027; special assessments cannot substitute; reserve-study "baseline" funding is no longer permitted (apps ≥ Aug 3, 2026).

Events: `property.eligibility.completed{result}`, `property.repair.required{safety_related}`, `project.review.started{review_type}` (opens the 3-year inspection look-back window), `project.review.completed` (baseline), `project.review.expired`, `project.ineligible.determined`, `project.cpm.status.recorded`, `holdback.established`, `holdback.completed`, `holdback.released`, `holdback.overdue`, `mh.verification.completed`.

#### State machine

`property_eligibility_reviews.result` is recomputed on every input; `project_reviews.status`: `not_required` (SFR/PUD-waived) | `pending_docs` → `in_review` → `cpm_entry_pending` (operator) → `certified` | `waived` | `ineligible` (terminal until new facts) | `expired` (validity lapse → `in_review`). `escrow_holdbacks.status`: `established` (at funding) → `in_progress` → `completed` (evidence accepted) → `released`; `overdue` at `completion_due_on + 1`. Guards: `consummate` requires `property_eligibility_reviews.result ≠ ineligible`, project review valid at the note date, and every safety-related repair either completed with a completion report or scheduled to complete before delivery (with the delivery gate below); `submitDelivery` (29.4) requires the completion evidence for every "subject to" item that is not an eligible postponed improvement, a valid `project_type_code`/CPM IDs, and SFC 859 where applicable.

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — Property classification and B2-3 tests.** From the 1003 interview, title and the appraisal, classify `property_class`; run: units ∈ [1,4]; residential in nature; year-round occupancy; no ineligible type (farm/ranch, condotel, boarding house, B&B, houseboat, timeshare); zoning ∈ {legal, legal_nonconforming with adverse-effect analysis}; coastal/wetland setback ineligibility; ADU rules (≤ 1 ADU; none on 2–4 units; kitchen minimum; unpermitted → insurance confirmation + appraisal analysis); multiple parcels (entirety, adjoining/road, same zoning, one dwelling, first lien on each); mixed-use conditions; leasehold lease-term test `lease_expiry ≥ maturity_date + 5 years` and the recording/assignability/cure clauses; solar/PACE (PACE must be paid at or before closing — 24.4 payoff; UCC fixture filing subordination; DTI/CLTV effects to 23.1); Hawaiian lava zones. Output feeds 24.1 (method exclusions), 23.1 (DU data), 24.4 (title requirements).

**R2 — Condition/quality routing.** From the UAD ratings: C1–C4 → pass; C5 → pass as-is unless any listed deficiency is safety/soundness/structural → those items `path = complete_before_sale`; C6 → ineligible until repaired to ≥ C5 with a completion report; Q6 → safety items must be repaired; infestation/dampness/settlement → corrected evidence or professional report. Any "subject to" appraisal → completion evidence before delivery unless the item qualifies as a postponed improvement (contract item, valid delay reason, non-safety, occupancy permit unaffected).

**R3 — Holdback arithmetic.** `escrow_cents = fixed_price_contract ? contract_cents : ceil(estimate_cents × 1.20)`; `completion_due_on = note_date + 180 calendar days`; funds deposited to the custodial holdback account at funding (26.3) and posted to `holdback_escrow`; release of the final draw only after accepted completion evidence; overdue → escalation and QC review.

**R4 — Worked example A: 40-unit Phoenix condo with a $2,400/unit special assessment.** Application Mon Oct 5, 2026 (post-Aug 3 → Full Review with CPM or waiver only). Facts: 40 attached units, 100% complete, 38 conveyed (95% ≥ 90%), HOA control transferred → established; not ≤ 10 units → no waiver. Special assessment approved Wed Aug 12, 2026: 40 × 240,000 cents = 9,600,000 cents total; 12 installments of 20,000 cents from Oct 1, 2026, paid in full by Thu Sept 30, 2027; purpose: re-roof Buildings A–D. Inspection lookback: an engineer's report dated Mon May 4, 2026 (within 3 years) is obtained. Branch A (report: roofs at end of service life, no active leaks, no water intrusion, no structural findings) → the re-roof is "normal capital replacement" funded "through special assessments that are within guidelines" → not a critical repair; per-unit unfunded test: 240,000 < 1,000,000 cents and funded → pass. Other tests: regular-assessment delinquency 3/40 = 7.5% ≤ 15% (pass); special-assessment delinquency computed separately 5/40 = 12.5% (recorded; pass); single-entity max 4 units = 10% ≤ 20% (pass); commercial 0% ≤ 35%; no litigation; reserves: annual budgeted assessment income 40 × 72,000 = 28,800,000 cents; budgeted reserve allocation 3,000,000 cents → 3,000,000 / 28,800,000 = 0.10417 → 10.42% ≥ 10% (pass for an Oct 5, 2026 application; the same project fails the 15% floor — 4,320,000 cents — for a Full Review application dated Jan 4, 2027 or later unless a ≤ 3-year reserve study's highest recommended allocation is budgeted). Documents (questionnaire dated Sept 22, 2026, budget FY2026, reserve study Mar 2025) are ≤ 120 days old → `SM_PROJECT_DOCS_AGE_120` pass. CPM: operator certifies Fri Oct 9, 2026 → `cpm_project_id`, `cpm_certification_id`, `project_type_code = S`, `expires_at = Oct 9, 2027` (one-year validity) — note date Fri Nov 6, 2026 is inside. The borrower's monthly 20,000-cent installment is passed to 23.1 as a housing-expense item (policy; see open question 3). Branch B (the same report notes "roof membrane failure with active water intrusion into three units") → "any mold, water intrusions or potentially damaging leaks" = critical repair; the special assessment "is associated with a critical repair" → `project.ineligible.determined` until the re-roof is complete and documented (updated inspection or contractor completion certificate plus HOA confirmation); the application proceeds only on a new review after completion.

**R5 — Worked example B: C5 rating → repair before sale plus a holdback (purchase fixture).** Columbus, OH purchase, $412,000, application Mon Oct 19, 2026, closing Wed Nov 18, 2026 (note date), dry funding Thu Nov 19. Appraisal received Fri Oct 30 rates C5 with two deficiencies: (1) rear deck with rotted support posts (safety) and (2) peeling exterior paint on the south elevation (cosmetic, weather-dependent). R2: item (1) `complete_before_sale` — the appraisal is "subject to" the deck repair; item (2) qualifies as a postponed improvement only if it is in the sales contract and delayed for a valid reason (November weather). Seller repairs the deck by Fri Nov 13; the appraiser's UAD 3.6 Completion Report is received Mon Nov 16 → `FNMA_B4_1_3_06_SAFETY_REPAIR_BEFORE_SALE_GATE` and `FNMA_B4_1_2_05_COMPLETION_BEFORE_DELIVERY_GATE` satisfied for item (1). Item (2): contractor estimate 320,000 cents, no fixed-price contract → `escrow_cents = ceil(320,000 × 1.20) = 384,000`; funded at closing from seller proceeds into the SM custodial holdback account (25.2 shows the holdback on the CD; 26.3 posts `holdback_escrow`); `completion_due_on = Nov 18, 2026 + 180 days = Mon May 17, 2027`; on receipt of a borrower attestation letter with geocoded, time-stamped photos on Fri Apr 9, 2027 → `holdback.completed`; final draw released by Fri Apr 16, 2027 (`SM_HOLDBACK_FINAL_DRAW_5BD`). Delivery on Mon Nov 23 carries the "subject to" completion evidence for item (1); the open holdback for item (2) is permitted under the postponed-improvement policy and is tracked by servicing 3.x after boarding.

**R6 — Manufactured housing.** Gate: HUD Certification Label numbers and Data Plate reported on the 1004C; missing → verification letter (`label_verification_letter_document_id`); `built_on ≥ 1976-06-15`; real-property evidence (affidavit of affixture/title surrender per `jurisdiction_rules.mh_titling`); foundation certification; single-width only for principal residence; second home requires multi-width; DU required; MH Advantage: sticker verified by the appraiser plus site-built features → SFC 859 and MH Advantage LTVs; else standard MH LTVs (Eligibility Matrix); no VA/VA+PD/desktop/hybrid; ALTA 7 (24.4).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`valuation` agent (property/project tools): `classifyProperty`, `runPropertyEligibility`, `routeCondition`, `planRepairs`, `computeHoldback`, `requestProjectDocs`, `extractProjectData`, `runProjectEligibility` (all B4-2.1-03/B4-2.2-01/-02 tests with dated rule sets), `prepareCpmSheet`, `recordCpmResult`, `verifyMh`, `writeDecision`, `fileEscalation`. Behavior: eligibility is evaluated at application from declared facts, re-evaluated on the appraisal/PDC and title, and frozen at consummation and delivery gates; project reviews are started at application for any condo/attached-project property so that the HOA turnaround (often the longest lead item) runs in parallel with valuation. Decision record: `{application_id, property_class, checks[], condition_route, repairs[], holdback{estimate, escrow, due}, project{status, tests[], reserve_pct, special_assessments[], critical_repairs[], cpm}, rule_set_version(with dated switches applied), model_version, prompt_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails: never mark a project eligible with an unreviewed in-window inspection report; never treat a special assessment tied to a safety/habitability repair as routine; never release holdback funds without accepted evidence; never key CPM itself; never waive a safety-related repair. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (CPM; SLA 1 `business_days_creditor`), `officer` (PERS submissions and exception requests; SLA 5 BD), `licensed_specialist` where a state requires a licensed reviewer, `human_agent` on request. Human path: review console mirrors every test with source-document links.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Application before Aug 3, 2026 still in pipeline: `limited_legacy` permitted with archived LTV limits; after that date, Full Review or waiver only.
- Five-to-ten-unit project inside a master association: no waiver → Full Review.
- Detached condo unit: waiver, but B2-3 eligibility, insurance and the ineligible-project characteristics still apply.
- Fannie Mae-to-Fannie Mae limited cash-out ≤ 80% LTV on a condo: review waived (confirm ownership via Loan Lookup API, 20.1).
- CPM shows "Unavailable": ineligible; read CPM comments; `officer` may request PERS.
- HOA refuses the questionnaire: use equivalent documentation (budget, minutes, reserve study, insurance, management letter); if deferred-maintenance evidence cannot be obtained, the project cannot be certified.
- Special assessment "planned" but not approved: record; if disclosed as likely for a critical repair, the project is treated as in need of critical repairs until the lender determines otherwise per the FAQ.
- Reserve study uses "baseline" funding (apps ≥ Aug 3, 2026): reject the exception; require the highest recommended allocation in the budget.
- Litigation over construction defects: ineligible unless the minor-litigation exception is documented.
- Condo in a state whose lien-priority statute exceeds six months of assessments: flag (`jurisdiction_rules.condo_lien_priority`) — B4-2.1-01 "in most jurisdictions" wording; legal review.
- Legal non-conforming use with a rebuild restriction: appraisal must analyze; if reconstruction to current density is prohibited and the market penalizes, treat as ineligible pending review (B4-1.3-04 setback rule analog).
- Unpermitted ADU: eligible only with the insurance-claim confirmation and appraisal analysis; ADU rent usable only on purchase/LCOR with Form 1007/1025 and the 30% cap.
- Solar lease with the third party named as loss payee: insurance must be corrected (24.5) before eligibility passes.
- PACE lien discovered at title: payoff at closing (24.4) or ineligible.
- Disaster declared after the appraisal (B2-3-05 policy unverified): platform default — post-disaster exterior inspection and, where damage is reported, repairs completed before sale.
- New construction without a certificate of occupancy at closing: completion verification before sale, or postponed-improvement path only for contract items not affecting occupancy permit.
- Holdback not completed by day 180: `holdback.overdue`; evaluate the rep-and-warrant exposure; 28.4 self-report analysis; servicing pursues completion.
- MH with missing labels and no verification letter available: ineligible; MH on leased land outside an approved project: ineligible.
- Multiple parcels where one parcel is agricultural-zoned while the other is residential: fails the "same basic zoning" test.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **24.3-T1** Given the 40-unit Phoenix project (Branch A) with an application date of Oct 5, 2026, then all Full Review tests pass, `reserve_pct = 0.1042`, `project_type_code = S`, and `expires_at = 2027-10-09` after CPM certification on Oct 9, 2026.
- **24.3-T2** Given the same project with an application date of Jan 4, 2027 and no reserve study, then the review fails on reserves (10.42% < 15%) and the decision cites LL-2026-03.
- **24.3-T3** Given Branch B (engineer's report with active water intrusion) and the $2,400/unit assessment, then `project.ineligible.determined` fires with reason "special assessment associated with unremediated critical repair", and the application cannot pass `consummate`.
- **24.3-T4** Given a 6-unit attached project not in a master association, then `review_type = waived`, `project_type_code = V`, and the B2-3/ineligible-characteristics checks still run; given the same project inside a master association, then Full Review is required.
- **24.3-T5** Given an application dated Jul 31, 2026 in a Limited-Review-eligible project, then `limited_legacy` is allowed; dated Aug 3, 2026 → not allowed.
- **24.3-T6** Given the C5 appraisal in R5 with a safety-related deck item, then the deck cannot enter a holdback, delivery is blocked until the Completion Report (received Nov 16, 2026) is accepted, and the paint item produces `escrow_cents = 384,000` with `completion_due_on = 2027-05-17`.
- **24.3-T7** Given a C6 rating, then `result = ineligible` until a "subject to" appraisal and completion evidence show ≥ C5; given Q6 with no safety items, then eligible.
- **24.3-T8** Given a leasehold with lease expiry Dec 31, 2061 and loan maturity Dec 1, 2056, then the lease-term test passes (≥ 5 years); expiry Nov 30, 2061 → fails.
- **24.3-T9** Given a manufactured home built May 1, 1976, then ineligible; built Jul 1, 1976 with labels reported and affidavit of affixture, MH Advantage sticker verified → eligible with SFC 859.
- **24.3-T10** Given a 1-unit purchase with a permitted ADU leased at 150,000 cents/month and total qualifying income of 1,000,000 cents/month, then usable ADU rent = min(112,500, 300,000) = 112,500 cents; on a cash-out refinance, ADU rent is not usable.
- **24.3-T11** Given a holdback established Nov 18, 2026 with no completion by May 17, 2027, then `holdback.overdue` fires May 18, 2027 with an `officer` escalation.
- **24.3-T12** Given CPM status "Unavailable" recorded by the operator, then `result = ineligible` regardless of the lender's own analysis.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Document age for lender Full Reviews.** Fannie Mae states four months only for PERS. Default: questionnaire/budget ≤ 120 calendar days old at review; reserve study ≤ 3 years.
2. **CPM certification validity.** Not stated in the CPM FAQ; default: use the expiry shown on the CPM record and never exceed the one-year/180-day B4-2.1-01 limits.
3. **Special-assessment installments in the housing expense.** Treatment in B3-6 not fetched; default: include the monthly installment in PITIA for DTI (conservative) — 23.1 to confirm.
4. **Environmental and disaster topics** (B4-2.1-04/-05/-06, B2-3-05) unread; default: post-disaster inspection policy above; environmental hazards handled via appraisal commentary and professional reports.
5. **Holdback custodial account.** Whether the SM custodial account used for holdbacks must be a Fannie Mae P&I/T&I-style custodial account or a segregated escrow; default: segregated custodial account meeting servicing 6.x criteria, with servicing 3.x administering after boarding.
6. **State lien-priority rule.** Six-month super-lien exceptions; default: `jurisdiction_rules.condo_lien_priority` flag with legal review per state.
