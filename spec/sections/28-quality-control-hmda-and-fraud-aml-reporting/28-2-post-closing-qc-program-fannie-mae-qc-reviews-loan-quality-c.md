### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**D1-3-01, Lender Post-Closing Quality Control Review Process (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED.** Headings: Introduction; Timing of QC Review Process; Loan Selection Process; Random Selections; Discretionary Selections. "Loans must be selected for post-closing QC reviews on at least a monthly basis." **"The entire QC cycle (selection, review, rebuttal, and reporting) must be completed within 90 days from the month of the disbursement date (loans they originate) or acquisition date (loans they acquire)."** "If the lender's QC cycle is in arrears more than one 30-day cycle, the lender must provide written notice to its Fannie Mae customer account team or QC Specialist." Random selections: "a 10% sample of all monthly loan production, or" "a statistically valid sample of all monthly loan production"; "the statistical sampling model (variables) must be calculated using a 95% confidence level with a 2% precision rate and a statistical statement of six months"; "The loans selected must be representative of the lender's overall book of business, including: all of the different types of loans the lender offers." Discretionary selections: "The process must target areas the lender identifies as having a higher potential for errors and loans that may have a high risk of misrepresentation or fraud, including early payment defaults." Fannie Mae's QC FAQs (February 24, 2023) illustrate the count: "loans that closed between March 1 and March 31 must be selected, reviewed, and reported on no later than June 30," and "Providing reporting to senior management is considered the final step in a cycle." Read together: the cycle for loans disbursed in month M ends 90 days after the last day of M. The EPD definition is not in the topic — **[UNVERIFIED — Fannie Mae definition not located; policy default in rule 3]**.

**D1-3-02, Lender Post-Closing Quality Control Review of Approval Conditions, Underwriting Decisions, Data, and Documentation (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED.** Headings: Introduction; Overview; Review of Underwriting Decision and Approval Conditions; Review of DU Findings and Conditions; Verification of Data; Review of Potential Red Flag and Alert Messages; Review of Social Security Numbers; Review of Transaction and Closing Documents. "The lender must confirm the loan was underwritten in accordance with Fannie Mae's requirements"; "confirm that all loan approval conditions required by the underwriter were satisfied"; "information on the closing documents … is consistent with the underwriting decision"; "ensure all data submitted to DU is true, correct, and complete"; "resubmit the loan to DU with the correct data, if discrepancies are outside the DU allowed tolerances"; "The DU limited waiver of representations and warranties is invalidated when loans that exceed DU tolerances are not resubmitted"; "review of any 'potential red flag' messages appearing in the DU Underwriting Findings report or alerts created by sources other than DU, such as those associated with credit reports"; "lender must ensure that any identified discrepancies have been appropriately addressed"; closing-document review of the "Recorded security instrument and any applicable riders or addenda," "Note," "Title evidence," "Final settlement statement," "Mortgage insurance certificate or policy," and "lender must review a copy of the document sent for recordation"; "adequate mortgage insurance coverage as required for the loan transaction"; and the lender must "advise Fannie Mae of these findings using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect when the lender determines the loan was not eligible as delivered."

**D1-3-03, Lender Post-Closing Quality Control Reverifications (04/01/2026) — VERIFIED.** Full-file reverification areas: "income and employment, income tax returns, assets, credit history, property eligibility and property value, and occupancy." "The lender must use the information in the reverification documentation to reconcile the information used to underwrite the loan"; "The lender must pay any applicable fees charged by employers, financial institutions, or other third-parties used to obtain reverification information"; "If the lender cannot obtain required post-closing QC reverifications, the lender must document the loan file with the request date, and that the information requested was not obtained"; "The lender must track reverification results, including successful reverifications and unsuccessful attempts." Income/employment: "When an automated income verification was obtained directly from an approved Fannie Mae vendor, reverification of that income is not required." Assets: "When an automated asset verification was obtained directly from an approved Fannie Mae vendor, reverification of that asset is not required." DU validation service: "The borrower's income, employment, or assets do not have to be reverified (or recalculated) if they were validated by the DU Validation Service, and the lender satisfied all the requirements for obtaining representation and warranty enforcement relief, and the loan closed by the 'Close by Date' stated in the DU validation message." Tax returns: "The post-closing QC review must include the lender's submission for tax transcripts to the IRS (or designee) using the IRS Form 4506-C (or an alternative IRS form or process that authorizes the release of comparable tax information) when tax returns were required and relied upon in underwriting"; "If the lender obtained the appropriate IRS transcripts during its preclosing process (processing and underwriting), it may use the same documents in its post-closing QC process, without ordering new transcripts." Credit: "If a borrower's credit was evaluated by using a traditional credit report, the lender must reverify the borrower's credit history by obtaining a new tri-merge credit report"; "The new report does not need to generate a new inquiry that will appear on future credit reports or include trended credit data"; "The liability information obtained on the new credit report must be reconciled against the credit report or references used at the time of underwriting to identify any discrepancies or the existence of any debt that may not have been taken into account when the loan was underwritten"; nontraditional credit: "the lender must reverify each of the credit references on that report." Occupancy: "The post-closing QC review must include occupancy assessment for the subject property. The lender must review the loan file documentation (for example, property insurance policy, appraisal, income tax returns or transcripts, lease agreements, etc.) to confirm the documented occupancy is accurate"; "When occupancy red flags are identified, the lender must further investigate." Collateral: "determine the property meets eligibility requirements including the LTV, CLTV, and HCLTV ratios"; "assess the appropriateness of comparable sales. Appraisal comps do not have to be reverified on appraisals with a Collateral Underwriter (CU) score of 2.5 or below if the lender satisfied all the requirements for obtaining representation and warranty enforcement relief"; "assess the appropriateness of the data presented and verify the appraisal report complies with [B4-1.1-04, Unacceptable Appraisal Practices]"; "conclude that the rationale for the reconciliation of value is supported"; "reconcile flags and messages that were identified in Collateral Underwriter (CU) if the property was able to be scored in CU"; "If the lender is unable to complete the collateral risk assessment or evaluate the origination appraisal, it may order either a desk review or field review from a licensed appraiser"; property data collection: "assess the accuracy of the data obtained through the property data collection," "identify any property eligibility issues and items of safety, soundness, or structural integrity concern," "determine whether the lender appropriately required repairs or inspections." Discrepancies: "If the reverification process reveals information that differs from what was used in underwriting, the lender must re-underwrite the loan to confirm its eligibility for delivery to Fannie Mae." The words "10%" do not appear on the page.

**D1-1-02 (04/01/2026) vendor oversight and D1-1-03 (04/01/2026) reporting — VERIFIED (28.1 quotes).** "The lender must perform a monthly review of at least 10% of the post-closing QC sample reviewed by the vendor to validate the accuracy and completeness of the vendor's work"; "This review must be performed by the lender itself and may not be contracted out"; reports "in writing and shared with management monthly"; post-closing reports must include "defect trending for at least three months," "data for the highest severity classification defect rate, benchmarked against its defined target rate, at least quarterly," a distinction "between defects related to compliance with applicable law and underwriting and eligibility defects," "a description of each sample selection, including the sampling criteria, number of loans reviewed, and percentage of total eligible loans reviewed," and "the final results of the 10% QC vendor review described in D1-1-02." D1-1-01: "A target defect rate must be established that is as reasonably low as possible."

**D2-1-01 (04/03/2019), D2-1-02 (03/06/2024), D2-1-03 (11/03/2015), D2-1-04 (08/30/2016) — VERIFIED.** D2-1-01: "Fannie Mae uses a statistically valid approach in selecting a random sample of new mortgage loan deliveries for review. The random sample is augmented with targeted, discretionary sampling." D2-1-02 (headings: Notification of a QC Review; Document Submission Requirements; Loan File Delivery Timeline): **"Lenders must send the requested documentation for an underwriting, servicing, or data validation review within 30 days after Fannie Mae notifies the lender"**; "Loan files or documents can be uploaded using the drag-and-drop feature or by uploading to the corresponding task in Loan Quality Connect"; the file is the "complete loan file, including all documents used to support the underwriting decision," with the Post-Closing Loan File Document Checklist (Form 1032) as the stacking reference; "Lenders must follow the electronic submission naming protocol to associate the Fannie Mae loan number with the electronic file"; "if a lender delays in providing the requested information, Fannie Mae, in its sole discretion, may require indemnification or repurchase (depending on the circumstances)"; "When a lender has a pattern of extensive delays or unresponsiveness, Fannie Mae may consider this a breach of contract and consider other actions against the lender, up to and including termination." D2-1-03: a "finding" is "one or more defects that, when considered with other loan features, does not necessitate a change in the price of the loan"; a "price-adjusted loan (PAL)" has "one or more defects that … result in a loan that was otherwise eligible for delivery had the correct data been delivered"; a "significant defect" is "one or more defects that either necessitates a change to the price … or result in the loan being unacceptable for purchase." D2-1-04: "A 'correction' is an action taken by a lender that demonstrates that a significant defect did not exist at loan purchase or has been corrected"; "At any time during the appeals process, a lender has the right to propose a repurchase alternative following the identification of a significant defect"; "If Fannie Mae designates a defect as resulting in a PAL, the lender must pay the applicable LLPA that should have been paid when the mortgage loan was purchased." **Post-purchase review process overview (Fannie Mae, Feb. 2024) — PARTIALLY VERIFIED (a process graphic, not Guide text):** "Fannie Mae requests the loan file, and the lender has 30 days to complete the request"; on a notice of potential defect "the lender has 30 days to upload documents that would resolve defects"; "Lender has 60 days to resolve the issue"; "First appeal within 60 days. Second appeal within 15 days of first response only with new information"; "lender repurchases unless Fannie Mae offers an alternative remedy." Loan Quality Connect FAQs (Nov 6, 2024): "NOPDs will remain in your queue until the earlier of 30 days and the Resolution Request being issued or until we respond to new uploaded documents"; "The lender is allowed to submit a maximum of two appeals"; roles "Document Technician, DVC Specialist, Remediation Specialist, Manager"; "The maximum loan file size that can be uploaded via the 'Bulk Document Upload' function is 400 MB (up to 3,000 pages) per PDF"; naming "FannieMaeLoanNumber_LoanFile.pdf"; access through "your organization's Corporate Administrator for Fannie Mae technology." No API or bulk-upload specification is published (00b-orig F1) → **[PORTAL-ONLY]**.

**A2-3.2-01 (08/29/2017) and A2-3.2-03 (08/30/2016) — VERIFIED (00a-fnma §7).** For loans acquired on/after Jan 1, 2013 "the lender must pay Fannie Mae the funds that are due in connection with a demand for repurchase, indemnification, or make whole payment within 60 days after receipt of the demand or within such other time frame as specified by Fannie Mae unless an appeal is made"; repurchase price "with accrued interest and other adjustments, including Fannie Mae's property-related expenses such as maintenance and marketing expenses, through the date of repurchase"; "Loan-level price adjustments (LLPAs) will not be included in the repurchase price or make whole payment calculation." Remedies framework alternatives for performing loans: pricing adjustment ("payment by the lender of a guaranty fee adjustment"), recourse, indemnification ("indemnify, defend, and hold Fannie Mae harmless"), collateralized indemnification, MI stand-in; non-performing: make-whole, split loss/loss share, loss reimbursement. Appeals: first appeal "within 60 days of receiving a demand"; Fannie Mae responds "within 60 days of its receipt"; second appeal "within 15 days of receiving a denial"; Fannie Mae responds within 60 days; impasse "within 15 days of receiving Fannie Mae's denial" with "30 days in which to attempt to resolve"; management escalation "within 15 days of conclusion of impasse," review "Within 30 days of receipt"; IDR: "15 days to initiate the IDR."

**A2-2-01 (02/05/2020), A2-2-04 (04/01/2026), A2-2-06 (06/03/2026), A2-2-07 (08/02/2023), A2-3.2-02 (08/06/2025) — VERIFIED (23.3 quotes).** Reps and warranties "are made to Fannie Mae as of the date a lender transfers mortgage loans to Fannie Mae and continue and survive" the sale, resale and MSSC termination. Payment-history relief: "upon payment by the borrower of the first 36 monthly payments due following the loan acquisition date" with "no more than two 30-day delinquencies" and "no 60-day or greater delinquencies"; alternatively when "Fannie Mae completes a full-file QC review of the loan file … and determines that the loan is acceptable"; "Fannie Mae will provide lenders with reports listing those loans that met the eligibility requirements for relief." Day 1 Certainty relief components and the "Close by Date" condition (A2-2-04); value acceptance and CU ≤ 2.5 relief (A2-2-06). Life-of-loan exclusions (A2-2-07): Charter Act matters; "Misstatements, Misrepresentations, and Omissions"; "Data Inaccuracies"; "Clear Title/First-Lien Enforceability"; "Compliance with Laws and Responsible Lending Practices"; "Unacceptable Mortgage Products"; misrepresentation on a relieved loan is pursued only if it involves "three or more mortgage loans delivered to Fannie Mae by the same lender," is "made pursuant to a common pattern of activity," and is "significant." The blueprint's "12 payments" alternative is not on the pages read **[UNVERIFIED — treat A2-3.2-02's 36-payment/satisfactory-QC paths as the only relief triggers]**.

**Self-reporting.** D1-1-01 (04/01/2026): notify Fannie Mae within 30 days of confirming defects affecting loan eligibility "using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect," with "a summary of its findings and copies of the relevant documentation to support the finding"; "The lender must maintain records of all loans self-reported to Fannie Mae and provide upon request." A3-2-01, Compliance With Laws (12/10/2025): where "the number of loans affected by the same potential breach exceeds the lesser of 500 loans or 1%" of prior-year deliveries, the lender must self-report "within 60 days of the later of: the end of the calendar quarter in which such loans were delivered" or "discovery of the potential breach," "using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect" (Reporting Category 1 — re-read 2026-09-11: "the number of loans affected by the same potential breach exceeds the lesser of 500 loans or 1% of the total number of loans delivered to Fannie Mae in the prior year, and all potentially affected loans were delivered to Fannie Mae within the same calendar quarter"). Reporting Category 2 (any volume): "If the breach could warrant a repurchase demand and has not been remedied or will not be remedied within 60 days, the seller/servicer must notify Fannie Mae within 60 days by using the self-report functionality in Loan Quality Connect"; no notice is needed where the breach "has been remedied or will be remedied within 60 days". The same topic requires a servicer that identifies a valid OFAC match in periodic screening to "notify Fannie Mae Ethics via email within 24 hours of identifying a match" (a servicing-side obligation outside 28.2's scope; 28.4 handles origination OFAC hits). A3-4-03 fraud self-report within 30 days — 28.4's `FNMA_A3_4_03_FRAUD_SELF_REPORT_30`. The LQC self-report job aid (RoboHelp 2026.03.13): report types "Self Report of Lender QC Findings" or "Self Report of Compliance with Laws"; required fields include the Fannie Mae loan number, the 9-digit seller/servicer loan number, borrower last name, note date, "a complete synopsis of the QC findings and the reason for the request," deficiency Category → Sub-Category → Defect, supporting documents; "Once you click Save and Submit, you will not be able to edit or add documents"; "The Self Report form for a loan should NOT be submitted more than once."

**Discrepancies vs blueprint.**
1. **D1-3 has three topics (D1-3-01, -02, -03 as of 04/01/2026), not "-01 through -06"**; the former valuation, closing-document and reporting/retention topics were consolidated by SEL-2026-03 into D1-3-02/-03 and D1-1-03.
2. **"Selection ≤ 30 days after month-end; review ≤ 60 days after selection" is superseded**: the only rule is the 90-day cycle from the month of the disbursement date, plus the written-notice duty when more than one 30-day cycle in arrears. The addendum's example code `FNMA_D1_3_QC_SELECT_30` is retired; `FNMA_D1_3_QC_CYCLE_90` replaces it.
3. **No 10% field-review minimum** in D1-3-03; desk or field reviews are optional tools "if the lender is unable to complete the collateral risk assessment."
4. **Gross/net defect-rate definitions are not in the Guide as written**; D1-1-03 requires the "highest severity classification defect rate, benchmarked against its defined target rate, at least quarterly." Gross/net are policy metrics defined in rule 6.
5. **The 36-payment relief lives in A2-3.2-02** (not A2-2-04/-07); the blueprint's "12 payments" alternative could not be verified.
6. **Three distinct self-report clocks** (QC-defect 30 days, D1-1-01; fraud 30 days, A3-4-03; compliance-with-laws 60 days with a threshold, A3-2-01) — the blueprint lists one.
7. **The LQC "30 days to upload documents" after an NOPD and "60 days to resolve" are process-graphic statements**, not Guide text; they are implemented as timers with a PARTIALLY VERIFIED flag.
8. **Reverification is narrower than "reverify everything"**: approved-vendor automated verifications, DU-validated components (closed by the Close by Date) and CU ≤ 2.5 comparables are exempt; a new tri-merge credit report and an occupancy assessment are always required.

### Build spec

#### Inputs and triggers
- Monthly schedule (`FNMA_D1_3_01_QC_SELECT_MONTHLY`): on the first business day of month M+1 build the population of loans with `fundings.disbursement_date` in month M (purchase and refinance; wet and dry) — the fixture refinance (disbursed Thu Nov 12, 2026) and purchase (funded Wed Nov 18 / Thu Nov 19) belong to the November 2026 population.
- Discretionary triggers (continuous): prefunding findings of severity ≤ 2 (28.1), `fraud_cases` opened (28.4), EPD signals from servicing (`loan.delinquency.changed` from 5.1/6.x), `document_integrity_checks` fails, occupancy red flags, high-risk strata (cash-out, investment, 2–4 units, manufactured, TX 50(a)(6), gift-heavy purchases), agent-version monitoring strata (31.2), any loan self-identified in 29.4 data revisions, Fannie Mae "top defects" categories (Quality Insider).
- Fannie Mae inputs (portal-only, human-observed): LQC file requests, missing-document notices, NOPDs, Resolution Requests, demands (repurchase / indemnification / make-whole / PAL), appeal responses, relief reports; e-mail notifications parsed into `fnma_qc_cases` by the agent, confirmed by the operator.
- Servicing inputs: payment history for the 36-payment relief tracking (5.1), escrow/insurance/occupancy indicators (3.x/9.x), payoff (16.x) which ends relief tracking.

#### Data model

`qc_cycles` (new): `cycle_id`, `partner_id`, `production_month date` (month of disbursement), `population int`, `random_method` ∈ {ten_percent, statistical}, `random_target int`, `statistical_params jsonb null` (`{confidence: 0.95, precision: 0.02, statement_months: 6, expected_defect_rate}`), `discretionary_count int`, `selected_at`, `cycle_due_on date` (= last day of `production_month` + 90 calendar days), `reviews_completed_at null`, `rebuttals_closed_at null`, `report_issued_at null`, `status` ∈ {planned, selected, in_review, rebuttal, reported, in_arrears}, `arrears_notice_sent_at null`.

#### State machine

`qc_cycles.status`: `planned` (population frozen on the 1st business day of M+1) → `selected` (random + discretionary rows created; policy by the 10th calendar day of M+1) → `in_review` → `rebuttal` (all reviews at `findings_released` or `closed`) → `reported` (monthly report issued and signed; `report_issued_at ≤ cycle_due_on`) ; any state → `in_arrears` when `today > cycle_due_on + 30 calendar days` without `reported` (more than one 30-day cycle behind → `arrears_notice_sent_at` required).
`qc_reviews.status` (post-closing) reuses 28.1's machine with two additions: `findings_released → reunderwrite` (D1-3-03: differing information → "re-underwrite the loan to confirm its eligibility") → `closed{eligible_as_delivered=true}` or `closed{eligible_as_delivered=false}` → `self_report_required` (a `qc_self_reports` row is drafted; `FNMA_D1_1_01_QC_SELF_REPORT_30` starts at the officer's `confirmed_at`).
`fnma_qc_cases.status`: `open` (notification ingested) → `package_ready` (agent-built, hash frozen) → `submitted` (operator confirms upload; `submitted_at`) → `fnma_responded` → `closed`; child cases (NOPD → resolution request → demand → appeals) link through `parent_case_id`; a demand case adds `remedy_ledger` and the appeal ladder states `appeal_1_filed` → `appeal_1_denied|granted` → `appeal_2_filed` → … → `impasse` → `management_escalation` → `idr` → `paid|resolved`.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Population and cycle deadline.** `population(M)` = loans with `disbursement_date ∈ M` (D1-3-01 "month of the disbursement date"; a loan consummated Nov 30 and disbursed Dec 3 belongs to December). `cycle_due_on = last_day(M) + 90 calendar days`. Worked: M = November 2026 → Nov 30 + 90 = **Sun Feb 28, 2027**; the reporting step is scheduled for Fri Feb 26, 2027. M = December 2026 → Dec 31 + 90 = Wed Mar 31, 2027. M = October 2026 → Oct 31 + 90 = Fri Jan 29, 2027.
2. **Random sample.** Default method `ten_percent`: `random_target = ceil(0.10 × population)`; stratified so that "all of the different types of loans the lender offers" appear (product × transaction × occupancy × channel); the statistical method (95% confidence, 2% precision, six-month statement) is enabled once the six-month history exists and the partner's plan adopts it — `n = (z² p(1−p)) / e²` with `z = 1.96`, `e = 0.02`, `p` = the trailing six-month defect rate, then the finite-population correction; the plan records the parameters. Worked: November 2026 population 118 → `ceil(11.8) = 12` random selections; with the statistical method at `p = 0.08`, `n₀ = (1.96² × 0.08 × 0.92)/0.02² = 706.8` → with FPC over a six-month population of 700 the sample is `706.8 / (1 + 705.8/700) = 351.7` → the 10% method is used until volume makes the statistical sample smaller.
3. **Discretionary sample.** Every loan with: an EPD (policy definition: a loan that becomes 60 or more days delinquent within the first six scheduled payments — **[UNVERIFIED as Fannie Mae's definition; policy]**), a prefunding severity-1/2 finding, an open `fraud_cases` row, a `document_integrity_checks` fail, an occupancy red flag from servicing (mailing-address change, insurance change to a landlord policy, tax-transcript address), a Fannie Mae top-defect category focus, an agent-version monitoring stratum, cash-out/investment/2–4-unit/manufactured/TX 50(a)(6) strata (at least 2 per month where volume exists), and any loan flagged by 29.4 data revisions. Discretionary loans are reported separately from the random defect rate.
4. **Scope engine (D1-3-02/-03 exemptions).** For each review: `income_reverify_required = NOT (automated income verification from an approved vendor) AND NOT (DU-validated income with relief conditions met AND close_by_date_met)`; same for employment and assets; `transcripts_required = tax returns relied upon` (reuse pre-closing transcripts from `tax_transcript_requests` if for the same years); `credit_refresh_required = true` for traditional credit (soft tri-merge, no trended data) with liability reconciliation; `comps_reverify_required = NOT (cu_score ≤ 2.5 AND relief conditions met)`; `occupancy_required = true`; `collateral_assessment_required = true`; `desk_or_field_review` only when the assessment cannot be completed. The engine writes `review_scope` and the rationale.
5. **Re-underwrite rule.** Any reverification variance that changes income, assets, liabilities, credit, occupancy or value beyond DU tolerances (B3-2-10) → `reunderwrite_required`; the QC agent re-runs the 23.3 risk assessment in shadow (never touching production records) and re-submits to DU only through 23.1 as a QC-purpose casefile copy (a resubmission on the production casefile is not allowed after closing except to reflect closed-loan data). Outcome `eligible_as_delivered ∈ {true, false}`; `false` requires the `qc_officer` to confirm and starts the self-report clock.
6. **Defect rates (policy definitions, D1-1-03 framework).** `gross_defect_rate(M) = reviews with initial_severity = 1 / random reviews completed`; `net_defect_rate(M) = reviews with final_severity = 1 (after rebuttal and correction) / random reviews completed`; both reported for the random sample only, with discretionary results shown separately; the target rate (partner plan; default **net ≤ 3%** for severity 1) is benchmarked quarterly; three-month trend required. Worked: November 2026 cycle — 12 random reviews, 2 with initial severity 1, 1 corrected on rebuttal → gross 16.7%, net 8.3% → above target → corrective-action plan required.
7. **Self-report decision.** `self_report_required = eligible_as_delivered = false AND loan was sold to Fannie Mae`; `confirmed_at` = the officer's decision timestamp; `due_at = confirmed_at::date + 30 calendar days`; content per the LQC job aid; one submission per loan (the job aid warns against duplicates); the record is kept indefinitely within `fnma_qc_3y` minimum and the loan file. Compliance-with-laws breaches are counted per quarter of delivery against the "lesser of 500 loans or 1%" threshold; below the threshold, the loan-level QC self-report path applies when eligibility is affected.
8. **LQC file request.** `due_at = notified_at::date + 30 calendar_days`. Worked: request notified **Wed Dec 16, 2026 → due Fri Jan 15, 2027**; policy submit by Wed Jan 13, 2027. Package: Form 1032 order, bookmarked single PDF `FannieMaeLoanNumber_LoanFile.pdf` ≤ 400 MB, plus any UCDP/DU artefacts requested; hash recorded; operator escalation with SLA `SM_PORTAL_OPERATOR_SLA_2BD` (29.4's operator SLA code is reused by reference if defined there; else policy 2 business days).
9. **Demands and appeals arithmetic.** Demand received Mon Mar 15, 2027 → payment due Fri May 14, 2027 (+60) unless appeal 1 is filed by Fri May 14, 2027 (+60); Fannie Mae's response expected within 60 days of receipt (e.g., filed Fri Apr 16 → response by Tue Jun 15, 2027); a denial received Tue Jun 15 → appeal 2 by Wed Jun 30, 2027 (+15) with new information only; a second denial received Mon Aug 16 → impasse declaration by Tue Aug 31 (+15) → 30-day resolution window to Thu Sep 30 → management escalation by Fri Oct 15 (+15) → Fannie Mae review within 30 days → IDR initiation within 15 days of that decision. Repurchase price = UPB + accrued interest through the repurchase date + Fannie Mae's property-related expenses (if any), LLPAs excluded; PAL = the LLPA that would have applied (from `llpa_tables` at the purchase date's version) × UPB.
10. **Relief tracking.** `target_36th_due_on = first_payment_due + 35 months`. Worked (refinance fixture): purchased Thu Nov 19, 2026; first payment Fri Jan 1, 2027 → 36th payment due **Sat Dec 1, 2029**; relief confirmed only when Fannie Mae's report lists the loan; `at_risk = delinquencies_30 ≥ 3 OR delinquencies_60_plus ≥ 1`. Life-of-loan exclusions (A2-2-07) never lapse — `rep_warrant_relief` rows carry `excluded_matters = [charter, misrepresentation, data_inaccuracy, title_lien, compliance_with_laws, unacceptable_products]` for the QC agent's discretionary targeting.
11. **Arrears.** If `today > cycle_due_on + 30` and the cycle is not `reported`, the agent drafts the written notice to the Fannie Mae customer account team/QC Specialist for the partner `officer` to send the same day; the notice states the cycles affected and the recovery plan.

End-to-end: it runs the monthly cycle to completion inside the 90-day window, prepares every Fannie Mae interaction as a complete package, and drafts every document a human must sign or submit. Decision record: `{review_id|case_id, cycle_id, scope_rationale, reverifications:[{kind, source, requested_at, result}], findings:[…], reunderwrite:{du_shadow_casefile_id, dti_before, dti_after, eligible}, self_report:{required, confirmed_at, due_at}, package:{form_1032_index, hash, page_count}, timers:[…], officer/operator escalations, rule_set_versions, model_version, prompt_version, inputs_hash, rationale, confidence}`.
Guardrails: never submits anything to Fannie Mae (operator-only), never pays or accepts a remedy (partner `officer`), never contacts a borrower about a post-closing review except for a required authorization (a new 4506-C/8821) through the app with a plain explanation, never alters production or servicing records (reverification results that matter for servicing — e.g., occupancy — go to servicing as `escalations`), never files a self-report without the officer's `confirmed_at`, never treats the 36-payment count as relief without Fannie Mae's report, never reads `applicant_demographics`. Escalations: `qc_officer` (severity ≤ 2 findings, `eligible_as_delivered=false`, unable-to-complete reverifications, report sign-off), `fnma_portal_operator` (every LQC action; SLA 2 business days, 1 business day when a Fannie Mae deadline is within 5 days), partner `officer` (self-report authorization, arrears notice, remedy decisions, appeals, repurchase alternatives, payments), `bsa_officer` (via 28.4 when a review reveals fraud/misrepresentation), `compliance-sentinel` (timer breaches). Human path: the operator console shows each case with its package, deadline and checklist; the officer console shows the report for signature with drill-down to each review.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Fannie Mae requests a file before the loan is purchased (pre-purchase data validation) or after a servicing transfer: same 30-day timer; the package includes the servicing file where a "servicing review" is requested (5.x/7.x records).
- Loan repurchased: servicing continues (partner-owned loan on the SM warehouse or balance sheet — 27.2); HMDA is unaffected (originated loan; purchaser type stays as reported for the year of sale); the relief ledger closes.
- Multiple notifications for one loan (missing documents after the file request): child case with its own 30-day expectation from the missing-document notice **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; the original timer is satisfied by the first upload.
- Reverification source refuses (employer policy): document request date and non-response; use alternative evidence (Work Number, W-2 transcript); if the alternative is unavailable, the finding is `unable`, not a defect, unless other evidence contradicts.
- Borrower's 4506-C authorization expired (120 days from signature): new authorization requested through the app with an explanation; if the borrower declines, the file documents the attempt; the finding is `unable` and the loan is flagged for Fannie Mae's discretionary attention only if other red flags exist.
- Credit refresh reveals undisclosed debt opened before the note date: liabilities reconciled; DTI recomputed; if outside tolerance → re-underwrite; if ineligible → self-report; 22.2's undisclosed-debt monitoring is reviewed for a control gap (corrective action).
- EPD within the first payments: discretionary full-file review opened immediately (outside the monthly draw) with a fraud/occupancy focus; 28.4 evaluation if misrepresentation is suspected.
- Statistical sample smaller than 10%: allowed only with the six-month statement documented in the plan and Fannie Mae's acceptance recorded.
- Partner fails to perform its 10% re-review: the report states the gap; `compliance-sentinel` escalates; SM cannot perform it in the partner's place.
- Appeal deadline falls on a holiday (e.g., +15 lands on Fri Dec 25, 2026): file on Thu Dec 24 (policy: never the next business day).
- Loan Quality Connect outage near a deadline: the operator records the outage evidence (screenshots, Fannie Mae status) and files as soon as available; the partner `officer` notifies the QC Specialist by e-mail before the deadline.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Sampling method.** Default `ten_percent` until six months of history; then evaluate the statistical method with Fannie Mae's QC Specialist.
2. **EPD definition.** Default: 60+ days delinquent within the first six scheduled payments (policy) **[UNVERIFIED — no Fannie Mae definition located]**; align with the partner's Fannie Mae QC Specialist.
3. **Target defect rate.** Default: net severity-1 ≤ 3% on the random sample; reviewed quarterly; Fannie Mae "may provide input on a more appropriate target."
4. **Who is "the lender itself" for the 10% vendor re-review.** Default: partner employees in the partner's QC function; not SM.
5. **NOPD/Resolution Request timers.** Default: 30/60 calendar days from the LQC notification as stated in Fannie Mae's process overview and FAQs **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]**; confirm on the current LQC job aids.
6. **Self-report anchor.** Default: the `qc_officer`'s sustain decision is "confirming" the defect (D1-1-01); the agent's draft finding is not.
7. **TPO stratification.** Under A3-3-01, SM-fulfilled loans may be "third-party originations"; if Fannie Mae treats them so, D1-1-01's stratified TPO random sample and separate reporting apply once volume exceeds 100 loans a year. Default: report SM-fulfilled loans as a distinct stratum from day one so the requirement is met either way.
8. **Repurchase funding and indemnity.** Default: partner pays within 60 days from its own funds or a warehouse advance (27.1 facility term); SM indemnity allocated per the services agreement — out of scope here but the `remedy_ledger` carries `sm_indemnity_share_cents`.
