# Section 5 — Investor Reporting & Remittance (Fannie Mae)

## Overview

Section 5 is the money-and-data spine between the platform and Fannie Mae. Every payment, curtailment, reversal, rate change, MI cancellation, payoff, repurchase, liquidation, delinquency status and (from December 1, 2026) escrow movement that the platform processes must be reported to Fannie Mae on a hard, time-of-day-specific clock, and the cash Fannie Mae is owed must be sitting in the right custodial account on the right day so that Fannie Mae's ACH draft succeeds. Fannie Mae reconciles what we reported against what it drafted; any difference becomes a shortage/surplus, a reject, a compensatory fee (A1-4.2-01) or, in the worst case, a Lender Contract breach for which the partner — the master servicer of record — is fully liable (A2-1-07).

Architecturally this section builds one **Investor Reporting Engine** in `core` + `integrations/fnma-*` operated by the `investor-reporting` agent, with the `custodial-recon` agent owning cash-position reconciliation. The engine keeps a canonical, MISMO-aligned `investor_events` model (architecture baseline §3) and projects each event into whichever Fannie Mae format is in force for that event type on that date: the legacy 80-character LAR records (transaction types 96/97/81/83/89/32) submitted through the `fnma-lsdu` adapter, or the LL-2026-05 JSON servicing events submitted through the `fnma-servicing-events` adapter. Which projection is live is a `rule_sets` decision keyed by event type and effective date, so the December 2026–March 2027 cut-overs (escrow events, delinquency, liquidation, loan-data-change) and the later payment-event/auto-draft phase are configuration changes with parallel-run and CIT evidence, not re-architecture. Every deadline in this section is a Timer Engine definition on the `fannie_et` calendar (baseline §4); every remittance is a `remittances` row that is reconciled to a CRS/Fannie Mae draft notification and to the custodial bank statement (Section 6 owns Forms 496/496A; this section produces the loan-level expected-cash figures they consume).

Partner/Supermortgage boundary: every LAR, servicing event, AMN delinquency record and CRS draft instruction carries the **partner's 9-digit servicer number** and is submitted under System IDs the partner authorized through Form 101 (baseline §0/§1). Supermortgage prepares, validates, submits, reconciles and corrects; the partner remains liable and must sign the officer-level items (Form 582; MORA responses). Portal-only steps — CRS batch import (no API), LSDU single-LAR/PPA screens, Servicing Platform escrow attestation, DRA viewing, Fannie Mae Connect report pulls without API coverage — are `human_portal_task` escalations to `fnma_portal_operator` with an AI-prepared package (Consolidated Technology Guide, Apr. 21, 2026: no robots/scrapers; credentials only to employees).

Dependencies: Section 2 (cashiering) emits the `payment.*` loan events that this section reports and remits; Section 3 (escrow) emits escrow deposits/disbursements for the Dec. 1, 2026 escrow-event mandate; Section 6 (custodial accounts, Forms 496/496A) consumes `remittance_calculations` and `cash_positions`; Section 11/12 (collections, loss mitigation) supply delinquency states, forbearance/repayment/trial data (reported through SMDU, not here); Section 13/15 (foreclosure, claims/REO) supply liquidation facts and Property 360/REOgram signals; Section 16.2 (payoff proceeds) is the cash side of action code 60; Section 15.4 (delinquency-advance reimbursement) is the recovery side of 5.4. Process IDs are cross-referenced inline.

Changes in flight that shape this section (verified 2026-09-09; see research/00a §3.1 and §6.3): **LL-2026-05** (June 24, 2026) — event-based reporting by 3:00 a.m. ET next business day, "no payment" event by calendar day 22, period close 5:00 p.m. ET BD2, escrow events mandatory Dec. 1, 2026, delinquency reporting through the Servicing Platform go-live Mar. 15, 2027 (CIT Oct. 14, 2026–Feb. 12, 2027), Loan Data Change go-live Mar. 8, 2027, liquidation reporting to Property 360 in Q1 2027, automatic A/A P&I draft two business days after payment-event acceptance (later phase; eliminates Schedule 3/Form 472); **SVC-2026-03** — no reactivation of erroneously reported payoffs after period close (July 1, 2026); **SVC-2026-02** — no guaranty-fee advance for Stop Delinquency Advance loans (Mar. 11, 2026); Investor Reporting Manual edition **April 8, 2026**; Servicing Guide edition **August 12, 2026**.
