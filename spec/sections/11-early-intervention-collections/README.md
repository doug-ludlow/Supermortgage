# Section 11 — Early Intervention & Collections

## Overview

Section 11 is the platform's **borrower-contact engine for delinquent loans** — the machinery that, from the first missed payment through foreclosure referral, decides *whom* to contact, *when*, *on which channel*, *with what words*, and *what must be written down afterward*. It implements five processes: **11.1 Live contact** (Reg X §1024.39(a) day-36 good-faith/live-contact duty plus the Fannie Mae D2-2-02 outbound cadence), **11.2 Written early intervention notice** (§1024.39(b) day-45/180-day notice with its bankruptcy and FDCPA variants, and the Fannie Mae D2-2-04 Borrower Solicitation Package), **11.3 Quality Right Party Contact** (Fannie Mae D2-2-01 QRPC, the structured "hardship conversation" that feeds loss mitigation and delinquency reporting), **11.4 FDCPA compliance** (the Regulation F engine for loans that were in default when Supermortgage obtained them), and **11.5 Imminent default evaluation** (Fannie Mae D2-1-01 for current/<60-day borrowers who report a hardship). Everything downstream depends on it: Section 12 (loss mitigation) consumes the QRPC record, hardship reason and imminent-default determination; Section 13 (foreclosure) consumes the breach-letter and referral gates that require QRPC and contact evidence; Section 5.7 (delinquency reporting) reports every outbound attempt, QRPC and solicitation package to Fannie Mae; Section 4.3 (continuity of contact) sits inside the same telephony and personnel model; Section 7.1 owns the D2-2-03 payment reminder that starts the cadence; Section 8 credit reporting is gated by the Reg F "no furnishing before contact" rule defined here.

**Architecture fit.** Two agents own this section: `borrower-comms` (the AI voice/chat/SMS/email agent that places and answers calls, verifies identity, gives the mandated disclosures, conducts the QRPC dialog, captures commitments and warm-transfers to humans) and `default-collections` (the strategy agent that runs the **Contact Engine** — cadence, channel selection, consent and frequency gating, good-faith-effort determinations, solicitation and notice generation, FDCPA state, imminent-default packages). `compliance-sentinel` owns the timers and the daily compliance report; `investor-reporting` consumes the delinquency-reporting events; `lossmit-underwriter` receives the imminent-default and BRP packages. Core services: **Timer Engine** (every §1024.39 window, Fannie Mae cadence step, Reg F counter and TCPA revocation deadline is a `timer_definition`), **Notice Registry** (EI notice variants, Form 745/710 package, Reg F Model Form B-1), the two delinquency counters from the baseline (`regx_days_delinquent`, FIFO per §1024.31, used for every Reg X and Fannie Mae day-count in this section; `fnma_delinquency_status` only where 5.7 reports), the `contacts`/`consents` tables, the `telephony/voice` adapter (Twilio + real-time voice model) and `print-mail`/`e-delivery`.

**Partner / Supermortgage boundary.** Supermortgage performs every step in its own name as subservicer (it is the entity "speaking on the telephone"), under its own state servicer and, where required, debt-collection licenses; the partner (master servicer of record) remains liable to Fannie Mae for QRPC/outbound-attempt compliance (A2-1-07) and therefore co-signs the contact policy, the FDCPA "debt collector" determination rule, the TCPA consent policy, and — critically — the decision to rely on AI voice for §1024.39(a) live contact and Fannie Mae QRPC (baseline §8: written legal opinion + partner sign-off gate; flag `live_contact.ai_voice_counts`). Form 745/710 packages and EI notices carry the Supermortgage name (and the partner's where branding requires), the exclusive NoE/RFI address (4.1) and the §1024.40 assigned-contact block (4.3).

**Changes in flight (research/00a).** The CFPB's 2024 Reg X NPRM is still not final (00a §1.1); it would add owner-name/option-list content to the §1024.39(b) notice and a mandatory live contact + notice before a forbearance ends (00a §1.2 item 7) — both designed here behind `rule_sets.regx.ei.2024nprm` (off). The May 2025 COVID rescission removed §1024.39(e) and the COVID clauses in comments 39(a)-3/-4/-6; eCFR §1024.39 is current as of Sept. 4, 2026. Fannie Mae Servicing Guide edition Aug. 12, 2026: D2-2-01 (11/14/2018), D2-2-02 (11/14/2018), D2-2-03 (08/13/2025 — reminder by the 20th), D2-2-04 (09/13/2017), D2-2-05 (06/09/2021), D2-2-06 (11/14/2018 — breach letter by day 75), D2-2-10 (05/10/2023), A4-2.1-04 (12/16/2015), D2-1-01 (12/12/2018). LL-2026-05 moves delinquency reporting to Servicing Platform events (go-live Feb–Mar 2027) whose *servicer action types* are exactly this section's outputs (Payment Reminder Notice, Outbound Contact Attempted, Quality Right Party Contact, Borrower Solicitation Package). LL-2026-04 (AI/ML governance, effective Aug. 6, 2026) makes the `borrower-comms` evaluation suites and decision records a contractual condition. FCC 24-17 (Feb. 2024) classifies AI-generated voice as "artificial or prerecorded voice"; the FCC's 2024 revocation rules are in 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(10)–(12) (eCFR current Sept. 8, 2026). No regulator has said whether an AI agent "speaking on the telephone" is §1024.39(a) live contact — this section's central open decision, analyzed in 11.1.
