# 15 — Entry, sign-up and sign-in (process 32.14)

Build plan for Claude Code. Save as `docs/ux/15-entry-sign-up-and-sign-in.md`; `tools/import_ux.py` imports it as process **32.14** (§0.3). It combines the two engagement concepts into one spec: the landing page is the thread; value before identity; every answer is a typed card or a lead fact with evidence, never text an LLM interpreted; nothing personal before a session; sign-up is the first sign-in and there is no account form. Everything below names states, commands, events, tables and roles that already exist in sections 20–32; the additions are declared in §6 as DELTA-11…DELTA-16 and nowhere else.

Governing source: projection of 20.3 (lead intake, assurance levels, disclosure, soft-pull prequalification, terms review), 20.4 (rate sheet, quote, disclaimer), 20.2 (advertising content checklist), 31.1 (state licensing gate), 21.1 (application intake), 22.6 (identity), 32.1/32.2 (shell, commands, sessions), 32.3 (the five-minute qualification this process hands off to), 32.13 (cross-cutting rules).

## 1. Principles (binding)

1. **The landing page is the conversation.** The root of the borrower host renders the thread, not a marketing page; the first assistant content is the automation disclosure (`entry.disclosure.first`, one line, state variant appended), then the goal card. No form, no account, no identity ask until the visitor has answered a few chips and has something to lose by leaving.
2. **Value before identity, on the lead.** The anonymous minute lives on the 20.3 lead (`L0_contact_unverified`), never in the browser alone and never in `card_instances` (which need a party). The disclosure delivery, the state variant, the Colorado pre-use notice and the range shown are logged as they happen (`lead.disclosure.delivered`, `co_admt.preuse_notice.delivered`, `lead.range.shown`), because T-03-01 and `assertPricingOutputAllowed` require a timestamped record before the substantive exchange, and a bounced visitor must leave a record too. Browser replay at L1 would backdate a compliance log; it is not built.
3. **Chips are lead facts pre-session and typed cards after.** Before L1 an answer is a 20.3 lead fact permitted by rule 6 (goal, occupancy, state, estimated value, an own-stated existing-loan balance, consents, soft-pull authorization); after L1 every answer is a card mapped to a 32.2 command. Never pre-application: demographics, marital status beyond married/unmarried/separated, alimony or child support, citizenship, documents, income (until the consumer proceeds).
4. **No personal number before identity; no personal rate before the MLO.** At L0 the only price is 20.3 rule 7's published range from the active rate sheet, written as an advertisement (§1026.24: APR beside the rate, no trigger terms, "not a commitment") and passed through 20.2's `runContentChecklist` like any creative. A personal quote exists only after a soft-pull tier and, under `origination.ai_mlo_intake=assisted`, after `mlo.review.completed{approved}` (`NO_RATE_BEFORE_MLO_REVIEW`). The promise made to the visitor is the underwriting answer in about six minutes of their time and a loan-officer-confirmed rate usually within the hour — never "your rate in four minutes".
5. **Sign-up is the first sign-in.** Three ways in, one session model: a code to a mobile or e-mail, Continue with Google (DELTA-12), a passkey for anyone who has signed in once. No passwords, ever. Google and passkeys open an L1 session without `last_l1_at`, so money movement still asks for a code (the fresh-L1 rule is unchanged).
6. **Three goal tiles, mapped one-to-one onto `transaction_type`:** Buy a home (`purchase`) · Lower my rate or payment (`limited_cash_out`) · Take cash out (`cash_out`). "Get preapproved" is Buy → Still looking (32.3 E3). No military-service question at entry (a VA routing question; we are Fannie Mae only); no timeline chip (nothing downstream reads it); occupancy is asked because it moves the range (LLPA investment rows) and 32.3 E3 asks it.
7. **State first, and it is a gate.** The state answer runs 31.1 `stateReadiness` (`nmls.sync{op=readiness}`); a closed state ends the lead with the platform's closed-state message (`licensing.gate.blocked`, no range, no identity ask). It also selects the disclosure variant (UT/CA/CO) and, for Colorado, delivers the pre-use notice before any pricing output.
8. **The Phase I front door is the deep link.** For the servicing book the first visit is `/d/{token}` from a 20.2 touch: OTP to the number on file, the `OfferCard` already priced by 20.1, L2 step-up (SSN4 + DOB) for on-file numbers. That page ships before the organic landing (Phase 1, §7).
9. **Everything measurable from the log.** The funnel is `lead.created → lead.disclosure.delivered → lead.goal.set → lead.state.set → lead.range.shown → lead.authenticated{L1} → application.started → credit.softpull.received → terms.presented → application.received → application.trid_received → du.findings.received → intent.to_proceed.received → lock.executed`; every one is an append-only event today except the three lead facts DELTA-11 adds.

## 2. Screens and states

Every screen lists Reads · Commands · Events · Timers · Documents · Evidence · Roles · Copy · Tests (32.1 §0 binding rules).

### S0 · Arrive — the root renders the thread (no session)
- **Trigger:** `GET /` on the borrower host (redirects to `/app` at the load balancer, §6.5), a referral link with `?ref=` / UTM, or a return visit.
- **Reads:** partner from configuration (`BORROWER_DEFAULT_PARTNER_ID`, DELTA-15) or the referral's partner; the active rate sheet's existence (`rate_sheets` global rows).
- **Commands:** `POST /v1/borrower/lead {action: "start", channel: "web_chat", referral?, utm?}` → 32.2 `lead.start` as `borrower-app` in global scope with `party_id: null` (the lead is global; `SUBJECT_FREE_COMMANDS` already lists `lead.start`), then `lead.acknowledgeAiDisclosure` on the render (no tap).
- **Events:** `lead.created{channel=organic, party_id=null}`, `lead.interaction.started{ai=true}`, `lead.disclosure.delivered{version, state_variant=null}`, `consent.granted{kind=ai_disclosure_ack}`.
- **Response:** `{lead_token, lead_id, lines: [entry.disclosure.first], step: {id: "goal", kind: "ChoiceCard", options: [buy, lower_rate, cash_out]}}`; the proxy sets the `sm_borrower_lead` cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, Path=/app, 30 days) the same way it sets the session cookie (DELTA-11).
- **Header:** the partner's phone number (`FAKE_SERVICER_CONTACT` until the telephony vendor lands) and **Sign in** (S6). Under the goal card one quiet line: `entry.landing.time_budget` ("About six minutes to a real underwriting answer"). The Record pane is hidden (no subject); on mobile the status strip reads "Getting started".
- **Roles:** none. **Copy:** `entry.disclosure.first`, `entry.landing.headline`, `entry.landing.time_budget`, `entry.goal.question`. **Tests:** T-15-01, T-15-05, T-15-19.

### S1 · The anonymous minute — up to four chips on the lead
Order is fixed: goal → (Buy: contract or still looking · refi/cash-out: occupancy) → state → one estimate. Each chip is a `ChoiceCard`/`ConfirmCard` rendered from the S0/S1 response, resolved by `POST /v1/borrower/lead {action: "answer", step, value}` → 32.14 tool `lead.answer` (§5), which writes the fact through 20.3 (DELTA-11 `explainProgram{op=set_fact}`) and returns the next step.
- **goal** → `lead.goal.set{transaction_intent}`; Buy adds `contract_status ∈ {signed, looking}`; refi/cash-out adds `occupancy ∈ {primary, second_home, investment}` (primary preselected visually only; the tap is required).
- **state** → `lead.state.set{consumer_state}`; then in this order: (i) 31.1 readiness — closed → `licensing.gate.blocked{state}` + `lead.status = closed_lost{reason=state_not_licensed}` + `lead.state_closed` line, stop; (ii) UT/CA → the disclosure is re-delivered with the state variant (`deliverDisclosure{reason=channel_change}` — the existing re-log pattern) as one appended line; (iii) CO → `deliverCoPreuseNotice` (`co_admt.preuse_notice.delivered`) before anything priced.
- **estimate** → refi/cash-out: "About what is your home worth?" and "About how much do you owe?" (`value_estimate_cents`, `stated_existing_balance_cents` — the balance is an own-stated existing-loan fact under rule 6, never the loan-amount item); purchase: "Price range" and "Down payment" (`price_range_cents`, `down_payment_cents`). Cash-out: the 80% LTV cap is shown as a plain limit (`PROGRAM_MAX_LTV_PCT.cash_out`), never as a decline.
- **Never at S1:** income, loan amount sought, SSN, name, e-mail, phone, demographics, marital status, citizenship, documents, military service (rule 6 and 20.3 T12).
- **Events:** `lead.goal.set`, `lead.state.set`, `lead.estimate.set` (DELTA-11). **Roles:** none. **Copy:** `entry.goal.*`, `entry.buy.contract_question`, `entry.occupancy.question`, `entry.state.question`, `entry.estimate.*`, `lead.state_closed`. **Tests:** T-15-02, T-15-03, T-15-05, T-15-06.

### S2 · The give-back — a published range, then the identity ask
- **Reads:** the active rate sheet (`activeSheetAt`), product `FRM30` by default, `FRM15`/ARM when the goal implies it; the lead's state, occupancy, LTV band from the S1 estimates (only to pick the *product* and to phrase the sentence — the range is the sheet's low–high for the product, 20.3 rule 7, never an LLPA-adjusted figure).
- **Commands:** 32.14 tool `lead.requestRange` → 20.3 `generalRateRange` → the rendered sentence with APR beside the rate and the "not a commitment; rates change daily; {{partner.legal_name}}, NMLSR ID {{partner.nmlsr_id}}" footer → 20.2 `runContentChecklist` (`regz_1026_24.apr_stated`, `trigger_terms_present=false`, `not_a_commitment=true`); a failing checklist refuses the card (`RANGE_CONTENT_CHECK`) and the lead sees the identity ask without a number.
- **Events:** `lead.range.shown{low_pct, high_pct, apr_low_pct, apr_high_pct, product_code, rate_sheet_id, checklist_run_id}` (DELTA-11); for Colorado the pre-use notice event precedes it.
- **Card:** `StatusCard{copy_key: entry.range.card, personal_terms: false}` with the range line, then the promise line `entry.range.promise` ("To see the rate you'd actually get, we run a soft credit check that doesn't affect your score and a few minutes of your time. A licensed loan officer confirms it — usually within the hour. Underwriting answers in about six minutes."), then S3's identity ask in the same message.
- **Roles:** none (`mlo_of_record` is not involved in a published range). **Copy:** `entry.range.card`, `entry.range.promise`, `entry.range.disclaimer`. **Tests:** T-15-04, T-15-02.

### S3 · Identity as saving progress — "Where should I send your numbers?"
- **Card:** `ChoiceCard{copy_key: auth.choose_method}` with three options: **Text me a code** (mobile field → `POST /v1/borrower/auth/otp {action: request, channel: sms}`), **E-mail me a code** (`channel: email`), **Continue with Google** (`POST /v1/borrower/auth/oidc {action: start, provider: google}` → redirect; DELTA-12). A returning device with a registered passkey shows **Use my passkey** first (`assert_options`).
- **On verify** (every method): the route reads the `sm_borrower_lead` cookie, links the lead to the party (DELTA-11 `party.authenticate{lead_id}` already emits `lead.authenticated{level=L1}`; add `lead.party_id`), opens the session, and calls `flows.sessionOpened`. The session hook then: (i) logs the disclosure line for the session (32.3 E2, unchanged); (ii) if the lead carries a goal, creates the application from the lead (`Runtime.createApplication{channel: organic, transaction_type, occupancy, property: {state, tbd}, borrowers: [{legal_name: party.legal_name}]}` — the name is the party's provisional name, replaced by the identity `ConfirmCard` exactly as 32.3 E5 already does through `confirmFields identity`), runs `application.setGoal` with the lead's facts (`value_estimate` rides through as 21.1 `property_value_estimate` only on the borrower's later Confirm — 32.3 T18 — so it is passed as a *prefill*, not captured), and posts one receipt line `entry.resumed` ("You told me: refinance · primary home · Arizona") instead of the goal card; (iii) if the lead has no goal (a direct visit to Sign in), the existing goal card is sent (32.3 E3 unchanged).
- **Mobile after Google:** a `ConfirmCard{copy_key: auth.add_mobile}` (`party.updateContact`) verified by a code — the number is needed for SMS deep links and fresh-L1 codes, but it does not block the flow.
- **Passkey offer:** retired by docs/ux/17 §0.4 — passkeys stay in the code (`register_options`, `assert_options`) and are not surfaced; no session posts `auth.passkey.offer`.
- **Events:** `lead.authenticated{level=L1, method}`, `session.opened{auth_method}` (DELTA-12 adds `oidc_google`), `application.started` (from the lead), `lead.linked{party_id}` (DELTA-11).
- **Roles:** none. **Copy:** `auth.choose_method`, `auth.sms.field`, `auth.email.field`, `auth.google.button` (Google's standard button, no custom styling), `auth.code.enter`, `auth.code.resent`, `auth.google.failed`, `auth.add_mobile`, `auth.passkey.offer`, `entry.identify.why` (existing), `entry.resumed`. **Tests:** T-15-07 … T-15-12.

### S4 · The prequalified rate — soft pull, tier, loan-officer review (lead states `exploring → prequal_requested → prequalified → terms_review → terms_presented`)
This is 20.3's own worked example rendered as cards; it runs *before* `application.received`, so 32.3's E5/E6/R-path are untouched and start when the borrower proceeds.
- **Identity for the pull:** `ChoiceCard{auth.identity.how}` — **Scan my ID (30 seconds)** → 32.3 E5 unchanged (`party.startIdentity`, Stripe FAKE, the identity `ConfirmCard` with `source=stripe_identity`, L3) · **Type it in** → the same `ConfirmCard{identity.confirm.title}` with empty fields (`source=borrower`). Either way the SSN card follows (`identity.ssn.title`, the one typed field; stored once, never echoed). Typing keeps the L1 session at L1; the hard pull later still needs L3 (32.3 T4), which is when Stripe runs for the typer.
- **Soft-pull authorization:** `ConsentCard{consent.credit.soft.title, consent_kind: credit_authorization, scope: [soft_pull], requires_typed_name: true}` → 32.2 `credit.authorize{kind: soft_pull, consumer_entered_identity: true}` → 20.3 `captureCreditAuthorization{kind: soft_prequal}` + `orderSoftPull` (`credit.softpull.requested`, idempotent by authorization id) → FAKE bureau → `credit.softpull.received{tier, frozen, fraud_alert}`. A freeze shows the lift instructions (`credit.freeze.lift`), no adverse inference. DELTA-13 relaxes `LEVEL_REQUIRED` for `soft_pull` to L1 with consumer-entered identity.
- **Quote and review:** `explainProgram{op: request_prequal}` → 20.4 `renderQuote` on the tier and the S1 estimates → `requestTermsReview` → `terms.presentation.requested`; `StatusCard{terms.pending_mlo}` + `PersonCard{mlo_of_record}` with `SM_MLO_PREAPP_TERMS_REVIEW_1BH.due_at` ("usually within the hour"); the borrower may leave — the review outcome is delivered by e-mail (always) and SMS (with `tcpa_sms`) as a deep link. Under `autonomous` the review is skipped.
- **Terms:** on `mlo.review.completed{approved}` → `presentTerms` → `terms.presented` → `StatusCard{terms.presented, personal_terms: true, mlo_review_approved: true}` with the 20.4 written-quote disclaimer (`REGZ_1026_19E2II_QUOTE_DISCLAIMER_GATE`), the attribution ("reviewed by {{mlo.name}}, NMLSR ID {{mlo.nmlsr_id}}") and the Record's Numbers block showing `figures_source: quote`. `NO_RATE_BEFORE_MLO_REVIEW` refuses the card otherwise (32.3 T21 holds).
- **Proceed:** `ChoiceCard{entry.proceed.question}` — **Show me my rate** → 32.14 tool `lead.proceed` → 20.3 `explainProgram{op: convert}` → `application.received` (Reg B; `REGB_1002_9_DECISION_30` arms) → 32.3's `consentCards` fire exactly as built (e-sign, TCPA optional, **hard-pull** authorization requiring L3) and R1… continue. **Not yet** → `intent.deferred` (no order, no pull, no document); the lead stays `terms_presented`, `SM_QUOTE_VALIDITY_GATE` governs a re-quote, `SM_LEAD_INACTIVITY_EXPIRY_90` retires the lead.
- **Roles:** `mlo_of_record` (terms review; ops console). **Copy:** `auth.identity.how`, `consent.credit.soft.title`, `consent.credit.soft.body`, `credit.freeze.lift`, `terms.pending_mlo` (existing), `terms.presented` (existing), `entry.proceed.question`, `entry.proceed.not_yet`. **Tests:** T-15-13, T-15-14, T-15-15.

### S5 · Re-entry — deep links and the return visit
- `/app/d/{token}` (page, DELTA-14): no session → S3's method chooser with the token retained; on verify → `GET /v1/borrower/deeplink/{token}` → the target card scrolled into view and pinned, or the document, or the route. 404/410 → `deep_link.unknown` / `deep_link.expired` with the sign-in offer; a token for another party → `error.party_scope`. `ui_events{deep_link_opened}` is written by the API (existing).

## 3. Assurance and the three sign-in methods

| Method | Level | `sessions.auth_method` | fresh-L1 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Code to e-mail | L1 | `otp_email` | yes | existing |
| Continue with Google | L1 | `oidc_google` (DELTA-12) | no | Authorization Code + PKCE (S256), server side; `id_token` verified against Google's JWKS (RS256 — `webauthn.ts` already verifies RS256); `iss ∈ {https://accounts.google.com, accounts.google.com}`, `aud = client_id`, `exp`, `nonce` = the challenge's; `email_verified` must be `true` or the sign-in is refused (`OIDC_EMAIL_UNVERIFIED`); the stable key is `sub`, stored in `oidc_identities`; the e-mail is used to resolve the party only because it is verified; `name` becomes the party's provisional legal name with `prefill.legal_name{source: oidc_google, confirmed_at: null}` (the Stripe prefill pattern) |

Google flow: `POST /v1/borrower/auth/oidc {action: "start", provider: "google", redirect_uri}` → `auth_challenges{kind: oidc, provider, state, nonce, code_verifier}` (10 minutes, single use) → `{authorization_url}`; the app navigates. Google returns to `/app/auth/google/callback?code&state` (a Next.js page under `AUTH_ROUTES` handling), which posts `{action: "callback", provider: "google", code, state}` through the proxy; the API exchanges the code through `OidcPort` (`FakeGoogleOidc` under `INTEGRATIONS=fake`, `GoogleOidcAdapter` over the token endpoint otherwise), verifies the id token, resolves or creates the party, opens the session (`otp: false`), links the lead cookie, runs `flows.sessionOpened(channel: app)`, and answers the session body; the proxy sets `sm_borrower_session` exactly as it does for `auth/otp`. Redirect URIs are allow-listed from `BORROWER_ORIGINS`. The `state` is bound to the lead token when one is present so a code cannot be replayed onto another visitor's lead.

## 4. Anonymous state — the L0 lead session (decision 1, resolved)

The lead is the aggregate: global, party-less, `L0_contact_unverified`, `sm_lead_36m`, 90-day inactivity expiry, already today. `lead_tokens` (DELTA-11) maps an opaque cookie to a lead; the cookie carries no loan data and no PII. Creation is throttled per IP in the route (`LEAD_START_PER_HOUR = 20`, in-memory, best effort across instances) on top of Cloud Armor's 300/min. A lead that never authenticates holds only `channel`, `consumer_state`, `transaction_intent`, `occupancy`, the estimates, the disclosure record and `utm` — nothing else. At L1 the lead is linked (`lead.linked{party_id}`), never copied: the party's thread starts with the session's disclosure line and the `entry.resumed` receipt; the L0 exchange stays on the lead's own events and is readable from the Record's history only as the receipt.

SMS and voice entry (phase 3) reuse the same lead: `lead.start{channel: sms|voice_inbound}` from the telephony webhook, the disclosure spoken or sent first (T-03-01), the chips as 20.3 script prompts, identity by the number the message came from.

## 5. AI agent design

Owning agent `borrower-app` (32.2's), executing as the thread-owning `intake` agent's card tools where a card is sent. 32.14 process-owned tools: `lead.answer` (the S1 facts through 20.3 `explainProgram{op=set_fact}`; refuses any fact outside rule 6), `lead.requestRange` (20.3 `generalRateRange` + 20.2 `runContentChecklist`; refuses on a failing checklist and for a closed state), `lead.proceed` (20.3 `explainProgram{op=convert}` → `application.received`; refuses before `terms.presented` under `assisted`), reusing `lead.start`, `lead.acknowledgeAiDisclosure`, `party.authenticate`, `credit.authorize`, `party.startIdentity`, `application.setGoal`, `party.updateContact`. Guardrails: `L0_FACTS_ONLY` (no name, contact, income, SSN or prohibited inquiry on a lead without a party), `RANGE_IS_PUBLISHED` (the range is the sheet's low–high; no tier, no LLPA, no borrower figure), `NO_RATE_BEFORE_MLO_REVIEW` (existing, on `send_card{personal_terms}`), `STATE_GATE_FIRST` (no range and no identity ask while `licensing.gate.blocked`), `CONSENT_VOICE_VOID` (existing). The agent proposes nothing and computes no regulatory date; every decision row names the lead id and the step (32.2's decision shape). Escalates to `human_agent` on "human" (existing 20.3 `transferToHuman`, SLA 10 s) and to `mlo_of_record` for the terms review.
