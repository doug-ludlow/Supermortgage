### Blueprint row
Between the credit decision and the first servicing statement a loan passes through fourteen sections' tools — the credit order (22.2), the DU casefile and findings (23.1/23.6/23.7), the decision and clear-to-close (23.2/23.3), the closing schedule and the CD with its waiting period (26.2, 25.2), closing documents (26.1), the signing session, the eNote and MERS (26.2, 26.4), rescission (25.3), funding authorization, the warehouse advance and the wire under dual control (26.3, 27.1), the disbursement and `loan.funded`, the hand-off into ONE `loans` row (30.2, 30.3), post-closing notices (25.4, 30.4), the ULDD package and Loan Delivery (29.3, 29.4), custodian certification, the purchase advice and Fannie Mae's wire (29.4, 30.1), the proceeds match, the warehouse paydown and the partner's residual (27.2). Every one of those tools exists on the bus and every vendor has a FAKE port constructed per runtime (`src/runtime/origination.ts:362-380`; the LSDU/SMDU/P360/Connect ports at `src/runtime/app.ts:126-131`). Nothing outside a test drives them in sequence: the chain runs only in `src/runtime/lifecycle.test.ts:412-732` and `src/runtime/borrower/fixtures/journey.ts:403-484`, in one process, with hand-assembled inputs (26.3's `requestWarehouseAdvance` is fed `conditions`, `rescission`, `ptf` and `cash_to_close` objects by the test, lifecycle.test.ts:598; 30.1's `matchPurchaseAdvice` is fed a net proceeds figure that is not the advice 29.4 ingested, :718 vs :722). The borrower flows execute six reads or side-steps of the chain — 23.1 `evaluateResubmission` (`src/runtime/borrower/flows/6-decision-property.ts:276`), 26.2 `decideClosingType` and 26.3 `computeDates` (`7-closing.ts:273`, `:294`), 25.2 `computeEarliestConsummation{op: deem}`, 25.4 `evaluateOwnershipTransfer` and 30.4 `explainFnmaLetter` (`7-closing.ts:489-547`) — and the sweep body (`app.ts:246-300`) has no closing, funding, delivery or purchase pass at all. `POST /v1/applications/{id}/fund` takes 26.3's `loan.funded`, 26.1's note hash, 26.2's consummation date and MIN from the record and fills everything else — the CD figures, the escrow analysis, consents, the document index, hazard, flood, MI, the warehouse advance id — from `demoSnapshot()`: a hard-coded $560,000 / 6.125% / 360-month note with fixed TINs, DOBs and document hashes (`src/runtime/origination.ts:193-245`; the route at `src/runtime/server.ts:260-263`; the comment at `origination.ts:498`). A loan funded on the hosted runtime therefore stays in warehouse, pre-purchase state forever, and a loan boarded there carries fixture facts as its servicing terms. This process is the runtime path: one state machine per application, driven by the owning sections' events and by the sweep, that runs each owning tool as its owning agent with inputs derived from the record, waits for exactly the human and vendor acts the owning sections name, hands the funded application to 30.2 from a snapshot whose every field cites its source row, and closes only when the purchase advice, the investor update and the bank credit reconcile to the cent on the same loan id.

### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-15)
**Three specific business days between CD receipt and consummation (Reg Z §1026.19(f)(1)(ii)(A); 25.2 `REGZ_1026_19F1_CD_3SBD_GATE`).** "The creditor shall ensure that the consumer receives the disclosures … no later than three business days before consummation", business day in the specific sense (§1026.2(a)(6): all calendar days except Sundays and federal holidays). The orchestrator never computes the date: 25.2's `computeEarliestConsummation` records it (`disclosure.cd.waiting_period.computed`) and `assertGateOpen{gate: REGZ_1026_19F1_CD_3SBD_GATE}` is asserted before any step that would lead to a signature (lifecycle.test.ts:517-521 shows the two calls; the fixture's e-signed receipt Mon Nov 2 gives `earliest_consummation_date = 2026-11-05`). **VERIFIED** (25.2's registry row, re-read in `spec/registry/timers.json`).

**Rescission before disbursement (Reg Z §1026.23(c); 25.3 `REGZ_1026_23_RESCISSION_3SBD_GATE`).** "Unless a consumer waives the right of rescission … no money shall be disbursed other than in escrow … until the rescission period has expired and the creditor is reasonably satisfied that the consumer has not rescinded." 25.3's sweep emits `rescission.confirmed_not_rescinded` (or the accepted waiver); 26.3's `requestWarehouseAdvance` refuses without it and any wire before expiry is a sev-1 incident to `officer` (the row's breach column). The orchestrator waits for 25.3's event; it never asserts "reasonably satisfied" itself. **VERIFIED.** 25.3's worked example E (amended 2026-09-15 on comment 23(f)-4: only the non-finance-charge costs of the refinancing are deducted) classifies even the same-creditor variant of the Phoenix fixture (a partner-originated existing loan, UPB $548,200.00, amount financed $556,150.05) as `rescindable_new_advance` — form H-9 for a new advance of $1,749.50, the three-business-day period runs and disbursement waits for expiry — where it had read `exempt_same_creditor_no_new_money`; so no variant of the fixture reaches 26.3's advance before 25.3's event, and example A's waiting window is the fixture's, not an assumption of this file.

**Purchase after certification; warehouse release effective by the purchase date (Selling Guide C2-2-04, C2-2-03, both 05/04/2022, re-read 2026-09-15; 29.4 `FNMA_C2_2_04_PURCHASE_EXPECTED_1BD`, `FNMA_C2_2_03_WAREHOUSE_RELEASE_BY_ACQUISITION_GATE`).** Fannie Mae funds whole-loan purchase proceeds once clean delivery data is in Loan Delivery by 9:00 p.m. ET, the document custodian has the package by first-morning delivery the following day and has certified it; data or documents received after the cut-off are funded "on the business day following the date it receives these materials" — 29.4's certification-plus-one-business-day expectation is an operating reading of that sequence, not a quotation; any warehouse lender's interest "must be released no later than the date Fannie Mae acquires the note" (bailee-letter procedure C1-2-05, or the eNote Secured Party release). 29.4 owns the expectation clock; 27.2's `releaseCollateral` emits the release. This process only sequences them: the release step follows the proceeds match and precedes `orchestration.completed`. **VERIFIED** (Selling Guide 09/02/2026 edition, pp. 975–977, re-read 2026-09-15).

**Same-day eNote Transfer of Control with the delivery (C1-2-04; 29.4 `FNMA_C1_2_04_ENOTE_TRANSFER_SAME_DAY_GATE`).** The eNote is eDelivered and the Transfer of Control and Location request transmitted with `effective_date = request_date` on the day of submission. 29.4's `requestEnoteTransfer{op: edeliver | transfer}` is the tool; its gate output is read, never recomputed. **VERIFIED** (lifecycle.test.ts:704-707 exercises both ops).

**One loan for life (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md "One product: origination and servicing").** "30.2 creates the servicing `loans` row at funding with `origination_application_id`, boards it through 1.1's pipeline … Purchase (30.1) is an investor update, never a re-board; `fnma_loan_number` is null until then." And of the route: "`POST /v1/applications/{id}/fund` takes 26.3's `loan.funded` from the application's log as 30.2's funded payload … only what the record does not carry yet (the CD figures, the escrow analysis, consents, the document index) comes from the demo fixture or the caller's `snapshot` overrides." The tree at HEAD matches that text (`src/runtime/server.ts:249-268`, `origination.ts:161-190`, `:484-500`). **VERIFIED** — and the fixture fill is the gap this process closes.

**Purchase advice interest convention [UNVERIFIED — inherited from 29.4 R3 / 27.2; Selling Guide C2-1.1-06 fixes the period (A/A: from the LPI date up to, but not including, the purchase date; prepaid interest deducted) but states no day-count basis].** Whether Fannie Mae deducts prepaid interest on a 30/360 or an actual/365 basis is stored as a range until the first real advice is observed (27.2 locks `fnma_interest_convention` per partner from the observed advice). This process's reconciliation uses the convention 27.2 has locked, or holds the loan in `exception` until 27.2 locks one; the worked examples below use 30/360 as 29.4 and 27.2 do. **[UNVERIFIED]** for the same reason those processes mark it.

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**: (1) The route fills what the record lacks from a fixture (`origination.ts:193-245`, `:498`; `server.ts:260-263`). The build replaces `demoSnapshot()` as a source with `funding_snapshots` built by `orchestration.snapshot` (rule 6); under `ENVIRONMENT=production` a snapshot with any gap is refused, and in every environment the row lists which fields the record supplied and which it could not. (2) The chain lives only in test harnesses (`lifecycle.test.ts:412-732`; `journey.ts:403-484`; the flows' six calls at `6-decision-property.ts:276` and `7-closing.ts:273, 294, 489-547`); the build adds the pass to the sweep (rule 10) and the state machine below; the flows keep their calls (they are the borrower's side of the same events) and lose nothing. (3) Delivery and purchase are not driven anywhere outside `lifecycle.test.ts:692-730`; the FAKE LSDU/SMDU/P360/Connect ports are constructed and never called by a pass (`app.ts:126-131`, `:246-300`). The build's steps 13–16 drive 29.3, 29.4, 30.1 and 27.2 and poll the FAKE Sellers API and collection bank each sweep. (4) The harnesses hand-feed engine state: 26.3's `requestWarehouseAdvance{conditions, rescission, ptf, cash_to_close}` at `lifecycle.test.ts:598`, 23.3's `runCtcChecklist{facts}` at `:485`, 26.2's `runPreSessionChecks{facts}` at `:554`. The build derives every such object from the record inside the pass (rule 2) and refuses a client-supplied one on the hosted API (`NO_CLIENT_STATE`, 35.1-T10's rule extended). (5) The two fixtures disagree with each other on money: 26.3's worksheet nets an escrow deposit of $1,665.00 (`lifecycle.test.ts:591`; 26.3 worked example 1) while the consummated CD and the final settlement statement carry $2,062.50 (`:499`, `:612`, `:633`; 30.3's initial analysis) — and 30.1 is fed a net of $565,903.33 (`:723`, price 101.375) against the $564,503.33 advice 29.4 ingested (`:718`, price 101.125). Neither is possible on the orchestrated path: rule 7 builds the worksheet from the consummated CD version and rule 8 hands the same advice row to all three sides, so worked example A's wire is $556,852.07 rather than 26.3's hand-fed $557,249.57, and a one-sided net is an `exception`, never a match. (6) 26.3 has no pre-signing authorization mode (`purchase-lifecycle.test.ts:20-32`, GAP 4), so a wet-state wire cannot be released before the session as `SM_O73_WET_FUNDS_AT_TABLE_GATE` requires; the build adds `evaluateFundingConditions{op: pre_signing}` to 26.3 (the subset 26.3 rule 6 names) — a cross-process dependency listed under Open questions — and step 9 uses it in wet states. (7) The stateful services this process drives (`cd-25-2`, `delivery-29-3`, `delivery-29-4`, `secondary`) keep their rows in private Maps (`origination.ts:362-380`; `src/domain/compliance-disclosures/ops-25-2.ts:343-356`), so a CD prepared on one instance is "no CD" to the sweep job; 35.1's `service_snapshots` (its T5) is a prerequisite, not repeated here. (7) The OH purchase fixture's lender credit is $0.00 in 26.3's worked example 5 and $515.00 in 27.2's worked example C — and 27.2's gain identity (premium 4,120.00 − credit 515.00 = 3,605.00 = costs 2,774.00 + retained 831.00) is the only derivation of the $831.00 retained residual 27.2 asserts; worked example B, T16 and T17 take 27.2's $515.00 (the credit is a CD figure and the waterfall must pass 27.2's check), so B's net wire is $410,339.52 and its partner contribution $6,579.52, not 26.3's $409,824.52 / $6,064.52; 26.3's example is stale on that line (open question 6).

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **One orchestration per application, driven by the record, idempotent per sweep.** `orchestration.pass` selects open rows with `SELECT … FROM closing_orchestrations WHERE status NOT IN ('completed','unwound','cancelled') AND (lease_until IS NULL OR lease_until < now()) ORDER BY updated_at FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED LIMIT 50` (35.3 rule 6's executor pattern; `lease_until` is wall time, 5 minutes), folds the application's `loan_events` newer than `last_event_sequence` and the typed rows the step needs, decides the next step by the state machine, and runs at most one step per row per pass in that row's own unit of work (`PgUnitOfWork.run({ applicationId, loanId? })`). A pass with no new fact and no due wait writes nothing (T12). A step is entered exactly once per orchestration (unique `(orchestration_id, step, kind = 'entered')` in the journal); re-entry after a hold is `released`, not a second `entered`. Guardrail `ONE_STEP_PER_PASS`, `PASS_IS_IDEMPOTENT`.
2. **Every command input is derived from the record inside the pass; nothing is hand-fed.** The objects the harnesses assemble by hand — 23.3's `facts`, 26.2's `facts`, 26.3's `conditions`/`rescission`/`ptf`/`cash_to_close`/`gifts`, 29.4's `gate_facts`, 30.1's `loan` — are built by typed readers in `src/domain/operations-runtime/facts-35-6.ts`, each field from a named event or row (the CTC fact `cd_delivered` from `disclosure.cd.received`; the funding fact `hazard_effective` from 24.5's policy row; `ptf_cleared` from 23.3's PTF checklist; `cash_to_close` from 22.4's `funds_to_close.reconciled`; `rescission` from 25.3's period row and its `confirmed_not_rescinded` event; `qc_hold` from 28.1's `qc_reviews`; `commitment` from 29.1's row; `wire_verification` from 24.4's `wire_verifications`). On the hosted API a request to `orchestration.step` (or to any owning tool the pass is responsible for) whose input carries such an object, a `*_cents` field or `state` is refused `NO_CLIENT_STATE` before anything is written (T14); the readers are the single source in both the pass and the screens. Guardrail `NO_CLIENT_STATE`.
3. **The owning tool, the owning agent, the owning event.** The pass never appends an owning section's event, never writes an owning section's row and never bypasses an owning guardrail: it calls the owner's tool through `Runtime.executeDef` with `{kind: "agent", id: <owner_agent>}` and its own `run` block, so the decision record, the escalations and the timers are the owner's. The only events this process appends are its own (`orchestration.*`, `funding_snapshot.built`). A contract test greps `src/domain/operations-runtime` for owning literals (`du.submitted`, `clear_to_close.issued`, `closing.consummated`, `funding.authorized`, `loan.funded`, `loan.purchased`, …) inside an `append(` call and fails on any hit (T2). Guardrail `OWNER_EMITS`.
4. **People keep their acts; the pass waits.** A step whose next command is reserved for a role enters `waiting_human{role}` and the pass does nothing on that row until the role's event arrives: `funding_approver` for `prepareWire{op: release}` (26.3 dual control: the releaser is never the preparer — the pass's `funder` actor prepared, so even a FAKE releaser is a distinct actor), `fnma_portal_operator` for `parseOperatorEvidence`, `underwriting_reviewer` for the conditions 23.3 marks reviewer-only and for `issueClearToClose` when 23.3's policy requires a reviewer, `settlement_agent` for the statement and the disbursement, `officer` for a money-field waiver, a purchase exception or an unwind. Under `INTEGRATIONS=fake` the FAKE reviewers (`reviewers.ts:40-46`) and this process's FAKE operator, settlement agent and notary act after the delay as `{kind: "human", id: "FAKE:<role>", role}`; under `FAKE_REVIEWERS=off` the item waits for a 35.7 staff holder and the stall clock does not arm (`clocked=false` — a person's queue is 35.7's `SM_ROLE_QUEUE_UNSTAFFED_1BD` concern). An agent actor attempting a reserved act is refused `ROLE_DENIED` by the owner (T8, T14). Guardrail `HUMAN_ACTS_STAY_HUMAN`.
5. **Gates are asserted through their owners, never recomputed.** Before `documents_released` the pass calls 25.2 `assertGateOpen{gate: REGZ_1026_19F1_CD_3SBD_GATE, requested_on}` and reads 22.2's, 22.4's and 23.3's gate outputs; before `funding_authorized` it reads 25.3's event and 26.3's `evaluateFundingConditions` result; before `delivered` 29.4's own gates; before `completed` 29.4's `FNMA_C2_2_03_WAREHOUSE_RELEASE_BY_ACQUISITION_GATE` through 27.2's release event. A closed gate is `held{gate_closed, gate}` with no owning command run (T5, T14). The registry's clocks are never satisfied, extended or cancelled by this process (34.4 rule 1). Guardrail `NO_GATE_RECOMPUTE`, `NO_CLOCK_EDIT`.
6. **The hand-off snapshot is built from the record, field by field, and its sources are stored.** `orchestration.snapshot` produces 30.2's `OriginationSnapshot` (`src/domain/orig-boarding/ops-30-2.ts`) with, per field: `note` ← 26.1's `closing_documents{kind: enote | note}` (`data_hash`, and the terms from `computeNoteTerms`'s snapshot: amount, rate, term, first payment, maturity, late charge) and 26.2's `closing.consummated{note_date}`; `closing` ← `closing.consummated{consummation_on}`, the note's `data_hash`, the security instrument's `closing_documents` id; `final_cd` ← the version `disclosure.cd.consummated{cd_version, disclosure_id}` names in 25.2's `disclosures` row (`pi_cents`, `monthly_escrow_cents`, `initial_escrow_payment_cents`, `lender_credits_cents`), prepaid interest and days from 26.3's `loan.funded`, `compliance_tests_passed` from 25.1's gate run; `escrow_analysis` ← 30.3's `escrow_analyses` row that `escrow.initial_analysis.refreshed_at_funding{result}` confirms (frozen or superseding) with its `escrow_lines`; `hpml`, `qm_type` ← 23.4's `compliance.hpml.determined`, `compliance.qm.determined`; Every path missing from the record is appended to `gaps`. Guardrail `SNAPSHOT_CITES_SOURCES`, `FIXTURE_REFUSED_IN_PRODUCTION`.
7. **Money identities the orchestrator asserts before it lets a step proceed (it computes nothing new; it compares the owners' figures).** (a) The funding worksheet is built from the consummated CD version: `gross_loan_cents` = the CD's loan amount; `escrow_deposit_cents` = the CD's `initial_escrow_payment_cents`; `lender_credits_cents` = the CD's; `prepaid_interest_cents` = 26.3's `computePerDiem` for the calendar's disbursement date; 26.3's net = gross − prepaid − escrow deposit + lender credit. (b) 26.3's reconciliation to the settlement agent's requested net is the owner's check (`reconcileToSettlementStatement`); a `fail` is `held{money_mismatch}`. (c) At `confirmDisbursement` the statement's `escrow_deposit_cents` must equal the CD's `initial_escrow_payment_cents`, and at the hand-off 30.2's `OB-003` (P&I) and the opening set (principal = note amount; escrow = that deposit; prepaid interest = 26.3's) must tie; a difference is `held{money_mismatch}` with the two figures and their sources in the journal — never adjusted (T7). (d) 27.1's advance = 98% × gross (the facility's advance rate as 27.1 computes it) and the partner contribution = net − advance; the haircut-reserve gate is 27.1's. (e) At purchase, rule 8. Decimal, precision 20; every figure is the owner's rounded cents; the comparisons are bigint equality.

8. **A purchase is reconciled on three sides of the same loan id, or it is an exception.** On `purchase_advice.received` the pass hands the SAME `purchase_advices` row to 29.4 `ingestPurchaseAdvice` (whose `variance_cents` against 29.4 R3's expected net is stored), 30.1 `matchPurchaseAdvice` (the investor update), and — on the collection bank's credit — 27.2 `matchProceeds` (advice vs forecast vs bank). `purchase_reconciliations` is written once with the three outcomes; `status = reconciled` requires 29.4 |variance| ≤ 100 cents, 30.1 `ok`, 27.2 `matched`, and `advice_net_proceeds_cents = investor_net_proceeds_cents = bank_received_cents`; then `postWaterfall`, `releaseCollateral` and `orchestration.purchase.reconciled`. Anything else is `exception` with 27.2's breakdown, one escalation (sev 2 → `officer`; the `secondary` agent's `explainVariance` attached), no waterfall and no release until the officer's decision — the money never moves on a one-sided figure (T11). Guardrail `THREE_SIDES_ONE_ADVICE`.
9. **The warehouse advance lives its own life between funding and purchase.** 27.1's `SM_WH_DAILY_ACCRUAL`, `SM_WH_BORROWING_BASE_DAILY`, `SM_WH_AGING_45_CURTAIL`, `SM_WH_AGING_60_REPURCHASE` and `SM_WH_WET_NOTE_DELIVERY_5BD` are referenced, never redefined: the accrual and borrowing-base runs are 35.3 cycles (`warehouse_daily_accrual`, `warehouse_borrowing_base`) with 27.1's runners; this process reads `warehouse_advances` for the payoff figure at purchase and shows the dwell day on the board. A curtailment or a repurchase demand is 27.1's event and does not change the orchestration's step; a kickout holds it `held{warehouse_kickout}` for the `officer`.
10. **The pass runs every sweep, bounded, after the planner and before the breach pass.** `orchestration.pass{as_of}` runs under 35.1's sweep lease (never a lease of its own), pages 50 rows per claim until 60 s of the sweep's budget have elapsed, polls each waiting vendor port at most once per row per pass, and emits nothing global; the daily receipt is 35.3's cycle `closing_orchestration_daily` (global; 06:00 ET; runner `orchestration.pass{op: daily_receipt}`; receipt literal `orchestration.daily.run_completed`; serves `SM_ORCH_OPEN_BOOK_DAILY`). A step that throws is journaled `command_failed{error_class}` with nothing else written (the unit of work rolls back), retried on the next pass, and after three failures the row is `held{failed}` with one `ops_analyst` escalation; an `unavailable`-class failure (a FAKE or real port down) holds at once, as 35.3 rule 7 does. The demo advance calls the pass inline per crossed day (35.3 rule 10), so a three-day advance moves a fixture from `funded` through `boarded` and `package_frozen` with the dates the calendar gives (T19).
11. **Unwind is the owners' work in the owners' order.** `rescission.exercised` or `funding.cancelled` before `loan.funded` → 26.3 `openUnwind` (its `SM_O73_UNWIND_STEPS_5BD`), 26.4's MIN reversal, 27.1's returned-funds match where a wire was accepted (`SM_O73_FUNDS_RETURN_2BD`), 25.3's refund clock for money the borrower paid; the orchestration is `unwinding` until 26.3's `funding.unwind.completed`, then `unwound`; no `loans` row ever exists for it (T15). After `loan.funded` there is no unwind — 30.2's row is the servicing record and a rescission-period error there is 25.3's remediation.
12. **Nothing here moves money or edits a clock.** The pass writes `closing_orchestrations`, `closing_orchestration_steps`, `funding_snapshots`, `purchase_reconciliations`, `orchestration_daily_receipts`, its own events and decision records; every ledger set, wire, advance, payoff and residual is the owner's tool under the owner's guardrails, with `officer` approval where the owner requires it (`NO_MONEY_FIELD`; the template's T3 rule holds: a money-field change proposed by the agent without an `officer` approval record is refused and writes nothing).

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`disclosures` agent (tools: `orchestration.open`, `orchestration.pass`, `orchestration.step`, `orchestration.snapshot`, `orchestration.fund`, `orchestration.reconcile`, `orchestration.hold`, `orchestration.release`, `orchestration.unwind`, `orchestration.board`, `writeDecision`). End-to-end: unprompted, every sweep, the agent folds each open application's new events, decides the next step and runs the owning section's tool as the owning agent with inputs derived from the record; it opens the orchestration on `clear_to_close.issued` (and journals the credit and DU steps before it), builds the hand-off snapshot and runs 30.2 in one transaction at `loan.funded`, drives the ULDD build, the operator task, the eNote transfer and the custodian package after boarding, polls the Sellers API and the collection bank after certification, and closes the orchestration only on a three-sided reconciliation. It proposes for a person exactly what the owners reserve: the wire release (`funding_approver`), the Loan Delivery submission (`fnma_portal_operator`), reviewer-only conditions and the CTC where policy says so (`underwriting_reviewer`), a money-field waiver, a purchase exception and an unwind (`officer`); it never performs them. Decision record schema `{orchestration_id, application_id, loan_id?, step, action ∈ {entered, ran, waited, held, released, snapshot_built, funded, reconciled, exception, unwound}, command: {process, name, op}, trigger_event_id, sources_sha256 (the facts object's hash), rule_set_version: orchestration.v1, model_version: deterministic, prompt_version: 35.6-v1, confidence: 1, rationale}`. Guardrails: `ONE_STEP_PER_PASS`, `PASS_IS_IDEMPOTENT`, `NO_CLIENT_STATE`, `OWNER_EMITS`, `HUMAN_ACTS_STAY_HUMAN`, `NO_GATE_RECOMPUTE`, `NO_CLOCK_EDIT`, `SNAPSHOT_CITES_SOURCES`, `FIXTURE_REFUSED_IN_PRODUCTION`, `THREE_SIDES_ONE_ADVICE`, `NO_MONEY_FIELD`. Escalations: `ops_analyst` for a stalled step, a failed step after three attempts, a snapshot gap, a missing daily receipt; `funding_approver` when a prepared wire waits past 26.3's hour; `fnma_portal_operator` for an open operator task; `underwriting_reviewer` for a reviewer-only condition; `settlement_agent` for a missing statement (26.3's `SM_O73_DISBURSEMENT_CONFIRM_1BD`); `officer` for a money mismatch, a purchase exception, an unwind or a warehouse kickout; `compliance` when a production hand-off would have used a fixture (a `FIXTURE_REFUSED` row is also a compliance finding).

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Two borrowers with different E-SIGN states → 25.2 delivers per consumer (mail with proof for the unconsented one), the waiting period is the later receipt, and the step stays `waiting_window` until 25.2's mailbox deeming or the receipt; the pass never shortens it.
- The closing is rescheduled after the CD (`closing.rescheduled`) → the pass re-asserts 25.2's gate for the new date, re-runs 25.2's `evaluateRedisclosure`, and re-enters `cd_delivered` as `released`, not a second `entered`; 26.3 `computeDates` re-runs on the new consummation.
- A corrected CD at consummation (interest credit, a fee change) → 25.2's `scheduleCorrectedCd` is the owner's act; the worksheet is rebuilt from the new consummated version (rule 7a); a corrected CD that changes P&I after 30.2 staged the loan is 30.2's correction rule (a replaced snapshot) with `officer` approval, never an edit.
- Wet state, session before funds → the pre-signing subset (Discrepancies (6)) authorizes the wire the business day before; the disbursement authorization follows 26.2's execution review the same day; a failed review after the wire → 26.3's recall (`SM_O73_FUNDS_RETURN_2BD`) and `unwinding`.
- Paper note → 26.4's MOM registration and the custodian shipment under 27.1's bailee letter (`SM_WH_BAILEE_LETTER_GATE`, `SM_O74_ENDORSEMENT_BEFORE_SHIP_GATE`); certification is the custodian's; the pass tracks the carrier scans as `waiting_vendor{custodian}`.
- The eNote's registration rejected → 26.2's `reverseRegistration`/retry; the orchestration holds `held{gate_closed, MERS_PROC_ENOTE_REGISTER_1BD}` and the sev-1 is 26.2's.
- DU findings change after the CTC (23.1 `evaluateResubmission` from the flow) → `du.resubmission.required` re-enters `du_submitted` as `released`; the closing date is re-checked against `FNMA_B3_2_02_DU_CLOSE_BY_GATE` by 23.1.
- A purchase error in Loan Delivery (`delivery.purchase_error.observed`) → 29.4's `SM_LD_PURCHASE_ERROR_RESUBMIT_1BD`; the orchestration stays at `delivered`, 27.1's aging continues, the board shows the dwell day.
- The advice arrives before the bank credit → 29.4 and 30.1 run; 27.2's `provisionalMatch` is recorded; `purchase_reconciliations` waits for the third side; `SM_ORCH_PURCHASE_RECON_1BD` runs from the advice.
- The interest convention is unresolved for the partner → the reconciliation stores both expected figures (29.4 R3's range), matches on either, and locks the convention through 27.2 on the first match.
- `FAKE_REVIEWERS=off` in nonprod with no staff holder → the row waits `waiting_human{role}` with `clocked=false`; 35.7's unstaffed-role clock is the alarm; nothing auto-approves.
- The application funded through the old route with a fixture in nonprod → `funding_snapshots.fixture_used = true`, paths listed; the daily receipt's `fixture_used_today` is non-zero; 35.11's steward reports it; in production impossible (rule 6).
- A second `orchestration.open` for the same application → refused `ALREADY_OPEN` with the existing id; a `POST /fund` on an application whose orchestration is not at `funded` → refused `NOT_FUNDED` unless an `officer` supplies 26.3's facts as a correction.

### Open questions / decisions
1. Should the pre-signing funding subset be a new `op` on 26.3's `evaluateFundingConditions` or a separate 26.3 tool? **Default: `evaluateFundingConditions{op: pre_signing}` evaluating exactly the items 26.3 rule 6 names, satisfying `SM_O73_WET_FUNDS_AT_TABLE_GATE` when the wire is accepted before the session; 26.3's spec gains the row and the T-id; this process depends on it (T16).**
2. Should 23.3's `issueClearToClose` remain an agent act when every checklist item passes, or always wait for an `underwriting_reviewer`? **Default: as 23.3 states — the agent issues when every item is pass/n-a; a reviewer clears what 23.3 marks reviewer-only; nothing changes in 23.3.**
3. Should the daily receipt count a nonprod fixture fill as a finding? **Default: yes — `fixture_used_today` on the receipt and on 35.11's daily report; zero is the production invariant.**
4. Should the purchase reconciliation match on a range while the interest convention is unresolved? **Default: yes, on either convention's expected figure, and 27.2 locks the convention on the first match; an advice matching neither is an exception.**
5. Should the pass run as 35.3 jobs (one unit per open orchestration) instead of its own claim loop? **Default: its own claim loop inside the sweep for the per-minute pass (a job row per minute per application is noise), and a 35.3 cycle only for the daily receipt; revisit when the open book exceeds 5,000.**
6. 26.3's worked example 5 (lender credit $0.00) and 27.2's worked example C ($515.00) disagree on the OH fixture. **Default: $515.00 — 27.2's identity is the only source of the $831.00 retained residual; 26.3's example should be corrected to match, a 26.3 change this file does not make.**
