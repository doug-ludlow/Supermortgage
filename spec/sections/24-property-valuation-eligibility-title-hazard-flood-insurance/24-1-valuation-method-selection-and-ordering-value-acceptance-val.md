### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-10)

**1. The menu of valuation methods is set by DU.** Selling Guide B4-1.4-10, *Value Acceptance* (last updated 06/03/2026): value acceptance may be exercised only when "the final submission of the loan casefile to DU resulted in a value acceptance offer", "an appraisal is not obtained for the transaction", and "the value acceptance offer is not more than four months old on the date of the note and the mortgage"; "The lender may not exercise a value acceptance offer if an appraisal is obtained for the transaction"; "Lenders that elect to exercise value acceptance must include SFC 801 at delivery." Ineligible: "two- to four-unit properties", "co-op units and manufactured homes", "proposed construction", "construction-to-permanent loans (single-close and two-close)", "HomeStyle Renovation and HomeStyle Refresh loans", "leasehold properties", "Texas Section 50(a)(6) loans", "community land trusts or other properties with resale price restrictions", "transactions where either the purchase price or estimated value provided to DU is $1,000,000 or more", "transactions using gifts of equity", "DU loan casefiles that receive an Ineligible recommendation", "manually underwritten loans" (00a-fnma §4.12). B4-1.4-11, *Value Acceptance + Property Data* (12/10/2025): the collection "consists of a visual observation of the interior and exterior areas of the subject property", must "adhere to the Uniform Property Dataset (UPD)", include "photos and a floor plan conforming to the ANSI Standard", be performed by a "trained and vetted property data collector" who is "vetted through an annual background check" and "professionally trained", subject to the "Property Data Collector Independence Requirements"; the "property data collection is submitted to the Property Data API prior to the note date"; the lender represents the property "does not have safety, soundness, or structural integrity issues" and "does not have significant items of incomplete construction or renovation"; if the offer is lost "after the property data collection was obtained, in some cases it may be possible for the lender to provide the property data collection to an appraiser to perform a hybrid appraisal assignment"; "Lenders that elect to exercise value acceptance + property data must include Special Feature Code 774 at loan delivery." Investment properties qualifying with rental income are additionally ineligible for VA+PD. LTV/CLTV ceilings are **not** in either Guide topic; Fannie Mae's Value Acceptance page ("Updated September 2025") states: purchase and limited cash-out, principal residence and second home "up to 90% LTV/CLTV" (VA+PD purchase "up to program limits, LTV/CLTV up to 97% and up to 105% CLTV for Community Seconds®"); limited cash-out investment "up to 75% LTV/CLTV"; cash-out principal residence "up to 70% LTV/CLTV"; cash-out second home and investment "up to 60% LTV/CLTV" **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — product page, not Guide text; DU is the deciding authority and the platform must never pre-compute eligibility as if it were the rule]**. B4-1.2-02, *Desktop Appraisals* (12/10/2025): "DU will issue a message informing the lender they can choose to obtain an appraisal reported on Form 1004 Desktop"; eligible only for a "purchase transaction (including new construction)", "principal residence", "one-unit property (including those with an ADU and units in a PUD)", "LTV ratio less than or equal to 90%", "DU loan casefile that receives an Approve/Eligible recommendation"; ineligible: "two- to four-unit properties; condo and co-op units; manufactured homes", "second homes and investment properties; all refinances", "HomeReady, HomeStyle Renovation, and HomeStyle Refresh loans"; "The minimum scope of work for Form 1004 Desktop does not include a current inspection of the subject property"; "Form 1004 Desktop requires the same exhibits as traditional appraisals, plus a floor plan." B4-1.2-03, *Hybrid Appraisals* (12/10/2025): "An appraisal option requiring a property data collection" in which "the appraiser relies on the data collected (and other sources if needed) to identify property characteristics including condition"; requires the DU message "DU offers hybrid appraisals"; eligible for "purchase, limited cash-out refinance, or cash-out refinance", "existing one-unit properties, including a condo or unit in a PUD", "principal residence, second home, or investment properties", including "Texas 50(a)(6) loans" and "community land trusts"; the PDC must "comply with the Uniform Property Dataset and be delivered to the Fannie Mae Property Data API"; "The lender must share the property data collection with the appraiser at the time of engagement."; forms 1004 Hybrid and 1073 Hybrid. B4-1.2-01, *Appraisal Report Forms and Exhibits* (09/03/2025): Form 1004 for "traditional appraisals of one-unit properties and units in PUDs"; 1004C "traditional appraisals of one-unit manufactured homes"; 1073 "traditional appraisals of one-unit properties in condo projects"; 1025 "traditional appraisals of two- to four-unit properties"; 1004D "appraisal updates and/or completion reports for all one- to four-unit properties"; 2090 co-ops (out of scope); "Lenders using UAD 3.6 must follow the requirements in the UAD 3.6 Policy Supplement."

**2. UAD 3.6 and the redesigned URAR.** 00a-fnma §4.13 / 00b-orig F2, N5: limited production Sept 8, 2025 – Jan 25, 2026; broad production Jan 26, 2026 – Nov 2, 2026; UAD 3.6 is mandatory "for all new appraisal reports submitted to UCDP on or after November 2, 2026"; UAD 2.6 revisions are allowed "through the retirement date on May 3, 2027"; a UAD 3.6 report is "packaged in a Zip file and include: UAD 3.6 XML" plus PDF and images; under UAD 3.6 "Restricted Appraisal Update Reports and Completion Reports are no longer one report. They will be two separate reports" and "must be submitted in UAD 3.6 ZIP format". The UCDP Fannie Mae Messaging Guide for UAD 2.6 (June 2026) now carries fatal edit FNM0391: "This appraisal report was submitted in UAD 2.6 format and is not accepted. As of November 2, 2026, all new UCDP submissions must be in UAD 3.6 format; after this date, only revisions to previously submitted UAD 2.6 appraisal reports are permitted." SEL-2026-08 (09/02/2026) updated the UAD 3.6 Policy Supplement (highest and best use). Feature flag `uad.3_6_required = 2026-11-02` (00b-orig Part (c)4).

**3. Appraisal age and reuse.** B4-1.2-04, *Appraisal Age and Use Requirements* (06/04/2025): "When an appraisal is obtained, the property must be appraised within the 12 months prior to the date of the note and mortgage." "When the effective date of the original appraisal report is more than four months but less than 12 months from the date of the note and mortgage, the appraiser must perform an appraisal update that includes inspecting the exterior of the property and reviewing current market data to determine whether the property has declined in value since the date of the original appraisal report." "The appraisal update must occur within four months prior to the date of the note and mortgage." "When the effective date of the original appraisal report is more than 12 months from the date of the note and mortgage (with or without an appraisal update) a new appraisal report is required." "If the appraiser indicates on the Form 1004D that the property value has declined, then the lender must obtain a new appraisal for the property." Reuse on a subsequent transaction: "The subsequent transaction may only be a limited cash-out refinance. The age of the appraisal report must be less than 12 months from the note date of the subsequent transaction."; "The borrower and the lender/client must be the same on the original and subsequent transaction."; the lender "must ensure that the property has not undergone any significant remodeling, renovation, or deterioration". The topic is silent on transfers between lenders; that is AIR §6 (below).

**4. Appraiser independence (AIR) and lender responsibility.** Fannie Mae's AIR (page singlefamily.fanniemae.com/property-valuation/appraiser-independence-requirements; current text last revised by SEL-2023-07, Aug 2, 2023, which "clarify[ied] that mortgage brokers, loan officers/loan originators, and production staff are not allowed to order appraisals" and introduced the PDCIR "for loans applied for on and after November 1, 2023" **[VERIFIED — announcement; the AIR page itself prints no version number]**): §1.2 "No person is allowed to influence or attempt to influence the development, reporting, result, or review of an appraisal through coercion, extortion, collusion, compensation, inducement, intimidation, bribery, or any other manner", including "Requesting from or providing to an Independent Party comparable sales prior to engaging", "Requesting from or providing…an anticipated, estimated, encouraged, or desired value or value range", "Providing to an Independent Party a proposed or targeted loan amount", and "Conditioning the ordering of an appraisal report or the payment of an appraisal fee, salary, or bonus on the opinion, conclusion, or valuation"; §4.1 "The Seller must separate its sales and Mortgage production functions from its appraisal functions. An agent or employee of the Seller involved in the Seller's sales or Mortgage production function must have no involvement whatsoever in the operations of the Seller's appraisal functions."; §4.1.1 restricted parties: "All members of the Seller's Mortgage production staff", "Any person who is compensated on a commission basis upon the successful closing of a Mortgage, including, but not limited to, Mortgage Brokers, Loan Originators, and real estate agents", "Any person whose immediate supervisor is a member of the Mortgage production staff"; small-seller exception only where "absolute lines of independence…cannot be achieved because of the Seller's small size and limited staff"; §4.2 "The Seller or any third party specifically authorized by the Seller (including, but not limited to, appraisal companies, appraisal management companies, and Correspondent lenders) shall be responsible for selecting, retaining, and providing for payment of all compensation to the appraiser."; §3 "Promptly upon completion of an appraisal report, but in any event at least three business days prior to the closing of the Mortgage, the Seller must provide to the Borrower a copy of the appraisal report at no additional cost" (24.2 executes); §6 "The Seller may deliver to Fannie Mae a Mortgage with an appraisal prepared by an Independent Party selected by another lender…The Seller delivering the loan to Fannie Mae must make to Fannie Mae all representations and warranties regarding the appraisal"; §7 "If the Seller has a reasonable basis to believe an Independent Party is violating applicable laws, or is otherwise engaging in unethical conduct, the Seller shall promptly refer the matter to the applicable state appraiser certifying and licensing agency". B4-1.1-02, *Lender Responsibilities* (06/04/2025): the lender is responsible for "compliance with the Appraiser Independence Requirements (AIR)", for "successful submission of the appraisal through the UCDP prior to sale of the loan", and must ensure appraisals "do not contain subjective and prohibited language relating to discriminatory practices". B4-1.1-03, *Appraiser Selection Criteria* (06/04/2025): "Fannie Mae requires a lender (or its authorized agent) to use appraisers or supervisory appraisers that are state-licensed or state-certified" with "the requisite knowledge required to perform a professional quality appraisal for the specific geographic location and particular property type"; "The lender is responsible for the selection of appraisers and for the qualifications and quality of work provided by the appraisers"; lenders "may not use appraisals ordered or received by borrowers or other parties with an interest in the transaction"; "if a lender enters into a contract with any vendor, contractor, or third-party service provider, the lender is accountable for the quality of the work performed as if it was performed by an employee".

**5. Reg Z §1026.42 (eCFR current as of 9/04/2026).** (a) applies to "any consumer credit transaction secured by the consumer's principal dwelling"; (b) "covered person" = "a creditor with respect to a covered transaction or a person that provides 'settlement services'" — SM is a covered person in its own right; "valuation" = "an estimate of the value of the consumer's principal dwelling in written or electronic form, other than one produced solely by an automated model or system"; (c)(1) "no covered person shall or shall attempt to directly or indirectly cause the value assigned to the consumer's principal dwelling to be based on any factor other than the independent judgment of a person that prepares valuations, through coercion, extortion, inducement, bribery, or intimidation of, compensation or instruction to, or collusion with a person that prepares valuations or performs valuation management functions"; (c)(3) permits requesting consideration of additional information or comparables, substantiation, error correction, obtaining multiple valuations, and withholding compensation for breach/substandard performance; (d)(1) "No person preparing a valuation or performing valuation management functions for a covered transaction may have a direct or indirect interest, financial or otherwise, in the property or transaction"; (e) a creditor that "knows, at or before consummation, of a violation" of (c) or (d) "shall not extend credit based on the valuation, unless the creditor documents that it has acted with reasonable diligence" to verify accuracy; (f)(1) "the creditor and its agents shall compensate a fee appraiser for performing appraisal services at a rate that is customary and reasonable for comparable appraisal services performed in the geographic market of the property being appraised", with the (f)(2) market-rate and (f)(3) third-party-survey presumptions (the (f)(3) survey must exclude AMC-ordered fees); (g)(1) "Any covered person that reasonably believes an appraiser has not complied with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice or ethical or professional requirements for appraisers under applicable state or Federal statutes or regulations shall refer the matter to the appropriate state agency if the failure to comply is material"; (g)(2) "within a reasonable period of time after the person determines that there is a reasonable basis to believe" a reportable failure occurred; (h) cross-references the joint AMC minimum-requirements rules (12 CFR part 34); (i) implements "quality control standards for the use of automated valuation models in determining the value of collateral in connection with making a credit decision or covered securitization determination" for mortgage originators and secondary market issuers not supervised by another agency, with five required standards (confidence, data-manipulation protection, conflict avoidance, random testing, nondiscrimination) **[VERIFIED text; effective date not re-read today — UNVERIFIED]**.

**6. AMC registration.** 12 U.S.C. 3353(a): AMCs must "register with and be subject to supervision by a State appraiser certifying and licensing agency", "verify that only licensed or certified appraisers are used for federally related transactions", ensure "appraisals coordinated by an appraisal management company comply with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice" and "are conducted independently and free from inappropriate influence and coercion" per 15 U.S.C. 1639e; (d) "No appraisal management company may perform services related to a federally related transaction in a State after the date that is 36 months after the date on which the regulations…are prescribed in final form unless such company is registered"; (b) states may impose additional requirements. State AMC statutes (registration numbers, appraiser-panel notice rules, fee-disclosure rules, turn-time and payment-timing rules) vary and were not fetched **[UNVERIFIED — `jurisdiction_rules.amc` matrix to be populated by 31.1]**.

**7. TRID interaction.** §1026.19(e)(2)(i)(A): "Neither a creditor nor any other person may impose a fee on a consumer in connection with the consumer's application…before the consumer has received the disclosures required under paragraph (e)(1)(i) of this section and indicated to the creditor an intent to proceed"; only "a bona fide and reasonable fee for obtaining the consumer's credit report" is excepted (00a-fed §1.4). Tolerance: the 10% aggregate standard of (e)(3)(ii) applies only if "(B) The charge for the third-party service is not paid to the creditor or an affiliate of the creditor" and "(C) the creditor permits the consumer to shop for the third-party service, consistent with paragraph (e)(1)(vi)"; (e)(1)(vi)(A) "A creditor permits a consumer to shop for a settlement service if the creditor permits the consumer to select the provider of that service". Because AIR §4.2 and B4-1.1-03 reserve selection to the Seller/its agent, the consumer cannot shop for the appraisal, so a borrower-paid appraisal fee falls under the (e)(3)(i) zero-tolerance rule (eCFR §1026.19, 9/08/2026; Official Interpretation comment 19(e)(3)(i)-1 not reachable today — **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — rule text read, comment not read]**). Reg B §1002.14(a)(3): the creditor "may require applicants to pay a reasonable fee to reimburse the creditor for the cost of the appraisal" but "shall not charge an applicant for providing a copy" (00a-fed §3.3). The §1002.14(a)(2) appraisal notice (≤ 3 business days after application) is 21.3's `NTC_REGB_1002_14_APPRAISAL_NOTICE`; the HPML §1026.35(c)(5) statement ("We may order an appraisal to determine the property's value and charge you for this appraisal. We will give you a copy of any appraisal, even if your loan does not close." — eCFR 9/03/2026) is delivered "no later than the third business day after the creditor receives the consumer's application" and is carried by the same notice (21.3; 24.2 §HPML).

**Discrepancies vs blueprint**
1. The appraisal fee is **not** a 10% tolerance item: the consumer is never permitted to shop for it, so when borrower-paid it is zero-tolerance under §1026.19(e)(3)(i); under the default SM-borne economics it is not a consumer charge at all (disclosed on the CD as paid by others).
2. "B4-1.1 (AIR)" is inaccurate: AIR is a standalone Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac document referenced from B4-1.1-02 (last revised Aug 2, 2023); a parallel PDCIR governs property data collectors for applications on/after Nov 1, 2023.
3. "B4-1.3 (appraisal review)" belongs to 24.2; 24.1 stops at receipt of the report.
4. The blueprint's "update after 4 months" omits that the update itself must be performed "within four months prior to the date of the note" and that a "declined" answer forces a new appraisal; under UAD 3.6 the update is a separate Restricted Appraisal Update Report, not a 1004D.
5. Value-acceptance LTV limits are not Guide text; the Guide defers to DU. The desktop option is purchase-only (≤ 90% LTV, principal residence) — unavailable on every refinance, including the fixture.
6. VA+PD requires the Property Data API submission "prior to the note date", not "before closing" generally; the Property Data ID must survive into ULDD (29.3).
7. "AMC state laws" has a federal floor (12 U.S.C. 3353) that the row omits; and Reg Z §1026.42 also carries mandatory misconduct reporting (g), the conflict-of-interest rule (d), and AVM quality-control standards (i).
8. Transfers of appraisals between lenders are governed by AIR §6 (permitted with full reps and warranties), not by B4-1.2-04; and UCDP "does not allow a lender to reuse a Document File ID created from another lender's UCDP submission" (00b-orig F2), so a transferred report must be resubmitted under the partner (24.2).

### Operational prerequisites
- Partner: registered UCDP lender (both GSEs, same primary lender administrator), Property Data API product enabled on a partner-scoped Developer Portal app, DU casefiles under the partner seller number, written AIR/PDCIR policy adopted by the partner and contractually flowed to SM (Lender Contract; LL-2026-04 vendor governance). Lead time: inside the TSP window (2–6 months, 00b-orig Part (a)).
- SM: UCDP lender-agent registration under the partner; AMC/ordering-platform contract(s) with API access (00b-orig N5 — Reggora-class; specs contract-gated); PDC vendor(s) holding Property Data API integrator access; direct appraiser panel where an AMC is not used (panel management = "valuation management functions" under §1026.42(b), so SM staff/agents performing it must meet (d)); `valuation_fee_benchmarks` sourced per §1026.42(f)(3) (third-party survey excluding AMC fees) or (f)(2) market data, refreshed at least annually; ASC National Registry lookup; state AMC-registration evidence per state of the subject property (`amc_registrations`).
- Both: `rule_sets` `fnma.selling.2026-09-02`, `fnma.uad.3.6`, `regz.trid.2017`; feature flags `valuation.value_acceptance_default=true`, `uad.3_6_required=2026-11-02`, `valuation.order_before_itp=false`; `holiday_calendars` `creditor`.
- Licensing: any state treating appraisal management or "valuation management" by a non-AMC as licensed activity → 31.1 (`licensed_specialist` touchpoint).

#### State machine

`valuation_orders.status`: `method_pending` → (`selectMethod`) → `fee_gate_wait` → (ITP received or `fee_paid_by = sm`) → `ready_to_order` → (`placeOrder`) → `ordered` → `assigned` (AMC/panel acceptance; license + registration verified) → `inspection_scheduled` → `inspected` → `report_received` (terminal for 24.1; opens 24.2 `appraisals`) ; side exits: `cancelled` (terminal; reason), `reassigned` (new order row linked by `reassigned_from_order_id`), `expired` (12-month or offer-age breach), `update_required` (4-month rule → child order `appraisal_update`). Value acceptance path: `offer_recorded` → `offer_exercised` (set at `consummate`, guarded by `FNMA_B4_1_4_10_VALUE_ACCEPTANCE_OFFER_4M`) or `offer_lost` → `method_pending`. VA+PD path: `offer_recorded` → `pdc_ordered` → `pdc_collected` → `pdc_submitted` → `pdc_accepted` → `offer_exercised`; `pdc_collected` with `safety_issue_flag` → `converted_to_hybrid` (new order, PDC shared with the appraiser at engagement) or `converted_to_traditional`. Guards: `assertGateOpen(applicationId, 'REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE')` before any order whose `fee_paid_by = borrower`; no order while `applications.status` ∈ {withdrawn, denied}; no order by a restricted-party actor (`air_contact_log.actor_is_restricted_party` must be false — enforced at the service boundary, not only logged).

#### Business rules and calculations

**R1 — Method selection (executed on every `du.findings.received` and on property/transaction change).** Inputs: `offer_type`, DU messages, `application_properties` (units, type, leasehold, TX 50(a)(6), CLT, purchase price/estimated value ≥ $1,000,000, gift of equity), `hpml_determinations`, `purchase_contracts` (flip test), partner policy overlays (`rule_sets.partner.<id>.valuation`), known-condition facts (any document classified as inspection/repair/disaster damage → `known_condition_issue = true`). Ordering of preference (cost and speed): `value_acceptance` → `value_acceptance_pd` → `hybrid` → `desktop` → `traditional`. Hard exclusions: HPML that is not exempt under §1026.35(c)(2) → `traditional` (interior physical visit) and, if the flip test fires, a second `traditional` by a different appraiser (24.2 owns the HPML copy timer; 24.1 places both orders); `known_condition_issue` → no VA/VA+PD; any appraisal already obtained for the transaction → VA forbidden ("may not exercise a value acceptance offer if an appraisal is obtained"); desktop only when transaction_type = purchase ∧ occupancy = primary ∧ units = 1 ∧ LTV ≤ 90% ∧ not condo/MH ∧ not HomeReady; 2–4 units → 1025 traditional; condo → 1073 (or 1073 Hybrid); MH → 1004C (24.3 MH rules). The agent never computes VA eligibility from the LTV product-page table; it only reads the DU offer.

**R2 — Fee gate and disclosure.** `fee_items.appraisal_fee` is created at LE time (21.2) from `valuation_fee_benchmarks` (median for the form/county) rounded to the cent; `paid_by` defaults to `sm` (operating model: SM bears third-party origination costs) — then no fee is imposed on the consumer, the (e)(2)(i)(A) gate is not legally engaged, and the CD shows the charge in "Paid by Others" with the lender designation (25.2). When a partner program sets `paid_by = borrower`, the charge is Section B "Services You Cannot Shop For", `tolerance_class = zero`, and the order command is blocked until ITP. Either way the platform orders only after ITP (`valuation.order_before_itp=false`) so that an SM-borne cost is not incurred on a dead application.

**R3 — Customary and reasonable fee.** For each order: `fee_quote_cents` must satisfy `p25 ≤ quote ≤ p75` of the matching `valuation_fee_benchmarks` row, or carry an adjustment reason from the §1026.42(f)(2) list (property type, scope, turnaround, qualifications, experience, quality). An AMC-split invoice records the appraiser share separately; the appraiser share, not the gross, is tested. Illustrative fixture: Maricopa County, Form 1004 (UAD 3.6 URAR), benchmark median 60,000 cents, p25 52,500, p75 70,000; AMC quote 65,000 gross = 55,000 appraiser + 10,000 AMC → appraiser share 55,000 ∈ [52,500, 70,000] → pass; `fee_items.appraisal_fee.current_amount_cents = 65,000`.

**R4 — Worked example 1 (refinance fixture, traditional URAR).** Application Mon Oct 5, 2026 (six items 09:10 MST); DU submission #1 same day returns Approve/Eligible with `value_acceptance_offer = none` (DU's proprietary determination; not explained in findings). ITP recorded Tue Oct 6, 2026 08:40 MST → `REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE` open. `selectMethod` → `traditional`, `form_code = urar_uad36`, `uad_version = 3.6` (the first UCDP submission will occur before Nov 2, 2026, but the platform engages UAD 3.6 from Sept 8, 2026 onward — broad production is open and it removes the FNM0391 risk). Order placed Tue Oct 6 09:15 MST (`SM_VALUATION_ORDER_SLA_1BD` due Wed Oct 7 → satisfied). AMC assigns a certified residential appraiser (AZ license verified, ASC check Oct 6) Wed Oct 7 (`SM_VALUATION_ASSIGN_SLA_2BD` due Thu Oct 8 → satisfied). Inspection Sat Oct 10 (`SM_VALUATION_INSPECT_SLA_7CD` due Wed Oct 14 → satisfied; Columbus Day Mon Oct 12 is irrelevant to a calendar-day timer). Report delivered Thu Oct 15 (`SM_VALUATION_REPORT_SLA_10CD` due Fri Oct 16 → satisfied); `effective_date = 2026-10-10`; `age_4m_update_after = 2027-02-10`; `age_12m_expires_on = 2027-10-10`. Consummation Fri Nov 6, 2026 is 27 calendar days after the effective date → no update. Had the closing slipped to Tue Feb 16, 2027, the effective date would be 4 months + 6 days old → a Restricted Appraisal Update Report (UAD 3.6) dated within Oct 16, 2026 – Feb 16, 2027 is required; "declined" → new appraisal. Fee: `paid_by = sm`; 65,000 cents posted to `third_party_costs` on `valuation.received`; the CD lists "Appraisal Fee $650.00" under Paid by Others (L).

**R5 — Worked example 2 (purchase fixture, value acceptance + property data).** Application Mon Oct 19, 2026; DU #1 same day: Approve/Eligible, `value_acceptance_offer = value_acceptance_pd` (purchase, principal residence, 90% LTV, $412,000, Columbus OH, no gift of equity). `offer_issued_at = 2026-10-19` → `offer_expires_on = 2027-02-19` (must be ≥ note date). ITP Tue Oct 20 → `pdc.ordered` Oct 20 10:05 EDT to the PDC vendor (collector background check dated Mar 3, 2026, < 12 months; PDCIR attestation on file). Collection Fri Oct 23 (interior + exterior, ANSI floor plan, images); `safety_issue_flag = false`. Vendor submits to the Property Data API Mon Oct 26; `property_data_id_fnma` returned → `pdc.accepted` → `FNMA_B4_1_4_11_PDC_API_SUBMIT_GATE` satisfied (Oct 26 < note date Wed Nov 18). Closing Wed Nov 18 is 30 days after the offer → `FNMA_B4_1_4_10_VALUE_ACCEPTANCE_OFFER_4M` satisfied; delivery carries SFC 774 and the Property Data ID (29.3). Counter-case: DU resubmission on Fri Nov 6 (loan amount raised $500 within B3-2-10 tolerance but occupancy changed to second home) returns `offer = none` → `valuation.offer.lost`; R1 selects `hybrid` (1004 Hybrid), the PDC is shared with the appraiser at engagement (B4-1.2-03), order Fri Nov 6, report by Mon Nov 16 (10 calendar days) — the Nov 18 closing holds only if 24.2 review and the Reg B copy timing (3 business days before consummation → copy by Fri Nov 13) are met.

**R6 — Offer-age arithmetic.** `offer_expires_on = add_months(offer_issued_at, 4)`: Oct 19, 2026 → Feb 19, 2027; Oct 31, 2026 → Feb 28, 2027 (clamped). `consummate` asserts `note_date ≤ offer_expires_on`.

**R7 — Transfer-in of an appraisal from another lender.** Allowed only when: the partner (as Seller) records an AIR attestation covering the original engagement (`transfer_air_attestation_document_id`), the report is < 12 months old at the note date, the original lender/client is named on the report (the platform stores it as-is; no re-addressing), the report is resubmitted to UCDP under the partner to obtain the partner's own Doc File ID (24.2), and all `rule_sets.fnma.selling` review checks pass. `transferred_from_lender = true` is delivered nowhere but is retained for QC.

#### Integrations
- `amc` adapter (per vendor; MISMO 3.x order/status/deliver where offered; otherwise vendor JSON): outbound `createOrder` (idempotency key = `order_id`), `cancelOrder`, `addDocument` (purchase contract, amendments, PDC), `getStatus`; inbound webhooks `assigned`, `scheduled`, `inspected`, `delivered` (UAD 3.6 ZIP: XML + PDF + images), `revised`, `fee_changed` (must be re-tested under R3). Retry with exponential back-off; on outage > 4 business hours, `fnma_portal_operator`-style manual placement through the AMC portal from an AI-prepared order sheet.
- `fnma-property-data` (Developer Portal REST; OAuth ROPC per 00b-svc F14; partner-scoped System ID): PDC vendor submits directly (supplier-of-record pattern, 00b-orig Part (c)5) and SM receives `property_data_id`; where SM is the integrator, `POST` the UPD payload, poll status, persist ID and rejection messages.
- `fnma-du` (23.1): consume offer messages; on `valuation.received`, 23.1 resubmits with the appraised value.
- ASC National Registry: license lookup (public site; machine interface **[UNVERIFIED]** — scrape is prohibited only for Fannie Mae UIs; a vendor feed is acceptable); state AMC registries: manual evidence upload with expiry.
- Borrower channels (`intake` agent voice/SMS/portal) for inspection access scheduling — content restricted to scheduling; the `valuation` agent never discusses value with the borrower except to deliver 24.2 notices.
- UCDP/UAD Compliance API: 24.2.

#### Outputs and artifacts

`valuation_orders` and child rows; engagement letter (document kind `appraisal_engagement`, template versioned, carries scope, form, UAD version, AIR/PDCIR clauses, fee, turn-time; retention `fnma_loan_file_life_plus_4y`); AIR attestation record per order; `air_contact_log` (exportable); PDC package and Property Data ID; the appraisal report as received (handed to 24.2 `appraisals`); ledger: `third_party_costs` debit / `accounts_payable_vendor` credit on `valuation.received` (SM-borne) or `origination_fees_receivable` when borrower-paid; `fee_items.appraisal_fee` baseline and current amounts; ULDD data points at 29.3: SFC 801 or 774, Property Data ID, appraisal form/UAD version, appraiser license; reports: turn-time, fee-benchmark compliance, AIR contact audit, offer-loss rate.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)

`valuation` agent (package `agents/valuation`; separate service identity from `intake`, `pricing`, `disclosure`, `underwriter`). Tools: `readDuOffer`, `selectMethod`, `assertFeeGate`, `selectVendor` (rotation across panel/AMC by geo-competency and turn-time, never by prior values), `verifyAppraiserLicense`, `verifyAmcRegistration`, `benchmarkFee`, `placeOrder`, `scheduleInspection`, `trackOrder`, `submitPropertyData`, `orderAppraisalUpdate`, `logContact`, `writeDecision`, `fileEscalation`. End-to-end behavior: on each trigger it re-runs R1, records a decision, opens the order the same business day, monitors SLAs, converts methods on offer loss or safety flags, and hands the received file to 24.2. Decision record (`agent_decisions`): `{application_id, order_id, offer_type, du_submission_id, exclusions_fired[], method, form_code, uad_version, vendor, fee_quote_cents, benchmark_row_id, fee_test, license_check, amc_check, rule_set_version, model_version, prompt_version, rationale, confidence}`. Guardrails (never alone, never at all): no value, value range, target LTV, loan amount, DU estimated value or "needed" value in any outbound payload (order-payload allowlist enforced by schema: address, legal description, unit count, occupancy, transaction type, access contact, purchase contract and amendments, HOA contact, PDC file, scope/form); no comparables sent before engagement; no fee conditioned on value; no vendor exclusion for "low values"; no order initiated by a restricted-party actor; no order before ITP when borrower-paid; no appraisal accepted from the borrower, seller, agent or another interested party. Escalations: `officer` for §1026.42(g)/AIR §7 referrals and for vendor termination; `licensed_specialist` where `jurisdiction_rules` require; `human_agent` on borrower request (scheduling only); `appraiser`/`property_data_collector` are external performers, not approvers. Borrower-facing disclosure: every voice/chat contact opens with the AI disclosure (baseline §8.10); ROV rights are disclosed with the report copy (24.2). Human path: ops console with the same order sheet and manual placement, all actions logged to `air_contact_log` with the operator's role; production users have no console access.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- Offer lost on DU resubmission (loan amount, occupancy, DTI, VantageScore-model switch): re-run R1 within the same run; if a PDC exists → hybrid; notify 21.5 of a changed-circumstance candidate only if a borrower-paid fee changes.
- Borrower refuses interior access for a PDC/appraisal: two documented attempts, then escalate to `human_agent`; VA+PD cannot be exercised without the PDC → method conversion is impossible without access → application stalls with NOIA logic in 21.6.
- Appraiser license lapses or ASC shows discipline between assignment and inspection: reassign; if after inspection, 24.2 review decides; log for AIR §7.
- AMC not registered in the property state: block; use a registered AMC or the direct panel.
- Disaster declaration (FEMA) between effective date and note date: `known_condition_issue = true`; VA/VA+PD blocked; a post-disaster inspection (1004D/UAD 3.6 completion or exterior update) ordered (24.3 owns the disaster policy cross-reference).
- Closing slips past the 4-month mark: automatic `appraisal_update` order; past 12 months: new appraisal; VA offer older than 4 months at note date: DU resubmission for a fresh offer (23.1) — if none, appraisal.
- UAD 2.6 deliverable received for an order placed for first UCDP submission on/after Nov 2, 2026: reject at receipt; re-engage; vendor scorecard.
- Report arrives addressed to the wrong lender/client (partner name missing): return for correction — the partner must be the lender/client for reuse rules and Reg B copies.
- Purchase contract amended after order (price, concessions, repairs): send the amendment through `addDocument` the same day; never the DU value.
- Appraiser asks "what value do you need?": scripted refusal, logged, counted toward the vendor's AIR score.
- HPML flip: second appraisal by a different appraiser; only one fee may be charged to the consumer.
- Rural market with no panel coverage: AMC broadcast; fee adjustment under (f)(2) with reason; SLA extension logged; lock-expiry warning to 21.4.
- Vendor outage: manual placement path; idempotency prevents duplicates when the API recovers.
- Withdrawal/denial after order: `cancelOrder`; trip fee (if any) posted to `third_party_costs`; never billed to the borrower unless borrower-paid and the fee was disclosed.

#### Test cases and acceptance criteria
- **24.1-T1** Given ITP received Tue Oct 6, 2026 08:40 MST and `fee_paid_by = borrower`, when the agent attempts `placeOrder` at 08:30 MST, then the command is refused with `REGZ_1026_19E2_INTENT_FEE_GATE` closed; at 08:41 it succeeds.
- **24.1-T2** Given the refinance fixture with `offer_type = none`, when R1 runs, then `method = traditional`, `form_code = urar_uad36`, `desktop` is excluded with reason "refinance", and a decision record with the exclusion list is written.
- **24.1-T3** Given order Tue Oct 6, assignment Wed Oct 7, inspection Sat Oct 10 and report Thu Oct 15, 2026, then `SM_VALUATION_ORDER_SLA_1BD`, `_ASSIGN_SLA_2BD`, `_INSPECT_SLA_7CD` and `_REPORT_SLA_10CD` are all satisfied and `age_4m_update_after = 2027-02-10`, `age_12m_expires_on = 2027-10-10`.
- **24.1-T4** Given effective date Oct 10, 2026 and a note date of Feb 16, 2027, when `consummate` is asserted, then `FNMA_B4_1_2_04_APPRAISAL_UPDATE_4M` blocks until an update dated between Oct 16, 2026 and Feb 16, 2027 with "not declined" is received; a "declined" update forces `method_pending`.
- **24.1-T5** Given the purchase fixture with `value_acceptance_pd` offered Oct 19, 2026 and PDC accepted Oct 26, when the note date is Nov 18, 2026, then both `FNMA_B4_1_4_10_VALUE_ACCEPTANCE_OFFER_4M` (expiry Feb 19, 2027) and `FNMA_B4_1_4_11_PDC_API_SUBMIT_GATE` are satisfied and delivery data carries SFC 774 and the Property Data ID.
- **24.1-T6** Given a PDC with `safety_issue_flag = true` (collector reports active roof leak), then VA+PD is blocked, `converted_to_hybrid` is recorded, the PDC is attached to the hybrid engagement, and the lender representation rule is cited in the decision.
- **24.1-T7** Given an outbound order payload containing `estimated_value` or `loan_amount`, then schema validation rejects the payload before transmission and an `air.guardrail.blocked` audit row is written.
- **24.1-T8** Given an AMC quote whose appraiser share is 45,000 cents against a benchmark p25 of 52,500 with no (f)(2) adjustment reason, then `benchmarkFee` fails and the order is held for vendor re-quote.
- **24.1-T9** Given an appraiser whose AZ license expires Oct 8, 2026 and an assignment attempt on Oct 9, then `SM_APPRAISER_LICENSE_GATE` blocks and the order is reassigned.
- **24.1-T10** Given an order placed Oct 20, 2026 whose first UCDP submission will occur Nov 3, 2026, when the vendor delivers a UAD 2.6 file, then receipt is rejected with reference FNM0391 and a re-engagement is issued.
- **24.1-T11** Given a DU resubmission that loses the VA offer on Nov 6, 2026 for the purchase fixture, then `valuation.offer.lost` fires, R1 selects `hybrid`, and the order is placed the same business day.

#### Audit and evidence

Per order: decision record with exclusions and rule-set version; engagement letter and payload hash (proving no value information was sent); `air_contact_log` export; license/ASC and AMC-registration snapshots; fee benchmark row and test result; SLA timer history; PDC package with collector vetting evidence and Property Data ID; offer-age and appraisal-age computations; misconduct-referral file (if any) signed by the `officer`. These satisfy Fannie Mae QC/MORA requests on AIR/PDCIR compliance (A2-2-07/A3-2-01 self-assessment items), CFPB §1026.42 examinations (independence, (f) compensation, (g) reporting), and state AMC audits.

### Open questions / decisions
1. **Fee economics.** Is the appraisal fee always SM-borne (`paid_by = sm`) or may partner programs pass it to the borrower? Default: SM-borne; borrower-paid allowed per partner program with zero tolerance and the ITP gate enforced.
2. **Direct panel vs AMC-only.** Running a direct panel makes SM a performer of "valuation management functions" (§1026.42(b)) and, in some states, a registrant. Default: AMC-only at launch; panel behind feature flag `valuation.direct_panel=false` pending 31.1's state matrix.
3. **UAD 3.6 engagement start.** Default: engage UAD 3.6 for every order placed on/after Sept 8, 2026 rather than waiting for Nov 2, 2026, to avoid FNM0391 rejections on late submissions.
4. **Fee-benchmark source.** §1026.42(f)(3) third-party survey (must exclude AMC-ordered fees) vs (f)(2) internal market data. Default: licensed third-party survey refreshed annually, supplemented by (f)(2) adjustments with logged reasons.
5. **"Reasonable period" for §1026.42(g)(2).** Default 30 calendar days from determination (`REGZ_1026_42G_MISCONDUCT_REFERRAL_30`).
6. **AVM quality-control (§1026.42(i)) scope.** Whether reliance on DU value acceptance is the partner's "use" of an AVM in a credit decision. Default: treat SM's own AVMs (pre-qualification, ROV analytics) as in scope and document DU reliance in the partner's policy; confirm the rule's effective date and coverage in 31.2.
