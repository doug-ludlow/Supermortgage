### Verified requirement (as of 2026-09-09)

**Servicing Guide E-4.5-01, Filing MI Claims for Conventional Mortgage Loans or for Other Mortgage Loans for which Fannie Mae Bears the Risk of Loss (07/09/2025; SVC-2025-04).** Before filing, the servicer consults the **MI Claims Portal Participants Exhibit** to determine whether the insurer participates in MICP and the participation effective date by liquidation date. **Fannie Mae files the claim** when "the mortgage insurer participates in MICP and the liquidation date is on or after the insurer's MICP participation effective date"; the **servicer files** when the insurer does not participate or the liquidation date precedes the effective date. When Fannie Mae files, the servicer must monitor its systems "for accurate insurer identity and claim deadlines," report inaccurate MICP information to Fannie Mae, "upload required claim documents to MICP within timeframes specified by MI master policy," and provide all liquidation and MI coverage information through investor reporting (C-4.3-01). When the servicer files, it must "file primary MI claims ensuring proceeds route directly to Fannie Mae" and follow F-1-06's "Ensuring Timely Settlement." The servicer must **reimburse Fannie Mae** for losses caused by: failure to supply timely/accurate documents; claims denied, delayed or curtailed because of servicer reporting failures; failure to timely submit expense reimbursement requests; expenses disallowed or curtailed under the master policy; interest cut-off due to improper conveyance procedures; and premiums/surcharges netted against claim benefits.

**F-1-06, Filing an MI Claim for a Liquidated Mortgage Loan or Acquired Property (05/13/2026; SVC-2026-04, SVC-2025-02).** *Short sale/third-party sale:* remove the loan with action code 71; report proceeds (357 short sale, 311 TPS); if the MI participates and the liquidation date is on/after its effective date, upload to MICP "all required claim documents … no later than **10 business days prior to the required claim filing deadline**" (final settlement valuation, property valuation, short-payoff approval letter with terms, other necessary information); otherwise file the claim "within **30 days after remitting** the applicable receipt code to Fannie Mae" and supply the insurer the same documents; submit the final expense reimbursement request "no later than 30 days following the settlement." *Mortgage Release/foreclosure (acquired property):* confirm the REOgram (E-4.1-01), remove the loan with action code 72, then either upload to MICP "a copy of the valuation for an executed Mortgage Release and any other information related to or necessary for the filing of a claim no later than 10 business days prior to the required claim filing deadline," or, for non-participants, file "within **30 days after the settlement of the Mortgage Release or the date of the foreclosure sale** or, in states that have redemption periods after the foreclosure sale, within 30 days after the redemption period expiration" (court-order jurisdictions: the docket-entry date); final expense request within 30 days on the same anchors. *Ensuring timely settlement:* when the servicer files directly it "must follow up with the mortgage insurer to ensure that the claim is settled in a timely manner"; the insurer "is contractually required to pay the claim within a specified period after it receives all required documentation. This period is usually 60 days"; if Fannie Mae does not receive the proceeds before the contractual period ends, "Fannie Mae may exercise any of its remedies, including but not limited to requiring the servicer to advance its own funds to pay the claim amount due Fannie Mae" (A1-3-02), and may require the documentation-complete date, follow-up dates and the insurer's responses. When Fannie Mae files, "the servicer must continue to monitor the MICP and inform Fannie Mae to ensure all necessary information is available and reflected accurately." Agreements with insurers affecting loss-claim settlements need prior written SF CPM approval.

**MICP (Fannie Mae page; FAQs 07/24/2025; Servicer User Guide 07/24/2025; Participants Exhibit June 1, 2025).** "An innovative online tool that MI Companies, servicers, and Fannie Mae will use to file MI claims" with "published due dates," standardized documentation and multi-party messaging; access via Technology Manager through each servicer's MICP administrator; **UI only** (drag-and-drop bulk of up to 10 documents; no API). The servicer works from the Dashboard's "Pending Doc Requests" and "Open Communications," acknowledging document requests (each with a Due Date and a "Potential Doc Denial Date") and uploading documents; "lack of activity could result in a denial." Participants and effective dates: Arch, Essent, MGIC, UGI — Oct. 1, 2021; Radian — Oct. 18, 2021; Enact, PMI — Nov. 15, 2021; NMI — Oct. 1, 2022 (RMIC merged into UGI June 1, 2025). Fannie Mae approves payments, including pro-rata premium refunds (00b F9).

**Master policy terms (exemplar: MGIC Master Policy 71-70384 (03/20) and MGIC Loan Servicing Guide, Mar. 12, 2026; other insurers' GSE-aligned policies are structurally similar — [UNVERIFIED per insurer; load each master policy version into `mi_master_policy_terms`]).** Claim deadline: "within 60 days of the first to occur" of completion of the foreclosure sale ("regardless of whether certification, confirmation or ratification of the sale has occurred"), conveyance by deed in lieu, or closing of a third-party sale (§64(b)); a late claim excludes interest and advances after the deadline and one more than 120 days late "may [be] den[ied]" (§65). **Perfected Claim** = all required information/documents received (and property access if requested); **Claim Settlement Period** = "60 days following the Perfected Claim Date" (§1); within 20 days of filing the insurer notifies of missing items and, if not received within 30 days, gives further direction. **Claim Amount** = unpaid principal + "accrued interest at the Contract Rate from the date of Default through the date" of the sale/DIL/TPS (§70) + advances for hazard/flood premiums, taxes/assessments, property maintenance/protection, condo/HOA fees, court costs and attorneys' fees (capped at 3% of UPB for loans ≥ $200,000; the lesser of $6,000 or 5% for loans < $200,000 — §56(e)) − credits (escrow balances, rents, insurance proceeds, physical-damage reductions, borrower funds — §§77–79); interest and advances are covered for no more than **36 months**. Settlement options: **Percentage Option** (coverage % × Claim Amount), **Acquisition Option** (insurer pays the full Claim Amount and takes title), **Third-Party Sale option** (Net Proceeds — gross proceeds less reasonable closing costs — offset the benefit; 60-day extension to close), **Anticipated Loss** (unrestored property). **Supplemental claim** for advances omitted from the initial claim "within 90 days after completion" of the sale/DIL/TPS, paid "within 60 days of our receipt" (§62(f)–(g)). Curtailment: late Notice of Default (due "no later than the 25th day of the month in which the Borrower's second consecutive missed payment remains unpaid," monthly updates thereafter) excludes interest/advances between the NOD deadline and actual notice (§53); material servicing failures reduce the Claim Amount by the "estimated resulting damage" (§23(a)); foreclosure delays beyond the state time frame (the Fannie Mae Foreclosure Time Frames exhibit) are covered only if "the delay occurred despite diligent servicing"; property-condition reductions; appeals to `appeals@mgic.com` (§§91–92; timeframes not extracted **[UNVERIFIED]**); rescission relief at 36/60 months (§17). The Insurance Benefit is payable to the insured/servicer only with the GSE Beneficiary's consent (§42(c)) — in practice to Fannie Mae.

**Related Guide rules.** Bidding on MI-insured loans: "the servicer must bid an amount approved by the mortgage insurer" (E-3.3-05); MI renewal premiums must be kept paid through the month of the liquidation event (F-1-05; Section 10); investor-reporting removal codes 71/72 and LPI/UPB accuracy feed Fannie Mae's claim (5.3); E-4.3-04 supplemental claims for servicer-counsel eviction costs; A1-3-02 make-whole/indemnification for servicer-caused claim losses; A1-4.2-02 compensatory fees for liquidation delays (13.x) — the same delays that curtail MI claims.

**Other law.** HPA/Reg X do not address claims; the FCRA/Reg V furnishing of the foreclosure (Section 8) is unaffected; state insurance law governs claim handling and appeals (unfair-claims-settlement statutes); MI rescission for misrepresentation triggers repurchase/indemnification (Selling Guide; 5.6), not this process.

**Discrepancies with the blueprint row:** "MI Claims Portal" is right but the row misses that **Fannie Mae, not the servicer, files** for every major insurer since 2021–22, making the servicer's obligations documentary (≥10 BD before the deadline), data-accuracy and monitoring; the deadlines are concrete (30-day direct filing; master-policy 60/120/60/90-day rules; 36-month cap); the row omits the servicer's reimbursement liability for claim shortfalls and the expense-request coupling (30 days). Automation is (a) for computation, document assembly and monitoring, (b) only for MICP/insurer-portal uploads.

#### Business rules and calculations
1. **Routing.** `filer = fnma_micp` iff `mi_master_policy_terms.micp_participant` and `liquidation_date ≥ micp_effective_date`; else `servicer_direct`. Liquidation date = foreclosure sale date, Mortgage Release settlement, short-sale closing or TPS completion (the Exhibit keys on liquidation date). The insurer identity is validated three ways: `mi_policies.insurer_code`, the MI fields on the REOgram case (15.1, editable 5 BD) and the MICP claim record; any mismatch → `FNMA_E4501_MICP_DATA_CORRECTION_1BD`.
2. **Deadlines.** `claim_filing_deadline = claim_anchor_date + claim_filing_days` (calendar); `micp_docs_due_at = claim_filing_deadline − 10 fannie_et BD`; direct-filing due = the F-1-06 30-day anchor (earlier than the policy's 60 days — Fannie Mae's rule governs), so `direct_file_due_at = anchor + 30 CD` where the anchor is the sale/DIL settlement, the 311/357 remittance date for TPS/short sale, the redemption expiry in redemption states, or the docket entry in court-order states. The platform always targets `SM_MICP_DOCS_TARGET_15`.
3. **Mandatory document package (assembled from the ledger and case files; insurer-specific list overlays).** Payment history from origination through the claim date; servicing notes/collection chronology and borrower correspondence; workout/bankruptcy history; property valuations and bidding instructions; foreclosure deed or Mortgage Release approval and deed; TPS evidence or short-sale approval letter + settlement statement; itemized advances with invoices (taxes, hazard/flood, HOA, preservation, inspections, attorney fees/costs); insurance evidence; eviction costs (if servicer counsel); the insurer's Claim for Loss form (direct path; e.g., MGIC 71-20019). Origination-file items only on request. All documents are hashed in `documents` and the manifest is attached to the decision record.
4. **Shadow claim computation (percentage option).** `interest = Σ_{months from interest_paid_to through anchor} round(UPB × note_rate ÷ 12)` (per-month rounding, consistent with the amortization schedule) `+ stub_days × round(UPB × note_rate ÷ 365, 4)` rounded to cents, capped at 36 months from the default date; `claimable_advances` = taxes/assessments + hazard/flood premiums (net of refunds received) + HOA + preservation/inspections + attorney fees & costs (capped: UPB ≥ $200,000 → 3% of UPB; else min($6,000, 5% of UPB)) — MI premiums and technology fees excluded; `credits` = escrow balance + rents + insurance proceeds + borrower funds + physical-damage reduction; `claim_amount = UPB + interest + claimable_advances − credits`; `benefit = round_half_up(claim_amount × coverage_pct)`. TPS option: `loss = claim_amount − net_proceeds`; `benefit = min(percentage benefit, loss)` **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED — insurer-specific mechanics]**. The shadow is reconciled to the insurer's EOB; variances > $250 or > 0.5% → analysis and, where servicer-caused, `servicer_caused_shortfall_cents`.
   *Worked example (fixture L15, REO, 25% BPMI with Radian → MICP participant since Oct. 18, 2021, so Fannie Mae files).* Sale Tue Oct 5, 2027 (anchor); `claim_filing_deadline` = Sat Dec 4, 2027; `micp_docs_due_at` = 10 `fannie_et` BD earlier = **Fri Nov 19, 2027** (Dec 3, 2, 1, Nov 30, 29, 26, 24, 23, 22, 19 — Nov 25 Thanksgiving); internal target Oct 20, 2027; final 571 by Nov 4, 2027 (15.2). UPB $249,088.61; interest at 6.25% from the paid-to date Nov 1, 2026 to Oct 5, 2027: 11 × $1,297.34 = $14,270.74 + 4 days × $42.6522 = $170.61 → **$14,441.35** (11 months < 36-month cap); advances: taxes $4,820.00; hazard $1,450.00 − refund $659.45 = $790.55; inspections $270.00; preservation $445.00; attorney fee $2,300.00 + costs $565.00 = $2,865.00 (cap 3% × 249,088.61 = $7,472.66 ✓); MI premiums $1,369.94 and tech fees $30.00 excluded; credits $0 (escrow negative, no rents). `claim_amount` = 249,088.61 + 14,441.35 + 4,820.00 + 790.55 + 270.00 + 445.00 + 2,865.00 = **$272,720.51**; `benefit` = 25% = **$68,180.13**, payable to Fannie Mae. If the third-party bid of $270,000 had prevailed (TPS option): loss = 272,720.51 − 270,000.00 = $2,720.51 → benefit ≈ $2,720.51.
5. **Curtailment monitor (daily, from day 60 of delinquency).** `risk_score` from: NOD lateness (days after the 25th → interest/advances for that gap excluded), monthly status gaps, `projected_excess_days = fcl_days_used − (fcl_days_allowable + allowable_delays)` from 13.x (each excess day ≈ one day's note-rate interest at risk: UPB × rate ÷ 365 — for L15 $42.65/day), interest months vs the 36-month cap, property-condition flags (9.8/9.9), premium-paid-through gaps, document readiness. Score ≥ 0.6 → `foreclosure-ops` task to document diligent-servicing evidence (court delays, bankruptcy, mediation) so the delay is "despite diligent servicing"; the same evidence feeds the A1-4.2-02 compensatory-fee rebuttal.
6. **Direct filing (non-participants).** File on the insurer's form via its portal/EDI/e-mail with instructions to pay the GSE Beneficiary (Fannie Mae) per SF CPM payee instructions; log acknowledgment; respond to missing-document notices within the insurer's 20/30-day windows; follow up weekly; if unpaid by `settlement_due_at`, escalate to `officer` with the documentation-complete date, follow-up dates and insurer responses (F-1-06) — Fannie Mae may require the partner to advance the claim amount.
7. **Proceeds.** Fannie Mae-filed claims are paid to Fannie Mae — no custodial entry; record the EOB and close. If a benefit arrives at the servicer, remit as a special remittance within 2 BD (`SM_MI_PROCEEDS_REMIT_2BD`) — code per SF CPM guidance. Pro-rata borrower-contribution sharing (short sales) is Section 12's 324 flow.
8. **Supplemental claims.** Post-claim advances (late tax bills, servicer-counsel eviction costs under E-4.3-04, HOA) are assembled into a supplemental claim before `supplemental_due_at`; for MICP loans the supplemental is filed by Fannie Mae from our uploaded documents/expense data — upload by `supplemental_due_at − 10 BD`.
9. **Shortfall attribution.** Curtailed/denied amounts are classified by cause: servicer-caused (late NOD, late documents, missing expense request, foreclosure delay without diligence evidence, improper conveyance, premium netting) → `servicer_caused_shortfall_cents` and an A1-3-02 exposure record for the partner; insurer/other → appeal or accept. Root causes feed the Compliance Sentinel's monthly report.
10. **Rescission.** A rescission for origination misrepresentation → 5.6/Selling-Guide repurchase path (`officer`), not an appeal by this process, unless the servicing file evidences rescission-relief eligibility (36/60-month rules) — then appeal.

- **MICP (portal-only).** `human_portal_task` types `micp.docs.upload` (package + manifest + claim id + due date), `micp.doc_request.respond`, `micp.message.reply`, `micp.appeal.submit`; the operator records request ids, due dates, "Potential Doc Denial Date" and status from the Dashboard/Repository into `mi_claims`/`mi_claim_documents` (structured form in `ops-console`); no scraping. E-mail notifications (if MICP sends them) parsed by `email-in` **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **`mi/*` adapters** (non-participants): portal/EDI X12/e-mail claim filing per insurer (MGIC/Link, EDI, SFT; Essent LM Online) **[vendor-specific]**; acknowledgments and EOBs ingested; sandbox stubs per insurer.
- **`fnma-lsdu`/servicing events** (5.3) — the 71/72 removal data Fannie Mae uses; **`fnma-p360`** (15.1/15.2) — REOgram MI fields, 571 expenses; **`fnma-crs`** — proceeds remittance if misdirected.
- Failure modes: MICP outage near `micp_docs_due_at` → attempt daily, screenshot, message Fannie Mae's MI claims contact (F-4-02) with the package hash; insurer portal outage → e-mail filing with proof of transmission.

#### Outputs and artifacts
- `mi_claims`, `mi_claim_calculations`, `mi_claim_documents`, `mi_curtailment_risk`, `mi_master_policy_terms`, claim packages (hashed), EOBs, appeal letters, shortfall/exposure records, decision records.

#### AI agent design (AI-first)
`claims-reo` agent with `pmi` (policy/insurer data, default reporting evidence) and `foreclosure-ops` (timeline evidence). Tools: `routeMiClaim`, `computeShadowClaim`, `assembleMiPackage`, `openPortalTask`, `fileDirectClaim` (adapter), `trackDocRequests`, `reconcileEob`, `scoreCurtailmentRisk`, `draftAppeal`, `attributeShortfall`, `recordDecision`. Decision record: `{claim_id, loan_id, insurer, filer, participant_effective_date, liquidation_date, anchors{deadline, docs_due, direct_due}, package_manifest[], shadow_calc_version, expected_benefit, eob{benefit, curtailments[]}, variance, attribution, confidence}`. Guardrails: never let `micp_docs_due_at` pass without an `officer` alert at −3 BD; never upload origination-file documents unless requested; never concede a curtailment as servicer-caused without the timeline evidence review; appeals are drafted by the agent and approved by a `human_agent` reviewer when the amount > $10,000. Escalations: `fnma_portal_operator` (MICP/insurer portals); `officer` (advance-claim demands, make-whole, rescission); `attorney` (rescission disputes, conveyance defects). Toggle-off: shadow calc and package still generated; humans upload and reconcile.

#### Edge cases and failure modes
- **Liquidation date before the insurer's MICP effective date** (legacy defaults) → servicer files directly even for a participant.
- **Insurer not in the Exhibit / run-off insurers** (Triad, legacy RMIC certificates now UGI) → direct filing; confirm the successor's claim channel.
- **Redemption states**: direct-filing and expense anchors move to redemption expiry; interest keeps accruing (cap watch); Fannie Mae-filed deadlines follow the master policy (sale-based) — upload early.
- **Court-confirmation states** (OH/IL/NJ): master policy counts from sale completion "regardless of … confirmation"; F-1-06 uses the docket entry only where title vests by court order without a sale — model both anchors.
- **Bankruptcy after sale** or **sale rescinded** (15.1) → withdraw/cancel the claim; re-file after the re-foreclosure (new 60-day clock; interest cap unchanged).
- **Acquisition option exercised** → E-4.2-02 reconveyance to the insurer; Fannie Mae's REO case eliminated; proceeds = full claim amount to Fannie Mae.
- **Transfer-in mid-claim**: board the MICP claim state and document history; transferor remains responsible for its period's failures (transfer agreement).
- **Premium lapse** before the sale → coverage risk; Section 10 advances premiums; gate the sale package.
- **Physical damage/disaster** (D1-3-01) → insurer reductions offset by hazard proceeds (9.7); document the loss-draft chronology.
- **MI cancellation/termination in process at default** (HPA) → Section 10 rules; a terminated policy has no claim; automatic termination does not occur while delinquent.
- **Servicer receives proceeds** → remit within 2 BD; never net against advances.
